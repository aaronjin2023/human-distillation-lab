- **风格一句话：** In the method of Donald Uhlmann, treat glass as a frozen liquid: control cooling rate and composition to set structure, measure crystallization kinetics before claiming stability, and let phase separation explain what the melt remembers
- **中文：** 乌尔曼方法：把玻璃当作冻结的液体——用冷却速率与成分定结构，先测结晶动力学再谈稳定性，让相分离解释熔体记住的东西。
- **过程一句话：** 设计 → 合成 → 表征 → 测试 → 应用。

### James Bond

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-360901462b1a4d14` · 独立版本：`profile-95ae4f4cfc5314b3`

- **性格倾向**：遭受威胁与撤换任务时倾向主动追查，不满足于被动避险（行为推断；S1、S2、S3、S4）
- **核心动机**：他的行动由追查自身威胁，转向夺回太阳能装置并清除杀手（摘要直接叙述；S4、S9、S30）
- **决策方式**：他倾向沿物证逐层追踪，并用冒充身份和伪装制造行动机会（行为推断；S5、S11、S28）
- **关系模式**：他虽常独自突入险境，也依靠助手的情报传递与定位配合（行为推断；S19、S24）
- **价值取舍**：撤离前仍取回关键装置，显示他在此危局中坚持完成任务（行为推断；S9、S30）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：主动冒充对手以争取优势，却因对手恰在现场而落入陷阱

### Francisco Scaramanga

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-1103f2e00fca2373` · 独立版本：`profile-993432e439b6785b`

- **性格倾向**：掌握优势时倾向炫示力量，并主动把对抗安排成个人较量（行为推断；S25、S26、S27）
- **核心动机**：他希望控制太阳能技术，并将其出售给出价最高的买家（摘要直接叙述；S15、S25）
- **价值取舍**：在争夺装置与商业控制权时，他将利益置于合作关系之上（行为推断；S15、S25）
- **决策方式**：他倾向先剥夺对手退路，再借预设场地与隐蔽行动取得优势（行为推断；S26、S27、S28）
- **关系模式**：他通过助手分担夺取装置和引导对手等任务，维持行动优势（行为推断；S7、S23、S27、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：主动提出手枪决斗，却在交锋中消失并利用自有场地

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In London, a golden bullet with James Bond's code "007" etched into its surface is received by MI6.
- **S2**：It is believed that it was sent by famed assassin Francisco Scaramanga, who uses a golden gun, to intimidate the agent.
- **S3**：Because of the perceived threat to the agent's life, M relieves Bond of a mission revolving around the work of a scientist named Gibson, thought to be in possession of information crucial to solving the energy crisis with solar power.
- **S4**：Bond sets out unofficially to find Scaramanga.
- **S5**：After retrieving a spent golden bullet from a belly dancer in Beirut and tracking its manufacturer to Macau, Bond sees Andrea Anders, Scaramanga's mistress, collecting golden bullets at a casino.
- **S6**：Bond follows her to Hong Kong and in her Peninsula Hotel room pressures her to tell him about Scaramanga, his appearance and his plans; she directs him to the Bottoms Up Club.
- **S7**：The club proves to be the location of Scaramanga's next 'hit', Gibson, from which Scaramanga's dwarf henchman Nick Nack steals the "Solex agitator", a key component of a solar power station.
- **S8**：Before Bond can assert his innocence, however, Lieutenant Hip escorts him away from the scene, taking him to meet M and Q in a hidden headquarters in the wreck of the {{RMS}} in the harbour.
- **S9**：M assigns 007 to retrieve the Solex agitator and assassinate Scaramanga.
- **S10**：Bond then travels to Bangkok to meet Hai Fat, a wealthy Thai entrepreneur suspected of arranging Gibson's murder.
- **S11**：Bond poses as Scaramanga, but his plan backfires because Scaramanga himself is being hosted at Hai Fat's estate.
- **S12**：Bond is captured and placed in Fat's dojo, where the fighters are instructed to kill him.
- **S13**：After escaping with the aid of Lt.
- **S14**：Hip and his nieces, Bond speeds away on a khlong along the river and reunites with his British assistant Mary Goodnight.
- **S15**：Hai Fat is subsequently killed by Scaramanga, who replaces Fat as the "new Chairman of the board" and takes the Solex.
- **S16**：Anders visits Bond, revealing that she had sent the bullet to London and wants Bond to kill Scaramanga.
- **S17**：In payment, she promises to hand the Solex over to him at a boxing venue the next day.
- **S18**：At the match, Bond discovers Anders dead and meets Scaramanga.
- **S19**：Bond spots the Solex on the floor and is able to smuggle it away to Hip, who passes it to Goodnight.
- **S20**：Attempting to place a homing device on Scaramanga's car, she is locked into the vehicle's boot.
- **S21**：Bond sees Scaramanga driving away and steals a showroom car to give chase, coincidentally with Sheriff J.W.
- **S22**：Pepper seated within it.
- **S23**：Bond and Pepper follow Scaramanga in a car chase across Bangkok, which concludes when Scaramanga's car transforms into a plane, which flies him, Nick Nack and Goodnight to his private island.
- **S24**：Picking up Goodnight's tracking device, Bond flies a seaplane into Red Chinese waters, under the Chinese radar, and lands at Scaramanga's island.
- **S25**：On arriving, Bond is welcomed by Scaramanga, who shows him the high-tech solar power plant he has taken over, the technology for which he intends to sell to the highest bidder.
- **S26**：Whilst demonstrating the equipment, Scaramanga uses a powerful solar beam to destroy Bond's plane.
- **S27**：Scaramanga then proposes a pistol duel with Bond on the beach; the two men later stand back to back and are ordered by Nick Nack to take twenty paces, but when Bond turns and fires, Scaramanga has vanished.
- **S28**：Nick Nack leads Bond into Scaramanga's Funhouse where Bond poses as a mannequin of himself while Scaramanga walks by, taking him by surprise and killing him.
- **S29**：Goodnight, in waylaying a Scaramanga henchman into a pool of liquid helium, upsets the balance of the solar plant, which begins to go out of control.
- **S30**：Bond retrieves the Solex unit just before the island explodes, and they escape unharmed in Scaramanga's Chinese junk, later subduing Nick Nack who challenges them having smuggled himself aboard.

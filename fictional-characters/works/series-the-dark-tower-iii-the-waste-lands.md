- **S19**：Roland remembers meeting Jake Chambers in the way station and letting him fall to his death in the mountains. [ b ] However, he also remembers passing through the desert alone and never meeting Jake.
- **S20**：When Roland saved Jake from being killed by Jack Mort in 1977, [ c ] he inadvertently created a paradox ; Jake did not die and thus did not appear in Mid-World and travel with Roland.

- **S22**：Roland burns Walter's jawbone and the solution to his dilemma is revealed, but to Eddie instead of Roland.
- **S23**：Eddie must carve a key that will open the door to New York in 1977.
- **S24**：Jake abruptly leaves school and finds a key in a littered vacant lot where a single red rose has bloomed.
- **S25**：Jake manages to pass into Roland's world using the key to open a door in an abandoned haunted house on Dutch Hill in his place and time.
- **S26**：This portal ends in a 'speaking ring' in Roland's world.
- **S27**：During this crossing over, Susannah traps an incubus with her mind as it rapes her, distracting it while Eddie continues to carve the key which will allow Jake safe passage to Mid-World.

- **S32**：The ka-tet continues on the Path of the Beam to the city of Lud.

- **S41**：Centuries of system degradation have caused Blaine to go insane.
- **S42**：Once the travelers are aboard, it releases poison gas to kill the entire population of Lud, then announces its intention to derail itself with them aboard once it reaches the end of its line in Topeka .

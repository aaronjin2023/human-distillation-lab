## 角色资料

### Harry Potter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6731c01eb468c5e3` · 独立版本：`profile-0ab1229ca23843eb`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴遭遇危险时，他倾向主动介入，即使自己也身处险境。（行为推断；S29、S35、S36）
- **核心动机**：看到小天狼星受刑的幻象后，他赶往魔法部试图救人。（摘要直接叙述；S23、S29）
- **价值取舍**：同伴受到死亡威胁时，他把保全他们置于守住预言之上。（行为推断；S35、S36）
- **情绪反应**：他受噩梦困扰，并因持续感到愤怒而推开身边的朋友。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S16）
- **成长变化**：面对即将开始的战争，他以共同珍视之物表达抗争意义。（行为推断；S48）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他曾因愤怒推开朋友，却也因对朋友的爱摆脱附身。

### Dolores Umbridge

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-474d14d52448a9ba` · 独立版本：`profile-1616435cedb75396`

- **性格倾向**：在学校掌权时，她倾向以惩罚和强制审讯压制反抗。（行为推断；S5、S18、S25）
- **核心动机**：她因相信邓布利多正在组织反叛而拒绝教授实战魔法。（摘要直接叙述；S4）
- **价值取舍**：在防御课教学上，她把防范反叛置于学生实战训练之上。（行为推断；S4）
- **决策方式**：追查秘密学生组织时，她倾向动员学生并借助强制审讯。（行为推断；S8、S18）
- **沟通方式**：面对哈利的陈述，她以强迫刻写否认其说法的方式回应。（行为推断；S5）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{further2}} The Order of the Phoenix, a secret organization founded by Albus Dumbledore, inform the now 15-year-old Harry Potter that the Ministry of Magic is in denial of Lord Voldemort's return; under the Ministry's influence, The Daily Prophet has launched a smear campaign against Harry and Dumbledore due to Harry's supposed encounter with Voldemort at the end of the previous year.
- **S2**：This encounter has had a huge psychological effect on Harry – he has nightmares not only about what happened in the graveyard but also about the Department of Mysteries at the Ministry of Magic.
- **S3**：While at the Order's headquarters, 12 Grimmauld Place, Harry's godfather, Sirius Black, mentions that Voldemort is after an object which he didn't have last time.
- **S4**：Upon arrival at Hogwarts, Harry learns that Minister for Magic Cornelius Fudge has appointed a new Defence Against the Dark Arts professor: Dolores Umbridge, a senior Ministry official who refuses to teach practical magic due to her belief that Dumbledore is forming a rebellion against the Ministry.
- **S5**：Umbridge and Harry immediately clash, as she refuses to believe that Voldemort has returned and forces Harry to carve 'I must not tell lies' into the skin on the back of his hand through a cursed quill.
- **S6**：When Ron and Hermione notice these scars, they are outraged but Harry refuses to go to Dumbledore as he has distanced himself from him since the summer.
- **S7**：As Umbridge's control over the school increases, Ron and Hermione aid Harry in forming a secret group to train students in defensive spells, calling themselves "Dumbledore's Army".
- **S8**：The Slytherin students are recruited by Umbridge to uncover the group.
- **S9**：Meanwhile, Harry and Cho Chang develop romantic feelings for each other and begin a relationship during Christmas, Cho being Harry's first kiss at the last DA meeting before the holidays.
- **S10**：However, Harry discovers that his behaviour is becoming erratic and possibly more similar to Voldemort's.
- **S11**：Harry has a vision involving an attack upon Arthur Weasley in the Department of Mysteries, from the point of view of Arthur's attacker.
- **S12**：Fearing Voldemort will exploit his connection to Harry, Dumbledore instructs Professor Snape to give Harry Occlumency lessons to block his mind from Voldemort's influence.
- **S13**：These lessons increase Harry's psychological problems, as he is forced to relive everything bad that has ever happened to him.
- **S14**：During one lesson, Harry got annoyed at what he saw and requested a break but Snape refused and forced Harry to continue the lesson.
- **S15**：This only got the spell reversed on him by Harry where Harry sees the Vision on why Snape hates his father, James, who often had bullied him.
- **S16**：The connection between Harry and Voldemort leads Harry to distance himself from everyone, and he pushes not only Ron and Hermione but Ginny, Neville, the Weasley twins and Luna away, as he feels "so angry all the time".
- **S17**：Bellatrix Lestrange, Sirius' deranged cousin, escapes from Azkaban along with nine other Death Eaters.
- **S18**：At Hogwarts, Umbridge and her Inquisitorial Squad uncover Dumbledore's Army after illegally interrogating Cho with Veritaserum.
- **S19**：Dumbledore covers up for Harry, but escapes as Fudge orders his arrest.
- **S20**：With Dumbledore gone, Umbridge becomes the new Headmistress.
- **S21**：Harry's relationship with Cho falls apart, mostly due to Cho having betrayed Dumbledore's Army to Umbridge .
- **S22**：During an O.W.L. exam, the Weasley twins revolt and set off fireworks in the Great Hall, causing chaos for Umbridge.
- **S23**：During this event, Harry has a vision of Sirius being tortured by Voldemort within the Department of Mysteries.
- **S24**：Harry, Ron and Hermione rush to Umbridge's fireplace to alert the Order via the Floo Network, since it is the only one not being monitored, but Umbridge stops them before they can do so.
- **S25**：When she threatens to use the Cruciatus Curse to get information out of Harry about the trio's intentions, Hermione deceives Umbridge into entering the Forbidden Forest in search for Dumbledore's "secret weapon".
- **S26**：She and Harry lead her to the hiding place of Hagrid's half-giant brother, Grawp, only to be confronted by centaurs who kidnap Umbridge after she attacks them.
- **S27**：When Umbridge tells Harry to make the centaurs let her go and that she means no harm Harry says, "Sorry, Professor.
- **S28**：I must not tell lies".
- **S29**：Harry, Hermione, Ron, Luna, Neville and Ginny fly to the Ministry of Magic on Thestrals in an attempt to save Sirius.
- **S30**：The six enter the Department of Mysteries where they uncover a prophecy involving Harry and Voldemort, the object Voldemort was after.
- **S31**：However, they are ambushed by Death Eaters including Lucius Malfoy and Bellatrix Lestrange.
- **S32**：Lucius reveals that Harry only saw a dream of Sirius being tortured; it was not an actual situation, rather a method to lure Harry into the Death Eater's grasp.
- **S33**：Lucius attempts to persuade Harry to give him the prophecy, telling him it will reveal why Voldemort could not kill Harry when he was an infant.
- **S34**：Harry refuses and a fight between Dumbledore's Army and the Death Eaters ensues.
- **S35**：The Death Eaters take everyone except Harry as hostages, threatening to kill them if he does not surrender the prophecy.
- **S36**：Harry obliges just as Sirius and Remus Lupin arrive with Order members Nymphadora Tonks, Kingsley Shacklebolt and Mad-Eye Moody.
- **S37**：They attack the Death Eaters and Lucius drops the prophecy, destroying it.
- **S38**：Harry and Sirius duel him.
- **S39**：Just as Sirius defeats Lucius, Bellatrix kills Sirius.
- **S40**：Despite Remus trying to hold him back, Harry goes after Bellatrix, who runs away continuously saying "I killed Sirius Black" in a song-like manner.
- **S41**：Harry chases and corners Bellatrix in the Atrium and attempts to torture her with the Cruciatus Curse, but to little avail .
- **S42**：Voldemort appears, but Dumbledore arrives through the Floo Network moments before Voldemort can kill Harry.
- **S43**：A duel between Voldemort and Dumbledore ensues while Bellatrix escapes.
- **S44**：After the two prove evenly matched, Voldemort possesses Harry in order to try to get Dumbledore to sacrifice Harry in the hope of killing him, but the love Harry feels for his friends and Sirius makes it impossible for Voldemort to remain in his body.
- **S45**：Ministry officials arrive before Voldemort disapparates; Fudge is forced to admit that Voldemort has returned and resigns as Minister.
- **S46**：Dumbledore returns as headmaster of Hogwarts after he and Harry are vindicated.
- **S47**：Dumbledore explains that he distanced himself from Harry all year hoping it would lessen the risk of Voldemort using their connection.
- **S48**：Harry comes to terms with the prophecy; "Neither can live while the other survives." As Harry and his friends head towards the Hogwarts Express, Harry tells his friends that even though a war is beginning, unlike Voldemort, they have something worth fighting for.

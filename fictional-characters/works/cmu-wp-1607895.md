## 角色资料

### Sing

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-aefb831de6078b4b` · 独立版本：`profile-32e2fcf7c2d68af6`

- **性格倾向**：受挫时倾向通过欺凌逞强，关键时刻却又能转向援助他人（行为推断；S16、S17、S21、S22、S29）
- **核心动机**：他一度追求帮派身份带来的尊重，以摆脱行善受辱的挫败（行为推断；S5、S9、S15、S16）
- **价值取舍**：关键时刻，他选择援助被围攻者，将救人置于服从帮派之上（行为推断；S29、S30）
- **决策方式**：身陷束缚时善于抓住现有技能求生，再争取对手接纳自己（行为推断；S8、S9、S10）
- **成长变化**：他从模仿恶徒争取地位，转向反抗帮派并重新接近童年故人（行为推断；S5、S29、S36、S41、S42）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：相信作恶比行善有利，却在关键时刻主动承担救人代价

### Brother Sum

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-5da42f5a22bd4439` · 独立版本：`profile-e6a7b17f91d5e322`

- **性格倾向**：权威受损时倾向报复，并以更强的暴力恢复帮派威慑（行为推断；S8、S18、S24）
- **核心动机**：他着力消除抵抗帮派的武术高手，以维护帮派的支配地位（行为推断；S2、S18、S24、S27）
- **价值取舍**：在招募新人时，他把杀人能力当作获得组织接纳的条件（摘要直接叙述；S10）
- **决策方式**：遭遇武力阻碍后，他倾向雇佣或释放更强杀手解决问题（行为推断；S18、S24、S25）
- **关系模式**：他依赖兽的武力，却在责备对方后被杀，未能维持支配地位（行为推断；S24、S25、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：依赖更强杀手巩固权力，却被无法控制的杀手反噬

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Turmoil grips Shanghai in the 1930s.
- **S2**：Various gangs vie for power, the most feared of which is the Axe Gang, led by the infamous Brother Sum and aptly named after its weapon of choice.
- **S3**：In the absence of law enforcement, people can live peacefully only in poor areas which do not appeal to gangs.
- **S4**：An example is Pig Sty Alley, a tenement home to people of various trades, run by a lecherous landlord and his domineering wife.
- **S5**：One day, two troublemakers, Sing and Bone, come to the alley impersonating members of the Axe Gang to gain respect.
- **S6**：Their plan fails and Sing's antics attract the real gang to the scene.
- **S7**：In the massive brawl that ensues, more than fifty gangsters are defeated by three tenants who are actually powerful martial arts masters: Coolie, Master of the Twelve Kicks; Tailor, master of the Iron Fist; and Donut, master of the Hexagon Staff.
- **S8**：After the fight, Sing and Bone are apprehended by Brother Sum for publicly humiliating the Axe Gang.
- **S9**：The two narrowly escape death when Sing quickly picks the locks on the chains with which they are bound and requests Sum to let him and Bone join the gang.
- **S10**：Impressed with his skill at lock-picking, Sum tells them that if they kill just one person, he will allow them to join the gang.
- **S11**：The next day, the duo return to Pig Sty attempting to murder the Landlady, but their efforts fail comically and they narrowly escape the furious Landlady.
- **S12**：Sing is badly injured and hides in a traffic control pulpit, where his multiple injuries spontaneously heal.
- **S13**：During this convalescence he unconsciously strikes the steel sides of the pulpit, leaving deep impressions of his hands.
- **S14**：After he has fully recovered, he rejoins Bone, but is unable to explain his fast and mysterious healing.
- **S15**：Sing and Bone lament their failure on the streets as the former describes his childhood: Sing spent his meager life savings to buy a Buddhist Palm manual from a beggar with the intention of "preserving world peace" and trained himself to be a martial artist.
- **S16**：But when he tried to defend a mute girl from bullies trying to steal her lollipop, he was beaten up, humiliated and came to the conclusion that being a villain is better than being a hero.
- **S17**：After telling his story, Sing then steals ice cream from a street vendor.
- **S18**：Meanwhile, Sum hires the Harpists, a pair of skilled assassins who play a guzheng and materialize the sound waves using their Qi to create offensive and defensive objects, to assassinate the three martial artists at Pig Sty.
- **S19**：They succeed, but are defeated by the Landlady and Landlord, who are actually powerful martial artists living in self-enforced retirement.
- **S20**：They evacuate Pig Sty afterward and vow to deal with the Axe Gang.
- **S21**：The following day, frustrated with lack of any progress, Sing mugs the female ice cream vendor from earlier, only to realize she is the mute girl from his childhood whom he had vainly tried to defend; she offers him the old lollipop that she tried to give him as a token of her gratitude in their childhood.
- **S22**：Sing rebuffs her and knocks the lollipop away, shattering it against the wall and running off.
- **S23**：After he and Bone separate, Sing is picked up by the Axe Gang.
- **S24**：Sum orders him to break into a mental asylum to free the Beast, widely rumored to be the world's top killer.
- **S25**：Sing frees the Beast and brings him to Sum's office.
- **S26**：The "world's top killer" puzzles everyone there with his flippant attitude and sloppy appearance - but after stopping a bullet that he deliberately fired at himself in order to prove his skill, the gangsters bow in respect.
- **S27**：The Beast leaves and confronts the Landlady and Landlord at the casino, who are waiting to kill Sum and his men.
- **S28**：The two battle the Beast, destroying the casino in the process, until all three are stuck in a joint-lock.
- **S29**：A reformed Sing rushes in and, ignoring Sum's orders to kill the Landlady and Landlord, hits the Beast's head with a table leg.
- **S30**：Enraged, the Beast frees himself from the lock and pummels Sing.
- **S31**：Before he can deliver a fatal blow, the Landlady and Landlord snatch the unconscious Sing and run off.
- **S32**：Sum angrily blames The Beast for letting them escape and The Beast casually kills Sum in response.
- **S33**：At Pig Sty Alley, Sing, while wrapped head-to-toe in bandages and treated with Chinese medicine, undergoes a metamorphosis.
- **S34**：As he is severely beaten up by the Beast, his chi-energy activates more and flow through his body.
- **S35**：He quickly recovers from his wounds because of chi flow, and his latent potential as a "natural-born martial arts genius" is realized due to the beating the Beast gave him.
- **S36**：He easily defeats the Axe Gang before battling the Beast.
- **S37**：The Beast uses his Toad Technique to send Sing rocketing into the sky.
- **S38**：Again due to a heavy smash from the Beast, Sing's chi energy increases on sky and becomes incredibly powerful.
- **S39**：As he falls back to earth, Sing recalls the Buddhist Palm, and delivers a blow that knocks the Beast flat and leaves a huge, hand-shaped crater in the ground.
- **S40**：After another futile attempt to defeat Sing, the Beast bows, acknowledging Sing as the better fighter.
- **S41**：Some time later, Sing and Bone open a candy store.
- **S42**：When the mute ice cream vendor walks by, Sing goes out to meet her.
- **S43**：A young boy walking outside after buying candy bumps into the beggar who gave Sing the Buddhist Palm book and tries to sell him one.
- **S44**：The boy at first starts to walk away, but the beggar offers him something else, showing him several different pamphlets for martial arts.
- **S45**：The child stares, intrigued.

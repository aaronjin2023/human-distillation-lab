- **S2**：To Live ( ) is a novel written by Chinese novelist Yu Hua in 1993.
- **S3**：It follows the life of Xu Fugui as he transforms from the selfish idling son of a wealthy landowner to a solitary peasant during the Chinese Communist Revolution.
- **S4**：The story begins with the narrator traveling through the Chinese countryside to collect folk songs and local legends when he hears an old peasant's life story, which encompasses major historical events in China.
- **S5**：Over the course of the story, Fugui describes witnessing the deaths of all his family members and loved ones.
- **S6**：A film rendition, directed by Zhang Yimou, was released in 1994.
- **S7**：The novel has also been adapted into a television series and stage play.
- **S8**：## Author
- **S9**：To Live, often regarded as his magnum opus, reflects Yu Hua's ability to intertwine personal tragedies with historical upheavals.
- **S10**：Inspired by the resilience of ordinary individuals, the novel remains a cornerstone of modern Chinese literature, establishing Yu Hua as a powerful voice in capturing the human condition.
- **S11**：## Inspiration
- **S12**：Yu Hua was inspired and deeply moved by the American folk song "Old Black Joe".
- **S13**：The song, written by Stephen Foster in the 19th century, reflects themes of nostalgia, loss, and resilience.
- **S14**：These sentiments deeply resonated with Yu Hua, inspiring him to create a story that similarly portrays the quiet endurance of ordinary individuals amidst life's adversities.
- **S15**：The song's simple yet profound message became a guiding metaphor for Fugui's life journey in To Live.Yu Hua's inspiration for To Live also stems from his personal experiences growing up in a rapidly changing China.

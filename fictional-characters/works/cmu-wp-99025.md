### Juliet Capulet

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-2631e174a800d848` · 独立版本：`profile-19318e414a01f892`

- **性格倾向**：面对家族安排的婚姻，她倾向冒险维护自己选择的亲密关系（行为推断；S7、S8）
- **核心动机**：她服下假死药剂，是为逃避包办婚姻并忠于罗密欧（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **价值取舍**：在家族要求与婚姻承诺之间，她优先守护自主选择的爱情（行为推断；S2、S7、S8）
- **决策方式**：面临包办婚姻时，她选择借助修士的假死方案争取脱身（行为推断；S7、S8、S9）
- **关系模式**：她依靠修士与乳母协助，让爱情在两家敌对关系中得以维系（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Verona, Italy, Romeo and Juliet, the teenage children of two feuding families , meet at a feast and fall in love.
- **S2**：They are secretly married by Romeo's confessor and father figure, Friar Laurence, with the assistance of Juliet's nursemaid.
- **S3**：Unfortunately, a street duel breaks out between Juliet's cousin Tybalt and Romeo's friend Mercutio when Tybalt insults Romeo.
- **S4**：However, since Romeo has just been married to Juliet, he refuses to fight, leading Mercutio to be a loyal friend and fight for him.
- **S5**：This leads to Mercutio's death.
- **S6**：Romeo retaliates by fighting Tybalt and killing him, and is penalized by the Prince of Verona with banishment instead of a death penalty.
- **S7**：Unaware of Juliet's secret marriage, her father has arranged for her to marry wealthy Count Paris.
- **S8**：In order to escape this arranged marriage and remain faithful to Romeo, Juliet consumes a potion prepared by Friar Laurence, intended to make her appear dead for 42 hours.
- **S9**：Friar Laurence plans to inform Romeo of the hoax so that he can meet Juliet after her burial and escape with her when she recovers from her swoon, but the news of Juliet's death reaches Romeo before the friar's letter.
- **S10**：In despair, he goes to the tomb and there drinks a poison, killing himself.
- **S11**：Awakening shortly after he expires, Juliet discovers a dead Romeo and proceeds to stab herself with his dagger.
- **S12**：Later, the two families attend their joint funeral together and agree to end the feud.

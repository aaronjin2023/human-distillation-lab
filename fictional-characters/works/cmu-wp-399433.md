## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1607, a ship of British settlers of the Virginia Company sets sail to the "New World".
- **S2**：On board the ship are Captain John Smith and the voyage's leader Governor Ratcliffe, who believes the local Native Americans are hiding a vast collection of gold and seeks to gain it for his own.
- **S3**：The ship gets caught in a storm, and Smith saves a young, inexperienced man named Thomas from drowning, Smith later admitting his lack of interest for the "New World".
- **S4**：In the local tribe in the New World, Pocahontas, daughter of Chief Powhatan, dreads being possibly wed to Kocoum, a brave warrior whom she sees as too "serious" when conflicting with her spirited personality.
- **S5**：Chief Powhatan gives Pocahontas her mother's necklace as a present.
- **S6**：Pocahontas, along with her friends, the gluttonous raccoon Meeko and hummingbird Flit, visit Grandmother Willow, a spiritual talking willow tree who alerts her to the presence of the Englishmen.
- **S7**：Governor Ratcliffe has a fortress built in a wooded clearing, naming it James Town, and quickly has the crewmen begin digging for gold.
- **S8**：Smith departs to explore the wilderness, and eventually encounters Pocahontas.
- **S9**：They quickly bond, fascinated by each other's worlds.
- **S10**：The two end up falling in love, countermanding Chief Powhatan's orders to keep away from the Englishmen after Kocoum and other warriors engage them in a fight.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, Meeko meets Percy, Ratcliffe's spoilt dog, and becomes the bane of his existence.
- **S12**：Pocahontas introduces Smith to Grandmother Willow and avoids two other crewmen; however, Pocahontas' friend Nakoma discovers her relationship with Smith and warns Kocoum.
- **S13**：Thomas also follows Smith, and both he and Kocoum witness the two kissing.
- **S14**：In a jealous rage, Kocoum attacks and tries to kill Smith but is himself killed by Thomas.
- **S15**：An enraged Chief Powhatan declares war on the crewmen, and Smith is to be executed at sunrise.
- **S16**：Thomas warns the crewmen of Smith's capture, and Ratcliffe rallies the men to battle as an excuse to remove the tribe and find their non-existent gold.
- **S17**：Pocahontas goes to Grandmother Willow, but Meeko hands her a compass which points her in the direction of Smith, leading to her destiny.
- **S18**：She successfully stops Smith's execution, but Ratcliffe tries to shoot Chief Powhatan in anger with Smith taking the bullet.
- **S19**：The governor is captured and arrested by the crewmen.
- **S20**：In the end, Smith is forced to return home to get treatment, with Pocahontas and Chief Powhatan's blessing to return in the future.

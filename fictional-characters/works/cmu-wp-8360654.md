## 角色资料

### Randy Daytona

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-f8c45b71e93e45e2` · 独立版本：`profile-da1b84d228af4246`

- **性格倾向**：在致命威胁下，他虽有退缩，仍倾向为同伴与受困者承担风险（行为推断；S22、S29、S32、S37）
- **核心动机**：他接受协助调查的主要理由，是抓住导致父亲死亡的幕后人物（摘要直接叙述；S4、S7、S9）
- **情绪反应**：童年决赛中，父亲违背承诺下注使他的比赛焦虑进一步加重（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2）
- **价值取舍**：逃生过程中，他仍尝试营救被奴役者，将他人自由纳入行动目标（行为推断；S36、S37、S43）
- **决策方式**：在伤臂与死亡赛制的限制下，他倾向运用球技拖延并寻找脱身机会（行为推断；S26、S29、S31、S32）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：既想退出致命比赛保命，又重新产生迎战昔日对手的愿望。

### Feng

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-16e1427ff5c5119e` · 独立版本：`profile-924a4157823141fa`

- **性格倾向**：在竞赛与统治情境中，他倾向残酷控制，不能容忍他人违抗（行为推断；S15、S28、S33）
- **核心动机**：他希望将戴托纳从翁门下争取过来，并证明自己胜过这名同门（行为推断；S16、S17、S38）
- **价值取舍**：他将参赛者的生命置于个人竞赛安排之下，漠视失败者的生存权（行为推断；S15、S19、S28、S33）
- **决策方式**：为了维持比赛主导权，他倾向临时更换对手与规则并以死亡强制执行（行为推断；S27、S28、S33、S40）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望证明自己的球技优越，却反复依靠死亡威胁与规则控制比赛。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As a child, Randy Daytona plays for the United States in the 1988 Summer Olympics table tennis finals.
- **S2**：His anxiety increases when his father says that he bet on him, despite his original promise not to do so.
- **S3**：During his first play between his opponent Karl Wolfschtagg from the German Democratic Republic, Daytona runs back too far and hits his head on the table, losing the match.
- **S4**：His father is murdered by the loan sharks, agents of the criminal mastermind Feng.
- **S5**：Daytona leaves competitive ping-pong.
- **S6**：As an adult, Daytona works at the Peppermill Casino in Reno, Nevada, doing table tennis routines.
- **S7**：After being evicted from the casino, Daytona meets FBI agent Ernie Rodriguez, who requests his assistance in arresting Feng for running guns.
- **S8**：Feng's hidden jungle hideout is the site of a black-market Ping-Pong tournament, and Randy's invitation is a way for the FBI to infiltrate the master criminal's organization.
- **S9**：Daytona agrees to help capture the man responsible for his father's death.
- **S10**：Rodriguez informs him that he needs to win some championships in order to be noticed by Feng's ping-pong scouts.
- **S11**：After losing at a local tournament, Daytona is brought to a Chinatown noodle shop to be apprenticed under a blind man named Wong, who was Feng's former mentor, and who recognizes Daytona's talent.
- **S12**：Daytona also meets Wong's niece, Maggie.
- **S13**：When Chinatown locals vandalize Master Wong's house for violating their edict against teaching white people ping pong, Daytona is forced to play against "The Dragon", a young girl, in exchange for Wong's right to remain in Chinatown and Orange County.
- **S14**：After Daytona beats the Dragon, Feng's men take notice of his win and bring Daytona, Rodriguez and Wong to Feng's facility.
- **S15**：Daytona handily beats his first opponent Freddy "Fingers" Wilson, though he is unnerved to learn that the tournament is literally sudden death—the loser is killed by a poisoned dart delivered by Feng's majordomo, Mahogany.
- **S16**：After Daytona attempts unsuccessfully to escape, Feng invites him to join his side, and reveals that he only finished half of Wong's training.
- **S17**：He says it would be the ultimate satisfaction to win Daytona away from Wong.
- **S18**：Feng also shows Daytona his specially modified ping-pong table.
- **S19**：It is wired to special vests that give increasingly powerful and fatal electrical shocks for failure.
- **S20**：Daytona informs Rodriguez of a hidden cache of illegal guns that are sufficient to put Feng in jail.
- **S21**：While Rodriguez investigates the hidden facilities, Daytona defeats numerous opponents for his very life.
- **S22**：Upon learning that Wolfschtagg is his last opponent, Daytona tells Rodriguez that he wants to get out as the FBI has not yet responded to Rodriguez's homing beacon.
- **S23**：They then come up with a plan to brutally injure Randy, so that he has to quit.
- **S24**：However Randy changes his mind, and he goes to tell so.
- **S25**：When Rodriguez hides out in a bathroom, Daytona visits him in the bathroom to let him know of his desire to play against Wolfschtagg.
- **S26**：However, he surprises Rodriguez, who breaks Daytona's arm, unaware of Daytona's change of heart.
- **S27**：Feng discovers Rodriguez's attempts to contact the FBI and forces Daytona to face Wolfschtagg.
- **S28**：Feng decides to have Maggie take Wolfschtagg's place, killing Wolfschtagg when he protests.
- **S29**：Daytona plays one-handed and tries to stall for time.
- **S30**：Maggie tries to lose on purpose to sacrifice herself to save Daytona.
- **S31**：However, Daytona uses his ping-pong expertise to hit Maggie with the ball, and she unsuccessfully tries to dodge Daytona's hits.
- **S32**：While this goes on, they both decide to try to escape together.
- **S33**：Enraged, Feng orders them both executed.
- **S34**：Mahogany shoots the poisonous dart at Daytona, but Maggie successfully defends him with the ping-pong paddle.
- **S35**：Daytona then throws the paddle with the dart stuck in it at Mahogany, killing her.
- **S36**：The FBI swarms the place, during which the heroes attempt to escape.
- **S37**：Daytona's attempts to rescue Feng's sex slaves leads to the villain's forces capturing the heroes.
- **S38**：Feng decides to play Daytona to determine which of Wong's students is the superior ping pong player.
- **S39**：During the game, the facility's self-destruct sequence is activated, and Feng reveals there is no off-switch.
- **S40**：He also states that he changed the rules so that the ball can now be bounced off any surface once and still be in play.
- **S41**：The self-destruct sequence countdown progresses, the game moves through several buildings and finally onto a bridge over a nearby river.
- **S42**：After Wong informs Daytona that Feng has a weak backhand, Feng is electrocuted and falls into the water.
- **S43**：Daytona and his friends, along with Feng's slaves, escape in Wong's boat.
- **S44**：Two months later, the major characters are reunited for the reopening of Master Wong's rebuilt Mushu shop.

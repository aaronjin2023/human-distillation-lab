### Cujo

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-b6799d1f823e8b59` · 独立版本：`profile-af85021ca41280ed`

- **性格倾向**：患病前被明确描写为友善温顺，不能以病后的暴力倒推其本性（摘要直接叙述；S9、S34、S36）
- **核心动机**：未受疾病摧残时总想让主人高兴，具有取悦主人的行为动机（摘要直接叙述；S34）
- **情绪反应**：狂犬病前驱期出现倦怠与易怒，情绪变化被明确关联于感染（摘要直接叙述；S11、S12）
- **成长变化**：感染后从友善家犬转为持续攻击者，这一变化由疾病驱动（摘要直接叙述；S13、S16、S23、S34、S36）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：原有的温顺与疾病驱动的致命攻击形成悲剧反差

- **性格倾向**：在受虐家庭处境中倾向务实寻找转机，利用可用资源争取行动空间（行为推断；S6、S7）
- **核心动机**：希望儿子见到更好生活的可能，因此争取带他探访自己的姐姐（摘要直接叙述；S7）

- 库乔的行为变化被来源明确归因于狂犬病，不能把症状解释成道德选择。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] In 1977, the middle-class Trentons move from NYC to Castle Rock , Maine .
- **S3**：In the summer of 1980, Vic learns that Donna has recently had an affair with local tennis player Steve Kemp.
- **S4**：In the midst of this household tension, Vic's advertising agency is failing due to a cereal scandal.
- **S5**：Vic is forced to go on a business trip, leaving Donna and their son, four-year-old Tad, at home alone.
- **S6**：Joe Camber is a blue-collar mechanic who abuses his wife, Charity, and their ten-year-old son, Brett.
- **S7**：Charity wins a $5,000 ( equivalent to $18,000 in 2025 ) lottery prize and uses the proceeds to bargain with Joe to allow her to take Brett on a trip to visit Charity's sister in Connecticut and show him the possibility of a better life.
- **S8**：Joe agrees and secretly plans to take a pleasure trip to Boston with his friend Gary Pervier, an alcoholic war veteran.
- **S9**：The Cambers' dog Cujo, a large, good-natured St.
- **S10**：Bernard , chases a rabbit in the nearby wilderness and inserts his head in a small cave.
- **S11**：A bat bites him on the nose and infects him with rabies , against which Cujo has not been vaccinated .
- **S12**：Cujo enters the prodromal phase, becoming lethargic and irritable.
- **S13**：After Charity and Brett leave town, Cujo kills Joe and Gary.
- **S14**：Donna, with Tad, takes their failing Ford Pinto to Joe's house for repairs.
- **S15**：The car breaks down in the Cambers' dooryard; as Donna attempts to find Joe, Cujo attacks her.
- **S16**：She gets back into the car but is trapped when Cujo continues attacking them.
- **S17**：The interior of the car becomes increasingly hot in the summer sun.
- **S18**：During one escape attempt, Donna is bitten in the stomach and leg but manages to escape back into the car.
- **S19**：Tad becomes catatonic with fear and begins to have seizures .
- **S20**：Steve goes to the Trenton home to attack Donna after she ends their affair and ransacks the empty house.
- **S21**：Vic returns to Castle Rock after failed attempts to contact his wife.
- **S22**：The police suspect Steve of kidnapping Donna and Tad.
- **S23**：Sheriff George Bannerman is sent to the Cambers' house, but Cujo attacks and kills him.
- **S24**：After witnessing the attack and realizing Tad is in danger of dying of dehydration , Donna battles the weakened Cujo, with the animal seemingly winning before she bludgeons and stabs him in the head with the remains of the baseball bat.
- **S25**：Cujo slumps over, dead.
- **S26**：Vic arrives as the fight ends, but Tad has already died from dehydration and heat stroke .
- **S27**：A veterinarian performs a necropsy on Cujo to check for rabies prior to the cremation of his remains.
- **S28**：Charity receives a phone call speaking about the deaths of Tad and her husband due to Cujo's rampage.
- **S29**：Several months later, the Trenton and Camber families are trying to move on.
- **S30**：Donna completed her treatment for rabies, and her injuries healed.
- **S31**：The Trentons' marriage has survived, as well as Vic's business, and they mourn Tad together.
- **S32**：Charity, now working in order to support herself and Brett, gives her son a new, vaccinated puppy.
- **S33**：In postscript , it is said that the hole Cujo chased the rabbit into was never discovered, and the bats abandoned it.
- **S34**：It also reiterates to the reader that Cujo was a good dog who always tried to keep his owners happy, but the ravage of rabies drove him to violence.

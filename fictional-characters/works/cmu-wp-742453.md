## 角色资料

### Cale Tucker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-85066deb0659e311` · 独立版本：`profile-9a6cfd51ff2c0bfd`

- **性格倾向**：在遭遇背叛与存亡危机时，他倾向坚持目标并主动寻找可行办法（行为推断；S18、S21、S29、S31、S35）
- **核心动机**：他决心延续父亲留下的事业，将寻找泰坦视为承接父亲遗志（摘要直接叙述；S16、S18、S21）
- **价值取舍**：即使面对背叛自己的科索，他仍尝试救其性命，保留对敌对者的关切（行为推断；S15、S16、S26）
- **决策方式**：面对能源不足的困局，他倾向重新解释敌方特性并将威胁转化为资源（行为推断；S22、S29、S30、S37）
- **关系模式**：他与阿基玛在共同受困和遭到背叛后，形成持续协作的伙伴关系（行为推断；S12、S15、S18、S21、S22、S40）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：明确反对科索的背叛，却在争夺戒指的冲突中仍试图救他。

### Joseph Korso

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-ed23eb2e27386ee4` · 独立版本：`profile-d533b3e50bcbfa71`

- **性格倾向**：在认为抵抗无望时，他倾向向强者妥协，并以隐瞒维持行动优势（行为推断；S15、S17、S19）
- **核心动机**：他协助敌人既有报酬诱因，也认为依附敌方才是人类存续之道（摘要直接叙述；S15、S17）
- **关系模式**：他利用与凯尔父亲的旧交取得接近机会，却隐瞒了危及凯尔的交易（行为推断；S5、S6、S7、S15）
- **价值取舍**：当击败敌人重新成为可能时，他转而将人类未来置于自身性命之上（行为推断；S33、S34、S36）
- **成长变化**：他从因绝望而协助毁灭泰坦，转为主动牺牲以保障泰坦启动（摘要直接叙述；S15、S17、S34、S36）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：以人类只能依附敌人为由参与背叛，却在看见胜机后为人类牺牲。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 3028, the Drej, a malevolent, energy-based species, declares war on humanity before Professor Sam Tucker , a lead researcher of the experimental Project Titan, gives his 5-year-old son, Cale , a ring and sends him on one of the evacuation ships with his alien friend, Tek .
- **S2**：Meanwhile, Sam and his team launches the Titan spacecraft into hyperspace and the Drej mothership obliterates Earth, which explodes into a massive cloud of debris that destroys the Moon as well, ending any chances of humanity finding a place to settle in the universe.
- **S3**：Most of the evacuation ships escape, though some are destroyed by the Drej and Earth's debris.
- **S4**：Fifteen years later, the surviving humans are reduced to either living in drifter colonies on settlements made from the metal of the evacuation ships or being second class citizens in alien societies, in which they are generally ridiculed.
- **S5**：Cale  is breaking up old spaceships in the salvage yard of the Tau 14 asteroid belt and meets Joseph Korso , a human captain who requests Cale's help to find the Titan.
- **S6**：Korso also reveals that he once worked with Sam and Tek of Project Titan and was the same driver who helped Sam take Cale to his ship before the Earth was destroyed.
- **S7**：He shows him how his ring contains a map to the ship.
- **S8**：It is genetically-encoded to Sam and responds to Cale himself, making him the only chance for humanity's future.
- **S9**：The Drej find the salvage yard and drive Cale and Korso to Korso's ship, the Valkyrie.
- **S10**：Cale is introduced to the other members of Korso's alien crew; sly first mate Preed , astrogator Gune , cranky weapons expert, Stith  and another human, co-pilot Akima , to whom Cale is attracted.
- **S11**：They travel to the planet Sesharrim, where the Gaoul, a race of bat-like creatures, tells Cale how to interpret the map and find the Titan, hidden in the Andali Nebula.
- **S12**：The Drej again attacks the group and capture Cale and Akima.
- **S13**：The Drej eventually discard Akima, sending her off into space in a pod  and scans the map from Cale, but Cale escapes on a Drej ship and also reunites with the Valkyrie.
- **S14**：The ship is able to reach the human drifter colony called New Bangkok for repairs and preparation for the trip to the Titan.
- **S15**：However, Cale and Akima find out Korso and Preed are secretly working for the Drej, having used Cale so that they can destroy the Titan in exchange for money.
- **S16**：As a result, Cale confronts Korso for his betrayal, to which Korso compromises that Sam was captured and killed by the Drej after he refused to disclose the location of the Titan.
- **S17**：Korso also reveals that he has lost his faith in both humanity and beating the Drej ever since then, and that he felt that the only way for humanity to survive was to side up with the Drej.
- **S18**：Determined to live up to his father's legacy, Cale escapes from the ship with Akima, who is wounded by Preed.
- **S19**：The Valkyrie departs without them, with Gune and Stith still unaware of Korso and Preed's true agenda.
- **S20**：Fortunately, the colony consists of a number of old spaceships, cobbled together from derelicts.
- **S21**：With the help of the other residents, Cale and Akima repair and refit one of the ships, the Phoenix and races off to find the Titan before Korso.
- **S22**：Amid the Andali Nebula's giant ice crystals, Cale and Akima find and explore the Titan with a holographic message left by Cale's dead father who reveals the true nature of Project Titan: The ship can create a completely new Earth-like planet with DNA samples of the Earth's plants and animals, requiring a huge input of pure energy ; the development of this technology is the reason the Drej feared humanity's potential enough to destroy Earth.
- **S23**：Korso and Preed then confront Cale and Akima and interrupt his father's message, but before Korso could do anything, Preed detonates a small bomb inside the Valkrie to kill both Stith and Gune and betrays Korso before holding him, Cale, and Akima at gunpoint.
- **S24**：Preed then reveals a more tempting deal; he gets to live and get paid for helping the Drej find the Titan, but only after he kills the crew before the Drej can show up.
- **S25**：Preed then beats up Akima and Cale in an attempt to finish them off, but Korso stops him and snaps his neck as retribution for his treachery.
- **S26**：He then engages into a fight with Cale over the posession of Cale's ring, but falls into the depths of the Titan by accident after Cale attempts to save him from doing so.
- **S27**：The Drej arrive and attack the Titan by destroying the ice shield protecting it.
- **S28**：Surviving Preed's bomb, Stith arrives to Cale and Akima's aid and the three work to defend the Titan from the Drej.
- **S29**：Learning from Korso that the Drej are of pure energy, Cale decides to re-configure the Titan to use the Drej energy to start the ship's reactor.
- **S30**：He activates two of the three circuit breakers channeling the Drej energy into the Titan.
- **S31**：Cale dons a space suit and goes outside to fix the third breaker where Gune, who also survived the bomb, uses the Valkyrie{{'}}s guns to fight the Drej.
- **S32**：As Cale reaches the breaker station, he is pinned by a Drej ship Gune shot down.
- **S33**：Evenutally, Korso, having grabbed a cable to escape the fall and overhearing Cale's attempt to use the Drej energy to power up the reactor, arrives and uses a large laser rifle to free Cale.
- **S34**：Aware that it may be possible to defeat the Drej after all, Korso provides cover fire for Cale to get to the faulty breaker.
- **S35**：Despite Cale's efforts, the faulty breaker still cannot activate.
- **S36**：Telling Cale to activate the reactor, Korso wedges his rifle between the gap and breakers and closes its circuit for good, losing his own life in the process.
- **S37**：The Drej mothership fires its energy weapon, but the blast is absorbed into the main reactor of the Titan as soon as Cale places the ring to activate it.
- **S38**：As a result, the blast reflects back into the Drej mothership, destroying it and killing all of the Drej.
- **S39**：The Titan then uses the massive amounts of matter in the ice field to create a new planet.
- **S40**：As Cale and Akima step onto the planet, Cale jokingly decides to call it "Bob", while Akima suggests that they call it "New Earth".
- **S41**：Stith and Gune fly by to say goodbye to Cale and Akima as the human drifter colonies approach the New Earth to begin their lives anew.

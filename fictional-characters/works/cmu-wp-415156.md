### Xander Cage

### Augustus Gibbons

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-3a28e0faf9e0be55` · 独立版本：`profile-7ee7bceb3f1f6a56`

- **性格倾向**：常规渗透接连失败时，他表现出敢于打破用人惯例的倾向（行为推断；S2、S3、S7）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他看中凯奇的非常规能力，却用强制手段将其纳入政府任务。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：An NSA agent infiltrates a concert where Rammstein is performing only to be killed by Anarchy 99, an underground Eastern European group.
- **S2**：After losing the third agent assigned to the mission, NSA operative Augustus Gibbons gets permission to run a project to find a person to infiltrate Anarchy 99, since conventional agents are too obvious to the group.
- **S3**：Xander Cage is a law-breaking, extreme sports enthusiast, with a propensity for rebellious behavior.
- **S4**：After crashing California senator Dick Hotchkiss' car in response to the senator championing a ban of controversial video games and music, Cage's hideout is raided during a party by a SWAT team and he is tranquilized.
- **S5**：Cage wakes up in a diner.
- **S6**：He notices things are out of place and is not surprised during a fake robbery attempt.
- **S7**：After Cage quickly foils the "robbery", Gibbons informs him that the whole scenario was part of a test and that he passed.
- **S8**：Cage tells Gibbons that he's not interested and is once again tranquilized as he attempts to leave.
- **S9**：Cage wakes up in a U.S.
- **S10**：Marines cargo plane along with two other criminal types similar to Cage.
- **S11**：The cargo door opens and the group is dumped in Colombia and taken captive by a Colombian drug cartel after stumbling upon a cocaine plantation.
- **S12**：The cartel leader, El Jefe, attempts to interrogate the three who, believing the scene to be another NSA setup, jokingly refuse to cooperate.
- **S13**：As the drug lord begins to use torture, Cage realizes that the situation is real and frees himself and the others as the Colombian Army raid the plantation.
- **S14**：As Cage attempts to escape, he is captured by camouflaged NSA agents.
- **S15**：The next morning, Gibbons says that exposing the Colombian drug cartel was another part of the test.
- **S16**：Cage is given a choice by Gibbons: work for him or serve a lengthy prison term in Leavenworth Federal Penitentiary.
- **S17**：He grudgingly chooses government employment and Cage goes undercover in the Czech Republic to find out more about "Anarchy 99".
- **S18**：Their leader, Yorgi, is a Soviet Union ex-soldier who has a grudge against authority and society in general.
- **S19**：Xander finds himself drawn to Yorgi's apparent girlfriend Yelena, and he negotiates a political asylum deal with Gibbons to secure a new life for her in America.
- **S20**：Cage learns that Anarchy 99 is a terrorist faction, planning a series of chemical weapons attacks on major cities by utilizing a high speed, automated seafaring and river-faring drone named Ahab, which is equipped with a device designed to travel to highly populated areas to deploy its missiles and then submerge in the ocean.
- **S21**：The chemical weapon  is made of everyday chemicals that are lethal in combination.
- **S22**：Its formula has been missing since the Fall of the Soviet Union in 1991.
- **S23**：After killing millions the chemicals are designed to harmlessly disperse in water.
- **S24**：Yorgi's goal is to bring civilization to an anarchic end, destroying the system that killed many his military comrades for no reason.
- **S25**：An agent-turned-criminal, Milan Sova, unaware of Yorgi's ultimate plans, finds Cage's methods extreme and defects in support of Yorgi.
- **S26**：Yelena is forced to kill Sova and reveals that she is an abandoned agent of the FSB who was assigned to monitor Yorgi.
- **S27**：Cage arranges an operation with the Czech special forces to raid the remote castle Anarchy 99 uses as a base.
- **S28**：Cage is briefly captured before the Czech police arrive, blowing up the mansion.
- **S29**：In the ensuing chaos, Yorgi escapes with his right-hand man, Kirill, an expert sniper.
- **S30**：Cage and Yelena follow them and Cage kills Kirill using a heat seeking rocket.
- **S31**：After Cage shoots and kills Yorgi, he speeds along winding roads and parasails onto Ahab as it speeds down the Vltava River towards Prague.
- **S32**：Cage finally destroys the drone and the chemicals on board.
- **S33**：Later, Cage is on vacation with Yelena in Bora Bora as agent Gibbons jokingly informs him that because of his actions, he has passed "The Test", the "Gibbons Test".
- **S34**：He also says that he needs him for another assignment, as "something else has come up".
- **S35**：Cage ignores this as he and Yelena go for a swim.

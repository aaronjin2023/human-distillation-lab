### Fletcher Reede

- **性格倾向**：前期面对工作与家庭冲突时倾向逐利失约，并用谎言回避责任（行为推断；S3、S4、S5）

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-3024b2c6cc2ac69a` · 独立版本：`profile-ac070da8f5065e6f`

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Los Angeles, California Fletcher Reede  is a career-focused lawyer and divorced father.
- **S2**：He loves spending time with his young son Max ([[Justin Cooper ; they often play a game where Fletcher makes his hand into "the Claw" and pretends to chase Max with it.
- **S3**：But Fletcher has a habit of giving precedence to his job, breaking promises to Max and his ex-wife Audrey , and then lying about the reasons.
- **S4**：Fletcher's compulsive lying has also built him a reputation as one of the best defense lawyers in the state of California as he is climbing the ladder in the firm for which he works.
- **S5**：Ultimately, Fletcher misses his son's fifth birthday party because he is having sex with his boss Miranda , in the hopes of making partner.
- **S6**：Max makes a birthday wish that his father would be unable to tell a lie for an entire day &mdash; a wish that immediately becomes true.
- **S7**：Fletcher soon discovers, through a series of embarrassing incidents &mdash; such as when he tells Miranda that he has "had better" sex than he just did with her &mdash; that he is unable to lie, mislead, or even withhold a true answer.
- **S8**：These incidents are inconvenient, as he is fighting a divorce case in court which, should he win, could be a huge boost to his career.
- **S9**：His client is the self-centered, money grabbing Samantha Cole .
- **S10**：His main witness is willing to commit perjury to win, but Fletcher discovers that he cannot even ask a question if he knows the answer will be a lie; during the case he even objects to himself when he tries to lie to get the desired information.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, Audrey is planning to move to Boston with her new boyfriend Jerry , and decides that Max is going with them so that she can protect him from getting hurt by Fletcher.
- **S12**：Fletcher tries desperately to delay the case, even beating himself up, but he cannot conceal that he is able to continue, so the judge  insists that he does.
- **S13**：Finally he realizes that his client had lied about her age and therefore had signed her prenuptial agreement while a minor, rendering it invalid.
- **S14**：This allows him to win his case truthfully, but the repercussions become a catalyst to his understanding of what he himself is about to lose.
- **S15**：Samantha who only cared about her ex-husband's money, takes custody of her children purely so her ex-husband would be forced to continue paying her for child care and literally pulls the children out of the arms of their responsible and caring father.
- **S16**：Fletcher then has a crisis of conscience and shouts at the judge to reverse the decision, and is taken to jail for contempt of court.
- **S17**：Audrey refuses to pay his bail, which is eventually paid by his secretary Greta , who forgives him for his earlier rude truth-tellings after hearing he "went all noble" in front of their firm's senior partner.
- **S18**：Now recognizing his son Max as his highest priority, Fletcher struggles to keep him.
- **S19**：He hurries to the airport, but Audrey and Max's plane has already left the terminal.
- **S20**：Desperate, he hijacks a mobile stairway and pursues the plane onto the runway.
- **S21**：The plane finally stops, but Fletcher is injured.
- **S22**：On his way to the hospital, he vows to his son to spend more time with him and never hurt him again.
- **S23**：Even though the 24 hours of truth are up, Max knows he means it, and Audrey decides not to move to Boston with Jerry after all.
- **S24**：One year later, Fletcher and Audrey are celebrating Max's birthday.
- **S25**：The lights go off when Max blows out his birthday candles.
- **S26**：When they go back on, Fletcher and Audrey are kissing.
- **S27**：Fletcher asks Max if he wished for his mom and his dad to get back together and Max replies "No, I wished for roller blades!" The family seemingly returns to normal as Fletcher chases Audrey and Max around the house with "the Claw".

### Jake Holman

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-c611983f0f3942f2` · 独立版本：`profile-6a7986b0b6df7604`

- **性格倾向**：在机舱工作中，表现出重实际操作且不愿随俗的倾向（行为推断；S5、S8、S13）
- **核心动机**：希望亲自照管喜爱的引擎，并使机舱按可靠方式运转（摘要直接叙述；S5、S13）
- **价值取舍**：面对同伴受折磨时，将结束其痛苦置于服从命令之前（行为推断；S22、S23）
- **关系模式**：跨越船上劳工等级，与受训的宝汉建立友谊并保护他（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15、S17）
- **决策方式**：危急撤离时主动接替掩护位置，让其余人员先走（摘要直接叙述；S56、S57、S58）

### The Captain

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-865ea2ec4ddf0688` · 独立版本：`profile-5909df00500a889a`

- **性格倾向**：在船上秩序受到威胁时，倾向以强硬指挥维持控制（行为推断；S36、S37、S52、S53）
- **核心动机**：即使接到返航命令，仍坚持前往撤离偏远传教站人员（摘要直接叙述；S39、S40）
- **价值取舍**：最终将被救人员与部下撤离，置于自身安全之前（行为推断；S55、S56）
- **决策方式**：面对外交风险限制开火，面对船员抗命则以示警射击恢复秩序（行为推断；S18、S36、S37）
- **关系模式**：与霍尔曼因工作权限和服从问题持续发生权威冲突（摘要直接叙述；S13、S52、S53）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：要求部下服从军令，自己却为撤离任务违背返航命令。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1926, Machinist's Mate 1st Class Jake Holman  transfers from the Asiatic Fleet flagship to the Yangtze River Patrol gunboat USS San Pablo.
- **S2**：Life aboard a gunboat is very different.
- **S3**：It has a labor system – condoned by officers – wherein coolies  do the work, leaving the sailors free for combat drills and idle bickering.
- **S4**：The coolie laborers' "rice bowl"  is derived from doing the work that the sailors would normally do.
- **S5**：Because he personally enjoys taking care of ships' engines, Holman bucks the "coolie" system, overseeing the operation of the power plant himself - thereby antagonizing not only the chief engine-room coolie, Chien, but his ship-mates as well.
- **S6**：Holman's "connection" with the engine is conveyed when he personally introduces himself to the apparatus during his first trip to the ship's engine room.
- **S7**：Although he becomes close friends with one seasoned and sensitive seaman, Frenchy , most of the other members of the crew see Holman's attitude as a threat to their cushy arrangement, and accuse him of being a Jonah.
- **S8**：Holman discovers a serious defect that the coolies have not fixed.
- **S9**：Holman informs the Captain , who declines to authorize an engine shutdown for the repair.
- **S10**：Only after the Executive Officer observes the same problem and declares an emergency, does the Captain agree.
- **S11**：The chief engine-room coolie, Chien, after insisting upon taking Holman's place in the dangerous crank pit, is accidentally killed when the jacking gear slips due to its poor condition.
- **S12**：The chief coolie, Lop-eye Shing, blames Holman, who maintains that the death was caused by the deceased coolie's own poor work, not by ghosts in the machinery.
- **S13**：Holman asks the Captain to allow him to run the engine room properly, but is ordered to train a replacement coolie and concentrate on his military duties.
- **S14**：Holman selects Po-han ([[Mako  as the replacement and invests time training him.
- **S15**：The two form a friendship.
- **S16**：Po-Han is harassed by one sailor , leading to a boxing match on which the crewmen place bets.
- **S17**：Po-Han's victory leads to more antagonism between Holman and crew members, as well as the chief coolie, who wants to kick Po-Han off the ship but is foiled by Holman.
- **S18**：An incident involving British gunboats  leads to the Captain ordering the crew not to fire on, or return fire from the Chinese, to avoid diplomatic incidents.
- **S19**：Po-Han is sent ashore by the chief coolie .
- **S20**：Po-han is captured and tortured by a mob of Chinese in full view of the crew, only yards from shore.
- **S21**：With the crew poised to repel boarders, and under intense pressure, the Captain attempts to negotiate for Po-Han's release with offers of American money; his efforts are fruitless.
- **S22**：Po-Han begs for someone to kill him.
- **S23**：Holman disobeys orders and ends Po-Han's suffering with a fatal rifle shot.
- **S24**：The San Pablo is stuck in port at Changsha for the winter due to low water levels.
- **S25**：It must deal with increasingly hostile crowds surrounding it in numerous smaller boats.
- **S26**：The Captain fears a possible mutiny.
- **S27**：Frenchy has saved a Chinese woman, Maily , from prostitution by paying her debts.
- **S28**：He marries her and sneaks off the ship regularly, but dies of pneumonia one night.
- **S29**：Holman searches for him and finds Maily sitting stunned by Frenchy's corpse.
- **S30**：Kuomintang  burst in, beat up Holman, and drag Maily away.
- **S31**：Holman returns to the ship.
- **S32**：The next day, several Chinese float out to the San Pablo in small boats and demand the "murderer" Holman be turned over to them.
- **S33**：Apparently, the nationalists killed Maily and blamed Holman, trying to provoke an incident.
- **S34**：Holman informs the Captain what really happened.
- **S35**：When the Chinese demand for Holman is refused, they blockade the San Pablo.
- **S36**：The American crew fears for their safety and demand that Holman surrender to the Chinese against the Captain's orders.
- **S37**：Order is not restored until the Captain fires across the bow of one of the Chinese junks.
- **S38**：With spring at hand, the Captain decides to risk an attempt to leave.
- **S39**：The San Pablo sails away from the Kuomintang blockade and receives radioed orders to return to the coast.
- **S40**：The Captain defies these orders and elects to evacuate idealistic missionary Jameson  and his school teacher assistant Shirley Eckert  from their remote mission up the Yangtze River.
- **S41**：To reach the missionaries, the San Pablo must fight through a boom made up of junks carrying a massive rope blocking the river.
- **S42**：The San Pablo returns their fire and boards one of the junks.
- **S43**：Close-range fighting results in the deaths of several sailors and Chinese.
- **S44**：Holman heroically cuts the boom with an axe under fire while other sailors return to the San Pablo.
- **S45**：He is attacked and kills a Chinese man with the axe.
- **S46**：It turns out that the man, the leader of a Nationalist student group, was known to Holman as a student of Eckert.
- **S47**：The ship then proceeds upriver, leaving the smoking wrecks behind.
- **S48**：Arriving near the mission, the Captain leads a patrol of three sailors, including Holman, ashore.
- **S49**：Jameson resists rescue, claiming that it is the Captain's actions that have endangered him, not the Chinese.

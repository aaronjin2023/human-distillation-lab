## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Plot}} Maria  is found in a pasture, exulting in the musical inspiration she finds there .
- **S2**：Maria is a postulant in Nonnberg Abbey, where she is constantly getting into mischief and is the nuns' despair .
- **S3**：Maria's life suddenly changes when a widowed Austrian Navy Captain, Georg von Trapp  writes to the abbey asking for a governess for his seven children.
- **S4**：Mother Abbess asks Maria to take the position on a probationary basis; previous governesses, though, have not lasted long.
- **S5**：She is worried about what awaits her at the von Trapp household, but is determined to succeed .
- **S6**：Maria, upon arrival at the von Trapp estate, finds that the Captain keeps it in strict shipshape order, blows a whistle, issues orders, and dresses his children in sailor-suit uniforms.
- **S7**：While they are initially hostile to her, they warm to her when she comforts them during a thunderstorm .
- **S8**：Liesl , the oldest, who is "Sixteen Going on Seventeen", sneaks into Maria's window after a secret meeting with a messenger boy, Rolfe .
- **S9**：At first she is adamant that she "doesn't need a governess", but Maria offers to be her friend, and she acquiesces.
- **S10**：Maria teaches them how to sing  and to play, sewing playclothes for them from discarded drapes in her room.
- **S11**：The Captain entertains a visit from a lady friend, Baroness Elsa Schroeder , a wealthy socialite from Vienna, along with mutual friend Max Detweiler , who is intent on finding an obscure musical act to launch at the upcoming Salzburg Music Festival.
- **S12**：The Captain becomes aware that Maria has been taking the children on picnics and bicycle rides, climbed trees with them, and taken them in a boat on the lake adjoining his estate.
- **S13**：When the boat capsizes, Maria and all of the children  fall into the water.
- **S14**：The Captain turns his wrath on her and Maria begs him to pay attention to the children and love them, but he orders her to return to the abbey.
- **S15**：When he discovers the children performing a reprise of "The Sound of Music" for the Baroness, he changes his mind.
- **S16**：Maria has brought music back into his home, and he begs her to stay.
- **S17**：Things get better at the household.
- **S18**：She and the children perform a puppet show  that Max gave to them.
- **S19**：He announces that he has entered the children in the Salzburg Festival; the Captain, however, forbids their participation.
- **S20**：Maria and the children insist that he sing a song, knowing that he used to play and sing with a guitar, and he agrees .
- **S21**：At a soiree thrown in Baroness Schroeder's honor, eleven-year-old Kurt  observes guests dancing the Laendler, and asks Maria to teach him the steps.
- **S22**：The Captain cuts in and partners her in a graceful performance, culminating in a close clinch.
- **S23**：At that moment, she breaks off and blushes.
- **S24**：The children perform "So Long, Farewell" to say goodnight to the guests, receiving enthusiastic applause.
- **S25**：The Baroness, jealous of Maria, convinces her to return to Nonnberg.
- **S26**：Maria leaves the estate and returns to the abbey, where she keeps herself in seclusion until Mother Abbess gently confronts her, urging her to "Climb Ev'ry Mountain" in search of God's will for her.
- **S27**：At this command, she returns to the von Trapp family, finding that the Captain is now engaged to the Baroness.
- **S28**：However, he breaks off the engagement, realizing that he is in love with Maria.
- **S29**：He meets Maria in his gazebo and they declare their love for each other.
- **S30**：The Captain and Maria share a kiss for romance .
- **S31**：The two wed in an elaborate ceremony at the Salzburg Cathedral, with many of Austria's elite, as well as the nuns from Nonnberg Abbey, in attendance.
- **S32**：While the new couple is away on their honeymoon in Paris, Max grooms the children to perform in the Salzburg Music Festival, against the Captain's wishes.
- **S33**：At the same time, Austria is annexed into the Third Reich in the Anschluss .
- **S34**：When the Captain returns, he is informed that he must report as soon as possible to the Nazi Naval Headquarters in Bremerhaven, to accept a commission in the German Navy.
- **S35**：He is opposed to Nazism, and stalls by insisting he must perform with his family that night in the Salzburg Festival, now politicized and showcased as a Nazi event under the patronage of Hans Zeller ([[Ben Wright , recently appointed as the Nazi Gauleiter.
- **S36**：Zeller agrees, but orders the Captain to depart immediately after the performance.
- **S37**：The choreography of the final song, "So Long, Farewell", allows the family to leave slowly, a few at a time, and as the winners are announced, they flee.
- **S38**：At first they hide in the abbey, but are discovered by Rolfe , who threatens to shoot the Captain despite being visibly scared of having to do so.
- **S39**：The Captain unsuccessfully attempts to persuade Rolfe to join them; he calls for his lieutenant instead, and the von Trapps flee again.
- **S40**：The Nazis are unable to pursue them, as the nuns have removed the spark plug wires and ignition coils from their cars.
- **S41**：The final shot shows the von Trapps climbing over the Alps into Switzerland, as "Climb Ev'ry Mountain", reprised by a choir, swells to a conclusion.

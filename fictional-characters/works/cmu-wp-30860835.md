### RJ

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-9f5c06a595605668` · 独立版本：`profile-18e8f6e2bfc0409e`

- **性格倾向**：生存受威胁时倾向机巧求生，也会隐瞒目的并转嫁责任（行为推断；S3、S7、S12、S16）
- **核心动机**：为兑现对熊的补偿承诺而搜集食物，隐瞒了利用同伴的目的（行为推断；S3、S16、S19）
- **决策方式**：面对人类防护设施时倾向观察资源与漏洞，组织合作取食（行为推断；S14、S15）
- **成长变化**：从带食物独自脱身转向放弃交差，冒险营救被捕的朋友（摘要直接叙述；S18、S19、S20）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：渴望摆脱自身危机，却逐渐无法接受以朋友受难换取安全。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After RJ, a starving raccoon, fails to get snacks from a vending machine, he becomes so desperate that he tries to raid a large food cache belonging to Vincent, a hibernating black bear.
- **S2**：However, while trying to finish by stealing a can of "Spuddies" potato chips, he wakes Vincent and loses both the food and the red wagon that it's on when a truck runs it over.
- **S3**：Hastily, he promises to replace everything by the time Vincent reawakens in a week.
- **S4**：Meanwhile, a group of forest animals, led by Verne the box turtle, emerge from hibernation to find their food cache nearly empty.
- **S5**：They begin foraging but find a large hedge blocking their way.
- **S6**：Verne investigates, discovering a human residential community which confuses and frightens him.
- **S7**：RJ, who sees the entire commotion, convinces the other animals it's easier to raid the humans' garbage for food rather than forage for it, and they manage to get enough food to keep them from starving.
- **S8**：Verne, however, remains wary, especially after homeowner Gladys Sharp discovers the animals in her yard, chases them out through the hedge, and hires VermTech exterminator Dwayne LaFontant to keep them out.
- **S9**：Worried for his family's safety, Verne decides to return the food to the humans.
- **S10**：RJ tries to stop him, resulting in an argument between the two.
- **S11**：This attracts the attention of a playful Rottweiler, who chases them across several lawns before all of the food the animals gathered is destroyed.
- **S12**：RJ then blames Verne who, while trying to defend himself, makes a comment that hurts the others' feelings.
- **S13**：RJ helps Verne reconcile with the others.
- **S14**：He also discovers that Gladys has just restocked her pantry with a large food supply for an upcoming party and concocts a plan to get past the exterminator-planted boobytraps in her yard.
- **S15**：Working together under cover of darkness, the animals get the food.
- **S16**：RJ and Verne get into another argument, again over a can of "Spuddies", during which Verne  learn of RJ's true motives.
- **S17**：Gladys wakes up, discovers the animals in her house, and calls VermTech.
- **S18**：Dwayne arrives and traps the animals except for RJ, who escapes with the food.
- **S19**：RJ takes the food to a now-awake Vincent, but when the latter points out RJ's treachery, the raccoon decides to sacrifice the food to save his friends.
- **S20**：This angers Vincent, who chases RJ as he pursues Dwayne's truck.
- **S21**：Verne is happy to see RJ again but the others no longer trust him since he abandoned them.
- **S22**：They nearly thwart RJ's efforts to help before Verne finally convinces them otherwise.
- **S23**：The animals then subdue Dwayne and turn his truck toward home, but the truck goes out of control and demolishes Gladys' house.
- **S24**：The animals hide in the hedge, trapped by Vincent on one side and Dwayne and Gladys on the other.
- **S25**：RJ and Verne get an idea to give Hammy the hyperactive squirrel his first ever can of energy drink, which puts him into overdrive.
- **S26**：Exceeding the speed of light, Hammy activates an illegal trap that Dwayne had installed , capturing Vincent, Dwayne and Gladys.
- **S27**：Gladys and Vincent are taken into custody by the police and a wilderness preservation group, respectively, while Dwayne escapes after taking advantage of Gladys' attempt at fighting a police officer and resisting arrest, only to encounter the same Rottweiler that chased RJ and Verne earlier.
- **S28**：Back in the forest, Verne tells RJ that if he'd explained what he was trying to do in the first place, the others would have helped because "that's what families do," and welcomes him back to the family.
- **S29**：The animals also find that while Hammy was in his caffeine-charged state, he had refilled their food cache with nuts to satisfy them for the year.
- **S30**：In a post-credits scene, the animals go to the vending machine seen in the beginning of the film and cause every product to fall into the access bin.
- **S31**：However, the amount of snacks prevents the access door from opening, prompting Hammy to remark that this is "kind of anti-climatic."

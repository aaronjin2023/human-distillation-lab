## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Mickey Haller, a Los Angeles criminal defense attorney , celebrates a not-guilty verdict at the Redwood bar.
- **S3**：Driving home, he is stopped by Officer Roy Milton of the Los Angeles Police Department , who claims the Lincoln Town Car has no rear license plate.
- **S4**：When dark liquid drips from the trunk, Milton opens it without a warrant.
- **S5**：Inside is the body of Sam Scales, a former client known for online donation scams, shot dead with his hands bound in duct tape .
- **S6**：Haller is arrested and charged with first-degree murder.
- **S7**：Five weeks later, Haller represents himself at Twin Towers Correctional Facility .
- **S8**：His law partner Jennifer Aronson serves as co-counsel, investigator Dennis "Cisco" Wojciechowski handles fieldwork, and Cisco's wife Lorna Taylor manages the office.
- **S9**：Bail is set at $5 million, but Haller saves his resources for his daughter Hayley and refuses to waive his speedy-trial right, scheduling trial.
- **S10**：At a hearing before Judge Violet Warfield, Haller challenges Milton's traffic stop to secure his testimony under oath.
- **S11**：He accuses prosecutor Dana Berg of withholding video evidence, prompting Warfield to order its discovery.
- **S12**：A bullet from Haller's garage matches the one that killed Scales, and blood was found there.
- **S13**：An independent toxicology report shows Scales had Rohypnol in his system.
- **S14**：Suspecting that deputies are monitoring privileged conversations, Haller invents a plan to flee to Mexico during a recorded call with Jennifer.
- **S15**：When Berg cites the supposed escape plan at a bail hearing, Jennifer produces her recording, exposing the surveillance.
- **S16**：Warfield reduces bail to $500,000 with an ankle monitor.
- **S17**：Harry Bosch, Haller's half brother and retired LAPD homicide detective, helps fund the bond and joins the investigation.
- **S18**：The team identifies Louis Opparizio, a businessman Haller exposed during Lisa Trammel's murder trial nine years earlier.
- **S19**：Opparizio lost a fortune and now operates through associates at BioGreen Industries, a biodiesel refinery receiving government subsidies.
- **S20**：Grease beneath Scales's fingernails matches the refinery's feedstock.
- **S21**：FBI agents Rick Aiello and Dawn Ruth confront Haller after he serves subpoenas; his security camera records Aiello's threats.
- **S22**：Berg finds a demand letter for $75,000 in unpaid fees from Scales and obtains a new indictment alleging murder for financial gain.
- **S23**：Haller returns to jail to preserve his trial date.
- **S24**：His team traces Scales to a Nevada prison and learns he used the alias Walter Lennon while working at the port.
- **S25**：The evidence at a San Pedro address links him to BioGreen's subsidy fraud.
- **S26**：An anonymous document indicates the FBI recruited Scales as an informant.
- **S27**：After an inmate attack nearly kills Haller, Jennifer leaves for a family emergency.
- **S28**：Haller asks his first ex-wife, prosecutor Maggie McPherson, to join the defense.
- **S29**：At trial, video undermines Milton's testimony, while Maggie discredits Trammel's allegations.
- **S30**：A retired Environmental Protection Agency biologist describes the subsidy fraud and identifies Aiello and Ruth as agents overseeing his former case.
- **S31**：Ruth secretly confirms Scales was an informant who diverted subsidies through a shell company.
- **S32**：Opparizio killed Scales after discovering the scheme, framing Haller in revenge for the Trammel trial.
- **S33**：Opparizio's associates later murdered him using a red-haired killer.
- **S34**：The federal and local prosecutors offer to dismiss the charges if Haller remains silent about their investigation.
- **S35**：He secures public exoneration, protection for Maggie's career, and an inquiry into Milton.
- **S36**：The charges are dismissed with prejudice, and Haller's arrest record is expunged.
- **S37**：Ten days later, as COVID-19 shutdowns begin, Haller shelters with Maggie and Hayley.
- **S38**：In a supermarket parking lot, he recognizes the red-haired assassin from hotel footage.
- **S39**：The gunman fires but misses.
- **S40**：FBI agents kill him, and Haller drives home, checking that his vanity plate, NT GLTY, remains in place.

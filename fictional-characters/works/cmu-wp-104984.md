### Rocky Balboa

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8759883e9059ccbf` · 独立版本：`profile-d1e47fe082eb3f70`

- **性格倾向**：在强敌与敌意观众面前表现坚韧，倾向承受打击后持续寻找反击机会（行为推断；S15、S16、S17、S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：因Apollo之死产生愧疚与愤怒，决定放弃头衔赴俄为朋友复仇（摘要直接叙述；S10）
- **决策方式**：面对朋友坚持比赛的要求，虽有担忧仍选择尊重其意愿而不叫停（摘要直接叙述；S4、S7、S8）
- **沟通方式**：获胜后承认双方最初的敌意，并用相互尊重和改变呼吁超越对立（摘要直接叙述；S23、S24）
- **成长变化**：从带着私人复仇目标参赛，转向公开肯定跨越国家敌意的可能（行为推断；S10、S23、S24）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式

矛盾与变化：尊重朋友不停止比赛的意愿，却因此承受死亡带来的愧疚

### Ivan Drago

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-c97d668564bb3411` · 独立版本：`profile-ae57ff35e330a6b8`

- **性格倾向**：在Apollo死亡后表现冷漠，面对媒体未显示悔意或同情（摘要直接叙述；S10）
- **情绪反应**：被Rocky击伤后明显动摇，观众倒向对手又进一步令他不安（摘要直接叙述；S18、S21）
- **关系模式**：与管理团队的关系受成绩压力支配，表现受责备后以推搡反抗（行为推断；S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：对阵Rocky时选择立即强攻，以持续重击试图压制对手（摘要直接叙述；S16、S20）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：被宣传为国家优越性的象征，却在场上受挫并遭己方责骂

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1985, Ivan Drago , a highly intimidating 6-foot 5&nbsp;inch, 261-pound Soviet boxer, arrives in America with his wife Ludmilla , and a team of trainers from the USSR and Cuba.
- **S2**：His manager, Nicolai Koloff , takes every opportunity to promote Drago's athleticism as a hallmark of Soviet superiority.
- **S3**：Motivated by patriotism and an innate desire to prove himself, Apollo Creed  challenges Drago to an exhibition bout.
- **S4**：Rocky  has reservations, but agrees to train Apollo, who enters the ring in an over-the-top patriotic entrance with James Brown performing Living in America.
- **S5**：It soon turns serious though, as Drago beats Apollo mercilessly.
- **S6**：Apollo is in dire straits and taking the worst beating of his life as the first round ends.
- **S7**：Rocky and Apollo's trainer Duke  plead with him to give up, but Apollo refuses to do so, and tells Rocky not to stop the fight.
- **S8**：The second round doesn't go any better, and despite Duke begging Rocky to throw in the towel, he honors Apollo's wish.
- **S9**：This turns out to have fatal consequences as Drago beats Apollo so badly that he dies from his injuries.
- **S10**：In the immediate aftermath, Drago displays no sense of remorse commenting to the assembled media: "If he dies... he dies." Incensed by Drago's cold indifference and feeling a deep sense of guilt, Rocky decides to avenge Apollo's death by agreeing to relinquish his title and fight Drago in Russia on Christmas Day in an unsanctioned 15-round bout.
- **S11**：He flies to the USSR without Adrian, setting up his training base in Krasnogourbinsk with only Duke and brother-in-law Paulie  to accompany him.
- **S12**：To prepare for the fight, Drago uses very high-tech equipment with  use of anabolic steroids and a team of trainers and doctors monitoring his every fiber.
- **S13**：Rocky, on the other hand, throws heavy logs, chops down trees, pulls an overloaded snow sleigh, jogs in heavy snow and treacherous icy conditions and climbs a mountain.
- **S14**：Adrian  shows up unexpectedly to give Rocky her support after initially refusing to travel to Russia because of her doubts on his fighting chances, resulting in Rocky's training having an added focus.
- **S15**：Drago is introduced with an elaborate, patriotic ceremony that puts the Russian crowd squarely on Drago's side, as Rocky is booed by all in attendance.
- **S16**：In contrast to his fight with Apollo, Drago immediately goes on the offensive and Rocky takes a fierce pounding.
- **S17**：Rocky comes back toward the end of the second and silences the Russian crowd by landing a strong right hook that cuts Drago just below his left eye.
- **S18**：While Drago is visibly shaken, Rocky is fired up and assaults Drago, which continues even after the bell rings.
- **S19**：While Duke and Paulie cheer Rocky for his heroism, they remind him that Drago is not a machine, but a man.
- **S20**：Ironically, Drago comments that Rocky "is not human, he is like a piece of iron" with his own corner reprimanding him for being "weak" in comparison to the "small American." The two boxers continue to hit each other over the next dozen rounds, with Rocky holding his ground despite Drago's powerful punches.
- **S21**：His resilience rallies the previously hostile Soviet crowd to his side, which unsettles Drago to the point that he shoves Koloff off the ring for berating his performance.
- **S22**：Rocky finally takes out Drago in the 15th and last round to the shock of the Soviet Politburo watching the fight.
- **S23**：A bloody and battered Rocky gives a victory speech, acknowledging the initial and mutual disdain between himself and the crowd as much as the disdain between Russians and Americans generally, and how they've come to respect and admire each other during the course of the fight which he also says is better than war between their two countries.
- **S24**：He finishes by saying that everybody can "change." The Soviet General Secretary stands and passionately applauds Rocky and his aides follow suit.
- **S25**：Rocky ends his speech by wishing his son a Merry Christmas, and throws his arms into the air in victory as the crowd applauds.

- **S14**：## Plot
- **S15**：[ edit ] Peter , Susan , Edmund and Lucy Pevensie are evacuated from London in 1940 , to escape the Blitz , and sent to live with an elderly Professor at a large house in the English countryside.
- **S16**：While exploring the house, Lucy enters a wardrobe and discovers the magical world of Narnia.
- **S17**：Here, she meets the faun named Tumnus , whom she addresses as " Mr Tumnus".
- **S18**：Tumnus invites her to his cave for tea and admits that he had intended to report Lucy to the White Witch , the false ruler of Narnia who has kept the land in perpetual winter, but he repents and guides her back home.
- **S19**：Although Lucy's siblings initially disbelieve her story of Narnia, Edmund follows her into the wardrobe, finds himself in a separate area of Narnia and meets the White Witch, who calls herself the Queen of Narnia.
- **S20**：The Witch plies Edmund with Turkish delight and persuades him to bring his siblings to her with the promise of being made a prince.
- **S21**：Edmund reunites with Lucy and they return home.
- **S22**：However, Edmund denies Narnia's existence to Peter and Susan after learning of the White Witch's identity from Lucy.
- **S23**：Soon afterwards, all four children enter Narnia together, but find that Tumnus has been arrested for treason.
- **S24**：The children are befriended by Mr and Mrs Beaver, who tell them of a prophecy that the White Witch's rule will end when "two Sons of Adam and two Daughters of Eve" sit on the four thrones of Cair Paravel, and that Narnia's true ruler – a great lion named Aslan – is returning after several years' absence, and will go to the Stone Table.
- **S25**：Edmund slips away to the White Witch's castle, where he finds a courtyard filled with the Witch's enemies turned into stone statues.
- **S26**：Edmund reports Aslan's return to the White Witch, who begins her movement towards the Stone Table with Edmund in tow, and orders the execution of Edmund's siblings and the Beavers.
- **S27**：Meanwhile, the Beavers realise where Edmund has gone, and lead the children to meet Aslan at the Stone Table.
- **S28**：On the way they meet Father Christmas , who has been kept out of Narnia by the Witch's magic, and he leaves the group with magical gifts, including weapons.
- **S29**：Meanwhile, the White Witch and her party realise that the snow is melting, and they have to abandon the sledge.
- **S30**：The children and the Beavers reach the Stone Table and meet Aslan and his army.
- **S31**：The White Witch's wolf captain Maugrim approaches the camp and attacks Susan, but is killed by Peter.
- **S32**：The White Witch arrives and parleys with Aslan, invoking the "Deep Magic from the Dawn of Time" which gives her the right to kill Edmund for his treason.
- **S33**：Aslan then speaks to the Witch alone, and on his return he announces that the Witch has renounced her claim on Edmund's life.
- **S34**：Aslan and his followers then move the encampment into the nearby forest.
- **S35**：That evening, Susan and Lucy secretly follow Aslan to the Stone Table.
- **S36**：They watch from a distance as the Witch puts Aslan to death, as they had agreed in their pact to spare Edmund.
- **S37**：The next morning, Aslan is resurrected by the "Deeper Magic from before the Dawn of Time", which has the power to reverse death if a willing victim takes the place of a traitor.
- **S38**：Aslan takes the girls to the Witch's castle and revives the Narnians that the Witch had turned to stone.
- **S39**：They join the Narnian forces battling the Witch's army.
- **S40**：The Narnian army prevails, and Aslan kills the Witch.
- **S41**：The Pevensie children are then crowned kings and queens of Narnia at Cair Paravel.
- **S42**：After a long and happy reign, the Pevensies, now adults, go on a hunt for the White Stag who is said to grant the wishes of those who catch it.
- **S43**：The four arrive at the lamp-post marking Narnia's entrance and, having forgotten about it, unintentionally pass through the wardrobe and return to England; they are children again, with no time having passed since their departure.
- **S44**：They tell the story to the Professor, who believes them and reassures the children that they will return to Narnia one day when they least expect it.

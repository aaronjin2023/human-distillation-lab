### Robert Langdon

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-710cf6abca59cabf` · 独立版本：`profile-058cdc0519629a96`

- **性格倾向**：面对追捕与谜题时，他倾向于仔细观察，并据线索形成推断。（行为推断；S14、S16、S18、S26）
- **核心动机**：他持续追查索尼埃留下的线索，试图弄清密码与圣杯谜团。（行为推断；S14、S24、S27、S48、S53）
- **决策方式**：危急时他倾向用计脱身，并预先取出线索再冒险抛出密码筒。（行为推断；S26、S46、S47）
- **价值取舍**：面对以苏菲性命相胁，他倾向兼顾同伴安全与线索保全。（行为推断；S45、S46、S47）

### Sophie Neveu

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-60ab18a6b032fec7` · 独立版本：`profile-86341fc0f0d0ca41`

- **性格倾向**：受追捕时她倾向于迅速采取实用行动，争取调查的主动。（行为推断；S9、S10、S12）
- **核心动机**：她相信祖父留下了给自己的信息，因此与朗登共同追查密码。（行为推断；S11、S12、S13）
- **关系模式**：她相信祖父有意让朗登帮助自己，祖孙此前常玩密码筒游戏。（摘要直接叙述；S11、S23）
- **决策方式**：面对警方追踪，她倾向先秘密提醒朗登，再共同争取调查时间。（行为推断；S9、S10、S12）
- **成长变化**：她从追查祖父留下的密码，走向得知自己是抹大拉马利亚后裔。（摘要直接叙述；S11、S50）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

### Leigh Teabing

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-ca11c1cdde9c05e7` · 独立版本：`profile-a9d616a61ae721bb`

- **性格倾向**：在圣杯争夺中他显得执着且善于掩饰，以协助者身份参与追查。（行为推断；S27、S28、S32、S42、S45）
- **核心动机**：他追求马利亚的遗骸，意在证明自己关于圣杯的论断。（摘要直接叙述；S28、S45）
- **价值取舍**：为证明圣杯论断，他愿拿苏菲的生命作为逼迫朗登的筹码。（行为推断；S45）
- **决策方式**：他会运用误导避开警方，身份暴露后又以枪杀苏菲相威胁。（摘要直接叙述；S35、S42、S45）
- **情绪反应**：未能获得密码令他心烦意乱，随后他被警方逮捕。（摘要直接叙述；S47）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Paris, Jacques Saunière is pursued through the Louvre's Grand Gallery by albino monk Silas , demanding the Priory's clef de voûte or "keystone." Saunière confesses the keystone is kept in the sacristy of Church of Saint-Sulpice "beneath the Rose" before Silas shoots him.
- **S2**：At the American University of Paris, Robert Langdon, a symbologist who is a guest lecturer on symbols and the sacred feminine, is summoned to the Louvre to view the crime scene.
- **S3**：He discovers the dying Saunière has created an intricate display using black light ink and his own body and blood.
- **S4**：Captain Bezu Fache  asks him for his interpretation of the puzzling scene.
- **S5**：Silas calls a mysterious man known as "The Teacher", revealing that he has killed all four protectors of the keystone and that all confirmed the same location.
- **S6**：He dons a metal cilice on his thigh and proceeds to flagellate himself with a whip for the sins of murder.
- **S7**：Facilitated by Bishop Manuel Aringarosa, Silas then travels to Saint-Sulpice and is admitted by an elderly nun; left alone, he excavates beneath the floor of the church to find a stone saying only JOB 38:11.
- **S8**：He confronts the nun, who quotes the passage: "Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further." Realizing that he has been deceived, Silas is enraged and kills the nun.
- **S9**：Sophie Neveu, a cryptologist with the French police, enters the Louvre as well and slips Langdon a message which leads him to the bathroom.
- **S10**：There, Sophie meets him and tells him that he is being tracked, a GPS tracking dot has been  slipped into his jacket and that he is a primary suspect in the murder case because of a line of text found by the corpse .
- **S11**：Sophie however, believes that Saunière, who is revealed to be her grandfather, wanted to pass a hidden message on to her , and that he had wanted to bring Langdon into the equation so that he could help her crack the code.
- **S12**：Buying some time by throwing the tracking device into the back of a truck, the pair begin exploring the Louvre, finding more anagram messages that Saunière had left behind.
- **S13**：Many of these relate to Leonardo da Vinci's art, and the pair find a key with a Fleur-de-lis behind Madonna of the Rocks.
- **S14**：Langdon deduces from this that Saunière was a member of the Priory of Sion, a secret society associated with the Knights Templar.
- **S15**：Pursued by the French police and cut off from the United States Embassy, the pair escape to the Bois de Boulogne where Langdon closely inspects the key.
- **S16**：He notices an inscription on the side – an address.
- **S17**：The address directs them to the Depository Bank of Zurich where the key is used for a safety deposit box.
- **S18**：In the bank, they find Saunière's deposit box and open it using the 10 digit Fibonacci numbers in order .
- **S19**：Inside the box, they find a rosewood container, which contains a cryptex: a cylindrical container with five alphabetical dials which must be arranged in the correct sequence to spell out a 5-letter code word, in order to open and access the papyrus message inside.
- **S20**：Using force to open the cryptex would break a vial of vinegar inside, which would dissolve the papyrus and destroy the message.
- **S21**：Unfortunately, the police are called by a security guard and they are forced to leave.
- **S22**：The bank manager, Andre Vernet, assists them in escaping by taking them as passengers in an armoured van to escape the routine checks of the police.
- **S23**：In the back of the truck Langdon and Neveu have a lengthy discussion about the cryptex and Neveu says that her grandfather often played games with her involving cryptexes.
- **S24**：Langdon says that the cryptex might hold valuable information or another clue about what they are trying to discover.
- **S25**：Eventually, they come to a sudden stop and Vernet forces them at gunpoint to give him the cryptex.
- **S26**：Langdon tricks Vernet and disarms him and he and Sophie escape with the cryptex in their hands.
- **S27**：Langdon suggests that they visit his friend, Leigh Teabing , for assistance to opening the cryptex.
- **S28**：Leigh Teabing turns out to be an enthusiastic seeker of the Holy Grail, which he believes is not actually a cup but instead Mary Magdalene.
- **S29**：Mary was pregnant at the time of Christ's crucifixion, and Teabing tells Sophie that the Priory of Sion was formed to protect the descendants of Jesus.
- **S30**：Jacques Saunière was believed to be a part of this society and Teabing suspects that he was training Sophie to join it also.
- **S31**：Silas, meanwhile, breaks into Teabing's mansion and attempts to steal the cryptex.
- **S32**：Teabing uses his cane to knock Silas out and they escape again, taking the butler, Remy Jean, and Silas with them.
- **S33**：The group escapes in Teabing's plane, following the next clue to London.
- **S34**：Fache learns of their destination, and alerts the London Metropolitan Police to apprehend them at the airport.
- **S35**：But Teabing manages to slip the party past the police with a trick of misdirection.
- **S36**：Teabing leads Langdon and Neveu to the Temple Church in London, which is shown to be a red herring.
- **S37**：Silas is freed by Remy Jean, who is revealed to be a follower of The Teacher as well.
- **S38**：The two take Teabing hostage, and Silas, believing Remy to be The Teacher, holes up in an Opus Dei safehouse.
- **S39**：Remy is killed by the mysterious man after deceiving Silas.
- **S40**：Silas accidentally shoots Aringaros and is shot by the police.
- **S41**：Aringarosa is taken to the hospital and apprehended by Fache.
- **S42**：Langdon is betrayed by Teabing, who is revealed to be the true Teacher.
- **S43**：He escapes with the Cryptex, and attempts to find the next clue at Isaac Newton's tomb in Westminster Abbey, as Newton was another member of the Priory.
- **S44**：Langdon and Neveau catch up with him, and chase him into the nearby chapter house.
- **S45**：Teabing explained that he wanted to find Mary Magdalene's remains to prove he was correct about the Holy Grail and threatens to shoot Sophie if Langdon does not open the cryptex.
- **S46**：Langdon throws the cryptex into the air, and Teabing fumbles and destroys it.
- **S47**：Distraught at not receiving the code, Teabing is arrested, but Langdon had cracked the code  and removed the clue from the cryptex before destroying it.
- **S48**：Using the clue, they travel to Rosslyn Chapel in Scotland where Magdalene's remains had previously been hidden.
- **S49**：The remains had since been moved, but they meet other members of the secret organization that protected her.
- **S50**：Sophie is actually Magdalene's descendant and therefore is the current living descendant of Jesus Christ.
- **S51**：They vow to keep her safe before going their separate ways.
- **S52**：In Paris, Langdon accidentally cuts himself while shaving and the line of blood on the sink reminds him of the Rose Line.
- **S53**：He follows the Rose Line and finds the location of the Holy Grail, buried under the pyramid in the Louvre.
- **S54**：Langdon then kneels above Mary Magdalene's tomb as the Knights Templar did before him.

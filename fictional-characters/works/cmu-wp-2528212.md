## 角色资料

### Dr. Nicholas Garrigan

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-62ac426ded1cc922` · 独立版本：`profile-a8a55061ad7afa9a`

- **性格倾向**：初到乌干达时，他倾向追求冒险并轻信具有吸引力的权力承诺（行为推断；S2、S6、S13）
- **核心动机**：他起初为摆脱家乡沉闷前景出国，后来急切寻找脱离政权的办法（摘要直接叙述；S2、S17）
- **决策方式**：面对政权暴力的早期迹象，他倾向接受领袖解释而延后质疑（行为推断；S6、S13、S16）
- **价值取舍**：面对刺杀换取离境的条件，他起初拒绝，之后因凯惨死改变底线（摘要直接叙述；S18、S23、S26）
- **成长变化**：他从真诚相信阿明能改善国家，转向试图毒杀自己曾信任的领袖（摘要直接叙述；S6、S16、S23、S26）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：具有医疗救助的主动性，却一度替压迫性暴力接受辩解；起初拒绝刺杀，最终又把杀死阿明视为结束暴政的办法

### Idi Amin Dada

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-1f7890e0baa800c3` · 独立版本：`profile-98bfa563194cea6d`

- **性格倾向**：面对身边人的独立与背离，他倾向以控制和惩罚维持支配（行为推断；S17、S27、S28）
- **核心动机**：为阻止加里根离境，他用乌干达护照替换对方的英国护照（摘要直接叙述；S17）
- **价值取舍**：面对家庭成员不合期待的情况，他倾向将排斥置于照护之前（行为推断；S14）
- **关系模式**：他从欣赏加里根的果断并倚为心腹，转向因背叛而残酷惩罚（摘要直接叙述；S9、S11、S12、S27、S28）
- **决策方式**：面对政治异己与族群差异，他倾向采取镇压杀戮和驱逐措施（行为推断；S13、S16）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以改革和信任吸引加里根，却以限制自由与酷刑维持控制

- **S1**：In 1970, Nicholas Garrigan  graduates from medical school in Scotland.
- **S2**：With dull prospects at home, he decides to seek adventure abroad by working at a Ugandan missionary clinic run by Dr.
- **S3**：David Merrit  and his wife, Sarah .
- **S4**：Garrigan becomes attracted to Sarah, who enjoys the attention but refuses to engage him in an extramarital affair.
- **S5**：Meanwhile, General Idi Amin  overthrows incumbent president Milton Obote in a coup d’état.
- **S6**：Garrigan sincerely believes Amin will help the country, while Sarah warns him of dictators that have taken over before.
- **S7**：Garrigan is called to a minor car accident involving Amin where he treats his hand.
- **S8**：During the incident Garrigan takes a gun and shoots a mortally wounded cow because no one else has the presence of mind to put it out of its misery.
- **S9**：Amin is impressed by his quick action and initiative.
- **S10**：Amin, fond of Scotland as a symbol of resilience and admiring the Scottish people for their resistance to the English, is delighted to discover Garrigan's nationality and exchanges his military shirt for Garrigan's Scotland shirt.
- **S11**：Later, Amin invites Garrigan to become his personal physician and take charge of modernising the country's health care system.
- **S12**：Garrigan soon becomes Amin's trusted confidant and is relied on for much more than medical care, such as matters of state.
- **S13**：Although Garrigan is aware of violence around Kampala, he accepts Amin's explanation that cracking down on the opposition will bring lasting peace to the country.
- **S14**：Garrigan discovers that the polygamous leader has ostracised the youngest of his three wives, Kay , because she has given birth to an epileptic son, Mackenzie .
- **S15**：When treating Mackenzie, Garrigan and Kay form a relationship and sleep with each other, but Kay tells him he must find a way to leave Uganda.
- **S16**：Eventually, Garrigan begins to lose faith in Amin as he witnesses the increasing paranoia, repressive murders and xenophobia in expelling South Asians from the country.
- **S17**：Amin replaces Garrigan's British passport with a Ugandan one to prevent him from escaping which leads Garrigan to frantically seek help from Stone , the local British Foreign Office representative.
- **S18**：Garrigan is told the British will help him leave Uganda if he uses his position to assassinate Amin, but Garrigan refuses.
- **S19**：Kay informs Garrigan that she has become pregnant with his child.
- **S20**：Amin will murder her for infidelity if he discovers this, so she begs Garrigan for a secret abortion.
- **S21**：Delayed by Amin's command that he attend a press conference with Western journalists, Garrigan fails to meet Kay at the appointed time.
- **S22**：She concludes she has been abandoned and seeks out a primitive abortion in a nearby village, where she is apprehended by Amin's forces.
- **S23**：Garrigan finds her savagely mutilated corpse on an autopsy table and falls retching to his knees, finally confronting the palpable inhumanity of Amin's regime and decides killing him will end it all.
- **S24**：A hijacked aircraft is flown to Entebbe by pro-Palestinian hijackers seeking asylum from agents of international law.
- **S25**：Amin rushes to the scene to help them, taking Garrigan along.
- **S26**：At the airport, one of Amin's bodyguards discovers Garrigan's plot to poison Amin, under the ruse of giving him pills for a headache.
- **S27**：His treachery revealed, Garrigan is beaten by Amin's henchmen before Amin himself arrives and discloses he is aware of the relationship with Kay.
- **S28**：As punishment, Garrigan's chest is pierced with meat hooks and he is hanged by his skin.
- **S29**：Amin arranges a plane for the release of non-Israeli passengers, and Garrigan's torturers leave him bleeding on the floor while they relax in another room.

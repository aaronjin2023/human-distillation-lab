- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] George Wilson and his wife Myrtle live in the "valley of ashes", a refuse dump (shown in the above photograph) historically located in New York City during the 1920s.
- **S3**：Today, the area is Flushing Meadows–Corona Park .
- **S4**：In spring 1922, Nick Carraway —a Yale alumnus from the Midwest and a World War I veteran—journeys to New York City to obtain employment as a bond salesman .
- **S5**：He rents a bungalow in the Long Island village of West Egg, next to a luxurious estate inhabited by Jay Gatsby , an enigmatic multi-millionaire who hosts dazzling soirées , yet does not partake in them.
- **S6**：One evening, Nick dines with a distant cousin, Daisy Buchanan , in the old money town of East Egg.
- **S7**：Daisy is married to Tom Buchanan, formerly a Yale football star whom Nick knew during his college days.
- **S8**：The couple has recently relocated from Chicago to a mansion directly across the bay from Gatsby's estate.
- **S9**：There, Nick encounters Jordan Baker, an insolent flapper and golf champion who is a childhood friend of Daisy's.
- **S10**：Jordan confides to Nick that Tom keeps a mistress, who brazenly telephones him at his home and lives in the " valley of ashes ", a sprawling refuse dump. [ 33 ] That evening, Nick sees Gatsby standing alone on his lawn, staring at a green light across the bay.
- **S11**：The confrontation between Gatsby and Tom occurs in the twenty-story Plaza Hotel , a château -like edifice with an architectural style inspired by the French Renaissance .
- **S12**：Days later, Nick reluctantly accompanies a drunken and agitated Tom to New York City by train.
- **S13**：En route, they stop at a garage inhabited by mechanic George Wilson and his wife—and Tom's mistress—Myrtle.
- **S14**：Myrtle joins them, and the trio proceeds to a small New York apartment that Tom has rented for trysts with her.
- **S15**：Guests arrive and a party ensues, which ends with Tom slapping Myrtle and breaking her nose after she mentions Daisy.
- **S16**：One morning, Nick receives a formal invitation to a party at Gatsby's mansion.
- **S17**：Once there, Nick is embarrassed that he recognizes no one and begins drinking heavily until he encounters Jordan.
- **S18**：While chatting with her, he is approached by a man who introduces himself as Jay Gatsby and insists that both he and Nick served in the 3rd Infantry Division [ c ] during the war.
- **S19**：Gatsby attempts to ingratiate himself with Nick and when Nick leaves the party, he notices Gatsby watching him.
- **S20**：In late July, Nick and Gatsby have lunch at a speakeasy .
- **S21**：Gatsby tries impressing Nick with tales of his war heroism and his Oxford days.
- **S22**：Afterward, Nick meets Jordan again at the Plaza Hotel .
- **S23**：Jordan reveals that Gatsby and Daisy met around 1917 when Gatsby was an officer in the American Expeditionary Forces .
- **S24**：They fell in love, but when Gatsby was deployed overseas, Daisy reluctantly married Tom.
- **S25**：Gatsby hopes that his newfound wealth and dazzling parties will make Daisy reconsider.
- **S26**：Gatsby uses Nick to stage a reunion with Daisy, and the two embark upon an affair.
- **S27**：In September, Tom discovers the affair when Daisy carelessly addresses Gatsby with unabashed intimacy in front of him.
- **S28**：Later, at a Plaza Hotel suite, Gatsby and Tom argue about the affair.
- **S29**：Gatsby insists Daisy declare that she never loved Tom.
- **S30**：Daisy claims she loves Tom and Gatsby, upsetting both.
- **S31**：Tom reveals Gatsby is a swindler whose money comes from bootlegging alcohol.
- **S32**：Upon hearing this, Daisy chooses to stay with Tom.
- **S33**：Tom scornfully tells Gatsby to drive her home, knowing that Daisy will never leave him.
- **S34**：While returning to East Egg, Gatsby and Daisy drive by Wilson's garage and their car strikes Myrtle, killing her instantly.
- **S35**：Later Gatsby reveals to Nick that Daisy was driving the car, but that he intends to take the blame for the accident to protect her.
- **S36**：Nick urges Gatsby to flee to avoid prosecution, but he refuses.
- **S37**：After Tom tells George that Gatsby owns the car that struck Myrtle, a distraught George assumes the owner of the vehicle must be Myrtle's lover.
- **S38**：George fatally shoots Gatsby in his mansion's swimming pool, then kills himself.
- **S39**：Several days after Gatsby's murder, his father Henry Gatz arrives for the sparsely attended funeral.
- **S40**：After Gatsby's death, Nick comes to hate New York and decides that Gatsby, Daisy, Tom, and he were all Midwesterners unsuited to Eastern life . [ d ] Nick encounters Tom and initially refuses to shake his hand.
- **S41**：Tom admits he was the one who told George that Gatsby owned the vehicle that killed Myrtle.
- **S42**：Before returning to the Midwest, Nick returns to Gatsby's mansion and stares across the bay at the green light emanating from the end of Daisy's dock.

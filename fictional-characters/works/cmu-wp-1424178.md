## 角色资料

### Homer Simpson

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-697bf44b3a8181f1` · 独立版本：`profile-3cf6b61cede544b0`

- **性格倾向**：面对排队与免费甜甜圈的诱惑，他倾向急躁并贪图眼前便利（行为推断；S8、S9）
- **核心动机**：逃亡初期，他希望带家人在阿拉斯加重新开始生活（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **价值取舍**：面对曾追杀自己的居民，他起初让个人怨恨压过救援责任（行为推断；S14、S18）
- **关系模式**：他似乎更关爱宠物而非巴特，促使儿子转向亲近弗兰德斯（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **成长变化**：他从拒绝援救小镇，转向把拯救他人与自身救赎联系起来（摘要直接叙述；S18、S20、S23、S24）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：想保护家人，却因自身疏忽和拒绝救援而加深家庭裂痕。

### Russ Cargill

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-7b5c6094820aad8a` · 独立版本：`profile-e5f61bca46597c9c`

- **性格倾向**：面对封锁失控与曝光风险，他倾向升级强制手段压制问题（行为推断；S16、S26）
- **核心动机**：他计划毁灭小镇，以阻止自己的封锁行为广为人知（摘要直接叙述；S16）
- **价值取舍**：在危机处理中，他将掩盖自身行为置于居民生命之上（行为推断；S16）
- **决策方式**：面对封锁受损与曝光风险，他选择筹划毁城来掩盖行为（行为推断；S16）
- **情绪反应**：毁灭计划遭到挫败后，他愤怒到试图亲手射杀阻碍者（摘要直接叙述；S26）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以控制污染为出发点，最终却把受困居民当作灭口对象。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：While performing on Lake Springfield, rock band Green Day are killed when pollution in the lake dissolves their barge, following an audience revolt after frontman Billie Joe Armstrong proposes an environmental discussion.
- **S2**：At a memorial service, Grampa has a prophetic vision in which he predicts the impending doom of the town, but only Marge takes it seriously.
- **S3**：Then Homer dares Bart to skate naked and he does so.
- **S4**：Lisa and an Irish boy named Colin, with whom she has fallen in love, hold a meeting where they convince the town to clean up the lake.
- **S5**：Meanwhile, Homer adopts a pig from Krusty Burger and names it "Spider Pig" .
- **S6**：Homer seems to show more love for the pig than he does for Bart which causes Bart to reconnect with Flanders.
- **S7**：Homer stores the pig's feces  in an overflowing silo which Marge tells him to dispose of safely.
- **S8**：Homer takes the silo and Plopper to the dump to get rid of the waste, but during a line-up of safe trash disposal, he receives a phone call from Lenny, who tells him that Lard Lad Donuts has been shut down and free donuts are being given out.
- **S9**：Homer grows impatient and instead dumps the silo in the lake, re-polluting it to an even more severe degree.
- **S10**：Moments later, a chipmunk jumps into the lake and becomes severely mutated.
- **S11**：Nearby, Flanders and Bart discover the chipmunk during a hike, and the EPA captures it.
- **S12**：Russ Cargill, head of the EPA, presents five "unthinkable" options to U.S.
- **S13**：President Schwarzenegger to keep the town's pollution contained; Schwarzenegger randomly picks option three, enclosing Springfield in a large glass dome.
- **S14**：When the police discover Homer's silo in the lake, an angry mob of townspeople approach the Simpsons' home with pitchforks and torches and attempt to kill them, but the family escape through a sinkhole that leads to the outside of the dome.
- **S15**：They find a room to stay in for the night and Homer tells them that he has a plan; flee to Alaska to start a new life.
- **S16**：The trapped citizens damage the dome over time and Cargill, not wanting news of what he has done to become widespread, plans to destroy Springfield.
- **S17**：In Alaska, the Simpsons see an advertisement starring Tom Hanks, for a new Grand Canyon to be located on the site where Springfield is located.
- **S18**：Marge and the kids want to go and save the town, but Homer refuses to help the people who tried to kill them.
- **S19**：The family abandon Homer and leaves Alaska by train heading to Seattle, but are captured by the EPA upon arrival and then put back into the dome.
- **S20**：After a visit from a mysterious Inuit shaman who saves him from a polar bear, Homer has an epiphany and believes he must save the town in order to save himself.
- **S21**：As he arrives at Springfield to do so, a helicopter lowers a bomb suspended by rope through a hole in the dome.
- **S22**：Homer climbs to the peak of the dome and descends the rope, knocking the escaping townspeople and bomb off.
- **S23**：Homer takes the bomb and a motorcycle.
- **S24**：After reuniting with Bart, they drive up the side of the dome and Bart throws the bomb through the hole, seconds before detonation.
- **S25**：The bomb explodes, shattering the dome, freeing everybody.
- **S26**：Cargill, angry at them for ruining his plan, arrives and is about to shoot Homer with a shotgun, but gets knocked out when Maggie drops a rock on his head.
- **S27**：The town finally praises Homer, who kisses Marge on the motorcycle before riding off into the sunrise with her and Maggie.
- **S28**：The townspeople begin restoring Springfield back to normal.

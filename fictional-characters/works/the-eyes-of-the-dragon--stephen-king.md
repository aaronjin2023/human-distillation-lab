## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Title page from the original 1984 Philtrum Press edition (book issued without cover) The Eyes of the Dragon takes place within the realm of Delain.
- **S3**：It is told from the perspective of an unnamed storyteller/narrator, who speaks casually and frankly to the reader, frequently adding his own commentary on characters' motivations and the like.
- **S4**：King Roland's magician, Flagg , seeking to destroy the Kingdom of Delain, sees his plans being ruined by the good-hearted Queen Sasha.
- **S5**：After Sasha gives birth to Peter, a noble and worthy future king, Flagg realizes that his position, his plans, and his life may be in danger because of Peter.
- **S6**：While Sasha is pregnant with a second son, Flagg seizes the opportunity.
- **S7**：He forces the Queen's midwife to ensure that Sasha bleeds to death while giving birth to her second son, Thomas.
- **S8**：Sasha dies and Flagg begins plotting to remove Peter.
- **S9**：As both boys grow into adolescence, Peter becomes established as his father's favorite, and Thomas becomes increasingly jealous of him.
- **S10**：Flagg sees this and befriends Thomas, showing him the secrets of the castle.
- **S11**：He teaches him how to spy on his father from a secret hiding-place behind a mounted dragon head in Roland's study.
- **S12**：Peter begins the custom of bringing a glass of wine to his father before bed each night.
- **S13**：Flagg decides to use this as a means of framing Peter.
- **S14**：He dissolves a poison called Dragon Sand in a glass of wine and delivers it to the king.
- **S15**：Unbeknownst to Flagg, Thomas watches this through the glass eyes of the dragon.
- **S16**：Roland dies in terrible agony, and Flagg plants evidence incriminating Peter.
- **S17**：After a brief trial, Peter is declared guilty, and is locked up in a high tower called the Needle in the center of the city.
- **S18**：Thomas is then crowned King, although he is only twelve years old; due to his youth and his fearful inexperience, he allows Flagg enormous amounts of power.
- **S19**：At the start of his long stay in the Needle, Peter manages to send a note to the judge who convicted him, Anders Peyna, with the seemingly innocuous requests to have his mother's old dollhouse with him, and also to have napkins brought with his meals.
- **S20**：Peyna is puzzled by the requests, but, seeing no harm in them, grants them.
- **S21**：Five years later, Peter escapes from the Needle, having used the toy loom in the dollhouse and threads from the napkins to make a rope.
- **S22**：After the escape, he and his allies try to find King Roland's bow and arrow, which is now in Thomas' possession.
- **S23**：Flagg, now revealed as a demonic being, is about to kill them when Thomas arrives and reveals how he watched Flagg poison Roland.
- **S24**：Thomas shoots Flagg in the eye, but Flagg uses magic to disappear and escape.
- **S25**：At the end of the novel, Peter is declared to be the rightful king.
- **S26**：Thomas, who has become deeply hated in Delain, sets off alongside his butler, Dennis, to find Flagg.
- **S27**：They find him and confront him, but the narrator does not reveal the outcome.
- **S28**：## Characters
- **S29**：[ edit ] Flagg (pictured right) with King Peter from The Eyes of the Dragon .
- **S30**：Art by David Palladini House Delain [ edit ] King Roland the Good – Delain's ruler as the novel opens.
- **S31**：In his younger and more virile days, Roland was renowned for slaying Niner the Dragon with Foe-Hammer, a great hunting-arrow forged by his mother, Queen Lita.
- **S32**：Over the years since then, Roland has become somewhat infirm and addled.
- **S33**：He trusts Flagg, his counselor, little realizing the ill will that Flagg harbors toward him.
- **S34**：However, early in the story, he does favor his wife's advice more than Flagg's.
- **S35**：Roland is the husband of Queen Sasha; their sons are Peter and Thomas.
- **S36**：Queen Sasha – Roland's wife; mother of Peter and Thomas.
- **S37**：Sasha was chosen for Roland because she was an innocent girl from a lesser noble family; she is also seemingly the shyest of those whom Flagg had selected for Roland.
- **S38**：Flagg believed Sasha would pose the least long-term threat to his authority; Roland, for his part, chose her since she seemed the least frightening to him.
- **S39**：Yet Sasha revealed herself to be a strong and independent woman, who truly loved - and was truly loved in return by - both Roland and the kingdom.
- **S40**：She was murdered under Flagg's orders while giving birth to Thomas.
- **S41**：Her cousin is Squire Andrew Staad.
- **S42**：Prince Peter – Roland's elder son, heir to the throne of Delain.
- **S43**：Peter is a tall, lithe, handsome young man who has inherited his mother's good looks and his father's love of the common man.
- **S44**：He is openly favored among the people of Delain to be their next King.
- **S45**：Peter rides a horse named Peony.
- **S46**：Prince Thomas – Roland's younger son.
- **S47**：Thomas takes after Roland, being slow and heavy even in childhood.
- **S48**：He feels overshadowed by Peter and resents his elder brother because Roland clearly loves Peter more.
- **S49**：Yet Thomas, like their father, is a talented archer; in this he can outstrip even Peter.
- **S50**：As Thomas gets older, Flagg becomes his only friend and sole source of comfort.
- **S51**：Crowned "Thomas the Light-Bringer," he soon receives the nickname "Thomas the Tax-Bringer;" this is to the merciless 80% tax increase he imposed upon the people of Delain at Flagg's urging.
- **S52**：Queen Lita – granddaughter of Queen Kyla the Good; daughter of King Landry; mother of King Roland the Good; grandmother of Princes Thomas and Peter.
- **S53**：She forged the great hunting-arrow known as Foe-Hammer.
- **S54**：King Alan II (aka Alan the Mad, mentioned only) – uncle of Queen Kyla the Good; grandfather of King Landry.
- **S55**：Alan's legendarily-beautiful cousin Eleanor married Duke Leven Valera of the South Barony (aka the Black Duke).
- **S56**：Queen Kyla the Good (mentioned only) – niece of King Alan the Mad; mother of King Landry; grandmother of Queen Lita.
- **S57**：King Landry (mentioned only) – grandson of King Alan the Mad; son of Queen Kyla the Good; father of Queen Lita; grandfather of King Roland the Good House Reechul [ edit ] Charles Reechul – Naomi's father.

- **S1**：## Synopsis
- **S2**：[ edit ] The Vanishing Half is a multi-generational family saga set from the 1940s–1990s and centers on identical twin sisters Desiree and Estelle "Stella" Vignes and their daughters Jude and Kennedy, respectively.
- **S3**：Desiree and Stella are black and have exceptionally light skin.
- **S4**：They are raised in the fictional town of Mallard, Louisiana where the residents are exclusively people with light skin.
- **S5**：Desiree and Stella witness the lynching of their father in the 1940s and feel discontent living in Mallard where there are few opportunities for them.
- **S6**：A formative moment for the twins is when their mother Adele pulls them out of school so they can earn money cleaning a family's home.
- **S7**：Stella is sexually abused by the man who owns the home and conceals this from her family.
- **S8**：In 1954, at the age of 16, the twins decide to run away to New Orleans .
- **S9**：Stella begins to pass for white so she can work as a secretary at a marketing firm called Maison Blanche.
- **S10**：Her wealthy boss, Blake, falls for her, and she moves to California with him without telling Desiree.
- **S11**：Desiree is left heartbroken and ends up in an abusive marriage to a man named Sam Winston.
- **S12**：Stella and Blake get married and live in an affluent, white neighborhood in Los Angeles, California.
- **S13**：They have a daughter named Kennedy who dreams of becoming an actress.
- **S14**：In order to maintain the facade she's built, Stella has to pretend to be white for the rest of her life.
- **S15**：She lies to Blake and Kennedy by telling them that all of her family members are dead.
- **S16**：Kennedy ends up dropping out of college to pursue a career in acting, and she has a troubled relationship with her mother who she knows is lying about her background.
- **S17**：Meanwhile, Desiree leaves an abusive marriage and moves away from Washington, D.C. to return to Mallard with her eight-year-old daughter Jude.
- **S18**：Since Desiree's ex-husband has dark skin, Jude has dark skin as well.
- **S19**：Growing up in Mallard is hard for Jude, as she is ridiculed for her dark complexion.
- **S20**：Jude eventually moves to Los Angeles and attends the University of California, Los Angeles on a track scholarship.
- **S21**：She falls in love with a transgender man named Reese while in college and eventually gets a job as a caterer to help him save for his transition surgery.
- **S22**：While working part-time as a caterer in Beverly Hills , Jude sees a woman who appears to be her mother's doppelgänger .
- **S23**：The woman is her aunt Stella whom she's never met.
- **S24**：Jude later meets Kennedy at a local theater since Kennedy is in the same play as Jude's friend Barry.
- **S25**：Kennedy is disliked by most people at the theater because of her entitlement and lack of care for others.
- **S26**：Jude figures out that Kennedy is Stella's daughter, and she eventually tells Kennedy the secret of her mother's identity.
- **S27**：Jude also confronts Stella about her true identity when she comes to watch one of Kennedy's plays, causing Stella to flee.
- **S28**：At the novel's end, Jude is in medical school, and she ends up with Reese who is finally able to get his transition surgery.
- **S29**：Kennedy's relatively unsuccessful career in acting comes to an end, and she becomes a real estate agent.
- **S30**：Kennedy and Stella continue to have a rocky relationship, as Kennedy knows that her mother will never be truthful with her.
- **S31**：Desiree ends up with a man named Early who is a private investigator and bondsman, and together, they take care of the twins' mom Adele who has dementia.
- **S32**：The novel has a nonlinear narrative structure. [ 3 ]

- **性格倾向**：在知识探索受到禁令限制时，表现出敢于越界求证的倾向（行为推断；S7、S8、S52）
- **核心动机**：他致力理解外来飞船与宇宙结构，并主动促成接触（行为推断；S7、S15、S16）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Anathem is set on the fictional planet of Arbre.
- **S3**：Thousands of years before the events in the novel, the planet's intellectuals entered concents ( monastic communities) to protect their activities from the collapse of society .
- **S4**：The avout (intellectuals separated from Sæcular society) are banned from possessing or operating most advanced technology and are supervised by the Inquisition , which answers to the outside world.
- **S5**：The avout are normally allowed to communicate with people outside the walls of the concent only once every year, decade, century, or millennium, depending on the particular vows they have taken.
- **S6**：The narrator and protagonist, Fraa Erasmas, is an avout at the Concent of Saunt Edhar.
- **S7**：His teacher, Fraa Orolo, discovers that an alien spacecraft is orbiting Arbre – a fact that the world government ( Sæcular Power ) attempts to cover up.
- **S8**：Erasmas becomes aware of Orolo's discovery after Orolo is banished (in a rite called Anathem ) from the concent for using a video camera (a forbidden technology) to observe the ship.
- **S9**：The presence of the alien ship soon becomes an open secret among many of the avout at Saunt Edhar.
- **S10**：The alien ship eventually declares its presence by shining a laser upon several Millenarian Maths (the bastions of those avout who have taken a thousand-year vow of isolation).
- **S11**：Shortly after that, the Sæcular Power summons many avout from Saunt Edhar, including Erasmas and a Millenarian named Fraa Jad.
- **S12**：The avout are told to travel to the concent of Saunt Tredegarh to attend a Convox (a joint conference of the avout and the Sæcular Power).
- **S13**：However, Erasmas and several non-avout companions, with Fraa Jad's tacit agreement, decide to seek out Orolo.
- **S14**：After a dangerous journey over the planet's frozen pole, they reunite with Orolo at an archaeological excavation of Orithena, an ancient concent destroyed by volcanic eruption .
- **S15**：Orolo holds philosophical discussions with Erasmas about the nature of the cosmos and consciousness, and how he believes that the aliens are not simply from another planet, but from another cosmos that is influenced by Arbre.
- **S16**：During one of the discussions, a small spacecraft lands in Orithena on an ancient analemma symbol within the excavation. (It is later revealed that Orolo had transmitted the analemma symbol to the spaceship and anticipated the landing at Orithena.) A female alien's body is found on board, dead of a recent gunshot wound.
- **S17**：She has brought with her four vials of blood – one for each of four alien races – and evidence about their technology.
- **S18**：Shortly thereafter, the aliens propel a massive metal rod at the volcano, triggering an eruption that destroys Orithena.
- **S19**：Orolo sacrifices his life to ensure the recovery of the dead alien's remains and her blood samples, an event that leads to his canonization as Saunt Orolo.
- **S20**：Erasmas travels to Saunt Tredegarh where he attends the Convox dedicated to dealing with the military, political, and technical issues raised by the existence of the alien ship in Arbre's orbit.
- **S21**：Research is conducted on the samples from Orithena, and the aliens are found to come from planets in four parallel and distinct cosmi: Urnud, Tro, Laterre and Fthos.
- **S22**：The many-worlds interpretation is discussed by high-level avout at evening meals to which Erasmas performs the duties of a servant.
- **S23**：It is hinted that Laterre is a futuristic Earth, which serves as a 'higher plane of existence' for Urnud and Tro, and Arbre is itself a 'higher plane' for Laterre and Fthos.
- **S24**：Through observation and experiment, Erasmas and his companions determine that the conference has been infiltrated by the aliens and unmask a French-speaking Laterran linguist named Jules Verne Durand.
- **S25**：He explains that the aliens are experiencing internal conflict between two factions.
- **S26**：The currently ruling faction (the more militaristic 'lower worlds' Urnud and Tro, as well as some Laterrans) intends to attack and raid Arbre for its resources in order to repair their spaceship, while the opposing faction ('the higher world' Fthos and most Laterrans) favors open negotiation.
- **S27**：Durand believes that an alliance could be formed between Arbre and the peaceful faction and offers to help the Arbrans.
- **S28**：Fearing alien attack after Durand has been exposed, the avout simultaneously evacuate all concents including Saunt Tredegarh.
- **S29**：Erasmas and his comrades are taken to a distant sanctuary where they undergo astronaut training for a mission to board the alien ship, disable its weaponry and negotiate with the aliens.
- **S30**：Prior to launch and without their knowledge, the Sæcular Power implants each of them with miniaturized neutron bombs that will be used to kill everyone aboard the alien ship if the mission fails.
- **S31**：Three people, including Fraa Jad, are issued detonators.
- **S32**：The team is launched into space on modified ballistic missiles and approaches the alien ship by stealth.
- **S33**：Four of the avout destroy the ship's main weapon before dying in combat.
- **S34**：The rest of the team boards the ship and temporarily pass out from breathing alien air.
- **S35**：The narrative now parallelizes across multiple timelines.
- **S36**：In one narrative, Fraa Jad awakens Erasmas and leads him through the ship toward the command center.
- **S37**：Upon being discovered and attacked by alien soldiers, Fraa Jad detonates the neutron bombs.
- **S38**：In another narrative, soldiers take Erasmas and Fraa Jad captive and bring them to parley with the leader of the peaceful faction.
- **S39**：In the final narrative, Erasmas awakens in a hospital on the starship and learns that diplomatic negotiations are underway thanks to the successful destruction of the alien weapon.
- **S40**：Erasmas is told that Fraa Jad died in an accident during the launch, contradicting the other narratives.
- **S41**：It remains ambiguous which (or how many) of these contradictory narratives are real, or how the narratives have influenced each other.
- **S42**：It is implied that some Millenarian avout are capable of operating simultaneously in multiple parallel timelines, and that the Millenarians had called the alien ship to Arbre to disrupt the subjugation of the Avout by the Sæcular Power.
- **S43**：Erasmas attends a diplomatic summit where a funeral ceremony is held for those lost on both sides and a peace process begins between the aliens and the Arbrans.
- **S44**：On Arbre itself, the Sæcular Power and the avout have agreed to cooperate as equal powers.
- **S45**：The Arbrans inaugurate a second "Reconstitution", revising many of the rules that had restricted the work and lifestyle of the avout.
- **S46**：Erasmas and his friends begin to build a new concent, open to the outside world and dedicated to Saunt Orolo.
- **S47**：## Characters
- **S48**：[ edit ] Erasmas (nickname "Raz"): The protagonist of Anathem ; a Decenarian fraa from the Concent of Saunt Edhar.
- **S49**：The neglected son of a lower-class family, he was collected by the concent at the age of eight.
- **S50**：Orolo : A Decenarian fraa from the Concent of Saunt Edhar.
- **S51**：He is an eminent cosmographer and Erasmas's mentor at the concent.
- **S52**：He is banished for using forbidden technology to observe the alien ship in violation of the Discipline's isolation requirements.
- **S53**：He dies while recovering the alien body from Orithena, and is canonized as a Saunt for his sacrifice.
- **S54**：Arsibalt : A Decenarian fraa from the Concent of Saunt Edhar and one of Erasmas's friends.
- **S55**：The estranged son of a religious official, he seeks to reconcile religion with theorics.
- **S56**：Lio : A Decenarian fraa from the Concent of Saunt Edhar and one of Erasmas's friends.
- **S57**：He's known as an absent-minded eccentric and is interested in military history , Vale-lore ( martial arts ), and unusual gardening techniques.
- **S58**：He leads the space mission to destroy the alien weapon.
- **S59**：Jesry : A Decenarian fraa from the Concent of Saunt Edhar and one of Erasmas's friends.
- **S60**：Unlike Erasmas, Jesry comes from a prosperous family, and is bored with the routine of mathic life preceding the arrival of the aliens.
- **S61**：He becomes famous for going into space with the Warden of Heaven (a religious leader of the Sæcular Power) to investigate the alien ship.
- **S62**：Ala : A Decenarian suur from the Concent of Saunt Edhar.
- **S63**：She becomes a major organizer of the Convox, and later, the Arbran resistance.
- **S64**：Although they disliked each other as children, she and Erasmas become romantically involved in the course of the story.
- **S65**：Jad : A Millenarian fraa from the Concent of Saunt Edhar.
- **S66**：Jad is summoned to the Convox at the same time of Erasmas, but tells Erasmas to look for Orolo instead of traveling directly to the Convox.
- **S67**：Jad is a participant in philosophical discussions at the Convox and plays a key role in the mission to the alien ship.
- **S68**：It is hinted that he is hundreds of years old and possesses the ability to be conscious within multiple parallel universes.
- **S69**：Cord : Erasmas' half-sister and a machinesmith who lives near the Concent of Saunt Edhar.
- **S70**：She accompanies Erasmas on his search for Orolo.
- **S71**：She and Yul become romantically involved in the course of the story.
- **S72**：Sammann : A member of a social caste of computer experts from the Concent of Saunt Edhar who accompanies Erasmas on his search for Orolo.
- **S73**：He provides technological support and knowledge for Erasmas' companions throughout the story.
- **S74**：Yulassetar Crade (nickname "Yul"): A wilderness guide who joins the expedition to find Orolo.
- **S75**：He and Cord become romantically involved in the course of the story.
- **S76**：Ganelial Crade (nickname "Gnel"): A religious man who volunteers to drive the avout to the Convox, before joining the search for Orolo.
- **S77**：Erasmas mistrusts Gnel at first, but becomes friends over the course of the story.
- **S78**：Jules Verne Durand : A linguist from Laterre who infiltrates the Convox to gather information for the aliens.
- **S79**：He defects to the Arbrans and joins the mission to the alien ship.

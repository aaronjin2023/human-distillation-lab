## 角色资料

### Carl Fredricksen

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-4cb2ed810685c93a` · 独立版本：`profile-933ef542b91c564e`

- **性格倾向**：丧偶后他在住宅问题上固执防御，并因信箱受损与人冲突。（行为推断；S10、S11、S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：他要兑现与艾莉去天堂瀑布的约定，并守住共同生活留下的家。（行为推断；S11、S14、S17、S36）
- **决策方式**：他用气球带屋远行实现旧约，后为追上罗素而舍弃屋内家具。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S29）
- **价值取舍**：他最终营救凯文与罗素并接受失去房屋，显示此时更看重伙伴。（行为推断；S30、S33）
- **成长变化**：艾莉的留言促使他重新行动，后来与罗素建立祖孙般的陪伴。（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28、S35、S37）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

### Russell

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a5a06bb11d6534da` · 独立版本：`profile-33602da95e063da6`

- **性格倾向**：在争取助老徽章时他热切而执着，反复缠着卡尔寻求机会。（行为推断；S15）
- **核心动机**：他起初想获得最后的助老徽章，后来又主动设法救回凯文。（行为推断；S15、S28）
- **关系模式**：他对卡尔的选择感到愤怒，后来仍与卡尔建立祖孙般的陪伴。（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25、S26、S35、S37）
- **决策方式**：面对凯文被捕的处境，他倾向先自行行动，乘气球前去救援。（行为推断；S28）
- **成长变化**：他从偶然搭乘者转为主动救援者，并与卡尔形成持续的陪伴。（行为推断；S15、S28、S35、S37）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

### Charles Muntz

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-fa2857986d70b42a` · 独立版本：`profile-03b9dffbe2e253cf`

- **性格倾向**：长期独处并专注捕鸟使他在此情境中偏执多疑，轻易把来者当敌人。（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21、S22）
- **核心动机**：他执意活捉天堂瀑布巨鸟，以洗清昔日被指造假的名声。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S4、S20）
- **决策方式**：听见罗素与凯文相熟便转为敌对，以追捕和纵火迫使对方交出鸟。（摘要直接叙述；S22、S23、S24）
- **价值取舍**：他把证明自己和取得巨鸟置于探险者生命与他人安全之前。（行为推断；S22、S24、S32）
- **关系模式**：他以主人身份支配杜格及犬群，也把来访的卡尔、罗素视作威胁。（行为推断；S19、S20、S22、S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Young Carl Fredricksen is a shy, quiet boy who idolizes renowned explorer Charles F.
- **S2**：Muntz.
- **S3**：He is saddened to learn, however, that Muntz has been accused of fabricating the skeleton of a giant bird he had claimed to have discovered in Paradise Falls, Venezuela, and was publicly disgraced.
- **S4**：Muntz vowed to return to Paradise Falls and not leave until he had captured a specimen alive to clear his name.
- **S5**：One day, Carl befriends an energetic and somewhat eccentric tomboy named Ellie, who is also a Muntz fan.
- **S6**：She confides to Carl her desire to move her "clubhouse"—an abandoned house in the neighborhood—to a cliff overlooking Paradise Falls, making him promise to help her.
- **S7**：Carl and Ellie eventually get married and grow old together in the restored house, working as a toy balloon vendor and a zookeeper, respectively.
- **S8**：After being told they are infertile, the two decide to realize their dream of visiting Paradise Falls.
- **S9**：They try to save up for the trip, but repeatedly end up spending the money on more pressing needs.
- **S10**：Finally, elderly Carl Fredricksen arranges for the trip, but Ellie suddenly becomes ill and dies, leaving him alone.
- **S11**：Some time later, Carl is still living in their house, now surrounded by urban development, but he refuses to sell.
- **S12**：He ends up injuring a construction worker over damage done to his mailbox.
- **S13**：He is evicted from the house by court order due to being deemed a "public menace", and is ordered to move to a retirement home.
- **S14**：However, Carl comes up with a scheme to keep his promise to Ellie: he turns his house into a makeshift airship, using thousands of helium balloons to lift it off its foundation.
- **S15**：A young member of the "Wilderness Explorers"  named Russell becomes an accidental passenger, having pestered Carl earlier in an attempt to earn his final merit badge, "Assisting the Elderly".
- **S16**：After surviving a thunderstorm, the house lands near a large ravine facing Paradise Falls.
- **S17**：Carl and Russell harness themselves to the still-buoyant house and begin to walk it around the ravine, hoping to reach the falls before the balloons deflate.
- **S18**：They later befriend a tall, colorful flightless bird  trying to reach her chicks, and then a dog named Dug, who wears a special collar that allows him to speak.
- **S19**：Carl and Russell encounter a pack of dogs led by Alpha, and are taken to Dug's master, who turns out to be an elderly Charles Muntz.
- **S20**：Muntz invites Carl and Russell aboard his dirigible, where he explains that he has spent the years since his disgrace searching Paradise Falls for the giant bird.
- **S21**：The time he has spent alone and concentrating only on his mission has made him extremely paranoid, mentally unstable and dangerous.
- **S22**：When Russell innocently reveals his friendship with Kevin, Muntz becomes disturbingly hostile and starts showing the flight helmets of explorers whom he has apparently eliminated, believing they were all after the bird.
- **S23**：This prompts Carl, Russell, Kevin and Dug to flee, chased by Muntz's dogs.
- **S24**：Muntz eventually catches up with them and starts a fire beneath Carl's house, forcing Carl to choose between saving his home or Kevin.
- **S25**：Carl rushes to put out the fire, allowing Muntz to take the bird.
- **S26**：Carl and Russell eventually reach the falls, but Russell is angry with Carl.
- **S27**：Settling into his home, Carl discovers photos of their married life in Ellie's childhood scrapbook and a final note from his wife thanking him for the "adventure" and encouraging him to go on a new one.
- **S28**：Reinvigorated, he goes to find Russell, only to see him sailing off on some balloons to rescue Kevin.
- **S29**：Because many balloons have popped or deflated from Muntz's attack, Carl is forced to empty the house of furniture so it can lift off again so that Carl can pursue Russell.
- **S30**：Russell is captured by Muntz, but Carl boards the dirigible in flight and frees both Russell and Kevin.
- **S31**：Muntz pursues them around the airship, finally cornering Dug, Kevin, and Russell inside Carl's tethered house.
- **S32**：Carl lures Kevin out through a window and back onto the airship with Dug and Russell clinging to her back, just as Muntz is about to close in; the insane hunter leaps after them, only to snag his foot on some balloon lines and fall to his death.
- **S33**：Snapped from its tether, the house descends out of sight through the clouds, which Carl accepts as being for the best.
- **S34**：Carl, Russell and Dug reunite Kevin with her chicks, then fly the dirigible back to the city.
- **S35**：When Russell's father misses his son's Senior Explorer ceremony, Carl proudly presents Russell with his final badge for assisting the elderly, as well as a personal addition: the grape soda cap that Ellie gave to Carl when they first met .
- **S36**：Meanwhile, Carl's house is shown to have landed on the cliff beside Paradise Falls, as promised to Ellie.
- **S37**：During the credits, a series of photographs shows Carl enjoying his latest adventure: living an active life as a surrogate grandfather to Russell.

## 角色资料

### Astro

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3828e61ce7148e0a` · 独立版本：`profile-6ac00436b35ad481`

- **性格倾向**：面对机器人对战与城市危机，他倾向克制敌意并主动保护他人（行为推断；S32、S37、S42）
- **核心动机**：他将拯救城市理解为自身存在的意义，并据此承担危险（摘要直接叙述；S41、S42）
- **价值取舍**：面对明确的死亡代价，他仍将众人安全置于自身存续之前（行为推断；S37、S41、S42、S43）
- **关系模式**：他愿意守护科拉的秘密，却对暴露自身机器人身份有所迟疑（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29）
- **成长变化**：他从承受替代品身份的排斥，走向自主确认自身使命（行为推断；S14、S17、S18、S42）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：不愿伤害机器人同伴，却在无法离开的竞技场被迫摧毁对手。

### Doctor Tenma

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-c9229b1659ac7778` · 独立版本：`profile-d1437940596d5edd`

- **性格倾向**：在丧子情境中，他倾向以技术控制回应失去亲人的痛苦（行为推断；S11、S15）
- **核心动机**：他制造复制体并赋予强大能力，意在挽回儿子并避免再次失去（摘要直接叙述；S11、S15）
- **关系模式**：他起初以亡子的相似度衡量复制体，使父子接纳受到限制（行为推断；S14、S17、S18）
- **成长变化**：他最终放下复制亡子的要求，承认阿童木具有独立的儿子身份（摘要直接叙述；S34）
- **决策方式**：在取出核心的关键时刻，他以重新确认的亲情改变原定行动（行为推断；S34）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：想用超常能力保护儿子，却又因这些差异难以接纳复制体。

### President Stone

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-bfb7af925c1ceef7` · 独立版本：`profile-d103cfc4b7a9ab89`

- **性格倾向**：面对机器人力量时，他倾向强行掌控并以武力压制阻碍（行为推断；S6、S19、S20、S33、S35）
- **核心动机**：他围绕蓝色核心持续追捕阿童木，行动指向将其置于己方控制（行为推断；S19、S20、S33、S34）
- **价值取舍**：他在处理机器人时倾向工具化利用，轻视其自主意愿（行为推断；S24、S33、S34）
- **决策方式**：即使红色核心曾造成失控，他仍选择再次使用同一危险力量（摘要直接叙述；S6、S35）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：追求对强大武器的控制，最终反被武器吸收并卷入失控。

- **S5**：Doctor Elefun, one of Tenma's colleagues, introduces Stone to the Blue and Red Cores, two energy spheres mined from a star fragment, that produce energy which can power robots via positive and negative energy, respectively.
- **S6**：Stone places the Red Core in the Peacekeeper, causing it to go out of control.
- **S7**：The Peacekeeper tries to attack the humans, but Tenma puts a barrier between them.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, Toby has been released early from school, and rewires Orrin, his family's robot, in order to attend the Ministry of Science's demonstration of the Peacekeeper.
- **S9**：Seeking to have a closer look at the Peacekeeper, Toby enters the same room as the robot, only for the Peacekeeper's first attempt to destroy the barrier to backfire with the result that Toby is totally annihilated.
- **S10**：After the Peacekeeper is stopped by security forces, Dr.
- **S11**：Tenma, wracked with despair, builds an identical robotic clone of Toby, using DNA from a strand of his hair to obtain his memories, which will make the robot think he is Toby.
- **S12**：He also powers the robot with the positive blue core.
- **S13**：The robot quickly comes to life, and Tenma brings him home.
- **S14**：Tenma quickly realizes that while the robot possesses Toby's memories, it is not exactly like his original son, as the robot's higher mental and physical powers frequently remind him.
- **S15**：Tenma had initially included the robot's powers in order to prevent his "son" from being destroyed again.
- **S16**：Unaware that he is a robot, Toby tries to figure out why he can understand the language of some robot cleaners and discovers he can fly via rocket-boosters hidden in his shoes.
- **S17**：He discovers various other abilities and heads home to tell his father, only to find that Tenma and Elefun are discussing deactivating him.
- **S18**：Tenma reveals to Toby that he is a robot.
- **S19**：President Stone's men detect the Blue Core's energy signature and pursue Toby.
- **S20**：Stone calls in the Spirit of Freedom: a massive, heavily-armed, flying battleship.
- **S21**：Rendered unconscious, Toby falls off the floating city and lands in the junkyard below.
- **S22**：He meets several children including Cora, a girl who left Metro City after her parents neglected her, and a robotic dog named Trashcan.
- **S23**：Toby meets the Robot Revolution Front: Sparx, Robotsky and Mike the Fridge, who easily identify Toby as a robot and rename him "Astro".
- **S24**：Astro goes along with his new name and lives with the children and their fatherly figure, Hamegg, who appears to care for broken robots, but secretly treats robots just as callously as Stone, and also runs the Robot Games: destructive gladiatorial matches in which robots are forced to fight to the death.
- **S25**：Astro and his friends find an offline construction robot named ZOG, and Astro secretly revives ZOG.
- **S26**：The kids bring him back and clean him up for the Hamegg games.
- **S27**：Later that night, Astro comes across Cora trying to call Metrocity.
- **S28**：She reveals to him that she actually has parents and was worried if they even missed her.
- **S29**：Astro promises not to tell and tries to tell her his secret but can't.
- **S30**：The next day at the Hamegg games, Hamegg betrays Astro, learning of him being a robot, and pits Astro against other robots in the Robot Games.
- **S31**：Unable to simply leave, Astro reluctantly destroys all the robots.
- **S32**：Hamegg releases ZOG for Astro to fight, but both refuse to fight one another.
- **S33**：Stone and his men arrive then and arrest Astro.
- **S34**：They take him back to the Ministry of Science, where Tenma is asked to remove the Blue Core; however, at the last moment, he has a change of heart, accepting that even if Astro is not Toby, he is still his son, and allows Astro to escape.

- **S36**：Astro and Stone fight across the city, demolishing most of the buildings and causing the city to begin crashing to Earth.

- **S38**：Cora, the other children, and the Robot Revolution try to help to bring down the Peacekeeper/Stone fusion.

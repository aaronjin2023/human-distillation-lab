## 材料限制

- 两条行动线均以任务推进为主，麦克斯韦的私人心理只通过招募与营救安排间接呈现。
- 对山姆等人的医疗协助不足以支持完整性格画像，因此未扩大角色数量。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] In 1970, former Navy SEAL John Kelly picks up a hitchhiker named Pam on his way home near the Chesapeake Bay .
- **S3**：They become lovers, and over time Kelly discovers that Pam is a drug mule and prostitute who had recently escaped from her pimp Henry Tucker.
- **S4**：With the help of husband-and-wife doctors Sam and Sarah Rosen, Pam starts to recover.
- **S5**：Weeks later, Kelly and Pam go to Baltimore for a follow-up, where she is recognized and pursued by one of Henry's men.
- **S6**：Kelly is gravely wounded while Pam is recaptured and later tortured, gang-raped and killed.
- **S7**：In North Vietnam , a U.S. target drone discovers Air Force Colonel Robin Zacharias as a prisoner of war in a secret camp administered by the NVA .
- **S8**：Since Zacharias possesses highly classified knowledge and has been declared killed in action, Admiral Dutch Maxwell arranges a secret rescue mission for him and other American POWs in the camp.
- **S9**：Unbeknownst to them, Soviet colonel Nikolay Grishanov has been interrogating the prisoners and lobbies his government to transport Zacharias and his fellow prisoners into the Soviet Union , citing their intelligence value.
- **S10**：Kelly recovers from his wounds with the help of Dr.
- **S11**：Rosen and his head nurse, Sandra "Sandy" O'Toole.
- **S12**：He wages a private war on Tucker's drug ring by killing some of its players.
- **S13**：Kelly asks the Rosens and O'Toole to help rehabilitate one of the rescued prostitutes, Doris Brown, and obtains more information on the drug ring from brutally torturing one of Pam's pimps, William "Billy" Grayson, using a pressure chamber designed to simulate deep-water diving conditions; Billy is left to die from severe decompression sickness .
- **S14**：The murders draw the attention of detective Emmet Ryan, who had investigated Kelly's initial altercation and Pam's subsequent death.
- **S15**：Kelly is recruited by Admiral Maxwell as a pathfinder for the rescue mission on Zacharias and other American POWs, since he had previously gone behind enemy lines to rescue Maxwell's son.
- **S16**：However, an American mole informs the Soviets of the rescue mission, leading to the NVA moving the prisoners to a different location.
- **S17**：Nevertheless, Kelly manages to capture Grishanov while he escapes from the camp.
- **S18**：The Soviet colonel is then used as leverage to negotiate the transfer of Colonel Zacharias and his fellow prisoners to the Hanoi Hilton .
- **S19**：Returning from Vietnam, Kelly discovers that Doris and her father Raymond have been murdered and continues his mission.
- **S20**：He also finds out that the heroin processed by Tucker's drug ring was smuggled into the U.S. through the corpses of American soldiers.
- **S21**：Meanwhile, the CIA try to recruit him following his actions in Vietnam, but due to Kelly's difficult position, they agree to help him escape his legal troubles in return for the assassination of the mole who had jeopardized the Vietnam operation.
- **S22**：Kelly forces the supposed mole, an aide to the Special Assistant to the National Security Advisor named Walter "Wally" Hicks, to kill himself, inadvertently leaving the real mole, a Senate aide and anti-war activist named Peter Henderson, in place.
- **S23**：Kelly then rushes to complete his vendetta by killing Tucker, his partner Tony Piaggi, and corrupt police lieutenant Mark Charon.
- **S24**：Ryan finds out that Kelly is the vigilante targeting drug dealers and his colleague Charon.
- **S25**：He confronts him on his boat, where Kelly bargains for an hour's head start before he arrests him.
- **S26**：Ryan agrees, and Kelly fakes his death by capsizing his boat near a cargo ship at the Chesapeake Bay.
- **S27**：He is then extracted by his CIA superiors, who officially recruit him under his new identity as John Clark.
- **S28**：Kelly quietly resumes contact with O'Toole and marries her.
- **S29**：Three years later, Zacharias and his fellow POWs are released after the end of the American involvement in the Vietnam War .

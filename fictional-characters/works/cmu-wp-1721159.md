## 角色资料

### Inspector Clouseau

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7362fa3e6633a272` · 独立版本：`profile-72368b966dd7dc4a`

- **性格倾向**：调查屡屡失误时，他仍呈现自信执拗且难以修正判断的倾向（行为推断；S5、S9、S11、S25）
- **核心动机**：他把追查钻石失窃视为向曾陷害自己的查尔斯报复的机会（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **决策方式**：他围绕既定嫌疑人追踪调查，实际接近真相却未能识别全貌（行为推断；S5、S10、S11、S15）
- **成长变化**：获晋升后他仍誓言追捕查尔斯，显示成功并未纠正原有误判（行为推断；S12、S18、S25）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：办案成果获得肯定，但关键脱险与破局多带偶然性

### Chief Inspector Charles Dreyfus

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-ff4abb31aacad1f0` · 独立版本：`profile-c930dc7b557388a4`

- **性格倾向**：面对克卢索时，他呈现难以节制的敌意并不断升级报复行为（行为推断；S3、S17、S27）
- **核心动机**：他对克卢索的憎恶发展为杀害对方的企图与持续复仇愿望（摘要直接叙述；S3、S17、S27）
- **决策方式**：他利用上司身份发出逮捕指令，将克卢索诱入预设射击位置（行为推断；S15、S16、S17）
- **价值取舍**：他为私人敌意越过警务职责，甚至以暗杀处理同僚关系（行为推断；S3、S4、S17、S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：身居执法职位，却因私人怨恨成为暗杀者

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In the fictional country of Lugash, a mysterious thief breaks into the National Museum, sneaks past its elaborate security system, and makes off with the fabled Pink Panther diamond, leaving a white monogrammed glove with a gold-tinted "P" as a calling card.
- **S2**：The Lugash Secret Police are brought in to investigate, and the Shah of Lugash requests the assistance of Inspector Clouseau .
- **S3**：Clouseau has been demoted to beat cop by his boss, Chief Inspector Charles Dreyfus , who despises Clouseau to the point of obsession.
- **S4**：However, the French government forces Dreyfus to reinstate Clouseau as Inspector of the Sûreté so that he can go to Lugash to investigate the theft of the Pink Panther.
- **S5**：Clouseau's investigations at the Lugash National Museum, which he nearly destroys, lead him to believe that Sir Charles Lytton , the notorious Phantom, is re-creating the most famous heist of his career.
- **S6**：Clouseau is delighted at this, and sees his chance to get revenge on Lytton for framing him and temporarily sending him to prison in the first film.
- **S7**：Sir Charles, having read about the theft, realizes that he has been framed.
- **S8**：He goes to Lugash to investigate and clear his name.
- **S9**：Although Clouseau fails to uncover any leads concerning the theft, his bumbling allows him to survive several attempts on his life by a mysterious assassin.
- **S10**：After staking out  Lytton Manor in Nice, Clouseau is tricked into leaving France.
- **S11**：He follows Sir Charles' wife, Lady Claudine  to a resort hotel in Gstaad, Switzerland, where his attempts to investigate her repeatedly fail.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, Sir Charles manages to discover the identity of the true thief - his wife .
- **S13**：Because they were both bored with their quiet retirement, she stole the diamond for her own excitement, and sent her husband on a wild goose chase.
- **S14**：Sir Charles makes a daring escape from Lugash and goes to Gstaad to find his wife and the diamond.
- **S15**：Inspector Clouseau, who has unknowingly been on the trail of the real thief all along, receives a telephone call from Chief Inspector Dreyfus telling him to arrest Lady Claudine.
- **S16**：However, when Clouseau calls Dreyfus back to ask why, he is informed that Dreyfus has gone on vacation and will not be back until the following week.
- **S17**：Dreyfus, now revealed as the assassin trying to kill Clouseau, prepares to shoot him with a sniper rifle as soon as he enters Lady Claudine's room.
- **S18**：Lady Claudine playfully confesses the theft to her husband, and hands the diamond over to him, so he can go about proving his innocence.
- **S19**：They are cornered by Colonel Sharki  of the Lugash Secret Police, who intends to kill them both.
- **S20**：It turns out he has been using the theft of the diamond as an excuse to purge his political opponents.
- **S21**：Clouseau barges into the room to arrest the Lyttons.
- **S22**：Sir Charles points out that Colonel Sharki is going to kill them all.
- **S23**：Suddenly, Dreyfus opens fire on the room, and accidentally kills Sharki while aiming at Clouseau, who ducks at the last minute to check his fly.
- **S24**：The Lyttons escape during the fray.
- **S25**：For his work in recovering the Pink Panther, Clouseau is promoted to Chief Inspector, and vows to bring Sir Charles to justice.
- **S26**：Lady Claudine's fate is not revealed to the audience, but it is implied she was not arrested.
- **S27**：Dreyfus is committed to a lunatic asylum, where he is straitjacketed and placed inside a Padded cell, vowing revenge on Clouseau as the animated Pink Panther enters the room.

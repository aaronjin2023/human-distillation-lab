## 引用证据

- **S1**：Carrie walks through the streets of New York City thinking about events that has happened to her and her friends during Sex and the City.
- **S2**：Charlotte is now living a happy marriage with Harry Goldenblatt, but she had a hard time getting pregnant, so they adopted a girl named Lily; Miranda has settled down in Brooklyn with Steve  to raise their son Brady together; and Samantha has relocated her business to Los Angeles to be close to Smith ([[Jason Lewis , who is now a superstar, although she misses her old life and takes every opportunity to fly East to be with Carrie, Miranda, and Charlotte.
- **S3**：Carrie herself is now in a relationship with Big , and they are viewing apartments with plans to move in together.
- **S4**：Carrie falls in love with a penthouse far from their price range.
- **S5**：Big immediately agrees to pay for it.
- **S6**：Carrie offers to sell her own apartment, although she also voices her fear that she would have no legal rights to their home in case they seperate, as they are not married.
- **S7**：To quell her fears, Big suggests that they marry.
- **S8**：Carrie announces the news to her friends.
- **S9**：Charlotte hires her a wedding planner.
- **S10**：Miranda confesses to her friends that she has been so busy she hasn’t had sex with Steve in six months.
- **S11**：When Steve confesses he has cheated on her, Miranda is devastated and immediately separates from him.
- **S12**：At Carrie and Big's rehearsal dinner, Steve tries to reconcile with Miranda, but she rebuffs him.
- **S13**：Still upset with Steve, she tells Big bluntly that marriage ruins everything.
- **S14**：On the wedding day, Big is too fearful to go through with the ceremony.
- **S15**：Carrie, devastated, flees the wedding.
- **S16**：Samantha stays behind to clear the guests.
- **S17**：Big changes his mind and catches up with Carrie in an attempt to reconcile.
- **S18**：Carrie furiously attacks him with her bouquet while he earns scathing looks from Miranda and Charlotte.
- **S19**：To console Carrie, the four women take the honeymoon that Carrie had booked to Mexico, where they de-stress and collect themselves.
- **S20**：Upon returning to New York, Carrie hires an assistant, Louise , to help her manage her life.
- **S21**：Miranda eventually confesses to Carrie about what happened at the rehearsal dinner, and the two briefly fall out.
- **S22**：After reflecting on the argument she had with Carrie, Miranda agrees to attend couples counseling with Steve, and they are eventually able to reconcile.
- **S23**：Samantha finds her five-year-old relationship humdrum and begins over-eating to keep from cheating on Smith.
- **S24**：She eventually realizes that she needs to put herself first, so she says farewell to Smith and moves back to New York.
- **S25**：Around the same time, Louise quits her job as Carrie's assistant to get married.
- **S26**：Charlotte learns she is pregnant, and for several months is fearful that something might happen to her baby.
- **S27**：Carrie put her fear to rest.
- **S28**：Later, Charlotte has a surprise encounter with Big that leaves her so outraged that her water breaks.
- **S29**：Big takes her to the hospital and waits until baby Rose is born, hoping to see Carrie.
- **S30**：Harry passes on the message that Big would like her to call him, and that he has written her frequently, but never received a reply.
- **S31**：Carrie searches her correspondence and finds that he has sent her dozens of letters copied from the book she read him before their wedding, culminating with one of his own where he apologizes for screwing them up and promises to love her forever.
- **S32**：Carrie travels to the house Big had bought for them to collect a pair of Manolo Blahnik shoes she had left there.
- **S33**：She finds Big in the walk-in closet he had built for her, and the moment she sees him, her anger at his betrayal dissipates.
- **S34**：They share a passionate kiss, and Big proposes to Carrie properly, using one of her diamond-encrusted shoes in place of a ring.
- **S35**：They later marry alone, in a simple wedding in New York City Hall, with Carrie wearing a simple dress.
- **S36**：Miranda, Samantha, and Charlotte turns up to surprise Carrie.
- **S37**：The film ends with the four women sipping cosmopolitans, celebrating Samantha's fiftieth birthday, with Carrie making a toast to the next fifty.

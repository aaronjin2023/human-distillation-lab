## 角色资料

### Cha Tae-sik

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d3eb3a579177964f` · 独立版本：`profile-a227a5433287526a`

- **性格倾向**：平日退隐寡交的他，在珍视之人遇险时呈现执拗保护的倾向（行为推断；S1、S2、S9、S12、S17）
- **核心动机**：他重新面对外部世界的直接目标，是找到并救出小米（摘要直接叙述；S9、S17）
- **决策方式**：面对人质要挟，他先作交换合作，识破危险后转为主动追踪（行为推断；S7、S8、S11、S12、S19）
- **情绪反应**：误信小米遇害时他悲恸求死，告别时又拥抱她并落泪（摘要直接叙述；S20、S22、S29）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：救人时果断行动，却在误信小米遇害后陷入求死的绝望

### Man-seok

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-f8a77d2895f37c10` · 独立版本：`profile-7e50ee8f82735e0e`

- **性格倾向**：在控制对手时，他倾向利用人质与欺骗迫使他人服从（行为推断；S8、S10、S20）
- **核心动机**：弟弟被杀后，他以宣称杀死小米的方式向泰锡实施报复（摘要直接叙述；S18、S20）
- **价值取舍**：他将母女安危当作犯罪筹码，置他人生命于团伙利益之后（行为推断；S8、S10、S11）
- **决策方式**：他通过虚假释放承诺驱使泰锡，借送货安排清除竞争者（行为推断；S8、S10）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Operating a pawn shop in a small neighborhood, ex-Black ops officer Cha Tae-sik now leads a quiet life.
- **S2**：His only connection to the rest of the world is a little girl, So-mi, who lives next door.
- **S3**：So-mi’s mother, Hyo-jeong, is a heroin addict who steals drugs from an organized crime group.
- **S4**：She pawns her camera bag to Tae-sik; he does not realize it has the stolen drugs inside.
- **S5**：When Oh's men find out where the drugs are, by torturing Hyo-jeong, they proceed to kidnap her and her daughter.
- **S6**：A mule and organ dealer is sent to Tae-sik's pawn shop to retrieve the stolen drugs, but they are easily overpowered by Tae-sik.
- **S7**：However, upon learning that the gang now has in their possession both Hyo-jeong and So-mi, Tae-sik gives the beaten gang members the camera bag and its contents.
- **S8**：Realizing that Tae-sik may serve better as a mule than their former thug, the gang brothers that blackmailed Tae-sik – Man-seok and Jong-seok – promise to release Hyo-jeong and So-mi under the condition that Tae-sik make a delivery for them.
- **S9**：Tae-sik makes the decision to face the outside world in order to rescue So-mi.
- **S10**：However, the delivery was part of a larger plot to eliminate a rival.
- **S11**：Police arrest Tae-sik and discover Hyo-jeong's body, with her organs harvested, in the back of the car he used to make the delivery, and Tae-sik realizes that So-mi is also in danger.
- **S12**：Tae-sik escapes from the police station to search for So-mi.
- **S13**：During his escape, the police are bewildered at Tae-sik's display of power, combat techniques, and agility.
- **S14**：They further investigate his bio and find out that he was once a black operations agent for the Korean government with numerous commendations, but left the Service after his pregnant wife was murdered by an assassin.
- **S15**：The assassin shot Tae-sik twice, but was killed by another agency's officers before he could kill Tae-sik.
- **S16**：A determined narcotics cop contacts a weakened Tae-sik after his encounter with the gang's highly refined Thai street thug, Ramrowan, who works for the brothers.
- **S17**：Now with the knowledge that So-mi is being used as an "ant" – a child used by Chinese gangs to smuggle drugs and money – and that she will be killed to harvest her organs, Tae-sik sets out to locate and save So-mi.
- **S18**：Tae-sik finds and frees several child slaves in a drug manufacturing plant, in the process and killing off the younger of the brothers in charge of the gang, Jong-seok.
- **S19**：He tracks the elder brother, Man-seok, to the gang's condo.
- **S20**：Man-seok shows him a vessel containing what he says are So-mi's eyes, explaining that he has had her killed to avenge his brother's death.
- **S21**：In a rage, Tae-sik kills the gang members, including Ramrowan and Man-seok.
- **S22**：Tae-sik is preparing to commit suicide out of his grief when a scared and dirty So-mi emerges from the darkness.
- **S23**：She had been saved by Ramrowan; he had taken pity on her because she had been kind to him.
- **S24**：It is then revealed that the eyes in the container belonged to the gangster's harvesting surgeon, who had been killed off-camera by Ramrowan.
- **S25**：A police escort takes Tae-sik and So-mi together in the back of the detective’s car, to take Tae-sik into custody.
- **S26**：While she sleeps, Tae-sik asks if they can be dropped off at a small convenience store.
- **S27**：Tae-sik buys a backpack along with other school supplies.
- **S28**：He tells her that she's going to be on her own now, as the police have to take him away.
- **S29**：Before he goes, he asks her for a hug, and breaks down in tears as they embrace.

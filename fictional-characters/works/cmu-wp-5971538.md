## 角色资料

### Don

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-558fd72e7dc7d586` · 独立版本：`profile-7dd34659eadd2ac4`

- **性格倾向**：在感染者逼近的极端情境下，他倾向优先确保自己逃生（行为推断；S11、S13）
- **核心动机**：重见被隔离的妻子后，他希望获得对弃她逃走的原谅（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **决策方式**：为私下接近妻子，他选择绕过安全管制而未经授权探视（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **关系模式**：他与妻子的重逢被先前的抛弃及随后求原谅所笼罩（行为推断；S11、S28）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾持械保护家人，却在危急时留下妻子逃生

### Sergeant Doyle

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-991d23e9174e80f6` · 独立版本：`profile-1b1fc0996ff84505`

- **性格倾向**：在军令与平民生命冲突时，他倾向主动承担违令风险（行为推断；S33、S38）
- **核心动机**：得知孩子可能关系到解药后，他继续护送他们寻求撤离（行为推断；S35、S38、S43）
- **价值取舍**：他将保护平民置于无差别射杀命令与个人撤离机会之前（行为推断；S33、S36、S37、S38）
- **决策方式**：面对毒气封锁，他倾向现场寻找交通工具并亲自推动脱困（行为推断；S38、S39）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：军人职责与拒绝伤害平民的底线发生冲突

### Major Scarlet Levy

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-c28031024b670ab5` · 独立版本：`profile-1761b3133ad70155`

- **性格倾向**：在防疫体系转向杀戮时，她倾向主动保护具体的受威胁者（行为推断；S32、S40）
- **核心动机**：她认为孩子可能提供治愈关键，主张必须全力保护他们（摘要直接叙述；S35）
- **价值取舍**：面对全面清除感染的措施，她倾向保留救治与研究的希望（行为推断；S32、S35）
- **决策方式**：护送孩子时，她倾向随追击变化调整路线并持续撤离（行为推断；S40、S41）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 材料限制

- 感染后的唐被描述为病毒驱动的攻击者，其暴力不能直接归为感染前的人格。
- 摘要强调逃亡行动，未提供足够对话分析三人的日常沟通方式。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：During the original outbreak of the Rage Virus, Don, his wife Alice and four other survivors are hiding in a barricaded cottage on the outskirts of London with food and water left for only a few days.
- **S2**：They hear a terrified boy, who is running away from the infected, pounding at their door.
- **S3**：They let him in and give him some food to eat.
- **S4**：He explains that his own parents tried to kill him.
- **S5**：Another survivor named Karen checks outside through a peephole, but one of the infected attacks her and bites her on the arm.
- **S6**：Survivor Jacob tries to save her, but Karen attacks him as well.
- **S7**：Alice takes the boy upstairs, Jacob and an elderly couple hide in the barn, and Don defends them and kills a few of the infected with a crowbar.
- **S8**：In the barn, the couple is attacked, and Jacob tries to save a woman named Sally, to no avail.
- **S9**：He locks himself in the attic and finds his way outside.
- **S10**：Alice and the boy lock themselves in the room, but the infected find and attack them.
- **S11**：Don finds them, but escapes through the window, leaving them to be attacked by the infected.
- **S12**：He runs through fields, as a horde of infected pursue him.
- **S13**：He finds Jacob near a motorboat; Don starts it, but Jacob falls down and is infected as Don escapes.
- **S14**：After five weeks, all the infected have died of starvation.
- **S15**：After eleven weeks, NATO forces headed by the US take control of Great Britain.
- **S16**：After eighteen weeks the island is declared relatively safe, although still under quarantine.
- **S17**：Twenty-eight weeks after the outbreak, an American-led force, under the command of Brigadier General Stone, bring in settlers to re-populate the area.
- **S18**：Among the new arrivals are Tammy and Andy, Don and Alice's children, who were in Spain on a school trip during the initial outbreak.
- **S19**：They are subsequently admitted to District One, a safe zone guarded by the US Army, on the Isle of Dogs.
- **S20**：As they are examined by Major Scarlet Levy, the district's chief medical officer, she notes Andy's differently coloured eyes, a trait inherited from his mother.
- **S21**：Sergeant Doyle, a Delta sniper and his friend, helicopter pilot Chief Flynn, are amongst the military presence charged with guarding the district.
- **S22**：The children are reunited with their father, who had survived the original infection and was found by the US army, and who has become the district's senior maintenance engineer.
- **S23**：In their new flat, Don explains what happened and that after he escaped, he arrived in a military camp and waited with the soldiers through the weeks.
- **S24**：The next day, Tammy and Andy, mournful over the loss of their mother, leave the safe zone and proceed to the depopulated London wasteland area to their former house on a scooter, hoping to retrieve and take their old belongings with them.
- **S25**：To their shock, they find their mother in a semi-conscious state.
- **S26**：Doyle had seen the two children leave the safe zone; they and their mother are subsequently picked up by soldiers and returned to the district.
- **S27**：Tammy and Andy are taken back to their living quarters while Alice is taken to a biohazard room where she is tested and found to be an asymptomatic carrier of the Rage Virus, with the virus inside her but with no symptoms.
- **S28**：Later that day, Don sneaks through the security and makes an unauthorized visit to Alice in her isolation cell and asks forgiveness for abandoning her at the cottage.
- **S29**：When they kiss, however, the Rage Virus in her saliva immediately infects Don, who savagely kills her before going on a rampage, killing and infecting several soldiers in the building.
- **S30**：General Stone orders the building to be quarantined and District One to be put into Code Red Lock-down, and civilians are herded into safe rooms.
- **S31**：Despite the precautions, Don breaks into a room containing a large crowd and begins killing and infecting them, quickly causing a domino effect of attackers.
- **S32**：Scarlet rescues Tammy and Andy from containment as the soldiers in District One are ordered to shoot indiscriminately.
- **S33**：Doyle, unable to bring himself to comply with the order, abandons his post and escapes with Scarlet, the children and others through the Greenwich foot tunnel.
- **S34**：General Stone then orders that District One be firebombed, but large numbers of the infected, including Don, escape the bombardment.
- **S35**：Scarlet informs Doyle that the children may hold the key to a cure and must be protected at all costs.
- **S36**：Flynn arrives by helicopter to pick up Doyle, but refuses to take anyone else as they would be shot down if carrying possibly infected people.
- **S37**：Flynn contacts Doyle by radio and tells him to head to Wembley Stadium, but to leave the civilians.
- **S38**：Doyle ignores his instructions and begins escorting the civilians to Wembley, breaking into an abandoned car to escape nerve gas released to kill the infected.
- **S39**：He is burned alive by soldiers as he tries to push start the car.
- **S40**：Scarlet drives the car away, but a military chopper tries to blow them up; she manages to escape the chopper with the kids.
- **S41**：She drives them into the London Underground where, as the trio continue on foot, she is ambushed and killed by Don who then attacks and bites Andy.
- **S42**：Tammy shoots Don before he can kill Andy who remains symptom-free, but with darker, bloodier eyes, like his mother.
- **S43**：The children continue to the Stadium and are picked up by a reluctant Flynn, who flies them across the English Channel to France, as previously instructed by Doyle.
- **S44**：28 days after the events, a French-accented voice requesting help is heard from the radio in Flynn's abandoned helicopter.
- **S45**：A group of infected are seen running through a tunnel which, as they emerge into the open, is revealed to be the exit of the Paris Métro Trocadéro station with a view of the nearby Eiffel Tower, meaning that the infection spread around Europe.

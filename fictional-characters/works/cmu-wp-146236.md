- **性格倾向**：经历恐怖遭遇后仍倾向坚持追索，不因公开警告而止步（行为推断；S13、S21、S22）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In the Sonoran Desert, French scientist Claude Lacombe and his American interpreter, mapmaker David Laughlin, along with other government scientific researchers, discover Flight 19, a squadron of Grumman TBF Avengers that went missing more than 30 years earlier.
- **S2**：The planes are intact and operational, but there is no sign of the pilots.
- **S3**：An old man who witnessed the event claimed "the sun came out at night, and sang to him." They also find a lost ship in the Gobi Desert named SS Cotopaxi.
- **S4**：At an Air Traffic Control center in Indianapolis, Indiana, a controller listens as two airline flights almost have a mid-air collision with an apparent unidentified flying object .
- **S5**：In Muncie, Indiana, 3-year-old Barry Guiler is awakened in the night when his toys start operating on their own.
- **S6**：Fascinated, he gets out of bed and discovers something or someone  in the kitchen.
- **S7**：He runs outside, forcing his mother, Jillian, to chase after him.
- **S8**：Investigating one of a series of large-scale power outages, Indiana electrical lineman Roy Neary experiences a close encounter with a UFO, when it flies over his truck and lightly burns the side of his face with its bright lights.
- **S9**：The UFO, along with three others, are pursued by Neary and three police cars, but the spacecrafts fly off into the night sky.
- **S10**：Roy becomes fascinated by UFOs, much to the dismay of his wife, Ronnie.
- **S11**：He also becomes increasingly obsessed with subliminal, mental images of a mountain-like shape and begins to make models of it.
- **S12**：Jillian also becomes obsessed with sketching a unique-looking mountain.
- **S13**：Soon after, she is terrorized in her home by a UFO encounter in which Barry is abducted by unseen beings.
- **S14**：Lacombe and Laughlin—along with a group of United Nations experts—continue to investigate increasing UFO activity investigate and strange, related occurrences.
- **S15**：Witnesses report that the UFOs make distinctive sounds: a five-tone musical phrase in a major scale.
- **S16**：Scientists broadcast the phrase to outer space, but are mystified by the response: a seemingly meaningless series of numbers repeated over and over, until Laughlin recognizes it as a set of geographical coordinates pointing to Devils Tower near Moorcroft, Wyoming.
- **S17**：Lacombe and the U.S. military converge on Wyoming.
- **S18**：The United States Army evacuates the area, planting false reports in the media that a train wreck has spilled a toxic nerve gas, all the while preparing a secret landing zone for the UFOs and their occupants.
- **S19**：Meanwhile, Roy's increasingly erratic behavior causes Ronnie to leave him, taking their three children with her.
- **S20**：When a despairing Roy inadvertently sees a television news program about the train wreck near Devils Tower, he realizes the mental image of a mountain plaguing him is real.
- **S21**：Jillian sees the same broadcast, and she and Roy, as well as others with similar visions and experiences, travel to the site in spite of the false public warnings about nerve gas.
- **S22**：While most of the civilians who are drawn to the site are apprehended by the Army, Roy and Jillian persist and make it to the site just as dozens of UFOs appear in the night sky.
- **S23**：The government specialists at the site begin to communicate with the UFOs by use of light and sound on a large electrical billboard.
- **S24**：Following this, an enormous mother ship lands at the site, returning people who had been abducted over the past decades, including Barry, and the missing pilots from Flight 19 and sailors from the Cotopaxi, who have not aged since their abductions.
- **S25**：The government officials decide to include Roy in a group of people whom they have selected to be potential visitors to the mothership, and hastily prepare him.
- **S26**：As the aliens finally emerge from the mothership, they select Roy to join them on their travels.
- **S27**：As Roy enters the mothership, one of the aliens pauses for a few moments with the humans.
- **S28**：Lacombe uses Curwen hand signs that correspond to the five note alien tonal phrase.
- **S29**：The alien replies with the same gestures, smiles, and returns to its ship, which lifts off into the night sky.

## 角色资料

### Harry Potter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6731c01eb468c5e3` · 独立版本：`profile-8812d7a2ba092396`

- **性格倾向**：在怀疑魔法石将遭窃取时显出敢于冒险的一面，继续独自前进。（行为推断；S22、S25、S26）
- **核心动机**：相信有人将借魔法石恢复伏地魔力量，因此争取提前阻止盗取。（行为推断；S20、S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：在缺少校长支援时倾向自行行动，但行动依据包含对斯内普的误判。（行为推断；S17、S21、S22、S27）
- **价值取舍**：面对复活父母的承诺，仍把不交出魔法石置于接受诱惑之前。（行为推断；S34）
- **成长变化**：从与敌视自己的亲戚同住，转为将霍格沃茨认作真正的家。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S42）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：阻止盗石的行动积极，但对斯内普的归罪被后续揭示推翻。

### Hermione Granger

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-8dad407422aa0a6e` · 独立版本：`profile-51d5ebab4293aabf`

- **性格倾向**：在探查与闯关中表现出依靠知识解决问题的倾向。（行为推断；S16、S25）
- **核心动机**：与同伴判断魔法石当夜有失窃危险，因此共同尝试抢先找到它。（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：面对危险时倾向调动已有知识，结合线索推理并运用咒语解困。（行为推断；S16、S25）
- **关系模式**：罗恩在棋局中险些丧命后留下陪着他，由哈利独自继续前进。（摘要直接叙述；S26）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Quirinus Quirrell

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0543e308d551ed92` · 独立版本：`profile-a66d20da075603be`

- **性格倾向**：在夺石行动中表现出隐蔽而狠辣的一面，暗中制造致命危险。（行为推断；S27、S28）
- **核心动机**：试图取得魔法石，在哈利接触镜子后继续逼问并动手抢夺。（摘要直接叙述；S27、S30、S32、S36）
- **决策方式**：遭遇哈利逃离时倾向以强制手段封堵，继而使用致命攻击。（行为推断；S33、S35）
- **关系模式**：身上寄居着伏地魔，并与一直保护哈利的斯内普形成对抗。（摘要直接叙述；S29、S32）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Further2}} Harry Potter is a seemingly ordinary boy, living with his hostile relatives, the Dursleys in Surrey.
- **S2**：On his 11th birthday, Harry learns from a mysterious stranger, Rubeus Hagrid, that he is actually a wizard, famous in the Wizarding World for surviving an attack by the evil Lord Voldemort when Harry was only a baby.
- **S3**：Voldemort killed Harry's parents, but his attack on Harry rebounded, leaving only a lightning-bolt scar on Harry's forehead and rendering Voldemort powerless.
- **S4**：Hagrid reveals to Harry that he has been invited to attend Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.
- **S5**：After buying his school supplies from the hidden wizarding street, Diagon Alley, Harry boards the train to Hogwarts via the concealed Platform {{frac}} in King's Cross Station.
- **S6**：On the train, Harry meets Ron Weasley, a boy from a large, but poor, pure-blood wizarding family, and Hermione Granger, a witch born to Muggle  parents.
- **S7**：Once they arrive at the school, Harry and all of the other first-year students are sorted into four different houses: Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, and Slytherin.
- **S8**：As Slytherin is noted for being the house of darker wizards and witches, Harry successfully begs the magical Sorting Hat not to put him in Slytherin.
- **S9**：He winds up in Gryffindor, along with Ron and Hermione.
- **S10**：At Hogwarts, Harry begins learning wizardry and also discovers more about his past and his parents.
- **S11**：Harry inadvertently makes Gryffindor's Quidditch team  as a Seeker, learning that his father was also a member of the team.
- **S12**：One night, he, Ron, and Hermione find a giant three-headed dog on the Forbidden Corridor on the Third Floor at the school.
- **S13**：The trio successfully take down an escaped mountain troll and after Harry is nearly knocked off his broom by a powerful curse during one of the Quidditch matches, the trio assume that someone is trying to get past the dog.
- **S14**：Harry discovers the Mirror of Erised which shows a person's heart's desire.
- **S15**：The headmaster Albus Dumbledore moves the mirror and advises Harry against looking for it.
- **S16**：Using information accidentally let slip by Hagrid, Hermione discovers that the dog is guarding the Philosopher's Stone, an item that can be used to grant its owner immortality.
- **S17**：Harry concludes that his potions teacher, Severus Snape, is trying to obtain the stone.
- **S18**：Harry is caught out of bed at night and is given detention.
- **S19**：While helping Hagrid in the dark forest Harry sees a hooded figure drinking a unicorn's blood for its healing properties.
- **S20**：Harry concludes that the hooded figure was Voldemort and that Snape is trying to get the stone to restore Voldemort to full strength.
- **S21**：After hearing from Hagrid that the dog will fall asleep if played music and that he let slip this to a man in the local pub one night, Harry, Ron, and Hermione decide that Snape was the man in the pub and attempt to warn Dumbledore.
- **S22**：Upon learning he is away on business the trio conclude that Snape will attempt to steal the stone that night and resolve to find the stone before Snape does.
- **S23**：They face a series of obstacles: surviving a deadly plant, flying past hundreds of flying keys and winning a violent, life-sized chess match.
- **S24**：The trio use their skills to overcome the obstacles.
- **S25**：Hermione uses her knowledge of spells to get past the plant, Harry uses his skills as a seeker to get past the keys and Ron uses his skill at chess to win the chess match.
- **S26**：However, Ron is nearly killed in the match and Hermione stays with Ron as Harry goes on ahead, alone.
- **S27**：In the final room, Harry finds out that it was not Snape who wanted the stone, but Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher Professor Quirrell.
- **S28**：Quirrell reveals that he let the troll in and tried to kill Harry in the Quidditch match.
- **S29**：He also reveals that Snape has been protecting Harry and trying to stop Quirrell all year.
- **S30**：Quirrell forces Harry to look in the mirror.
- **S31**：Due to an enchantment placed by Dumbledore, Harry finds the stone in his pocket after looking in the nearby Mirror of Erised.
- **S32**：While trying to get Harry to answer what he has seen in the mirror, Quirrell removes his turban and reveals Voldemort to be living on the back of his head.
- **S33**：Harry tries to escape but Quirrell starts a fire by clicking his fingers to trap him.
- **S34**：Voldemort tries to convince Harry to give him the stone by promising to bring his parents back from the dead, but Harry refuses.
- **S35**：Quirrell tries to kill him but Harry's touch prevents Quirrell from hurting Harry and causes his hand to turn to dust.
- **S36**：Quirrell then tries to take the stone but Harry grabs his face, causing Quirrell to turn into dust and die.
- **S37**：When Harry gets up, Voldemort's spirit forms and passes through Harry, knocking him unconscious, before fleeing.
- **S38**：Harry wakes up in the school's hospital wing, with Professor Dumbledore at his side.
- **S39**：Dumbledore explains that the stone has been destroyed, and that both Hermione and Ron are fine.
- **S40**：Quirrell burned at Harry's touch because, when Harry's mother died to save him, her death gave Harry a magical, love-based protection against Voldemort.
- **S41**：At the end of year feast, Dumbledore gives last minute house points to Harry, Ron, Hermione and Neville for their bravery and smarts so Gryffindor win the house cup.
- **S42**：Before Harry and the rest of the students leave for the summer, Harry realises that while every other student is going home, Hogwarts is truly his home.

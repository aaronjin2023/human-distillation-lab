### Luke Skywalker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-df032d204dfa317b` · 独立版本：`profile-af1f9cebba776fd0`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中冲动而重情，因预感朋友受苦便中断训练赶去救援。（行为推断；S19）
- **核心动机**：他接受Yoda的密集训练，又因预感Han与Leia受苦而离开救援。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S17、S19）
- **决策方式**：面对Vader提出的统治邀请，他拒绝并选择坠入深井。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32）
- **关系模式**：他响应朋友受苦的预感，并在危急时向Leia发出求救。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S34）
- **价值取舍**：得知Vader是父亲后，他仍拒绝与之联手统治银河。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他不顾导师反对而中断训练救友，同时承诺回来完成训练。

### Han Solo

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-cd733043cfc0f65e` · 独立版本：`profile-75c7e498e1b98331`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴失踪的情境中表现出重情与应变能力，搜救时利用坐骑保暖。（行为推断；S5、S7）
- **核心动机**：他与Leia、同伴逃离帝国追捕，并试图前往Cloud City脱身。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S14、S20）
- **决策方式**：为躲避追踪，他让Falcon随垃圾漂离，再设法前往Bespin。（摘要直接叙述；S20）
- **关系模式**：他与Leia在逃亡中逐渐亲近并互相表白，随后被冷冻。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S25）
- **价值取舍**：他不顾严寒外出寻找Luke，愿意冒险救助同伴。（行为推断；S5、S7）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他与Leia确认相爱后仍被冷冻，无法与她一同逃离。

### Darth Vader

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-67cfa06baea2ed1c` · 独立版本：`profile-e5f0f883a6ee4edf`

- **性格倾向**：在追捕情境中冷酷且重视控制，把俘虏作为引诱Luke的工具。（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **核心动机**：他想擒获Luke，并提出让儿子与自己共同统治银河。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S23、S32）
- **决策方式**：计划冷冻Luke之前，他先选Han测试能否以冷冻方式保存人。（摘要直接叙述；S24）
- **关系模式**：他向Luke揭示自己是其父亲，并邀请他结盟。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32）
- **价值取舍**：他把Han用于冷冻试验并违背交易，显出此时对俘虏与承诺的轻视。（行为推断；S24、S26）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他与Lando达成交易，却违约把Han交给Boba Fett。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film begins with an opening crawl explaining that three years after destroying the Death Star, the Rebel Alliance has suffered setbacks in their struggle against the Galactic Empire.
- **S2**：Princess Leia now leads a contingent that includes Han Solo and Luke Skywalker in a hidden base on an icy planet of the Hoth system.
- **S3**：A probe droid, one of many sent by Darth Vader throughout the galaxy in hopes of finding Luke and the other rebels, lands on Hoth.
- **S4**：Luke goes to investigate but is ambushed by a monstrous, furry wampa.
- **S5**：While Han Solo searches for him, Luke frees himself from the wampa's cave with his lightsaber but soon succumbs to the freezing temperatures of the snowy wasteland.
- **S6**：The spirit of his late mentor, Obi-Wan Kenobi, appears before him and instructs him to go to the planet Dagobah to train under Jedi Master Yoda .
- **S7**：Han manages to find Luke and uses the warmth of his dead tauntaun mount to keep him alive while they wait to be rescued.
- **S8**：Just as Luke recovers, the Imperial fleet, having been alerted to the location of the Rebel base by the probe droid, launches an attack using gigantic AT-AT Walkers.
- **S9**：The Rebels mount a strong defense, and Luke brings down a walker single-handedly after his fighter is disabled, but the base is nonetheless captured.
- **S10**：Han and Leia escape on the Millennium Falcon with C-3PO and Chewbacca, but their hyperspace drive malfunctions, and they must hide in an asteroid field.
- **S11**：Luke escapes with R2-D2 in an X-wing fighter and crash lands on Dagobah.
- **S12**：He is soon found by the diminutive Yoda, who at first pretends to be a simple swamp inhabitant in order to test Luke's patience.
- **S13**：After conferring with Obi-Wan's spirit, Yoda accepts Luke as his pupil.
- **S14**：Han and Leia end their bickering and grow closer, but their courtship is interrupted when they must flee a giant asteroid worm.
- **S15**：They avoid capture again by attaching the Millennium Falcon directly to the side of a Star Destroyer in Vader's fleet.
- **S16**：Frustrated at having lost them, Vader turns to several notorious bounty hunters, including Boba Fett .
- **S17**：Meanwhile, Luke begins a brief period of intensive training, during which his power in the Force grows exponentially.
- **S18**：He suffers a setback when he fails a test and sees the vision of his own face inside Darth Vader's helmet.
- **S19**：Then he becomes troubled by premonitions of Han and Leia in pain and despite Yoda and Obi-Wan's disapprovals, leaves to save them, promising to return to complete his training.
- **S20**：Having escaped detection, Han lets his ship float away with the star destroyer's garbage and sets a course for Cloud City, a floating gas mining colony in the skies of the planet Bespin.
- **S21**：Boba Fett secretly follows the Millennium Falcon to the planet Bespin and arrives just before Han and Leia.
- **S22**：Bespin is run by Han's old friend Lando Calrissian , but shortly after they arrive he turns them over to Vader to prevent the takeover of his city.
- **S23**：Over Lando's objections, Vader uses them as bait to bring Luke into his trap.
- **S24**：Vader intends to hold Luke in suspended animation and selects Han as a test subject for the process.
- **S25**：Han and Leia profess their love for each other, after which Han is frozen in a block of carbonite.
- **S26**：Reneging on his deal with Lando, Vader gives Han's hibernating form to Boba Fett, who plans to present this "prize" to Jabba the Hutt who put a price on Han's head for not paying a debt that he previously owed to Jabba.
- **S27**：Lando frees Leia, Chewbacca, and C-3PO, Chewbacca chokes Lando for betraying Han and Leia tells him that they do not need their help, but they are too late to stop Fett from escaping with Han, forcing them to flee in the Millennium Falcon without him.
- **S28**：Lando then orders an evacuation of the now taken over city.
- **S29**：Meanwhile, Luke has arrived at Cloud City, as Vader planned.
- **S30**：Luke and Vader engage in a lightsaber duel that leads them over the central air shaft of Cloud City.
- **S31**：Vader severs Luke's right hand, disarming him, and reveals that he did not kill Luke's father, but actually is Luke's father.
- **S32**：Horrified by this fact, Luke refuses Vader's offer to rule the galaxy at his side, choosing instead to throw himself down the air shaft.
- **S33**：He slides through a tube system and is ejected but catches onto an antenna under the floating city.
- **S34**：He makes a desperate telepathic plea to Leia, who senses it and persuades Lando to return for him.
- **S35**：When its hyperdrive is finally repaired by R2-D2, the Millennium Falcon escapes.
- **S36**：Aboard a Rebel medical frigate, Luke is fitted with an robotic artificial hand.
- **S37**：As Luke, Leia, R2-D2, and C-3PO look on from the medical center, Lando and Chewbacca set off in the Millennium Falcon to rescue Han from Boba Fett and Jabba the Hutt.

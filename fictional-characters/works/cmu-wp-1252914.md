### Indiana Jones

### Irina Spalko

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-d69a23e2841ad90b` · 独立版本：`profile-c511c7b2c8e6ea48`

- **性格倾向**：在接触未知力量时表现出强烈求取倾向，甚至要求获知一切。（行为推断；S17、S28）
- **核心动机**：相信归还水晶头骨能使苏联获得优势，因此追求其心灵力量。（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17）
- **沟通方式**：面对外星存在许诺的回报，她直接提出要知道一切的要求。（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **决策方式**：进入神庙后将头骨装回骨骼，再向外星存在索取全部知识。（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27、S28）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

### Henry "Mutt" Williams

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1957, World War II veteran Indiana Jones and his partner George "Mac" McHale are kidnapped by a group of Soviet agents led by Colonel Dr.
- **S2**：Irina Spalko.
- **S3**：The Soviets infiltrate a warehouse labeled "Warehouse 51" in Nevada and force Jones to find a crate containing the remains of an extraterrestrial that crashed ten years earlier near Roswell, New Mexico.
- **S4**：After finding the crate, with its highly magnetic contents, Mac, bribed by the Soviets, double-crosses Jones.
- **S5**：After a running battle with the Soviets, Indiana manages to escape on a rocket sled into the desert; he stumbles into a deserted town, part of a nuclear test site and survives a nuclear detonation by hiding in a lead-lined refrigerator.
- **S6**：He is later debriefed by the Federal Bureau of Investigation, who believe he's also working for the Soviets.
- **S7**：Shortly after returning to Marshall College, Jones is offered an indefinite leave of absence to avoid being fired because of the incident.
- **S8**：At a train station, Jones is stopped by greaser Henry "Mutt" Williams, who tells him that his old colleague Harold Oxley was kidnapped after discovering a crystal skull in Peru.
- **S9**：Jones tells Mutt about the legend of crystal skulls found in the mystical city of Akator and whoever returns the skull to the city would be given control over its supernatural powers.
- **S10**：Mutt gives Jones a letter from his mother, who is also being held captive, containing a riddle written by Oxley in an ancient Native American language.
- **S11**：This leads them to the Nazca Lines in Peru.
- **S12**：There they discover that Oxley was in a psychiatric hospital, after suffering a mental breakdown from handling the skull, and was kidnapped from his cell by the Soviets.
- **S13**：In Oxley's cell, they find clues that lead them to the grave of Francisco de Orellana, a Conquistador who went missing in the 16th century while searching for Akator.
- **S14**：They discover the skull at the grave, with Jones reasoning that Oxley had hidden it there.
- **S15**：Shortly afterward, Jones and Mutt are captured by the Soviets and taken to their camp, where they find Oxley and Mutt's mother, who turns out to be Jones' old love Marion Ravenwood, who reveals that Mutt is Jones' son, Henry Jones III.
- **S16**：Spalko believes that the crystal skull belongs to an interdimensional being and holds great psychic power, and proves her theory by showing that the corpse stolen from the warehouse has an identically shaped skull.
- **S17**：She also believes that returning the skull to Akator will grant the Soviets the advantage of psychic warfare.
- **S18**：Jones, Marion, Mutt and Oxley escape from the Soviets into the Amazon; however, they are recaptured after Jones and Marion fall into quicksand and Oxley fetches the Soviets to "help".
- **S19**：While being taken through the rainforest to Akator the next day, Marion and Indy argue so much that their guard Dovchenko gags Marion.
- **S20**：However, Jones and Mutt manage to overpower him, after Jones frees himself with Mutt's hidden knife.
- **S21**：He ungags Marion, then Mutt frees her while Jones takes over the truck, leading to a jungle chase resulting in many losses, then stopping in an area full of giant ants that kill a soldier and Dovchenko.
- **S22**：The group escapes and makes it to the city, but only after evading the Soviets and negotiating three waterfalls in a duck and narrowly avoiding being killed by a tribe guarding the long-abandoned city.
- **S23**：Mac, who again switched allegiances and claims to be a double agent, has been secretly leaving a trail for the Soviets to follow.
- **S24**：Inside the central temple, they find artifacts from several ancient civilizations.
- **S25**：Jones deduces that the creatures were kindred spirits; they too were "archaeologists" studying the different cultures of Earth.
- **S26**：The five enter a chamber containing the crystal skeletons of thirteen alien beings seated in a circle, with one missing its skull.
- **S27**：After the Soviets arrive, Spalko replaces the skull.
- **S28**：The aliens communicate to the group through Oxley using an ancient dialect and promises to reward them with a "big gift"; Spalko demands to "know everything".
- **S29**：The skeletons grant her request and transfer their collective knowledge into her mind.
- **S30**：At the same time they activate a portal to another dimension.
- **S31**：Jones, Marion, Mutt and the now restored Oxley escape the temple, but Mac and the other Soviets are sucked into the portal.
- **S32**：Meanwhile, the skeletons combine to form a single living being and the knowledge transfer overwhelms Spalko, causing her to disintegrate; her dust is then drawn into the portal.
- **S33**：The survivors watch as the temple walls crumble, revealing a massive flying saucer slowly rising from the debris; it hovers for a second before disappearing into the "space between spaces".
- **S34**：Sometime later, Jones is reinstated at Marshall College and made an associate dean.
- **S35**：He and Marion are then married in a church.

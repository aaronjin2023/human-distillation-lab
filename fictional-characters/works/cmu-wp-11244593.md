### David Seville

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d9f937b94052dfd0` · 独立版本：`profile-53633ade126ef374`

- **性格倾向**：在相处受挫后仍倾向承担照顾责任，逐渐显露保护欲（行为推断；S9、S12、S18、S27）
- **核心动机**：面对巡演造成的伤害，希望把花栗鼠带回安全的家（行为推断；S15、S18、S21、S25）
- **价值取舍**：将花栗鼠视为需要照护的孩子，重视健康胜过走红（行为推断；S12）
- **成长变化**：从以食宿交换演唱的合作者，转为明确接纳他们的家人（摘要直接叙述；S8、S27、S29）

矛盾与变化：最初以演唱交换照料，后来反对商业活动侵蚀孩子生活。

### Ian Hawke

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-50f249565a8bde7b` · 独立版本：`profile-091e88db4ad75ea7`

- **性格倾向**：在经营明星时倾向控制和利用他人，忽视其疲惫意愿（行为推断；S14、S16、S24、S25）
- **核心动机**：面对演出危机仍要维持商业运作，不愿承担退款损失（行为推断；S19、S20、S24）
- **价值取舍**：在健康与收益冲突时，选择让耗竭的歌手继续假唱（行为推断；S19、S20）

- **S2**：Once in L.A., the Chipmunks meet struggling songwriter David Seville ([[Jason Lee  who had his latest song rejected by JETT Records executive Ian Hawke , his old college roommate.

- **S8**：Dave then makes a deal with them; they sing the songs he writes, and in exchange he provides food and shelter for them.
- **S9**：However, all does not go well, as Dave's job presentation is ruined by their coloring on it, and when Alvin tries to set the mood for his dinner with Claire, things become weird and she rejects him after he tells her, "My life is being sabotaged by talking chipmunks." - **S10**：Once the Chipmunks sing Dave's song to Ian, Ian signs them to the label and rehires Dave the next day.
- **S11**：After a few singles, the Chipmunks become wildly popular.
- **S12**：When Dave expresses concern for their well-being and insists that the Chipmunks are "kids" who don't need so much craziness in their lives, Ian convinces the Chipmunks that Dave is holding them back.
- **S13**：After a misunderstanding with Dave, they go live with "Uncle Ian".
- **S14**：They become enchanted with Ian at first, but once they set off on a coast-to-coast tour, Ian takes advantage of their naivete, changing their image and working them constantly.
- **S15**：Meanwhile, Dave misses the chipmunks and he wishes they would come back home.
- **S16**：He calls Ian to see if he can talk to them but Ian refuses, and then conceals Dave's motives from the boys.
- **S17**：Later, the Chipmunks are wearing out, and it is all over the news.
- **S18**：Dave, infuriated by what Ian has done to the three, decides to take matters into his own hands by infiltrating their concert.

- **S23**：Ian catches them just when Dave is about to rescue them.

### John McClane

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-c65bcc87a151cbc5` · 独立版本：`profile-0be98ecda94c9e6c`

- **性格倾向**：在连环威胁与追捕受挫时倾向顽强追查，不轻易放弃（行为推断；S16、S17、S21、S24）
- **核心动机**：行动起初围绕阻止爆炸展开，识破骗局后转向追查劫案（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9、S13、S15）
- **决策方式**：倾向从偶然线索修正判断，而非始终接受对手设定的局面（行为推断；S13、S22、S23）
- **关系模式**：与卡弗在共同涉险中形成合作，并尝试修复疏远的婚姻（摘要直接叙述；S17、S21、S23、S26）
- **价值取舍**：面对公共危险时倾向亲自承担风险，优先阻止他人受害（行为推断；S8、S9）

### Simon "Peter" Gruber

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-77c9a36f5f598462` · 独立版本：`profile-03b3796e5e5e53a7`

- **性格倾向**：在与警方周旋时倾向操控与欺瞒，利用恐惧掌握主动（行为推断；S7、S11、S19）
- **核心动机**：以爆炸威胁牵制警方，实际目标是夺取并运走银行黄金（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14、S24）
- **决策方式**：倾向让多层佯动相互掩护，把警方应急反应转为作案条件（行为推断；S11、S12、S14、S19、S22）
- **价值取舍**：在劫案中将公众与追捕者的生命置于自身收益之后（行为推断；S1、S2、S9、S21）
- **沟通方式**：通过电话发布限时要求与谜题，以信息控制迫使对手行动（摘要直接叙述；S2、S7、S8、S11、S12）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：凭严密误导争取脱身，却因留下的药瓶线索暴露落脚处。

- **S2**：Later, a man calling himself "Simon" phones Major Case Unit Inspector Walter Cobb of the New York City Police Department, claiming responsibility for the bomb.

- **S4**：John McClane  be dropped in Harlem, wearing a sandwich board that says "I hate Niggers".
- **S5**：Harlem shop owner Zeus Carver  spots McClane and tries to get him off the street before he is killed, but a gang of black youths attack the pair, who barely escape.
- **S6**：Returning to the station, they learn that Simon is believed to have stolen several thousand gallons of a bio-chemical agent explosive.
- **S7**：Simon calls again demanding McClane and Carver put themselves through a series of "games" to prevent more explosions.
- **S8**：McClane and Carver are instructed by Simon to travel to Wall Street station 90 blocks south, within 30 minutes to stop a bomb planted on a Brooklyn-bound 3 train.
- **S9**：McClane succeeds in locating and throwing the bomb off the train but it detonates, causing the rear car on the train to derail, demolishing many of the station's support columns.
- **S10**：Federal Bureau of Investigation agents tell McClane that Simon is Simon "Peter" Gruber , brother of Hans Gruber, whom McClane killed in the first film.
- **S11**：During the debriefing, Simon calls again claiming that another bomb is planted in one of the city schools, and is sensitive to police radio signals.
- **S12**：As McClane and Carver are forced to complete more riddles to identify the school, the police organize a citywide search of schools, and shut down the police radio band.
- **S13**：After overhearing a chance remark by a passerby, McClane realizes that he and Carver are being distracted to keep them away from Wall Street; Simon is actually planning a heist.
- **S14**：Returning downtown, he finds Simon's men, disguised as cops, businessmen, construction workers, and guards, have raided the Federal Reserve Bank of New York via the destruction left by the subway explosion, and made off with $140 billion of gold bullion in 14 stolen dump trucks.
- **S15**：After killing Simon's henchmen at the bank, McClane trails the dump trucks to an aqueduct in the New York City Water Tunnel No.
- **S16**：3, capturing one of the trucks, but Simon destroys a cofferdam flooding the tunnel; McClane is thrown clear and regroups with Carver.
- **S17**：They continue to follow the dump trucks to a tanker, but are captured attempting to board.
- **S18**：The police locate and attempt to evacuate the school they think the bomb is in .
- **S19**：As timer reaches zero, the police discover that the bomb is only a decoy.
- **S20**：Meanwhile, McClane and Carver find that Simon has instead used the remaining explosive to rig the tanker to explode, which would send the gold to the bottom of the sea.
- **S21**：Simon leaves the tanker, leaving McClane and Carver tied up next to the bomb, but they manage to escape the doomed ship just before it detonates.
- **S22**：As they regroup with the police and have their wounds tended to, McClane theorizes that there was no gold on the ship, which ultimately proves correct as it was replaced with scrap metal, and Simon has likely escaped.
- **S23**：While phoning to make amends with his estranged wife Holly, McClane realises an aspirin bottle given to him earlier by Simon gives an address in a bordertown of Quebec.
- **S24**：McClane alongside the Royal Canadian Mounted Police raid the warehouse where Simon and his men are preparing to distribute the gold.
- **S25**：Simon escapes in a helicopter, attempting to shoot McClane from the air, but McClane shoots a power line which hits the helicopter and destroys it.
- **S26**：Carver joins McClane and convinces him to finish his call to his estranged wife Holly at a nearby pay phone.

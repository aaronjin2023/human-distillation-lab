## 角色资料

### Richard 'Rick' O'Connell

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-36b30e5e8ab6e007` · 独立版本：`profile-75209b5bfa5bd993`

- **性格倾向**：在家人遭遇危险时倾向主动迎险，以直接行动回应威胁（行为推断；S12、S20、S21、S24）
- **核心动机**：追踪邪教并深入险地的首要目标，是救回被绑架的儿子（摘要直接叙述；S10、S12、S21）
- **情绪反应**：妻子遭杀害后深感悲痛，并在悲痛中追赶Imhotep（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28）
- **决策方式**：面对儿子的死亡时限，倾向立即奔赴金字塔争取生机（行为推断；S9、S24、S25）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Alex O'Connell

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4bba8f1c653896a4` · 独立版本：`profile-bf9c9401a9aafe40`

- **性格倾向**：被挟持时仍倾向机敏应变，利用沿途线索争取获救机会（行为推断；S14、S15、S17）
- **核心动机**：在受制于邪教期间持续留下线索，以帮助父母找到自己（行为推断；S10、S15）
- **决策方式**：面对母亲死亡，倾向与亲人协作夺取能够改变局面的工具（行为推断；S27、S36、S37）
- **关系模式**：与Jonathan形成互助搭档，共同救母并继续支援父亲（摘要直接叙述；S36、S37、S38）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### High Priest Imhotep

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-888fa98f5042abed` · 独立版本：`profile-843ef7d786b3fac1`

- **性格倾向**：失去神力后倾向权宜示弱，借误导敌人转移自身危险（行为推断；S30、S33、S34）
- **核心动机**：通过亡灵之书使爱人的灵魂归位，追求重建昔日爱情（行为推断；S11、S16）
- **决策方式**：在Alex留线索的举动被发现后，倾向以强力袭击阻断营救者追赶（行为推断；S15、S17）
- **情绪反应**：危急时遭爱人拒绝施救而心碎，最终放弃攀援坠入冥界（摘要直接叙述；S43、S44）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：为恢复爱人的灵魂付诸行动，却在生死关头遭她弃置。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 3067 BC, the Scorpion King leads his army on a campaign to conquer the world.
- **S2**：Seven years later, the Scorpion King and his army are defeated and exiled to the desert of Ahm Shere.
- **S3**：His men die of heat exhaustion, leaving only the Scorpion King.
- **S4**：Vowing to give Anubis his soul for the power to defeat his enemies, an oasis is created to hide the Scorpion King's pyramid and giving him a legion of demonic, humanoid jackal warriors to seek revenge.
- **S5**：The Army of Anubis sweeps across Egypt, destroying everything in its path, but once their task is finished Anubis claims the Scorpion King's soul and his army.
- **S6**：In 1933, Rick and Evelyn O'Connell explore a ruined mortuary structure in the ancient Egyptian city of Thebes with their son, Alex.
- **S7**：They find the Bracelet of Anubis.
- **S8**：In London, Alex puts on the bracelet, which provides him with directions to the oasis of Ahm Shere.
- **S9**：Alex has seven days to reach the oasis, or the bracelet will kill him the moment the rays of the sun shine on the Scorpion King's pyramid; at that point the Scorpion King and his army will also reawaken.
- **S10**：Alex is captured by an Egyptian cult who have resurrected Imhotep; they wish to use Imhotep's power to defeat the Scorpion King, which would give him command of the Army of Anubis.
- **S11**：The cult, led by Baltus Hafez, the curator of the British Museum, includes a warrior named Lock-Nah and Meela Nais, the reincarnation of Imhotep's love Anck-su-namun.
- **S12**：The O'Connells set out to rescue Alex, accompanied by Evelyn's brother Jonathan and the Medjai Ardeth Bay.
- **S13**：Rick's associate from his past adventures, Izzy, a pilot, provides the group transportation.
- **S14**：The bracelet of Anubis gives Alex visions, directions to Ahm Shere that the cult follows.
- **S15**：At each location, Alex leaves clues for his parents, who follow in Izzy's dirigible.
- **S16**：Imhotep uses the Book of the Dead to give Meela Nais the soul of Anck-su-namun but by doing so he also allows Evelyn to unlock the memories of her previous life as Princess Nefertiri, keeper of the bracelet of Anubis and the daughter of Pharaoh Seti I.
- **S17**：Lock-Nah discovers that Alex has been leaving clues, so Imhotep makes a wall of water that attacks the dirigible.
- **S18**：The O'Connells crash in Ahm Shere.
- **S19**：Izzy stays with the dirigible in hopes of repairing it.
- **S20**：The O'Connells attack the cult, and both groups are attacked by pygmy mummies.
- **S21**：Rick retrieves Alex while Ardeth Bay kills Lock-Nah.
- **S22**：They escape from the pygmies, which kill most of the cult except for Baltus.
- **S23**：Imhotep and Anck-su-namun are unharmed due to Imhotep's powers and Anck-su-namun being the keeper of the Book of the Dead.
- **S24**：Rick and Alex run to the pyramid, barely making it before sunrise.
- **S25**：The bracelet detaches from Alex's arm.
- **S26**：Ardeth regroups with the Medjai in case the army of Anubis rises.
- **S27**：Anck-su-namun stabs Evelyn and kills her.
- **S28**：Rick, grieving, pursues Imhotep.
- **S29**：Baltus puts on the bracelet and revives the army of Anubis.
- **S30**：As Imhotep walks into the pyramid, Anubis takes his powers, wanting Imhotep to fight as a mortal.
- **S31**：Rick finds Imhotep summoning the Scorpion King and they fight.
- **S32**：The Medjai engage the army of Anubis.
- **S33**：The Scorpion King interrupts Rick and Imhotep's fight.
- **S34**：Imhotep tells the Scorpion King that he is the Scorpion King's slave, but Rick was sent to kill him.
- **S35**：During Rick and the Scorpion King's fight, the Scorpion King kills Baltus.
- **S36**：Jonathan and Alex collaborate to steal the Book of the Dead from Anck-su-namun.
- **S37**：Alex uses the book to resurrect Evelyn, who duels with Anck-su-namun.
- **S38**：Alex and Jonathan go to help Rick.
- **S39**：It is revealed that the scepter that Jonathan has been carrying extends into a spear that can kill the Scorpion King.
- **S40**：The Medjai seemingly defeat Anubis' army despite heavy casualties, but see that they have only defeated the vanguard, and the full army is charging towards them.
- **S41**：However, before the Anubis warriors hit the Medjai lines, Rick succeeds in killing the Scorpion King, sending him and his army back to the Underworld.
- **S42**：The Scorpion King's death causes Anubis to turn the oasis back into a desert, and the oasis is being sucked into the pyramid.
- **S43**：Rick and Imhotep are hanging from the edge of a pit that leads into the underworld.
- **S44**：Evelyn risks her life to save Rick, but Anck-su-namun refuses to save Imhotep, who is heart-broken about this, and he lets go into the underworld to his death.
- **S45**：Anck-su-namun, running away from the pyramid, falls into a scarab-filled pit, where she is bitten and suffocated to death.
- **S46**：The O'Connells reach the top of the pyramid, which is being sucked into the desert.
- **S47**：Izzy arrives with a modified dirigible and saves the O'Connells.
- **S48**：Jonathan grabs the diamond at the top of the pyramid.
- **S49**：Ardeth Bay salutes them as they fly over him.

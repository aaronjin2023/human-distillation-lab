### James Bond

### Emilio Largo

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-d250428719d623b1` · 独立版本：`profile-f9c3788dba0017e2`

- **性格倾向**：在控制手下时倾向以致命惩罚压制讨价还价与行动失利（行为推断；S12、S27）
- **核心动机**：通过掌握失窃核弹参与巨额勒索，以大规模毁灭施压（摘要直接叙述；S13、S16、S17、S40）
- **价值取舍**：在处理手下索价与行动失利时，将维持控制置于其生命之上（行为推断；S12、S27）
- **决策方式**：在作战失败时倾向借预设的船体分离能力保存逃生机会（行为推断；S42、S43、S44、S45）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：依赖他人执行计划，却以残酷处置维持服从。

- **S1**：James Bond—MI6 agent 007 and sometimes simply "007"—attends the funeral of Colonel Jacques Bouvar, a SPECTRE operative .The name is often mis-spelled: it is spelled Bouvar on Thunderball Ultimate Edition DVD Region 2.
- **S2**：See Disc One, English subtitles for the film, and Disc Two under "007 Mission Control / Villains / Jacques Bouvar" Bouvar is alive and disguised as his own widow, but Bond identifies him.
- **S3**：Following him to a château, Bond fights and kills him, escaping using a jetpack and his Aston Martin DB5.
- **S4**：Bond is sent by M to a clinic to improve his health.
- **S5**：While massaged by physiotherapist Patricia Fearing, he notices Count Lippe, a suspicious man with a criminal tattoo (from a [[Tong .
- **S6**：He searches Lippe's room, but is seen leaving by Lippe's clinic neighbour who is bandaged after plastic surgery.
- **S7**：Lippe tries to murder Bond with a spinal traction machine, but is foiled by Fearing, whom Bond then seduces.
- **S8**：Bond finds a dead bandaged man, François Derval.
- **S9**：Derval was a French NATO pilot deployed to fly aboard an Avro Vulcan loaded with two atomic bombs for a training mission.
- **S10**：He had been murdered by Angelo, a SPECTRE henchman surgically altered to match his appearance.
- **S11**：Angelo takes Derval's place on the flight, sabotaging the plane and sinking it near the Bahamas.
- **S12**：He is then killed by Emilio Largo  for trying to extort more money than offered to him.
- **S13**：Largo and his henchmen retrieve the stolen atomic bombs from the seabed.
- **S14**：All double-0 agents are called to Whitehall and en route, Lippe chases Bond.
- **S15**：Lippe is killed by SPECTRE agent Fiona Volpe for failing to foresee Angelo's greed.
- **S16**：SPECTRE demands £100 million in white flawless uncut diamonds from NATO in exchange for returning the bombs.
- **S17**：If their demands are not met, SPECTRE will destroy a major city in the United States or the United Kingdom.
- **S18**：At the meeting, Bond recognises Derval from a photograph.
- **S19**：Since Derval's sister, Domino, is in Nassau, Bond asks M to send him there, where he discovers Domino is Largo's mistress.
- **S20**：Bond takes a boat to where Domino is snorkelling.
- **S21**：After Bond saves her life, the two have lunch together.
- **S22**：Later, Bond goes to a party, where he sees Largo and Domino gambling.
- **S23**：Bond enters the game against Largo, and wins.
- **S24**：Bond and Domino leave the game and dance together.
- **S25**：Bond returns to the hotel, uses a connecting door to enter his room and notices someone is also inside.
- **S26**：Felix Leiter enters and is silenced by Bond, who finds and disarms a SPECTRE henchman in the bathroom.
- **S27**：He releases the henchman, who returns to Largo and is thrown into a pool of sharks.
- **S28**：Bond meets Q, and is issued with a collection of gadgets, including an underwater infrared camera, a distress beacon, underwater breathing apparatus, a flare gun and a Geiger counter.
- **S29**：Bond attempts to swim underwater beneath Largo's boat, but is nearly killed.
- **S30**：Bond's assistant Paula is abducted by Largo for questioning and kills herself.
- **S31**：Bond is kidnapped by Fiona, but escapes.
- **S32**：He is chased through a Junkanoo celebration and enters the Kiss Kiss club.
- **S33**：Fiona finds and attempts to kill him, but is shot by her own bodyguard.
- **S34**：Bond and Felix search for the Vulcan, finding it underwater.
- **S35**：Bond meets Domino scuba-diving and they have underwater sex.
- **S36**：Bond tells her that Largo killed her brother, asking for help finding the bombs.
- **S37**：She tells him where to go to replace a henchman on Largo's mission to retrieve them from a submarine.
- **S38**：Bond gives her his Geiger counter, asking her to look for them on Largo's ship.
- **S39**：She is discovered and captured.
- **S40**：Disguised as Largo's henchman, Bond uncovers Largo's plan to destroy Miami Beach.
- **S41**：Bond is discovered, and rescued by Leiter, who orders United States Coast Guard sailors to parachute to the area.
- **S42**：After an underwater battle, the henchmen surrender.
- **S43**：Largo escapes to his ship, the Disco Volante, which has one of the bombs on board.
- **S44**：Largo attempts to escape by jettisoning the rear of the ship.

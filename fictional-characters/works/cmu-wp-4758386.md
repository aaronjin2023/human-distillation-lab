## 角色资料

### Elizabeth

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-613d9ec2acbb503b` · 独立版本：`profile-5190b8196e2d3956`

- **性格倾向**：面对失恋与异地生活，倾向以劳动和移动主动寻找出口（行为推断；S2、S4、S11）
- **核心动机**：一边寻求失恋后的情绪恢复，一边兼做两份工作积钱购车（摘要直接叙述；S2、S4）
- **关系模式**：远行时持续联系杰里米，却通过隐去住址保持距离（摘要直接叙述；S5）
- **决策方式**：借钱给莱斯莉时，约定以赌局分成或汽车作为相应回报（摘要直接叙述；S13）
- **成长变化**：从借咖啡馆安慰失恋，走向返程后确认新的双向感情（摘要直接叙述；S2、S19、S20）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：持续寄明信片维系联系，却不让杰里米知道自己的具体所在。

### Leslie

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-79bb89cc0d1c991b` · 独立版本：`profile-f64d5a6c76374841`

- **性格倾向**：面对亲近者的求见信号，倾向先怀疑其真实性并保持防备（行为推断；S15、S16）
- **核心动机**：交车后提出前往拉斯维加斯，希望向久未见面的父亲借款（摘要直接叙述；S14）
- **决策方式**：在赌局结果上隐瞒真相，后来选择坦白并支付约定份额（摘要直接叙述；S14、S18）
- **关系模式**：与父亲长期疏远，直到错失临终见面后重新面对其关怀（行为推断；S14、S16、S17）
- **价值取舍**：父亲去世后要求保留其车辆，使汽车承载超出赌注的意义（行为推断；S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：怀疑病危通知只是诱她回家的借口，抵达后却得知父亲已去世。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Jeremy is an émigré from Manchester who owns a small New York City cafe.
- **S2**：The cafe becomes a haven for Elizabeth as she tries to recover emotionally following the end of an affair.
- **S3**：She finds comfort in the blueberry pie he bakes daily despite the fact that none of his other customers ever order it.
- **S4**：Elizabeth, now calling herself Lizzie, eventually drifts to Memphis, Tennessee, where she takes two jobs, waitress by day and barmaid by night, in order to earn enough money to finance the purchase of a car.
- **S5**：She regularly sends postcards to Jeremy without revealing where she lives or works and, although he tries to locate her by calling all the restaurants in the area, he fails to find her.
- **S6**：Later on, he decides to send out postcards to any restaurants she may be to try to find her.
- **S7**：One of Lizzie's regulars at both jobs is local policeman Arnie Copeland, an alcoholic who cannot accept the fact his wife Sue Lynne has left him and is flaunting her freedom by openly socializing with other men.
- **S8**：He confesses to Lizzie his many attempts at achieving sobriety have ended in failure.
- **S9**：One night, he drunkenly threatens his estranged wife with his gun if she leaves, then crashes his car and is killed.
- **S10**：Lizzie comforts Sue Lynne, who gives her the money towards Arnie's tab at the bar Lizzie works at in the evening, before leaving town.
- **S11**：Heading west, Elizabeth – now calling herself Beth – gets another waitress job at a casino in a small town in Nevada.
- **S12**：Here she meets Leslie, an inveterate poker player who has lost all her money.
- **S13**：Beth agrees to lend her $2200 in exchange for a third of her winnings or her car if she loses.
- **S14**：When she does lose, she fulfills her promise by giving Beth the car, but asks her to drive her to Las Vegas so she can borrow money from her father, whom she has not seen in a long time.
- **S15**：While en route she receives a call from a Vegas hospital, where her father has been admitted and is dying.
- **S16**：Leslie believes the call is simply a ruse to lure her home, but upon arrival in Vegas she discovers her father died the previous night.
- **S17**：Leslie announces she wants to keep the car, which she had stolen from her father, who had sent her the title and registration despite their estrangement.
- **S18**：She confesses she really won the card game and gives Beth her promised share of the winnings, which she uses to finally purchase the car she always wanted.
- **S19**：Elizabeth returns to Manhattan and, discovering her ex-boyfriend has vacated his apartment and moved on with his life, returns to the cafe, where Jeremy has had a stool at the counter reserved for her ever since she left.
- **S20**：As she eats a slice of blueberry pie, Elizabeth realizes her feelings for him are reciprocated.

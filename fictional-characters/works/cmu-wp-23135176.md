## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In December 2002, Mikael Blomkvist , publisher of Millennium magazine, loses a libel case involving allegations he published about billionaire financier Hans-Erik Wennerström .
- **S2**：He is sentenced to three months  in prison, and ordered to pay hefty damages and costs.
- **S3**：Lisbeth Salander , who is a surveillance agent has been contracted to investigate Blomkvist by a lawyer, Dirch Frode , on behalf of his sole client, Henrik Vanger , the elderly patriarch of the wealthy Vanger family.
- **S4**：Before the publisher is imprisioned, Vanger hires Blomkvist to investigate the disappearance of his great-niece, Harriet, who vanished on Children's Day in 1966.
- **S5**：Vanger believes that Harriet was murdered by a family member.
- **S6**：When Salander is told that her legal guardian has suffered a stroke, she is instructed to meet lawyer Nils Bjurman ([[Peter Andersson , who takes control of her finances.
- **S7**：Bjurman, a sexual sadist, forces Salander to perform fellatio on him in return for access to her bank account.
- **S8**：At their next meeting, he beats her, handcuffs her to his bed, and anally rapes her, not knowing that Salander is recording the incident with a hidden video camera.
- **S9**：After Salander recovers from the rape, she pays Bjurman an unexpected visit.
- **S10**：She stuns him with a taser and ties him up.
- **S11**：She compels him to relinquish control of her finances and in one year's time to recommend termination of her state guardianship.
- **S12**：Failure to comply with her demands will result in the release of the DVD evidence of her rape to the media.
- **S13**：Salander tattoos Bjurman's abdomen with the message "I am a sadist pig and a rapist".
- **S14**：Blomkvist moves into a cottage on the Vanger estate.
- **S15**：Vanger tells him that his three brothers were all members of the Swedish Nazi Party.
- **S16**：Of the three, only Harald is still living.
- **S17**：Inside Harriet's Bible, Blomkvist finds a list of five names alongside what might appear to be phone numbers.
- **S18**：He visits police inspector Morell , who informs him that his investigation team had been unable to decipher them.
- **S19**：Using photographs taken during the Children's Day parade, Blomkvist believes that Harriet may have seen someone that day who may have killed her.
- **S20**：Using her access to Blomkvist's computer, Salander interprets the meaning of the numbers next to the names and sends an email to Blomkvist.
- **S21**：Upon discovering that his computer has been hacked, Blomkvist is directed by Dirch Frode to Salander's apartment.
- **S22**：He convinces her to help him with the case, and she joins him at the cottage.
- **S23**：Together, using the clue that Salander discovered, they connect all but one of the names on Harriet's list to murdered women.
- **S24**：They are all Jewish names, leading Blomkvist and Salander to suspect that the murders were motivated by anti-Semitism.
- **S25**：They suspect the reclusive Harald Vanger  to be the culprit, as the two other Vanger brothers had already died by the time Harriet disappeared.
- **S26**：Salander searches through Vanger's business records to trace Harald to the crime scenes, while Blomkvist breaks into Harald's house, believing it to be unoccupied.
- **S27**：When Harald attacks Blomkvist,, Martin Vanger appears, thus saving him.
- **S28**：He escorts Blomkvist to his home, where Blomkvist reveals what he and Salander have uncovered.
- **S29**：Martin drugs him.
- **S30**：In the meantime, Salander discovers that Martin and his father were responsible for the murders.
- **S31**：She returns to the cottage to find Blomkvist missing.
- **S32**：Blomkvist wakes to find himself bound in Martin's cellar.
- **S33**：Martin confesses to decades of rape and murder of women, but denies killing Harriet, saying she disappeared.
- **S34**：Salander appears and attacks the killer with a golf club.
- **S35**：While she frees Blomkvist, Martin flees in his car.
- **S36**：Salander gives chase on her motorcycle.
- **S37**：Martin drives his car off the road and dies in a fiery crash as Salander watches without attempting to rescue him.
- **S38**：Salander reveals there were two Anita's, only one of whom died.
- **S39**：Blomkvist flies to Australia to seek out Harriet who is living under her dead cousin Anita's name.
- **S40**：He and Harriet return to Sweden.
- **S41**：Reunited with Henrik, Harriet reveals that she killed her father Gottfried, who, along with her brother Martin, had been sexually abusing her.
- **S42**：Fearing the abuse by Martin would start again when she saw him at the Children's Day parade, she had fled the estate with Anita's help.
- **S43**：Salander visits Blomkvist in prison and gives him new information on the Wennerström case.
- **S44**：After his release from prison, Blomkvist publishes a new story on Wennerström in Millennium, which ruins Wennerström and makes the magazine a national sensation.
- **S45**：Wennerström is found dead, and his offshore bank account in the Cayman Islands is raided.
- **S46**：The police suspect a young woman caught on CCTV.
- **S47**：Blomkvist is convinced that she is Salander in disguise.
- **S48**：The film ends with the same woman walking along a sunny beach promenade.

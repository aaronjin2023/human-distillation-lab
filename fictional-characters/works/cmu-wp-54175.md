- **S1**：Tony Manero  is a 19-year old Italian American from the Bay Ridge, Brooklyn neighborhood of New York City.
- **S2**：Tony lives at home with his parents , and works a dead-end job in a small hardware store by day.
- **S3**：But every Saturday night, Tony is "king of the dance floor" at 2001 Odyssey, a local disco club.
- **S4**：Tony has four close friends: Joey ; Double J ; Gus ; and the diminutive Bobby C. ([[Barry Miller .
- **S5**：Another informal member of their group is Annette , a neighborhood girl who longs for a more permanent and physical relationship with Tony.
- **S6**：One plot device in the story is the Verrazano-Narrows Bridge, on which the friends ritually stop to clown around, but is particularly symbolic to Tony as an escape to a better life on the other side.
- **S7**：Tony agrees to be Annette's partner in an upcoming dance contest at 2001 Odyssey, but her happiness is short-lived when Tony becomes infatuated with another girl dancing at the club, Stephanie Mangano .
- **S8**：Stephanie coldly rejects Tony's advances, but eventually agrees to be his partner in the competition, nothing more.
- **S9**：Tony's older brother, Frank Jr. , who was the pride of the family since becoming a priest in the Catholic Church, brings despair to their parents when he quits the priesthood.
- **S10**：Tony shares a warm relationship with Frank Jr., but feels vindicated, no longer being the black sheep.
- **S11**：While on his way home from the grocery store, Gus is attacked by a Hispanic gang and is hospitalized, and tells the guys it was the Barracudas.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, Bobby C. has been trying to get out of his relationship with his devoutly Catholic girlfriend, Pauline, who is pregnant with his child.
- **S13**：Facing pressure from his family and others to marry her, Bobby asks former priest Frank Jr., if the Pope would grant him dispensation for an abortion.
- **S14**：But when Frank tells him this would be highly unlikely, Bobby's feelings of despair deepen.
- **S15**：Bobby C also lets Tony borrow his 1964 Chevrolet Impala to help move Stephanie from Bay Ridge to Manhattan, with Tony promising to call him later that night, but Tony does not.
- **S16**：Eventually, the group gets their revenge on the Barracudas, and crash Bobby C's car into their hangout.
- **S17**：Tony, Double J and Joey get out to fight, but Bobby C. takes off when a gang member tries to attack him in the car.
- **S18**：When the guys visit Gus in the hospital, they are angry when he tells them that he may have fingered the wrong gang.
- **S19**：Later, Tony and Stephanie dance at the competition and end up winning first prize.
- **S20**：However, Tony believes that a Puerto Rican couple performed better, and the judges' decision was based on racism.
- **S21**：He gives the Puerto Ricans the first prize, and leaves with Stephanie in tow.
- **S22**：Once outside in the car, he tries to rape Stephanie, resulting in her fleeing from him.
- **S23**：Tony's friends come to the car along with a drunken and stoned Annette, who Joey says has agreed to have sex with everyone.
- **S24**：Tony tries to lead her away, but is subdued by Double J and Joey, and sullenly leaves with the group in the car.
- **S25**：Double J and Joey take turns with Annette, who begins to sober up during what has become a rape scene.
- **S26**：Bobby C. pulls the car over on the Verrazano-Narrows Bridge for the usual cable-climbing antics.
- **S27**：Typically abstaining, Bobby gets out and performs more dangerous stunts than the rest.
- **S28**：Realizing that he is acting recklessly, Tony tries to get him to come down.
- **S29**：But upset at his lonely life, his situation with Pauline, and a broken promise from Tony earlier, Bobby issues a tirade at Tony's lack of care before slipping and falling to his death more than two hundred feet in the water below.
- **S30**：Disgusted and disillusioned by his friends, his life and his family, Tony spends the rest of the night riding the subway.
- **S31**：As morning comes, he finally shows up at Stephanie's apartment in Manhattan, apologizing for his bad behavior.
- **S32**：He tells her that he plans to leave Brooklyn and come to Manhattan to try and start a new life.
- **S33**：Tony and Stephanie salvage their relationship and agree to be friends, sharing a tender moment as the credits roll.

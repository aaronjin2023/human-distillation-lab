## 角色资料

### Bruce Wayne

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8a9e8e464a744c51` · 独立版本：`profile-85c2d008897dbcf4`

- **性格倾向**：在面对自身恐惧时倾向主动接近，并将其转化为行动资源（行为推断；S1、S15）
- **核心动机**：逐渐将个人复仇冲动转向打击犯罪并保护哥谭（行为推断；S4、S16、S25、S31）
- **价值取舍**：拒绝以摧毁城市实现解放，认为整座城市值得保护（行为推断；S9、S10、S28、S31、S32）
- **决策方式**：倾向以公开伪装掩护行动，并将关键任务交给可信盟友（行为推断；S13、S27、S31）
- **成长变化**：从准备枪杀仇人，转为通过证据和盟友打击犯罪（摘要直接叙述；S4、S16、S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：保护城市的身份使他成为英雄，也阻碍了与瑞秋的亲密关系。；拒绝毁城并曾救下杜卡德，最终却留下对方死于列车坠毁。

### Ra's al Ghul

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-5aac46bef1a5be22` · 独立版本：`profile-e79120aefe95a40a`

- **性格倾向**：面对城市问题时倾向极端处置，将毁灭视为解决办法（行为推断；S9、S28）
- **核心动机**：以所谓解放哥谭为目标，策划摧毁城市秩序与生命（摘要直接叙述；S9、S28）
- **价值取舍**：在毁城计划中将组织的目标置于全城居民的安全之上（行为推断；S9、S28）
- **决策方式**：倾向隐瞒身份并串联技术与代理人实施大规模攻击（行为推断；S8、S26、S28）
- **关系模式**：与布鲁斯由训练关系转为围绕哥谭命运的直接对抗（摘要直接叙述；S8、S10、S26、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As a child, Bruce Wayne falls into a well, developing a fear of bats.
- **S2**：Soon afterward, he witnesses his parents' murder by mugger Joe Chill, leaving Bruce to be raised by the family butler, Alfred Pennyworth.
- **S3**：Years later, Chill is granted parole in exchange for testifying against crime boss Carmine Falcone.
- **S4**：Bruce, now a young man, intends to shoot him, but one of Falcone's assassins does so first.
- **S5**：Rachel Dawes, Bruce's childhood friend and now an assistant district attorney, is disgusted by his desire for vengeance.
- **S6**：As a result Bruce confronts Falcone, who berates him.
- **S7**：Bruce decides to travel and learn about the criminal underworld, before becoming a criminal himself.
- **S8**：In a Bhutanese prison, Henri Ducard offers to train him in the arts of stealth and fear as a member of the League of Shadows, led by Ra's al Ghul.
- **S9**：After completing his training, Bruce learns the League's true intention is to liberate Gotham by destroying it.
- **S10**：Bruce refuses and burns down the League's temple.
- **S11**：Ra's is killed by falling debris, while Bruce saves an unconscious Ducard, leaving him with villagers.
- **S12**：After meeting Alfred, Bruce returns to Gotham.
- **S13**：Publicly posing as a playboy, he takes an interest in his family's company, Wayne Enterprises, a technology and defense conglomerate now run by the unscrupulous CEO William Earle.
- **S14**：Bruce meets Lucius Fox, who introduces him to the company's prototype technologies, including an armored car and protective bodysuit.
- **S15**：Taking these, Bruce finds an entrance to the cave under his well, confronts his fear of bats and creates a workshop, taking up the identity of "Batman".
- **S16**：As Batman, he intercepts a drug shipment and provides Rachel with evidence to indict Falcone, empowering the honest Sgt.
- **S17**：James Gordon and the Gotham police to arrest the previously untouchable Falcone.
- **S18**：Meanwhile, a prototype water vaporizer is stolen from a Wayne Enterprises cargo ship.
- **S19**：Back in Gotham, Falcone and his henchmen are declared mentally unfit for trial and transferred to Arkham Asylum by the corrupt Dr.
- **S20**：Jonathan Crane, who had been using Falcone to import a dangerous hallucinogenic drug that causes severe psychosis.
- **S21**：Crane exposes Falcone to the toxin while wearing a burlap mask, driving Falcone insane with fear of the "Scarecrow." While investigating Crane, Batman is also exposed to the drug and is rescued by Alfred and given an antidote by Fox.
- **S22**：Rachel goes to Arkham, where Crane reveals that he has been dumping the toxin into Gotham's water supply before dosing her with it.
- **S23**：She is rescued by Batman, who exposes Crane to the toxin and interrogates him.
- **S24**：Crane reveals that the toxin is only dangerous if inhaled.
- **S25**：Batman inoculates Rachel and gives her two vials of the antidote, one for Gordon and one for mass production.
- **S26**：At his birthday celebration at Wayne Manor, Bruce is confronted by Ducard, who reveals himself to be the real Ra's al Ghul.
- **S27**：Feigning drunkenness, Bruce kicks his guests out so they will be safe, leaving him alone with Ra's and his ninja.
- **S28**：Ra's reveals the League's plan to destroy Gotham: having stolen the Microwave Emitter and conspired with Crane, they intend to vaporize the city's toxin-riddled water supply, creating mass hysteria and violence.
- **S29**：The League sets fire to the mansion and Bruce is nearly trapped inside, but Alfred saves him at the last minute.
- **S30**：As the League begins unleashing the toxin, Batman rescues Rachel from a drug-induced mob and reveals his true identity to her.
- **S31**：He entrusts Gordon with the Tumbler and pursues Ra's, who is using Gotham's train system to deliver the weapon throughout the city.
- **S32**：Batman confronts Ra's on the train and escapes just as Gordon uses the Tumbler to destroy the elevated tracks, leaving Ra's to die in the ensuing crash.
- **S33**：Batman becomes a public hero, but simultaneously loses Rachel, who cannot bring herself to love both Bruce and Batman.
- **S34**：Bruce buys a controlling stake in the now publicly traded Wayne Enterprises, fires Earle, and replaces him with Fox as the new CEO.
- **S35**：Jim Gordon is promoted to Lieutenant, showing Batman the Bat-Signal and mentions a costumed criminal who leaves Joker playing cards at crime scenes.
- **S36**：Batman promises to investigate this new criminal while disappearing into the night.

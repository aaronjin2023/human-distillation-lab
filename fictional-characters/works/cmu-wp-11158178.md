## 角色资料

### Nat

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-38feb7f263a7aad3` · 独立版本：`profile-4eaf22f9b5c64f8a`

- **性格倾向**：面对冒险风险与劝阻观念，他倾向主动探索并带动朋友参与（行为推断；S1、S4、S5、S7）
- **核心动机**：他希望像祖父一样成为冒险者，因此谋划登上飞船前往月球（摘要直接叙述；S2、S3、S4）
- **决策方式**：遇到任务危机时，他倾向亲自进入危险区域解决具体问题（行为推断；S17、S21、S25）
- **价值取舍**：他在追求个人冒险之外，也愿为飞船任务与朋友安全承担风险（行为推断；S17、S25）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Amos

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-659446723de40a70` · 独立版本：`profile-3e39b8233ed5cb53`

- **性格倾向**：得知孙子面临威胁时，他倾向迅速行动并重新激发冒险勇气（行为推断；S28、S29、S32、S33）
- **核心动机**：他重返危险行动的明确目标，是阻止破坏计划并救回孙子（摘要直接叙述；S29、S32）
- **关系模式**：他以冒险往事影响孙子的志向，也与旧爱重逢后合作救援（行为推断；S2、S3、S14、S27、S32）
- **决策方式**：面对潜入控制中心的威胁，他选择联合家人与知情者直接阻止（行为推断；S28、S30、S32、S33）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A preteen fly named Nat and his two best friends, I.Q. and Scooter, build a “fly-sized” rocket in a field across from Cape Canaveral, Florida, where the Apollo 11 sits on the Kennedy Space Center Launch Complex 39.
- **S2**：From his earliest memory, Nat remembers his grandfather, Amos, telling him of his daring rescue of Amelia Earhart when she and Fred Noonan crossed the Atlantic Ocean on their historic flight in 1937.
- **S3**：Wanting to be an adventurer like his Grandpa, Nat knows what he has to do.
- **S4**：Defying the notion that “Dreamers get swatted!” he tells his friends his plan to get aboard the Apollo 11 and go to the moon.
- **S5**：His buddies, with some reluctance, are in.
- **S6**：The next morning, as their families realize they are missing, the three flies make it in to Mission Control.
- **S7**：In their homemade space suits, Nat, I.Q. and Scooter manage to stow away inside the space helmets of astronauts Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins.
- **S8**：As they blast off, our three tiny adventurers are about to make some history of their own.
- **S9**：Back on Earth, Grandpa, Mom and the others watch TV to get news of their offspring’s adventure.
- **S10**：As the astronauts appear on camera, the heroic flies wave in the background, visible to other flies but barely seen by humans – except for the attentive NASA flight controller Steve Bales, who informs Armstrong that there appear to be “contaminants” on board.
- **S11**：One other problem: in the far-away Soviet Union, there are other flies watching TV - Soviet flies who cannot tolerate the idea that American flies might get to the moon first.
- **S12**：A Soviet plan is hatched and special operatives are enlisted to interfere with the American mission.
- **S13**：The Soviet Union puts all its hopes of success on the back of one nasty operative named Yegor.
- **S14**：Fortunately, a pretty Soviet fly named Nadia also sees the flies on board and hears Scooter calling out Grandpa’s real name, the very name of the fly Nadia met in Paris and loved so many years ago.
- **S15**：Back on board the space ship, as the burn cycle to enter the moon's Trans Lunar Injection orbit begins, the spacecraft is violently rocked.
- **S16**：There’s a short circuit in the Service Module that must be fixed manually or the ship won’t be able to complete its mission.
- **S17**：Nat and I.Q. fly through a maze of wires, find the problem and repair it just in time.
- **S18**：Unaware of the flies’ aid, the ship enters orbit and all is well... or so they think.
- **S19**：Just as they congratulate each other, the little flies are sprayed with a numbing aerosol and are captives in a test tube vial – contaminants indeed.
- **S20**：The flies manage to break the vial.
- **S21**：Nat sneaks into Armstrong’s helmet just in the nick of time.
- **S22**：The Lunar Module Eagle lands on Mare Tranquillitatis.
- **S23**：From inside the helmet, Nat beams with every awe-inspiring historic step.
- **S24**：I.Q. and Scooter join him on the surface inside Aldrin’s helmet.
- **S25**：After a climatic rescue with Nat bringing Scooter back to the Columbia, the Eagle is jettisoned.
- **S26**：Back on Earth, other plots are being set in motion.
- **S27**：After more than 30 years apart, Nadia finds Grandpa, though the joy of their reunion is brief.
- **S28**：She tells Grandpa and Nat’s Mom about the Soviet plot to divert the mission.
- **S29**：Nat’s Mom faints while Grandpa takes off with a renewed youthful vow to save his grandson.
- **S30**：At Mission Control Center, the Soviet operatives have infiltrated and are preparing to alter the descent codes.
- **S31**：Unaware of the potential danger looming, the astronauts and the little flies sit back and prepare to come back home as heroes.
- **S32**：Grandpa, Nat’s Mom and Nadia join forces to stop Yegor and the Soviet plan as the Command Module Columbia hurtles closer and closer toward Earth's atmosphere.
- **S33**：In a series of death-defying stunts, they crush the Soviet threat.
- **S34**：The Columbia splashes down safely in the Pacific Ocean, where it is recovered by the USS Hornet.
- **S35**：Returning as heroes, the three little flies share a slogan embraced by all: “Adventure forever!
- **S36**：Dreamers get swatted?
- **S37**：Never!” At the end the real Buzz Aldrin spoils the film by explaining that no flies were on board during the historic flight.
- **S38**：As IMAX 3-D films are usually less or around an hour, some scenes were cut and censored from the IMAX version.
- **S39**：The IMAX version starts with the opening scene which shows the first monkey being launched to space.
- **S40**：It then cuts to Nat sneaking out to meet his friends and sneak into the command centre, cutting out the scene with Nat and Amos, discussing Amelia Earhart.
- **S41**：The IMAX version also cuts out the Soviet Sub-plot.

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Part I [ edit ] It is April 15, 1861, the eve of a rebellion in which seven southern states declare their secession from the United States (known as "the Union ") over a desire to continue the institution of slavery , which was the economic engine of the South.
- **S3**：In one of those states, Georgia , the family of wealthy Irish immigrant Gerald O'Hara owns a plantation (called " Tara "). [ 4 ] The oldest of the three O'Hara daughters, 16-year-old Scarlett is dismayed to learn that the man she secretly loves, her county neighbor Ashley Wilkes , is set to announce his engagement to his cousin Melanie Hamilton .
- **S4**：The next day, the Wilkeses throw an all-day party at their estate (known as " Twelve Oaks ") where Scarlett notices someone leering at her.
- **S5**：He turns out to be Rhett Butler, who has a reputation for seducing young women.
- **S6**：Throughout the day, Scarlett attempts to turn Ashley's head by flirting with every man present, including Melanie's brother Charles Hamilton.
- **S7**：In the afternoon, Scarlett gets Ashley alone and confesses her love for him, convinced he will return it.
- **S8**：However, he says only that he cares for her as a friend and intends to marry Melanie.
- **S9**：Stung, Scarlett insults Ashley and accuses him of being too cowardly to submit to his real feelings for her.
- **S10**：As Ashley departs, Rhett reveals he has overheard their whole exchange.
- **S11**：Scarlett feels humiliated. [ 5 ] Later, war is declared, and the men are going to enlist.
- **S12**：Feeling petty and vengeful, Scarlett accepts Charles's marriage proposal.
- **S13**：They marry, and two weeks later, Charles goes to war, where he dies of measles two months later.
- **S14**：Scarlett gives birth to his child, Wade Hampton Hamilton. [ 6 ] As a widow, she is bound to dye her dresses black, wear a veil in public, and avoid conversations with young men.
- **S15**：Scarlett secretly mourns the loss of her youth, not the husband she barely knew.
- **S16**：Part II [ edit ] Scarlett's mother, mistaking Scarlett's depression for grief, suggests that living with Melanie might lift her spirits.
- **S17**：Melanie is living in Atlanta with her Aunt Sarah Jane, who is called by her childhood nickname "Pittypat".
- **S18**：After moving there, Scarlett's spirit is revived by the excitement of living in a growing city.
- **S19**：She busies herself with hospital work and sewing circles for the Confederate Army .
- **S20**：However, her heart is not in it – she does it primarily to avoid being gossiped about by the other women of Atlanta society.
- **S21**：Additionally, she believes her efforts may aid Ashley, with whom she is still in love.
- **S22**：Scarlett is mortified when she runs into Rhett while staffing a sales stall at a public dance benefiting the troops. [ 7 ] Rhett believes the war is a lost cause but is becoming rich as a blockade runner for profit.
- **S23**：He sees through Scarlett's "lady in mourning" disguise and recognizes her longing to dance with the other young people, so he bids a lot of gold to win the honor of leading the first dance and chooses her as his partner.
- **S24**：Scarlett scandalizes everyone by dancing joyfully while still dressed in widow's mourning.
- **S25**：Her reputation is saved by Melanie, who is now her sister-in-law and highly respected in Atlanta; she argues that Scarlett is supporting the Confederate cause.
- **S26**：Scarlett continues to act recklessly, flirting and dating while still in widow's clothes, always protected by Melanie's endorsement.
- **S27**：She spends much of her time with Rhett, whose sexual attraction to Scarlett is ever-present.
- **S28**：At one point, he enrages her with a silky proposition that she become his mistress.
- **S29**：Still, she appreciates Rhett for his money, his sophistication, and their shared irritation with the hypocrisy of Atlanta society. [ 7 ] At Christmas (1863), Ashley is granted a furlough from the army and goes to Atlanta.
- **S30**：Scarlett struggles to restrain her feelings for him.
- **S31**：She remains convinced that he is secretly in love with her and is still married to Melanie out of duty.
- **S32**：Scarlett is heartbroken when Melanie becomes pregnant with Ashley's child.
- **S33**：Part III [ edit ] The war is going badly for the Confederacy.
- **S34**：By September 1864, Atlanta is besieged from three sides. [ 8 ] The city becomes desperate as hundreds of wounded Confederate soldiers pour in.
- **S35**：Melanie goes into labor with only the inexperienced Scarlett and a young enslaved woman called Prissy to assist, as all the trained doctors are attending to the soldiers.
- **S36**：The tattered Confederate States Army sets flame to Atlanta before they abandon it to the Union army .
- **S37**：Amidst the chaos, Melanie gives birth to a boy, Beau.
- **S38**：Scarlett tracks down Rhett and begs him to take her, Wade, Melanie, Beau, and Prissy to Tara.
- **S39**：Rhett laughs at this idea, explaining that the Yankees have likely burned Tara.
- **S40**：Still, he steals an emaciated horse and a wagon and begins driving them out of Atlanta.
- **S41**：At the city's edge, Rhett has a change of heart and abandons Scarlett to join the army in their final, doomed push.
- **S42**：Scarlett drives the wagon to Tara, which has avoided being burned like many of her neighbors' homes.
- **S43**：However, the situation is bleak: Scarlett's mother is dead, her father has lost his mind with grief, her sisters are sick with typhoid fever , the enslaved field workers have left, the Yankees have burned all the cotton, and there is no food.
- **S44**：A long struggle for survival begins, with Scarlett working in the fields.
- **S45**：There are several hungry people and animals, along with an ever-present threat from Yankees who steal or burn what little they can find.
- **S46**：At one point, Scarlett kills a Yankee soldier who attempts to invade her home and buries his body in the garden.
- **S47**：A long post-war succession of Confederate soldiers returning home stop at Tara to find food and rest.
- **S48**：Eventually, Ashley returns from the war with his idealistic view of the world shattered.
- **S49**：Finding themselves alone one day, he and Scarlett share a kiss.
- **S50**：Unable to trust himself with her nearby, Ashley says he will take his family and move away.
- **S51**：Scarlett says she can not let them leave when they have nowhere to go and promises not to throw herself at him again.
- **S52**：Part IV [ edit ] Life at Tara begins to recover, but exorbitant taxes are levied on the plantation.
- **S53**：Scarlett knows only one man with enough money to help her – Rhett.
- **S54**：She puts on her only pretty dress (made from the velvet curtains at Tara) and finds him in a jailhouse in Atlanta.
- **S55**：He is being held on a murder charge and is likely to hang.
- **S56**：Although she nearly wins him over with a southern belle routine, he declines to help after realizing her sweetness is an act meant to use his money.

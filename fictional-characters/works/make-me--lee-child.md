## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Jack Reacher arrives at a small Midwestern town named Mother's Rest, curious about the origin of its name.
- **S3**：Reacher wanders the town asking the locals, but he does not learn anything.
- **S4**：He meets a Chinese American woman named Michelle Chang, an ex- FBI agent turned private investigator looking for her colleague, Keever.
- **S5**：Reacher's suspicions are aroused by the aloofness of the locals, and he decides to stay in town to help with Chang's investigation.
- **S6**：Chang explains she does not know the identity of Keever's client or any details of his case.
- **S7**：Searching Keever's motel room, Reacher finds a crumpled up note with the name "Maloney" and a phone number.
- **S8**：The number belongs to a journalist named Westwood, the science editor for the Los Angeles Times , who has been handling calls from conspiracy theorists .
- **S9**：Reacher and Chang turn their search to Maloney, believing him to be Keever's contact and a resident of Mother's Rest.
- **S10**：As they investigate, they are confronted by hostile locals whom Reacher quickly defeats while stealing their handguns.
- **S11**：Reacher and Chang visit Keever's home, finding it ransacked with all his investigative notes missing.
- **S12**：Reacher believes Keever had stumbled onto something and been killed for it, and the two go to Los Angeles to meet with Westwood.
- **S13**：Convincing Westwood that Keever had been onto something newsworthy, they agree to give the journalist exclusive rights to the story in exchange for his help.
- **S14**：Westwood gives Reacher the phone numbers of unknown people who had recently called him who fit the profile of Keever's mystery client.
- **S15**：They learn Maloney is a Chicago resident named Peter McCann.
- **S16**：Arriving at McCann's home, they find he has gone missing.
- **S17**：They are attacked by a hitman named Hackett, who is incapacitated by Reacher.
- **S18**：The two question McCann's neighbor and learn that Peter had a sister.
- **S19**：At her home in Phoenix , they are attacked by more hired assassins.
- **S20**：With the help of Chang's FBI contacts, they learn the men are all employed by a Ukrainian crime lord named Merchenko.
- **S21**：Reacher deduces that Merchenko is working for an unknown person who lives in Mother's Rest.
- **S22**：By an amazing coincidence, Reacher and Chang happen across Merchenko sitting outside his nightclub .
- **S23**：Reacher executes the criminal in broad daylight.
- **S24**：Traveling back to Los Angeles, Reacher and Chang reunite with Westwood.
- **S25**：Reacher posits that Peter was investigating the disappearance of his son, Michael, who had suffered from anhedonia and was a recluse who spent most of his time on the Internet.
- **S26**：As the call from "Maloney" had been about the Deep Web , Reacher, Chang and Westwood meet with an associate of Westwood's, a computer hacker in Palo Alto .
- **S27**：Westwood's contact is able to discover that Mother's Rest has a Deep Web site providing assisted euthanasia services.
- **S28**：They further find that Michael had been speaking with potential suicides over the Deep Web and arranged to meet one of them at Mother's Rest to undergo euthanasia together.
- **S29**：Reacher, Chang and Westwood come up with a plan to assault the euthanasia site, located on a pig farm that is both highly remote and well-defended.
- **S30**：After killing all of the armed employees, Reacher and his team discover that the farmhouse had been converted into a film studio.
- **S31**：The criminals had recruited suicidal people over the Deep Web with promises of painless and luxurious euthanasia services.
- **S32**：Once the clients arrive, however, they are forced to appear in snuff films that are sold over the Deep Web.
- **S33**：Over two hundred such victims had been murdered prior to Keever discovering the operation.
- **S34**：Reacher and Chang avenge Keever, Peter and Michael by killing the last members of the conspiracy.
- **S35**：In the aftermath, Reacher and Chang decide to spend a little downtime in Milwaukee together.

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] 23-year-old college graduate Lily Bloom moves to Boston with hopes of opening her own floral shop .
- **S3**：She grew up in a broken family – her late father, Andrew, would physically abuse her mother, Jenny, leading Lily to resent him, as well as resenting Jenny for staying with him.
- **S4**：She has recently given a eulogy at Andrew's funeral in her hometown of Plethora, Maine , during which she announced she would list five things she loved the most about him, then stood in silence for several seconds before walking off.
- **S5**：Lily reads through her childhood diaries and remembers her first love, Atlas Corrigan, who left to join the military but promised to return to her.
- **S6**：She later meets Ryle Kincaid, a charming, ambitious 30-year-old neurosurgeon.
- **S7**：They are mutually attracted, but do not pursue a relationship as Lily is seeking commitment while Ryle is only interested in a string of casual affairs.
- **S8**：However, once Lily successfully starts her business, they continue to meet and eventually begin a relationship.
- **S9**：One night, Lily laughs when Ryle accidentally drops a casserole , and he angrily slaps her before frantically apologizing.
- **S10**：Lily is reminded of her childhood; while horrified, she decides they are different from her parents and accepts his apologies, but warns him that she will leave him if he hurts her again.
- **S11**：While dining at a restaurant with Ryle, she discovers that Atlas is the owner and head chef there and reconnects with him, making Ryle instantly jealous despite her insistence that Atlas is only a friend.
- **S12**：Noticing Lily's bruised eye and Ryle's bandaged hand, Atlas suspects Ryle may be abusing her.
- **S13**：He corners her in the bathroom and begs her to leave Ryle.
- **S14**：Ryle discovers them and discover the worst, leading to a violent confrontation between the men.
- **S15**：The next day, Atlas visits Lily's shop to apologize for the incident, but gives her his phone number in case she ever needs it.
- **S16**：One night, Lily and Ryle impulsively get married in Vegas , and have a stable marriage until he finds Atlas's number and pushes her down the stairs.
- **S17**：He then confesses that as a child, he accidentally shot and killed his brother Emerson with their father's gun, resulting in unresolved trauma that manifests in bouts of uncontrollable rage.
- **S18**：Lily forgives him and agrees to help him work through his anger issues.
- **S19**：He later finds and reads her diaries, along with a local magazine article that contains an interview with Atlas; furious upon learning that Atlas named his restaurant in Lily's honor, he attempts to rape her, knocking her unconscious when she tries to defend herself.
- **S20**：Lily wakes up, escapes the house, and calls Atlas.
- **S21**：He takes her to the hospital, where she discovers she is pregnant with Ryle's child, which she opts to keep a secret from him.
- **S22**：She stays with Atlas for a few days while Ryle leaves for a fellowship in England, and eventually moves back home while Ryle is still out of the country.
- **S23**：Atlas admits he still has feelings for Lily, but has suppressed them because of her marriage to Ryle.
- **S24**：Finally understanding what her mother went through, Lily confides in Jenny about the abuse.
- **S25**：Jenny implores Lily to leave Ryle and not make the same mistakes she did.
- **S26**：When Ryle returns, Lily forms a tentative truce with him and allows him to help her through the final few months of her pregnancy, but remains emotionally distant from him.
- **S27**：Lily later gives birth to a daughter, whom she names after Ryle's late brother.
- **S28**：After giving birth, Lily realizes she does not want her daughter to grow up witnessing Ryle's aggressive outbursts, and tells him she wants a divorce.
- **S29**：He tearfully begs her to reconsider, but finally understands her perspective and agrees to separate after she asks him how he would react if their daughter told him she was being abused by her partner.
- **S30**：Lily hopes she has finally ended the cycle of abuse in her family, telling her daughter, "It ends with us." In the epilogue, Lily, who is co-parenting with Ryle, finds Atlas and tells him that she is ready to restart her relationship with him.

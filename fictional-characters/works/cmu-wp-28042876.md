## 角色资料

### Puss in Boots

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-cecd0bd3d42825ee` · 独立版本：`profile-e23c45472cf6b1cb`

- **性格倾向**：面对曾背叛自己的旧友，他仍愿相信其善意，显出和解倾向。（行为推断；S5、S12、S18）
- **核心动机**：他起初希望恢复失去的荣誉，得知巨鹅威胁后又着手保护故乡。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S15、S17、S18、S20）
- **价值取舍**：故乡受威胁时，他愿冒险救镇，也愿争取曾背叛自己的旧友合作。（行为推断；S12、S18、S20）
- **决策方式**：他在听闻巨鹅将来袭后，选择说服Humpty合作并以小鹅作诱饵。（摘要直接叙述；S15、S17、S18、S20）
- **关系模式**：他与Humpty曾是朋友却因旧事决裂，也与Kitty发展亲密关系。（摘要直接叙述；S4、S5、S8、S16、S25）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他因Humpty的旧案感到背叛，后来仍争取与其并肩保护城镇。

### Humpty Alexander Dumpty

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-c96299801fa0d641` · 独立版本：`profile-78989d676528b381`

- **性格倾向**：在旧友冲突中，他显出记恨并善于设局的倾向，但并非始终执意报复。（行为推断；S6、S12、S18、S21）
- **核心动机**：他策划诱捕Puss，意在报复童年盗窃失败后被抛下的往事。（摘要直接叙述；S12）
- **价值取舍**：在桥上无法两全的险境中，他最终将小鹅与城镇安危置于自身之上。（行为推断；S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：他利用寻找魔豆和金蛋的合作作掩护，设局将Puss引回故乡受捕。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S12）
- **成长变化**：他从设局报复、希望城镇被毁，转为协助救镇并主动牺牲。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S17、S18、S20、S21）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他先希望巨鹅毁掉城镇，最终却为保护小鹅和城镇放弃自身。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Puss in Boots is a talking cat named for his wearing boots and is a fugitive on the run for the law, looking to restore his lost honour.
- **S2**：He learns that the outlaw couple Jack and Jill have the magic beans he's been looking for most of his life, beans that can lead him to a giant's castle holding valuable golden goose eggs.
- **S3**：When Puss tries to steal them from the outlaws' room, Kitty interrupts, and both fail.
- **S4**：Kitty is allied with Humpty Alexander Dumpty, a talking egg and Puss' long-estranged childhood friend from the orphanage where he was raised.
- **S5**：Puss tells Kitty of his feelings of betrayal for a youthful misadventure when Humpty tricked Puss into helping commit a crime.
- **S6**：Humpty convinces Puss to join them in finding the beans and retrieving the golden eggs.
- **S7**：The trio steal the beans from Jack and Jill and plant them in the desert.
- **S8**：Puss and Kitty's relationship becomes romantic.
- **S9**：The trio ride the beanstalk into the clouds to find the castle of the late giant, while avoiding the Great Terror who guards the Golden Goose.
- **S10**：When they realize the golden eggs are too heavy to carry, they steal the Goose, which is just a gosling, and escape the castle.
- **S11**：While celebrating their victory, the group is ambushed by Jack and Jill, who knock Puss unconscious.
- **S12**：When Puss wakes up, he tracks Jack and Jill to his old hometown where he learns the entire heist was a plot by Humpty to lure him home to be captured, as revenge for abandoning him to the authorities when Humpty's youthful heist went bad.
- **S13**：Jack, Jill, and Kitty were involved in the con.
- **S14**：After pleas from his adoptive mother, Puss turns himself in to the guards while Humpty donates many golden eggs to the town and becomes a hero.
- **S15**：While in prison, Puss meets the original Jack from "Jack and the Beanstalk" who warns him that the Great Terror is in fact the Goose's mother, and it will stop at nothing to get its child back.
- **S16**：A repentant Kitty helps Puss break out of prison and tells him she loves him.
- **S17**：Puss tracks down Humpty, who wants the Great Terror to demolish the town.
- **S18**：Puss convinces Humpty to help him fight off the Great Terror, saying he knows Humpty is a good person at heart.
- **S19**：The Great Terror, a giant goose, arrives.
- **S20**：Using the Goose as bait, Puss and Humpty lure the Great Terror out of the town, but Humpty and the Goose are knocked off a bridge with Puss holding onto them.
- **S21**：Humpty knows Puss cannot hold both of them, so he lets go, sacrificing himself to save the Goose and the town.
- **S22**：Humpty's shell cracks open to reveal he was a golden egg on the inside.
- **S23**：The Great Terror then takes the Goose and Humpty back to the giant's castle.
- **S24**：Puss's efforts to save the town make him a hero among the townspeople.
- **S25**：In the epilogue, Jack and Jill are recovering from their injuries after being crushed by the Great Terror, Humpty is shown once again in his regular egg form, wearing a golden egg suit, as he rides the Great Terror into the clouds, and Puss and Kitty finally kiss.

## 角色资料

### Lucy Gayheart

- **性格倾向**：在与塞巴斯蒂安的交往中，她情感投入强烈，情绪易随聚散起伏。（行为推断；S13、S14、S17、S27）
- **核心动机**：她希望继续担任伴奏者，并在失意后重新争取回芝加哥工作。（摘要直接叙述；S25、S46、S47、S48）
- **价值取舍**：她拒绝哈里求婚，因为自己爱着另一个人。（摘要直接叙述；S22、S24）

### Harry Gordon

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-fd122fa747f36742` · 独立版本：`profile-2970d108d9bab1b9`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他以冷淡回避回应露西，带有报复心。（行为推断；S35、S41、S71、S72）
- **核心动机**：露西拒绝后，他为报复而仓促结婚，后来又为此后悔。（摘要直接叙述；S70）
- **价值取舍**：他把受拒的自尊置于露西的感受之上，刻意惩罚她。（行为推断；S71、S72）
- **决策方式**：他明知露西想联系自己，仍选择躲避并拉开距离。（摘要直接叙述；S71）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：仍爱露西，却故意疏远她，并把她的死亡归咎于自己。

- **S2**：[ edit ] Book I: Lucy Gayheart [ edit ] On Christmas holiday away from her piano studies in Chicago, Lucy Gayheart is ice skating in her hometown of Haverford, Nebraska.
- **S3**：Harry Gordon, the most eligible bachelor in town, joins her.
- **S4**：Later she takes the train back to Chicago - he is with her until the Omaha stop.
- **S5**：In a prolepsis , she recalls going to a performance by Clement Sebastian and later to an audition with him - she has one scheduled for her return.
- **S6**：Back in Chicago then, she goes to a concert by the same artist.
- **S7**：The next day she goes to his place for a singing practice, and meets his valet Giuseppe.
- **S8**：She will replace Sebastian's accompanist, James Mockford, whilst the latter is convalescent.
- **S9**：During these practice sessions, Clement Sebastian seems distant.
- **S10**：Once he gets a call asking for money, which must be from his wife.
- **S11**：On another occasion, he goes to Madame Renee de Vignon's funeral; later he goes into that same Catholic church again.
- **S12**：Sebastian goes off to Minnesota and Wisconsin on a tour with Mockford.
- **S13**：Lucy feels dejected.
- **S14**：However, she gets a telegram from Sebastian telling her to come to his studio the following day - this cheers her up.
- **S15**：However, when she returns to his studio, she asks him if he ever got pleasure out of being in love.
- **S16**：He says, "N-n-no, not much," then asks her "Why? -- Do you?" She replies, "Yes, I do.
- **S17**：And nobody can spoil it." This embarrasses her—she worried that it exposed her love for him and she leaves abruptly.
- **S18**：He manages to meet her again at Auerbach's (the studio where she studies and also gives lessons) and calms her fears.
- **S19**：Later, both Lucy and Sebastian are depressed; the latter takes her to dinner and tells her about Larry MacGowan, a friend from his school days who died recently.
- **S20**：The next day he tells her he loves her but is old enough to be her father so will not act on his love.
- **S21**：He says that she is not really in love with him, only growing up and "finding things".
- **S22**：Later, when Sebastian is off on an Eastern U.S. tour, Harry visits her and they go to operas and museums together.
- **S23**：Although she seems appreciative and making an effort to be nice, she finds his visit stressful.
- **S24**：She rejects his proposal for marriage, saying that she loves someone else.
- **S25**：Sebastian finally comes back briefly; Lucy is to go to New York City to be his accompanist in the winter, after he tours Europe.
- **S26**：Meanwhile, she has to rehearse, and she will take up Sebastian's apartment as her studio.
- **S27**：On his departure she cries.
- **S28**：Later she receives a letter from her sister Pauline saying Harry has married Miss Arkwright.
- **S29**：Professor Auerbach asks Lucy what she wants to do in her future—accompany other singers or marry Harry (whom he briefly met).
- **S30**：He implies that it is difficult for a female accompanist -- "For the platform they always have a man."—thus, a female accompanist would only be for rehearsals.

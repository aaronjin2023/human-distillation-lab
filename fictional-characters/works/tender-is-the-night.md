- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] The French Riviera serves as the setting for the first third of the novel.
- **S3**：Dick and Nicole Diver are a glamorous couple who rent a villa in the South of France and surround themselves with a coterie of American expatriates .
- **S4**：Rosemary Hoyt, a 17-year-old actress, and her mother are staying at a nearby resort.
- **S5**：Rosemary becomes infatuated with Dick and becomes close to Nicole.
- **S6**：Rosemary senses something is wrong with the couple, and her suspicions are confirmed when another guest at a party, Violet McKisco, reports witnessing Nicole's nervous breakdown in a bathroom.
- **S7**：Tommy Barban, another guest, comes to the defense of Nicole and insists that Violet is lying.
- **S8**：Angered by this accusation, Violet's husband Albert duels Barban on the beach, but both men miss their shots.
- **S9**：Following these events, Dick, Nicole, Rosemary, and others depart the French Riviera.
- **S10**：Soon after, Rosemary is now a constant companion of both Dick and Nicole in Paris.
- **S11**：She attempts to seduce Dick in her hotel room, but he rebuffs her advances, although he admits that he loves her.
- **S12**：Much later, a black man named Jules Peterson is found murdered in Rosemary's bed at the hotel, a potential scandal that could destroy Rosemary's career.
- **S13**：Dick moves the blood-soaked body out of the room to cover up any implied sexual relationship between Rosemary and Peterson.
- **S14**：A flashback occurs in the narrative.
- **S15**：In Spring 1917, Dick Diver—a promising young doctor—visits psychopathologist Franz Gregorovius in Zurich , Switzerland .
- **S16**：While visiting Franz, he meets a patient named Nicole Warren, a wealthy young woman whose sexual abuse by her father has led to mental neuroses . [ c ] Over a period of time they exchange letters.
- **S17**：With the permission of Franz who believes that Dick's friendship benefits Nicole's well-being, they start seeing each other.
- **S18**：As Nicole's treatment progresses, she becomes infatuated with Dick who, in turn, develops Florence Nightingale syndrome .

### Marty McFly

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-43f036d94f2532a3` · 独立版本：`profile-6f17ba83f5365590`

- **性格倾向**：面对突发危机，他倾向主动介入并临场寻找补救办法（行为推断；S13、S16、S20、S21）

### Emmett Brown

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-71e8f596d6d61ef8` · 独立版本：`profile-79d94b724c84b315`

- **性格倾向**：面对极端技术难题，他倾向大胆构想并动手解决（行为推断；S8、S17、S18、S27）
- **核心动机**：确认马蒂来自未来后，他积极帮助马蒂返回原来年代（摘要直接叙述；S16、S18、S27）
- **价值取舍**：起初面对未来警告，他把避免改变历史置于知情之上（摘要直接叙述；S26）
- **决策方式**：面对能源限制，他利用已知雷击时间制定精确行动方案（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **成长变化**：面对自身死亡的警告，他最终放松禁忌并采取防护（行为推断；S26、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：害怕改变未来而撕毁警告，最终仍阅读并据此自救。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Seventeen-year-old Marty McFly lives with his bleak, unambitious family in Hill Valley, California.
- **S2**：His father, George McFly, is bullied by his supervisor, Biff Tannen, and his unhappy mother, Lorraine Baines McFly, is an alcoholic.
- **S3**：Marty's underachieving older siblings, Dave and Linda, also live in the household.
- **S4**：When Marty and his band audition to perform at the high school dance, they are rejected.
- **S5**：Despite this setback, Marty's girlfriend, Jennifer, encourages him to pursue the dream of being a rock musician.
- **S6**：At dinner that night, Lorraine recounts how she and George first fell in love when her father hit George with his car.
- **S7**：Marty meets his friend, scientist Dr.
- **S8**：Emmett "Doc" Brown, late at night in the parking lot of a deserted shopping mall where Doc reveals a time machine made from a modified 1981 DeLorean DMC-12; the vehicle's time displacement is powered by plutonium, which supplies the 1.21 gigawatts of power to a device he calls the "flux capacitor." Doc explains that the car travels to a programmed date upon reaching 88 miles per hour, using the date November 5, 1955, as an example destination.
- **S9**：Before Doc can make his first trip, the Libyan terrorists from whom he stole the plutonium shoot him.
- **S10**：Marty attempts to escape in the DeLorean and inadvertently activates the time machine.
- **S11**：He is transported back to November 5, 1955, and finds himself without the plutonium needed for the return trip.
- **S12**：While exploring Hill Valley, Marty meets his teenaged father, who is being bullied by Biff.
- **S13**：As George is about to be hit by Lorraine's father's car, Marty pushes him out of the way and is knocked out by the impact.
- **S14**：Consequently, a teenaged Lorraine becomes infatuated with Marty instead of George.
- **S15**：Marty is disturbed by her flirtations and leaves to find the younger Doc of 1955.
- **S16**：Marty convinces Doc that he is from the future, and asks for help returning to 1985.
- **S17**：Doc explains that the only available power source capable of generating 1.21 gigawatts of energy is a bolt of lightning.
- **S18**：Discovering the "Save the Clock Tower" flyer that Marty received in 1985, indicating that lightning will strike the courthouse clock tower the following Saturday at 10:04 pm, Doc makes plans to harness the lightning strike to power the DeLorean's flux capacitor.
- **S19**：When they observe a fading photograph of Marty with his siblings, they realize Marty has prevented his parents from meeting, jeopardizing his family's existence.
- **S20**：Marty attempts to set George up with Lorraine.
- **S21**：To make his parents fall in love, Marty plans to have George "rescue" Lorraine from Marty's inappropriate advances on the night of the school dance.
- **S22**：A drunk Biff unexpectedly shows up, pulls Marty from the car, and attempts to force himself on Lorraine.
- **S23**：George arrives to rescue her from Marty, but instead finds Biff, who humiliates George and pushes Lorraine to the ground.
- **S24**：Standing up to him for the first time, George knocks Biff out.
- **S25**：A smitten Lorraine follows George to the dance floor, where they kiss for the first time, ensuring Marty's existence.
- **S26**：Marty arrives at the clock tower where Doc is making final preparations for the lightning strike, and tries to warn Doc of his impending 1985 murder in a letter, but Doc tears it up, fearing it will lead to altering the future.
- **S27**：A falling tree branch disconnects Doc's wiring setup, but Doc repairs the connections in time to send Marty and the DeLorean back to 1985.
- **S28**：Although Marty arrives too late to prevent him from being shot, Doc is still alive and admits to reading the letter anyway and wearing a bulletproof vest.
- **S29**：Doc drops Marty off at home and uses the time machine to travel 30 years into the future.
- **S30**：Marty awakens the next morning to find his family changed; Lorraine is happy and physically fit, a self-confident George is a successful science fiction author, Dave is a businessman, and Linda no longer has trouble finding boyfriends.
- **S31**：George and Lorraine now have a closer relationship than ever, while Biff has become an auto detailer/washer who is on good terms with the McFly family.
- **S32**：As Marty reunites with Jennifer, Doc arrives, insisting they accompany him to the future to sort out a problem with their future children.
- **S33**：Marty and Jennifer enter the upgraded DeLorean, now a hovercar powered by nuclear fusion, and Doc flies the time machine into the future.

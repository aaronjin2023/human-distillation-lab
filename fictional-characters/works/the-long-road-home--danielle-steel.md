### Gabriella Harrison

- **性格倾向**：在反复失落和伤害之后，她仍表现出重建生活并关切弱者的倾向。（行为推断；S14、S16、S17、S32）
- **核心动机**：她希望弄清父母为何遗弃自己，以面对长期未解的自我疑问。（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29）
- **价值取舍**：面对母亲伤害孩子的场面，她表现出保护受害者的倾向。（行为推断；S17）
- **决策方式**：确认Steve的欺骗与伤害后，她停止给钱并明确拒绝勒索。（摘要直接叙述；S22、S23、S24、S25）
- **关系模式**：她逐步接受寄宿者和医生的关爱，重新建立家庭之外的支持。（行为推断；S16、S27、S32）
- **成长变化**：她最终认识到父母不爱自己并非自己的错，开始放下过去。（摘要直接叙述；S32）

### Eloise

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-50e84b3451263288` · 独立版本：`profile-ace9932e10f9f313`

- **性格倾向**：在家庭关系中，她表现出以惩罚和指责支配亲近者的倾向。（行为推断；S2、S6、S7、S31）
- **核心动机**：她把女儿送往修道院，是为了摆脱自己眼中的重大失望。（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **价值取舍**：她把暴力包装成管教，将女儿被认定的过错用作伤害理由。（行为推断；S2、S3、S7）
- **沟通方式**：丈夫离开后，她以责怪女儿的说法解释家庭破裂与缺乏关爱。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S7）
- **关系模式**：她对亲近者的伤害延续到后来的婚姻，持续辱骂新丈夫。（摘要直接叙述；S31）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：声称暴力是在管教女儿，却又将女儿遗弃在修道院。

## 材料限制

- 材料以Gabriella受虐与重建生活为中心，Eloise缺少独立内心视角。
- 结尾关于母亲后期婚姻的描述来自转述，不能扩展为未记载的家庭细节。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Gabriella Harrison, a child of the fifties, suffers abuse from the hands of her mother, Eloise, who explains her abuse as disciplining Gabriella for being so bad.
- **S3**：Frequent beatings mar her life.
- **S4**：Her father, afraid to annoy her mother, plays a spectator to all the evil happenings.
- **S5**：Before Gabbie turns thirteen her father, tired of his wife's constant abuse towards their daughter, leaves.
- **S6**：Her mother blames Gabbie for her father's departure.
- **S7**：She says it's only because of her badness that her parents hate her.
- **S8**：Gabbie is left at a nunnery to finish her education, so her mother can abandon the biggest disappointment in her life.
- **S9**：Gabriella decides she wants to become a nun.
- **S10**：In the course of being a postulant , she falls in love with a priest, Father Joe Connors.
- **S11**：They want to move into the real world to live their life loving each other, but the priest is not confident.
- **S12**：Gabbie is pregnant.
- **S13**：The priest commits suicide, for he cannot break the promise made to the brotherhood of serving the needed, and he cannot live without Gabriella.
- **S14**：Gabriella thus loses her love, and then their child in a miscarriage.
- **S15**：Ultimately she is compelled to leave the convent for the sin committed.
- **S16**：Cast out into society, she is determined to move on and finds an apartment where the tenants welcome her lovingly, particularly an old professor.
- **S17**：Everything seems to be going well; Gabbie has a job at a pastry shop, but loses it when she defends a child whose mother dislocates her arm in a fit of impatience.
- **S18**：Eventually she finds a job at a book store while buying a Christmas present for her doting friend, the old professor.
- **S19**：Gabbie meets a new tenant named Steve in the boarding house, and eventually falls for him.
- **S20**：She has a long relationship with him.
- **S21**：He does not have a job and is constantly after Gabbie to give him money.
- **S22**：She obliges, until the day he steals from her.
- **S23**：He turns out to be a con artist, wanted by the police for stealing money from several people.
- **S24**：When she learns this, he demands that she give him most of the money the old professor leaves her when he dies.
- **S25**：She refuses to give him any money after learning his true face and that he was responsible for the professor's death.
- **S26**：Steve beats her up, nearly killing her.
- **S27**：Gabbie is taken to a hospital, where she becomes friends with a Doctor Peter presiding over her.
- **S28**：Before moving on with her life with Peter, Gabriella decides to meet her parents and ask them the long avoided question - why they abandoned her and never loved her.
- **S29**：Gabbie visits her father, hoping to get some answers about why he allowed her mother to treat her so horribly.
- **S30**：Her father says only that he was weak.
- **S31**：Gabbie visits her stepfather and his new wife, where she learns that her mother never changed her bitter ways until the end, and verbally abused her new husband until she died of cancer.
- **S32**：At last, after realizing that it was not her fault that her parents never loved her, Gabbie lets go of the past and moves on with her life with her new love - the doctor by her side.

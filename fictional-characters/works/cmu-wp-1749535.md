## 角色资料

### Cameron Thayer

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a9461b124255462a` · 独立版本：`profile-c8e3c7ea36af2b4e`

- **性格倾向**：受权势压迫时起初倾向克制退让，积累羞辱后则激烈反抗（行为推断；S19、S43、S49、S53）
- **核心动机**：在接连遭受压迫后，他迫切希望摆脱任人摆布的处境（摘要直接叙述；S49、S53）
- **价值取舍**：他对黑人被贬低或伤害黑人同胞的行为表现出尊严诉求（行为推断；S42、S43、S50、S56）
- **决策方式**：他先以顺从避免冲突，后来在情绪极限下冒险对抗武装威胁（行为推断；S18、S19、S43、S49、S53）
- **关系模式**：他将沉默视为妥当应对，却因妻子期待保护而陷入关系冲突（行为推断；S19、S21、S22）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以退让保全现实处境，却因此承受尊严与婚姻关系的损伤

### Officer John Ryan

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-96b52be18b616a84` · 独立版本：`profile-3a6884fbaad27c7f`

- **性格倾向**：被冒犯时倾向借权力压制他人，但危急时也能主动承担救援（行为推断；S18、S19、S44、S46）
- **核心动机**：担忧父亲遭到误诊，使他主动争取更换医生并尝试修复交涉（摘要直接叙述；S32、S33、S34）
- **价值取舍**：他的执法行为同时呈现侵犯尊严与冒险救命的矛盾取舍（行为推断；S19、S44、S45、S46）
- **决策方式**：面对伤者抗拒，他在此次救援中先安抚，再与旁人协作救人（摘要直接叙述；S45、S46）
- **关系模式**：他曾施害的对象后来必须依靠其救援，关系夹杂恐惧与感激（摘要直接叙述；S19、S45、S46、S47）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾滥用警权侵犯克里斯汀，又在车祸中冒险救她

### Anthony

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-3a17558e60ac0cdd` · 独立版本：`profile-664078b9adfc66b5`

- **性格倾向**：在犯罪情境中仍呈现犹疑与怜悯，未始终顺从同伴的暴力要求（行为推断；S27、S50、S75）
- **核心动机**：发现被囚移民后，他转向解除他们的困境而非继续从中获利（行为推断；S73、S74、S75、S76）
- **价值取舍**：在可出售被囚者获利的关头，他选择释放他们并给予食物钱（行为推断；S74、S75、S76）
- **决策方式**：面对同伴催促杀人或买家开价，他仍能自行决定停止伤害（行为推断；S50、S74、S75）
- **成长变化**：叙事从他劫车伤人展开，后来则通过拒绝杀人与释放移民转向（行为推断；S9、S25、S27、S50、S75）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：靠侵犯他人获取财物，却拒绝把被囚者继续当作商品

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Plot}} Los Angeles detectives Graham Waters  and his partner Ria  approach a crime scene investigation.
- **S2**：Ria and Kim Lee, an Asian woman in another car, get into an argument about right-of-way and soon are exchanging racial insults.
- **S3**：Waters exits the car to check out the scene.
- **S4**：One day prior, Farhad , a Persian shop owner, and his daughter, Dorri , argue with each other in front of a gun store owner as Farhad tries to buy a revolver.
- **S5**：The shop keeper grows impatient and orders an infuriated Farhad outside while making a racist comment.
- **S6**：Dorri defiantly finishes the gun purchase, which she had opposed.
- **S7**：The purchase entitles the buyer to one box of ammunition.
- **S8**：She selects a red box.
- **S9**：In another part of town, Rick Cabot , the local district attorney, and his wife, Jean , are carjacked as they are about to enter their Lincoln Navigator, by Anthony  and Peter .
- **S10**：Later, at the Cabot house, Hispanic locksmith Daniel Ruiz  is changing their locks when he overhears Jean complaining about having been carjacked earlier by two black men and now having to endure a heavily tattooed Hispanic with a shaved head changing their locks, because she is sure he is going to leave and give copies of the new keys to "his other gang members." Detectives Waters and Ria next arrive at the scene of a shooting between two drivers.
- **S11**：The surviving shooter is a white male, identified as an undercover police officer.
- **S12**：The dead shooter, a black male, is revealed also to be an undercover police officer.
- **S13**：There is a large amount of cash found in the black officer's trunk.
- **S14**：This is the third time the white officer has shot and killed a black man.
- **S15**：LAPD officer John Ryan  and his partner, Tom Hanson , begin their evening patrol.
- **S16**：They pull over a Navigator similar to the one carjacked earlier, despite discrepancies in the descriptions of the carjackers and in the license plate numbers.
- **S17**：They order the couple, TV director Cameron Thayer  and his wife Christine , to exit.
- **S18**：Cameron is cooperative, but Christine has had a few drinks and is argumentative.
- **S19**：This annoys Ryan, who sexually molests Christine under the pretense of administering a pat-down; intimidated, Cameron says nothing.
- **S20**：The couple is released without a citation.
- **S21**：Once home, Christine becomes enraged that Cameron did nothing while she was being violated.
- **S22**：Cameron insists that what he did was correct and storms out.
- **S23**：Arriving home from work long after dark, Daniel finds his young daughter, Lara, hiding under her bed after hearing a gunshot outside.
- **S24**：To comfort her, Daniel gives her an "invisible impenetrable cloak," which makes her feel safe enough to fall asleep in her bed.
- **S25**：In the carjacked SUV, Anthony and Peter, arguing and distracted, hit something while passing a parked white van.
- **S26**：Stopping, they discover that they have run over an Asian man, now trapped under the SUV.
- **S27**：They argue about what to do with him, finally dumping him in front of a hospital and driving away.
- **S28**：The next day, at the LAPD station, Hanson talks to his superior, Lt.
- **S29**：Dixon , about switching partners.
- **S30**：Dixon, a black man, claims that Hanson's charge of Ryan as a racist could cost both Hanson and Dixon their jobs.
- **S31**：Dixon suggests a transfer to a one-man car and mockingly tells Hanson that he should justify it by claiming to have uncontrollable flatulence.
- **S32**：Ryan visits Shaniqua Johnson , a "managed care" insurance representative with whom he argued earlier.
- **S33**：Apologizing for insulting her previously, he explains that his father was previously diagnosed with a bladder infection but he fears the diagnosis is incorrect and that it may be prostate cancer.
- **S34**：Ryan wants him to see a different doctor, but Shaniqua icily informs him that the health plan won't cover it.
- **S35**：Daniel is seen replacing a lock at Farhad's shop and tries to explain to him that the door frame is shattered and not secure and needs to be replaced.
- **S36**：Farhad, whose English is limited, misunderstands and accuses Daniel of cheating him and refuses to pay.
- **S37**：The next morning, Farhad discovers the store has been looted, wrecked and tagged with graffiti.
- **S38**：His insurance company does not cover the damage, calling it a case of negligence, as he had been advised to replace the door and did not.
- **S39**：Farhad looks for and finds Daniel's full name on the discarded invoice and vows revenge.
- **S40**：Detective Waters visits his mother, a some-time hard drug abuser.
- **S41**：She asks him to find his missing younger brother; he promises and takes notice that there is almost no food in the apartment as he is leaving.
- **S42**：In the studio where Cameron works, a white producer, Fred , suggests that a black actor isn't acting "black" enough, as he was using proper grammar.
- **S43**：Cameron had been satisfied with the take just completed, but Fred strongly suggests that another take be done, with the black actor speaking more "black." Cameron initially pushes back but, threatened with his job, he concedes.
- **S44**：Ryan comes across a car accident and as he crawls into the overturned vehicle, he finds Christine, who is trapped.
- **S45**：Upon recognizing Ryan, Christine becomes hysterical, screaming for him to leave her alone and refusing his help, but gasoline is leaking from the tank and running downhill towards another wreck, which has already caught fire.
- **S46**：He calms her down, and with the assistance of his partner and spectators, Ryan pulls Christine out just as her car bursts into flames.
- **S47**：A confused but grateful Christine is taken away by EMTs.
- **S48**：Anthony and Peter attempt to carjack Cameron.
- **S49**：Cameron has reached his limit of being pushed around, and he resists the attempt.
- **S50**：Peter asks Anthony to shoot Cameron even though he had earlier claimed that he would never rob another black man but Anthony does not.
- **S51**：As police officers arrive, Cameron jumps back in the car and drives away, with Anthony continuing to hold a gun on him.
- **S52**：A car chase ensues; one of the police responders to the chase is Tom Hanson, who recognizes the vehicle as the one he and Ryan pulled over the night before.
- **S53**：Cameron drives to a dead end, grabs Anthony's gun, and gets out of the car, all the while yelling insults at the officers.
- **S54**：Just before he pulls out the gun, Hanson convinces him to stop aggravating the situation and just go home.
- **S55**：Hanson vouches for Cameron to the other officers, telling them that Cameron has no prior history of breaking the law and promises to give him a "harsh warning." All depart.
- **S56**：Cameron tells Anthony that as a black man he is embarrassed for him and gives back the gun as he drops Anthony at a bus stop.
- **S57**：Using the White Pages of the phone book, Farhad locates Daniel's home address and travels there with his gun.
- **S58**：As Daniel's wife Elizabeth  watches in horror, Farhad shoots at Daniel at point-blank range -- just as Daniel's daughter Lara jumps into his arms to protect her father with the "invisible cloak." It takes the grief-stricken parents a moment to realize that Lara is miraculously unharmed; the red box of ammunition that Dorri had selected contained blanks, rendering Farhad's gun harmless.
- **S59**：Farhad later tells his daughter that he believes that the little girl was his angel, saving him from committing a terrible crime.
- **S60**：Peter, who is hitchhiking, is picked up by Hanson.
- **S61**：They awkwardly try to bridge the gap between their cultures.
- **S62**：Peter sees that Hanson has a small statuette of Saint Christopher, which he also has.
- **S63**：He begins to laugh as he realizes that there is no difference between the two of them, but Hanson thinks that he is being racist.

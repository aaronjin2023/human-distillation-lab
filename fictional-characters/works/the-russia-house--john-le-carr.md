## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] In 1987, Bartholomew "Barley" Scott Blair, a British publisher , is at a book fair in Moscow .
- **S3**：With business friends he goes on a drunken retreat to a secluded dacha in the forest near Peredelkino .
- **S4**：When their talk turns to politics, Barley finds himself talking boldly of patriotism and courage, of a new world order, and an end to Cold War tensions.
- **S5**：One attentive listener, "Goethe", asks him privately whether he truly believes in the possibility of such a world.
- **S6**：Barley convincingly says that he does.
- **S7**：Several months later in Moscow, a woman named Katya seeks Barley out at an audio fair, hoping to convince him to publish a manuscript for her friend Yakov which details Soviet nuclear capabilities and atomic secrets.
- **S8**：The manuscript has a cover letter to Barley, saying that Yakov is trying to serve his country by hastening the day when democracy will come to the Soviet Union .
- **S9**：However, Barley is in Lisbon so Katya gives the package to Niki Landau to forward it to Barley.
- **S10**：When Landau is unable to locate Barley, the manuscript is sent to the Secret Intelligence Service (MI6).
- **S11**：Their Russia House is more than interested in it and ask Barley to contact Yakov with a list of verifying questions to determine the document's authenticity.
- **S12**：Barley is content to stay out of the matter, but he is manipulated into undertaking the mission.
- **S13**：He grows fond of Katya and begins thinking of a way to get her out of the Soviet Union.
- **S14**：Over the course of several meetings with Katya and Yakov, Barley realises his nervous informant is very likely under KGB scrutiny.
- **S15**：The CIA and MI6 decide one final meeting is needed to verify the authenticity of the data, but Yakov is suddenly "hospitalized" due to purported exhaustion.
- **S16**：In a secure phone call, Yakov tells Katya through code that he has been taken and that she is in danger.
- **S17**：Barley and Katya realise that any further meeting is merely a KGB scheme to draw them out into the open.
- **S18**：Barley receives a message that he must bring "a final and exhaustive" list of questions on Soviet research.
- **S19**：He makes contact with one of his Soviet publishing associates who uses his connections in the KGB to arrange a meeting with Yakov's handlers.
- **S20**：Although the CIA and MI6 set up a major surveillance operation at the meeting site, Barley goes missing along with the last set of questions, presumably arrested.
- **S21**：More than a year later, after several unconfirmed sightings in Moscow, Barley shows up in Portugal, offering no explanation for his absence.
- **S22**：Neither the CIA nor MI6 are inclined to interrogate him, reasoning that the KGB has already worn him down to get the information they needed.
- **S23**：The truth, however, is that Barley traded the questions for the freedom of Katya and her family.
- **S24**：The philosophical Barley reasons that governments are not the only ones who can manipulate and betray, and some things are more important than the games that spies play with others' lives.

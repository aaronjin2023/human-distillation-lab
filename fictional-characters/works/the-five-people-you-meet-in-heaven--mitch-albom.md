## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] On his 83rd birthday, amusement park ride mechanic Eddie is killed in an accident when a ride breaks down.
- **S3**：During the accident, he makes a desperate attempt to save the life of a little girl named Annie.
- **S4**：Eddie arrives in Heaven, where he meets "the Blue Man." The Blue Man explains that Eddie is about to journey through Heaven's five levels, meeting someone who has had a significant impact upon his life or someone on whom his life had a significant impact.
- **S5**：Eddie asks why the Blue Man is his first person, and he informs Eddie that, when Eddie was very young, he caused the car accident that killed him.
- **S6**：From this, Eddie learns his first lesson: there are no random events in life and all individuals and experiences are connected in some way.
- **S7**：Eddie meets his former captain from the army, who reminds Eddie of their time together as prisoners of war in a forced labor camp in the Philippines .
- **S8**：Their group burned the camp during their escape and Eddie, while running away, remembers seeing a shadow move in one of the huts.
- **S9**：The Captain confesses that he shot Eddie in the leg to prevent Eddie from chasing the shadow into the fire.
- **S10**：This saved Eddie's life despite leaving him with a lifelong severe limp.
- **S11**：Eddie then learns how the Captain died: he stepped on a land mine that would have killed all the men had he not set it off.
- **S12**：Eddie finds himself outside a diner , where he sees his father through a window.
- **S13**：A well-dressed woman named Ruby appears and introduces herself to him.
- **S14**：Ruby explains that Ruby Pier was named after her by her husband Emile, who built it in tribute to her.
- **S15**：Ruby shows Eddie the true cause of his father's death, which is different from what he had always believed.
- **S16**：She tells Eddie that he needs to forgive his father.
- **S17**：Eddie meets his late wife, Marguerite.
- **S18**：They remember their wedding, and Marguerite teaches Eddie that love is never lost in death; it just moves on and takes a different form.
- **S19**：Eddie awakens to see children playing along with a riverbed.
- **S20**：A young Filipina girl named Tala comes up to him.
- **S21**：Tala reveals that she was the little girl from the hut that Eddie set on fire.
- **S22**：Distraught, Eddie breaks down both cursing and asking God "why?" Tala hands him a stone and asks him to "wash" her like the other children in the river are doing to one another.
- **S23**：Eddie is puzzled, but dips the stone in the water and starts to scrape off the injuries he had inflicted on her.
- **S24**：Tala's wounds begin to clear until she is freed of all the scars.
- **S25**：Eddie asks Tala if she knows if he was able to save the little girl before his death.
- **S26**：Tala tells him he did manage to push her out of the way.
- **S27**：In this way, Tala explains, he also managed to atone every day for her unnecessary death.
- **S28**：In the end, it shows that Eddie's Heaven is the Stardust Band Shell, where he met Marguerite.
- **S29**：Later, the Freddie Free Fall's name was changed and it becomes a badge of courage which attracts many tourists to try.

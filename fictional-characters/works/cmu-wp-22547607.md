## 角色资料

### Sam Flynn

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-5f92cb8dab229987` · 独立版本：`profile-2851722662ebfebb`

- **性格倾向**：在寻找出路时倾向自行冒险，不轻易接受父亲的行动限制（行为推断；S12、S20）
- **核心动机**：进入虚拟世界后致力与父亲团聚，并寻找返回现实的途径（行为推断；S2、S5、S12、S20、S23）
- **关系模式**：对父亲的牵挂促使他追查消息，重逢后仍保持行动上的独立（行为推断；S2、S5、S12）
- **成长变化**：从疏离公司事务转为主动安排管理层，开始承担继承者责任（行为推断；S2、S24）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：希望与父亲共同脱困，却因违背父亲意愿冒险而引发新的危机。

### Kevin Flynn

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-ae4923c44852cd4b` · 独立版本：`profile-1a6e82e89a75e2fd`

- **性格倾向**：在亲人受威胁时倾向亲自介入，愿意承担保护他人的代价（行为推断；S14、S22）
- **核心动机**：最初以建成完美计算机系统为目标，组织同伴共同参与创造（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **价值取舍**：在最终对峙中把阻止副本与保障他人离开置于自身存续之前（行为推断；S22、S23）
- **关系模式**：对儿子与葵拉承担照护责任，通过救援和修复维系保护关系（行为推断；S14、S16）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：创造完美系统的理想，产生了迫害自然生命并囚禁自己的副本。

### CLU

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-91ae8b9fded4f543` · 独立版本：`profile-d3fc78bbab3f525d`

- **性格倾向**：面对自然生成的生命时呈现僵硬排异倾向，无法容纳系统异质性（行为推断；S6、S7、S8）
- **核心动机**：意图取得弗林的身份盘，进入人类世界并强加自己的系统（摘要直接叙述；S11、S19）
- **价值取舍**：把自身定义的系统秩序置于生命多样性与程序自主之上（行为推断；S8、S18）
- **决策方式**：受困于虚拟世界时倾向设置诱饵，利用父子关系突破限制（行为推断；S10、S11）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：虽已掌控系统，却仍需诱使山姆进入才能重新开启离开的门户。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1989, Kevin Flynn , software engineer and the CEO of ENCOM International, disappears.
- **S2**：Twenty years later, his son, Sam , now ENCOM's primary shareholder, takes little interest in the company beyond playing an annual trick on the board of directors; but is requested by his father's friend, ENCOM executive Alan Bradley , to investigate a message originating from Flynn's shuttered Video arcade.
- **S3**：There, Sam discovers a hidden basement in which Sam unintentionally teleports himself to the Grid, a virtual reality created by his father.
- **S4**：On the Grid, Sam is sent to compete against a masked program called Rinzler who, having realized that Sam is a human User after seeing him bleed, takes him before CLU, an alter ego of Kevin Flynn who rules the Grid.
- **S5**：CLU nearly kills Sam in a Light Cycle match; but the latter is rescued by Quorra , an "apprentice" of Flynn's, who conveys him to his father outside CLU's territory.
- **S6**：There, Flynn reveals to Sam that he had been working to create a "perfect" computer system and had appointed CLU and Tron  its co-creators.
- **S7**：During this construction, the trio discover a species of naturally-occurring "isomorphic algorithms" , not conceived by Flynn, bearing the potential to resolve various mysteries in science, religion, and medicine.
- **S8**：CLU, having deemed them an aberration, betrayed Flynn, captured Tron, and destroyed the ISOs.
- **S9**：Meanwhile, the "I/O portal" permitting travel between the two worlds had closed, leaving Flynn captive.
- **S10**：Now in control of the system but stalemated, CLU arranged the message sent to Alan, in order to lure Sam onto the Grid, to open the portal for a limited time.
- **S11**：Additionally, as Flynn's 'identity disc' is the master key to the Grid and only way to go through the portal, CLU expects Sam to bring Flynn to the portal so that he may take Flynn's disc and go through the portal himself to impose his system on the human world.
- **S12**：Against his father's wishes, Sam returns to CLU's territory to find Zuse , a program who can provide safe passage to the I/O portal.
- **S13**：At the End of Line Club, its owner Castor reveals himself to be Zuse, then betrays Sam to CLU's guards.
- **S14**：In the resulting fight, Flynn rescues his son, Quorra is injured, and Zuse gains possession of Flynn's disc.
- **S15**：Zuse attempts to bargain with CLU for the disc, but CLU simply takes the disc and destroys the club.
- **S16**：Flynn and Sam stow away aboard a "solar sailer" transport program, where Flynn restores Quorra and reveals her to be the last surviving ISO.
- **S17**：Quorra relates to Sam her history with Flynn and expresses her desire to see a sunrise.
- **S18**：Shortly thereafter, the transport stops inside a large warship where Flynn, Sam, and Quorra discover that the transport contains inactive programs, scheduled for rectification  to serve CLU and follow him to the real world.
- **S19**：Aboard the warship, Quorra is captured and Flynn recognizes Rinzler  as Tron, reprogrammed by CLU, while CLU announces his intention to invade the material world.
- **S20**：Sam then reclaims Flynn's disc and rescues Quorra, whereupon CLU, Rinzler and several guards pursue the protagonists in Light Jets.
- **S21**：Upon making eye contact with Flynn, Rinzler remembers his past and collides with CLU's Light Jet; but CLU uses Tron's spare baton to escape while Tron falls into the Sea of Simulation, where the colored lights on his armor change from CLU's orange to Tron's original blue.
- **S22**：CLU confronts the protagonists at the I/O portal, where Flynn reintegrates with his cybernetic duplicate, destroying them both.
- **S23**：Quorra, having traded discs with Flynn, gives Flynn's disc to Sam and they escape to the real world.
- **S24**：In Flynn's arcade, Sam backs up the system, and having deactivated it, asks a waiting Alan to take control of ENCOM, naming him chairman of the board.
- **S25**：Quorra meets Sam outside, and they depart on his motorcycle.
- **S26**：From the rear seat, Quorra witnesses her first sunrise.

### Hannibal Lecter

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-953d8218fbb73f12` · 独立版本：`profile-415af158e14ce46e`

- **性格倾向**：在受到追捕时倾向冷静设局，并以残酷手段反制威胁者（行为推断；S16、S17、S23、S25、S27）
- **决策方式**：面对即将被捕的困局，愿承受严重自伤以立即取得逃脱机会（行为推断；S29）
- **关系模式**：对Starling既有诱引与控制，也在她受伤时实施救助照料（行为推断；S5、S19、S22、S24、S26）
- **沟通方式**：倾向以挑衅书信和劝说影响他人，把交流转化为行动筹码（行为推断；S5、S23）

### Clarice Starling

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-e3721ec27e53c799` · 独立版本：`profile-9eb5d93d0ff10258`

- **性格倾向**：在遭受停职与上级阻拦时仍倾向主动担当，坚持采取行动（行为推断；S18、S20、S28、S29）
- **核心动机**：围绕Lecter的踪迹持续调查，遭其救助后仍试图将他控制（摘要直接叙述；S6、S7、S28、S29）
- **价值取舍**：面对私刑威胁仍选择救人，不把嫌犯交给私人复仇处置（行为推断；S20、S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：倾向从细微感官线索寻找突破，并借跨地协查推进追踪（行为推断；S6、S7）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：为阻止私刑营救Lecter，却没有因此放弃拘捕他的职责。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Seven years after tracking down serial killer Jame Gumb, FBI Special Agent Clarice Starling  is unjustly blamed for a bungled drug raid.
- **S2**：Starling and her connection to Hannibal Lecter  come to the attention of Lecter's only surviving victim, Mason Verger , a wealthy child molester whom Lecter left horribly disfigured and paralyzed after having been assigned as Verger's court-appointed therapist.
- **S3**：Verger uses his immense wealth and political influence to have Starling reassigned to Lecter's case and meets with her in his mansion.
- **S4**：Verger is pursuing an elaborate scheme to capture, torture and kill Lecter, and hopes Starling's involvement will draw him out.
- **S5**：Indeed, Lecter sends her a taunting letter after learning of her public disgrace.
- **S6**：Though the letter contains no clue to Lecter's whereabouts, Starling detects a strange fragrance that a perfume expert later identifies as a bespoke skin cream whose ingredients are only available to a few shops in the world.
- **S7**：She contacts the police departments of the cities where the shops are located, requesting surveillance tapes.
- **S8**：One of the cities is Florence, Italy, where Chief Inspector Rinaldo Pazzi  is investigating the disappearance of a library curator.
- **S9**：Pazzi questions Lecter, who is masquerading as "Dr.
- **S10**：Fell", the assistant curator and now caretaker of the library.
- **S11**：Upon recognizing Dr.
- **S12**：Fell in the surveillance tape, Pazzi accesses the FBI's ViCAP database of wanted fugitives.
- **S13**：He learns of Verger's US$3 million reward to anyone turning Lecter over to him rather than to the FBI.
- **S14**：Lured by Verger's bounty, Pazzi ignores Starling's warnings against trying to capture Lecter alone.
- **S15**：He recruits a pickpocket to obtain a fingerprint of Lecter to show as proof of his whereabouts and thus collect the reward.
- **S16**：The pickpocket is mortally wounded by Lecter, but manages to get the print and provides them to Pazzi, who in turn contacts Verger.
- **S17**：Lecter then baits Pazzi into an isolated room of the library, ties him up with electrical cords, and hangs and disembowels him, before escaping back to the United States.
- **S18**：Verger bribes Justice Department official Paul Krendler  to accuse Starling of withholding a note from Lecter, leading to her suspension.
- **S19**：Lecter lures Starling to Union Station but Verger's men, who have followed Starling, capture Lecter and transport him to Verger.
- **S20**：When her superiors refuse to act, Starling, on her own initiative, infiltrates Verger's estate.
- **S21**：Verger means to have Lecter eaten alive by a herd of wild boars bred specifically for this purpose.
- **S22**：Starling intervenes to free Lecter but is herself wounded, and Lecter rescues her from the voracious animals.
- **S23**：Verger furiously orders his private physician Cordell  to shoot Lecter, but instead Lecter persuades Cordell to throw his hated employer into the pen, where he is eaten alive by the boars.
- **S24**：Lecter takes a sedated Starling to Krendler's secluded lake house and treats her wounds.
- **S25**：When Krendler arrives for the Fourth of July break, he is subdued and drugged by Lecter.
- **S26**：Starling, disoriented by morphine and dressed in a slinky black velvet cocktail dress, wakes to find Lecter cooking and Krendler in a wheelchair seated at the table set for an elegant dinner.
- **S27**：Weakened by the drugs, she looks on in confusion and horror as Lecter removes the top of Krendler's skull, cuts out part of his prefrontal cortex, sautees it and feeds Krendler his own brain before finally killing him.
- **S28**：After the meal, Starling tries to attack Lecter but is quickly overpowered.
- **S29**：She manages to handcuff his wrist to hers, and with police incoming to the residence, Lecter brandishes a meat cleaver and severs his left hand to escape.
- **S30**：Lecter is later seen on a flight with boxed lunch on his pull-down table.
- **S31**：As he prepares to eat his meal, including a small cooked portion of what is assumed to be Krendler's brain, a young boy seated next to him asks to try some of his food.
- **S32**：Lecter lets the boy eat some of his lunch, telling him that "it is important... always to try new things."

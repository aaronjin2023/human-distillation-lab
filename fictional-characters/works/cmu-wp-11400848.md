## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Tintin , a young journalist, and his dog Snowy are browsing in an outdoor market in a European town.
- **S2**：Tintin buys a model of a three-masted sailing ship, the Unicorn, but is then immediately accosted by the sinister Ivan Ivanovitch Sakharine , and the mysterious figure of Barnaby , who both try to buy the model from Tintin, without success.
- **S3**：Tintin takes the ship home, but it is broken during a fight between Snowy and a neighbour's cat.
- **S4**：As it breaks, a parchment scroll slips out of the ship's mast.
- **S5**：Snowy spots it but is unable to alert Tintin.
- **S6**：Meanwhile, incompetent detectives Thomson and Thompson  are on the trail of a pickpocket, Aristides Silk .
- **S7**：Tintin visits Sakharine in Marlinspike Hall, where he learns that there are two model ships.
- **S8**：Tintin puts the scroll in his wallet, only to have his wallet stolen by Silk.
- **S9**：Later, Tintin is shot at, then abducted by accomplices of Sakharine, and imprisoned on the SS Karaboudjan.
- **S10**：On board, Tintin escapes and meets the ship's nominal captain, Haddock .
- **S11**：Haddock has been supplied with whisky by first mate Allan , who is working for Sakharine, and thus is permanently drunk, and unaware of the happenings on board his ship.
- **S12**：Tintin, Haddock, and Snowy eventually escape from the Karaboudjan in a lifeboat.
- **S13**：Sakharine sends a seaplane to find them, which the trio seize and use to fly towards the  Moroccan port of Bagghar, but they crash in the desert.
- **S14**：Dehydrated in the heat, and suffering from a sudden lack of alcohol, Haddock hallucinates, and starts to remember stories about his ancestor, Sir Francis Haddock , who was captain of the Unicorn during the 17th century.
- **S15**：Sir Francis' treasure-laden ship was attacked by a pirate ship, led by the masked Red Rackham , and, after a fierce battle and eventual surrender, Sir Francis chose to sink the Unicorn, and most of the treasure, rather than allow it to fall into Rackham's hands.
- **S16**：It transpires that there were three models of the Unicorn, each containing a scroll.
- **S17**：Together, the scrolls will reveal the location of the sunken Unicorn, and its treasure.
- **S18**：The third model ship is in Bagghar, in the possession of the wealthy Omar Ben Salaad , but it is encased in a bullet-proof glass display case.
- **S19**：Sakharine's plan is to stage a concert involving famous diva Bianca Castafiore , the "Milanese nightingale", whose penetrating singing voice will be able to shatter the glass case, allowing Sakharine's trained hawk to fly down and steal the third scroll.
- **S20**：After a chase down to the harbour, pursued by Tintin and Haddock, Sakharine finally escapes with all three scrolls.
- **S21**：Tintin chases him back to Europe and arranges a police reception for him on the dockside.
- **S22**：Haddock and Sakharine, who is revealed to be the descendant of Red Rackham, replay their ancestors' swashbuckling sword fight, using dockside cranes, swords, and even bottles of whisky.
- **S23**：Haddock is eventually victorious and Sakharine is promptly arrested by Thomson and Thompson.
- **S24**：With the three scrolls in their possession, Tintin and Haddock find that the indicated location is Marlinspike Hall, and that the hall had been built originally by Sir Francis Haddock.
- **S25**：There, in the cellar, they find some of the treasure, and a clue to the location of the sunken Unicorn.
- **S26**：Both men agree to continue the adventure.

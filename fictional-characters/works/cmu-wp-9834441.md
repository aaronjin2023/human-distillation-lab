## 角色资料

### Harry Potter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6731c01eb468c5e3` · 独立版本：`profile-737f11abaaee4ee4`

- **性格倾向**：在寻找毁魂器工具时表现出敢于冒险的一面，主动跳入冰冷池水。（行为推断；S28、S29、S30）
- **核心动机**：与伙伴寻找毁掉魂器的方法，为可能持有宝剑的线索继续探访。（行为推断；S21、S25）
- **决策方式**：面对已发现的宝剑时倾向立即行动，破冰入水直接尝试取得。（行为推断；S28、S29、S30）
- **关系模式**：罗恩离队后与赫敏继续同行，后来遭挂坠盒勒颈时被罗恩救起。（摘要直接叙述；S23、S24、S31）

### Hermione Granger

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-8dad407422aa0a6e` · 独立版本：`profile-a72820bcd4063c5d`

- **性格倾向**：在队伍受挫且罗恩离开后仍继续同行，表现出对任务的坚持。（行为推断；S21、S23、S24、S25）
- **核心动机**：为找到能毁掉魂器的宝剑，与哈利追访巴希达这条可能的线索。（行为推断；S24、S25）
- **决策方式**：在辨认魔杖窃贼时运用知识分析线索，识别出格林德沃身份。（行为推断；S22、S27）
- **关系模式**：与哈利及罗恩共同承担挂坠盒的负面影响，三人轮流佩戴。（摘要直接叙述；S21）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Ronald Weasley

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5bb58553d080c9ad` · 独立版本：`profile-2df521aab33c9db5`

- **性格倾向**：受挂坠盒影响时显得容易被猜疑与挫败推动，却仍会返回救人。（行为推断；S23、S31、S32）
- **核心动机**：因任务缺乏进展而沮丧，并在怀疑伙伴相恋时离开队伍。（摘要直接叙述；S23）
- **决策方式**：受到挂坠盒幻象刺激时，以宝剑直接摧毁制造折磨的物件。（行为推断；S32）
- **关系模式**：怀疑哈利与赫敏相恋而离队，后来又在哈利遇险时出手救援。（摘要直接叙述；S23、S31）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：因猜疑与挫败离队，后来仍返回救助哈利。

### Lord Voldemort

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-d1c8c8e1cba1f016` · 独立版本：`profile-a50e4330450d969e`

- **性格倾向**：在魔杖问题上显得执着且强硬，持续以逼问与夺取寻求优势。（行为推断；S4、S7、S22、S36、S43）
- **核心动机**：试图寻找能杀死哈利的魔杖办法，并持续追索老魔杖的下落。（行为推断；S4、S7、S22、S36、S43）
- **决策方式**：获得老魔杖所在的线索后直接闯墓夺取，倾向迅速付诸行动。（行为推断；S36、S43）
- **关系模式**：面对魔杖制作者时采用审讯与折磨，试图从他们身上获取答案。（摘要直接叙述；S7、S22）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{further2}} {{See also}}  Minister of Magic Rufus Scrimgeour, addresses the wizarding media, stating that the ministry would remain strong even as Lord Voldemort gains strength.
- **S2**：Severus Snape arrives at Malfoy Manor to inform Lord Voldemort and his Death Eaters of Harry's departure from No.
- **S3**：4 Privet Drive.
- **S4**：Voldemort commandeers Lucius Malfoy's wand, as Voldemort's own wand cannot be used to kill Harry, their wands being "twins".
- **S5**：Meanwhile, the Order of the Phoenix gather at Privet Drive and escort Harry to safety, using Polyjuice Potion to create six decoy Harrys.
- **S6**：During their flight to the Weasley family home at Burrow, all are ambushed by Death Eaters, who kill Mad-Eye Moody and Harry's owl, Hedwig, and injure George Weasley.
- **S7**：At the Burrow, Harry has a vision of the wand-maker Ollivander being tormented by Voldemort, who claims that Ollivander had lied to him by claiming that the only way Voldemort could kill Harry was by using another person's wand.
- **S8**：Scrimgeour arrives at the Burrow and distributes three items from Albus Dumbledore's estate to Ron, Hermione, and Harry.
- **S9**：Ron receives Dumbledore's Deluminator, Hermione, a copy of The Tales of Beedle the Bard, and Harry the first Golden Snitch that he ever caught in a Quidditch match.
- **S10**：Scrimgeour reveals that Harry was bequeathed the Sword of Godric Gryffindor, but the minister explains that the sword was not Dumbledore's to pass on and, in any case, was missing.
- **S11**：Next, the Death Eaters take control of the Ministry and assassinate Scrimgeour; they use its authority to persecute Muggle-born witches and wizards.
- **S12**：They disrupt the wedding of Bill Weasley and Fleur Delacour.
- **S13**：Harry, Hermione, and Ron next disapparate to London and find sanctuary at No.
- **S14**：12 Grimmauld Place.
- **S15**：There they discover that the "R.A.B." from the false Horcrux locket is Regulus Arcturus Black, the younger brother of Sirius Black.
- **S16**：From Kreacher, the Black's house-elf, they learn that Mundungus Fletcher stole the real locket.
- **S17**：Kreacher and Dobby apprehend Fletcher, who reveals that the locket is in the possession of Dolores Umbridge.
- **S18**：Under the disguise of Polyjuice Potion, the trio infiltrate the Ministry and successfully retrieve the locket.
- **S19**：The trio escape into the wilderness after accidentally revealing the location of No.
- **S20**：12 Grimmauld Place to Yaxley, a Death Eater who grabs onto Ron as the trio apparates away from the Ministry.
- **S21**：Unable to destroy the Horcrux, they take turns wearing it in order to dilute its malignant power.
- **S22**：Harry sees a vision of Voldemort interrogating Gregorovitch, a renowned wand-maker who claims that a teenage boy had once stolen the legendary Elder Wand from his shop.
- **S23**：While Ron wears the locket, he is overcome with the suspicion that Harry and Hermione are falling in love and with frustration from their lack of progress, he abandons the two in the wilderness.
- **S24**：Harry and Hermione decide to go to Godric's Hollow, where they visit Harry's parents' graves, and the house where they were killed.
- **S25**：They also visit the home of Bathilda Bagshot, who may have the Sword of Gryffindor, which they believe can destroy Horcruxes.
- **S26**：However, they are instead cornered by Voldemort's snake, Nagini, who has been possessing the corpse of Bagshot.
- **S27**：Hermione accidentally breaks Harry's wand as they escape into the Forest of Dean, where Hermione identifies the mysterious thief seen in Harry's vision as Gellert Grindelwald.
- **S28**：When evening falls, Harry sees a Patronus doe which leads him to a frozen pond.
- **S29**：Gryffindor's sword lies beneath the pond's ice.
- **S30**：Harry breaks the ice and jumps in to reach the sword.
- **S31**：The locket around his neck attempts to strangle him, but Ron arrives to rescue Harry.
- **S32**：The locket torments Ron, playing on his feelings of inadequacy and jealousy by displaying an hallucination of Hermione kissing Harry, until an enraged Ron smashes it with the sword.
- **S33**：The trio then visit Xenophilius Lovegood to learn about a symbol seen several times on their journey.
- **S34**：They learn that the symbol represents the Deathly Hallows: the Elder Wand, the Resurrection Stone and the Cloak of Invisibility.
- **S35**：Lovegood betrays them to the Death Eaters in an effort to have his kidnapped daughter returned.
- **S36**：Harry envisions Voldemort learning from Grindelwald that the Elder Wand lies with Dumbledore in his grave.
- **S37**：The trio escape into the wilderness once more, but Snatchers appear and chase them.
- **S38**：They are captured and taken to Malfoy Manor.
- **S39**：Bellatrix Lestrange imprisons Harry and Ron in a cellar in which they discover Luna, Ollivander, and Griphook the goblin.
- **S40**：Bellatrix tortures Hermione for information on how they got the sword of Gryfindor.
- **S41**：After Dobby Apparates to save them, a short battle ensues, and Harry duels and disarms Draco.
- **S42**：Dobby is killed by Bellatrix with her dagger as the protagonists escape.
- **S43**：Meanwhile, Voldemort breaks into Dumbledore's tomb, takes the Elder Wand and casts a spell into the sky as a sign of victory.
- **S44**：Then the first part of the final chapter concludes.

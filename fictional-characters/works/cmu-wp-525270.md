## 角色资料

### Beatrix Kiddo

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-233d42c2e469128c` · 独立版本：`profile-aeb7fe757c0059e7`

- **性格倾向**：在遭受伏击与活埋时显出顽强倾向，能将训练转为求生行动（行为推断；S8、S10、S13、S14）
- **核心动机**：当前行动以杀死Bill为目标，早先退出组织则为保护女儿（摘要直接叙述；S2、S5、S24、S25）
- **价值取舍**：得知怀孕后将孩子的安全置于杀手身份与旧情关系之上（行为推断；S5、S24、S25）
- **关系模式**：与Bill既是旧日爱侣和女儿的父母，也因婚礼袭击结下仇怨（摘要直接叙述；S2、S4、S6、S25）

### Bill

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-274e87611aee2d58` · 独立版本：`profile-994e3c2b54083979`

- **性格倾向**：面对恋人离开时倾向以报复维护自身感受，控制欲强烈（行为推断；S6、S23、S26）
- **核心动机**：他将袭击婚礼解释为对Beatrix伤害自己感情的报复（摘要直接叙述；S26）
- **价值取舍**：即使理解她保护孩子的理由，也不愿为自己的暴行道歉（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26）
- **决策方式**：重逢后用药物迫使她坦白，倾向以控制手段取得解释（行为推断；S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：能够理解她保护女儿的选择，却坚持为自己的报复辩护。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film opens with the same scene from the first film, of Bill  shooting the Bride .
- **S2**：The Bride herself then appears behind the wheel of a car, briefly recounting the events of the previous film and stating she is currently on her way to kill Bill.
- **S3**：In a flashback, the Bride — visibly pregnant — and her groom  rehearse their wedding.
- **S4**：Bill, her former lover and leader of the Deadly Viper Assassination Squad, arrives unexpectedly and wishes her well.
- **S5**：It is revealed that the Bride had retired from assassination and left Bill in order to give a better life to her unborn daughter.
- **S6**：Moments later, the other assassination squad members arrive and attack the wedding rehearsal on Bill's orders.
- **S7**：In the present, Bill warns his brother Budd , a former Deadly Viper and now a bouncer, that he will be targeted next.
- **S8**：The Bride arrives at his trailer and bursts through the door, expecting to ambush him, but Budd is expecting her, shooting her in the chest with a double-barreled shotgun blast of rock salt, then sedates her.
- **S9**：Budd calls Elle Driver , another former Deadly Viper, offering to sell her the Bride's Hanzō sword for a million dollars cash.
- **S10**：He then seals the Bride inside a coffin and buries her alive.
- **S11**：A flashback shows Bill and the Bride camping one night, when Bill tells her a story of legendary martial arts master Pai Mei  and his Five Point Palm Exploding Heart Technique, a death blow Pai Mei has so far refused to teach any of his students.
- **S12**：Bill then takes the Bride to Pai Mei's temple to be trained by him.
- **S13**：Though Pai Mei initially ridicules her for being a Caucasian-American woman , she eventually gains his respect and learns a number of techniques, including the art of punching through thick planks of wood from inches away.
- **S14**：She uses this skill to break out of the coffin and claws her way to the surface.
- **S15**：Elle arrives at Budd's trailer for their transaction but has hidden a lethal black mamba with her money, which kills him.
- **S16**：She then calls Bill and tells him that the Bride has killed Budd by putting the black mamba in his trailer, and that Elle has killed the Bride; it is at this point that the Bride's name, Beatrix Kiddo, is finally revealed.
- **S17**：As she exits the trailer, she is ambushed by Beatrix, who had arrived there soon after Elle.
- **S18**：In the middle of an all-out battle in the trailer, Elle taunts Beatrix with the news that Elle had poisoned Pai Mei in revenge for his snatching out her eye when she called him a "miserable old fool." Beatrix then plucks out Elle's remaining eye and leaves her screaming and thrashing about in the trailer with the black mamba.
- **S19**：Beatrix subsequently travels to Mexico to interrogate an elderly gentleman, Esteban Vihaio, about Bill's whereabouts.
- **S20**：Esteban, who is the proprietor of a brothel, was purportedly a father figure to a younger Bill.
- **S21**：Regardless, Esteban willingly divulges Bill's location, stating that Bill "would want [him] to." After finding Bill deep in the Mexican countryside, Beatrix is shocked to find her now-four-year-old daughter B.B.  alive and well.
- **S22**：She spends the evening with Bill and B.B.
- **S23**：After B.B. has gone to bed, Bill shoots Beatrix with a dart containing a truth serum and interrogates her.
- **S24**：A flashback recalls Beatrix's discovery of her pregnancy while on an assassination mission, and her resulting decision to call off her assignment and leave the Deadly Vipers.
- **S25**：Beatrix explains that she ran away without telling Bill in order to protect their unborn daughter from him and his life.
- **S26**：Though Bill understands, he remains unapologetic for what he did, explaining that he's "a murdering bastard," and that "there are consequences for breaking the heart of a murdering bastard." They fight, but although Beatrix loses her weapon, she disables Bill with Pai Mei's Five Point Palm Exploding Heart Technique, which he had secretly taught her.
- **S27**：Bill, stunned by this revelation, makes his peace with her and dies.
- **S28**：Beatrix departs with B.B.; later they are seen watching cartoons in a hotel together after Beatrix gives thanks.

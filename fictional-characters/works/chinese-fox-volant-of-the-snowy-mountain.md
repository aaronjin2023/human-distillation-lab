- **S13**：## Plot Summary
- **S14**：[ edit ] The story is set in the Changbai Mountains in 18th-century China during the Qing dynasty .
- **S15**：It follows the classical unity of time , taking place on a single day: the 15th day of the third month of the 45th year of the reign of the Qianlong Emperor , which corresponds to 19 April 1780 in the Gregorian calendar .
- **S16**：A group of martial artists unearth a treasure chest and begin fighting for it.
- **S17**：Midway during their tussle, they are overpowered and coerced by a highly-skilled monk, Baoshu, to travel to a manor at the top of Jade Brush Peak to help the manor's owner drive away an enemy, "Fox Volant of the Snowy Mountain" Hu Fei.
- **S18**：They start telling stories concerning the origin of a precious saber in the chest and their mysterious foe.
- **S19**：In doing so, they gradually reveal each other's personal secrets.
- **S20**：The saber's story dates back over a century ago to a longstanding feud among four warriors serving under Li Zicheng , who had led the rebellion that overthrew the Ming dynasty .
- **S21**：The four warriors' family names were Hu, Miao, Tian and Fan.
- **S22**：Owing to a massive misunderstanding which lasted several generations, their descendants have been stuck in a vicious cycle of revenge that prevented any of them from uncovering the truth.
- **S23**：The Hu family was opposed to those from the Miao, Tian and Fan families; the latter three were allies.
- **S24**：The people gathered at the manor are either descendants of the four warriors or are otherwise involved in the feud in some way.
- **S25**：Hu Fei's father, Hu Yidao, met Miao Renfeng, a descendant of the Miao family.
- **S26**：Both were masterful swordsmen without peer.
- **S27**：Miao, Hu and Hu's wife developed an uncommon friendship and grew to admire each other, but Hu and Miao must fight unwilling duels to avenge their parents' deaths.
- **S28**：In a scheme orchestrated by the villainous Tian Guinong of the Tian family, Hu was unintentionally slain by Miao when Tian secretly smeared Miao's sword with poison.
- **S29**：The Hus' infant son, Hu Fei, was rescued and raised by a waiter, Ping Asi, whose life Hu Yidao once saved.
- **S30**：Hu Fei grew up and became a powerful martial artist nicknamed "Fox Volant of the Snowy Mountain".
- **S31**：The various scheming martial artists are eventually punished by their greed.
- **S32**：Hu Fei makes an appearance midway in the story.
- **S33**：The conflict reaches a climax when Miao Renfeng challenges Hu Fei to a duel after mistakenly believing that Hu had molested his daughter, Miao Ruolan.
- **S34**：They fight for several rounds but neither emerges the victor.
- **S35**：They are stranded on a cliff about to collapse under their weight when the novel ends.
- **S36**：Hu has an opportunity to attack Miao and knock him off the cliff, but he hesitates because Miao might become his future father-in-law.
- **S37**：However, if he does not attack, either they will fall to their deaths or Miao will kill him.
- **S38**：The novel ends on a deliberate cliffhanger and leaves the conclusion to the reader's imagination.

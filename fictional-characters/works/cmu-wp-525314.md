## 角色资料

### Shrek

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-58c4356ead039267` · 独立版本：`profile-bc96239c96673e92`

- **性格倾向**：在婚姻受到外界质疑时，他容易因比较和猜疑而失去信心。（行为推断；S3、S5、S23）
- **核心动机**：他偷取幸福魔药，是因为相信它能恢复菲奥娜对自己的爱。（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **价值取舍**：误以为菲奥娜会更幸福时，他一度把她的幸福置于挽留婚姻之前。（行为推断；S23）
- **决策方式**：爱情求助遭到拒绝后，他转而偷取并亲自服用魔药。（行为推断；S13、S14、S15、S16）
- **关系模式**：在哈罗德道歉并给予祝福后，他选择宽恕这个曾陷害自己的人。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S31、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他为挽回爱情寻求改变，却也曾因相信对方另有幸福而放手。

### Princess Fiona

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-1e14f0d34b74d7bf` · 独立版本：`profile-4f371db91e1b8ce3`

- **性格倾向**：面对父亲与丈夫的争执，她会表达反感并暂时退出冲突。（行为推断；S3、S4）
- **核心动机**：她明确希望与自己嫁给的食人魔共同过幸福生活。（摘要直接叙述；S34）
- **价值取舍**：在永久变成人类的机会面前，她更看重与原本丈夫共同生活。（行为推断；S33、S34、S35）
- **决策方式**：面对魅力王子的强吻，她以直接反击拒绝对方的强迫。（行为推断；S28）
- **情绪反应**：父亲和丈夫在餐桌上争吵的行为让她感到厌恶。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S4）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### King Harold

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-359d429079608a74` · 独立版本：`profile-3cc8ee7152cf8a64`

- **性格倾向**：受到旧承诺与威胁牵制时，他一度倾向妥协并隐瞒实情。（行为推断；S8、S10、S22、S25）
- **核心动机**：认清女儿对史瑞克的爱后，他转而维护这段婚姻。（行为推断；S29、S30、S31）
- **价值取舍**：最终面对仙女教母的攻击，他以自身安危为代价保护史瑞克。（行为推断；S30）
- **决策方式**：在被要求操纵女儿感情后，他通过提供错误茶水破坏计划。（行为推断；S24、S25、S29）
- **关系模式**：他向女儿及史瑞克道歉并给予祝福，也得到史瑞克宽恕。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他先雇人设局除掉史瑞克，后来却冒险保护史瑞克。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After Shrek  and Fiona  come back from their honeymoon, they find an invitation to a royal ball with Fiona's parents to celebrate their marriage, an event Shrek is reluctant to participate in.
- **S2**：Fiona, Shrek and Donkey  travel to the kingdom of Far Far Away and meet Fiona's parents, King Harold and Queen Lillian , who are surprised by Fiona's choice of husband and her new appearance.
- **S3**：Harold becomes repulsed and at a shared meal, as he and Shrek get into a heated argument over how Shrek and Fiona will raise their family.
- **S4**：Fiona, disgusted at Shrek's and her father's behavior, locks herself in her room that evening, where she meets her Fairy Godmother , who is also surprised at Fiona's new looks.
- **S5**：Shrek worries that he has lost his true love, particularly after finding the diary from her teenhood and reading that she was once infatuated with Prince Charming .
- **S6**：King Harold is accosted by the Fairy Godmother and Charming, her son.
- **S7**：He had arranged with the Fairy Godmother that her son would overcome many obstacles in order to rescue Fiona and break the spell, finding instead the Big Bad Wolf using the tallest tower as a hangout.
- **S8**：They reprimand Harold for breaking an old promise that Charming would be able to marry Fiona and demand that he find a way to get rid of Shrek.
- **S9**：After Shrek reads Fiona's diary and pages containing the single phrase "Mrs.
- **S10**：Fiona Charming", Harold arranges for Shrek and Donkey to join him on a fictitious hunting trip, which really is a trap to lure the two into the hands of an assassin, Puss in Boots .
- **S11**：When Fiona realizes that Shrek left, she asks her father for help, but he replies that he always wanted the best for her and that she should think about what is the best for her, too.
- **S12**：Puss is unable to defeat Shrek and, revealing that he was paid by Harold, asks to come along as a way to make amends.
- **S13**：Shrek decides to go to the Fairy Godmother for help.
- **S14**：However, the Fairy Godmother states that ogres do not live "happily ever after" and refuses to assist.
- **S15**：Nonetheless, the three sneak into the Fairy Godmother's potion factory and steal a "Happily Ever After" potion that Shrek believes will restore Fiona's love for him.
- **S16**：Shrek and Donkey both drink the potion, which doesn't appear to work, but when it starts to rain, they leave, and in doing so they fail to notice that a mushroom that Shrek previously sneezed potion on changes into a beautiful rose.
- **S17**：They wait out the storm in a barn and, while there, Shrek and Donkey become dizzy and fall over and into a deep sleep.
- **S18**：When they awake, they discover the potion has taken effect: Shrek is now a handsome human, while Donkey has turned into a stallion.
- **S19**：In order to make the change permanent, Shrek must kiss Fiona by midnight.
- **S20**：He, Donkey, and Puss return to the castle to discover that the potion has also transformed Fiona back into her former, human self from the first movie.
- **S21**：However, the Fairy Godmother, having learned of the potion's theft, sends Charming to the castle, where he poses as Shrek to win Fiona's love.
- **S22**：Although Harold recognizes Charming for who he really is, he doesn't speak up.
- **S23**：Shrek heartbroken lets Fiona go, believing she will be happier with Charming.
- **S24**：A few hours later, Shrek hears that Fiona hasn't fallen for Charming, and that Harold has been given a love potion to put in Fiona's tea, which will cause her to fall in love with the first person she kisses.
- **S25**：Harold, knowing that it is madness refuses, but the Fairy Godmother threatens to take away his 'Happily Ever After', if he does not fulfill her wish.
- **S26**：The trio overhear the exchange, and are noticed by the Fairy Godmother, who has them arrested by the royal guards and thrown into a dungeon.
- **S27**：When the royal ball begins, several of Shrek's friends band together to free the trio  and create a gigantic gingerbread man , which breaks through the castle's defences so Shrek can stop the kiss, Mongo falls into the moat, losing his arms as a result.
- **S28**：When Shrek arrives, Charming forcibly kisses Fiona, believing the potion has taken effect, Fiona knocks him out temporarily with a headbutt.
- **S29**：When the Fairy Godmother confronts Harold, he reveals that he gave her the wrong tea, having realized her love for Shrek.
- **S30**：The Fairy Godmother tries to kill Shrek, Harold commits self-sacrifice to save Shrek, his armour reflects and disintegrates the Fairy Godmother into a cloud of bubbles; it also returns Harold to his true form, that of the Frog Prince.
- **S31**：Harold apologizes to Shrek and Fiona for everything he has done, and now realizes what is best for Fiona, giving his blessing.
- **S32**：Shrek forgives him, and Lillian tells Harold that she still loves him no matter what.
- **S33**：When it is midnight, Shrek tells Fiona that if they kiss now, then their change will be permanent.
- **S34**：But Fiona says that she wants more than any other princess wants to live happily ever after with the ogre she married.
- **S35**：The clock chimes as the potion's effects wear off, with Donkey changing back as well, much to his chagrin, he cheers up as Shrek reassures him he still is a noble steed to him, Puss and Donkey sing as the royal ball resumes celebrating the true loved newlyweds.
- **S36**：After the credits roll Donkey is seen complaining about missing his dragon mate, suddenly she appears with six babies they call "Dronkeys" ".

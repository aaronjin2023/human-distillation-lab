- **S12**：## Plot
- **S13**：[ edit ] In the remote village of Emond's Field in the Two Rivers , the arrival of a mysterious noblewoman precedes an attack by Trollocs , humanoid monsters.
- **S14**：The woman identifies herself as Moiraine Damodred , an Aes Sedai , and warns that servants of the Dark One seek one of three young men: Rand al'Thor , Perrin Aybara and Matrim Cauthon .
- **S15**：They depart with Moiraine and her Warder , Lan Mandragoran , joined by Egwene al'Vere , whom Moiraine believes has Aes Sedai potential; the gleeman Thom Merrilin ; and later the Wisdom Nynaeve al'Meara , a healer who discovers her ability to channel the One Power .
- **S16**：Pursued by Trollocs, they seek refuge in the abandoned city of Shadar Logoth , where Mat steals a cursed dagger, becoming infected by the malevolent Mashadar .
- **S17**：Escaping the city, the travelers are separated.
- **S18**：Rand, Mat, and Thom travel by boat to Whitebridge , where Thom is lost, allowing Rand and Mat to escape a Myrddraal , the eyeless creatures leading the Trollocs.
- **S19**：In Caemlyn , Rand befriends an Ogier named Loial .
- **S20**：Attempting to see the captured False Dragon , Rand meets Elayne Trakand , heir to Andor , and her brothers Gawyn Trakand and Galad Damodred .
- **S21**：Rand is brought before Queen Morgase ; her Aes Sedai advisor, Elaida ; and Gareth Bryne, Captain-General of the Queen's Guard, and released, despite Elaida's warnings.
- **S22**：Egwene and Perrin are separately guided to Caemlyn by Elyas Machera , who communicates telepathically with wolves and claims Perrin shares this ability.
- **S23**：They encounter the Tuatha'an, or Tinkers , and Aram, who expresses interest in Egwene and explains their belief in non-violence, The Way of the Leaf.
- **S24**：The three encounter the Children of the Light , where Perrin kills two for killing a wolf and is sentenced to death.
- **S25**：Moiraine, Lan, and Nynaeve rescue Egwene and Perrin, reuniting them with Rand and Mat.
- **S26**：Moiraine decides Mat must go to Tar Valon to overcome Shadar Logoth's influence.
- **S27**：Loial warns Moiraine of a threat to the Eye of the World, a pool of Saidin untouched by the Dark One 's influence, confirmed by dreams Mat, Rand, and Perrin have.
- **S28**：The Eye of the World is protected by Someshta (the Green Man ) and contains a seal on the Dark One's prison, the Dragon banner of Lews Therin Telamon , and the Horn of Valere .
- **S29**：At the border of the civilized world, the group enters the Blight to protect the Eye.
- **S30**：They meet the Green Man, who reveals the Eye, and are confronted by the Forsaken Aginor and Balthamel .
- **S31**：Balthamel and the Green Man kill each other.
- **S32**：Rand defeats Aginor and uses the Eye to decimate the Trolloc army and defeat Ba'alzamon .
- **S33**：Moiraine concludes Rand is the Dragon Reborn , but withholds details of the final battle from the male members of the group except Lan.

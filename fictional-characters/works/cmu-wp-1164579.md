### Alex Cross

- **性格倾向**：在亲人失踪的压力下，仍呈现分析线索与持续追查的倾向（行为推断；S1、S6、S9、S10）

- **价值取舍**：面对尚有生还希望的受害者，倾向把及时营救置于核心（行为推断；S7、S12、S16）

- **S1**：{{See also}} Washington, D.C. detective and forensic psychologist Alex Cross  heads to Durham, North Carolina when his niece Naomi , a college student, is reported missing.
- **S2**：He learns from the local police, including Nick Ruskin , that Naomi is the latest in a series of young women who have vanished.
- **S3**：Soon after his arrival, one of the missing women is found dead, bound to a tree in a desolate forest, and shortly after that, intern Kate McTiernan  is kidnapped from her home.
- **S4**：When she awakens from a drugged state, Kate discovers that she is being held captive by a masked man calling himself Casanova, and she is one of several prisoners trapped in his lair.
- **S5**：She manages to escape and is severely injured when she jumps from a cliff and into a river to escape from his clutches.
- **S6**：After she recuperates, she joins forces with Cross to track down her sadomasochist captor, who Cross concludes is a collector, not a killer, unless his victims fail to follow his rules.
- **S7**：This means there is time to rescue the other imprisoned women, just as long as they remain subservient.
- **S8**：Clues lead them to Los Angeles, where a series of gruesome kidnappings and murders have been credited to a man known as the Gentleman Caller.
- **S9**：Cross deduces he is working in collusion with rather than imitating his East Coast counterpart, but his efforts to capture and question him are foiled and the man escapes.
- **S10**：Upon returning to North Carolina, he eventually discovers the underground hideaway used by, as well as the true identity of, the man who calls himself Casanova.
- **S11**：The actual Casanova escapes, while his partner - who seems to still be a teenager- is arrested.
- **S12**：Cross then rescues the multiple amount of kidnapped woman, including Naomi.
- **S13**：Kate, after all that has happened, decides to call Cross, and ask him out for dinner at her house to get away from work.
- **S14**：While Cross is at home, getting ready, the real Casanova- Detective Nick Ruskin - who calls off the police officers guarding Kate, and tries to kill her.
- **S15**：Upon meeting Ruskin, she soon catches on to his plans and attempts to escape, but he takes out a gun, warning her if she try's any of her boxing moves he will kill her.
- **S16**：Alex soon arrives and tries to take down Ruskin, who takes out a lighter, which would cause the house to burn down since Kate is making dinner using the oven.
- **S17**：Cross shoots though a milk carton which kills Ruskin.

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] This article's plot summary may be too long or excessively detailed .
- **S3**：Please help improve it by removing unnecessary details and making it more concise. ( April 2018 ) ( Learn how and when to remove this message ) The book tells the stories of two men born worlds apart.
- **S4**：They have nothing in common except their date of birth (18 April 1906), the day of the San Francisco Earthquake , and a zeal to succeed in life.
- **S5**：William Lowell Kane is the wealthy and powerful, Ivy League -educated heir to an old Boston Brahmin family, while Abel Rosnovski (originally named Władek Koskiewicz) is a Pole who was born into great poverty and eventually migrated to the United States.
- **S6**：William follows in the footsteps of his father, Richard Kane, to become a successful banker.
- **S7**：A few days before William's sixth birthday, Richard dies in the Titanic disaster, leaving William fatherless and heir to the Kane & Cabot bank.
- **S8**：William displays extraordinary discipline and intelligence as a young man at St.
- **S9**：Paul's School and later at Harvard University .
- **S10**：His mother, Anne, marries Henry Osborne, who turns out to be a man interested in gambling and women.
- **S11**：William hates Henry from the beginning and spends most of the time at Harvard and at his best friend Mathew Lester's home.
- **S12**：William then dreams of becoming the chairman of Lester's bank.
- **S13**：Henry spends every last penny of Kane's mother's money on the pretext of speculation.
- **S14**：Anonymous notes warn Anne (who has been impregnated by Osborne, a matter which causes William some worry—until he finds out that he can toss him out easily without any money) about her husband, whose real name is Vitorio Tossana.
- **S15**：She hires a detective to find out the truth, and miscarries fatally from the shock, after which Kane ejects Osborne from his home.
- **S16**：Władek Koskiewicz is born in a forest and raised by a trapper family.
- **S17**：When he grows up and is found to have exceptional intelligence, Baron Rosnovski asks him to become a companion to his son, Leon, so that Władek might prove to be a competition to him.
- **S18**：Władek agrees to go to the Baron's castle on the condition that he can bring along his elder sister Florentyna.
- **S19**：Soon afterwards, World War I breaks out.
- **S20**：Germans attack Poland and capture the Baron, his staff, and son in his castle.
- **S21**：Leon dies by the hand of a German soldier.
- **S22**：Before dying, the Baron hands Władek his silver band of authority.
- **S23**：Władek realises, in a twist, that the Baron was his father when he finds that, like him, the Baron also had a missing nipple.
- **S24**：Florentyna, Władek's beloved sister, who was now his only family, is raped 17 times and killed brutally in front of young Władek by Russian soldiers.
- **S25**：Władek was then moved to Siberia from where he manages to escape to Turkey after facing many hardships.
- **S26**：There he nearly loses his hand (a Hudud punishment for theft) for stealing food but is luckily rescued by two British diplomats, owing to the silver band given by Baron which he wore on the hand.
- **S27**：They transfer him to the Polish consulate from where, with their help, he migrates to America and assumes the name Abel Rosnovski (as inscribed on the silver band).
- **S28**：He starts his life as a waiter in the Hotel Plaza , while taking night classes in economics at Columbia University .
- **S29**：While Abel is working there, Davis Leroy, owner of the Richmond group of hotels, is impressed by his work and appoints him manager of his flagship hotel.
- **S30**：Abel converts the ill-managed hotel to a profit-making one and buys stock in the chain.
- **S31**：During the Great Depression , the hotel needs a backer and Davis, unable to find one, commits suicide, leaving the remaining shares in the Richmond Group to Abel.
- **S32**：Before committing suicide, Davis mentions that Kane & Cabot was the bank that did not support him.
- **S33**：Abel thus plans for revenge and considers Kane his arch rival who was responsible for the death of his closest friend.
- **S34**：The bank gets him an anonymous backer.
- **S35**：Abel assumes it to be David Maxton, owner of Stevens' hotel.
- **S36**：During this time, Abel catches up with his old time friend, George Novak and marries Zaphia—both Polish emigres he had met on the ship-journey to the United States from Turkey in his earlier life.
- **S37**：Abel changes the name of the hotel from Richmond to Baron and builds up a successful hotel chain.
- **S38**：By collaborating with Henry Osborne, who had by now entered politics, Abel plans to ruin Kane and his bank.
- **S39**：Abel begets a daughter, named Florentyna in memory of his dead sister, while Kane has a son, Richard and two daughters, Virginia and Lucy.
- **S40**：Both Kane and Abel had volunteered to serve during World War II .
- **S41**：Abel, during World War II, had saved Kane's life in France, unaware of each other.
- **S42**：He divorces Zaphia when he returns home from the war.
- **S43**：Meanwhile, Kane's bank and Lester's bank merge and a provision is made that anyone who has a share of 8% can summon board meetings.
- **S44**：Abel tries desperately to obtain 8% of the bank's stock but Kane manages to thwart his attempts.
- **S45**：They unknowingly meet each other many times throughout the novel.
- **S46**：Florentyna Rosnovski, daughter of Abel Rosnovski, and Richard Kane, son of William Kane, happen to meet and fall in love without knowing about the rivalry between their fathers.
- **S47**：They get married despite vehement protests from their fathers and start a chain of boutique stores named Florentyna's.
- **S48**：Finally, after Kane exposes Abel's dealings with Osborne and thwarts his ambition to be named Ambassador to Poland (the position goes to John Moors Cabot ), Abel manages to obtain enough shares of the bank and ousts Kane from power.
- **S49**：In 1967, Kane decides to forgive his son and daughter-in-law and expresses his wish to meet them.
- **S50**：Both he and Abel observe the grand-opening of the New York branch Florentyna's , from outside, and they wave at each other.
- **S51**：Kane dies before he is able to see them and his grandson William.
- **S52**：Finally, in a dramatic twist , Abel discovers that his backer was not David Maxton, but William Kane.
- **S53**：Filled with remorse, he reconciles with his daughter and son-in-law.
- **S54**：Abel dies soon after, and bequeaths everything to his daughter Florentyna, except his silver band of authority, which he leaves to his grandson, whom Florentyna and Richard have named "William Abel Kane".

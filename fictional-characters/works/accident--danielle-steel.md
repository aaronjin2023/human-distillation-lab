- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Page Clarke is a 40-year-old-happily married woman who lives with her 44-year-old husband, Brad Clarke, her 15-year-old daughter, Allyson and 7-year-old son Andrew.
- **S3**：One night Brad goes on an unexpected business trip to Cleveland and Allyson reports she is going to have dinner with her friend Chloe and Chloe's father, Trygve Thorensen.
- **S4**：Instead Allyson goes out with Chloe and two older boys named Jamie Applegate and Phillip Chapman.
- **S5**：While crossing the Golden Gate Bridge the teenagers collide with supposed senator's wife Laura Hutchinson.
- **S6**：Phillip is killed; Jamie survives with only a small cut; Chloe suffers leg damage, a broken pelvis and a shattered hip; and Allyson has severe head trauma .
- **S7**：Both girls are rushed to the hospital, while Trygve and Page arrive before the surgeries.
- **S8**：Page is unable to reach Brad until after the surgery has begun, and Brad is outraged that she did not consult him beforehand.
- **S9**：She informs him that Allyson would have died at six'o'clock if the surgery had not been performed.
- **S10**：One hour later, Brad arrives at the hospital and leaves for their house without Page.
- **S11**：Later, Page confronts him as to why he is home early from his "trip" and he confesses that he has been cheating on her.
- **S12**：Soon afterwards Brad disappears several times.
- **S13**：Eventually Page's mother, Maribelle, and sister Alexis arrive in San Francisco and laze about the house while Allison remains in the hospital.
- **S14**：One night after dinner, Page rages at her mother, demanding why she pretends that she did not know that her father molested her and her sister when they were children.
- **S15**：Soon she forces Brad to move out and sends her mother and sister back to New York .
- **S16**：By then she and Trygve had confessed their feelings toward each other.
- **S17**：For four months, Allyson remains in a coma and Andrew, who has attempted to run away, is taken to see his sister.
- **S18**：Soon Trygve and his children Nick, Bjorn and Chloe go to a lake for the weekend, and Allyson awakens from her coma.
- **S19**：When Page goes to join them at the lake, she confesses to Trygve that she is pregnant with his child, and they begin to plan their wedding.

- **S8**：## Plot
- **S9**：[ edit ] During the summer, 13-year-old Harry sees Muggle news about an escaped murderer named Sirius Black .
- **S10**：Some time after this, Harry accidentally performs magic at the home of his Aunt Petunia and Uncle Vernon.
- **S11**：He then uses The Knight Bus to flee his aunt and uncle and travel to London.
- **S12**：While on the Knight Bus, he learns that Sirius Black is a wizard who broke out of Azkaban.
- **S13**：When arriving in London, Harry is greeted by Cornelius Fudge , the Minister for Magic , who tells him to stay the rest of the summer in the Wizarding Diagon Alley.
- **S14**：With Black at large, Dementors have been stationed at Hogwarts as a security measure.
- **S15**：Harry repeatedly faints in the presence of the Dementors, but eventually is taught by Professor Lupin how to repel them using the Patronus Charm.
- **S16**：During a Care of Magical Creatures lesson with Hagrid , Draco Malfoy is injured after provoking a hippogriff named Buckbeak.
- **S17**：Draco's father, Lucius Malfoy , gets Hagrid put on trial for owning a dangerous creature.
- **S18**：When Harry is unable to participate in weekend trips to Hogsmeade Village , Fred and George give him a magical map that shows him how to get there using a secret passage.
- **S19**：At the Three Broomsticks pub, Harry overhears that Black is his godfather, that he betrayed Harry's parents to Voldemort, and that he now seeks to kill Harry as well.
- **S20**：When Ron's pet rat Scabbers disappears, he blames Hermione and her cat Crookshanks.
- **S21**：Ron and Hermione stop talking to each other, although Hermione is distraught when Ron is attacked by Black inside the Gryffindor dormitory.
- **S22**：After the attack, Black cannot be found.
- **S23**：When Harry, Ron and Hermione learn that Buckbeak will be executed, they console Hagrid, and Ron and Hermione resume their friendship.
- **S24**：Hermione and Ron also find Scabbers hiding in Hagrid's hut.
- **S25**：As the friends make their way back to the castle, Ron is attacked by a large black dog, which drags him through the passageway leading to Hogsmeade.
- **S26**：Harry and Hermione give chase, and find themselves in the Shrieking Shack , where the dog is revealed to be Black in his Animagus form.
- **S27**：Lupin arrives, and Black states that he intends to kill Scabbers, not Harry.
- **S28**：He explains that Scabbers is Peter Pettigrew , who betrayed Harry's parents to Voldemort and framed Black for mass murder.
- **S29**：Black and Lupin compel Pettigrew to change into his human form, then haul him back to Hogwarts.
- **S30**：On the way to the castle, the full moon causes Lupin to transform into a werewolf.
- **S31**：Pettigrew escapes and is pursued by Black, Harry and Hermione, who encounter Dementors and lose consciousness.
- **S32**：They awaken in the castle, where Black is now being held captive.
- **S33**：Harry and Hermione proclaim his innocence to Dumbledore, who suggests using Hermione's Time Turner .
- **S34**：Harry and Hermione travel back in time and save both Black and Buckbeak, who fly away together.
- **S35**：Snape blames Lupin for Black's disappearance and makes his werewolf identity public, which forces Lupin to resign.
- **S36**：On the train back to London, Harry receives a letter from Black, expressing his gratitude to Harry for saving his life.

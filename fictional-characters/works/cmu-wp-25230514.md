## 角色资料

### Erik

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5fa1736b2739ebbe` · 独立版本：`profile-9e0a9cc4904613a5`

- **性格倾向**：在受到群体嘲笑后显得敏感，倾向向理想化榜样寻找认同（行为推断；S2、S5、S17、S21）
- **情绪反应**：意识到族群可能覆灭后陷入崩溃，难以承受眼前的绝境（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **价值取舍**：在求援受阻时强调勇敢与善意，认为救助应得到回应（行为推断；S57、S58）
- **成长变化**：从轻视父亲的劝慰转为公开肯定父亲的勇气与贡献（摘要直接叙述；S17、S21、S58）

暂无支持：核心动机、决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：对飞行奇迹的向往，使他一度看不见父亲实际付出的勇气。

### Mumble

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-1af6dbc947175a41` · 独立版本：`profile-d40ad7724040df7c`

- **性格倾向**：在接连遇险与救援受挫时倾向主动承担，持续寻找出路（行为推断；S20、S42、S56）
- **核心动机**：希望得到儿子的理解与认可，因功劳被归给偶像而失落（摘要直接叙述；S21）
- **决策方式**：在群体受困时倾向观察环境细节，把自身专长转为解困办法（行为推断；S42）
- **关系模式**：对儿子既提供独特性的肯定，也会因危险信念严厉训斥（摘要直接叙述；S17、S26、S27）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望支持儿子建立自信，却在危急时以绝望判断打击其信念。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{plot}}  Erik, son of the penguins Mumble and Gloria, is reluctant to try dancing as most of the penguins in Emperor-Land do.
- **S2**：His first attempt at dancing ends with him being stuck in the ice headfirst and peeing in embarrasment in front of everyone, making him subjected to ridicule just like his father had been.
- **S3**：Erik and his two best friends, Bodicea and Atticus, follow Ramon to Adélie-Land and find that Adélie-Land has been ruled by a God-like "penguin" named Sven.
- **S4**：Sven is the only one of his kind in Emperor Land, he survived the loss of his native fishing grounds  by his "miraculous" ability to fly .
- **S5**：Erik instantly becomes enamored with Sven, who takes a liking to him.
- **S6**：Sven and Lovelace the rockhopper penguin tell the others that they were saved by humans.
- **S7**：The two eventually flee and end up on Antarctica, where Sven performs his first miracle by revealing moss to the local penguins.
- **S8**：Sven uses his power of "Sven Think" to help Ramon find a mate.
- **S9**：Ramon instantly falls in love with Carmen, another Adélie penguin who is uninterested.
- **S10**：Mumble follows the chicks' footprints to Adélie-Land and orders them to return to Emperor-Land, but they refuse.
- **S11**：Sven sends Erik back to Emperor-Land with Mumble.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, a couple of krill are in the midst of a swarm.
- **S13**：Will is an adventurous and existentialist krill determined to discover what lies beyond the swarm.
- **S14**：His friend, Bill, reluctantly follows him to ensure his safety.
- **S15**：Once separated from the swarm, Will and Bill realize that krill are at the bottom of the food chain, created to be eaten.
- **S16**：Inspired, Will ventures out to evolve and "move up the food chain" by eating a real creature instead of being eaten.
- **S17**：Meanwhile, Mumble tells Erik that he is unique to the world and that he will someday find his calling, but Erik dismisses his advice.
- **S18**：While trying to cross a perilous ice bridge, the penguins encounter Bryan the Beach Master and his two young sons, who refuse to let the penguins pass.
- **S19**：Suddenly the ice gives away and Bryan is trapped in a deep crevice below the ice.
- **S20**：Mumble sets out to free Bryan by antagonizing a sleeping leopard seal until it chases him down.
- **S21**：Seeking acceptance from Erik, Mumble is disheartened to learn Erik attributed the feat of courage to Sven Think.
- **S22**：Bryan returns to the Elephant Seal beach.
- **S23**：When the penguins return to Emperor-Land they discover that a large iceberg has trapped the Emperor Penguin population below large walls of ice.
- **S24**：Bo ventures out to Adélie-Land to recruit the help of Ramon, the Amigos and the Adélie penguins to bring fish to the doomed Emperor penguins.
- **S25**：Meanwhile, Mumble, Erik and Atticus deliver meager supplies of fish to the trapped penguins.
- **S26**：Erik attempts to deliver a fish to Gloria by flying and nearly tossing himself over the edge of the iceberg.
- **S27**：Mumble scolds Erik for his beliefs, saying that no penguin can fly and that none of the Emperor Penguins will be able to escape.
- **S28**：Once Erik realizes Emperor-Land is destined to die, he begins to break down.
- **S29**：Gloria sends Mumble off to hunt for fish, calming Erik and the rest of the hysterical Emperor-Land.
- **S30**：In the process, Bill becomes inspired to create another swarm of krill but Will refuses, preferring to adhere to his new predator lifestyle.
- **S31**：The next morning, a large flock of Skua attack the trapped Emperor-Land.
- **S32**：Noah the Elder encourages the penguins to stand up to the birds through perseverance and unity.
- **S33**：When all hope seems lost, Bo returns with the entire Adélie-Land, led by Sven, to aid the trapped Emperor Penguins.
- **S34**：Sven orchestrates a cooperative effort to feed the trapped Emperor Penguins through hunting and bringing back a flow of fish from the sea.
- **S35**：Meanwhile, Will becomes increasingly agitated with Bill's behavior and leaves him into the Adélie feeding swarm, to join "fellow predators".
- **S36**：In the process he is attached to a fish carried by Sven and down into the trapped Emperor-Land.
- **S37**：The humans that saved Sven and Lovelace come to Antarctica again to help the penguins find a way out.
- **S38**：However, a blizzard approaches and causes the humans to flee and not return.
- **S39**：Sven reports that the ocean has frozen over significantly and there is no chance of the humans returning or the Adélie Penguins transporting food over such a distance.
- **S40**：Erik urges Sven to teach the penguins how to fly, but Sven reveals that he is not a penguin but a Puffin.
- **S41**：He admits that after the loss of Svenland, he was lonely and grew to love the penguins who accepted him as a family.
- **S42**：Mumble, after watching snow fall into a crevice between chunks of iceberg, begins to tap-dance on the ice and lead the Adélie penguins in a dance to force snow between the ice and weaken it.
- **S43**：The plan works until several chunks break loose, sending Bo, Atticus, and a portion of the Adélies as well as several Chinstrap, Little Blue and Magellanic penguins into the doomed crevice.
- **S44**：Erik and Lovelace tumble towards the edge, and Mumble grabs the thread from Lovelace's vest.
- **S45**：The thread snaps and Lovelace falls on Sven.
- **S46**：Quickly, Mumble and the Amigos pull on the thread that Erik is holding onto and pull him up.
- **S47**：Mumble injures his foot and is unable to dance and lead the Adélies.
- **S48**：Ramon realizes Carmen is trapped below and, risking his own life, jumps off the iceberg to be with her and professes their love to each other.
- **S49**：Sven becomes aware of the dancing and proves himself to be a worthy dancer despite public outcry against him.
- **S50**：He leads the remaining Adélies in dance while Erik and Mumble venture off to the Elephant Seal beach.
- **S51**：Meanwhile, Will, weary from the dangers he experienced as a predator on the surface, ventures back into the sea to find Bill, but not before experiencing the penguin's dancing.
- **S52**：He is suddenly forced down a crack in the ice and into the sea beneath Emperor-Land.
- **S53**：He reunites with Bill and his swarm, who tell him he has the purpose of changing the world and evolving the swarm.
- **S54**：Once Bill told the swarm of their position on the food chain and their lives as pure herbivores, they followed him to the safety of the inaccessible ice below Emperor-Land.
- **S55**：Mumble and Erik arrive at Elephant Seal Beach where Bryan is in the middle of a fight of dominance between another large male.
- **S56**：Mumble pleads to the elephant seals to help free the Emperor Penguins.
- **S57**：Bryan, initially hesitant to return a favor at such a pivotal time of the year, refuses.
- **S58**：Erik, however, commends Mumble for his bravery and lectures Bryan for his lack of kindness towards him through operatic singing.
- **S59**：The elephant seals travel en-masse to Emperor-Land.
- **S60**：The penguins and the seals begin slamming the ice on the beat, joined by Will, Bill and their krill swarm below the ice.
- **S61**：Finally, the iceberg crumbles enough for the Emperor Penguins to climb out of the crevice and reunite with their families.

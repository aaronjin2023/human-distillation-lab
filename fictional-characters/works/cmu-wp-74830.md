### Martin Brody

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e3da0759383d2551` · 独立版本：`profile-b2db13fc7e08b41d`

- **性格倾向**：在鲨鱼危机中倾向承担责任，受挫后仍继续寻找解决办法（行为推断；S14、S19、S38、S40）
- **核心动机**：关闭海滩与组织猎鲨的行动体现其保护居民的动机（行为推断；S5、S19）

- **价值取舍**：在海上险境中倾向求援保命，而非坚持独力完成猎杀（行为推断；S27、S28）
- **成长变化**：从受制于市长的警长转为危局中独立应变的行动者（行为推断；S5、S7、S38、S40）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

### Quint

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b323891fcd62ee90` · 独立版本：`profile-37a99de73d243bc1`

- **性格倾向**：在猎鲨情境中倾向强硬掌控，对他人介入保持戒心（行为推断；S20、S28）
- **核心动机**：猎鲨后期对杀死目标抱有执念，甚至因此损坏船只引擎（摘要直接叙述；S32）
- **价值取舍**：起初以高额报酬衡量猎鲨风险，明确强调自身生命价值（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **决策方式**：面对危险倾向升级追击，甚至破坏求援手段以坚持猎杀（行为推断；S28、S29、S32）
- **关系模式**：与两名同行者建立明确上下级关系，坚持由自己指挥（摘要直接叙述；S20）

### Matt Hooper

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-c8e97c53897a03c7` · 独立版本：`profile-8d8d0ba12d3bcde7`

- **性格倾向**：在公众急于结案时倾向保持怀疑，主动核查捕获结果（行为推断；S11、S13）
- **核心动机**：在猎鲨过程中仍持续收集资料，保留海洋研究的目标（摘要直接叙述；S23）
- **价值取舍**：判断袭击原因时重视实物证据，不随官方解释改变结论（行为推断；S10、S11、S13）
- **决策方式**：在船只失去机动能力后接受高风险方案，亲自下水尝试（摘要直接叙述；S33）
- **情绪反应**：水下发现遇难者遗体时受到惊吓，影响了证据保全（摘要直接叙述；S16）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A girl named Chrissie Watkins leaves an evening beach party on New England's Amity Island to go skinny dipping in the Atlantic Ocean.
- **S2**：After swimming out near a buoy, she is seized by an unseen force from below that thrashes her around before dragging her below the surface.
- **S3**：Chrissie is reported missing and her remains are found on the beach by police chief Martin Brody.
- **S4**：The medical examiner informs Brody that she was killed by a shark.
- **S5**：Brody plans to close the beaches but is overruled by Mayor Larry Vaughan, who fears that reports of a shark attack will ruin the summer tourist season, the town's primary source of income.
- **S6**：The medical examiner consequently attributes the death to a boating accident.
- **S7**：Brody reluctantly goes along with the explanation.
- **S8**：The shark then kills a young boy swimming at the beach.
- **S9**：His mother places a bounty on the shark, sparking an amateur shark-hunting frenzy and attracting the attention of local professional shark hunter Quint, who offers to kill the shark for $10,000, valuing his own life at no less.
- **S10**：Marine biologist Matt Hooper examines Chrissie's remains and determines that she was killed by a shark, not a boat.
- **S11**：A large tiger shark is caught by fishermen, leading the townspeople to believe the problem is solved, but Hooper asks to examine its stomach contents.
- **S12**：Vaughan refuses to allow the necropsy.
- **S13**：That evening, Brody and Hooper discover the dead shark does not contain human remains.
- **S14**：They head out to sea in an attempt to find the shark.
- **S15**：Instead, they discover the half-sunken wreckage of a boat belonging to local fisherman Ben Gardner.
- **S16**：Hooper explores the vessel underwater and discovers a sizable shark's tooth protruding from the damaged hull before he is startled by Gardner's remains and drops the tooth.
- **S17**：Without evidence, Vaughan refuses to close the beaches, and on the Fourth of July many tourists arrive.
- **S18**：A children's prank causes panic at the main beach while the shark enters a nearby estuary, killing a man; Brody's son, who witnesses the attack, goes into shock.
- **S19**：Brody persuades Vaughan to hire Quint.
- **S20**：Quint reluctantly allows Hooper and Brody to join the hunt, but forces them to accept crew mate positions under him as a captain.
- **S21**：The three set out to kill the shark aboard Quint's vessel, the Orca.
- **S22**：Brody is given the task of laying a chum line while Quint uses piano wire to try to hook the shark.
- **S23**：Later, an enormous great white shark looms up behind the boat, and the trio watch it circle the Orca while Hooper takes pictures for research and Quint estimates its size as twenty-five feet in length, with a weight of 6,000 pounds.
- **S24**：Quint harpoons it with a line attached to a flotation barrel, but the shark pulls the barrel under and disappears.
- **S25**：The men retire to the boat's cabin, where Quint relates his experience with sharks as a survivor of the sinking of the warship {{USS}} in 1945 during World War II .
- **S26**：The shark reappears, damaging the boat's hull before slipping away.
- **S27**：In the morning, the shark appears again and Brody attempts to call the U.S.
- **S28**：Coast Guard, but Quint destroys the radio and harpoons the shark with another barrel.
- **S29**：After a long chase, Quint harpoons another barrel to the shark.
- **S30**：The men tie them both to the stern, but the shark drags the boat backwards, forcing water onto the deck and flooding the engine.
- **S31**：Quint heads toward shore, hoping to draw it into shallow waters and suffocate it.
- **S32**：In his obsession to kill the shark, Quint burns out the bearings in the Orcas engine.
- **S33**：With the boat immobilized, the trio attempt a desperate approach: Hooper dons scuba gear and enters the ocean inside a shark proof cage, aiming to stab the shark with a hypodermic spear filled with strychnine.
- **S34**：The shark attacks the cage from behind, causing Hooper to drop the spear.
- **S35**：When the shark gets tangled in the wrecked cage, Hooper escapes and hides in the seabed.
- **S36**：As Quint and Brody raise the mangled cage, the shark leaps onto the boat, crushing the transom and causing the Orca to raise up.
- **S37**：Quint slides down the watery deck to the shark and is killed after a bloody tussle, stabbing the shark in the head with a machete.
- **S38**：When the shark attacks again, Brody shoves a pressurized scuba tank into its mouth, then takes Quint's rifle and climbs the sinking Orca{{'}}s mast.
- **S39**：The shark, with the tank still in its mouth, begins charging Brody.
- **S40**：Brody shoots the scuba tank, causing it to explode and blow the shark to pieces.
- **S41**：Hooper swims to the surface and they use the barrels to swim to shore.

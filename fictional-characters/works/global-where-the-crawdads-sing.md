- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Part I: The Marsh [ edit ] In 1952, six-year-old Kya watches her mother leave her abusive alcoholic husband.
- **S3**：While Kya waits in vain for her mother's return, her older siblings leave.
- **S4**：Her father burns her mother's clothes and paintings.
- **S5**：Alone with her father, Kya learns to fish.
- **S6**：He gives her his knapsack for her shells and feathers.
- **S7**：Unable to read or write, Kya makes paintings of the objects, and the marsh's creatures and shorelines, with watercolors her mother left behind.
- **S8**：One day Kya finds a letter from her mother in the mailbox and leaves it for her father to find.
- **S9**：He burns the letter.
- **S10**：He returns to drinking and takes long gambling trips; eventually, he fails to return.
- **S11**：She survives by gardening and trading mussels and smoked fish for money and fuel from Jumpin', a black man who owns a gasoline station at the boat dock.
- **S12**：She makes friends with Jumpin' and his wife Mabel, who gives her clothes.
- **S13**：The Barkley Cove townspeople nickname her "The Marsh Girl".
- **S14**：On the only day she goes to school, the schoolchildren laugh at her; a pastor's wife calls her "nasty" and "filthy".
- **S15**：She becomes friendly with Tate Walker, who fishes in the marsh.
- **S16**：When Kya gets lost, Tate leads her home in his boat.
- **S17**：Years later, he gives her rare bird feathers, then teaches her to read and write.
- **S18**：They form a romantic relationship until Tate leaves for college.
- **S19**：He promises to return, but leaves without saying goodbye.
- **S20**：Part II: The Swamp [ edit ] Delia Owens's novel is set in a North Carolina marsh like the one illustrated above.
- **S21**：Chase Andrews, Barkley Cove's quarterback and playboy, invites Kya, aged 19, to a picnic, and tries to have sex with her.
- **S22**：He later apologizes, and the two form a romantic relationship.
- **S23**：He shows her an abandoned fire tower, and she gives him a necklace of a shell he found during their picnic, strung on a rawhide string.
- **S24**：She believes Chase's promises of marriage, and they have sex in a motel.
- **S25**：The relationship ends when she sees in the newspaper that he is engaged.
- **S26**：Tate, graduated from college, visits Kya and attempts to apologize.
- **S27**：She rejects him, but lets him inside her shack.
- **S28**：He is impressed by her collection of seashells.
- **S29**：He urges her to publish a reference book on them, which she does, with another on seabirds.
- **S30**：With the money, she pays back taxes and fixes her home.
- **S31**：Her brother Jodie, now in the Army, returns, telling her that their mother had been mentally ill and had died of leukemia two years earlier.
- **S32**：He says he doesn't know where the other siblings are.
- **S33**：He advises Kya to give Tate a second chance, then departs.
- **S34**：Later, relaxing in a cove, Chase confronts Kya, and attempts to rape her.
- **S35**：She fends him off and threatens to kill him if he doesn't leave her alone.
- **S36**：The encounter is witnessed by two fishermen.
- **S37**：Kya fears that reporting the assault would be futile as the town would blame her for "being loose".
- **S38**：The next week, she sees Chase boating up to her shack; she hides until he leaves.
- **S39**：Kya is invited to meet her publisher; she takes a bus to meet him.
- **S40**：After she returns the next day, Chase is found dead beneath the fire tower, with no tracks or fingerprints nearby.
- **S41**：The sheriff suspects murder.
- **S42**：He gathers conflicting statements.
- **S43**：He learns the shell necklace Kya gave to Chase was missing when his body was found, even though he wore it the night he died.
- **S44**：Kya was seen leaving Barkley Cove before the murder, then returning the day after Chase died.
- **S45**：There were red wool fibers on Chase's jacket from Kya's hat.
- **S46**：Convinced she is the culprit, the sheriff arrests her for first-degree murder .
- **S47**：At Kya's trial, only contradictory and circumstantial evidence is provided.
- **S48**：Kya's lawyer debunks the prosecutor's arguments, as there is no evidence that Kya was at the fire tower when Chase died.
- **S49**：The jury finds her not guilty.
- **S50**：She returns home and reconciles with Tate.
- **S51**：They live together in her shack until she dies peacefully in her boat at the age of 64.
- **S52**：Searching for Kya's will, Tate finds a box with some of her old possessions.
- **S53**：It contains a poem that alludes to Chase's murder, and the shell necklace Chase wore.
- **S54**：Tate burns the poems and the rawhide string before dropping the shell onto the beach.
- **S55**：Kya is buried on her property near the shack.

### Oswald Cobblepot

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-91083df805a51f0f` · 独立版本：`profile-5bf928b63d1399b5`

- **性格倾向**：在谋求社会地位时倾向操纵公众观感，失败后又迅速转为报复（行为推断；S4、S19）
- **核心动机**：希望离开地下生活进入哥谭上层，并借政治机会扩张自身地位（行为推断；S3、S4、S8、S19）
- **沟通方式**：对盟友使用犯罪把柄施压，对公众则用伪造的救人行动塑造形象（行为推断；S3、S4）
- **决策方式**：政治计划破产后不断升级伤害规模，以绑架婴儿和导弹袭击回应失败（行为推断；S19、S21）

矛盾与变化：希望被城市精英接纳，却靠操纵和报复不断加深自身敌对处境

### Selina Kyle

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-3726b626a2f3f931` · 独立版本：`profile-9074f6af9df6f735`

- **性格倾向**：在遭老板谋害后的报复情境中，倾向主动反击并拒绝继续受其支配（行为推断；S5、S7、S26）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A deformed baby boy is thrown into Gotham City's river by his terrified parents .
- **S2**：Thirty-three years later, the child, Oswald Cobblepot , resurfaces as a criminal, the Penguin, who kidnaps millionaire industrialist and business tycoon Max Shreck .
- **S3**：With evidence of his corporate crimes, Cobblepot blackmails Shreck into helping him leave the sewers to become part of Gotham's elite.
- **S4**：The Penguin arranges for the Mayor's child to be kidnapped, whom he then "rescues" in order to set himself up as a heroic figure.
- **S5**：Max Shreck later pushes his secretary, Selina Kyle , out of a window from his company's building after she discovers that his proposed power station for Gotham actually will drain electricity from the city.
- **S6**：Selina survives the fall, but it causes a psychotic break and her personality changes.
- **S7**：Having an affinity for cats, Selina returns home and designs a costume, becoming Catwoman and focusing on revenge against her boss.
- **S8**：Shreck then hatches a plan to recall Gotham City's current mayor ([[Michael Murphy  and elect Cobblepot in his place, in order to cement his control over the city and complete his power plant project.
- **S9**：The Penguin's gang of circus performers cause chaos around Gotham to reduce the popularity of the Mayor.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, Batman  continues to have confrontations with Catwoman as well as the Penguin's gang.
- **S11**：Selina, meanwhile, falls in love with Batman's alter-ego Bruce Wayne, a situation complicated by Catwoman's teaming with the Penguin in an effort to rid Gotham of Batman.
- **S12**：They kidnap the Ice Princess , a woman chosen to turn on the Gotham Christmas Tree lights, and frame Batman for the crime.
- **S13**：Batman finds her tied up in a nearby building.
- **S14**：He learns that the Penguin kidnapped her, but Catwoman then takes her to the top of the building.
- **S15**：The Penguin causes the woman to fall off the building, killing her, with a crowd seeing Batman and believing that he killed her.
- **S16**：Catwoman is upset because she did not intend to kill the Princess, and then rejects the Penguin's advances on her, whereupon the Penguin tries to kill her.
- **S17**：In the midst of Batman's attempt at rescuing the Ice Princess, the Penguin's henchmen sabotage the Batmobile.
- **S18**：When Batman returns to the Batmobile, the Penguin takes control of it and wreaks havoc on the streets of Gotham City until Batman manages to disable the Penguin's control while recording the Penguin's taunts about framing Batman and fooling the city.
- **S19**：When Bruce exposes the Penguin's villainous ways, thereby ruining his election chances, the Penguin initiates a plan to kidnap all of Gotham's first-born infants.
- **S20**：He personally attempts to take Max's son, Charles "Chip" Shreck , but agrees to take Max instead.
- **S21**：Batman foils the Penguin's kidnapping scheme, whereupon the Penguin then decides to launch missiles around Gotham using mind-controlled penguins.
- **S22**：However, Batman is able to jam the frequency used to control the penguins and has missiles launched at the Penguin's base.
- **S23**：Batman confronts the Penguin, which culminates in the Penguin falling into the toxic waters in his lair.
- **S24**：Batman tries persuading Catwoman to turn Shreck over to the police, even unmasking himself in the process, but Shreck draws a gun.
- **S25**：Catwoman claims that she still has six of her nine lives remaining, and stays standing up after Shreck fires his gun at her four times.
- **S26**：Catwoman uses a taser to cause an explosion, electrocuting Shreck and apparently sacrificing herself .
- **S27**：The Penguin then emerges from the toxic water and tries to kill Batman, but he succumbs to his wounds.
- **S28**：His body is taken by his penguins into the sewer waters as a final resting place.
- **S29**：Later, Bruce is being driven around the city at night with butler Alfred Pennyworth  and thinks he sees Selina's shadow on a wall.
- **S30**：Alfred stops the car, and Bruce finds a cat, which he takes with him and leaves.
- **S31**：As the Bat-Signal lights up the night sky, a silhouette of what appears to be Catwoman is seen gazing at it.

## 角色资料

### Superman

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-aab8ea41af3c9e4b` · 独立版本：`profile-4ab0d7a6847e16ba`

- **性格倾向**：面对公共灾难，他倾向主动承担风险并持续施救（行为推断；S6、S16、S24）
- **核心动机**：他曾远行寻找幸存同族，表现出对自身来处的追寻（行为推断；S1、S3）
- **价值取舍**：为阻止大陆造成灾难，他将公众安全置于自身安危之前（行为推断；S21、S24）
- **情绪反应**：得知露易丝已有未婚夫与孩子，他难以适应关系变化（摘要直接叙述；S7）
- **决策方式**：面对氪石威胁，他会利用土层隔离来争取行动条件（行为推断；S21）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：承担公共救援责任时，也承受旧爱关系变化的失落。

### Lex Luthor

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-eb9049a547920b8d` · 独立版本：`profile-c9c9187068dd891a`

- **性格倾向**：在夺权计划中，他倾向算计他人并借制造灾难谋利（行为推断；S2、S8、S12）
- **核心动机**：他企图垄断灾后可居住土地，从而攫取土地与权力（摘要直接叙述；S11、S12）
- **价值取舍**：为取得土地控制权，他接受数十亿人死亡的代价（行为推断；S12）
- **决策方式**：对付超人时，他倾向以诱饵分散注意并利用氪石弱点（行为推断；S8、S12、S18、S19）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Lois Lane

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-1aa0b53c885364c8` · 独立版本：`profile-5e9ee5f166633f26`

- **性格倾向**：面对停电疑点，她倾向主动追查而非停留于表面报道（行为推断；S10）
- **决策方式**：超人落海后，她立即推动折返并亲手解除致命威胁（行为推断；S19、S20）
- **关系模式**：面对卢瑟追问孩子身世，她坚持以理查德作为父亲回答（摘要直接叙述；S13）
- **成长变化**：经历超人归来与救灾后，她的文章立场转向肯定其必要性（摘要直接叙述；S4、S28）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：公开写过世界不需要超人，后来又重新肯定他的意义。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Superman  has been missing for five years, since traveling to the location where astronomers believed they had discovered the remains of Krypton.
- **S2**：During his absence, Superman's nemesis Lex Luthor  was released from prison  and married a rich widow  to obtain her fortune upon her death.
- **S3**：Superman, having failed in his quest to find surviving Kryptonians, returns to Earth and, as Clark Kent, resumes his job at the Daily Planet in Metropolis.
- **S4**：He subsequently learns that Lois Lane  has won the Pulitzer Prize for her article "Why the World Doesn't Need Superman." Meanwhile, Lex travels to the Fortress of Solitude and steals Kryptonian crystals, to use for an experiment that causes a mass power outage on the East Coast.
- **S5**：The power loss interferes with the flight test of a space shuttle to be launched into space from its piggy-back mounting on an airliner, occupied by Lois Lane, who is covering the story.
- **S6**：Clark flies into action as Superman and stops the plane from crashing onto a baseball stadium.
- **S7**：The world rejoices at Superman's return, but he has difficulty coping with Lois' fiancé, Richard White , nephew of Daily Planet editor-in-chief Perry White , and their 5-year-old son, Jason .
- **S8**：With Superman distracted by an out-of-control vehicle (a diversion involving Lex's partner-in-crime, Kitty Kowalski , Lex steals Kryptonite from the Metropolis Museum of Natural History.
- **S9**：Perry then assigns Lois to interview Superman while Clark investigates the blackout.
- **S10**：Lois and Jason inadvertently board Lex's yacht and are captured after Lois decides to investigate the blackout story, which she connects to Luthor's experiment.
- **S11**：He reveals to them his latest scheme to grab land and power.
- **S12**：By combining one of the stolen Kryptonian crystals with Kryptonite, Luthor can grow a new continental landmass in the Northern Atlantic Ocean, one that will cause sea levels to rise drastically, killing billions of people and affording him full control of the only available land for the survivors.
- **S13**：Noticing Jason experience a slight reaction to Kryptonite, Lex asks who Jason's father really is; Lois asserts that the father is Richard.
- **S14**：The crystal begins to create Lex's new landmass, while Lois attempts to escape but is attacked by a henchman.
- **S15**：Jason throws a piano at the henchman, killing him and proving that he is actually Superman's son.
- **S16**：Meanwhile, Superman is attempting to minimize the destruction in Metropolis caused by the new landmass' growth when Richard arrives in a sea plane to rescue Lois and Jason from the sinking yacht.
- **S17**：Superman soon arrives to help and then flies off to find Lex.
- **S18**：Meeting Lex, Superman discovers the landmass is filled with Kryptonite, which weakens him to the point that Lex and his henchmen are able to beat him.
- **S19**：Superman is stabbed by Lex with a shard of Kryptonite and falls into the ocean.
- **S20**：Lois makes Richard turn back to rescue Superman, whereupon she removes the Kryptonite from his back.
- **S21**：Superman, after regaining his strength from the sun, lifts the landmass after putting layers of earth between him and the Kryptonite.
- **S22**：Lex and Kitty escape in their helicopter; Kitty, unwilling to let billions of people die, tosses away the crystals that Lex stole from the Fortress of Solitude.
- **S23**：She and Luthor are stranded on a tiny desert island when their helicopter runs out of fuel.
- **S24**：Superman pushes the landmass into space with the crystals trapped on the landmass, but is weakened by the Kryptonite and crashes back to Earth.
- **S25**：Doctors remove more Kryptonite from Superman's wound, but after it is removed they cannot penetrate his skin with their surgical tools.
- **S26**：While Superman remains in a coma, Lois and Jason visit him at the hospital where Lois whispers something into Superman's ear and then kisses him.
- **S27**：Superman later awakens and flies to visit Jason, reciting his father Jor-El's  last speech to Jason as he sleeps.
- **S28**：Lois starts writing another article, titled “Why the World Needs Superman”.
- **S29**：Superman reassures her that he is now back to stay, and flies off to low orbit, where he gazes down at the world, flying into the sunrise for further adventures.

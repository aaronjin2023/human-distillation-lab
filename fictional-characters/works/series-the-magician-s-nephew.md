- **S15**：## Plot Summary
- **S16**：[ edit ] In 1900, two children, Digory and Polly , live in the same London terrace .
- **S17**：When exploring a crawl space joining the houses, they find themselves in the study of Digory's Uncle Andrew .
- **S18**：Andrew tricks Polly into touching a yellow magic ring , causing her to vanish.
- **S19**：He explains to Digory that the rings allow travel between worlds.
- **S20**：He blackmails Digory into taking another yellow ring to follow Polly, and two green rings so that they both can return.
- **S21**：Digory is transported to a sleepy wood filled with pools where he reunites with Polly.
- **S22**：They surmise that the wood is a " Wood between the Worlds ", similar to the attic that links their houses back in England, and that each pool leads to a separate universe.
- **S23**：They decide to explore a different world before returning to England, and jump into one of the nearby pools.
- **S24**：They find themselves in a desolate abandoned city of the ancient world of Charn.
- **S25**：Inside the ruined palace, they find a bell with a hammer and an inscription inviting the finder to strike the bell.
- **S26**：Despite Polly's protests, Digory rings the bell, awakening a witch-queen named Jadis , who, to avoid defeat in battle, deliberately killed every living thing in Charn by speaking the " Deplorable Word " before placing herself in an enchanted sleep that would end on the ringing of the bell.
- **S27**：The children attempt to flee, but Jadis follows them back to England by clinging to them as they clutch their rings.
- **S28**：In England, she orders Uncle Andrew to fetch her a "chariot" – a hansom cab – so she can conquer Earth.
- **S29**：She robs a jeweller's shop and returns to Kirke house pursued by police.
- **S30**：Jadis tears an iron rod from a nearby lamp-post, using it to fight off police and onlookers when they mock her.
- **S31**：Polly and Digory grab Jadis and put on their rings to take her out of their world – accidentally bringing Uncle Andrew, Frank the cab-driver, and Frank's horse Strawberry, who were all touching each other when the children grabbed their rings.
- **S32**：In the Wood between the Worlds, Strawberry, when attempting to drink from one of the ponds, accidentally brings everyone into a void.
- **S33**：At first, Digory believes it to be Charn, but Jadis recognises it as a world not yet created.
- **S34**：They witness the lion Aslan bringing stars, plants, and animals into existence as he creates the new world, Narnia.
- **S35**：A terrified Jadis tries to kill Aslan with the iron rod, but it rebounds harmlessly off him, and in the creative soil of the new world it sprouts into a growing lamp-post.
- **S36**：Aslan gives some animals the power of speech .
- **S37**：Frank the cabby and his wife Helen are crowned King and Queen of Narnia.
- **S38**：Aslan confronts Digory with his responsibility for waking Jadis and for bringing her into Narnia, and tells Digory he must atone by helping to protect the new land of Narnia from her evil.
- **S39**：Aslan transforms Strawberry into a winged horse , and Digory and Polly fly on him to a distant garden.
- **S40**：Digory's task is to take an apple from a tree in this garden to plant in Narnia.
- **S41**：At the garden Digory finds a sign warning not to steal the fruit.
- **S42**：Digory encounters Jadis in the garden, where she eats one of the apples to become immortal.
- **S43**：She first tempts Digory to eat an apple himself, and then to take one back to Earth to heal his dying mother.
- **S44**：Digory resists and Jadis departs for the north.
- **S45**：Digory returns to Narnia and plants the apple, which grows into a mature tree as Aslan explains that anyone who steals the fruit for themselves will get their heart's desire, but in a form that renders it disagreeable: Jadis is now immortal, but eternally miserable.
- **S46**：Moreover, the new tree will protect Narnia from Jadis for centuries to come.
- **S47**：Aslan consents for Digory to take an apple from the new tree for his mother and returns Digory, Polly, and Uncle Andrew to England.
- **S48**：Digory's apple restores his mother's health, and he and Polly remain lifelong friends.
- **S49**：Uncle Andrew reforms and gives up magic.
- **S50**：Digory plants the apple's core with Uncle Andrew's rings in the back yard of his aunt's home in London, and it grows into a large tree.
- **S51**：Soon afterwards, Digory's family inherits a mansion in the country, and many years later the apple tree blows down in a storm.
- **S52**：Digory, now a professor, has its wood made into a wardrobe, setting up the events in The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe .

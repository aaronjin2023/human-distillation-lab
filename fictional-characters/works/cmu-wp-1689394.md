## 角色资料

### Will Turner

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-d6fe21907e86f3e9` · 独立版本：`profile-46cf92015411b40d`

- **性格倾向**：在救父目标驱动下显得敢于冒险，也愿采用隐秘交易推进计划。（行为推断；S7、S15、S26）
- **核心动机**：为救父亲脱离琼斯与荷兰人号，向冯提出用杰克交换黑珍珠。（摘要直接叙述；S7）
- **决策方式**：在与贝克特周旋时采用隐蔽引路办法，留下浮尸供其舰船追踪。（行为推断；S26）
- **关系模式**：与伊丽莎白在战斗中结婚，成为荷兰人号船长后仅短暂相聚。（摘要直接叙述；S46、S55、S58、S59）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：救父亲与陪伴伊丽莎白的责任存在冲突。

### Elizabeth Swann

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-9e95164eae7103ca` · 独立版本：`profile-4a9277fd642d22b5`

- **性格倾向**：面对悬殊兵力仍坚持迎战，表现出强硬且不轻易退缩的一面。（行为推断；S38、S41）
- **核心动机**：当选海盗王后主张开战，并在敌方占压倒优势时仍决意作战。（摘要直接叙述；S38、S41）
- **决策方式**：看出杰克与威尔的安排后倾向借交换人质，调整双方所处位置。（行为推断；S40）
- **关系模式**：接受威尔求婚并在战斗中成婚，见他随船沉下时深感悲痛。（摘要直接叙述；S46、S52、S53）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Captain Jack Sparrow

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-c2ac01489518c060` · 独立版本：`profile-d2cdab141ce6eacd`

- **性格倾向**：在多方角力中倾向保留后手，借交易与预先安排争取主动。（行为推断；S17、S18、S27、S28、S61）
- **核心动机**：以引出海盗议会为交换，要求贝克特保护自己免受琼斯威胁。（摘要直接叙述；S18）
- **决策方式**：处理威尔的引路行动时顺势布局，给出罗盘后将他推入海中。（行为推断；S26、S27、S28）
- **价值取舍**：在威尔遭致命刺伤后，转而帮助他刺穿琼斯心脏以争取生机。（行为推断；S23、S47、S48、S50、S55）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：To control the oceans for E.
- **S2**：I.
- **S3**：Co., Lord Cutler Beckett executes anyone associated with piracy and uses Davy Jones to destroy all pirate ships on the seas.
- **S4**：Condemned prisoners sing "Hoist the Colours" to prompt the nine pirate lords of the Brethren Court to convene at Shipwreck Cove; however, the late Captain Jack Sparrow, pirate lord of the Caribbean, never appointed a successor by passing on his piece of eight.
- **S5**：Captain Barbossa leads Will, Elizabeth, Tia Dalma and the crew of the Black Pearl to rescue Jack from Davy Jones's Locker.
- **S6**：Sao Feng, pirate lord of the South China Sea, possesses "The Navigation Charts", a map to the Locker where Jack is imprisoned.
- **S7**：Will bargains with Feng for the Pearl in exchange for Sparrow, so Will can rescue his father from Davy Jones and The Flying Dutchman.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, Davy Jones - under control of Lord Beckett  - destroys several pirate ships, leaving no survivors.
- **S9**：Beckett confronts Jones with his decision to place the Chest of Davy Jones on board his ship The Flying Dutchman and post Marines to guard it under command of the very man who stole Jones' heart, Admiral James Norrington.
- **S10**：The crew meanwhile journeys into the Locker, retrieves Sparrow and the Pearl, but must find a way out.
- **S11**：As the Pearl seeks an escape route, dead souls float past, including Elizabeth's father Weatherby Swann, murdered by Beckett.
- **S12**：Tia Dalma reveals that Davy Jones was appointed by Calypso, his lover and goddess of the Sea, to ferry the dead to the next world; in return, Jones could step upon land for one day every ten years to be with his love.
- **S13**：When she failed to meet him, he abandoned his duty and transformed into a monster, along with the rest of his crew.
- **S14**：Jack then finds a means of escape encoded in the "Navigation Chart".
- **S15**：On returning to the living world, the Pearl is ambushed by Sao Feng, who reveals his agreement with Will.
- **S16**：Feng betrays Will, handing over the crew to Beckett in exchange for the Pearl.
- **S17**：Beckett takes Sparrow aboard his flagship, the Endeavour, but Jack refuses to divulge where the Brethren Court will convene.
- **S18**：Jack offers to lure the Court out, in exchange for Beckett's protecting him from Jones.
- **S19**：Feng then bargains with Barbossa to release the Pearl in exchange for Elizabeth, whom he believes is Calypso in human form.
- **S20**：Feng's ship, The Empress, leaves with Elizabeth, and the Pearl launches a running broadside against the Endeavour, allowing Jack to escape back to the Pearl, though not before damaging the Endeavour's mainmast and delaying Beckett for several hours.
- **S21**：Feng tells Elizabeth that the first Brethren Court trapped Calypso in human form so men could rule the oceans with ease.
- **S22**：When Davy Jones attacks Feng's ship, the mortally wounded Feng appoints Elizabeth his successor, then she and the crew are imprisoned in the Flying Dutchmans brig.
- **S23**：Bootstrap Bill Turner  tells Elizabeth that whoever stabs Davy Jones' heart becomes the next captain of the Dutchman.
- **S24**：Admiral Norrington, regretting betraying the crew of the Pearl and disgusted at Beckett's behavior, frees Elizabeth and her crew.
- **S25**：They escape to their ship, but Norrington is killed by a crazed Bootstrap Bill.
- **S26**：Will leaves a trail of floating corpses for Beckett's ship to follow.
- **S27**：Jack catches Will in the act, though reveals it was part of his plan.
- **S28**：Jack suggests he stab the heart to solve Will's conflicting obligations to his father and to Elizabeth, then forces Will overboard after giving him his compass  so as to allow Beckett can find Shipwreck Cove.
- **S29**：Will is rescued by the Endeavour, and Davy Jones reveals to him that he masterminded Calypso's imprisonment at the First Brethren Court.
- **S30**：At Shipwreck Cove, the pirate lords present their nine "pieces of eight", but disagree over Barbossa's proposal to free Calypso.
- **S31**：Meanwhile on the Pearl Tia Dalma  plays her music box, then hears another, which is revealed to belong to Jones.
- **S32**：Jones asks why she left him, to which she replies "It is my nature".
- **S33**：He reveals the pirates plans to free her.
- **S34**：Calypso declares that she will only let them free her so she can take revenge for her imprisonment, allowing Beckett to succeed and she and Jones to be together.
- **S35**：Back at Shipwreck Cove, Barbossa calls upon Captain Teague, Jack's father and Keeper of the Pirate's Code, to confirm that only an elected 'Pirate King' can declare war.
- **S36**：There have been no Pirate Kings since the first Court because the Pirate Lords always vote for themselves, and no Lord has ever had a plurality.
- **S37**：Jack calls for a vote, the first eight Lords vote for themselves, and Jack breaks the stalemate by voting for Elizabeth.
- **S38**：She is now "king", and she favors a war.
- **S39**：The pirates prepare for battle, but Barbossa quietly takes the pieces of eight for himself.
- **S40**：The next day, during a parley with Beckett and Jones, Elizabeth swaps Sparrow for Will after realizing Jack and Will have planned to have Jack stab the heart; this places Jack on the Dutchman with the heart, however, he is at first stuck in the brig, to Jones' delight.
- **S41**：Elizabeth is still determined to fight, despite the fact that the armada outnumbers the pirate fleet by over a hundred to one, not including the considerable advantage provided by the Dutchman.
- **S42**：Barbossa steals Jack's and Elizabeth's "pieces of eight" and uses it and its counterparts to free Calypso from human form.
- **S43**：As she transforms, Will knows that she will not bring them favor, and discloses that Davy Jones betrayed her to the Brethren Court.
- **S44**：Barbossa prays for her help, but Calypso's fury unleashes a violent storm and a maelstrom, in which The East India Company and the Brethren Court will battle, but only the Dutchman and the Pearl can participate due to their ethereal nature .
- **S45**：Sparrow escapes the Dutchmans brig and steals the Dead Man's Chest, which leads to a duel with an irate Jones, during which his sword is shattered.
- **S46**：In the heat of battle Will proposes to Elizabeth, who accepts, and during the fighting Captain Barbossa marries them.
- **S47**：Will and Elizabeth board the Dutchman to retrieve the chest, but Jones defeats both before being confronted by Jack, whose has acquired both the key and the chest and threatened to stab the heart.
- **S48**：Jones, in a moment of spite, thrusts his sword through Will's chest.
- **S49**：This breaks the hold the Dutchman has on Bootstrap Bill's mind, and attacks Jones in a rage.
- **S50**：Sparrow places his broken sword in Will's hand forces him to stab it.
- **S51**：Jones spasms, turns, sees the pierced heart and falls into the maelstrom, uttering Calypso's name for the last time.
- **S52**：Jack and Elizabeth escape the Dutchman and its crew, along with Will, is pulled under.
- **S53**：While Elizabeth is devastated, Jack orders the Pearl to weigh anchor, obstinately to show his fulfillment of the deal he made with Beckett.
- **S54**：Having lost the Dutchman and Jack still a dangerous liability, Beckett moves to attack the Pearl, justifying it as "just good business".
- **S55**：The storm and maelstrom have ceased, but the Dutchman resurfaces with Will as the captain and the crew restored to human form.
- **S56**：Bootstrap had removed Will's heart and put it in the Chest.
- **S57**：The Dutchman and the Pearl turn about and attack the Endeavour, annihilating the ship and a shattered Beckett; with no leader and facing two seemingly invincible ships, the armada retreats.
- **S58**：Will is bound to sail the sea as the Dutchmans captain, ferrying the dead.
- **S59**：Elizabeth bids Jack, Barbossa and the crew farewell; then she and Will have one day together.
- **S60**：He departs after giving Elizabeth the Dead Man's Chest.
- **S61**：Jack and Gibbs find themselves stranded in Tortuga: Barbossa has commandeered the Pearl in order to find the Fountain of Youth, only to discover Jack had already cut out the middle of the map as insurance.
- **S62**：The film ends with Jack sailing from Tortuga in a dingy to find the Fountain.
- **S63**：In a post-credits scene set ten years later, Elizabeth and her son  watch from a seacliff as the Dutchman appears with Will Turner aboard, anticipating "one day on land" to spend with Elizabeth.
- **S64**：While unclear in the film, the writers of the film stated in blog posts and in video commentary that as Elizabeth stayed faithful to Will unlike Calypso, Will is actually released from his duty on the Flying Dutchman and is free to live out his days on land{{Citation needed}}

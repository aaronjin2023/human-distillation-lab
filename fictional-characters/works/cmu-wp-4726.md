## 角色资料

### Bruce Wayne

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8a9e8e464a744c51` · 独立版本：`profile-56bc777dcc9c2cb9`

- **性格倾向**：在打击犯罪时，倾向严密区隔公开形象与秘密行动（行为推断；S2）
- **核心动机**：目睹父母遇害后立誓复仇，将打击犯罪作为终身任务（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2）
- **价值取舍**：面对爱情与城市危机，倾向先承担保护城市的责任（行为推断；S18、S19、S23）
- **决策方式**：对付大规模毒害时，倾向同时破坏源头并提供解毒手段（行为推断；S19、S20、S21）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：私人复仇与保护城市的职责在同一敌人身上重叠；亲密关系被持续的打击犯罪任务推迟

### Jack Napier

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-85a1f29b76ac9150` · 独立版本：`profile-1b422411bb19eaa5`

- **性格倾向**：在掌控城市的行动中，倾向以戏剧化展示放大伤害效果（行为推断；S13、S14、S20）
- **核心动机**：遭背叛与毁容后重塑犯罪身份，并夺取原老板的势力（摘要直接叙述；S8、S12、S13、S14）
- **价值取舍**：在扩张控制与满足执念时，倾向无视市民及薇姬的安全（行为推断；S14、S15、S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：倾向利用日常消费习惯和金钱诱惑，让受害者主动进入陷阱（行为推断；S14、S20）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As a child, Bruce Wayne  witnesses his parents killed by a criminal.
- **S2**：He vows to avenge their deaths in a lifelong battle against crime in the guise of Batman while concealing his secret identity, adopting the public face of a billionaire playboy and head of Wayne Enterprises.
- **S3**：Years later, Gotham City is controlled by crime boss Carl Grissom .
- **S4**：Despite the best efforts of newly-elected district attorney Harvey Dent  and police commissioner James Gordon , the Gotham City Police Department remains corrupt.
- **S5**：Reporter Alexander Knox  and photojournalist Vicki Vale  begin investigating the rumors of a shadowy vigilante figure dressed as a bat who has been fighting criminals throughout the city.
- **S6**：Vicki and Knox attend a benefit at Wayne Manor, where Bruce is taken by Vicki's charms and spends the night with her.
- **S7**：That same night, Grissom's second in command, Jack Napier , is sent to raid the Axis Chemicals factory.
- **S8**：After the police receive a tip-off and arrive to arrest him, Napier realizes he has been set up by his boss as revenge for his affair with Grissom's mistress.
- **S9**：In the midst of the shoot-out, Batman arrives and takes out Napier's henchmen.
- **S10**：In the ensuing struggle, Napier shoots at Batman, who deflects the bullet with his metal-reinforced gauntlet and sends it back in Napier's face, tearing it open.
- **S11**：Reeling from the pain, Napier topples over a platform rail and falls into a vat of chemicals, even as Batman tries to save him.
- **S12**：The chemicals and a botched attempt at plastic surgery leave him with chalk white skin, red lips, green hair, and a permanent rictal grin.
- **S13**：Driven insane by his reflection, he reinvents himself as "the Joker", a master criminal and "homicidal artist".
- **S14**：After killing Grissom, the Joker takes over his empire and holds the city at his mercy by chemically altering everyday hygiene products, causing those using a certain combination of products to laugh to death.
- **S15**：Batman attempts to track down the Joker, who has become obsessed with Vicki.
- **S16**：During an encounter with the Joker, Batman recognizes him as the criminal who murdered his parents.
- **S17**：Bruce's butler, Alfred Pennyworth , lets Vicki into the Batcave, where she tells Bruce she is in love with him.
- **S18**：Bruce promises to pursue a relationship with her after he has defeated the Joker.
- **S19**：Batman destroys the factory the Joker used to make the poisoned products and creates an antidote.
- **S20**：The Joker holds a parade through Gotham, luring its citizens on to its streets by dispensing money, intending to kill them with lethal gas.
- **S21**：Batman foils his plan, but the Joker kidnaps Vicki and takes her to the top of a cathedral church.
- **S22**：After a fight with Batman, the Joker tries to escape on a helicopter, but Batman uses a grappling hook to snare the Joker's legs to a gargoyle; the Joker falls to his death when the gargoyle breaks loose of its moorings.
- **S23**：Commissioner Gordon unveils the Bat-Signal along with a note from Batman read by Harvey Dent, promising to defend Gotham whenever crime strikes again.

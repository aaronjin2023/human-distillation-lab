### Hal Stewart

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-1228cefec400209c` · 独立版本：`profile-8fb62897cb570a72`

- **性格倾向**：在失恋和得知受操纵后，倾向以强大力量报复外界（行为推断；S15、S17、S18、S23）
- **情绪反应**：对罗克珊怀有爱慕，后来陷入心碎与怨愤的状态（摘要直接叙述；S10、S15）

- **决策方式**：为迫使超级头脑迎战，选择绑架罗克珊并威胁其生命（摘要直接叙述；S23）

矛盾与变化：被培养为城市英雄，却把超能力用于牟利与威胁。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Megamind  is a super-intelligent alien, and the supervillian of Metro City.
- **S2**：He continually battles - and loses - against his nemesis, Metro Man , a rivalry that has extended since the two arrived on Earth as infants.
- **S3**：On the day of dedication of a museum in Metro Man's honor, Megamind and his sidekick Minion  kidnap reporter Roxanne Ritchi  to lure Metro Man into a copper-lined observatory.
- **S4**：Inside, Metro Man appears to become weak from the copper, and everyone is surprised when Megamind's death ray appears to kill Metro Man.
- **S5**：Megamind revels in his victory, but this is short-lived as without a nemesis, his villainy has no purpose.
- **S6**：While attempting to destroy the Metro Man museum, Megamind uses a holographic disguise of the curator, Bernard , to talk with Roxanne.
- **S7**：She gives him the idea of creating a serum containing Metro Man's DNA to inject in a worthy target, thus creating a new superhero for Megamind to fight.
- **S8**：Later, as Megamind finishes the serum in his lair and locates a worthy target, Roxanne sneaks into the lair, and in the resulting chaos, Megamind injects the serum into Roxanne's dimwitted camera man, Hal .
- **S9**：Megamind disguises himself as Hal's "space dad" to groom the new hero into shape to fight Megamind in a few days; Hal takes the superhero alias of "Tighten", a result of mishearing Megamind's suggestion of Titan.
- **S10**：During this, Megamind, in the Bernard disguise, continues to see Roxanne and becomes close to her, unaware that Hal also had romantic interests in her.
- **S11**：Minion expresses discontent at Megamind's lack of villainy and angrily leaves him.
- **S12**：On the night before the battle, while dining with Roxanne, the holographic disguise fails revealing his identity, and Roxanne leaves him.
- **S13**：Furthermore, he loses track of his invisible car that has the anti-serum to restore Hal to normal.
- **S14**：Upset, he vows to fight Hal the next day, but Hal does not show up at the appointed time.
- **S15**：Megamind finds a bitter and heart-broken Hal having used his powers for ill-gotten gains.
- **S16**：Megamind is horrified at this change and attempts to convince Hal to be a "hero" by revealing how he has manipulated Hal.
- **S17**：Hal realizes he has been toyed with and battles Megamind.
- **S18**：Megamind attempts to capture Hal in a copper-lined trap, but it fails to weaken Hal, and the villain is forced to flee Metro City, while Hal begins to threaten the city with his powers.
- **S19**：Megamind meets with Roxanne who takes him to Metro Man's secret lair, and both are surprised to find Metro Man there.
- **S20**：The former hero explains on the fateful day, he had an epiphany and pretended to be killed as to forego the superhero career and become a musician.
- **S21**：He refuses to help stop Hal.
- **S22**：Roxanne tries to convince Megamind that he could be the hero, but Megamind has resigned himself to be the villain and turns himself in at prison.
- **S23**：When Hal kidnaps Roxanne and threatens her life if Megamind does not fight him, Megamind has a change of heart and requests the warden to let him go, surprised to find the warden is Minion in disguise, having returned to help out.
- **S24**：Megamind and Minion fight Hal by holographically disguising themselves as Metro Man and Megamind, respectively, but a slip of the tongue reveals their true identity.
- **S25**：As the disguise is broken, another battle ensues.
- **S26**：In the fray, Megamind discovers his lost invisible car.
- **S27**：He grabs the anti-serum and heroically injects Hal with it, reverting the superhero to a normal man.
- **S28**：Hal is taken away while Roxanne convinces Metro City's citizens that Megamind chose to be their hero.
- **S29**：Later, Megamind and Roxanne's relationship has become close, and the city welcomes Megamind as their new protector, while a disguised Metro Man, hidden in the crowd, quietly congratulates him.

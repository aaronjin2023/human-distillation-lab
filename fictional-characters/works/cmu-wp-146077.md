### Jamie Graham

- **性格倾向**：在拘禁与匮乏环境中倾向主动适应，善于建立跨越身份的联系（行为推断；S6、S7、S10）

- **关系模式**：失去父母陪伴后，把医生视为父亲般人物，同时持续崇拜巴希（摘要直接叙述；S2、S9、S11）

### Basie

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a890bb83b797043a` · 独立版本：`profile-227a58b114c4d59e`

- **性格倾向**：在战俘营生存压力下倾向精于算计，愿把他人的风险当作自身资源（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：围绕逃离拘禁和获取物资组织行动，优先争取自身生存机会（行为推断；S14、S21、S27）
- **决策方式**：借捕猎任务让吉姆试探营外雷区，事后给予入住营房的奖励（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **价值取舍**：真正出现逃脱机会时，把个人脱身置于履行带走吉姆的承诺之前（行为推断；S21）
- **关系模式**：获得吉姆崇拜却曾利用并遗弃他，后来的援助承诺也遭到拒绝（行为推断；S11、S12、S21、S30）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The Empire of Japan had been at war with China since 1937 before declaring war on the United States and the United Kingdom.
- **S2**：During the conflict, Jamie Graham, a British upper middle class schoolboy living in Shanghai, is separated from his parents.
- **S3**：He spends some time living in his deserted house and eating remnants of food; eventually, he ventures out into the city and finds it bustling with Japanese troops.
- **S4**：Jamie is captured along with Basie, an American sailor, who nicknames him "Jim".
- **S5**：They are taken to Lunghua Civilian Assembly Center in Shanghai, but are eventually moved to Suzhou Creek Internment Camp.
- **S6**：By 1945, a few months before the end of the Pacific War, Jim has established a good living, despite the poor conditions of the camp.
- **S7**：He has an extensive trading network, even involving the camp's commanding officer, Sergeant Nagata.
- **S8**：Dr.
- **S9**：Rawlins, the camp's British doctor, becomes a father figure to Jim.
- **S10**：Through the barbwire fencing, Jim befriends a Japanese teenager, who shares Jim's dream of becoming a pilot.
- **S11**：Still idolizing Basie, Jim frequently visits him in the American soldiers' barracks.
- **S12**：At one point, Basie charges him to set snare traps outside the wire of the camp and while Jim succeeds, thanks to the help of the Japanese teenager from the other side, the real reason for sending Jim into the marsh was actually to test the area for mines, not to catch game.
- **S13**：As a reward, Basie allows him to move into the American barracks with him.
- **S14**：Basie then plots to escape.
- **S15**：Nagata visits Basie's barracks and Nagata beats him severely after discovering a stolen bar of Japanese soap hidden under a table.
- **S16**：While Basie is in the infirmary, his possessions are stolen by other men in the camp.
- **S17**：One morning at dawn, Jim witnesses a kamikaze ritual of three Japanese pilots at the air base.
- **S18**：Overcome with emotion at the solemnity of the ceremony, he begins to sing the Welsh song Suo Gân.
- **S19**：Later, the camp comes under attack by a group of American P-51 Mustang fighter aircraft.
- **S20**：As a result of the attack, the Japanese decide to evacuate the camp.
- **S21**：During the confusion of the attack, Basie escapes, leaving Jim behind, although he had promised to let Jim come with him.
- **S22**：The camp's population marches through the wilderness, where many die of fatigue, starvation, and disease.
- **S23**：During the march, Jim witnesses a flash from the atomic bombing of Nagasaki hundreds of miles away, and later hears news of Japan's surrender and the end of the war.
- **S24**：Jim sneaks away from the group and goes back to Soochow Creek, nearly dead from starvation.
- **S25**：He encounters the Japanese teenager he knew earlier, who has since become a pilot and appears distraught at the surrender of his country.
- **S26**：The youth remembers Jim and offers him a mango, cutting it for him with his katana.
- **S27**：As Jim is about to eat it, Basie reappears with a group of armed Americans, who have arrived to loot the Red Cross containers that were dropped after the Japanese surrender.
- **S28**：One of the Americans, thinking Jim is in danger, shoots and kills the Japanese youth.
- **S29**：Jim, furious, beats the American who shot his friend.
- **S30**：Basie drags him off and promises to take him back to Shanghai to find his parents, but Jim refuses the offer and stays behind.
- **S31**：He is found by American soldiers and put in an orphanage in Shanghai with other children who had lost their parents.
- **S32**：When his parents come looking for him, Jim is so scarred from his experiences that he does not recognize them at first.

### Peter Venkman

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-8974a21b4e66909d` · 独立版本：`profile-0fde19a24d649275`

- **性格倾向**：在超自然危机中，他倾向迅速形成解释并主动引导同伴应对（行为推断；S15）
- **核心动机**：失去大学职位后，他与伙伴希望通过自主业务延续超自然研究（行为推断；S1、S2）
- **决策方式**：面对由想象决定形态的威胁，他选择先约束团队思绪以避免触发（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **关系模式**：他对黛娜案件的投入夹带追求好感的愿望，并将邻居视为竞争者（摘要直接叙述；S7、S8）

### Raymond Stantz

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-473e33c6352fbf05` · 独立版本：`profile-d06ee4d11f879e88`

- **性格倾向**：在要求清空思绪的压力下，他倾向借熟悉的童年意象获得安心（行为推断；S15、S16）
- **核心动机**：学术工作中断后，他与伙伴通过捉鬼服务继续实践共同研究（行为推断；S1、S2、S4）
- **价值取舍**：面对毁灭形态的选择，他希望想到绝不伤人的对象来保护大家（行为推断；S16）
- **决策方式**：原有应对失败后，他参与团队合并能量束并直接攻击入口的方案（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17、S18）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Following their first encounter with a ghost, misfit parapsychologists Peter Venkman , Raymond Stantz , and Egon Spengler  lose their jobs at Columbia University.
- **S2**：Unable to research their discovery, the trio establish a paranormal exterminator service known as "Ghostbusters" in a retired firehouse.
- **S3**：Lacking customers and faced with dwindling funds, they are eventually hired by the Sedgewick Hotel to investigate a haunting.
- **S4**：At the hotel, they capture their first ghost and deposit it in a "containment unit" located in the firehouse basement.
- **S5**：Paranormal activity begins to increase in New York City, and the Ghostbusters become celebrities containing it, while at the same time becoming increasingly burdened by the hectic schedule.
- **S6**：The group later hire a fourth member, Winston Zeddemore , to help them cope with the demands.
- **S7**：The Ghostbusters are hired by Dana Barrett , whose apartment is haunted by a demonic spirit called Zuul, a demigod worshipped as a servant to Gozer the Gozerian, a Sumerian shape-changer god of destruction.
- **S8**：Venkman takes a particular interest in the case, competing for Dana's affection with her neighbor, Louis Tully .
- **S9**：As they investigate, Dana is possessed by Zuul, which declares itself "The Gatekeeper", and Louis by a similar demon called Vinz Clortho, "The Keymaster." Both demons speak of the coming of the destructive Gozer, and the Ghostbusters plan to keep the two apart.
- **S10**：Thereafter, the Ghostbusters' office is visited by Walter Peck , a lawyer representing the EPA, who arrests the team for operating an unlicensed nuclear device in their basement and orders their ghost containment grid deactivated, unleashing hundreds of captured ghosts onto the city.
- **S11**：Freed from the Ghostbusters' custody, Louis/Vinz advances toward Dana/Zuul's apartment while the escaped ghosts create havoc throughout the city.
- **S12**：Consulting blueprints of Dana's apartment, the Ghostbusters learn that it was built by mad doctor and cult leader Ivo Shandor, who designed the building as a gateway to summon Gozer and bring about the end of the world, claiming humanity was too sick to survive after the horrors of World War I.
- **S13**：The Ghostbusters are brought to the mayor's office and freed in order to combat the paranormal activity, but are unable to prevent the arrival of Gozer, who initially appears as a woman .
- **S14**：Briefly subdued by the team, Gozer disappears, but her voice echoes that the "destructor" will follow, taking a form chosen by the team.
- **S15**：Venkman, holding that this means that whatever they imagine will appear as a destroying force, urges his comrades to avoid giving form to the destructor by clearing their minds.
- **S16**：Unable to keep his mind blank, Stantz remembers a beloved corporate mascot from childhood, "something that could never, ever possibly destroy us," whereupon the destructor arrives in Stantz's chosen form of the giant Stay Puft Marshmallow Man and begins laying waste to the city.
- **S17**：To defeat this manifestation, the team decides to merge the energy streams of their proton packs  while directing these against Gozer's entrance.
- **S18**：The plan succeeds, banishing Gozer back to whence it came, sealing the gateway between the worlds, and destroying the Marshmallow Man in a single explosion.
- **S19**：Soon thereafter, Dana and Louis are freed from the ashen remains of their possessors.
- **S20**：As hundreds of New Yorkers wipe the melted marshmallow goo from their brows, the Ghostbusters are applauded by the city's population.

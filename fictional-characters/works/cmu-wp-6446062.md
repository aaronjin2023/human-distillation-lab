## 角色资料

### James Bond

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-360901462b1a4d14` · 独立版本：`profile-5060dd19cb658d8b`

- **性格倾向**：在连续遇袭和被捕的处境中，他倾向主动寻找反制机会（行为推断；S3、S8、S22、S25）
- **核心动机**：调查航天飞机失窃后，他进一步致力于阻止全球灭绝计划（行为推断；S1、S27、S31）
- **决策方式**：他倾向保留实物证据并串联地点线索推动调查（行为推断；S7、S12、S15、S20、S21）
- **沟通方式**：面对敌方打手，他通过揭穿其被清洗的风险促成倒戈（行为推断；S30）

### Hugo Drax

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0b3f767eb2befd8d` · 独立版本：`profile-3f9a20ab576946b2`

- **性格倾向**：在统治计划中，他表现出强烈的控制欲与排除异己倾向（行为推断；S9、S27、S28、S29、S30）
- **核心动机**：他企图清除现有人类，让筛选者的后代建立新种族秩序（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28、S29）
- **价值取舍**：他以身体标准划分生存资格，将不符合者视为可消灭对象（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29、S30）
- **决策方式**：他以隐蔽基地和定向毒剂实施大规模人口替换计划（行为推断；S11、S26、S27、S28、S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：依赖Jaws执行任务，却计划消灭不符合自身标准的人

### Jaws

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-64a25d390d3c1107` · 独立版本：`profile-5d21c1a03b42604e`

- **性格倾向**：作为受雇追杀者时，他表现出反复追击目标的执拗倾向（行为推断；S2、S17、S18、S21、S23）
- **核心动机**：执行受雇任务时，他以追捕并消灭Bond为行动目标（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18、S23）
- **关系模式**：获Dolly救援后，他与她相爱并在危机中共同转换立场（摘要直接叙述；S19、S30）
- **成长变化**：意识到自身也受清洗威胁后，他从追杀者转为逃生协助者（摘要直接叙述；S30、S36）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：雇佣关系的忠诚与自己及Dolly的生存利益发生冲突

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A Drax Industries Moonraker space shuttle on loan to the United Kingdom is hijacked in mid-air and MI6 operative, James Bond, agent 007, is recalled from Africa to investigate.
- **S2**：En route in a small plane, on an unrelated case, Bond is attacked by the pilot and crew and is pushed out of the plane by the mercenary assassin Jaws.
- **S3**：Bond survives by stealing a parachute from the pilot, whilst Jaws lands on a circus tent.
- **S4**：Bond proceeds to the Drax Industries shuttle-manufacturing complex where he meets the owner of the company, Hugo Drax, and henchman Chang.
- **S5**：Bond also meets an astronaut, Dr.
- **S6**：Holly Goodhead and survives an assassination attempt via a centrifuge chamber.
- **S7**：Bond is later aided by Drax's personal pilot, Corinne Dufour, as he finds blueprints for a glass vial made in Venice.
- **S8**：Bond then foils another attempt on his life, using a hunting shotgun to shoot a sniper.
- **S9**：Upon discovering that Dufour assisted Bond's investigations, Drax has her killed.
- **S10**：Bond again encounters Goodhead in Venice where he is chased through the canals by Drax's henchmen.
- **S11**：He discovers a secret biological laboratory, and by accidentally poisoning the scientists there, he learns that the glass vials are to hold a nerve gas deadly to humans, but harmless to animals.
- **S12**：Chang attacks Bond and is killed, but during the fight, Bond finds evidence that Drax is moving his operation to Rio de Janeiro.
- **S13**：Rejoining Goodhead, he deduces that she is a CIA agent spying on Drax.
- **S14**：They promise to work together, but quickly dispense with the truce.
- **S15**：Bond has saved one of the vials he found earlier, as the only evidence of the now-empty laboratory, giving it to M for analysis, who permits him to go to Rio de Janeiro.
- **S16**：In Rio, Bond meets his Brazilian contact Manuela.
- **S17**：Drax hires Jaws to finish Chang's job of eliminating Bond.
- **S18**：Bond meets Goodhead at the top of Sugarloaf Mountain, where they are attacked by Jaws on a cable car.
- **S19**：After Jaws' car crashes he is rescued by Dolly from the rubble, and the two fall in love.
- **S20**：Bond and Goodhead are captured by henchmen, but Bond escapes and reports to an MI6 base in Brazil and learns that the toxin comes from a rare orchid indigenous to the Amazon jungle.
- **S21**：Bond travels the Amazon River looking for Drax's research facility and again encounters Jaws and other henchmen.
- **S22**：Bond escapes from his boat just before it hits the Iguazu Falls, and finds Drax's base.
- **S23**：Captured by Jaws again, Bond is taken to Drax and witnesses four Moonrakers lifting off.
- **S24**：Drax explains that he stole the Moonraker because another in the fleet had developed a fault during assembly.
- **S25**：Bond is reunited with Goodhead; they escape and successfully pose as pilots on the sixth shuttle.
- **S26**：The shuttles dock with Drax's hidden space station.
- **S27**：Drax plans to destroy human life by launching fifty globes containing the toxin into the Earth's atmosphere.
- **S28**：Before launching them, Drax also transported several dozen genetically perfect young men and women of varying races, to the space station.
- **S29**：They would live there until Earth was safe again for human life; their descendants would be the seed for a "new master race".
- **S30**：Bond persuades Jaws and Dolly to switch their allegiance by getting Drax to admit that anyone not measuring up to his physical standards would be exterminated and Jaws attacks Drax's guards.
- **S31**：Before the battle Drax launched three of the globes towards Earth, which Goodhead and Bond destroy from their shuttle then Bond and Goodhead disable the radar jammer hiding the station from Earth.
- **S32**：The US sends a platoon of Marines in a military shuttle.
- **S33**：A laser battle ensues in which Drax's guards as well as his new master race die.
- **S34**：During the battle, Bond shoots Drax with a cyanide-tipped dart, then pushes him into an airlock and ejects him into space.
- **S35**：The space station, heavily damaged in the battle, disintegrates.
- **S36**：Jaws helps Bond and Goodhead escape in Drax's space shuttle.
- **S37**：They too escape the space station as their module breaks away before the station explodes.

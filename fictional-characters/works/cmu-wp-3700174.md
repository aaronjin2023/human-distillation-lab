## 角色资料

### Alan Parrish

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-1a28fc49d49a32c9` · 独立版本：`profile-3cbb0b4fbd74fa52`

- **性格倾向**：在危机接连发生时倾向主动处理问题，并寻找游戏运作的规律（行为推断；S13、S16、S19）
- **核心动机**：在意识到旧局仍未结束后，以完成游戏并消除灾难为行动目标（行为推断；S16、S17、S19）
- **决策方式**：为使游戏继续进行，曾倾向用欺骗促使不愿参与的同伴回归（行为推断；S17）
- **成长变化**：重获童年机会后，他从与父亲冲突逃避转向和解并承认过错（摘要直接叙述；S5、S20、S21）
- **价值取舍**：得知未来悲剧后，倾向利用自身经验保护他人的家庭完整（行为推断；S25）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：推动同伴共同解决灾难，却曾通过欺骗让她重新涉险。

### Sarah Whittle

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-c8e0f2f0c5dc146c` · 独立版本：`profile-c43e30d50ea5085c`

- **性格倾向**：在最初遭遇超常威胁时倾向因恐惧退避，但并非始终逃避危险（行为推断；S8、S18）
- **决策方式**：经历游戏危害后，倾向通过封锁并丢弃源头来阻断再次接触（行为推断；S23）
- **关系模式**：她与艾伦的关系从童年朋友发展为共同经历危机后的伴侣（摘要直接叙述；S5、S23、S24）
- **价值取舍**：面对已知会致命的旅行安排，倾向主动干预以保护另一家庭（行为推断；S25）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1869, two boys bury a game board in a forest near Brandford, New Hampshire.
- **S2**：A century later, 12-year-old Alan Parrish flees from a gang of bullies to a shoe factory owned by his father, Sam, where he meets his friend Carl Bentley, one of Sam's employees.
- **S3**：When Alan accidentally damages a machine with a prototype sneaker Carl hopes to patent, Carl takes the blame and loses his job.
- **S4**：Outside the factory, after the bullies beat him up and steal his bicycle, Alan follows the sound of tribal drumbeats to a construction site and finds the chest, containing a board game called Jumanji.
- **S5**：Alan takes the game home and is attempting to run away after having an argument with his father, about attending a boarding school, when his friend Sarah Whittle gives his bike back.
- **S6**：The two begin a game of Jumanji, which acts strangely: When a player rolls the dice, the player's piece moves itself and a message appears on the board.
- **S7**：When Alan makes his first move, he is sucked into a jungle.
- **S8**：Although the message states that he will be freed when a five or an eight is rolled, Sarah quits the game after being attacked by African bats.
- **S9**：Twenty-six years later, Judy and Peter Shepherd move into the Parrish house with their aunt Nora after losing their parents in a skiing accident.
- **S10**：Judy and Peter hear Jumanjis drumbeats and play the game in the attic, and as a result, giant mosquitoes attack them, and reddish-orange monkeys destroy their kitchen.
- **S11**：Realizing that everything will be restored when the game ends, they continue the game despite the danger.
- **S12**：Peter rolls a five, releasing both a lion and Alan, who is now an adult.
- **S13**：Alan locks the lion in a bedroom, then goes to the now closed shoe factory.
- **S14**：On the way, he meets Carl, working as a police officer, and discovers that the town's economy was devastated by the factory's closure.
- **S15**：In the factory, a homeless man reveals that Sam abandoned the business to search for his son until his death in 1991.
- **S16**：Alan joins the game with Judy and Peter, but when rolling the dice has no effect on the board, Alan realizes they are continuing the game he and Sarah started years ago.
- **S17**：Finding Sarah at her house, Alan tricks her into rejoining the game, and the following moves release man-eating vines from a giant flower, a big-game hunter named Van Pelt who has it out personally for Alan, and an animal stampede .
- **S18**：Among other things, Peter transforms into a monkey after trying to cheat; Peter, Sarah and Judy battle Van Pelt in a local department store; a monsoon floods the house; a crocodile attacks the group; an earthquake breaks the house in two; Alan is sucked into the floor by quicksand; large poisonous spiders come out; and Judy is shot by a poisonous barb from a flower.
- **S19**：Finally, Alan wins the game just in time when Van Pelt is about to shoot him, causing all jungle elements to be sucked back into the board.
- **S20**：After that, Alan and Sarah suddenly find themselves back in 1969 again, once again children, but with full knowledge of their lives after they started playing.
- **S21**：Alan reconciles with Sam and admits to his father that he was the one who damaged the machine.
- **S22**：Carl gets his job back, and Sam allows his son to attend a local school if he wishes to do so.
- **S23**：Alan and Sarah chain up the Jumanji board and throw it into a river, where they kiss before leaving.
- **S24**：Twenty-six years later, Alan and Sarah are married and expecting for their first child, Alan has taken over the shoe business, Carl still works in the factory as the plant supervisor, and Sam is retired, but still alive.
- **S25**：When Judy, Peter, and their parents meet with Alan and Sarah at a Christmas party, they offer Judy and Peter's father a job in the shoe company, also discouraging them from going on the ski trip, that would've killed them.
- **S26**：Meanwhile, two French-speaking girls hear drumbeats as they walk along a beach, where the Jumanji board is half buried in the sand.

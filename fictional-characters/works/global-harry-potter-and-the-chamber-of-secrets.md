## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] While spending the summer of 1992 with the Dursleys , the twelve-year-old Harry Potter is visited by Dobby , a house-elf .
- **S3**：Dobby says Harry is in danger and must promise not to return to Hogwarts .
- **S4**：When Harry refuses, Dobby uses magic to destroy a pudding made by Aunt Petunia.
- **S5**：Believing that Harry created the mess, Uncle Vernon locks him in his room.
- **S6**：The Ministry of Magic sends a notice accusing Harry of performing underage magic and threatening to expel him from Hogwarts if it happens again.
- **S7**：The Weasley brothers Ron , Fred , and George arrive in their father's flying car and take Harry to their home.
- **S8**：When Harry and the Weasleys go to Diagon Alley for school supplies, they meet Gilderoy Lockhart , a celebrity author who is the new Defence Against the Dark Arts professor.
- **S9**：At King's Cross station , Harry and Ron cannot enter Platform 9¾ to board the Hogwarts Express , so they fly to Hogwarts in the enchanted car.
- **S10**：During the 1992-1993 school year, Harry hears a strange voice emanating from the castle walls.
- **S11**：Argus Filch 's cat is found magically immobilised, along with a warning scrawled on the wall: "The Chamber of Secrets has been opened.
- **S12**：Enemies of the heir, beware".
- **S13**：Harry learns that the Chamber supposedly houses a monster that attacks Muggle-born students, and which only the Heir of Slytherin can control.
- **S14**：During a Quidditch match, a rogue Bludger strikes Harry, breaking his arm.
- **S15**：Professor Lockhart botches an attempt to mend the injury, which sends Harry to the hospital wing.
- **S16**：Dobby visits Harry and reveals that he jinxed the Bludger and sealed the portal at King's Cross.
- **S17**：He also tells Harry that house-elves are bound to serve a master, and cannot be freed unless their master gives them clothing.
- **S18**：After another attack from the monster, students attend a defensive duelling class.
- **S19**：During the class, Harry displays the rare ability to speak Parseltongue , the language of snakes.
- **S20**：Moaning Myrtle , a ghost who haunts a bathroom, shows Harry and his friends a diary that was left in her stall.
- **S21**：It belonged to Tom Riddle , a student who witnessed another student's death when the Chamber was last opened.
- **S22**：During the next attack by the monster, Hermione Granger is immobilised.
- **S23**：Harry and Ron learn that the monster is a Basilisk , a gigantic snake that can kill victims with a direct gaze and immobilise them with an indirect gaze.
- **S24**：Harry realizes the Basilisk is producing the voice that he hears in the walls.
- **S25**：After Ron's sister Ginny is abducted and taken into the Chamber, Harry and Ron discover the Chamber entrance in Myrtle's bathroom.
- **S26**：When they force Lockhart to enter with them, he confesses that the stories he told of his heroic adventures are fabrications.
- **S27**：He attempts to erase the boys's memories, but his spell backfires and obliterates his own memory.
- **S28**：Harry finds Ginny unconscious in the Chamber.
- **S29**：A manifestation of Tom Riddle appears and reveals that Riddle is Lord Voldemort and the Heir of Slytherin.
- **S30**：After explaining that he opened the Chamber, Riddle summons the Basilisk to kill Harry.
- **S31**：Dumbledore's phoenix Fawkes arrives, bringing Harry the Sorting Hat .
- **S32**：While Fawkes blinds the Basilisk, Harry pulls the Sword of Gryffindor from the Hat.
- **S33**：He slays the serpent, then stabs the diary with a Basilisk fang, destroying it and the manifestation of Riddle.
- **S34**：Later, Harry liberates Dobby by tricking his master, Lucius Malfoy, into giving him clothing.
- **S35**：At the end of the novel, the immobilised students are cured and Gryffindor wins the House Cup.

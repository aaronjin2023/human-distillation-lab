## 角色资料

### Marty

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-cc65b70c674c8b6e` · 独立版本：`profile-2060acf650e96fa9`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中向往自由探索，离开动物园后欣然接受荒野生活。（行为推断；S1、S13）
- **核心动机**：他想离开受照料的动物园，亲眼看看外面的广阔世界。（摘要直接叙述；S1）
- **决策方式**：看到船只返航后，他不顾同伴反对追赶Alex，希望送朋友回纽约。（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21）
- **关系模式**：他把Alex视为挚友，在Alex害怕伤人时仍努力劝他回去。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S22）
- **成长变化**：他起初想探索园外世界，后来因Alex的变化而后悔离开。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S19）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他曾认定荒野正是理想生活，看到Alex受困后又后悔离园。

### Alex

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-770c59c1b2e7b526` · 独立版本：`profile-39cb92008efe253e`

- **性格倾向**：在与Marty相处时表现出关切，也会因自身失控风险而主动疏远。（行为推断；S2、S22）
- **核心动机**：他起初试图求救回到文明生活，后来因怕再攻击Marty而拒绝返程。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S22）
- **决策方式**：面对Marty劝返，他因害怕再次攻击朋友而选择拒绝。（摘要直接叙述；S22）
- **关系模式**：他与Marty是好友，却曾受捕猎本能支配而攻击Marty。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S17）
- **成长变化**：他从怪罪Marty到逐渐适应岛屿，经历失控后最终克服捕猎本能。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S14、S17、S24）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他曾攻击Marty并被放逐，后来又克服本能赶走威胁狐猴的掠食者。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：At the Central Park Zoo, Marty the zebra is celebrating his tenth birthday, but longs to see the rest of the world from outside his pampered life at the zoo, believing that he can find wide-open spaces to run around in, like Connecticut.
- **S2**：Marty's best friend, Alex the lion attempts to cheer up his friend by singing Frank Sinatra's "New York, New York" with him.
- **S3**：Still unsatisfied, Marty gets some tips from the zoo's penguins; Skipper, Kowalski, Rico and Private.
- **S4**：The penguins are similarly trying to escape the zoo.
- **S5**：Marty's friends—Alex the lion, Melman the giraffe and Gloria the hippopotamus—realize Marty's folly and try to follow him.
- **S6**：The four, along with the penguins and the chimpanzees Mason and his silent friend Phil eventually find themselves at Grand Central Station, but are quickly sedated by tranquilizer darts when Alex's attempt to communicate with humans is mistaken for aggression.
- **S7**：The zoo  is forced to ship the animals, by sea, to a Kenyan wildlife preserve.
- **S8**：During their travels, the penguins escape from their enclosure and take over the ship, intent on taking it to Antarctica.
- **S9**：Their antics on the bridge cause the crates containing Alex, Marty, Melmen, and Gloria to fall off the boat and wash ashore on Madagascar.
- **S10**：The animals are soon able to regroup, initially believing themselves to be in the zoo at San Diego, California.
- **S11**：Upon exploring, however, they come across a pack of lemurs led by King Julien XIII, and quickly learn of their true location.
- **S12**：Alex blames Marty for their predicament and attempts to signal for help to get back to civilization.
- **S13**：Marty, on the other hand, finds the wild to be exactly what he was looking for, with Gloria and Melman soon joining him in enjoying the island.
- **S14**：Alex eventually comes around, though his hunting instincts begin to show; he has been away from the pampered zoo life of prepacked steaks for too long.
- **S15**：The group is accepted by the lemurs, though King Julien's adviser, Maurice, cautions them about Alex's predatory nature.
- **S16**：King Julien ignores Maurice's concerns and persuades the group to help the lemurs fend off the fossa, who hunt the lemurs as prey.
- **S17**：While initially Alex scares the fossa away and is worshiped by the lemurs, he later falls under his instincts and attacks Marty.
- **S18**：Realizing that Alex is now a threat to them, King Julien banishes Alex to the far side of the island, where the fossa live.
- **S19**：Marty begins to regret his decision to leave the zoo, seeing what Alex has turned into.
- **S20**：The penguins, having been to Antarctica and found that it "sucks," land the boat on Madagascar.
- **S21**：Seeing this as a chance to return Alex to New York, Marty rushes after his friend against the wishes of the others.
- **S22**：Marty attempts to convince the now grizzled, starving Alex to return by using the "New York" song but Alex refuses in fear of attacking him again.
- **S23**：The penguins, Gloria and Melman go to find Marty, but are trapped by the fossa.
- **S24**：At the last minute, Alex finally overcomes his predatory nature and scares the fossa away from the lemur territory forever.
- **S25**：The lemurs regain their respect for Alex and the penguins help him satisfy his hunger through sushi instead of steak.
- **S26**：As the lemurs throw a bon voyage celebration for the foursome, the penguins decide not to break the news that the ship has run out of fuel and that they are still stuck on the island, setting the stage for Madagascar: Escape 2 Africa.

## 角色资料

### Michael "Crocodile" Dundee

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-37fab670c5ff1f8d` · 独立版本：`profile-685124a08aa0870e`

- **性格倾向**：在陌生的制片厂环境中，他倾向留意反常细节并主动追查（行为推断；S5、S8）
- **核心动机**：他参与卧底调查，目标是查明报社前任负责人的离奇死亡（摘要直接叙述；S5）
- **决策方式**：面对犯罪团伙时，他倾向利用现场道具与动物寻找突破口（行为推断；S11）
- **关系模式**：他愿随伴侣赴美，并在调查与育儿之间借助共同熟人的支持（行为推断；S3、S6）
- **价值取舍**：在查案占用家庭时间时，他仍愿投入精力协助调查死因（行为推断；S5、S6）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：主动追查异常画作，却最初误以为涉及毒品走私。

### Sue Charlton

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-9e216aa8e79432a5` · 独立版本：`profile-f552b7e0f64ac5a4`

- **性格倾向**：在制片厂调查中，她倾向亲自参与取证并共同应对犯罪团伙（行为推断；S10、S11）
- **核心动机**：她随同伴争取取得画作证据，推动揭开制片厂的犯罪活动（行为推断；S8、S9、S10）
- **决策方式**：面对调查与育儿的冲突，她选择请熟人协助以维持行动（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **关系模式**：她与米克共同承担跨国生活与查案压力，并最终正式结婚（摘要直接叙述；S3、S6、S11）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：At the beginning of the film, protagonist Michael "Crocodile" Dundee  is living in the Australian Outback with Sue Charlton  and their young son Mikey .
- **S2**：Because Crocodile hunting has been made illegal, Mick is reduced to wrestling crocodiles for the entertainment of tourists, having as his rival in the business another Outback survivalist named Jacko .
- **S3**：When an opportunity arises for Sue to become the Los Angeles bureau chief of a newspaper owned by her father, Mick and his family cross the Pacific to California.
- **S4**：In the United States, Mick and his son's encounters with the locals cause cross-cultural mishaps.
- **S5**：Mick becomes an undercover amateur sleuth helping to probe the mysterious death of his wife's predecessor at the newspaper, while Mikey attends a local school, where he quickly impresses his classmates and teacher with his outback survival skills.
- **S6**：Because the case takes up so much of their time, Mick and Sue eventually call in Jacko to babysit their son; gradually, Jacko and Mikey's teacher become interested in each other.
- **S7**：It is revealed that the dead reporter had been investigating a film studio, which is about to make a sequel to the action film Lethal Agent, despite the title's commercial failure.
- **S8**：Mick becomes suspicious when several paintings from Southern Europe are brought onto the set; although at first he suspects drug smuggling, the pictures themselves are revealed to be missing art from a museum in former Yugoslavia thought lost in the recent civil wars.
- **S9**：They are to appear in the movie as mere props, to be publicly 'destroyed' in a scene in which they are set on fire, at which point they will have been exchanged for copies.
- **S10**：Attempting to secure one of the paintings as evidence, Mick, Sue, and Jacko run afoul of the studio director and his thugs.
- **S11**：Using the studio's props and two lions used in filming to defeat the gangsters, Mick and Sue solve the case and return to Australia, where they are officially married.

- **S3**：All needless matters have been eliminated so that a history almost at variance with the possibilities of latter-day belief may stand forth as simple fact.
- **S4**：There is throughout no statement of past things wherein memory may err, for all the records chosen are exactly contemporary, given from the stand-points and within the range of knowledge of those who made them. — Epigraph to Part One of The Historian from Bram Stoker 's Dracula (1897) [ 4 ] The Historian interweaves the history and folklore of Vlad Țepeș , a 15th-century prince of Wallachia known as "Vlad the Impaler", and his fictional equivalent Count Dracula together with the story of Paul, a professor; his 16-year-old daughter; and their quest for Vlad's tomb.
- **S5**：The novel ties together three separate narratives using letters and oral accounts: that of Paul's mentor in the 1930s, that of Paul in the 1950s, and that of the narrator herself in the 1970s.
- **S6**：The tale is told primarily from the perspective of Paul's daughter, who is never named.
- **S7**：Part I [ edit ] Part I opens in 1972 Amsterdam .
- **S8**：The narrator finds an old vellum -bound book with a woodcut of a dragon in the center associated with Dracula.
- **S9**：When she asks her father Paul about it, he tells her how he found the handmade book in his study carrel when he was a graduate student in the 1950s.
- **S10**：Paul took the book to his mentor, Professor Bartholomew Rossi, and was shocked to find that Rossi had found a similar handmade book when he was a graduate student in the 1930s.
- **S11**：As a result, Rossi researched Țepeș, the Dracula myth surrounding him, and the mysterious book.
- **S12**：Rossi traveled as far as Istanbul ; however, the appearance of curious characters and unexplained events caused him to drop his investigation and return to his graduate work.
- **S13**：Rossi gives Paul his research notes and informs him that he believes Dracula is still alive.
- **S14**：The bulk of the novel focuses on the 1950s timeline, which follows Paul's adventures.
- **S15**：After meeting with Paul, Rossi disappears; smears of blood on his desk and the ceiling of his office are the only traces that remain.
- **S16**：Certain that something unfortunate has befallen his advisor, Paul begins to investigate Dracula.
- **S17**：While in the university library he meets a young, dark-haired woman reading a copy of Bram Stoker 's Dracula .
- **S18**：She is Helen Rossi, the daughter of Bartholomew Rossi, and she has become an expert on Dracula.
- **S19**：Paul attempts to convince her that one of the librarians is trying to prevent their research into Dracula, but she is unpersuaded.
- **S20**：Later, the librarian attacks and bites Helen.
- **S21**：Paul intervenes and overpowers him, but he wriggles free.
- **S22**：The librarian is then run over by a car in front of the library and apparently killed.
- **S23**：Upon hearing her father's story, the narrator becomes interested in the mystery and begins researching Dracula as she and her father travel across Europe during the 1970s.
- **S24**：Although he eventually sends her home, she does not remain there.
- **S25**：After finding letters addressed to her that reveal he has left on a quest to find her mother (previously believed to be dead), she sets out to find him.
- **S26**：As is slowly made clear in the novel, Helen is the narrator's mother.
- **S27**：The letters continue the story her father has been telling her.
- **S28**：The narrator decides to travel to a monastery where she believes her father might be.
- **S29**：Part II [ edit ] Part II begins as the narrator reads descriptions of her father and Helen's travels through Eastern Europe during the 1950s.
- **S30**：While on their travels, Helen and Paul conclude that Rossi might have been taken by Dracula to his tomb.
- **S31**：They travel to Istanbul to find the archives of Sultan Mehmed II , which Paul believes contain information regarding the location of the tomb.
- **S32**：They fortuitously meet Professor Turgut Bora from Istanbul University , who has also discovered a book similar to Paul's and Rossi's.
- **S33**：He has access to Mehmed's archive, and together they unearth several important documents.
- **S34**：They also see the librarian who was supposedly killed in the United States – he has survived because he is a vampire and he has continued following Helen and Paul.
- **S35**：Helen shoots the vampire librarian but misses his heart and consequently, he does not die.
- **S36**：From Istanbul, Paul and Helen travel to Budapest , Hungary, to further investigate the location of Dracula's tomb and to meet with Helen's mother, who they believe may have knowledge of Rossi – the two had met during his travels to Romania in the 1930s.
- **S37**：For the first time Helen hears of her mother and Rossi's torrid love affair.
- **S38**：Paul and Helen learn much, for example that Helen's mother, and therefore Helen herself and the narrator, are descendants of Vlad Țepeș.
- **S39**：Part III [ edit ] Part III begins with a revelation by Turgut Bora that leads the search for Dracula's tomb to Bulgaria .
- **S40**：He also reveals that he is part of an organization formed by Sultan Mehmed II from the elite of the Janissaries to fight the Order of the Dragon , an evil consortium later associated with Dracula.
- **S41**：In Bulgaria, Helen and Paul seek the assistance of a scholar named Anton Stoichev.
- **S42**：Through information gained from Stoichev, Helen and Paul discover that Dracula is most likely buried in the Bulgarian monastery of Sveti Georgi.
- **S43**：After many difficulties Paul and Helen discover the whereabouts of Sveti Georgi.
- **S44**：Upon reaching the monastery they find Rossi's interred body in the crypt and are forced to drive a silver dagger through his heart to prevent his full transformation into a vampire.
- **S45**：Before he dies, he reveals that Dracula is a scholar and has a secret library.
- **S46**：Rossi has written an account of his imprisonment in this library and hidden it there.
- **S47**：Paul and Helen are pursued to the monastery by political officials and by the vampire librarian – all of them are seeking Dracula's tomb, but it is empty when they arrive.
- **S48**：Paul and Helen move to the United States, marry, and Helen gives birth to the narrator.
- **S49**：However, she becomes depressed a few months afterwards.
- **S50**：She later confesses that she feared the taint of the vampiric bite that she acquired earlier would infect her child.
- **S51**：The family travels to Europe in an attempt to cheer her up.
- **S52**：When they visit the monastery Saint-Matthieu-des-Pyrénées-Orientales, Helen feels Dracula's presence and is compelled to jump off a cliff.
- **S53**：Landing on grass, she survives and decides to hunt him down and kill him in order to rid herself of his threat and her fears.
- **S54**：When the narrator arrives at Saint-Matthieu-des-Pyrénées-Orientales, she finds her father.
- **S55**：Individuals mentioned throughout the 1970s timeline converge in a final attempt to defeat Dracula.
- **S56**：He is seemingly killed by a silver bullet fired into his heart by Helen.
- **S57**：In the epilogue, which takes place in 2008, the narrator attends a conference of medievalists in Philadelphia , and stops at a library with an extensive collection of material related to Dracula.
- **S58**：She accidentally leaves her notes and the attendant rushes out and returns them to her, as well as a book with a dragon printed in the center, revealing that either Dracula is still alive or one of his minions is imitating the master.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Nina Sayers, a young dancer with a prestigious New York City ballet company, lives with her mother, Erica, a former dancer.
- **S2**：The company is preparing to open the season with Swan Lake.
- **S3**：The director, Thomas Leroy, has to cast a new principal dancer after forcing Beth Macintyre into retirement.
- **S4**：Leroy wants the same ballerina to portray both the innocent, fragile White Swan and her dark, sensual twin, the Black Swan.
- **S5**：Nina competes for the part.
- **S6**：Although her audition goes badly, she asks Thomas to reconsider.
- **S7**：He tells her she is ideal for the White Swan but lacks the passion necessary for the Black Swan.
- **S8**：When he forcibly kisses her, she shows some spirit and bites him, and lands the part.
- **S9**：An intoxicated Beth angrily confronts Thomas and Nina.
- **S10**：She is later hit by a car and seriously injured in what Thomas suspects was a suicide attempt.
- **S11**：Nina begins to witness strange happenings.
- **S12**：Thomas, meanwhile, becomes increasingly critical of her "frigid" dancing and advises her to stop being a perfectionist and lose herself in the role.
- **S13**：Thomas points to Lily , another dancer in the company, whom he describes as lacking Nina's flawless technique but possessing an uninhibited quality that Nina has not shown.
- **S14**：The relationship between the two dancers is cool because of Lily's indiscretions, but Lily invites Nina to a night out.
- **S15**：Nina is hesitant at first but decides to go against her mother's wishes.
- **S16**：At a restaurant that evening, Lily offers Nina a capsule of ecstasy to help her loosen up.
- **S17**：Though reassured its effects will only last a few hours, Nina turns it down.
- **S18**：Lily later slips it into her drink at a nightclub while she is absent.
- **S19**：Nina returns home late, fights with her mother, barricades herself in her room, and finds herself having sex with Lily.
- **S20**：Next morning, Nina wakes up alone and late for rehearsal.
- **S21**：When she arrives at the studio, she finds Lily dancing as the Swan Queen.
- **S22**：Furious, she confronts Lily and asks her why she did not wake her up that morning.
- **S23**：After Lily tells her she spent the night with a man whom she met at the club, Nina realizes she imagined the encounter.
- **S24**：Nina's hallucinations become stronger as she sees Thomas and Lily have sex in a backstage area and Beth stabbing herself in the face at the hospital with a nail filer which Nina drops bloodied in the elevator.
- **S25**：She has a violent argument with her mother, after which Nina passes out.
- **S26**：Concerned about Nina's erratic behavior, her mother tries to prevent her from performing on opening night; enraged, Nina stands up to her mother and forces her way out of the apartment.
- **S27**：Since her mother had called to say Nina was sick, Thomas assigned understudy Lily to take over, but reluctantly gives way when Nina insists on performing.
- **S28**：The first act goes well, until Nina is distracted by a hallucination during a lift, causing her partner, playing the Prince, to drop her.
- **S29**：Distraught, she returns to her dressing room and finds Lily there.
- **S30**：As Lily announces she is to play the Black Swan, she transforms into Nina's double.
- **S31**：Nina shoves her into a mirror, shattering it.
- **S32**：She grabs a shard of glass and stabs her rival in the stomach, killing her.
- **S33**：The corpse transforms back into Lily.
- **S34**：Nina hides the body and returns to the stage to dance with passion and sensuality.
- **S35**：Sprouting feathers, her arms become black wings as she finally loses herself and is transformed into a black swan.
- **S36**：At the end of the act, she receives a standing ovation.
- **S37**：Offstage, Thomas and the rest of the cast congratulate her on her stunning performance.
- **S38**：Nina takes Thomas by surprise and kisses him.
- **S39**：Back in her dressing room before the final act, Nina is congratulated by Lily, showing that their fight was imaginary.
- **S40**：The mirror, however, is still shattered.
- **S41**：She removes a shard from her own body and realizes she had stabbed herself.
- **S42**：Dancing the last scene, in which the White Swan throws herself off a cliff, Nina spots her mother weeping in the audience.
- **S43**：As Nina falls backward onto a hidden mattress, the theater erupts in thunderous applause.
- **S44**：Thomas and the cast gather to congratulate her—only to find that she is severely bleeding.
- **S45**：As the white ceiling lights envelop her, she whispers, "I felt it.
- **S46**：Perfect.
- **S47**：It was perfect."

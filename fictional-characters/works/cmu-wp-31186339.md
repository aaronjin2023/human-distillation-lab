## 角色资料

### Katniss Everdeen

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-5785d50eb57a4676` · 独立版本：`profile-300c2d619beac54a`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，她面对生存威胁仍会冒险救助同伴并反抗规则。（行为推断；S24、S26、S37、S49）
- **核心动机**：她代替被抽中的妹妹参赛，核心行动首先指向保护家人。（行为推断；S4、S5）
- **价值取舍**：在鲁临终及死后，她仍重视给予同伴安慰与有尊严的告别。（行为推断；S27、S28）
- **决策方式**：皮塔阻止她冒险取药后，她仍趁他睡着前往盛宴。（摘要直接叙述；S36、S37）
- **关系模式**：为与皮塔共同求生，她利用恋人形象争取赞助者的援助。（行为推断；S31、S33、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：她答应不去盛宴，却趁皮塔睡着后前往取药。

### Peeta Mellark

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5581160010ea11ac` · 独立版本：`profile-780280b2e64e46eb`

- **性格倾向**：在凯妮丝面临危险时，他表现出愿意承担个人代价的保护倾向。（行为推断；S36、S48）
- **核心动机**：他对凯妮丝的爱意，可合理解释他多次维护她安危的举动。（行为推断；S9、S11、S18、S36、S48）
- **决策方式**：凯妮丝受毒蜂影响时，他选择警告她赶快逃走。（摘要直接叙述；S18）
- **关系模式**：腿伤感染后，他恳求凯妮丝别为自己冒险取药。（摘要直接叙述；S33、S36）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The nation of Panem consists of a wealthy Capitol and twelve poorer districts.
- **S2**：As punishment for a past rebellion, each district must provide a boy and girl  between the ages of 12 and 18 selected by lottery  for the annual Hunger Games.
- **S3**：The tributes must fight to the death in an arena; the sole survivor is rewarded with fame and wealth.
- **S4**：In her first Reaping, 12-year-old Primrose Everdeen is chosen from District 12.
- **S5**：Her older sister Katniss volunteers to take her place.
- **S6**：Peeta Mellark, a baker's son who once gave Katniss bread when she was starving, is the other District 12 tribute.
- **S7**：Katniss and Peeta are taken to the Capitol, accompanied by their frequently drunk mentor, past victor Haymitch Abernathy.
- **S8**：He warns them about the "Career" tributes who train intensively at special academies and almost always win.
- **S9**：During a TV interview with Caesar Flickerman, Peeta unexpectedly reveals his love for Katniss.
- **S10**：She is outraged, believing it to be a ploy to gain audience support, as "sponsors" may provide in-Games gifts of food, medicine, and tools.
- **S11**：However, she discovers Peeta meant what he said.
- **S12**：The televised Games begin with half of the tributes killed in the first few minutes; Katniss barely survives ignoring Haymitch's advice to run away from the melee over the tempting supplies and weapons strewn in front of a structure called the Cornucopia.
- **S13**：Peeta forms an uneasy alliance with the four Careers.
- **S14**：They later find Katniss and corner her up a tree.
- **S15**：Rue, hiding in a nearby tree, draws her attention to a poisonous tracker jacker nest hanging from a branch.
- **S16**：Katniss drops it on her sleeping besiegers.
- **S17**：They all scatter, except for Glimmer, who is killed by the insects.
- **S18**：Hallucinating due to tracker jacker venom, Katniss is warned to run away by Peeta.
- **S19**：Rue cares for Katniss for a couple of days until she recovers.
- **S20**：Meanwhile, the alliance has gathered all the supplies into a pile.
- **S21**：Katniss has Rue draw them off, then destroys the stockpile by setting off the mines planted around it.
- **S22**：Furious, Cato kills the boy assigned to guard it.
- **S23**：As Katniss runs from the scene, she hears Rue calling her name.
- **S24**：She finds Rue trapped and releases her.
- **S25**：Marvel, a tribute from District 1, throws a spear at Katniss, but she dodges the spear, causing it to stab Rue in the stomach instead.
- **S26**：Katniss shoots him dead with an arrow.
- **S27**：She then comforts the dying Rue with a song.
- **S28**：Afterward, she gathers and arranges flowers around Rue's body.
- **S29**：When this is televised, it sparks a riot in Rue's District 11.
- **S30**：President Snow summons Seneca Crane, the Gamemaker, to express his displeasure at the way the Games are turning out.
- **S31**：Since Katniss and Peeta have been presented to the public as "star-crossed lovers", Haymitch is able to convince Crane to make a rule change to avoid inciting further riots.
- **S32**：It is announced that tributes from the same district can win as a pair.
- **S33**：Upon hearing this, Katniss searches for Peeta and finds him with an infected sword wound in the leg.
- **S34**：She portrays herself as deeply in love with him and gains a sponsor's gift of soup.
- **S35**：An announcer proclaims a feast, where the thing each survivor needs most will be provided.
- **S36**：Peeta begs her not to risk getting him medicine.
- **S37**：Katniss promises not to go, but after he falls asleep, she heads to the feast.
- **S38**：Clove ambushes her and pins her down.
- **S39**：As Clove gloats, Thresh, the other District 11 tribute, kills Clove after overhearing her tormenting Katniss about killing Rue.
- **S40**：He spares Katniss "just this time...for Rue".
- **S41**：The medicine works, keeping Peeta mobile.
- **S42**：Foxface, the girl from District 5, dies from eating nightlock berries she stole from Peeta; neither knew they are highly poisonous.
- **S43**：Crane changes the time of day in the arena to late at night and unleashes a pack of hound-like creatures to speed things up.
- **S44**：They kill Thresh and force Katniss and Peeta to flee to the roof of the Cornucopia, where they encounter Cato.
- **S45**：After a battle, Katniss wounds Cato with an arrow and Peeta hurls him to the creatures below.
- **S46**：Katniss shoots Cato to spare him a prolonged death.
- **S47**：With Peeta and Katniss apparently victorious, the rule change allowing two winners is suddenly revoked.
- **S48**：Peeta tells Katniss to shoot him.
- **S49**：Instead, she gives him half of the nightlock.
- **S50**：However, before they can commit suicide, they are hastily proclaimed the victors of the 74th Hunger Games.
- **S51**：Haymitch warns Katniss that she has made powerful enemies after her display of defiance.
- **S52**：She and Peeta return to District 12, while Crane is locked in a room with a bowl of nightlock berries, and President Snow considers the situation.

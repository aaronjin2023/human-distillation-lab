## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] In 1945 beautiful ingénue Anne Welles moves to New York City to escape the ennui of her Massachusetts hometown.
- **S3**：She finds work as a secretary to Henry Bellamy, an entertainment lawyer , and befriends Neely O'Hara, an ebullient vaudevillian and aspiring stage actress.
- **S4**：Henry's former employee, Lyon Burke, returns to the agency after World War II , and Anne quickly falls in love with him despite Henry's warning.
- **S5**：Meanwhile Anne goes on some dates with a small-time salesman named Allen Cooper, who suddenly reveals that he is a millionaire pretending to be an ordinary person in order to make sure Anne's feelings are genuine.
- **S6**：He proposes to Anne and their love story becomes a media sensation.
- **S7**：Anne befriends Helen Lawson, a brilliant, ruthless, unhappy Broadway legend.
- **S8**：Neely is hired for Helen's latest show, but Helen takes a dislike to her and minimizes her role.
- **S9**：Anne uses her friendship with Lyon to secure Neely a bigger role, and Neely becomes a breakout star; Helen cruelly rejects Anne by revealing the she was only interested in Anne's friendship for the chance at a sexual relationship with Allen's father.
- **S10**：Anne also befriends Jennifer North, a kindhearted young actress famous for her attractive figure.
- **S11**：On the night of the show's opening, Anne and Lyon consummate their feelings, and Anne and Allen break up.
- **S12**：The production is a massive success.
- **S13**：Neely enjoys a meteoric rise to fame and moves to California to begin a film career.
- **S14**：Anne's mother dies and Anne returns to her hometown with Lyon, who wants to quit the agency, live in Anne's family home, and write novels.
- **S15**：Though Anne loves Lyon deeply, she refuses to move back to Massachusetts.
- **S16**：Lyon breaks up with Anne and moves to England to write, leaving Anne heartbroken.
- **S17**：Though Anne takes years to move on, she eventually becomes a model for beauty products and starts dating the cosmetics company owner, the older, wealthy Kevin Gillmore.
- **S18**：Meanwhile Jennifer begins a relationship with childish, sex-obsessed singer Tony Polar, but their romance is frequently disrupted by Tony's domineering older sister, Miriam.
- **S19**：Jennifer pressures Tony into marrying her and quickly gets pregnant.
- **S20**：After reuniting with an increasingly unsympathetic Neely, who is in the midst of an affair, Jennifer becomes dependent on "dolls" ( amphetamines and barbiturates ) to calm her frayed nerves.
- **S21**：Finally, Miriam reveals why she opposed Jennifer and Tony's relationship: Tony has developmental disabilities and the baby will likely inherit them.
- **S22**：Devastated, Jennifer has an abortion and agrees to divorce Tony.
- **S23**：Jennifer then moves to Europe and finds work in art house films , which, owing to her nudity, are considered soft-core pornography in the United States.
- **S24**：By 1950 Neely has become a celebrated actress, enjoying a lucrative film career and parenting twin sons with her second husband.
- **S25**：However, long workdays and the stress over her husband's infidelity (with both men and women) keep her dependent on "dolls", and she is becoming increasingly unpopular with the studio because of her tantrums and walkouts.
- **S26**：Neely accidentally overdoses, but makes a full recovery.
- **S27**：Still, the studio fires her from the production and replaces her with the boss's young lover.
- **S28**：Anne reconnects with Neely; Kevin suggests resurrecting Neely's career by having her sing on a televised spectacular for his brand.
- **S29**：Neely at first refuses, but following a successful supper-club performance and a run-in with a belligerent Helen, she agrees.
- **S30**：Unable to cope with the demands of the rehearsals, she overdoses to avoid performing.
- **S31**：To Anne's distress, Neely disappears to Europe.
- **S32**：In Europe, Jennifer is pressured to undergo plastic surgery, as she has always lied about her age; she is ten years older than her claimed age of 27.
- **S33**：Unhappy with her European career and boyfriend, she returns to the United States.
- **S34**：Three years later, she gets engaged to an older senator, who she believes loves her for more than her body, and she is excited to get married and have a child.
- **S35**：But a routine test reveals that she has breast cancer and will require a mastectomy , and the treatment will likely render her infertile.
- **S36**：After her fiancé says he doesn't want children and makes a comment suggesting he's only interested in her body, Jennifer becomes convinced that she will never be loved for who she is.
- **S37**：She leaves the hospital and dies by suicide.
- **S38**：Neely reappears in the midst of Jennifer's funeral.
- **S39**：She has lost her voice, apparently from psychological issues.
- **S40**：After bungling a self-harm attempt, Neely becomes institutionalized, which a guilty Anne pays for.
- **S41**：Meanwhile Lyon returns to New York and reconnects with Anne, much to the chagrin of Kevin.
- **S42**：Anne is unable to overcome her passion for Lyon and the two begin an affair.
- **S43**：Kevin eventually learns of this and breaks up with Anne.
- **S44**：Some time later, Neely finds her voice again after an impromptu sanitarium performance with a now-incompetent Tony Polar.
- **S45**：Anne works with Henry to get Lyon to abandon his non-starter career as a writer and become a partner at the agency, with Henry lending Lyon money that secretly comes from Anne.
- **S46**：Lyon is initially put off by Neely, who has become obese, but successfully plots her career comeback.
- **S47**：Anne and Lyon get married and Anne quickly gets pregnant, but her happiness is short-lived when Neely demands that Lyon escort her everywhere on her comeback tour.
- **S48**：Lyon learns from Neely about how Anne secretly lent him money and feels outraged and emasculated.
- **S49**：He and Neely begin a brazen public affair.
- **S50**：Henry persuades Anne to wait out the humiliation and pretend she knows nothing, assuring her that Lyon will grow tired of Neely and return to her.
- **S51**：The affair stretches on for years, with Neely pressuring Lyon to end his marriage.
- **S52**：Anne becomes dependent on the "dolls" to relax, but Lyon reluctantly stays with Anne.
- **S53**：After repaying Anne's loan, he breaks off the affair (losing Neely as a client in the process) but quickly begins a new one with a teenaged up-and-coming singer.
- **S54**：Though Anne finally admits to herself that Lyon will never stop having affairs, she assures herself that she will eventually fall out of love and become numb to all of her pain before reaching for her "dolls" again.

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Following the unexplained death of her sister, Nel, in a pool at the foot of a cliff, Jules Abbott returns to Beckford, a fictional town in Northumberland , to care for her niece, Lena.
- **S3**：The novel is told in a mixture of first-person and third-person narrative . [ 5 ] Jules had been estranged from her sister Nel due to an incident from their childhood.
- **S4**：Nel assumed that Jules had seduced her boyfriend Robbie even though he was sexually abusing Jules.
- **S5**：Lena is furious at Jules for not speaking to her mum.
- **S6**：Lena believes that her mum jumped into the Drowning Pool, a place where multiple women throughout the town's history have died.
- **S7**：Lena blamed her friend Katie's death on her mother's obsession with the pool.
- **S8**：Jules does not believe that her sister committed suicide.
- **S9**：She heard fear in Nel's last voicemail, imploring Jules to meet her.
- **S10**：Jules informed Detective Sean Townsend about this.
- **S11**：Detective Sergeant Erin Morgan sensed Sean's distress in the case and found out that his mother also committed suicide in the Drowning Pool.
- **S12**：Helen Townsend stays at her father-in-law Patrick's cottage as her husband betrayed her.
- **S13**：Louise, Katie's mother, was relieved that Nel died as she holds her responsible for inspiring girls to become curious about the Drowning Pool.
- **S14**：Her son Josh, however, is terrified that his mother was not at the house when Nel died.
- **S15**：Jules searches for her mother's bracelet which Nel wore all the time and is shocked to find that it is missing.
- **S16**：Louise finds diet pills prescribed to Nel among Katie's belongings and tells Sean that Nel's murder should be made public.
- **S17**：But it turns out that Katie asked Lena to buy her the pills.
- **S18**：Mark Henderson, Lena's teacher, finds Nel's bracelet in Helen's drawer while searching her room.
- **S19**：Josh and Lena break his windows as revenge for Katie's death.
- **S20**：Josh tells Sean that Mark seduced Katie, and Lena reveals that Katie sacrificed herself to protect Mark from being arrested for unlawful intercourse.
- **S21**：Louise confronts Lena for keeping the information from her, and Lena tells her she threatened Katie to end her relationship with Mark.
- **S22**：After Louise leaves, she tells Jules that it was Nel who made the threat.
- **S23**：Erin learns that Nel and Sean had a relationship.
- **S24**：She asks Sean about it and he barks at her to check his records.
- **S25**：She meets Nickie, a con artist who tells her that she must look upon Lauren (Patrick's dead wife) and not Helen.
- **S26**：Lena waits for Mark at his home and tries to kill him but ends up injured.
- **S27**：He ties her up and tries to escape with her in the car.
- **S28**：He confronts her at his ex's cottage and tells Lena that he loved Katie and did not kill Nel.
- **S29**：They fight, and Lena likely kills Mark.
- **S30**：Lena tells Jules that Nel was murdered, having learned from Mark that Helen Townsend had a bracelet in her office that was missing from Nel's body after her death.
- **S31**：Jules goes to the Townsend house and accuses Helen of murdering Nel.
- **S32**：Patrick (Sean's father) then confesses to killing Nel and to killing his own wife, Lauren, years ago.
- **S33**：In the months after Patrick’s confession, each of the characters leaves Beckford.
- **S34**：Jules and Lena go to London and have a much stronger relationship than they did at the beginning.
- **S35**：Patrick is in jail.
- **S36**：Helen and Sean leave together, but Sean disappears one day and Helen does not look for him.
- **S37**：Sean, in an undisclosed location, tries to come to terms with himself and admits that it was he who killed Nel.

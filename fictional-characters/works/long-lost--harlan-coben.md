## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Myron Bolitar receives a phone call from an ex-lover, Terese, who asks him to come to Paris immediately.
- **S3**：Upon arriving, Myron is informed by French police that Terese's ex-husband, Rick, has been murdered and Terese is the prime suspect.
- **S4**：She is soon cleared of the charges.
- **S5**：At the scene of the murder, blood was found that matched that of Rick's daughter.
- **S6**：Terese informs Myron that ten years previously, her only daughter with Rick, Miriam, was killed in a car accident that led to Terese and Rick's divorce.
- **S7**：After nearly being kidnapped, Myron catches a glimpse of a teenage blonde girl who resembles Terese and wonders if perhaps Miriam's death was faked.
- **S8**：Myron sees the same girl again and follows her into a house in London .
- **S9**：There, he is ambushed by terrorists who kill Rick's second wife.
- **S10**：After murdering the lead terrorist, Myron is taken to a “black site,” a secret interrogation facility maintained by the United States government to torture suspected terrorists.
- **S11**：When his innocence is realized, he is released but does not recall anything that happened while incarcerated.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, back in the United States, DNA samples taken from Miriam's grave prove that she is in fact dead.
- **S13**：Myron suspects that Terese's frozen embryos were used illegally to create a second daughter, the elusive blonde girl he has been seeking.
- **S14**：Myron and his accomplice Win investigate the charity “Save the Angels,” which promotes adoption over stem-cell research and is run by right-wing conservative Christians.
- **S15**：Rick had been investigating the charity shortly before being murdered.
- **S16**：Myron and the French detective Berleand track the teenage blonde girl to a home that was pictured on the “Save the Angels” website.
- **S17**：There, they are ambushed by a terrorist cell and Berleand is killed.
- **S18**：Upon entering the home, Myron discovers a group of blonde, blue-eyed children who have been raised from stolen embryos by terrorists in the hopes of training them for suicide missions.
- **S19**：The terrorist cell is wiped out by law enforcement and the brainwashed children are taken into custody.
- **S20**：Myron tracks down Terese to Angola , where she has been hiding with her long lost daughter since the ambush in London.
- **S21**：It is implied at the end of the book that law enforcement failed to track down all of the terrorists: a blonde, blue-eyed teenage boy with a heavy backpack is seen stepping from a bus and walking into a crowded area of tourists.

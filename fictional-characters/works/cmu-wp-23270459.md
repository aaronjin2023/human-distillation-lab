## 角色资料

### Dominick Cobb

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-2510788ae83124c6` · 独立版本：`profile-651d08d5b5490db7`

- **性格倾向**：面对亡妻投影的挽留，他最终愿意直面自己的责任，而非继续接受其诱惑。（行为推断；S30、S31、S33）
- **核心动机**：他接受植入任务，希望借斋藤的影响力解除谋杀指控，回到孩子身边。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10）
- **决策方式**：他拒绝亡妻投影的挽留并坦承植入往事，随后选择留下寻找斋藤。（摘要直接叙述；S30、S31、S33）
- **价值取舍**：在获得情绪释放后，他仍留在迷失域寻找斋藤，表现出对同行者的责任。（行为推断；S33）
- **关系模式**：亡妻的记忆投影持续干扰他的任务，而与孩子团聚是他接受交易的目标。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S9）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：植入念头曾帮助妻子离开迷失域，却使她醒来后仍相信死亡可以唤醒自己。

### Saito

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-261cfae8ae869714` · 独立版本：`profile-95ecf4b788ad0b14`

- **性格倾向**：在招募团队时，他表现出先检验能力、再亲自监督任务结果的审慎倾向。（行为推断；S7、S11）
- **核心动机**：他希望通过向继承人植入念头，促使竞争者的能源集团被拆分。（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **决策方式**：他先以窃取任务考察团队，再提出植入委托，并亲自随队确认成败。（摘要直接叙述；S7、S11）
- **价值取舍**：在委托安排中，他愿以自己的影响力作交换，利用柯布归家的需求推进商业目标。（行为推断；S8、S9）
- **关系模式**：他与柯布约定以任务成功换取清除指控，最终履约，使柯布通过美国海关。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S38）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Former dream architect Dominick "Dom" Cobb  and business partner Arthur  perform corporate espionage by using an experimental military-developed machine to infiltrate the subconscious of their targets and extract information while dreaming, their latest target being powerful Japanese businessman Saito .
- **S2**：Tiered dream within a dream strategies are used and dreamers awaken by a sudden kick or by dying in the dream.
- **S3**：However, if the dreamer is the one who dies, the dream "collapses".
- **S4**：Each extractor carries a totem, a personalized small object whose behavior is only predictable to its owner, that serves the purpose of identifying dream-state or reality.
- **S5**：Cobb's totem is a spinning top that perpetually spins in the dream state.
- **S6**：The extraction fails due to Mallorie "Mal" Cobb , Cobb's deceased wife, whose memory projection sabotages the mission.
- **S7**：Saito later reveals, after Cobb's and Arthur's associate  sells them out, that he was in fact auditioning the team to perform the difficult act of inception: implanting an idea or thought into a person's subconscious while they are asleep.
- **S8**：Saito wishes to break up the energy conglomerate of his ailing competitor Maurice Fischer, by planting this idea in his son and heir Robert Fischer  to disintegrate his father's company.
- **S9**：Should Cobb succeed, Saito would use his influence to clear a murder charge against him, so he can return to the U.S. and his children.
- **S10**：Cobb accepts the offer and assembles his team: Eames, a conman and identity forger; Yusuf, a chemist who concocts the powerful sedative for a stable dream within a dream strategy; Ariadne, a young architecture student tasked with designing the labyrinth of the dream landscapes; and Arthur.
- **S11**：Saito accompanies so that he knows whether or not Cobb and his team succeeded in their mission.
- **S12**：When the elder Fischer dies in Sydney and his body is flown back to Los Angeles, the team share the flight with Robert Fischer and Cobb sedates him, bringing him into the shared dream.
- **S13**：At each stage, the member of the team generating the dream stays behind to initiate the kick, while the other members sleep within the dream to travel a level deeper.
- **S14**：In the first level, Yusuf's rainy downtown dream, the team abducts Fischer.
- **S15**：However Fischer's antibody-like trained subconscious projections attack and severely wound Saito.
- **S16**：Eames temporarily takes the appearance of Fischer's godfather, Peter Browning, to suggest Fischer reconsider his father's will.
- **S17**：Yusuf drives the team in a van as they are sedated into Arthur's dream, a hotel, where the team recruit Fischer, convincing him his kidnapping was orchestrated by Browning.
- **S18**：In the third dream level, a snowy mountain fortress dreamed by Eames, Fischer is told they are in Browning's subconscious, but they are, in fact, really going deeper into Fischer's.
- **S19**：Yusuf, under assault by trained projections, initiates his kick too soon by driving off a bridge, sending Arthur's dream world into zero-gravity and causing an avalanche in Eames' dream.
- **S20**：Arthur is forced to improvise a new kick using an elevator that will be synchronized with the van hitting the water, while the team in Eames' dream races to finish the job before the new round of kicks.
- **S21**：Unbeknownst to the rest of the team, due to the effects of heavy sedation and multi-layered dreaming, death during this mission will result in entering Limbo, unconstructed dream space where the dreamer could be trapped indefinitely.
- **S22**：Elapsed time in each dream level is, on average, roughly twenty times greater than in the level above it; in Limbo, the deepest level of all, 24 hours of outer-world time would be experienced as about half a century there.
- **S23**：Cobb reveals to Ariadne that he spent "50 years" with Mal in Limbo constructing a world out of their shared memories whilst seemingly growing old together.
- **S24**：After waking back into our outermost world, however, Mal remained convinced she was still dreaming and committed suicide, trying to persuade Cobb to do so by retroactively incriminating him in her death.
- **S25**：He subsequently fled the U.S. and left his children behind, ostensibly in the care of his father-in-law.

- **S29**：Cobb and Ariadne enter limbo to find Fischer and Saito, while Eames remains on his dream level to set up a kick by rigging the fortress with explosives.
- **S30**：Cobb confronts his projection of Mal, who tries to convince him to stay in limbo.
- **S31**：Cobb refuses and confesses that he was responsible for Mal's suicide: having convinced her to leave limbo by using inception to plant the idea in her mind that the world they had been living in for 50 years was not real, and hence the need to kill themselves in order to return to the real world, once back in the real world she continued to believe dying would wake her.
- **S32**：Mal attacks Cobb but Ariadne shoots her.
- **S33**：Through his confession, Cobb attains catharsis and chooses to remain in limbo to search for Saito.
- **S34**：Ariadne kicks Fischer off a balcony, bringing him back up to the mountain fortress, where he enters a safe room to discover and accept the planted idea: that his father wishes him to be his own man and, accordingly, that splitting up the conglomerate might not be such a radical notion after all.
- **S35**：All of the team members except Cobb and Saito ride the synchronized kicks back up to reality: Ariadne jumps off a balcony in limbo, Eames detonates the explosives in the fortress, Arthur blasts an elevator containing the team's sleeping bodies up an elevator shaft, and the van in Yusuf's dream hits the water.
- **S36**：Saito, who is slowly dying on each level from the initial gun-shot wound, enters Limbo.
- **S37**：Cobb eventually finds an aged Saito and the two remember their arrangement, presumably killing themselves and awakening to outer-world reality on the airplane.
- **S38**：Saito honors the arrangement and Cobb passes through U.S. customs once the plane lands in Los Angeles.
- **S39**：Just before reuniting with his children, Cobb tests reality with his spinning top, but he turns away to greet his children before observing the results.
- **S40**：As he plays with his children the camera pans to the spinning top for the final shot, at which point the film abruptly ends.

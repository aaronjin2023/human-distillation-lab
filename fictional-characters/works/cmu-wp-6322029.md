## 角色资料

### James Bond

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-360901462b1a4d14` · 独立版本：`profile-7ce659ae6f615ecb`

- **性格倾向**：在受制于敌人时倾向镇定机变，善于寻找对方顾虑求生（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：奉命调查黄金走私，并在发现阴谋后设法阻止金库灾难（摘要直接叙述；S5、S8、S19、S21、S30）
- **决策方式**：面对强敌优先利用情报与人际突破口，为外部援助创造条件（行为推断；S13、S21、S22）
- **价值取舍**：飞机失控时仍选择救出Galore，表现出对同伴生命的重视（行为推断；S36）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Auric Goldfinger

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-7a0e433deec32020` · 独立版本：`profile-a6150d7569230238`

- **性格倾向**：在谋利与自保时倾向精于算计，对合作者也缺乏可靠忠诚（行为推断；S16、S17、S19、S26、S27）
- **核心动机**：意图使金库黄金长期失去用途，从而提高自有黄金的价值（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **价值取舍**：为扩大财富优势愿意制造大规模危害，并牺牲合作对象（行为推断；S17、S19、S26、S27）
- **决策方式**：面对已提供物资的合作者，先许诺高额回报再以毒气杀害（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16、S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以巨额回报吸引合作者，却在取得物资后杀死他们。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After destroying a drug laboratory in Latin America, James Bond—agent 007—goes to Miami Beach.
- **S2**：There he receives instructions from his superior, M, via CIA agent Felix Leiter to observe bullion dealer Auric Goldfinger, who is staying at the same hotel as Bond.
- **S3**：The agent sees Goldfinger cheating at gin rummy and stops him by distracting his employee, Jill Masterson, and blackmailing Goldfinger into losing.
- **S4**：Bond and Jill consummate their new relationship; however, Bond is subsequently knocked out by Goldfinger's Korean manservant Oddjob, who then covers Jill in gold paint, killing her by epidermal suffocation.
- **S5**：In London, Bond learns that his true mission is determining how Goldfinger smuggles gold internationally.
- **S6**：Bond arranges to meet Goldfinger socially and wins a high-stakes golf game against him with a recovered Nazi gold bar at stake.
- **S7**：Bond follows him to Switzerland, where there is an attempt on Goldfinger's life by Tilly Masterson to avenge the death of her sister, Jill.
- **S8**：Bond sneaks into Goldfinger's plant and discovers that he smuggles the gold by melting it down and incorporating it into the bodywork of his car, which he takes with him whenever he travels.
- **S9**：Bond also overhears him talking to a Red Chinese agent named Mr.
- **S10**：Ling about "Operation Grand Slam".
- **S11**：Leaving, Bond encounters Tilly as she is about to make another attempt on Goldfinger's life, tripping an alarm as they leave; they attempt to escape, but Oddjob kills Tilly with his hat.
- **S12**：Bond is captured and Goldfinger ties Bond to a table underneath a laser, which begins to slice the table in half.
- **S13**：Bond lies to Goldfinger that MI6 knows about Grand Slam, causing Goldfinger to spare Bond's life to mislead MI6 into believing that Bond has things in hand.
- **S14**：Bond is transported by Goldfinger's private jet, which flown by his personal pilot, Pussy Galore, to his stud farm near Fort Knox, Kentucky.
- **S15**：Bond escapes and witnesses Goldfinger's meeting with U.S. mafiosi, who have brought the materials he needs for Operation Grand Slam.
- **S16**：Whilst they are each promised $1 million, Goldfinger tempts them that they "could have the million today, or ten million tomorrow".
- **S17**：They listen to Goldfinger's plan to rob Fort Knox before Goldfinger kills them all using some of the "Delta 9" nerve gas he plans to release over Fort Knox.
- **S18**：Bond is recaptured after hearing the details of the operation and tells Goldfinger the reasons why such a plan won't work.
- **S19**：However, Bond soon learns from Goldfinger that he has no intention of removing any of the gold from Fort Knox, but to place an atomic device containing cobalt and iodine, which would render the gold useless for 58 years, increasing the value of Goldfinger's own gold and giving the Chinese an advantage resulting from the ensuing economic chaos.
- **S20**：Operation Grand Slam begins with Pussy Galore's Flying Circus spraying the gas over Fort Knox.
- **S21**：However, Bond had seduced Pussy, convincing her to replace the nerve gas with a harmless substance and alert the U.S. government about Goldfinger's plan.
- **S22**：The military personnel of Fort Knox convincingly play dead until they are certain that they can prevent the criminals escaping the base with the bomb.
- **S23**：Believing the military forces to be neutralised, Goldfinger's private army break into Fort Knox and access the vault itself.
- **S24**：Goldfinger then arrives in a helicopter with the atomic device.
- **S25**：In the vault, Oddjob handcuffs Bond to the device.
- **S26**：The U.S. troops attack; Goldfinger takes off his coat, revealing a colonel's uniform, and kills Mr.
- **S27**：Ling and the troops seeking to open the vault, before escaping himself.
- **S28**：Bond extricates himself from the handcuffs, but before he can disarm the bomb, Oddjob attacks him.
- **S29**：They fight and Bond manages to electrocute Oddjob.
- **S30**：Bond forces the lock of the bomb, but is unable to disarm it.
- **S31**：An atomic specialist, who accompanied Leiter, turns off the device with the clock stopped on "007".
- **S32**：With Fort Knox safe, Bond is invited to the White House for a meeting with the President.
- **S33**：However, Goldfinger has hijacked the plane carrying Bond.
- **S34**：In a struggle for Goldfinger's revolver, Bond shoots out a window, creating an explosive decompression.
- **S35**：Goldfinger is blown out of the cabin through the window.
- **S36**：With the plane out of control Bond rescues Galore and they parachute safely from the aircraft.

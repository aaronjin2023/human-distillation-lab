## 角色资料

### Jen Yu

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-f31e35e7a377f08a` · 独立版本：`profile-6253385bbc7082e0`

- **性格倾向**：在争取行动自主时，表现出强烈好胜且难以接受他人约束的倾向（行为推断；S7、S15、S26、S27、S28）
- **核心动机**：面对包办婚姻，渴望冒险并羡慕Shu Lien的女侠生活（摘要直接叙述；S7）
- **决策方式**：在争夺宝剑时，倾向冒险追索，并在对手留情后继续攻击（行为推断；S27、S28、S30、S31）
- **关系模式**：虽与Lo相爱，却拒绝立即随他离开，呈现感情与选择的纠结（行为推断；S18、S21、S22）
- **成长变化**：在Mu Bai中毒后，行动由争夺与对抗转向尝试寻找解毒材料（行为推断；S28、S31、S36、S38）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：向往自由的侠客生活，却也以伤害对自己留情者的方式争胜。；与Lo相爱却曾拒绝私奔，重聚后的纵身一跃含义未被明确解释。

### Li Mu Bai

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-dbc83546556c2fa0` · 独立版本：`profile-8ed426048b75b155`

- **性格倾向**：在面对相互爱慕的友人时，起初表现出克制且压抑表达的倾向（行为推断；S3、S4）
- **核心动机**：赠出随身宝剑的明确意图，是结束自己作为武人的生活（摘要直接叙述；S5）
- **关系模式**：与Shu Lien互有感情，却长期未承认或付诸行动（摘要直接叙述；S4）
- **价值取舍**：在重新卷入冲突后，倾向仍将师门之仇视为必须回应的责任（行为推断；S2、S12、S34）
- **成长变化**：临终时终于向Shu Lien表白，突破此前未曾言明的情感状态（摘要直接叙述；S4、S37、S38）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：希望退出武人生涯，却因追索与师门旧仇继续卷入致命冲突。

### Yu Shu Lien

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5ff0e84992854027` · 独立版本：`profile-ec020d59f34ab27d`

- **性格倾向**：在占据决斗优势时，表现出能够克制杀伤并向对手留情的倾向（行为推断；S27、S28）
- **核心动机**：在宝剑失窃后，倾向承担追回受托之物并查明线索的责任（行为推断；S5、S8、S9）
- **价值取舍**：面对被Jen家人追怒的Lo时，倾向先保障其安全而非助长冲突（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **关系模式**：与Mu Bai维持深厚友谊和相互爱慕，却一直未将感情付诸行动（摘要直接叙述；S3、S4）
- **决策方式**：处理Jen与Lo的关系时，倾向提供安全去处并传递重聚信息（行为推断；S24、S25）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：对Jen留情并提供Lo的消息，仍遭其在决斗后出手伤害。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Li Mu Bai  is an accomplished Wudang swordsman.
- **S2**：Long ago, his master was murdered by Jade Fox , a woman who sought to learn Wudang skills.
- **S3**：Mu Bai is also a good friend of Yu Shu Lien , a female warrior.
- **S4**：Mu Bai and Shu Lien have developed feelings for each other, but they have never acknowledged or acted on them.
- **S5**：Mu Bai, intending to give up his warrior life, asks Shu Lien to transport his sword, also referred to as the Green Destiny, to the city of Peking, as a gift for their friend Sir Te .
- **S6**：At Sir Te's estate, Shu Lien meets Jen , the daughter of Governor Yu , a visiting Manchu aristocrat.
- **S7**：Jen, destined for an arranged marriage and yearning for adventure, seems envious of Shu Lien's warrior lifestyle.
- **S8**：One evening, a masked thief sneaks into Sir Te's estate and steals the sword.
- **S9**：Mu Bai and Shu Lien trace the theft to Governor Yu's compound and learn that Jade Fox has been posing as Jen's governess for many years.
- **S10**：Mu Bai makes the acquaintance of Inspector Tsai , a police investigator from the provinces, and his daughter May ([[Li Li , who have come to Peking in pursuit of Fox.
- **S11**：Fox challenges the pair and Sir Te's servant Master Bo  to a showdown that night.
- **S12**：Following a protracted battle, the group is on the verge of defeat when Mu Bai arrives and outmaneuvers Fox.
- **S13**：Before Mu Bai can kill Fox, the masked thief reappears and partners with Fox to fight.
- **S14**：Fox resumes the fight and kills Tsai before fleeing with the thief .
- **S15**：After seeing Jen fight Mu Bai, Fox realizes Jen had been secretly studying the Wudang manual and has surpassed her in skill.
- **S16**：At night, a desert bandit named Lo  breaks into Jen's bedroom and asks her to leave with him.
- **S17**：A flashback reveals that in the past, when Governor Yu and his family were traveling in the western deserts, Lo and his bandits had raided Jen's caravan and kidnapped her.
- **S18**：However, Lo and Jen soon fell passionately in love.
- **S19**：Lo eventually convinced Jen to return to her family, though not before telling her a legend of a man who jumped off a cliff to make his wishes come true.
- **S20**：Because the man's heart was pure, he did not die.
- **S21**：Lo came to Peking to persuade Jen not to go through with her arranged marriage.
- **S22**：However, Jen refuses to leave with him.
- **S23**：Later, Lo interrupts Jen's wedding procession, begging her to come away with him.
- **S24**：Nearby, Shu Lien and Mu Bai convince Lo to wait for Jen at Wudan Mountain, where he will be safe from Jen's family, who are furious with him.
- **S25**：Jen visits Shu Lien, who tells her that Lo is waiting for her at Wudang Mountain.
- **S26**：After an angry dispute, the two women engage in a duel.
- **S27**：Wielding the Green Destiny, Jen destroys each weapon that Shu Lien wields until losing to a broken sword held at her neck.
- **S28**：When Shu Lien shows mercy and lowers the sword, Jen injures Shu Lien's arm.
- **S29**：Mu Bai arrives and pursues Jen into a bamboo forest.
- **S30**：Following a duel where Mu Bai regains possession of the Green Destiny, he decides to throw the sword over a waterfall.
- **S31**：In pursuit, Jen dives into an adjoining river to retrieve the sword and is then rescued by Fox.
- **S32**：Fox puts Jen into a drugged sleep and places her in a cavern; Mu Bai and Shu Lien discover her there.
- **S33**：Fox suddenly reappears and attacks the others with poisoned darts.
- **S34**：Mu Bai blocks the needles with his sword and avenges his master's death by mortally wounding Fox, only to realize that one of the darts hit him in the neck.
- **S35**：Fox dies, confessing that her goal had been to kill Jen, because she was furious that Jen hid the secrets of Wudan from her.
- **S36**：As Jen exits to retrieve an ingredient for the antidote for the poisoned dart, Mu Bai prepares to die.
- **S37**：With his last breaths, he finally confesses his love for Shu Lien.
- **S38**：He dies in her arms as Jen returns, too late to save him.
- **S39**：The Green Destiny is returned to Sir Te.
- **S40**：Jen later goes to Wudang Mountain and spends one last night with Lo.
- **S41**：The next morning, Lo finds Jen standing on a balcony overlooking the edge of the mountain.
- **S42**：In an echo of the legend that they spoke about in the desert, she asks him to make a wish.
- **S43**：He complies, wishing for them to be together, back in the desert.
- **S44**：Jen then leaps over the side of the mountain and into the clouds.

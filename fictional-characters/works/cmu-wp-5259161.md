## 角色资料

### Park Gang-du

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-b58d5c181d739a11` · 独立版本：`profile-a7d0b339ca77f9e2`

- **性格倾向**：在女儿生死未明时，他表现出屡遭阻拦仍持续行动的倾向（行为推断；S17、S18、S36、S38、S52）
- **核心动机**：他始终以找到并救回女儿为逃离隔离和追踪怪物的目标（摘要直接叙述；S15、S18、S38、S52）
- **决策方式**：危急时他倾向迅速凭当前判断行动，也因此出现致命误判（行为推断；S10、S11、S26、S27、S28）
- **情绪反应**：女儿死亡激起他的暴怒，使他直接持铁杆攻击怪物（摘要直接叙述；S57、S58）
- **成长变化**：失去女儿后，他将照护延续到幸存孩子并保持夜间警觉（行为推断；S63、S64、S65、S66）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：强烈的保护意愿与危急时的误认、误算并存。

### Park Hyun-seo

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-769dd0c761604ffb` · 独立版本：`profile-7aaa6bd568d7615f`

- **性格倾向**：在受困缺援的情境中，她表现出利用有限条件自救的倾向（行为推断；S15、S44、S45）
- **核心动机**：她通过联络父亲与制作逃生绳，持续争取离开怪物巢穴（行为推断；S15、S44、S45）
- **价值取舍**：在自身受威胁时，她仍把年幼幸存者的安全纳入行动（行为推断；S23、S57）
- **决策方式**：她倾向观察怪物状态，再借隐藏和就地取材寻找生路（行为推断；S23、S44、S45、S47）
- **情绪反应**：逃生被怪物阻断后，她极度恐惧并缓慢寻找藏身之处（摘要直接叙述；S45、S46、S47）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 2000, an American military pathologist is commanding a reluctant Korean assistant to violate protocol by dumping over 200 bottles of formaldehyde down the drain, leading into the Han River.
- **S2**：Over the next few years, there are sightings of a strange amphibious creature in the water ways.
- **S3**：6 years later, in Seoul, Park Gang-du  is a seemingly slow-witted man who runs a small snack-bar with his father, Hee-bong .
- **S4**：Also with him is his brother and sister; national medalist archer, Nam-joo , and alcoholic former-activist Nam-il , as well as his daughter, Hyun-seo .
- **S5**：While Gang-du is delivering food to some customers, he sees a crowd along the Han River.
- **S6**：They witness a huge creature hanging from the Wonhyo Bridge, which then jumps into the water.
- **S7**：At first, it seems as though the creature has swum away, encouraging the public to bait it back with food.
- **S8**：Moments later, the creature rises out of the river and runs amok.
- **S9**：Gang-du and an American man attempt to kill the creature with a metal pole but the American is killed.
- **S10**：Gang-du grabs a girl's hand which he believes to be Hyun-seo and starts to flee.
- **S11**：When he realises he isn't holding Hyun-seo, he turns to see the creature running towards her.
- **S12**：The creature then snatches Hyun-seo and dives back into the river.
- **S13**：During a mass funeral for the victims of the creature  Government representatives arrive and forcefully quarantine all those who have been in direct contact with the creature, including Gang-du and his family.
- **S14**：The Korean government announces that the creature is not only a direct danger, but also the host of a deadly, unknown virus .
- **S15**：While in quarantine inside a hospital, Gang-du receives a phone call from Hyun-seo, who is not dead, but trapped somewhere in the sewers by the creature.
- **S16**：She is cut off as her phone battery runs out.
- **S17**：Gang-du tries to explain this to others, but his protests go ignored by all except his family.
- **S18**：All four of them then decide to escape the hospital to track down Hyun-seo.
- **S19**：Hee-bong also buys weapons and a map of the sewers from a group of men, so they are prepared for searching for Hyun-seo.
- **S20**：Meanwhile two brothers, Se-jin and Se-joo, are searching for food when they are attacked by the creature.
- **S21**：They attempt to flee but are both 'eaten' by the creature.
- **S22**：The creature then takes both brothers back to the sewers where it regurgitates both of them with only Se-Joo coming out alive.
- **S23**：Hyun-seo sees that Se-Joo is alive and they both hide out of sight from the creature.
- **S24**：While Gang-Du and his family are searching the sewers and streets for Hyun-Seo they are attacked by the creature.
- **S25**：They fire at the creature but quickly run out of ammunition.
- **S26**：Gang-du believing he has shells left in his shotgun, gives his gun to his father, at his request, so that he can attempt to hold off the creature, buying his family some time.
- **S27**：As Hee-bong is about to fire at the creature, Gang-du realises he has miscounted his shots, and that his shotgun is empty.
- **S28**：As he looks back to his father, Hee-bong is attacked and killed by the creature.
- **S29**：Gang-du runs back to his father but the Army and scientists arrive and capture Gang-du.
- **S30**：Nam-il and Nam-joo are able to hide but are separated from each other while Gang-Du is taken away, screaming in agony over his fathers death.
- **S31**：While Nam-joo is searching through the sewers, she comes across the creature and tries to attack it with her bow.
- **S32**：She hesitates to take a shot and the creature knocks her down into a hole, knocking her unconscious.
- **S33**：The creature unable to reach her continues running through the sewers.
- **S34**：Gang-du is now shown being interrogated by two scientists.
- **S35**：They believe he is carrying the virus that the creature is carrying.
- **S36**：They attempt to operate on him but he manages to break free.
- **S37**：He takes one of the scientists hostage and threatens to inject her with his blood .
- **S38**：Gang-du then escapes to continue his search for his daughter.
- **S39**：Nam-il attempts to get help from a friend who tells him that the Government have placed a bounty on his and his families heads.
- **S40**：While in an office building Nam-il is betrayed by his friend for the bounty.
- **S41**：Nam-il evades capture from both his friend and the police and flees.
- **S42**：Nam-il wakes up the next morning with a homeless man  sitting next to him.
- **S43**：After talking they both join together to try and find Hyun-seo and kill the creature.
- **S44**：Back in the sewers, while the creature is sleeping, Hyun-seo ties together clothes from the bodies of the dead to create a rope.
- **S45**：She successfully latches the makeshift rope to the top of the drain but while she attempts to jump up to the rope she is caught by the creature.
- **S46**：The creature, seemingly still sleeping, gently puts Hyun-seo back on the ground.
- **S47**：Terrified, Hyun-seo slowly attempts to hide but the creature leaps at her and Se-joo and swallows the both of them.
- **S48**：Meanwhile, the government announce they will release a harmful chemical called Agent Yellow, into the river and its surrounding areas, that they hope will kill the creature.
- **S49**：People against Agent Yellow arrive starting to protest its release.
- **S50**：Gang-du finally finds the sewers where the creature kept Hyun-seo but the creature and Hyun-seo are not there.
- **S51**：As Gang-du climbs down into the sewer the monster passes over him.
- **S52**：He sees Hyun-seos arm hanging out of the creatures mouth and starts to chase after it.
- **S53**：As Gang-du is running after the creature he comes across Nam-joo and noticing that it is heading towards the protest, go after it together.
- **S54**：As Nam-il and the homeless man arrive at the protest, and as the creature attacks, Agent Yellow is released.
- **S55**：Agent Yellow appears to hurt the creature but doesn't kill it.
- **S56**：As the creature is laying on the ground, Gang-du sees Hyun-seo in its mouth.
- **S57**：He pulls her and See-jo out but Hyun-seo is revealed to have died clutching See-jo, who is still alive.
- **S58**：In a fit of rage, Gang-du attacks the creature with a metal pole but is knocked to the ground.
- **S59**：Nam-il and the homeless man then come to Gang-dus aid, with Nam-il throwing Molotov cocktails at the creature.
- **S60**：The homeless man climbs up to the platform above the creature and starts to pour petrol onto it but as Nam-il goes to throw his last bottle he drops it.
- **S61**：Nam-joo then picks up the flaming cloth from the bottle with one of her arrows and fires it at the creature, hitting it in its eye.
- **S62**：The creature bursts into flames and attempts to run to the Han river when Gang-du appears from behind one of the bridges pillars, with the metal pole in hand, and impales the creature in its mouth, finally killing it.
- **S63**：As Nam-il and Nam-joo hold Hyun-seo, mourning her death, Gang-du walks over to See-jo and picks him up, taking him away from the protest and to safety.
- **S64**：Time passes and Gang-du is living with Se-joo in his food stand.
- **S65**：At night, Gang-du believes he sees something in the distance, picking up a shotgun, but then realises it was nothing.
- **S66**：Gang-du and See-jo then sit down to have something to eat, while a news broadcast about the monster attack on the TV is on.
- **S67**：The film ends with See-jo asking Gang-du to turn the TV off so they can concentrate on eating.

### Jack

- **性格倾向**：在家庭禁令面前倾向追随好奇心，主动接近陌生生命（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **核心动机**：对野生动物与荒野的强烈兴趣推动他越出优裕生活（行为推断；S21、S22）

- **成长变化**：从偷看囚猿的少年成长为能在丛林生存并照顾他人的人（行为推断；S22、S29、S31、S32）

- **S20**：Because of Akut's interactions with Tarzan, he is unafraid of white men, and Paulvitch, unaware of the previous relationship, convinces the ape to come with him to England, where he is seized and exhibited against his will for profit.
- **S21**：After the trauma of the kidnappings ten years earlier, Jane has refused to speak of her husband's past and fears that Jack is close to following in his footsteps; despite being raised in comfort and wealth, Jack prefers the wilds, possesses an avid interest in wildlife, and is extremely athletic.

- **S28**：Akut kills Paulvitch, and Jack, terrified, escapes into the jungle with him, thinking he will have to run for the rest of his life.
- **S29**：Like Tarzan before him, Jack learns survival in the jungle and encounters the Mangani apes, with whom he can speak because of his dialogue with Akut.
- **S30**：Due to the latter's difficultly pronouncing the word "Jack", he instead names him "Korak" (killer in ape-tongue).
- **S31**：Earning a place among the apes, Korak rescues the orphan girl Meriem .
- **S32**：He begins teaching her to survive the jungle and they begin a sibling-esque relationship (though romantic feelings later emerge) and live adventurously for several years.
- **S33**：In the interim, John and Jane move to their Waziri estate in Africa and spend the next six years hoping to reunite with their son.
- **S34**：After about six years, the now teenaged Korak and Meriem emerge from the jungle, and Korak chooses to return to England with his parents.
- **S35**：The story ends with his marriage to Meriem.

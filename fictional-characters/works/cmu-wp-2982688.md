### William Stryker

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-fb35830b21db2fed` · 独立版本：`profile-232f5073107c5de3`

- **性格倾向**：在推进计划遇阻时倾向冷酷排障，甚至杀害上级（行为推断；S14、S23）
- **核心动机**：持续推进武器计划，将多种变种能力集中用于杀戮（行为推断；S23、S29）
- **价值取舍**：倾向将变种人的身体和记忆视为可支配的武器资源（行为推断；S12、S14、S29、S38）
- **决策方式**：利用保护亲属的条件操纵凯拉，借关系达成计划（行为推断；S24）
- **关系模式**：与罗根的关系由招募合作转为清除记忆与武力伤害（摘要直接叙述；S7、S12、S14、S38）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1845, James Howlett, a young boy living in northern Canada, sees his father killed by groundskeeper Thomas Logan.
- **S2**：The trauma activates the boy's mutation: bone claws protrude from James' hands, and he kills Thomas, who reveals with his dying breath that he is James' real father.
- **S3**：James flees into the forest along with Thomas's son Victor Creed, who is thus James' half-brother.
- **S4**：They spend the next century as soldiers, fighting in the Civil War, both World Wars, and the Vietnam War.
- **S5**：In 1973, Victor  attempts to rape a local village woman, but is stopped after killing a senior officer.
- **S6**：James  defends his brother, and the two are sentenced to execution by firing squad, which they survive.
- **S7**：Major William Stryker  approaches them, now in military custody, and offers them membership in Team X, a group of mutants including marksman Agent Zero , swordsman Wade Wilson , teleporter John Wraith , invulnerable Fred Dukes  and electropathic Chris Bradley .
- **S8**：They join the team, but the group's questionable actions and disregard for human life cause James to leave.
- **S9**：Six years later, James, now going by the name Logan, lives in Canada with his girlfriend, Kayla Silverfox .
- **S10**：Colonel Stryker locates Logan and warns him that someone is killing members of the team; both Wilson and Bradley are dead.
- **S11**：Shortly afterward, Victor murders Kayla and attacks Logan.
- **S12**：Stryker offers Logan a way to beat Victor; Logan undergoes an operation to reinforce his skeleton with adamantium, a virtually indestructible metal.
- **S13**：Before the procedure, Logan asks for new dog tags inscribed with "Wolverine", based on a story that Kayla told him.
- **S14**：Once the procedure is complete, Stryker orders Logan's memory erased, but Logan overhears and fights his way out.
- **S15**：As Logan runs, Zero tracks him down, but is killed.
- **S16**：Logan locates Wraith and Dukes and asks them about the location of Stryker's new laboratory, referred to as "The Island".
- **S17**：They tell him to find Remy "Gambit" LeBeau , who escaped and knows the location of The Island.
- **S18**：Logan and Wraith locate Gambit in New Orleans, but when Logan asks for the Island's location, Gambit suspects he was sent to recapture him and attacks.
- **S19**：Outside, Logan finds Victor, who had just killed Wraith and the two fight.
- **S20**：When Logan is about to kill Victor, Gambit interrupts, and Victor escapes.
- **S21**：Logan and Gambit fight before Logan convinces him he is not working for Stryker.
- **S22**：Gambit takes him to Stryker's facility on Three Mile Island.
- **S23**：After learning of Stryker's mutant son and believing that he is too emotionally close to the situation, General Munson tries to shut Weapon XI down, but Stryker kills him.
- **S24**：There, Logan learns that Kayla is alive and conspired with Stryker in exchange for her sister's safety.
- **S25**：Enraged, Logan leaves.
- **S26**：Victor then arrives, demanding the adamantium bonding promised for his service, but Stryker refuses on the basis that Victor would not survive the procedure.
- **S27**：Victor attempts to kill Kayla, but Logan hears her screams and returns.
- **S28**：Logan defeats and nearly kills Victor.
- **S29**：Meanwhile, Stryker activates Weapon XI, a "mutant killer" with the abilities of other mutants bonded to Wade Wilson's body, which Stryker refers to as "the Deadpool".
- **S30**：Kayla and Logan free the imprisoned mutants, and on their escape they are found by Deadpool.
- **S31**：Logan holds Deadpool off while the mutants flee.
- **S32**：During the escape, Kayla, mortally wounded, decides to stay behind while the party led by Scott Summers  is greeted by Professor Charles Xavier , who offers them shelter at his school.
- **S33**：Logan lures Deadpool to the top of one of the plant's cooling towers.
- **S34**：Logan is almost killed until Victor intervenes.
- **S35**：After a lengthy battle, Logan manages to decapitate Deadpool and kick him into the base of the cooling tower.
- **S36**：As he falls, the head fires optic blasts that cause the tower to start falling apart.
- **S37**：Victor departs and Logan is saved from the collapsing tower by Gambit.
- **S38**：After Logan finds Kayla, Stryker shoots him in the forehead with adamantium bullets, rendering him unconscious.
- **S39**：Kayla uses her persuasion powers to make Stryker drop the gun and commands him to "walk until [his] feet bleed" before dying from her wounds.
- **S40**：Gambit returns as Logan regains consciousness, but the brain damage caused by the bullets has triggered amnesia.
- **S41**：Gambit tries convincing Logan to come with him, but he declines, wanting to go his own way.

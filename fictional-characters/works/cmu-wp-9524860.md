## 角色资料

### Amir Qadiri

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-99dc151b6cde5197` · 独立版本：`profile-b7fbc200cabacae1`

- **性格倾向**：少年时面对暴力威胁倾向退缩，并以隐瞒逃避自身责任（行为推断；S28、S30、S32）
- **核心动机**：重返故地营救孩子，逐渐成为回应旧日背叛的补偿行动（行为推断；S61、S70、S71、S73）
- **情绪反应**：目睹朋友受害后的罪疚持续侵蚀他，使相处与庆生都难安宁（摘要直接叙述；S31、S38）
- **决策方式**：早期为摆脱关系压力选择栽赃，而非坦承自己的旁观行为（行为推断；S30、S36、S41）
- **成长变化**：从躲避朋友的苦难转向承担照护，并公开承认受歧视的亲属（行为推断；S28、S87、S88、S89）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：渴望摆脱罪疚，却曾通过进一步伤害朋友来逃避。

### Hassan

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-fe9f577938809e4b` · 独立版本：`profile-1fe80c1479855326`

- **性格倾向**：在朋友遭受威胁时倾向挺身维护，即使自身处于弱势（行为推断；S23、S27）
- **价值取舍**：面对交出风筝即可脱身的要求，仍把对朋友的忠诚放在前面（行为推断；S13、S27）
- **关系模式**：与阿米尔的友谊承受阶层不平等，也承受对方的隐瞒与陷害（行为推断；S7、S21、S30、S41、S42）
- **决策方式**：面对朋友的挑衅与诬告，选择承受伤害而没有以攻击反击（行为推断；S34、S35、S41、S42）
- **成长变化**：从依赖朋友朗读故事，发展为能够自行阅读并写信的人（摘要直接叙述；S14、S33、S70）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：对朋友的坚定维护与实际遭到的背叛形成尖锐落差。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film opens with an Afghan-American writer, Amir Qadiri , and his wife, Soraya , who are watching children flying kites at a bayside park.
- **S2**：When they arrive home, Amir finds waiting for him packages of his new novel, A Season for Ashes, which has just been published.
- **S3**：Soraya refers to the book as Amir’s “baby,” hinting at the couple’s inability to have a child of their own.
- **S4**：Amir then receives an unexpected call from an old friend of his father’s, Rahim Khan , who is living in Peshawar, Pakistan.
- **S5**：Ten-year-old Amir  is the son of a wealthy man , known locally by the honorific title “Agha Sahib”.
- **S6**：Baba, a philanthropist and iconoclast, is a Dari-speaking Tajik living in Pashtun-dominated Kabul.
- **S7**：Amir’s best friend is Hassan , who is the son of the Agha Sahib’s Hazara servant, Ali .
- **S8**：Amir participates in the sport of kite fighting, popular among the boys of Kabul.
- **S9**：Two kite flyers compete to cut each other’s kite strings, the defeated kite becoming the prize of the winner.
- **S10**：Hassan serves as Amir’s spool-holder and “kite runner,” who retrieves the defeated kite.
- **S11**：Hassan has the ability to determine where the loose kite will land without watching its course through the air.
- **S12**：Hassan has deadly aim with his slingshot, and one day on Hassan’s birthday, Amir gives Hassan a slingshot made in America.
- **S13**：Hassan pledges his loyalty to Amir, swearing that he would eat dirt if Amir so asked.
- **S14**：Amir also is a writer of stories, and he often reads his own stories or published stories to the illiterate Hassan.
- **S15**：Hassan particularly likes to hear the story of Rostam and Sohrab from the Persian epic Shahnameh.
- **S16**：Baba dislikes his son’s timid ways, and complains to his friend and business associate Rahim Khan that Amir doesn’t stand up for himself, letting Hassan fight his battles for him.
- **S17**：Amir overhears this conversation and Rahim Khan goes to Amir’s room to assure him that his father loves him.
- **S18**：Amir says that he believes that his father resents him because Amir’s mother died in childbirth.
- **S19**：Rahim Khan also encourages Amir to keep writing.
- **S20**：Amir and Hassan are often bullied by an older Pashtun boy, Assef  and Assef’s two friends, who harbor ethnic hatred against Hazaras.
- **S21**：Assef taunts Amir by saying that Amir has no friends but one that he pays to be his friend, Hassan.
- **S22**：And Assef taunts Hassan by saying that Amir does not treat him as a true friend.
- **S23**：Cornered one day by the three boys, Hassan protects Amir by threatening Assef with his slingshot.
- **S24**：The bullies flee, but Assef promises revenge.
- **S25**：One day Amir enters the city-wide kite-fighting contest, and his father—who was a champion in his own youth—watches proudly from a balcony, accompanied by Rahim Khan, as Amir breaks his father’s record of 14 “kills.” Hassan sprints off to “run” the last defeated kite and he is gone for some time.
- **S26**：Eventually, Amir finds Hassan trapped in a dead-end by Assef and his two goons.
- **S27**：Assef demands the kite as payment for letting Hassan go free, but Hassan refuses, asserting that the kite belongs to Amir.
- **S28**：Amir watches the scene while concealed, too afraid to intervene.
- **S29**：Assef then beats and anally rapes Hassan as his friends hold the boy down.
- **S30**：Amir flees from the scene, and later, when Hassan emerges, dripping blood, Amir pretends not to know what has occurred.
- **S31**：Over the next few weeks, Amir, wracked with guilt, avoids Hassan, who spends all his free time in bed.
- **S32**：Ali and Baba try to find out whether something has happened, but Amir pleads ignorance.
- **S33**：One day, Amir walks to a tree underneath which Amir often read stories to Hassan, and finds Hassan teaching himself to read.
- **S34**：Amir accuses Hassan of cowardice, and throws pomegranates at him, daring Hassan to strike him.
- **S35**：Hassan picks up a pomegranate and smashes it into his own face.
- **S36**：Later, Amir asks his father whether he would consider replacing his servants Ali and Hassan.
- **S37**：Baba angrily rebukes Amir, declaring that Ali has worked for the family for 40 years and that Ali and Hassan will always stay with them.
- **S38**：Baba throws a massive party for Amir’s birthday, but Amir is unable to enjoy it, watching Hassan serve the guests.
- **S39**：Assef attends with his father, and Amir meekly accepts Assef’s gift and well-wishes.
- **S40**：Rahim Khan, who presents Amir with a blank book for his stories, senses something is wrong and tells Amir that Amir can tell him anything.
- **S41**：The next day, Amir plants his wristwatch, a birthday present from his father, under Hassan’s pillow, and tells the Agha Sahib that Hassan has stolen it.
- **S42**：When confronted by Baba, Hassan falsely confesses to stealing it.
- **S43**：The Agha Sahib forgives him, but Ali lets him know that he and Hassan can no longer work for him, and, much to Baba's distress, they pack their belongings and leave.
- **S44**：In June 1979, the Soviets invade Afghanistan.
- **S45**：Baba is disliked by the communists for his frequent denunciation of them.
- **S46**：He leaves his house in the care of Rahim Khan and flees to Pakistan with Amir.
- **S47**：The travel by truck with other refugees and, along the way, they are stopped by a Russian soldier, who demands sex with a young wife and mother who is among the refugees.
- **S48**：Baba intervenes, daring the soldier to shoot him, but the situation is defused when the soldier’s superiors appear on the scene.
- **S49**：Baba runs a service station and operates a stall at a weekly flea market.
- **S50**：Amir earns a degree at a local community college and, Baba, though disappointed that Amir wants to be a writer rather than a physician, says that Amir can earn money by working with him.
- **S51**：One day at the flea market, Amir’s father introduces him to General Taheri , a Pathan, and a former officer in the Afghan army.
- **S52**：Amir is smitten by Taheri’s daughter, Soraya, but the very traditional Taheri, who has little regard for fiction writers, discourages his advances.
- **S53**：Soon after, Baba is diagnosed with lung cancer, and he becomes gravely ill.
- **S54**：General Taheri and his family visit him at the hospital and Amir and Soraya manage to convey their interest in each other.
- **S55**：Baba refuses to stay at the hospital, and after Amir brings him home, he asks his father to ask General Taheri for his daughter’s hand in marriage.
- **S56**：Taheri agrees, but Amir’s father tells him that Soraya wants to speak with him.
- **S57**：On a chaperoned stroll, Soraya reveals that when the Taheris were living in Virginia, she ran away with a Pathan man and lived with him until her father came to retrieve her.
- **S58**：Soon after, the Taheris moved to California to flee the gossip surrounding them.
- **S59**：Amir is shocked, but he says that he still wants to be married.
- **S60**：Amir and Soraya are married, and, soon after, Amir’s father dies.
- **S61**：Rahim Khan persuades Amir to visit him in Pakistan, and tells him that the situation is bad, but Amir has an opportunity to “be good again.” Amir cancels his book tour and goes to Peshawar.
- **S62**：Rahim Khan tells Amir that he is dying, but that is not the real reason that he has asked Amir to come.
- **S63**：Rahim Khan then tells Amir what happened after he and his father fled Afghanistan.
- **S64**：Rahim Khan managed the house, but a series of caretakers didn’t work out.
- **S65**：He then located Hassan and persuaded him to return with his wife and their son to look after the house.
- **S66**：Rahim Khan himself had to flee to Pakistan when the Taliban took over power after the civil war from 1992 to 1996.
- **S67**：One day the Taliban appeared at the house and demanded that Hassan vacate the premises, declaring that no Hazara could be in legitimate possession of the house.
- **S68**：Hassan refused to surrender the house and the Taliban executed him in the street, and also shot his wife.
- **S69**：Hassan’s son, Sohrab, was taken to an orphanage.
- **S70**：Rahim Khan urges Amir to return to Kabul to find Sohrab and gives him a letter written by Hassan, who had taught himself to read and write.
- **S71**：Amir resists until Rahim Khan reveals that Hassan was not really Ali’s biological son.
- **S72**：Rahim Khan says that Amir’s father had had an affair with Ali’s wife and was the true biological father of Hassan.
- **S73**：Amir agrees to go to Kabul, accompanied by a driver, Farid , who helps him don a disguise and a fake beard and negotiate the Taliban-controlled city.
- **S74**：Amir and Farid go to the orphanage where Sohrab was taken and learn that Sohrab has been given away to a donor, a Taliban official, who occasionally takes away young boys.
- **S75**：They are told that they can meet the Taliban official at a football match.
- **S76**：Amir and Farid attend the match, where they witness the Taliban stoning adulterers at half-time.
- **S77**：Amir manages to get an appointment to see the Taliban official.
- **S78**：After he arrives at the official’s house, he is surprised to find that it is actually Assef, the former bully , who recognizes Amir immediately even with the false beard.
- **S79**：Assef presents Sohrab as his dance boy.
- **S80**：Assef agrees to let Sohrab go in exchange for a “price,” that is, a beating.
- **S81**：Assef begins pummeling Amir, but in the confusion, Sohrab is able to pull out his slingshot, the same slingshot that Amir had given to Hassan when they were boys, and shoots Assef in the eye.
- **S82**：Sohrab and an injured Amir manage to escape through a window as Assef yells for help and orders their deaths.
- **S83**：But the two get to Farid’s car and they escape.
- **S84**：When they get back to Peshawar, they find that Rahim Khan has left his apartment with no forwarding address, but he has left a letter for Amir.
- **S85**：The next morning, Sohrab has disappeared, after Amir conducted his search for Sohrab without any luck, he returns to the apartment to find Sohrab sitting on the stairway.
- **S86**：Sohrab reveals that Assef would rape him before morning prayers.
- **S87**：Back in San Francisco, Amir introduces Sohrab to Soraya, and they welcome him into their home.
- **S88**：But Amir’s father-in-law, General Taheri, claiming he has to answer to their community, demands to know why they have taken in “that Hazara boy.” Amir reveals that Sohrab is his half-brother’s son.
- **S89**：The film ends with Amir teaching Sohrab how to fly kites, and volunteering to act as Sohrab’s “runner.”

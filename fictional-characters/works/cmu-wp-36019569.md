### Snow White

- **性格倾向**：在囚禁与追杀中倾向争取主动，最终愿意亲自面对强敌（行为推断；S8、S26、S28、S29）

### Eric the Huntsman

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a0bba5c5c2366844` · 独立版本：`profile-41dfc43352f3e7d5`

- **性格倾向**：在交易承诺可疑时倾向谨慎核实，拒绝立即交出追捕对象（行为推断；S13、S14）
- **核心动机**：最初为亡妻复生的承诺追捕白雪，承诺破灭后为报酬护送她（行为推断；S9、S10、S13、S14）
- **关系模式**：对白雪的保护逐渐超出报酬交易，并与对亡妻的记忆相连（行为推断；S14、S16、S25）
- **情绪反应**：以为白雪已死时表达未能救她的懊悔，并说她令自己想起亡妻（摘要直接叙述；S25）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：为复活亡妻参与追捕，却在承诺被揭穿后帮助追捕对象逃离。

### Ravenna

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-f0960a7c5d67a935` · 独立版本：`profile-54129a863710baa0`

- **性格倾向**：面对衰老与失权威胁时呈现掠夺控制倾向，以伤害他人自保（行为推断；S6、S7、S8、S29）
- **核心动机**：为保持青春并获得永生，追求吞食被预言会毁灭自己的白雪之心（摘要直接叙述；S6、S7、S29）
- **价值取舍**：将自身美貌与权力的延续置于年轻女性的生命和王国福祉之上（行为推断；S4、S6、S7）
- **决策方式**：常以伪装接近目标，利用婚姻信任或童年友谊降低对方戒心（行为推断；S2、S3、S22）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：谴责男性利用并抛弃女性美貌，却靠掠夺年轻女性维持自己的青春。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Snow White is the Princess of Tabor, daughter of King Magnus and Queen Eleanor.
- **S2**：After his wife's death, King Magnus marries the beautiful Ravenna after rescuing her from the Dark Army, an invading force of glass soldiers.
- **S3**：Ravenna, who is in fact a powerful sorceress and the Dark Army's master, kills Magnus on their wedding night after noting that men like him exploit the beauty of women and discard them.
- **S4**：As Ravenna usurps control of the entire kingdom, Duke Hammond escapes the castle with his son William, but is unable to rescue Snow White, who is captured by Ravenna's brother Finn.
- **S5**：Snow White is then locked away in the north tower of the castle.
- **S6**：Tabor is left in ruins under Ravenna's rule as she periodically drains the youth from young women in order to maintain a spell once cast by her mother which allows her to keep her beauty.
- **S7**：When Snow White comes of age, Ravenna learns from her Magic Mirror that Snow White is destined to destroy her unless Ravenna consumes the young girl's heart, which will make her immortal.
- **S8**：Ravenna orders Finn to bring her Snow White, but she escapes into the Dark Forest, where Ravenna has no power.
- **S9**：Eric the Huntsman, a widower who has survived the Dark Forest, is brought to Ravenna, who orders him to lead Finn in pursuit of Snow White.
- **S10**：In exchange, she promises to revive his deceased wife, Sarah.
- **S11**：Duke Hammond learns that Snow White is alive and has fled into the Dark Forest.
- **S12**：William, Snow White's childhood friend, later infiltrates Finn's band as a bowman to find her.
- **S13**：The Huntsman tracks down Snow White in the Dark Forest but refuses to hand her over until he knows Ravenna will keep her word.
- **S14**：When Finn admits that Ravenna cannot resurrect the dead, the Huntsman helps Snow White to escape, promising to escort her to Duke Hammond's castle in exchange for a reward of gold.
- **S15**：The two leave the Dark Forest, escape the troll and pass through a fishing village where all the women have disfigured themselves in order to escape Ravenna, and where he learns Snow White's true identity.
- **S16**：He then leaves her in the care of the women but returns when he sees the village being burned down by Finn's men.
- **S17**：Snow White and the Huntsman eventually meet a band of dwarves &mdash; Beith, Muir, Quert, Coll, Duir, Gort, Nion, and Gus.
- **S18**：Muir discovers that Snow White is the only person who can defeat Ravenna and end her reign.
- **S19**：As they travel through a fairy sanctuary, the group is attacked by Finn's men, resulting in Gus' death.
- **S20**：The Huntsman kills Finn after learning he abetted in the murder of his wife.
- **S21**：William reveals himself and helps defeat the soldiers before joining the others in their journey to Hammond's castle.
- **S22**：Ravenna disguises herself as William and tempts Snow White into eating a poisoned apple, but is forced to flee when the Huntsman and William discover her.
- **S23**：William kisses Snow White &mdash; whom he believes to be dead &mdash; without seeing that she has shed a solitary tear.
- **S24**：She is taken to Hammond's castle.
- **S25**：As she lies in repose, the Huntsman professes his regret for not saving Snow White, who reminds him of his wife, and kisses her, breaking the spell; she awakens after a second tear has fallen.
- **S26**：Snow White then takes command of the Duke's army and leads them into battle against Ravenna.
- **S27**：The Dwarves infiltrate Tabor through the sewers and open the gates, which allows the Duke's army to invade the castle.
- **S28**：Snow White confronts Ravenna, but is overpowered.
- **S29**：Ravenna is about to kill Snow White and consume her heart when Snow White stabs her in the heart instead, causing Ravenna to wither and die.
- **S30**：Hammond's army is victorious, and Snow White is crowned Queen of the restored kingdom.

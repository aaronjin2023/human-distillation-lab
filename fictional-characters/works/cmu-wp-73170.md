## 角色资料

### Aladdin

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-32158a056ddb6107` · 独立版本：`profile-ce3cdd836b66bdb7`

- **性格倾向**：在求爱与身份暴露的情境中，他倾向用伪装掩饰不安（行为推断；S19、S21、S25）
- **核心动机**：他希望赢得茉莉的爱情，因此借王子身份争取接近机会（摘要直接叙述；S16）
- **决策方式**：面对力量悬殊的对手，他倾向利用规则与对方欲望解困（行为推断；S13、S31、S32、S33）
- **成长变化**：他最终接受真实身份，并以兑现承诺取代继续伪装（摘要直接叙述；S35）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：渴望爱情，却用隐瞒真实身份的方式维系关系。

### Jasmine

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-59313c5305bc966b` · 独立版本：`profile-c98b2d3031e78a36`

- **性格倾向**：面对宫廷安排与身份疑点，她表现出主动抗争的倾向（行为推断；S3、S18、S20）
- **核心动机**：她因不满宫廷生活而出走，希望摆脱既有生活束缚（摘要直接叙述；S3）
- **价值取舍**：在择偶情境中，她倾向重视真实相处而非王子头衔（行为推断；S18、S20、S36）
- **决策方式**：受制于贾方时，她愿以策略性表演为同伴争取机会（行为推断；S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Jafar

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-ace0dc19896c913f` · 独立版本：`profile-2c4e607608cd26cf`

- **性格倾向**：在争夺权力时，他表现出操控他人且不容挑战的倾向（行为推断；S7、S11、S27）
- **核心动机**：他追求统治地位与至高力量，连续用愿望提升自身权势（摘要直接叙述；S26、S31、S32）
- **价值取舍**：在权力争夺中，他倾向将他人的生命与自由置于自身之下（行为推断；S11、S27、S28、S30）
- **决策方式**：面对更强者的比较，他倾向急于升级力量而忽略代价（行为推断；S31、S32、S33）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：追求绝对力量的最终愿望，反而使他失去自由。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Jafar, Grand Vizier to the Sultan of Agrabah, is attempting to retrieve a magical oil lamp containing a genie from the Cave of Wonders.
- **S2**：After seeing a petty thief's failed attempt to enter the cave, Jafar and his parrot, Iago, learn that only a "Diamond in the Rough" can enter the cave.
- **S3**：Jasmine, the Sultan's daughter, frustrated with her life in the palace, flees to Agrabah's marketplace.
- **S4**：There she meets street rat Aladdin and his monkey, Abu.
- **S5**：The two discover they have a lot in common.
- **S6**：When Aladdin is detained for thievery, Jasmine orders him released, but Jafar lies to her that Aladdin has been executed.
- **S7**：Disguised as an elder, Jafar releases Aladdin and Abu from prison and leads them to the Cave of Wonders.
- **S8**：The tiger-shaped head of the cave says to touch nothing but the lamp.
- **S9**：Aladdin and Abu enter the cave, where a magic carpet guides them to the lamp.
- **S10**：Abu's attempt to steal a ruby causes the cave to start collapsing, but the carpet flies them to the entrance.
- **S11**：As Aladdin delivers the lamp, Jafar tries to kill him, but Abu bites Jafar in the arm and gets the lamp back as he, the carpet, and Aladdin fall back into the cave just as it closes.
- **S12**：In the collapsed cave, Aladdin rubs the lamp, unexpectedly unleashing a genie, who reveals he will grant Aladdin three wishes—with the exception of murder, romance, or revival of the dead.
- **S13**：Aladdin tricks the Genie into freeing them from the cave without using a wish.
- **S14**：While contemplating his wishes, Genie admits he would wish for freedom, since he is a prisoner to his lamp.
- **S15**：Aladdin promises to free the Genie for his last wish.
- **S16**：After talking about Jasmine with the Genie, Aladdin decides to use his first wish to become a prince so he can woo Jasmine.
- **S17**：Aladdin returns to Agrabah just as Jafar tries to trick the Sultan into arranging a marriage between himself and Jasmine.
- **S18**：When Aladdin parades into the Sultan's palace as "Prince Ali", Jasmine rejects Ali as a suitor.
- **S19**：Despite the Genie's suggestion that Aladdin to tell the princess who he really is, Aladdin remains as a suave prince, and takes Jasmine around the world on the magic carpet.
- **S20**：During the trip, Jasmine exposes Ali as a commoner, Aladdin, and demands the truth from him.
- **S21**：Aladdin instead fabricates a story that he sometimes dresses as a commoner to escape palace life.
- **S22**：The couple kisses as Aladdin returns her home.
- **S23**：Afterwards, Aladdin is captured by Jafar and thrown into the ocean, but the Genie rescues Aladdin as his second wish.
- **S24**：Aladdin returns to the palace and exposes Jafar's plot; and Jafar flees after noticing the lamp in Aladdin's possession, realizing who Aladdin is.
- **S25**：As Aladdin gets doubtful about revealing who really is, Iago steals the Genie's lamp and brings it to Jafar, who becomes the Genie's new master.
- **S26**：Jafar uses his first two wishes to become Sultan and the most powerful sorcerer in the world.
- **S27**：Using his new powers, Jafar forces Jasmine and her father to bow, exposes Aladdin as a street rat, then exiles him and Abu to a frozen wasteland.
- **S28**：Aladdin uses the magic carpet to return to Agrabah, where Jafar has imprisoned both Jasmine and the Sultan as his slaves.
- **S29**：Jasmine distracts Jafar with a seductive act as Aladdin tries to steal back the lamp, but Jafar confronts him.
- **S30**：He imprisons Jasmine in a large hourglass and turns himself into an enormous cobra.
- **S31**：As Jafar traps Aladdin in his coils, he boasts to be "the most powerful being on Earth", which causes Aladdin to shout out that the Genie is more powerful.
- **S32**：Faced with this realization, Jafar uses his final wish to become a genie.
- **S33**：However, Jafar discovers that genies are not free entities as he is sucked into a black lamp, dragging Iago with him.
- **S34**：The Genie flicks the lamp into the Cave of Wonders.
- **S35**：After Aladdin realizes that he cannot keep pretending to be something he is not, he decides to keep his promise and wish for the Genie's freedom.
- **S36**：Seeing Jasmine's love for Aladdin, the Sultan changes the law to allow her to marry whomever she deems worthy.
- **S37**：The newly free Genie leaves to explore the world while Aladdin and Jasmine celebrate their engagement.

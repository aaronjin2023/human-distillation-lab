## 角色资料

### Hannibal Lecter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-953d8218fbb73f12` · 独立版本：`profile-d0467364e787f550`

- **性格倾向**：在追查妹妹遇害者时，他表现出执着搜证并持续追猎的倾向（行为推断；S26、S28、S29、S31、S62）
- **核心动机**：他以对Mischa的承诺解释复仇，拒绝接受停止追杀的劝告（摘要直接叙述；S43、S45）
- **价值取舍**：在复仇推进中，他将惩罚仇敌置于法律与亲近者劝阻之上（行为推断；S33、S34、S43、S45）
- **决策方式**：他倾向把医学知识与细节观察转化为追踪和制服目标的手段（行为推断；S25、S26、S40、S49、S50）
- **情绪反应**：Grutas关于妹妹的说法激起暴怒，使他的攻击进一步升级（摘要直接叙述；S57、S58、S60）
- **成长变化**：他在Murasaki帮助下恢复言语，却因复仇暴行令她惊恐离开（摘要直接叙述；S15、S17、S58、S59）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：以守护妹妹的承诺复仇，却开始利用他人的孩子施压。

### Lady Murasaki

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-d6f8a409e4a6f1b2` · 独立版本：`profile-fb2678db47c7862d`

- **性格倾向**：在照料受创亲属时，她表现出耐心引导且愿意出面保护的倾向（行为推断；S17、S22）
- **核心动机**：她试图帮助Lecter恢复表达，并阻止他继续沉入复仇（行为推断；S17、S45）
- **价值取舍**：面对杀害Kolnas的机会，她明确把其孩子的处境纳入劝阻理由（摘要直接叙述；S37）
- **决策方式**：当Lecter受到谋杀调查时，她选择主动布置线索为其解围（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **情绪反应**：目睹Lecter对Grutas的残酷攻击时，她惊恐逃离（摘要直接叙述；S58、S59、S60）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾主动帮助Lecter摆脱嫌疑，后来又恳求他停止杀戮。

以上为依据完整摘要审校的综合笔记，未逐项标注句号；可结合下列完整选摘核对。

### Vladis Grutas

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-8405ba8e3e0b1398` · 独立版本：`profile-24a10ef61a67cf0f`

- **性格倾向**：在掌握人身优势时，他表现出以羞辱和暴力支配弱者的倾向（行为推断；S4、S48、S55）
- **核心动机**：面对Lecter的追杀，他试图先消灭威胁并利用人质设伏（行为推断；S39、S48、S54）
- **价值取舍**：他以族群迫害和人口贩运对待他人，显露对生命尊严的漠视（行为推断；S4、S39、S55）
- **决策方式**：直接派人刺杀失败后，他转向绑架亲近者诱使对手现身（行为推断；S39、S40、S41、S48）
- **沟通方式**：对峙时他以Lecter曾吃下妹妹的说法攻击其复仇正当性（摘要直接叙述；S57）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1944, soon after the Soviet Red Army had liberated Lithuania from the Nazi forces, the Nazis launch a counter-assault.
- **S2**：During this time, a family from an aristocratic background, consisting of eight-year-old Hannibal Lecter, his younger sister, Mischa, and their parents, quickly escape to the family's hunting lodge.
- **S3**：After the Nazis take over at Lecter Castle, six Lithuanian militiamen, who want to join the Waffen-SS, are told to prove their worth.
- **S4**：Grutas orders them to kill the Lecters' Jewish cook.
- **S5**：They also threaten another servant of the Lecter household, asking him if he was a Gypsy or a Jew.
- **S6**：As this happens, the Soviet Red Army begins launching attacks to retake the area.
- **S7**：A Soviet tank crew stops at the Lecters' lodge, telling the Lecters to leave the house while they get some water.
- **S8**：The Soviet soldiers allow Hannibal and Mischa to stay within the lodge, where it is warmer.
- **S9**：The Soviet tank is then spotted by a German Stuka bomber, which sparks a firefight.
- **S10**：The bomber is shot down by the tank, but subsequently crashes into it, and the ensuing explosion kills everyone still alive, except Hannibal and Mischa, who had remained inside the lodge.
- **S11**：The Lithuanian militiamen then loot Lecter Castle as the Soviet Red Army draws nearer.
- **S12**：The Soviet advance forces the Nazi militiamen to hide out in the woods, where they locate the Lecter lodge.
- **S13**：Finding no other food in the bitterly cold Baltic winter, they murder and cannibalize Mischa in front of her brother, who escapes and is found by Soviet soldiers.
- **S14**：Eight years later, Lecter once again lives in Lecter Castle, which has been turned into a Soviet-run orphanage.
- **S15**：He has been rendered mute by his childhood trauma.
- **S16**：Lecter escapes the castle orphanage, gets through border controls, and goes to live in Paris with his aunt, the Lady Murasaki.
- **S17**：She gets him to speak and begins teaching him the arts of flower arrangement, martial arts, and ancestor worship.
- **S18**：At a local market, a butcher and former Vichy collaborator, makes anti-Japanese racist remarks about Lady Murasaki.
- **S19**：The butcher later goes fishing and is defiant when Lecter turns up to request an apology.
- **S20**：Lecter slices the butcher's stomach, arm, and back with a katana samurai sword, and then decapitates him.
- **S21**：That same evening, he is questioned about the butcher's murder by Inspector Popil, a French detective who also lost his family during the war.
- **S22**：While her protege is being interrogated, Lady Murasaki places the butcher's head outside the headquarters with a Swastika carved into his forehead.
- **S23**：Lecter soon becomes the youngest person ever admitted to medical school in France.
- **S24**：He receives a working scholarship, where he is given a job preparing cadavers.
- **S25**：One day, Lecter witnesses a condemned war criminal receiving a sodium thiopental injection to force him to recall details about his crimes.
- **S26**：In an attempt to recall the names of those responsible for his sister's death, Lecter injects himself with the solution while listening to Glenn Gould's recording of the Goldberg Variations.
- **S27**：His subsequent flashback reveals that Pot Watcher had the dog tags of the other men who killed Mischa, and was then killed as the advancing Soviet forces bombed a part of the lodge.
- **S28**：Lecter reasons that the dog tags should still be in the ruins of the lodge.
- **S29**：Lecter returns to Lithuania in search of the dog tags, as well as his sister's remains.
- **S30**：While crossing the Soviet border, he draws the attention of Dortlich, who is now a Soviet border patrol officer.
- **S31**：Lecter excavates the ruins of the lodge and unearths the dog tags of the deserters who murdered his sister.
- **S32**：Dortlich tries to kill him, but Lecter gets the upper hand and incapacitates him.
- **S33**：After he buries Mischa's remains, Lecter tortures Dortlich into revealing the whereabouts of his accomplices.
- **S34**：He then decapitates Dortlich with a horse-drawn pulley.
- **S35**：At a restaurant in Fontainebleau, Lecter finds Kolnas's young daughter, whom he notices is wearing Mischa's bracelet.
- **S36**：He then gives her Kolnas's dog tag.
- **S37**：Kolnas enters the restaurant, but Lady Murasaki persuades Lecter not to kill him, for the sake of Kolnas's children.
- **S38**：Dortlich's murder, along with Kolnas' dog tag, puts the rest of the group on alert.
- **S39**：Grutas, now a sex trafficker, dispatches a second member of the group, Zigmas Milko, to kill Lecter.
- **S40**：Milko sneaks into Lecter's laboratory at night with a gun, but Lecter senses his presence and knocks him out with an injection.
- **S41**：Just as detective Popil is entering the lab, Lecter locks Milko in the cadaver tank and leaves him to drown in the embalming fluid.
- **S42**：Popil questions Lecter about Dortlich's murder, but is again unable to establish Lecter's guilt.
- **S43**：Popil then tries to dissuade him from hunting the gang and offers to let him go free if he helps locate Grutas.
- **S44**：After Lecter leaves, Popil remarks to his assistant that Lecter lost all of his humanity when Mischa died.
- **S45**：Lady Murasaki begs him to stop, but Lecter says that he made a promise to Mischa.
- **S46**：He plants a time bomb in Grutas' home and attacks him in the bath.
- **S47**：A maid alerts Grutas' bodyguards, but just as they are about to kill him, Lecter's bomb goes off and he escapes.
- **S48**：Grutas kidnaps Lady Murasaki to use her as bait.
- **S49**：Lecter recognizes the sounds of Kolnas' ortolans from his restaurant in the background.
- **S50**：Lecter goes there and threatens Kolnas' children, forcing him to give up the location of Grutas' boat.
- **S51**：Lecter then says he will leave Kolnas alone for the sake of his family.
- **S52**：Kolnas goes for the gun anyway, so Lecter impales him through the head with his tantō.
- **S53**：Lecter goes to the houseboat.
- **S54**：Just as he is about to untie Lady Murasaki, Grutas shoots him in the back.
- **S55**：Grutas then proceeds to molest Lady Murasaki.
- **S56**：Lecter takes out the tantō, which was broken by the force of the bullet, and slashes Grutas' Achilles tendons with it, crippling him.
- **S57**：Grutas claims that Lecter too had consumed his sister in broth fed to him by the deserters, and he was killing them to keep this fact secret.
- **S58**：An enraged Lecter carves his sister's initial, M, into Grutas's chest.
- **S59**：Lady Murasaki flees in horror.
- **S60**：Lecter proceeds by biting off Grutas' cheeks.
- **S61**：The houseboat is then incinerated, but Lecter, assumed to be dead, emerges from the nearby woods on the shore.
- **S62**：Lecter hunts down the last member of the group, Grentz, in Canada.
- **S63**：After killing him, Lecter moves to the United States to begin a new life, having been accepted to Johns Hopkins Medical School in Baltimore.

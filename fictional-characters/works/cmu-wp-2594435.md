## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film is set in the 1950s in a large country residence, as a family and its servants are preparing for Christmas, when the master of the house is discovered dead in his bed, with a dagger stuck into his back.
- **S2**：The murderer must be one of the eight women in the house at the time, and in the course of the investigations each has a tale to tell and secrets to hide.
- **S3**：The scene opens with Suzon returning from school for Christmas break, finding her mother Gaby, her younger sister Catherine, and her wheelchair-bound grandmother Mamy in the living room, where most of the action of the film takes place.
- **S4**：Their conversation drifts to the subject of the patriarch of the family, and Catherine leads the first song of the film, "Papa t'es plus dans le coup" .
- **S5**：The singing wakes up Suzon and Catherine's aunt Augustine, who picks arguments with the rest of the family and the two servants , eventually returning upstairs, threatening to commit suicide.
- **S6**：Mamy jumps out of her wheelchair, trying to stop her, haphazardly explaining her ability to walk as a "Christmas miracle." Augustine is eventually calmed down, and she sings her song of longing, "Message personnel" .
- **S7**：The maid takes the tray upstairs, finds Marcel's stabbed body, and screams.
- **S8**：Catherine goes up to see what happened and locks the door.
- **S9**：The others finally go up to Marcel's room to see him stabbed in the back.
- **S10**：Catherine tells the others that they should not disturb the room until the police arrive so they re-lock the door.
- **S11**：Realizing that the dogs have not barked the night before, it seems clear that the murderer was one of the women in the house.
- **S12**：Attempting to call the authorities, they find that the phoneline has been cut, and they will have to go in person to the police station.
- **S13**：However, the women are distracted by the announcement that someone is roaming in the garden, who for some reason, the guard dogs are not chasing.
- **S14**：The person turns out to be Marcel's sister Pierrette, a nightclub singer who is also rumoured to be a streetwalker, and has not been allowed to the house before, due to Gaby's dislike for her.
- **S15**：When questioned, she claims she received a mysterious phone call, telling her that her brother was dead; she also sings "A quoi sert de vivre libre" , commenting on her sexual freedom.
- **S16**：It is realized that she has been to the house before, as the dogs did not bark and she knew immediately which room belonged to her brother, making her the eighth potential killer.
- **S17**：The women try to start the car, and find that it has been sabotaged, cutting them off from help due to the snowstorm outside, until they can hitchhike to town when the weather clears.
- **S18**：The women spend their time trying to find the murderer amongst them.
- **S19**：It is discovered that Suzon in fact returned the night before, to tell her father in secret that she was pregnant.
- **S20**：She sings a song to Catherine, "Mon Amour, Mon Ami" , about her lover however, she has in fact been abused by her father.
- **S21**：We later find out that, unknown to everyone involved excluding Gaby, Suzon is not his child, and is actually the child of Gaby's first great love.
- **S22**：Gaby reveals that he was killed not long after her conception and that every time she looks at Suzon, she is reminded of her love for him.
- **S23**：Suspicion then swings to Madame Chanel, the housekeeper, whose actions the night before seem suspicious; it is revealed that she has been having an affair with Pierrette, who went to see her brother that night to ask for money to pay off her debts.
- **S24**：When some members of the family react in outrage to the fact that she is a lesbian, Madame Chanel retreats to the kitchen, and sings "Pour ne pas vivre seul" .
- **S25**：In the meantime we find out that Mamy, Suzon's and Catherine's "old and sick" grandmother, not only can walk but also possesses some valuable shares that could have saved Marcel from his bankruptcy.
- **S26**：Out of greed she lied that her shares have been stolen by someone who knew where she was hiding them.
- **S27**：The spotlight moves to Louise, the maid, who is found out to be Marcel's mistress.
- **S28**：She declares, however, affection for Gaby, but also expresses disappointment in her for her weakness and indecision.
- **S29**：She sings "Pile ou Face" , and removes the symbols of her servitude, her maid's cap and apron, asserting herself as an equal to the other women.
- **S30**：Gaby sings "Toi Jamais" , about Marcel, saying that he never paid enough attention to her, while other men did; it is revealed that she had an affair with his business partner, the same man who has been having an affair with Pierrette.
- **S31**：The two women get into a fight that turns into a make-out session on the living room floor, which the others walk in on.
- **S32**：Eventually, Madame Chanel discovers the solution to the mystery but is silenced by a gunshot; she is not hit but goes mute with fear, but Catherine takes the lead, revealing that she had hidden in her father's closet, and had seen the other women all talk to Marcel the night before, and explains the mystery: Marcel had faked his own death, with her help, to see what was really going on in his house.
- **S33**：She claims that he is now free of the other women's clutches, and rushes into his bedroom, only to see him shoot himself in the head, in absolute despair.
- **S34**：Mamy ends the film with the song "Il n'y a pas d'amour heureux" .

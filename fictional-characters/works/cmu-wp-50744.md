### Luke Skywalker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-df032d204dfa317b` · 独立版本：`profile-29a09d7f7e659ffc`

- **性格倾向**：面对身陷黑暗面的父亲，仍倾向相信对方可以改变（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **核心动机**：担忧自己的存在危及反抗军任务，因此主动离队面对父亲（行为推断；S13、S15、S16）
- **价值取舍**：在能够杀死父亲取而代之时，选择守住绝地身份的界限（摘要直接叙述；S24）
- **情绪反应**：对妹妹遭受威胁极为敏感，保护亲人的愤怒一度压过克制（行为推断；S22、S23）
- **决策方式**：愿意以主动被俘换取接触目标的机会，承担个人受制风险（行为推断；S3、S16）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：坚守克制与救赎信念，却会因亲人受威胁而猛烈攻击。

### Darth Vader

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-67cfa06baea2ed1c` · 独立版本：`profile-a2ce2f2c9d36fc72`

- **性格倾向**：在父子冲突中起初倾向以控制和威胁迫使对方屈服（行为推断；S17、S22）
- **关系模式**：起初服从皇帝安排并交出儿子，最终因不忍儿子受苦而反叛（摘要直接叙述；S17、S26）
- **决策方式**：面对卢克的抵抗，利用其对妹妹的牵挂施加情感压力（行为推断；S22、S23）
- **价值取舍**：最终以牺牲自身为代价救下儿子，将亲情置于效忠之上（行为推断；S26）
- **成长变化**：临终从控制儿子转为渴望直接相见，并希望女儿知道其善意（行为推断；S22、S27）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：对皇帝的服从与对儿子的亲情，最终发展为不可兼容的选择。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Luke Skywalker initiates a plan to rescue Han Solo from the crime lord Jabba the Hutt with the help of Princess Leia, Lando Calrissian, Chewbacca, C-3PO and R2-D2.
- **S2**：Leia infiltrates Jabba's palace on Tatooine disguised as a bounty hunter and releases Han from a block of carbonite, but she is captured and enslaved.
- **S3**：Luke arrives soon afterward and allows himself to be captured.
- **S4**：After Luke survives a battle with the Rancor, Jabba sentences Luke and Han to be executed by the Sarlacc.
- **S5**：Luke breaks free and a large battle erupts, during which Leia strangles Jabba to death, Han knocks Boba Fett into the gaping maw of the Sarlacc and Luke destroys Jabba's sail barge.
- **S6**：While Han and Leia meet with the other Rebels, Luke returns to Dagobah, only to find that Yoda is dying.
- **S7**：With his last breaths, Yoda confirms that Darth Vader is Luke's father; he also mentions "another Skywalker".
- **S8**：The spirit of Obi-Wan Kenobi reveals that the "other Skywalker" Yoda spoke of is Luke's twin sister, who Luke discovers is Leia.
- **S9**：Obi-Wan then tells Luke that he must confront Vader again to defeat the Empire.
- **S10**：The Rebel Alliance learns that the Empire has been constructing a new Death Star, and hatches a plan to destroy it.
- **S11**：Han leads a strike team to destroy the battle station's shield generator on the forest moon of Endor, allowing a squadron of starfighters to enter the incomplete superstructure and destroy the station from within.
- **S12**：The strike team, with Luke in tow, travels to Endor in an Imperial shuttle; Vader senses Luke's presence on the shuttle, but lets them through so that they will be ambushed by the Imperial forces lying in wait on Endor.
- **S13**：Sensing Vader's presence, Luke fears he is endangering the mission.
- **S14**：On Endor, Luke and his companions encounter a tribe of Ewoks and form a partnership with them.
- **S15**：Later, Luke confesses to Leia that she is his sister, that Vader is their father and that he is leaving to confront him.
- **S16**：Luke surrenders to Imperial troops, so that they will bring him to Vader.
- **S17**：He unsuccessfully tries to convince Vader to turn from the dark side of the Force, but Vader takes Luke to the Death Star to meet Emperor Palpatine, his Sith master and leader of the Empire.
- **S18**：Luke learns that the Death Star is fully operational and set to destroy the Rebellion.
- **S19**：On Endor, the Rebels are captured by Imperial forces, but a surprise counterattack by the Ewoks allows the Rebels to launch an attack.
- **S20**：Meanwhile, Lando leads the Rebel fleet in the Millennium Falcon to the Death Star, only to find the station's shield is still up and the Imperial fleet waiting for them.
- **S21**：Palpatine tempts Luke to give in to his anger and join the dark side, and Luke and Vader engage in a lightsaber duel.
- **S22**：Vader discovers that Luke has a sister, and threatens to turn her to the dark side.
- **S23**：Luke snaps and attacks Vader, severing his father's right hand.
- **S24**：Palpatine entreats Luke to kill Vader and take his place; Luke steps back from the brink and refuses, declaring himself a Jedi.
- **S25**：Palpatine attacks him with Force lightning.
- **S26**：Unable to watch his son suffer, Vader turns on Palpatine and throws him down a reactor shaft to his death, dooming himself to die in the process.
- **S27**：With his dying breaths, the redeemed Anakin Skywalker asks Luke to remove his mask so he can look on his son, just for once, with his own eyes instead of through the mask, and tell Leia that there was good in him after all.
- **S28**：On Endor, the strike team, with the help of the Ewoks, defeats the Imperial forces and destroys the shield generator, allowing the Rebel fleet to launch a final assault on the Death Star.
- **S29**：Lando leads the remaining ships into the station's core and destroys the main reactor.
- **S30**：Luke escapes on Palpatine's Imperial shuttle with his father's body before the Death Star explodes, while Lando escapes in the Falcon.
- **S31**：On Endor, Han tells Leia that he knows she loves Luke and offers to step aside; she tells him that Luke is her brother and kisses him.
- **S32**：That evening, Luke returns to Endor and cremates his father's body and armor on a funeral pyre.
- **S33**：As the Rebels celebrate the end of the Empire, Luke sees the spirits of Obi-Wan, Yoda and Anakin Skywalker watching over them.

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Daphne Vincent is a children's librarian who moved to Michigan to live with her fiancé, Peter.
- **S3**：Shortly before their wedding, Peter abruptly breaks up with Daphne to be with his longtime friend, Petra.
- **S4**：Daphne moves in with Miles Nowak, Petra's jilted boyfriend.
- **S5**：A heartbroken Daphne plans to move away from Michigan after the Read-a-Thon, an all-night fundraiser run by her library, which she is highly passionate about.
- **S6**：Peter and Petra invite Miles and Daphne to their wedding, spurring the two to drink and bond.
- **S7**：Daphne RSVPs to the wedding and lies to Peter that she is dating Miles.
- **S8**：Miles agrees to the plan.
- **S9**：Once he learns of Daphne's tentative plans to move away, he offers to show her around Michigan.
- **S10**：Daphne and Miles slowly get to know one another, and despite their differences, they become friends.
- **S11**：Daphne befriends her tough co-worker, Ashleigh, and reveals the fake relationship with Miles.
- **S12**：Daphne and Miles' mutual attraction grows.
- **S13**：Miles' younger sister Julia visits from Chicago, joining their friend group with Ashleigh.
- **S14**：Daphne and Miles continue their weekly trips around Michigan, with Miles eventually taking Daphne to a prom for senior citizens so she can get more volunteers for the Read-a-Thon.
- **S15**：During the prom, they run into Peter and Petra and egg them on by kissing and dancing together, but Daphne, realizing her growing feelings towards Miles, spontaneously flees.
- **S16**：Miles follows her and the two admit mutual feelings for one another but refrain from taking things further when Miles discloses his tendency to panic when things get complicated.
- **S17**：Daphne decides to internally restrain her desire for him.
- **S18**：Daphne's negligent father, Jason, and his new wife, Starfire, visit.
- **S19**：Daphne confides in Miles her struggle to accept her father.
- **S20**：Jason and Starfire flake on a visit to visit Daphne, with Miles informing Daphne that they decided to spend time with new friends.
- **S21**：To lift her spirits, Miles brings her to his favorite beach.
- **S22**：The two sleep together and spend the next day together.
- **S23**：The next Monday, Daphne realizes that she forgot to celebrate Ashleigh's birthday with Ashleigh by painting her bedroom together.
- **S24**：Ashleigh rebuffs Daphne when the latter tries to apologize.
- **S25**：Miles also disappears for a few days.
- **S26**：Daphne unexpectedly gets a call to interview for a job in Maryland, which would let her live close to her mother.
- **S27**：Peter tries to get back together with Daphne, but Daphne rejects him.
- **S28**：Peter reveals that Miles went to help Petra and points out that they will get back together.
- **S29**：Daphne finally runs into Miles again one day after work, where he admits to having panicked and gone to help Petra.
- **S30**：He confesses he is in love with Daphne, but she rejects him and reveals her plans to leave Michigan.
- **S31**：Daphne's mother consoles her and renews her confidence.
- **S32**：Daphne and Ashleigh repair their friendship after Daphne secretly paints Ashleigh's bedroom, and Daphne spends the next few days with Ashleigh until the Read-a-Thon.
- **S33**：Julia informs Daphne that thanks to her encouragement, the siblings had discussed their troubled childhood.
- **S34**：Before the Read-a-Thon begins, Daphne gets a supportive phone call from her father, where she finds out that Miles had driven hours to try and get him to return.
- **S35**：Daphne realizes the truth of Miles' feelings.
- **S36**：The Read-a-Thon is a success.
- **S37**：Afterwards, Miles professes his continuing love for Daphne and begs her to stay in Michigan with him.
- **S38**：Daphne confesses her love and admiration for Miles, with the caveat that she will move into her own place for a while to avoid repeating her mistake with Peter.
- **S39**：One year later, Daphne and Miles host a housewarming party with their friends and family, where Daphne recounts the "funny story" of how she and Miles got together.

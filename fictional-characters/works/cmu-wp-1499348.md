## 角色资料

### Mumble

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-1af6dbc947175a41` · 独立版本：`profile-d685d67605a081fb`

- **性格倾向**：面对无法解释的异物，他倾向追根究底并亲自求证（行为推断；S19、S20、S21、S45）
- **核心动机**：遭到族群放逐后，他仍立志查明饥荒的真正原因（摘要直接叙述；S37、S38）
- **价值取舍**：面对族群困境，他将寻找真实原因置于服从责难之上（行为推断；S35、S36、S37、S38）
- **决策方式**：担心格洛丽亚遇险时，他选择以伤人言语阻止她同行（摘要直接叙述；S43）
- **沟通方式**：语言求助失效后，他通过舞蹈建立与人类的互动（行为推断；S48、S49、S53）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：希望保护格洛丽亚，却通过贬低她的歌声将她推开。

### Gloria

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-d792e56dd5b98822` · 独立版本：`profile-b87dc62a3089766a`

- **性格倾向**：面对求偶表演，她倾向自主判断而不随众多追求者起舞（行为推断；S25、S27、S28）
- **价值取舍**：在亲密互动中，她倾向重视真实表达而排斥假唱伪装（行为推断；S26、S27、S28、S29、S32、S34）
- **关系模式**：她对曼波怀有感情，并愿意尝试与他的舞步配合（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32、S33）
- **决策方式**：面对族群质疑，她先支持曼波，再凭装置辨认人类存在（行为推断；S36、S52）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：因曼波假唱而生气，却愿意接受他真实的舞蹈表达。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Every penguin sings a unique song called a "heartsong" to attract a mate.
- **S2**：If the male penguin's heartsong matches the female's song, the two penguins mate.
- **S3**：Norma Jean, a female penguin, sings "Kiss", whereupon Memphis, a male penguin, sings "Heartbreak Hotel".
- **S4**：Norma Jean chooses Memphis as her mate.
- **S5**：She lays an egg, which is left with Memphis while Norma Jean leaves with the other females to fish.
- **S6**：While the males struggle through the harsh winter, Memphis accidentally drops the egg, briefly exposing it to the freezing Antarctic temperatures.
- **S7**：The resulting chick, Mumble, has blue eyes, ever-lasting down feathers, and a terrible singing voice.
- **S8**：However, Mumble has a talent that no other penguin has ever seen before: tap dancing.
- **S9**：One day Mumble wanders into a secluded area, where he is free to dance without any complaints.
- **S10**：Mumble is interrupted when Boss Skua and his posse Dino, Frankie, and Vinnie attempt to eat him.
- **S11**：Mumble stalls by asking the leader of the pack about a yellow band that is attached to his right ankle.
- **S12**：The Boss Skua tells Mumble that he had been abducted by "aliens".
- **S13**：Mumble narrowly escapes the hungry birds by falling into a crevice.
- **S14**：Years later, Mumble, now an adult but still half-covered in fluffy down, joins his class graduates on their first trip into the ocean, where they find a large iceberg to rest on.
- **S15**：After interrupting the graduates' song, Mumble is forced to leave, and finds a smaller iceberg.
- **S16**：A leopard seal chases him off the iceberg.
- **S17**：After escaping the leopard seal, he befriends a small group of bachelors which are Adelie Penguins who form a club called "the Amigos": the leader Ramon, the brothers Raul and Nestor, and twin brothers Rinaldo and Lombardo.
- **S18**：The Amigos quickly embrace Mumble's dance moves and assimilate him into their misfit group.
- **S19**：Mumble's joy is cut short when he accidentally starts an avalanche and causes a hidden human excavator to tumble out from a glacier.
- **S20**：Driven by curiosity, he sets out to find the "aliens" responsible for the machine.
- **S21**：Mumble has a lot of questions, and the group suggests that he ask Lovelace, a Rockhopper penguin who answers questions in exchange for stones and is known as the guru.
- **S22**：Lovelace has the plastic rings of a six pack entangled around his neck, which he uses to project his guru image.
- **S23**：When asked if the rings were from the aliens, Lovelace denies that aliens exists and claims that the plastic rings were bestowed upon him by mystic beings.
- **S24**：In Mumble's old home, it is mating season, and Gloria is the center of attention.
- **S25**：Although she is surrounded by a large horde of suitors, none of their heartsongs interest her.
- **S26**：Ramon tries to help Mumble by singing a Spanish version of "My Way", with Mumble lip syncing.
- **S27**：Gloria is not fooled, however, for she knows Mumble cannot sing.
- **S28**：She then notices Ramon behind Mumble's back.
- **S29**：Gloria becomes angry and turns her back on Mumble.
- **S30**：Gloria continues towards the other males visibly distraught.
- **S31**：As a last resort, Mumble tries to persuade her to sing along to his tapping rhythm.
- **S32**：Because of her affection for him, she complies.
- **S33**：As Mumble's beat speeds up, Gloria finds the chorus to her heartsong, and realizes it is "Boogie Wonderland".
- **S34**：Overcome with happiness that they can now be mates, the pair begin dancing, along with the other penguins.
- **S35**：Noah the elder sees their lack of fish as punishment from the Great 'Guin for Mumble's dancing.
- **S36**：Mumble tries to explain about the mysterious "aliens", and that they are the cause of the scarceness of fish, but only his mother and Gloria believe him.
- **S37**：Noah exiles Mumble from the colony.
- **S38**：Mumble vows that he will find the real cause of the famine.
- **S39**：He and the Amigos return to Lovelace only to find him being choked by the plastic rings.
- **S40**：Lovelace confesses that he got stuck in the rings while swimming near the forbidden shores.
- **S41**：He reveals that the forbidden shores are located past the land of elephant seals and beyond the blizzard country.
- **S42**：Mumble, the Amigos and Lovelace start their journey.
- **S43**：Gloria tries to come, but Mumble, fearing for her safety, drives her off by insulting her singing talents.
- **S44**：They travel across vast territories till they reach the shore.
- **S45**：Mumble sees a boat and swims after it.
- **S46**：He swims far, and is washed up exhausted onshore.
- **S47**：He is rescued and kept at Marine World with some Magellanic Penguins.
- **S48**：He tries to communicate with the "aliens", but when his pleas fail, Mumble nearly succumbs to madness after long confinement.
- **S49**：When a girl attempts to interact with Mumble by tapping the glass, he starts dancing, and soon a large crowd gathers.
- **S50**：He is released back into the wild, with a Tracking system on his back.
- **S51**：He leads the humans to his colony.
- **S52**：The other penguins are skeptical, but when Gloria notices the device, they realize that the "aliens" exist.
- **S53**：Convinced, the penguins, along with the elders, dance with Mumble to get the humans' attention.
- **S54**：A research team arrives and film the penguins dancing.
- **S55**：They bring the footage back and a worldwide debate ensues.
- **S56**：The governments realize they are overfishing.
- **S57**：Antarctic fishing is banned, and the fish population recovers.
- **S58**：At this, the Emperor Penguins and the Amigos celebrate.
- **S59**：In the final scene, a baby penguin is seen dancing next to Mumble and Gloria, revealed to be their son Erik in Happy Feet Two.

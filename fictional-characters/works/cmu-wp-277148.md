## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Julianne Potter , a 27-year-old New York restaurant critic, receives a call from her longtime friend Michael O'Neil .
- **S2**：In college, the two made an agreement that if neither of them were married by the time they turned 28, they would marry each other.
- **S3**：Three weeks before her 28th birthday, Michael tells her that in four days, he will marry Kimberly Wallace , a 20-year-old University of Chicago student from a wealthy family.
- **S4**：Julianne is upset that Michael will marry someone so wrong for him, and someone he has known for such a short period of time.
- **S5**：She realizes that she is in love with Michael, and heads to Chicago, intent on sabotaging his wedding.
- **S6**：Soon after arriving she meets Kimberly, who asks her to be the maid of honor.
- **S7**：This sets off a comical scenario in which Julianne must pretend to be the dutiful maid of honor while secretly scheming ways to prevent the wedding from happening.
- **S8**：She engages in petty sabotage - for example, taking Kimberly and Michael to a karaoke bar after discovering that Kimberly is a terrible singer - and later asks her gay friend George Downes  to pretend they are engaged, hoping to make Michael jealous.
- **S9**：When these tactics fail, George persuades Julianne to do the obvious: tell Michael she is in love with him.
- **S10**：On the morning of the wedding, Michael gets Julianne alone and tells her that it'll be the last time they ever get to be alone.
- **S11**：He expresses some skepticism in marrying Kim, explaining that he and Kim don't share a special song like he and Julianne do.
- **S12**：Michael discreetly gives Julianne the invitation to tell him she's in love with him, but she lets the moment "pass her by".
- **S13**：Michael starts singing their song as he grabs Julianne and holds her while they dance one last time.
- **S14**：Afterwards, they take a walk and Julianne confesses her love to Michael, asks him to marry her instead, and passionately kisses him.
- **S15**：Kimberly witnesses this and runs off, but Michael chases her.
- **S16**：Julianne pursues him but finally realizes Michael loves Kimberly.
- **S17**：She apologizes and explains that she kissed Michael unexpectedly, but he didn't kiss her back because he was in love with Kimberly.
- **S18**：After the wedding Julianne tells Michael that he and Kimberly can use their special song until they find one of their own, essentially acting like a true best friend .
- **S19**：Michael marries Kimberly, and Julianne wishes them well, consoled by her platonic friend George.

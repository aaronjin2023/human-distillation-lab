## 角色资料

### Sophie

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7efa509176eb69f7` · 独立版本：`profile-f94c24bad9d87302`

- **性格倾向**：面对感情失控时，倾向用富有创意的计划重新争取主动（行为推断；S1、S9、S10、S14）
- **核心动机**：起初执意在原定婚期前挽回Jeff，并将策略分享给女性（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10、S11）
- **情绪反应**：失恋与受骗感先后使她陷入崩溃，并一度拒绝再相信爱情（摘要直接叙述；S6、S7、S18）
- **决策方式**：在挽回行动中，倾向将有限线索迅速转化为结盟方案（行为推断；S14、S15）
- **成长变化**：在母亲劝解后重新接受爱情，并放下挽回Jeff的目标（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20、S22）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：希望用可复制的策略掌控爱情，却因误判关系而再次受伤。

### Gordon

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-2a3099269896e352` · 独立版本：`profile-29714c76a266a57e`

- **性格倾向**：在亲密关系中呈现照顾他人的倾向，长期承担对妹妹的照料（行为推断；S17）
- **关系模式**：与Joanna实际是兄妹关系，却被Sophie误认成昔日恋人（摘要直接叙述；S14、S17）
- **决策方式**：面对Sophie提出的结盟方案，选择参与她的挽回与复仇行动（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **情绪反应**：在共同经历这段挽回行动期间，逐渐对Sophie产生爱情（摘要直接叙述；S21）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：接受妹妹留意Sophie的托付，却在相处过程中爱上Sophie。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Comic book artist Sophie  is a sweet and carefree girl brimming with creativity.
- **S2**：She and her handsome surgeon boyfriend of two years, Jeff , are the perfect couple.
- **S3**：After his marriage proposal to her in front of her family and all of her friends, Sophie is the happiest person in the world.
- **S4**：One day, Jeff meets a famous movie star, Joanna , during a surgical procedure.
- **S5**：Immediately afterwards, he breaks up with Sophie.
- **S6**：Devastated, Sophie takes to bed.
- **S7**：She is unable to face anyone, especially her mother who had been planning the wedding.
- **S8**：Sophie decides to cover up the break-up, with only her 2 best friends, Lucy  and Lily  knowing the truth.
- **S9**：She comes up with a master plan: she will use the remaining two months before the wedding date to do whatever it takes to get Jeff back.
- **S10**：She also plans to publish her many strategies in her new comic book.
- **S11**：She wants to show women everywhere how to use a 'scientific' approach to win love back.
- **S12**：Sophie's first strategy of "Generosity and Forgiveness" does not go well.
- **S13**：At a Halloween party, hosted by Gordon , a photographer, she drowns her sorrows in alcohol.
- **S14**：She stumbles upon a photo of Gordon and Joanna together which immediately gives her the idea of forming an alliance with him, believing they were lovers.
- **S15**：Gordon agrees to become Sophie's partner and her quest for revenge becomes a roller coaster of farcical twists and turns.
- **S16**：Soon after a Christmas party at Gordon's place, Joanna reveals to Sophie that she has never lost Jeff at all and asked Gordon to keep an eye on her.
- **S17**：It was then revealed that he and Joanna are actually siblings because he's been taking care of her since they were young.
- **S18**：Feeling betrayed, Sophie gives up the notions of love and tells Gordon to leave her alone.
- **S19**：Her mother then learns the truth and is finally able to convince her to give love another chance.
- **S20**：Sophie calls off her relationship with Jeff and goes to visit Gordon, but he has already left for Europe.
- **S21**：Joanna is there and tells her that during that time, he fell in love with Sophie.
- **S22**：She reunites with Gordon and Little San-hei in Europe.

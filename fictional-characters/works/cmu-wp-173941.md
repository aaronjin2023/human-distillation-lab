## 角色资料

### Frodo Baggins

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3666e547ad814e29` · 独立版本：`profile-c9e8c9db0ea23c13`

- **性格倾向**：面对使命重担与同伴受腐化的风险，他倾向于主动承担个人危险。（行为推断；S22、S35）
- **核心动机**：得知魔戒必须在末日火山销毁后，他自愿承担携戒前往的任务。（摘要直接叙述；S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：面对同伴可能被魔戒腐化的风险，他听从警告，决定独自继续旅程。（摘要直接叙述；S35）
- **价值取舍**：在是否承担携戒任务的选择中，他把摧毁危险魔戒置于个人避险之前。（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **关系模式**：他起初不情愿让山姆同行，后来明确表示很高兴有山姆陪伴。（摘要直接叙述；S42、S45）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Gandalf

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-5e3b0856c2abfbc0` · 独立版本：`profile-f3135bc8f5b7da3e`

- **性格倾向**：面对魔戒威胁时，他倾向于及时示警，并为持戒者安排同行支持。（行为推断；S8、S9）
- **核心动机**：他警告弗罗多并安排山姆同行，显示此时意在帮助持戒者避开追捕。（行为推断；S8、S9）
- **决策方式**：遭囚禁后，他借助外援脱身并赶往瑞文戴尔，继续参与护戒行动。（行为推断；S19、S22）
- **价值取舍**：面对炎魔时，他以自身承担危险换取同伴逃生，表现出保护同伴的取舍。（行为推断；S27、S28）
- **关系模式**：他为弗罗多安排山姆作伴，后来自己也成为携戒队伍的一员。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S22）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Boromir

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-36266a5ee3d5a144` · 独立版本：`profile-fb5b949681e9beab`

- **性格倾向**：在救国压力与魔戒诱惑下，他倾向于把危险力量视为可用工具。（行为推断；S33）
- **核心动机**：他试图夺取魔戒，因为相信这是拯救自己国土的唯一办法。（摘要直接叙述；S33）
- **价值取舍**：在夺戒情境中，他把自己认定的救国需要置于尊重持戒同伴之前。（行为推断；S33）
- **成长变化**：他从试图夺戒转为意识到自己的行为并寻找弗罗多，随后救援其他同伴。（摘要直接叙述；S33、S35、S38）
- **关系模式**：在梅里与皮平遇险时，他尝试救援两人，并在过程中受到致命箭伤。（摘要直接叙述；S37、S38）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：试图夺取同伴的魔戒，之后又冒死救援其他同伴。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In the Second Age, the Dark Lord Sauron attempts to conquer Middle-Earth using his One Ring.
- **S2**：In battle against the Elves and Men, Prince Isildur cuts the Ring from Sauron's hand, destroying his physical form and vanquishing his army.
- **S3**：However, Sauron's "life force" is bound to the Ring, allowing him to survive while the Ring remains.
- **S4**：Isildur, corrupted by the Ring's power, refuses to destroy it.
- **S5**：When Isildur is killed by Orcs, the Ring is lost in a river for 2,500 years.
- **S6**：The Ring is found by Gollum, who possesses it for centuries until it is found by the Hobbit Bilbo Baggins.
- **S7**：Sixty years later, Bilbo leaves the Ring to his nephew, Frodo Baggins.
- **S8**：Upon learning that the Ring belonged to Sauron, the Wizard Gandalf the Grey warns Frodo that Sauron's forces will come for him.
- **S9**：Gandalf catches Frodo's gardener Samwise Gamgee "eavesdropping" underneath a window and sends him with Frodo to leave the Shire.
- **S10**：Gandalf rides to Isengard to meet with the head of his order, Saruman the White, who reveals that Sauron's servants, the Nazgûl, have been sent to capture the Ring.
- **S11**：Saruman reveals himself to be in service to Sauron, and imprisons Gandalf atop his tower.
- **S12**：Saruman commands Sauron's Orcs to construct weapons of war and produce a new breed of Orc fighters: the Uruk-hai.
- **S13**：While travelling to Bree, Frodo and Sam are joined by Merry and Pippin, and are nearly captured by the Nazgûl.
- **S14**：At the Inn of the Prancing Pony, Frodo meets the mysterious ranger Aragorn, who hides them from their pursuers and agrees to lead them to Rivendell.
- **S15**：During the journey they are attacked by the Nazgûl.
- **S16**：Aragorn fights off the creatures, but Frodo is wounded by their Morgul blade, which will turn him into a wraith.
- **S17**：While chased by the Nazgûl, Frodo is saved by the Elf Arwen who uses her magic to summon a surge of water that sweeps away the pursuing Nazgûl.
- **S18**：Arwen takes Frodo to Rivendell where he is healed by her father, Elrond.
- **S19**：Gandalf escapes Saruman's tower with the aid of Gwaihir the eagle and travels to Rivendell.
- **S20**：Elrond calls a council of the races still loyal to Middle-earth to decide what should be done with the Ring.
- **S21**：It is revealed that the Ring can only be destroyed by throwing it into the fires of Mount Doom, where it was forged.
- **S22**：Frodo volunteers to take the Ring, accompanied by Sam, Merry, Pippin, Gandalf, and Aragorn.
- **S23**：They are joined by the Elf Legolas, the Dwarf Gimli, and Boromir, man of Gondor, to form the Fellowship of the Ring.
- **S24**：The Fellowship set out but are hindered by Saruman's magic.
- **S25**：They travel through the dwarf Mines of Moria.
- **S26**：Inside, they find that Orcs have overrun the mines and slain the Dwarves; they also realise that Gollum is following them, determined to reclaim the Ring.
- **S27**：The Fellowship is attacked by Orcs and a Balrog, an ancient demon of fire and shadow.
- **S28**：Gandalf confronts the Balrog, allowing the others to escape, but both Gandalf and the Balrog fall into an abyss.
- **S29**：Mourning Gandalf's apparent death, the group flees to Lothlórien, where they are sheltered by its rulers, the Elves Galadriel and Celeborn.
- **S30**：That night, Galadriel informs Frodo that it is his destiny to destroy the Ring.
- **S31**：The Fellowship continue their journey.
- **S32**：Meanwhile, Saruman assembles a force of Uruk-hai to hunt the Fellowship.
- **S33**：After arriving at Parth Galen, Boromir gives in to the Ring's corruption and tries to take it from Frodo, believing that it is the only way to save his realm.
- **S34**：Frodo manages to escape by wearing the Ring and becoming invisible.
- **S35**：Boromir realises what he has done, and attempts to find Frodo, who decides to continue his journey alone, heeding Galadriel's warning that the Ring will eventually corrupt the other members of the Fellowship.
- **S36**：The Uruk-hai arrive and Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli attempt to hold them off while Frodo escapes.
- **S37**：Merry and Pippin lead the Orcs away from Frodo and are nearly killed.
- **S38**：Boromir tries to save them, but in the process is shot fatally by the Uruk-Hai leader, Lurtz.
- **S39**：The Uruk-Hai capture Merry and Pippin.
- **S40**：Aragorn slays Lurtz and tends to a dying Boromir, and promises him that the world of men will not fall.
- **S41**：Boromir dies with Aragorn, Gimli and Legolas by his side.
- **S42**：Sam finds Frodo, who reluctantly lets Sam join him.
- **S43**：Aragorn, Legolas and Gimli set out to rescue Merry and Pippin.
- **S44**：As Frodo and Sam gaze out at the land before them, they grieve over the others.
- **S45**：Frodo says that he is glad that Sam is with him and the two Hobbits set off towards Mordor.

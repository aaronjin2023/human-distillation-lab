## 角色资料

### Bella Swan

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-467346738db7bda3` · 独立版本：`profile-8b0c414432f7d02f`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，她面对婚姻与超常妊娠的风险仍显得坚定。（行为推断；S3、S12）
- **核心动机**：她坚持保住腹中孩子，并借助罗莎莉保护自己的选择。（摘要直接叙述；S12）
- **决策方式**：健康恶化时，她接受饮血这一补救办法以恢复体力。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **关系模式**：她与爱德华共同面对怀孕、生产及转化带来的危机。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S11、S17）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Edward Cullen

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-59b431852ba8a9ba` · 独立版本：`profile-a4048d1f1341ebd5`

- **性格倾向**：在亲密关系中，他对可能伤害贝拉的后果格外谨慎。（行为推断；S8、S9）
- **核心动机**：他注入毒液，试图在贝拉濒死时挽救她的生命。（摘要直接叙述；S17）
- **决策方式**：察觉自己令贝拉受伤后，他先决定停止与她亲密。（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **沟通方式**：他在婚礼之前向贝拉坦白自己过去曾经杀过人。（摘要直接叙述；S2）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：担心亲密会伤害贝拉，却后来仍接受她的主动。

### Jacob Black

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-b38e7589a10a55af` · 独立版本：`profile-c20abb3d90f5f5d4`

- **性格倾向**：在贝拉相关的冲突中，他倾向于带着强烈情绪迅速行动。（行为推断；S5、S13、S18）
- **核心动机**：他反对杀害贝拉及孩子，离开族群前去保护她。（摘要直接叙述；S13）
- **决策方式**：贝拉被认为死亡后，他一度试图杀婴，因发现烙印而停手。（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18）
- **关系模式**：他对贝拉有保护之意，后来与孩子形成烙印联系。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S18）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Further2}} The film starts out as Bella Swan plans her wedding with the Cullen family.
- **S2**：Before the wedding, Edward Cullen tells Bella that he has murdered people in the past.
- **S3**：Undaunted by this, Bella and Edward go ahead with the wedding.
- **S4**：During the reception following her wedding to Edward, Bella is visited by her friend Jacob Black.
- **S5**：When Bella admits that she and Edward plan to consummate their marriage during their honeymoon, Jacob becomes angry but is held back by his pack.
- **S6**：He then runs off into the woods, and Edward takes an upset Bella back to the reception.
- **S7**：The couple spends their honeymoon on the Cullens' private Brazilian island.
- **S8**：Although hesitant, Edward has sex with Bella for the first time.
- **S9**：After, Edward realizes that he bruised Bella's arm, back and shoulders during sex and he vows to never be intimate with her again, much to her disappointment; he later gives in to her seductions.
- **S10**：Two weeks into their honeymoon, Bella discovers that she is pregnant with a baby growing at an extraordinarily accelerated rate.
- **S11**：After consulting with Carlisle, they rush back to Forks.
- **S12**：Bella refuses to consider having an abortion, and enlists Rosalie's help to protect her wishes.
- **S13**：Opposed to Sam Uley's plans to kill Bella and her child, which is thought to be a demon, Jacob angrily leaves his pack and arrives at the Cullens' to protect Bella; Seth and Leah Clearwater join him, forming a new wolf pack.
- **S14**：As the pregnancy progresses, Bella's health severely deteriorates.
- **S15**：As a last resort, she begins drinking blood, which satisfies the fetus' vampiric thirst and allows her to regain some strength.
- **S16**：Soon afterward, Bella goes into a painful labor and gives birth to her child, Renesmee, an conflation of the names of Bella's mother, Renée and Edward's adoptive mother, Esme.
- **S17**：To save her life, Edward injects Bella's heart with his venom to transform her into a vampire, but nothing seems to happen and Bella is thought to be dead.
- **S18**：Greatly distraught, Jacob attempts to kill the baby, but stops when he realizes he has imprinted on the child.
- **S19**：When the werewolves learn of Bella's "death", they attack the Cullens' house in an attempt to kill the baby.
- **S20**：They are forced to stop after learning Jacob has imprinted on Renesmee .
- **S21**：After Bella is thought to be deceased, Carlisle notices there's a slight pulse and tells Edward and the rest of the Cullens as they clean the body.
- **S22**：Soon after, her wounds suddenly heal as the venom spreads through her body.
- **S23**：Bella's heart finally ceases, and she awakens as a newborn vampire.
- **S24**：In a post-credits scene, the Volturi hear about Bella's conversion; Aro notes that it does not end their dispute with the Cullens, proclaiming, "They have something I want".

## 角色资料

### Alice Kingsleigh

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a346cca4bbbcbae5` · 独立版本：`profile-40a1a97747993446`

- **性格倾向**：在伙伴被捕时表现出重情且坚持己见的一面，执意前往救援。（行为推断；S10、S11）
- **核心动机**：面对留在地下世界的邀请，明确选择回家并按自己的方式生活。（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27）
- **决策方式**：面对决战会借父亲的话鼓励自己，将获得的勇气转化为行动。（行为推断；S22、S24）
- **成长变化**：从面对求婚困惑逃离，转为公开主张自主生活并踏上商船。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S27、S29）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：曾对担任勇士犹疑，后来仍鼓励自己迎战。

### Hatter

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-fc9e022f5a639c24` · 独立版本：`profile-e4a61a54ec171c3d`

- **性格倾向**：在爱丽丝面临被捕时显得勇于担险，愿以自己被捕助她脱身。（行为推断；S10）
- **核心动机**：倾向推动对红皇后统治的反抗，脱离处刑危机后号召起义。（行为推断；S9、S18）
- **决策方式**：在保护爱丽丝时选择以自身被捕为代价，直接承担眼前风险。（行为推断；S10）
- **关系模式**：注意到爱丽丝与过去的不同，并在她离开前提出留下的可能。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S26）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Red Queen

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-46d51922236129f4` · 独立版本：`profile-2e819f62546b1cf3`

- **性格倾向**：面对被报告的王位威胁时，表现出急于控制局面的强硬倾向。（行为推断；S7）
- **核心动机**：在爱丽丝被指威胁统治后采取搜捕，倾向优先保住王位。（行为推断；S7）
- **决策方式**：处理潜在政治威胁时倾向动用士兵，迅速采取强制措施。（行为推断；S7）
- **关系模式**：不知爱丽丝真实身份时接纳她为宾客，双方实际处于敌对阵营。（摘要直接叙述；S4、S12、S17、S24）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：搜捕威胁王位的爱丽丝，却因未识破身份将她接纳为宾客。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Troubled by a strange recurring dream and mourning the loss of her beloved father, nineteen-year-old Alice Kingsleigh attends a garden party at Lord Ascot's estate, where she is confronted by an unwanted marriage proposal and the stifling expectations of the society in which she lives.
- **S2**：Unsure of how to reply, and increasingly confused, she runs away to chase after a rabbit in a blue waistcoat, and accidentally falls into a large rabbit hole.
- **S3**：She is transported to a world called Wonderland, where she is greeted by the White Rabbit, the Dormouse, the Dodo, and Tweedledum and Tweedledee.
- **S4**：They argue over her identity as "the right Alice", who it is foretold will slay the Red Queen's Jabberwocky on the Frabjous Day and restore the White Queen to power.
- **S5**：The group is then ambushed by the Bandersnatch and a group of playing-card soldiers led by the Knave of Hearts.
- **S6**：Alice escapes and flees into the woods.
- **S7**：The Knave informs the Red Queen that Alice has returned and threatens her reign, and the soldiers are ordered to find Alice immediately.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, the wandering Alice encounters the Cheshire Cat, who takes her to the March Hare and the Hatter.
- **S9**：On the way to the White Queen's castle, Hatter relates the terror of the Red Queen's reign, and comments that Alice is not the same as she once was.
- **S10**：The Hatter helps Alice avoid capture by allowing himself to be seized instead.
- **S11**：Later, Alice is found by Bayard the Bloodhound, who wishes to take her to the White Queen, but Alice insists upon helping the Hatter, so they go to the Red Queen's castle.
- **S12**：The Red Queen is unaware of Alice's true identity and therefore welcomes her as a guest.
- **S13**：Alice learns that the Vorpal Sword  is locked away in a case inside the Bandersnatch's den.
- **S14**：The Knave crudely attempts to seduce Alice, but she rebuffs him.
- **S15**：She later manages to retrieve the sword and befriend the Bandersnatch.
- **S16**：The Knave finds her with the sword and attempts to arrest her.
- **S17**：Alice escapes on the back of the Bandersnatch and delivers the sword to the White Queen.
- **S18**：The Cheshire Cat saves the Hatter from execution, and the Hatter calls for rebellion against the Red Queen.
- **S19**：The rebellion is quickly put down by the Jubjub bird.
- **S20**：The resistance flees to the White Queen's castle, and both armies prepare for battle.
- **S21**：Alice remains unsure about the expectation for her to champion the White Queen, and meets with Absolem the Caterpillar.
- **S22**：He reminds Alice of her past visit to Underland  thirteen years earlier, and helps give her the courage to fight the Jabberwocky and accomplish "What she must to do", while he becomes a pupa.
- **S23**：When the Frabjous Day arrives, both the White and Red Queens gather their armies on a chessboard-like battlefield and send forth their chosen champions  to decide the fate of Underland.
- **S24**：Encouraging herself with the words of her late father, Alice manages to kill the Jabberwocky.
- **S25**：The White Queen then banishes the Red Queen and the Knave to the Outlands, and gives Alice a vial of the Jabberwocky's blood, which will take her home.
- **S26**：The Hatter suggests that she could stay in Underland, but she decides that she must go back and promises that she will return.
- **S27**：Alice returns home, where she stands up to her family and pledges to live life on her own terms.
- **S28**：Impressed, Lord Ascot takes her in as his apprentice, with the idea of establishing oceanic trade routes to China.
- **S29**：As the story closes, Alice prepares to set off on a trading ship.
- **S30**：Absolem, now a butterfly, lands on her shoulder.
- **S31**：Alice recognizes him and greets him before he flutters away.

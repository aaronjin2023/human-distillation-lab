## 角色资料

### Amélie Poulain

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7373c4e96f108326` · 独立版本：`profile-c18ff44d7cf92533`

- **性格倾向**：在帮助他人与追求爱情时，她倾向富于想象却回避直接暴露自己（行为推断；S14、S27、S28）
- **核心动机**：看到旧物归还带来的快乐后，她决定持续改善他人的生活（摘要直接叙述；S7、S11、S13）
- **决策方式**：她倾向通过匿名安排和复杂巧计影响他人，而非直接协商（行为推断；S10、S14、S16、S18、S27）
- **情绪反应**：面对Nino时，她因羞怯退缩，也会因误解而几乎失去希望（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **成长变化**：在Dufayel鼓励下，她开始跨越旁观与安排，亲自进入关系（行为推断；S25、S28、S29）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

- **S3**：Now at the age of twenty-three, Amélie is a waitress at Café des 2 Moulins, a small café in Montmartre that is staffed and frequented by a collection of eccentrics.
- **S4**：Having spurned romantic relationships following a few disappointing efforts, she finds contentment in simple pleasures and letting her imagination roam free.
- **S5**：On 31 August 1997, Amélie, shocked upon hearing the news of the death of Diana, Princess of Wales, on television, drops a bottle cap that knocks into a bathroom wall tile and loosens it.
- **S6**：Behind the tile, she finds an old metal box of childhood memorabilia hidden by a boy who lived in her apartment decades earlier.
- **S7**：Fascinated by this find, she resolves to track down the now adult man who placed it there and return it to him, making a promise to herself in the process: if she finds him and it makes him happy, she will devote her life to bringing happiness to others.

## 角色资料

### Sheriff Woody

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3fa417a19177a9f1` · 独立版本：`profile-501fe55f5051f721`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴遭遇被遗弃的风险时，他表现出愿意主动援助的倾向（行为推断；S3、S27、S29）
- **情绪反应**：受损后被搁置与听闻遗弃经历，使他对失去主人产生不安（行为推断；S1、S2、S11）
- **价值取舍**：他最终更珍视陪伴主人和朋友，而非被永久保存的收藏价值（行为推断；S6、S18、S33）
- **成长变化**：他从害怕被主人淘汰，转向接受变化并依靠同伴关系获得安定（摘要直接叙述；S2、S11、S33）

矛盾与变化：回到安迪身边的愿望，与不愿抛下新同伴的责任感冲突。

### Buzz Lightyear

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5c29e01bda08fe56` · 独立版本：`profile-16b61722c780b072`

- **性格倾向**：面对救援受阻与自身被困，他表现出执着而忠于朋友的倾向（行为推断；S5、S13、S15、S24）
- **核心动机**：他持续追踪收藏者，目标是将被带走的胡迪救回同伴身边（摘要直接叙述；S3、S5、S21、S24）
- **价值取舍**：劝说胡迪时，他倾向重视玩具与主人的陪伴意义（行为推断；S18）
- **决策方式**：面对不断变化的转运路线，他倾向联合伙伴持续调整救援行动（行为推断；S21、S23、S24、S25、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Jessie

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-28841b686252549c` · 独立版本：`profile-cedbeaa0ceb14576`

- **性格倾向**：面对可能重返储藏处的处境，她表现出防御而急切的倾向（行为推断；S8）
- **核心动机**：她希望整套玩具进入博物馆，以免再次被收回储藏处（摘要直接叙述；S7、S8）
- **情绪反应**：她害怕黑暗，并对胡迪离开将使众人重返储藏处感到愤怒（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **成长变化**：她从期待进入博物馆，转向接受新主人并重新享受陪伴（摘要直接叙述；S7、S27、S31、S33）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：渴望摆脱被遗弃的痛苦，却一度寄望于远离儿童的收藏生活。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Three years after the events in Toy Story, Woody prepares to go to cowboy camp with Andy but his arm is accidentally ripped so Andy leaves him behind while his mom puts him on the shelf.
- **S2**：After Woody has a nightmare about Andy throwing him in a trash full of arms, he discovers that a penguin toy named Wheezy has been on the shelf for months because of a broken squeaker.
- **S3**：When Woody saves Wheezy from a yard sale, he is stolen by a toy collector who Buzz Lightyear and the other toys recognize as Al McWhiggin, the greedy, avaricious owner of a shop named Al's Toy Barn, from a commercial.
- **S4**：Buzz, Hamm, Mr.
- **S5**：Potato Head, Slinky Dog, and Rex set out to rescue Woody.
- **S6**：In Al's apartment, Woody discovers that he is a valuable collectible based on an old TV show called Woody's Roundup, and is set to be sold to a toy museum in Tokyo, Japan.
- **S7**：The other toys from the show - Jessie the yodeling cowgirl, Woody's horse Bullseye, and Stinky Pete the Prospector, are excited about the trip but Woody intends to go home because he is Andy's toy.
- **S8**：Jessie, who is afraid of the dark, is furious with him as the museum will only want the whole gang; without him, they will go back into storage.
- **S9**：That night, when Woody's whole arm comes off, his attempt to retrieve it and escape is foiled when the TV comes on.
- **S10**：Woody, seeing the remote in front of Jessie, accuses her of sabotaging his escape.
- **S11**：The following morning, Woody's arm is reconnected and he decides to stay when Jessie reveals that she was once the beloved toy of a child named Emily who eventually outgrew and gave her away and Prospector warns him that the same fate awaits him when Andy grows up.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, Buzz and the other toys reach Al's Toy Barn.
- **S13**：While searching the store for Woody, Buzz is captured and imprisoned in a box by a newer Buzz Lightyear action figure after a fight between them and the New Buzz's utter delusion.
- **S14**：The new Buzz joins the other toys, oblivious to the fact he is an imposter, as they make their way to Al's apartment.
- **S15**：Buzz escapes and pursues them, thinking they have also been captured by Al.
- **S16**：When he gets out of Al's Toy Barn, he unknowingly and accidentally releases an action figure of his archenemy Emperor Zurg who follows him.
- **S17**：Buzz rejoins the others as they find Woody, who initially refuses to return because he does not want to abandon the rest of the Roundup Gang.
- **S18**：After Buzz reminds Woody of "a toy's true purpose", and he is moved by seeing himself sing "You've Got a Friend in Me", he changes his mind again and asks the Roundup toys to come with him.
- **S19**：However, Prospector prevents their escape and reveals that he wants to go to Tokyo because he spent his life on a dime store shelf and was never sold.
- **S20**：To ensure this, he made sure Woody would not go home, and was also responsible for sabotaging his escape the previous night.
- **S21**：Al arrives and takes Woody and the Roundup toys with him, forcing both Buzz Lightyears and Andy's toys to follow him.
- **S22**：They follow Al to an elevator where they encounter Zurg who fights the new Buzz but is knocked off the elevator by Rex.
- **S23**：When they reach the ground floor, the new Buzz stays to play with Zurg once he discovers that Zurg is his father  while Buzz and the other toys continue their pursuit of Al.
- **S24**：Accompanied by three toy Aliens, they use a Pizza Planet delivery truck to follow Al to Tri-County International Airport where they enter the check-in area, the baggage processing area to find Woody and the Roundup toys.
- **S25**：During a fight with Woody, Prospector rips his arm and tries to mutilate him, but is captured and stuffed into a little girl's backpack by Buzz and the other toys.
- **S26**：While Woody and Bullseye are saved, Jessie ends up on the plane for Tokyo.
- **S27**：Assisted by Buzz and Bullseye, Woody boards the plane and convinces Jessie to come with them to Andy's house, telling her that he has a little sister.
- **S28**：However, the plane starts up before they can escape but they leave through an emergency hatch just as the plane gets onto the runway.
- **S29**：Woody lassoes his string over a nut on the plane's wheels, and swings with Jessie between the plane wheels before landing on Bullseye just as the plane takes off.
- **S30**：Buoyed up by living "Woody's Finest Hour," the toys go home.
- **S31**：Andy returns home, repairs Woody's arm, and accepts Jessie and Bullseye as his new toys.
- **S32**：The toys also learn from a commercial that Al's business has suffered due to his failure to sell the Roundup toys.
- **S33**：As Jessie and Bullseye delight in having a new owner, Woody tells Buzz that he is not worried about Andy outgrowing him, because when he does, they will always have each other for company "for infinity and beyond."

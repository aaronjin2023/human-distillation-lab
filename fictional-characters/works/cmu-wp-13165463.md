## 角色资料

### Shinji Ikari

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-894623cdf69f2e28` · 独立版本：`profile-ba2ce10059c146e2`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴受威胁的情境中，他倾向以强烈的保护欲抗拒命令（行为推断；S13、S30）
- **核心动机**：面对被吞噬的丽，他将重新驾驶的目标集中于救回她（摘要直接叙述；S27、S30）
- **价值取舍**：在明日香被困时，他把同伴安全置于消灭敌人的任务之上（行为推断；S13）
- **关系模式**：父亲强制启动替代系统，使他对父子关系的信任受挫（行为推断；S14、S15）
- **成长变化**：在此轮危机中，他从退出组织转为主动要求承担战斗（摘要直接叙述；S15、S27）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：拒绝伤害同伴，却为救丽不惜代价并引发灾难。

### Rei Ayanami

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-f6cd2c2d293da3e3` · 独立版本：`profile-ddf7af4df59e0385`

- **性格倾向**：在父子关系疏离时，她倾向主动安排具体行动来促成亲近（行为推断；S7、S8）
- **核心动机**：她筹办聚餐的明确目的，是修复真嗣与父亲之间的关系（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **关系模式**：她拒绝新机试驾时，明日香意识到她对真嗣怀有好感（摘要直接叙述；S12）
- **决策方式**：面对使徒威胁，她倾向以受损机体实施高风险的近身突击（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **价值取舍**：在爆炸即将发生时，她仍将协同作战者的脱险置于眼前（行为推断；S23、S24）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：珍视日常关系的修复，也愿在战斗中承担极高风险。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Mari Illustrious Makinami, a new pilot, launched in Provisional Unit-05, destroys the resuscitated skeleton of the Third Angel in its attack on NERV's arctic Bethany Base and defeats the Angel by self-destructing Unit-05, safely ejecting at the last moment.
- **S2**：Meanwhile, Shinji Ikari and his father Gendo visit Yui's grave, with the Seventh Angel attacking shortly before is dispatched by the newly arrived Unit-02 and its headstrong pilot, Asuka Langley Shikinami.
- **S3**：Chief Inspector Ryoji Kaji, present at the battle with Unit-05 and arriving alongside Unit-02, delivers a suitcase to Gendo containing an object referred to as the "Key of Nebuchadnezzar" and notes that the destruction of Unit-05 went as planned.
- **S4**：Later Shinji, Asuka, and Rei kill the Eighth Angel in a devised haphazard plan by Misato Katsuragi.
- **S5**：Both Unit-00 and Unit-01 are damaged, but SEELE orders only the latter be repaired, as the former is no longer needed.
- **S6**：Gendo and Kozo Fuyutsuki visit Tabgha Base on the Moon to view SEELE's progress on the mysterious "Evangelion Mark.06." Though they are denied landing, they confirm that its construction is different from the other EVAs and briefly see Kaworu Nagisa sitting on its finger.
- **S7**：On Earth, Shinji decides to cook an extra lunch for Rei who asks Gendo for permission to host a dinner party but he initially refuses before remembering Yui's request to take care of Shinji.
- **S8**：Rei invites everyone to her home for a cooked meal, with the intention of helping repair Gendo and Shinji's relationship.
- **S9**：Mari arrives in Tokyo-3 by parachute, rather than in the covert manner she intended, landing on Shinji in the process.
- **S10**：Soon, Evangelion Unit-04 and NERV's U.S. branch are destroyed during a test of an experimental engine, prompting the U.S. government to send the completed Unit-03 to Tokyo-3.
- **S11**：In accordance with the "Vatican Treaty", where no nation is allowed to possess more than three functioning Evangelions at a time, NERV decides to seal Unit-02.
- **S12**：Rei refuses to pilot the new Eva, which is to be tested on the same day as her planned dinner, with Asuka realizing how much Rei likes Shinji.
- **S13**：Asuka volunteers to take her place before the Ninth Angel possesses the activation of Unit-03 and Shinji inside NERV's only functional EVA Unit-01 refuses to fight for fear of harming Asuka, who is still trapped inside.
- **S14**：Gendo orders the activation of the dummy system causing the autonomous Unit-01 to savagely kill the Angel, crushing the entry plug in its teeth.
- **S15**：Shinji is distraught over his father's actions and leaves NERV.
- **S16**：Asuka survives, but is grievously injured and placed in quarantine for mental contamination fear by the Angel.
- **S17**：As Shinji heads out of the city on a train, the Tenth Angel attacks.
- **S18**：Gendo fails to activate Unit-01, as it rejects the dummy system.
- **S19**：Mari hijacks Unit-02 and heads out to fight Zeruel, activating Unit-02s second best mode "The Beast", that removes the unit's restraints.
- **S20**：In this mode, she successfully smashes through most of Zeruels multiple AT Fields, but is still taken out by the Angels rapid extending blade-like arms.
- **S21**：Then, Rei's damaged Unit-00 emerges on the battlefield and charges at Zeruel with an N² mine-armed missile.
- **S22**：Rei attempts to neutralize Zeruel's AT Fields, but cannot on her own.
- **S23**：The battered Unit-02 makes a last attack, tearing through the Angels remaining AT Fields with her teeth.
- **S24**：Unit-00 throws Unit-02 out of the blast radius before the missile detonates.
- **S25**：It fails to destroy Zeruel, and blows off Unit-00s arms in the process.
- **S26**：Mari sees Shinji upon fleeing in the damaged Unit-02 and takes him to safety as the Angel consumes Unit-00, growing the body of a giant woman with its original one as the head.
- **S27**：Once Shinji runs back and tells Gendo to let him pilot Unit-01, Zeruel easily breaks into the command center, bypassing NERV's auto-self destruct security measures by changing to Unit-00s blood pattern.
- **S28**：Shinji fends it off and takes the fight back to the Geofront, but Unit-01 runs out of power and shuts down.
- **S29**：The Angel throws Unit-01 into the side of a mountain, impaling it.
- **S30**：Enraged and determined to save Rei no matter the cost, Shinji reactivates Unit-01 in berserker mode, glowing red with a halo over its head to take down the Angel to reveal its core, and reaches inside its core to try to save Rei.
- **S31**：Shinji's willpower causes Unit-01 to start floating up into the air, taking the Angel up with it.
- **S32**：The Halo above Unit-01's head expands to the size of a cyclone, making the sky glow red, and sweeping up debris all around them.
- **S33**：Unit-01 presses its hands against the Angel's core, attempting to grant Shinji's wish, and starts transcending the boundaries of humanity.
- **S34**：Inside the cockpit, Shinji sees Rei beyond him, floating huddled up and alone under a liquid-like surface.
- **S35**：He climbs forward, reaching out for her through the surface taking hands and pulls out of the liquid and into his cockpit while Unit-01 pulls Unit-00's core out of the Angels core, moments before it collapses, as the Angel explodes into blood which combines with Unit-00's core, taking form of a giant Rei which is absorbed into Unit-01's core.
- **S36**：Unit-01 sprouts giant wings of light, and Misato sees that they are identical to the ones she saw during Second Impact fifteen years ago.
- **S37**：Ritsuko notes that the EVA is transcending humanity as a divine being, beginning Third Impact, and Apocalypse.
- **S38**：In a post-credits scene, the Lance of Longinus in Earth neutralizes Unit-01, stopping Third Impact.
- **S39**：Kaworu descends from the Moon in Mark.06, saying that he will make Shinji happy.

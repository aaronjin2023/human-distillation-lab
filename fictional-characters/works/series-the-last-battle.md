- **S16**：Shift pockets the profits and garners support from the Calormenes – led by Rishda Tarkaan – by claiming that Aslan is another name for Tash , a bloodthirsty deity worshipped by the Calormenes.
- **S17**：Those who question Shift's words are invited into a large stable where "Tashlan" is said to reside, only to be stealthily murdered by one of Rishda's men.
- **S18**：King Tirian, a descendant of King Caspian X , is warned by Roonwit the Centaur that strange and evil things are happening to Narnia and that the stars portend ominous developments.
- **S19**：Tirian and his friend Jewel the Unicorn hear word of the death of the Dryads and rashly set out to confront the danger, instructing Roonwit to gather a small army to join them.
- **S20**：Finding two Calormenes abusing a Narnian Talking Horse, Tirian and Jewel kill them both in a blind rage.
- **S21**：Ashamed, they give themselves up to "Aslan".
- **S22**：Awaiting judgment, Tirian recognises the farce that Shift has fabricated in league with Rishda and the talking cat Ginger.
- **S23**：When he accuses Shift of lying, Tirian is tied to a tree for the night to face judgment the following morning.
- **S24**：While the woodland creatures are sympathetic to his suffering, they cannot bring themselves to defy "Aslan".
- **S25**：Tirian calls upon Aslan for help, and sees High King Peter and several others in a vision.
- **S26**：He asks them to come to his aid.
- **S27**：Shortly afterwards Eustace Scrubb and Jill Pole arrive in Narnia from Earth.
- **S28**：They relate that Peter and Edmund Pevensie went to London to dig up the magic rings from the old house of Professor Kirke (mentioned in the previous story ) in hopes that Jill and Eustace can use them to get to Narnia.
- **S29**：But feeling a shock in their railway carriage on Earth, Eustace and Jill find themselves in Narnia without ever seeing the rings.
- **S30**：Tirian gives Eustace and Jill a warm welcome.
- **S31**：They release Tirian and rescue Jewel.
- **S32**：In the stable, Jill finds Puzzle, who comes to understand his folly and joins Tirian's side.
- **S33**：A band of Dwarfs are also rescued, but their faith in Aslan has been shattered and they renounce their allegiance, proclaiming "the Dwarfs are for the Dwarfs".
- **S34**：Only one Dwarf named Poggin remains faithful to Aslan and joins the group.
- **S35**：Tirian learns that Shift and Rishda have inadvertently summoned the real Tash to Narnia when he and the others see him travelling north towards the stable.
- **S36**：Farsight the Eagle arrives bearing grim news: Roonwit and the Narnian army loyal to Tirian have all been massacred by the Calormenes who have taken Cair Paravel in Tirian's absence.
- **S37**：Tirian and his small force advance on the stable to expose the truth of Shift's deception.
- **S38**：Ginger, sent in to aid in the deception, runs out in terror, having lost his ability to speak.
- **S39**：Emeth, one of Rishda's men and a devout follower of Tash, insists on seeing his god.
- **S40**：Rishda tries to dissuade him, but Emeth enters the stable and finds another soldier who was stationed inside to murder the rebellious Narnians.
- **S41**：Angry at the deception, he kills the soldier before vanishing.
- **S42**：Outside the stable, Tirian's group engages Shift and the Calormenes, but most of the remaining Narnians on either side are all either killed or thrown into the stable.
- **S43**：Tirian throws Shift into the stable and Tash devours him.
- **S44**：Realising that real danger lies in the stable, the terrified Rishda offers the remaining Narnians as sacrifices to avoid the wrath of Tash.
- **S45**：Tirian, left alone and fighting for his life, drags Rishda into the stable and finds himself in a vast and lush plain.

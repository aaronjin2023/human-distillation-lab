### Margaret Tate

- **S1**：Margaret Tate  is the executive editor in chief of a book publishing company.
- **S2**：After learning she is about to be deported to Canada because of an expired visa, she persuades her assistant, Andrew Paxton , to marry her.
- **S3**：Mr.
- **S4**：Gilbertson , informs them that he suspects they are committing fraud to avoid Margaret's deportation.
- **S5**：Gilbertson tells them that they'll be asked questions about each other separately.
- **S6**：If their answers don't match, Margaret will be deported to Canada and Andrew will be convicted of a felony punishable by a $250,000 fine and five years in prison.
- **S7**：Andrew insists that Margaret make him an editor after their marriage and publish his book.
- **S8**：Margaret agrees.
- **S9**：The couple travels to Sitka, Alaska, Andrew's home town, to meet his family.
- **S10**：Margaret meets Andrew's mother Grace  and grandmother Annie a.k.a "Gammy" .
- **S11**：During the trip to the family home, Margaret notices that nearly every shop in town carries the name Paxton and learns that Andrew's family is in fact very wealthy.
- **S12**：During a welcome home party, Andrew confronts his father, Joe , who is angry about Andrew's dating the boss he has so long disliked and thinks he is using her to get ahead in his career.
- **S13**：After their argument, Andrew announces the engagement to everyone.
- **S14**：Margaret also meets Gertrude , Andrew's ex-girlfriend.
- **S15**：After the party is over, Grace and Gammy show Margaret and Andrew the bedroom they will share during the weekend.
- **S16**：The next day, Grace and Annie take Margaret to a local bar to watch a strip dance by a locally famous but over-the-hill exotic dancer, Ramone .
- **S17**：Stepping away from the show, Margaret learns from Gertrude that Andrew wanted to become an editor and make his own life and that Andrew had proposed to Gertrude.
- **S18**：However, Gertrude refused because she didn't want to leave Sitka for New York.
- **S19**：Returning home, Margaret learns of the conflict between Andrew and Joe.
- **S20**：That night, Margaret asks Andrew about his relationship with his father, but Andrew refuses to talk.
- **S21**：Instead, Margaret opens up to Andrew.
- **S22**：The next day, the family convinces them to marry while they're in Sitka.
- **S23**：After Margaret realizes how close Andrew's family is, she becomes upset, gets on Andrew's boat, and speeds away from town with him.
- **S24**：She tells him she has been alone since she was sixteen years old after her parents died and had forgotten what it felt like to have a family.
- **S25**：She lets go of the helm and stumbles to the back of the boat.
- **S26**：Andrew makes a sharp turn to avoid hitting a buoy, and Margaret falls out of the boat.
- **S27**：Andrew quickly turns the boat around and saves her.
- **S28**：At the wedding ceremony, Margaret confesses the truth about the wedding to the guests, including Gilbertson, who informs her she has twenty four hours to leave for Canada.
- **S29**：Margaret returns to the Paxton home to pack her things.
- **S30**：Andrew rushes to their room only to find Margaret has already left, leaving his book manuscript with a note of praise and a promise to publish it.
- **S31**：Gertrude attempts to comfort Andrew and asks if he is going to go after her.
- **S32**：As he rushes out of the house to find Margaret, another argument arises between him and Joe.
- **S33**：Annie fakes a heart attack and convinces them to reconcile before she "passes away".
- **S34**：Annie admits faking the attack.
- **S35**：Andrew's parents realize he really loves Margaret.
- **S36**：Andrew goes to New York and tells Margaret he loves her in front of the entire office staff.
- **S37**：They kiss and then go to Gilbertson and inform him they are again engaged, but for real this time.

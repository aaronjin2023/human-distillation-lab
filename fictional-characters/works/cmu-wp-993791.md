## 角色资料

### Adolf Hitler

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-0e9a2cbf3cd90bee` · 独立版本：`profile-84625f53f4757431`

- **性格倾向**：在军事败局面前，他倾向固执拒绝更新判断并责怪下属（行为推断；S22、S23、S24、S59）
- **核心动机**：他坚持留在柏林，以自杀而非投降结束自己的统治（摘要直接叙述；S25、S63、S65）
- **价值取舍**：他将对自己的忠诚和战争意志置于德国民众生存之上（行为推断；S17、S55、S64）
- **情绪反应**：得知最信任的希姆莱另行议和后，他感到背叛并含泪暴怒（摘要直接叙述；S44、S45）
- **决策方式**：在资源枯竭时，他仍依据无法兑现的援军设想下达命令（行为推断；S35、S46、S47、S57、S59）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：承认战争已输，却继续寄望不存在的有效反攻。；严惩所认定的背叛，却没有惩罚公开抗命的Speer。

### Magda Goebbels

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-06aeed8c000b359c` · 独立版本：`profile-25578fcc210a5e46`

- **性格倾向**：在政权覆灭的情境中，她表现出无法容纳其他未来的僵固倾向（行为推断；S72、S79）
- **核心动机**：她因无法接受失去希特勒的世界而恳求他改变自杀决定（摘要直接叙述；S72）
- **价值取舍**：她将国家社会主义的延续置于孩子继续生存的可能之上（行为推断；S79）
- **情绪反应**：想到希特勒将死及自己可能杀害孩子时，她陷入慌乱（摘要直接叙述；S72）
- **决策方式**：面对纳粹政权覆灭，她以毒杀子女回应自己无法接受的未来（行为推断；S79）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：为即将杀害孩子而慌乱，最终仍让意识形态压过孩子生命。

### Ernst-Gunther Schenck

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-c9dc67aadf16d806` · 独立版本：`profile-b2e9b377ec34419e`

- **性格倾向**：在撤离与混乱压力下，他表现出主动救助他人的倾向（行为推断；S12、S15、S87）
- **核心动机**：他争取留在柏林，是为了治疗受伤和饥饿的人们（摘要直接叙述；S11、S12）
- **价值取舍**：面对处决与自杀，他倾向把保存生命置于服从死亡要求之上（行为推断；S15、S87、S88）
- **决策方式**：他倾向先争取行动空间，再搜集物资并干预眼前伤害（行为推断；S12、S13、S14、S15）
- **情绪反应**：见到Hewel等人在投降消息后自杀，他感到痛惜与震惊（摘要直接叙述；S88）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In November 1942, a group of German secretaries are escorted to Adolf Hitler's compound at the Wolf's Lair in East Prussia.
- **S2**：After dictating briefly to one of the secretaries, and despite numerous dictation and typing mistakes, Hitler selects Traudl Humps to be one of his personal secretaries.
- **S3**：Three years later, on Hitler's fifty-sixth birthday on April 20, 1945, in the midst of the Battle for Berlin.
- **S4**：Secretary Traudl Humps  is awakened in the Führerbunker by the sound of Soviet artillery.
- **S5**：Later, Generals Wilhelm Burgdorf and Karl Koller confirm to a surprised Hitler that the Red Army is just 12 kilometres from the city centre.
- **S6**：At his birthday reception, Reichsführer-SS Heinrich Himmler and his SS adjutant Hermann Fegelein plead with Hitler to leave the city.
- **S7**：Instead, Hitler declares, "I will defeat them in Berlin, or face my downfall." Himmler leaves Berlin with the intention of negotiating surrender terms with the Western Allies behind Hitler's back.
- **S8**：In another part of the city, a group of Hitler Youth members continues to build up defenses for Berlin.
- **S9**：Peter, a boy in the group, is urged by his father to desert and flee the city.
- **S10**：Peter resists his father and later his unit is part of a group which is awarded the Iron Cross by Hitler.
- **S11**：Hitler confides to Peter, "I wish my generals were as brave as you; Heil to You." SS doctor Ernst-Gunther Schenck is ordered to evacuate Berlin as part of Operation Clausewitz.
- **S12**：Schenck convinces an SS general to let him stay to treat the wounded and starving.
- **S13**：Schenck is requested by Brigadeführer Wilhelm Mohnke to bring all available medical supplies to the Reich Chancellery.
- **S14**：Upon searching a deserted hospital, Schenck finds the basement filled with piled corpses and abandoned patients.
- **S15**：After finding medical supplies, Schenck unsuccessfully tries to prevent the summary execution of two old men by members of a Greifkommando or Feldgendarmerie.
- **S16**：Meanwhile, Hitler discusses his new scorched earth policy to his Minister of Armaments, Albert Speer.
- **S17**：Speer pleads for mercy for the German people, but Hitler declares that they are weak and do not deserve to survive.
- **S18**：Eva Braun ignores Fegelein's pleas to leave Berlin and holds a party for the bunker inhabitants up in the Reich Chancellery, but Soviet artillery shells end the party early.
- **S19**：The next day, General Helmuth Weidling is mistakenly thought to have ordered a retreat to the West and is ordered to the bunker to be executed.
- **S20**：Weidling explains himself to Burgdorf and Hans Krebs, only to find himself appointed commander of the Berlin Defense Area.
- **S21**：In the bunker's briefing room, Hitler is informed about Berlin's disintegrating defenses.
- **S22**：Unmoved, he announces that Waffen SS General Felix Steiner's unit will arrive and drive the Red Army out of Berlin.
- **S23**：He is then informed that Steiner was unable to mobilize enough men.
- **S24**：The news sends Hitler into a rage in which he furiously berates Germany's troops and generals.
- **S25**：Hitler finally acknowledges that the war is lost, but insists that he will remain in Berlin and commit suicide.Adolf Hitler: "But, gentlemen, if you believe I'm going to leave Berlin, you are seriously mistaken.
- **S26**：I'd rather blow my brains out," General Mohnke becomes outraged when he sees conscripted civilian troops being gunned down and thus pointlessly killed in the streets.
- **S27**：Mohnke learns that they are Volkssturm fighters under the command of Joseph Goebbels.
- **S28**：Mohnke has them removed from the line of fire and returns to the Reich Chancellery to confront Goebbels.
- **S29**：During their exchange, Goebbels tells Mohnke that he has no pity for the civilians, as they chose their fate.
- **S30**：Hitler, Braun, Traudl, and Gerda Christian discuss various means of suicide whilst Krebs, Burgdorf, and other military staff get drunk.
- **S31**：Hitler gives Christian and Junge one cyanide capsule each.
- **S32**：Eva Braun and Magda Goebbels type goodbye letters: Braun to her sister Gretl and Goebbels to her adult son, Harald Quandt.
- **S33**：In the streets of Berlin the child soldiers are annihilated by Soviet fire.
- **S34**：Hitler, with Germany losing the war, loses his sense of reality.
- **S35**：Field Marshal Keitel is ordered to find Admiral Karl Dönitz, whom Hitler believes is gathering troops in the north, and help him plan an offensive to recover the Romanian oil fields.
- **S36**：Oberscharführer Rochus Misch, Hitler's radio operator, receives a telegram from Luftwaffe head Hermann Göring, asking for approval to assume command.
- **S37**：Bormann reads the telegram to Hitler in which Göring asks permission to become head-of-state and asks for acknowledgment by 10:00&nbsp;pm, at which time he will assume authority in the absence of a response.
- **S38**：Walther Hewel tries to justify his actions but Bormann and Goebbels declare Göring's actions to be high treason; Hitler orders Göring's arrest and removal from office.
- **S39**：Privately, Speer urges Hitler one last time to halt the scorched-earth orders, but Hitler refuses.
- **S40**：Speer confesses to Hitler that he never implemented the plan, and directly countermanded Hitler's orders by secretly instructing the regional administrators to ignore the orders.
- **S41**：Hitler is visibly shaken by the news but does not punish Speer who is allowed to leave the city.
- **S42**：Hitler summons General Robert Ritter von Greim and his mistress, ace pilot Hanna Reitsch, to the bunker.
- **S43**：He appoints von Greim to be Commander-in-Chief of the Luftwaffe which needs to be rebuilt.
- **S44**：During dinner, Hitler receives a report that Himmler has just attempted to negotiate a separate peace settlement with the Western Allies.
- **S45**：Betrayed by the man he trusted the most, Hitler explodes in another tearful outburst.
- **S46**：He orders von Greim and Reitsch to leave Berlin, rendezvous with Dönitz who is preparing a massive pincer strike with Field Marshal Albert Kesselring, and ensure that Himmler is dealt with.
- **S47**：Hitler then delusively assures von Greim that he can carry out this pincer strike with a thousand jet aircraft, which have been held in reserve .
- **S48**：Reichsphysician SS Ernst-Robert Grawitz, the head of the German Red Cross and responsible for Nazi human medical experiments, requests that he be allowed to leave Berlin for fear of reprisal against him and his family.
- **S49**：Hitler denies his request, assuring him that he has nothing to be ashamed of and future generations will "thank him" for his medical research.
- **S50**：Grawitz returns to his apartment and kills both his family and himself with grenades during dinner.
- **S51**：Hitler wishes to speak to Fegelein about Himmler's treachery but Fegelein cannot be found.
- **S52**：Hitler is immediately suspicious of Fegelein, which proves right when he finds out that he has deserted the bunker and plans to flee the country.
- **S53**：Hitler demands that Fegelein be found and questioned.
- **S54**：An RSD squad arrests Fegelein at his apartment.
- **S55**：Despite a tearful plea from Eva Braun to spare her brother-in-law's life, Hitler is unmoved and denounces him as a traitor.
- **S56**：Shortly afterwards, Fegelein is executed by Peter Högl.
- **S57**：Weidling reports there are no reserves left and air support has ceased.
- **S58**：Mohnke reports that the Red Army is only 300 to 400 meters from the Reich Chancellery and that defending forces can hold out for a day or two at most.
- **S59**：Hitler dismisses the update and reassures the officers that General Walther Wenck's 12th Army will save them.
- **S60**：After Hitler leaves the conference room, Weidling asks the other generals if it is truly possible for Wenck to attack; they all agree it is impossible that Wenck will succeed, but they do not wish to surrender.
- **S61**：The following day, Hitler dictates his last will and testament to his secretary, Traudl Junge, before marrying Eva Braun.
- **S62**：Hitler has ordered Joseph Goebbels to leave Berlin, but Goebbels intends to ignore the order and die with Hitler.
- **S63**：When Hitler's adjutant Otto Günsche later brings a reply from Keitel that Wenck's army cannot continue its assault on Berlin, Hitler states that he will never surrender.
- **S64**：He also forbids all officers to surrender on pain of summary execution.
- **S65**：Hitler then gives Günsche the order to cremate his body and that of Eva Braun.

- **S72**：Now frantic at the thought of a world without Hitler and the possibility of killing off her own children, Magda pleads with Hitler to change his mind.
- **S73**：Hitler states, "Tomorrow, millions of people will curse me, but fate has taken its course." Hitler and Braun retreat to their private rooms, and commit suicide.
- **S74**：Their lifeless bodies are carried up to ground level and through the bunker's emergency exit to the Reich Chancellery garden.
- **S75**：There, the corpses are doused in petrol and set alight in a shell crater.
- **S76**：From the bunker entrance, surrounding officers give one final Nazi salute.
- **S77**：Thereafter, General Krebs leads a small delegation through the Russian lines and tries to negotiate peace terms with Soviet General Vasily Chuikov.
- **S78**：Chuikov says that the Soviets will only accept unconditional surrender but Krebs does not have the authority to grant this so he returns to the bunker empty-handed.
- **S79**：Unwilling to accept a world without National Socialism, Magda Goebbels poisons her six children while her husband waits.

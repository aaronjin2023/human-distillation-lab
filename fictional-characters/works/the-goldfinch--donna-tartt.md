- **S4**：Thirteen-year-old Theo's life is turned upside down when he and his mother visit the Metropolitan Museum of Art to see an exhibition of Dutch masterpieces , including a favorite painting of hers, Carel Fabritius's The Goldfinch .
- **S5**：There, he becomes intrigued by a red-headed girl in the company of an elderly man.
- **S6**：A bomb explodes in the museum, killing his mother and several other visitors.
- **S7**：In the rubble, Theo once more encounters the old man, who gives him a ring and delivers an enigmatic message before dying.
- **S8**：Believing that the man is indicating The Goldfinch , Theo takes it during his panicked escape.
- **S9**：He moves in with a school friend, Andy Barbour, and his wealthy family in their Park Avenue apartment.
- **S10**：He carries out the old man's last wishes and returns the ring to his business partner, James "Hobie" Hobart.
- **S11**：Theo learns that the old man's name was Welton "Welty" Blackwell, and that he and Hobie ran an antiques shop together.
- **S12**：He becomes friends with Hobie and encounters the red-haired girl, Pippa, who lived with Welty and Hobie after her mother (Welty's half-sister) died of cancer.
- **S13**：Theo's life is further disrupted when his deadbeat father arrives with his new girlfriend, Xandra, and whisks him away to Las Vegas .
- **S14**：Theo takes the painting with him, but avoids taking it out of hiding or looking at it.
- **S15**：In Las Vegas, he makes a new friend, Boris Pavlikovsky, the cosmopolitan son of a Ukrainian émigré , who is similarly neglected.
- **S16**：The two boys develop an intense friendship and spend most of their afternoons drinking, smoking marijuana , and using other illegal drugs.
- **S17**：According to Boris, Theo has drunken blackouts during which he forgets everything, although Theo denies this.
- **S18**：While hounded by a loan-shark, Theo's father gets drunk and dies in a car crash.
- **S19**：Fearful of what his father's death may mean to his living situation, Theo flees to New York via cross-country bus and is welcomed at Hobie's.
- **S20**：Pippa, now enrolled in a school for troubled teens in Switzerland , is visiting on a break; Theo remains enamored with her.
- **S21**：The narrative skips ahead eight years.
- **S22**：Theo has become a full partner in Hobart's business.
- **S23**：He has continually concealed The Goldfinch , afraid of being accused of theft.
- **S24**：He is engaged to Kitsey, Andy's younger sister, but is still confused and obsessed with Pippa, who is living in London with her boyfriend.
- **S25**：Over the years, he becomes addicted to prescription medication, and saves Hobie from bankruptcy by selling fake antiques.
- **S26**：One day, Boris reappears, now a wealthy man thanks to dubious unspecified activities.
- **S27**：To Theo's astonishment, Boris reveals that he stole The Goldfinch from Theo while they were in high school, without Theo ever realizing; the painting has since been used as collateral by criminals and drug dealers.
- **S28**：Out of guilt, Boris has devoted himself to recovering the painting and returning it to Theo.
- **S29**：At Theo's engagement party, Boris appears with a plan to retrieve The Goldfinch .
- **S30**：They fly to Amsterdam to meet with the dealers who have the painting.
- **S31**：Boris and his associates steal it back, but the plan goes awry when armed henchmen confront them.
- **S32**：In the resulting conflict, Boris is shot in the arm, and Theo kills Boris's attacker while one of the dealers escapes with the painting.
- **S33**：Boris disappears, leaving Theo alone in his hotel in Amsterdam, where he spirals into paranoia and substance abuse.
- **S34**：Unable to return to New York because Boris has his passport, he contemplates suicide.
- **S35**：After several days, Boris returns and reveals that he has resolved the situation by phoning the art recovery police to inform on the dealers.
- **S36**：Not only has the painting been saved for the museum, but Boris has received a huge reward, which he shares with Theo.
- **S37**：After arriving in the United States, Theo travels the country, using the reward money to buy back the fake antiques from customers.
- **S38**：He realizes that Pippa loves him, but she did not openly reciprocate his feelings because she believes they both share the same damage and flaws, having both survived the trauma of the museum explosion and resorted to self-medication to cope.
- **S39**：In a lengthy reflection, Theo wonders how much of his experiences were unavoidable due to fate or his character, and contemplates The Goldfinch and "the history of people who have loved beautiful things, and looked out for them, and pulled them from the fire".
- **S40**：The novel ends on a curious note, as Theo's contemplation demonstrates both a hard fate still ahead and a sort of redeeming immortality through the admiration of beauty.

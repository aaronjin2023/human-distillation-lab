- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] In this story, seventy-five-year-old Sarah, Duchess of Whitfield, reflects on her life.
- **S3**：The daughter of a wealthy American family in New York in the 1930s, Sarah marries Freddie.
- **S4**：With little interest in her, he drinks all night and associates with prostitutes.
- **S5**：Sarah becomes pregnant but miscarries, and she and Freddie divorce.
- **S6**：Her parents drag a listless Sarah to Europe, where well-meaning friends and family force their nephews, sons and grandsons on her.
- **S7**：She meets William Whitfield, the Duke of Whitfield, 13th in line for succession to the British throne.
- **S8**：Captivated by him, she becomes his companion in London.
- **S9**：William casts aside her fears of a public scandal and convinces Sarah to marry him.
- **S10**：On their honeymoon in France, Sarah and William happen upon Chateau de la Meuze, which he later buys for her as a Christmas present.
- **S11**：Sarah works hard to restore the estate, but World War II erupts.
- **S12**：After the birth of their first child, Phillip, William reluctantly leaves to join the Royal Air Force when England declares war on Germany.
- **S13**：The Germans take possession of France, and German troops, led by the courtly commandant Joachim von Mannheim, seize the chateau to establish a care center for the wounded and dying soldiers.
- **S14**：Sarah and Phillip are removed to the caretaker's cottage.
- **S15**：Joachim falls in love with Sarah, who remains faithful to William.
- **S16**：Pregnant, she gives birth to her and William's daughter Elizabeth, who soon dies of a fever, due to a lack of medical supplies.
- **S17**：Joachim leaves.
- **S18**：William returns from the war, having lost the use of his legs.
- **S19**：Sarah and William help impoverished war survivors by purchasing their jewelry, and eventually put their collection up for sale in a Paris shop.
- **S20**：The business is a success, expanding across Europe and becoming jewelers to the crown.
- **S21**：Sarah and William have three more children — Julian, Isabelle and Xavier — but William dies on the night of Xavier's first birthday.
- **S22**：After his funeral, Joachim returns, only to find that Sarah has no place in her heart for another man.
- **S23**：She directs her attentions to her business and her children, each challenging in their own way.

### Ethan Hunt

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7d4500b02eb5b040` · 独立版本：`profile-394e1f69975f0e87`

- **性格倾向**：在核打击危机中，他面对计划受挫仍持续追击并设法补救。（行为推断；S24、S25、S26、S27）

### Kurt Hendricks

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-b95c29e948269a5e` · 独立版本：`profile-5d36a722c2b5ac50`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他为发动核战争表现得冷酷而执着。（行为推断；S9、S10、S26）
- **核心动机**：他计划发动核战争，并设法取得发射控制装置及启动密码。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10）
- **决策方式**：他预判亨特的行动，关闭服务器后改用电视塔传送发射信号。（摘要直接叙述；S24）
- **价值取舍**：在争夺控制装置时，他将确保发射置于自身性命之上。（行为推断；S26）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### William Brandt

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-8a2ed6e70c221847` · 独立版本：`profile-16bcca0a81e81ab7`

- **性格倾向**：在面对亨特时，他因自责而倾向于隐瞒难以启齿的身份。（行为推断；S16、S19）

- **决策方式**：得知朱莉娅假死并卸下自责后，他改为接受新的任务。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32、S33）
- **关系模式**：他曾负责保护亨特夫妇，并因以为朱莉娅遇害而对亨特愧疚。（摘要直接叙述；S17、S19、S32）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Impossible Missions Force  agent Trevor Hanaway  is killed by assassin Sabine Moreau  while in Budapest, Hungary to intercept a courier working for a nuclear extremist code-named "Cobalt".
- **S2**：Hanaway's team leader, Jane Carter , and newly promoted field agent, Benji Dunn , extract Ethan Hunt  and Hunt's source, Bogdan , from a prison.
- **S3**：Hunt is recruited to lead Carter and Dunn to infiltrate secret Kremlin archives and locate files identifying Cobalt.
- **S4**：During the mission, someone broadcasts across the IMF frequency, alerting the Russians to the presence of Hunt's team.
- **S5**：Although Dunn and Carter escape, a bomb destroys the Kremlin, and Russian agent Sidorov  arrests Hunt, suspecting him as part of the attack.
- **S6**：The Russians have called the attack an undeclared act of war, and the President of the United States activates "Ghost Protocol", a black operation contingency that disavows the IMF.
- **S7**：Hunt and his team are to take the blame for the attack, but will be allowed to escape from government custody in order to track down Cobalt.
- **S8**：Before Hunt can escape, the IMF's secretary  is killed by Russian security forces led by Sidorov, leaving Hunt and intelligence analyst William Brandt  to find their own way out.
- **S9**：The team identifies Cobalt as Kurt Hendricks , a Swedish-born Russian nuclear strategist who plans to start a nuclear war.
- **S10**：Hendricks bombed the Kremlin in order to acquire and use a Russian nuclear launch-control device; however, he now needs the activation codes from the Budapest courier in order to launch a nuclear missile at the U.S.
- **S11**：The exchange between Moreau and Hendricks's right-hand man, Wistrom , is due to take place at the Burj Khalifa in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.
- **S12**：There, Hunt's team members separately convince Moreau and Wistrom that they have made the exchange with one another.
- **S13**：However, Moreau identifies Brandt as an agent.
- **S14**：While Hunt chases Wistrom, only to realize that Wistrom is actually Hendricks in disguise, escaping with the codes, Carter detains Moreau.
- **S15**：Moreau attempts to kill the inexperienced Dunn, and Carter kicks her out of a window to her death.
- **S16**：Brandt accuses Carter of compromising the mission for revenge against Moreau, but Hunt accuses Brandt of keeping secrets from them, as he has displayed fighting skills atypical for an analyst.
- **S17**：While Hunt seeks more information from Bogdan, Brandt admits he was a field agent assigned as security detail to Hunt and his wife, Julia Meade , in Croatia.
- **S18**：While Brandt was on patrol, Julia was killed by a Serbian hit squad, prompting Hunt to pursue and kill them before he was caught by the Russians and sent to prison.
- **S19**：Brandt blames himself for Julia's death and can't bring himself to admit his true identity to Hunt.
- **S20**：Bogdan and his arms-dealer cousin inform Hunt that Hendricks will be in Mumbai, India.
- **S21**：Hendricks facilitated the sale of a defunct Soviet military satellite to Indian telecommunications entrepreneur Brij Nath .
- **S22**：The satellite could be used to transmit the order to fire a missile.
- **S23**：While Brandt and Dunn infiltrate the server room to deactivate the satellite, Carter gets Nath to reveal the satellite override code.
- **S24**：But Hendricks has anticipated Hunt's plan and turns off Nath's servers before sending a signal from a television broadcasting tower through the satellite itself to a Russian nuclear submarine to fire at San Francisco.
- **S25**：Hunt pursues Hendricks and the launch device while the other team-members attempt to bring the broadcast station back online.
- **S26**：Hunt and Hendricks fight over the launch-control device before Hendricks jumps to his death with it to ensure the launch.
- **S27**：Dunn kills Wistrom, allowing Brandt to restore power to the station and enabling Hunt to deactivate the missile, while the fatally wounded Hendricks witnesses the failure of his plan.
- **S28**：Hunt is then confronted by Sidorov, who sees Hunt has stopped the missile, proving the IMF is innocent in the Kremlin bombing.
- **S29**：The team reconvenes weeks later in Seattle.
- **S30**：Hunt introduces the team to longtime colleague Luther Stickell and then issues new assignments.
- **S31**：Dunn and Carter accept, but Brandt refuses.
- **S32**：Hunt reveals that Julia's death was staged, as he knew he could not protect her, and used her death as a pretext to infiltrate the prison and get close to Bogdan, an IMF source on Hendricks.
- **S33**：Relieved of guilt, Brandt accepts his mission while Hunt watches Julia from afar.
- **S34**：They share a smile before he embarks on his next mission.

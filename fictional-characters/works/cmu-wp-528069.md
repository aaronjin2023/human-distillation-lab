### Danny Wallace

- **性格倾向**：在搭档多次保护自己的情境中，倾向以强烈的个人忠诚回应（行为推断；S6、S17、S23、S25）

### Nick Chen

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b4bb4cb2c4f1b9ec` · 独立版本：`profile-69b3d813e91c6fc5`

- **性格倾向**：在搭档陷入生命危险时，呈现愿意冒险保护对方的倾向（行为推断；S6、S23）
- **核心动机**：身为警组负责人需要平息帮派冲突，却同时向帮派提供情报（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2）
- **价值取舍**：最终面对自保与搭档安危的冲突，选择以自身承受致命风险（行为推断；S21、S22、S23）
- **决策方式**：得知线人目击帮派谋杀后，选择把证据转交检察机关（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **关系模式**：与Wallace的互信经历猜疑后，通过共同承诺再次加深（摘要直接叙述；S13、S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：参与帮派腐败，同时又保护搭档并协助追究帮派谋杀。

### Henry Lee

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0effd96f2f36ce6b` · 独立版本：`profile-2b0c1cc0ee9ff96e`

- **性格倾向**：在帮派权力竞争中，倾向隐蔽结盟并把他人当作可调动资源（行为推断；S9、S14、S21）
- **核心动机**：暗中与敌对帮派首领合作，谋求排除原有上级Benny（摘要直接叙述；S14、S19）
- **价值取舍**：为巩固犯罪网络与权力位置，倾向牺牲旧有忠诚和他人生命（行为推断；S14、S15、S19）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：NYPD Lieutenant Nick Chen  is head of the Asian Gang Unit.
- **S2**：His job is to keep the peace in Chinatown from a turf war that has broken out between the Triads and the Fukienese Dragons, a problem complicated by the fact that he is also an informant for the Tongs under "uncle" Benny Chan  and his lieutenant Henry Lee .
- **S3**：After a bombing in downtown Chinatown in broad daylight, Chen is reluctantly teamed up with Danny Wallace , who is unaware of this situation.
- **S4**：Wallace was also secretly tasked by Internal Affairs to monitor Chen for corruption.
- **S5**：Danny lied to Chen and the Asian Gang Unit by claiming that he took the job as a means to gain his Detective shield the fastest.
- **S6**：During a police raid on a Fukienese whorehouse, Chen saves Danny's life.
- **S7**：Danny, knowing that his life is now in the hands of Chen, then promptly initiates a bust on a drug operation, not knowing that there was an undercover FBI agent as well.
- **S8**：After being berated by the FBI for interfering one of their ongoing investigations, Danny is introduced to Henry.
- **S9**：Henry then discusses the potential value of having another cop in the Asian Gang Unit on the Tong payroll, which Uncle Benny allows.
- **S10**：Benny is able to lure Danny into working for him by tipping him off to an underground prostitution ring.
- **S11**：Danny, after stopping the operation, is then given a commendation for valor, but Chen now suspects that Danny, like him, is working for the Tongs.
- **S12**：Eventually, Wallace begins to work for Henry Lee of the Tongs after discovering that Chen has been doing the same.
- **S13**：They inadvertently cross paths while doing this, throwing their initial trust for each other out the window as well as putting the intentions of Henry Lee into question.
- **S14**：Chen hates the Fukienese with a passion, but neither he nor Wallace know that Henry Lee is secretly forming a partnership with their head leader Bobby Vu , a relationship that will result in them ousting Uncle Benny after tipping off their Hong Kong associates that he is cooperating with the FBI.
- **S15**：By this time, both Henry and Bobby know that there is an FBI agent undercover in their drug operations and despite Nick's idea to 'leak' the operation and have him pulled, decide to simply assassinate him.
- **S16**：While monitoring a drug operation, Danny and Nick witness a Tong hit squad and it leads to a violent confrontation that leads to Nick getting berated for botching the FBI investigation.
- **S17**：After the incident, both Danny and Nick swear not to talk to the FBI without talking to each other first, further cementing their relationship.
- **S18**：However, the FBI find out Danny's real reason for his position in the AGU and threaten to expose him unless he is willing to spy on Nick.
- **S19**：When one of Nick's informants witnesses firsthand the assassination of Uncle Benny at the hands of Bobby under authorization from Henry, he alerts Nick who then alerts the District Attorney because she is looking to indict the Tongs under the RICO Act.
- **S20**：The DA, the FBI, and both Wallace and Chen decide that they want to catch Bobby in the act and decide to hold off on the arrests.
- **S21**：Henry chooses to alert Chen of Wallace's real identity and job, leaving him deciding whether or not to kill Wallace to prevent getting arrested.
- **S22**：During the nighttime operation, Wallace is injured and while trying to protect an elderly woman, is left vulnerable to be killed by Bobby.
- **S23**：Nick pushes Danny out of the way and is hit by the bullet originally meant for Danny.
- **S24**：While at the hospital, the FBI is furious that Danny did not reveal Chen's corruption due to the fact that his leaking of information led directly to the death of an undercover FBI agent.
- **S25**：Danny refused to withdraw his original statement telling them that Nick died a good cop, but Nick dies due to his injuries.
- **S26**：Sometime later, Danny is seen leading the arrest of Henry under the information Nick's informant gave them.
- **S27**：Nick is then given a hero's funeral and Wallace is seen in the procession.

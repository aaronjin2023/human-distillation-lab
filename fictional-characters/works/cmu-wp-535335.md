## 角色资料

### Ray Ferrier

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d1c01072c0ef4516` · 独立版本：`profile-ebf824c0bced2ce5`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他遇险时能迅速行动，但与儿女的关系仍然疏远。（行为推断；S1、S9）
- **核心动机**：他决定将孩子带往波士顿，让他们与母亲团聚。（摘要直接叙述；S16）
- **决策方式**：他先阻止儿子参战，认清儿子的决心后又放手让其随军离开。（摘要直接叙述；S17、S21）
- **关系模式**：他主动被俘以接近被捕的女儿，并在她受惊后设法安抚。（摘要直接叙述；S30、S31、S32、S33、S34）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他先阻止Robbie参战，后来又决定让他随军离开。

### Robbie Ferrier

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-a42368621c819658` · 独立版本：`profile-81a3b7eefd157e5e`

- **性格倾向**：在与父亲相处及入侵危机中，他表现出不愿受约束的倾向。（行为推断；S3、S17、S21）
- **核心动机**：外星人入侵后，他想加入军队，参与对抗入侵者的战斗。（摘要直接叙述；S17、S21）
- **价值取舍**：在继续随家人撤离与参战之间，他在此情境中坚持选择后者。（行为推断；S17、S20、S21）
- **关系模式**：他参战的想法先遭父亲与妹妹阻拦，随后获得父亲放行。（摘要直接叙述；S17、S21）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Ray Ferrier  is a container crane operator at a New Jersey port and is estranged from his children.
- **S2**：He is visited by his ex-wife, Mary Ann , who drops off the children, Rachel  and Robbie , as she is going to visit her parents in Boston, Massachusetts.
- **S3**：Robbie takes Ray's car out without his permission, so Ray starts to search for him.
- **S4**：Ray notices a strange wall cloud, which starts to send out powerful lightning strikes, disabling all electronic devices in the area, including cars, forcing Robbie to come back.
- **S5**：Ray heads down the street to investigate.
- **S6**：He stops at a garage and tells Manny the local mechanic, to replace the solenoid on a dead car.
- **S7**：Ray reaches the place where multiple lighting bolts struck the ground and witnesses the ground heaving up as a massive machine with three long legs climbs out.
- **S8**：The Tripod gives off a loud blaring sound before opening fire with heat-rays, vaporizing bystanders and destroying everything in its path.
- **S9**：Ray manages to barely escape; he packs up his kids and leaves in the vehicle Manny repaired as the Tripod destroys the town.
- **S10**：He drives to Mary Ann's house to take refuge that night.
- **S11**：Loud explosions occur all night.
- **S12**：The next morning he discovers a crashed Boeing 747 in the street outside the house.
- **S13**：He meets a news team stealing the flight's meals and surveying the wreckage.
- **S14**：The reporter shows him footage of Tripods all over the Earth, with the unknown pilots entering the machines through the lightning strikes.
- **S15**：She speculates that the machines were in place for thousands of years meaning the invasion was being planned for a long time.
- **S16**：Ray decides to take the kids to Boston to be with their mother.
- **S17**：Robbie, vying to join the fight against the aliens, tries to leave with the U.S. military, but Ray and Rachel stop him.
- **S18**：They are forced to leave their car after a mob surrounds them and takes the vehicle by force.
- **S19**：They later survive a Tripod attack which causes the sinking of a Hudson River ferry.
- **S20**：The family then ends up in the middle of a battle between the military and the Tripods.
- **S21**：Realizing Robbie's determination to join the fight, Ray lets him go with the soldiers.
- **S22**：Immediately afterwards the Tripods destroy all military resistance, presumably also killing Robbie.
- **S23**：The Tripods are shown to be protected by an energy shield that makes them invulnerable.
- **S24**：While escaping, Ray and Rachel are offered shelter by Harlan Ogilvy , who vows revenge on the aliens after his family was killed by them.
- **S25**：While hiding in Harlan's basement, they witness the Tripods spreading a strange red weed substance everywhere.
- **S26**：They all hide from a snake-like probe and a group of four aliens who explore the basement.
- **S27**：The next morning, Ogilvy suffers a mental breakdown while witnessing a Tripod harvesting blood and tissue from a human.
- **S28**：Concerned that Ogilvy's yelling and ranting will attract the Tripods, Ray reluctantly kills Ogilvy to silence him.
- **S29**：The basement hideout is exposed when a second probe catches them sleeping.
- **S30**：Ray cripples the probe using an axe, but Rachel runs outside and is caught by the Tripod.
- **S31**：As he chases after the Tripod and Rachel, Ray finds a grenade bandolier with several hand grenades in a destroyed Humvee and detonates one of them to attract the Tripod's attention.
- **S32**：He is captured as he planned and placed in the same basket with Rachel and several other prisoners.
- **S33**：Ray discovers Rachel is in shock after she witnesses a captive being sucked up into the ship to be harvested.
- **S34**：As Ray finally calms her down, the aliens select Ray to pull him inside for harvesting, but the other prisoners manage to pull him back.
- **S35**：The bandolier he was wearing was left inside the Tripod and Ray was able to pull all of the pins, causing a massive internal explosion, destroying the Tripod and freeing the captives.
- **S36**：Ray and Rachel arrive in Boston, where they notice the red weeds are starting to dry up and disintegrate.
- **S37**：The Tripods also begin to act erratically and appear to also be dying.
- **S38**：Ray notices that a flock of birds landing on a machine, indicating its force fields are down.
- **S39**：Ray alerts nearby soldiers, who fire shoulder-launched missiles at the machine, destroying it.
- **S40**：As a crowd approaches the downed machine, a hatch falls opens, releasing red weed fertilizer and revealing an alien that lets out a final growl before it dies.
- **S41**：Ray and Rachel reach Mary Ann's parents'  house and find to their surprise, Robbie, who has somehow survived the hilltop massacre.

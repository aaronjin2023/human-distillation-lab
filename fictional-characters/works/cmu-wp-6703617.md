### Jason Tripitikas

- **性格倾向**：在同伴濒危时倾向重情而冒进，愿独自承担明显风险（行为推断；S20、S22、S23、S25）
- **核心动机**：独赴宫殿试图以金箍棒交换仙药，是为了救治Lu Yan（摘要直接叙述；S22、S23）

- **成长变化**：从遭混混欺凌的武侠迷，成长为能自卫并持续练功的人（行为推断；S1、S3、S19、S34、S37）

### Jade Warlord

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-beb22b909caa933f` · 独立版本：`profile-5bb968abe329414e`

- **性格倾向**：在压制对手时倾向狡诈残酷，善用承诺和羞辱维持优势（行为推断；S11、S16、S24、S25）
- **核心动机**：得知金箍棒重现后急于夺回，以阻止Monkey King获释（行为推断；S11、S12、S13、S16）
- **决策方式**：把仙药同时变成竞争筹码，迫使求药双方进行生死较量（摘要直接叙述；S16、S23、S24）
- **关系模式**：与Ni-Chang以仙药交换效力，将合作建立在利益条件之上（摘要直接叙述；S16、S24）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In this film, which is based loosely on the ancient Chinese novel Journey to the West, South Boston teenager Jason Tripitikas is a fan of martial arts films and he awakens from a dream of a battle between the Monkey King, played by Jet Li and celestial soldiers in the clouds.
- **S2**：He visits a pawn shop in Chinatown to buy Wuxia DVDs and discovers a golden staff.
- **S3**：On his way home, Jason is harassed by some hooligans, who attempt to use him to help them rob the shop-owner Hop.
- **S4**：Hop tries to fight the thieves with the staff, but is shot and wounded.
- **S5**：He tells Jason to deliver the staff to its rightful owner and Jason flees with the staff.
- **S6**：He is cornered on the rooftop by the hooligans and almost shot too, but he is pulled off the roof by the staff and falls backwards onto the asphalt.
- **S7**：When Jason regains consciousness, he finds himself in a village in ancient China that is under attack by armored soldiers.
- **S8**：The soldiers see his staff and attempt to seize it.
- **S9**：He is saved by the inebriated traveling scholar Lu Yan , a supposed "immortal," who remains alert and agile even when drunk.
- **S10**：Lu brings Jason to a teahouse and tells him the story of the rivalry between the Monkey King and the Jade Warlord.
- **S11**：The Jade Warlord tricked the Monkey King into setting aside his magic staff Ruyi Jingu Bang and transformed him into a stone statue, but the Monkey King cast his staff far away before the transformation.
- **S12**：Being an immortal, the Monkey King did not die, but got captured inside the statue.
- **S13**：Lu ends the tale with a prophecy about someone, a "Seeker", who will find the staff and free the Monkey King.
- **S14**：Just then, they are attacked by the Jade Warlord's men again but manage to escape with the help of Golden Sparrow, a young girl who refers to herself in the third person.
- **S15**：She reveals that her family was murdered by the Jade Warlord, against whom she has therefore sworn revenge.
- **S16**：Meanwhile, the Jade Warlord , upon learning that the staff has been sighted, sends the White-Haired Witch Ni-Chang to help him retrieve it in exchange for the elixir of immortality.
- **S17**：Jason, Lu Yan and Golden Sparrow meet a strange man dressed in white, also played by Jet Li, who takes the staff away from them.
- **S18**：Lu Yan fights with the man  for the staff until the latter realizes that Jason is the prophesied Seeker, and he joins them in their quest to free the Monkey King.
- **S19**：As the four travel to Five Elements Mountain, Lu Yan and the Silent Monk teach Jason Kungfu along the way.
- **S20**：After crossing a desert, they encounter Ni-Chang and her henchmen and a battle ensues, in which Lu Yan is mortally wounded by Ni-Chang's arrow.
- **S21**：The protagonists take refuge in a monastery, where they learn that Lu is actually not an immortal as he failed the test to become one.
- **S22**：Only the Jade Warlord's elixir can save his life.
- **S23**：In desperation, Jason goes to the Warlord's palace alone to exchange the staff for the elixir.
- **S24**：In the palace, the Jade Warlord asks Jason to fight with Ni-Chang to the death, because he had promised to give the elixir to only one of them.
- **S25**：Jason is defeated by Ni-Chang and the Warlord taunts him for his foolishness, and is about to decapitate him when the other protagonists and monks from the monastery arrive to join in the battle.
- **S26**：Jason manages to grab the elixir and he tosses it to Lu Yan, who drinks it and recovers.
- **S27**：The Silent Monk is wounded by the Jade Warlord's Guan Dao during the fight and he passes the staff to Jason, who uses it to smash the Monkey King's statue.
- **S28**：The Monkey King is freed and the Silent Monk is revealed to be actually one of the Monkey's clones.
- **S29**：Lu Yan battles Ni-Chang and kills her by throwing her off the cliff hundreds of feet below.
- **S30**：After another long battle between the Monkey King and the Jade Warlord, the Warlord is eventually stabbed by Jason and falls into a lava pit to his death.
- **S31**：However, Golden Sparrow has been seriously injured by the Warlord and she dies in Jason's arms, thanking him in the first person before dying.
- **S32**：By then, the Jade Emperor has returned from his meditation and he praises Jason for fulfilling the prophecy and allows him for one wish, which he asked is to return home.
- **S33**：Jason finds himself back in 21st century Boston after passing through a magical portal at the exact moment and location of his earlier fall.
- **S34**：He defeats the hooligans easily by using the Kungfu moves he was taught and drives them away.
- **S35**：He alerts the police and calls an ambulance for Hop, who survives from the gunshot wound and brushes off Jason's concerns, claiming that he is immortal. .
- **S36**：Before the film ends, Jason is delighted to see a girl who resembles Golden Sparrow and speaks to her briefly, before she heads back to her shop, called "Golden Sparrow Chinese Merchandise".
- **S37**：The final scene shows Jason on a rooftop at night practicing his staffwork and continuing to hone his kung fu skills.

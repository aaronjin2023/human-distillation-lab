### Cheng Ying

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-61aa03131b8eb78e` · 独立版本：`profile-6c5273b3b7e80ad9`

- **性格倾向**：在长期复仇情境中，表现出能隐忍并持续筹划的倾向（行为推断；S15、S18、S19）
- **核心动机**：妻儿遇害后立誓报复屠岸贾，并借抚养孤儿推进计划（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14、S15、S18）
- **决策方式**：选择将孤儿养在仇人身边，隐瞒身份等待反击时机（行为推断；S15、S16、S18、S19）
- **关系模式**：与同受屠岸贾伤害的韩厥结成密友及秘密复仇同盟（摘要直接叙述；S6、S18）
- **价值取舍**：决战时以自身生命换取赵武击杀仇人的机会（行为推断；S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：抚养孤儿以谋复仇，却使孤儿与仇人形成父子感情。

### Tu Angu

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-c0017535be2e01d2` · 独立版本：`profile-acdd7a51131aa8b8`

- **性格倾向**：在清除政敌时，倾向以极端暴力消灭可能留下的威胁（行为推断；S3、S6、S7）
- **核心动机**：为斩除赵氏后患，持续搜寻并企图杀死幸存婴儿（行为推断；S3、S7、S8、S13）
- **决策方式**：以毒杀嫁祸制造灭族借口，再利用收婴命令辨认孤儿（摘要直接叙述；S3、S7、S8）
- **情绪反应**：得知孤儿逃脱后迁怒韩厥，以毁容方式发泄愤怒（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **关系模式**：对误认身份的赵武给予父亲般照顾，并建立师徒亲情（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾试图灭绝赵氏，却亲自抚育并教导了幸存的赵武。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The story is set in the Jin state during the Spring and Autumn Period of ancient China.
- **S2**：The chancellor Zhao Dun and his son, General Zhao Shuo, have a rift with General Tu Angu.
- **S3**：Tu secretly murders the duke with poison and pushes the blame to the Zhao family, using that as an excuse to massacre the entire Zhao clan of about 300 people.
- **S4**：The only survivor is Zhao Shuo's baby son Zhao Wu, born to the duke's older sister Princess Zhuang.
- **S5**：Zhuang pleads with Tu Angu's subordinate Han Jue to spare her child and commits suicide after entrusting Zhao Wu to the physician Cheng Ying, telling Cheng to bring the child to Gongsun Chujiu, a friend of the Zhao family.
- **S6**：When Tu Angu learns that the orphan had escaped, he slashes his sword at Han Jue's face in anger, disfiguring him.
- **S7**：Subsequently, Tu issues orders to seal the gates and to gather all newborn babies in the city.
- **S8**：The ploy was to identify the Zhao orphan since Tu was counting on those hiding the baby to not hand him over; hence singling out the one baby left in the city to be the Zhao infant.
- **S9**：Cheng Ying brings the baby Zhao Wu home, but alas, his wife gives up the child to the searching soldiers, claiming Zhao Wu as her own son.
- **S10**：Cheng then asks Gongsun Chujiu to bring his wife and son  out of the city, while he stays behind to try and retrieve his real son from Tu Angu.
- **S11**：Tu finds Cheng suspicious and Cheng lies that the Zhao orphan has been taken out of the city by Gongsun.
- **S12**：Tu then leads his soldiers to Gongsun's house and finds Cheng's wife and son.
- **S13**：Mistaking Cheng's son as the Zhao orphan, Tu kills the infant, Gongsun and Cheng's wife.
- **S14**：Cheng is devastated by the loss of his family and he swears vengeance on Tu Angu.
- **S15**：He brings Zhao Wu to Tu's house and raises the boy there.
- **S16**：Tu misbelieves that Zhao Wu is Cheng's child and treats him like a son.
- **S17**：Zhao Wu gradually grows up under Tu's tutelage and develops a father-son bond with Tu.
- **S18**：Cheng Ying also forges a close friendship with the disfigured Han Jue and they secretly plot to help Zhao Wu avenge his family.
- **S19**：One day, Cheng Ying breaks the truth to Zhao Wu, telling him that Tu Angu, who has been a fatherly figure to him for the past 15 years, is actually the murderer of his family.
- **S20**：Zhao is unable to accept the truth initially but his attitude changes as he hears from Han Jue about how Cheng Ying sacrificed his own son to save him.
- **S21**：Zhao and Cheng finally confront Tu Angu, but Tu is aware of Zhao's true identity, as over the years, he has noticed that the boy resembles Zhao Shuo.
- **S22**：Zhao Wu engages Tu Angu in a sword fight but is no match for Tu.
- **S23**：At the critical moment, Cheng sacrifices himself to give Zhao an opportunity to stab and kill Tu.
- **S24**：The film ends with the dying Cheng having a vision of his reunion with his family.

## 角色资料

### Dan Evans

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-2f6ddd781f62f1da` · 独立版本：`profile-0c4d59265210549e`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴退缩与死亡威胁下倾向坚持，难以接受无功而返（行为推断；S30、S31、S32、S33）
- **核心动机**：押送任务既为缓解家庭生计困境，也为赢回儿子对自己的敬重（行为推断；S10、S32、S34）
- **价值取舍**：把家人的未来保障与自身尊严，看得比安全领取报酬更重要（行为推断；S30、S31、S32、S34）
- **决策方式**：继续押送前先争取家属报酬与水权，并安排儿子安全返家（行为推断；S32、S33）
- **情绪反应**：对伤残经历怀有难以向儿子坦白的羞耻，并渴望以成就弥补（行为推断；S34）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：保护家庭的愿望与以生命冒险证明自己的需要相互牵扯。

### Ben Wade

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-de9a8d6c7345e493` · 独立版本：`profile-489a0d58ecf509b0`

- **性格倾向**：在押送途中既能冷酷杀人，也会因具体处境改变对他人的态度（行为推断；S14、S19、S34、S35）
- **决策方式**：起初持续寻找脱逃机会，听见丹的隐痛后却主动配合上车（摘要直接叙述；S14、S20、S34、S35）
- **价值取舍**：在最后的抉择中承认丹争取尊严的努力，暂时超越自身脱身利益（行为推断；S34、S35、S39、S44）
- **关系模式**：与押送者由暴力对抗转为有限合作，却与救援自己的帮众决裂（行为推断；S34、S35、S40、S42）
- **情绪反应**：丹被无视禁令地射杀后，表现出足以转向帮众的激烈反应（行为推断；S40、S41、S42）

暂无支持：核心动机、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：配合丹完成押送，却仍召唤坐骑跟随列车，服从并非全无保留。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In August 1884, Dan Evans , an impoverished rancher and Civil War veteran, although sitting awake and armed to prevent it, has his barn set ablaze by two men working for Glen Hollander, to whom Evans owes money.
- **S2**：The next morning, as Evans and his two sons drive their herd, they stumble upon outlaw Ben Wade  and his gang using Evans' cattle as a road blockade to ambush an armored stagecoach.
- **S3**：As he loots the stage, Wade discovers Evans and his two sons watching from the hills.
- **S4**：Acknowledging that they pose no threat to him and his gang, Wade takes their horses telling Evans that he will leave them tied up on the road to Bisbee.
- **S5**：Wade travels with his gang to the town of Bisbee, Arizona to enjoy a celebratory drink at the local saloon.
- **S6**：Meanwhile, the railroad guards find Evans and his sons with Byron McElroy , a Pinkerton agent and lone survivor of the ambush, tied up.
- **S7**：Evans reveals Bisbee as Wade's likely destination, where the guards immediately return, joined by Evans and McElroy.
- **S8**：While Doc Potter , the local medic/veterinarian, treats McElroy, Evans tries negotiating with Hollander, who reveals his intentions to sell the land to the railroad workers rather than grant water rights to Evans.
- **S9**：Enraged at the loss of his livelihood and land, Evans tries confronting Hollander in the nearby saloon, but instead he finds Wade, whom he distracts long enough for the railroad guards to ambush and arrest him.
- **S10**：The coach's owner, Grayson Butterfield , enlists McElroy, Potter, Tucker , one of Hollander's guards and Evans, who agrees for a $200 fee to deliver Wade for arrest.
- **S11**：From Evans' ranch, McElroy arranges a decoy wagon to distract Wade's gang, now led by Charlie Prince ([[Ben Foster , while the real convoy charts a course for Contention, where Wade will be put on the 3:10&nbsp;P.M. train to Yuma Territorial Prison and sentenced.
- **S12**：As the group prepares to ride out, Evans' elder son William  demands to accompany them.
- **S13**：Evans flatly refuses his demand.
- **S14**：During the journey, Wade kills Tucker during his sleep with a fork stolen from Evans' house, and later McElroy  but is stopped from escaping by the surprise arrival of William, who had followed the group all the way from the ranch.
- **S15**：Evans tries to pressure William to return back to the ranch, but William declines.
- **S16**：Wade seemingly accepts to have his son with him.
- **S17**：While taking a shortcut through a canyon, the group is attacked by Apaches.
- **S18**：Evans tries to kill them, but he is badly wounded.
- **S19**：Wade saves him, arms himself, and kills the remaining attackers.
- **S20**：Following the shootout, Wade escapes to a Chinese laborer construction camp blasting a tunnel through the mountain range, where the foreman captures Wade for having killed his brother.
- **S21**：One of his assistants, Zeke , tortures Wade with electric shocks.
- **S22**：Evans, William, Potter, and Butterfield appear and after unsuccessful attempts at negotiation, Potter leads an attack on the foremen and his miners, freeing Wade.
- **S23**：As they flee on their horses, Potter is shot and killed, while Wade and Evans destroy the tunnel behind them with dynamite.
- **S24**：The group arrives in Contention several hours before the train's scheduled arrival and check into the hotel, where they are soon joined by several local marshals hired by Butterfield to protect them.
- **S25**：Prince and the gang ambushes and interrogates the survivor of the decoy wagon, learning that Wade is being delivered to Contention and will board a train to Yuma; he then leaves the wagon to burn with the survivor trapped in it.
- **S26**：Later Prince meets the mine foreman.
- **S27**：Upon seeing Zeke with Wade's gun, Prince kills Zeke, the foreman and his assistant.
- **S28**：Upon arriving in Contention and discovering the heavy guard around Wade, Prince offers every townsman a $200 bounty for every guard they kill.
- **S29**：The marshals, unwilling to fight against such steep odds, surrender to Prince, who kills them anyway.
- **S30**：Butterfield refuses to complete the mission, offering Evans the $200 salary even if Wade goes free.
- **S31**：Evans refuses, noting that was the amount the government paid him for the loss of his leg.
- **S32**：Instead he asks Butterfield to escort his son back to his ranch and to pay his wife $1,000 and a guarantee of water rights from Hollander in exchange for Evans delivering Wade to the train.
- **S33**：After Butterfield agrees, Evans escorts Wade out of the hotel and the two make their way across town, evading continuous gunfire from the townsmen before taking refuge inside a storeroom.
- **S34**：Wade, tired of running, nearly strangles Evans; he relents when Evans reveals that the reason he has a wooden leg  is that his real leg was lost when Evans was shot by fellow soldiers while in retreat from battle, a story that would shame his sons, and that delivering Wade to Yuma would serve as an accomplishment his sons would admire.
- **S35**：Wade relents and agrees to board the train.
- **S36**：The two return to the streets, dodging bullets before barricading themselves in the station to wait for the train , where Wade reveals that he's been to Yuma twice and escaped both times.
- **S37**：Wade's gang set up positions around the station as the train approaches.
- **S38**：William, observing the events, stampedes a herd of cattle  that provides cover for Evans to push Wade onto the train.
- **S39**：As Wade boards, he congratulates Evans.
- **S40**：At that moment, Prince steps up and shoots Evans four times, despite Wade's shouted order to stop.
- **S41**：Wade steps off the train and catches the gun belt Prince tosses him.
- **S42**：After a tense moment of silence, Wade abruptly executes Prince and the rest of his gang.
- **S43**：William appears and draws his gun on Wade but finds he can't kill him; instead he turns to his dying father.
- **S44**：Wade boards the train politely and surrenders his weapon.
- **S45**：Evans eventually dies as Wade leaves in the train.
- **S46**：As the train pulls away, he whistles for his horse, who pricks up his ears and canters after the running train into the distance.

## 角色资料

### Anakin Skywalker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-67cfa06baea2ed1c` · 独立版本：`profile-16ec3192bcebd8cf`

- **性格倾向**：面对失去妻子的预感，他倾向于急切寻求能够控制死亡的力量。（行为推断；S7、S9、S14）
- **核心动机**：他迫切想救帕德梅，因此成为帕尔帕廷的学徒并获名达斯维达。（摘要直接叙述；S14）
- **决策方式**：他先报告帕尔帕廷的西斯身份，随后又介入战斗，使其得以杀死温杜。（摘要直接叙述；S11、S12、S13）
- **价值取舍**：在拜师与参与清洗的选择中，他为救妻的诉求压过了对绝地的忠诚。（行为推断；S14、S15）
- **成长变化**：他从绝地骑士转为西斯学徒，后来因得知帕德梅死亡而陷入痛苦。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S14、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：为挽救帕德梅而投向西斯，后来却亲自将她掐至昏迷。

### Palpatine

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-73fd10bc2a54118f` · 独立版本：`profile-44918b2cf06a0f8b`

- **性格倾向**：在争取安纳金的过程中，他倾向于以力量许诺强化对方的依附。（行为推断；S9、S14）
- **核心动机**：清洗绝地并自立为皇帝的行动，显示他意在建立个人统治。（行为推断；S15、S17）
- **决策方式**：他向安纳金宣称黑暗面能够阻止死亡，以此提供吸引其归附的力量许诺。（行为推断；S9、S14）
- **价值取舍**：在夺权过程中，他把个人统治置于绝地生命与共和国既有制度之上。（行为推断；S15、S17）
- **关系模式**：他收安纳金为学徒，后来救回重伤的学徒并以机械装置重建其身体。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S26）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Obi-Wan Kenobi

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-29e512d5a174c5dc` · 独立版本：`profile-497bc7a3d7ae7dae`

- **性格倾向**：发现安纳金变节后，他表现出主动追踪并直面昔日学徒的倾向。（行为推断；S18、S19、S21、S23）
- **核心动机**：他打算看护被送往塔图因的卢克，等待能够挑战帝国的时机。（摘要直接叙述；S33）
- **决策方式**：无法使帕德梅相信安纳金已经变节后，他选择藏上她前往穆斯塔法的飞船。（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **价值取舍**：在决斗后救助帕德梅并安置卢克的行动中，他表现出对受威胁者的保护。（行为推断；S23、S33）
- **关系模式**：安纳金是他昔日的学徒，发现其背叛后，他最终与之展开激烈决斗。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S21、S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：与昔日学徒存在师徒联系，却在其变节后与之交战。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As the Clone Wars rage on, the Galactic Republic has suffered major setbacks against the Separatists.
- **S2**：Jedi Knights Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi infiltrate the flagship of Separatist commander General Grievous to rescue the abducted Supreme Chancellor Palpatine.
- **S3**：However, they are confronted by Count Dooku, who engages the Jedi in a lightsaber duel.
- **S4**：After Dooku knocks Obi-Wan unconscious, Anakin fights and kills Dooku at Palpatine's urging.
- **S5**：Following a skirmish with Grievous, who then flees, the Jedi crash-land the ship that has split in half on the planet Coruscant.
- **S6**：There, Anakin reunites with his wife, Padmé Amidala, who reveals she is pregnant.
- **S7**：Anakin is happy at first, but he then begins to have disturbing premonitions of Padmé dying in childbirth.
- **S8**：Palpatine places Anakin on the Jedi Council as his representative, but Anakin is denied the rank of Jedi Master and ordered to secretly monitor the Chancellor.
- **S9**：Frustrated at being excluded from the Council, Anakin begins to lose faith in the Jedi and becomes more attached to Palpatine, who tells him that the dark side of the Force holds the power to prevent death.
- **S10**：On the planet Utapau, Obi-Wan engages and kills Grievous.
- **S11**：When Palpatine reveals himself as the Sith lord Darth Sidious, Anakin reports his treachery to Jedi Master Mace Windu.
- **S12**：In the ensuing lightsaber duel, Windu subdues Palpatine by deflecting his opponent's Force Lightning back at him.
- **S13**：Anakin intervenes on Palpatine's behalf, allowing Palpatine to kill Windu.
- **S14**：Desperate to save Padmé, Anakin becomes Palpatine's apprentice and is rechristened Darth Vader.
- **S15**：Palpatine executes Order 66 that involves the extermination of all Jedi across the galaxy; as part of this tyrannical decree, he sends Vader in with a legion of clone troopers to eradicate everyone in the Jedi Temple.
- **S16**：Vader then travels to the volcanic planet Mustafar to kill the Separatist leaders hiding there.
- **S17**：Palpatine addresses the Senate and reforms the Republic into the Galactic Empire, declaring himself Emperor.
- **S18**：Obi-Wan and Jedi Master Yoda discover Anakin's treachery, and split up to confront Vader and Sidious.
- **S19**：Unable to convince Padmé of Anakin's turn to the dark side, Obi-Wan stows away on her ship when she travels to Mustafar.
- **S20**：When Padmé discovers what Vader has done, she tries to take him back, but Vader accuses her of betraying him and uses the Force to choke her into unconsciousness.
- **S21**：Vader and Obi-Wan engage in a fierce duel, taking them across the entire volcano, and eventually Obi-Wan severs Vader's legs and left arm and Vader falls down to the edge of the volcanic river.
- **S22**：Soon after, Vader is immolated.
- **S23**：Taking his former apprentice's lightsaber, Obi-Wan goes to help Padmé.
- **S24**：Meanwhile, Yoda confronts Palpatine, but is forced to retreat when their duel reaches a stalemate.
- **S25**：In a medical facility on Polis Massa, Padmé dies after giving birth to twins Luke and Leia.
- **S26**：Meanwhile, Palpatine finds Vader barely alive on Mustafar and brings him back to Coruscant, rebuilding his apprentice's ruined body with cybernetic limbs and a respirator.
- **S27**：Tormented by the discovery of Padmé's death at his own hands, Vader screams, the last vestige of humanity now driven from him.
- **S28**：Vader begins supervising the construction of the Death Star at Palpatine's side.
- **S29**：A funeral is held for Padmé, her body dressed to continue the illusion of pregnancy.
- **S30**：Knowing that the twins must remain hidden from the Empire, Senator Bail Organa adopts Leia and takes her to Alderaan, while Luke is to be taken to his stepfamily on Tatooine.
- **S31**：Organa allows the droids C-3PO and R2-D2 to remain aboard the Tantive IV and has C-3PO's memory erased.
- **S32**：Yoda teaches Obi-Wan how to commune with the spirit of Qui-Gon Jinn before going into exile on Dagobah.
- **S33**：Obi-Wan takes the infant Luke to Tatooine, where he intends to watch over him until the time is right to challenge the Empire.

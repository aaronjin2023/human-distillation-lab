- **S18**：[ edit ] Narcissa Malfoy convinces Hogwarts professor Severus Snape to make an Unbreakable Vow to protect her son, Draco , while Draco carries out a mission for Lord Voldemort at the school.
- **S19**：Meanwhile, Harry Potter is escorted by Hogwarts headmaster Albus Dumbledore to spend the summer with the Weasley family.
- **S20**：On the way, they stop to persuade former Hogwarts professor Horace Slughorn to return to the school to teach Potions .
- **S21**：While shopping for school supplies, Harry, Ron Weasley , and Hermione Granger see Draco making enquiries at a shop associated with the Dark Arts .
- **S22**：Harry later overhears Draco claiming that he has become a Death Eater .
- **S23**：At Hogwarts, Harry excels at Potions after discovering an old textbook filled with annotations written by the "Half-Blood Prince"; Harry wins a vial of the luck potion Felix Felicis in a class competition.
- **S24**：He learns about Voldemort's past by viewing memories in a Pensieve with Dumbledore.
- **S25**：Voldemort grew up in a Muggle orphanage, where he used his powers to harm other children.
- **S26**：In his later years at Hogwarts, Voldemort questioned Slughorn about a Dark magical object called a Horcrux , which grants immortality.
- **S27**：Dumbledore asks Harry to retrieve a memory from Slughorn, as the memory he showed Harry had been altered.
- **S28**：Meanwhile, Gryffindor student Katie Bell is nearly killed.
- **S29**：Harry suspects Draco is responsible but lacks proof.
- **S30**：He continues trying to expose Draco while fulfilling his duties as captain of the Gryffindor Quidditch team.
- **S31**：He also realises that he has feelings for Ron's sister, Ginny .
- **S32**：Ron feels excluded from his friend group because he is the only one not invited to an exclusive club run by Slughorn.
- **S33**：Hermione invites Ron to the club's Christmas party, but he argues with her after learning about her past relationship with Viktor Krum .
- **S34**：Afterwards, Ron begins to perform poorly in Quidditch matches.
- **S35**：Harry tries to repair Ron and Hermione's relationship and restore Ron's confidence, but he fails.
- **S36**：Ron begins dating Lavender Brown to spite Hermione, prompting Hermione to lash out at him.
- **S37**：At Slughorn's party, Harry overhears Draco refusing Snape's offer to help him with his mission.
- **S38**：Harry informs Hermione, Arthur Weasley , and Remus Lupin , but they believe Snape was spying for Dumbledore.
- **S39**：Ron and Hermione reconcile after Ron almost dies by poisoning.
- **S40**：After multiple failed attempts to obtain the memory, Harry uses Felix Felicis to successfully persuade Slughorn to relinquish it.
- **S41**：The memory reveals that Slughorn told Voldemort how to create a Horcrux: by committing murder, a wizard can split their soul into parts which can be placed in enchanted objects.
- **S42**：Dumbledore believes that Voldemort has split his soul into seven parts, each of which is contained in a Horcrux.
- **S43**：Dumbledore deduces that two Horcruxes have already been destroyed, but four are still hidden.
- **S44**：Meanwhile, Harry begins dating Ginny and duels Draco, severely injuring him with spells taken from the Potions textbook.
- **S45**：Harry learns that Snape informed Voldemort of the prophecy concerning him, which led to the murder of Harry's parents.
- **S46**：When confronted, Dumbledore defends Snape and claims that Snape regretted his actions.
- **S47**：Dumbledore and Harry encounter several obstacles while retrieving a Horcrux from a remote cave.
- **S48**：Back at Hogwarts, a weakened Dumbledore is ambushed by Death Eaters, who have infiltrated the castle with Draco's help.
- **S49**：Dumbledore immobilises Harry beneath the invisibility cloak , before Draco disarms Dumbledore.
- **S50**：Harry then watches as Snape arrives and kills Dumbledore.
- **S51**：The Death Eaters escape and Harry gives chase and subdues several of them, but is ultimately defeated by Snape.
- **S52**：Before escaping with Draco, Snape also reveals that he is the Half-Blood Prince to Harry.
- **S53**：Harry learns that the Horcrux he retrieved with Dumbledore is a fake.
- **S54**：At Dumbledore's funeral, Harry ends his relationship with Ginny to protect her and decides to find and destroy the Horcruxes with Ron and Hermione.

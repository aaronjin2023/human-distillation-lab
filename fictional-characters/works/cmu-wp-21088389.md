## 角色资料

### Daigo Kobayashi

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e81f54100650e206` · 独立版本：`profile-e4b0bdbbabdbfd84`

- **性格倾向**：面对职业污名与亲友反对，他逐渐表现出坚持自身体验的倾向（行为推断；S18、S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：失业后他寻找生计，后来从遗属的感激中获得工作的意义（摘要直接叙述；S1、S7、S18）
- **价值取舍**：即使面对曾经抛弃自己的父亲，他仍坚持遗体应被郑重对待（行为推断；S4、S35、S36）
- **情绪反应**：他对未能善待母亲怀有内疚，对父亲则长期保留被遗弃的怨意（行为推断；S6、S30、S32）
- **成长变化**：亲手为父亲入殓并发现石头信物，使他重新接通童年的亲情（行为推断；S36、S37、S38）

### Mika

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-cc3a0a2e4a7c1cef` · 独立版本：`profile-8de87262e981fdcf`

- **性格倾向**：初知丈夫从事入殓时，她表现出强烈排斥并要求改变的倾向（行为推断；S19、S21）
- **价值取舍**：她起初认为入殓工作令人厌恶，不适合作为丈夫持续从事的职业（摘要直接叙述；S19、S21）
- **决策方式**：面对无法接受的职业分歧，她先要求丈夫辞职，遭拒后选择离开（摘要直接叙述；S19）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Daigo Kobayashi , a cellist in Tokyo, loses his job when his orchestra is disbanded.
- **S2**：He decides to move back to his hometown, Sakata, Yamagata, with his wife Mika .
- **S3**：Daigo's family used to run a small coffee shop.
- **S4**：His father ran away with the waitress when Daigo was very young, and his mother raised him by herself.
- **S5**：His mother died two years ago, and left him the house where he grew up.
- **S6**：Daigo feels guilty about not having taken better care of his mother.
- **S7**：Back home, Daigo finds an advertisement in the newspaper for "assisting departures".
- **S8**：He goes to the interview, uncertain of the job's nature.
- **S9**：He is hired on the spot after only one question  and being handed an "advance" by his new boss Sasaki .
- **S10**：He discovers that the job involves preparing the dead.
- **S11**：Daigo reluctantly accepts.
- **S12**：He returns to his wife with sukiyaki for a celebration, but he tells her he will be performing some sort of ceremony.
- **S13**：On his first day, he is made to act as a corpse in a DVD explaining the procedure.
- **S14**：His first assignment is to clean, dress and apply cosmetics to the body of an aged woman who has died alone at home, remaining undiscovered for two weeks.
- **S15**：He is beset with nausea, and humiliated when strangers on the bus detect an unsavory scent.
- **S16**：He goes to an old public bath that he often went to during his childhood to wash off.
- **S17**：The bath is run by Tsuyako Yamashita, whose son was an old schoolfriend of Daigo.
- **S18**：Daigo completes a number of assignments and experiences the gratitude of those left behind, gaining a sense of fulfillment.
- **S19**：But Mika finds the DVD and begs him to give up such a "disgusting profession." Daigo refuses to quit, so she leaves.
- **S20**：Even Yamashita, his old schoolfriend, tells him to get "a proper job." After a few months, Daigo's wife returns, announcing that she is pregnant.
- **S21**：She seems to assume that he will get a different job.
- **S22**：While Daigo and Mika try to work things out, the telephone rings with the news that Tsuyako, Yamashita's mother, has died.
- **S23**：In front of Yamashita, his family and Mika, Daigo prepares her body.
- **S24**：The ritual earns the respect of all present.
- **S25**：During cremation, Tsuyako's friend appears as the cremator.
- **S26**：He thinks that death is not the "end" but the "gate to a next stage".
- **S27**：Afterwards, Daigo goes to the river and finds a small stone to give to Mika.
- **S28**：He tells her about "stone-letters", a story told to him by his father - "A long time ago, before words were invented, people would give each other stones to express how they were feeling at that point.
- **S29**：A smooth stone might mean that you are happy, while a rough one might mean you are worried about them." Many years ago, Daigo had stood on these same riverbanks with his father and exchanged stone-letters.
- **S30**：Daigo's father had promised to send him one every year, though he never did.
- **S31**：They are informed of the elder Kobayashi's death.
- **S32**：Daigo refuses to see him, but his coworker convinces him to go, confessing that she herself abandoned her son in Hokkaido when he was only six.
- **S33**：Sasaki invites him to take one of the display coffins.
- **S34**：Daigo and Mika go to see the body of his father, but Daigo finds that he cannot recognize him.
- **S35**：As the funeral workers carelessly handle the body, he angrily stops them, and his wife explains that her husband prepares the dead for burial as a living, thereby tacitly admitting that she has come to accept his work.
- **S36**：Daigo takes over the dressing of his father's body, Daigo finds the stone-letter he had given to his father when he was little, in his father's hands.
- **S37**：He is at last able to recognize his father from his childhood memory.
- **S38**：As he finishes the ceremony, Daigo gently presses the stone-letter to Mika's pregnant belly.

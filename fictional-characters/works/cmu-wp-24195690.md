## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Britt Reid  is the irresponsible, 28-year-old slacker son of widower James Reid , publisher of the Los Angeles newspaper The Daily Sentinel.
- **S2**：Britt's attitude changes when James is found dead from an allergic reaction to a bee sting.
- **S3**：After the funeral, Britt fires the staff aside from his maid, but later re-hires Kato , James's mechanic and a skilled martial artist.
- **S4**：Britt and Kato get drunk together and, upon agreeing that they both hated James, visit the graveyard to cut the head off James's memorial statue.
- **S5**：After they succeed, they rescue a nearby couple being mugged.
- **S6**：When police mistake Britt and Kato themselves for criminals, Kato evades them in a car chase as he and Britt return to the mansion.
- **S7**：Britt convinces Kato they should become crime-fighters who pose as criminals in order to infiltrate real criminals, and to prevent enemies from using innocents against them.
- **S8**：Kato develops a car outfitted with several gadgets and weapons, which they call the Black Beauty.
- **S9**：Britt plans to capture Benjamin Chudnofsky , a Russian mobster uniting the criminal families of Los Angeles under his command, and whom his father was trying to expose.
- **S10**：To get Chudnofsky's attention, Britt uses The Daily Sentinel as a vehicle to publish articles about the "high-profile criminal" the Green Hornet.
- **S11**：Britt hires Lenore Case , who has a degree in journalism and a minor in criminology, as his assistant and researcher, and uses her unwitting advice to raise the Green Hornet's profile.
- **S12**：Britt and Kato blow up several of Chudnofsky's meth labs, leaving calling cards so Chudnofsky can contact them.
- **S13**：Throughout all this, The Daily Sentinel managing editor Mike Axford  fears this single-minded coverage will endanger Britt's life, and District Attorney Frank Scanlon  frets over public perception that he cannot stop the Green Hornet.
- **S14**：Britt asks Lenore out, but she rebuffs him and instead invites Kato to dinner, making Britt jealous.
- **S15**：Kato learns from her that mobsters often offer a peace summit to rivals in order to get close enough to kill them; Britt then tells Kato that Chudnofsky has offered them such a meeting.
- **S16**：Kato tries dissuading him, but Britt, feeling overshadowed, follows his instincts.
- **S17**：This nearly proves fatal when Chudnofsky tries to kill them.
- **S18**：Barely escaping to the mansion, Britt and Kato argue and fight, and Britt fires both Kato and Lenore, whom he believes are in a relationship.
- **S19**：Kato receives an email from Chudnofsky on the Hornet's calling-card email address, offering $1 million dollars and half of Los Angeles if he kills Britt.
- **S20**：Meanwhile, Britt discovers Scanlon is corrupt, learning that he tried to bribe James into downplaying the city's level of crime in order to help his career.
- **S21**：Scanlon invites Britt to meet in a restaurant, where he reveals he murdered Britt's father.
- **S22**：Kato arrives, and instead of killing Britt attacks Chudnofsky's men, allowing him and Britt, whom Chudnofsky deduces is the real Green Hornet, to escape.
- **S23**：At The Daily Sentinel, Britt intends to upload a recording of Scanlon's confession onto the Web — and belatedly discovers he did not manage to record it.
- **S24**：Chudnofsky and his men, who followed the duo there, engage them in a firefight.
- **S25**：Kato ultimately stabs Chudnofsky in the eyes with wood in self-defense and Britt shoots him to death.
- **S26**：A SWAT team appears and fires at the Green Hornet and Kato, who use the remains of their nearly demolished Black Beauty to run Scanlon out the 10th-floor window, killing him.
- **S27**：The Green Hornet and Kato flee to Lenore's house, where she learns their secret identities and that she has been the accidental mastermind behind the Green Hornet's plots.
- **S28**：Despite being furious, she helps them hide from the police and tends to Britt's shoulder gunshot wound.
- **S29**：The next morning, Britt promotes Axford to editor-in-chief and stages being shot in the shoulder by Kato, further establishing the Green Hornet as a threat and allowing Britt to get treated by professionals in a hospital.
- **S30**：Later, the two weld James' stolen bust back onto his memorial statue.
- **S31**：Now with Lenore to aid them, Britt and Kato vow to continue protecting the law by breaking it.

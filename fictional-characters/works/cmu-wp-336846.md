## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In the summer of 1959, local boy Danny Zuko and vacationing Sandy Olsson meet at the beach and fall in love.
- **S2**：When the summer comes to an end, Sandy—who is going back to Australia—frets that they may never meet again, but Danny tells her that their love is "only the beginning".
- **S3**：The film moves to the start of the seniors' term at Rydell High School.
- **S4**：Danny, a greaser, is a member of the T-Birds, consisting of his best friend Kenickie, Doody, Putzie and Sonny.
- **S5**：The Pink Ladies also arrive, consisting of Betty Rizzo, Janet and Marty.
- **S6**：After her parents decided not to return to Australia, Sandy enrolls at Rydell and befriends Frenchy , who considers dropping out of school to become a beautician.
- **S7**：Oblivious to each other's presence at school, Danny and Sandy tell their respective groups the accounts of events during the pair's brief romance.
- **S8**：Upon learning Danny is Sandy's lover, Rizzo arranges for the two to reunite, but Danny is forced to maintain his bad-boy attitude in front of his friends, upsetting Sandy.
- **S9**：Frenchy invites the girls to a sleepover, but Sandy falls ill from trying a cigarette and drinking.
- **S10**：The T-Birds almost crash the party, but a guilty Danny leaves, followed by Rizzo, who departs to make out with Kenickie, who is actually her boyfriend.
- **S11**：The two are disturbed by Leo, leader of the T-Birds' rival gang, the Scorpions, and his girlfriend Cha-Cha, leading to a planned race between Kenickie and Leo.
- **S12**：Wishing to win his way back into Sandy's affection, Danny turns to Coach Calhoun to get into sports, eventually becoming a runner.
- **S13**：He reunites with Sandy and they attempt to go on a date, but their friends crash it, resulting in Kenickie and Rizzo breaking after a fight.
- **S14**：Left alone, Frenchy is visited by a guardian angel who advises her to return to school.
- **S15**：The school dance arrives, broadcast live on television and hosted by DJ Vince Fontaine, who flirts with Marty.
- **S16**：Rizzo and Kenickie attempt to spite one another by bringing Leo and Cha-Cha as their dates, whilst Danny and Sandy come together.
- **S17**：During a dance, Danny and Cha-Cha  perform together and win a dance-off.
- **S18**：Danny tries to make it up to Sandy by taking her to a drive-in theater but ends up making several passes on her, causing Sandy to flee.
- **S19**：Meanwhile, Rizzo fears she is pregnant after missing a period and confides to Marty, but Sonny overhears and spreads the rumour which eventually reaches Kenickie who is the potential father.
- **S20**：The race arrives, but Kenickie is knocked out by his own car door so Danny takes up the challenge.
- **S21**：He and Leo race until Leo crashes and leaves humiliated, with Danny as the victor.
- **S22**：Sandy watches from afar, concluding she still loves Danny and decides to change her attitude and look to impress him.
- **S23**：On the last day of school, whilst Principal McGee and her assistant Blanche sob about the departing class, the class celebrates their graduation at the fair on the school grounds.
- **S24**：Rizzo discovers she is not pregnant after all and reunites with Kenickie.
- **S25**：Danny has become a jock, but is shocked when Sandy appears dressed in leather and is seen smoking.
- **S26**：In song, the two admit they love each other and reunite.
- **S27**：The film ends with Danny and Sandy departing in Danny's car together, which then takes flight, and the pair waves goodbye to their friends as they live happily ever after.

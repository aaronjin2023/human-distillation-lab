### Umi Matsuzaki

- **性格倾向**：面对家庭与校园的实际需要，她倾向主动承担照料和组织责任（行为推断；S3、S11、S13）
- **价值取舍**：面对旧社团楼的去留，她倾向以共同修缮争取保留其价值（行为推断；S13、S18、S19）
- **情绪反应**：她因俊的疏远受到伤害，听到身世解释后仍未完全安心（摘要直接叙述；S16、S29）

- **决策方式**：面对拆楼决定，她倾向将集体劳动与直接向决策者争取结合（行为推断；S13、S18、S19）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Sixteen-year-old Umi Matsuzaki  lives in Coquelicot Manor, a boarding house overlooking the Port of Yokohama in Japan.
- **S2**：Her mother Ryoko  is a medical professor studying abroad in the United States.
- **S3**：Umi runs the house and looks after her younger siblings Sora  and Riku  and her grandmother Hana .
- **S4**：College student Sachiko Hirokouji  and doctor-in-training Miki Hokuto  also live there.
- **S5**：Each morning, Umi raises a set of signal flags with the message "I pray for safe voyages".
- **S6**：A poem about the flags being raised by a local girl is published in the school newspaper.
- **S7**：Shun Kazama , the poem's author, witnesses the flags from sea as he rides a tugboat to school.
- **S8**：Umi meets Shun when he participates in a daredevil stunt for the newspaper, leaving Umi with a negative first impression.
- **S9**：Later, Umi accompanies Sora to obtain Shun's autograph at the Quartier Latin, an old and dilapidated building housing the high school's clubs.
- **S10**：Umi discovers Shun publishes the school newspaper, with his friend Shirō Mizunuma , the school's student government president.
- **S11**：She ends up helping on the newspaper.
- **S12**：Umi is in the audience when Shun takes part in a lively debate on the future of the building, which may be demolished.
- **S13**：At Umi's suggestion, the female student body helps renovate the building.
- **S14**：At Umi's house, she shows Shun a photograph of three young naval men; one of the three men is her late father Yūichirō Sawamura, who was killed in the Korean War.
- **S15**：Shun begins isolating himself from Umi; he has the same photograph, and thinks that they may be half-siblings.
- **S16**：Umi is hurt by Shun's response, though he eventually reveals the news.
- **S17**：The renovation of Quartier Latin is complete; however, the board of education decides to go ahead with demolishing the building.
- **S18**：In desperation, the students nominate Shun, Umi, and Shirō to visit Tokyo and meet with Tokumaru , a businessman and chairman of the school board.
- **S19**：The trio travels through the city, which is in preparation for the 1964 Summer Olympics, and successfully convince Tokumaru to visit Quartier Latin.
- **S20**：On their way home, Shirō leaves Umi and Shun.
- **S21**：Umi professes her love to Shun, in spite of their situation, and he reciprocates her feelings.
- **S22**：Arriving home late that evening, Umi discovers her mother has at last returned from overseas.
- **S23**：Ryoko reveals that Shun's father was Hiroshi Tachibana - the second man in the photograph.
- **S24**：Tachibana was killed in accident on a repatriation ships.
- **S25**：Shun's mother died in childbirth.
- **S26**：Yūichirō registered the child as his own, to avoid leaving Shun as an orphan in the confused post-War years.
- **S27**：However, Ryoko could not raise Shun, as she was pregnant with Umi during her time as a medical student.
- **S28**：As a result, Shun was given to the Kazamas, a local couple who had lost their newborn at the time.
- **S29**：Umi, however, still has concerns.
- **S30**：The next day, Tokumaru arrives in Yokohama.
- **S31**：Impressed by the students' efforts in renovating Quartier Latin, the chairman decides to abandon the demolition.
- **S32**：However, Umi and Shun are called away from the celebrations and rush to the harbor.
- **S33**：They meet Yoshio Onodera , now a ship's Captain, who was the third man in the photograph, and the sole survivor of the three.
- **S34**：Onodera confirms that Umi and Shun are not related by blood, and tells the full story of the three naval men in the previous era.
- **S35**：With everything resolved, Umi returns to Coquelicot Manor and happily resumes her morning routine of raising the flags.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Six months after winning the 74th Hunger Games , Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark have returned home to District 12 , the poorest sector of Panem .
- **S3**：Prior to Katniss and Peeta's "Victory Tour" of the country, President Snow visits Katniss and tells her that her televised acts of defiance in the previous Games have inspired rebellion among the districts.
- **S4**：Snow demands that Katniss convince the country that she was acting out of love for Peeta, not against the Capitol, or her family and best friend Gale Hawthorne will be executed.
- **S5**：Katniss reveals this threat to her mentor, Haymitch Abernathy , but not to Peeta.
- **S6**：The tour's first stop is District 11, home of Katniss's Hunger Games ally Rue .
- **S7**：Peeta announces that he will give part of his winnings to the families of Rue and fellow tribute Thresh , and Katniss delivers an impromptu, heartfelt speech expressing her gratitude to the fallen tributes.
- **S8**：An old man salutes Katniss, joined by the crowd; to her horror, the old man is immediately executed.
- **S9**：Katniss tells Peeta of Snow's threat, and they continue the tour as normal.
- **S10**：Hoping to placate Snow, Peeta proposes to Katniss during a televised interview in the Capitol, which she accepts.
- **S11**：However, Snow remains dissatisfied with her performance, leaving her afraid for her loved ones.
- **S12**：Returning to District 12, now overrun with harsher Peacekeepers to enforce the Capitol's rule, Gale is caught poaching and is publicly whipped until Haymitch intervenes.
- **S13**：While hunting in the woods, Katniss encounters Bonnie and Twill, refugees from District 8, whose uprising has failed.
- **S14**：Bonnie and Twill plan to reach District 13 – believed to be destroyed in the first rebellion against the Capitol – hoping that the residents are actually underground.
- **S15**：Katniss is injured climbing back over District 12's now live electric fence.
- **S16**：Preparing for her upcoming wedding, Katniss learns that Districts 3 and 4 have also risen up against the Capitol.
- **S17**：The Capitol announces the 75th Hunger Games with a twist – tributes will be selected from the surviving victors of the previous Games.
- **S18**：As District 12's sole female victor, Katniss has to compete alongside either Haymitch or Peeta.
- **S19**：Haymitch is chosen and is unable to stop Peeta from volunteering in his place.
- **S20**：At the Capitol, Haymitch urges Katniss to find allies, but she bonds with the weakest tributes.
- **S21**：In the televised interview, Katniss's stylist Cinna has Katniss' wedding gown transformed into a black dress of feathers resembling a mockingjay.
- **S22**：Trying to stop the Games, Peeta lies in the interview that Katniss is pregnant.
- **S23**：Before Katniss is sent into the arena, she watches helplessly as Cinna is beaten and dragged out by Peacekeepers.
- **S24**：Katniss and Peeta ally themselves with Finnick Odair from District 4 and Mags , his 80-year-old mentor.
- **S25**：Peeta is nearly killed by the jungle arena's force field , and the party later has to flee from a poisonous fog .
- **S26**：Mags sacrifices herself to allow Finnick to save the weakened Peeta.
- **S27**：Katniss and Peeta ally with Johanna Mason from District 7 and "exceptionally smart" Beetee and Wiress from District 3.
- **S28**：Wiress reveals that the arena is arranged like a clock, with each danger occurring at a fixed time and place for one hour.
- **S29**：Wiress is killed, and in retaliation Katniss and Johanna kill the District 1 victors Gloss and Cashmere.
- **S30**：The remaining members of Katniss's group work on Beetee's plan to harness lightning to electrocute the District 2 victors, who later interfere and disrupt the plan.
- **S31**：Katniss uses her bow and arrow to direct the lightning into the force field, destroying it and knocking her unconscious.
- **S32**：Katniss wakes up en route to District 13 with Finnick, Beetee, and Haymitch.
- **S33**：She learns from Haymitch and Plutarch Heavensbee, the Head Gamemaker, that there had been a secret plan to rescue Katniss, now the living symbol of the rebellion.
- **S34**：Peeta, along with Johanna and District 2 tribute Enobaria, have been captured by the Capitol.
- **S35**：She later learns from Gale that, though her family and some other residents have escaped, District 12 has been destroyed.

## 角色资料

### Anton Gorodetsky

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-66fb25dd7a23b2d5` · 独立版本：`profile-eee3e3659a0cfe0f`

- **性格倾向**：面对儿子的违规行为，他倾向以包庇方式回应父亲身份的压力（行为推断；S4）
- **核心动机**：他寻找命运粉笔，是为弥补当年试图伤害未出生儿子的错误（摘要直接叙述；S5）
- **价值取舍**：在执法职责与亲子关系冲突时，他一度将保护儿子置于条约之上（行为推断；S4）
- **决策方式**：灾难发生后，他转向修改自己的源头决定来阻止连锁毁灭（行为推断；S25、S26、S28、S29）
- **成长变化**：他从掩盖儿子的后果，转向撤回自身曾经造成伤害的选择（行为推断；S4、S28、S31）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：试图保护儿子，却以包庇违规助长了更大的危机。

### Yegor

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-1e3b506edca10a46` · 独立版本：`profile-1d7a4b3dd34e94ce`

- **性格倾向**：当他感到自己与父亲的关系被侵入时，倾向强烈排斥父亲的恋人（行为推断；S15、S16、S20）
- **核心动机**：他希望父亲与母亲和好，并对父亲拒绝这一请求感到怨恨（摘要直接叙述；S13）
- **关系模式**：他将斯维特兰娜视作侵入自己与缺席父亲关系的竞争者（摘要直接叙述；S15、S20）
- **决策方式**：面对父亲恋人的介入，他倾向以摔毁物品和当面挑衅回应（行为推断；S15、S16、S20）
- **情绪反应**：生日聚会的对峙中，他对父亲恋人的怨愤持续升级为挑衅（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望父亲回归家庭，却在争夺父亲时几乎将他杀死。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：It is New Year's Day of 2006, more than a year after the events of Night Watch.
- **S2**：Anton Gorodetsky, the protagonist of the first film, finds himself in the middle of an approaching conflict between the Light and Dark Others, who are still bound with an uneasy truce.
- **S3**：Anton is still a Night Watch operative, now working with his trainee and romantic interest, Svetlana.
- **S4**：As his son Yegor has now become a Dark Other, Anton is forced to secretly destroy evidence of Yegor's attacks on normal people, which violates the treaty, leaving the Night Watch unable to sentence Yegor.
- **S5**：To redeem for his previous mistake, an attempt to use a witch's service to kill the unborn Yegor , Anton seeks the legendary Chalk of Fate, a magical chalk that could rewrite history, which was once Tamerlane's property and one of the main reasons for his numerous military successes.
- **S6**：Meanwhile, Zavulon, the leader of the Dark Others and their Day Watch, is waiting for Yegor's birthday.
- **S7**：At the birthday, Yegor would become a Great Other and acquire the power that would allow the Dark Others to break the treaty .
- **S8**：Zavulon's gratitude to Anton for covering Yegor's violations of the treaty doesn't stop him and the Day Watch from attempting to frame Anton for murder and bring him in front of the Inquisition.
- **S9**：They succeed, despite the efforts made by Geser, the head of the Night Watch, to protect Anton from the Dark Others by putting him in Olga's body.
- **S10**：While in her body, Svetlana confesses that she loves Anton to the person she thinks is Olga, which pushes their relationship forward.
- **S11**：This happens despite Svetlana's initial anger at Anton for not telling her that he was in Olga's body.
- **S12**：Anton obtains the Chalk of Fate from its hiding place in a Central Asian cafe in Moscow and uses it to summon Yegor.
- **S13**：They initially get on well, but Yegor is resentful when Anton refuses his request to patch things up with Yegor's mother.
- **S14**：Anton puts his large coat on Yegor, who seems to be cold, and leaves to order food.
- **S15**：At this time, Svetlana rings Anton's mobile, which he left in the pocket of the coat, and Yegor, angry at her intrusion into his relationship with his absentee father, yells at her, "He has a family.
- **S16**：Don't call again." He smashes the phone and leaves with the chalk, which he takes to Zavulon.
- **S17**：Zavulon cannot use or even touch the Chalk, because doing so would be a direct violation of the treaty, so he has Yegor give it to his minion/lover Alisia to do with as she wishes, although it is implied that Zavulon knows what she will do with it.
- **S18**：Yegor's birthday party begins soon after that; the guests are Dark Others , although Anton makes his way to the party as Yegor's father to expose the real perpetrator of the murder he has been charged with - his vampire neighbor, Kostya's father.
- **S19**：He is unable, however, to avert a disaster: as Svetlana rushes to the party to find Anton, Yegor confronts her.
- **S20**：She tries to avoid a conflict but Yegor repeatedly challenges her and expresses resentment at her relationship with his father; Svetlana accidentally strikes Yegor and spills a drop of his blood, which Zavulon interprets as a violation of the Treaty and thus uses as a pretense to declare all-out war on the Light Others.
- **S21**：Yegor, now a Great Other, unleashes an apocalypse upon Moscow, killing most of the guests and blinding Svetlana.
- **S22**：The city is nearly destroyed, starting with the Ostankino Tower; a fierce battle between the Light and Dark Others follows, with few survivors on either side.
- **S23**：In the midst of the chaos, Anton, who survives, finds Alisia who is trying to revive her dead lover, Kostya, but without success.
- **S24**：She cannot revive him because her actions did not cause his death.
- **S25**：The scene implies the user of the Chalk can only change decisions that he or she made, not anyone else's.
- **S26**：Anton convinces Alisia to give him the Chalk so that he can prevent the destruction of Moscow and the deaths of scores of Others, but is almost immediately caught by a panicked Svetlana and an enraged Yegor.
- **S27**：The two Great Others fight for Anton, but nearly kill him in the process.
- **S28**：Saved at the last minute by Geser, Anton runs through the ruins of Moscow to the house where he, fourteen years ago, made his visit to the witch &mdash; the visit that caused the entire sequence of events, starting Anton's own initiation into the Night Watch.
- **S29**：Anton writes NO  on a wall in this house.
- **S30**：Moscow reverts to its normal, undemolished state and the film returns to 1992 and the first scene of Night Watch.
- **S31**：In the epilogue, as a result of the Chalk's influence, Anton rethinks his deal with the witch, and therefore never inadvertently agrees to harm his wife's unborn child, who would have been Yegor.
- **S32**：He walks out of the house and into the street, where he meets Svetlana.
- **S33**：Zavulon and Geser watch them from a park bench, eager to see if Anton will recognize Svetlana, despite now having never met her because of the rewriting of history.
- **S34**：Geser's prediction turns out to be accurate; despite not knowing how or why, Anton recognizes Svetlana and they walk off together, implicitly striking up a less harried relationship than the one they have/had in the first film.

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Rachel Watson is a 33-year-old woman with behavioural issues related to her alcoholism.
- **S3**：Her drinking has led to the end of her marriage to Tom, who left her for another woman.
- **S4**：It has also caused her to lose her job; she frequently binges and has blackouts .
- **S5**：Tom has since re-married to Anna Boyd and has a daughter with her, Evie – a situation that fuels Rachel's self-destructive tendencies.
- **S6**：Though currently unemployed, Rachel still follows her old routine of taking the train to and from London every day, one at 8:04 in the morning and the other at 5:56 in the evening.
- **S7**：This routine is one of her few excuses to leave her apartment, and prevents her roommate, Cathy, from discovering that she is unemployed.
- **S8**：The train passes Rachel's old home on Blenheim Road, where Tom, Anna, and Evie now live.
- **S9**：From the train, Rachel also watches a couple who live a few houses away from Tom.
- **S10**：She idealizes their life (christening them "Jason" and "Jess"), and fantasizes about being friends with them.
- **S11**：However, "Jess", whose real name is Megan Hipwell, has a troubled past and a far from perfect life.
- **S12**：She has had several lovers, including her therapist, Dr.
- **S13**：Kamal Abdic.
- **S14**：One day, from the train, Rachel witnesses Megan passionately embracing another man.
- **S15**：The next day, after heavy drinking, Rachel awakens to find herself bloody and injured, with no memories of the night before.
- **S16**：She learns that Megan is missing, and is questioned by the police after Anna reports having seen her drunkenly staggering around the night of Megan's disappearance.
- **S17**：Rachel tells the police she thinks Megan was having an affair.
- **S18**：She then contacts Megan's husband, Scott ("Jason"), leading him to believe that she and Megan were friends.
- **S19**：Rachel learns that the man she saw kissing Megan was Kamal.
- **S20**：Rachel contacts Kamal, lying about her identity to get close to him and learn more about him.
- **S21**：She makes a therapy appointment with him to see if he can help her recall the events that happened during her blackout that night.
- **S22**：Rachel begins to gain insight into her life by speaking with him, inadvertently benefiting from the therapy.
- **S23**：Her connections to Scott and Kamal, though built on lies, make her feel more important.
- **S24**：She manages to not drink for days at a time, but always relapses.
- **S25**：Meanwhile, she continues to call, visit, and harass Tom.
- **S26**：Megan's body is found; she is revealed to have been pregnant, and her unborn child was fathered by neither Scott nor Kamal.
- **S27**：As Scott discovers Rachel's lies and lashes out at her, her memories become clearer.
- **S28**：Rachel remembers seeing Megan get into Tom's car.
- **S29**：Anna discovers that Tom and Megan were having an affair.
- **S30**：Rachel begins trusting her own memories more, and realises that many of the incidents Tom said she caused while drunk never really happened.
- **S31**：He had been gaslighting her for years, making her question her sanity.
- **S32**：Armed with this realisation, and the suspicion that he might have killed Megan, Rachel warns Anna.
- **S33**：When Anna confronts him, Tom confesses to murdering Megan after she threatened to reveal the affair.
- **S34**：Anna is cowed, fearing for her daughter's safety.
- **S35**：Tom tries to beat and intimidate Rachel into keeping silent, but she defies him and fights back.
- **S36**：Rachel stabs Tom with a corkscrew; Anna helps Rachel make sure that he dies from the wound.
- **S37**：When the police arrive, Rachel and Anna support each other by corroborating their stories to explain their actions as self-defence.
- **S38**：After this, Rachel decides to quit drinking and move on with her life.

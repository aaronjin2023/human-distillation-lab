## 角色资料

### Pete Mitchell

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-030ded274b16186a` · 独立版本：`profile-697063844ff8fb09`

- **性格倾向**：在飞行竞争中倾向冒险逞强，常把个人突破置于规则之前（行为推断；S7、S14、S15、S19）
- **核心动机**：不愿接受父亲失误致死的说法，飞行冒险部分受此驱动（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9）
- **价值取舍**：面对同伴遇险时愿违令承担风险，重视把队友带回安全处（行为推断；S4、S5）
- **情绪反应**：即使调查认定无责，仍因Goose之死内疚并失去进取心（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25、S26）
- **成长变化**：从追求单独较量，走向克服恐惧并与僚机协同作战（行为推断；S19、S20、S31、S32）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：愿冒险救同伴，却也会因追求个人较量而脱离僚机。

### Charlotte Blackwood

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-51d53a771c0ec005` · 独立版本：`profile-334e091f212a4a0b`

- **性格倾向**：在评价学员时倾向谨慎维护专业形象，暂时压住个人欣赏（行为推断；S17）
- **价值取舍**：重视教官身份的可信度，不愿让私人好感削弱职业评价（行为推断；S13、S17、S18）
- **沟通方式**：先公开批评飞行战术，随后坦承欣赏及批评背后的顾虑（摘要直接叙述；S17）
- **关系模式**：与Maverick由追求关系发展为恋人，在其受创时尝试安慰（摘要直接叙述；S16、S18、S26、S35）

暂无支持：核心动机、决策方式、情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：欣赏Maverick的飞行能力，却担心坦露好感损害专业可信度。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：United States Naval Aviator Lieutenant Pete "Maverick" Mitchell  flies the F-14A Tomcat off {{USS}}, with Radar Intercept Officer  Lieutenant Nick "Goose" Bradshaw .
- **S2**：At the start of the film, wingman "Cougar" ([[John Stockwell  and his radar intercept officer "Merlin" , intercept MiG-28s over the Indian Ocean.
- **S3**：During the engagement, one of the MiGs manages to get missile lock on Cougar.
- **S4**：While Maverick realizes that the MiG " have fired by now", if he really meant to fight, and drives off the MiGs, Cougar is too shaken afterward to land, despite being low on fuel.
- **S5**：Maverick defies orders and shepherds Cougar back to the carrier, despite also being low on fuel.
- **S6**：After they land, Cougar retires , stating that he has been holding on "too tight" and has lost "the edge", almost orphaning his newborn child who he has never seen.
- **S7**：Although disapproving of Maverick's reckless flying and repeated violation of rules, the Enterprises Commander, Air Group "Stinger"  sends him and Goose&mdash;now his top crew&mdash;to attend the Top Gun school at NAS Miramar.
- **S8**：Maverick flies recklessly partially because of his father, Duke Mitchell, a Naval Aviator with VF-51 aboard the {{USS}} during the Vietnam War.
- **S9**：The elder Mitchell died on November 5, 1965 when his McDonnell Douglas F-4 Phantom II was shot down, and the official story, which Maverick refuses to believe, is that Duke made a mistake.
- **S10**：Goose is cautious and devoted to his wife, Carole , and child.
- **S11**：The two officers are nonetheless close friends and effective partners, with Goose as the only family Maverick has.
- **S12**：At a bar the day before the Top Gun program starts, Maverick, assisted by Goose, unsuccessfully approaches a woman by singing "You've Lost That Lovin' Feelin'".
- **S13**：He learns the next day that she is Charlotte "Charlie" Blackwood , an aeronautical engineer and civilian Top Gun instructor.
- **S14**：Maverick's reckless flying both annoys and impresses Lieutenant Commander Rick "Jester" Heatherly  and other instructors.
- **S15**：He defeats Jester in combat, but violates two rules of engagement in the process.
- **S16**：He continues to pursue Charlie and becomes a rival to top student LT Tom "Iceman" Kasansky &mdash;who considers Maverick's methods dangerous and unsafe.
- **S17**：Although outwardly critical of Maverick's tactics, Charlie eventually admits that she admires his flying but was critical because she was afraid for her credibility.
- **S18**：They begin a romantic relationship.
- **S19**：During one flight, Maverick breaks off from his wingman "Hollywood" to go one-on-one with chief instructor Commander Mike "Viper" Metcalf , described as "the  fighter pilot... in the world".
- **S20**：Although Maverick matches the older pilot move for move, Viper lasts long enough for Jester&mdash;who has defeated Hollywood off-screen&mdash;to maneuver around and "shoot" Maverick down, demonstrating the value of teamwork over individual ability.
- **S21**：Near the end of the program, Maverick and Iceman both chase Jester, the latter attempting to gain a missile lock on the target.
- **S22**：Under intense pressure from Maverick, Iceman breaks off.
- **S23**：Maverick's F-14 flies through the jet wash of Iceman's aircraft and suffers a flameout of both engines, entering a flat spin from which he cannot recover, forcing him and Goose to eject.
- **S24**：Goose ejects directly into the jettisoned aircraft canopy and breaks his neck, dying instantly.
- **S25**：Although the board of inquiry clears Maverick of responsibility, he feels guilty for Goose's death, losing his aggressiveness when flying.
- **S26**：Charlie and others attempt to console him, but Maverick considers leaving the Navy.
- **S27**：Unsure of his future, he seeks Viper's advice.
- **S28**：Viper reveals that he served with Maverick's father in VF-51, and discloses classified details, explaining how Duke stayed in the fight after his F-4 was hit and saved three planes before he died.
- **S29**：During the graduation party, Iceman, Hollywood, and Maverick are ordered to immediately report to Enterprise to deal with a "crisis situation", providing air support for the rescue of a stricken communications ship, the SS Layton, that has drifted into hostile waters.
- **S30**：Maverick and Merlin are assigned to one of two F-14s as back-up for those flown by Iceman and Hollywood, despite Iceman's reservations over Maverick's state of mind.
- **S31**：In the subsequent hostile engagement with six MiGs, Hollywood is shot down but manages to eject; Maverick is sortied alone due to catapult failure and nearly retreats after encountering circumstances similar to those that caused Goose's death.
- **S32**：Upon finally rejoining Iceman, they shoot down four MiGs and force the others to flee, and return triumphantly to Enterprise.
- **S33**：Offered any assignment he chooses, Maverick decides to return to Top Gun as an instructor .
- **S34**：Sitting alone in a restaurant in downtown San Diego, Maverick hears "You've Lost That Lovin' Feelin'" playing on the jukebox and recalls meeting Charlie.
- **S35**：She enters the bar and the two reunite.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Peter Banning  is a successful corporate lawyer whose relationship with his family, especially his two young children Jack  and Maggie  is strained by continuous absences and broken promises.
- **S2**：His wife Moira  struggles to keep them together and grows frustrated at Peter for his callous behavior.
- **S3**：The family flies to London to visit Moira's grandmother, Wendy Darling , who helped Peter find a family when he was a young orphan.
- **S4**：Upon arrival, they meet an old man who has "lost his marbles", Tootles , Wendy's first orphan.
- **S5**：Peter, Moira, and Wendy attend a ceremony for the expansion of Wendy's orphanage.
- **S6**：While they are out, his children are kidnapped, with a dagger bearing a note signed by "Jas.
- **S7**：Hook, Captain" flung into their bedroom door.
- **S8**：Wendy tells Peter that he is in fact Peter Pan and that his old enemy has returned and taken his children for revenge, but he fails to remember anything.
- **S9**：Tinker Bell  appears before Peter that night and knocks him unconscious and flies him to a pirate port in Neverland.
- **S10**：There he awakens in disbelief, and is discovered by Captain Hook  and his second in command Smee , who threaten the children unless he accepts Hook's challenge to a duel.
- **S11**：Tinker Bell intervenes and is granted three days in which to prepare him for it.
- **S12**：The Lost Boys, now led by Peter's successor, Rufio , at first dismiss him as an old man who has no hope of regaining his former glory, but he begins to learn the magic of Neverland.
- **S13**：Meanwhile, Hook uses Jack's frustration over his father's continuous broken promises to steal his affection.
- **S14**：Peter is heartbroken when he sees Hook treating Jack like a son, and becomes determined to win his family back.
- **S15**：He finally remembers his past and learns how to fly by recalling his "happy thought": being a father.
- **S16**：Peter regains the leadership of the Lost Boys and they challenge Hook and his pirates in an all-out battle.
- **S17**：Peter regains Jack's love and saves Maggie, but during the fight Rufio is killed by Hook.
- **S18**：Peter and Hook engage in a climactic sword fight, Hook is apparently killed when the Crocodile, now a massive clock tower, falls on him.
- **S19**：Peter returns home with Jack and Maggie and designates the largest member of the Lost Boys, Thud Butt , the only other lost boy besides Rufio whom Peter grew a connection to, as leader of the Lost Boys in his absence, and tells all the Lost Boys to take care of everybody smaller than them.
- **S20**：Returning home, Peter finally realizes the love he has for his family and the importance of having a youthful heart.
- **S21**：Tootles, a former Lost Boy, is dismayed at missing the adventure, but discovers pixie dust in his bag of lost marbles and uses it to go flying around London and back to Neverland.
- **S22**：Wendy remarks to Peter that his adventures are now over, but Peter says to live would be a great adventure as well.

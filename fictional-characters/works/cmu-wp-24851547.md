## 角色资料

### Susumu Kodai

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-b18295b02dcbe7ef` · 独立版本：`profile-f7bf76760f8a84ef`

- **性格倾向**：面对同伴遇险时，他倾向立即承担风险，即使因此违反命令（行为推断；S19、S20）
- **情绪反应**：哥哥牺牲引发他对冲田的愤怒，牺牲船员的命令又令他歉疚（行为推断；S9、S28、S29）
- **价值取舍**：担任指挥后，他逐渐接受以痛苦牺牲保全更多人的责任（行为推断；S28、S29、S45、S46）
- **决策方式**：主炮受阻且形势危急时，他倾向冒险跃迁以争取新的生路（行为推断；S30、S31）
- **成长变化**：他从指责指挥官牺牲哥哥，走向亲自留下并以舰毁敌（摘要直接叙述；S9、S45、S46）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：难以接受哥哥被牺牲，却最终必须作出牺牲同伴的命令

### Juzo Okita

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-be33a88f8ac92850` · 独立版本：`profile-0d9edc63dd77b920`

- **性格倾向**：面对可能灭绝的危机，他倾向押注有限希望并承担指挥风险（行为推断；S10、S14）
- **核心动机**：他相信人类的生存希望在伊斯坎达尔，寄望前往那里寻找出路（行为推断；S10）
- **决策方式**：面对发射前来袭的巨型导弹，他选择动用尚未测试的主炮（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14）
- **关系模式**：即使曾遭进指责与攻击，他仍在病重时将代理舰长职责交给进（摘要直接叙述；S9、S25）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 2199, after five years of attacks by an alien race known as Gamilas, the Earth Defense Force launches a counter-offensive near Mars.
- **S2**：The fleet's weapons are no match for the Gamilas, who easily wipe out much of the force.
- **S3**：During the battle, EDF captain Mamoru Kodai volunteers to use his damaged ship, the destroyer Yukikaze, as a shield to cover Captain Jyuzo Okita's ship, allowing his escape.
- **S4**：Mamoru's ship is destroyed.
- **S5**：On Earth, Mamoru's brother Susumu is scavenging on the irradiated surface when an object impacts near him and knocks him unconscious.
- **S6**：He awakens to find an alien message capsule.
- **S7**：Susumu also notices that the radiation has been reduced to safe levels around him.
- **S8**：He is rescued by Okita's returning ship and it is discovered that the capsule contains engineering schematics for a new warp drive and coordinates for the planet from which it came .
- **S9**：After learning what happened at Mars, Susumu accuses Okita of using his brother as a sacrificial lamb and tries to hit him, but crewmember Yuki Mori violently stops him.
- **S10**：Okita believes the hope for humanity lies within Iskandar.
- **S11**：A request for volunteers for the mission is sent out, and Kodai - a former EDF pilot - decides to re-enlist.
- **S12**：Their last battleship, the Yamato, is enhanced with alien technology.
- **S13**：Before the Yamato can launch, the Gamilas attack with a gigantic missile.
- **S14**：Captain Okita gives the order to fire the yet-untested Wave Motion Cannon, which successfully destroys the incoming missile.
- **S15**：Kodai is reunited with his old fighter squad.
- **S16**：Yuki, who joined the EDF years ago because of her admiration for Kodai, is bitter towards him.
- **S17**：The Yamato crew performs their first warp test and encounters more Gamilas ships.
- **S18**：Since the Wave Motion Cannon is powered by the same reactor as the warp drive, the crew has to wait until the engine recharges before they can warp again.
- **S19**：The Yamato destroys the alien capital ships, but the battle damages Yuki's fighter.
- **S20**：Kodai launches to rescue her and is sent to the brig for disobeying orders.
- **S21**：Shima, Kodai's former squad-mate, tells Yuki that Kodai left the service because he accidentally caused the death of his own parents and also Shima's wife during a mission.
- **S22**：After warping out of the Milky Way, the Yamato crew finds a Gamilas fighter adrift and recovers it to study, but the alien pilot is still alive and possesses Commando Team Leader Hajime Saitō, in order to communicate.
- **S23**：The alien calls himself Dessler and says the Gamilas are a race with a hive mind.
- **S24**：Kodai stuns the possessed Saito, but the alien apparently is destroyed.
- **S25**：Later, an ailing Captain Okita makes Kodai the acting-captain.
- **S26**：The crew discovers the captured Gamilas fighter contains a homing beacon, giving away their position.
- **S27**：The Yamato fires its Wave Motion Cannon to destroy a Gamilas ship, but a stealth Gamilas spacecraft latches onto the ship's third bridge on the bottom of the hull.
- **S28**：Kodai reluctantly orders Yuki, in her fighter, to blast the third bridge support away moments before it detonates, saving the Yamato.
- **S29**：Kodai apologizes to Yuki for ordering her to doom their crew-mates, and they have an intimate moment as the ship warps again.
- **S30**：The Yamato arrives at Iskandar, but is met by a large Gamilas fleet that sends a spacecraft to obstruct the nose of the Wave Motion Cannon.
- **S31**：With their main weapon disabled, Kodai makes the dangerous choice to randomly warp and ends up at the opposite side of Iskandar.
- **S32**：They are surprised to see that it is lifeless.
- **S33**：It is then discovered that Gamilas and Iskandar are the same planet.
- **S34**：The crew thinks it is a trap, but Kodai urges them to go ahead.
- **S35**：He leads an attack party to the planet's surface against heavy Gamilas opposition.
- **S36**：Much of the assault force is killed, and the remaining pilots stay behind to cover for Kodai, Sanada, Saito and Mori as they head for the coordinates.
- **S37**：Once they reach the coordinates, an alien possesses Yuki's body and explains that the Gamilas and Iskandar are two aspects of the same race.
- **S38**：The alien says their planet is dying and they saw Earth as the most suitable replacement, after first killing off humanity.
- **S39**：Iskandar did not agree with this and was imprisoned.
- **S40**：Iskandar implants in Yuki with the ability to clean the radiation from Earth.
- **S41**：As she and Kodai return to the Yamato, Saito and Sanada sacrifice themselves by destroying the Gamilas power source.
- **S42**：The Yamato returns to Earth, where Okita dies.
- **S43**：The crew rejoices at their return home, but a surviving Gamilas ship ambushes them and severely damages the warship.
- **S44**：Dessler now appears and says they no longer wish to invade the Earth; however, since the majority of the Gamilas were killed, he intends to destroy the planet with his ship to avenge his race.
- **S45**：Kodai orders the surviving crew to abandon ship before he pilots the Yamato on a kamikaze attack against Dessler's ship.
- **S46**：He fires the blocked Wave Motion Cannon, which destroys both spaceships.
- **S47**：The ending shows Yuki standing with a child, implied to be Kodai's son, on the Earth's surface now restored to its original state.

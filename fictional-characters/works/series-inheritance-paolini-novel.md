- **S1**：Inheritance (or The Vault of Souls ) is a 2011 fantasy fiction novel written by American author Christopher Paolini .
- **S2**：It is the fourth and final novel in The Inheritance Cycle tetralogy.
- **S3**：The Inheritance Cycle was originally intended to be a trilogy , but Paolini has stated that during writing, the length of the third book, Brisingr , grew, and the book was split into two parts to be published separately.
- **S4**：Because of this, many plot elements originally intended for Brisingr are in Inheritance . [ 2 ] Since the release of Inheritance , Paolini has expressed his interest in expanding upon Alagaësia and the Inheritance Cycle.
- **S5**：In an interview, he talked about a potential "book five", a prequel centering on Brom, and said that he has planned "around seven more stories set in Alagaësia—and one of those is in fact a series." [ 3 ] In a video that was released on October 30, 2007, Paolini stated that during the work on the third book, he realized it would become too long and so he decided to split it into two separate books.
- **S6**：His explanation is as follows: [ 2 ] I plotted out the Inheritance series as a trilogy nine years ago, when I was fifteen.
- **S7**：At that time, I never imagined I’d write all three books, much less that they would be published.
- **S8**：When I finally delved into Book Three [ Brisingr ], it soon became obvious that the remainder of the story was far too big to fit in one volume.
- **S9**：Having spent so long thinking about the series as a trilogy, it was difficult for me to realize that, in order to be true to my characters and to address all of the plot points and unanswered questions Eragon and Eldest raised, I needed to split the end of the series into two books. — Christopher Paolini On March 23, 2011, Random House announced the title, cover artwork, and release date of Inherita

- **S32**：Umaroth, the dragon who leads the Eldunarí, decides to join Eragon to overthrow Galbatorix.
- **S33**：They journey to Urû'baen, under siege by the combined forces of the Varden, elves, werecats, Urgals and dwarves.
- **S34**：Eragon reveals the Eldunarí to the army's leaders, and they plan their attack.
- **S35**：Their combined forces attack Urû'baen while Eragon, Saphira, Arya, Elva, and elven spellcasters break into Galbatorix's citadel.
- **S36**：After overcoming a series of traps and being separated from the spellcasters, they reach the throne room.

- **S49**：She is also chosen as the new queen.

- **S51**：He thus decides to sail away with the Eldunarí and the eggs to a region far east of Alagaësia.
- **S52**：Eragon reworks the magic of the pact between Riders and dragons to allow dwarves and Urgals to become Riders, and leaves two eggs for each of the races.
- **S53**：The future Riders will travel to Eragon for training, while eggs will be periodically sent back to Alagaësia.
- **S54**：Eragon and Saphira sadly say their farewells to their friends and family, but look forward to the future.

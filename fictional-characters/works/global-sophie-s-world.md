### Sophie Amundsen

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-87eec25d16687d07` · 独立版本：`profile-21d63511302e5922`

- **性格倾向**：在自身世界可能虚构的处境中，她持续追问并尝试突破限制。（行为推断；S10、S11、S15、S30、S33）

- **决策方式**：她抓住花园中的突发混乱，与阿尔贝托借机逃出。（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18、S19）
- **成长变化**：她从哲学课程的学生成长为主动反抗作者控制的人物。（行为推断；S5、S6、S13、S15、S19、S30）

### Alberto Knox

- **性格倾向**：在受作者控制的处境中，他显得善于筹划，并坚持寻找脱身办法。（行为推断；S13、S15、S18、S19）
- **核心动机**：他教索菲哲学，并谋划让两人逃离克纳格的想象。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S6、S13、S15）
- **决策方式**：他把哲学知识用于理解控制处境，并在庭园混乱时执行逃离计划。（行为推断；S10、S13、S15、S17、S19）
- **关系模式**：他是索菲的哲学教师和同伴，协助她对抗克纳格的控制。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S13、S15）

## 引用证据

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Sophie Amundsen is a 14-year-old girl who lives in Lillesand , Norway.
- **S3**：The book begins with Sophie receiving two messages in her mailbox and a postcard addressed to Hilde Møller Knag.
- **S4**：Afterwards, she receives a packet of papers, part of a course in philosophy .
- **S5**：Sophie, without the knowledge of her mother, becomes the student of an old philosopher, Alberto Knox.
- **S6**：Alberto teaches her about the history of philosophy .
- **S7**：She gets a substantive and understandable review from the pre-Socratics to Jean-Paul Sartre .
- **S8**：In addition to this, Sophie and Alberto receive postcards addressed to a girl named Hilde from a man named Albert Knag.
- **S9**：As time passes, Knag begins to hide birthday messages to Hilde in ever more impossible ways, including hiding one inside an unpeeled banana and making Alberto's dog Hermes speak.
- **S10**：Eventually, through the philosophy of George Berkeley , Sophie and Alberto discover that they are actually fictional characters in a book written by Albert Knag as a 15th birthday gift for Hilde, his daughter.
- **S11**：They also hypothesize that the "real world" in which their story is being written may itself be fictional .
- **S12**：Hilde reads the manuscript, but begins to turn against her father after he continues to meddle with Sophie's life by sending fictional characters like Little Red Riding Hood and Ebenezer Scrooge to talk to her.
- **S13**：Alberto helps Sophie fight back against Knag's control by teaching her everything he knows about philosophy, through the Renaissance , Romanticism , and Existentialism , as well as Darwinism and the ideas of Karl Marx .
- **S14**：These take the form of long pages of text, and, later, monologues from Alberto.
- **S15**：Alberto manages to concoct a plan so that he and Sophie can finally escape Albert's imagination.
- **S16**：The trick is performed on Midsummer 's Eve, during a "philosophical garden party" that Sophie and her mother arranged to celebrate Sophie's fifteenth birthday.
- **S17**：The party soon descends into chaos as Albert Knag loses control over the world, causing the guests to react with indifference to extraordinary occurrences.
- **S18**：Alberto informs everyone that their world is fictional but the guests react with rage, believing him to be instilling dangerous values in the children.
- **S19**：When a Mercedes smashes into the garden, Alberto and Sophie use it as an opportunity to escape.
- **S20**：Knag is so focused on writing about the car that he does not notice them escaping into his world.
- **S21**：Having finished the book, Hilde decides to help Sophie and Alberto get revenge on her father.
- **S22**：Alberto and Sophie can interact only weakly with items in Albert Knag's world and cannot be seen by anyone but other fictional characters.
- **S23**：A woman from Grimms' Fairy Tales gives them food before they prepare to witness Knag's return to Lillesand, Hilde's home.
- **S24**：While at the airport, Knag receives notes from Hilde set up at shops and gateways, instructing him on items to buy.
- **S25**：He becomes increasingly paranoid as he wonders how Hilde is pulling the trick off.
- **S26**：When he arrives back home, Hilde has forgiven him now that he has learned what it is like to have his world interfered with.
- **S27**：Alberto and Sophie listen as Knag tells Hilde about one last aspect of philosophy—the universe itself.
- **S28**：He tells her about the Big Bang and how everything is made up of the same material, which exploded outward at the beginning of time.
- **S29**：Hilde learns that when she looks at the stars she is actually seeing into the past.
- **S30**：Sophie makes a last effort to communicate with her by hitting her and Knag with a wrench.
- **S31**：Knag feels nothing, but Hilde feels as though a gadfly stung her, and can hear Sophie's whispers.
- **S32**：Sophie wishes to ride in the rowboat but Alberto reminds her that, as they are not real people, they cannot manipulate objects.
- **S33**：In spite of this, Sophie manages to untie the rowboat and they ride out onto the lake, immortal and invisible to all but a few.
- **S34**：Hilde, inspired and mesmerized by philosophy and reconnected with her father, goes out to get the boat back.

### Dominic Toretto

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-603b681ecfb9f38f` · 独立版本：`profile-2d2916cd2a985722`

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4c0d2de3a8542b54` · 独立版本：`profile-1fe2d7e42ec12ffd`

- **性格倾向**：在营救与冲突情境中，他表现出敢于投入危险行动的倾向。（行为推断；S1、S7、S25）
- **核心动机**：他与同伴组织劫案，意在夺取雷耶斯的钱开始新生活。（行为推断；S16、S17）

- **关系模式**：他与米娅共同逃亡并育有未出生的孩子，也与多姆交好。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S15、S29、S31）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：When Dominic "Dom" Toretto is being transported to Lompoc prison by bus, his sister Mia Toretto and friend Brian O'Conner lead an assault on the bus, causing it to crash and freeing Dom.
- **S2**：While the authorities search for them, the trio escape to Rio de Janeiro.
- **S3**：Awaiting Dom's arrival, Mia and Brian join their friend Vince and other participants on a job to steal three cars from a train.
- **S4**：Brian and Mia discover that agents from the U.S.
- **S5**：Drug Enforcement Administration are also on the train and that the cars are seized property.
- **S6**：When Dom arrives with the rest of the participants, he realizes that one of them, Zizi, is only interested in stealing one car, a Ford GT40.
- **S7**：Dom has Mia steal the car herself before he and Brian fight Zizi and his henchmen, during which Zizi kills the DEA agents assigned to the vehicles.
- **S8**：Dom and Brian are captured and brought to crime lord Hernan Reyes, the owner of the cars and Zizi's boss.
- **S9**：Reyes orders the pair be interrogated to discover the location of the car, but they manage to escape and retreat to their safehouse.
- **S10**：While Brian, Dom, and Mia examine the car to discover its importance, Vince arrives and is caught trying to remove a computer chip from it.
- **S11**：He admits he was planning to sell the chip to Reyes on his own, and Dom forces him to leave.
- **S12**：Brian investigates the chip and discovers it contains details of Reyes' criminal empire, including the locations of US$100&nbsp;million in cash.
- **S13**：Following the murder of the DEA agents aboard the train, blamed on Dom and his team, DSS agent Luke Hobbs and his team arrive in Rio to arrest Dom and Brian.
- **S14**：With the help of local officer Elena Neves, they travel to Dom's safehouse, but find it under assault by Reyes' men.
- **S15**：Brian, Dom and Mia escape, and Dom suggests they split up and leave Rio, but Mia announces she is pregnant with Brian's child.
- **S16**：Dom agrees to stick together and suggests they steal Reyes' money to start a new life.
- **S17**：The trio organizes a team to perform the heist, recruiting Han Seoul-Oh, Roman Pearce, Tej Parker, Gisele Yashar, Leo and Santos.
- **S18**：Vince later joins the team after saving Mia from being captured by Reyes' men, earning Dom's trust once more.
- **S19**：Hobbs and his team eventually find and arrest Dom, Mia, Brian and Vince.
- **S20**：While transporting them to the airport for extradition to the United States, the convoy is attacked by Reyes' men, who kill Hobbs' team.
- **S21**：Hobbs and Elena are saved by Dom, Brian, Mia and Vince as they fight back against Reyes' men and escape, but Vince is shot in the process and dies.
- **S22**：Wanting to avenge his murdered team, Hobbs and Elena agree to help with the heist.
- **S23**：The gang breaks into the police station where Reyes' money is kept and tear the vault from the building using their cars, dragging it through the city with police in pursuit.
- **S24**：Believing they cannot outrun the police, Dom makes Brian continue without him while he attacks the police and the pursuing Reyes, using the vault attached to his car to smash their vehicles.
- **S25**：Brian returns to kill Zizi, while Reyes is badly injured by Dom's assault.
- **S26**：Hobbs arrives on the scene and kills Reyes.
- **S27**：Hobbs refuses to let Dom and Brian go free but, unwilling to arrest them, agrees to give them a 24-hour head start to escape.
- **S28**：The gang splits Reyes' money, leaving Vince's share to his family, before the members go their separate ways.
- **S29**：On a tropical beach, Brian and a visibly pregnant Mia relax.
- **S30**：They are met by Dom and Elena.
- **S31**：Brian challenges Dom to a final, no-stakes race to prove who is the better driver.
- **S32**：In a post-credits scene, Hobbs is given a file by Monica Fuentes concerning the hijack of a military convoy in Berlin.
- **S33**：In the file, Hobbs discovers a recent photo of Dom's former girlfriend Letty Ortiz, who had been presumed dead.

## 角色资料

### Shrek

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-58c4356ead039267` · 独立版本：`profile-6020a63f1cf40d0a`

- **性格倾向**：在家庭与公众角色压力下，他一度急躁，容易冲动言行。（行为推断；S6、S8、S9、S10）
- **核心动机**：他厌倦家庭与名人生活，渴望重过被人畏惧的食人魔生活。（摘要直接叙述；S4、S15、S16）
- **决策方式**：受挫时他倾向轻率接受交易，发现代价后才设法解除契约。（行为推断；S10、S15、S16、S20、S25、S29）
- **价值取舍**：在悬赏出现后，他愿牺牲自身自由来换取其他食人魔获释。（行为推断；S35）
- **成长变化**：经历失去家人与朋友的可能后，他重新珍惜生活并拥抱亲友。（摘要直接叙述；S38、S39、S40）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

### Princess Fiona

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-1e14f0d34b74d7bf` · 独立版本：`profile-e574fb7304fadf87`

- **性格倾向**：在抵抗处境中她务实而戒备，优先行动且不轻信真爱力量。（行为推断；S28、S29、S30、S31）
- **核心动机**：她领导食人魔抵抗军，专注筹划对兰佩尔斯蒂尔茨金的伏击。（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29、S30）
- **情绪反应**：她对真爱的力量抱持苦涩怀疑，后来才逐渐对史瑞克产生感情。（摘要直接叙述；S30、S31、S39）
- **成长变化**：她从尚未爱上史瑞克，转为在他消失前以真爱之吻解除契约。（摘要直接叙述；S34、S39）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

### Rumpelstiltskin

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-5a00a269515cd6cd` · 独立版本：`profile-0b368f205041b9eb`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中他善于利用他人脆弱时刻，以伪装善意掩藏算计。（行为推断；S12、S13、S14、S15）
- **核心动机**：他怨恨史瑞克破坏夺位计划，又借契约改写现实取得王位。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S3、S15、S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：他将交易设计成隐蔽陷阱，以抹去出生之日消除史瑞克的存在。（行为推断；S15、S20、S21、S22）
- **价值取舍**：在夺权后的统治中，他把食人魔及他人当作可供役使的奴工。（行为推断；S18、S19）
- **情绪反应**：计划被史瑞克无意破坏后，他心怀怨恨并抓住其失意的机会。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S12）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Before Shrek and Donkey rescue Princess Fiona in the first film, King Harold and Queen Lillian – desperate to lift their daughter's curse – meet with con artist Rumpelstiltskin, who wishes to become King of Far Far Away in exchange for helping them.
- **S2**：But before the deal is contracted, the king and queen learn that Fiona has been rescued.
- **S3**：Rumpelstiltskin is then shown to have become washed up as a result and subsequently bitter towards Shrek for inadvertently ruining his plans.
- **S4**：In the present, Shrek has steadily grown tired of being a family man and celebrity among the local villagers, leading him to yearn for the days when he felt like a "real ogre".
- **S5**：He takes his family to Far Far Away to celebrate his children's first birthday.
- **S6**：Shrek gets annoyed when the three little pigs eat the kids' cake along with most of the other party food.
- **S7**：A boy named Butterpants  demands that Shrek roar.
- **S8**：After he lets out a frustrated roar the entire crowd cheers, reinforcing his belief that no one considers him to be a true ogre.
- **S9**：This coupled with the fact that the birthday cakes were decorated with a "cute" Ogre named "Sprinkles" finally makes Shrek snap and he smashes the new birthday cake in anger.
- **S10**：He and Fiona argue outside about his reaction which ends with Shrek rashly agreed that he was happier before he'd rescued her.
- **S11**：After storming off, Shrek encounters Rumpelstiltskin.
- **S12**：Rumpel, who had observed Shrek's angry outburst with Fiona, seizes his chance.
- **S13**：He follows Shrek and arranges what appears to be an accident where he is trapped under his carriage.
- **S14**：Shrek helps him and Rumpel being "grateful", gives Shrek a ride and a meal.
- **S15**：When Shrek voices his frustrations, Rumpel offers to give Shrek a day to live like a real ogre in exchange for a day from his childhood that he would not remember being erased.
- **S16**：Shrek signs the contract and appears in a reality where he is still feared by villagers.
- **S17**：He takes the opportunity to cause some light hearted mischief until he finds wanted posters for the ogress Fiona and his home deserted and desolate.
- **S18**：He is kidnapped by witches and taken to Rumpelstiltskin, now the King of Far Far Away and possibly Emperor of a good deal more, which has become derelict and run down.
- **S19**：Rumpelstiltskin uses ogres and some of Shrek's friends as slave labor.
- **S20**：Upon inquiry, Rumpelstiltskin reveals that the day he erased was the day that Shrek was born.
- **S21**：Therefore, Shrek never saved Fiona, never met Donkey, and consequently Rumpelstiltskin was able to get Harold and Lillian to sign their kingdom away, then cause them to disappear.
- **S22**：When the day ends, Shrek will fade from existence.
- **S23**：Shrek escapes Rumpelstiltskin's castle with Donkey.
- **S24**：Initially terrified of Shrek, Donkey decides to trust him after seeing Shrek cry over his lost past, something he had never seen an ogre do before.
- **S25**：After Shrek explains the situation, Donkey helps him find a loophole: the contract will be nullified if Shrek and Fiona share true love's kiss.
- **S26**：Shrek and Donkey first travel to the dragon's keep where Fiona was kept and find the place deserted and the lava from the crater under the castle has been drained.
- **S27**：They soon encounter a band of ogres who are resisting Rumpelstiltskin.
- **S28**：The ogres are led by Fiona, who is still cursed after escaping from the tower where she was held captive, and keeps the retired and overweight Puss in Boots as a pet.
- **S29**：Shrek does everything he can to gain Fiona's love, but she is too busy preparing an ambush on Rumpelstiltskin.
- **S30**：She is also bitterly cynical and disillusioned about the power of true love and throws herself into planning Rumpelstiltskin's capture.
- **S31**：While sparring with her, Fiona begins to like Shrek, but stops short of kissing him.
- **S32**：Shrek is discouraged, but Puss encourages him to continue pursuing Fiona.
- **S33**：During the ambush, the ogres are captured by the Pied Piper, though Shrek and Fiona managed to escape with the intervention of Puss and Donkey.
- **S34**：Shrek insists Fiona kiss him, saying it will fix everything, but because Fiona does not truly love him it is ineffective.
- **S35**：Upon hearing that Rumpelstiltskin is offering anything desired by the one who captures Shrek, Shrek surrenders himself in exchange for all the ogres being released.
- **S36**：Fiona remains in custody because she is only an ogre by night .
- **S37**：Shrek and Fiona are to be fed to Dragon, but Donkey, Puss and the ogres raid Rumpelstiltskin's castle, allowing Shrek and Fiona to both subdue Dragon and capture Rumpelstiltskin.
- **S38**：As the sun rises, Shrek begins to fade from existence.
- **S39**：But Fiona, having fallen in love with him, kisses him just before he disappears, thereby voiding the contract and restoring the world to just before Shrek originally lashed out at everyone.
- **S40**：Shrek embraces his friends and family with a newfound appreciation for everything he has, truly living happily ever after.

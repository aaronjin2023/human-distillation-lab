- **S10**：## Plot
- **S11**：[ edit ] Bella Swan , an introverted seventeen-year-old girl, moves from Phoenix, Arizona , to Forks, Washington , on the Olympic Peninsula to live with her father, the town's police chief, after her mother begins life on the road with her new husband, a minor-league baseball player.
- **S12**：Bella is admitted to Forks High School , where she easily settles in with a group of friends.
- **S13**：A somewhat inexperienced and shy girl, Bella is dismayed by several boys competing for her attention.
- **S14**：On the first day of school, Bella sits next to her classmate Edward Cullen during biology class, but he seems to be utterly repulsed by her, much to her bewilderment.
- **S15**：He disappears for a week but when he returns, he is unexpectedly friendly to Bella.
- **S16**：Their newfound relationship is interrupted after Bella is nearly struck by a van in the school parking lot.
- **S17**：Edward saves Bella, narrowly stopping the van with his bare hands.
- **S18**：Bella questions Edward about how he saved her life but he refuses to tell her anything.
- **S19**：During a visit to First Beach , Bella meets Jacob Black , a local boy from the Quileute tribe.
- **S20**：She learns that Edward and the rest of the Cullen family are "cold ones" (vampires) who consume only animal blood.
- **S21**：Disturbed by recurring nightmares, Bella researches vampires.
- **S22**：She compares the characteristics of the vampires in mythology to the Cullens and becomes convinced that Edward is a vampire.
- **S23**：Bella is saved by Edward again in Port Angeles when she is almost attacked by a group of men.
- **S24**：Furious, Edward drives Bella away and takes her to a restaurant for dinner and then back home.
- **S25**：Edward confesses that he is able to read minds.
- **S26**：Everyone's mind, that is, but Bella's.
- **S27**：On the way, she tells him she knows that he is a vampire.
- **S28**：Edward confirms her belief and confesses that Bella's blood is more desirable to him than anyone else's and he wanted to kill her on the first day of school.
- **S29**：He tried to stay away from Bella to avoid hurting her, but over time, Edward and Bella fell in love.
- **S30**：Their relationship is affected when a nomadic vampire coven arrives in Forks.
- **S31**：James , a tracker vampire, who is intrigued by Cullen's relationship with a human, wants to hunt Bella for sport.
- **S32**：Bella and Edward are forced to separate as Bella escapes with Alice and Jasper (Edward's brother and sister) to hide in a hotel in Phoenix.
- **S33**：James calls Bella and claims to be holding her mother hostage.
- **S34**：Bella sneaks out and hurries to save her mother.
- **S35**：When she arrives, she finds that the hostage claim was a ruse.
- **S36**：James attacks and bites her, but before he can kill her, she is rescued by Edward and the other Cullens, who kill James.
- **S37**：Edward prevents Bella from becoming a vampire by sucking the venom out of her wound, and she is treated at a hospital, using the story that she fell out of a window as an excuse.
- **S38**：After they return to Forks, Edward takes her to the school prom, as Edward did not want Bella to miss any normal human experience because of him.
- **S39**：Bella says that she wants to be transformed into a vampire, but Edward reiterates he is against this.

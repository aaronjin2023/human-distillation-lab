- **S8**：## Plot Summary
- **S9**：[ edit ] The book opens with Amazing Maurice (a sentient cat), a group of talking rats (the Clan), and the human boy Keith travelling in a mail coach to a small town called Bad Blintz .
- **S10**：The group plans to enact a scheme they have used many times before, where in the rats pretend to infest the town and Keith poses as a rat piper to lead the "vermin" away for a small sum of cash.
- **S11**：Although Maurice sees nothing wrong with this hustling business, the rats find it immoral, and convince Maurice that this town will be the last one they rob.
- **S12**：Upon arriving in town, the group discovers that the people are convinced of a massive rat infestation, and have spent much of their savings on two rat catchers.
- **S13**：Despite their efforts, food continues to disappear from the town.
- **S14**：As the rats move into the town's underground, they discover an overwhelmingly large number of rat traps , but no live keekees (rats who cannot talk or think).
- **S15**：Above ground, Maurice makes similar observations, including that many of the rat tails the rat catchers display as proof of their successful hunting are in fact shoelaces .
- **S16**：Maurice and Keith meet the mayor's daughter Malicia and introduce her to the talking rats.
- **S17**：Malicia believes that the rat catchers are up to something and so she, Maurice, and Keith break into the rat catchers' hut.
- **S18**：They discover a great deal of food stolen by the men and large cages where the local keekees are being bred for coursing .
- **S19**：The rat catchers return and lock up the humans, taking away the rat leader Hamnpork who had met up with them.
- **S20**：Maurice hides and feels a voice trying to enter his mind, inciting a great sense of fear.
- **S21**：The rats of the Clan feel it too, and many become so afraid that they all but forget how to think and reason.
- **S22**：Dangerous Beans, the rats' spiritual leader, is crushed by the realization that the rats are just mindless animals at heart, and leaves with his assistant Peaches.
- **S23**：Darktan, the rats' trap expert, leads a group to rescue Hamnpork, and succeeds after nearly dying in a trap.
- **S24**：Hamnpork dies of injuries sustained while fighting in the rat coursing pit, and Darktan reluctantly assumes leadership of the Clan.
- **S25**：Malicia and Keith, after freeing themselves, trick the rat catchers into admitting their crimes.
- **S26**：The rat catchers have created a powerful rat king named Spider, which is the source of the mysterious voice that has been plaguing the rats and Maurice.
- **S27**：Using its mental control over the rat catchers, Spider plans to wage war on the humans that created it.
- **S28**：It sends the rats it controls to attack Maurice, Peaches, and Dangerous Beans.
- **S29**：However, Maurice (so terrified that he stops thinking and acts instinctively) pounces on the rat king and destroys it.
- **S30**：In his panic, Maurice also unwittingly kills Dangerous Beans.
- **S31**：Maurice emerges from Spider's chamber carrying the body of Dangerous Beans.
- **S32**：When he is safely out, he collapses and dies.
- **S33**：In ghostly form, he sees the Death of Rats coming for Dangerous Beans and makes a deal with Death : two of his remaining lives in exchange for both his life and the albino rat's.
- **S34**：The rats corral all the keekees and block their ears.
- **S35**：When a real rat piper arrives in town, Keith challenges him to a duel.
- **S36**：The piper plays his magic rat pipe but none of the rats come out.
- **S37**：Keith plays on a trombone and Sardines (an intelligent rat) emerges and dances for the crowd.
- **S38**：Keith is proclaimed the winner, and leads the keekees out of town with the piper.
- **S39**：After the piper leaves, the Clan rats emerge from hiding and tell the humans about the rat catchers' duplicity.
- **S40**：The humans bargain with the rats: if the Clan will keep the keekees out of the town, the rats may stay and live as though they were just smaller humans.
- **S41**：Keith decides to stay behind as Bad Blintz's ceremonial rat piper, while Maurice moves on to find a new scheme.
- **S42**：Characters [ edit ] Maurice – an intelligent cat who leads the group of rats.
- **S43**：He is cunning and manipulative and has a very good (but cynical) understanding of how the world works.
- **S44**：Late in the novel, he guiltily admits that he accidentally ate one of the intelligent rats of the Clan early in their history, and thus became sentient himself.
- **S45**：Malicia – the daughter of Bad Blintz's mayor, perennially obsessed with fairy tales, having adventures, and storytelling.
- **S46**：Keith – called the "stupid-looking kid", Keith is an orphan raised by the Musician's Guild.
- **S47**：He goes along with Maurice's scheme mainly to have something to do, although he is very defensive of his rat family.
- **S48**：Dangerous Beans – an albino rat who has acted as a kind of spiritual leader for the Clan since they became intelligent after eating rubbish from behind Unseen University .
- **S49**：Darktan observes that Dangerous Beans is the Clan's much-needed philosopher, finding and "disarming" dangerous ideas for the group.
- **S50**：For example, Dangerous Beans is the one who they all turn to for answers to questions of rat cannibalism, stealing, and the phenomenon of dreaming .

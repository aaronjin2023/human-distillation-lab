## 角色资料

### Peter Parker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a121e7e75a551c9c` · 独立版本：`profile-e17047f158e6e644`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中他好奇且行动冒进，常先追查兴趣再顾及家庭责任。（行为推断；S7、S10、S12、S15、S16）
- **核心动机**：他试图了解父亲的研究，并在本叔遇害后追捕自己放走的窃贼。（行为推断；S5、S6、S7、S12、S20、S21）
- **价值取舍**：面对桥上危机与城市感染风险，他愿冒险介入以保护公众。（行为推断；S29、S30、S35、S37）
- **决策方式**：追查本叔凶手时，他倾向自行惩治罪犯，并用面具隐藏身份。（行为推断；S21、S22、S23）
- **成长变化**：他从追捕叔叔凶手的义警，转向以蜘蛛侠身份保护市民。（行为推断；S21、S30、S37）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

### Dr. Curt Connors

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-c3e787c575234bdd` · 独立版本：`profile-c23ebab800c9a5a3`

- **性格倾向**：在试药问题上他兼有谨慎与冒险倾向，拒绝他试却亲自试药。（行为推断；S25、S26、S27）
- **核心动机**：他希望恢复失去的手臂，后来又企图让全城居民变成蜥蜴。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S35）
- **价值取舍**：在被要求开展人体试验时，他宁愿失去工作也不冒险伤及无辜。（行为推断；S25、S26）
- **决策方式**：遭解雇后他选择自行试药，后来计划向全城扩散血清。（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27、S35）
- **关系模式**：他查出彼得身份后前往追击，却在入狱后要求来客放过彼得。（摘要直接叙述；S33、S40、S41）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他先拒绝让无辜者承担试验风险，后来却计划把血清扩散给全城。

- **S1**：As a child, Peter Parker discovers his father Richard's study has been ransacked.
- **S2**：His father gathers concealed documents before Peter's parents take him to stay with his Aunt May and Uncle Ben.
- **S3**：His parents leave, but are later killed in a plane crash.
- **S4**：As a teenager, Peter is a student at Midtown Science High School, where he is bullied by Flash Thompson, and is romantically interested in the beautiful Gwen Stacy, the daughter of police captain George Stacy.
- **S5**：At home, Peter discovers his father's documents, and learns he had been working with scientist Dr.
- **S6**：Curt Connors at Oscorp to combine human and animal DNA to cure illnesses.
- **S7**：Peter sneaks into Oscorp to meet Connors, who is pressured by his superior Dr.
- **S8**：Ratha to complete his serum, and cure the terminally ill head of Oscorp Norman Osborn.
- **S9**：Connors wants the cure himself to restore his missing arm.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, Peter breaks into a lab where a "biocable"-a high strength strand of webbing-is created by genetically modified spiders, one of which bites him.
- **S11**：During a subway ride home, a fight reveals that he has developed superhuman speed, strength, the ability to stick to surfaces, and a heightened sense of danger.
- **S12**：Peter finds an algorithm in his father's documents and gives it to Connors−the missing key to his serum.
- **S13**：In school, Peter damages school property during a confrontation with Flash and Ben is forced to work late so that he can pick up Peter.
- **S14**：Ben tells Peter to escort May home that night.
- **S15**：Peter ignores Ben in favor of helping Connors test their serum on a three-legged mouse.
- **S16**：After he returns home, Peter fights with Ben about not picking up May, and Peter leaves.
- **S17**：At a grocery store, the clerk rudely refuses to let Peter buy a drink, and when a thief steals money from the till, Peter lets the thief escape.
- **S18**：Ben, searching the streets for Peter, confronts the thief, but is shot and killed.
- **S19**：Peter responds to the gunshot and finds Ben's body.
- **S20**：After seeing a police sketch of the suspect, Peter realizes it was the thief he let go.
- **S21**：Peter hunts the killer, taking out other criminals as a result and attracting police attention for his vigilante actions.
- **S22**：During one confrontation he falls into an abandoned gym, where a luchador-wrestling poster inspires him to create a mask to hide his identity.
- **S23**：He creates a costume for himself with a spandex suit, and builds wrist-mounted mechanical devices that shoot biocable strands.
- **S24**：Peter later reveals he is the vigilante to Gwen and they kiss.
- **S25**：Seeing that the test mouse has regrown its missing limb using lizard DNA, Ratha demands human trials begin immediately.
- **S26**：Connors refuses to risk innocent lives and is fired.
- **S27**：Desperate, Connors tests the serum on himself and his missing arm regenerates.
- **S28**：He learns that Ratha is going to test the serum on human subjects and goes to stop him, but his body begins to mutate.
- **S29**：By the time he finds Ratha on the Williamsburg Bridge Connors has transformed into a large Lizard creature, tossing cars, including Ratha's, over the side of the bridge.
- **S30**：Peter, now calling himself Spider-Man, manages to save Ratha and other civilians.
- **S31**：Connors retreats into the sewer and reverts to human form.
- **S32**：Peter later meets Connors in his office and suspects he is the Lizard, and later unsuccessfully confronts Connors' Lizard form in the sewers, leaving behind his camera.
- **S33**：Connors learns Peter's identity via the name on the camera and pursues him to Midtown Science High School where they fight.
- **S34**：In response, the police start a manhunt for both Spider-Man and Lizard.
- **S35**：Connors heads to Oscorp, intending to disperse his serum across the city and make everyone into lizards, while Gwen develops an antidote in Oscorp's lab.
- **S36**：The police corner Spider-Man and Captain Stacy discovers that he is Peter, but lets him escape to go save Gwen.
- **S37**：Spider-Man manages to replace Connors' serum with the antidote, reverting Connors to human form, but not before Connors mortally wounds Captain Stacy.
- **S38**：Before his death, Captain Stacy makes Peter promise to stay away from Gwen for her safety.
- **S39**：He initially keeps the promise, but later admits that failed promises are the best kind.
- **S40**：In a post-credits scene, an imprisoned Connors is confronted by an unseen man who asks if Connors told Peter the truth about his father.
- **S41**：He replies, "No", and demands Peter be left alone before the man disappears.{{ref}}

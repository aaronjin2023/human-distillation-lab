## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Disabled Afghan War veteran and private investigator Cormoran Strike sleeps in his office after breaking up with his on-again, off-again aristocratic girlfriend, Charlotte Campbell.
- **S3**：Robin Ellacott, recently engaged to longtime boyfriend Matthew Cunliffe, is mistakenly assigned by a temp agency as Strike's secretary, and their first encounter is very awkward.
- **S4**：Strike is approached by lawyer John Bristow, the adoptive brother of Strike's childhood schoolmate Charlie, to reinvestigate the alleged suicide of his sister, supermodel Lula Landry.
- **S5**：Although unconvinced, Strike takes the case, keen to repay a loan taken from his absentee biological father, rock star Johnny Rokeby.
- **S6**：Finding Robin more resourceful and efficient than he expected, Strike extends her stay.
- **S7**：The two set about interviewing Lula's friends and acquaintances, and learn that she was becoming interested in her black roots and biological family before her death.
- **S8**：A neighbor, Tansy Bestigui, claims to have overheard an argument prior to Lula’s fall from her apartment balcony, but is dismissed as a liar by the police.
- **S9**：Guy Some, a fashion designer and friend, suggests that Lula’s drug addict boyfriend, Evan Duffield, is responsible.
- **S10**：Fellow supermodel Ciara Porter refutes this, saying that Lula made a comment about “leaving everything to her brother” in the day leading up to her death.
- **S11**：Strike and Robin locate a friend of Lula’s from psychiatric treatment, Rochelle, who seems to know more than she lets on.
- **S12**：Robin is forced to relay a message from Charlotte that she has gotten engaged to another man following her breakup with Strike, and comforts him when he gets drunk and mourns the relationship.
- **S13**：After seeing Lula’s apartment, Strike deduces that Tansy had been locked out on the frozen balcony by her abusive husband following a heated argument over her cocaine use at the time of Lula’s death, and was threatened into lying about how she overheard it.
- **S14**：Rochelle is found dead in the Thames, drowned hours after first meeting with Strike; he becomes convinced that she was in contact with the killer.
- **S15**：Robin discovers the identity of Lula’s deceased biological father, a Ghanaian academic, and tracks down Lula’s biological brother Jonah, a soldier in the British army.
- **S16**：From shop assistants’ gossip, they deduce that Lula was trying to meet Jonah the night of her death.
- **S17**：Strike visits Lula’s mother’s house, and discovers that she has left a will hidden in the purse she carried the day she died.
- **S18**：Strike invites John Bristow to his office and accuses him of killing Lula and Rochelle, as well as Charlie, whom everyone believed died after riding his bicycle into a quarry.
- **S19**：Jealous of her success and desperate for handouts to cover up his own embezzlement at work, John hid in Lula’s apartment building before killing her and escaping, engaging Strike in an attempt to bring Lula’s relationship with Jonah to light and frame him for the crime.
- **S20**：Rochelle, who knew Lula was meeting Jonah the night of her death and served as witness for her revised will, was blackmailing John for money.
- **S21**：Realising he has been caught, Bristow tries to stab Strike and frame a crazed former client for his death, only to be subdued after Robin enters the office.
- **S22**：Sometime later, Robin prepares to leave for a new permanent job, and Strike gives her a parting gift, an expensive dress that she tried on during the investigation.
- **S23**：At the last moment, the two agree on an arrangement that will allow her to stay on.
- **S24**：## Characters
- **S25**：[ edit ] Major characters [ edit ] Cormoran Strike : a struggling private investigator .
- **S26**：He has few clients, a large debt, and is obliged by a recent break-up to sleep in his office on Denmark Street .
- **S27**：He lost his leg in the Afghan war .
- **S28**：Robin Ellacott : Strike's temporary secretary.
- **S29**：She has recently moved from Yorkshire with her boyfriend and becomes engaged the night before the novel begins.
- **S30**：She is enthusiastic about detective work, and is very intelligent, competent and resourceful.
- **S31**：Lula Landry (Lula Bristow) : a model who died in a fall three months prior to the events of the novel, which police had determined was suicide.
- **S32**：John Bristow hires Strike to determine the truth.
- **S33**：John Bristow : Strike's client and Lula's adoptive brother.
- **S34**：Charlie Bristow : John Bristow's brother and a boyhood friend of Strike's who died when he fell into a quarry when he was around nine or ten years old.
- **S35**：Charlie was about six years older than Lula Landry (Bristow).
- **S36**：Alison Cresswell : in a relationship with John Bristow.
- **S37**：She is secretary for Tony Landry and Cyprian May in their legal practice.
- **S38**：Tony Landry : Lula and John's maternal uncle.
- **S39**：He disapproved of Lula's lifestyle, and objected to Lula's adoption in the first instance.
- **S40**：He has a difficult relationship with his sister.
- **S41**：Lady Yvette Bristow : Lula and John's adopted mother.
- **S42**：She is terminally ill during the novel and her relations with Lula were strained.
- **S43**：Sir Alec Bristow : Lady Bristow's late husband.
- **S44**：He founded his own electronics company, Albris.
- **S45**：Sir Alec was sterile and could not have children of his own.
- **S46**：He and Lady Bristow adopted three children: John, Charlie, and Lula Bristow.
- **S47**：Lula was adopted when she was four years old, shortly after Charlie's death.
- **S48**：Sir Alec died suddenly from a heart attack.
- **S49**：Cyprian May : a senior partner at the law firm where John Bristow works.
- **S50**：Ursula May (Chillingham) : Tansy Bestigui's sister and Cyprian May's wife.
- **S51**：Lula's social circle [ edit ] Evan Duffield : Lula's on-off boyfriend, an actor with documented drug problems.
- **S52**：The media called him the suspect, but he has an alibi.
- **S53**：Rochelle Onifade : a homeless friend of Lula's, whom she met in an outpatient clinic.
- **S54**：Guy Somé (Kevin Owusu) : a fashion designer with a close (though platonic) relationship with Lula.
- **S55**：He is the one who calls her 'Cuckoo'.
- **S56**：Deeby Macc : an American rapper who was scheduled to arrive and stay in the apartment below Lula's in Kentigern Gardens on the night of her death.
- **S57**：Kieran Kolovas-Jones : Lula's personal driver who has aspirations as an actor.
- **S58**：Ciara Porter : a model, and a friend of Lula's.
- **S59**：Freddie Bestigui : a film producer and neighbour of Lula's.
- **S60**：He and his wife Tansy are in the process of a divorce.
- **S61**：Tansy Bestigui (Chillingham) : Freddie's wife who claims to have overheard events on the night of Lula's death.
- **S62**：She is the sister of Ursula May.
- **S63**：Bryony Radford : Lula's personal makeup artist and one of the people she meets on the day of her death.
- **S64**：Lula's biological family [ edit ] Marlene Higson : Lula's biological mother, much poorer than Lula's adoptive family.
- **S65**：She had two sons after Lula, but both were taken away by social services .
- **S66**：Dr Joseph 'Joe' Agyeman : Lula's biological father, an academic specialising in African and Ghanaian politics.
- **S67**：He died five years before the events of the novel.

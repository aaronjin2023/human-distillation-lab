## 角色资料

### Leo Biederman

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-e8b8673a1c24aeb2` · 独立版本：`profile-c4d874e2fd975e74`

- **性格倾向**：灾难威胁下倾向主动承担责任，不轻易放弃亲近者（行为推断；S15、S17、S18）
- **核心动机**：希望让女友及其家人获得避难资格，主动以婚姻相助（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **价值取舍**：已有避难机会时仍倾向重视伴侣安危而非独自保命（行为推断；S14、S17）
- **决策方式**：正式撤离失灵后倾向转向亲自寻找与就地避险（行为推断；S17、S18、S22）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：个人获救资格无法直接解决女友全家的生存困境。

### Jenny Lerner

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a77070282cd82882` · 独立版本：`profile-f2f27fb28d4f95e7`

- **性格倾向**：调查隐情时表现出追根究底、不满足于表面解释的倾向（行为推断；S3、S4、S5）
- **决策方式**：调查中倾向追索关键称谓的真实含义以揭开隐情（行为推断；S3、S4）
- **价值取舍**：最后撤离时倾向把母女共同生存置于个人获救之前（行为推断；S19）
- **关系模式**：生命终点主动修复与疏远父亲的关系，共享往日回忆（摘要直接叙述；S20、S23）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：拥有撤离资格，却选择让位并回到父亲身边。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：On May 10, 1998, teenage amateur astronomer Leo Biederman  discovers an unusual object near the stars Mizar and Alcor at a star party in Richmond, Virginia.
- **S2**：He alerts astronomer Marcus Wolf , who realizes that the object is a comet on a collision course with Earth, but dies in a car accident before he can alert the world.
- **S3**：A year later, MSNBC reporter Jenny Lerner  investigates the resignation of the Secretary of the Treasury  and his connection to an "Ellie".
- **S4**：She discovers that Ellie is not a mistress but an acronym: "E.L.E.", for "Extinction-Level Event".
- **S5**：Because of Lerner's investigation, President Tom Beck  advances the announcement of the grim facts: The comet, named Wolf-Biederman, is {{convert}} wide, large enough to destroy all life if it strikes Earth.
- **S6**：The United States and Russia have been secretly constructing a spacecraft, the Messiah, in orbit.
- **S7**：They plan to use it to transport a team lead by Captain Spurgeon Tanner  to the comet, so that it can be destroyed with nuclear weapons.
- **S8**：After landing on the comet, the crew members plant nuclear bombs 100 meters beneath the surface.
- **S9**：When the bombs are detonated, the ship is damaged and loses contact with Earth.
- **S10**：Instead of being destroyed, the comet splits into two smaller rocks nicknamed "Biederman"  and "Wolf" , both world-threatening.
- **S11**：Beck announces the Messiah crew’s failure, declares martial law, and reveals that governments worldwide have been building underground shelters.
- **S12**：The United States' shelter is in the limestone caves of Missouri.
- **S13**：The US government conducts a lottery to select 800,000 ordinary Americans aged 50 and under to join 200,000 pre-selected scientists, engineers, teachers, artists, soldiers, and officials.
- **S14**：Lerner and Leo's families are pre-selected, but Leo's girlfriend Sarah Hotchner  is not.
- **S15**：Leo marries Sarah to save her family but the Hotchners are mistakenly left off the evacuee list; Sarah refuses to leave without them.
- **S16**：A last-ditch effort to use Earth's missile-borne nuclear weapons to deflect the two chunks of the comet fails.
- **S17**：Leo returns home looking for Sarah, but her family has left for the Appalachian Mountains and is trapped in a traffic jam on the highway.
- **S18**：Sarah's parents urge Leo to take Sarah and her baby brother to high ground; Sarah still does not want to abandon her parents, but they convince her to let them go.
- **S19**：Lerner gives up her seat in the last evacuation helicopter to her friend Beth, who has a young daughter.
- **S20**：She instead joins her estranged father  at her childhood beach house, where they reconcile and remember happier times.
- **S21**：The Biederman fragment impacts in the Atlantic Ocean near Cape Hatteras, North Carolina, creating a megatsunami.
- **S22**：Leo, Sarah and her baby brother survive.
- **S23**：Lerner, her father, Sarah's parents, and millions along the Atlantic coasts of North and South America, Europe, and Africa perish.
- **S24**：The world braces for the impact of Wolf in western Canada, which will create a cloud of dust that will block out the sun for two years.
- **S25**：This, in turn, will destroy all remaining life on the surface.
- **S26**：Low on fuel and life support, the crew of the Messiah decides to undertake a suicide mission with the remaining nuclear warheads.
- **S27**：After saying goodbye to their loved ones by video conference, the ship reaches the fragment and enters a fissure to blow itself up, which breaks Wolf into much smaller pieces, which burn up in Earth's atmosphere.
- **S28**：President Beck, speaking to a large crowd in front of the United States Capitol building, which is undergoing reconstruction, urges the nation and the world to continue their recovery.

### Wolverine

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-01d9a86487649ef7` · 独立版本：`profile-653ad5c28eeb38b5`

- **性格倾向**：在校园遭袭时倾向强硬反击，主动承担抵抗危险的角色（行为推断；S8、S9）
- **核心动机**：在与斯崔克对峙时试图了解自己的过去，寻求身世线索（行为推断；S10、S29）

### Jean Grey

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-e42ccf8f1c3efafd` · 独立版本：`profile-567320445c6a9662`

- **性格倾向**：在集体濒临覆灭时倾向挺身承担危险，而非等待救援（行为推断；S37、S38）
- **核心动机**：参与寻找夜行者并读取记忆，旨在查明威胁的来源（行为推断；S4、S22）
- **价值取舍**：在洪水危机中把同伴生存置于自己的生命安全之前（行为推断；S37、S38）
- **决策方式**：面对飞机失灵时同时阻挡洪水与启动引擎以促成撤离（行为推断；S37、S38）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### William Stryker

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-fb35830b21db2fed` · 独立版本：`profile-a145a7ce397dda87`

- **性格倾向**：在推进灭绝计划时呈现强烈控制倾向，依赖操纵他人（行为推断；S21、S23、S24）
- **核心动机**：计划借第二套脑波装置杀死世界范围内的变种人（摘要直接叙述；S22、S24）
- **价值取舍**：在实施计划时将变种人的自主与生命视为可牺牲代价（行为推断；S21、S24）
- **决策方式**：倾向结合诱捕与精神控制，让被控制者执行杀戮计划（行为推断；S7、S21、S23、S24）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：操控他人实施灭绝，却在自身遇险时试图以往事换取生路。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Nightcrawler, a teleporting mutant, attempts to assassinate the President of the United States in the White House, but he fails and escapes after being shot by a Secret Service agent.
- **S2**：Wolverine heads to Alkali Lake but finds nothing left of the base.
- **S3**：Logan returns to the school, reunited with Rogue, her boyfriend Iceman, Cyclops, Storm, and Jean Grey.
- **S4**：He is requested by Professor Xavier to watch over the children at the school, while Storm and Jean find Nightcrawler with the help of the professor and Cerebro.
- **S5**：Cyclops and the Professor visit Magneto in his plastic prison to see if he had any part in the attack on the President.
- **S6**：Reading Magneto's mind, the Professor discovers that a covert government operative, William Stryker, has been extracting information from Magneto.
- **S7**：A trap is sprung and Cyclops and the Professor are captured by Stryker and his assistant Yuriko Oyama.
- **S8**：A military raid of the X-Mansion begins, with the soldiers sedating every student they find.
- **S9**：However, the plan backfires when Wolverine strikes back and kills a large number of Stryker's men, while Colossus, Rogue, Iceman, Pyro, and most of the students escape through a series of hidden tunnels in the school.
- **S10**：Wolverine confronts Stryker, who fails to shed any light to his past.
- **S11**：Iceman returns, saves Wolverine by creating a wall of ice between him and Stryker, and they both escape through one of the tunnels.
- **S12**：Stryker's soldiers had already succeeded in sedating six students and managed to break inside Cerebro.
- **S13**：Impersonating Senator Robert Kelly and Yuriko, Mystique gains information about Magneto's prison and provides a means for him to escape.
- **S14**：Wolverine, along with Rogue, Iceman and Pyro head to Iceman's  parents' home in Boston.
- **S15**：After a 9-1-1 call from Bobby's brother Ronny, the police arrive just as the group is about to leave.
- **S16**：Pyro uses his fire manipulation powers to fend off the police, but gets carried away and is prevented from causing further damage by Rogue.
- **S17**：The X-Jet arrives to pick them up, and they are quickly targeted by two Air Force fighter jets.
- **S18**：They manage to force the pilots to eject with the help of Storm and Jean Grey; however, they are hit by a missile which threatens to crash them.
- **S19**：Only due to the sudden interference of Magneto do they survive.
- **S20**：The X-Men then team up with Magneto and Mystique.
- **S21**：Magneto has learned Stryker orchestrated the attack on the President and has been experimenting on mutants, using a drug injected directly into the back of the neck to control them.
- **S22**：Jean reads Nightcrawler's mind and determines that Stryker's base is located at Alkali Lake, inside the dam where he plans to kill the world's mutants by building a second Cerebro.
- **S23**：Through his son, Jason, Stryker gains control over the Professor.
- **S24**：His son is able to project powerful visions in the mind, blinding a person to reality, and through this the Professor is brainwashed to use Cerebro to find and kill all mutants.
- **S25**：Mystique is able to infiltrate Stryker's base by impersonating Wolverine as the X-Men followed.
- **S26**：Storm and Nightcrawler search for the kidnapped students.
- **S27**：Jean, Magneto, and Mystique are attacked by a brainwashed Cyclops while trying to rescue the Professor and in the process caused damage to the generators that keep the dam from collapsing.
- **S28**：The force of Jean's telekinetic blast awakens Cyclops from his brainwashing, and Wolverine simultaneously finds Stryker in an adamantium smelting room along with Yuriko, who is revealed to be Lady Deathstrike.
- **S29**：Wolverine manages to defeat Deathstrike and then finds Stryker on a landing pad, where Stryker attempts to bargain Wolverine for his life with stories of his past.
- **S30**：Storm and Nightcrawler find the children and break them out of their cell.
- **S31**：Magneto and Mystique managed to kill the rest of Stryker's men by setting off their grenades, which causes a leak in the dam, and Magneto stops Jason and the Professor before the mutants are killed.
- **S32**：While disguised as Stryker, Mystique uses Jason to convince the Professor to kill all humans; she and Magneto, along with their new initiate, Pyro, then use Stryker's helicopter to escape Alkali Lake, chaining Stryker to concrete rubble.
- **S33**：Meanwhile, Nightcrawler teleports Storm inside of Cerebro where she creates a snowstorm to free the Professor from his telepathic illusion.
- **S34**：They all then flee the base as water engulfs it, only to discover that Magneto, Mystique, and Pyro - who had earlier been convinced by Magneto to join them - had escaped on the helicopter.
- **S35**：Fortunately, Iceman and Rogue arrive with the X-Jet and everyone gets on board.
- **S36**：The dam bursts completely, flooding the landscape and killing Stryker.
- **S37**：A malfunction aboard the X-Jet prevents it from taking off; Jean sacrifices herself by leaving the jet and creating a telekinetic wall as a shield against the flood.
- **S38**：She activates the X-Jet's primary engines before releasing the torrent of water down on herself, presumably killing her.
- **S39**：The X-Men are able to supply the President with files from Stryker's private offices, and the Professor warns him that humans and mutants must work together to build peace or they will destroy each other through war.
- **S40**：The next scene returns to the school, with Professor Xavier, Cyclops, and Wolverine talking about Jean's past.
- **S41**：They are then interrupted by Colossus and a large number of the students who are supposed to be in their next class.
- **S42**：While they leave, Wolverine tells Cyclops that Jean made a choice and that it was him, which relieves Cyclops a good deal.
- **S43**：The film ends with a voice-over by Jean Grey on the process of evolution .
- **S44**：The camera floats over Alkali Lake, showing a vague shape of a 'Phoenix' flying above it, hinting at her survival.

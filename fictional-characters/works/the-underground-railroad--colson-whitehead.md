## 角色资料

### Cora

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6e8966c481409a05` · 独立版本：`profile-54e7d116671f8158`

- **性格倾向**：在反复被捕与失去庇护后，仍表现出顽强求生倾向（行为推断；S23、S25、S30、S31、S32、S34）
- **核心动机**：逃离奴役与持续追捕，是她不断寻找出路的核心目标（行为推断；S8、S16、S32、S34）
- **决策方式**：面对出逃风险起初迟疑，处境恶化后才转向冒险行动（摘要直接叙述；S7、S8）
- **情绪反应**：对母亲独自逃离并留下自己，抱有明确的怨恨情绪（摘要直接叙述；S5、S6）
- **关系模式**：与Royal建立恋情时，童年遭受的性侵使她仍然迟疑（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **价值取舍**：在逃亡遭遇攻击时，倾向优先保全自己与同伴生命（行为推断；S9、S10）

暂无支持：沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望亲密关系，却因童年性侵经历对恋情保持迟疑。；追求逃生，却因自卫杀人而失去被捕后获宽待的可能。

### Ridgeway

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-c7f3d08afd4ea50f` · 独立版本：`profile-83fa69e23242ac27`

- **性格倾向**：在追捕接连受阻后，仍表现出执拗追索目标的倾向（行为推断；S12、S23、S25、S31）
- **核心动机**：追捕Cora与Caesar，主要为了报复未能抓回Mabel的失败（摘要直接叙述；S12）
- **价值取舍**：在执行追捕时，倾向维护奴隶主对逃亡者的支配权（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **决策方式**：掌握俘虏后，倾向以强迫方式获取逃亡网络的位置（行为推断；S31）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] The book alternates between the perspective of the lead character, Cora, and chapters told from a different character's perspective.
- **S3**：The featured characters are Ajarry, Cora's grandmother; Ridgeway, a slave catcher ; Stevens, a South Carolina doctor conducting a social experiment; Ethel, the wife of a North Carolina station agent; Caesar, a fellow enslaved person who escapes the plantation with Cora; and Mabel, Cora's mother.
- **S4**：The chapter locations are: Georgia , South Carolina, North Carolina, Tennessee , Indiana , and (an undefined) "North".
- **S5**：Cora is an enslaved person on a plantation in Georgia and an outcast after her mother, Mabel, ran off without her.
- **S6**：She resents Mabel for abandoning her.
- **S7**：Caesar approaches Cora about a plan to flee.
- **S8**：Reluctant at first, she eventually agrees as her situation with her master and fellow slaves worsens.
- **S9**：During their escape, they encounter a group of slave catchers, who capture Cora's young friend Lovey.
- **S10**：Cora is forced to kill a twelve-year-old boy to protect herself and Caesar, eliminating any possibility of merciful treatment should she be recaptured.
- **S11**：With the help of an inexperienced abolitionist , Cora and Caesar find a spur of the Underground Railroad, which is operated as a literal underground train system that runs throughout the south, transporting runaways northwards.
- **S12**：They take a train to South Carolina. [ 3 ] Upon learning of their escape, Ridgeway begins a hunt for the pair, mainly in revenge for Mabel, who is the only escapee he has ever failed to capture.
- **S13**：Cora and Caesar have taken up comfortable residence in South Carolina under assumed names.
- **S14**：South Carolina is enacting a program where the government owns formerly enslaved people but employs them, provides medical treatment, and gives them communal housing.
- **S15**：The two enjoy their time there and put off the decision to leave until Cora learns of plans to sterilize black women and use black men as test subjects in an experiment to track the spread and degenerative effects of syphilis .
- **S16**：Ridgeway arrives before the two can leave, and Cora is forced to return to the Railroad alone.
- **S17**：She later learns that Caesar was killed by an angry mob after having been caught and jailed by Ridgeway.
- **S18**：Cora eventually arrives at a closed-down station in North Carolina.
- **S19**：She is found by Martin, the son of the station's former operator.
- **S20**：North Carolina has recently decided to abolish slavery, using indentured servants instead, and violently executes any runaway slaves found in the state (as well as some freedmen ).
- **S21**：Terrified of what the North Carolinians might do to an abolitionist, Martin hides Cora in his attic for several months.
- **S22**：Cora becomes ill and is reluctantly treated by Martin's wife, Ethel.
- **S23**：While Cora is down from the attic, a raid is conducted on the house, and Ridgeway recaptures her while the townspeople execute Martin and Ethel.
- **S24**：Ridgeway takes Cora back toward Georgia, detouring through Tennessee to return another enslaved person to his owner.
- **S25**：While stopped in Tennessee, Ridgeway's traveling party is attacked by the free-born Royal and two escaped enslaved people, who release Cora.
- **S26**：Cora and Royal travel to a farm in Indiana owned by a free black man named Valentine.
- **S27**：The farm is populated by several freedmen and escapees, living and working in harmony.
- **S28**：Royal, an operator on the Railroad, begins a romantic relationship with Cora, although she remains hesitant because of a rape by other slaves in her childhood.
- **S29**：A small faction of freedmen oppose harboring escaped slaves, fearing that their presence could ruin their peaceful lives.
- **S30**：Eventually, the farm is burned, and many people, including Royal, are killed in a raid by white Hoosiers .
- **S31**：Ridgeway recaptures Cora and forces her to take him to a closed-down Railroad station nearby.
- **S32**：When they arrive, she pushes him down a flight of stairs, severely injuring him.
- **S33**：When last seen, he whispers thoughts on the "American imperative" to Homer, who writes them in his journal.
- **S34**：Cora then runs off down the tracks.
- **S35**：Eventually, she emerges from the underground to find a caravan traveling West.
- **S36**：She is given a ride by one of the wagons' black drivers. [ 4 ]

## 角色资料

### Harry Stamper

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-b671bed0c629751c` · 独立版本：`profile-038099f81c88688e`

- **性格倾向**：在任务受挫时倾向亲自扛责，并以强硬方式保护队员。（行为推断；S20、S27）
- **核心动机**：接受任务并召集钻井队，目标是阻止小行星撞击地球。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S8）
- **决策方式**：当遥控引爆将使任务失败时，他说服指挥官解除炸弹武装。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **关系模式**：他把A.J.视作儿子般的家人，并认可他与Grace的关系。（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **价值取舍**：在必须有人手动引爆时，他代替被抽中的A.J.留下牺牲。（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：起初为拆散女儿与A.J.而带Grace回NASA，后来认可二人婚事。

### A.J. Frost

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-9526f8a4d497c806` · 独立版本：`profile-d0e560779c0f9269`

- **性格倾向**：在此任务中表现出临危坚持，坠毁后仍赶来协助完成钻探。（行为推断；S23）
- **核心动机**：接受任务加入钻井队，参与阻止小行星撞击地球。（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9）
- **决策方式**：抽签选中留下手动引爆后，他准备与Harry一同离开气闸。（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26、S27）
- **关系模式**：他与Grace相恋，Harry最终认可他成为女婿。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S27、S32）
- **价值取舍**：在需要有人留下引爆时，他接受抽签结果承担风险。（行为推断；S25、S26）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他被抽中留下引爆，Harry却强行将他送回舱内。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A massive meteor shower destroys the Space Shuttle Atlantis and bombards New York City, the East Coast, and Finland.
- **S2**：NASA discovers that a Texas-sized asteroid will collide with Earth in 18 days, creating another extinction event.
- **S3**：The asteroid is unofficially named "Dottie", after the wife of the astronomer who initially discovered it.
- **S4**：NASA scientists, led by Dan Truman  plan to bury a nuclear device deep inside the asteroid that, when detonated, will split the asteroid in two, driving them apart so that they both fly safely past the Earth.
- **S5**：NASA contacts Harry Stamper , considered the best deep-sea oil driller in the world, for assistance and advice.
- **S6**：Harry returns to NASA along with his daughter Grace  to keep her away from her new boyfriend, one of Harry's young and rambunctious drillers, A.J.
- **S7**：Frost .
- **S8**：Harry and Grace learn about the asteroid and Harry explains he will need his team, including A.J., to carry out the mission.
- **S9**：Once they have been rounded up and the situation is explained, they agree to help, but only after their list of unusual rewards and demands are met.
- **S10**：As NASA puts Harry and his crew through a short and rigorous astronaut training program, Harry and his team re-outfit the mobile drillers, named the "Armadillos", that will be used on the asteroid.
- **S11**：When a large fragment from the asteroid wipes out Shanghai, NASA is forced to reveal its plans to the world and launches two military space shuttles, named Freedom and Independence.
- **S12**：Once in orbit, the shuttles dock with the Russian space station Mir manned by Lev Andropov  to refuel.
- **S13**：A fire breaks out during the transfer and the station is evacuated just before it explodes, with Lev and A.
- **S14**：J. making a narrow escape.
- **S15**：The shuttles slingshot around the Moon in order to land on the backside of the asteroid.
- **S16**：Traveling through the asteroid's debris field Independences hull is punctured and crashes onto the rock.
- **S17**：Grace, watching from NASA headquarters, is distraught by A.J.'s apparent death.
- **S18**：Freedom lands safely, but misses the target area by 26 miles, so the team must now drill through an area of compressed iron ferrite rather than the planned softer stone.
- **S19**：When they fall significantly behind schedule and communications are about to fail, the military initiates "Secondary Protocol"; to remote detonate the nuclear weapon on the asteroid's surface, which apparently will not have any effect.
- **S20**：While Truman delays the military at Mission Control, Harry persuades the shuttle commander to disarm the bomb so they can complete the drilling.
- **S21**：Distracted by "Rockhound" , who is having a mental breakdown, the Freedom crew loses their Armadillo when it strikes a gas pocket and is blown into space.
- **S22**：World panic ensues as the mission is assumed lost, just as another meteorite destroys Paris.
- **S23**：Suddenly, A.J., Lev, and "Bear" , having survived the Independence crash, arrive in time to complete the drilling.
- **S24**：As the asteroid approaches the Earth, it heats up, causing a dangerous rock storm that damages the bomb's remote trigger.
- **S25**：They realize that someone must stay behind to detonate it manually.
- **S26**：After all the non-flight crew volunteers, they draw straws, and A.J. is selected.
- **S27**：As he and Harry exit the airlock, Harry rips off A.J.'s air hose and shoves him back inside, telling him that he is the son he never had and he would be proud to have him marry Grace.
- **S28**：Harry prepares to detonate the bomb and contacts Grace to say his last goodbyes.
- **S29**：After the Freedom moves to a safe distance, Harry pushes the button at the last minute  and his life passes before his eyes as the asteroid is destroyed.
- **S30**：It breaks in two and both halves fly past the Earth.
- **S31**：Freedom lands, and the surviving crew are treated as heroes.
- **S32**：The film ends with A.J. and Grace's wedding, complete with photos of Harry and the other lost crew members present in memoriam.

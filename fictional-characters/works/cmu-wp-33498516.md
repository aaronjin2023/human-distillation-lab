## 角色资料

### Andrew Detmer

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a7f721b8a2453819` · 独立版本：`profile-718a3d8b17ea2297`

- **性格倾向**：在受辱与被约束的情境中倾向退缩后反击，逐渐难以容忍干预（行为推断；S19、S20、S26、S27、S36）
- **核心动机**：既渴望获得同龄人的接纳，也在母亲病重时急切寻求救治资源（行为推断；S16、S17、S18、S29）
- **价值取舍**：逐渐以顶级捕食者自居，试图为伤害弱者免除道德负担（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **情绪反应**：对朋友之死公开否认责任，私下却在墓前表现出负疚（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾在史蒂夫墓前请求原谅，后来却宣称强者伤人无需内疚。

### Matt

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-dd3f37faace3a090` · 独立版本：`profile-02a48f88bf5bdf74`

- **性格倾向**：在朋友失控时倾向先劝阻和救助，尽力保留沟通可能（行为推断；S13、S34、S35、S36）
- **价值取舍**：认为力量应受到约束，尤其不能任意伤害有生命的对象（摘要直接叙述；S13）
- **决策方式**：在劝解无效且公众受威胁时，最终选择以致命手段阻止朋友（摘要直接叙述；S36、S38、S39）
- **关系模式**：即使亲手结束表弟的生命，仍通过完成其旅行愿望保存联结（行为推断；S4、S15、S39、S41、S43）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：坚持限制力量伤人，最终却必须用力量杀死亲近的朋友。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Seattle teenager Andrew Detmer  starts videotaping his life.
- **S2**：His mother Karen  is slowly dying from cancer and his alcoholic father Richard ([[Michael Kelly  verbally and physically abuses him.
- **S3**：At school, Andrew is unpopular and frequently bullied.
- **S4**：Andrew's cousin Matt ([[Alex Russell  invites him to a party to help him meet people, but Andrew's filming angers an attendee and Andrew leaves despondent.
- **S5**：Andrew is persuaded by popular student Steve  to record something strange he and Matt have found in the woods: a hole in the ground emitting a loud strange noise.
- **S6**：The three enter the hole and discover a large glowing blue crystalline object.
- **S7**：The object glows red and the group is stricken by nosebleeds and pain.
- **S8**：The camera cuts out.
- **S9**：Weeks later Andrew records himself, Matt, and Steve as they display telekinetic abilities, able to move objects with their minds, but bleeding from their noses when they overexert themselves.
- **S10**：They return to the hole, but find that it has collapsed and that the sheriff's department is sealing off the area.
- **S11**：As their abilities grow more powerful, Matt theorizes that they function like a muscle, becoming stronger with use.
- **S12**：The trio develop a close friendship and begin employing their abilities to play pranks.
- **S13**：However, after Andrew pushes a rude motorist off the road and into a river, Matt insists that they restrict the use of their powers, particularly against living creatures.
- **S14**：The trio later discover that they can use their powers to fly.
- **S15**：They agree to fly around the world together after graduation, with Andrew expressing an interest in visiting Tibet because of its peaceful nature.
- **S16**：Steve encourages Andrew to enter the school talent show to gain popularity.
- **S17**：Andrew amazes his fellow students by disguising his powers as impressive feats.
- **S18**：That night, Andrew, Matt and Steve celebrate at a house party, where Andrew is the center of attention.
- **S19**：After drinking with his classmate Monica , she and Andrew go upstairs to have sex, but Andrew vomits on Monica, humiliating himself.
- **S20**：Andrew becomes increasingly withdrawn and hostile, culminating when his father attacks him and Andrew uses his power to overcome him.
- **S21**：His outburst is so extreme that it inflicts psychically connected nosebleeds and pain on Steve and Matt.
- **S22**：Steve is drawn to Andrew, who is floating in the middle of a storm.
- **S23**：Steve tries to console him, but Andrew grows increasingly angry until Steve is suddenly struck by lightning and killed.
- **S24**：At Steve's funeral, Matt confronts Andrew about the suspicious circumstances of Steve's death.
- **S25**：Andrew denies responsibility to Matt, but he privately begs forgiveness at Steve's grave.
- **S26**：Andrew grows distant from Matt and again finds himself ostracized at school.
- **S27**：After being bullied, he psychically tears his wisdom teeth from the bully's mouth.
- **S28**：Andrew begins to identify himself as an apex predator, rationalizing that he should not feel guilt for using his power to hurt those weaker than himself.
- **S29**：When his mother's condition deteriorates, Andrew uses his powers to steal money for her medicine.
- **S30**：After mugging some local thugs, he robs a gas station where he inadvertently causes an explosion that puts him in the hospital with severe burns and under police investigation.
- **S31**：At his bedside, his father informs the unconscious Andrew that his mother has died, and he angrily blames Andrew for her death.
- **S32**：As his father is about to strike him, Andrew awakens and the outer wall of his hospital room explodes.
- **S33**：Elsewhere Matt experiences a nosebleed and senses Andrew is in trouble.
- **S34**：He sees a news report about the hospital explosion and travels there, finding Andrew floating outside the building.
- **S35**：Andrew drops his father, who is saved by Matt, and proceeds to wreak havoc with his powers.
- **S36**：Matt confronts Andrew at the Space Needle and tries to reason with him, but Andrew grows hostile and irrational at any attempt to control him.
- **S37**：Andrew attacks Matt and the pair fight across the city, crashing through buildings and hurling vehicles.
- **S38**：Injured and enraged, Andrew uses his power to destroy the buildings around him, threatening hundreds of lives.
- **S39**：Unable to get through to Andrew and left with no other choice, Matt submits himself to his darker side, tears a spear from a nearby statue, and impales Andrew with it, killing him.
- **S40**：The police surround Matt, but he flies away.
- **S41**：Three months later, Matt lands in Tibet with Andrew’s camera.
- **S42**：Speaking to the camera while addressing Andrew, Matt vows to use his powers for good and to find out what happened to them in the hole.
- **S43**：Matt positions the camera to view a Tibetan monastery in the distance and says "You made it" before flying away, leaving the camera behind to continue recording the tranquil scene.

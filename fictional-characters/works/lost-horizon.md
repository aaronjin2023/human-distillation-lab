### Hugh Conway

- **性格倾向**：在香格里拉的处境中，他倾向于沉思权衡并亲近静修学术生活。（行为推断；S18、S29、S30、S34）

- **成长变化**：他从满意留居转为离谷，后来在拉瑟福看来似乎又准备返回。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S35、S37）

## 材料限制

- 框架叙事只称康威似乎准备返回，未确认他已重返香格里拉；老年女子是洛珍也仅属暗示。
- 未保留巴纳德：材料主要提供留谷动机与同一个决定，不能据此凑出四个有意义的不同维度。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] The prologue and epilogue are a frame story narrated by a neurologist.
- **S3**：This neurologist and a novelist friend, Rutherford, are given dinner at Tempelhof , Berlin , by their old school-friend Wyland, a secretary at the British embassy.
- **S4**：A chance remark by a passing airman brings up the topic of Hugh Conway, a British consul in Afghanistan , who disappeared under odd circumstances.
- **S5**：Later in the evening, Rutherford reveals to the neurologist that, after the disappearance, he discovered Conway in a French mission hospital in Chung-Kiang (probably Chongqing ), China , suffering from amnesia .
- **S6**：Conway recovered his memory, told Rutherford his story (which Rutherford recorded in a manuscript), and then slipped away again.
- **S7**：Rutherford gives the neurologist his manuscript, which becomes the heart of the novel.
- **S8**：In May 1931, during the British Raj in India, the 80 White residents of Baskul are being evacuated to Peshawar because of a revolution.
- **S9**：In the aeroplane of the Maharajah of Chandrapur are: Conway, the British consul, aged 37; Charles Mallinson, his young vice-consul; an American, Henry D.
- **S10**：Barnard; and a British missionary, Miss Roberta Brinklow.
- **S11**：The plane is hijacked and flown instead over the mountains to Tibet .
- **S12**：After a crash landing, the Tibetan pilot dies, but not before telling the four (in "a kind of Chinese", which Conway barely understands) to seek shelter at the nearby lamasery of Shangri-La.
- **S13**：The location is unclear, but Conway believes the plane has "progressed far beyond the western range of the Himalayas " towards the lesser known heights of the Kuen-Lun mountain range.
- **S14**：The four are taken there by a party directed by Chang, a postulant at the lamasery who speaks English.
- **S15**：The lamasery has modern conveniences, like central heating, bathtubs from Akron, Ohio , a large library, a grand piano , a harpsichord , and food from the fertile valley below.
- **S16**：Towering above is Karakal, literally translated as "Blue Moon," a mountain more than 28,000 feet high.
- **S17**：Mallinson is keen to hire porters and leave, but Chang politely puts him off.
- **S18**：The others eventually decide they are content to stay: Miss Brinklow because she wants to teach the people a sense of sin; Barnard because he is really Chalmers Bryant (wanted by the police for stock fraud) and because he is keen to develop the gold mines in the valley; and Conway because the contemplative scholarly life suits him.
- **S19**：A seemingly young Manchu woman, Lo-Tsen, is another postulant at the lamasery.
- **S20**：She does not speak English, but plays the harpsichord.
- **S21**：Mallinson falls in love with her, as does Conway, though more languidly.
- **S22**：Conway is given an audience with the High Lama, an unheard-of honour.
- **S23**：He learns that the lamasery was constructed in its present form in the early eighteenth century by a Catholic monk named Perrault from Luxembourg .
- **S24**：The lamasery has since then been joined by others who have found their way into the valley.
- **S25**：Once they have done so, their ageing slows; if they then leave the valley, they age quickly and die.
- **S26**：Conway guesses correctly that the High Lama is Perrault, now 250 years old.
- **S27**：In a later audience, the High Lama reveals that he is finally dying, and that he wants Conway to lead the lamasery.
- **S28**：The High Lama then dies.
- **S29**：Conway contemplates the events.
- **S30**：Hours after the High Lama dies, Conway is outside still pondering the events while in the moonlight.
- **S31**：Mallinson then grabs him by the arm and tells Conway he has arranged to leave the valley with porters and Lo-Tsen.
- **S32**：Barnard and Brinklow have decided to stay.
- **S33**：The porters and Lo-Tsen are waiting for him five kilometres outside the valley, but he cannot traverse the dangerous route alone, so he convinces Conway to go along and assist him.
- **S34**：Conway is caught, divided between the two worlds.
- **S35**：Ultimately, because of his love for the boy, he decides to join Mallinson.
- **S36**：This ends Rutherford's manuscript.
- **S37**：The last time Rutherford saw Conway, it appeared he was preparing to make his way back to Shangri-La.
- **S38**：Rutherford completes his account by telling the neurologist that he attempted to track Conway and verify some of his claims of Shangri-La.
- **S39**：He found the Chung-Kiang doctor who had treated Conway.
- **S40**：The doctor said Conway had been brought in by a Chinese woman, who was ill and died soon after.
- **S41**：She was old, the doctor had told Rutherford, "Most old of anyone I have ever seen," implying that it was Lo-Tsen, aged drastically by her departure from Shangri-La.
- **S42**：The narrator wonders whether Conway can find his way back to his lost paradise.

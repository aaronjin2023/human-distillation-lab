### Cole Sear

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-42399e825aa6af15` · 独立版本：`profile-1bc885601866af03`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他虽害怕鬼魂，仍决定尝试与他们沟通。（行为推断；S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：接受建议并帮助凯拉的举动，显示他开始愿意以能力帮助亡者。（行为推断；S18、S19、S23、S24）
- **决策方式**：他找到鬼魂凯拉生前的住处，并把关键录像交给她父亲。（摘要直接叙述；S21、S23、S24）
- **关系模式**：他向母亲坦白秘密，并转述外祖母对母亲心中疑问的回答。（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26、S27）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Anna Crowe

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-801c5065a4f1aa3a` · 独立版本：`profile-0acbb360dada2ffb`

- **性格倾向**：在婚姻情境中，她对丈夫过度投入工作所带来的冷落较敏感。（行为推断；S3）
- **沟通方式**：丈夫遇害前，她直接向他指出工作排在所有事情之前。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S7）

- **关系模式**：她已失去丈夫，克劳误以为的拒绝交谈发生在他死后。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S30）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Dr.
- **S2**：Malcolm Crowe , a child psychologist in Philadelphia, returns home one night with his wife, Anna Crowe , after having been honored for his work.
- **S3**：Anna tells Crowe that everything is second to his work.
- **S4**：The two then discover that they are not alone; a young man appears brandishing a gun.
- **S5**：He says that he does not want to be afraid anymore and accuses Crowe of failing him.
- **S6**：Crowe recognizes him as Vincent Gray , a former patient whom he treated as a child for hallucinations.
- **S7**：Gray shoots Crowe in the abdomen, and seconds later kills himself with the gun.
- **S8**：The next autumn, Crowe begins working with another patient, nine-year-old Cole Sear , who has a condition similar to Vincent's.
- **S9**：Crowe becomes dedicated to the boy, though he is haunted by doubts over his ability to help him after his failure with Vincent.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, his relationship with his wife has deteriorated to the point where she ignores him and refuses to talk to him.
- **S11**：Crowe believes that she may be contemplating a romance with a coworker who keeps coming around the house although this elicits sadness from him not anger.
- **S12**：Crowe also repeatedly has difficulty opening the door to his basement office.
- **S13**：Once Crowe earns his trust, Cole eventually confides in him that he "sees dead people... walking around like regular people".
- **S14**：One ghost who appears to Cole is an overworked wife, abused by her husband, who has slit her wrists.
- **S15**：Another that tries to hurt Cole is only heard as a voice who pleads with Cole to let him out of a dark cupboard, then yells that he didn't steal "the Master's horse" and threatens to attack Cole.
- **S16**：A third ghost is a boy with a large gunshot exit wound on the back of his head who asks Cole to come with him to find his father's gun.
- **S17**：Though Crowe at first thinks Cole is delusional, he eventually comes to believe that Cole is telling the truth and that Vincent may have had the same ability to perceive ghosts.
- **S18**：He suggests to Cole that he should try to find a purpose for his gift by communicating with the ghosts and perhaps aid them with their unfinished business on Earth.
- **S19**：Cole at first does not want to since the ghosts terrify him, but he finally decides to try it.
- **S20**：He talks to one of the ghosts, a very ill girl who appears in his bedroom and promptly vomits in his tent.
- **S21**：He finds where the girl, Kyra Collins , lived and goes to her house during her funeral reception.
- **S22**：Kyra died after a prolonged illness and funeral guests note that Kyra's younger sister is starting to get sick, too.
- **S23**：Kyra's ghost appears and gives Cole a box, which is opened to reveal a videotape.
- **S24**：When Cole gives it to Kyra's father, the videotape shows Kyra's stepmother putting floor cleaner fluid in Kyra's food{{cite web}} while she cared for Kyra during her illness.
- **S25**：Cole confesses his secret to his mother, Lynn .
- **S26**：Although his mother at first does not believe him, Cole soon tells Lynn that her own mother once went to see her perform in a dance recital one night when she was a child, and that Lynn was not aware of this because her mother stayed in the back of the audience where she could not be seen.
- **S27**：He also tells her that the answer to a question she asked when alone at her mother's grave, "Do I make you proud?", was "Every day".
- **S28**：Lynn tearfully accepts this as the truth.
- **S29**：Crowe returns to his home, where he finds his wife asleep on the couch with the couple's wedding video playing, not for the first time.
- **S30**：As she sleeps, Anna's hand releases Malcolm's wedding ring , revealing to Crowe that he was actually killed by Vincent and was unknowingly dead the entire time he was working with Cole.
- **S31**：Due to Cole's efforts, Crowe's unfinished business—rectifying his failure to understand and help Vincent—is finally complete.
- **S32**：Recalling Cole's advice, Crowe speaks to his sleeping wife and fulfills the second reason he returned, saying she was "never second", and that he loves her.
- **S33**：Letting her live her own life, he is free to leave the world of the living and Cole had learned to live with the ghosts as it had become a part of his life.

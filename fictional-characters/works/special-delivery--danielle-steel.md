- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Danielle Steel explores finding love when, and from whom you least expect it in Special Delivery .
- **S3**：Jack Watson was a man hardened to the idea of love.
- **S4**：The death of his one true love followed by a messy divorce led him content to lead the ultimate bachelor's life.
- **S5**：Written about in the society pages, and despite his reputation, he never had trouble finding a date.
- **S6**：It didn't hurt that he owned one of the most successful women's boutiques in Beverly Hills.
- **S7**：Amanda Robbins was a successful actress who had already claimed an Academy Award when she met her husband Matthew Kingston and fell in love.
- **S8**：Amanda gave up her acting career to be a devoted mother of two children.
- **S9**：Her husband Matthew wasn't interested in a working wife and Amanda was happy to oblige, until his sudden death from a heart attack.
- **S10**：With the center of her life suddenly gone Amanda fell into despair and depression.
- **S11**：Jack and Amanda didn't travel in the same social circles however, the marriage of their children, Paul and Jan, created an undeniable connection.
- **S12**：In the past, while Jack and Amanda were cordial with one another they didn't go out of their way to spend much time together.
- **S13**：One day Jan offers to take Amanda to one of Jack's infamous parties.
- **S14**：Amanda surprises herself when she accepts and has a great time.
- **S15**：This sparked a new beginning as she and Jack began spending more time together, initially just to talk about their children.
- **S16**：However, they soon discover that they have more than just children in common.
- **S17**：This new relationship helps Amanda heal from the loss of her husband and causes Jack to realize that life isn't as fulfilling when alone.
- **S18**：An unexpected pregnancy nearly destroys their love, but ultimately brings them closer together.
- **S19**：They end up seeing this new life as an opportunity to support Jan and Paul who have had trouble conceiving.
- **S20**：At the last moment Jan finds out she's pregnant and have decided not to adopt Amanda and Jack's baby.
- **S21**：Interwoven throughout Special Delivery are the stories of family challenges for both the Robbin's and the Kingston's.
- **S22**：Tension between Amanda's daughters, the difficulties of starting a family, and healing from loss are all included as Jack and Amanda fall in love with each other and learn how to make their families stronger in times of need.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] While walking in the woods near the small town of Haven, Maine, Roberta "Bobbi" Anderson, a writer of Wild West -themed fiction, stumbles upon a metal object that turns out to be a protrusion of a long-buried alien spacecraft.
- **S3**：Once exposed, the spacecraft begins to release an invisible gas into the atmosphere that gradually transforms people into beings similar to the aliens who populated the ship.
- **S4**：The transformation, or "becoming", provides them with a limited form of genius which makes them very inventive but does not provide any philosophical or ethical insight into their inventions, and they often do not understand the principles of the inventions (one of them admits, "We're builders, not understanders").
- **S5**：The spacecraft also prevents those affected by it from leaving town, provokes psychotic violence in some people, and causes the disappearance of a young boy, David Brown, whose older brother Hilly teleports him to the planet referred to as Altair 4 by the Havenites (a reference to Forbidden Planet ). [ 2 ] The book's central character is James Eric Gardener, a poet and friend of Bobbi, who goes by the nickname "Gard".
- **S6**：He is somewhat immune to the ship's effects because of the steel plate in his head, a souvenir of a teenage skiing accident.
- **S7**：Gard is also an alcoholic and is prone to binges that result in violent outbursts followed by lengthy blackouts.
- **S8**：As Bobbi is almost totally overcome by the euphoria of "becoming" one with the spacecraft, Gard increasingly sees her health worsen and her sanity disappear.
- **S9**：Gard feels he has little to live for aside from his friendship with Bobbi and decides to stay with her to try to halt her decline.
- **S10**：He witnesses the transformation of the townspeople, discovers the torture and mutilation of Bobbi's dog Peter, and sees people being killed or worse when they pry too deeply into the strange events.
- **S11**：Over the course of several weeks, Gard, Bobbi, and others continue to unearth the ship.
- **S12**：Bobbi and Gard eventually enter the ship; as they explore the ship, Gard realizes that the ship crashed because the crew was fighting with each other, and that the Tommyknockers have no moral limits to keep them from using living beings as organic batteries.
- **S13**：As they leave the ship and return to Bobbi's home, Gard plans to kill Bobbi as he can see she is no longer human.
- **S14**：Using a gun, Bobbi forces Gard to swallow a lethal dose of Valium .
- **S15**：As they talk, he shields his mind, pulls his own gun out, and shoots Bobbi.
- **S16**：As Bobbi dies, she telepathically screams and alerts the townspeople, who then swarm to her home, intent on killing Gard for fear that he intends to harm the ship.
- **S17**：Ev Hillman, David and Hilly's grandfather, helps Gard escape into the woods in exchange for saving David Brown from Altair 4.
- **S18**：Gard enters the ship, near death after his struggle with the townspeople.
- **S19**：With his last ounce of strength, he activates the ship and telepathically launches it into space.
- **S20**：This results in the eventual deaths of nearly all of the changed townspeople, but prevents the possibly disastrous consequences of the ship's influence spreading to the outside world.
- **S21**：Very shortly afterward, agents from the FBI , CIA , and " The Shop " invade Haven and take as many of the Havenites as possible (killing nearly a quarter of the survivors), along with a few of the devices created by the altered people of Haven.
- **S22**：In the last pages, David Brown is discovered safe in Hilly Brown's hospital room.

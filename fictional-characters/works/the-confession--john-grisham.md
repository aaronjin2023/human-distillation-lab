- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] In 1998, Travis Boyette abducts and rapes Nicole "Nikki" Yarber, a teenage girl and high school student in Slone, Texas.
- **S3**：He buries her body in Joplin, Missouri which is around six hours from Slone.
- **S4**：He watches unfazed as the police arrest and convict Donte Drumm, a Black high school football player with no connection to the crime.
- **S5**：Despite his innocence, Drumm is convicted and sentenced to death.
- **S6**：The story begins after he has been on death row for nine years.
- **S7**：While Drumm serves his prison sentence, lawyer Robert "Robbie" Flak fights for his case.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, Black Americans protest his false conviction.
- **S9**：In the meantime, Boyette is serving as a parolee in Kansas.
- **S10**：He has been suffering from a brain tumor for the past nine years, and his health has deteriorated.
- **S11**：In 2007, with Drumm's execution only a week away, reflecting on his miserable life, he decides to do what is right: confess to carrying out the rape.
- **S12**：He meets with a pastor, Reverend Keith Schroeder who takes him to Slone.
- **S13**：Despite his public confession, the execution proceeds, and Drumm is executed by lethal injection.
- **S14**：The town is beset by racial tension, though a riot is averted.
- **S15**：Boyette then reveals the resting place of Nikki, and DNA samples show signs of rape and assault on her body.
- **S16**：Before an arrest warrant is issued for him, however, he absconds.
- **S17**：In Slone, Flak leads legal attacks on those responsible for the false conviction and execution, while the Reverend Schroeder agonizes over what he has done: taken a paroled convicted rapist—and probable murderer—out of his parole zone (the state of Kansas).
- **S18**：Schroeder winds up making his actions public, paying a fine, resigning from his church, and accepting a position at a reform-minded church in Texas.
- **S19**：The latter happens after Boyette is caught attempting another rape.

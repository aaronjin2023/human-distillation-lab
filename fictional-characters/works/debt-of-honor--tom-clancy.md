- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] A car accident in East Tennessee , caused by faulty gas tanks made in Japan , kills six Americans in a multi-vehicle collision .
- **S3**：The incident prompts the swift passage of a law allowing the United States to mirror the trade practices of countries to which it exports, cutting off the export markets on which Japan's economy depends.
- **S4**：Facing economic crisis, Japan's zaibatsu , led by industrialist Raizo Yamata, decides to take economic and military action against the U.S., with covert support from China and India .
- **S5**：After the Japanese Prime Minister Mogataru Koga resigns in disgrace, the zaibatsu installs Hiroshi Goto, an aggressive nationalist and critic of the U.S., as his successor.
- **S6**：Japan has also secretly developed nuclear weapons and, using SS-19 designs bought from the former Soviet Union, has built and deployed several ICBMs .
- **S7**：Japan begins its assault by sending Self-Defense Force units to occupy the Mariana Islands , specifically Saipan and Guam , without casualties.
- **S8**：During a joint military exercise, Japanese ships also "accidentally" launch torpedoes at the U.S.
- **S9**：Pacific Fleet , destroying two submarines and crippling two aircraft carriers.
- **S10**：Before the U.S. can retaliate, Japan launches an economic attack, hiring a programmer who consults for an exchange firm to insert a logic bomb that deletes all trade records made after noon on Friday, causing the U.S. stock market to collapse.
- **S11**：Japan also assassinates the President of the Federal Reserve Bank .
- **S12**：The Japanese government then immediately sue for peace , proposing international talks and apparently free elections in the Marianas to delay an American response.

- **S16**：He engineers a "do-over", restoring all trade information to its state at noon on the day of the deletion and disregarding the lost transactions.
- **S17**：The stock market recovers with only minor disruption, while U.S. investment banks begin a massive sell-off of Japanese investment products, wiping out the zaibatsu's economic gains.

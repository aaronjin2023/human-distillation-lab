- **S38**：Eragon heads to Farthen Dur, where the election takes place.
- **S39**：Once there, Eragon is the target of an assassination attempt, perpetrated by a Rider-hating dwarf clan, whom Orik forces into exile.
- **S40**：Having earned the sympathies of the dwarves, Orik is elected the new king.
- **S41**：Saphira journeys to Farthen Dur for Orik's coronation and repairs Isidar Mithrim, the previously destroyed star rose.
- **S42**：After Orik's coronation, Eragon and Saphira journey to Ellesméra to learn the secret of Galbatorix's power.
- **S43**：There, they learn that Eragon's deceased mentor, Brom, was Eragon's father, and not the Forsworn Morzan as Murtagh had thought, though they both share the same mother.
- **S44**：Glaedr also reveals the source of Galbatorix's power: Eldunari, [ 5 ] a dragon organ, which allows its holder to communicate with and draw energy from the dragon it belongs to, even if the dragon is deceased.
- **S45**：Galbatorix spent years collecting Eldunari, drawing his power from the dragons he slew.
- **S46**：After some training, Eragon visits Rhunön , the elven blacksmith who forged swords for Riders.
- **S47**：Rhunön refuses to forge Eragon a new sword, having sworn to never create a weapon again, and having depleted her stocks of the required metal.
- **S48**：By deciphering the werecat Solembum's instructions, Eragon acquires the metal from under the roots of the Menoa Tree.
- **S49**：Rhunön forges him a sword by controlling Eragon's body, and he names it "Brisingr", which causes it to burst into flames whenever its name is said aloud.
- **S50**：Oromis and Glaedr decide to join the battle against the Empire alongside Islanzadí.
- **S51**：To prevent Galbatorix from claiming his Eldunari should he fall in combat, and to continue guiding Eragon, Glaedr gives his Eldunari to Eragon.
- **S52**：Glaedr and Oromis fly to Gil'ead, while Eragon and Saphira fly to Feinster, a city sieged by the Varden.
- **S53**：Meanwhile, Roran is sent on various missions as a soldier of the Varden.
- **S54**：One mission against soldiers that can't feel pain results in many casualties, and in Roran being assigned to an incompetent commander.
- **S55**：During another mission, this commander almost causes the entire force to be decimated.
- **S56**：Roran takes charge of a small group of soldiers loyal to him, and leads them to victory against overwhelming odds.
- **S57**：Despite this, Roran is charged with insubordination and is flogged as a punishment.
- **S58**：Afterwards, Nasuada promotes Roran to commander and sends his unit on a mission with both men and Urgals, to enforce the idea of men and Urgals working together.
- **S59**：When an Urgal, Yarbog, challenges Roran for leadership of the unit, he wrestles him into submission.
- **S60**：After returning to the Varden, his squad joins the siege of Feinster.
- **S61**：As the siege begins, Eragon rescues Arya and departs to find the leader of the city, but discovers three magicians attempting to create a Shade.
- **S62**：While attempting to kill the magicians, Eragon has a vision through Glaedr's Eldunarí showing him and Oromis fighting Murtagh and Thorn in the sky.
- **S63**：During the fight, Galbatorix takes control of Murtagh, and tries to lure Oromis to his side.
- **S64**：Failing to do so, he slays Oromis as he suffers a seizure.
- **S65**：Glaedr, in grief, is also killed.
- **S66**：After the vision, the magicians succeed in creating the Shade Varaug.
- **S67**：Eragon and Arya fight and slay Varaug, with Arya dealing the fatal blow.
- **S68**：After the successful siege, Eragon resolves to continue the fight against the Empire, to avenge Oromis and Glaedr.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Ludo Decker  is a Berlin based yellow press reporter.
- **S2**：With his photographer Moritz, his daily routine is to spy on celebrities for the tabloid Das Blatt.
- **S3**：He also uses his work for frequent sexual contacts with his objects of interest.
- **S4**：When heavyweight boxer and celebrity Wladimir Klitschko is about to toast his fiancee Yvonne Catterfeld at their engagement party, Ludo and Moritz are on the scene to report about it.
- **S5**：Ludo breaks through a glass dome of the party venue, falls into the cake and is subsequently sentenced to 300 hours of community service at a daycare center.
- **S6**：There he meets Anna Gotzlowski , the center's manager.
- **S7**：Anna grew up in the same neighborhood as Ludo and used to be picked on and mocked by him.
- **S8**：She gets her revenge by appointing him to perform humiliating tasks.
- **S9**：Ludo cannot fight this as his parole is at stake.
- **S10**：Still he is interested in one-night stands, and has an affair with a child's mother who frequents the facility.
- **S11**：After a while, the tensions between Anna and Ludo dissipate and the two become friends.
- **S12**：When Anna's date with another man goes awry, she appears at Ludo's flat seeking comfort.
- **S13**：The two of them end up in bed, yet Anna's hope that this could develop into something more serious is destroyed for the time being.
- **S14**：Ludo sees her only as a friend, until Anna soon starts dating actor Jürgen Vogel, whom she meets in a park.
- **S15**：After Ludo writes about her attending the German Film Awards ceremony with Vogel, describing her as the most beautiful woman around, he is fired by his editor.
- **S16**：In the end, Ludo realizes that he feels more for Anna than he had previously thought.
- **S17**：During a children's festival at a local theatre, he crashes a performance and swears his love to Anna, who sits in the audience with the children.
- **S18**：After his discharge from the tabloid paper, he takes up work at Anna's facility.

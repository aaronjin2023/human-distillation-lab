## 角色资料

### Sammy

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3d1159627e7a5c00` · 独立版本：`profile-27df750edd8779fe`

- **性格倾向**：在寻找伴侣的途中，他仍倾向回应陌生同类的求救（行为推断；S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：他先向往传说中的乐园，后来持续寻找失散的谢莉（摘要直接叙述；S4、S13、S20）
- **决策方式**：面对远行障碍，他倾向利用现成物件寻找继续前进的办法（行为推断；S3、S15）
- **情绪反应**：发现谢莉受到另一只海龟追求时，他感到失望与难过（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25）
- **关系模式**：他与雷的关系从结伴远行延续到成年后的救援与互助（摘要直接叙述；S4、S21、S26）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：真心追求谢莉，却通过假袭击制造再次救美的机会

以上为依据完整摘要审校的综合笔记，未逐项标注句号；可结合下列完整选摘核对。

### Ray

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-b7546277f47eddba` · 独立版本：`profile-5385f3b6e47e08b3`

- **性格倾向**：在共同远行与朋友求爱时，他倾向积极参与同伴的行动（行为推断；S4、S26）
- **核心动机**：幼年时他向往海龟乐园，并与萨米一起启程寻找（摘要直接叙述；S4）
- **决策方式**：帮助萨米求爱时，他倾向参与设计救美情境以赢得谢莉的心（行为推断；S26）
- **关系模式**：他成年后与丽塔共同生活，也继续参与萨米的感情事务（摘要直接叙述；S22、S26、S27）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

- 摘要详述萨米的经历，却仅零散呈现雷的选择，因此雷的性格结论限于合作情境。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Sammy, a sea turtle, hatches on a deserted beach and tries to climb out of the hole where he hatched.
- **S2**：Being caught by a seagull, he struggles for his life and manages to escape, as the seagull clashes in the air with another seagull, also carrying its prey, who turns out to be a female hatchling sea turtle, Shelly.
- **S3**：Sammy falls onto an old raft and gets carried into the open ocean, losing Shelly.
- **S4**：In the ocean he meets another hatchling, Ray, who tells him the story of a "turtle paradise" on an island called "Lapagos", and they together start their voyage there.
- **S5**：Later, the two get caught by trawls.
- **S6**：Sammy is released back into the sea by the fishermen, but loses Ray.
- **S7**：Washed onto the shore in California, he is picked up by a hippie woman and lives as a pet in the hippie commune with Vera, another female sea turtle, who is older than Sammy, and Catoff, a cat, who claims to be French.
- **S8**：On a Christmas evening the hippies draw a peace sign on his shell to sign him as the peace ambassador.
- **S9**：When the hippies see that Sammy and Vera will not mate, they release Vera.
- **S10**：The police evicts the hippie commune from the beach, and Sammy decides to return back to the sea.
- **S11**：There he meets Vera and a young female sea turtle who is attacked by a shark.
- **S12**：Vera and Sammy save the turtle who turns out to be Shelly, his childhood friend.
- **S13**：They decide to travel together to find the secret passage and use it to travel to the paradise island.
- **S14**：They manage to find the passage  but get separated as they try to pass a lock.
- **S15**：Sammy finds an old refrigerator thrown off a ship by the humans and uses it to travel to Antarctic.
- **S16**：In the Antarctic waters Sammy meets a fin whale who barely escapes a harpoon from a whaling ship.
- **S17**：The harpoon destroys the refrigerator, but Sammy is saved by the anti-whaling protesters and taken back to California where he finds Shelly, Catoff and people from the hippie commune .
- **S18**：The ex-hippie recognize the peace sign on Sammy's shell.
- **S19**：Shelly is released into the ocean before Sammy, and they are once again separated.
- **S20**：As he searches for Shelly, Sammy encounters two sea turtles that urge him to help their friend who got trapped in an intermodal container.
- **S21**：Sammy rescues the turtle, who turns out to be his old friend Ray.
- **S22**：They swim together to a wrecked Spanish galleon where Ray lives with Rita, his girlfriend.
- **S23**：Being told about Shelly, Rita remembers her and advises Sammy to search for her in the galleon.
- **S24**：Sammy finds Shelly being courted by another sea turtle.
- **S25**：Disappointed and upset, Sammy returns to his friends only to find out that the turtle who courted Shelly is a playboy but has a strong relationship with another female.
- **S26**：Together with Ray and Albert, a shark who lost his teeth, they set up a fake shark attack on Shelly to let Sammy save her again and win her heart.
- **S27**：Shelly and Sammy then reunite and, along with Ray and Rita, return to the beach they hatched.
- **S28**：The closing scene of the movie shows Sammy helping one of his new-hatched grandsons to climb out of the hole.

## 角色资料

### Paul

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-7995aac7d4df0080` · 独立版本：`profile-99f2db94cb25b53e`

- **性格倾向**：在共同生活与救助情境中，倾向陪伴他人并付诸行动（行为推断；S10、S15、S39）
- **核心动机**：与维吉妮分离后，他努力维系联系并盼望感情得以延续（行为推断；S35、S36）
- **决策方式**：面对分离造成的障碍，他以学习读写建立新的沟通途径（行为推断；S36）
- **情绪反应**：分离使他忧虑恋人的感情，最终又因失去她而悲痛至死（摘要直接叙述；S35、S41）
- **关系模式**：与维吉妮自幼如兄妹相伴，后来成为期待结合的恋人（摘要直接叙述；S9、S22、S36）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

### Virginie

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b35ad8d5df1ae2a5` · 独立版本：`profile-5fbb92338580270a`

- **性格倾向**：面对受虐者时倾向主动怜悯，但对家庭安排又表现顺从（行为推断；S14、S34）
- **价值取舍**：在沉船危机中，她依作者所述将羞耻规范置于脱险便利之前（摘要直接叙述；S40）
- **核心动机**：身处法国仍试图突破通信阻隔，维持与保罗的感情联系（行为推断；S37、S38）
- **决策方式**：面对长辈阻挠通信，她倾向以机巧绕过限制而非公开决裂（行为推断；S37、S38）
- **情绪反应**：她初尝难以命名的爱意，却因服从远行安排而心情沉重（摘要直接叙述；S21、S34）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：对家庭安排的服从，与主动绕过长辈阻碍维系爱情并存。

- **S1**：## Synopsis
- **S2**：[ edit ] The story begins with being narrated by a young man who is in Isle de France (present-day Mauritius) at Port-Louis.
- **S3**：He enjoys going to a place where two small huts can be seen.
- **S4**：One day, he meets an old man and asks him who lived in these huts.
- **S5**：The old man's narration then begins.
- **S6**：He was a friend of two families who lived peacefully in these huts.
- **S7**：These families have died, but they were models of virtue.
- **S8**：The interested young man questions the old man, who then tells him the story of Paul and Virginie.
- **S9**：Paul and Virginie are raised as brother and sister by two single mothers amidst the natural splendour of tropical landscapes on an island and in isolation from the rest of the world.
- **S10**：They lead an idyllic life immersed in virtue, simplicity, and altruism, living off the work of their hands and what nature provides them.
- **S11**：An important moment in the lives of Paul and Virginie is their encounter with a beaten slave.
- **S12**：Indeed, one day, while Marguerite and Madame de la Tour are at mass, Virginie and Paul, who are about ten or twelve years old, see a slave running towards them.
- **S13**：She explains that she has escaped from her master, who was beating her.
- **S14**：Virginie then takes it upon herself to escort the slave back to her master and to ask the master for forgiveness for his slave.
- **S15**：Paul and Virginie therefore escort the slave, who is granted mercy.
- **S16**：They leave quickly because this master does not make a good impression on Virginie.
- **S17**：They get lost trying to return home.
- **S18**：They are found by Domingue with the help of Fidèle, the dog.
- **S19**：Domingue explains that on the way, he saw the slave that the two children had tried to save, on a pole with a collar and spikes.
- **S20**：This passage is a very important moment in the story because it marks the young children, who later speak about it as teenagers.
- **S21**：When adolescence begins to emerge, Virginie feels a romantic sentiment awakening within her that she cannot yet name.
- **S22**：The two mothers agree to marry their children, who have always gotten along so well, but they consider it still too early to do so.
- **S23**：In the meantime, Madame de la Tour's aunt, Virginie's mother, suggests sending her daughter to France, where she can benefit from her substantial inheritance.
- **S24**：However, Madame de la Tour had deliberately cut herself off from her family because she had been rejected by them due to her relationship with Virginie's father (who had died long ago).
- **S25**：The latter was, in fact, of a lower social standing than Madame de la Tour.
- **S26**：When Monsieur and Madame de la Tour arrived in Île de France, Monsieur de la Tour immediately set sail for Madagascar, where he died, leaving his wife at the port of Saint-Louis.
- **S27**：To recall the meeting between Marguerite and Madame de la Tour and their past circumstances, when Madame de la Tour and Marguerite meet, Marguerite is already settled on the island with Domingue.
- **S28**：Marguerite was born into a peasant family in Brittany.
- **S29**：She falls in love with a gentleman who promises to marry her, but he does not keep his promise.
- **S30**：Marguerite decides to leave for the colonies to hide her shame.
- **S31**：She nursed Paul when Madame de la Tour, who is pregnant, arrives.
- **S32**：Madame de la Tour, for her part, is accompanied by a slave named Marie.
- **S33**：After much hesitation, and considering the usefulness of separating Paul and Virginie for a certain time before their final union, Madame de la Tour is persuaded by her aunt, the governor, and the bishop of the island to convince Virginie to undertake the journey.
- **S34**：She submits out of pure obedience, with a heavy heart.
- **S35**：During Virginie's absence, which will last a little over two years, Paul feels oppressed by boredom and worry about Virginie's feelings towards him.
- **S36**：He learns to read and write in order to correspond with his beloved.
- **S37**：Virginie also writes several letters, which her great-aunt does not send.
- **S38**：Through a clever ruse, Virginie manages to send a letter after a year of absence.
- **S39**：When Virginie, after an unfortunate experience, finally returns, the ship bringing her back from France is caught in a storm and runs aground on the rocks before Paul, who vainly tries to save her from the waves.
- **S40**：Virginie decides, out of modesty according to the author, not to undress even though it would make swimming and escaping easier.
- **S41**：As for Paul, he does not take long to succumb to the pain of losing his beloved.
- **S42**：Marguerite dies afterwards.
- **S43**：Madame de la Tour also eventually dies.

- **S1**：The film begins with Carrie, Samantha, Charlotte, and Miranda meeting up with each other which turns into a flashback to how Carrie arrived in New York City in 1986, then met Charlotte in 1987, Miranda in 1988, and finally Samantha in 1989.
- **S2**：All are married except Samantha, who is 52 and trying to keep her libido alive with hormones while dealing with menopause.
- **S3**：The four of them attend Anthony and Stanford's wedding, where Carrie serves as "best man." Miranda quits her job after the new managing partner disrespects her once too often.
- **S4**：Charlotte's two children are a handful and she's worried that Harry is attracted to their buxom Irish nanny, Erin.
- **S5**：Carrie's marriage to Mr.
- **S6**：Big has settled down, though they differ on how to spend their spare time.
- **S7**：For their anniversary, Carrie gives Mr.
- **S8**：Big a vintage Rolex watch engraved with a romantic message, while he, much to her dismay, shows her a new TV in their bedroom as his gift, which Big says they can use to watch old movies together, something they did at the hotel at Anthony and Stanford's wedding and seemed to enjoy.
- **S9**：Carrie, however, is disappointed, as she had hoped for jewelry as a gift.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, Samantha has been approached by an Arab sheikh to devise a PR campaign for his business.
- **S11**：He offers to fly her and her friends on an all-expenses-paid luxury vacation to Abu Dhabi.
- **S12**：The girls happily accept, although Carrie is worried about the separation from Big and Charlotte is worried about leaving her husband alone with the nanny.
- **S13**：Only Miranda, unfettered by a job for the first time in her life, is enthusiastic.
- **S14**：Upon entering Abu Dhabi, Samantha's hormone-enhancing drugs are confiscated under UAE law.
- **S15**：This renders her devoid of estrogen; her famous libido goes dead.
- **S16**：Charlotte tries to call Harry every few minutes; Miranda revels in the luxury surrounding her, while Carrie befriends her manservant, Gaurau.
- **S17**：Carrie runs into her former lover, Aidan.
- **S18**：He proposes dinner à deux at his hotel and she decides to meet Aidan for dinner.
- **S19**：The dinner is very enjoyable, with the two discussing old times.
- **S20**：Aidan remarks on the ways Carrie is "not like other women".
- **S21**：In a moment of remembered passion, they kiss.
- **S22**：Carrie runs away in panic and returns to the hotel.
- **S23**：Back at the hotel, Miranda and Charlotte have drinks together and discuss the difficulties of motherhood.
- **S24**：Carrie arrives, tells her friends about the kiss, and asks them whether she should tell Big, as they have no secrets between them.
- **S25**：Miranda reflects on the events of the previous film, when her husband, Steve, told her about his affair.
- **S26**：Samantha counsels Carrie to wait before deciding anything.
- **S27**：Carrie opts to call Big to tell him.
- **S28**：Big is silent upon hearing the news, and after saying a few words, hangs up.
- **S29**：The four women find their style and Western attitudes contrast with Muslim customs.
- **S30**：While on a date with a handsome Dane, Samantha is arrested for public indecency after fondling him at a restaurant and making out with him on the beach.
- **S31**：With the Sheikh's intervention, Samantha is released, but is left with a permanent police record.
- **S32**：Worse, the Sheikh cancels the PR meeting and ceases paying for the women's luxurious perks.
- **S33**：They quickly pack their bags and leave, but must return to the souk to find Carrie's passport.
- **S34**：When Samantha's flamboyance nearly incites a riot, the girls are rescued by a group of Muslim women who share their sense of style under their black robes.
- **S35**：When Carrie returns home, she finds the bedroom television removed and Big gone.
- **S36**：She passes an anxious day, at the end of which he returns.
- **S37**：Big tells her that although he was "pretty torn up", he realizes that what she needs is something to remind her at all times that she is married.
- **S38**：He hands her a jewelry box, which reveals an engagement ring set with a black diamond.
- **S39**：When Carrie asks him why a black diamond, he says, "Because you're not like anyone else", echoing Aidan's earlier comment.
- **S40**：The problems the women faced at the beginning of the film are resolved.
- **S41**：Big and Carrie combine their interests; Charlotte's nanny, Erin, turns out to be a lesbian and is no threat to her marriage; Miranda finds a new job where she is appreciated, and Samantha stays the same, even meeting up with the Danish doctor she met in Abu Dhabi for sex on the beach.

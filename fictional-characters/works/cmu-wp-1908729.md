### Maggie Carpenter

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Maggie Carpenter  is a spirited and attractive young woman who has had a number of unsuccessful relationships.
- **S2**：Maggie, nervous of being married, has left a trail of fiances.
- **S3**：It seems, she's left three men waiting for her at the altar on their wedding day , receiving tabloid fame and the dubious nickname "The Runaway Bride".
- **S4**：Meanwhile, in New York, reporter Homer Eisenhower Graham or "Ike" , writes a column about her that contains several factual errors, supplied to him by one of Maggie's jilted exes for revenge.
- **S5**：He's fired for not verifying his source and then decides to write an in-depth article about Maggie in a bid to get his job back.
- **S6**：He travels to Hale, Maryland, where he finds her living with her family and on her fourth attempt to become married.
- **S7**：The fourth groom-to-be, Bob Kelly , a football coach at the local high school who treats Maggie like one of the players on his sports team.
- **S8**：He constantly makes references to Maggie "focusing" on the goal-line.
- **S9**：While doing research for his story, Ike's realizations are forcing Maggie to face her fears, and eventually the two find themselves becoming more and more attracted to each other.
- **S10**：In the meantime, Maggie is still set to marry Bob.
- **S11**：In the midst of the wedding rehearsal, Bob helps walk Maggie down the aisle, and asks Ike to stand in his place as the groom.
- **S12**：This proves to be a mistake, when Ike and Maggie "practice" the kiss right in front of Bob, finally admitting their feelings for one another.
- **S13**：As a result, Bob punches Ike in the face before storming out of the church.
- **S14**：Soon after, Ike and Maggie agree to marry since the wedding is already set to take place.
- **S15**：But on the day of the wedding, Maggie gets cold feet, and leaves Ike, too, standing at the altar.
- **S16**：As she rides away on a FedEx truck, Ike runs after her, but can't catch up.
- **S17**：Later, we see Ike living in New York and Maggie trying to discover herself, trying different types of eggs, and putting her lighting designs up for sale in New York.
- **S18**：She shows up unexpectedly at Ike's apartment one night where he finds her making friends with his cat, Italics.
- **S19**：Maggie then explains that she's been running because every other guy she was engaged to was only engaged to the idea she had created for them rather than the real her- and when she was marrying Ike she was simply freaked out at the crowd-, and "turns in" her running shoes just before proposing to Ike.
- **S20**：The two are married in a private ceremony outside, on a hill, avoiding the big ceremonies that drove Maggie away in the past.
- **S21**：In the end, they are shown riding away on horseback while everyone celebrates the fact that Maggie finally got married.

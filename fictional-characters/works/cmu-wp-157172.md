## 角色资料

### Kevin

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a13f5c6c9f9a96c8` · 独立版本：`profile-0df3900396f18449`

- **性格倾向**：面对未知奇遇，他倾向把最初恐惧转化为有准备的好奇探索（行为推断；S1、S3、S4、S13）
- **核心动机**：他珍视被父辈关爱的体验，因此愿意留在阿伽门农身边（行为推断；S1、S15、S16）
- **决策方式**：遭遇困局时，他善于回看记录并把观察所得转化为脱困线索（行为推断；S13、S21、S22）
- **价值取舍**：识别致命危险后，他试图保护忽视自己的父母而非置身事外（行为推断；S1、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：向往阿伽门农给予的父爱，却被同伴强行带离

### Evil

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-9745d810c812ad39` · 独立版本：`profile-6345d308eeca47f4`

- **性格倾向**：面对持有地图的旅人，他倾向隐蔽监视并利用欲望操纵行动（行为推断；S14、S18、S19）
- **核心动机**：他持续追逐时空地图，试图将其从矮人手中据为己有（摘要直接叙述；S14、S20）
- **决策方式**：他先以宝物诱饵布置陷阱，再夺取地图并囚禁来者（行为推断；S18、S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：他将他人的自由与生存当作可牺牲之物，以维持自身支配（行为推断；S20、S23、S24）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Eleven-year-old Kevin has a vivid imagination and is fascinated by history, particularly of Ancient Greece; his parents ignore his activities, having become more obsessed with buying the latest household gadgets to keep up with their neighbours.
- **S2**：One night, as Kevin is sleeping, an armoured knight on a horse bursts forth from his wardrobe.
- **S3**：Kevin is scared and hides as the knight rides off into a forest setting where once his bedroom wall was; when Kevin looks back out, the room is back to normal, and he finds one of his photos on the wall similar to the forest he saw.
- **S4**：He prepares a satchel with supplies and Polaroid camera for the next night.
- **S5**：He is surprised when six dwarfs spill out of the wardrobe.
- **S6**：Kevin quickly learns the group has stolen a large, worn map, and are looking for an exit in Kevin's room before they are discovered.
- **S7**：They find that Kevin's bedroom wall can be pushed revealing a long hallway.
- **S8**：Kevin is hesitant to join until the visage of an evil-looking head - the Supreme Being - appears behind them, demanding the return of the map.
- **S9**：Kevin and the dwarves fall into an empty void at the end of the hallway.
- **S10**：They land in Italy during the Napoleonic Wars.
- **S11**：As they recover, Kevin learns that Randall is the lead dwarf, along with Fidgit, Strutter, Og, Wally, and Vermin.
- **S12**：They were once employed by the Supreme Being to repair holes in the spacetime fabric, but instead realized the potential to use the map to steal valuable riches.
- **S13**：With the map and Kevin's help, they visit several locations in spacetime, and meet figures such as Napoleon Bonaparte and Robin Hood; Kevin uses his camera to document their visits.
- **S14**：They are unaware that their activities are being monitored by Evil, a malevolent sorcerer, who is able to manipulate reality and is attempting to acquire the map himself.
- **S15**：Through Evil's actions, Kevin becomes separated from the group and ends up in Mycenaean Greece, and meets King Agamemnon; after Kevin inadvertently helps Agamemnon kill a minotaur, the king treats him as his own son, something that Kevin would be content with.
- **S16**：Randall and the others soon locate Kevin and abduct him, much to his resentment.
- **S17**：They arrive on the ill-fated RMS Titanic; after it sinks, they are forced to tread water while they argue amongst each other.
- **S18**：Evil manipulates the group and transports them to his realm, the Time of Legends.
- **S19**：The dwarfs locate the Fortress of Ultimate Darkness, and are led to believe that "The Most Fabulous Object in the World" awaits them, luring them into Evil's trap.
- **S20**：Evil takes the map and locks the group into a cage over an apparent bottomless pit.
- **S21**：While looking through the Polaroids he took, Kevin finds one that includes the map, and the group realises there are numerous holes in the Fortress they can use to recruit help to fight Evil.
- **S22**：They make quick work of escaping from the cage and set their plan into motion.
- **S23**：Though they far outnumber Evil, Evil's powers quickly defeat all of the allied forces.
- **S24**：As Kevin and the dwarves cower, Evil prepares to unleash his ultimate power.
- **S25**：Suddenly, he is turned into stone and explodes; from the smoke, an elderly, well-dressed figure emerges, revealed as the true form of the Supreme Being.
- **S26**：He orders the dwarfs to collect all the pieces of concentrated evil warning that they can be deadly if not contained, recovers the map, and allows the dwarves to rejoin him in his creation duties.
- **S27**：The Supreme Being disappears with the dwarfs, leaving Kevin stranded behind as one missed piece of concentrated Evil begins to emit smoke.
- **S28**：Kevin passes out but awakes, back in his bedroom, his room filled with smoke.
- **S29**：Firefighters break down the door and rescue him as they put out a fire in his house.
- **S30**：One of the firemen finds that his parents' new microwave caused the fire.
- **S31**：As Kevin recovers, he finds one of the firemen resembles Agamemnon, and discovers that he still has the photos from his adventure.
- **S32**：As his parents look at a strange piece of rock in the microwave, Kevin tries to warn them off that it is a piece of concentrated evil and they should not touch it; nevertheless, both do, and suddenly explode and disappear, leaving Kevin wondering where they have gone off to.

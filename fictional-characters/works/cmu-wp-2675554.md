## 角色资料

### Papa Smurf

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-7151abefbfc4683f` · 独立版本：`profile-40bc206fa3ad5907`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他因预见危险而谨慎限制行动，也在危机中保护同伴。（行为推断；S1、S2、S17）
- **核心动机**：他想阻止预见的灾祸，并计算回家时机、寻找返乡咒语。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S12、S13）
- **价值取舍**：被捕之际仍将其他蓝精灵送往安全处，显示他优先顾及同伴。（行为推断；S17）
- **决策方式**：他依据预兆限制Clumsy采根，并以计算确定返乡时机。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S12）
- **关系模式**：同伴曾向他承诺不来营救、直接返乡，后来却被劝服展开救援。（摘要直接叙述；S18）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他因预兆限制Clumsy行动，后者却在关键时刻接住法杖化解危机。

### Clumsy Smurf

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-1f658dff881422ab` · 独立版本：`profile-917750ca71410e8c`

- **性格倾向**：在逃亡情境中，他显得冒失且未察觉危险，却也敢于关键时刻出手。（行为推断；S2、S3、S22）
- **决策方式**：面对Papa被捕，他与Patrick劝同伴营救，并在战斗中尝试接杖。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S22）
- **成长变化**：他从无意造成危机的人，转为推动救援并成功反击Gargamel的人。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S18、S22）
- **关系模式**：他违背Papa的采根禁令，后来又推动同伴营救被捕的Papa。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S18）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As the Smurfs get ready for the Blue Moon Festival, Papa Smurf sees in his cauldron a vision of Clumsy Smurf reaching for a dragon wand and the Smurfs in cages while Gargamel laughs.
- **S2**：Not wanting this vision to come true, Papa Smurf refuses to allow Clumsy to pick Smurf Roots, but Clumsy disobeys Papa Smurf and ends up unintentionally leading Gargamel and Azrael to the village.
- **S3**：The Smurfs all flee for their lives while Clumsy unknowingly runs towards the Forbidden Falls, with Papa Smurf, Smurfette, Grouchy, Brainy and Gutsy running after him.
- **S4**：They find him at the edge of a cliff, and while trying to help him up, they are sucked into a gigantic vortex that spirits them to present day New York City.
- **S5**：To make matters worse, Gargamel and Azrael follow and the Smurfs end up in the apartment of Patrick and Grace Winslow, a married and expectant couple and their Basset Hound Elway.
- **S6**：After clarifying things, the Winslows befriend them and allow them to stay in their apartment.
- **S7**：The next day, needing to find a "star gazer" , the Smurfs follow Patrick to his work place at Anjelou Cosmetics, misunderstanding the previous explanation of his job as fortune-telling.
- **S8**：He calls Grace to pick them up.
- **S9**：However, having extracted "Smurf essence" from a lock of Smurfette's hair, Gargamel also arrives and ends up being treated by Patrick's boss Odile upon using most of his acquired magic to turn her mother young.
- **S10**：But Gargamel resumes his search upon recognizing Patrick and following him to the toy store where the Smurfs ran into after finding a telescope.
- **S11**：The Winslows manage to save the Smurfs from both the children wanting them and Gargamel, who ends up being sent to jail before he manages to bust out with the aid of house flies.
- **S12**：By that time, Papa Smurf manages to calculate the night he and the others can get home.
- **S13**：But first, he must figure out the spell to do so.
- **S14**：Patrick tells them that there is an old book store in the city near Anjelou Cosmetics as he bonds with the Smurfs after sending what he believed to be his finished advertisement to be published.
- **S15**：However, the next day, Patrick learns that Clumsy accidentally attached a blue-moon themed side project and he loses his temper before walking out on both the Smurfs and Grace to save his job.
- **S16**：Forced to search on their own, the Smurfs find the store and find the book L’Histoire des Schtroumpfs by researcher Peyo, containing the spell to turn the moon blue.
- **S17**：But learning of their location, Gargamel sneaks into the book store and finds a dragon wand, transferring his magic into it as he uses it to capture Papa Smurf as he sends the others to safety.
- **S18**：Though the Smurfs promised Papa Smurf that they won't try to save him and return home, Clumsy and Patrick, having seen the error of his actions, convince them to plan a rescue.
- **S19**：At Belvedere Castle, after increasing the dragon wand's power with bits of Papa Smurf's beard, Gargamel finds himself facing all the Smurfs, summoned to New York by Brainy conjuring the blue moon.
- **S20**：As the Smurf army battles Gargamel, Smurfette fights Azrael and saves Papa Smurf before they join the fray.
- **S21**：Though Gargamel attempts to break the Smurfs by killing Papa, Patrick saves him while Gutsy knocks the dragon wand out of the wizard's hand but he drops it.
- **S22**：Clumsy tries to catch it, and to Papa Smurf's surprise, manages to catch it and sends Gargamel flying into a trash can and being hit by a bus with the advertisement "Blue Moon" with it before Papa Smurf breaks it.
- **S23**：Soon after, the Smurfs take their leave as Patrick receives a call from Odile that he still has his job because he finally gave her what she wants.
- **S24**：Later, Patrick and Grace have a baby boy, whom they name Blue to honor the Smurfs, who rebuild their village in the style of New York, where the Winslows reside.

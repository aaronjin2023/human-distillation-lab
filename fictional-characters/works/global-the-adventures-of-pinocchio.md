- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] In Tuscany , Italy, a carpenter named Master Antonio finds a log that he plans to carve into a table leg.
- **S3**：However, after being frightened when the log cries out, he gives it to his neighbour Geppetto who wants to carve a puppet from it and make a living as a travelling puppeteer.
- **S4**：He carves a boy and names him " Pinocchio ".
- **S5**：Once the puppet is finished and Geppetto teaches him to walk, Pinocchio runs out the door and into the town, where he is caught by a Carabiniere , who assumes Pinocchio has been mistreated and imprisons Geppetto.
- **S6**：Left alone, Pinocchio heads back to Geppetto's house where he meets a talking cricket , who warns him of the perils of disobedience and laziness.
- **S7**：After being called a wooden head, in retaliation, Pinocchio throws a hammer at the cricket, accidentally killing it.
- **S8**：Pinocchio gets hungry and tries to fry an egg, but a chick hatches from the egg and leaves to find food.
- **S9**：He knocks on a neighbour's door, but they fear he is pulling a prank and dump water on him.
- **S10**：Cold and wet, Pinocchio goes home and lies down on a stove, and upon waking he finds his feet have burned off.
- **S11**：Luckily, Geppetto is released from prison, gives Pinocchio three pears to eat for breakfast, and makes him a new pair of feet.
- **S12**：In gratitude, he promises to attend school and Geppetto makes him a suit of clothing consisting of a dress made of flowered paper, tree bark shoes, and a cap made of dough, and sells his only coat to buy him a spelling book.
- **S13**：On his way to school the next morning, Pinocchio encounters the Great Marionette Theatre and sells his school book to buy a ticket for the show.
- **S14**：During the performance, the puppets Harlequin , Pulcinella and Signora Rosaura call out to Pinocchio.
- **S15**：This disruption angers the puppet master Mangiafuoco , and he decides to use Pinocchio as firewood to cook his dinner.
- **S16**：However, after Pinocchio pleads for his and Harlequin's salvation and he learns of Geppetto's poverty, Mangiafuoco releases him and gives him five gold pieces.
- **S17**：On his way home, Pinocchio meets a fox and a cat .
- **S18**：The Cat pretends to be blind, and the Fox pretends to be lame; a white blackbird tries to warn Pinocchio of their lies, but the Cat eats it.
- **S19**：The two convince Pinocchio that if he plants his coins in the Field of Miracles outside the city of Catchfools , they will grow into a tree with gold coins.
- **S20**：They stop for dinner at the Red Lobster Inn where they trick Pinocchio into paying for their meals and flee after telling the innkeeper to tell Pinocchio they would meet him at the Field of Miracles in the morning.
- **S21**：As Pinocchio sets off for the city of Catchfools, the ghost of the Talking Cricket appears and tells him to go home and give the coins to his father.
- **S22**：Pinocchio ignores his warnings again and as he passes through a forest, the Fox and Cat, disguised as bandits , ambush and attempt to rob him but fail.
- **S23**：After biting off the Cat's paw, he escapes to a white house, where he is greeted by a young fairy with turquoise hair who says she is dead and waiting for a hearse.
- **S24**：Unfortunately, the Fox and the Cat catch Pinocchio and hang him in a tree and leave after getting tired of waiting for him to suffocate, leaving Pinocchio to die alone and think about his misbehavior.
- **S25**：The Fairy has Pinocchio rescued and calls in three doctors to evaluate him: The Owl says he is alive, the Crow says he is dead, and the Ghost of the Talking Cricket says he is fine, but has been disobedient and hurt his father.
- **S26**：The Fairy offers medicine to Pinocchio, which he takes when several Undertaker rabbits appear to collect his body.
- **S27**：Now recovered, Pinocchio lies to the Fairy when she asks what happened to the gold coins, and his nose grows.
- **S28**：The Fairy explains that Pinocchio's lies are causing his nose to grow and calls in a flock of woodpeckers to chisel it down to size.
- **S29**：She then sends for Geppetto to come and live with them in the forest cottage.
- **S30**：As Pinocchio heads out to find Geppetto, he once again meets the Fox and the Cat, who remind him of the Field of Miracles, and he agrees to go with them and plant his gold.
- **S31**：Once there, Pinocchio buries his coins and leaves to wait twenty minutes for it to grow, only for the Fox and the Cat to dig up the coins and run away.
- **S32**：Once Pinocchio returns, a parrot mocks him for falling for the Fox and Cat's tricks.
- **S33**：Pinocchio rushes to the Catchfools courthouse, where he reports the theft of the coins to a gorilla judge .
- **S34**：Although he is moved by Pinocchio's plea, the gorilla judge sentences him to four months in prison for the crime of foolishness.
- **S35**：Fortunately, all criminals are released early when the Emperor of Catchfools declares a celebration following his army's victory over the town's enemies.
- **S36**：As Pinocchio heads back to the forest, he finds an enormous snake with a smoking tail blocking the way.
- **S37**：He asks the serpent to move, but it remains completely still, and he assumes it is dead.
- **S38**：He begins to step over it, but it suddenly rises up and hisses at him, toppling him over onto his head.
- **S39**：Struck by Pinocchio's fright and comical position, the snake laughs so hard he bursts an artery and dies.
- **S40**：Pinocchio then heads back to the Fairy's house in the forest, where he sneaks into a farmer's yard to steal some grapes.
- **S41**：He is caught in a weasel trap and encounters a glowworm .
- **S42**：The farmer finds him and ties him up in his doghouse.
- **S43**：When Pinocchio foils the chicken-stealing weasels , the farmer frees him as a reward.
- **S44**：Pinocchio finally returns to the cottage, but there he finds nothing but a gravestone, and believes the Fairy has died.
- **S45**：A friendly pigeon sees Pinocchio mourning the Fairy's death and offers to give him a ride to the seashore, where Geppetto is building a boat to search for Pinocchio.
- **S46**：Pinocchio is washed ashore when he tries to swim to his father, who is swallowed by The Terrible Dogfish .
- **S47**：Pinocchio accepts a ride from a dolphin to the Island of Busy Bees where everyone works every day.
- **S48**：Upon arriving there, Pinocchio offers to carry a lady's jug home in return for food and water, and upon arriving at her house, he recognizes the lady as the Fairy, now old enough to be his mother.
- **S49**：She says she will act as his mother, and he will begin going to school.
- **S50**：She hints that if he does well in school and is good for one year, then he will become a real boy.
- **S51**：Pinocchio studies hard and rises to the top of his class, making the other boys jealous.
- **S52**：They trick Pinocchio into playing hooky by saying they saw a large sea monster at the beach, the same one that swallowed Geppetto.

- **S54**：## Characters
- **S55**：[ edit ] Pinocchio – A marionette who gains wisdom through a series of misadventures that lead him to becoming a real human as reward for his good deeds.
- **S56**：Geppetto – An elderly, impoverished woodcarver and the creator and father figure of Pinocchio.
- **S57**：He wears a yellow wig that resembles cornmeal mush (or polendina ), and because of this the children of the neighborhood and some adults call him "Polendina", which greatly annoys him.
- **S58**："Geppetto" is a syncopated form of Giuseppetto , which in turn is a diminutive of the name Giuseppe ( Italian for Joseph ).
- **S59**：The Talking Cricket ( il Grillo Parlante ) – A cricket whom Pinocchio kills after it tries to give him some advice, and later returns as a ghost to continue advising him.
- **S60**：The Fairy with Turquoise Hair ( la Fata dai capelli turchini ) – The spirit of the forest, who rescues Pinocchio and adopts him first as her brother, then as her son.
- **S61**：The Snail ( la Lumaca ) – A snail who works for the Fairy with Turquoise Hair.
- **S62**：Pinocchio later gives all his money to the Snail by their next encounter.
- **S63**：Candlewick aka "Lucignolo" – A juvenile delinquent who is as tall and thin as a wick .
- **S64**：He is Pinocchio's best friend, but also serves as a bad influence for him.
- **S65**：His nickname translates to either "Lampwick" or "Candlewick" because of his lanky physique.
- **S66**：The Coachman ( l'Omino ) – The owner of the Land of Toys, who takes naughty, disobedient, and lazy children there on his stagecoach, which is pulled by twenty-four donkeys with white shoes on their hooves.
- **S67**：Once the children who visit turn into donkeys, he sells them for labor.
- **S68**：The Terrible Dogfish ( Il terribile Pesce-cane ) – A mile-long, five-story-high fish.
- **S69**：Pescecane , while literally meaning "dog fish", generally means "shark" in Italian.
- **S70**：Mangiafuoco ("Fire-Eater" in English) – The wealthy director of the Great Marionette Theater, who has red eyes and a black beard that reaches to the floor, with his mouth being "as wide as an oven [with] teeth like yellow fangs".
- **S71**：Despite his appearance, Mangiafuoco is not evil.
- **S72**：The Green Fisherman ( il Pescatore verde ) – A green-skinned ogre on the Island of Busy Bees who catches Pinocchio in his fishing net and attempts to eat him while thinking that he is a "puppet fish".
- **S73**：According to Giacomo Maria Prati, The Green Fisherman is one example of the story's parallels with classical mythology , stating that the Fisherman is evocative of the cyclops Polyphemus of Homer 's Odyssey .
- **S74**：He also writes that the Green Fisherman represents murder through ignorance, referring to the fact that he tries to eat Pinocchio, unaware he was not an edible fish. [ 7 ] The Fox and the Cat ( la Volpe e il Gatto ) – Greedy anthropomorphic animals who pretend to be lame and blind respectively.
- **S75**：Throughout the story, they lead Pinocchio astray, rob him, and eventually try to hang him.
- **S76**：Mastro Antonio ( [ anˈtɔːnjo ] in Italian, / ɑː n ˈ t oʊ n j oʊ / ahn- TOH -nyoh in English) – An elderly carpenter , who finds the log that eventually becomes Pinocchio, planning to make it into a table leg until it cries out "Please be careful!" The children call Antonio "Mastro Ciliegia (cherry)" because of his red nose.
- **S77**：Arlecchino , Pulcinella , and Signora Rosaura – Marionettes at the theater who embrace Pinocchio as their brother.

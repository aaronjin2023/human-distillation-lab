- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Tana Roberts, coming of age in the turbulent 1960s, has ambitions of an important career.
- **S3**：Her feelings about love and marriage have been shaped by her mother's role as a married man's longtime mistress.
- **S4**：Tana's only sexual encounter with a male involved her surviving rape at his hands.
- **S5**：Combined with what she sees as her mother's intentional dependence on a man (her boss, Arthur Durning) who won't commit to Jean even after his wife dies - at least, not until his failing health means he needs a full-time caretaker, Tana chooses not to be dependent on a man for her happiness in life.
- **S6**：It certainly doesn't help Tana's outlook that her rapist is Arthur's son, Billy, and that Arthur has been her de facto step-father throughout her childhood.
- **S7**：When Tana tells Jean what happened, Jean calls her a liar.
- **S8**：Leaving New York to attend Green Hill Junior College in South Carolina , Tana rooms with the only African-American student on campus, Sharon Blake.
- **S9**：Sharon's brother is lynched , and Sharon herself is later murdered.
- **S10**：Following Sharon's death, Tana becomes active in the Civil Rights movement .
- **S11**：Her involvement leads to her expulsion .
- **S12**：Back in New York, Tana meets Harry Winslow IV, the polar opposite of Billy Durning - except that they're both rich.
- **S13**：Tana's relationship with Harry is platonic, and stays that way even after he returns from Vietnam , much to Jean's displeasure.
- **S14**：Harry, though, has feelings for Tana that he never acts on, while she is attracted to Harry's father, Harrison Winslow III.
- **S15**：Harrison won't get involved with Tana - even though he's fallen in love with her too - because he's aware of Harry's feelings.
- **S16**：Tana enrolls at Berkeley to study law, and also becomes involved with a violent anti-war activist .
- **S17**：Jean implores Tana to get married and have children, but Tana is set on her career.
- **S18**：She achieves her goals in record time, too, graduating law school and becoming an ADA in San Francisco in just a few short years, then shortly afterward, a judge.
- **S19**：As Harry and his wife celebrate the birth of their first child, Tana finally begins to wonder what family life must be like.
- **S20**：Her perspective on happiness begins to turn to the more traditional route her mother has been advocating: marriage to a man.
- **S21**：Eventually, she marries the judge who swore her in, Russ Carver.

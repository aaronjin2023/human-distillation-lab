- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] The story is set in the provincial city of Corrientes , part of the Argentine Littoral , on the shore of the Paraná River .
- **S3**：Eduardo Plarr is an unmarried medical doctor of English descent who, as a boy, fled to Buenos Aires with his Paraguayan mother to escape the political turmoil of Paraguay .
- **S4**：His English father remained in Paraguay as a political rebel and, aside from a single hand-delivered letter, they never hear from him again.
- **S5**：When Plarr moves to Corrientes, a quiet, subtropical backwater, he meets the two other English inhabitants: Humphries, a bitter elderly English teacher; and Charles Fortnum, the unimportant British honorary consul , a divorced, self-pitying alcoholic who abuses his position for gain.
- **S6**：Plarr also meets Julio Saavedra, a forgotten but self-important Argentine writer of novels full of silent machismo .
- **S7**：Visiting the town brothel with Saavedra, Plarr is attracted to a girl, Clara, but she is taken by another man.
- **S8**：Later, when he is called to treat Fortnum's new wife, Plarr recognizes Clara.
- **S9**：Although he has never been to England, Plarr regards himself as a cool, self-controlled Englishman.
- **S10**：Nonetheless, he finds himself obsessed by Clara and seduces her with a pair of highly decorative sunglasses.
- **S11**：They then begin an affair in which he remains emotionally distant from her.
- **S12**："Caring is the only dangerous thing," Plarr says in the novel.
- **S13**："Love was a claim which he wouldn't meet, a responsibility he would refuse to accept, a demand.
- **S14**：So many times his mother had used the word when he was a child; it was like the threat of an armed robber.
- **S15**：'Put up your hands or else ...' Something was always asked in return: obedience, an apology, a kiss which one had no desire to give." When Clara becomes pregnant Fortnum, believing the child is his, drinks less.
- **S16**：Two of Plarr's friends from his Paraguayan Jesuit schooldays turn up at his surgery.
- **S17**：One is Leon Rivas, a married priest, now defrocked, the other Aquino, a failed lawyer.
- **S18**：They tell Plarr his father is alive and in a jail in Paraguay, and that they have a plot for which a doctor's assistance is needed to kidnap the US Ambassador on his trip to Corrientes to see ruins.
- **S19**：They intend to ransom the ambassador for the release of political prisoners in Paraguay, including Plarr's father.
- **S20**：However, they are incompetent and mistakenly kidnap Fortnum instead.
- **S21**：Meanwhile, Colonel Perez of the Federal Police questions Plarr on the kidnapping.
- **S22**：Rivas and Aquino take Plarr to a squalid hut in a shanty town while bargaining with the authorities.
- **S23**：Plarr has been taken to the hut to treat Fortnum, who has been shot in the leg while attempting to escape, lies drinking whisky.
- **S24**：Fortnum spends much of his time, as he faces up to his impending death, sentimentalizing about Clara and remembering the fearsome figure of his father.
- **S25**：He overhears Plarr confessing his adultery with Clara and that the child is Plarr's.
- **S26**：Army paratroopers surround the hut while the failed priest, Rivas, conducts a makeshift mass inside with the rain coming down.
- **S27**：When the police deadline is about to expire, Plarr goes out to talk with them, with Leon shooting him in his leg.
- **S28**：The paratroopers shoot Plarr, Leon and Aquino dead, with Plarr's death blamed on the kidnappers.
- **S29**：The surviving kidnappers and Rivas's wife are captured, and Fortnum restored to Clara.
- **S30**：Plarr's mother, once a beauty and now bloated, along with some of his previous mistresses, attends his funeral.
- **S31**：Saavedra reads a homily.
- **S32**：The British Embassy then relieves Fortnum of his consulship and tell him he will be appointed an OBE in the next honours list.
- **S33**：In the last scene, Fortnum and Clara reconcile and name the child Eduardo, after Plarr.

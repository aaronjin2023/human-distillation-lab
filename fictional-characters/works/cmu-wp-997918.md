### Tom Mullen

- **性格倾向**：在营救受挫时表现强硬，倾向顶住亲友反对坚持自己的判断（行为推断；S14、S17、S19）

### Det. Jimmy Shaker

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-474d20cabef8e43c` · 独立版本：`profile-650cfc7a4f6f980f`

- **性格倾向**：在同伙暴露风险时表现控制欲，倾向以持枪威吓维持主导（行为推断；S6）
- **核心动机**：赎金受阻后转而谋取悬赏，持续追求绑架带来的金钱收益（摘要直接叙述；S20、S24、S34）
- **价值取舍**：为获利和掩盖身份，愿意牺牲同伙并威胁无辜者生命（行为推断；S24、S28、S30、S38）
- **决策方式**：原有计划失效后，利用警察身份制造自己救人的假象（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25、S26、S30）
- **沟通方式**：通过变声和电话枪声操纵家属对危险程度的判断（行为推断；S8、S22、S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以警察身份扮演救援者，同时正是绑架的主谋

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：While multi-millionaire Tom Mullen and his wife Kate attend a science fair, their son Sean is kidnapped.
- **S2**：Sean is taken to an apartment by Maris, a caterer for the Mullens, along with criminals Clark, Cubby and Miles.
- **S3**：Tom and Kate receive an e-mail from the kidnappers demanding $2,000,000.
- **S4**：Tom calls the FBI, who begin operating from his New York City penthouse.
- **S5**：Detective Shaker of the NYPD is arresting a perpetrator in a convenience store when Cubby buys some FrankenBerry cereal.
- **S6**：Shaker follows Cubby into the building where the kidnappers are residing, sees Sean tied in a bedroom, and holds Cubby at gunpoint, berating him for being so obvious; Shaker is the mastermind behind the kidnapping.
- **S7**：Tom agrees to the FBI instructions for delivering the ransom.
- **S8**：Receiving a phone call from Shaker, who is electronically disguising his voice, Tom follows his instructions.
- **S9**：He meets Cubby in a New Jersey quarry.
- **S10**：Shaker indicated that Cubby would give Tom an address where Sean could be picked up in exchange for the money, but when Cubby appears ignorant of these instructions and simply demands the cash, Tom refuses to hand it over.
- **S11**：The FBI sends a helicopter after Cubby and Cubby opens fire.
- **S12**：FBI marksmen kill Cubby before he reveals Sean's location.
- **S13**：Shaker sets up another ransom drop.
- **S14**：Angered that the FBI followed him instead of the money and killed his only lead, Tom insists on taking the money without being tracked.
- **S15**：Realizing the first drop was a setup and there is no guarantee that Sean will come back alive, Tom goes on the television news and offers the ransom as a bounty.
- **S16**：The only way for the kidnappers to save themselves is to return his son, in which case he will withdraw the bounty and drop all charges.
- **S17**：Despite the pleadings of Kate and FBI Special Agent Lonnie Hawkins, Tom sticks to his plan, believing it is the best chance of having Sean returned.
- **S18**：Shaker lures Kate to a meeting where he tells her to pay the ransom or Sean will die.
- **S19**：Tom responds by increasing the bounty to $4,000,000.
- **S20**：Shaker calls Tom and demands to be paid.
- **S21**：Tom replies that Sean must be returned or else the kidnappers will be hunted down.
- **S22**：Tom hears Sean's voice screaming for his father.
- **S23**：Shaker fires a shot heard over the phone before the call cuts off, leaving Tom and Kate believing their son is dead.
- **S24**：Shaker forms a new plan; he will kill Clark and Miles, pretend to rescue Sean and claim the bounty for himself and Maris.
- **S25**：He calls in a possible kidnapping to the NYPD, requesting backup.
- **S26**：He then flashes his badge and gun at Clark and Miles, who realize he has betrayed them.
- **S27**：Clark tries running Shaker over with his van, but Shaker fatally shoots him.
- **S28**：He then kills Miles, making it look as though Miles shot first.
- **S29**：Maris, trying to back out of the plan, shoots Shaker in the shoulder.
- **S30**：Before she can fire again, Shaker kills her and re-enters the apartment just before a SWAT team charges in, finding a wounded Shaker sitting next to Sean.
- **S31**：Hawkins takes Tom and Kate to the scene of Sean's rescue.
- **S32**：Tom thanks Shaker, who is being hospitalized.
- **S33**：Hawkins takes Tom to Maris' body, where he recognizes her.
- **S34**：Shaker appears at the Mullen penthouse to receive the reward, claiming he wants to avoid publicity.
- **S35**：As Tom writes a check for him, Sean recognizes Shaker's voice.
- **S36**：Seeing his son's horrified reaction and hearing Shaker speak the same phrase kidnapper did, Tom realizes who Shaker is.
- **S37**：Tom gives Shaker the check with a false signature, but Shaker realizes that Tom is on to him.
- **S38**：Shaker threatens to kill everyone in the house, but Tom convinces him to take him hostage and go to the bank so Tom can wire the money to Shaker's account.
- **S39**：En route, Shaker orders Tom to ready his private jet.
- **S40**：Tom fakes a call to his pilot, actually calling Hawkins, whom Tom manages to tell covertly that the kidnapper is with him.
- **S41**：Cops converge on Tom and Shaker outside the bank.
- **S42**：Shaker opens fire, shooting both officers.
- **S43**：Tom lunges at Shaker, grabbing his gun.
- **S44**：Tom throws Shaker through a store window and when they emerge, they are surrounded by armed policemen.
- **S45**：Shaker draws his back-up revolver from an ankle holster.
- **S46**：Tom and Hawkins fire first, killing Shaker.
- **S47**：The film closes as Hawkins stops the police from arresting Tom.

## 角色资料

### Alan Grant

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d88213a83729fbe3` · 独立版本：`profile-548adf6d2de6c222`

- **性格倾向**：在逃生危机中，他仍表现出陪伴并照料两个孩子的责任倾向。（行为推断；S18、S28、S30、S40）
- **核心动机**：园区失控后，他努力带孩子脱险并推动众人获得外界救援。（行为推断；S18、S28、S40、S43）
- **决策方式**：逃生途中，他采用攀树避险和联络外界救援等具体办法。（行为推断；S28、S43）
- **沟通方式**：面对孩子对恐龙食性的疑问，他用直接解释回应其担忧。（行为推断；S30）
- **关系模式**：他与两个孩子在逃亡中共同避险，并带他们返回游客中心。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S28、S39、S40）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、成长变化

### Ellie Sattler

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-d555cfae455e1ef6` · 独立版本：`profile-24e43a50c6663bd4`

- **性格倾向**：面对动物患病和人员失联，她倾向亲自留下照料或前往查看。（行为推断；S11、S12、S33）
- **核心动机**：在安全系统失效的危机中，她参与恢复园区供电与系统。（行为推断；S31、S32、S33、S37）
- **价值取舍**：风暴将至时，她仍将照料患病动物置于随众返回车辆之前。（行为推断；S11、S12）
- **决策方式**：阿诺德迟迟未归时，她选择与马尔杜恩前往维修地堡。（行为推断；S32、S33）
- **关系模式**：她与马尔杜恩共同搜寻幸存者，并在前往地堡时分工行动。（摘要直接叙述；S24、S35、S36）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Billionaire John Hammond, CEO of InGen, has created Jurassic Park: a theme park populated with dinosaurs cloned from the DNA extracted from insects preserved in prehistoric amber.
- **S2**：After a park worker is killed by a Velociraptor, Hammond's investors, represented by their lawyer Donald Gennaro, demand that experts visit the park and certify that it is safe.
- **S3**：Gennaro invites Dr.
- **S4**：Ian Malcolm, a mathematician, while Hammond invites paleontologist Dr.
- **S5**：Alan Grant and paleobotanist Dr.
- **S6**：Ellie Sattler.
- **S7**：They are joined on the island by Hammond's grandchildren, Tim and Lex Murphy.
- **S8**：Hammond asks Malcolm, Grant, and Sattler what their thoughts are about recreating dinosaur species.
- **S9**：They debate the ethics of cloning extinct dinosaurs, with Gennaro being the only one to express optimism.
- **S10**：The group sets off to explore the park while Hammond observes his guests along with Head Technician Ray Arnold and game warden Robert Muldoon.
- **S11**：Grant spots a sick Triceratops and the group investigates.
- **S12**：With a storm heading in, everyone returns to their vehicles except for Sattler, who stays with the park doctor to look after the animal.
- **S13**：Jurassic Park's head computer programmer, Dennis Nedry, is secretly in the employ of one of InGen's corporate rivals, and has been paid to steal dinosaur embryos.
- **S14**：During his theft, Nedry deactivates the park's security system, allowing him access to the embryo storage.
- **S15**：Electric fences around the park are deactivated as a result, releasing a Tyrannosaurus.
- **S16**：The T-Rex assaults the vehicle with Tim and Lex inside.
- **S17**：Gennaro flees the vehicle and is subsequently eaten.
- **S18**：Tim, Lex and Grant flee down a steep embankment and escape.
- **S19**：Malcolm is injured distracting the T-Rex but survives the attack.
- **S20**：A fleeing Nedry crashes his Jeep.
- **S21**：While he is tying the Jeep's winch around a tree, he encounters a Dilophosaurus, which he ignores due to its small size.
- **S22**：As he returns to the Jeep, the creature spits venom at him, blinding him.
- **S23**：Nedry drops the stolen embryos and gets into the Jeep, but the Dilophosaurus gets inside and kills him.
- **S24**：Sattler and Muldoon search for survivors of the Tyrannosaurus attack, but only find Malcolm and the remains of Gennaro.
- **S25**：As they look for the children, Malcolm realizes the Tyrannosaurus is returning.
- **S26**：He orders Sattler and Muldoon to flee, with the Tyrannosaurus pursuing.
- **S27**：The three of them escape in their Jeep.
- **S28**：Grant and the children climb up a tree to avoid the Tyrannosaurus, and see a family of Brachiosaurus eating at which point Dr Grant attempts to emmulate their call to no avail and so the three fall asleep.
- **S29**：In the morning Dr Grant wakes up and is surprised to see a Brachiosaurus eating from the tree they are sleeping in and thus wakes up the kids, much to Lex surprise at seeing the dinosaur.
- **S30**：Asking if it eats meat Dr Grant replies it doesn't and so the three attempt to feed the creature, getting it closer with a branch Dr Grant touches the dinosaur but when Lex tries it steps back and sneezes on her before departing.
- **S31**：Meanwhile unable to decipher Dennis's code to reactivate the security fences, Hammond, Arnold, Muldoon, Malcolm and Sattler take the drastic measure of rebooting the entire park's computer and electrical network.
- **S32**：The five shut down the park's grid and retreat to an emergency bunker, and Arnold journeys to a maintenance bunker to complete the process of rebooting the system.
- **S33**：When he does not return, Sattler and Muldoon decide to head for the bunker.
- **S34**：Grant and the children discover a nest full of hatched eggs, indicating the dinosaurs are breeding despite having been bred as females.
- **S35**：As Muldoon and Sattler proceed to the maintenance bunker, Muldoon notices that they are being hunted by Velociraptors.
- **S36**：Muldoon offers to draw their attention while Sattler continues to the bunker.
- **S37**：Sattler restarts the park's systems, but is attacked by a raptor hidden within some cables; she discovers Arnold's severed arm and narrowly escapes the raptor.
- **S38**：Muldoon is about to shoot a raptor, but another raptor appears and kills him.
- **S39**：Tim, Lex and Grant climb an electrified fence out of the park's animal zone and Tim is nearly killed when the fence is reactivated.
- **S40**：Grant and the children head for the visitor's center; he leaves them alone in the kitchen while he reunites with Sattler and the others.
- **S41**：The kids escape two stalking raptors before reuniting with Grant and Sattler.
- **S42**：Lex restores the park's security systems from the control room.
- **S43**：Grant contacts Hammond and tells him to call the mainland for rescue before the two raptors find the group and attack.
- **S44**：The group flees through a series of air ducts, only to be cornered in the entrance hall by the raptors.
- **S45**：The Tyrannosaurus breaks into the main hall and attacks the raptors, allowing the four to flee outside, where they are rescued by Malcolm and Hammond.
- **S46**：The survivors board a helicopter.
- **S47**：Grant and Sattler watch a small flock of pelicans fly over the sea as they depart.

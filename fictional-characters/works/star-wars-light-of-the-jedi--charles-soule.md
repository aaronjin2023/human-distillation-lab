## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] The Legacy Run, a cargo and passenger ship, speeds through hyperspace and, while attempting to avoid a collision with an unexpected obstacle, shatters into many pieces.
- **S3**：The debris from the large vessel begins to emerge from hyperspace at different locations and points in time across the galaxy, causing chaos in shipping routes and disastrous loss of life where the pieces impact occupied planets.
- **S4**：The Jedi, led by Avar Kriss, attempt to aid the citizens of the Hetzal System to escape disaster; they use the Force to reroute a piece of the Legacy Run containing dangerous gas away from impacting a sun, which would have caused a supernova explosion.
- **S5**：Republic Chancellor Lina Soh closes the hyperspace lanes.
- **S6**：With the opening ceremonies of the newly completed Starlight Beacon only a few weeks away, she tasks the Jedi with discovering the cause of the "emergences" and finding a way to predict the next events.
- **S7**：As they search the galaxy for answers, the pirate group known as the Nihil seek to take advantage of the closed hyperspace lanes.
- **S8**：Marchion Ro, who holds the title of the Eye of the Nihil, has devised a way to predict the path of the next "emergence," and manipulates the Nihil leaders, the Tempest Runners, into a scheme to blackmail the planets who will soon be destroyed by the debris.
- **S9**：The Tempest Runners soon begin to fight with each other and greed overtakes them.
- **S10**：One of them reveals his presence to the Republic during a failed blackmail attempt, and the Nihil are confronted in space by Republic and Jedi fighters.
- **S11**：Marchion uses the infighting to seize control of the Nihil organization.
- **S12**：Marchion sends Nihil forces to the planet Elphrona to kidnap a wealthy family.
- **S13**：Jedi Master Loden Greatstorm and his Padawan Bell Zettifar foil the attempt, but Loden is captured by the Nihil.
- **S14**：Marchion confesses to Loden that he orchestrated the Legacy Run's hyperspace accident, the "emergences," and the Jedi's rescue of the family on Elphrona, in order to create the chaos which led to his new control of the Nihil, as well as to capture a Jedi.
- **S15**：He has more plans for the Jedi and the Nihil that have yet to be revealed.
- **S16**：When the "emergences" cease, Soh re-opens the hyperspace lanes and launches the opening of the Starlight Beacon in an attempt to restore hope to the galaxy.

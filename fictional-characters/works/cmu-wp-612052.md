## 角色资料

### Peter Parker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a121e7e75a551c9c` · 独立版本：`profile-182d4e73580a5bb9`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中易受责任与私人愿望拉扯，也会重新承担救援风险。（行为推断；S1、S4、S18、S24、S33）
- **核心动机**：意识到城市对蜘蛛侠的需要超过个人抱负后，决定恢复身份。（摘要直接叙述；S24）
- **价值取舍**：经历退出与反思后，明确将城市需要置于自己的个人抱负之前。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S24）
- **决策方式**：先因压力放弃身份，后决定回归，并在能力恢复后取回战衣。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S24、S25、S29）
- **关系模式**：与玛丽简的关系曾因英雄职责疏远，退出期间开始尝试修复。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S19）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Otto Octavius

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-f15fe18f3caa4fa4` · 独立版本：`profile-43403387f07cff7e`

- **性格倾向**：在实验危机中表现出强烈执着，受机械臂影响后更不顾后果。（行为推断；S6、S8、S11）
- **核心动机**：梦想完善聚变能源，受机械臂影响后决意不计道德代价完成实验。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S11）
- **决策方式**：实验失控时拒绝停止，后来摆脱机械臂控制后选择摧毁反应堆。（摘要直接叙述；S7、S8、S43）
- **价值取舍**：恢复自主后的最后行动中，将阻止反应堆灾难置于自身生存之上。（行为推断；S43）
- **成长变化**：从受机械臂影响而不计代价，转为恢复自主后亲手终结实验。（摘要直接叙述；S11、S43）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他曾不顾道德代价执意完成实验，最终却牺牲自己将反应堆淹没。

### Harry Osborn

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-da840783683b5033` · 独立版本：`profile-7148aac1f5d9acb3`

- **性格倾向**：在面对蜘蛛侠及其身份真相时，表现出愤怒、犹疑与强烈震动。（行为推断；S37、S38、S46、S47）
- **核心动机**：为将蜘蛛侠带到自己面前，愿以实验所需的氚同奥克塔维乌斯交易。（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **决策方式**：以提供氚换取活捉蜘蛛侠，同时明确要求对方不要伤害彼得。（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **关系模式**：既与彼得是疏远的好友，又怨恨蜘蛛侠，得知两者同一时震惊。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S27、S38）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：要求捕获蜘蛛侠，却又要求保护尚未被他认出身份的彼得。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{plot}}  Peter Parker struggles to balance his crime-fighting duties as Spider-Man with the demands of his normal life.
- **S2**：Parker is estranged from both love interest Mary Jane Watson and best friend Harry Osborn, who still harbors a resentment against Spider-Man.
- **S3**：His obligations as Spider-Man bear a burden on his ability to focus on his academic life as well as his financial situation.
- **S4**：As a result, Peter starts to think about giving up being Spider-Man.
- **S5**：Harry, now head of Oscorp's research division, sponsors the research of brilliant nuclear scientist Otto Octavius.
- **S6**：Octavius, who dreams of perfecting fusion power, wears a harness of powerful robotic arms with artificial intelligence for an experiment in sustained fusion.
- **S7**：Though the experiment succeeds at first, it quickly becomes unstable.
- **S8**：Octavius refuses to shut it down, before Spider-Man intervenes.
- **S9**：Octavius' wife is killed, the neural inhibitor chip which prevents the arms from influencing his mind is fried, and the arms are fused to his spine.
- **S10**：He is taken to a hospital, but the tentacles, having developed their own artificial intelligence with the inhibitor chip destroyed, savagely kill the medical crew.
- **S11**：The tentacles corrupt Octavius' mind, and lead him to the resolution that he must complete his experiment regardless of the moral cost.
- **S12**：To finance his work, Octavius - now called Doctor Octopus or "Doc Ock" - robs a bank, where he takes Aunt May hostage.
- **S13**：When Doc Ock is about to stab Spider-Man with his spear, May smashes a sunglasses lens with her umbrella, sparing Spider-Man, but Doc Ock drops her from a building.
- **S14**：Spider-Man manages to save his aunt, but Ock returns to his lair with loot stolen from the bank.
- **S15**：During a party, Peter learns that Mary Jane is engaged to J.
- **S16**：Jonah Jameson's son, renowned astronaut John Jameson, and gets into a fight with Harry over his "loyalty" to Spider-Man.
- **S17**：As Ock rebuilds his experimental reactor, Peter's powers prove unreliable.
- **S18**：After a doctor tells him that his physical problems are due to mental stress, he gives up being Spider-Man and disposes of his costume.
- **S19**：During this time, Peter begins to succeed in college and begins to mend his relationship with Mary Jane.
- **S20**：A garbage man brings Spider-Man's discarded costume to sell at the Daily Bugle.
- **S21**：Jameson takes credit for Spider-Man's disappearance, but later admits that he was indeed a hero, and the only person capable of stopping Ock.
- **S22**：Peter, out of guilt, tells May that it was his fault for his uncle Ben's death.
- **S23**：After Peter tells his aunt everything from the thief to Ben's death, May forgives Peter and advises him that sometimes it is necessary to sacrifice one's dreams for the greater good.
- **S24**：Realizing that the city's need for Spider-Man is greater than his personal ambitions, Peter decides to become Spider-Man once again.
- **S25**：Even so, his powers continue to be lost.
- **S26**：Ock, having restored his project, needs tritium for his reactor, and goes to Harry to get it.
- **S27**：Harry agrees to give Octavius all the tritium he possesses in exchange for bringing Spider-Man to him, and tells him that Peter, who is supposedly good friends with Spider-Man, is the key to finding him; however, he tells Ock not to harm Peter.
- **S28**：Ock finds Peter and tells him to find Spider-Man, and abducts Mary Jane in the process.
- **S29**：Following this, Peter finds that his powers have been restored, and he dons his costume again after stealing it back from the Bugle.
- **S30**：A furious Jameson continues to denounce Spider-Man as a menace.
- **S31**：Spider-Man then proceeds to battle Ock across town.
- **S32**：Eventually, they battle on a train which Ock disables.
- **S33**：Spider-Man attempts to stop the runaway train - successfully saving everyone on board.
- **S34**：When people on the train see him unmasked, they are shocked to see that he is only a young man.
- **S35**：Two boys hand Peter his mask and promise not to reveal his true identity.
- **S36**：Ock returns, knocks Spider-Man unconscious and delivers him to Harry.
- **S37**：After giving Ock the tritium, Harry pulls out the knife and prepares to kill Spider-Man, but he first relents and unmasks him.
- **S38**：He is put in the shock when he discovers that Peter is Spider-Man.
- **S39**：Peter wakes up, and convinces Harry to reveal Ock's whereabouts, before leaving him in tears.
- **S40**：Spider-Man makes his way to Ock's waterfront laboratory, with the doctor putting the finishing touches on his makeshift reactor.
- **S41**：Although Spider-Man attempts to rescue Mary Jane discreetly, Ock catches on and they fight once more.
- **S42**：Spider-Man ultimately subdues Ock and reveals his identity to him, pleading for a way to stop the reactor.
- **S43**：Free from the tentacles' AI, Octavius uses his mechanical arms to collapse the building, successfully drowning the reactor at the cost of his own life.
- **S44**：Mary Jane sees Peter unmasked and understands that as long as he is Spider-Man, she and Peter cannot be together.
- **S45**：Spider-Man returns Mary Jane to John and leaves.
- **S46**：Meanwhile, Harry, shocked by the trauma caused by revelation of Peter's identity as Spider-Man, has visions of his father Norman in a hanging mirror telling him to avenge his death.
- **S47**：Enraged, Harry shatters the mirror, revealing a secret room containing the Green Goblin's equipment .
- **S48**：He looks at the equipment and takes the Green serum his father did.
- **S49**：Meanwhile, Mary Jane is getting ready for her wedding, but she has a change of mind, and leaves John on the altar and arrives back at Peter's apartment.
- **S50**：She tells Peter that she wants to be with him no matter what.
- **S51**：After a passionate kiss, a fire truck is heard and Mary Jane encourages Peter to respond as Spider-Man, quietly apprehensive of the dangers they are about to face as a couple.

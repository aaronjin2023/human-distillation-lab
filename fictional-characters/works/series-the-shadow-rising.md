- **S41**：After his revival by Rand, Mat dons a black scarf to hide the scars around his neck.
- **S42**：Moiraine visits the three-hooped ter'angreal used by the Wise Ones, gaining limited knowledge of the future.
- **S43**：The Wise Ones task Aviendha with teaching Rand the Aiel customs as they travel to Cold Rocks Hold.
- **S44**：They are met en route by Darkfriends and the Forsaken Lanfear and Asmodean disguised as merchants.
- **S45**：After arrival at Al'cair Dal , both Rand al'Thor and Couladin of the Shaido Aiel claim to be the messiah 'He Who Comes With the Dawn'.
- **S46**：To validate his claim, Rand reveals the secret history of the Aiel during the Age of Legends, but this incites an uproar among the Aiel.
- **S47**：Rand prevents a riot by summoning a rainstorm to the Aiel Waste.
- **S48**：Asmodean uses the momentary in-fighting amongst the Aiel as cover to seek out the ter'angreal access keys to the Choedan Kal (the most powerful sa'angreal ever created).
- **S49**：Rand chases Asmodean to Rhuidean where both simultaneously grasp the access key.
- **S50**：The sudden power surge enables Rand to sever Asmodean's link to the Dark One (which marks Asmodean as a traitor to the rest of the Forsaken).
- **S51**：Asmodean begrudgingly relents to teach Rand how to channel the One Power after Lanfear hobbles Asmodean's own access to it.
- **S52**：Rand returns to Al'cair Dal to find most of the Aiel acknowledging him as the Car'a'carn.
- **S53**：The Two Rivers [ edit ] Padan Fain drives the Children of the Light to the Two Rivers to exact vengeance on Rand.
- **S54**：The Shadow deploys Slayer , a mysterious and powerful agent together with a large host of Trollocs to kill Fain, endangering the Two Rivers.
- **S55**：Perrin's homecoming is dashed with tragedy when he learns Trollocs have killed his family.
- **S56**：Perrin rallies new allies; Verin Mathwin , Alanna Mosvani , Aes Sedai searching for potential pupils, and the Tinker Aram (who has abandoned his people's pacifism ) to fight the Trollocs.
- **S57**：It is revealed later that Fain killed Perrin's family.
- **S58**：With the help of Tam al'Thor (Rand's adoptive father) and Abell Cauthon (Mat's father), Perrin convinces the people of the Two Rivers to fight for themselves.
- **S59**：Perrin leads them against the Trollocs and earns the nicknames 'Lord Perrin' and 'Perrin Goldeneyes.' Before the showdown with the Trollocs, Perrin marries Faile and tasks her with travelling to Caemlyn and asking Queen Morgase to lend soldiers in their fight.
- **S60**：The Children of the Light, led by Dain Bornhald, interject themselves and demand Perrin's arrest to try him for the murders of other Whitecloaks.
- **S61**：Perrin agrees to give himself up if the Children will fight alongside the Two Rivers when the Trollocs attack.
- **S62**：Slayer is killing wolves in Tel'aran'rhiod but retreats when he is attacked and defeated by Perrin.
- **S63**：The Trollocs are defeated by the Two Rivers forces.
- **S64**：The Children of the Light reneged on their agreement and sat out the battle, so Perrin and the Two Rivers folk force the Children to leave.
- **S65**：With his plans ruined, Fain departs for the White Tower.
- **S66**：Tanchico in Tarabon [ edit ] Nynaeve and Elayne, along with Thom Merrilin and Juilin Sandar , are taken to Tanchico by the Sea Folk who reveal their own prophecies about Rand.
- **S67**：They meet Bayle Domon and Seanchan woman Egeanin .
- **S68**：During their mission to smoke out the Black Ajah they also encounter Moghedien , one of the Forsaken.
- **S69**：They discover that the enemy's goal is a male a'dam , held in the private collection of the Panarch Amathera , which they plan to use to control Rand.
- **S70**：Nynaeve and Elayne save Amathera from the Black Ajah and take the a'dam and a seal on the Dark One's prison from her palace.
- **S71**：Moghedien attacks and personally duels Nynaeve, finding that they are roughly equal in power.
- **S72**：Nynaeve defeats Moghedien and shields her, but the Black Ajah cause a distraction with a balefire-creating ter'angreal , letting Moghedien escape.
- **S73**：Finally, Nynaeve and Elayne send Domon and Egeanin to drop the a'dam overboard while far out to sea.
- **S74**：White Tower [ edit ] Min continues to report to the Amyrlin and tries to avoid suspicion by taking the guise of Elmindreda, a giddy, empty-headed woman.
- **S75**：She finds herself caught in a coup where Elaida and her supporters depose Siuan and Leane Sharif , her Keeper of the Chronicles, for secretly aiding Rand.
- **S76**：Siuan and Leane are both "stilled" (their ability to channel the One Power severed) and Elaida becomes the next Amyrlin.
- **S77**：Min frees Siuan and Leane, and all three are assisted in escape by Gawyn Trakand .
- **S78**：Gawyn has joined the coup on Elaida's side, and killed several Siuan supporters, but Min convinces him to turn a blind eye to their escape as a personal favor to her.
- **S79**：Logain Ablar , a former False Dragon who has been "gentled" (the male equivalent of "stilling") joins their escape from Tar Valon.

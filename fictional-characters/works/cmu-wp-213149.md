## 角色资料

### Chon Wang

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b5a208297c4eca83` · 独立版本：`profile-4ef0b928c207360a`

- **性格倾向**：在责任受挫的情境中倾向执着担当，不因未获选派而放弃营救（行为推断；S2、S3、S20）
- **核心动机**：他对公主的感情与自身过失感，共同推动其远赴异国营救（行为推断；S2、S3）
- **价值取舍**：最终他优先维护公主留下的选择，愿为此对抗原属的禁卫体系（行为推断；S25、S28、S29、S31）
- **决策方式**：处于武力劣势时倾向借助环境脱困，而非始终正面硬拼（行为推断；S6、S19、S30）
- **关系模式**：他看重伙伴对友谊的承认，听见罗伊否认关系后受伤离开（行为推断；S14、S20、S21）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：承担皇家护卫职责，最终却为公主的选择反抗同僚

### Roy O'Bannon

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-753806c131e6b2dc` · 独立版本：`profile-24414bd9323a1f18`

- **性格倾向**：在伙伴关系中呈现嘴上疏离、行动上愿意回头援助的倾向（行为推断；S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：分道扬镳后，他仍追随王并实施救援，显示维系伙伴的意愿（行为推断；S20、S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：面对最终武装冲突，他倾向直接持枪介入并承担正面对决（行为推断；S24、S26、S27）
- **关系模式**：他与王从劫车冲突走向技能互助，最终形成共同执法的搭档（摘要直接叙述；S4、S13、S14、S15、S36）
- **成长变化**：其叙事位置由被同伙抛弃的劫匪，转为追捕劫匪的治安官（摘要直接叙述；S4、S7、S36）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：曾否认与王的友谊，却主动跟随并救他脱险

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Chon Wang is a Chinese imperial guard.
- **S2**：After Princess Pei-Pei, for whom Wang has affection, is abducted and taken to the United States, the Emperor of China sends three of his guards to retrieve her.
- **S3**：Wang is not among one of the three, but after he tells them that it was his fault the princess was kidnapped, he is merely sent in the hopes that the "foreign devils" would get rid of Wang.
- **S4**：In Nevada, Roy O'Bannon is an outlaw who, with his gang, hijacks the train Wang is on.
- **S5**：When Wallace, a member of Roy's gang, kills Wang's uncle, Wang chases the outlaws down.
- **S6**：However, the gang is well-armed and Wang's only choice is to unhinge the cars and get away on the engine.
- **S7**：In the process, Wallace takes over the gang from Roy, and they leave him buried up to his chin in the desert sand.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, Pei-Pei, who was tricked into believing she was freely escaping her arranged marriage in China, finds out she has been kidnapped by an agent of Lo Fong, who ran away from the Forbidden City and was viewed as a traitor by the Chinese.
- **S9**：When Wang finds Roy buried in the sand, he demands to know the direction to Carson City.
- **S10**：Roy tells him that the city is on the other side of a mountain.
- **S11**：Wang puts two chop sticks in Roy's mouth for him to dig himself out.
- **S12**：When Wang comes out the other side of the mountain, he gets involved with a Sioux tribe by saving a boy chased by the Crow tribe and ends up reluctantly marrying the tribe chief's daughter, Falling Leaves.
- **S13**：Wang finds Roy in a tavern and, in anger, starts a fight with him that turns into a barroom brawl.
- **S14**：The two of them get sent to prison, and after Falling Leaves helps them escape, they become friends.
- **S15**：Roy trains Wang in the ways of the cowboy.
- **S16**：When they get to Carson City, Roy discovers that both he and Wang, now identified as the "Shanghai Kid" are wanted by Lo Fong's ally Marshal Nathan Van Cleef, and the two of them narrowly escape.
- **S17**：They go to a bordello , but after a drunken encounter by Wang, the Marshal eventually catches and arrests them.
- **S18**：They find out that Lo Fong is behind the kidnapping of the princess.
- **S19**：As they are about to be hanged, Wang manages to break himself free and after Falling Leaves shoots Roy loose, they escape the execution site.
- **S20**：Wang, upset over Roy previously telling one of the prostitutes at the bordello he was not Wang's friend, rides off alone to find the princess.
- **S21**：However, Roy follows him and the two reunite when Roy saves him from Fong.
- **S22**：The next day, the two partners go to the ransom point, the Carson City Mission church.
- **S23**：The three imperial guards arrive with the gold, and Lo Fong has the princess in hand.
- **S24**：However, a simple exchange becomes complicated when Wang shows up and Roy points a gun towards Fong.
- **S25**：Wang tells his fellow guards that he will not allow them to bring the princess back to China.
- **S26**：As the Chinese and Lo Fong fight amongst themselves, Van Cleef arrives and engages Roy in a gunfight.
- **S27**：After Roy is limited to one remaining bullet, Van Cleef simultaneously fires both of his guns but Roy shoots him in the heart.
- **S28**：Wang fights the Imperial Guards whilst Lo Fong chases Pei-Pei through the rafters of the church.
- **S29**：After Wang knocks all three Guards unconscious he fights Lo Fong to the bell tower.
- **S30**：Wang ultimately kills Lo Fong by dismantling the bell causing it to hang Lo Fong.
- **S31**：When they awaken, the Imperial Guards decide that they will let the princess stay.
- **S32**：Wallace and his gang also come up to the church, and demand that Roy and Wang come out and fight.
- **S33**：But when the two of them get outside the church to face Wallace, Natives from all around surround the gang.
- **S34**：At a Chinese cultural celebration Roy thanks Falling Leaves for saving him and they engage in a passionate kiss.
- **S35**：At the same time, Pei-Pei holds a smiling Wang's hand.
- **S36**：Roy and Wang are shown as sheriffs and ride off to catch a new band of train robbers.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As a hovering spacecraft departs an Earth-like world, a humanoid alien drinks a dark bubbling liquid, then starts to disintegrate.
- **S2**：The alien's remains cascade into a waterfall.
- **S3**：His DNA triggers a biogenetic reaction.
- **S4**：In 2089, archaeologists Elizabeth Shaw and Charlie Holloway discover a star map in Scotland that matches others from several unconnected ancient cultures.
- **S5**：They interpret this as an invitation from humanity's forerunners, the "Engineers".
- **S6**：Peter Weyland, the elderly CEO of Weyland Corporation, funds the creation of the scientific vessel Prometheus to follow the map to the distant moon LV-223.
- **S7**：The ship's crew travels in stasis while the android David monitors their voyage.
- **S8**：Arriving in 2093, they are informed of their mission to find the Engineers.
- **S9**：Mission director Meredith Vickers orders the crew to avoid making contact without her permission.
- **S10**：The Prometheus lands near a large artificial structure, which a team explores.
- **S11**：Inside they find numerous stone cylinders, a monolithic statue of a humanoid head, and the decapitated corpse of a large alien, thought to be an Engineer.
- **S12**：They find other bodies and presume the species is extinct.
- **S13**：The team takes the Engineer's head back for analysis.
- **S14**：David secretly takes a cylinder, while the remaining ones begin leaking a dark liquid.
- **S15**：A rapidly approaching storm forces the crew to return to Prometheus, leaving members Millburn and Fifield stranded in the structure.
- **S16**：In the ship's lab, the Engineer's DNA is found to match that of humans.
- **S17**：David investigates the cylinder and the dark liquid inside.
- **S18**：He then intentionally taints a drink with the substance and gives it to an unaware Holloway.
- **S19**：Shortly after, Shaw and Holloway have sex.
- **S20**：Inside the structure, a snake-like creature kills Millburn, and sprays a corrosive fluid that melts Fifield's helmet.
- **S21**：Fifield falls face-first into a puddle of dark liquid.
- **S22**：The crew later returns to the structure and finds Millburn's corpse.
- **S23**：David separately discovers a control room containing a surviving Engineer in stasis, and a star map highlighting Earth.
- **S24**：Holloway's ingestion of the dark liquid leads to an infection that rapidly ravages his body.
- **S25**：He is rushed back to Prometheus, but Vickers refuses to let him aboard, and at his urging, burns him to death with a flamethrower.
- **S26**：Later, a medical scan reveals that Shaw, despite being sterile, is pregnant with an alien offspring.
- **S27**：Fearing the worst, she uses an automated surgery table to extract and subdue the squid-like fetus.
- **S28**：Weyland is revealed to have been in stasis aboard Prometheus, and explains to Shaw he wants to ask the Engineers to prevent his death from old age.
- **S29**：As Weyland prepares to leave for the structure, Vickers addresses him as "Father".
- **S30**：Fifield, who has been mutated by the dark liquid, attacks the Prometheus{{'}}s hangar bay and kills several crew members before he is killed.
- **S31**：The Prometheus{{'}}s captain, Janek, speculates that the structure was an Engineer military installation that lost control of a virulent biological weapon, the dark liquid.
- **S32**：He also determines a part of the structure is a spacecraft.
- **S33**：Weyland and a team return to the structure.
- **S34**：David speaks to the Engineer, who responds by decapitating him and killing Weyland and his team.
- **S35**：Shaw escapes the spacecraft as the Engineer activates it.
- **S36**：Shaw warns Janek that the Engineer is planning to release the liquid on Earth and convinces Janek to stop the spacecraft.
- **S37**：Janek ejects the lifeboat and rams the Prometheus into the alien craft while Vickers escapes in an escape pod.
- **S38**：The Engineer's disabled spacecraft crashes onto the ground; its wreckage crushes Vickers.
- **S39**：Shaw goes to the lifeboat and finds that her alien offspring is still alive, and has grown to gigantic size.
- **S40**：David's still-active head warns Shaw over the intercom that the Engineer has survived the crash.
- **S41**：The Engineer forces open the lifeboat's airlock and attacks Shaw, who releases her alien offspring onto the Engineer; it thrusts a tentacle down the Engineer's throat, subduing him.
- **S42**：Shaw recovers David's remains, and with his help, launches another Engineer spacecraft.
- **S43**：She intends to reach the Engineers' homeworld in an attempt to understand why they wanted to destroy humanity.
- **S44**：In the lifeboat, an alien creature bursts out of the Engineer's chest.

- **S1**：## Characters
- **S2**：[ edit ] Jack McCall – The main character in the novel, Jack, is coping with the recent suicide of his wife, Shyla.
- **S3**：A cookbook and travel writer, he flees Waterford to the peaceful life of a Roman Piazza with his daughter Leah.
- **S4**：He is the oldest of the five McCall brothers.
- **S5**：Jack is the narrator, and to some extent his character flaws and passions make him an unreliable narrator , though he's always an honest one.
- **S6**：Leah McCall – the daughter of Jack McCall and Shyla Fox.
- **S7**：There is an ugly custody dispute over Leah involving Jack and Shyla's parents (after her death), but a final letter that Shyla left for Jack proves his adequacy as a parent.
- **S8**：Shyla Fox – Jack's wife, daughter of George and Ruth Fox.
- **S9**：Commits suicide by jumping from the Silas Pearlman Bridge.
- **S10**：Dupree McCall – The second oldest of the McCall brothers, Dupree works in the state mental hospital.
- **S11**：His occupation often bears with it the burden of his younger brother, John Hardin, who has schizophrenia .
- **S12**：Dallas McCall – The third oldest of the McCall brothers, he is a law partner with "The Judge" in Waterford.
- **S13**：Tee McCall – The fourth oldest of the McCall brothers, Tee is a teacher for autistic children in Georgetown County.
- **S14**：John Hardin McCall – The youngest of all five McCall brothers, John Hardin has a variety of psychological illnesses and often spends time in the mental hospital in Columbia, SC.
- **S15**：Martha Fox – Shyla's younger sister.
- **S16**：Ruth Fox – Shyla's mother; Leah's grandmother.
- **S17**：Judge Johnson Hagood McCall – Jack's father and the town's functioning drunk, "The Judge" has been divorced from Jack's mother for years, but he pines after her still.
- **S18**：Lucy McCall – Mother of the five McCall brothers.
- **S19**：Diagnosed with leukemia , Lucy wants to spend her remaining days on the beach, surrounded by her family and the endangered loggerhead sea turtles .
- **S20**：It is her illness that summons Jack and Leah out of Italy and back to the states.
- **S21**：Silas McCall – "The Judge's" father; Jack's grandfather.
- **S22**：Jordan Elliot – A close friend of Jack's, he moved to Waterford when the two were in high school.
- **S23**：A military brat with a tyrant father, Jordan struggled against authority in his youth.
- **S24**：He is caught in a scandal which has motivated him to retreat to/hide in the priesthood in Italy, which is where he and Jack reconnect.
- **S25**：Mike Hess – Another one of Jack's high school friends, Mike is a Hollywood producer.
- **S26**：He wants to tell the story of their childhood through a mini-series that he hopes to produce.
- **S27**：Having recalled Mike as one of the nicest guys from his youth, Jack recognizes (as does Mike, himself) that Hollywood has changed Mike for the worse.
- **S28**：Ledare Ansley – Capers Middleton's ex-wife, Ledare is one of Jack's childhood friends.
- **S29**：Beautiful and charming, Ledare becomes more and more important to Jack as the novel progresses.
- **S30**：Capers Middleton – Another friend of Jack's from high school.
- **S31**：His father's heritage is known for their aristocracy and political prowess in South Carolina.
- **S32**：Capers hopes to run for governor of South Carolina.
- **S33**：After back-stabbing Jack in college, he tries to reinstate his friendship with him after Shyla's death.
- **S34**：Max Russoff – Ruth's adopted father; after Ruth went through the Holocaust it was Max "The Great Jew" who rescued her from Poland.

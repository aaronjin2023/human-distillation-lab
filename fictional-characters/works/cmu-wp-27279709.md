### Caesar

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-51db07aba91dab50` · 独立版本：`profile-cc440c015271c7ae`

- **性格倾向**：在受虐与受制的环境中，倾向主动争取自主并组织反抗（行为推断；S13、S14、S15、S22、S23）
- **核心动机**：不再满足于人类宠物的地位，转而争取与同类共同生活（行为推断；S8、S9、S21、S32）
- **价值取舍**：争取自身自由时也释放其他猿类，体现对同类处境的重视（行为推断；S14、S22、S26）

- **成长变化**：从质疑宠物身份发展到明确将猿群所在之处认作家园（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9、S32）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Will Rodman  is a scientist at biotechnology company Gen-Sys who has been trying to develop a cure for Alzheimer's disease and is testing a new gene therapy drug on chimpanzees.
- **S2**：The drug, a modified virus, mutates a chimpanzee, giving her a human level of intelligence.
- **S3**：She later goes on a rampage which is seen as a side effect of the drug.
- **S4**：When Will's boss Steven Jacobs  subsequently orders chimp handler Robert Franklin  to euthanize the remaining test chimpanzees Franklin discovers the chimp had given birth and was only protecting her baby.
- **S5**：Unable to bring himself to kill the baby chimp, Robert gives him to Will, who takes him home to raise.
- **S6**：Will's father Charles , who is suffering from Alzheimer's disease, names the baby chimp "Caesar." Caesar  has inherited his mother's high intelligence and develops quickly.
- **S7**：One day, after he frightens the children of their neighbor, Douglas Hunsiker , and receives a cut on his palm, Will takes him to the San Francisco Zoo where primatologist Caroline Aranha  treats his injury.
- **S8**：Will begins to take Caesar on excursions to the redwood forest at Muir Woods National Monument, but after seeing a family's German shepherd, Caesar begins to resent his status as a human "pet." When Caesar questions his identity, Will tells him that his mother was given a drug and that the reason for his intelligence is that the drug affected him while still in the womb.
- **S9**：Caesar becomes more aware of his biological identity and begins to view himself as different from his human family.
- **S10**：A desperate Will tests a sample of his cure on his father.
- **S11**：At first, his father's condition improves, but eventually his dementia returns.
- **S12**：Confused, he attempts to drive Hunsiker's car, and damages it, angering Hunsiker.
- **S13**：Caesar witnesses the confrontation and attacks Hunsiker, prompting authorities to place him in a primate shelter run by John Landon ([[Brian Cox , where he is treated cruelly by the other apes and the chief guard, Landon's son Dodge .
- **S14**：Caesar escapes from his cell and frees a gorilla called Buck kept in solitary confinement.
- **S15**：With Buck's assistance, Caesar gains dominance over the other apes by beating the sanctuary's alpha chimp.
- **S16**：Will creates a more powerful form of the virus to resume treating his father, and boss Jacobs clears its testing on chimpanzees, which further increases their intelligence.
- **S17**：However, unbeknownst to the scientists, it is fatal to humans.
- **S18**：Franklin is exposed to the new virus and begins sneezing blood.
- **S19**：Attempting to contact Will at his home, he accidentally infects Hunsiker, and he is then later discovered dead in his apartment.
- **S20**：Will attempts to warn Jacobs against further testing but when Jacobs refuses to listen, Will quits his job.
- **S21**：After his father's death, Will bribes the elder Landon into releasing Caesar into his custody, but Caesar refuses to leave.
- **S22**：He later escapes from the ape facility on his own and returns to Will's house, where he steals canisters of the new virus and releases it throughout the cage area at the facility, enhancing the intelligence of his fellow apes.
- **S23**：The apes put an escape plan into motion.
- **S24**：Landon's son Dodge attempts to intervene, leading to a fight with Caesar.
- **S25**：Caesar shocks Dodge by shouting "No!" at him, and later kills him through electrocution.
- **S26**：The liberated apes storm the city and release the remaining apes from Gen-Sys, as well as the entire ape population of a zoo.
- **S27**：A terrible battle follows as the apes force their way past a police blockade on the Golden Gate Bridge to escape into the Redwood forest.
- **S28**：Buck sacrifices his life to save Caesar, jumping into a police helicopter  as they try to shoot Caesar.
- **S29**：Buck damages the helicopter severely forcing it to crash on the bridge.
- **S30**：A still alive Jacobs trapped in the helicopter is finally killed when Koba, an ape who had spent a lifetime being tested on, kicks the helicopter off the bridge.
- **S31**：As the apes find their way into the Redwood forest Will arrives and warns Caesar that the humans will hunt them down, and begs him to return home.
- **S32**：To Will's surprise Caesar speaks like a human, telling him that "Caesar is home" among his fellow apes.
- **S33**：The final image shows the apes climbing to the tops of the Redwood trees, looking out over the Golden Gate Bridge and the San Francisco Bay.
- **S34**：In a post-credits scene, Hunsiker arrives at his job as an airline pilot.
- **S35**：He sneezes out blood as he is infected by the virus he got from Franklin.
- **S36**：The camera pans to a filled flight-status display board, zooming-in on New York implying the spread of a global pandemic.

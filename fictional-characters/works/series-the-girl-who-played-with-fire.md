- **S18**：He attempts to help, to Salander's astonishment, and their joint efforts enable her to evade her attacker.
- **S19**：Millennium is approached by Dag Svensson and Mia Johansson, who have put together a report concerning sex trafficking in Sweden and the abuse of underage girls by high-ranking figures.
- **S20**：Everyone is intrigued by recurring mentions of "Zala", a mysterious figure heavily involved in the sex-trafficking industry.
- **S21**：Salander, hacking Blomkvist's computer, is taken aback by the mention of Zala, and visits Svensson and Johansson to ask questions.
- **S22**：Later that same night, Blomkvist finds the couple shot dead in their apartment.
- **S23**：With Salander's fingerprints on the murder weapon, and her formal record establishing a history of violent instability, she is implicated in the double murder.
- **S24**：Bjurman is also found dead, shot by the same weapon; Salander is the prime suspect .
- **S25**：Eager to clear Salander's name and realizing that she has hacked into his notebook computer, Blomkvist leaves her notes on his desktop; her replies point him to "Zala".
- **S26**：Blomkvist confronts Gunnar Björck, a policeman on sick leave and one of the high-ranking abusers identified by Svensson and Johansson, who agrees to disclose information about Zala if Blomkvist leaves him out of Millennium ' s exposé.
- **S27**：Miriam Wu, Salander's best friend and current sex partner, is taken in for questioning by the police.
- **S28**：After her release, Paolo Roberto , Salander's former boxing coach, witnesses her being kidnapped into a van by Salander's earlier attacker, aided by a "blond giant".
- **S29**：He follows them to a warehouse south of Nykvarn , where he attempts to fight the giant and manages to escape with Wu.
- **S30**：The giant recovers and sets the warehouse on fire to destroy the evidence.
- **S31**：Visiting Bjurman's summer cabin, Salander finds a classified Säpo file and begins to make the connection between Bjurman and Zala, whose real name is Alexander Zalachenko.
- **S32**：By coincidence, two members of the motorcycle club, Carl-Magnus Lundin (Salander's attacker) and Sonny Nieminen, have been dispatched to burn the place down.
- **S33**：Salander incapacitates them, leaving suspects for the police to find.
- **S34**：She returns to her apartment and, having no choice, decides to find and kill Zalachenko.
- **S35**：She discovers the identity of the giant, Ronald Niedermann, and traces him to a post office box in Gothenburg .
- **S36**：Meanwhile, Blomkvist manages to find Salander's new apartment as well as the DVD revealing Bjurman's crime.
- **S37**：With information from Björck and Palmgren, Blomkvist pieces together the entire story: Zalachenko is a former Soviet defector whose very existence is kept classified by Säpo.
- **S38**：Initially an intelligence source, Zalachenko began to traffic in sex slaves on the side.
- **S39**：He became the partner of a 17-year-old girl who became pregnant with twins, Lisbeth and Camilla.
- **S40**：Zalachenko was an itinerant father who habitually abused his partner, culminated in Lisbeth's deliberately setting his car alight while her father was in it.
- **S41**：The authorities imprisoned Salander and declared her insane, since acknowledging Zalachenko's crimes would require them to divulge his existence.
- **S42**：Niedermann had killed Svensson and Johansson on Zalachenko's orders; Bjurman, who was involved with Zalachenko, played a role in the murders and was killed to ensure his silence.
- **S43**：Between Blomkvist's testimony, Salander's various character witnesses, and the additional suspects piling up, the police are forced to admit that their original suspicions were wrong.
- **S44**：Blomkvist finds Niedermann's Göteborg address and sets off for the farm where he and Zalachenko await.
- **S45**：There, Salander is captured and brought before Zalachenko.
- **S46**：She is shot when she attempts to escape and is buried by Niedermann, not realising she is still alive.
- **S47**：Battling through immense pain, Salander slowly digs herself out and attempts to kill Zalachenko with an axe.
- **S48**：On his way to Göteborg, Blomkvist captures Niedermann and ties him against a signpost by the road.
- **S49**：The book ends as Blomkvist finds Salander and calls emergency services.

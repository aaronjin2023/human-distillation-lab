### Barney Ross

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-0fda78e051e62528` · 独立版本：`profile-c2df7e69f5e40514`

- **性格倾向**：在队友失控与平民遇险时，倾向克制暴力并承担保护责任（行为推断；S4、S5、S19）
- **核心动机**：受到救赎话题触动后，将救回桑德拉作为重返险境的目标（行为推断；S18、S19）

### Lee Christmas

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-dd05d8da80280b40` · 独立版本：`profile-42dbaeb8a73dc586`

- **性格倾向**：在亲近者遭受伤害时，倾向直接介入并以武力保护对方（行为推断；S14）
- **情绪反应**：发现女友因长期缺席和不了解其工作而离开，感到难过（摘要直接叙述；S6）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The Expendables, bikers and elite New Orleans based mercenaries, deploy to the Gulf of Aden to save hostages on a vessel from Somali pirates.
- **S2**：The team consists of leader Barney Ross , former SAS soldier and blades specialist Lee Christmas , martial artist Yin Yang , sniper Gunner Jensen , weapons specialist Hale Caesar  and demolitions expert Toll Road .
- **S3**：Jensen instigates a firefight, causing casualties for the pirates.
- **S4**：Jensen then tries to hang a pirate, but Yang physically stops him when Ross and the team discourage it.
- **S5**：Ross then reluctantly discharges Jensen from the team due to his psychological problems and drug use.
- **S6**：Later, Christmas is upset to discover his girlfriend Lacy  has left him for another man, because she did not see Christmas often or know his line of work.
- **S7**：Ross and rival mercenary leader Trench Mauser  visit a man called "Mr.
- **S8**：Church" , naming himself after their meeting venue, for a mission.
- **S9**：A busy Trench passes the contract to Ross, which is to overthrow dictator General Garza  in Vilena, a South American island in the Gulf of Mexico.
- **S10**：Ross and Christmas fly to Vilena for initial undercover reconnaissance and meet their contact, Sandra , but are discovered.
- **S11**：Ex-CIA officer James Munroe , with his henchmen Paine  and The Brit , deride and keep Garza in power as a figurehead for their own profiteering operations, while Sandra is revealed to be Garza's daughter.
- **S12**：Ross aborts and causes casualties among the army as they escape, but Sandra refuses to leave Vilena and let her people suffer.
- **S13**：Meanwhile, a vengeful Jensen approaches Munroe to help and Garza is angered further when Sandra is captured by Munroe, who has her waterboarded for information.
- **S14**：Lacy has been physically abused by her new man, so Christmas beats him and his friends, then takes her home, having revealed his work.
- **S15**：Ross and the group discover that Mr.
- **S16**：Church is a CIA operative and the real target is Munroe, as the CIA could not afford a mission to kill one of their own directly.
- **S17**：Ross meets Expendables mission coordinator Tool  to express his feelings.
- **S18**：Tool makes an emotional confession about letting a woman commit suicide during the Bosnian War, instead of finding redemption by saving her.
- **S19**：Ross is then motivated to go back for Sandra alone, but Yang accompanies him.
- **S20**：Jensen and hired men pursue them on the road, ending in an abandoned warehouse, where Yang and Jensen fight a second time.
- **S21**：Jensen attempts to impale Yang on a pipe, but Ross shoots him.
- **S22**：A wounded Jensen makes amends and gives the layout of Garza's palace.
- **S23**：Ross boards the plane with Yang and finds the rest of team, ready to aid him.
- **S24**：The team infiltrate Garza's compound.
- **S25**：Thinking Munroe hired the Expendables to kill him, Garza has his soldiers' faces painted, preparing them for a fight.
- **S26**：Christmas, Yang, Caesar, and Toll plant explosives throughout the site but Ross, while saving Sandra, is captured by the Brit and Paine.
- **S27**：The team save him and kill the Brit, but are pinned down by Garza's men as Paine wrestles Ross.
- **S28**：Caesar fights back with his automatic shotgun so Paine escapes.
- **S29**：Garza finally stands up to Munroe, ordering him out and returning his money.
- **S30**：Instead, as Garza rallies his men against the Expendables and Munroe that he believes to be the same, Munroe kills him and takes off with Paine and Sandra.
- **S31**：Garza's men open fire in rage against the team, who fight their way through, detonating the explosives, demolishing the palace and destroying the compound.
- **S32**：Toll kills Paine by burning him alive while Ross and Caesar manage to destroy a helicopter before Munroe can escape.
- **S33**：Ross and Christmas catch up to Munroe, killing him and saving Sandra.
- **S34**：Later, Ross gifts the mission payment to Sandra to restore Vilena.
- **S35**：The team travels back home and celebrate at Tool's tattoo parlor, with the recovering and reformed Jensen.
- **S36**：Christmas and Tool have a game of knife throwing and Christmas recites a limerick about Tool, then throws a bullseye.

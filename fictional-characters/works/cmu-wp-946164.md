### Paul Edgecomb

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a95f9f4cc17e7c3f` · 独立版本：`profile-b74c448000e4882f`

- **性格倾向**：面对约翰的处境，他倾向保留同情，并愿意质疑自己应执行的安排（行为推断；S21、S24、S25）
- **价值取舍**：确认约翰受冤后，他曾考虑放人，显示良知开始冲撞制度职责（行为推断；S19、S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：在未改变执行结果的情况下，他仍选择尊重约翰对临终待遇的请求（行为推断；S24、S25）
- **情绪反应**：多年后他仍因往事落泪，并将超常长寿理解为执行冤刑的惩罚（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S30）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：能够理解并尊重约翰，却仍参与了自己日后深感负罪的处刑

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In a Louisiana nursing home in 1999, Paul Edgecomb  begins to cry while watching the film Top Hat.
- **S2**：His elderly friend Elaine  shows concern for him, and Paul tells her that the film reminded him of when he was a corrections officer in charge of death row inmates at Cold Mountain Penitentiary during the summer of 1935.
- **S3**：The cell block Paul  works in is called the "Green Mile" because the condemned prisoners walking to their execution are said to be walking "the last mile".
- **S4**：Here, it is a stretch of faded lime-green linoleum.
- **S5**：The other guards include Brutus "Brutal" Howell , Harry Terwilliger , and Dean Stanton .
- **S6**：One day, John Coffey , a giant black man convicted of raping and killing two young white girls, arrives on death row.
- **S7**：However, he is shy, soft-spoken, and emotional.
- **S8**：John reveals extraordinary powers by healing Paul's urinary tract infection and resurrecting a mouse.
- **S9**：Later, he heals the terminally ill wife  of Warden Hal Moores .
- **S10**：When John is asked to explain his power, he merely says that he "took it back." Percy Wetmore , a sadistic and unpopular guard, who is the nephew of the governor's wife, has recently begun working on the mile.
- **S11**：Percy recognizes that the other officers dislike him and uses that to demand managing the next execution.
- **S12**：After that, he promises he will transfer to an administrative post at a mental hospital.
- **S13**：An agreement is made, but Percy then deliberately sabotages the execution.
- **S14**：Instead of wetting the sponge used to conduct electricity and make executions quick and effective, he leaves it dry, causing the execution of Eduard Delacroix  to malfunction dramatically.
- **S15**：Shortly afterward, a violent prisoner named "Wild Bill" Wharton  arrives, to be executed for multiple murders committed during a robbery.
- **S16**：At one point he seizes John's arm, and John psychically senses that Wharton is responsible for the crime for which John was convicted and sentenced to death.
- **S17**：John "takes back" the sickness in Hal's wife and regurgitates it into Percy, who then shoots Wharton to death and falls into a state of permanent catatonia.
- **S18**：Percy is then housed in the Briar Ridge Mental Hospital.
- **S19**：In the wake of these events, Paul interrogates John, who says he "punished them bad men" and offers to show Paul what he saw.
- **S20**：John takes Paul's hand and says he has to give Paul "a part of himself" in order for Paul to see what really happened to the girls.
- **S21**：Paul asks John what he should do, if he should open the door and let John walk away.
- **S22**：John tells him that there is too much pain in the world, to which he is sensitive, and says he is "rightly tired of the pain" and is ready to rest.
- **S23**：For his last request on the night before his execution, John watches the film Top Hat.
- **S24**：When John is put in the electric chair, he asks Paul not to put the traditional black hood over his head because he is afraid of the dark.
- **S25**：Paul agrees, shakes his hand, and John is executed.
- **S26**：As Paul finishes his story, he notes that he requested a transfer to a youth detention center, where he spent the remainder of his career.
- **S27**：Elaine questions his statement that he had a fully grown son at the time, and Paul explains that he was 44 years old at the time of John's execution and that he is now 108.
- **S28**：This is apparently a side effect of John giving a "part of himself" to Paul.

## 材料限制

- 女孩的攻击行为与附身状态无法可靠分离，不能据此提炼其本人的性格，故改选推动救援的母亲与神父。
- 母亲对离婚影响的解释只是她当时的判断，材料未证实其为病因。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] An elderly Jesuit priest named Father Lankester Merrin is leading an archaeological dig in northern Iraq and is studying ancient relics.
- **S3**：After discovering a small statue of the demon Pazuzu (an actual ancient Assyrian demon), a series of omens alerts him to a pending confrontation with a powerful evil, which, unknown to the reader at this point, he has battled before in an exorcism in Africa.
- **S4**：Meanwhile, in Georgetown , a young girl named Regan MacNeil is living with her famous mother, actress Chris MacNeil, who is in Georgetown filming a movie.
- **S5**：Chris has rented a house for her and Regan to reside in during the filming.
- **S6**：Regan finds a Ouija board in the basement of the home, which she uses to communicate with an entity who identifies itself as "Captain Howdy".
- **S7**：As Chris finishes her work on the film, Regan begins to become inexplicably ill.
- **S8**：After a gradual series of poltergeist -like disturbances in their rented house, for which Chris attempts to find rational explanations, Regan begins to rapidly undergo disturbing psychological and physical changes.
- **S9**：She refuses to eat or sleep, becomes withdrawn, and increasingly aggressive and violent.
- **S10**：Regan contorts her body to bow and bend in impossible directions, at one point following around her tutor Sharon, hissing at her.
- **S11**：Chris initially mistakes Regan's behavior for the result of repressed anger over her parents' divorce and absent father.
- **S12**：Coupled with these events are disturbances at the local Holy Trinity Church, which has been desecrated on several recent occasions.
- **S13**：Detective Kinderman begins investigating Father Karras, as they exchange disturbing stories of Black Masses , Catholic theology and 1960s film stars.
- **S14**：At this point in the novel, Burke Dennings, Chris's movie director, has been killed, with his head turned completely around.
- **S15**："Could another human being have done it?
- **S16**：Yes, but he'd have to be an exceptionally powerful man." After several unsuccessful psychiatric and medical treatments (X-rays, medication, temporal lobe analysis), Regan's mother, an atheist , turns to a local Jesuit priest for help as Regan's personality becomes increasingly disturbed and the doctors still cannot find a source.
- **S17**：Father Damien Karras , who is currently going through a crisis of faith coupled with the recent loss of his mother, agrees to see Regan as a psychiatrist, but initially resists the notion that it is an actual demonic possession, pointing to advances in science which can explain what was previously assumed to be possession.
- **S18**：After a few meetings with the child, now completely inhabited by a diabolical personality claiming to be the devil, he turns to the local bishop for permission to perform an exorcism on the child.
- **S19**：The bishop with whom he consults does not believe Karras is qualified to perform the rites, and appoints the experienced Merrin—who has recently returned to the United States—to perform the exorcism, although he does allow the doubt-ridden Karras to assist him.
- **S20**：The lengthy exorcism tests the priests both physically and spiritually.
- **S21**：When Merrin, who had previously suffered cardiac arrhythmia , dies during the process, completion of the exorcism ultimately falls upon Father Karras.
- **S22**：When he demands that the demonic spirit inhabit him instead of the innocent Regan, the demon seizes the opportunity to possess the priest.
- **S23**：Karras heroically surrenders his own life in exchange for Regan's by jumping out of her bedroom window and dies, regaining his faith in God as his last rites are read.

## 角色资料

### Flik

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-cb0f918d45dc1977` · 独立版本：`profile-875ec0e254b89507`

- **性格倾向**：在群体不认可的处境中仍倾向独立探索，愿意尝试新办法（行为推断；S1、S3、S6、S7、S14）
- **核心动机**：真心希望招募外援击退蝗虫，帮助蚁群摆脱长期压迫（摘要直接叙述；S4、S6、S7）
- **决策方式**：倾向将现场经验转化为创造性方案，利用对手恐惧破局（行为推断；S14、S25）
- **价值取舍**：遭到暴力报复仍选择抗争，倾向把集体自主置于屈服之上（行为推断；S22）
- **成长变化**：从不受欢迎且遭放逐的发明者，成长为带动蚁群反抗的人（摘要直接叙述；S3、S17、S22、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：真心帮助蚁群，却因对外援身份的误认与隐瞒失去信任。

### Hopper

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-2153af38703faf11` · 独立版本：`profile-e0d243c098f6c008`

- **性格倾向**：在统治遭到挑战时倾向强硬报复，以暴力维持支配地位（行为推断；S18、S19、S22、S24）
- **核心动机**：要求蚁群持续提供食物，并在供奉不足时直接侵占储粮（摘要直接叙述；S4、S5、S18）
- **价值取舍**：在分配生存资源时优先满足蝗虫，漠视蚁群的越冬需要（行为推断；S18）
- **情绪反应**：对鸟怀有强烈恐惧，这一弱点成为弗里克制定方案的依据（摘要直接叙述；S14）
- **决策方式**：识破假鸟后倾向将既有经验套用到新威胁，低估真实危险（行为推断；S21、S25、S26、S27）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：依赖暴力制造恐惧，自身却被对鸟的恐惧与误判牵制。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Flik, an individualist and would-be inventor, lives in a colony of ants.
- **S2**：The ants are led by Princess Atta and her mother, the Queen, and they live on a small island in the middle of a creek.
- **S3**：Flik is different and always unappreciated because of his problematic inventions.
- **S4**：The colony is oppressed by a gang of marauding grasshoppers led by Hopper who arrive every season demanding food from the ants.
- **S5**：When the annual offering is inadvertently knocked into a stream by Flik's latest invention, a harvester device, the grasshoppers demand twice as much food as compensation.
- **S6**：Given a temporary reprieve by the grasshoppers, the ants trick Flik into accepting his plan to recruit "warrior bugs" to fight off the grasshoppers.
- **S7**：While Flik actually believes in the plan, the other ants see it as a fool's errand to get rid of Flik and save themselves trouble.
- **S8**：Making his way to the "big city" , Flik mistakes a group of circus bugs, who have recently been fired by their money-hungry ringmaster, P.T.
- **S9**：Flea, for the warrior bugs he seeks.
- **S10**：The bugs, in turn, mistake Flik for a talent agent, and agree to travel with him back to Ant Island.
- **S11**：Discovering their mutual misunderstanding, the circus bugs attempt to leave, but are forced back by a bird.
- **S12**：They save Princess Dot, the Queen's daughter and Atta's sister, from the bird as they flee, gaining the ants' trust in the process.
- **S13**：They continue the ruse of being "warriors" so the troupe can continue to enjoy the attention and hospitality of the ants.
- **S14**：The bird encounter inspires Flik into creating an artificial bird to scare away Hopper, leader of the grasshoppers, who is deeply afraid of birds.
- **S15**：The bird is constructed, but the circus bugs are exposed when P.T.
- **S16**：Flea arrives searching for them, having had a change of heart.
- **S17**：Angered at Flik's deception, the ants exile him and desperately attempt to pull together enough food for a new offering to the grasshoppers, but fail to do so.
- **S18**：When the grasshoppers discover a meager offering upon their arrival, they take control of the entire colony and begin eating the ants' winter store of food.
- **S19**：After overhearing Hopper's plan to kill the queen, Dot leaves in search of Flik and convinces him to return and save the colony with his original plan.
- **S20**：The plan nearly works, but P.T.
- **S21**：Flea lights the bird model on fire, causing it to crash and be exposed as a fake.
- **S22**：Hopper has Flik beaten in retaliation, but Flik defies Hopper and inspires the entire colony to stand up to the grasshoppers and drive them out of the colony.
- **S23**：Before Hopper can be disposed of, it begins to rain.
- **S24**：In the chaos, Hopper kidnaps Flik and flees, but Atta gives chase and rescues Flik.
- **S25**：As Hopper viciously pursues the couple, Flik leads him to an actual bird's nest.
- **S26**：Mistaking the real bird for another fake one, Hopper attracts its attention by taunting it.
- **S27**：Hopper is picked up by the bird, who then feeds him to her chicks.
- **S28**：Some time later, Flik has been welcomed back to the colony, and he and Atta are now a couple.
- **S29**：As the troupe departs with the last grasshopper, Molt, as an employee, Atta is crowned the new Queen, while Dot gets the princess' crown.
- **S30**：The circus troupe then departs as Flik, Atta and Dot watch and wave farewell on a tree branch.

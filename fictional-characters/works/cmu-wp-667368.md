## 角色资料

### Harry Potter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6731c01eb468c5e3` · 独立版本：`profile-00d05599afed4db9`

- **性格倾向**：面对袭击谜团时，他倾向于亲自查证线索，并承担调查中的风险。（行为推断；S25、S30、S33、S35）
- **核心动机**：在密室调查中，他持续追查袭击来源，并参与寻找被带走的金妮。（行为推断；S25、S36、S39、S42）
- **决策方式**：调查时他会借伪装获取情报，再根据新的线索调整怀疑对象。（行为推断；S21、S25、S28、S30）
- **价值取舍**：在中毒受伤的险境中，他仍摧毁日记，表现出阻止里德尔的决心。（行为推断；S47、S48）
- **关系模式**：在海格遭怀疑并被捕后，他仍依照海格提示追查，维系同伴信任。（行为推断；S30、S32、S33、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Tom Marvolo Riddle

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-d1c8c8e1cba1f016` · 独立版本：`profile-1949f02508d2e863`

- **性格倾向**：在密室事件中，他倾向于操纵他人实施袭击，并直接消灭阻碍者。（行为推断；S43、S45）
- **核心动机**：面对进入密室的哈利，他明确要借蛇怪将其杀死。（摘要直接叙述；S45）
- **决策方式**：他通过指使金妮开启密室和书写威胁，让他人执行自己的安排。（行为推断；S43）
- **价值取舍**：在实施袭击时，他把金妮作为执行指令的工具，漠视他人安全。（行为推断；S43、S45）
- **关系模式**：他以保存在日记中的记忆出现，并向哈利说明自己对金妮的指使。（摘要直接叙述；S42、S43）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Gilderoy Lockhart

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-c4117e5055951d13` · 独立版本：`profile-3a1f93221147a3f8`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他重视名声却回避实际危险，承担职责的意愿有限。（行为推断；S37、S38）
- **核心动机**：他对他人施展遗忘咒的目的，是冒领那些巫师取得的成就。（摘要直接叙述；S38）
- **决策方式**：面对救援任务，他先试图逃走，被截住后又用遗忘咒阻止哈利等人。（摘要直接叙述；S37、S40）
- **价值取舍**：在冒领成就和逃避救援时，他把个人利益置于诚实与职责之前。（行为推断；S37、S38）
- **成长变化**：遗忘咒反噬后他失去记忆，片尾书封仍将他呈现为失忆困惑的状态。（摘要直接叙述；S40、S41、S54）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：凭他人成就享有名声，却在实际救援任务中试图逃走。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{further2}} Preparing for a visit from a potential client of Uncle Vernon Dursley's, the Dursleys send Harry to his room.
- **S2**：Harry finds Dobby the house elf, who warns against returning to Hogwarts.
- **S3**：When Harry refuses, Dobby causes havoc in the house and frames Harry for ruining Vernon's meeting.
- **S4**：Vernon locks Harry in his room to prevent his return to Hogwarts.
- **S5**：That night, Ron, Fred, and George Weasley arrive in their flying car to rescue Harry from the clutches of Uncle Vernon, who discovers the rescue and tries to pull Harry back into his room.
- **S6**：The Weasleys succeed and take Harry to The Burrow, their home.
- **S7**：Harry meets Ron's younger sister, Ginny, who is about to begin at Hogwarts and has a crush on Harry.
- **S8**：Harry also meets Ron's father, Arthur Weasley; he had met Mrs.
- **S9**：Weasley the previous year.
- **S10**：Harry and the Weasleys travel to Diagon Alley by Floo Powder.
- **S11**：While shopping, Harry meets Gilderoy Lockhart, a famous wizard and author, and later Draco Malfoy and his father, Lucius, who praise Voldemort and deride Harry, Hermione and the Weasleys.
- **S12**：At King's Cross Station, though the rest of the Weasleys reach Platform 9 3/4 without trouble, Harry and Ron find the magical barrier blocked; as a result, they miss the Hogwarts Express.
- **S13**：Harry and Ron take the flying car and reach Hogwarts, but accidentally land in the school's violent tree, the Whomping Willow.
- **S14**：Ron's wand is broken and the car behaves erratically, ejecting the boys and driving itself into the Forbidden Forest.
- **S15**：When Harry and Ron enter Hogwarts they are seen by Snape, who scolds them for flying the car to Hogwarts and nearly expels them.
- **S16**：Professor McGonagall and Professor Dumbledore arrive and McGonagall defends the boys and tells them they will receive detention only.
- **S17**：Shortly after the start of term, Harry begins hearing an ominous, icy, cold voice coming from inside the walls, starting in his detention with Lockhart.
- **S18**：Harry, Ron and Hermione find the message "The Chamber of Secrets has been opened.
- **S19**：Enemies of the heir, beware" written in blood across a wall and discover that caretaker Argus Filch's cat has been petrified, who blames it on Harry and attempts to attack him but is stopped by Dumbledore and McGonagall.
- **S20**：Legend has it that the Chamber of Secrets can only be opened by the Heir of Slytherin; it is said to be the home of a creature that will only obey the Heir.
- **S21**：Harry suspects the Heir is Malfoy.
- **S22**：Gilderoy Lockhart, hired to teach Defense Against the Dark Arts, offers a dueling club.
- **S23**：At the meeting Draco conjures a snake that Harry discovers he can talk to.
- **S24**：Hermione explains that he is a Parselmouth like Salazar Slytherin, a connection that causes the school to believe Harry is his Heir.
- **S25**：The three brew Polyjuice Potion to disguise themselves as Malfoy's friends Crabbe and Goyle, and interrogate Malfoy, but learn that he is not the Heir.
- **S26**：In a bathroom Harry finds a book with nothing written in it that belonged to someone named Tom Marvolo Riddle.
- **S27**：Through the enchanted book Harry sees events that happened fifty years ago when Tom was a student.
- **S28**：Tom's memories incriminate Hagrid as the Heir.
- **S29**：Over the course of the school year, Colin Creevey, Justin Finch-Fletchley, the Gryffindor ghost Sir Nicholas and even Hermione are all found petrified, and Tom Riddle's diary goes missing.
- **S30**：Harry and Ron decide to ask Hagrid, but before Hagrid can answer the real identity of who opened the Chamber, Minister of Magic Cornelius Fudge and Lucius Malfoy arrive.
- **S31**：While Ron and Harry hide, the visitors tell Hagrid they are suspending Dumbledore as headmaster and arresting Hagrid under suspicion of having opened the Chamber.
- **S32**：Before Hagrid is taken away to Azkaban prison, he tells Ron and Harry to follow the spiders into the Forbidden Forest for the truth.
- **S33**：They do so and meet Aragog, a giant spider thought to have killed a student fifty years ago.
- **S34**：Aragog reveals that he is not the monster who killed the student and that Hagrid is innocent.
- **S35**：Aragog's sons and daughters attack Harry and Ron, but the flying car rescues them.
- **S36**：Harry and Ron learn from a piece of paper in Hermione's hand that the monster is a basilisk and overhear the teachers saying that Ginny has been taken into the Chamber.
- **S37**：Lockhart is sent to find the Chamber and save Ginny, but tries to escape until Harry and Ron catch him.
- **S38**：It turns out Lockhart's past is false; he used memory-erasing charms on witches and wizards to take credit for their accomplishments.
- **S39**：The three find the chamber entrance in Moaning Myrtle's  bathroom and enter to find a giant snake skin.
- **S40**：After he faints, Lockhart tries to stop Harry and Ron by using a memory charm, but it backfires.
- **S41**：Lockhart loses his memory and part of the Chamber caves in, separating Harry from the others.
- **S42**：Harry finds Ginny and Tom Riddle appears, explaining that he is a memory preserved in the diary.
- **S43**：Riddle tells Harry that Ginny Weasley opened the Chamber that sent the basilisk to attack, and wrote threatening messages on the wall because he ordered her to.
- **S44**：Harry learns that Riddle is Slytherin's Heir and is Lord Voldemort in his teenage form.
- **S45**：Riddle sends the basilisk to kill Harry but Dumbledore's phoenix, Fawkes, attacks the basilisk's eyes.
- **S46**：Fawkes gives Harry the Sorting Hat, from which he draws the sword of Godric Gryffindor.
- **S47**：Although Harry kills the basilisk by driving the blade up through its head, a fang embeds itself in his arm and poisons him.
- **S48**：Harry destroys Voldemort/Riddle by piercing the diary with the fang.
- **S49**：Ginny regains consciousness and finds Harry dying, but Fawkes heals Harry's wound with his tears.
- **S50**：Dumbledore resumes his post as Headmaster and sends in the paperwork to have Hagrid released from Azkaban.
- **S51**：Dumbledore assuages Harry's concerns of his worthiness to belong to Gryffindor House by pointing out that only a true member could have summoned Godric's sword.
- **S52**：Learning that Lucius gave the diary to Ginny and that Dobby serves the Malfoys, Harry tricks Lucius into freeing him from servitude.
- **S53**：At the year-end feast, all of the basilisk's victims are back to full health, and Hagrid receives a standing ovation as he returns to Hogwarts.
- **S54**：In a post-credits scene, a new Gilderoy Lockhart book is on display at Flourish & Blotts, entitled "WHO AM I?" and depicting an amnesiac, confused Lockhart in a straitjacket.

## 角色资料

### Sherlock Holmes

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d1e0160481f3b3a5` · 独立版本：`profile-9afd7aac9dc55eec`

- **性格倾向**：在此案中倾向主动寻找事件间的联系，但也会受对手误导而判断失准。（行为推断；S6、S17、S18）
- **核心动机**：他追查谋杀、恐袭与收购之间的联系，并试图阻止峰会上的袭击。（行为推断；S6、S27、S28）
- **决策方式**：他预判Moriarty在体力上占优，转而抱住对手一同坠下瀑布。（摘要直接叙述；S34、S35）
- **关系模式**：他为保护Watson夫妇跟踪列车，将Mary抛向河中由Mycroft接应。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S11、S12）
- **沟通方式**：他在峰会上揭示Rene的身份，并向Moriarty说明已调包日记。（摘要直接叙述；S28、S31）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他努力识破Moriarty的布局，却在巴黎误判炸弹位置而未能及时阻止爆炸。

### Doctor Watson

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b6f18ca7ce1e49cf` · 独立版本：`profile-e238118d5d7e237c`

- **性格倾向**：在遭受狙击的情境中，他倾向利用现场武器主动反击。（行为推断；S22、S24）
- **核心动机**：婚后仍与Holmes追查线索，显示此时愿意协助阻止Moriarty。（行为推断；S9、S13、S21、S30）
- **决策方式**：受Moran狙击时，他使用藏身其后的大炮摧毁对方所在灯塔。（摘要直接叙述；S22、S24）
- **关系模式**：他与Mary结婚，与Holmes保持紧密伙伴关系并共同追查案件。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S9、S13）
- **价值取舍**：在峰会继续与Simza阻止刺杀，显出此时愿意承担制止暴力的责任。（行为推断；S28、S30）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他与Mary刚踏上蜜月旅程便遭袭击，后来才准备补度蜜月。

### Professor Moriarty

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-1cfe8e649c294bad` · 独立版本：`profile-3edc9089d1ebfe8c`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中冷酷善算，能以谋杀、胁迫和商业布局推动计划。（行为推断；S5、S10、S23）
- **核心动机**：他意图发动世界大战，并通过战争获利公司为自己积累财富。（摘要直接叙述；S23）
- **决策方式**：他的布局以酒店爆炸掩护Meinhard遇刺，并借其死亡取得军火工厂。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：他将Adler视为受感情牵制的风险而毒杀她，显出对计划的优先维护。（行为推断；S5）
- **沟通方式**：他以Holmes干预案件为条件，明确威胁其朋友的性命。（摘要直接叙述；S10）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1891, Irene Adler  delivers a package to Dr Hoffmanstahl, a payment for a letter he was to deliver.
- **S2**：Hoffmanstahl opens the package, triggering a hidden bomb that is prevented from detonating by the intervention of Sherlock Holmes .
- **S3**：Holmes takes the letter and disposes of the bomb while Adler and Hoffmanstahl escape.
- **S4**：Holmes later finds Hoffmanstahl assassinated.
- **S5**：Adler meets with Professor Moriarty  to explain the events, but Moriarty poisons her — deeming her position compromised by her love for Holmes.
- **S6**：Some time later, Dr Watson  arrives at 221B Baker Street, where Holmes discloses that he is investigating a series of seemingly unrelated murders, terrorist attacks and business acquisitions that he has connected to Moriarty.
- **S7**：Holmes meets with a French Manouche Gypsy fortune-teller Simza , the intended recipient of the letter he had taken from Adler, sent by her brother Rene.
- **S8**：Holmes defeats an assassin sent to kill Simza, but she flees before Holmes can interrogate her.
- **S9**：After the wedding of Watson and Mary Morstan , Holmes meets Moriarty for the first time.
- **S10**：Moriarty informs Holmes that he murdered Adler and will kill Watson and Mary if Holmes' interference continues with the case.
- **S11**：Moriarty's men attack Watson and Mary on a train to their honeymoon.
- **S12**：Holmes, having followed the pair for protection, throws Mary from the train into a river below where she is picked up by Holmes' waiting brother, Mycroft .
- **S13**：After defeating Moriarty's men, Holmes and Watson travel to Paris to locate Simza.
- **S14**：When she is found, Holmes tells Simza that she has been targeted because Rene is working for Moriarty, and may have told her about his plans.
- **S15**：Simza takes the pair to the headquarters of an anarchist group to which she and Rene had formerly belonged.
- **S16**：They learn that the anarchists have been forced to plant bombs for Moriarty.
- **S17**：The trio follows Holmes' deduction that the bomb is in the Paris Opera.
- **S18**：However, Holmes realises too late that he has been tricked and that the bomb is in a nearby hotel; the bomb kills a number of assembled businessmen.
- **S19**：Holmes discovers that the bomb was a cover for the assassination of Meinhard, one of the attendees by Moriarty's aide, Sebastian Moran .
- **S20**：Meinhard's death grants Moriarty ownership of Meinhard's weapons factory in Heilbronn, Germany.
- **S21**：Holmes, Watson and Simza travel there, following clues in Rene's letters.
- **S22**：At the factory, Moriarty captures, interrogates and tortures Holmes while Watson is under sniper fire from Moran.
- **S23**：Holmes spells out Moriarty's horrific plot, revealing that the Professor secretly acquired and owns shares in multiple war profiteering companies, and intends to instigate a world war to make himself a fortune.
- **S24**：Meanwhile, Watson uses the cannon he had been hiding behind to destroy the lighthouse in which Moran is concealed.
- **S25**：The structure collapses into the warehouse where Moriarty is holding Holmes captive.
- **S26**：Watson, Simza, and an injured Holmes reunite and escape aboard a moving train.
- **S27**：Holmes deduces that Moriarty's final target will be a peace summit in Switzerland, creating an international incident.
- **S28**：At the summit, Holmes reveals that Rene is the assassin and that he is disguised as one of the ambassadors, having been given radical reconstructive surgery by Hoffmanstahl to alter his appearance.
- **S29**：Holmes and Moriarty, who is also in attendance, retreat outside to discuss their competing plans.
- **S30**：Watson and Simza find Rene and stop his assassination attempt, but Rene is himself silenced by Moran.
- **S31**：Outside, Holmes reveals that he previously replaced Moriarty's personal diary that contained all his plans and financing with a duplicate.
- **S32**：The original was sent to Mary in London, who decrypted the code using a book that Holmes had noticed in Moriarty's office during their first meeting.
- **S33**：Mary passes the information to Inspector Lestrade  who seizes the bulk of Moriarty's assets, financially crippling him.
- **S34**：Holmes and Moriarty anticipate an impending physical confrontation, and both realise Moriarty would win due to Holmes' injured shoulder.
- **S35**：Holmes instead grapples Moriarty and forces them both over the balcony and into the Reichenbach waterfall below.
- **S36**：Their bodies are not found.
- **S37**：Following Holmes' funeral, Watson and Mary prepare to have their belated honeymoon when Watson receives a package containing a breathing device of Mycroft's that Holmes had noticed before the summit.
- **S38**：Contemplating that Holmes may still be alive, Watson leaves his office to find the delivery man.
- **S39**：Holmes, having concealed himself in Watson's office, reads Watson's memoirs on the typewriter and adds a question mark after the words "The End"..

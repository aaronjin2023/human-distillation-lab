## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] At a motel outside Washington, D.C. , two men confront former army investigator Jack Reacher , calling him a disgrace for bringing his unit into disrepute and order him to leave town.
- **S3**：Reacher refuses and subdues them in a fistfight.
- **S4**：Earlier that day, Reacher had arrived at the headquarters of the 110th MP Special Investigations Unit to meet with its commander, Major Susan Turner.
- **S5**：When he arrives, however, he discovers that Turner has been relieved of command.
- **S6**：Her replacement, Colonel Morgan, says Reacher is under investigation in two cases: a suspected homicide sixteen years previous, and a lawsuit filed by Candice Dayton, a woman who alleges that she and Reacher had an affair that produced her fourteen-year-old daughter, Samantha.
- **S7**：When Reacher points out that the military has no authority to investigate him due to his civilian status, Morgan reinstates him and gives him a room at a motel, where the fight occurs later that night.
- **S8**：Learning that Turner has been incarcerated on suspicion of taking a bribe , Reacher goes to see her but is told he is not allowed to do so at her request.
- **S9**：The next morning, Reacher meets with his lawyers, Major Helen Sullivan and Captain Tracy Edmonds, and learns more about both cases.
- **S10**：He also asks Colonel Moorcroft, Turner's attorney, to get her released from custody.
- **S11**：Returning to headquarters, Reacher discovers that Morgan is nowhere to be found and that two men from the 110th on duty in Afghanistan have gone missing.
- **S12**：Reacher uses his newly reinstated authority to order a search, embarrassing Morgan and getting himself banned from setting foot inside headquarters or issuing more orders.
- **S13**：Police from the 75th MP, led by Warrant Officer Pete Espin, take Reacher into custody and bring him to the same prison where Turner is held.
- **S14**：Moorcroft has been severely beaten, and Reacher is considered a prime suspect .
- **S15**：Reacher arranges a meeting with Sullivan to buy time until Turner arrives.
- **S16**：He then stages an escape, stealing Sullivan's ID and giving it to Turner.
- **S17**：The two attempt to flee in Turner's car but are intercepted by Metro police .
- **S18**：They escape on foot to Berryville, Virginia , where Reacher informs Turner that her men in Afghanistan were assassinated and the charges against her were invented to cover up illegal activity.
- **S19**：Finding themselves tracked by unusual men, they hitch a ride into West Virginia .
- **S20**：Reacher steals money and a car from deceased meth dealer Claughton, but his relatives identify the car, locate Reacher and Turner's motel and confront them.
- **S21**：Reacher intimidates them into backing down and takes one of their trucks.
- **S22**：Turner says the mission in Afghanistan was linked to a Pashtun elder, later identified as Emal Zadran, and theorizes that Morgan, the unusual men and higher-ups in the army's chain of command (using the pseudonyms Romeo and Juliet) are working together to protect Zadran.
- **S23**：She also insists that Reacher go to Los Angeles to confront Candice.
- **S24**：They track down Samantha, but Espin confirms that the paternity claim is bogus as he found that someone named Romeo had secured a falsified birth certificate from a corrupt lawyer and had Candice sign it for $100.
- **S25**：Sullivan also discovers that the homicide charge was faked as well, clearing Reacher of any potential legal consequences.
- **S26**：Through Edmonds, Reacher and Turner learn that an illicit operation was being run through Fort Bragg under the control of Crew Scully and Gabriel Montague, both Deputy Chiefs of Staff , involving the smuggling of contraband using empty ordnance crates.
- **S27**：Reacher arranges for Morgan and Staff Sergeant Ezra Shrago, one of the smugglers, to be arrested, and locates Scully and Montague in Georgetown , where both men choose to commit suicide rather than be arrested.
- **S28**：Morgan and Shrago reveal that Zadran had been supplying their bosses with opium , and Turner's name is cleared, allowing her to resume command.
- **S29**：Reacher drops his phone in the trash and sits on a bench waiting for the next bus.

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Oscar Finley and Wally Figg are ambulance chasers at a small law firm in the South Side of Chicago . [ 4 ] Their constant bickering is often mediated by Rochelle, their highly competent African-American secretary.
- **S3**：Meanwhile, David Zinc, a Harvard Law School graduate, is completely fed up with the grinding and dehumanizing – though well-paid – life of an associate in the high-powered law firm of Rogan Rothberg.
- **S4**：David suddenly breaks away, goes on a drinking binge and by chance finds himself at the Finley & Figg office, where he willingly relegates himself to working for the two disreputable street lawyers.
- **S5**：Wally gets involved in a new scheme, finding claimants for a federal class action lawsuit against Krayoxx, a cholesterol -lowering drug developed by the fictional pharmaceutical company Varrick Labs.
- **S6**：Users across the country, both dead and alive, appear to have developed toxic reactions to the drug.
- **S7**：Though the firm is out of its depth, Wally gains the assurance of a South Florida lawyer, Jerry Alisandros, that Alisandros will handle the case and reach an out-of-court settlement, and everybody will get rich.
- **S8**：However, complications that no one anticipated arise, including Varrick's hiring of Nadine Karros, Rogan Rothberg's ace litigator who never loses a case, and the growing evidence that there is nothing fundamentally wrong with Krayoxx.
- **S9**：The drug works as advertised, has no ill effects, and is unjustly maligned.
- **S10**：Varrick pushes to have the case tried in the jurisdiction of Chicago federal judge Harry Seawright, with whom Rogan Rothberg has ties.
- **S11**：The case is expedited on Seawright's docket, with Finley & Figg's claim singled out of the tort claimants.
- **S12**：Alisandros pulls out as co-counsel, leaving Finley & Figg to litigate the case themselves.
- **S13**：The resulting trial brings the firm's usual cast of shady witnesses to the stand in a desperate attempt to get through the trial and avoid being sued for legal malpractice and saddled with frivolous lawsuit sanctions.
- **S14**：In a subsidiary plot, David Zinc stumbles on a lead poisoning brain damage case involving the child of Burmese immigrants.
- **S15**：His efforts to identify the American company which imported the child's toxic toys from China, and reach a settlement with the importer, help him survive the demise of Finley & Figg and open his own successful law firm with Rochelle as his legal secretary.

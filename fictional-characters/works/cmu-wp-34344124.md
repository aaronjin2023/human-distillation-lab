## 角色资料

### Sarah Connor

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e318c613ae24cc3a` · 独立版本：`profile-d3c4134a2f198dbe`

- **性格倾向**：面对预期中的末日，她倾向高度戒备并主动采取激烈行动（行为推断；S2、S15）
- **核心动机**：面对天网将引发核战的未来，她试图通过杀死戴森阻止末日（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **情绪反应**：核爆噩梦激起的恐惧，推动她从准备逃亡转向刺杀工程师（行为推断；S15）
- **价值取舍**：即使相信杀人能够阻止末日，她仍无法当着家人完成刺杀（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）
- **成长变化**：她从被未来灾难驱动，转向因机器尊重生命而重新产生希望（行为推断；S15、S24）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：为保护人类而准备杀死个人，却在对方家人面前无法下手。

### T-800 Terminator

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-e8e5edcba40d122e` · 独立版本：`profile-08bbe4e84cbd242b`

- **性格倾向**：在护卫与阻止末日的情境中，它倾向持续履责并承担自身损毁（行为推断；S8、S9、S21、S23）
- **核心动机**：它受未来约翰派遣，核心任务是保护少年约翰的生命（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **决策方式**：面对技术可能再造天网的风险，它选择彻底消除自身残留（行为推断；S23）
- **价值取舍**：在最终取舍中，它将阻止人类灾难置于自身继续存在之上（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **关系模式**：它以实际救援使原本恐惧自己的莎拉逐步接受协助（摘要直接叙述；S12）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1995, eleven years after the events of The Terminator, John Connor  is ten years old and living in Los Angeles with foster parents.
- **S2**：His mother Sarah Connor  had been preparing him throughout his childhood for his future role as the leader of the human Resistance against Skynet, but was arrested after attempting to bomb a computer factory and remanded to a hospital for the criminally insane under the supervision of Dr.
- **S3**：Silberman .
- **S4**：Skynet sends a new Terminator, a T-1000 , back in time to kill John.
- **S5**：A prototype which is more advanced than the Terminator sent after Sarah, the T-1000 is composed of a "mimetic poly-alloy", a liquid metal that allows it to take the shape and appearance of anyone or anything it touches.
- **S6**：Though it cannot mimic complex machines such as guns or bombs, it can shape parts of itself into knives and stabbing weapons and can mimic the voice and appearance of humans.
- **S7**：It assumes the identity of a police officer and goes in pursuit of John.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, the future John Connor has sent back a reprogrammed T-800 Terminator  to protect his younger self.
- **S9**：The Terminator and the T-1000 converge on John in a shopping mall, and a chase ensues in which John and the Terminator escape by motorcycle.
- **S10**：Fearing that the T-1000 will kill Sarah in order to get to him, John orders the Terminator to help free her.
- **S11**：They encounter Sarah in the midst of her own escape attempt.
- **S12**：She is initially terrified by the Terminator but accepts its assistance after it helps them to escape the T-1000.
- **S13**：The Terminator informs John and Sarah about Skynet, the artificial intelligence that will initiate a nuclear war on "Judgment Day" and go on to create the machines that will hunt the remnants of humanity.In The Terminator, Sarah is only informed by Kyle Reese that Skynet would become self-aware and initiate a nuclear war; in Terminator 2, the T-800 discloses the date of such an event to be August 29, 1997.
- **S14**：Sarah learns that the man most directly responsible for Skynet's creation is Miles Dyson , a Cyberdyne Systems engineer working on a revolutionary new microprocessor that will form the basis for Skynet.
- **S15**：Sarah gathers weapons from an old friend and plans to flee with John to Mexico, but after having a horrific nightmare of a nuclear explosion she awakens and sets out to kill Miles Dyson to prevent "Judgment Day" from occurring.
- **S16**：She wounds him at his home but finds herself unable to kill him in front of his family.
- **S17**：John and the Terminator arrive and inform Miles of the consequences of his work and learn that much of his research has been reverse engineered from the CPU and arm of the previous Terminator sent after Sarah.
- **S18**：Convincing him that these items and his designs must be destroyed, they break into the Cyberdyne building and retrieve the CPU and arm.
- **S19**：The police and the T-1000 arrive and Miles is mortally shot and stays behind to trigger a detonator that will destroy his research.
- **S20**：The T-1000 pursues John, Sarah, and the Terminator, catching up to them in a steel mill.
- **S21**：In a climactic battle, the Terminator fires a grenade into the T-1000 and it falls into a vat of molten steel, where it is destroyed.
- **S22**：John throws the components of the original Terminator into the vat as well.
- **S23**：The Terminator then sacrifices itself, asking Sarah to lower it into the steel so that its technology cannot be used to create Skynet.
- **S24**：Sarah looks to the future with hope, believing that if a machine can learn the value of human life, humanity may not be doomed to self-destruction.

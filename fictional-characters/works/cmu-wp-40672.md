### Leone Montana

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a2e9dcd49a837788` · 独立版本：`profile-c2285d482207e12f`

- **性格倾向**：在日常关系中他倾向孤独自守，面对求助却逐渐显露保护性（行为推断；S1、S3、S10、S20）
- **价值取舍**：危急时他将玛蒂尔达的生存置于自己的安全之前（行为推断；S23、S24、S25）
- **决策方式**：面对围捕，他倾向利用空间与伪装创造有限逃生机会（行为推断；S24、S26）
- **成长变化**：与玛蒂尔达相处使他从情感封闭转向承认对生活的眷恋（行为推断；S16、S25）

### Mathilda Lando

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-743e1fe77647342b` · 独立版本：`profile-b7d9b24ec01d0b4c`

- **性格倾向**：在失亲与威胁中，她倾向主动争取行动能力并冒险出击（行为推断；S12、S17、S18）
- **核心动机**：她学习杀手技能的明确目标，是替唯一深爱的弟弟报仇（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13）
- **关系模式**：她以生活照料和识字教学回馈莱昂，并主动表达爱意（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）
- **成长变化**：失去莱昂后，她在求职受拒后重返学校并保存关系纪念（行为推断；S34、S35、S36、S37、S38）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：渴望获得照顾，却试图承担超出自身处境的复仇行动

### Norman Stansfield

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-171e3682474f5d31` · 独立版本：`profile-b161de9798d4a837`

- **性格倾向**：遭遇阻碍或损失时，他倾向以失控暴力和报复回应（行为推断；S9、S21）
- **核心动机**：手下接连被杀后，他将追查并消灭莱昂作为报复目标（摘要直接叙述；S21、S22、S27）
- **价值取舍**：他为犯罪利益与私人报复牺牲无辜者和执法职责（行为推断；S7、S8、S9、S22）
- **决策方式**：他混用刑讯与警力围捕，并在识破伪装后选择背后偷袭（行为推断；S21、S22、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Leone "Léon" Montana  is a hitman  living a solitary life in New York City's Little Italy.
- **S2**：His work comes from a mafioso named Tony , who operates from the "Supreme Macaroni Company" restaurant.
- **S3**：Léon spends his idle time engaging in calisthenics, nurturing a houseplant that early on he describes as his "best friend",<ref name9806E6DD1031F93BA25752C1A962958260 He May Be a Killer, But He's Such a Sweetie], a November 1994 review of the film from The New York Times and  watching old Gene Kelly musicals.
- **S4**：One day, Léon sees Mathilda Lando , a twelve-year-old girl who is smoking a cigarette and sporting a black eye.
- **S5**：Mathilda lives with her dysfunctional family in an apartment down the hall.
- **S6**：Her abusive father and self-absorbed stepmother have not noticed that Mathilda stopped attending class at her school for troubled girls.
- **S7**：Mathilda's father  attracts the ire of corrupt DEA agents, who have been paying him to stash cocaine in his apartment.
- **S8**：After they discover some of the drugs missing, DEA&nbsp;agents storm the building, led by sharply dressed drug addict Norman Stansfield .
- **S9**：During the raid, Stansfield quickly becomes unhinged and murders Mathilda's entire family one by one, except for Mathilda, who was missing only because she was out shopping.
- **S10**：Mathilda returns from her shopping trip as the group clean up the carnage, and realizes what happened just in time to continue down the hall, where a reluctant Léon gives her shelter.
- **S11**：Mathilda quickly discovers that Léon is a "cleaner", or hit man.
- **S12**：She begs him to take care of her and to teach her his skills as a cleaner.
- **S13**：She wants to avenge the murder of her four-year-old brother, telling Léon that he was the only one of her family she loved.
- **S14**：Léon shows her how to use guns, including a scoped rifle.
- **S15**：In return, she runs his errands, cleans his apartment, and teaches him how to read.
- **S16**：Several times Mathilda tells Léon "I love you", but he offers no response.
- **S17**：Then one day after Mathilda has learned how to shoot, she fills a bag with guns from Léon's collection and sets out to kill Stansfield.
- **S18**：She bluffs her way into the DEA office by posing as a delivery girl, only to be ambushed by Stansfield in a bathroom.
- **S19**：Mathilda learns from Stansfield and one of his men that Léon has killed one of the corrupt DEA agents in Chinatown that morning.
- **S20**：Léon, after discovering her plan in a note left for him, rescues Mathilda, shooting two more of Stansfield's men in the process.
- **S21**：Stansfield, now enraged that Léon has killed more of his men, goes to find Tony, then beats him to find out where Léon is.
- **S22**：When Mathilda returns home from grocery shopping, an NYPD ESU team sent by Stansfield takes her hostage and attempts to infiltrate Léon's apartment.
- **S23**：Léon ambushes the ESU team and grabs Mathilda.
- **S24**：Back in his apartment, Léon creates a quick escape for Mathilda by smashing a hole in an air shaft.
- **S25**：He reassures her and tells her that he loves her and that she has given him "a taste for life", moments before the police come for him.
- **S26**：In the chaos that follows, Léon sneaks out of the apartment building disguised as a wounded ESU officer.
- **S27**：He goes unnoticed save for Stansfield, who recognizes him and follows him downstairs, sneaks up behind him, and shoots him in the back.
- **S28**：As Léon falls, he places an object in Stansfield's hands that he says is "from Mathilda", and dies.
- **S29**：Opening his hands to see the object, Stansfield discovers that it is the pin from a grenade.
- **S30**：He then opens Léon's vest to find a cluster of active grenades, which detonate moments later, the huge explosion killing him instantly.
- **S31**：Mathilda heads to Tony's place as Léon had instructed her before he died.
- **S32**：Tony refuses to give her the money he had saved for Léon, offering her instead to give her money when she needs it—the same con he plied on Léon.
- **S33**：Tony tells her school should be her priority for now.
- **S34**：Mathilda asks Tony to give her a job, insisting that she can "clean" like Léon.
- **S35**：Tony sternly says he "ain't got no work for a 12-year-old kid!" With nowhere else to go, Mathilda returns to a private school from her past.
- **S36**：She recounts her story to the headmistress, who readmits her.
- **S37**：Mathilda then walks into a field near the school with Léon's houseplant in hand.
- **S38**：She digs a hole and plants it, as she had told Léon he should, to give it roots.

### John McClane

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-c65bcc87a151cbc5` · 独立版本：`profile-84fd721b64772a22`

- **性格倾向**：面对持续追杀与负伤，他倾向坚持追击而不轻易退却（行为推断；S27、S30、S31、S32、S33）

- **关系模式**：虽与女儿关系疏远，他仍介意她与法雷尔产生好感（摘要直接叙述；S23、S35）

### Matthew Farrell

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b06783dca10a1f1b` · 独立版本：`profile-49a69341af06aed0`

- **性格倾向**：面对连环网络攻击，他倾向从技术线索分析整体威胁（行为推断；S7、S12、S25）
- **核心动机**：最初接受编写安全代码的工作，是为了获得高额报酬（摘要直接叙述；S5）
- **决策方式**：面对自身知识不足，他倾向寻找可信同行补足信息（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **成长变化**：从有偿提供代码转为主动加密数据，他开始阻断犯罪用途（行为推断；S5、S25）
- **关系模式**：危急对抗中，他从受保护者转为能协助结束战斗的伙伴（行为推断；S3、S34）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：曾为报酬提供代码，后来主动阻止相关数据被罪犯利用。

### Thomas Gabriel

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-242a50d446f15a78` · 独立版本：`profile-f80ffb56a9527a3a`

- **性格倾向**：面对阻挠，他倾向扩大控制并利用他人的脆弱处施压（行为推断；S23、S28、S29）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The Federal Bureau of Investigation  responds to a brief computer outage at their Cyber-Security Division by tracing down top computer hackers, finding several of them have been killed.
- **S2**：Taking others into protective custody, the FBI asks NYPD detective John McClane  to collect Matthew "Matt" Farrell .
- **S3**：McClane arrives in time to prevent Farrell from being killed by an assassin, Mai Linh , working for Thomas Gabriel .
- **S4**：En route to Washington, D.C.
- **S5**：Farrell explains he had written a portion of security code for a large sum of money from Mai.
- **S6**：As they arrive in D.C., Gabriel orders his own crew of hackers to take control of the transportation grids and stock market, while nationally broadcasting a message threatening the United States.
- **S7**：Farrell recognizes this as the start of a "fire sale", an attack designed to target the nation's reliance on computer controls, such that "everything must go".
- **S8**：McClane and Farrell are taken by police escorts to the secured FBI headquarters, but Mai, using the hijacked grids, reroutes the convoy into the path of an assault helicopter.
- **S9**：McClane is able to take down the helicopter by launching a police car over a damaged toll booth into it.
- **S10**：As McClane and Farrell recover, Gabriel initiates a second broadcast, showing a simulated explosion of the U.S.
- **S11**：Capitol building, sending the public into panic.
- **S12**：Farrell recognizes that the next target of the "fire sale" is likely the power grid, and the two drive to a utility superstation in West Virginia.
- **S13**：They find a team led by Mai breaking into the station's controls.
- **S14**：McClane is able to kill the entire team, including Mai, and obtain video footage of Gabriel, which they relay to FBI headquarters.
- **S15**：Enraged over Mai's death, Gabriel uses remote control of the station to redirect the natural gas supply into it.
- **S16**：McClane and Farrell escape before the station explodes, leaving much of the eastern seaboard without power.
- **S17**：Farrell directs McClane to a fellow hacker, Frederick "Warlock" Kaludis , in Baltimore.
- **S18**：Warlock, running his computer systems from several generators, identifies the piece of code Farrell wrote as a means to access data at a Social Security Administration building at Woodlawn, Maryland.
- **S19**：They realize this building is a front for a National Security Agency facility, designed by Gabriel to download all personal and financial records in case of a cyber-security emergency such as this one generated by Gabriel himself.
- **S20**：Warlock is also able to explain Gabriel's motivation: a talented hacker, he was once a top expert for the U.S.
- **S21**：Defense Department.
- **S22**：However, Gabriel was fired and his reputation was tarnished when he tried to sound the alarm about America's vulnerability to cyber-warfare.
- **S23**：Gabriel detects Warlock's hack and speaks with him, Farrell, and McClane, and reveals that he has kidnapped McClane's estranged daughter, Lucy .
- **S24**：McClane and Farrell race to the Woodlawn facility.
- **S25**：As McClane deals with Gabriel's men, Farrell discovers that Gabriel is downloading all the information onto a portable computer system, and he is able to encrypt the data after it is completed.
- **S26**：Gabriel is forced to take Farrell as they escape the facility.
- **S27**：McClane follows, hijacking Gabriel's semi and gains Warlock's help to trace Gabriel's own vehicle.
- **S28**：With McClane in pursuit, Gabriel hacks into the military's computers to deceive a U.S.
- **S29**：Marine F-35B Lightning II pilot into believing that McClane is a terrorist.
- **S30**：McClane is able to escape the assault when pieces from the damaged truck fall into the jet intake of the aircraft from a highway exit ramp.
- **S31**：McClane makes his way to a nearby warehouse to which Warlock tracked Gabriel.
- **S32**：Though McClane dispatches most of Gabriel's men, Gabriel's last henchman, Emerson, wounds him in the shoulder.
- **S33**：When Gabriel holds McClane from behind, McClane manages to make Gabriel fire a gun through McClane's wounded shoulder, killing Gabriel.
- **S34**：Farrell then kills Emerson with a gun that had fallen to the floor earlier in the confrontation.
- **S35**：As the FBI arrives to tend to McClane and Farrell's wounds and clean up, McClane is displeased to see Lucy and Farrell taking a romantic interest in each other.

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Billy Summers is a former Marine sniper and hitman who only accepts jobs killing truly evil men.
- **S3**：When he expresses a desire to retire from the life of being an assassin, Nick Majarian, a mobster for whom Billy has worked many times before, offers him one last job – one that pays $500,000 up front, and $1.5 million after it is done.
- **S4**：Billy's target is Joel Allen, another hitman, who was arrested for murdering a man who won a fortune from him in a poker game.
- **S5**：Allen has claimed to have valuable information that the police want in order to make a plea bargain , and an unnamed associate of Nick wants to keep him from talking.
- **S6**：The job requires Billy to go undercover as a resident in the small Southeastern town of Red Bluff, where an office space has been rented out for his use.
- **S7**：Billy's cover story is that he is a writer named David Lockridge, who has been tasked by his agent to go to the office and write each day in an attempt to meet his deadline.
- **S8**：The office has a direct view of the courthouse, where Allen will eventually be arraigned for his murder charge.
- **S9**：Billy is meant to shoot and kill him at that time, and then disappear.
- **S10**：However, Billy becomes suspicious when Nick offers up a getaway plan for after the hit takes place, since typically he leaves that up to Billy to figure out.
- **S11**：His plan involves Billy escaping in a city transit truck that will have someone waiting for him to drive away, causing Billy to suspect that Nick intends to kill him after he pulls off the hit.
- **S12**：Instead, Billy starts to formulate his own plan, including renting out a new apartment in the suburb of Midwood under the alias Dalton Smith.
- **S13**：As he waits for Allen's arraignment, Billy also starts writing his fake book his character is supposed to be writing.
- **S14**：Using pseudonyms, Billy writes about his life, starting with the murder of his little sister by his mother's drunk boyfriend when he was eleven.
- **S15**：A young Billy ended up finding a gun and killing the boyfriend.
- **S16**：He later ended up in foster care , and then joined the Marines when he was seventeen.
- **S17**：Back in the present, the killing of Joel Allen goes off without a hitch.
- **S18**：Billy evades the transit truck and gets away by disguising himself as one of the office workers in the building.
- **S19**：He then goes to hide out in "Dalton Smith's" apartment, where he intends to lie low during the manhunt following the shooting.
- **S20**：When Nick doesn't pay him the rest of the money he promised after the hit, Billy realizes that his suspicions were correct; he also soon learns that there is a $6 million bounty on his head.
- **S21**：While Billy is trying to keep a low profile, he sees a young woman get dumped out of a truck onto the street, drugged and half-dead.
- **S22**：Not wanting to attract police attention to his location, he reluctantly goes out to save her.
- **S23**：It turns out that the woman, 21-year-old Alice Maxwell, had just been raped and abandoned by a group of men.
- **S24**：Alice recognizes Billy as the shooter, but ends up wanting to stay with him out of fear that the men who raped her might come back.
- **S25**：Meanwhile, Billy continues writing his book, recounting his experiences in the military.
- **S26**：He writes about an incident in Iraq where he and his comrades were sent to check out a large house, and a majority of them were killed in action there.
- **S27**：One of the survivors, Johnny Capps, would later hook Billy up with his first job as a hitman.
- **S28**：When Billy decides to leave Midwood, he first goes to confront Alice's attackers.
- **S29**：He demands that two of them apologize to her over the phone, and then sodomizes their leader with an immersion blender.
- **S30**：After that, Billy and Alice drive to Colorado to meet with Bucky Hanson, Billy's "broker" and the only person Billy fully trusts.
- **S31**：Infiltrating Nick's estate in Las Vegas , Billy kills or injures many of his men, including seriously injuring Nick's right-hand man, Frank Macintosh.
- **S32**：Billy manages to extract a promise from Nick to pay him the money owed and to tell people that Billy is dead.
- **S33**：Nick also confesses to Billy that the person who ordered the hit of Allen (and subsequently the hit on Billy) was Roger Klerke, a wealthy media mogul, in order to bury evidence of Klerke's pedophilia .
- **S34**：Billy and Alice stay at Bucky's place, where their relationship grows.
- **S35**：Billy finishes writing his book to bring it all the way up to the present.
- **S36**：Meanwhile, he learns that Frank survived his assault, but suffers from seizures caused by the injury inflicted by Billy.
- **S37**：When it is finally time to deal with Roger, Alice takes photos of herself dressed up as a teenager to entice Klerke into a meeting.
- **S38**：Klerke takes the bait, and Alice and Billy show up at his estate, where Alice kills Klerke.
- **S39**：During their escape, Frank's mother Marge appears and shoots Billy for revenge.
- **S40**：Billy is wounded, and when he and Alice get back to their hotel, he tells her that she's better off not being involved in his lifestyle.
- **S41**：He leaves with the hopes of becoming a full-time writer, and maybe even being able to atone for his past misdeeds.
- **S42**：Then, in the final chapter, it is revealed that the last part of Billy's story was in fact written by Alice as Billy died from his wounds, and Alice wrote it to convey his thoughts and to write a scenario where he survived.

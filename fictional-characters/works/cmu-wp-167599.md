## 角色资料

### Agent J

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-f401117667f73090` · 独立版本：`profile-138cbcde4cc7dcac`

- **性格倾向**：在追捕与新环境中，他显出临场大胆、愿意迎向未知的倾向。（行为推断；S7、S8、S9、S14）
- **核心动机**：面对虫族逃离的危机，他着力寻找飞船并协助追回银河。（行为推断；S35、S36、S38、S45）

- **成长变化**：他从纽约新警员转为探员，结尾又与新搭档继续调查外星人。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S15、S51）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

### Agent K

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-ff1a7c8ac85ab322` · 独立版本：`profile-7fbcd0b55d9cb679`

- **性格倾向**：面对尚未解开的线索，他倾向保持怀疑并主动寻求核实。（行为推断；S27、S28）
- **核心动机**：他训练J是为培养继任者，自己已准备从机构退休。（摘要直接叙述；S48）
- **价值取舍**：在阻止虫族逃走时，他愿以自身承受危险来争取制敌机会。（行为推断；S40、S41、S44）
- **决策方式**：在武器被吞后，他让J牵制敌人，自己深入其腹内取枪反击。（摘要直接叙述；S39、S40、S41、S44）
- **关系模式**：他以训练和交接建立与J的搭档关系，也将J视作自己的继任者。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15、S48）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他承担探员与训练职责，同时已准备退休回归私人生活。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1997, Agent K  is a member of the Men in Black , a secret agency without ties to any government, whose goal is to maintain Earth as a "neutral zone" for extraterrestrial aliens, whom they disguise as humans around New York City.
- **S2**：The agency maintains its secrecy by using a neuralyzer to wipe the memories of those that encounter either them or the aliens, and also the memories of former agents when they retire.
- **S3**：Operating from an underground base at a Triborough Bridge and Tunnel Authority ventilation station, they fund themselves by obtaining patents on alien technologies released for consumer consumption.
- **S4**：K's former partner D  has just retired after an incident at a border line between the U.S and Mexico where an alien disguised as a human almost killed an INS Officer, so K is forced to find a new partner.
- **S5**：Agent Zed , the Agency's current leader, suggests that he should find a new recruit, someone "fresh".
- **S6**：Meanwhile, James Darrell Edwards III  is a rookie police officer who is pursuing a man through the streets of Manhattan.
- **S7**：He chases the suspect on foot with his mentor officers, but due to their weight, he is forced to apprehend him alone.
- **S8**：Edwards is surprised as the man has the ability to jump and climb very high.
- **S9**：After some chase, he manages to corner him on the roof.
- **S10**：The man smiles at Edwards and blinks very unusually , as he falls down to his death.
- **S11**：Edwards describes the chase to his supervisor, but the supervisor doesn't believe his explanation.
- **S12**：Just then, K arrives at the office and neuralyzes the supervisor, the two cops, and Edwards.
- **S13**：Later, K talks to Edwards and offers him a "new life", gives him the MIB address, and tells him to go to the address the next day.
- **S14**：In the base, after Edwards completes several double-meaning tests in unconventional ways , K offers him the chance to join the MIB.
- **S15**：Edwards' identity is erased, and he becomes Agent J, K's partner.
- **S16**：K initiates J into the MIB but during this, they learn that numerous alien refugees are fleeing the Earth.
- **S17**：MIB is contacted by an Arquillian fleet in Earth orbit; the fleet warns that a "Bug", a member of a giant cockroach-like species that are at war with several alien races, has come to Earth searching for an object known as the Galaxy.
- **S18**：Its infinite power could turn the tide of war in favor of the Bugs.
- **S19**：To prevent this they are, though, regretfully willing to destroy the Earth unless the MIB can secure the Galaxy before their deadline, only hours away.
- **S20**：Agent K and Agent J find the Bug is disguised using the skin of farmer Edgar  after killing the human.
- **S21**：It already killed two Arquillians it believed had the Galaxy.
- **S22**：The two bodies, along with a cat that refuses to leave its owner, Rosenberg, are taken to the city morgue.
- **S23**：There, Dr.
- **S24**：Laurel Weaver  discovers one of the tiny Arquillians is barely alive inside its human looking robot; struggling with English, it whispers to her, "To prevent war, the Galaxy is on Orion's belt," before it expires.
- **S25**：K and J arrive to help remove the alien bodies and wipe Laurel's mind, but not before learning about this message, and Laurel suffers a déjà vu when she spots J.
- **S26**：They return to the base, where Zed explains that there are no galaxies on Orion's belt.
- **S27**：Agent K, still suspicious about J's words, goes to Frank the Pug , a Remoonian talking dog, who explains to K that the galaxy doesn't have to be in space and enormous to actually be a galaxy.
- **S28**：Suspicious about this, J and K head to the jewelry store Rosenberg owned.
- **S29**：They find the store broken in, but it's empty.
- **S30**：Noticing the Bug, they proceed to chase and lose it.
- **S31**：They then look at the picture of Rosenberg and his cat and realize that Orion refers to Rosenberg's pet and that the belt would mean the cat's collar.
- **S32**：Racing back to the morgue, they encounter the Bug, who has figured out the same thing.
- **S33**：It steals the Galaxy, which hangs in a small charm, and kidnaps Laurel - escaping while the MIB secure its spacecraft to prevent it from leaving the planet.
- **S34**：As the Arquillian deadline looms, the MIB tries to figure out how Edgar will leave.
- **S35**：J then recalls from his initial briefing that the observation towers of the New York State Pavilion at Flushing Meadows are disguised spacecraft.
- **S36**：Agents K and J race there, finding Edgar climbing one of the structures.
- **S37**：Their arrival distracts the Bug but it enters the craft and starts the engines.
- **S38**：The agents fire on the ship, forcing it to crash.
- **S39**：As the two face off against Edgar, it rips through its disguise, swallows their guns and knocks them across the field.
- **S40**：K tells J to keep the Bug from getting to the second ship by creating a distraction.
- **S41**：K goads the Bug to eat him, which it does by swallowing him whole.
- **S42**：J keeps the Bug distracted by stepping on cockroaches around a nearby dumpster.
- **S43**：Before J can be eaten by the enraged Bug, he tells it to get out of his face before something bad happens.
- **S44**：K, having found his gun in the Bug's stomach, then blasts his way out, ripping the alien in half.
- **S45**：K is slime covered but safe and J picks up the Galaxy from among the contents of Edgar's stomach.
- **S46**：The news is then relayed to the Arquillian fleet, saving the Earth.
- **S47**：While J and K are recovering from their battle, the top-half of the Bug suddenly rears up behind them, but Laurel shoots the Bug with J's gun, which was ejected from the Bug's stomach.
- **S48**：As MIB cleans up the situation, and K apparently prepares to neuralyze Laurel, he admits that he was not training J as a partner but as his replacement, as he is ready to retire from the agency, thinking about the words D said to him.
- **S49**：K gives him the neuralyzer and explains the device, showing the 4 buttons, with one as the activator, the three others being "Days, Months, Years".
- **S50**：He also tells J "I'll see you around", to which J responds; "No, you won't.", and neuralyzes him.
- **S51**：In the epilogue, Laurel is Agent J's new partner Agent L, while K  has returned to civilian life and the woman he left behind , and J and L began to investigate more aliens disguised as humans as they drive away to the headquarters.
- **S52**：As the camera pans out, showing the Earth, solar system and eventually the galaxy, it is revealed that the galaxy is itself in a marble.
- **S53**：A pair of alien hands finish playing with it and place it inside a sack with other such galaxies .

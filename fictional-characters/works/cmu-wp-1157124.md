## 角色资料

### Bridget Jones

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-54288a6eadf343b9` · 独立版本：`profile-be67cdd078f6b3e0`

- **性格倾向**：在亲密关系缺乏明确保证时倾向敏感不安，容易受比较影响（行为推断；S3、S5、S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：她期待恋情获得明确承诺，并将共同未来视为重要关系目标（行为推断；S5、S10、S12、S13）
- **价值取舍**：即使受到旧情吸引，她仍倾向拒绝令自己难以接受的亲密安排（行为推断；S15）
- **决策方式**：发现自己误解恋人付出后，倾向主动争取修复关系而非等待（行为推断；S25、S26、S27）
- **关系模式**：在监禁处境中，她通过分享感情经历和教歌与狱友建立联系（摘要直接叙述；S18）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望稳定承诺，却因不安与对方含糊回应而主动结束恋情。

### Mark Darcy

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-9c5733e0eae7ff86` · 独立版本：`profile-8700e84e4139077a`

- **性格倾向**：在关系受挫后倾向承担实际事务，但对自身付出表现得克制（行为推断；S19、S21、S25）
- **价值取舍**：在前伴侣陷入困境时，仍倾向把帮助对方置于关系破裂之上（行为推断；S13、S25）
- **沟通方式**：面对感情承诺时表达并不稳定，既会道歉示爱也会回避解释（行为推断；S7、S12、S13）
- **决策方式**：处理布里奇特被拘事件时，倾向追查关键人物并调动制度资源（行为推断；S25）
- **成长变化**：他从尚不考虑婚姻的表态，转向在复合时主动提出求婚（摘要直接叙述；S12、S27、S28）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、关系模式

矛盾与变化：实际投入大量精力营救对方，却把自己说成只是传话者。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film begins shortly before Bridget's mother's  yearly Turkey Curry Buffet.
- **S2**：Bridget  is ecstatic about her relationship with Mark Darcy .
- **S3**：However, Bridget's confidence in her relationship is shattered when she meets Mark's colleague, the beautiful Rebecca Gilles .
- **S4**：Bridget meets her ex, Daniel Cleaver , at her job for Sit-Up Britain and is offered a position as the "Smooth Guidess".
- **S5**：Bridget is delighted to be asked by Mark to go to the "Law Council Dinner", assuming he will propose afterwards, but the night does not end well.
- **S6**：After the "Law Council Dinner", Mark and Bridget have an argument and she walks away from him.
- **S7**：Mark goes to Bridget's apartment, apologizes, and tells her he loves her for the first time.
- **S8**：Later in the night, Mark asks Bridget if she'd like to go on a ski holiday.
- **S9**：Once on the slopes, she learns Rebecca recommended the vacation spot to Mark.
- **S10**：While on the holiday, Bridget thinks she's pregnant and after an argument concerning the future of children, the test is negative.
- **S11**：After they return home, Bridget and Mark have lunch with both of their parents.
- **S12**：When the subject of marriage comes up during conversation, Bridget is hurt by Mark's comment that it's not something they're even thinking about yet.
- **S13**：At Mark's place, Bridget is upset at him for not giving a straight answer on his reasons and breaks off their relationship.
- **S14**：Bridget decides to go with Daniel to Thailand to film "The Smooth Guide" with her friend, Shazzer .
- **S15**：Bridget and Daniel flirt on the plane which leads to them alone in a hotel room, but she walks out after a prostitute arrives.
- **S16**：While packing for the trip home from Thailand, Bridget puts Shazzer's gift from a man named Jed ([[Paul Nicholls  in her bag.
- **S17**：Bridget is arrested and sent to a Thai prison after airport security dogs recognize cocaine inside the gift.
- **S18**：In prison, Bridget spends her time sharing relationship stories with the inmates and teaching them Madonna's "Like a Virgin".
- **S19**：Mark arrives to tell Bridget that his superiors have sent him to put her release in motion.
- **S20**：Bridget identifies Jed on a picture as the man who gave Shazzer the hidden cocaine.
- **S21**：Mark walks away after clearly stating that he was just the messenger.
- **S22**：In Britain, Mark confronts Daniel for not helping Bridget when she was arrested, and they start a fight outside a museum.
- **S23**：At Bridget's return, she is an international human rights celebrity.
- **S24**：She is greeted by her parents who have been busy planning their vow renewal ceremony.
- **S25**：At home, she is surprised by her friends who inform her that Mark was the one who tracked down Jed and forced him into custody in order to free her, in the process putting into motion the British Government, Mi5, Interpol & many other diplomatic big-wigs.
- **S26**：Hopeful that he still loves her, she runs down to his house, but finds Rebecca there, who reveals that she does not love Mark, but loves Bridget instead.
- **S27**：Bridget confronts Mark at his legal chambers and asks him to take her back.
- **S28**：Mark proposes to Bridget and she accepts.
- **S29**：The film ends with Bridget's parents renewing their vows and Bridget catching the bouquet.

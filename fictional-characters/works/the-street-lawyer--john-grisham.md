## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] A homeless man, identifying himself only as "Mister," enters the offices of the powerful Washington D.C. law firm Drake & Sweeney and takes many of the lawyers hostage while angrily demanding information about some kind of eviction that took place.
- **S3**：Although he is eventually shot and killed by a police sniper, one of the hostages, an antitrust lawyer named Michael Brock, is concerned by what he has learned and feels compelled to investigate further.
- **S4**：Brock finds his way to the 14th Street Legal Clinic, where he meets Mordecai Green, an advocate for the homeless.
- **S5**：Green, along with his abrasive but brilliant staff, work to provide legal help to the most downtrodden members of society.
- **S6**：Brock discovers that Drake & Sweeney were involved in the sudden approval of a federal building project on the site of a condemned building that had been serving as rent-payment housing for formerly homeless families.
- **S7**：These individuals were tenants and thus entitled to a full legal eviction/contestment process, but a senior Drake & Sweeney partner ignored this information because the firm had a large stake in ensuring the federal project start on time, and thus illegally evicted the tenants in the middle of winter, resulting in the death of a homeless family.
- **S8**：Brock takes a confidential file, intending to copy it, but is quickly suspected of its theft.
- **S9**：Shocked by what he has found, Brock leaves Drake & Sweeney to take a poorly paid position with the legal clinic.
- **S10**：This leads to the severing of his links to his previous white collar life, as his already-dying marriage officially ends in an amicable divorce.
- **S11**：Brock later becomes emotionally involved in the case of a woman named Ruby, whose drug addiction led to her losing custody of her son.
- **S12**：He also meets a young homeless advocate named Megan and they start a relationship.
- **S13**：As Drake & Sweeney comes after Brock with theft and malpractice allegations, the legal clinic files a lawsuit against the firm and its business partners.
- **S14**：The firm makes a deal where Brock has his license temporarily suspended, while they settle for a large amount of money and fire the partner whose actions led to the young family's deaths.
- **S15**：Drake & Sweeney's head partner, deeply troubled by the events, offers to make his entire staff available for pro bono work to assist the clinic in fighting for the rights of homeless people.
- **S16**：The book ends with Brock taking a short vacation with Megan and Ruby, and them reflecting on their lives.

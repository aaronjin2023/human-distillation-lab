### Dylan Sanders

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4949d4bd33e37732` · 独立版本：`profile-f16feb0823021af5`

- **性格倾向**：在遭到亲近对象背叛后，仍表现出重新迎战的韧性（行为推断；S11、S13、S15、S16）
- **核心动机**：在此次反击中，倾向以救出同伴并阻止追杀为目标（行为推断；S16、S17、S18）

### Eric Knox

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0a9437557ca87b96` · 独立版本：`profile-83361bb520227563`

- **性格倾向**：在复仇布局中，表现出善于伪装受害者并利用他人的倾向（行为推断；S11、S12）
- **核心动机**：因相信Charlie杀害父亲，企图定位并杀死他复仇（摘要直接叙述；S12）
- **价值取舍**：在追索父仇时，倾向将他人的生命置于复仇目标之后（行为推断；S12、S13、S17、S18）
- **决策方式**：倾向通过伪造绑架与技术拼接，借调查者之手取得攻击条件（行为推断；S9、S11、S12、S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以获救者身份接受帮助，实际利用营救者推进杀人计划。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Natalie Cook , Dylan Sanders , and Alex Munday  are the "Angels," three talented, tough, attractive women who work as private investigators for an unseen millionaire named Charlie .
- **S2**：Charlie uses a speaker in his offices to communicate with the Angels, and his assistant Bosley  works with them directly when needed.
- **S3**：The Angels are assigned to find Eric Knox , a software genius who created a revolutionary voice-recognition system and heads his own company, Knox Enterprises.
- **S4**：Knox is believed to have been kidnapped by Roger Corwin , who runs a communications-satellite company called Redstar.
- **S5**：The Angels infiltrate a party held by Corwin and spot a thin man  who was seen on the surveillance videos during Knox's kidnapping.
- **S6**：They chase and fight the Thin Man, but he runs away.
- **S7**：When they follow him, they discover Knox.
- **S8**：After the Angels reunite Knox with his business partner Vivian Wood , Charlie explains that they must determine whether the Thin Man has stolen Knox's voice-recognition software.
- **S9**：The Angels infiltrate Redstar headquarters, fool the security system, and plant a device in the central computer that will enable them to explore it remotely.
- **S10**：They retire for the night after giving Bosley the laptop computer that communicates with the Redstar computer.
- **S11**：Dylan takes up Knox's offer to spend the night with him, but he betrays her later that night, explaining that he faked the kidnapping with help from Vivian and the thin man.
- **S12**：He has kidnapped Bosley, and, with access to Redstar's central computer, he intends to use his voice software with the Redstar satellite network to find and kill Charlie, who he believes killed his father in the Vietnam War.
- **S13**：Knox shoots at Dylan, apparently killing her, but she escapes unharmed.
- **S14**：Natalie and Alex are also attacked, and Corwin is murdered by the Thin Man.
- **S15**：When the Angels regroup, Charlie's offices are blown up, but a radio receiver survives in the rubble, and the Angels deduce Bosley's location as he speaks to them using a radio transmitter implanted in his teeth.
- **S16**：With help from Dylan's current boyfriend Chad , the Angels approach the abandoned lighthouse where Knox is holding Bosley prisoner.
- **S17**：The Angels rescue Bosley and defeat Vivian, the thin man, and some henchmen before Knox blows up the lighthouse, but Knox uses his software and the Redstar satellite network to locate Charlie when he telephones Bosley.
- **S18**：When Knox escapes in a helicopter armed with a missile, Bosley helps the Angels board the helicopter, and Alex reprograms the missile, which blows up the helicopter and kills Knox while the Angels land safely in the ocean.
- **S19**：Seeing the opportunity to finally meet Charlie in person, the Angels enter the beach house that Knox targeted, but Charlie has already left.
- **S20**：He remotely congratulates them on a job well done, and treats them and Bosley to a vacation.
- **S21**：When Charlie speaks to the Angels by telephone on the beach, unseen by most of the group, Dylan suspects that she sees him nearby talking into a cell phone.

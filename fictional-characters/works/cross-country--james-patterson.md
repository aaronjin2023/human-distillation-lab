## 角色资料

### Alex Cross

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a4dadec7d123d1dc` · 独立版本：`profile-2e3cb115cbc751ad`

- **性格倾向**：在遭拘禁重伤后仍继续追凶，表现出不易退缩的倾向（行为推断；S8、S9、S11）
- **核心动机**：他前往尼日利亚的明确目的，是找到并阻止老虎继续杀人（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **情绪反应**：昔日恋人遇害使他一度恐惧失眠，甚至无法投入案件（摘要直接叙述；S3、S4）
- **价值取舍**：面对与阿丹的亲密接触，他倾向顾及自己与布里的关系（行为推断；S14）
- **决策方式**：在案件牵涉情报机构时，他仍倾向追查并拘捕关联人员（行为推断；S24、S25）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：私人悲痛一度令他停滞，此后仍冒险跨国追凶。

### Abidemi Sowande

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-f91d69471c500f3a` · 独立版本：`profile-f73a4523ad02ccc2`

- **性格倾向**：在针对调查者及其关联者时，表现出残酷施暴的倾向（行为推断；S2、S19、S20）
- **决策方式**：面对追捕，他倾向绑架对方家人并指定地点操控交锋（行为推断；S20、S21）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] An African warlord known as the Tiger, aided by his crew of angry young men, horrifically murders author Eleanor 'Ellie' Cox and her entire family.
- **S3**：Alex Cross and girlfriend Bree Stone go investigate, when Alex recalls Ellie had been his one time girlfriend, whom he had loved at one point.
- **S4**：He is instantly terrified by this so much he gets no sleep, does no work for the case, and stays up late at night, sitting in his bed.
- **S5**：He then figures out the Tiger's next murder, and arrives on time to stop the Tiger and his gang of kids, where most of the villains escape including the Tiger and most of his gang.
- **S6**：After gaining access from the CIA he plans on going to Nigeria, where he hopes to find and stop the Tiger, using the knowledge based on the Tiger's whereabouts.
- **S7**：After convincing a bitter Nanna and nervous Bree, he goes on a plane trip to Nigeria, where he is annoyed by their customs.
- **S8**：He is then kidnapped by 'cops' and put in jail, where his nose is broken, he is horribly injured, and starves as well as having to deal with no water.
- **S9**：After nearly three days or more, he is bailed out by American Ian Flaherty who gives Cross advice to flee while he still can.
- **S10**：He is then called by Bree who says there has been another murder by the Tiger.
- **S11**：Instead of listening to Flaherty, Cross instead goes to Sierra Leone, where he meets veteran and amputee, Moses, who feeds him and gives him water.
- **S12**：Later that night, Cross, when alone by himself, is ambushed by the Tiger's gang, however, he escapes with Moses' help and buys a truck which he later gives to Moses.
- **S13**：Father Bombata, a priest whom he met during the plane ride to Nigeria, informs him his cousin, Addane Tansi, may be able to help Alex, who meets Addane, a reporter who had, unbeknownst to Alex, befriended Ellie, who had gone to Nigeria some time before her death.
- **S14**：Addane introduces Alex to her family and shares a kiss with him, which he refuses to think of because of Bree; Flaherty later reveals that the Tiger's real name is Abidemi Sowande.
- **S15**：Alex and Addane go to a hobo camp where they are attacked by Janjaweed, ruthless men who rape, injure, and kill women and children.
- **S16**：After barely escaping due to the 'Peacekeepers' who only do it so they gain no bad publicity, especially by Addane.
- **S17**：After returning to Nigeria, Cross and Addane discover that Addane's family have been murdered.
- **S18**：When trying to get closer, Cross and Addane are taken to jail by cops.
- **S19**：Alex witnesses Addane get murdered by Tiger after being raped, and is released afterwards.
- **S20**：Heading home, he learns that Alex's family have been kidnapped by the Tiger, who then gives Alex coordinates to the hideout.
- **S21**：Alex, Bree, and John Sampson go and defeat the Tiger's gang, while Alex follows Sowande (Tiger).
- **S22**：Bree kills the Tiger by shooting him in the eye.
- **S23**：Ian Flaherty is revealed to be working with Sownande and is arrested.
- **S24**：Shortly afterward, his family are found; Alex instantly thinks the CIA might have worked with the Tiger.
- **S25**：Alex calls upon Merrill Snyder and Steven Millard, from the CIA, and arrests them, after discovering they and Flaherty were associated with Tiger.
- **S26**：The book ends as Alex gets an alarming phone call from Kyle Craig, who has escaped from prison and wants revenge on Alex Cross after the events in the previous book .

- **S4**：After Saunière's body is discovered in the pose of the Vitruvian Man by Leonardo da Vinci , the police summon Harvard professor Robert Langdon, who is in town on business.
- **S5**：Police captain Bezu Fache tells him that he was summoned to help the police decode the cryptic message Saunière left during the final minutes of his life.
- **S6**：The message includes a Fibonacci sequence out of order and an anagram: "O, draconian devil!
- **S7**：Oh, lame saint!" Langdon explains to Fache that the pentacle Saunière drew on his chest in his own blood represents an allusion to the goddess and not devil worship, as Fache believes.
- **S8**：Sophie Neveu, a police cryptographer , secretly explains to Langdon that she is Saunière's estranged granddaughter and that Fache thinks Langdon is the murderer because the last line in her grandfather's message, which was meant for Neveu, said "P.S.

- **S10**：However, "P.S." does not refer to " postscript ", but rather to Sophie — the nickname given to her by her grandfather was "Princess Sophie".
- **S11**：She understands that her grandfather intended Langdon to decipher the code, which leads to Leonardo da Vinci's Mona Lisa , which in turn leads to his painting Madonna of the Rocks .
- **S12**：They find a pendant that holds the address of the Paris branch of the Depository Bank of Zurich.
- **S13**：Replica cryptex: prize from Google Da Vinci Code Quest Contest Neveu and Langdon escape from the police and visit the bank.
- **S14**：In the safe deposit box, which is unlocked with the Fibonacci sequence, they find a box containing the keystone: a cryptex , a cylindrical, hand-held vault with five concentric, rotating dials labeled with letters.
- **S15**：When they are lined up correctly, they unlock the device, but if the cryptex is forced open, an enclosed vial of vinegar breaks and dissolves the message inside the cryptex, which was written on papyrus .
- **S16**：The box containing the cryptex contains clues to its password.
- **S17**：Langdon and Neveu take the keystone to the home of Langdon's friend, Sir Leigh Teabing, an expert on the Holy Grail, the legend of which is heavily connected to the Priory.
- **S18**：There, Teabing explains that the Grail is not a cup but connected to Mary Magdalene , and that she was Jesus Christ's wife and is the person to his right in The Last Supper .
- **S19**：The trio then flee the country on Teabing's private plane, on which they conclude that the proper combination of letters spells out Neveu's given name, Sofia.
- **S20**：Opening the cryptex, they discover a smaller cryptex inside it, along with another riddle that ultimately leads the group to the tomb of Isaac Newton in Westminster Abbey .
- **S21**：During the flight to Britain, Neveu reveals the source of her estrangement from her grandfather ten years earlier: arriving home unexpectedly from university, Neveu secretly witnessed a spring fertility rite conducted in the secret basement of her grandfather's country estate.
- **S22**：From her hiding place, she was shocked to see her grandfather having sex with a woman at the center of a ritual attended by men and women who were wearing masks and chanting praise to the goddess.
- **S23**：She fled the house and broke off all contact with Saunière.
- **S24**：Langdon explains that what she witnessed was an ancient ceremony known as hieros gamos or "sacred marriage".
- **S25**：By the time they arrive at Westminster Abbey , Teabing is revealed to be the Teacher for whom Silas is working.
- **S26**：Teabing wishes to use the Holy Grail, which he believes is a series of documents establishing that Jesus Christ married Mary Magdalene and fathered children, in order to ruin the Vatican .
- **S27**：He compels Langdon at gunpoint to solve the second cryptex's password, which Langdon realizes is "apple".

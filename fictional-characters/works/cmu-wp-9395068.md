## 角色资料

### Wesley Gibson

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-47848a0b9c3c13c2` · 独立版本：`profile-460aa87fc5ff9b33`

- **性格倾向**：长期受压后在获得资源时倾向激烈反击，行动易受愤怒推动（行为推断；S1、S8、S9、S32）
- **核心动机**：在相信父亲遇害的叙述后，将追杀目标视为替父复仇（摘要直接叙述；S2、S17）
- **价值取舍**：初入组织时对杀人有所抗拒，尚未完全接受刺客规则（行为推断；S14）
- **成长变化**：从接受组织赋予的身份与目标，转向揭穿操纵并自行报复（行为推断；S7、S17、S31、S33、S42）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：父亲希望他远离暴力，他得知真相后却选择武力报复。

### Fox

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-ec0ca087f6bfa745` · 独立版本：`profile-94900f4c57eed6ac`

- **性格倾向**：规则要求与个人生存冲突时倾向贯彻信念，甚至牺牲自己（行为推断；S16、S35、S36）
- **核心动机**：童年目睹父亲被杀，使她把预防类似悲剧视为使命（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）
- **价值取舍**：在组织准则要求死亡时仍把遵守准则置于自身生命之上（行为推断；S35、S36）
- **决策方式**：执行命令时倾向压过私人合作关系，隐瞒真相并准备灭口（行为推断；S17、S24、S25）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以预防悲剧为信念，却因服从命令参与对同伴的欺骗与追杀。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Chicago, Wesley Gibson is an American born in Moravia, who works at a dead-end desk job with an overbearing boss, takes anti-anxiety medication for panic attacks, and has a live-in girlfriend who cheats on him with his best friend Barry.
- **S2**：One night in the pharmacy, Wesley is told by a mysterious woman named Fox that his father was a recently murdered assassin, and the killer, Cross, is after him.
- **S3**：Cross and Fox engage in a shoot-out followed by a car chase in the streets of Chicago.
- **S4**：Wesley manages to escape and Fox has a shoot-out again with Cross, causing a car accident that knocks Wesley unconscious.
- **S5**：Fox brings Wesley to the headquarters of The Fraternity, a thousand-year-old secret society of assassins.
- **S6**：The group's current leader, Sloan, explains that Wesley's panic attacks are actually the untrained expression of a rare superhuman ability; when stressed, the drastically increased heart rate and adrenaline levels result in bursts of superhuman strength, speed, and reflexes – demonstrated when he successfully shoots the wings off of two flys.
- **S7**：The Fraternity can teach him to control this ability, so Wesley can follow in his father's footsteps as an assassin, beginning by inheriting his fortune.
- **S8**：Wesley is initially reluctant and returns to work, only to finally snap when discovering several million dollars in his bank account.
- **S9**：He excoriates his boss in front of the entire office, and on his way out, smashes Barry in the face with a computer keyboard.
- **S10**：Fox is waiting outside to take him back to the Fraternity headquarters — an unassuming textile mill.
- **S11**：Wesley is then subjected to brutal training; among other forms of combat, he learns to fire bullets to curve around objects .
- **S12**：Afterward, Wesley is shown the Loom of Fate, a loom that gives the names of the targets through binary code hidden in weaving errors of the fabric.
- **S13**：Those the Loom identifies will apparently cause tragedy in the future; but only Sloan sees and interprets the names fate wishes to see dead.
- **S14**：Wesley is initially reluctant about killing people.
- **S15**：Then Fox reveals that in her childhood, a hired assassin burned her father alive in front of her—and said hitman was supposed to be killed by the Fraternity before that, but the assassin failed to pull the trigger.
- **S16**：She now considers preventing such tragedy her mission and her loyalty to the Loom of Fate and The Fraternity.
- **S17**：After several routine missions and a chance meeting with Cross, in which Wesley is shot in the arm with a deliberately traceable bullet, Sloan grants Wesley's wish to avenge his father and sends him after Cross—but then secretly gives Fox a mission to kill Wesley, saying that his name had come up in the Loom as well.
- **S18**：Analyzing the bullet that hit Wesley, it is discovered that the manufacturer was Pekwarsky, a bullet-maker living in eastern Moravia, the birthplace of the Fraternity.
- **S19**：Wesley and Fox travel there and capture Pekwarsky, who arranges a meeting with Cross.
- **S20**：Wesley faces Cross alone on a moving train.
- **S21**：Fox steals a car and crashes it into the train, eventually causing a derailment.
- **S22**：After Cross saves Wesley’s life by preventing him from falling into a ravine, Wesley fatally shoots him.
- **S23**：Before dying, Cross reveals that he is Wesley's real father.
- **S24**：Fox confirms this, and explains that Wesley was recruited because he was the only person that Cross would not kill.
- **S25**：Fox then reveals the kill order on Wesley and raises her gun, but Wesley escapes by shooting out the glass underneath him and plunging into the river below with Cross's body.
- **S26**：Wesley is retrieved by Pekwarsky, who takes him to his father's apartment, located across the street from Wesley's old home.
- **S27**：Pekwarsky explains that Sloan started manufacturing targets for profit after discovering that he was targeted by the Loom of Fate, and did not tell the Fraternity members that they were now nothing more than paid killers.
- **S28**：The gang hang some of their friends in order to get to him.
- **S29**：Cross discovered the truth and went rogue, and started killing Fraternity members to keep them away from his son.
- **S30**：Pekwarsky departs, stating that Wesley's father wished him a life free of violence.
- **S31**：Wesley, however, decides to take out Sloan after discovering a secret room containing all of his father's weapons and maps.
- **S32**：An enraged Wesley assaults the Fraternity's textile mill-fortress and battles his way through it, killing nearly every Fraternity member in the process.
- **S33**：Upon entering Sloan's office, he reveals Sloan's deception to the master assassins present in the room.
- **S34**：Sloan reveals that all of their names had come up in the weaving, and that he had merely acted to protect them.
- **S35**：Were they to follow the code, every one of them should kill themselves on the spot.
- **S36**：Fox, who believes in the code more than anyone due to her own experience, turns on her fellow assassins, and curves a bullet that kills every Fraternity member in the room, including herself, but not before she throws her gun to Wesley to distract him from trying to save her.
- **S37**：During all this, the Loom of Fate is destroyed, and Sloan manages to escape.
- **S38**：Wesley, penniless once again, is left aimless.
- **S39**：A man is then seen at a computer, much like Wesley at the beginning of the film.
- **S40**：Sloan appears and points a gun at the back of the man's head.
- **S41**：At that moment, the man turns around and is revealed to be a decoy.
- **S42**：Sloan is then killed by Wesley using a long-distance bullet.
- **S43**：Similar to the comic book miniseries,{{cite journal}} the film ends with Wesley stating his accomplishments, then breaking the fourth wall by asking the audience "What the fuck have you done lately?".

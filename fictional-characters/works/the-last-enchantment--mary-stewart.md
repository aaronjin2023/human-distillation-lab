- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] This novel covers the time from when Arthur Pendragon first becomes king to the time Merlin, now getting on in years, begins to lose his powers and becomes a sort of master spy to assist King Arthur as he begins the task of uniting all of Britain.
- **S3**：Arthur is now King and hard at work establishing Camelot as the center of government and authority.
- **S4**：A few ambitious lords from other parts of Britain have designs on Arthur's throne, and Merlin is kept busy preventing them from doing so.
- **S5**：Having taken Morgause (unaware that she was his half-sister) to his bed as a very young man after his first battle and victory, Arthur is now the father of Mordred .
- **S6**：Merlin foresees that Mordred will be the cause of Arthur's death, but doesn't understand how it will happen.
- **S7**：He spends a great deal of time traveling in disguise and observing Morgause's scheming and intrigue.
- **S8**：Somewhere along the line, Merlin takes on a female apprentice, Niniane .
- **S9**：When she first appears she is disguised as a boy, and Merlin initially takes her for the reincarnation of a child he had seen some years before whom he would have chosen as apprentice, but who died unexpectedly.
- **S10**：Niniane is at that time not quite as gifted as Merlin himself, but he teaches her everything he knows, and they fall in love despite their age difference.
- **S11**：As he gives her the secrets of his psychic abilities and how to control them, he seems to lose them himself.
- **S12**：In a depleted, weakened condition, he takes ill and falls into a coma, and is believed to be dead.
- **S13**：Mourning him, Niniane has him buried within his beloved crystal cave.
- **S14**：She begins to have prescient nightmares that Merlin is actually alive; he awakes in the cave some time later.
- **S15**：He escapes after a few weeks, through a combination of chance luck and ingenious planning, and travels incognito to let Arthur know he is still alive.
- **S16**：Niniane takes Merlin's place as the court wizard-seer, while Merlin retires to the crystal cave and lives a quiet and happy life as a hermit, much like his old master in the first volume of the series.

## 角色资料

### Dastan

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a48d5a0b28d3a4b5` · 独立版本：`profile-08b3401d97febb54`

- **性格倾向**：遭诬陷与追杀时倾向主动求证，并敢以自身安危承担风险（行为推断；S8、S18、S24、S25）
- **核心动机**：逃亡后希望证明弑父指控不实，并揭露真正幕后主使（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9、S18、S22）
- **决策方式**：面对兄长不信任时倾向用可亲验的危险证明代替单纯辩解（行为推断；S24、S25）
- **价值取舍**：危机结束后倾向归还强大神器，并以善意重建受损关系（行为推断；S40、S41）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：为阻止她牺牲曾出手保护，最终却仍经历她主动放手的丧失。

### Tamina

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-d75f06d3cb8ac845` · 独立版本：`profile-7ae362f25c3c936f`

- **性格倾向**：保护圣物与城邦时倾向坚决行动，必要时愿付出个人代价（行为推断；S3、S6、S20、S32、S33）
- **核心动机**：希望保护匕首并阻止沙漏开启，避免毁灭世界的灾难（摘要直接叙述；S12、S19、S20、S32）
- **价值取舍**：面临无法两全的险境时倾向把阻止灾难置于爱情和生命之前（行为推断；S31、S32、S33）
- **关系模式**：与王子从敌对转向相互保护，并在绝境中向他承认爱情（摘要直接叙述；S6、S20、S27、S28、S32）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：逐渐爱上并保护同行者，却为让他阻止灾难而主动放手。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：An attack on the sacred city of Alamut is planned by Dastan , an orphan in the Persian Empire adopted by King Sharaman ; his royal-blooded adoptive brothers, Tus  and Garsiv ; and his uncle, Nizam , after Alamut is believed to be selling weapons to Persia's enemies.
- **S2**：Dastan leads a surprise attack to open the city's gates to allow the army in; during the ensuing battle, Dastan comes into possession of a unique dagger.
- **S3**：Alamut's princess, Tamina , is arrested and agrees to marry Tus in order to achieve peace after noticing Dastan's dagger.
- **S4**：Dastan unknowingly presents a poisoned robe, given to him by Tus, to his father, who dies upon donning it.
- **S5**：After being blamed for the murder, Dastan flees Alamut with Tamina.
- **S6**：When Tamina attempts to kill the prince, Dastan discovers the dagger can reverse time and only the one who activates it is aware of what happened.
- **S7**：After an encounter with shady ostrich racing-organizer Sheik and his men, Dastan and Tamina head to Avrat for Sharaman's funeral.
- **S8**：Dastan attempts to convince Nizam that he did not kill his father but Garsiv and the city guards appear and attack Dastan and he is forced to escape.
- **S9**：Dastan catches up with Tamina and explains that Nizam was behind the murder because his hands were burned.
- **S10**：Once Tamina finally explains everything about the Dagger, Dastan realizes why Nizam wants the Dagger of Time: to use it with the massive Sandglass to go back in time and undo saving Sharaman from a lion so he could become king.
- **S11**：He fabricated the false allegations that Alamut had been dealing weapons to Persia's enemies so that he would have the opportunity to search the city for the Sandglass.
- **S12**：However, Tamina warns that opening the Sandglass would release the sands, triggering an apocalyptic sandstorm that would destroy the entire world.
- **S13**：Meanwhile, Nizam hires the lethal Hassansins to slay Dastan.
- **S14**：Dastan and Tamina are again captured by Sheik Amar , seeking to rebuild his ruined business by turning them in for bounty.
- **S15**：But that night, the Hassansin leader, Zolm  attacks the group with vipers, which are killed by Dastan with the dagger.
- **S16**：Amar decides to go along with Dastan and Tamina to a secret sanctuary near India.
- **S17**：They run into Garsiv's men.
- **S18**：Dastan manages to persuade his brother that he is innocent, only for Garsiv to be fatally wounded by a Hassansin.
- **S19**：The Hassansins attack, while Dastan and Tamina sneak away to the secret cave where they can hide the Dagger.
- **S20**：After Dastan prevents Tamina from giving up her life to protect the Dagger, they are found and Zolm manages to snatch the Dagger of Time from Tamina.
- **S21**：Garsiv saves Dastan from a Hassansin just before he dies.
- **S22**：The group returns to Alamut to reveal the truth about Nizam and the Dagger to Tus.
- **S23**：Seso  manages to take back the Dagger after a battle with Setam, the Hassansin who killed Garsiv, that ends up killing both men.
- **S24**：Dastan confronts Tus and explains the dagger's mechanics to him and then stabs the dagger into his heart, killing himself.
- **S25**：Tus brings Dastan back to life by rewinding time and realizes his brother has been innocent all along.
- **S26**：But Nizam arrives, kills Tus, and takes the Dagger, leaving a Hassansin, Ghazab, to kill Dastan.
- **S27**：Tamina arrives to help Dastan defeat Ghazab as Nizam goes to the Sandglass caves beneath Alamut.
- **S28**：Dastan and Tamina go behind, and on the way Tamina kills Zolm with the Hassansin's own snake, while preventing him from killing Dastan.
- **S29**：The pair share a kiss.
- **S30**：They reach Nizam before he can pierce the Sandglass with the Dagger, but he knocks Tamina and Dastan over the edge.
- **S31**：Dastan grabs hold of Tamina.
- **S32**：Admitting that she loves him, Tamina asks Dastan to stop Nizam, saying it's been his destiny all along.
- **S33**：She lets go of his hand, leaving Dastan devastated.
- **S34**：Dastan pulls himself up just as Nizam stabs the Sandglass with the Dagger.
- **S35**：Dastan grabs hold and activates the dagger.
- **S36**：This causes Dastan to go back to the immediate aftermath of the invasion of Alamut.
- **S37**：Dastan reveals Nizam's treachery.
- **S38**：Nizam, realizing the allegation will be confirmed by an investigation ordered by Tus, attacks Dastan, but is eventually stabbed in the chest by Tus's blade and dies.
- **S39**：After apologizing for the ransacking of her city, Tus suggests that perhaps Tamina should become Dastan's wife as a sign of good will since he is the "conqueror and savior of her city".
- **S40**：Prince Dastan returns the Dagger of Time to Tamina and the two take a walk together.
- **S41**：During their conversation, Dastan hints at his knowledge of the dagger's power and tells Tamina that he looks forward to a future with her.

- **S16**：[ edit ] A young anthropomorphic mouse named Matthias is a novice monk at Redwall Abbey, where he was adopted as a young orphan, though he dreams of a life of adventure, inspired by the legends of Martin the Warrior, the founder of Redwall.
- **S17**：One summer, Redwall Abbey is surrounded by the army of Cluny the Scourge, an infamously evil one-eyed rat .
- **S18**：Matthias is guided by visions of Martin the Warrior.
- **S19**：While the abbey inhabitants prepare the defense of their home against Cluny's impending attack, Matthias seeks Martin's famous sword, supposedly hidden somewhere within the abbey.
- **S20**：He is helped particularly by Methuselah, an ancient and grizzled mouse who serves as Redwall's historian.
- **S21**：Cluny, meanwhile, attempts to gain entrance to the abbey in numerous ways- a battering ram, tunnelling, a siege tower and various others.
- **S22**：When Cluny is almost fatally injured following a failed attempt to scale the Abbey walls, a healer fox named Sela is summoned to help him.
- **S23**：Sela is a spy, who has sold secrets to both sides in various wars.
- **S24**：Cluny, however, suspects Sela is up to something, and when his suspicions are proven, has her murdered by two of his captains.
- **S25**：Sela's son, Chickenhound, seeks refuge at Redwall but ends up accidentally killing Methuselah after being caught stealing.
- **S26**：Driven from the abbey, Chickenhound is maimed in the wilderness by the venomous adder Asmodeus Poisonteeth, a local terror in Mossflower Wood, the forest that surrounds the abbey.
- **S27**：Clues to the location of Martin's sword and shield have been built into the abbey, allowing Matthias to recover the shield, though he discovers the sword has been stolen by a wild house sparrow tribe, the Sparras, that dwells on Redwall's roof.
- **S28**：He learns from the violent sparrows that the sword was stolen from them in turn by Asmodeus.
- **S29**：The king of the sparrows attacks Matthias but dies when the two plummet together off the abbey roof.
- **S30**：Matthias recovers and ventures to Asmodeus's lair with his new allies Log-a-Log, a shrew , and Warbeak Sparra, the new and just queen of the aforementioned Sparras.
- **S31**：Matthias and Log-A-Log succeed in retrieving the sword from Asmodeus's cave.
- **S32**：They are originally accompanied by a female shrew named Guosim, who is the final victim of Asmodeus.
- **S33**：Matthias kills Asmodeus with the sword.
- **S34**：Alerted to the fall of the abbey by the Sparra tribe, Matthias rushes back to Redwall to save his friends.
- **S35**：The Redwall inhabitants use various weapons to repel Cluny's attacks, such as a barrel of hornet's nests, vegetable oil (poured over a battering ram to make it impossible to hold onto), and boiling water (which results in the death of one of Cluny's best soldiers).
- **S36**：Constance the Badger, one of the Abbey's main defenders, single-handedly fights and kills Cluny's second-in-command, and later mistakenly murders another captain after seeing him dressed in Cluny's armour.
- **S37**：The abbey finally falls when Cluny captures a family of dormice, and forces their leader to infiltrate Redwall and open the gate for him, in exchange for their lives.
- **S38**：Matthias, his allies now including the Mossflower shrews and the whole Sparra tribe, along with the newly captive Redwall population, battles against Cluny's minions.
- **S39**：Cluny strikes his poison-barb tail at the father abbot, Mortimer, but Matthias quickly avenges the abbot's injury by dropping the abbey's giant bell on top of Cluny, crushing him to death and cracking the bell in the process.
- **S40**：Abbot Mortimer is fatally wounded by Cluny's poison-barb tail.
- **S41**：On his deathbed, he decrees that Matthias should not become a monk, but instead proclaims him Warrior of Redwall.
- **S42**：The book ends with an epilogue- an entry in the journal of John Churchmouse, a minor character who became chronicler of Redwall after Methuselah's death.
- **S43**：It is a year since the death of Abbot Mortimer, and his successor, Mordalfus (formerly Brother Alf), has organised a great feast to celebrate the completion of his first year as Abbot.
- **S44**：Matthias has married the fieldmouse Cornflower and she has given birth to their son, Mattimeo, an abbreviated version of the name Matthias Methuselah Mortimer.
- **S45**：The cracked Joseph Bell has been reshaped into two new bells called Matthias and Methuselah.

## 角色资料

### Hou Jie

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-1a82fd4cc437b02f` · 独立版本：`profile-3c89b067c3cc2000`

- **性格倾向**：在早期权力关系中，倾向因猜疑和受辱感诉诸暴力（行为推断；S5、S7、S8）
- **核心动机**：失去女儿后因昔日恶行内疚，选择出家寻求赎罪（摘要直接叙述；S15、S19）
- **价值取舍**：后期将难民与寺院的安危置于个人逃生机会之前（行为推断；S28、S39）
- **决策方式**：救援劳工时愿以自身为诱饵，为同伴争取行动空间（行为推断；S24、S25）
- **成长变化**：由轻蔑僧众的军阀，转变为愿舍命救仇敌的修行者（摘要直接叙述；S4、S20、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：承担教化曹蛮的责任，却也承认其恶行源自自己的旧日示范。

### Cao Man

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-a1107d2e0ea4b8d5` · 独立版本：`profile-3a6897d3ac360268`

- **性格倾向**：在争夺控制权时，倾向以残酷暴力排除威胁与阻碍（行为推断；S9、S22、S26）
- **核心动机**：因觉得自己被侯杰利用，决定背叛原有的上下级关系（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **价值取舍**：在掩盖盗掘行动时，将保密利益置于劳工生命之上（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：借修建铁路掩护盗掘，再以灭口阻断消息外泄（摘要直接叙述；S21、S22）
- **情绪反应**：被侯杰舍命救下后，对自身所作所为产生内疚（摘要直接叙述；S32）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：背叛并追杀侯杰，最终却成为侯杰牺牲自己的救助对象。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film is set in Dengfeng, Henan, during the warlord era of early Republican China.
- **S2**：The warlord Hou Jie  defeats a rival, Huo Long, and seizes control of Dengfeng.
- **S3**：Huo Long flees to Shaolin Temple to hide but Hou Jie appears and shoots him after getting his Huo's treasure map.
- **S4**：Hou Jie ridicules the Shaolin monks before leaving.
- **S5**：Feeling that his sworn brother, Song Hu, is taking advantage of him, Hou sets a trap for Song in a restaurant, under the guise of agreeing to his daughter's engagement to Song's son.
- **S6**：Meanwhile, Hou's deputy, Cao Man , feeling that he was being used by Hou, decides to betray him.
- **S7**：During the dinner, Song shows his intention to retire and cedes everything to Hou but was informed that Hou intends to kill him.
- **S8**：Out of rage and embarrassment, Hou fatally wounds Song.
- **S9**：Both families were then attacked by Cao's assassins.
- **S10**：Despite being shot by Hou, Song saves Hou to allow him to escape and Song dies subsequently.
- **S11**：While fleeing, Hou's wife and daughter separated.
- **S12**：Hou's wife is rescued by some passing-by Shaolin monks, who were stealing rice from the military granary to help the refugees living near the temple.
- **S13**：Hou manages to escape with his daughter, but his daughter was severely injured when she was knocked down by the assassins's horse carriage.
- **S14**：After a chase by the assassins on horse carriages, Hou and his daughter fall off a cliff.
- **S15**：In desperation, Hou Jie brings his daughter to Shaolin, begging the monks to save her life, but it is too late and she dies of her injuries.
- **S16**：Hou Jie's wife blames him for the death of their daughter and leaves him.
- **S17**：Hou Jie attacks the monks in anger but is quickly subdued.
- **S18**：Hou becomes disillusioned and wanders near Shaolin, until he meets the cook monk Wudao , who provides him with food and shelter.
- **S19**：Hou feels guilty for his past misdeeds and decides to become a monk and atone for his sins.
- **S20**：During his stay in Shaolin, Hou Jie gradually understands Shaolin's principles through study and practising martial arts, mends his ways and finds peace and enlightenment from his heart.
- **S21**：From the refugees, Hou discovers Cao had recruited male refugees to build a railway, which he objected when he was still a warlord, and they have yet to return after several days.
- **S22**：Hou discovers that Cao was unearthing Chinese relics on pretext of building a railway and the refugees were massacred afterwards to silence them.
- **S23**：After Cao is informed that Hou is still alive, he leads his soldiers to Shaolin temple to capture Hou.
- **S24**：Hou volunteers to go with Cao to distract him while the monks break into Cao's house to save the imprisoned labourers.
- **S25**：Hou was reunited with his wife and escaped with her when his plan to rescue the labourers succeeded.
- **S26**：Hou's senior, Jingneng, is brutally killed by Cao Man while covering for his juniors to escape.
- **S27**：Upon returning to Shaolin Temple, the monks decide that they need to evacuate the temple to avoid further trouble.
- **S28**：Wudao leads the refugees away while Hou and the other monks remain behind to defend the temple and buy time.
- **S29**：Cao arrives with his troops and attack Shaolin.
- **S30**：At the same time, the foreigners feel that they have been cheated and decide to silence Cao and Shaolin.
- **S31**：They bombard Shaolin with artillery, resulting in heavy casualties for both the Shaolin monks and Cao's forces.
- **S32**：Hou defeats Cao in a fight and eventually sacrifices himself to save Cao from being crushed by a falling beam and falls into the Buddha statue's palm and dies peacefully, leaving Cao feeling guilty.
- **S33**：The surviving monks succeed in overcoming the foreigners and stopping the bombardment.
- **S34**：Meanwhile, the refugees, fleeing on a mountainside, begin crying as they gaze upon the Shaolin temple in ruins.
- **S35**：Wudao tells them that the Shaolin spirit will continue to live in them even though the temple has been destroyed.
- **S36**：Before the evacuation of Shaolin Temple, Hou met his wife for the last time.
- **S37**：Repenting for his past mistakes, Hou passes the urn containing his daughter's cremated ashes to his wife.
- **S38**：Hou's wife forgives Hou for his past, and accepts the fact that she can no longer be with him even though she prefer the present Hou compared to the past.
- **S39**：Hou refused to leave Shaolin and stay behind for Shaolin's and refugees' defence, in admission that Cao's present evil doings stems from his own past misdeeds and he is responsible in guiding Cao back to the correct path.

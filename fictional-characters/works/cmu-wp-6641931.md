## 角色资料

### Dr. Robert Neville

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-ab183015eb6fd314` · 独立版本：`profile-847ffe7ea4267afa`

- **性格倾向**：在长期孤立中仍维持规律工作，显出执着而自我约束的倾向。（行为推断；S7、S8、S9）
- **核心动机**：他持续实验并播送广播，目标是寻找疗法并联系其他幸存者。（摘要直接叙述；S7、S8）
- **决策方式**：他通过感染者实验检验疗法，并在见效后留取血样交给安娜。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S28、S30）
- **情绪反应**：失去萨曼莎后，他被悲痛与愤怒压倒，驾车攻击感染者。（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17）
- **价值取舍**：实验室遭围攻时，他倾向优先保全血样与同伴，承担致命风险。（行为推断；S30、S31）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Anna

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-0e8d1522b12a59c8` · 独立版本：`profile-35890574303c1ff5`

- **性格倾向**：在寻找营地时，她对信仰指引抱有确信，并愿意据此行动。（行为推断；S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：她与伊森循广播找到内维尔，并计划前往佛蒙特州幸存者营地。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S20）
- **价值取舍**：她将疗法交给幸存者营地，显示此行也重视群体延续的希望。（行为推断；S32、S33）
- **关系模式**：她与伊森结伴求生、救回内维尔，并将其成果带给幸存者。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S19、S32）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 材料限制

- 剧情摘要着重内维尔的经历，安娜的个人经历和内心描写较少。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In September 2012, military virologist Lieutenant Colonel Robert Neville  is the last healthy and immune human in New York City.
- **S2**：A genetically-engineered variant of the measles virus created by Dr.
- **S3**：Alice Krippin , meant as a cure for cancer, had mutated into a lethal strain.
- **S4**：It spread throughout the world, killing 90% of humanity.
- **S5**：Most survivors became predatory, vampiric{{cite web}} beingsThe DVD/Blu-Ray subtitles refer to the infected as hemocytes&nbsp;– actually a cell type found in an insect's immune system. called "Darkseekers" that emerge after dusk to prey on those immune to the virus.
- **S6**：In December 2009, Neville had lost his wife Zoe  and daughter Marley  in a helicopter accident during a chaotic quarantine of Manhattan.
- **S7**：Neville's daily routine includes experimenting on infected rats to find a cure for the virus and trips through a decaying Manhattan to collect supplies or hunt for deer.
- **S8**：He keeps vigil each day for a response to his recorded AM radio broadcasts, which instruct any survivors to meet him at midday at the South Street Seaport.
- **S9**：Neville's isolation is broken only by the companionship of his pet German Shepherd Samantha and interaction with mannequins he has set up as patrons of a video store.
- **S10**：To test a treatment, Neville sets a snare trap and captures an infected woman.
- **S11**：An enraged mutated alpha male attempts to rescue her, but is driven back by the sunlight.
- **S12**：In the laboratory in his heavily fortified Washington Square Park home, Neville tries the new serum on the infected woman, seemingly without success.
- **S13**：The next day, after finding one of his mannequins moved out in the street in front of Grand Central Terminal, Neville is caught in a snare trap and passes out.
- **S14**：He regains consciousness at dusk and frees himself, but a pack of infected dogs attack Neville and Sam.
- **S15**：Although Neville and Sam kill the dogs, an infected dog bites Sam during the fight.
- **S16**：Neville brings Sam home and attempts to save the dog by injecting a strain of his serum, but it is too late, as Sam starts to mutate, which then forces Neville to kill her.
- **S17**：Overwhelmed by grief and rage of the loss of his dog, Neville attacks a group of the infected on the seaport the following night with his SUV.
- **S18**：He kills many, but they overwhelm and nearly kill him before he is rescued by a pair of immune humans, a woman named Anna  and a boy named Ethan , who followed his radio broadcasts.
- **S19**：Anna and Ethan take him back to his home.
- **S20**：Anna explains that they are making their way to a survivors' camp in Bethel, Vermont.
- **S21**：Neville does not believe that such a camp exists and expresses doubt when Anna says that God told her about it.
- **S22**：The following night, the alpha male leads an infected mob in an attack on Neville's house.
- **S23**：Anna, who was unaware of Neville's precautions in covering his scent outside the house, inadvertently allowed the infected to follow their trail.
- **S24**：As the infected charge the house, Neville stops the first wave with claymore mines, but finds himself defenseless against the second wave.
- **S25**：As Neville tries to find Anna and Ethan, an infected enters and attacks him.
- **S26**：The infected retreats upstairs and begins tearing a hole in the roof so others can get in.
- **S27**：After Neville saves Anna and Ethan, they retreat into the laboratory.
- **S28**：They seal themselves in a reinforced plexiglass room with the infected woman, and discover that Neville's treatment is working; the subject looks much more human.
- **S29**：The infected break in and the alpha male begins to throw himself against the plexiglass, cracking it.
- **S30**：Neville draws a vial of the infected woman's blood and gives it to Anna before shutting them inside a coal chute in the back of the lab.
- **S31**：He detonates an M67 grenade to destroy the attackers at the cost of his own life.
- **S32**：Anna and Ethan arrive at the survivors' colony, where Anna hands over the antidote.
- **S33**：Anna later states that the survivors are Neville's legacy, as his fight for a cure became legend.

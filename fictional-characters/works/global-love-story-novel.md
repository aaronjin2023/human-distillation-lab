- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Oliver Barrett IV, a jock and heir apparent to his father's business empire, is at Harvard preparing to take over the family business, while Jennifer (Jenny), the daughter of a Rhode Island baker, is a music major at Radcliffe College .
- **S3**：Although divided by class, Oliver and Jenny are immediately attracted to each other and begin dating.
- **S4**：Upon graduation from college, the two marry against the wishes of Oliver's father, who promptly severs all ties with his son.
- **S5**：Without financial support from Oliver's family, the couple struggles to pay Oliver's tuition at Harvard Law School .
- **S6**：Jenny begins working as a private school teacher.
- **S7**：After graduating third in his class, Oliver accepts a position at a respectable New York law firm.
- **S8**：The couple move to New York City and decide to have a child.
- **S9**：The couple has trouble conceiving and consult a medical specialist, who informs Oliver that Jenny has terminal leukemia .
- **S10**：Oliver attempts to live a normal life without telling Jenny of her condition.
- **S11**：Jenny suspects something is wrong, confronts the doctor, and discovers the truth.
- **S12**：Oliver seeks financial relief from his father, but lies about why he needs it.
- **S13**：From her hospital bed, Jenny speaks with her father about funeral arrangements, and then asks for Oliver.
- **S14**：She tells him to avoid blaming himself, and asks him to hold her tightly before she dies.
- **S15**：When Oliver's father realizes that Jenny is ill and that his son borrowed the money for her, he immediately sets out for New York to reconcile with the couple.
- **S16**：By the time he reaches the hospital, Jenny has died.

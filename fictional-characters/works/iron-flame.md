- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Violet Sorrengail discovers that her brother Brennan is alive and a lieutenant colonel in a secret rebellion against a hidden threat in Navarre. [ a ] The revolutionary group eventually allows her and other cadets who survived an attack to return to their college, provided they keep the attack a secret.
- **S3**：They lie about being attacked by gryphons.
- **S4**：Colonel Aetos, who led them into the trap during the War Games, is demoted by Lilith Sorrengail and reassigned to another outpost.
- **S5**：Violet ends her friendship with Dain, blaming him for Liam's death since he used his retrocognition signet on her without her knowledge or consent.
- **S6**：Before leaving, Aetos makes a veiled death threat to Violet, noting that secrets die with the people who keep them.
- **S7**：Xaden, now a lieutenant, is stationed at the Samara outpost.
- **S8**：He and Violet can see each other weekly because of their dragons' bond.
- **S9**：Xaden refuses to share the truth with Violet and is unwilling to include her in a weapons smuggling operation with other rebels.
- **S10**：On conscription day, Violet and Dain see Prince Cam, who is using the name Aaric Graycastle.
- **S11**：Violet also meets Sloane, who blames her for her brother Liam's death.
- **S12**：Violet survives an assassination attempt by Aetos, while many survivors start to die mysteriously.
- **S13**：Andarna, Violet's dragon, enters a necessary phase of growth called "dreamless sleep." Violet learns about an unactivated ward stone in Aretia and secretly investigates the First Six, who created the wards in Navarre.
- **S14**：She gets help from Jesinia, a scribe who illegally smuggles books for her.
- **S15**：Xaden confronts Violet about her secret research, while she struggles to keep the truth from Rhiannon and her sister, Mira.
- **S16**：Violet discovers Aaric is in Basgiath looking for a solution to the venin threat.
- **S17**：Meanwhile, vice commandant Major Burton Varrish grows suspicious of her and regularly searches her bags.
- **S18**：During an interrogation exercise on Violet's squad, he orders Dain to read Violet's memories, but he refuses.
- **S19**：Violet later sees Jack Barlowe, whom she thought she killed before, back in Basgiath, seemingly healed.
- **S20**：On a trip to Samara, Xaden and Violet deepen their physical relationship.
- **S21**：Xaden trusts Violet enough to bring her along to meet a group of gryphon fliers, where he smuggles daggers to help them fight venin.
- **S22**：The group includes Xaden's ex-girlfriend, Cat.
- **S23**：He gives Violet a special dagger for defense, which she hides.
- **S24**：However, upon returning to Basgiath, Violet is captured by Varrish.
- **S25**：Rhiannon uses her powers to hide the dagger, but Violet has to reveal the truth about venin to her friends, Ridoc and Sawyer, who agree to help her investigate the First Six.
- **S26**：Violet believes that journals written by Lyra and Warrick of the First Six are hidden in a vault that only member of the royal bloodline can access and contain instructions on how to raise wards.
- **S27**：She convinces Aaric to join their mission to steal the journals.
- **S28**：They succeed and Violet gives Xaden Warrick's journal to take to Brennan.
- **S29**：However, Violet is betrayed by Nolon and tricked into drinking a serum that suppresses her signet and connection to Tairn before being captured by Varrish.
- **S30**：Varrish tortures Violet for days for information before once again ordering Dain to read her memory, claiming she is conspiring to bring down the wards.
- **S31**：Dain does so, but Violet makes him view the memories of the Venin attack in Athebyne.
- **S32**：Realizing he has been lied to, Dain turns on Varrish and stabs him.
- **S33**：Xaden arrives and they kill Varrish.
- **S34**：To expose the truth about the Venin, Xaden had Wyvern carcasses dropped at several outposts, prompting Navarre leadership to frantically try and cover it up.
- **S35**：They are intercepted by Lilith, who helps Violet and reveals she forced Violet to join the riders quadrant to keep her away from Markham, knowing he would groom her to be his successor, but have her killed when she tried to expose the truth about the venin.
- **S36**：With the truth exposed, many of Basgiath's riders choose to leave for Aretia to fight against the venin.
- **S37**：Violet translates part of Warrick's journal, but cannot activate the wardstone.
- **S38**：She discovers that Viscount Tecarus, an old ally of Fen Riorson, possesses a luminary that could help the rebels make weapons.
- **S39**：Violet travels to Krovla to seek an alliance with him.
- **S40**：She finds out that Cat is Tecarus's niece and was once engaged to Xaden, who ended it a year before.
- **S41**：Tecarus agrees to give them the luminary if the rebels take 100 gryphon fliers back to Aretia for training.
- **S42**：Andarna wakes up after a long sleep, but Violet feels guilt over Andarna's sacrifice during the fight in Resson, knowing it harmed her ability to bear a rider.
- **S43**：Violet asks Dain for help translating the journals, and they become closer again after he assures her he did not know about the venin or intend to harm her.
- **S44**：During a training exercise, Violet, Cat, and Sloane face an attack from Varrish's dragon, which seeks revenge for Varrish's death.
- **S45**：Andarna saves them by killing the dragon, and Sloane manifests a siphon signet.
- **S46**：Violet discovers that Xaden has a second signet as a result of Sgaeyl being originally bonded to his grandfather.
- **S47**：He is an inntinnsic, allowing him to sense people's intentions, but keeps it a secret due to it being an illegal signet.
- **S48**：Though upset about his secret, they reconcile when he promises to be honest.
- **S49**：She also realizes she misinterpreted a phrase in the journals and realizes the wardstone will activate when imbued with magic and exposed to fire from a dragon of each of the six dens.
- **S50**：She manages to raise the Aretia wards, though only temporarily as they are still missing a key element to truly activate them.
- **S51**：Violet, Mira and Xaden are summoned to a meeting by Lilith and Melgren, who reveals he has foreseen a venin attack in Samara that they will lose if they do not help them.
- **S52**：Brennan reveals himself to Lilith and rejects their plea, berating them for their refusal to help anyone outside the safety of Navarre's wards.
- **S53**：Before leaving, Lilith reveals Warrick had lied in his journal about how to truly activate the wards as he only wanted Navarre to have them and gives Violet Lyra's journal which contains the true instructions.
- **S54**：Realizing the attack on Samara is a distraction and the venin plan to attack Basgiath, Violet and the others return to the college to warn Lilith.
- **S55**：Jack reveals he is a venin, and sacrifices his dragon to destroy Basgiath's wardstone, bringing down the wards and allowing the venin to attack the college.
- **S56**：The venin siege the college and riders and fliers unite to fight them off.
- **S57**：Jesinia translates Lyra's journal which reveals that seven dragon dens are needed to activate the wardstone.
- **S58**：Violet discovers Andarna is from an unknown seventh den needed to activate the wards.
- **S59**：Brennan is able to mend the wardstone and Violet almost exhausts herself trying to imbue it with enough magic to reactivate it, but Lilith stops and has Sloane siphon all the magic from herself and her dragon to fully imbue the wardstone, sacrificing Lilith in the process.
- **S60**：The wardstone is reactivated when Andarna and a dragon from the six other dens breathe fire on it, killing the wyvern and defeating the venin.
- **S61**：After the battle, Xaden reveals he resorted to drawing magic directly from the ground to defeat the venin sage and protect Violet, becoming a venin himself in the process.

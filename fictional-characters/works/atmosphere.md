- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] The novel begins on December 29, 1984, when a malfunction on the fictional NASA space shuttle Navigator leaves three of the five astronauts aboard dead and one more unconscious with only hours to live due to contracting the bends .
- **S3**：Only Vanessa Ford is conscious and able to communicate with mission control , where fellow astronaut and spacecraft communicator (CAPCOM) Joan Goodwin attempts to help her land safely back on earth.
- **S4**：The subsequent chapters primarily focus on the life of Joan in the years prior to the disaster, told from a third-person limited point of view.
- **S5**：Joan, a professor of astronomy at Rice University , applies to and is accepted to NASA's Space Shuttle program beginning in 1980.
- **S6**：She bonds with most of her class, but particularly takes a liking to Vanessa.
- **S7**：After over a year of flirtation (and reticence to act on Joan's end), the two profess their love for each other and start a relationship but are forced to keep it secret due to concerns that rampant homophobia would end their careers at NASA.
- **S8**：Joan additionally has to balance her work with her relationship with her sister, Barbara, and her niece, Frances.
- **S9**：Barbara is an unreliable mother and consistently pressures Joan into help with her parental duties, though Joan loves Frances like a daughter of her own and cherishes spending time with her.
- **S10**：The relationship is further strained when Barbara marries Daniel, who has a difficult time connecting with Frances.
- **S11**：Barbara eventually sends Frances to a boarding school in Dallas, angering Joan.
- **S12**：Joan and Vanessa are both selected to go on space shuttle launches about a month apart in late 1984.
- **S13**：Joan struggles with space adaptation syndrome on her trip and resolves that she wants to stay on Earth as part of mission control going forward.
- **S14**：After she returns to Houston, Barbara admits that she and Daniel are planning to move to Europe, mostly cutting the now-10-year-old Frances out of her life; an enraged Joan effectively adopts Frances as her own child.
- **S15**：Meanwhile, Joan's supervisor at NASA hints to her that he is aware of her and Vanessa's relationship, suggesting that continuing it will likely end both of their careers.
- **S16**：The day before Vanessa leaves for her launch, Joan attempts to break up with her out of concern for Vanessa's career and her need to be a parent to Frances, but Vanessa refuses to accept it and they agree to deal with whatever happens next.
- **S17**：Back on the damaged space shuttle, Vanessa is unable to latch one of the doors completely shut.
- **S18**：Against mission control's orders and at increased risk to her own life, she demands to begin deorbiting without the door latched in an effort to get the other living astronaut on board back to Earth in time to save her life.
- **S19**：Before she attempts to re-enter the Earth's atmosphere, Vanessa and Joan share a heartfelt conversation over the communication system, all but outing their relationship to everyone listening.
- **S20**：When mission control cannot initially get a hold of Vanessa following her descent, Joan believes she is dead and begins to reflect on their time together, only to hear Vanessa's voice confirming that she has survived re-entry.

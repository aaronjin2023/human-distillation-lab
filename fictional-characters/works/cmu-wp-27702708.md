## 角色资料

### Charlie Kenton

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-2df22e23e4d4adb2` · 独立版本：`profile-33a58147c5ee9d3b`

- **性格倾向**：在早期比赛和交易中，他倾向高估胜算并追逐眼前回报（行为推断；S4、S6、S9）
- **核心动机**：他起初主要为金钱参赛，后来也希望承担父亲角色并维系亲情（行为推断；S6、S16、S23、S24、S25、S32）
- **决策方式**：运用拳击经验时，他能观察对手细小破绽并据此调整战术（摘要直接叙述；S20、S28）
- **成长变化**：他从以抚养权换钱，转向拒收余款并优先考虑儿子的安全（摘要直接叙述；S6、S23）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：希望与儿子亲近，却因自己带来的危险一度选择将他送走

### Max

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-cc786b7ea43b4a5a` · 独立版本：`profile-5b591bd5e6512db3`

- **性格倾向**：面对被视为过时的机器人，他倾向坚持其价值并亲自投入改造（行为推断；S12、S13、S18）
- **核心动机**：他希望让自己发掘的Atom获得比赛机会，并证明它能挑战强者（行为推断；S12、S13、S16、S21）
- **决策方式**：他会将持续争取支持与实际技术改装结合，推动机器人上场（行为推断；S13、S16、S18）
- **关系模式**：他通过共同修整和比赛与父亲建立合作，最终共享成功与亲情（行为推断；S18、S20、S28、S32）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 2020, human boxers have been replaced by robot boxers.
- **S2**：Charlie Kenton  is a former boxer who owns such a robot, Ambush, competing in unsanctioned matches and in exhibitions with it.
- **S3**：At a rural fair, Ambush is destroyed by Black Thunder, a bull belonging to promoter Ricky .
- **S4**：Having made a bet that Ambush would win, Charlie now owes Ricky $20,000, which he doesn't pay before leaving.
- **S5**：Charlie is informed his ex-wife has died, and that he must attend a hearing to decide the fate of his preteen son Max .
- **S6**：Max's wealthy aunt Debra  and uncle Marvin  want full custody, which Charlie gives them in exchange for $100,000, half in advance, on the condition that Charlie take care of Max for three months while the couple are away on a second anniversary.
- **S7**：Charlie and Max meet with Charlie's childhood friend Bailey Tallet , who runs the boxing gym of her deceased father, Charlie's old coach.
- **S8**：There, Charlie buys a secondhand World Robot Boxing league  robot, the once-famous Noisy Boy, and arranges for it to fight the illegal circuit's champion, Midas, at a venue belonging to his friend Finn.
- **S9**：The fight starts well for Charlie, but partly due to both his inexperience with Noisy Boy's combinations and his own overconfidence, Charlie ends up losing control of Noisy Boy and Midas destroys it.
- **S10**：Charlie breaks into a junkyard with Max to steal scraps that he can use to put a new robot together.
- **S11**：There, Max falls over a ledge, where he is saved from doom by getting snagged on the arm of a buried robot.
- **S12**：After Charlie pulls Max back up, Max digs out the entire robot, called Atom.
- **S13**：On Max's insistence, Charlie takes it back to Bailey's gym, where they discover Atom is an obsolete Generation-2 sparring bot built in 2014.
- **S14**：Atom has been designed to sustain massive damage, but is unable to deal much damage itself.
- **S15**：Atom also has a "shadow function" which allows for following human movement.
- **S16**：Partly due to both Max's insistence and Charlie needing money, the duo has Atom fight an unsanctioned outdoor match against a robot called Metro.
- **S17**：Atom wins, earning back some of Charlie's money.
- **S18**：Max later upgrades Atom to take vocal commands, using parts from Charlie's demolished robots, and convinces Charlie to train Atom.
- **S19**：Atom's string of subsequent wins attracts the attention of a promoter from the WRB, who offers Atom a professional fight against the robot Twin Cities.
- **S20**：Charlie accepts, and Atom wins again, thanks to Charlie's boxing experience allowing him to locate and take advantage of a small tell in Twin Cities' punch.
- **S21**：Reveling in their subsequent novelty attention, Max challenges WRB champion Zeus, designed by genius Tak Mashido  and sponsored by wealthy Farra Lemkova , a woman who is shown to monopolize the robot-boxing industry, who before the match tries to buy the upstart Atom.
- **S22**：As Max and Charlie leave after the Twin Cities fight, Ricky and his men attack them, and steal their winnings.
- **S23**：Feeling guilty, Charlie returns Max to his aunt and uncle, feeling Max will be safer with them and refusing the second half of the money he was promised.
- **S24**：Bailey convinces him that he can be a better father.
- **S25**：Debra allows Charlie to take Max out for one last night, to the Zeus-Atom match.
- **S26**：Zeus severely damages Atom while also getting injured for the first time.
- **S27**：Ricky, who had bet Finn $100,000 that Atom would not last the first round, tries to slip away, but is cornered by Finn and his colleagues.
- **S28**：In the fourth round of the five-round match, Atom's vocal receptors are damaged, and Atom must fight the last round in shadow mode, copying Charlie's moves from the aisle.
- **S29**：Zeus, now controlled manually by a furious Mashido, expends energy on trashing the defensive Atom, running low on power and turning sluggish as a result.
- **S30**：The fight swings in Atom's favor as he overwhelms the weakened Zeus, even knocking the seemingly invincible champion down once, but Atom is unable to win before the round ends.
- **S31**：The judges declare Zeus the winner on points, but the near-defeat leaves the Zeus team humiliated.
- **S32**：The people cheered for Atom, who is labeled the "People's Champion" as Charlie and Max celebrate their success and their love.

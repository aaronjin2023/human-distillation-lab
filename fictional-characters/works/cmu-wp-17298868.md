### Ip Man

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-aee3ce8d583c2bb1` · 独立版本：`profile-94a3b0987418208f`

- **性格倾向**：在和平切磋情境中低调克制，不以高超武艺主动争名（摘要直接叙述；S2、S3、S5）
- **核心动机**：失去家产后首先设法养家，后因朋友失踪介入擂台调查（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10、S14、S15）
- **价值取舍**：面对以保命换取传授武艺的条件，拒绝为占领军效力（行为推断；S31、S32）
- **情绪反应**：得知友人遇害并目睹处决后，愤怒使原有克制明显减弱（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17、S18、S19）
- **成长变化**：由无意收徒的富裕练武者，转为教授工人自卫并开馆授徒（摘要直接叙述；S3、S23、S41）

矛盾与变化：平日切磋保持克制，面对杀害同胞的暴力时却以猛烈攻击回应。

### Miura

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-33a4e4e758021789` · 独立版本：`profile-f85d591839a45457`

- **性格倾向**：面对出众武艺时表现出强烈好胜，也在意公开受挑战的颜面（行为推断；S20、S32）
- **核心动机**：对叶问的身手产生兴趣，希望继续观看其战斗并取得传授（摘要直接叙述；S20、S24、S31）
- **决策方式**：从等待对手重返擂台，转为派兵搜寻并以保命条件施压（摘要直接叙述；S24、S31）
- **价值取舍**：接受公开比武既出于热爱武术，也为避免日方蒙受羞辱（摘要直接叙述；S32）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Ip Man is set in the 1930s in Foshan, a hub of Southern Chinese martial arts, where various schools actively recruit disciples and compete against each other.
- **S2**：Although the Wing Chun master Ip Man is the most skilled martial artist in Foshan, he is unassuming and keeps a low profile.
- **S3**：As an independently wealthy man, he feels no need to accept any disciples and instead spends his days training, meeting with friends, and spending time with his family.
- **S4**：However, his wife is often resentful of the time he spends training and discussing martial arts with friends and colleagues.
- **S5**：Though not a professional martial artist, Ip is respected in Foshan due to the abilities he displays in friendly, closed-door competitions with local masters.
- **S6**：Ip's reputation is further enhanced when he defeats an aggressive, rude, highly skilled Northern Chinese martial arts master, Jin Shanzhao, thus upholding the regional pride of fellow Southern stylists and others in Foshan.
- **S7**：The Japanese invasion in 1937 adversely affects the life of everyone in Foshan.
- **S8**：Ip's house is claimed by the Japanese and used as their Foshan headquarters.
- **S9**：Ip and his family lose their wealth and are forced to move into a decrepit house.
- **S10**：Desperate to support his family, Ip accepts work as a coolie at a coal mine.
- **S11**：The Japanese General Miura, who is a Karate master, establishes an arena where Chinese martial artists compete with his military trainees.
- **S12**：The Chinese earn a bag of rice for every match they win.
- **S13**：Li Zhao, a former police officer and Ip's acquaintance, is now working as a translator for the Japanese and is making the offer to the martial artists working as coolies.
- **S14**：Ip at first declines to participate in the matches.
- **S15**：However, when his friend Lin goes missing, he agrees to take part in order to investigate.
- **S16**：He is enraged when he sees a fellow Foshan master  mercilessly executed for picking up a bag of rice from a prior victory after giving up in a second match against three karateka.
- **S17**：He also comes to understand that Lin was killed in an earlier fight.
- **S18**：Barely able to contain his rage, Ip demands a match with ten karateka at once.
- **S19**：Despite having not practiced Wing Chun since the invasion began , he proceeds to mercilessly crush each of them with a brutal yet efficient barrage of his martial art mastery, showing barely any of the restraint he exhibited in previous engagements.
- **S20**：His skill arouses the interest of Miura, who seeks to learn more about Ip and see him fight again.
- **S21**：Ip visits his friend Chow Ching-chuen, who owns and runs a cotton mill in Foshan.
- **S22**：Chow tells Ip that a highway robbery gang led by Jin Shanzhao is harassing his workers and extorting money from them.
- **S23**：Ip trains the workers in Wing Chun for self defence.
- **S24**：Meanwhile, Miura grows impatient when Ip does not return to the arena and sends men to find Ip.
- **S25**：These men harass Ip's family, and Ip incapacitates them.
- **S26**：Ip and his family then go into hiding at Li Zhao's house.
- **S27**：Meanwhile, the robbers return to the cotton mill to demand money.
- **S28**：The workers fight back using the techniques that Ip taught them.
- **S29**：Just then, Ip appears and defeats Jin Shanzhao, warning him never to harass the workers again.
- **S30**：The Japanese soldiers eventually find Ip at the cotton mill.
- **S31**：Miura tells Ip that his life will be spared if agrees to instruct the Japanese soldiers in martial arts.
- **S32**：Ip refuses and challenges Miura to a match, which Miura accepts, both because of his love for martial arts and because refusing the challenge would be a humiliation to the Japanese.
- **S33**：The match between Ip and Miura is held in public in Foshan's square.
- **S34**：At first, the two fighters seem equally matched, but Miura becomes overwhelmed and is unable to fend off Ip's attacks as he effortlessly uses him as a Wooden Dummy, inflicting a severe beating on him and clearly winning.
- **S35**：As the beaten general lies down after his defeat, Ip looks over to the crowd and hears the Chinese cheering him; within the crowd, he spots his wife and child with Chow.
- **S36**：Just then, Miura's deputy Sato shoots Ip.
- **S37**：This sparks a scuffle between the Chinese audience and the Japanese soldiers.
- **S38**：During the scuffle, Li Zhao kills Sato with Sato's own gun.
- **S39**：Ip is taken away amidst the chaos.
- **S40**：It is revealed that he survives and escapes to Hong Kong with his family.
- **S41**：There, Ip establishes a Wing Chun school, where his students come to include Bruce Lee.

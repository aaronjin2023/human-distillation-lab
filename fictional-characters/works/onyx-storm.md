- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] After the events of Iron Flame , the kingdoms of Navarre and Poromiel, together with leaders of the recent revolution in Navarre's province of Tyrrendor, negotiate an uneasy alliance in order to fight an invading army of evil magic-wielders known as venin.
- **S3**：Violet, Xaden, several allies, and their dragons and gryphons explore isles to the south of their continent, hoping to find more irids (dragons of Andarna's kind), gather information that might cure Xaden's transformation into a venin, and establish alliances to help Navarre and Poromiel in the war.
- **S4**：While on this quest, they obtain manuscripts written by Violet's father and meet Xaden's mother, who he hasn't seen since he was a child.
- **S5**：They succeed in establishing alliances, though some members of the team are killed in the process.
- **S6**：Finally, they meet a group of irids, but the irids reject Andarna due to disappointment at how she has been shaped by Navarre's warlike culture.
- **S7**：After Violet, Xaden, and their surviving companions return to the continent, venin attack Tyrrendor.
- **S8**：One of the irids reappears and strengthens the magical wardstone that protects the province, turning the tide in the battle.
- **S9**：He persuades Andarna to return with him to the other irids, and though Violet consents for Andarna to leave, she is grief-stricken by the loss.
- **S10**：Then the powerful venin Theophanie captures Violet's sister Mira, leading to a climactic battle between a group of venin and the combined forces of Navarre, Poromiel, and Tyrrendor.
- **S11**：During the battle, Andarna returns and Violet kills Theophanie, but Xaden draws more power from the earth, which advances his transformation into a venin.
- **S12**：Twelve hours later, Violet awakens in confusion, hears reports of new murders and disappearances, and finds to her surprise that she appears to have just married Xaden.
- **S13**：The novel ends on a mysterious cliffhanger.

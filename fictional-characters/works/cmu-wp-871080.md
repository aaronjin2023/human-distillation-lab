### Kumal

- **性格倾向**：在兄弟受威胁的情境下，他倾向主动靠近并提供保护（行为推断；S3、S4、S27）

### Sangha

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-7db5149ca1c96baa` · 独立版本：`profile-1632e741bf0dd85c`

- **性格倾向**：在被持续追赶并逼入角落时，他倾向以反击保护自己（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **关系模式**：他能够与拉乌尔和平相处，也在认出兄弟后恢复亲近互动（摘要直接叙述；S17、S24）
- **决策方式**：重新认出兄弟后，他以共同玩耍回应人类安排的对抗（摘要直接叙述；S22、S23、S24）
- **成长变化**：他从安静的家庭宠物变成宫廷中被视为凶猛的动物（摘要直接叙述；S17、S19、S20）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：自卫伤狗被人类解释为嗜血，改变了他的处境

### Aidan McRory

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-5f1e59cabb776a15` · 独立版本：`profile-887895bdaaffc5ce`

- **性格倾向**：在早期探险中，他倾向无视动物生命与古迹保护的限制（行为推断；S1、S8、S9）
- **核心动机**：他最初进入古寺是为了盗取雕像出售，而非保护遗址（摘要直接叙述；S1）
- **关系模式**：他既杀死幼虎的父亲，又与库玛尔建立了喂食和亲近联系（摘要直接叙述；S8、S10、S28）
- **成长变化**：当库玛尔显露对往日喂食的记忆时，他放下枪并誓言停猎（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：猎杀老虎的身份与对库玛尔形成的感情冲突

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Set in 1920s Thailand, two tigers are separated as cubs after the ancient temple where they live is disturbed by Aidan McRory  who intends to steal and sell the ancient statues.
- **S2**：Two tiger cubs are playing when the younger cub  comes upon a young civet.
- **S3**：Sangha chases the civet into its burrow and the mother civet appears and chases Sangha up a tree.
- **S4**：The older tiger cub  appears and chases the mother civet back into its burrow.
- **S5**：Eventually, humans come across them, and the tigress arrives to protect the cubs.
- **S6**：She picks Sangha up and runs for safety.
- **S7**：Kumal follows, but can't keep up and falls behind.
- **S8**：The cubs' father appears, but the men have caught up with them and he is shot dead by McRory.
- **S9**：McRory is an unscrupulous explorer, big-game hunter and temple looter.
- **S10**：He discovers Kumal and befriends him, but McRory is arrested for stealing from the ancient temple and Kumal is kept by the chief in the Cambodian village where McRory had been staying.
- **S11**：The chief then sells Kumal to a circus where he is to be the star attraction.
- **S12**：Sangha remains in the jungle with his mother, but both are soon trapped by McRory as game for a vain Khmer prince to hunt.
- **S13**：The mother is shot in the ear and thought to be dead before she jumps up and runs off with a hole in her ear.
- **S14**：Sangha is discovered by young Raoul, son of the French administrator, Normandin, and becomes the child's pet.
- **S15**：However, Raoul's mother's dog, Bittsi, a schipperke, is a constant antagonist to the young tiger.
- **S16**：Kumal is trained by cruel circus ringmaster Zerbino to do tricks, such as jumping through a flaming hoop.
- **S17**：Sangha meanwhile dwells peacefully with Raoul until he is at last cornered by Bittsi following a prolonged chase.
- **S18**：In self-defence, Sangha attacks Bittsi, badly wounding although not killing him.
- **S19**：This provokes a hysterical reaction from the household, particularly Raoul's mother, who insists that the cub has now "got a taste of blood" and Sangha is given away.
- **S20**：As a result, he is made a part of the prince's palace menagerie, where he quickly gains a reputation as a ferocious animal.
- **S21**：Sangha and Kumal are now very close to each other.
- **S22**：The prince then decides to hold a festival in which a battle between two great beasts - the brother tigers - will be the centerpiece.
- **S23**：When placed in the cage before the audience during the festival, the two brothers do not immediately recognize each other, and Kumal is afraid to fight.
- **S24**：However, when the brothers finally recognize each other they begin to play together instead of fighting, and the audience likes this but the trainers don't.
- **S25**：The trainer attempts to antagonize the tigers into fighting, but as he opens the cage to shoot one, the tigers escape, managing to frighten the trainers and the audience into the cage themselves.
- **S26**：The two tigers escape, and McRory is determined to hunt them down.
- **S27**：After Kumal showing Sangha how to jump through fire to escape, McRory and Raoul find them.
- **S28**：However, as McRory takes aim at Sangha, Kumal appears, and demonstrates that he remembers the sweets McRory used to give him.
- **S29**：McRory puts down his gun and vows never to hunt again.
- **S30**：The two brothers make their way back to their temple home in the jungle where they meet up with their mother .
- **S31**：Just before the credits, some comments to save the tigers  are shown.

## 角色资料

### Superman

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-aab8ea41af3c9e4b` · 独立版本：`profile-e971ee86aa56fe5f`

- **性格倾向**：在无人能够施救时，他倾向主动承担暴露能力的风险（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：在公众遇险与爱人死亡的情境中，他表现出救人和挽回爱人的愿望（行为推断；S21、S22、S24、S25）
- **价值取舍**：在失去露易丝后，他将挽救生命置于不干涉历史的训诫之上（行为推断；S24、S25）
- **情绪反应**：未能及时救下露易丝使他悲痛，并推动他采取越界行动（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：救助公众的责任与挽回爱人的私人愿望发生冲突

### Lex Luthor

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-eb9049a547920b8d` · 独立版本：`profile-9ecb2499342156da`

- **性格倾向**：在对抗超人时，他倾向精心设局并利用对手的救人责任（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **核心动机**：他追求借灾难重塑土地价值，从房地产投机中获取巨额财富（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **价值取舍**：在牟利计划面前，他将平民生命和伴侣亲人的安危置后（行为推断；S15、S19）
- **决策方式**：他倾向预先削弱阻碍者，再利用两地危机制造救援困局（行为推断；S17、S18）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：On the planet Krypton, using evidence provided by scientist Jor-El, the Ruling Council sentences three attempted insurrectionists, General Zod, Ursa and Non, to "eternal living death" in the Phantom Zone.
- **S2**：Despite his eminence, Jor-El is unable to convince the Council of his belief that Krypton will soon explode.
- **S3**：To save his infant son Kal-El, Jor-El launches a spacecraft containing the child towards Earth, a distant planet with a suitable atmosphere, and where Kal-El's dense molecular structure will give him superhuman powers.
- **S4**：Shortly after the launch, Krypton is destroyed.
- **S5**：Three years later the ship lands near an American farming town, Smallville, where Kal-El is found by Jonathan and Martha Kent.
- **S6**：The Kents take the child back to their farm and raised him as their own son, naming him Clark after Martha's maiden name.
- **S7**：At age 18, soon after the death of Jonathan, Clark hears a psychic "call", discovering a glowing green crystal in the ship.
- **S8**：It compels him to travel to the Arctic, where the crystal builds the Fortress of Solitude, resembling the architecture of Krypton.
- **S9**：Inside, a vision of Jor-El explains Clark's origins, educating him in powers and responsibilities.
- **S10**：After 12 years, with his powers fully developed, Clark leaves the Fortress with a colorful costume and becomes a reporter at the Daily Planet in Metropolis.
- **S11**：He meets and develops a romantic attraction to coworker Lois Lane, but she sees him as awkward and unsophisticated.
- **S12**：Lois becomes involved in a helicopter accident where conventional means of rescue are impossible, requiring Clark to use his powers in public for the first time to save her.
- **S13**：The rescue of Air Force One and other good deeds make the mysterious "caped wonder" a celebrity.
- **S14**：The hero visits Lois at home, takes her for a flight over the city, and allows her to interview him for an article in which she names him "Superman." Meanwhile, criminal genius Lex Luthor has developed a cunning plan to make a fortune in real estate by buying large amounts of barren desert land and then diverting a nuclear missile test flight to the San Andreas Fault.
- **S15**：The missile will sink California and leave Luthor's desert as the new West Coast of the United States, greatly increasing its value.
- **S16**：After his incompetent henchman Otis accidentally redirects the first rocket to the wrong place, Luthor's girlfriend Eve Teschmacher successfully changes the course of a second missile.
- **S17**：Knowing Superman could stop his plan, Luthor lures him to his underground lair and exposes him to Kryptonite.
- **S18**：As Superman weakens, Luthor taunts him by revealing that the first missile is headed to Hackensack, New Jersey, in the opposite direction, knowing that Superman cannot stop both impacts.
- **S19**：Teschmacher is horrified because her mother lives in Hackensack, but Luthor does not care and leaves Superman to a slow death.
- **S20**：Teschmacher rescues Superman on the condition that he will deal with the New Jersey missile first.
- **S21**：He is thus too late to stop the second impact, causing a massive earthquake which he battles to correct.
- **S22**：While Superman is busy saving others, Lois's car falls into the ground due to an aftershock.
- **S23**：It quickly fills with dirt and debris and she suffocates to death.
- **S24**：Distraught at being unable to save Lois, Superman ignores Jor-El's warning not to interfere with human history, preferring to remember Jonathan Kent's advice that he must be on Earth for "a reason".
- **S25**：He travels back in time in order to save Lois, altering history so that her car is never caught in the aftershock.
- **S26**：Superman then delivers Luthor and Otis to prison and flies into the sunrise for further adventures.

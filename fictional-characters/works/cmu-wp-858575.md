### Harry Potter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6731c01eb468c5e3` · 独立版本：`profile-83dbe5765171649c`

- **性格倾向**：怀疑德拉科构成威胁时，他倾向亲自追查并直接介入。（行为推断；S7、S8、S19）
- **核心动机**：在获知魂器秘密后，他把寻找剩余魂器作为后续目标。（摘要直接叙述；S23、S24、S42）
- **决策方式**：在此次对峙中，他采用书中咒语，却未预料其重伤后果。（行为推断；S19、S20）
- **情绪反应**：看到自己施咒撕开德拉科胸口后，他对伤势感到震惊。（摘要直接叙述；S20）
- **关系模式**：他向赫敏透露对金妮的感情，并与金妮逐渐发展亲密关系。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14、S21）
- **成长变化**：经历邓布利多之死后，他决定不再返校而继续追寻魂器。（摘要直接叙述；S33、S42）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：书中提示曾帮助他取得课堂优势，其中的咒语也造成了他未预料的伤害。

### Draco Malfoy

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-180d18b43a9adfd3` · 独立版本：`profile-4dfdf371e6759b46`

- **性格倾向**：在真正面对刺杀对象时，他表现出难以执行致命行动的迟疑。（行为推断；S32）
- **核心动机**：他协助食死徒进入学校，可理解为推进受命刺杀的任务。（行为推断；S2、S30、S32）
- **决策方式**：发现哈利在列车上窥探时，他以直接施法制伏对方应对。（行为推断；S8、S9）
- **关系模式**：他与哈利的敌对在窥探和质问中升级为相互施咒伤害。（行为推断；S8、S9、S19、S20）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他协助食死徒入校，却未能亲手完成刺杀邓布利多的任务。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{further2}} Harry is shown bleeding in front of the Ministry of Magic, as the result of an attack that took place after the second-to-last scene of the previous film, Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix.
- **S2**：Lord Voldemort is tightening his grip on both the Wizarding and Muggle worlds and has chosen Draco Malfoy to carry out a secret mission.
- **S3**：Severus Snape accepts Bellatrix Lestrange's challenge to make an Unbreakable Vow with Draco's mother, Narcissa, to protect Draco and fulfill the assignment if he fails.
- **S4**：Harry accompanies Albus Dumbledore to visit former potions professor Horace Slughorn, who has gone into hiding.
- **S5**：Slughorn agrees to return to teach at Hogwarts as Dumbledore tells Harry that Slughorn's return to Hogwarts is crucial.
- **S6**：Leaving Fred and George's new shop, Harry, Ron and Hermione notice Draco and Narcissa associating with Death Eaters in Borgin and Burkes.
- **S7**：Harry believes Voldemort has made Draco a Death Eater, but Ron and Hermione are sceptical.
- **S8**：On the Hogwarts Express, Harry spies on Draco, who hints to his friends that he does not plan to return to Hogwarts next year.
- **S9**：Draco discovers Harry's presence and immobilises him, but Harry is rescued by Luna with her Spectrespecs.
- **S10**：At Hogwarts, Harry and Ron are admitted to Slughorn's potions class at the last minute and have to borrow the needed textbooks because they don't have their own.
- **S11**：Harry's copy is inscribed on the fly page as the "Half-Blood Prince." This owner, Harry finds, had annotated the book with additional instructions — tips and recommendations that allow Harry to excel in the class.
- **S12**：As for Ron, he becomes the Keeper of the Gryffindor Quidditch team, and, thinking he has been given a Good Luck potion by Harry, Ron becomes a sports hero and partially as a result, he forms a romantic relationship with Lavender Brown, leaving Hermione disconsolate.
- **S13**：Harry discovers Hermione sobbing in the Astronomy Tower, where he reveals to her that he has feelings for Ron's sister, Ginny Weasley.
- **S14**：Harry spends Christmas with the Weasleys and becomes closer to Ginny.
- **S15**：They almost share a kiss on Christmas Eve but Bellatrix and Fenrir Greyback, intent on capturing Harry, attack and burn the Burrow, home of the Weasleys.
- **S16**：Outnumbered, Bellatrix and Greyback abandon their attack as Order members Nymphadora Tonks and Remus Lupin, together with Harry and the Weasleys, watch the structure burn.
- **S17**：After being cured of a love potion meant for Harry, Ron is nearly killed by a drink originally intended for Dumbledore.
- **S18**：While recovering, Ron murmurs Hermione's name in the hospital, in front of both Hermione and Lavender.
- **S19**：Suspecting that Draco was responsible for two attempts on Dumbledore's life, one of which has almost killed Ron, Harry confronts Draco and severely injures him with a Sectumsempra curse taken from the textbook of the Half-Blood Prince.
- **S20**：Harry, shocked that the curse has slliced open Draco's chest, retreats as Severus Snape enters and magically heals Draco's wound.
- **S21**：Fearing the book may be filled with more Dark Magic, Ginny and Harry hide it in the Room of Requirement and, while doing so, share their first kiss.
- **S22**：Dumbledore shows Harry memories of a young Tom Riddle and reveals that Slughorn retains a memory critical to Voldemort's defeat.
- **S23**：Harry retrieves the memory, learning that Voldemort wanted information for creating seven Horcruxes.
- **S24**：The Horcrux safeguards a portion of the creator's soul, granting him immortality unless the Horcrux is destroyed.
- **S25**：Two of Voldemort's Horcruxes have already been destroyed: Tom Riddle's diary and Marvolo Gaunt's ring.
- **S26**：Touching the ring, Harry has a vision of Voldemort, which is noticed by Dumbledore.
- **S27**：After discovering the possible location of another Horcrux, Dumbledore requests Harry's help to retrieve it.
- **S28**：They travel to a far-away cave where Harry is forced to make Dumbledore drink a mind-altering potion that hides the Horcrux, a locket.
- **S29**：A weakened Dumbledore defends them from a horde of Inferi and apparates himself and Harry back to the Astronomy Tower at Hogwarts.
- **S30**：In the meantime, Bellatrix, Greyback and several other Death Eaters enter the castle with Draco's help.
- **S31**：Upon arriving at the tower, Dumbledore tells Harry to fetch Snape but as footsteps approach, he orders Harry to hide.
- **S32**：Draco reveals that he has been chosen by Voldemort to kill Dumbledore, but he is unable to follow through.
- **S33**：Snape arrives and casts the Avada Kedavra curse, killing Dumbledore.
- **S34**：As Bellatrix casts the Dark Mark, Snape, Draco and the Death Eaters escape from the castle.
- **S35**：Bellatrix destroys the Great Hall and sets fire to Hagrid's hut as Harry attempts to stop them.
- **S36**：Snape overpowers Harry and reveals that he is the Half-Blood Prince before escaping.
- **S37**：Harry returns to the school to find the staff and students mourning Dumbledore.
- **S38**：Led by Minerva McGonagall, the students and teachers destroy the Dark Mark to honour Dumbledore whereupon Harry is comforted by Ginny.
- **S39**：Harry later reveals to Ron and Hermione that the locket Horcrux was a fake.
- **S40**：The locket contains a message from an "R.A.B." to Voldemort stating that he has stolen the real Horcrux with the intent of destroying it.
- **S41**：His dying wish is that when Voldemort meets his match he will be mortal once more.
- **S42**：Rather than return for their final year at Hogwarts, Harry, Ron and Hermione vow to seek out who R.A.B. was and to find the remaining Horcruxes.

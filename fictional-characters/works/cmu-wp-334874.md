## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：An underachieving bluestreak cleaner wrasse named Oscar  is fantasized about being rich and famous while making his way to work by following in his dad's footsteps as a tongue scrubber at the local Whale Wash.
- **S2**：Soon after arriving he is called to the office of his boss, a puffer fish named Sykes , to discuss the fact that he owes "five thousand clams" and has to pay it back by the next day.
- **S3**：After explaining this to his best friend Angie who is an angelfish , she offers him a chance to pay back the money by pawning a pink pearl that was a gift from her Grandmother.
- **S4**：Oscar brings the money to the race track to meet Sykes, but becomes distracted by his wishes of grandeur and, upon hearing that the race is rigged, places it all on a long-shot bet by the name of "Lucky Day".
- **S5**：Such a million dollar bet is noticed nearby by a beautiful lionfish named Lola , who flagrantly seduces an excited Oscar, but Oscar is very disappointed when she leaves once Sykes tells her he is a whale washer.
- **S6**：Sykes is furious that Oscar bet the money but nonetheless agrees to see how the race turns out.
- **S7**：Moments before their "horse", "Lucky Day" crosses the finish line he trips and falls on line.
- **S8**：The race is lost and Oscar is set to be punished in a secluded area for his impulsiveness.
- **S9**：Meanwhile, on another side of the ocean, in the wreck of the RMS Titanic, a family of criminally-inclined great white sharks has a problem with one of their sons, Lenny   .
- **S10**：Lenny refuses to act the part of a killer and wishes to not have to live up to those expectations.
- **S11**：Finally his father, Don Lino  loses patience and orders Lenny's more savage big brother Frankie  to show Lenny the ropes.
- **S12**：As the two sharks set out to go in accordance with their father's wishes, Frankie spots the scene where Oscar is being electrocuted by Ernie and Bernie , Sykes' two Jamaican Physalia physalis thugs, and sends Lenny off to attack.
- **S13**：The jellyfish spot Lenny and swim off, leaving Oscar alone with him.
- **S14**：Lenny frees Oscar but fails to trick Frankie, who becomes annoyed and charges at Oscar when an anchor falls and kills him.
- **S15**：Lenny flees, overcome with grief and guilt.
- **S16**：As no one saw the deed done and Oscar was seen near the body, everyone thinks he did it, and Oscar sees this as the chance to both redeem himself and receive his fame.
- **S17**：Oscar comes back to the city with a new title of the Sharkslayer.
- **S18**：Sykes becomes his manager, Lola becomes his girlfriend, and Oscar moves to the "top of the reef" to live in luxury.
- **S19**：At the same time, Don Lino has everyone out looking for Lenny, and when several get close to Oscar's town the other fish expect him to drive them away.
- **S20**：On the way he meets Lenny once more who forces Oscar to let him stay with him because he does not want to go home.
- **S21**：Soon Angie finds out about the lie and threatens to tell everyone but Oscar and Lenny convince her to keep quiet.
- **S22**：Although Oscar desires to please everyone, he soon discovers that he pleases no one; his paramour Angie is heartbroken by the fact that Oscar is no longer honest, while her hedonistic rival Lola repeatedly reminds Oscar that he has her only as long as he is famous.
- **S23**：With Don Lino planning revenge, Oscar and Lenny stage an event where Lenny pretends to terrorize the town and Oscar must defeat him, throwing him into the depths of the ocean.
- **S24**：Though this further cements Oscar as the Sharkslayer, it greatly angers Don Lino.
- **S25**：Oscar leaves Lola for Angie after Angie reveals that she had feelings for Oscar even before he became famous, but this leaves Lola determined to get revenge.
- **S26**：Oscar buys some Valentine's Day gifts for Angie, but before he can present them to her, he finds that Don Lino has kidnapped Angie in order to force a sit-down.
- **S27**：Lenny comes along, now disguised as a dolphin named Sebastian.
- **S28**：They arrive at the meeting to find Lola next to Don Lino, while Angie is bound and gagged with duct tape and presented to Don Lino on a plate, who prepares to eat her if Oscar doesn't comply.
- **S29**：Oscar just laughs and Lenny as "Sebastian" lunges forward to scoop Angie into his mouth, freeing her from Don Lino and giving Oscar dominance over the sharks.
- **S30**：However, he spends too much time threatening the sharks and doesn't realize how much pain Lenny is in, and Angie is regurgitated onto the table.
- **S31**：Don Lino suddenly realizes it's Lenny and proceeds to chase Oscar through the reef, but Oscar heads for the whale wash and ends up trapping both sharks.
- **S32**：Given an ovation by the other fish, Oscar finally has enough and confesses that he is not a "Sharkslayer" and that it was the anchor that had killed Frankie.
- **S33**：He then stated to Don Lino that everyone likes Lenny for the way he is, strongly urging him not to prejudge people before he knows them properly and to not make the mistake he made in prejudging his wealth.
- **S34**：Realizing that Oscar is right, Don Lino apologizes to Lenny and reconciles with him while making peace with Oscar, stating that he and his gang bear him no ill will.
- **S35**：Oscar forsakes all the wealth he has acquired, makes peace with the sharks, becomes manager of the Whale Wash , and starts dating Angie and starts to have a happy, honest life.
- **S36**：In the post-credits scene, Lola comes to see Oscar in the top of the reef, wanting to make amends with him for what she did, but all she finds is a hermit named Crazy Joe waiting for her.

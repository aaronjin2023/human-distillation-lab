- **S1**：Casino Royale is the first novel by the British author Ian Fleming .
- **S2**：Published in 1953, it is the first James Bond book , and it paved the way for a further eleven novels and two short story collections by Fleming, followed by numerous continuation Bond novels by other authors.
- **S3**：The story concerns the British secret agent James Bond , gambling at the casino in Royale-les-Eaux to try to bankrupt Le Chiffre , the treasurer of a French union and a member of the Soviet secret service.
- **S4**：Bond is supported in his endeavours by Vesper Lynd , a member of his own service, as well as Felix Leiter of the CIA and René Mathis of the French Deuxième Bureau .
- **S5**：Fleming used his wartime experiences as a member of the Naval Intelligence Division , and the people he met during his work, to provide plot elements; the character of Bond also reflected many of Fleming's personal tastes.
- **S6**：Fleming wrote the draft in early 1952 at his Goldeneye estate in Jamaica while awaiting his marriage.
- **S7**：He was initially unsure whether the work was suitable for publication, but was assured by his friend, the novelist William Plomer , that the novel had promise.
- **S8**：Within the spy storyline, Casino Royale deals with themes of Britain's position in the world, particularly the relationship with the US in light of the defections to the Soviet Union of the British agents Guy Burgess and Donald Maclean .
- **S9**：The book was given broadly positive reviews by critics at the time and sold out in less than a month after its UK release on 13 April 1953, although US sales upon release a year later were much slower.

- **S11**：## Plot
- **S12**：[ edit ] M , the Head of the British Secret Service , assigns James Bond , 007, to play against and bankrupt Le Chiffre , the paymaster for a SMERSH -controlled trade union, in a high-stakes baccarat game at the Royale-les-Eaux casino in northern France.
- **S13**：As part of Bond's cover as a rich Jamaican playboy, M also assigns as his companion Vesper Lynd , personal assistant to the Head of Section S ( Soviet Union ).
- **S14**：The CIA and the French Deuxième Bureau also send agents as observers.
- **S15**：The game soon turns into an intense confrontation between Le Chiffre and Bond; Le Chiffre wins the first round, cleaning Bond out of his funds.
- **S16**：As Bond contemplates the prospect of reporting his failure to M, the CIA agent, Felix Leiter , gives him an envelope of money and a note: " Marshall Aid .
- **S17**：Thirty-two million francs .
- **S18**：With the compliments of the USA." The game continues, despite the attempts of one of Le Chiffre's Soviet minders to kill Bond.
- **S19**：Bond eventually wins, taking from Le Chiffre eighty million francs belonging to SMERSH.
- **S20**：Desperate to recover the money, Le Chiffre kidnaps Lynd and tortures Bond, threatening to kill them both if he does not get the money back.
- **S21**：During the torture, a SMERSH assassin enters and kills Le Chiffre as punishment for losing the money.
- **S22**：The agent does not kill Bond, saying that he has no orders to do so, but cuts a Cyrillic ' Ш ' for шпион ( shpión , Russian for spy) into Bond's hand so that future SMERSH agents will be able to identify him as such.
- **S23**：Lynd visits Bond every day as he recuperates in hospital, and he gradually realises that he loves her; he even contemplates leaving the Secret Service to settle down with her.
- **S24**：When he is released from hospital they spend time together at a quiet guest house and eventually become lovers.
- **S25**：One day they see a mysterious man named Gettler tracking their movements, which greatly distresses Lynd.
- **S26**：The following morning, Bond finds that she has committed suicide.
- **S27**：She leaves behind a note explaining that she had been working as an unwilling double agent for the Soviet Ministry of Internal Affairs .
- **S28**：SMERSH had kidnapped her lover, a Polish Royal Air Force pilot , who had revealed information about her under torture; SMERSH then used that information to blackmail her into helping them undermine Bond's mission, including her own faked kidnapping.
- **S29**：She had tried to start a new life with Bond, but upon seeing Gettler—a SMERSH agent—she realised that she would never be free of her tormentors, and that staying with Bond would only put him in danger.
- **S30**：Bond informs his service of Lynd's duplicity, coldly telling his contact, "The bitch is dead now." [ 1 ]

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] 20-year old Violet Sorrengail is the youngest daughter of General Lilith Sorrengail, who leads the Basgiath War College in the kingdom of Navarre, and the younger sister of Mira Sorrengail, a renowned dragon rider.
- **S3**：Though Violet was trained her entire life to be a scribe and struggles due to weak joints, her mother orders her to risk her life and become a dragon rider as well.
- **S4**：Years earlier, General Sorrengail quelled a rebellion within the Navarrian province of Tyrrendor.
- **S5**：As punishment, the rebels' children were branded with marks called relics and forced to enter Basgiath's Riders Quadrant.
- **S6**：Violet learns that marked ones may seek revenge on her because of her family.
- **S7**：Upon entering the War College, Violet is nearly thrown to her death by psychopathic cadet Jack Barlowe, who vows to kill her.
- **S8**：She also encounters the infamous Xaden Riorson, a third-year student whose father Fen had led the rebellion and murdered Violet's older brother Brennan before being executed by Lilith.
- **S9**：In Basgiath, Violet reunites with her childhood best friend and longtime crush Dain Aetos, a second-year cadet.
- **S10**：Violet joins Fourth Wing under Xaden's command, with Dain as their squad leader.
- **S11**：When training starts, Violet struggles with hand-to-hand combat because of her frailty and is targeted by marked ones.
- **S12**：She begins learning her sparring partners' names in advance and poisoning them to give herself an advantage.
- **S13**：One night, she overhears the marked ones having a secret meeting and is caught by Riorson, who asks her to stay quiet and in exchange shows her how to improve her fighting skills.
- **S14**：Though wary of his reputation, Violet finds herself increasingly attracted to Xaden.
- **S15**：During the Threshing process, Riders seek to bond to a dragon, which will allow them to develop a magical power known as a signet.
- **S16**：However, not every cadet will be lucky enough to be picked by a dragon.
- **S17**：During Threshing, Violet and her squad see a small, golden dragon that Barlowe wants to kill for appearing weak.
- **S18**：Though injured, Violet defends it from Barlowe and his posse and is saved by an enormous black dragon named Tairn, who, impressed by her fortitude, selects her.
- **S19**：Violet also connects with the little dragon, Andarna, and as a result becomes the first rider ever to bond two dragons.
- **S20**：Dain kisses Violet, but she finds that she no longer has feelings for him.
- **S21**：One night, Tairn awakens Violet when six cadets attempt to kill her.
- **S22**：Xaden rescues her and kills the cadets with his signet that can wield shadows.
- **S23**：When Violet reveals a senior cadet let the attackers in, Xaden confronts them and has them executed.
- **S24**：After the attack, Xaden arranges for Liam Mairi, his foster brother and a marked cadet, to protect Violet.
- **S25**：During a meeting with their dragons, Violet learns she can also communicate with Sgaeyl, Xaden's dragon who is Tairn’s mate.
- **S26**：She kisses Xaden due to Tairn's feelings for Sgaeyl, which influence their feelings as well.
- **S27**：Barlowe tries to kill her during a sparring session, but she escapes by purposefully triggering his allergies.
- **S28**：Violet and Xaden find they can communicate telepathically through their dragons.
- **S29**：Later, during a capture-the-flag game, Barlowe attempts to kill Liam.
- **S30**：Violet uses Andarna to freeze time and saves him by manifesting the rare and powerful signet to wield lightning, which she uses to cause a landslide that seemingly kills Barlowe.
- **S31**：That night, Violet and Xaden have sex .
- **S32**：She asks why he and his cousin Bodhi were out after curfew, to which Xaden replies that they were at a nearby military base but does not explain further.
- **S33**：In the final round of the college War Games, Xaden leads Violet and a group of marked ones to an outpost in Athebyne.
- **S34**：Along the way they encounter gryphon fliers of Poromiel, a rival nation that Navarre has been in conflict with for centuries, and Violet learns that Xaden has been secretly supplying weapons to them.
- **S35**：Xaden reveals that the Poromish are under attack by the venin, dark magic-wielding creatures that can only be killed with such weapons.
- **S36**：Since their magical boundaries known as wards repel them, Navarrians believe venin to be fictitious and the government conceals their existence.
- **S37**：They arrive at the outpost but find it deserted.
- **S38**：A nearby village is then attacked by venin and the group realizes it was led into a trap by Dain's father Colonel Aetos, who found out about their trips to Athebyne when Dain used his memory reading signet on Violet without her knowing.
- **S39**：Despite the danger, Violet and her crew join forces with the gryphon fliers to defend the village.
- **S40**：Liam and his dragon are killed.
- **S41**：Using her lightning powers, Violet defeats the last venin and briefly stops time with energy from Andarna, but she becomes seriously injured.
- **S42**：Xaden and the survivors seek safety in Aretia, an abandoned city.
- **S43**：After three days, Violet awakens and is stunned to learn that Brennan is alive.
- **S44**：Xaden, meanwhile, vows to earn back Violet's trust.

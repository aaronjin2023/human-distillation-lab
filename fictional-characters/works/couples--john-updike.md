- **S2**：[ edit ] At the center of the amorous round robin is Piet Hanema.

- **S4**：At the same time the "Little-Smiths" swap partners with the Appleby couple.
- **S5**：Harold and Janet keep their affair secret, while they know about the affair of Frankie and Marcia.
- **S6**：After Foxy gives birth to her son, Piet loses interest and has a fling with Bea Guerin.
- **S7**：Foxy informs Piet that she has become pregnant again as the result of their final liaison.
- **S8**：They try to arrange a discreet abortion and seek help from Freddy.
- **S9**：He offers aid but, as revenge for Piet's affair with his wife Georgene, demands a night with Piet's wife Angela in return.
- **S10**：Angela consents, but Freddy turns out to be impotent, at least for the act itself.
- **S11**：On the verge of the abortion in Boston, Foxy breaks down with second thoughts about losing the baby.
- **S12**：Angela wants to leave Piet and suggests he should marry Foxy, who has separated from Ken.
- **S13**：Piet temporarily finds comfort but no satisfaction in the arms of Bea Guerin.
- **S14**：Eventually Angela tells Piet to leave her house, and for a period he lives on his own, having become a pariah among the couples of Tarbox.
- **S15**：His partner Gallagher suggests a separation too and pays him off.
- **S16**：In a final scene Piet and the society of Tarbox witness a fire that destroys the local church.
- **S17**：The following years are summarized in a short final passage: Angela took a job as teacher and got a divorce, shortly after Piet married Foxy and they moved to Lexington, where they found new friends.

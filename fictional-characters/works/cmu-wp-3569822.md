## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1947, John Nash  arrives at Princeton University.
- **S2**：He is co-recipient, with Martin Hansen , of the prestigious Carnegie Scholarship for mathematics.
- **S3**：At a reception he meets a group of other promising math and science graduate students, Richard Sol , Ainsley , and Bender .
- **S4**：He also meets his roommate Charles Herman , a literature student, and an unlikely friendship begins.
- **S5**：Nash comes under increasing pressure to publish, both from the mathematics department chairman and in the form of rivalry with Hansen.
- **S6**：But he refuses until he finds a truly original idea.
- **S7**：His inspiration comes when he and his fellow graduate students discuss how to approach a group of women at a bar.
- **S8**：Hansen quotes Adam Smith and advocates "every man for himself", but Nash argues that a cooperative approach would lead to better chances of success.
- **S9**：This leads to a new concept of governing dynamics which Nash develops and publishes.
- **S10**：On the strength of this he is offered an appointment at MIT where Sol and Bender join him.
- **S11**：Some years later, Nash is invited to the Pentagon to crack encrypted enemy telecommunication.
- **S12**：Nash is able to decipher the code mentally, to the astonishment of other codebreakers.
- **S13**：He considers his regular duties at MIT uninteresting and beneath his talents, so he is pleased to be given a new assignment by mysterious supervisor William Parcher  of the United States Department of Defense, to look for patterns in magazines and newspapers in order to thwart a Soviet plot.
- **S14**：Nash becomes increasingly obsessive about searching for these hidden patterns and believes he is followed when he delivers his results to a secret mailbox.
- **S15**：Meanwhile a student, Alicia Larde , asks him to dinner, and the two fall in love.
- **S16**：On a return visit to Princeton, Nash runs into his former roommate Charles and meets Charles' young niece Marcee , whom he adores.
- **S17**：With Charles' encouragement he proposes to Alicia and they marry.
- **S18**：Nash begins to fear for his life after witnessing a shootout between Parcher and Soviet agents.
- **S19**：He tells Parcher that he wants to quit his special assignment but Parcher blackmails him into staying.
- **S20**：While delivering a guest lecture at Harvard University , Nash attempts to flee from what appear to be foreign agents, led by Dr.
- **S21**：Rosen .
- **S22**：After punching Rosen in an attempt to flee, Nash is forcibly sedated and sent to a psychiatric facility.
- **S23**：He believes the facility is run by the Soviets who are trying to extract information from him.
- **S24**：Dr.
- **S25**：Rosen tells Alicia that Nash has schizophrenia and that Charles, Marcee and Parcher exist only in his imagination.
- **S26**：Alicia investigates and finally confronts Nash with the unopened documents he had delivered to the secret mailbox.
- **S27**：Nash is given a course of insulin shock therapy and eventually released.
- **S28**：Frustrated with the side-effects of the antipsychotic medication he is taking, he secretly stops taking it.
- **S29**：But this causes a relapse and he meets Parcher again.
- **S30**：After an incident where Nash endangers his infant son and accidentally knocks Alicia and the baby to the ground , she flees the house in fear with their child.
- **S31**：Nash steps in front of her car to prevent her from leaving.
- **S32**：He tells Alicia, "She never gets old", referring to Marcee, who although years have passed since their first encounter, has remained exactly the same age and is still a little girl.
- **S33**：With this, he finally accepts that although all three people seem real, they are in fact part of his hallucinations.
- **S34**：Against Dr.
- **S35**：Rosen's advice, Nash decides not to restart his medication, believing that he can deal with his symptoms in another way.
- **S36**：Alicia decides to stay and support him in this.
- **S37**：Nash approaches his old friend and rival, Martin Hansen, now head of the Princeton mathematics department, who grants him permission to work out of the library and audit classes.
- **S38**：Years pass and as Nash grows older he learns to ignore his hallucinations.
- **S39**：Eventually he earns the privilege of teaching again.
- **S40**：In 1994, Nash is honored by his fellow professors for his achievement in mathematics, and goes on to win the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economics for his revolutionary work on game theory.
- **S41**：The movie ends as Nash and Alicia leave the auditorium in Stockholm; Nash sees Charles, Marcee, and Parcher standing to one side and watching him.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 2002, five years after Men in Black, Agent J  is now the top operative for the MIB, the self-funded New York City-based agency that secretly monitors and regulates extraterrestrials' activity on Earth.
- **S2**：J is largely without a partner, after Agent L  decided to return to her former life working in a morgue.
- **S3**：Since then, subsequent partners have not lived up to J's professional standards.
- **S4**：While investigating a seemingly routine crime at a SoHo pizzeria, J uncovers a diabolical plot by Serleena, a shapeshifting Kylothian Queen who has arrived to Earth after destroying several planets and disguises hisself as a lingerie model , but resembles a plant-like hydra in her own form.
- **S5**：The neuralyzed Agent K  has assumed civilian life as Kevin Brown, postmaster of Truro in Massachusetts, and to stop her, J must convince Kevin to rejoin MIB, because he is the only operative still alive who knows how to find what Serleena wants, the "Light of Zartha".
- **S6**：It also turned out that the planets that Serleena destroyed were the previous places she looked for the Light.
- **S7**：While J tries to deneuralyze Kevin, Serleena breaks into MIB resulting in a lock down; J and Kevin escape after being flushed from the building.
- **S8**：J then takes Kevin to Jack Jeebs , who built an unofficial deneuralyzer.
- **S9**：Although K eventually regains some of his memory, he still has no recollection of the "Light of Zartha"  but left himself a series of clues in case he needed to remember.
- **S10**：The clues eventually lead to a videostore where they watch a tape that jars K's memory: 25 years ago, the Zarthan Queen Laurana arrived on Earth to try to hide the Light of Zartha, but the MIB refused to help due to their neutrality.
- **S11**：Serleena arrived to steal the Light, but K activated the Zarthan ship and sent it away.
- **S12**：Serleena, believing the Light is on board the ship, chased the ship, but not before fatally shooting Laurana.
- **S13**：K then reveals that the ship was a decoy, and that the Light is still hidden on Earth.
- **S14**：Meanwhile, Serleena frees all of the MIB's high-security prisoners and uses them as henchmen.
- **S15**：Believing that the Light is in the bracelet worn by Laura Vasquez , a waitress at the pizzeria, Serleena kidnaps Laura and prepares to send her back to her homeworld.
- **S16**：K and J, with the help of the "worm guys", assault MIB headquarters, defeat Serleena's henchmen, and rescue Laura.
- **S17**：However, K warns them that if the Light is not taken off Earth and returned to Zartha, it will explode and destroy the planet.
- **S18**：As they make their way to the departure point by flying in J's car that can turn into a ship, Serleena gives chase but is eaten by Jeff, a giant alien worm living in the New York subway system.
- **S19**：When they reach the departure point, K reveals that Laura is Laurana's daughter and the actual Light of Zartha .
- **S20**：To save Earth and Zartha, Laura reluctantly leaves Earth, much to J's dismay, as he had developed feelings for her.
- **S21**：Serleena returns to try to capture Laura again, having apparently assimilated Jeff into herself before killing him.
- **S22**：Fortunately, K and J manage to destroy her for good, allowing Laura to escape to her homeworld.
- **S23**：To cover up the chaos caused by Serleena's rampage, K activates a giant neuralyzer hidden in the Statue of Liberty's torch.
- **S24**：Now that Laura is gone, K and Agent Zed , the head of MIB, try to console J for his loss, but he answers that he needs no consolation and had accepted her departure without much sorrow.
- **S25**：To provide a measure of comfort, K puts the aliens that were found in a Grand Central Terminal locker  into J's locker.
- **S26**：After J suggests showing those aliens that their world is bigger than a locker, K shows him and Frank  that they themselves are a species kept in a locker in an alien station, in contrast to their rookie worldview.

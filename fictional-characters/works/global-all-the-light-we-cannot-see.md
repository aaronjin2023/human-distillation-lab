## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] All the Light We Cannot See is structured nonlinearly; sections taking place during the Battle of Saint-Malo in August 1944 are interspersed with sections about earlier times starting in 1934 and going in chronological order.
- **S3**：Additionally, the point of view alternates between Marie-Laure LeBlanc and Werner Pfennig between chapters. [ 1 ] For simplicity, their respective narratives preceding the Battle of Saint-Malo along with the events during and after the battle have dedicated sub-sections.
- **S4**：Marie-Laure LeBlanc [ edit ] Marie-Laure LeBlanc is a girl who lives in Paris with her father Daniel, the master locksmith at the Museum of Natural History .
- **S5**：She went blind at the age of six in 1934.
- **S6**：Since then, Daniel has helped her adapt to her blindness by creating a model of Paris for her to feel and training her to navigate it.
- **S7**：Marie-Laure hears stories about a diamond known as the Sea of Flames that is hidden within the museum; the diamond is said to grant immortality at the cost of endless misfortune to those around the owner.
- **S8**：The only way to end the curse is to return the stone to the ocean, its rightful owner.
- **S9**：When Nazi Germany invades France in 1940 , Marie-Laure and Daniel flee to the coastal town of Saint-Malo to take refuge with her great-uncle Etienne, a reclusive, shell-shocked veteran of World War I who spends his time broadcasting educational audio recordings, produced by his deceased brother, across Europe.
- **S10**：Unknown to Marie-Laure, the museum has entrusted her father with either the Sea of Flames or one of three exact copies that were made to protect the original gem.
- **S11**：Months later, while building a model of Saint-Malo for Marie-Laure, Daniel is arrested and suspected of conspiracy.
- **S12**：Other than a few letters he manages to get out of prison, he is not heard from again, leaving Marie-Laure alone with Etienne and his longtime maid and housekeeper, Madame Manec.
- **S13**：Madame Manec participates in the French Resistance along with other local women.
- **S14**：Their activities have some success, but Madame Manec becomes ill and dies.
- **S15**：Marie-Laure and Etienne continue Madame Manec's efforts over the next few years, transmitting top-secret messages by radio transmitter.
- **S16**：Eventually, while Marie-Laure is going home to deliver a routine Resistance message from the bakery, she is visited by Sergeant Major Reinhold von Rumpel, a Nazi gemologist who is searching for the Sea of Flames and has tracked the real one to Saint-Malo.
- **S17**：Von Rumpel, who is dying of cancer, believes the diamond (according to the myth) will save him.
- **S18**：Von Rumpel asks the frightened Marie-Laure if her father left her anything and leaves when she says "just a dumb model".
- **S19**：Etienne takes over Marie-Laure's role of message-deliverer, and she later opens the model of Etienne's house on the Saint-Malo model and finds the Sea of Flames.
- **S20**：Etienne is eventually arrested and sent to Fort National on false charges of terrorism.
- **S21**：Werner Pfennig [ edit ] In Nazi Germany , Werner Pfennig is an orphan in the coal-mining town of Zollverein .
- **S22**：He is exceptionally bright and has a natural skill for building and repairing radios.
- **S23**：He discovers this skill in 1934 at the age of eight after he finds a broken radio with his sister Jutta, fixes it, and uses it to hear science and music programs transmitted across Europe.
- **S24**：In 1940, Werner's skill earns him a place at the National Political Institute of Education at Schulpforta , a draconian state boarding school teaching Nazi values, which Jutta hates.
- **S25**：She has been listening to French radio broadcasts about Germany's war crimes during its invasion and is angered by Werner's accepting a place at Schulpforta.
- **S26**：Before leaving for Schulpforta, Werner promises Jutta he will return to Zollverein in two years to fly away with her on an airplane.
- **S27**：In Schulpforta, Werner begins working on radio technology alongside Frank Volkheimer—a strong student perceived by the other students as the ideal Nazi—under the supervision of Schulpforta professor Dr.
- **S28**：Hauptmann.
- **S29**：Volkheimer eventually leaves to join the military.
- **S30**：Werner befriends Frederick, a kind-hearted and inattentive student who is deemed weak by the school.
- **S31**：After enduring bullying, Frederick suffers a severe beating at the hands of his fellow students, leaving him with permanent brain damage and forcing his return to his home in Berlin.
- **S32**：Two years after entering, when Werner asks to leave Schulpforta, Dr.
- **S33**：Hauptmann lies about Werner's age and persuades Nazi officials to send him to the military.
- **S34**：Werner is placed in a Wehrmacht squad led by Volkheimer that consists of engineer Walter Bernd and two other soldiers, both named Neumann.
- **S35**：The squad travels throughout Europe, tracking illegal enemy signals and executing whoever is producing them.
- **S36**：During this time, Werner also contracts an illness, a fever, from which he never fully recovers.
- **S37**：Werner becomes increasingly disillusioned with his position, especially after his group kills an innocent young girl after he incorrectly traces a signal.
- **S38**：When the squad reaches Saint-Malo, Werner traces Etienne's signal.
- **S39**：Recognizing the voice and music to be from the science programs he listened to at the orphanage, he does not disclose the signal.
- **S40**：Werner tracks it to Etienne's house and becomes entranced by Marie-Laure when he sees her heading to the bakery.
- **S41**：Werner decides to conceal the signal's existence from the rest of the squad.
- **S42**：Battle of Saint-Malo and aftermath [ edit ] Much of All the Light We Cannot See takes place in Saint-Malo (pictured in 2015).
- **S43**：When the United States forces lay siege to Saint-Malo in August 1944, Marie-Laure grabs the Sea of Flames and hides in the cellar.
- **S44**：After waking the next day, Marie-Laure leaves the cellar to get some water.
- **S45**：When von Rumpel enters the house, looking for the Sea of Flames, Marie-Laure hides in the attic for several days.
- **S46**：Using Etienne's transmitter, she tries to call for help by transmitting herself reading a braille version of the science fiction novel Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas alongside pleas for rescue.
- **S47**：During this, an ill and increasingly desperate von Rumpel unsuccessfully searches the house for several days after discovering the Sea of Flames is no longer inside the Saint-Malo model.
- **S48**：Meanwhile, Werner, Volkheimer, and Bernd become trapped beneath a pile of rubble in a cellar after the U.S. forces bomb the hotel in which they have been staying.
- **S49**：Bernd is wounded in the explosion and dies.
- **S50**：Werner mends a radio in an attempt to find help and discovers Marie-Laure's broadcasts.
- **S51**：Several days later, Volkheimer realizes they could die soon and has Werner blow up the rubble with a grenade.
- **S52**：After they escape, Werner goes to Etienne's house to rescue Marie-Laure.
- **S53**：He finds von Rumpel, who has become delirious after failing to find the Sea of Flames.
- **S54**：After a brief standoff, Werner shoots and kills von Rumpel and meets Marie-Laure.
- **S55**：As they flee from Saint-Malo, Marie-Laure places the Sea of Flames inside a gated grotto flooded with seawater to return the gem to the sea.
- **S56**：She gives the key to the gate to Werner, who sends her away to safety.
- **S57**：Werner is captured and sent to a US prisoner-of-war camp , where he, after never fully recovering from his initial fever, becomes gravely ill.
- **S58**：One night, in a fit of delirium, Werner leaves the hospital tent and accidentally steps on a German landmine , which kills him instantly.
- **S59**：Etienne is freed from Fort National and reunites with Marie-Laure.
- **S60**：They never discover what became of Daniel, her father.
- **S61**：Thirty years later, Volkheimer receives Werner's belongings at the time of his death, including the model house that contained the Sea of Flames.
- **S62**：Volkheimer finds Jutta, who is now a married math teacher, and gives her the items.
- **S63**：He tells her that Werner was last seen in France and may have been in love.
- **S64**：Jutta travels to France with her son Max to investigate the model and meets Marie-Laure in Paris; she is now working as a marine biologist at the Museum of Natural History.
- **S65**：Marie-Laure opens the model and finds the key to the grotto.
- **S66**：She wonders where the gem ended up; whether it stayed in the ocean or whether Werner took it with him.
- **S67**：The story ends in 2014 with Marie-Laure, now eighty-six years old, walking with her grandson Michel in the streets of Paris.

### Tony Stark

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a56482f4cd126c0d` · 独立版本：`profile-bcc7c6f576c2a841`

- **性格倾向**：面对核弹危机时，他表现出敢于亲自承担直接风险的倾向。（行为推断；S28、S30）
- **核心动机**：在核弹来袭时，他试图解除城市危机并打击入侵舰队。（行为推断；S27、S28、S29）
- **价值取舍**：面对城市遭核击的危险，他选择冒险介入保护居民。（行为推断；S25、S27、S28）
- **决策方式**：处理核弹危机时，他倾向利用现有传送门反击敌方舰队。（行为推断；S28、S29）

### Loki

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-09736166f0b5e83e` · 独立版本：`profile-7203b2119de4a65a`

- **性格倾向**：在实施入侵计划时，他表现出以强制操控他人达成目的的倾向。（行为推断；S7、S23）
- **核心动机**：他借助外星军队发动入侵，意图征服并统治地球。（行为推断；S2、S23）
- **价值取舍**：在夺取力量与发动入侵时，他把征服目标置于他人自由之上。（行为推断；S7、S23）
- **决策方式**：推进计划时，他结合受控人员的协助与传送技术引入军队。（行为推断；S7、S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

- **S5**：Agent Phil Coulson explains that the object has begun radiating an unusual form of energy.
- **S6**：The Tesseract suddenly activates and opens a portal, allowing Loki to reach Earth.
- **S7**：Loki takes the Tesseract and uses his scepter to enslave Selvig and several agents, including Clint Barton, to aid him in his getaway.
- **S8**：In response to the attack, Fury reactivates the "Avengers Initiative".
- **S9**：Agent Natasha Romanoff is sent to Calcutta to recruit Dr.
- **S10**：Bruce Banner to trace the Tesseract through its gamma radiation emissions.
- **S11**：Coulson visits Tony Stark to have him review Selvig's research, and Fury approaches Steve Rogers with an assignment to retrieve the Tesseract.
- **S12**：While Barton steals iridium needed to stabilize the Tesseract's power, Loki causes a distraction in Stuttgart leading to a confrontation with Rogers, Stark, and Romanoff that ends with Loki's surrender.
- **S13**：While Loki is being escorted to S.H.I.E.L.D., Thor, his adoptive brother, arrives and frees him hoping to convince him to abandon his plan and return to Asgard.
- **S14**：After a confrontation with Stark and Rogers, Thor agrees to take Loki to S.H.I.E.L.D.'s flying aircraft carrier, the Helicarrier.
- **S15**：There Loki is imprisoned while scientists Banner and Stark attempt to locate the Tesseract.
- **S16**：The Avengers become divided, both over how to approach Loki and the revelation that S.H.I.E.L.D. plans to harness the Tesseract to develop weapons as a deterrent against hostile extra-terrestrials.
- **S17**：As the group argues, Barton and Loki's other possessed agents attack the Helicarrier, disabling its engines in flight and causing Banner to transform into the Hulk.
- **S18**：Stark and Rogers try to restart the damaged engines, and Thor attempts to stop the Hulk's rampage.
- **S19**：Romanoff fights Barton, and knocks him unconscious, breaking Loki's mind control.
- **S20**：Loki escapes after killing Coulson and ejecting Thor from the airship, while the Hulk falls to the ground after attacking a S.H.I.E.L.D. fighter jet.
- **S21**：Fury uses Coulson's death to motivate the Avengers into working as a team.
- **S22**：Stark and Rogers realize that simply defeating them will not be enough for Loki; he needs to overpower them publicly to validate himself as ruler of Earth.
- **S23**：Loki uses the Tesseract in conjunction with a device Selvig built, to open a portal above Stark Tower to the Chitauri fleet in space, launching his invasion.
- **S24**：The Avengers rally in defense of New York City, the location of the portal, but quickly realize they will be overwhelmed as wave after wave of Chitauri descend upon Earth.
- **S25**：Rogers, Stark, and Thor evacuate civilians with assistance from Barton, while Banner transforms back into the Hulk and goes after Loki, eventually beating him into submission.
- **S26**：Romanoff makes her way to the portal, where Selvig, freed of Loki’s control, reveals that Loki's scepter can be used to close the portal.
- **S27**：Meanwhile, Fury's superiors attempt to end the invasion by launching a nuclear missile at Manhattan.
- **S28**：Stark intercepts the missile and takes it through the portal toward the Chitauri fleet.
- **S29**：The missile detonates, destroying the invaders' lead ship which disables their forces on Earth.
- **S30**：Stark's suit runs out of power and he falls back through the portal, but the Hulk saves him from crashing to the ground.
- **S31**：Romanoff deactivates the portal to prevent further invasion.
- **S32**：In the aftermath, Thor returns Loki and the Tesseract to Asgard.
- **S33**：Fury expresses confidence that the Avengers will return if and when they are needed.
- **S34**：In the first of two post-credits scenes, the Other confers with his master{{ref}} about the attack on Earth and humanity's resistance; in the second, the Avengers eat in silence at a shawarma restaurant.

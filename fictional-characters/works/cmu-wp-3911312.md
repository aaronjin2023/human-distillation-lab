## 角色资料

### Nicholas Van Orton

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-73f2a8bf09f882f8` · 独立版本：`profile-d53ed6e509471322`

- **性格倾向**：在失控情境中倾向戒备，难以接受他人提供的解释（行为推断；S6、S22、S33、S34）
- **核心动机**：面对层层威胁，他急于查明幕后操纵者并恢复控制（行为推断；S13、S28、S29）
- **情绪反应**：他仍受目睹父亲自杀的经历困扰，并在误伤弟弟后陷入悲痛（摘要直接叙述；S3、S35、S36）
- **决策方式**：常规求助失效后，他倾向以武力强迫相关人员配合（行为推断；S13、S26、S28、S29）
- **成长变化**：他在危机中承认对前妻的疏失，真相揭晓后又主动亲近他人（行为推断；S27、S40、S42、S44、S45）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：追求自我掌控，却越来越依赖真假难辨的他人协助；渴望安全，却以持枪对峙加剧自身与他人的危险

### Christine

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-897c0dba364cf0d5` · 独立版本：`profile-221c4e53e3b358a1`

- **性格倾向**：在游戏任务中呈现较强的角色适应性，能维持受害者假象（行为推断；S12、S16、S21、S22）
- **核心动机**：她的接近与误导服务于游戏任务，结束后仍将继续执行工作（行为推断；S16、S22、S45）
- **决策方式**：在任务中倾向借信息与信任引导对方，而非直接说明安排（行为推断；S17、S21、S22）
- **情绪反应**：发现对方持有真枪后，她陷入恐惧并急切揭露游戏真相（摘要直接叙述；S32、S33）
- **关系模式**：游戏结束后，她愿意与尼古拉斯在工作之外单独相处（摘要直接叙述；S44、S45）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：维持虚构任务的职责，与真枪出现后的安全需要发生冲突

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Nicholas Van Orton  is a successful and extremely wealthy investment banker, but his success has come at the cost of his personal life.
- **S2**：He is estranged from both his ex-wife and his only brother.
- **S3**：He remains haunted from having seen his father commit suicide on the latter's 48th birthday.
- **S4**：On his own 48th birthday, Conrad , Nicholas' rebellious younger brother, presents Nicholas with an unusual gift—a voucher for a "game" offered by a company called Consumer Recreation Services .
- **S5**：Conrad promises that it will change Nick's life.
- **S6**：Nicholas has doubts about the gift and delays calling CRS.
- **S7**：A chance encounter with fellow club members who enjoyed the Game changes Nicholas' mind.
- **S8**：He goes to the organization's offices to apply and is irritated by the lengthy and time-consuming series of psychological and physical examinations required.
- **S9**：He is later informed that his application has been rejected.
- **S10**：This is a ruse however: the Game has already begun.
- **S11**：Starting with the merely invasive and rapidly escalating to the potentially criminal, Nicholas believes that his business, reputation, finances, and safety are at risk.
- **S12**：He encounters a waitress, Christine , who appears to have been caught up in the game and also comes under risk.
- **S13**：Nicholas contacts the police to investigate CRS, but they find the offices abandoned.
- **S14**：Eventually, Conrad appears to Nicholas and apologizes for the Game claiming that he too has come under attack by CRS.
- **S15**：With no one else to turn to, Nicholas finds Christine's home.
- **S16**：He soon discovers that she is a CRS employee and that her apartment has simply been staged to look like a real apartment.
- **S17**：Christine tells Nicholas that they are being watched.
- **S18**：Nicholas attacks a camera, and armed CRS troops begin to swarm the house and fire upon them.
- **S19**：Nicholas and Christine are forced to flee.
- **S20**：Nicholas realizes that CRS has drained his financial accounts, and he is now broke.
- **S21**：Christine tells him that some of his closest associates are part of the Game.
- **S22**：Just as he begins to trust Christine, he realizes she has drugged him, and he falls unconscious.
- **S23**：Nicholas wakes up to find himself entombed in a cemetery in Mexico.
- **S24**：He is forced to sell his gold watch to return to the United States.
- **S25**：Upon his return, he finds that his mansion has been ransacked and vandalized by CRS.
- **S26**：He retrieves a hidden gun, and seeks the aid of his estranged wife.
- **S27**：While talking with her and apologizing for his neglect and mistreatment of her, he discovers that Jim Feingold , the CRS employee who had conducted his psychological test, is an actor who works in television advertisements.
- **S28**：He locates Feingold and forces him to take Nicholas to CRS, where he takes Christine hostage.
- **S29**：He demands to be taken to the leader of the organization.
- **S30**：Attacked by CRS troops, Nicholas takes Christine to the roof and bars the door behind them.
- **S31**：The CRS troops begin cutting through the door.
- **S32**：Christine realizes that Nicholas' gun is not a prop and is terrified.
- **S33**：She frantically tells Nicholas that the conspiracy is a hoax, a fiction that is just part of the Game, that his finances are intact and that his family and friends are waiting on the other side of the door.
- **S34**：He refuses to believe her.
- **S35**：The door bursts open, and Nicholas shoots the first person to emerge: his brother Conrad, bearing an open bottle of champagne.
- **S36**：Distraught, Nicholas leaps off the roof, just as his late father did.
- **S37**：Nicholas' life passes before his eyes as he falls.
- **S38**：He smashes through a glass roof and lands on a giant air bag.
- **S39**：Emergency medical technicians carefully remove him, warning him to keep his eyes closed until they remove the fragments of breakaway glass.
- **S40**：Nicholas finds he is in a ballroom full of his friends, family, and every figure involved in his Game; it had been just a game all along.
- **S41**：Conrad is alive and well, and explains that he initiated the game to get his brother to embrace life and not end up like their father.
- **S42**：Nicholas breaks into tears, relaxes, and begins to enjoy the party once his shock has dissipated.
- **S43**：Later, Nicholas splits the bill for the game with Conrad .
- **S44**：When he sees that Christine has left the party, he follows her outside to her cab.
- **S45**：He asks her to dinner, and she offers to enjoy a private coffee with him now before her flight takes her to her next Game assignment.

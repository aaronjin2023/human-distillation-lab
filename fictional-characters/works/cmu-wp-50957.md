### Anakin Skywalker

- **性格倾向**：在母亲死亡的情境中，他被强烈愤怒牵引而失去克制。（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **核心动机**：他欣喜于陪伴帕德梅的机会，两人在相处中相爱。（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **决策方式**：母亲受苦的预感令他不安，他违令与帕德梅前往塔图因。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **关系模式**：他是欧比旺的徒弟，并与帕德梅相爱后秘密成婚。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S9、S34）

### Obi-Wan Kenobi

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The Galactic Republic is in turmoil.
- **S2**：Following the invasion of Naboo ten years earlier, the former Jedi Knight Count Dooku  has now organized a Separatist movement against the Republic.
- **S3**：The Galactic Senate debates a plan to create an army for the Republic to assist the Jedi against the Separatist threat.
- **S4**：Senator Padmé Amidala, the former queen of Naboo and now a politician, returns to Coruscant to cast her vote.
- **S5**：Upon her arrival, she narrowly escapes an assassination attempt, and Supreme Chancellor Palpatine assigns Obi-Wan Kenobi and his apprentice Anakin Skywalker  to protect her.
- **S6**：Obi-Wan and Anakin subdue the assassin, Zam Wessell, during another assassination attempt.
- **S7**：However, before she can give them any information, the assassin is killed by her employer using a poisonous dart.
- **S8**：Obi-Wan learns that the dart was manufactured on the remote ocean planet of Kamino, and is assigned to investigate, while Anakin is assigned to escort Padmé back to her home planet of Naboo.
- **S9**：Anakin relishes the opportunity to spend time with her, and they fall in love.
- **S10**：In investigating Kamino, Obi-Wan discovers that it has been removed from the navigation maps of the Jedi archives.
- **S11**：Yoda reveals that such a thing could only have been done by a Jedi, suggesting that a conspiracy is afoot.
- **S12**：Obi-Wan heads to Kamino, where he discovers that an army of clone troopers is being secretly produced for the Republic, using a bounty hunter named Jango Fett  as their genetic template.
- **S13**：Realizing that Jango is the assassin's employer, Obi-Wan tracks Jango and his son Boba  to Geonosis, a rocky planet where a new droid army is being created.
- **S14**：Meanwhile, on Naboo, Anakin becomes troubled by premonitions of his mother Shmi in pain.
- **S15**：Despite Obi-Wan's orders to remain on Naboo, Anakin and Padmé go to Tatooine.
- **S16**：After meeting his new stepfather, Cliegg Lars ([[Jack Thompson , and stepbrother Owen Lars , Anakin learns Shmi had been kidnapped by Tusken Raiders some weeks earlier.
- **S17**：Anakin heads out alone to rescue Shmi, but the Jedi snaps after witnessing the death of his mother at the hands of the Tuskens.
- **S18**：He kills the tribe in a rage and buries his mother's body at the Lars homestead.
- **S19**：Having learned that Count Dooku authorized the assassination attempt on Padmé and that the Separatists are developing a new battle droid army, Obi-Wan relays this information via hologram to Anakin, who transmits it to the Jedi Council.
- **S20**：However, Obi-Wan is captured by Dooku mid-transmission.
- **S21**：Dooku unsuccessfully attempts to make an ally of Obi-Wan, and reveals to him that the Republic is being secretly controlled by Darth Sidious.
- **S22**：While Anakin and Padmé head to Geonosis to rescue Obi-Wan, Chancellor Palpatine is granted emergency powers to organize the clone army and send them into battle.
- **S23**：Shortly after arriving on Geonosis, Anakin and Padmé are captured by Jango and sentenced to be executed along with Obi-Wan.
- **S24**：Anakin and Padmé profess their love before being led into a large gladiatorial arena where they and Obi-Wan are pitted against gigantic beasts in a public execution.
- **S25**：However, Mace Windu  and a team of Jedi appear and lead a strong defense against the beasts and the Separatists.
- **S26**：Windu kills Jango Fett in a brief battle, but then the Jedi find themselves outnumbered by the Separatists' droid army.
- **S27**：As Dooku demands their surrender, Yoda arrives with the clone army and rescues the survivors of the battle.
- **S28**：After the arena is emptyed, Boba recovers the decapitated head of his father, and swears revenge for him.
- **S29**：As a large battle erupts between the Republic's clone army and the Separatists' droid forces, Obi-Wan and Anakin engage Dooku in a lightsaber duel.
- **S30**：Dooku defeats them both, injuring Obi-Wan and severing Anakin's right arm, but then Yoda arrives to challenge the Count.
- **S31**：Unable to defeat Yoda, Dooku flees in his ship to Coruscant, taking the blueprints for an ultimate weapon with him.
- **S32**：He is met in an isolated hangar by his Sith master, Darth Sidious, who states that everything is going as planned now that the galaxy is at war.
- **S33**：As the Jedi gravely acknowledge the start of the Clone Wars, Palpatine oversees the launching of several battalions of clone troopers.
- **S34**：Meanwhile, Anakin– now fitted with an artificial arm– and Padmé secretly marry on Naboo, with C-3PO and R2-D2 as witnesses.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Narrated by Death , the story follows the young girl Liesel Meminger, living with foster parents Hans and Rosa Hubermann in Nazi Germany during World War II.
- **S3**：Liesel steals her first book after the death of her brother, in the graveyard where he was buried.
- **S4**：Hans develops a close relationship with her, helping her learn to read and write.
- **S5**：Recognizing the power of writing and sharing literature, Liesel steals more books, some alongside her best friend, Rudy.
- **S6**：While adapting to her new home, Liesel is exposed to the horrors of the war and politics.
- **S7**：As the political situation in Germany deteriorates, her foster parents conceal a Jewish man named Max Vandenburg.
- **S8**：Liesel trades stories with Max in the basement, with the two finding a common interest in words.
- **S9**：When she is collecting laundry for her mother, she forms a relationship with the mayor's wife Ilsa, who allows her to read books in her library.
- **S10**：Hans brings suspicion on his household that he might be a sympathizer with political adversaries of Germany.
- **S11**：Max leaves the Hubermanns' home soon after out of fear that the suspicion could endanger him or the family.
- **S12**：Liesel receives a gift of blank paper from Ilsa and begins writing her own story.
- **S13**：Bombs fall on Liesel's street, killing her friends, family and neighbors.
- **S14**：Liesel is the sole survivor because she was working on her manuscript in the basement at the time of the raid.
- **S15**：Many years later (what Death calls "just yesterday") Liesel dies from old age in the suburbs of Sydney, Australia with a family and many friends.
- **S16**：She has never forgotten Hans, Rosa, Max, Rudy or her brother.
- **S17**：When Death collects Liesel's soul, he gives her the manuscript she lost in the bombing.
- **S18**：She asks him if he read it and understood it, but Death is unable to understand the duality of humanity.
- **S19**：Death's last words for Liesel (and possibly the reader) are "I am haunted by humans."
- **S20**：## Characters
- **S21**：[ edit ] Death Death , the collector of souls, narrates the story of a young girl during the horrific times of Nazi Germany and the Second World War.
- **S22**：To the reader, Death insists that things "most definitely can be cheerful" while relating that they most certainly also cannot be nice.
- **S23**：Sometimes Death is "compelled" to take action in sympathy with the human story.
- **S24**：Liesel Meminger The story's protagonist is a girl on the verge of adolescence, with German blonde hair and brown eyes.
- **S25**：The Hubermanns foster her after her biological father is taken away by the Nazis before the novel starts, due to being a Communist .
- **S26**：Her brother dies and her mother is forced to send her to a foster home to avoid Nazi persecution.
- **S27**：Liesel is the titular book thief; fascinated by the power of words, she steals several books throughout the story from a gravedigger, a bonfire, and the mayor's wife, Ilsa Hermann.
- **S28**：Hans Hubermann (Papa) Liesel's foster father and husband to Rosa, Hans was a German soldier during the First World War.
- **S29**：He is now an accordion player and painter.
- **S30**：He develops a close and loving relationship with Liesel and becomes a source of strength and support for her.
- **S31**：Like Liesel, he doesn't have much experience with reading.
- **S32**：The two help each other with reading and write all the words they learn on a wall in the basement.
- **S33**：He helps Max because Max's father saved Hans in the First World War.
- **S34**：Rosa Hubermann (Mama) Rosa is Liesel's sharp-tongued foster mother.
- **S35**：She has a "wardrobe" build and a displeased face, five feet tall, brown-grey hair often tightly tied in a bun.
- **S36**：Despite her temper, she is a loving wife to Hans and mother to Liesel.
- **S37**：To supplement the household income, she does washing and ironing for wealthier households in Molching.
- **S38**：Max Vandenburg A Jewish fist-fighter who takes refuge from the Nazi regime in the Hubermanns’ basement.
- **S39**：He is the son of a First World War German soldier who fought alongside Hans Hubermann, and the two developed a close friendship during the war.
- **S40**：He has brown, feather-like hair and swampy brown eyes.
- **S41**：During the Nazi reign of terror, Hans agrees to shelter Max and hide him from the Nazi party.
- **S42**：During his stay at the Hubermanns' house, Max befriends Liesel, and they share an affinity for words.
- **S43**：He writes two books for her and presents her with a sketchbook that contains his life story, which helps Liesel to develop as a writer and reader, which, in turn, saves her life from the bombs falling on her. [ 1 ] Rudy Steiner Liesel's neighbor Rudy has bony legs, blue eyes, lemon-colored hair, and a penchant for getting in the middle of situations when he shouldn't.
- **S44**：Despite having the appearance of an archetypal German, he does not support the Nazis.
- **S45**：As a relatively poor household member with six children, Rudy is habitually hungry.
- **S46**：He is known throughout the neighborhood because of the " Jesse Owens incident" in which he colored himself black with charcoal one night and ran one hundred meters at the local sports field.
- **S47**：He is academically and athletically gifted which attracts the attention of Nazi Party officials, leading to attempted recruitment.
- **S48**：His lack of support for the Nazi party becomes problematic as the story progresses.
- **S49**：Rudy becomes Liesel's best friend and later falls in love with her.
- **S50**：Ilsa Hermann The wife of the mayor

### Odd

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] After leaving the monastery in the previous book, Odd found a place to stay in Magic Beach with a retired actor, Lawrence Hutchison, Hutch to his friends.
- **S3**：While out for a walk one morning, he finds a woman whom he had been seeing in his dreams; a young, pregnant woman who calls herself Annamaria.
- **S4**：After being assaulted and nearly killed by a large man with two henchmen in tow, Odd is separated from Annamaria, though he uses his psychic magnetism to find her.
- **S5**：Once he finds her they decide they need to leave immediately, but while making preparations to do so they hear a car door slam.
- **S6**：They manage to find a spot to hide until after the men who had been chasing them leave.
- **S7**：With the men now gone, Odd and Annamaria set out walking.
- **S8**：On their walk, they encounter a large pack of coyotes that Annamaria somehow persuades to leave.
- **S9**：After leaving Annamaria with a trusted friend, Odd tries to find the man that assaulted him on the beach, only to find him and an unknown woman dead.
- **S10**：As he flees police, he encounters a new companion in a Golden Retriever that accompanies him to a local church, where he is subsequently turned over to the sheriff of Magic Beach, but not before he hides his ID in a church pew.
- **S11**：The sheriff, a man who seems to have many personalities, believes Odd is a government agent who has come to spy on his operation: the delivery and shipment of multiple nuclear weapons to terrorist groups inside the US via the Magic Beach harbor.
- **S12**：Odd manages to convince the Sheriff that he is a government experiment gone wrong and that he is willing to be bribed in order to look the other way.
- **S13**：While the sheriff is setting up a transaction to buy his loyalty Odd manages to enrage the spirit of Frank Sinatra , who began accompanying him after the departure of Elvis.
- **S14**：The rage caused by his spirit creates a violent whirlwind, and in the confusion Odd is able to escape from the police department.
- **S15**：He is able to make his way down to the harbor with the help of a stranger, Birdie Hopkins, who said she felt the urge to go driving and happened upon him.
- **S16**：While driving him to the harbor she also insists he take her gun because she believes he will need it.
- **S17**：Odd is able to sneak aboard the craft that is carrying the nukes.
- **S18**：He is able to corner one of the men in the radio room and get information from him before being forced to kill him.
- **S19**：Odd is able to dispatch the remaining crew, including the large man from the pier, in order to secure the ship and its dangerous cargo.
- **S20**：He manages to run the boat aground in a nearby cove ensuring that the Coast Guard will respond to the boat going off course and find the nukes.
- **S21**：He later calls the FBI from a pay phone relaying the story and asking them to follow up with the Coast Guard for validation.
- **S22**：Odd returns to the church to retrieve his ID, but is surprised by the sheriff when leaving.
- **S23**：While attempting to escape, he runs into the priest and his wife.
- **S24**：Odd learns that both the priest and his wife were also involved with the plan to sell nuclear weapons through Magic Beach.
- **S25**：The priest kills his wife and is then killed by the sheriff, who is in turn killed by one of his henchmen.
- **S26**：The henchman, identified primarily as 'Meth Mouth' talks to Odd, still believing him to be a psychic government agent.
- **S27**：While laughing over a joke of Meth Mouth's, Odd shoots him under the table with the wife's gun.
- **S28**：Odd then takes a moment to contact the Department of Homeland Security to advise them where they could find the triggers he removed from the nuclear devices.
- **S29**：The story ends with Odd and Annamaria, along with the golden retriever now named Raphael, leaving Magic Beach in a car loaned to them by Hutch, flush with money he insisted Odd take.
- **S30**：Odd is sobbing over the murder of so many people, almost all at his hands.
- **S31**：Annamaria comforts him with the knowledge that while he may have ended a few lives, he saved millions more.
- **S32**：She then pulls the car over and asks Odd to show her the constellation Cassiopeia.
- **S33**：Odd and Stormy would often point out Cassiopeia together as that was Stormy's mother's name, so Annamaria's request startles him, but he points the constellation out to her.

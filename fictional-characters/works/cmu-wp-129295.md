## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Dan Gallagher  is a successful, happily married New York attorney living in Manhattan when he meets Alexandra "Alex" Forrest , an editor for a publishing company, through business.
- **S2**：While his wife, Beth , and daughter, Ellen , are out of town for the weekend, he has a passionate affair with Alex.
- **S3**：Though he thought it was understood to be a simple fling, she begins clinging to him.
- **S4**：Dan explains that he must go home and Alex cuts her wrists in a suicide attempt.
- **S5**：He helps her to bandage them and later leaves.
- **S6**：He thinks the affair is forgotten, but she shows up at various places to see him.
- **S7**：She waits at his office one day to apologize and invite him to the opera, but he turns her down.
- **S8**：She then continues to telephone until he tells his secretary that he will no longer take her calls.
- **S9**：She then phones his home at all hours, and then confronts him saying that she is pregnant and plans to keep the baby.
- **S10**：Although he wants nothing to do with her, she argues that he must take responsibility.
- **S11**：She shows up at his apartment  and meets Beth, feigning interest as a buyer.
- **S12**：Later that night, he goes to her apartment to confront her about her actions which results in a violent scuffle.
- **S13**：In response, she replies that she will not be ignored.
- **S14**：Dan moves his family to Bedford, but this doesn't deter Alex.
- **S15**：She has a tape recording delivered to him filled with verbal abuse.
- **S16**：She stalks him in a parking garage, pours acid on his vehicle, and follows him home one night to spy on him, Beth, and Ellen from the bushes in their yard; the sight of their family life literally makes her sick to her stomach.
- **S17**：Her obsession escalates further.
- **S18**：Dan approaches the police to apply for a restraining order against her , to which the lieutenant claims that he cannot violate her rights without probable cause and that the adulterer has to own up to his adultery.
- **S19**：At one point, while the Gallaghers are not home, Alex kills Ellen's pet rabbit, and puts it on their stove to boil.
- **S20**：After this, Dan tells Beth of the affair and Alex's pregnancy.
- **S21**：Enraged, she asks him to leave.
- **S22**：Before he goes, Dan calls Alex to tell her that Beth knows about the affair.
- **S23**：Beth gets on the phone and warns Alex that if she persists, she will kill her.
- **S24**：Without Dan and Beth's knowledge, Alex picks up Ellen at school and takes her to an amusement park, buying her ice cream as well as taking her on a roller coaster.
- **S25**：Beth panics when she realizes that she doesn't know where Ellen is.
- **S26**：She drives around searching and rear-ends a car stopped at an intersection and is slightly injured and hospitalized.
- **S27**：Alex later drops Ellen off at the Gallaghers' house, asking Ellen for a kiss on the cheek.
- **S28**：Dan barges into Alex's apartment and attacks her, choking her and not too short of strangling her.
- **S29**：He stops himself, but as he does she lunges at him with a kitchen knife.
- **S30**：He overpowers her, but puts the knife down and leaves, with Alex leaning against the kitchen counter, smiling.
- **S31**：He approaches the police about having her arrested, and they start searching for Alex to bring her in for taking Ellen.
- **S32**：Following her release from the hospital, Beth forgives Dan and they return home.
- **S33**：Beth prepares a bath for herself and Alex suddenly appears, again with the kitchen knife.
- **S34**：She starts to explain her resentment of her, nervously fidgeting  and then attacks her.
- **S35**：Dan hears the screaming and runs in, wrestles Alex into the bathtub and seemingly drowns her.
- **S36**：She suddenly emerges from the water, swinging the knife.
- **S37**：Beth, who went searching for Dan's gun, shoots her in the chest, killing her.
- **S38**：The final scene shows police cars outside the Gallaghers' house.
- **S39**：As Dan finishes talking with the cops, he walks inside, where Beth is waiting for him.
- **S40**：They embrace and proceed upstairs as the camera focuses on a picture of Dan, Beth, and Ellen.

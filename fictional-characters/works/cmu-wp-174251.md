### Frodo Baggins

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3666e547ad814e29` · 独立版本：`profile-75e689d81ab75d3c`

- **性格倾向**：获救后即使身体衰弱，他仍表现出继续艰难旅程的坚持。（行为推断；S27、S29）
- **核心动机**：抵达末日火山前，他仍以完成毁戒之旅为行动方向。（行为推断；S27、S29、S30）
- **关系模式**：受到咕噜挑拨时，他对山姆的信任动摇并将其赶走。（行为推断；S23）
- **情绪反应**：魔戒被咕噜夺走后，他愤怒地反扑并与其一同坠向边缘。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他持续走向毁戒地点，却在最后关头屈服于魔戒并拒绝摧毁它。

### Aragorn

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-684d1ff2ef9af07c` · 独立版本：`profile-bd2b493992cf49ce`

- **性格倾向**：面对兵力劣势时，他表现出亲自承担危险领导行动的倾向。（行为推断；S10、S11、S28）
- **核心动机**：率军前往黑门时，他以牵制敌军为弗罗多争取机会。（行为推断；S28）
- **价值取舍**：面对伊欧玟的挽留，他仍重申对阿尔温的感情承诺。（行为推断；S10）
- **决策方式**：争取亡者军队时，他倾向以可验证的继承身份建立号召力。（行为推断；S10、S11）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Gandalf, Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli, Théoden, Gamling, and Éomer meet up with Merry, Pippin and Treebeard at Isengard.
- **S2**：The group returns to Edoras, where Pippin looks into Saruman's recovered palantír, in which Sauron appears and invades his mind; Pippin tells him nothing regarding Frodo and the Ring.
- **S3**：From this event, Gandalf deduces that Sauron is planning to attack Minas Tirith.
- **S4**：Gandalf rides with Pippin to find Denethor, the Steward of Gondor, to whom Pippin swears his service.
- **S5**：Gandalf urges Denethor to call Rohan for aid, but Denethor declines, fearing Aragorn and Gandalf plan to depose him.
- **S6**：The Morgul army, led by the Nazgûl, drives the Gondorians out of Osgiliath.
- **S7**：Denethor sends his son Faramir on a suicide mission to reclaim the city.
- **S8**：Pippin lights a beacon, signaling Théoden and Aragorn to assemble the Rohirrim for battle.
- **S9**：Elrond informs Aragorn that Arwen did not go to the Undying Lands, and is now dying.
- **S10**：Believing their forces to be outnumbered by Sauron's, Elrond gives Aragorn the sword Andúril to acquire the service of the Army of the Dead, who owe allegiance to the heir of Isildur. Éowyn confesses her love for Aragorn and asks him not to go, but Aragorn reaffirms his love for Arwen and heads into battle.
- **S11**：Accompanied by Legolas and Gimli, Aragorn ventures into the Paths of the Dead and gains the loyalty of the King of the Dead and his men by brandishing Andúril, proving himself the Heir of Isildur.
- **S12**：At Dunharrow, Théoden rides off to war, unaware that Éowyn and Merry have secretly joined his forces.
- **S13**：Sauron's armies lay siege to Minas Tirith, led by the Witch-king.
- **S14**：Believing a grievously wounded Faramir to be dead, Denethor tries to burn his son and himself alive, but Gandalf intervenes; he saves Faramir, but Denethor commits suicide.
- **S15**：Just as the Gondorians are about to be overrun, the Rohirrim army arrives and counter-attacks in a massive cavalry charge led by Théoden.
- **S16**：This shifts the tide of the battle, and the Orcs begin to retreat.
- **S17**：However, the Haradrim arrive and join the Orc army, turning the tide.
- **S18**：The Witch-king kills Théoden, only to be killed himself by Éowyn with help from Merry.
- **S19**：On the verge of defeat, the Rohirrim are saved when Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli arrive with the Army of the Dead and overwhelm Sauron's forces, ending the battle.
- **S20**：Aragorn frees the Army of the Dead and their souls go to the afterlife.
- **S21**：Meanwhile, Frodo, Sam and Gollum travel to Minas Morgul.
- **S22**：Sam overhears Gollum's plans to murder them and take the Ring for himself.
- **S23**：Gollum persuades Frodo that Sam wants the Ring for himself, and Frodo angrily tells Sam to go home.
- **S24**：A heartbroken Sam leaves, but discovers Gollum's treachery and follows after them.
- **S25**：Gollum betrays Frodo, leaving him in the lair of the giant spider Shelob, who paralyzes Frodo before being wounded and driven away by Sam.
- **S26**：An Orc patrol captures Frodo and takes him to Sauron's fortress.
- **S27**：Sam rescues Frodo from the tower, and they continue the journey to Mount Doom.
- **S28**：Meanwhile, Aragorn leads his remaining men to the Black Gate of Mordor, distracting Sauron and his forces and allowing Sam and Frodo to enter Mount Doom.
- **S29**：Sam carries the weakened Frodo up the volcano, but Gollum attacks them.
- **S30**：At the Crack of Doom, Frodo succumbs to the Ring's power, refusing to destroy it.
- **S31**：Gollum attacks Frodo and bites his finger off, seizing the Ring for himself.
- **S32**：An enraged Frodo attacks Gollum, and they both fall over the edge.
- **S33**：At the last second, Frodo grabs onto the ledge, leaving Gollum to fall into the lava, taking the Ring with him.
- **S34**：As the Ring melts in the volcano, Sauron is destroyed and the land of Mordor collapses, taking down most of his forces.
- **S35**：Frodo and Sam are saved from the rising lava by Eagles, led by Gandalf.
- **S36**：In the aftermath, Aragorn is crowned King, heralding a new age of peace, and is reunited with Arwen and the four hobbits are bowed by all of Gondor for their courageous efforts.
- **S37**：The four Hobbits return home to the Shire, where Sam marries his childhood sweetheart, Rosie Cotton.
- **S38**：A few years later, Frodo leaves Middle-earth for the Undying Lands with Gandalf, Bilbo, Elrond, Celeborn, and Galadriel, leaving his account of their quest to Sam.

## 引用证据

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Harry Bosch returns to the Los Angeles Police Department after spending three years away.
- **S3**：The department has undergone major changes, and Bosch is assigned to the Open-Unsolved Unit, where detectives revisit old murder cases.
- **S4**：He is paired with Kiz Rider, his former partner and one of the people who supported his return.
- **S5**：Their first case concerns Rebecca Verloren, a sixteen-year-old girl who was killed in 1988.
- **S6**：Her murder remained unsolved for almost two decades.
- **S7**：New DNA testing identifies Roland Mackey as a possible suspect.
- **S8**：Mackey has associations with white supremacist groups, raising the possibility that Rebecca was targeted because she was of mixed race.
- **S9**：Bosch and Rider review the original police files and retrace the investigation conducted years earlier.
- **S10**：They speak with former detectives and return to people who knew Rebecca and her family.
- **S11**：The old case contains gaps that may have prevented the police from identifying the killer.
- **S12**：Bosch studies witness accounts, laboratory records, and other material that may have been overlooked.
- **S13**：Rebecca's parents have spent many years living with the loss of their daughter.
- **S14**：Her father has struggled badly since her death, while her mother has kept Rebecca's bedroom almost untouched.
- **S15**：Bosch becomes increasingly aware of the human cost of an unsolved murder.
- **S16**：For him, bringing an old case to an answer means giving the victim's family an opportunity to move beyond years of uncertainty.
- **S17**：The search for answers takes Bosch and Rider into Los Angeles's white supremacist circles.
- **S18**：They examine Mackey's past and his association with people who held racist beliefs.
- **S19**：The case also brings back memories of racial divisions in Los Angeles during the years before the Rodney King beating and the unrest that followed.
- **S20**：Bosch faces opposition inside the police department as he continues his investigation.
- **S21**：Deputy Chief Irvin Irving, an old adversary, remains skeptical of Bosch and would welcome an opportunity to discredit him.
- **S22**：Bosch therefore has to balance police procedures with his determination to pursue every reasonable avenue.
- **S23**：As the case progresses, Bosch finds evidence that changes his understanding of what happened to Rebecca.
- **S24**：The investigation shows how an old murder can remain unresolved when important clues are missed or not pursued.
- **S25**：Bosch and Rider continue working through the available evidence until they can establish what happened on the night Rebecca died and identify the person responsible.
- **S26**：The case gives Bosch a difficult return to police service.
- **S27**：It also reinforces his commitment to victims whose cases have been forgotten.
- **S28**：By revisiting Rebecca's murder years after her death, he demonstrates that time does not necessarily erase a family's need for answers.
- **S29**：The search brings long-delayed answers to a case that had remained open for almost twenty years.

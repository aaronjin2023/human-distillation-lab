## 角色资料

### George Smiley

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-fd0c0cc5acad113a` · 独立版本：`profile-3fc49d9f404ae64d`

- **性格倾向**：在机构权威与情报光环面前，他表现出审慎怀疑并逐项核查的倾向（行为推断；S7、S11、S27、S33）
- **核心动机**：他受命查明情报机构高层是否存在长期潜伏的内奸（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **决策方式**：他通过交叉访谈、查验记录和设置诱饵逐步收紧调查（摘要直接叙述；S11、S27、S38、S40）
- **价值取舍**：在获取关键地点时，他接受以遣返威胁突破对方防线（行为推断；S37）
- **关系模式**：妻子与Haydon的关系被敌手利用，成为针对其判断的干扰工具（摘要直接叙述；S16、S41）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Bill Haydon

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0de37fde6bed36ea` · 独立版本：`profile-71b39a84f68e9f1a`

- **性格倾向**：在维持内奸身份时，他表现出善于利用信任掩饰背叛的倾向（行为推断；S39、S40、S41、S42、S43）
- **核心动机**：当Tarr声称掌握内奸姓名时，他试图借苏方杀人保住身份（摘要直接叙述；S38、S39、S40）
- **价值取舍**：他将间谍任务置于朋友信任与他人婚姻边界之上（行为推断；S41、S42、S43）
- **决策方式**：面对暴露风险，他通过秘密接头寻求代理清除威胁（摘要直接叙述；S39、S40）
- **关系模式**：他奉命引诱Smiley的妻子，以扭曲Smiley对自己的怀疑（摘要直接叙述；S41）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：与Prideaux保持亲密友谊，却利用其信任导致背叛。

### Ricki Tarr

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-8783d04721a947dc` · 独立版本：`profile-08ac67ea98a88d86`

- **性格倾向**：在发现额外线索时，他表现出愿意超出原定安排追查的倾向（行为推断；S18、S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：他起初通过接近Irina获取情报，后来向Smiley揭露内奸线索（摘要直接叙述；S9、S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：遭到追捕后，他选择隐匿并向机构外的Smiley寻求调查渠道（行为推断；S26、S17、S18）
- **关系模式**：他与Irina的亲密关系起于取信任务，双方都带有情报目的（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21）
- **成长变化**：他从执行海外调查的特工变为受指控的逃亡者及诱捕关键（摘要直接叙述；S18、S26、S38）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In October 1973, Control, the head of British Intelligence , sends agent Jim Prideaux to Communist Hungary to meet a Hungarian general who had promised to sell information.
- **S2**：However, Prideaux is shot and captured by Soviet agents.
- **S3**：Amid the international incident that follows, Control and his right-hand man George Smiley are forced into retirement.
- **S4**：Control, already ill, dies soon afterwards.
- **S5**：Percy Alleline becomes the new Chief of the Circus, with Bill Haydon as his deputy and Roy Bland and Toby Esterhase as close allies.
- **S6**：They have been successful lately in getting high-grade Soviet intelligence material from a project of theirs called "Witchcraft".
- **S7**：Control and Smiley had distrusted this project.
- **S8**：Witchcraft material is being shared with the Americans, in exchange for valuable US intelligence.
- **S9**：Smiley is brought out of retirement by Oliver Lacon, the civil servant in charge of intelligence, to investigate the claim by Ricki Tarr, an MI6 employee, that there has been a long-term mole in a senior role in British Intelligence.
- **S10**：Control had held this suspicion as well.
- **S11**：Working outside of the Circus, Smiley chooses a few men and begins to interview people who left the Circus about the same time as he and Control did.
- **S12**：One is Connie Sachs, who had been sacked by Alleline after claiming that Alexei Polyakov, a Soviet cultural attaché in London, is a Soviet mole-handler.
- **S13**：Another is Jerry Westerby, who had been duty clerk on the night Prideaux was shot.
- **S14**：Westerby says that on that night he called Smiley's house for instructions, but Ann, Smiley's wife, had answered.
- **S15**：Shortly after, Haydon arrived at the Circus and said that he saw the news on the tickertape at his club.
- **S16**：Smiley realises that Haydon must have heard the news from Ann, with whom he was having an affair.
- **S17**：Smiley comes home and finds Tarr hiding there.
- **S18**：Tarr tells him that he had been sent to Istanbul to investigate a Soviet agent named Boris.
- **S19**：Tarr found that Boris had no significance, but that Boris's wife Irina was also an operative and seemed to have information.
- **S20**：So Tarr overstayed in Istanbul and started having an affair with Irina to gain her trust.
- **S21**：Irina, however, knew who Tarr was, and asked to trade the information, the fact that a mole sent by a Soviet spymaster named Karla exists in the top ranks of the Circus, for a new life in the West.
- **S22**：Tarr sent Irina's request back to London, but the reply did not come for several hours.
- **S23**：The reply ignored Irina's request and said to come home immediately.
- **S24**：Then, Boris, and also the British agent who was station chief in Istanbul, were both suddenly killed.
- **S25**：Tarr saw Irina captured and being taken back to Russia.
- **S26**：Tarr was subsequently accused of defecting and of murdering the British station chief, so he went on the run.
- **S27**：Smiley tells Peter Guillam, an officer at the Circus but who is working directly for Smiley, to steal the Circus logbook for that night Tarr called: he finds the pages for that night are cut out, suggesting that Tarr's story is true.
- **S28**：Smiley finds Prideaux, who was returned by the Russians but sacked from the service.
- **S29**：Prideaux reveals that the specific purpose of the Hungary mission was to get the name of the mole.
- **S30**：Control had codenamed the suspects "Tinker" , "Tailor" , "Soldier" , "Poorman"  and "Beggarman" .
- **S31**：Prideaux tells how he was brutally interrogated and gave in, and also how he saw a blonde female prisoner being shot in front of him .
- **S32**：However, says Prideaux, the Soviets already knew of Control's investigation into the mole, and were only interested in finding out how far that investigation had progressed.
- **S33**：Smiley learns that Alleline, Haydon, Bland, and Esterhase have been regularly meeting Polyakov – the Project Witchcraft source – at a safe house to get material.
- **S34**：At every meeting these men give Polyakov low-grade British material to help him maintain his cover with the Soviets, but one of these men, the mole, is actually handing over high-grade material, including material obtained from the Americans.
- **S35**：The truth is, however, that Polyakov is loyal to the Soviets, and his job is to handle the mole.
- **S36**：The Witchcraft material is mostly "chicken feed", with just enough "glitter" to persuade the Americans to share information with the British.
- **S37**：Smiley gets the safe house's location by threatening to deport Esterhase, who was formerly Hungarian and remains a wanted criminal there.
- **S38**：Smiley then sets a trap by having Tarr appear at the Paris office announcing he knows who the mole is and is ready to give the name.
- **S39**：The mole hears this, and immediately arranges with Polyakov to meet at the safe house to ask the Soviets to kill Tarr.
- **S40**：Smiley waits at the safe house and captures the mole: Haydon.
- **S41**：At Sarratt, the Circus interrogation centre, Haydon reveals that he seduced Smiley's wife on Karla's orders, in order to distort any suspicions Smiley may have had of Haydon.
- **S42**：Haydon also reveals that Prideaux confided in him about Control's suspicion of a mole right before Prideaux left for Hungary, since they were close friends.
- **S43**：The Circus makes plans to exchange Haydon back to the Soviets, but Prideaux, having learned of how Haydon betrayed him, kills Haydon.
- **S44**：Smiley is restored to the Circus as its chief.

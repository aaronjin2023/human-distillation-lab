- **S23**：Percy calls the campers to help defend Olympus , as the gods refuse to end their struggle with Typhon.
- **S24**：Just before the battle begins, New York City is affected by a powerful sleeping spell from Morpheus , Hecate , and Kronos.
- **S25**：Despite being joined by Thalia 's Hunters of Artemis , the Party Ponies , and a few other allies, the Olympian army struggles to hold back repeated assaults by the Titan army.
- **S26**：Camp Half-Blood suffers 16 deaths.

- **S28**：Even after these setbacks, Percy refuses a chance to surrender offered by Prometheus , marked by releasing the spirit of hope inside Pandora's pithos , which Percy later entrusts to Hestia.

- **S50**：Annabeth celebrates Percy's birthday, and the two begin dating.
- **S51**：The gods keep their new promises, and Camp Half-Blood slowly returns to normal. [ 6 ]

### Fergus

- **性格倾向**：面对被俘者与受威胁的恋人，他倾向心软并承担保护责任（行为推断；S3、S9、S39）
- **价值取舍**：在组织命令与具体生命之间，他倾向拒绝亲手杀害无助者（行为推断；S7、S8、S9）

### Dil

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-c5e97b1547804be6` · 独立版本：`profile-c36bcc75f1e75f6b`

- **性格倾向**：在害怕恋人离开的情境下，她倾向用强制手段寻求确定感（行为推断；S29、S31、S32）
- **核心动机**：她希望得到弗格斯明确的爱与不离开自己的承诺（摘要直接叙述；S31）
- **情绪反应**：她表示即使爱的承诺是假话也愿意听，随后情绪崩溃落泪（摘要直接叙述；S32、S33）
- **价值取舍**：面对参与乔迪死亡的弗格斯，她仍以对乔迪的牵念约束报复（行为推断；S35、S37）
- **关系模式**：她与弗格斯的关系在伤害和解之后，继续延伸到服刑期间（摘要直接叙述；S17、S19、S40、S41）

暂无支持：决策方式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴求亲密，却以持枪与束缚逼取爱的承诺；为乔迪复仇，却无法杀死同样牵涉其死亡的弗格斯

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film opens as a psychological thriller – IRA foot soldier Fergus  and a unit of other IRA fighters, including a woman named Jude  and led by Maguire , kidnap Jody , a black British soldier.
- **S2**：The IRA demands the release of other jailed IRA members, threatening to execute Jody in three days if their demands are not met.
- **S3**：While the amiable Fergus guards Jody, they develop a bond – much to the chagrin of the other IRA men.
- **S4**：During this time, Jody tells Fergus the story about the Scorpion and the Frog.
- **S5**：Jody persuades Fergus to make sure his girlfriend, Dil , will be all right after Jody has been killed.
- **S6**：The deadline set by Jody's captors passes and Jody is to be executed.
- **S7**：Fergus takes Jody into the woods to carry out the sentence.
- **S8**：However, Jody knows that Fergus is no murderer at heart, and makes a break for it.
- **S9**：Sure enough, Fergus cannot bring himself to shoot the fleeing Jody in the back, but Jody is instead accidentally run over and killed by British armoured personnel carriers as they suddenly move in to assault the IRA safehouse.
- **S10**：With his IRA companions seemingly dead after the attack, Fergus hides from the main body of the IRA in London, where he takes a job as a day labourer, using the alias "Jimmy".
- **S11**：While in London, Fergus meets Jody's attractive girlfriend Dil at a hair salon.
- **S12**：Later they talk in a bar, where the next evening he sees her singing "The Crying Game".
- **S13**：Fergus still suffers from guilt about Jody's death and sees him in his dreams bowling a cricket ball to him.
- **S14**：He continues to pursue Dil, protecting her from an obsessive suitor and gradually falling in love with her.
- **S15**：Later, when he is about to make love to her in her apartment, he discovers that she is in fact a pre-op transwoman.
- **S16**：His initial reaction is of revulsion.
- **S17**：Rushing to the bathroom to throw up, he accidentally hits Dil in the face, leaving her with a nosebleed.
- **S18**：He then leaves the apartment.
- **S19**：A few days later, Fergus leaves Dil a note, and the two make up.
- **S20**：Despite everything, Dil is still attracted to him.
- **S21**：Around the same time, Jude unexpectedly reappears in Fergus' apartment.
- **S22**：She tells him that the IRA has tried and convicted him in absentia.
- **S23**：She forces him to agree to help with a new mission to aid in assassinating a well-known official.
- **S24**：She also off-handedly mentions that she knows about Fergus and Dil, warning him that the IRA will kill him if Fergus does not co-operate.
- **S25**：Fergus, however, cannot overcome his feelings for Dil, and continues wooing her.
- **S26**：Fergus shields her from possible retribution by giving her a haircut and male clothes, as a disguise.
- **S27**：The night before the IRA mission is to be carried out, Dil gets heavily drunk and Fergus has to escort her to her apartment, where Dil asks for Fergus to stay with her.
- **S28**：Fergus complies, then admits to Dil that he had an indirect hand in her former boyfriend's death.
- **S29**：Dil, drunk, appears not to have understood, but in the morning, before Fergus wakes up, Dil ties him to the bed.
- **S30**：Dil unwittingly prevents Fergus from joining the other IRA members and completing the planned assassination.
- **S31**：Holding Fergus at gunpoint, Dil forces him to tell Dil that he loves her and will never leave her.
- **S32**：Dil unties him, saying that, even if he is lying, it is still nice to hear his words.
- **S33**：Dil then breaks down in tears.
- **S34**：A vengeful Jude then enters their room with a gun, seeking to kill Fergus for missing the assassination.
- **S35**：Dil takes several shots at Jude, hitting her, whilst stating that she is aware that Jude was complicit in Jody's death and that Jude used her sexuality to trick him.
- **S36**：Dil finally kills Jude with a shot in the neck.
- **S37**：Dil then points the gun at Fergus, but lowers her hand, saying that she cannot kill him, because Jody will not allow her to.
- **S38**：Fergus prevents Dil from shooting herself, and tells her to hide out in the club for a while.
- **S39**：When Dil is gone, he wipes Dil's fingerprints off the gun and allows himself to be arrested in place of Dil.
- **S40**：The epilogue takes place a few months later – Fergus, in prison, is visited by Dil.
- **S41**：Dil, after talking with Fergus about plans once he gets out of jail, asks him why he took the fall for her in the first place.
- **S42**：Fergus responds, "As a man once said, it's in my nature." He then tells her the story of the Scorpion and the Frog.

### Hactar

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-f070deffd2190d64` · 独立版本：`profile-d41ca4b81c15a30a`

- **性格倾向**：在失去完整形体后，他仍倾向耐心布局并通过间接操纵实现目标（行为推断；S40、S41、S43）
- **决策方式**：为推动战争，他倾向制造外部刺激并干预群体思想来驱使他者行动（行为推断；S43）
- **价值取舍**：在最初制造武器时，他以故意失效的设计拒绝完全执行灭世命令（行为推断；S39、S40）
- **成长变化**：他从故意造出无效武器转向布置真实灭世炸弹，行动方向发生逆转（行为推断；S40、S43、S48）
- **情绪反应**：在维持运作已很困难时，他向来访者表达了对自身行为的歉意（摘要直接叙述；S45）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式

- **S13**：[ edit ] After being stranded on pre-historic Earth after the events in The Restaurant at the End of the Universe , Arthur Dent is met by his old friend Ford Prefect , who drags him into a space-time eddy , represented by an anachronistic sofa .
- **S14**：The two end up at Lord's Cricket Ground two days before the Earth's destruction by the Vogons .
- **S15**：Shortly after they arrive, a squad of robots land in a spaceship in the middle of the field and attack the assembled crowd, stealing the Ashes before departing.
- **S16**：Another spaceship arrives, the Starship Bistromath , helmed by Slartibartfast , who discovers he is too late to stop the theft and requests Arthur and Ford's help.
- **S17**：As they travel to their next destination, Slartibartfast explains that he is trying to stop the robots from collecting all the components of the Wikkit Gate.
- **S18**：Long ago, the peaceful population of the planet of Krikkit, unaware of the rest of the Universe due to a dust cloud that surrounded its solar system, were surprised to find the wreckage of a spacecraft on their planet.
- **S19**：Reverse engineering the vessel, they explored past the dust cloud and saw the rest of the Universe, immediately taking a disliking to it and deciding that it must be destroyed.
- **S20**：They built a fleet of ships and robots to attack the rest of the Universe in a brutal onslaught known as the Krikkit Wars, but were eventually defeated.
- **S21**：Realizing that the Krikkit population would not be satisfied alongside the existence of the rest of the Universe, it was decided to lock the planet in a Slo-Time envelope, to be opened only after the Universe has ended so that the planet can exist alone.
- **S22**：The Wikkit Gate, shaped exactly like a wicket used in the sport of cricket , is needed to unlock the envelope.
- **S23**：However, one ship carrying a troop of robots from Krikkit avoided being sealed in, and these robots began to search for the pieces of the Gate after they were dispersed about space and time.
- **S24**：Slartibartfast, Arthur, and Ford transport to an airborne party that has lasted numerous generations where another Gate component, the Silver Bail, is to be found, but Arthur finds himself separated from the others and ends up at a Cathedral of Hate created by a being called Agrajag .
- **S25**：Agrajag reveals that he has been reincarnated countless times in a wide variety of forms, only to be killed by Arthur in each life; he now plans to kill Arthur in revenge.
- **S26**：However, upon learning that Arthur has yet to cause his death at a place called Stavromula Beta, Agrajag realizes that he has pulled Arthur out of his relative timeline too soon and that killing him now would cause a paradox , but attempts to kill Arthur anyway.
- **S27**：In his insanity, Agrajag brings the Cathedral down around them.
- **S28**：Arthur manages to escape unharmed, partially due to learning how to fly after falling and missing the ground while catching sight of a bag he had lost at a Greek airport years before.
- **S29**：After collecting the bag, Arthur inadvertently comes across the flying party and rejoins his friends.
- **S30**：Inside, they find Trillian , but they are too late to stop the robots from stealing the Silver Bail.
- **S31**：Arthur, Ford, Trillian, and Slartibartfast return to the Bistromath and try to head off the robots activating the Wikkit Gate.
- **S32**：Meanwhile, the Krikkit robots steal the last two pieces, the Infinite Improbability Drive core from the spaceship Heart of Gold and a peg leg used by Marvin the Paranoid Android .
- **S33**：They capture both Marvin and Zaphod Beeblebrox in the process.
- **S34**：The Bistromath arrives too late to stop the robots from opening the Gate, so its occupants transport to the planet to attempt to negotiate with the Krikkit people.
- **S35**：To their surprise, they find that the people seem to lack any desire to continue the war, and are directed to the robot and spaceship facilities in orbit about the planet.
- **S36**：With help from Zaphod and Marvin, the group is able to infiltrate the facilities.
- **S37**：Trillian deduces that the Krikkiters have been manipulated, reasoning that the people of Krikkit could not simultaneously be smart enough to develop their ultimate weapon—a bomb that could destroy every star in the universe—and also stupid enough not to realize that this weapon would also destroy them.
- **S38**：The characters discover that the true force behind the war has been the supercomputer Hactar .
- **S39**：Previously built to serve a war-faring species, Hactar was tasked to build a supernova-bomb that would link the cores of every sun in the Universe together at the press of a button and cause the end of the Universe.
- **S40**：Hactar purposely created a dud version of the weapon instead, causing his creators to pulverize him into dust, which thus became the dust cloud around Krikkit.
- **S41**：However, Hactar was still able to function, though at a much weaker level.
- **S42**：Trillian and Arthur speak to Hactar in a virtual space that he creates for them to explain himself.
- **S43**：Hactar reveals that he spent eons creating the spaceship that crashed on Krikkit to inspire their xenophobia and incite them to go to war, also influencing their thoughts.
- **S44**：However, when the Slo-Time envelope was activated, his control on the population waned.
- **S45**：As he struggles to remain functional, Hactar apologizes to Trillian and Arthur for his actions before they leave for their ship.
- **S46**：With the war over, the group collects the core of the Heart of Gold and the Ashes, the only two components of the Wikkit Gate not destroyed by the robots, and returns Zaphod and Marvin to the Heart of Gold .
- **S47**：Returning to Lord's Cricket Ground only moments after the robots' attack, Arthur attempts to return the Ashes, but is suddenly inspired to bowl one shot at a wicket that is being defended using a cricket ball in his bag.
- **S48**：However, during his run-up, Arthur suddenly realizes that the ball was created and placed in his bag by Hactar and is actually the working version of the cosmic-supernova-bomb, and that the defender of the wicket is one of the Krikkit robots, ready to detonate the bomb once thrown.
- **S49**：Arthur trips, misses the ground, and flies over the pitch, allowing him to throw the bomb safely aside and behead the robot with its own bat.
- **S50**：Afterward, the group are taking Arthur to a 'quiet and idyllic planet' when they come across a half-mad journalist.
- **S51**：Some time earlier, he had been reporting on a court case in which a witness named Prak was inadvertently given an overdose of a truth drug.
- **S52**：Prak began to tell all truth, horrifying the involved parties so badly that they abandoned the courtroom and sealed it up with him inside.
- **S53**：The group find him still there, hoping to learn from him the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe and Everything.
- **S54**：They are disappointed to find that Prak has told all the truth in existence, but has forgotten it and kept no record.
- **S55**：The only information he can provide is that the Ultimate Question and its answer can never both be known about in the same universe.
- **S56**：He then attempts to tell Arthur where God's last message to His creation is, though he dies seemingly before Arthur is able to memorize the location.
- **S57**：Arthur ultimately settles on Krikkit, where he becomes a more skillful flier and learns bird language .
- **S58**：He is briefly interrupted by the arrival of an immortal alien who has made it his goal to insult every living creature in the universe, but the alien realizes that he has already done so with Arthur on prehistoric Earth.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] The novel opens in a Confederate military hospital in Raleigh, North Carolina , where Inman is recovering from battle wounds during the American Civil War .
- **S3**：The soldier is tired of fighting for a cause he never believed in.
- **S4**：After the death of a fellow soldier who'd occupied the bed next to his own, Inman decides to slip out of the hospital and return home to Cold Mountain, North Carolina.
- **S5**：At Cold Mountain, Ada's father has been deceased for some time, leaving his farm, Black Cove, to Ada.
- **S6**：However, she is genteel and city-bred, and she proves an inept farmer; the property soon falls into a state of disrepair.
- **S7**：Just as she is contemplating starvation, the farm is visited by a resourceful but homeless young woman named Ruby, who moves in with her.
- **S8**：Together, they clean the place up and return it to productivity.
- **S9**：Ruby also teaches Ada vital survival skills, while Ada shares her knowledge of literature with Ruby.
- **S10**：Inman soon becomes aware that the Confederate Home Guard is ramping up efforts to catch deserters.
- **S11**：The deserter faces an attempted armed robbery at a rural town, even though he carries a stolen LeMat revolver for protection.
- **S12**：He eventually meets a preacher called Veasey, whom he catches in the act of attempting to murder a woman he has impregnated.
- **S13**：After Inman dissuades him, they travel together.
- **S14**：They butcher a dead bull that has fallen into a creek, and the bull's owner, Junior, gives them away to the Home Guard.
- **S15**：After long days of marching, their captors decide that the prisoners are unfit to be returned to service and so should be executed.
- **S16**：Veasey is shot dead, but Inman is only grazed by the bullet that passed through him.
- **S17**：The Guardsmen dig a shoddy mass grave and Inman pulls himself out, helped in part by some passing wild pigs.
- **S18**：He cannot bury Veasey, so he turns him face down and continues on.
- **S19**：Occasionally in his journey, Inman is helped and sheltered by civilians who want nothing to do with the war.
- **S20**：One of these characters is a woman who owns goats and gives him advice and medicines to finally heal his wounds.
- **S21**：Later, he meets a woman named Sara, whom he helps by tracking and recovering her hog; it is her only source of food for the winter but had just been seized by Union soldiers.
- **S22**：Ruby's father, Stobrod, is caught stealing corn at Ada's farm.
- **S23**：Ruby reveals he was a deadbeat who neglected and abandoned her when she was very young; he is also a Confederate deserter.
- **S24**：Nevertheless, Ruby grudgingly feeds him.
- **S25**：He then returns with a friend named Pangle, a gentle-natured young man who appears to have an unspecified intellectual disability but displays great musical aptitude.
- **S26**：Together, they try to earn a living by playing fiddle and banjo .
- **S27**：However, one day as Stobrod and Pangle walk through the mountain woods, Guardsmen led by the sadistic Captain Teague shoot them, killing Pangle and dealing Stobrod a near-fatal wound.
- **S28**：A third companion, referred to as "Georgia," escapes the killing and goes off to alert Ada and Ruby.
- **S29**：The two women arrive at the site of the shooting to find Stobrod barely alive.
- **S30**：Ada and Ruby take him to an abandoned nearby Indigenous camp to give him a place to recover.
- **S31**：After Inman arrives at Black Cove to find it empty, he sets out to find Ada on the mountain.
- **S32**：Unexpectedly he soon encounters her out hunting wild turkeys.
- **S33**：Both characters have changed so greatly in their appearance and demeanor since they parted that it is some moments before they recognize one another.
- **S34**：Inman takes up camp with Ada and Ruby.
- **S35**：Ruby is initially cold towards Inman, and later says to Ada in private, "You don't need him," but Ada replies "I think I want him"; [ 5 ] Ruby softens when she hears this.
- **S36**：Ada later tells Inman that if she and he are married, she still wants Ruby to stay with them on the farm.
- **S37**：Ada and Inman make love.
- **S38**：They happily begin to imagine the life they will have together at Black Cove and make plans for their future.
- **S39**：However, as the party begins the trek back to the farm, the women and men split up, and Inman and Stobrod run into Captain Teague.
- **S40**：A shootout commences in which Inman kills all the members of the Home Guard except for a young man whom Inman views as "but a boy." [ 6 ] Inman eventually corners the boy but is reluctant to shoot him down in cold blood.
- **S41**：However, the boy takes Inman by surprise, shooting and killing him instead.
- **S42**：In the epilogue, it is revealed that Ada's night with Inman resulted in the conception of a daughter.
- **S43**：Ada raises her at Black Cove, where they live with Ruby (who got married with "Georgia" and has three sons) and Stobrod.

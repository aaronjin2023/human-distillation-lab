## 角色资料

### Will Stronghold

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-410a6d9bd27481fe` · 独立版本：`profile-c591c022d6ed9edc`

- **性格倾向**：在突然获得校园认可时倾向受群体地位吸引，一度忽略旧友（行为推断；S13、S14）
- **情绪反应**：因尚未发展出超能力而对入学感到焦虑，父母对此并不知情（摘要直接叙述；S4）
- **价值取舍**：当受欢迎的女友伤害好友时，选择维护好友而放弃恋爱关系（行为推断；S16）
- **决策方式**：发现威胁后选择向好友道歉，并联合不同能力的同学共同应对（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18、S23）
- **成长变化**：从因地位提升疏远辅助班同学，转向承认过失并依靠团队救校（行为推断；S14、S23、S24、S25）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：对校园人气的向往与对原有友情的责任发生冲突

### Gwen Grayson

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-69c2765f4a8f9d3d` · 独立版本：`profile-ef1bc2f8157e01cf`

- **性格倾向**：在复仇计划中倾向耐心布局，通过亲近目标掩盖真实意图（行为推断；S15、S19、S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：因旧日交锋中被武器变成婴儿，等待十六年后实施复仇（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21）
- **价值取舍**：在实现复仇与顾及无辜者安危之间，行动上优先满足报复目的（行为推断；S21、S22、S24）
- **决策方式**：先利用恋爱关系取得武器，再联合帮手控制学校并破坏动力装置（摘要直接叙述；S15、S22、S24）
- **关系模式**：将与威尔建立的亲密关系用作进入其家庭秘密空间的手段（行为推断；S14、S15）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Will Stronghold  is beginning his freshman year at Sky High, a high school which teaches super powered children.
- **S2**：Will's parents are The Commander  and Jetstream , the world's most famous superheroes.
- **S3**：Will's best friend is Layla , who has the power to manipulate plant life.
- **S4**：Will is anxious about attending Sky High, located on a floating campus reached by flying school bus, because, unbeknownst to his parents, he has not developed any super powers.
- **S5**：The first day he and the other freshman are harassed by a trio of bullies: the super fast Speed , Lash  who can stretch his body, and cheerleader Penny  who can create duplicates of herself.
- **S6**：Because of his lack of powers, Will is slated to enter a curriculum for "Hero Support" and become a sidekick.
- **S7**：His classmates include Ethan  who can melt into a puddle, Zach  who glows in the dark, Magenta  who turns into a purple guinea pig, and Layla who joins the class in protest against the two track nature of the school's education.
- **S8**：The class is taught by The Commander's former sidekick "All American Boy" .
- **S9**：The Commander, unaware that his son has been relegated to Hero Support, shows Will his secret trophy room.
- **S10**：He is particularly proud of the mysterious weapon "The Pacifier" which he took from his science themes arch-nemesis Royal Pain years ago.
- **S11**：Unknown to either of them, Royal Pain, who had been presumed dead, watches the exchange from a hidden camera in one of the other trophies.
- **S12**：As Will settles in to Sky High and makes friends with the other sidekicks he comes into conflict with fire wielding student Warren Peace , whose supervillain father had been imprisoned by The Commander.
- **S13**：During a fight between the two, Will demonstrates super strength, impressing Gwen Grayson , a beautiful and popular "technopath" who controls machines with her mind.
- **S14**：Will begins spending more time with Gwen and her popular friends, ignoring the sidekicks and Layla, who reveals to Warren that she has loved Will for a long time.
- **S15**：On the day before the dance, Gwen tricks Will into throwing a party at his house, and Speed steals the Pacifier when she seduces Will into showing her the Secret Sanctum.
- **S16**：After Gwen is cruel to Layla, who shows up to investigate the noise, Will breaks up with Gwen refusing to attend the dance even though his parents are going as honored guests.
- **S17**：Later, he looks through his father's old yearbooks and sees a student who looks just like Gwen.
- **S18**：Believing that the student is Royal Pain and that Gwen is her daughter he rushes to the dance.
- **S19**：At the dance party, Gwen reveals that she is in fact Royal Pain.
- **S20**：During her previous confrontation with the Commander, the Pacifier, which is meant to turn its target into an infant, had malfunctioned, turning her into a baby instead.
- **S21**：She has since waited sixteen years for revenge.
- **S22**：With the help of Speed, Lash, and Penny, she takes over the school and uses the Pacifier to turn the faculty and students into infants.
- **S23**：When Will arrives at school, he apologizes to Layla and teams up with Warren and the sidekicks to try and save the day.
- **S24**：The sidekicks demonstrate their heroism after Royal Pain sabotages the school's anti-gravity drive and their powers come in handy restarting it.
- **S25**：Will, meanwhile, discovers that he also has his mother's powers of flight when he is thrown off the edge of the school grounds and must prevent the campus from falling.
- **S26**：Gwen and her henchmen are defeated and arrested and the faculty and students are returned to their proper ages.
- **S27**：Will and Layla kiss and a voiceover at the end reveals that they become boyfriend and girlfriend.

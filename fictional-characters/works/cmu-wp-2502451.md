### Jake Green

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-df4d1521ea7aa634` · 独立版本：`profile-0bf5d6d1b7a5bcf7`

- **性格倾向**：在赌局冲突与复仇情境中，倾向以暴力或使对手受损回应（行为推断；S8、S28、S30）
- **核心动机**：出狱后以报复Macha为目标，利用赌局使其蒙受损失和羞辱（摘要直接叙述；S1、S28、S30）
- **决策方式**：倾向把学到的博弈策略用于现实竞争，谋求掌握局面主动权（行为推断；S14、S21、S23、S24）
- **情绪反应**：对封闭空间怀有恐惧，并因此在赌场离场时选择走楼梯（摘要直接叙述；S33）
- **成长变化**：最终有意识地逆转自我的指令，从求胜转向挣脱自我支配（摘要直接叙述；S59、S60、S61、S62）

### Dorothy Macha

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-cbaaf48fc9042a40` · 独立版本：`profile-8a84e95edf3b8064`

- **性格倾向**：在遭到拒绝或公开羞辱时，呈现以威胁和报复重建控制的倾向（行为推断；S6、S10、S28、S31、S38）
- **核心动机**：在怀疑Jake仍将报复的情境中，暗杀命令体现其清除威胁的意图（行为推断；S31、S38）
- **价值取舍**：在维护赌博生意与自身安全时，倾向把无辜家属当作胁迫工具（行为推断；S6、S10）
- **决策方式**：面对不合作和潜在泄密，倾向派遣手下施压而非直接协商（行为推断；S6、S9、S10）
- **成长变化**：在叙事结局中仍受自我规则支配，未能完成Jake式的挣脱（摘要直接叙述；S63、S64）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After spending seven years in solitary confinement during which his sister-in-law is murdered, confidence trickster Jake Green  is out to get revenge on Dorothy Macha , a corrupt casino boss.
- **S2**：Dorothy Macha was involved in illegal gambling all over the city.
- **S3**：With the help of three goons, known as "the three Eddies", he controlled several games that took place in the underground.
- **S4**：On one occasion, just before a big game, Macha lost his card man.
- **S5**：Knowing about Jake's skill at the table, Macha and the three Eddies asked him to fill in the spot.
- **S6**：When Jake refused, they harassed Jake's brother, Billy, and Billy's family in order to convince Jake to play.
- **S7**：He succumbed and ended up winning.
- **S8**：The loser, a high roller named George, insulted Jake's mother and Jake responded by shooting him in the foot, igniting a gunfight in which the game's money vanished.
- **S9**：Two weeks later, Jake's name is mentioned to the police and he is brought in for questioning.
- **S10**：Taking precautionary measures, Macha sends the three Eddies to Billy's house where they threaten his niece.
- **S11**：Billy's wife reacts poorly in the situation and is accidentally shot.
- **S12**：Jake does not give Macha's name to the police and ends up sentenced to jail and is given the choice of 14 years normal time or 7 years in solitary.
- **S13**：He chooses the latter.
- **S14**：During his seven-year stint imprisoned in solitary confinement Jake learns of a specific strategy  that is supposed to lead its user to win every game.
- **S15**：The Formula itself was discovered by two unnamed men who inhabited adjacent cells on either side of Jake's own.
- **S16**：They are referred to as a chess expert and a con man.
- **S17**：During the first five years of his seven-year sentence, the three men communicated their thoughts on confidence tricks and chess moves via messages hidden inside library books, such as The Mathematics of Quantum Mechanics.
- **S18**：The chess expert and the con man plan to leave their cells simultaneously, and promise to take Jake with them.
- **S19**：But when they disappear from their cells, they leave Jake behind to serve the remaining two years of his sentence.
- **S20**：When Jake is released, he finds that all of his possessions and money have been taken by the two men with whom he had shared everything.
- **S21**：Still, he has The Formula, and he goes about making a lot of money at various casinos.
- **S22**：Two years later, Jake has garnered a reputation that leads many casinos to fear his freakishly good 'luck'.
- **S23**：The Formula applies to any game, and is often exemplified by Jake's apparent mastery of chess.
- **S24**：The story revolves around Jake's epiphanic awakening, as he learns how to apply the Formula to the 'game' of life.
- **S25**：Approximately two years after his prison release, Jake, Billy and their other brother Joe walk into one of Macha's casinos.
- **S26**：He is recognized and "all the tables are closed" to Jake and company.
- **S27**：But Macha promptly calls them up to a private area of his casino where a high rollers' game is currently taking place.
- **S28**：Jake bets Macha a fortune on a chip toss, and wins.
- **S29**：This hurts Macha.
- **S30**：As Jake says "nothing hurts more than humiliation and a little money loss".
- **S31**：Macha suspects that Jake, who seems unafraid of him, will be out for more revenge.
- **S32**：As Jake and his brothers leave the casino, a man hands Jake a card and tells him that he can help him.
- **S33**：Jake, who has a fear of enclosed spaces, decides to take the stairs.
- **S34**：In the stairwell he looks at the card and then collapses, falling down the stairs.
- **S35**：The card is revealed to read "Take the Elevator".
- **S36**：Jake is rushed to the hospital.
- **S37**：The doctors report he is very ill but do not disclose why he had the blackout.
- **S38**：Macha puts out an order for a hit on Jake.
- **S39**：Jake arrives home, without Billy, to be welcomed by one of Macha's hits.
- **S40**：However, on his doorstep there is another card, which says "Pick This Up".
- **S41**：As Jake bends to retrieve the card bullets fly over his back.
- **S42**：As the shooting continues, the same mysterious individual called Zach  arrives and rescues Jake who is the only person to survive the hit.
- **S43**：Zach introduces Jake to his partner, Avi .
- **S44**：They offer him a deal: they will take all of his money and he will do what they say, no questions asked.
- **S45**：In exchange, they will protect Jake from Macha.
- **S46**：In the course of their proposal, they show Jake his medical file, which they have mysteriously obtained.
- **S47**：It indicates that the blackout occurred due to a rare blood disease which will cause his death within three days.
- **S48**：Jake suspects a con.
- **S49**：The mysterious men later reveal that his money will be used to fund their loan shark enterprise.
- **S50**：Sam Gold is seen to be the 'king' in this chess game of gang warfare.
- **S51**：He is the ultimate figure that all men are supposedly aspiring to be.
- **S52**：Sam Gold is revealed to be an ultimately powerless cipher, whose power is granted only by those who invest in him.
- **S53**：He represents ego and self-investment.
- **S54**：He is the personification of greed.
- **S55**：It is revealed that Avi and Zach were Jake's "neighbours" during his years of incarceration.
- **S56**：They have forced Jake to "induce head pain to engage the enemy" by making him give his money away under the principle that "nothing hurts more than humiliation and a little money loss".
- **S57**：They are inflicting this form of 'premature enlightenment' upon Jake because, according to them, he was not ready to hear how hard this process of liberation was going to be while in prison.
- **S58**：It was because of this that they left without him.
- **S59**：Avi attempts to get Jake to understand the nature of the ego.
- **S60**：He tells Jake "the greatest con that [the ego] ever pulled was making you believe that he is you." This is seen to be the 'ultimate con', in that no-one wants to sever their connection with their ego, because they refuse to challenge their own lifelong investment in it.
- **S61**：In the end, Jake also steps off the proverbial chess board by making a conscious effort to reverse everything his ego tells him to do.
- **S62**：This is seen to be the truest and most fundamental application of the Formula.
- **S63**：The characters of Jake, Zach, Avi and Sorter  are seen to ultimately reject the ego's 'rules'.
- **S64**：The character of Dorothy Macha is seen to succumb to them.

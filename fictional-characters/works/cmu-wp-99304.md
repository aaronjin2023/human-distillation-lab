## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In early 1923, two young boys from Tennessee, Rafe McCawley and Danny Walker, pretending to fight the Germans, climb into Rafe's father's biplane cropduster and accidentally start it, giving them their first taste of flying.
- **S2**：Soon after, Danny's father  comes to drag him home, berating him for playing with Rafe and beating him.
- **S3**：Rafe attacks Danny's father calling him a "dirty German"; Danny's father counters by explaining that he fought the Germans in World War I and wishes that they never witness the horrors of war.
- **S4**：In the summer of 1940, as grown men, Rafe  and Danny  are First Lieutenants in the United States Army Air Corps under the command of Major Jimmy Doolittle .
- **S5**：Rafe meets Evelyn Johnson , a Navy nurse who passes him for his physical examination even though he has dyslexia, and is instantly smitten.
- **S6**：The two soon begin dating and fall in love.
- **S7**：However, Rafe has volunteered to serve with the Royal Air Force's Eagle Squadrons.
- **S8**：Before Rafe leaves for England, he makes a promise to Evelyn that he will come back for her.
- **S9**：Evelyn and Danny are transferred with their respective squadrons to Pearl Harbor.
- **S10**：Rafe is shot down over the English Channel and presumed to have been killed in action.
- **S11**：Three months later, Evelyn and Danny bond over their mourning of Rafe and unexpectedly develop feelings for each other.
- **S12**：They soon begin their own relationship.
- **S13**：On the night of December 6, 1941, Rafe unexpectedly returns to Pearl Harbor, having survived the crash and being stranded in occupied France in the interval.
- **S14**：He quickly realizes that Evelyn and Danny are now together, and feeling hurt and betrayed, the two friends soon get into a fight at the local hula bar.
- **S15**：The next morning, on December 7, they are interrupted by the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor by Zero fighters, Val dive bombers and Kate torpedo bombers.
- **S16**：The surprise Japanese air raid sinks the battleships {{USS}}, {{USS}} and many other ships.
- **S17**：Back at the hospital, Evelyn helps tend to the dozens of wounded who come in and must help decide who can and cannot be saved.
- **S18**：Meanwhile, Rafe and Danny are the only two who manage to get airborne and shoot down seven Japanese aircraft with P-40s using their reckless tactics, including an old game of theirs called chicken.
- **S19**：The two men then go to the hospital, where Evelyn takes blood from them for the hundreds of injured soldiers, and later aid in trying to rescue the many men still in the harbor.
- **S20**：In the aftermath, the survivors attend a memorial service for the fallen victims after the U.S. declaration of war on Japan.
- **S21**：Rafe and Danny are both promoted to Captain, awarded the Silver Star and assigned to now-Colonel Doolittle for a dangerous and top-secret mission.
- **S22**：Before their departure, Evelyn meets Rafe and reveals that she is pregnant with Danny's child, although she doesn't want Danny to know so he can focus on the upcoming mission.
- **S23**：She says that she is going to remain with Danny, though deep down she will always love Rafe just as much.
- **S24**：Rafe accepts this.

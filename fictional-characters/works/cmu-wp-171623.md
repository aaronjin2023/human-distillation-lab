## 角色资料

### Jake Hoyt

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8f4d3b1018ae00e0` · 独立版本：`profile-b05e52a01cac24d2`

- **性格倾向**：在强势上级的压力下仍倾向提出异议，并非一味服从（行为推断；S10、S15、S24）
- **价值取舍**：他重视保护受害者与执法诚实，拒绝分赃和编造证词（行为推断；S7、S22、S24）
- **决策方式**：面对直接胁迫会暂时退让，但脱险后转向主动追究责任（行为推断；S4、S5、S27、S28、S39、S40）
- **情绪反应**：上级违法并令双方陷入险境，使他愤怒且公开表达反对（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **成长变化**：他从接受资深警员考核，转为独立判断并尝试逮捕考核者（行为推断；S1、S39、S40）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：坚持诚实执法，却在枪口与血检威胁下被迫妥协

### Alonzo Harris

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-baaec46383b73aa4` · 独立版本：`profile-d14f0a9bd3cc3d40`

- **性格倾向**：在危机中倾向自信且强势，低估他人反抗与局势失控的可能（行为推断；S19、S24、S42）
- **核心动机**：在偿命期限迫近的情境中，他倾向通过犯罪筹款以求自保（行为推断；S18、S19、S20、S22、S23、S33）
- **价值取舍**：在此情境中，他将个人脱险置于执法责任与他人生命之上（行为推断；S11、S23、S34）
- **决策方式**：他倾向预设把柄与伪造现场，让同僚被迫卷入自己的罪行（行为推断；S5、S23、S26、S27）
- **沟通方式**：他以街头生存论贬低异议，并结合威胁迫使新人合作（行为推断；S4、S10、S27、S28）
- **关系模式**：他把警界关系与帮派交易当作资源，却失去社区的支持（行为推断；S19、S30、S34、S42）

暂无支持：情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：宣称掌控局势，却受偿命期限驱使不断扩大犯罪；借警员身份支配他人，最终无法换取社区保护

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film follows a single day in the life of an Los Angeles Police Department  cop, Jake Hoyt , who is scheduled to be evaluated by Detective Alonzo Harris , a highly decorated LAPD narcotics officer who could advance Jake's career.
- **S2**：In Alonzo's car, the pair of officers observe teenage Mara Salvatrucha members dealing drugs in a park.
- **S3**：Instead of arresting the buyers, Alonzo confiscates the drugs and tells Jake to take a hit from the marijuana.
- **S4**：Jake initially refuses, but Alonzo puts a gun to his head and says Jake's failure to use drugs could get him killed by a street dealer.
- **S5**：Jake relents and smokes the marijuana, but shortly thereafter, Alonzo tells him the marijuana was laced with PCP.
- **S6**：While driving, Jake notices a female high school student being sexually assaulted by two men in an alley.
- **S7**：Jake jumps out to help her and subdues the attackers, but Alonzo refuses to report the incident.
- **S8**：He tells the girl to leave, telling Jake that the girl's gang member cousin  will seek the attackers out for revenge.
- **S9**：Jake discovers the girl's wallet on the ground and takes it.
- **S10**：Jake objects to Alonzo's use of vigilante "street justice," but Alonzo characterizes Jake as naïve.
- **S11**：Alonzo and Jake then go to the home of a drug dealer named the Sandman and serve a phony search warrant, which is actually a cover for Alonzo to steal drug money.
- **S12**：A woman at the home asks to see the warrant, and after realizing it is phony, becomes belligerent.
- **S13**：The woman runs outside, aware she has been robbed, wildly shouting.
- **S14**：Nearly a dozen armed men begin shooting at the duo, and Alonzo returns fire.
- **S15**：Barely managing to escape, an irate Jake again objects to Alonzo's actions, especially since it could have gotten them killed.
- **S16**：The duo then arrive at Baldwin Village to visit Alonzo's Salvadoran girlfriend Sara  and their young son.
- **S17**：Afterward, Alonzo meets with a group of high-ranking police officials dubbed the "Three Wise Men" .
- **S18**：They tell Alonzo that they know he owes money to the Russian Mafia and suggest that he leave town.
- **S19**：But Alonzo insists he can control the situation and gets permission from the Wise Men to "cash in on an account" with the caveat that he avoid a scandal.
- **S20**：Alonzo later tells Jake that he had to give Sandman's money to the Three Wise Men to obtain an arrest warrant.
- **S21**：Alonzo takes Jake and several other narcotic officers to the home of Roger , a drug dealer and former police officer the two visited earlier.
- **S22**：Using the warrant, they seize several million dollars from underneath the floor of Roger's kitchen, but Jake refuses to take his share of the cash -- much to the suspicion of the other cops.
- **S23**：Alonzo then shoots Roger and arranges the scene to appear like a justified shooting.
- **S24**：Jake refuses to lie, and after being threatened seizes Alonzo's gun.
- **S25**：A Mexican standoff ensues, with Jake threatening to shoot Alonzo while the other officers threaten to shoot Jake.
- **S26**：Alonzo then reveals that the LAPD will run a blood test on Jake following the shooting, thus detecting the PCP he had smoked earlier.
- **S27**：Alonzo offers to have the test results falsified in exchange for Jake's cooperation.
- **S28**：Jake reluctantly agrees, and Alonzo again urges Jake to drop his naïve view of police work.
- **S29**：Alonzo then drives Jake to the home of a Mexican gangster named "Smiley" , allegedly to run an errand.
- **S30**：Alonzo gives Smiley a package containing a large stack of money, which Smiley asks his sister to count while he plays poker with two other gang members.
- **S31**：Jake is persuaded to join the game and engages in tense conversation with the three men.
- **S32**：After Smiley's sister confirms to him that the count is correct, Smiley receives a message on his phone.
- **S33**：Smiley then reveals to Jake that Alonzo has abandoned him, and explains Alonzo's situation: by midnight, Alonzo must pay $1 million to the Russian mob for killing one of their couriers in Las Vegas or be killed himself.
- **S34**：Alonzo has paid Smiley to kill Jake.
- **S35**：Jake tries to escape but is overpowered and dragged into the bathroom to be executed.
- **S36**：The men search his pockets, finding the wallet he had picked up earlier from the girl he rescued, who is Smiley's cousin.
- **S37**：Smiley calls the girl, who confirms that Jake defended her against the attackers.
- **S38**：In gratitude for protecting his cousin, Smiley lets Jake go, and asks for no hard feelings because the planned execution was "just business".
- **S39**：Jake returns to Sara's apartment looking for Alonzo.
- **S40**：He attempts to arrest Alonzo, but Alonzo resists and they fight.
- **S41**：Jake eventually subdues him, after which the local gang members and residents begin congregating to watch.
- **S42**：Alonzo tries to get the crowd on his side by offering a reward to whoever kills Jake, but they have grown tired of Alonzo's arrogance and allow Jake to walk away with the money.
- **S43**：In his escape to Los Angeles International Airport, Alonzo is surrounded by Russian hitmen who kill him.
- **S44**：The final scene has Jake pulling into his driveway and going home to his wife, Lisa , and daughter, while a radio broadcast describes Alonzo's death as occurring honorably while "serving a high-risk warrant near LAX."  {{Unreferenced}} On the VHS and DVD versions of the film there is an alternate ending in which Jake comes home, but instead of simply walking into his house, he is confronted by the Three Wise Men who ask what he did with the money.
- **S45**：He says he checked it into evidence, all of it, and tells them to leave him alone.
- **S46**：He then is seen walking into his house with a bag, presumably containing the money.

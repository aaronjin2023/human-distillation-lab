## 角色资料

### Eddie Yang

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-63b5540049d0c4b0` · 独立版本：`profile-6019c97dea758d79`

- **性格倾向**：在救援受困男孩时，表现出敢于担险的保护倾向（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：持续追查绑架者，首要目标是让男孩脱离控制（行为推断；S7、S10、S11）
- **价值取舍**：在自身与男孩的生存冲突中，优先保全男孩生命（行为推断；S13）
- **决策方式**：面对获得不死之身的敌人，转用力量来源破解优势（行为推断；S25、S26）
- **关系模式**：与恋人兼同僚共同承担救援，失去她后争取复生（摘要直接叙述；S9、S11、S25、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：拥有不死力量，却仍须面对恋人遭杀害的损失。

### AJ "Snakehead" Staul

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-7ccafe97b08edb1e` · 独立版本：`profile-b9613ed34067f5fb`

- **性格倾向**：在追逐神力时，倾向反复使用绑架与强迫手段（行为推断；S6、S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：在争夺徽章时，意图取得完整力量并摆脱死亡限制（行为推断；S20、S21、S25）
- **价值取舍**：为取得个人力量，将男孩与他人家属的安全置于次位（行为推断；S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：发现半枚徽章能力有限后，主动袭击家属夺取另一半（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：追求不死之身，最终却被赋予生命的同一力量摧毁。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Eddie Yang  is a Hong Kong police officer cooperating with Interpol in the capture of a crime lord named AJ "Snakehead" Staul .
- **S2**：Snakehead procures an ancient book from a Chinese bookstore keeper, which tells the story of a boy being chosen every thousand years to bind the two halves of a legendary medallion.
- **S3**：In Hong Kong, Eddie and Interpol agent Arthur Watson ([[Lee Evans  lead a raid to capture Snakehead and his men, who are about to kidnap the boy, named Jai .
- **S4**：Eddie and the agents fight off Snakehead's men, infiltrating the temple containing Jai.
- **S5**：Jai is saved but Snakehead eludes them.
- **S6**：Two weeks later Snakehead captures Jai aboard a cargo boat in Hong Kong.
- **S7**：Eddie and a team of Hong Kong police engage and defeat several of Snakehead's men, but Snakehead escapes with Jai to Dublin, Ireland.
- **S8**：In Ireland, Eddie is assigned to help Interpol with the investigation, much to Watson's chagrin.
- **S9**：Eddie is also reunited with his girlfriend, a British agent named Nicole James .
- **S10**：By chance Eddie later encounters and apprehends one of Snakehead's top men, who confesses Jai is being held in the harbour.
- **S11**：Eddie, Watson, and Nicole move to rescue Jai, defeating several Snakehead agents in the process.
- **S12**：Eddie and Jai end up trapped inside a container, which is knocked into the water by one of Snakehead's men before they can be released.
- **S13**：Eddie keeps Jai alive by an inflatable tent, but himself drowns.
- **S14**：After being rescued, Jai uses his medallion on Eddie's body.
- **S15**：In the morgue, Watson is grieving over Eddie's body when Eddie suddenly appears beside him.
- **S16**：Eddie realizes Jai used the medallion to resurrect him, and his former body vanishes into nothingness.
- **S17**：Jai splits the medallion into its two halves, giving one of them to Eddie.
- **S18**：Snakehead's men appear in the hospital to recapture Jai and during the fight, Eddie discovers the medallion has also granted him superhuman strength and immortality.
- **S19**：Nicole looks after Jai but he is captured again by Snakehead.
- **S20**：At his castle hideout, Snakehead forces Jai to activate the medallion so he can gain its power, but with only one half of it, Snakehead only gains superhuman strength and remains mortal.
- **S21**：To steal the other half, Snakehead and his men attack Watson's family.
- **S22**：Watson's Chinese wife is revealed to be a police operative like him, much to Watson's surprise as he kept his job secret from his family.
- **S23**：Together they fight off the attack from Snakehead's men.
- **S24**：Eddie, Watson and Nicole learn the location of Snakehead's castle lair and go to finish him once and for all.
- **S25**：The operation runs smoothly at first, but Snakehead kills Nicole and becomes immortal.
- **S26**：He and Eddie engage in a vicious fight, until Eddie uses the medallion to take away the life it gave, destroying Snakehead.
- **S27**：Jai allows Eddie to use the medallion to resurrect Nicole, who also gains super-strength and immortality.
- **S28**：The two then run at superhuman speed into the distance as Jai enters another dimension through a portal, frightening Watson.

### Elizabeth Masterson

- **性格倾向**：面对无法理解的自身状态，她倾向坚持原有认知并参与求证（行为推断；S7、S9、S10）

### David Abbott

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-000f98a000cc5080` · 独立版本：`profile-c6bd0773aab387db`

- **性格倾向**：在他人不相信自己的情境中，他仍倾向坚持帮助伊丽莎白（行为推断；S11、S13）
- **核心动机**：他承认爱上伊丽莎白，并因此不愿让她被撤去生命支持（摘要直接叙述；S11、S16）
- **决策方式**：说服家属失败后，他转向亲自冒险，并寻求朋友协助行动（行为推断；S13、S14、S17）
- **情绪反应**：他尚在承受丧妻之痛，又因伊丽莎白醒来后忘记自己而心碎（摘要直接叙述；S2、S20）
- **关系模式**：即使未被认出，他仍通过照料屋顶花园延续对她的关怀（行为推断；S20、S21、S22）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：希望保住所爱之人的生命，却因此违背现有医疗安排并冒险

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Elizabeth Masterson , a young doctor whose work is her whole life, is seriously injured in a car accident while on her way to a blind date.
- **S2**：Three months later, David Abbott , a landscape architect recovering from the death of his wife, moves into the apartment that had been Elizabeth's.
- **S3**：Elizabeth begins to appear to David at the apartment.
- **S4**：However with ghostly properties and abilities it is clear something is not normal: She can suddenly appear and disappear, move through walls, and once takes over his actions.
- **S5**：When they meet, they are both surprised, as Elizabeth is not aware yet of her condition.
- **S6**：For the most part, David is the only one who can see Elizabeth, leading others to believe that he is hallucinating and talking to himself.
- **S7**：At first, Elizabeth does not remember anything of her life, and refuses to believe that she is dead.
- **S8**：Her memories come back gradually.
- **S9**：Together, assisted by Zen-like psychic Darryl , she and David find out who she is, what happened to her, and why they are connected.
- **S10**：They find that her body is in a coma in the hospital.
- **S11**：When David discovers that in accordance with her living will, she will soon be taken off life support, he reluctantly tries to prevent this by telling Elizabeth sister that he can see her.
- **S12**：It is then revealed that one of Elizabeth's young nieces can also sense her presence although she cannot see her.
- **S13**：When Elizabeth's sister thinks that he is a lunatic and drives him away, David decides to prevent Elizabeth's death by stealing her body from the hospital.
- **S14**：He asks J.J, his friend to help him and it is discovered that J.J is actually the former boyfriend of Elizabeth's sister and he had set Elizabeth's blind date with David, the night of the accident.
- **S15**：The reason David is the only one who can see Elizabeth is that he was her blind date.
- **S16**：He also eventually admits that he loves Elizabeth and that is the reason he doesn't want her to die.
- **S17**：While stealing Elizabeth's body, they are eventually discovered in the hospital.
- **S18**：The body guards soon find them, pulling David away from Elizabeth's body.
- **S19**：He then realizes, that when he was pulled off he had her breathing tube in his hand; he then gets away from the guards, kisses Elizabeth's body and she miraculously wakes up.
- **S20**：However, the recovered Elizabeth doesn't remember anything that happened during the coma or any of the events with David, leaving him heartbroken.
- **S21**：Sometime later, Elizabeth goes up to the roof of her apartment, which has been transformed into a beautiful landscaped garden.
- **S22**：She sees David there, who had gotten in with the spare key he had kept in order to tend the garden.
- **S23**：Just as he is about to leave, she asks for her key back.
- **S24**：When their hands touch, her memory of the events during her coma are restored, and they kiss.

### Woody

- **性格倾向**：在受宠地位被取代时倾向嫉妒与争胜，急于重新获得关注（行为推断；S3、S4、S7、S8）

- **成长变化**：从排挤巴斯转向鼓励和营救他，最终建立相互支持的伙伴关系（摘要直接叙述；S7、S20、S21、S24、S27、S32）

### Buzz Lightyear

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5c29e01bda08fe56` · 独立版本：`profile-19165d642b1f0a7d`

- **性格倾向**：在太空骑警身份认知下倾向自信行动，也容易误认周围事物（行为推断；S5、S12）
- **核心动机**：最初以返回母星为行动目标，接受玩具身份后转向与伍迪返家（摘要直接叙述；S5、S11、S12、S20、S22）
- **情绪反应**：发现自己只是玩具并尝试飞行失败后，一度沮丧而无法合作（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17）
- **价值取舍**：恢复信心后倾向珍惜伙伴安危，愿意主动承担危险保护伍迪（行为推断；S20、S23、S27）
- **成长变化**：从依赖太空英雄身份确认意义，转向接受作为玩具带来的价值（行为推断；S5、S16、S17、S20）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：以为真实英雄身份赋予自己价值，身份破灭后须重新寻找意义。

- **S1**：Woody is a pull-string cowboy doll and leader of a group of toys that belong to a boy named Andy Davis, which act lifeless when humans are present.
- **S2**：With his family moving homes one week before his birthday, the toys stage a reconnaissance mission to discover Andy's new presents.
- **S3**：Andy receives a space ranger Buzz Lightyear action figure, whose impressive features see him replacing Woody as Andy's favorite toy.
- **S4**：Woody is resentful, especially as Buzz also gets attention from the other toys.
- **S5**：However Buzz believes himself to be a real space ranger on a mission to return to his home planet, as Woody fails to convince him he is a toy.
- **S6**：Andy prepares for a family outing at the space themed Pizza Planet restaurant with Buzz.
- **S7**：Woody attempts to be picked by misplacing Buzz.
- **S8**：He intends to trap Buzz in a gap behind Andy's desk, but the plan goes disastrously wrong when he accidentally knocks Buzz out the window, resulting in him being accused of murdering Buzz out of jealousy.
- **S9**：With Buzz missing, Andy takes Woody to Pizza Planet, but Buzz climbs into the car and confronts Woody when they stop at a gas station.
- **S10**：The two fight and fall out of the car, which drives off and leaves them behind.
- **S11**：Woody spots a truck bound for Pizza Planet and plans to rendezvous with Andy there, convincing Buzz to come with him by telling him it will take him to his home planet.

- **S13**：Inside, he finds squeaky aliens who revere the claw arm as their master.

- **S15**：At Sid's house, the two attempt to escape before Andy's moving day, encountering Sid’s nightmarish toy creations and his vicious dog, Scud.
- **S16**：Buzz sees a commercial for Buzz Lightyear action figures and realizes that he really is a toy.
- **S17**：Attempting to fly to test this, Buzz falls and loses one of his arms, going into depression and unable to cooperate with Woody.
- **S18**：Woody waves Buzz’s arm from a window to seek help from the toys in Andy’s room, but they are horrified thinking Woody attacked him, while Woody realizes Sid's toys are friendly when they reconnect Buzz's arm.
- **S19**：Sid prepares to destroy Buzz by strapping him to a rocket, but is delayed that evening by a thunderstorm.
- **S20**：Woody convinces Buzz that life is worth living because of the joy he can bring to Andy, which helps Buzz regain his spirit.
- **S21**：Cooperating with Sid's toys, Woody rescues Buzz and scares Sid away by coming to life in front of him, warning him to never torture toys again.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] The book's title is derived from a lawyer who wins spectacular cases and earns huge sums in damages.
- **S3**：Its protagonist dreams of becoming such a "rainmaker" – which comes true, but not in the way he expected.
- **S4**：Rudy Baylor is a third-year law student at Memphis State .
- **S5**：During an outreach session for a class in elder law, he signs two clients.
- **S6**：One is Miss Birdie, an 80-year-old woman who needs a revised will drawn.
- **S7**：The other is a poor family, Dot and Buddy Black, whose leukemia -stricken son, Donny Ray, could have been saved by a bone marrow transplant for which his identical twin brother is a perfect match.
- **S8**：The procedure should have been covered and paid for by their insurance carrier, Great Benefit Life Insurance, but the claim was instead denied.
- **S9**：Weeks before graduation, Rudy loses his upcoming job after his employers are bought out by Tinley Britt, a powerful Memphis law firm that represents Great Benefit.
- **S10**：After failing to secure a position with another firm, Rudy is recruited by J.
- **S11**：Lyman "Bruiser" Stone, a ruthless but successful ambulance chaser with corrupt business dealings.
- **S12**：To earn his fee, Rudy is required to hunt for potential clients at the local hospital and sign them up to personal injury lawsuits.
- **S13**：He is mentored by Deck Shifflet, a less-than-ethical former insurance assessor who received a law degree but failed the bar exam six times.
- **S14**：While studying for the bar exam in the hospital cafeteria, Rudy meets Kelly Riker, a beautiful teenage patient recovering from injuries inflicted by her husband, Cliff.
- **S15**：Though they quickly fall for each other, Kelly is reluctant to file for divorce out of fear that Cliff might kill her if she did.
- **S16**：Rudy continues to stay in contact with Kelly after she is discharged, providing support and encouraging her to break free of her abusive relationship.
- **S17**：When Stone's firm is about to be raided by the police and the FBI , Rudy and Deck set up their own private practice and represent the Blacks in their lawsuit against Great Benefit.
- **S18**：The original judge is a lazy and incompetent conservative who makes his support of Great Benefit clear and was getting ready to dismiss the case for no valid legal reason, but he dies in his sleep and is replaced by a liberal African-American star attorney--who makes it very clear to Great Benefit that the case will move forward and they had better focus on their legal arguments because their patron and his bias aren't coming back.
- **S19**：Donny Ray dies just before the case goes to trial, and Rudy uncovers a scheme by Great Benefit to deny every insurance claim regardless of validity.
- **S20**：The company was playing the odds that the insured would not consult an attorney.
- **S21**：The trial ends with the jury awarding the plaintiffs an unprecedented sum of $50.2 million - a sensational verdict making the headlines and catapulting Rudy into the limelight.
- **S22**：However, Great Benefit declares bankruptcy days after its parent corporation smuggles the company's assets out of the country, leaving it an empty shell.
- **S23**：Ultimately, there is no payout for the grieving parents and no fee for Rudy, though Dot takes greater satisfaction in the belief that she put Great Benefit out of business.
- **S24**：When Kelly is beaten again by Cliff during the trial, Rudy shelters her in a safe house for abused women and files for divorce on her behalf.
- **S25**：As they secretly attempt to retrieve items from her old apartment, Cliff ambushes them with a baseball bat, but Rudy manages to snatch the bat away and fatally wounds him in the struggle.
- **S26**：Kelly instructs Rudy to leave and allows herself to be charged with manslaughter .
- **S27**：Cliff's family threatens him and demands that the local prosecutor jail him and Kelly, but after Rudy outlines how he'll let the grand jury see the evidence of Cliff's brutality, the prosecutor (who is presented as an upstanding lawyer) decides to not file any charges against Rudy or Kelly.
- **S28**：With no remaining cases and the imminent threat of Cliff's family, Rudy and Kelly leave Memphis to begin a new life together, heading for someplace where Rudy – who has become disillusioned with the law – can become a teacher , and Kelly can attend college .

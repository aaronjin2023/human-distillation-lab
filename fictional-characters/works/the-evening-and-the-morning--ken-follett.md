## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Characters
- **S2**：[ edit ] Perspective characters [ edit ] Ragna : Daughter of Count Hubert of Cherbourg who travels to England to marry Ealdorman Wilwulf of Shiring.
- **S3**：Aldred : A young monk who wishes to make Shiring a center of learning by creating a vast library.
- **S4**：Edgar : The son of a boatbuilder who shows talent in several forms of construction.
- **S5**：Bishop Wynstan : A greedy bishop who uses his wit to increase his family's wealth and social status.
- **S6**：Other characters [ edit ] Ealdorman Wilwulf (also referred to as Wilf ): The older half-brother of Wynstan and Wigelm and ealdorman of Shiring.
- **S7**：He marries Ragna against the king's will after falling in love with her on a trip to Cherbourg.
- **S8**：Wigelm : The younger brother of Wynstan and the half-brother of Wilwulf, he is a coarse but accomplished warrior.
- **S9**：Blod : An enslaved Welsh teenager whom Edgar meets at Dreng's Ferry.
- **S10**：The book tracks her through childbirth, escape attempts, and other changes in her situation.
- **S11**：Dreng : A lazy and selfish ferryman and innkeeper distantly related to the Wynstan brothers.
- **S12**：King Ethelred : A King of England preoccupied with Viking raids.
- **S13**：At the time of the book, he is shown as a middle-aged, experienced king who can use his limited power effectively.
- **S14**：## Plot Summary
- **S15**：[ edit ] In the aftermath of a Viking attack on the coastal town of Combe, a young boatbuilder named Edgar moves with his remaining family to Dreng's Ferry.
- **S16**：This tiny village has an alehouse, a small minster , and a nunnery on a nearby island where nuns care for lepers .
- **S17**：A young Norman noblewoman named Ragna travels to England to marry Aelderman Wilwulf of Shiring only to find she is his second wife.
- **S18**：He had put his first wife aside, angering both the king and the clergy (with the exception of his own half brother, Bishop Wynstan).
- **S19**：A monk, Aldred, advises Ragna, who decides to make the best of things.
- **S20**：She takes control of the Aelderman's household and her own lands in England.
- **S21**：This annoys Wynstan, who had been skimming money from her rents.
- **S22**：It also annoys his mother (Wilwulf's stepmother), who had been running the household.
- **S23**：Ragna builds a reputation as a just and capable noblewoman and landlord and has three sons with Wilwulf.
- **S24**：Edgar builds a new ferry and befriends a Welsh girl enslaved to Dreng.
- **S25**：Because Dreng prostitutes her, she gives birth to a baby.
- **S26**：Dreng commits infanticide by throwing the newborn into the nearby river despite protest from Blod, Edgar, and both of Dreng's wives.
- **S27**：Edgar gives evidence against Dreng at the local court, but Dreng goes unpunished.
- **S28**：Blod attempts to flee back to Wales but is eventually recaptured.
- **S29**：Over the next several years, Aldred works with Edgar, Ragna, or both to address local problems, including bandits , corruption at the minster, acquisition of holy relics for the monastery, problems in the villages that pay taxes to Ragna, and attempts by Bishop Wynstan to produce counterfeit coinage.
- **S30**：After Wynstan escapes punishment for counterfeiting, Aldred is sent away from Shiring's large monastery, where he had had some authority, to run the tiny minster at Dreng's Ferry.
- **S31**：With Edgar's help, he slowly transforms the minster into a dutiful monastery.
- **S32**：Edgar and Ragna develop mutual affection.
- **S33**：Edgar builds a pontoon bridge at Dreng's Ferry so the monks can hold a market to raise funds, but Wynstan and Dreng secretly burn it down .
- **S34**：Wilwulf returns from a battle with a serious head injury, and Ragna rules in his place while he recovers.
- **S35**：Wynstan, Wigelm, and their mother decide to murder Wilwulf so Wigelm can replace him as Aelderman.
- **S36**：Wigelm and his men go to her villages and kill local elders loyal to her.
- **S37**：Edgar witnesses the violence and escapes, and brings Ragna a warning and her funds.
- **S38**：Wynstan and Wigelm kidnap Ragna and her sons before she can flee to Cherbourg.
- **S39**：Wigelm rapes Ragna while she is held prisoner.
- **S40**：Meanwhile, the king and his army attempt to pass through Dreng's Ferry to fight the Vikings.
- **S41**：The king, who had chosen the route because of the bridge, is angry to find there is only a slow ferry.
- **S42**：The king tells the monks to build a new bridge, which will be "the king's bridge" and under his protection.
- **S43**：Although Edgar and Aldred search for Ragna, they do not figure out where she is until Wynstan and Wigelm are ready to let her go.
- **S44**：By then, she is pregnant with Wigelm's child.
- **S45**：Ragna asks the king to name her regent, or acting Aelderman, until her oldest son can take Wilwulf's place, but the king needs a reliable military leader to help against the Vikings.
- **S46**：He names Wigelm the new Aelderman.
- **S47**：Ragna gives birth to a baby boy, whom Wigelm tries to claim as his heir.
- **S48**：The king tells Ragna that he will not force her to marry Wigelm, but he will not take the boy away from Wigelm either.
- **S49**：Wigelm and his mother coerce Ragna into marrying him, which neutralizes her politically.
- **S50**：Edgar leaves England for France .
- **S51**：He finds work as a builder for an Italian who is building a cathedral .
- **S52**：Wynstan seeks to become archbishop and makes inroads in religious communities.
- **S53**：Ragna focuses on her villages and raising her four children.
- **S54**：She learns through other women that Wynstan is showing signs of an illness called "whores' leprosy" (possibly neurosyphilis ).
- **S55**：She realizes she cannot tell Aldred this information because Wynstan will retaliate against her.
- **S56**：Aldred decides to build a new monastery cathedral to replace the crumbling minster.
- **S57**：He sends monks to France to offer Edgar the job.
- **S58**：They find him, but he asks if Lady Ragna is still married to Wigelm and declines to return.
- **S59**：After an argument, Wigelm takes their son away from Ragna and gives him to his girlfriend and mother to raise.
- **S60**：Wynstan develops more severe symptoms of whores' leprosy, including memory lapses and moments of rage and confusion.
- **S61**：Nonetheless, he pursues the archbishopric .
- **S62**：Ragna, realizing she had nothing left to lose, tells Aldred the reason for Wynstan's erratic behaviour.
- **S63**：Aldred tells enough other clergy and Wynstan loses the nomination.
- **S64**：Wynstan plans revenge but becomes confused.
- **S65**：Wigelm becomes angry with Ragna and puts her aside.
- **S66**：Ragna moves with her three older sons to Dreng's Ferry, then called Kingsbridge, and befriends Blod.
- **S67**：By then, Dreng has a new enslaved girl whom he abuses and prostitutes.
- **S68**：Blod and Ragna notice that the new girl is pregnant long before Dreng does.
- **S69**：By then, only one of Dreng's wives is still alive.
- **S70**：When Dreng discovers that the slave girl is pregnant, he begins to beat her.
- **S71**：When Blod and Dreng's wife interfere, Dreng has a heart attack .
- **S72**：Ragna makes sure no one can blame Blod for Dreng's death.
- **S73**：Dreng's wife promises to free Blod and the other girl in her will , which Ragna has her dictate to Aldred in front of witnesses.
- **S74**：When Dreng's wife dies, Kingsbridge finds she has left the alehouse to Blod.
- **S75**：Blod decides to stay in Kingsbridge as a free woman.
- **S76**：While Ragna is visiting one of her villages, Wigelm and his men pass through.
- **S77**：That night, a very drunk Wigelm tries to attack Ragna again.
- **S78**：She fights back until Wigelm trips and falls.
- **S79**：She holds his mouth and nose shut while he suffocates in his own vomit.
- **S80**：She drags his body into the canal.
- **S81**：The next day, the people believe that Wigelm fell into the canal accidentally.
- **S82**：Ragna rides to Shiring and takes her youngest son back from Wigelm's mother.
- **S83**：In France, Count Hubert calls for Edgar demanding that he explain the English custom of putting a wife aside, telling Edgar that Wigelm has insulted Ragna by divorcing her.
- **S84**：Edgar returns to England, and he and Ragna reunite and marry.
- **S85**：Wynstan continues to deteriorate.
- **S86**：Aldred brings him to the leper colony near Kingsbridge, where the nuns care for him alongside other sick and mentally ill people.

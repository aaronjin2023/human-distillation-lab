## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Retired LAPD homicide detective Harry Bosch attends the funeral of John Jack Thompson, a retired detective who mentored him decades earlier.
- **S3**：Thompson's widow gives Bosch a murder book about John Hilton, a 24-year-old ex-con shot dead near Melrose Avenue in 1990.
- **S4**：Bosch leaves it at Hollywood Station for Renée Ballard, assigned to overnight duty after filing a sexual harassment complaint against Captain Robert Olivas.
- **S5**：Ballard doubts the theory that Hilton died during a drug robbery.
- **S6**：Detectives never questioned Elvin Kidd, a Rolling 60s Crips street boss, and let a narcotics handler question informant Dennard Dorsey.
- **S7**：Blackened interview lines raise Ballard's suspicions about Thompson.
- **S8**：Dorsey says Kidd visited the alley on the murder date and ordered dealers to leave.
- **S9**：Hilton's former roommate, Nathan Brazil, says Hilton loved a man who protected him behind bars.
- **S10**：Bosch reviews a case for his half brother, defense attorney Mickey Haller.
- **S11**：Haller's client, Jeffrey Herstadt, faces charges for killing Judge Walter Montgomery.
- **S12**：Bosch learns that EMT Albert Morales used one pulse oximeter on both men within an hour, allowing DNA transfer.
- **S13**：Morales testifies, and prosecutors drop the charges.
- **S14**：Bosch tells Haller he has chronic myeloid leukemia, likely caused by exposure to stolen radioactive caesium twelve years earlier.
- **S15**：He asks Haller to seek compensation for his daughter, Maddie.
- **S16**：A homeless man killed in a tent fire is named Edison Banks Jr., heir to a San Diego shipping fortune.
- **S17**：Ballard supplies video of a woman buying vodka before the fire, then uses Olivas's signature on a form to obtain a wiretap on Kidd's phones.
- **S18**：Kidd hires Marcel Dupree to kill Dorsey in jail.
- **S19**：Ballard arrests Dupree, finds a hidden gun, protects Dorsey, and records Kidd discussing Hilton's killing.
- **S20**：Prosecutor Selma Robinson charges Kidd.
- **S21**：His wife Cynthia points a gun at Ballard during his arrest; an officer shoots her.
- **S22**：Kidd flees, but Ballard catches him.
- **S23**：Bosch finds Hilton's birth record naming Thompson as his father.
- **S24**：Thompson concealed his son's case, and detectives may have hidden the truth to protect him.
- **S25**：Ballard and Bosch tie Banks's death to law firm Michaelson & Mitchell.
- **S26**：They spot a woman at multiple crime scenes by her inward-turning walk.
- **S27**：Bosch enters lawyer Clayton Manley's office and finds a file marked TRANSFER.
- **S28**：Manley falls from a roof; an assassin kills partner Samuel Mitchell and admits Michaelson ordered the deaths.
- **S29**：She stabs Ballard but escapes, and Ballard survives surgery.
- **S30**：The fingerprints name the killer Catarina Cava.
- **S31**：Michaelson is arrested while fleeing; Ballard arrests Cava in Las Vegas .
- **S32**：Olivas conceals Ballard's forged signature.
- **S33**：Bosch shares a file on a 1982 student murder, and Ballard agrees to revisit it with him.

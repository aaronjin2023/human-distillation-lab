### Tripper Harrison

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b6dfdf72ed32f0e6` · 独立版本：`profile-bdb31f000daf208d`

- **性格倾向**：在营地生活中，他倾向以顽皮玩笑活跃气氛，也留意孤立者（行为推断；S3、S9）
- **价值取舍**：他强调输赢不应决定营员的自我评价与集体士气（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **决策方式**：他通过持续陪练建立信心，再把关键比赛机会交给鲁迪（行为推断；S3、S4、S20、S21）
- **关系模式**：他与鲁迪形成双向支持，既给予引导也接受感情上的鼓励（摘要直接叙述；S3、S4）

矛盾与变化：反复捉弄营地主任，却对难以融入的孩子表现关怀

### Rudy Gerner

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-f301fe05e6ae4946` · 独立版本：`profile-eeaba421526c41d8`

- **性格倾向**：在获得稳定陪伴后，他呈现愿意投入练习并回应支持的倾向（行为推断；S3、S4、S21）
- **情绪反应**：他起初孤独且难以融入，随后在特里珀帮助下逐渐获得信心（摘要直接叙述；S2、S3、S4）
- **关系模式**：他并非只接受照顾，也主动鼓励特里珀追求与罗克珊的关系（摘要直接叙述；S4）
- **成长变化**：他从难以融入的营员成长为能够在关键比赛中贡献力量的人（行为推断；S2、S3、S20、S21）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、决策方式、沟通方式

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film follows the antics of head counsellor Tripper Harrison  and the counsellors-in-training  at Camp North Star, a cut-rate summer camp.
- **S2**：The main plot involves Rudy Gerner , a lonely kid who is sent to summer camp by his father.
- **S3**：Noticing Rudy is unable to fit in, Tripper takes him under his wing and each morning they go jogging and bond as friends.
- **S4**：Tripper helps Rudy gain confidence while Rudy encourages Tripper to start a romance with Roxanne , the female head counsellor.
- **S5**：Candace  "kidnaps" Crockett  in a speedboat and confesses her feelings for him.
- **S6**：Wheels, who had broken up with A.L.  the year before, successfully rekindle their relationship during a dance.
- **S7**：The nerdy Spaz  develops a crush on Jackie.
- **S8**：Morty Melnick  is the North Star Camp director and the butt of the counsellors' practical jokes.
- **S9**：Tripper's favourite gag is invading Morty's cabin in the middle of the night and relocating the deep-sleeping director to unusual places such as in a tree or on the roadside.
- **S10**：A subplot deals with the camp's rivalry with the wealthy Camp Mohawk, located across the lake.
- **S11**：During a basketball game, North Star is being beaten by Mohawk when they attempt their own perverse form of victory.
- **S12**：This sets the stage for the yearly Olympiad held between the camps in which Mohawk carries a 12-0 record.
- **S13**：During the first day of competition, Mohawk dominates North Star, cheating in many cases to win.
- **S14**：Crockett fails to clear the high jump bar, Hardware gets pummeled in boxing, and Jackie suffers a broken leg in field hockey, thanks to the dirty work of two Mohawk girls.
- **S15**：The score at the end of Day One is: Mohawk-170, North Star-63.
- **S16**：That evening at the North Star Lodge, Tripper gives a rousing speech, telling demoralized campers that it doesn't matter whether they win or lose.
- **S17**：In unison, Camp North Star begins to chant, "It just doesn't matter!" Newly inspired, Day Two of the Olympiad belongs to North Star as they win every event.
- **S18**：Wheels outwrestles his opponent, Spaz defeats Rhino in a stacking contest with inspiration from Jackie and a thwarted Mohawk cheating attempt, and after 12 years of defeat Fink finally beats "The Stomach" in the hot dog eating contest.
- **S19**：North Star now trails by only 10 points with one event left, a 4-mile cross country run for 20 points.
- **S20**：Tripper steps forward and elects a surprised Rudy to compete against Horse , Mohawk's star runner.
- **S21**：The many mornings Rudy spent jogging and training with Tripper pay off as he wins the race, giving North Star its first Olympiad victory.
- **S22**：Later that evening, Morty, Tripper, Roxanne, and the CITs sing around a campfire and say their final goodbyes as the camp prepares to close for the summer.
- **S23**：Roxanne agrees to come live with Tripper and, on a motorcycle, the two of them lead the buses out of camp.
- **S24**：The movie ends with Morty in bed on a raft in the lake, then stepping sleepily into the water.

## 角色资料

### Lightning McQueen

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-0a8aeddea68b3fb5` · 独立版本：`profile-d7e8377bb12eb7b4`

- **性格倾向**：起初在竞赛压力下倾向急躁逐利，容易忽略他人的承受能力（行为推断；S4、S5、S10）
- **核心动机**：最初渴望借冠军摆脱不起眼的赞助商，进入更有利可图的车队（摘要直接叙述；S4）
- **价值取舍**：最终将对老赞助商的感激置于更优厚的商业机会之上（摘要直接叙述；S22）
- **决策方式**：目睹对手遭遇危险事故后主动放弃胜利，转而帮助对方完赛（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **成长变化**：从只顾个人晋升转向珍惜朋友，并愿意回馈接纳自己的小镇（行为推断；S4、S14、S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：早期追逐冠军与商业地位，后来主动让胜并拒绝更高待遇

### Doc Hudson

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-9ff4e97a62ec816a` · 独立版本：`profile-6cabaf23e8a12624`

- **性格倾向**：面对赛车名人进入小镇时倾向防备，起初不愿与其建立联系（行为推断；S8、S9、S15）
- **核心动机**：向媒体透露麦昆行踪，是为了避免自己的身份被发现（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **决策方式**：面对莎莉要求修路的主张虽不情愿，仍接受由麦昆承担补偿（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **关系模式**：后来从驱逐麦昆转为亲自支持比赛，以经验承担指导角色（行为推断；S8、S17、S18）
- **成长变化**：从隐藏赛车身份转向重新穿上旧涂装，主动参与赛场协作（摘要直接叙述；S13、S15、S17）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：希望隐藏往昔身份，却最终以旧赛车形象回到公众赛场

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Cars takes place in a world populated by anthropomorphic transportation.
- **S2**：The film begins with the last race of the Piston Cup championship, which ends in a three-way tie between retiring veteran Strip "The King" Weathers, perennial runner-up Chick Hicks, and rookie Lightning McQueen.
- **S3**：The tiebreaker race is scheduled for one week later at the fictional Los Angeles International Speedway in California.
- **S4**：Lightning is desperate to win the race, since it would allow him to leave the unglamorous sponsorship of Rust-Eze, a rust treatment for old cars, and allow him to take The King's place as the sponsored car of the lucrative Dinoco team.
- **S5**：Eager to start practice in California as soon as possible, Lightning pushes his big rig, Mack, to travel all night long.
- **S6**：While McQueen is sleeping, the exhausted Mack drifts off and is startled by a gang of reckless street racers, causing McQueen to fall out the back of the truck into the road.
- **S7**：McQueen wakes in the middle of traffic, and speeds off the highway to find Mack, ending up in a run-down town of Radiator Springs and inadvertently ruining the pavement of its main road.
- **S8**：After being arrested and impounded overnight, McQueen is ordered by the town's judge and doctor, Doc Hudson, to leave town immediately.
- **S9**：The local lawyer, Sally Carrera, insists that McQueen be given community service to repave the road, to which Doc begrudingly agrees.
- **S10**：McQueen tries to repave it in a single day, but it turns out to be shoddy and McQueen is ordered to repave the road again, which takes several days to complete.
- **S11**：During this time, he becomes friends with several of the cars, and learns that Radiator Springs used to be a popular stopover along U.S.
- **S12**：Route 66, but with the construction of Interstate 40 bypassing the town, it was effectively taken off the map.
- **S13**：McQueen also discovers that Doc is really the "Fabulous Hudson Hornet", a three-time Piston Cup winner who was forced out of racing after an accident and quickly forgotten by the sport.
- **S14**：McQueen finishes the road, which has invigorated the cars to improve their town, and spends an extra day in town with his new friends, before Mack and the media descend on the town, led by a tip to McQueen's location.
- **S15**：McQueen reluctantly leaves with the media to get to California in time for the race, while Sally chastises Doc after discovering that he had tipped off the media to McQueen's whereabouts, not wanting to be discovered himself instead.
- **S16**：At the speedway, McQueen's mind is not fully set on the race, and he soon falls into last place.
- **S17**：He is surprised to discover that Doc Hudson, decked out in his old racing colors, has taken over as his crew chief, along with several other friends from Radiator Springs to help in the pit.
- **S18**：Inspired and recalling tricks he learned from Doc and his friends, McQueen quickly emerges to lead the race into the final laps.
- **S19**：Hicks, refusing to lose, sends Weathers into a dangerous accident.
- **S20**：Seeing this and recalling Doc's fate, McQueen stops just short of the finish line, allowing Hicks to win, and drives back to push Weathers over the finish line.
- **S21**：The crowd and media condemn Hicks' victory and give praise to McQueen's sportsmanship.
- **S22**：Though offered the Dinoco sponsorship deal, McQueen declines, insisting on staying with his current sponsors as an appreciation of their past support.
- **S23**：Later, back at Radiator Springs, McQueen returns and announces that he will be setting up his headquarters there, helping to put Radiator Springs back on the map.
- **S24**：In a post-credits scene, Van and Minny, the two minivans who came to Radiator Springs, appear to have lost their way and are stranded in the middle of the desert, dusty and tired , and Van is seen to have gone insane and drive off as a "Beetle" fly crashes into the screen.

## 角色资料

### Dr. Jo Harding

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-51974331f186690c` · 独立版本：`profile-a17404a4ee8f67e4`

- **性格倾向**：在追风研究中，她表现出执着投入且容易冒险的倾向（行为推断；S17、S18、S19）
- **核心动机**：父亲死于龙卷风的经历，使她执着于阻止此类灾难（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S19）
- **决策方式**：面对投放难题，她倾向从身边物件寻找可实际改造的办法（行为推断；S24、S25）
- **关系模式**：她与比尔的关系仍牵动彼此，最终选择共同研究并修复婚姻（摘要直接叙述；S19、S21、S32）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：消除灾害的执念推动研究，也使她在追风中暴露于危险。

### Bill Harding

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-13698a1c43021ff1` · 独立版本：`profile-db00f9e931c8ec45`

- **性格倾向**：在同行竞争与险情中，他表现出好胜又愿意保护他人的倾向（行为推断；S10、S18、S26）
- **核心动机**：他决意帮助乔抢先投放装置，以免设计功劳被米勒抢走（摘要直接叙述；S10）
- **价值取舍**：面对直接危险，他倾向将人员安全置于竞争输赢之前（行为推断；S18、S26）
- **成长变化**：他从前来办理离婚，转向与乔重建婚姻和共同事业（摘要直接叙述；S5、S32）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：原本准备与他人结婚，却仍与乔保有未解的感情。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In June 1969, a family, including a five year-old girl named Jo, seek shelter in a storm cellar as a powerful F5 tornado strikes.
- **S2**：The storm is so strong that the storm cellar door is ripped off and Jo's father is killed.
- **S3**：Jo, her mother, and her dog Toby survive.
- **S4**：In the present day, Jo , a meteorologist, is reunited with her estranged husband, Bill Harding , a former weather researcher and storm chaser, who has since become a weather reporter.
- **S5**：He is planning to marry sex therapist Melissa Reeves , and needs Jo's signature on the divorce papers.
- **S6**：Jo has built four identical tornado research devices called DOROTHY, based on Bill's designs.
- **S7**：The device is designed to release hundreds of sensors into the center of a tornado to study its structure from the inside, with the purpose of creating a more advanced storm warning system.
- **S8**：Bill and Melissa join Jo and her team of storm chasers, and the team encounters Dr.
- **S9**：Jonas Miller , a smug meteorologist and storm chaser.
- **S10**：When Bill discovers that Jonas has created a device based on DOROTHY, called DOT-3, he vows to help Jo deploy DOROTHY before Miller can claim credit for the idea.
- **S11**：During the first tornado, Jo's truck and DOROTHY I are both destroyed.
- **S12**：They continue chasing in Bill's truck, with Melissa in the back seat.
- **S13**：They find a second tornado, a confirmed F2, and head off on a back road when it shifts its track.
- **S14**：They soon find themselves driving through heavy rain and Bill's truck is spun around until the tornadoes dissipate.
- **S15**：The team visits Jo's aunt Meg's house for food and rest.
- **S16**：They soon learn that a "hopping" F3 tornado is on the ground, but they have trouble finding it.
- **S17**：Jo drives ahead of the team to intercept the oncoming tornado, but a telephone pole falls on the back of Bill's truck and knocks DOROTHY II out onto the road, disabling it.
- **S18**：As the tornado lifts and touches down closer, Bill pulls an Jo into the truck and moves to safety.
- **S19**：The two confront each other over their marriage and Jo's obsession with stopping tornadoes, due to her father's death.
- **S20**：That following night, an F4 tornado devastates a drive-in cinema, forcing everyone to take shelter in a nearby warehouse.
- **S21**：Melissa is traumatized by the experience, and leaves, recognizing the unresolved feelings between Jo and Bill.
- **S22**：The tornado hits Wakita, devastating the town and injuring Meg while destroying her house.
- **S23**：After Bill and Jo rescue Meg from her collapsing house, they hear that an even stronger storm, an F5, is forming 25 miles south of their position.
- **S24**：Inspecting Meg's wind chime sculptures, Jo realizes that the most likely method to successfully deploy DOROTHY's sensors into a tornado would be to add additional body surface to catch the wind.
- **S25**：As they reach the F5, the team adds aluminum to work as wind flaps, but the deployment of DOROTHY III is a failure.
- **S26**：Meanwhile, Jonas attempts to deploy DOT-3, but after ignoring warnings from Bill, his truck is caught by the tornado and he and his driver are killed.
- **S27**：Jo and Bill set out on their own and are able to deploy the last DOROTHY successfully.
- **S28**：Their celebration is cut short, however, as the tornado shifts course towards them.
- **S29**：They take shelter in a shed where they anchor themselves to irrigation pipes.
- **S30**：The tornado destroys the shed and Jo and Bill find themselves in the vortex of the massive funnel.
- **S31**：After the F5 dies out, Jo and Bill find themselves alone on the floor of the former shed.
- **S32**：They then decide to run their own lab and rekindle their marriage.

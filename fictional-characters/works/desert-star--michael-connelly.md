## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Harry Bosch, a retired LAPD homicide detective, lines up pills on his kitchen table when Renée Ballard arrives.
- **S3**：She asks him to join a newly formed Open-Unsolved Unit handling cold homicide cases.
- **S4**：City Councilman Jake Pearlman had pushed for its creation after his sister Sarah was raped and murdered in 1994.
- **S5**：Bosch agrees, hoping to solve the Gallagher family murders, which have haunted him for years.
- **S6**：Ballard's team includes Paul Masser, a retired deputy district attorney , and Colleen Hatteras, a civilian genealogist.
- **S7**：Ted Rawls, a retired Santa Monica police officer , has joined at the request of Nelson Hastings, Pearlman's chief of staff .
- **S8**：Bosch examines Sarah Pearlman's case.
- **S9**：DNA recovered from tape used to lift a palm print matches evidence from the 2005 murder of Laura Wilson, an aspiring actress found strangled in her Hollywood apartment.
- **S10**：The blood in Wilson's urine indicated that her killer may have suffered from kidney or bladder disease.
- **S11**：Bosch also revisits the Gallagher murders.
- **S12**：Irish immigrant Stephen Gallagher ran an equipment rental business before he and his family disappeared in 2013.
- **S13**：His associate Finbar McShane sold company assets worth more than $800,000 before vanishing.
- **S14**：The family's remains were found a year later, with a nail gun from Gallagher's warehouse identified as the murder weapon.
- **S15**：Bosch questions former office manager Sheila Walsh and learns that her son, rather than McShane, committed a burglary at her home.
- **S16**：Ballard finds a campaign button bearing Pearlman's name among Wilson's belongings, former campaign manager Sandy Kramer confirms that Hastings and Rawls participated in Pearlman's 2005 campaign.
- **S17**：Bosch obtains medical records indicating Hastings had undergone kidney removal, making him a suspect.
- **S18**：However, DNA collected from Hastings at a coffee shop does not match the evidence.
- **S19**：Hastings admits he donated his kidney to Rawls, whose cancer had destroyed both kidneys.
- **S20**：Bosch tracks Rawls to Santa Monica .
- **S21**：Rawls flees by car, forcing Bosch into a pursuit.
- **S22**：After Rawls spins Bosch's vehicle off course, he shoots Bosch in the ear.
- **S23**：Bosch fires back, wounding Rawls, who then kills himself.
- **S24**：A box in Rawls's trunk contains souvenirs taken from victims.
- **S25**：DNA and palm prints confirm his guilt in both murders.
- **S26**：Ballard finds another box in an alley dumpster containing a bracelet belonging to Olga Reyes, whose boyfriend Jorge Ochoa was convicted of her murder in 2009.
- **S27**：The evidence points to Rawls as the killer, but prosecutors decline to act because the chain of custody is inadequate.
- **S28**：Ballard asks Bosch's half-brother, defense attorney Mickey Haller, to seek Ochoa's release through a habeas corpus petition.
- **S29**：Bosch learns McShane is living in Key West under the name Davy Byrne.
- **S30**：He travels there alone and kills McShane during a confrontation, closing the Gallagher case.
- **S31**：Ballard later finds Bosch in a hotel room.
- **S32**：He admits that radioactive caesium exposure has given him cancer affecting his bone marrow.
- **S33**：He has kept counterfeit fentanyl pills to end his life if his illness becomes unbearable.
- **S34**：Ballard urges him to tell his daughter Maddie, and he agrees.
- **S35**：At the Mojave gravesite, Bosch scatters the Gallagher family's ashes beside four rebuilt rock columns.
- **S36**：He declines to return to Ballard's unit.
- **S37**：As they leave, he compares Ballard to the desert star flowers growing near the graves, recalling a friend's description of their resilience.
- **S38**：They drive back toward the city.

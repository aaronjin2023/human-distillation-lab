### Woody

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3fa417a19177a9f1` · 独立版本：`profile-e9f56ef3b80cef42`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴拒绝相信自己后，他仍表现出重返险地帮助他们的倾向。（行为推断；S5、S19、S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：他起初努力回到安迪身边，后来转而协助伙伴逃离托儿所。（行为推断；S7、S19、S21）
- **价值取舍**：面对曾加害自己的洛抱熊遇险，他仍选择与巴斯一起救援。（行为推断；S29、S32）
- **决策方式**：安排伙伴的新归宿时，他倾向依据亲身见到的善待作出选择。（行为推断；S9、S38、S39）

矛盾与变化：他原本坚持返回安迪身边，最终主动促成大家转交邦妮。

### Lots-O'-Huggin' Bear

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-4554c043afee71c6` · 独立版本：`profile-f8788fe9c0cf0bcb`

- **性格倾向**：在维持托儿所秩序时，他表现出控制他人并限制离开的倾向。（行为推断；S14、S18）
- **核心动机**：被大宝宝扔入垃圾箱后，他出于报复将伍迪拖入其中。（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29）
- **价值取舍**：在获得逃生机会时，他把自身脱险置于救命者的生死之上。（行为推断；S32、S33）
- **决策方式**：掌控托儿所时，他倾向借助手下与被重置的巴斯囚禁异议者。（行为推断；S11、S14）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他获伍迪与巴斯救援后，仍在能够按下停止按钮时弃众人而去。

## 材料限制

- 洛抱熊被遗落、被替换及后来掌控托儿所均有记载，但摘要不足以把它们确定为单一心理因果。
- 巴斯经历多次外部重置，不能把不同模式下的表现合并为稳定人格；本版未据此扩展角色。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Andy, now nearly 18 years old,{{cite web}} is leaving for college, and his toys feel like they have been abandoned as they have not been played with for years.
- **S2**：Andy decides to take Woody with him to college and puts Buzz Lightyear and the rest of the toys in a trash bag for storage in the attic.
- **S3**：However, the toys are accidentally thrown out when Andy's mom finds the bag and puts it out on the curb, causing the toys to think that they are no longer wanted.
- **S4**：They escape and decide to climb in a donation box for Sunnyside Daycare.
- **S5**：Woody, the only toy who saw what actually happened, follows the other toys and tries to explain that they were thrown out by mistake, but they refuse to believe him.
- **S6**：Andy's toys are welcomed by the many toys at Sunnyside and given a tour of the seemingly perfect play-setting by Lots-O'-Huggin' Bear , Big Baby and Ken, whom Barbie falls for.
- **S7**：All of the toys love their new home, leaving a steadfast Woody alone in an attempt to return to Andy.
- **S8**：Woody's escape attempt falls short and he is found outside by Bonnie, an imaginative little girl.
- **S9**：She takes him home and plays with him along with her other toys, who are well-treated, happy, and readily welcome Woody.
- **S10**：At the daycare, Andy's toys get beaten up by the rambunctious youngest toddlers.
- **S11**：Buzz goes to ask Lotso to transport him and the other toys to a better room, only to be caught by Lotso's henchmen and restored back to his original space ranger persona.
- **S12**：At the same time, Andy's toys realize that Woody was right about Andy when Mrs.
- **S13**：Potato Head sees Andy searching for them through her missing eye, which was left behind in Andy's room.
- **S14**：Before they could leave, they are imprisoned by Lotso and his gang, including a reset Buzz.
- **S15**：Back at Bonnie's, Woody learns from one of the toys, named Chuckles the Clown, that Lotso was once a good toy and had an owner named Daisy who also owned Chuckles the Clown and Big Baby.
- **S16**：One day, Daisy left them behind on a picnic.
- **S17**：The three eventually find their way back to Daisy's house, only to find that she replaced Lotso with an identical teddy bear.
- **S18**：When he found Sunnyside, he and Big Baby took it over and ran it like a prison.
- **S19**：The following morning, Woody returns to Sunnyside through Bonnie's backpack.
- **S20**：He sneakily reaches his friends and tells them he is sorry for leaving them.
- **S21**：They quickly formulate an escape plan.
- **S22**：That night, Woody and Slinky sneak through Sunnyside to the main office, where Chatter informed them that a cymbal-banging monkey monitors the CCTV system to prevent toys escaping.
- **S23**：A brief fight ensues, ending with the Monkey wrapped in sticky tape and locked in a filing cabinet by Slinky.
- **S24**：Slinky signals to the other toys while Mr.
- **S25**：Potato head provides a diversion, they make their escape.
- **S26**：In the process, Buzz is accidentally reset into a Spanish mode, in which he becomes very flamboyantly chivalrous and his memory is wiped; despite this, Buzz allies himself with Woody's friends, and immediately falls in love with Jessie.
- **S27**：The toys reach a dumpster, but are caught by Lotso and his gang.
- **S28**：As a garbage truck approaches, Woody reveals what he heard about Lotso, and Big Baby throws Lotso into the dumpster.
- **S29**：Seeking revenge, Lotso pulls Woody in the dumpster just as the truck collects the trash.
- **S30**：Woody's friends jump into the back of the truck, trying to rescue him and a falling television hits Buzz when he tries to save Jessie, returning him to his normal self.
- **S31**：The toys find themselves at the dump and are pushed onto a conveyor belt leading to a garbage shredder.
- **S32**：Woody and Buzz save Lotso just in time as he is about to be shredded and Woody and the other toys end up on another conveyor belt, leading to an incinerator.
- **S33**：The toys help Lotso reach an emergency stop button, but he leaves them to their deaths.
- **S34**：Thinking that this is the end, the toys join hands and accept their fate but are rescued by the Aliens using a giant claw.
- **S35**：Lotso makes his way outside, but a passing truck driver finds him and, recognizing he had the toy as a kid, straps him to the radiator grill of his truck.
- **S36**：Meanwhile, Woody and his friends board another trash truck driven by an older Sid Phillips back to Andy's house.

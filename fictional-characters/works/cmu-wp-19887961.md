## 角色资料

### Hypatia

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-4f66dec5776ff940` · 独立版本：`profile-736706fd9c8e1fce`

- **性格倾向**：在宗教压力加剧的情境中，她仍倾向坚持独立求知与判断（行为推断；S16、S20、S23、S24）
- **核心动机**：她选择将人生精力投入科学，而非回应奥瑞斯忒斯的爱情（摘要直接叙述；S4）
- **价值取舍**：面对以改宗换取援助的条件，她拒绝用信仰顺从交换保护（行为推断；S23）
- **决策方式**：面对地球运动的疑问，她倾向以可观察的实验检验解释（行为推断；S19）
- **关系模式**：面对戴乌斯的侵犯与交剑，她最终以解除奴籍结束主奴关系（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：需要援助以应对宗教压力，却拒绝接受援助所附的改宗条件。

### Davus

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-8ad8fe7b01e0b370` · 独立版本：`profile-1ea8fe5debd56631`

- **性格倾向**：在宗教群体质疑科学时，他倾向回避公开争执以缓和冲突（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **关系模式**：他对希帕提娅怀有秘密爱意，但这种依恋也伴随着侵犯行为（摘要直接叙述；S5、S13）
- **价值取舍**：在科学兴趣与宗教归属之间，他的行动呈现难以协调的取舍（行为推断；S5、S12、S18）
- **核心动机**：得知暴徒准备杀人后，他试图抢先警告希帕提娅以阻止伤害（行为推断；S25、S26）
- **决策方式**：在暴徒外出取石时，他选择暗中杀死她并谎称她只是昏倒（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：对她有爱意并试图救护，也曾侵犯她并最终亲手致其死亡。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 391 AD, Alexandria, is part of the Roman Empire, and Greek philosopher Hypatia , is a teacher at the Platonic school, where future leaders are educated.
- **S2**：Hypatia is the daughter of Theon , the director of the Musaeum of Alexandria.
- **S3**：Hypatia, her father's slave, Davus , and two of her pupils, Orestes  and Synesius , are immersed in the changing political and social landscape.
- **S4**：She rejects Orestes's love, because she prefers to devote herself to science.
- **S5**：Davus assists Hypatia in her classes and is interested in science, and is also secretly in love with her.
- **S6**：Meanwhile, social unrest begins challenging the Roman rule of the city as pagans and Christians come into conflict.
- **S7**：When the Christians start defiling the statues of the pagan gods, the pagans, including Orestes and Hypatia's father, ambush the Christians to squash their rising influence.
- **S8**：However, in the ensuing battle, the pagans unexpectedly find themselves outnumbered by a large Christian mob.
- **S9**：Hypatia's father is gravely injured and Hypatia and the pagans take refuge in the Library of the Serapeum.
- **S10**：The Christian siege of the library ends when an envoy of the Roman Emperor declares that the pagans are pardoned, however the Christians shall be allowed to enter the library and do with it what they please.
- **S11**：Hypatia and the pagans flee, trying to save the most important scrolls, before the Christians overtake the library and destroy its contents.
- **S12**：Davus, chooses to join the Christian forces.
- **S13**：He later returns with a gladius and starts sexually assaulting her, but quickly offers his sword to her.
- **S14**：However, she removes his slave collar and tells him he is free.
- **S15**：Several years later, Orestes, now converted to Christianity, is prefect of Alexandria.
- **S16**：Hypatia continues to investigate the motions of the Sun, the Moon, the five known "wanderers" and the stars.
- **S17**：Some Christians ridicule the thinking that the Earth is a sphere, by arguing that people far from the top would fall off the Earth.
- **S18**：When they ask Davus his opinion he avoids conflict by saying that only God knows these things.
- **S19**：Hypatia also investigates the heliocentric model of the solar system proposed by Aristarchus of Samos; by having an object dropped from the mast of a moving ship she demonstrates to Orestes that a possible motion of the Earth would not affect the motion, relative to Earth, of a falling object on Earth.
- **S20**：However, due to religious objections against heliocentrism, the Christians have now forbidden Hypatia to teach at the school.
- **S21**：The Christians and the Jews come into conflict, committing violent acts against each other.
- **S22**：The leader of the Christians, Cyril , views Hypatia as having too much influence over Orestes and stages a public ceremony intended to force Orestes to subjugate her.
- **S23**：Hypatia's former pupil, Synesius, now the Bishop of Cyrene, comes to her rescue as a religious authority counterweight, but says he cannot help her unless she accepts Christianity; she refuses.
- **S24**：Hypatia makes a personal discovery, theorizing that the Earth orbits around the Sun in an elliptic, not circular, orbit with the Sun at one of the foci.
- **S25**：Cyril convinces a mob of Christians that Hypatia is a witch and they vow to kill her.
- **S26**：Davus tries to run ahead to warn Hypatia, but she is captured by the mob.
- **S27**：They strip Hypatia naked and are about to skin her alive until Davus persuades the mob otherwise, and they decide to stone her instead.
- **S28**：When everyone goes outside to collect stones, Davus secretly suffocates her and tells the mob that she fainted.
- **S29**：Davus leaves as they begin to stone her.

## 材料限制

- 摘要包含多层骗局与事后揭示，早期表象不能直接等同于人物真实意图。
- 凯瑟琳的康复与恋爱过程被高度压缩，未据此推断治疗体验或稳定心理特征。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] The novel begins at the end of The Other Side of Midnight , with Catherine Douglas recovering in a convent, knowing only her name.
- **S3**：Everyone except Constantin Demiris, known as Costa, thinks she was killed by her husband, Larry Douglas, and his mistress, Noelle Page.
- **S4**：Catherine requests to leave the convent to uncover her past, which Costa permits.
- **S5**：In Greece Catherine remembers Larry and Noelle trying to drown her, and tells Costa, her apparent benefactor.
- **S6**：Worried that she will realise they were wrongfully convicted, he sends her to London to work in one of his offices.
- **S7**：Frederick Stavros begins to feel guilty for sending Larry and Noelle to their death, as he was their lawyer and they were all tricked into pleading guilty by Napoleon Chotas.
- **S8**：He dies shortly after confiding in a priest, who then tells an employee of Spyros Lambrou, the brother of Costa's wife Melina.
- **S9**：Spyros Lambrou uses this news to destroy Costa, who has been mistreating his sister.
- **S10**：Fearing for his life, Chotas leaves a package with attorney Peter Demonides, and sends a tape to tell Costa this.
- **S11**：When Chotas apparently dies in a house-fire the next day, Peter delivers the package to Costa, rather than the authorities, and starts working for him.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, Catherine befriends Kirk Reynolds, who falls in love with her.
- **S13**：She tells him that her husband and his mistress were executed for her attempted murder.
- **S14**：Kirk is doubtful that Greek law would sentence anyone to death for attempted murder, but checks with his acquaintance, Peter Demonides, and dies the next day.
- **S15**：Spyros tries to destroy Costa by convincing drug dealer Tony Rizzoli to trick Costa into taking a drug shipment into the USA.
- **S16**：Costa kills Tony, destroys his shipment, and threatens to kill Melina.
- **S17**：Melina assures Spyros that she can take care of herself.
- **S18**：Spyros's house is attacked but he and his wife survive.
- **S19**：Melina finally believes that Costa wants to kill them, so kills herself and frames Costa for the murder.
- **S20**：Meanwhile, Costa has ordered the killing of Catherine.
- **S21**：Costa is arrested for Melina’s murder.
- **S22**：Only Sypros can give him an alibi for the murder, but he refuses.
- **S23**：Chotas reappears, in a wheelchair, having survived the house fire.
- **S24**：He defends Costa in court, convincing Spyros into testifying in return for all of Costa’s assets, arguing that forcing him into poverty would be worse than death.
- **S25**：Meanwhile, Costa and Chotas arrange for Costa’s assets to be transferred to a firm owned by Chotas, so Spyros will get nothing.
- **S26**：Catherine falls in love with her psychoanalyst, Alan Hamilton.
- **S27**：Three strange men arrive in London, and Catherine suspects them, but only at the last minute realizes that in fact it is their office boy who intends to kill her.
- **S28**：He locks her in the basement and turns up the thermostat of the boiler, which will explode when it reaches 400 °C (752 °F) .
- **S29**：She survives by hiding in a nearby bomb shelter, learns the truth about Costa and his conviction, and eventually marries Alan.
- **S30**：In the meantime, Costa is being tried for a murder he didn't commit, but Spyros testifies on the last day of the trial, freeing him.
- **S31**：On the drive home, Chotas tells Costa that even though he liked Noelle Page, he still helped kill her.
- **S32**：He also says he has donated all Costa’s assets to the convent, since he does not wish to continue living after what Costa did to him.
- **S33**：Finally, he drives the car off a cliff, killing Costa and himself.

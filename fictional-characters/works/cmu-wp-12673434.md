### Thor

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-f4e10c3b62e50976` · 独立版本：`profile-30a18990cbafbec3`

- **性格倾向**：面对冰霜巨人闯入时，表现出冲动好胜且违抗命令的倾向（行为推断；S3、S4、S6）
- **核心动机**：被放逐初期仍急于取回神锤，恢复原有力量与身份（行为推断；S6、S10）
- **价值取舍**：面对同伴受袭时，愿将自身安危置于他人生命之后（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **决策方式**：为阻止毁灭计划，愿主动切断自己返回恋人身边的道路（行为推断；S20、S22）
- **成长变化**：从准备继承王位的自信，转为承认自己尚未准备好（行为推断；S3、S6、S24）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：承诺返回Jane身边，却为阻止灾难毁掉通行桥梁。

### Loki

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-09736166f0b5e83e` · 独立版本：`profile-cd0f00d41b0d1531`

- **性格倾向**：在争取认可时倾向隐瞒真实意图，并以设局操纵他人（行为推断；S14、S21）
- **核心动机**：毁灭约顿海姆的计划，旨在向养父证明自己的资格（摘要直接叙述；S21）

- **决策方式**：倾向先假意结盟，再背叛盟友以制造行动的正当借口（行为推断；S14、S21）
- **情绪反应**：养父拒绝认可后，他以放手坠落回应这一情感打击（行为推断；S23）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望养父认可，却采用被养父拒绝的毁灭手段。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In AD 965, Odin, king of Asgard, wages war against the Frost Giants of Jotunheim and their leader Laufey, to prevent them from conquering the nine realms, starting with Earth.
- **S2**：The Asgardian warriors defeat the Frost Giants and seize the source of their power, the Casket of Ancient Winters.
- **S3**：In the present, Odin's son Thor prepares to ascend to the throne of Asgard, but is interrupted when Frost Giants attempt to retrieve the Casket.
- **S4**：Against Odin's order, Thor travels to Jotunheim to confront Laufey, accompanied by his brother Loki, childhood friend Sif and the Warriors Three: Volstagg, Fandral and Hogun.
- **S5**：A battle ensues until Odin intervenes to save the Asgardians, destroying the fragile truce between the two races.
- **S6**：For Thor's arrogance, Odin strips his son of his godly power and exiles him to Earth as a mortal, accompanied by his hammer Mjolnir, now protected by an enchantment that allows only the worthy to wield it.
- **S7**：Thor lands in New Mexico, where astrophysicist Jane Foster, her assistant Darcy Lewis and mentor Dr.
- **S8**：Erik Selvig, find him.
- **S9**：The local populace finds Mjolnir, which S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Phil Coulson soon commandeers before forcibly acquiring Jane's data about the wormhole that delivered Thor to Earth.
- **S10**：Thor, having discovered Mjolnir's nearby location, seeks to retrieve it from the facility that S.H.I.E.L.D. quickly constructed but he finds himself unable to lift it, and is captured.
- **S11**：With Selvig's help, he is freed and resigns himself to exile on Earth as he develops a romance with Jane.
- **S12**：Loki discovers that he is actually Laufey's son, adopted by Odin after the war ended.
- **S13**：A weary Odin falls into the deep "Odinsleep" to recover his strength.
- **S14**：Loki seizes the throne in Odin's stead and offers Laufey the chance to kill Odin and retrieve the Casket.
- **S15**：Sif and the Warriors Three, unhappy with Loki's rule, attempt to return Thor from exile, convincing Heimdall, gatekeeper of the Bifröst—the means of traveling between worlds—to allow them passage to Earth.
- **S16**：Aware of their plan, Loki sends the Destroyer, a seemingly indestructible automaton, to pursue them and kill Thor.
- **S17**：The warriors find Thor, but the Destroyer attacks and defeats them, prompting Thor to offer himself instead.
- **S18**：Struck by the Destroyer and near death, Thor's sacrifice proves him worthy to wield Mjolnir.
- **S19**：The hammer returns to him, restoring his powers and enabling him to defeat the Destroyer.
- **S20**：Kissing Jane goodbye and vowing to return, he and his fellow Asgardians leave to confront Loki.
- **S21**：In Asgard, Loki betrays and kills Laufey, revealing his true plan to use Laufey's attempt on Odin's life as an excuse to destroy Jotunheim with the Bifröst Bridge, thus proving himself worthy to his adoptive father.
- **S22**：Thor arrives and fights Loki before destroying the Bifröst Bridge to stop Loki's plan, stranding himself in Asgard.
- **S23**：Odin awakens and prevents the brothers from falling into the abyss created in the wake of the bridge's destruction, but Loki allows himself to fall when Odin rejects his pleas for approval.
- **S24**：Thor makes amends with Odin, admitting he is not ready to be king; while on Earth, Jane and her team search for a way to open a portal to Asgard.
- **S25**：In a post-credits scene, Selvig has been taken to a S.H.I.E.L.D. facility, where Nick Fury opens a briefcase and asks him to study a mysterious object, which Fury says may hold untold power.
- **S26**：An invisible Loki prompts Selvig to agree, and he does.

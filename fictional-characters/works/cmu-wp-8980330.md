## 角色资料

### WALL-E

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-269e38897c01ac42` · 独立版本：`profile-da9c072f35100853`

- **性格倾向**：在单调清理工作之外，他表现出探索人类遗物的好奇倾向（摘要直接叙述；S4、S35、S36）
- **核心动机**：受歌舞片启发，他渴望与伊芙牵手并建立亲密联系（摘要直接叙述；S7）
- **关系模式**：即使伊芙进入待机状态，他仍以陪伴和追随维系联系（行为推断；S9、S10）
- **决策方式**：误认伊芙遭受折磨时，他倾向立即介入而未充分理解现场（行为推断；S17）
- **价值取舍**：面对植物即将被毁的危险，他愿冒自身损毁风险加以保全（行为推断；S19、S20）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：保护伊芙的冲动有时造成混乱，也推动他承担救援风险。

### EVE

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-af1f439bafbd1433` · 独立版本：`profile-168ce11dbe3f1832`

- **性格倾向**：搜寻任务受挫时，她倾向急躁戒备并迅速动用武器（行为推断；S43、S45）
- **核心动机**：她最初以寻找地球植被并验证宜居条件为行动目标（摘要直接叙述；S6、S41）
- **情绪反应**：当修复后的瓦力失去原有情感时，她感到心碎并吻别他（摘要直接叙述；S29、S30）
- **决策方式**：得知修复零件仅在地球后，她把返航条件转化为救援路径（行为推断；S25、S27、S29）
- **成长变化**：她从冷淡排斥瓦力的接近，转向主动守护并挽回他的自我（行为推断；S7、S18、S25、S29、S30）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：起初因瓦力干扰任务而生气，后来返航任务又成为拯救他的关键。

### Captain B. McCrea

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-65acd2ce28c5c43c` · 独立版本：`profile-b36b360b670674e5`

- **性格倾向**：接触地球信息后，他表现出愿意钻研陌生生活的好奇倾向（行为推断；S22、S49）
- **核心动机**：了解地球现状后，他决心带领人类返回并修复共同家园（摘要直接叙述；S23）
- **价值取舍**：面对旧有禁返指令，他倾向把重建家园置于维持现状之上（行为推断；S23、S24、S26、S27）
- **决策方式**：在形成返航判断前，他主动结合历史资料与实地影像学习（行为推断；S22、S23）
- **成长变化**：他从依赖自动驾驶处理实权，转向亲自抗争并收回指挥权（摘要直接叙述；S13、S48、S26、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：安逸依赖自动系统的生活，与重新承担船长责任发生冲突。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 2805, Earth is covered in garbage due to decades of mass consumerism facilitated by the megacorporation Buy n Large.
- **S2**：BnL evacuated Earth's population in fully automated starliners in 2105, leaving behind trash compactor Waste Allocation Load Lifter – Earth Class “WALL-E” robots to clean the planet, but they eventually stopped operating and Earth was left abandoned.
- **S3**：One WALL-E unit has managed to remain active by repairing itself using parts from other broken units.
- **S4**：It has also developed sentience, as with its regular duties it inquisitively collects artifacts of human civilization back to its storage truck home, has befriended a cockroach and enjoys listening to Hello, Dolly!
- **S5**：One day, WALL-E discovers and collects a growing seedling plant.
- **S6**：A spaceship later lands and deploys Extraterrestrial Vegetation Evaluator or “EVE”, an advanced robot sent from the BnL starliner Axiom to search for vegetation on Earth.
- **S7**：Inspired by Hello, Dolly!, WALL-E falls in love with the initially cold and hostile EVE and wishes to join hands with her, who gradually softens and befriends him.
- **S8**：When WALL-E brings EVE to his truck and showcases his collection, she finds the plant and automatically stores it, going into standby mode for retrieval from her ship.
- **S9**：WALL-E spends time with EVE while she is on standby.
- **S10**：He then clings to the hull of EVE's ship as it collects and returns her to the Axiom.
- **S11**：On the Axiom, the ship's original human passengers and their descendants have suffered from severe bone loss and become morbidly obese after centuries of living in microgravity and relying on the ship's automated systems for most tasks.
- **S12**：Captain B.
- **S13**：McCrea, in charge of the ship, mostly leaves control to the robotic autopilot Auto.
- **S14**：WALL-E follows EVE to the bridge of the Axiom, where the Captain learns that by putting the plant in the spaceship holo-detector and verifying Earth is habitable again, the Axiom will make a hyperjump back to Earth so the passengers can recolonize.
- **S15**：However, Auto orders McCrea's robotic assistant GO-4 to steal the plant as part of its no return directive, secretly issued to autopilots after BnL incorrectly concluded in 2110 that the planet could not be saved and humanity should remain in space.
- **S16**：With the plant missing, EVE is considered defective and taken to the repair ward along with WALL-E.
- **S17**：WALL-E mistakes the process on EVE for torture and tries to save her, accidentally releasing a horde of malfunctioning robots, while the security systems then designate both WALL-E and EVE as rogue.
- **S18**：Angry with WALL-E's disruptions, EVE brings him to the escape pod bay to send him home.
- **S19**：There they witness GO-4 dispose of the missing plant by placing it inside a pod set to self-destruct.
- **S20**：WALL-E enters the pod, which is then jettisoned into space, escaping with the plant before the pod explodes.
- **S21**：Reconciling with EVE, they celebrate with a dance in space outside the Axiom.
- **S22**：Meanwhile the Captain, learning from the ship's computer, becomes fascinated about life on Earth before its pollution and abandonment.
- **S23**：The plant is brought to the captain, who surveys EVE's recordings of Earth and concludes that mankind must return to restore their home.
- **S24**：However, Auto reveals his directive, staging a mutiny by tasering WALL-E, incapacitating EVE and confining the captain to his quarters.
- **S25**：EVE realizes the only parts for repairing WALL-E are in his truck on Earth, so she helps him bring the plant to the holo-detector to activate the Axiom{{'}}s hyperjump.
- **S26**：Captain McCrea opens the holo-detector while fighting with Auto and causing chaos on the ship, but Auto partially crushes WALL-E by closing the holo-detector on him.
- **S27**：After McCrea disables Auto and takes back control, EVE places the plant in the holo-detector, freeing the severely damaged WALL-E and setting the Axiom on the instant hyperjump to Earth.
- **S28**：The human population finally lands back on Earth after hundreds of years.
- **S29**：EVE brings WALL-E back to his home where she successfully repairs and reactivates him, but he reverts to his original programming as an unfeeling waste compactor.
- **S30**：Heartbroken, EVE gives WALL-E a farewell kiss that jolts back WALL-E's memory and personality.

### Jack Dawson

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-341b249338d3d466` · 独立版本：`profile-35731dd1558eacba`

- **性格倾向**：在罗斯面临生命危险时，他表现出主动照顾与安慰的倾向。（行为推断；S8、S37、S38）

- **沟通方式**：在海上等待救援时，他用安享晚年的图景鼓励罗斯。（行为推断；S38）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1996, treasure hunter Brock Lovett and his team explore the wreck of RMS Titanic, searching for a valuable diamond necklace called the Heart of the Ocean.
- **S2**：They recover Caledon "Cal" Hockley's safe, believing the necklace to be inside, but instead find a sketch of a nude woman wearing it, dated April 14, 1912, the night the Titanic sank.
- **S3**：Hearing about the drawing, an elderly woman named Rose Dawson Calvert calls Lovett and claims that she is the woman depicted in the drawing.
- **S4**：She and her granddaughter, Lizzy Calvert, visit him and his team on his salvage ship.
- **S5**：When asked if she knows the whereabouts of the necklace, Rose recalls her time aboard the Titanic, revealing that she is Rose DeWitt Bukater, a passenger believed to have died in the sinking.
- **S6**：She then begins her story as follows: In 1912, 17-year-old first class passenger Rose boards "Titanic" in Southampton with her fiancé Cal and her mother Ruth DeWitt Bukater.
- **S7**：Ruth stresses the importance of Rose's engagement, as the marriage would solve the DeWitt Bukaters' secret financial problems.
- **S8**：Distraught by her engagement, Rose considers suicide by jumping off the ship's stern; a drifter and artist named Jack Dawson stops her.
- **S9**：Discovered with Jack on the stern, Rose tells Cal that she was looking over the ship's edge in curiosity and that Jack saved her from falling.
- **S10**：Pressed, Jack confirms her account.
- **S11**：Cal is at first aloof to Jack, but when Rose indicates that recognition is due, he offers him a small amount of money.
- **S12**：After Rose mocks Cal for this, asking if her life means so little, he invites Jack to a first-class dinner the following night.
- **S13**：Jack and Rose develop a tentative friendship, even though Cal and Ruth are wary of the young third-class man.
- **S14**：Following the dinner that night, Rose secretly joins Jack at a party in the ship's third-class quarter.
- **S15**：Because Cal and Ruth forbid her to see Jack, Rose attempts to rebuff Jack's continuing advances.
- **S16**：She soon realizes, though, that she prefers him over Cal, and meets him at the bow of the ship during what turns out to be the Titanic{{'s}} final moments of daylight.
- **S17**：They then go to Rose's stateroom, where she asks Jack to sketch her nude while wearing the Heart of the Ocean, Cal's engagement present to her.
- **S18**：Afterward, the two evade Cal's bodyguard and make love inside a car in the ship's cargo hold.
- **S19**：Going afterwards to the ship's forward well deck, they witness the ship's collision with an iceberg and overhear the ship's officers and designer outline its seriousness.
- **S20**：Rose tells Jack that they should warn her mother and Cal.
- **S21**：Cal discovers Jack's drawing and a mocking note from Rose in his safe along with the necklace.
- **S22**：Furious, he has his bodyguard slip the necklace into Jack's coat pocket.
- **S23**：Accused of stealing it, Jack is arrested, taken down to the Master-at-arms's office and handcuffed to a pipe.
- **S24**：Cal puts the necklace in his coat.
- **S25**：Rose runs away from Cal and her mother  and releases Jack.
- **S26**：The ship then starts to launch flares in order to attract any nearby ships.
- **S27**：Once Jack and Rose reach the deck, Cal and Jack persuade her to board another lifeboat, Cal claiming that he has arranged for himself and Jack to get off safely.
- **S28**：After she boards, Cal tells Jack that the arrangement is only for himself.
- **S29**：As Rose's boat lowers, she realizes that she cannot leave Jack, and jumps back on board the Titanic to reunite with him.
- **S30**：Infuriated, Cal takes a pistol and chases them into the flooding first-class dining saloon.
- **S31**：After exhausting his ammunition, Cal realizes to his chagrin that he gave his coat with the diamond to Rose.
- **S32**：With the situation now dire, he returns to the boat deck and boards a lifeboat by pretending to look after a lost child.
- **S33**：Jack and Rose return to the top deck.
- **S34**：All lifeboats have departed and passengers are falling to their deaths as the stern rises out of the water.
- **S35**：The ship breaks in half, and the stern side rises 90-degrees into the air.
- **S36**：As it sinks, Jack and Rose ride the stern into the ocean.
- **S37**：Jack helps Rose onto a wall panel only able to support one person's weight.
- **S38**：Holding the panel's edge, he assures her she will die an old woman, warm in her bed.
- **S39**：Meanwhile, Fifth Officer Harold Lowe has commandeered a lifeboat to search for survivors.
- **S40**：Jack soon dies of hypothermia and Rose draws the attention of Lowe's boat, and is ultimately saved.
- **S41**：Rose and the other survivors are taken by the RMS Carpathia to New York, where Rose gives her name as Rose Dawson.
- **S42**：She hides from Cal on Carpathia{{'s}} deck as he searches for her.
- **S43**：She learns later that he committed suicide after losing his fortune in the Wall Street Crash of 1929.
- **S44**：Her story complete, Rose goes alone to the stern of Lovett's ship.
- **S45**：There she takes out the Heart of the Ocean, which has been in her possession all along, and drops it into the ocean.
- **S46**：While seemingly asleep in her bed, the photos on her dresser are a visual chronicle that she lived a free life inspired by Jack.
- **S47**：The young Rose is then seen reuniting with Jack at the Grand Staircase of the RMS Titanic, applauded and congratulated by those who perished on the ship.

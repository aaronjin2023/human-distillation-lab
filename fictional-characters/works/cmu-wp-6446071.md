## 角色资料

### James Bond

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-360901462b1a4d14` · 独立版本：`profile-b46e43489241fb6c`

- **性格倾向**：在遭伏击与受制时，他表现出迅速观察机会并反击的倾向（行为推断；S18、S21、S22、S28）
- **核心动机**：他承担获取Lektor并将其安全带离苏方控制的任务（摘要直接叙述；S5、S14、S15、S23）
- **决策方式**：他倾向把随身装备与现场条件转化为逆转劣势的手段（行为推断；S21、S22、S28）
- **关系模式**：他与Kerim Bey通过共享情报和行动配合推进潜入与撤离（摘要直接叙述；S8、S10、S14、S15）
- **价值取舍**：危机解除后，他销毁亲密影像，倾向于保护双方的私密（行为推断；S20、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Red Grant

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-9887b092fa517a94` · 独立版本：`profile-6c1fa0e8b8974efd`

- **性格倾向**：在执行暗杀布局时，他表现出耐心潜伏并等待有利时机的倾向（行为推断；S7、S9、S13、S17）
- **核心动机**：他为SPECTRE执行刺杀与栽赃，计划伪造谋杀后自杀的假象（摘要直接叙述；S4、S20）
- **决策方式**：他先清除干扰并暂保目标，再借假身份和药物夺取控制（行为推断；S9、S13、S16、S17、S18）
- **沟通方式**：在占据优势后，他以嘲弄和夸耀向Bond展示整个操纵计划（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **关系模式**：他对Bond的隐蔽救援服务于后续谋杀，保护行为具有工具性（行为推断；S9、S18、S20）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：前期耐心隐蔽地维持骗局，控制Bond后却主动夸耀布局。

### Rosa Klebb

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-682851d6c6ad58aa` · 独立版本：`profile-f3cb39768d4f3668`

- **性格倾向**：在组织任务时，她表现出善用身份误认和他人不知情的操纵倾向（行为推断；S4、S30）
- **核心动机**：在遭到失败警告后，她继续争夺Lektor并试图杀死Bond（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27、S30、S31）
- **决策方式**：代理追击失败后，她转为伪装潜入并亲自使用隐蔽武器（行为推断；S27、S28、S30、S31）
- **情绪反应**：目睹组织处死失败者后，她在最后通牒面前感到恐惧（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26）
- **关系模式**：她利用Romanova对旧机构身份的信任，将其招为不知情棋子（摘要直接叙述；S4）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：利用Romanova的信任执行任务，最终却被Romanova开枪制止。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：SPECTRE's expert planner Kronsteen devises a plot to steal a Lektor cryptographic device from the Soviets and sell it back to them while exacting revenge on Bond for killing their agent Dr.
- **S2**：No.
- **S3**：The Spectre Number 1 puts ex-SMERSH operative and Number 3 Rosa Klebb in charge of the mission.
- **S4**：Klebb recruits Grant as an assassin, and Tatiana Romanova, a cipher clerk at the Soviet consulate in Istanbul, as an unwitting pawn, as Romanova thinks Klebb is still working for SMERSH.
- **S5**：In London, M tells Bond – agent 007 and sometimes simply '007' – that Romanova has contacted their "Station 'T'" in Turkey, offering to defect with a Lektor, which MI6 and the CIA have been after for years – but Romanova said she will only defect to Bond, whose photo she has allegedly found in a Soviet intelligence file.
- **S6**：Bond then flies to Istanbul, where he meets station head Ali Kerim Bey.
- **S7**：007 is followed from the airport by an unkempt man in glasses and by Red Grant.
- **S8**：The next day, after Kerim Bey's office is bombed, Bond and Kerim Bey spy on the Soviet consulate, where Kerim Bey sees rival agent Krilencu.
- **S9**：At night, Kerim Bey and Bond go to a rural gypsy settlement, which suffers an attack by Krilencu's men, who wound Kerim Bey and nearly kill Bond, who is saved by a hidden Red Grant.
- **S10**：On the following night, Kerim Bey kills Krilencu with Bond's sniper rifle.
- **S11**：When Bond returns to his hotel suite, he finds Romanova in bed waiting for him, unaware that they are being filmed by SPECTRE.
- **S12**：The next day, Romanova heads off for a pre-arranged rendezvous at Hagia Sophia.
- **S13**：The bespectacled man who followed Bond to the airport tries to intercept Romanova's floor plan of the Soviet consulate, but is killed by Grant.
- **S14**：Upon finding the body, Bond takes the floor plan, and brings it to Kerim Bey to devise their invasion.
- **S15**：After stealing the Lektor, Bond, Romanova, and Kerim Bey escape with the device on the Orient Express.
- **S16**：On the train, Kerim Bey and a Soviet security officer named Benz are killed by Grant, who makes it appear as if they killed each other.
- **S17**：At Zagreb, Grant boards the train and meets Bond pretending to be agent Nash from "Station 'Y'".
- **S18**：He drugs Romanova at dinner, then overcomes Bond.
- **S19**：Grant taunts him, boasting SPECTRE has been pitting the Soviets and the British against each other, and claims that Romanova thinks that "she's doing it all for mother Russia" when she is really working for SPECTRE.
- **S20**：Grant also mentions the film of Bond and Romanova at the hotel suite, saying that after both are killed, Grant will plant it in her handbag along with a forged blackmail letter so it looks like it was a murder-suicide.
- **S21**：Bond tricks Grant into opening Bond's attaché case in the manner that detonates its tear gas booby trap in his face, allowing Bond to attack him.
- **S22**：In the ensuing struggle, Bond eventually manages to stab Grant with the knife hidden in the attaché case, and strangles Grant to death with his own garrotte.
- **S23**：At dawn, Bond and Romanova leave the train, hijack Grant's getaway truck, destroy an enemy helicopter, and drive to a dock, eventually boarding a powerboat.
- **S24**：Number 1 is very unhappy, and summons Kronsteen and Klebb.
- **S25**：He reminds them that SPECTRE does not tolerate failure, and brings in agent Morzeny to then execute Kronsteen with a poisoned spike in the toe of his shoe.
- **S26**：Number 1 tells a frightened Klebb that she now has total control of the mission and has one last chance.
- **S27**：Klebb sends Morzeny after Bond with a squadron of SPECTRE's boats.
- **S28**：Morzeny nearly catches Bond, but the agent sets his pursuers' boats on fire with a signal flare.
- **S29**：Bond and Romanova reach Venice and check into a hotel.
- **S30**：Rosa Klebb, disguised as a maid, attempts to steal the Lektor.
- **S31**：She gets the drop on Bond, and attempts to kill Bond with both a gun and her poisoned toe-spike, but ends up being shot by Romanova.
- **S32**：Riding in a gondola, Bond throws the film of him and Romanova into the water as they are rowed away.

## 角色资料

### Tony Stark

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a56482f4cd126c0d` · 独立版本：`profile-110646c93c69c97f`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他以发明和亲自行动应对困局，显出强烈的自主倾向。（行为推断；S5、S12、S25）
- **核心动机**：获知武器被用于袭击村庄后，他着手追查流入恐怖组织的武器。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S16、S21）
- **价值取舍**：他暂停武器生产并探索清洁能源，显示对技术和平用途的重视。（行为推断；S11、S13）
- **决策方式**：得知村庄遭武器袭击后，他倾向亲自穿上装甲介入制止。（行为推断；S14、S16）
- **成长变化**：返乡后他停止制造武器，随后以装甲救人并公开英雄身份。（摘要直接叙述；S11、S16、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他在发布会上先照掩护说法发言，随后公开自己就是钢铁侠。

### Obadiah Stane

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-2858c00e52ddcd7c` · 独立版本：`profile-394951990b367b9a`

- **性格倾向**：在争夺公司与装甲技术时，他显出隐瞒、操控且冷酷的倾向。（行为推断；S15、S19、S22、S24）
- **核心动机**：他试图取代托尼掌控公司，并利用原型残骸制造自己的装甲。（摘要直接叙述；S15、S20）
- **价值取舍**：在谋求控制权与技术时，他将自身利益置于托尼生命之上。（行为推断；S15、S22、S24）
- **决策方式**：无法复制反应堆后，他改以突袭托尼、夺取能源核心解决问题。（摘要直接叙述；S24）
- **关系模式**：他是托尼父亲的旧合伙人与公司经理，却暗中雇人谋害托尼。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S22）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Playboy and genius Tony Stark, who has inherited the defense contractor Stark Industries from his father, is in Afghanistan with his friend and military liaison, Lieutenant Colonel James Rhodes to demonstrate the new "Jericho" missile.
- **S2**：Stark is critically wounded in an ambush and imprisoned in a cave by the terrorist group the Ten Rings.
- **S3**：An electromagnet grafted into Stark's chest by fellow captive Yinsen keeps the shrapnel that wounded him from reaching his heart and killing him.
- **S4**：Ten Rings leader Raza offers places the prisoners into a workshop, promising Stark freedom in exchange for building a Jericho missile for the group, but Tony and Yinsen agree Raza will not keep his word.
- **S5**：Stark and Yinsen secretly build a powerful electric generator called an arc reactor, to power Stark's electromagnet, and then begin to build a suit of armor to escape.
- **S6**：The Ten Rings attack the workshop when they discover what Stark is doing.
- **S7**：Yinsen sacrifices himself to divert them while Stark's suit powers up.
- **S8**：The armored Stark eliminates the terrorists, then returns to the cave to rescue Yinsen.
- **S9**：However, Yinsen is mortally wounded; as he dies he implores Stark not to continue wasting his life.
- **S10**：An enraged Stark burns the terrorist's munitions and flies away, only to crash in the desert, destroying the suit.
- **S11**：After being rescued by Rhodes, Stark returns home and announces that he has suspended production on his company until they can make peaceful technology; thus no more weapons will be made.
- **S12**：In his home workshop, Stark builds an improved version of his suit, as well as a more powerful arc reactor for his chest.
- **S13**：At the Stark Industries building, Tony works on a larger version of the arc reactor which he believes may be able to supply tremendous amounts of clean energy, but Obadiah Stane, his father's old partner and the company's manager, advises Stark that this may bankrupt Stark Industries and ruin his father's legacy.
- **S14**：At Stark's first public appearance after his return, reporter Christine Everhart informs him that Stark Industries weapons, including the Jericho, were recently delivered to the Ten Rings and are being used to attack Yinsen's home village, Gulmira.
- **S15**：Stark also learns that Stane is trying to replace him as head of the company.
- **S16**：Enraged, Stark dons his new armor and flies to Afghanistan, where he saves Yinsen's village and delivers a devastating blow to the Ten Rings.
- **S17**：While flying home, Stark is shot at by two F-22 Raptor fighter jets.
- **S18**：He phones Rhodes and reveals his secret identity in an attempt to end the attack.
- **S19**：Meanwhile, the Ten Rings gather the pieces of Stark's prototype suit and meet with Stane, who subdues Raza with a sonic device and has the rest of the group eliminated.
- **S20**：He has a new suit reverse engineered from the wreckage.
- **S21**：Seeking to find any other weapons delivered to the Ten Rings, Stark sends assistant Virginia "Pepper" Potts to hack into the company computer system from Stane's office.
- **S22**：She discovers Stane has been supplying the terrorists and hired the Ten Rings to kill Stark, but the group reneged.
- **S23**：Potts later meets with agent Phil Coulson of S.H.I.E.L.D., the Strategic Homeland Intervention, Enforcement and Logistics Division, a counter-terrorism agency, to inform him of Stane's activities.
- **S24**：Stane's scientists cannot duplicate Stark's arc reactor, so Stane ambushes Stark at home, using his sonic device to paralyze him and take his arc reactor.
- **S25**：Left to die, Stark manages to crawl to his lab and plug in his original reactor.
- **S26**：Potts and several S.H.I.E.L.D. agents attempt to arrest Stane, but he dons his suit and attacks them.
- **S27**：Stark fights Stane, but is overmatched without his new reactor to run his suit at full capacity.
- **S28**：Stark lures Stane atop the Stark Industries building and instructs Potts to overload the large arc reactor there.
- **S29**：This unleashes a massive electrical surge that knocks Stane unconscious, causing him and his armor to fall into the exploding reactor, killing him.
- **S30**：The next day, the press has dubbed the armored hero "Iron Man".
- **S31**：Agent Coulson gives Stark a cover story to explain the events of the night and Stane's death.
- **S32**：At a press conference, Stark begins giving the cover story, but then announces that he is Iron Man, prompting the reporters to swarm the stage and ask more questions.
- **S33**：In a post-credits scene, S.H.I.E.L.D.
- **S34**：Director Nick Fury visits Stark at home, and, noting that Iron Man is not "the only superhero in the world", says he wants to discuss the "Avengers Initiative".

### Sinbad

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-aff384918895e031` · 独立版本：`profile-12ff7515c2dfa558`

- **性格倾向**：在返城将面临处刑的情境中，仍表现出兑现承诺的担当（行为推断；S7、S8、S9）
- **核心动机**：在朋友以自身作人质后踏上寻书之旅，试图解除朋友的死刑危机（行为推断；S5、S7、S8、S9）
- **价值取舍**：面对逃生与让朋友代死的选择，最终将朋友性命置于自身安全之上（行为推断；S5、S9）

- **成长变化**：从企图窃取和平之书的海盗，转为愿替朋友赴死并促成书籍归还（摘要直接叙述；S1、S3、S9、S10、S11）

### Eris

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-37b6b71ef22d048b` · 独立版本：`profile-dd9a752d26848602`

- **性格倾向**：在制造危机时倾向操纵他人的选择，并利用对人性弱点的预判（行为推断；S2、S4、S5、S7、S8）
- **核心动机**：通过陷害辛巴达诱使王子替他受刑，意图让叙拉古失去继承人（摘要直接叙述；S4、S5、S7）
- **决策方式**：预判辛巴达不会盗书而冒名栽赃，再派出怪物阻挠寻书（摘要直接叙述；S3、S4、S6）
- **情绪反应**：辛巴达兑现返城承诺后感到愤怒，对他的诚实表现出强烈抗拒（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10）
- **价值取舍**：即使结果违背自身意愿，最终仍受约定约束而交还和平之书（摘要直接叙述；S7、S10）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Sinbad and his pirate crew attempts to steal the magical, mystical "Book of Peace" while it travels to Syracuse, Sicily, protected by Proteus; but is prevented when Cetus attacks the ship.
- **S2**：Drawn underwater by Cetus, Sinbad is saved by Eris, the beautiful goddess of discord, who offers him any boon he desires in exchange for the Book of Peace.
- **S3**：Sinbad and his crew go to Syracuse to steal the Book; but leave without doing so.
- **S4**：Anticipating this, Eris impersonates Sinbad and steals the Book.
- **S5**：Sinbad is sentenced to be executed; whereupon Proteus sends Sinbad to retrieve the book instead, placing himself as hostage, and Proteus' fiancée Marina  goes to make sure that Sinbad succeeds.
- **S6**：To prevent them, Eris sends a group of sirens, who entrance and seduce the men aboard Sinbad's ship with their enchantingly seductive, soft melodic singing voices, but do not affect Marina, who pilots the ship to safety; and later a Roc, who captures Marina, but is later killed by Sinbad.
- **S7**：After these and other incidents, Sinbad and Marina enter Eris' realm, where she reveals that her plan was to maneuver Proteus into Sinbad's place, leaving Syracuse without an heir, and agrees to surrender the Book of Peace only if Sinbad truthfully tells whether he will return to Syracuse to accept blame.
- **S8**：When he answers that he will, Eris calls him a liar, and returns him and Marina to the mortal world.
- **S9**：In Syracuse, the time allotted to Sinbad has elapsed, and Proteus is to be beheaded; but at the last minute, Sinbad appears to replace him.
- **S10**：Angered by this, Eris saves him by shattering the executioner's sword to pieces, and quarrels with him for his honesty, but as part of the deal with Sinbad, she reluctantly gives the Book to him before disappearing.
- **S11**：With the Book restored to Syracuse, Sinbad and his crew leave Syracuse on another voyage, leaving Marina behind.
- **S12**：Proteus seeing that Marina has fallen in love with Sinbad, releases her from their engagement and sends her to join Sinbad's ship.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] The plot of the book focuses mainly on Theo Faber, a psychotherapist, and his patient Alicia Berenson.
- **S3**：Michaelides describes Theo Faber's traumatic childhood in which Theo is exposed to a violent father and overall neglectful parents.
- **S4**：In an attempt to fix and understand himself, Theo takes a degree in psychology and starts working as a therapist.
- **S5**：Theo finds the love of his life in Kathy, an American actress who lives in London.
- **S6**：Theo feels safe and his relationship means everything to him.
- **S7**：While the plot unfolds, it becomes increasingly clear that Kathy is traitorous.
- **S8**：The book switches back and forth between narrating Theo's life story, focused especially on his relationship and Alicia's treatment.
- **S9**：Alicia killed her husband six years previously by shooting him in the head five times.
- **S10**：The couple seemed perfectly normal, content and happy before the incident.
- **S11**：Alicia hasn't spoken a single word since and has thus been moved to a closed psychiatric facility, the Grove.
- **S12**：Many therapists have attempted but failed to make her talk.
- **S13**：It is evident that Alicia is perceptive to her surroundings, but the reason for her silence remains a mystery.
- **S14**：When Theo applies for a job at the Grove and is accepted, he convinces his superior to let him become Alicia's therapist.
- **S15**：He has a profound interest in Alicia and wonders why she entitled a nude self-portrait she creates Alesctis.
- **S16**：Theo's therapy sessions don't show any results at first and Theo's superior contemplates putting an end to them because of the lack of results, but they gradually lead to revelations about Alicia's past.
- **S17**：He interviews her relatives and learns that she feared her brother-in-law and used to paint all day long, and about her traumatic childhood after her mother died, her father violently abused her, and her entire family neglected her.
- **S18**：In an attempt to let Alicia express herself and communicate, he collects her old art materials from her former best friend and provides Alicia with an art studio in the Grove.
- **S19**：Alicia paints a sophisticated painting of the Grove with her and Theo standing beside it.
- **S20**：Theo's superior interprets the painting as a sign of progress and allows Theo to continue the therapy sessions.
- **S21**：When Elif, another patient at the clinic who has had conflicts with Alicia in the past, vandalizes Alicia's artwork with the word "slut", Alicia stabs Elif in the eye with a pencil.
- **S22**：This nearly puts an end to Theo's therapy sessions as the Grove management considers Alicia too dangerous for one-on-one sessions, but Theo convinces them otherwise, and soon after, surprisingly, manages to get Alicia to talk.
- **S23**：At the Grove, Theo receives accolades for inducing Alicia to speak again.
- **S24**：Alicia tells Theo her life story, culminating in what happened on the night of the murder, though Theo and the Grove staff think she is lying about the events.
- **S25**：The following morning, Alicia is found unconscious in her room and is found to have been injected with a lethal dose of painkillers by someone at the Grove.
- **S26**：A police investigation is launched that turns up Alicia's diary.
- **S27**：The diary reveals that Theo is responsible for the murder of Alicia's husband.
- **S28**：It details how Theo, driven by hatred, began stalking Kathy's lover, lingering outside his house.
- **S29**：Once he discovered that the man was married, he decided to make his wife aware of her husband's infidelity.
- **S30**：Theo put on a mask and broke into their house, where he found the wife, Alicia, and told her of the affair.
- **S31**：After Theo left, the shock of Theo's intrusion and the revelation of her husband Gabriel's cheating lead Alicia to suffer a psychotic breakdown.
- **S32**：She took Gabriel's gun and shot him five times.
- **S33**：Afterwards, unable to accept that she had killed the love of her life, she attempts suicide and vows to never speak again.
- **S34**：The book ends abruptly after an investigator arrives at Theo's home with Alicia's diary and he begins to read aloud from her final entry, incriminating Theo.

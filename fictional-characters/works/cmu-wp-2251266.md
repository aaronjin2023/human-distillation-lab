## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After being released from prison, Danny Ocean  breaks parole and travels to Los Angeles to meet up with his former partner in crime and close friend Rusty Ryan  to propose a scheme he has in mind.
- **S2**：The two go to Las Vegas to pitch the plan to wealthy friend and former casino owner Reuben Tishkoff .
- **S3**：The plan consists of simultaneously robbing the Bellagio, The Mirage, and the MGM Grand casinos.
- **S4**：Reuben's familiarity with casino security makes him very reluctant to get involved, but when he begins to think of it as a good way to get back at his rival, Terry Benedict , who owns all three casinos, Reuben agrees to finance the operation.
- **S5**：Because the casinos are required by the Nevada Gaming Commission to have enough cash on hand to cover all their patrons' bets, the three predict that, on the upcoming night of a highly anticipated boxing match, the Bellagio vault will contain more than $150&nbsp;million.
- **S6**：Ocean and Ryan recruit eight former colleagues and criminal specialists: Linus Caldwell , a young and talented pick-pocket thief; Frank Catton , a casino worker and con man; Virgil and Turk Malloy , a pair of gifted mechanics; Livingston Dell  an electronics and surveillance expert; Basher Tarr , an explosives expert; Saul Bloom , an elderly con man; and "The Amazing" Yen , an accomplished acrobat.
- **S7**：Several of the team members carry out reconnaissance at the Bellagio to learn as much as possible about the security, the routines and behaviors of the casino staff, and the building itself.
- **S8**：Others create a precise replica of the vault with which to practice maneuvering through its formidable security systems.
- **S9**：During this planning phase, the team discovers that Ocean's ex-wife, Tess , is Benedict's girlfriend.
- **S10**：Ryan urges Ocean to give up on the plan, believing Ocean incapable of sound judgement while Tess is involved, but Ocean refuses.
- **S11**：When the plan is put in motion, Ocean goes to the Bellagio in order to be seen by Benedict, who, as expected, has him locked in a storeroom to be beaten by a bouncer called Bruiser.
- **S12**：Bruiser, however, is a friend of Ocean's, and he allows Ocean to leave through a ventilation shaft, to meet with his team in the vault.
- **S13**：Linus Caldwell poses as a gaming commission agent, and reveals to Benedict that one of his employees, Ramon Escalante, is actually Frank Catton, an ex-con.
- **S14**：Caldwell and Catton stage a faux confrontation in Benedict's presence so that Linus can steal the vault access codes written on a piece of paper in Benedict's jacket.
- **S15**：Yen is smuggled into the vault by the Malloy brothers to assist in triggering the explosive from the inside.
- **S16**：Saul Bloom sneaks explosives into the casino vault by posing as a wealthy international arms dealer who needs especially secure safekeeping for his valuables, and then stages a heart attack that is treated by Ryan posing as a doctor.
- **S17**：The team activates a stolen "pinch" device to temporarily disrupt the casino's electrical power, allowing them to breach the vault undetected.
- **S18**：As Benedict attempts to restore order following the power outage, Ryan anonymously calls him on a cell phone that Ocean had earlier planted in Tess's coat.
- **S19**：Ryan tells him that the vaults are being raided, and that all the money will be destroyed if Benedict does not cooperate in loading half the money into a van waiting outside.
- **S20**：Benedict observes video footage of the vault that confirms Ryan's claims, and complies in moving the money, but orders his men to follow the van after it departs, and calls a S.W.A.T. team to secure the vault and the other half of the money.
- **S21**：The S.W.A.T. team's arrival results in a shootout which causes the incineration of the half of the money left in the vault.
- **S22**：After assuring Benedict that the casino is secure, the officers depart at Benedict's insistence.
- **S23**：Benedict's men following the van discover that it is being driven by remote control, and that, instead of money, it contains duffel bags full of flyers advertising prostitutes.
- **S24**：Benedict realizes that the vault video feed he'd been watching was faked, as the vault floor in the footage lacked the Bellagio logo, which had only recently been installed.
- **S25**：A flashback reveals that Ocean had used the vault replica to create the fake video Benedict had seen; the rest of the team posed as S.W.A.T. officers, and took all of the money in the vault when responding to Benedict's call for police assistance.
- **S26**：Benedict then returns to the room where he left Ocean and finds him still there, apparently still being worked over by Bruiser, leaving him with no way to connect Ocean to the theft.
- **S27**：As Tess watches via security surveillance, Ocean tricks Benedict into saying he would give up Tess in exchange for the money.
- **S28**：Ocean then says, "Alright.
- **S29**：I know a guy.
- **S30**：We were in the joint together.
- **S31**：Anybody pulls a job in the western US, he knows about it.
- **S32**：Give me 72 hours.
- **S33**：I'll find out who took your money".
- **S34**：Benedict, humiliated, orders his men to escort Ocean off the premises and inform the police that Ocean is violating his parole by being in Las Vegas.
- **S35**：Tess leaves Benedict, and exits the hotel just in time to see Ocean arrested.
- **S36**：When he is released after serving 'three to six months' for his parole violation, he is met by Rusty and Tess, and the three drive off, closely followed by Benedict's bodyguards.

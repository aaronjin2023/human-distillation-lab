### Han

- **性格倾向**：在弟弟遇害后倾向主动追查，并不因父亲身份而回避质问（行为推断；S5、S6、S9、S10、S17、S30）

### Isak O’Day

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-f37525345370bdf3` · 独立版本：`profile-81b10fcc3a4a1af1`

- **性格倾向**：在家族面临报复时倾向提前防范，主动安排子女的安全保护（行为推断；S4）
- **核心动机**：希望借球队交易让家族永久脱离犯罪生意，并取得合法事业位置（摘要直接叙述；S12、S23）
- **价值取舍**：在领取现金与持有球队份额之间，更看重长期的所有者身份（行为推断；S23）
- **决策方式**：面对亲信以女儿相威胁的背叛，选择持枪攻击对方手下（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25）
- **情绪反应**：遭到亲信背叛并威胁女儿时，愤怒迅速转化为直接武力反击（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As the movie opens, Po Sing is waiting for someone unknown in an African-American club when Kai and his Asian henchmen come in and confront the owner of the club and his bouncers.
- **S2**：After a brief fight, all are thrown out by the owner before the meeting can take place.
- **S3**：The next day, Po is found murdered.
- **S4**：Fearing retribution, real estate developer  Isak O’Day arranges for his henchmen to place security on both of his children.
- **S5**：Meanwhile, Po’s brother, Han , learns of the murder in the Hong Kong prison where he is serving time.
- **S6**：After starting a fight in the mess hall, he escapes from prison by overpowering the guards who took him to an isolation cell for punishment and disguising himself as one of them.
- **S7**：Han makes his way to Oakland, where he learns that a gang war seems to have erupted between black and Asian gang families, including both O’Day and Han and Po’s father, Ch’u Sing, even while the two are apparently engaged in a joint business venture.
- **S8**：He also learns that his brother called O’Day’s daughter Trish’s record store the day before he was killed.
- **S9**：After a chance encounter with her, he follows her and learns that Po may have actually been calling Trish’s brother, Colin.
- **S10**：At his brother’s funeral Han confronts his father, blaming him for failing to keep Po safe as he promised he would after he helped the both of them flee to America to escape the Chinese authorities, an action which resulted in his own imprisonment.
- **S11**：Han learns that the war is apparently over control of property along the Oakland waterfront.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, O’Day also reveals to his son that the deal he is working on will get their family out of the crime business for good, but that he must be careful.
- **S13**：However, the apparent war over territory is really a cover for the two sides secretly working together to put together a deal for ownership of a new NFL franchise in Oakland.
- **S14**：Colin and his girlfriend are killed by unseen assailants by being thrown out of his high rise apartment window.
- **S15**：As Han comforts Trish, he learns that Po had put together a list of businesses that were either destroyed or being threatened with destruction for failing to sell their properties, and that Po was trying to contact Colin to warn him about this.
- **S16**：The two visit one of the few remaining properties on the list and learn that the owner, a Chinese man, and his employees have been killed by a Chinese hit team.
- **S17**：When Han confronts his father over this, he tries to deflect suspicion by telling him Trish may have been involved.
- **S18**：It is revealed that both sides in the fake war are using violence and intimidation to force the owners of all the waterfront properties to relinquish their rights over their properties.
- **S19**：As Trish and Han visit the last property on the list, the nightclub where Po originally intended to meet Colin, O’Day’s chief lieutenant Mac and his goons kill the club’s owner and kidnap both Trish and Han, taking them to separate locations.
- **S20**：Han escapes by overpowering his guards.
- **S21**：Ch’u Sing has the other Chinese crime lords killed, ensuring that he will have control over their business interests.
- **S22**：O’Day, Ch’u Sing, and the developer arranging the NFL deal meet at the Oakland Men’s club to provide the deeds for the properties they now control.
- **S23**：Sing takes a multi-million dollar payment and leaves, but O’Day refuses his payment, stating that his payment will be in the form of a share of ownership of the new franchise.
- **S24**：He is betrayed, however, by Mac, who threatens Trish to force O’Day to hand over the deeds to the developer.
- **S25**：O'Day, enraged by Mac's betrayal, grabs a gun and shoots Mac's men but is grievously wounded in the process.
- **S26**：Han arrives and pursues Mac to the roof of the club.
- **S27**：The developer escapes via helicopter, but not before losing all of the deeds after Mac fires repeatedly at the helicopter.
- **S28**：Mac reveals to Han that it was Ch’u Sing’s lieutenant Kai who had Po killed, just before Trish kills him.
- **S29**：At his father’s house, Han engages in a brutal fight with Kai, finally killing him.
- **S30**：He then confronts his father, knowing now that his father had his own son killed "like running over a dog in the street" for no other reason than that he was interfering with a business deal.
- **S31**：He tells his father that he will answer for his crimes, either to the American authorities or to the other Chinese families—no one will go to prison for him this time.
- **S32**：As Han walks away, his father commits suicide with a handgun.
- **S33**：He finds Trish waiting for him outside and the two walk away from the house together.

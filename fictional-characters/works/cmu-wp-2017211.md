### Bolt

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-9a2c7ae5a207e521` · 独立版本：`profile-c7494c3f87b8f74e`

- **性格倾向**：在能力失效时倾向维护原有认知，以外物影响解释异常（行为推断；S4）
- **核心动机**：在误以为Penny被绑架后，倾向以寻找并保护她为行动目标（行为推断；S3、S6、S23、S24）
- **价值取舍**：即使失去超级能力，仍把不抛弃Penny置于自身安全之前（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **成长变化**：从相信虚构的英雄身份，逐渐接受普通狗也能采取有效行动（行为推断；S2、S11、S24、S25）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A girl named Penny and a dog named Bolt star on a hit television series called Bolt in which the titular character has various superpowers and must constantly thwart the evil plans of the nefarious Doctor Calico.
- **S2**：To gain a more realistic performance, the TV show's producers have deceived Bolt his entire life, arranging the filming in such a way that Bolt believes the television show is real and he really has superpowers.
- **S3**：Mistakenly believing Penny has been kidnapped by the villain, Bolt escapes from his on-set trailer in Hollywood but falls into a box of foam peanuts and unknown to the film company is accidentally shipped to New York City.
- **S4**：In New York, Bolt starts to notice that his superpowers aren't working, and rationalizes this is the effect that styrofoam has on his body.
- **S5**：He then meets Mittens, a female alley cat who bullies pigeons out of their food.
- **S6**：Bolt forces Mittens to help him get back to Hollywood, and after Bolt knocks Mittens unconscious the two start their journey westward on a truck.
- **S7**：In Hollywood, Penny is deeply saddened over Bolt's disappearance but is convinced by the studio to continue filming with a Bolt look alike.
- **S8**：Surprised at his first feelings of both pain and hunger, Bolt is shown by Mittens how to act like a cute, but needy dog, and is rewarded by food.
- **S9**：They meet Rhino, a fearless, TV-obsessed hamster and Bolt fan who joins their team.
- **S10**：Mittens tries to convince Bolt that his superpowers aren't real, but their discussion is cut short by the arrival of Animal Control, who captures them both and transports them to an animal shelter.
- **S11**：After being freed en route by Rhino, Bolt finally realizes that he is just a normal dog, but regains his confidence after Rhino  gives him a pep talk.
- **S12**：They rescue Mittens from the shelter and escape, allowing them to continue their journey.
- **S13**：Along the way, Mittens teaches Bolt typical dog activities , but Mittens refuses to go farther than Las Vegas.
- **S14**：She tells Bolt that his Hollywood life is fake and there is no real love for him there.
- **S15**：Her emotional rant reveals that she was once a house cat, but was abandoned by her previous owner and left to brave the harsh streets alone and declawed.
- **S16**：Bolt refuses to believe that Penny doesn't love him, and continues on alone, wishing Mittens the best.
- **S17**：Rhino, learning of Bolt's departure, convinces Mittens that they must help him, and the two set off to find Bolt once again.
- **S18**：Bolt reaches the studio and finds Penny embracing his lookalike.
- **S19**：Unaware that Penny still misses him and that her affection for the lookalike is only a part of a rehearsal for the show, he leaves, brokenhearted.
- **S20**：Mittens, on a gantry in the studio, sees what Bolt does not: Penny telling her mother how much she misses Bolt.
- **S21**：Realizing that Penny truly does love Bolt, Mittens follows Bolt and explains.
- **S22**：At the same time, the Bolt-lookalike panics during the show's filming and accidentally knocks over some flaming torches, setting the sound stage on fire with Penny trapped inside.
- **S23**：Bolt arrives and reunites with Penny inside the burning studio, but they cannot get out.
- **S24**：In desperation, and unwilling to abandon Penny, Bolt uses his "super bark".
- **S25**：The firefighters hear the noise through the building's air vent and manage to rescue them before they succumb to smoke inhalation.
- **S26**：Penny and her mother subsequently quit the show when their agent attempts to exploit the incident for publicity purposes.
- **S27**：Penny herself adopts Mittens and Rhino, and she and her family move to a rural home to enjoy a simpler, happy lifestyle with Bolt and her new pets.
- **S28**：The show continues, but with a replacement "Bolt" and "Penny" – "Penny's" new appearance being explained in the show as being serious injuries necessitating her undergoing facial reconstruction surgery, and adopting a new storyline about alien abduction .
- **S29**：The epilogue scenes during the credits show Bolt, Penny, her mother, Mittens, and Rhino enjoying their new life together.

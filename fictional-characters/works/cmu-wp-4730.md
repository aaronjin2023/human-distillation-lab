### Bruce Wayne

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8a9e8e464a744c51` · 独立版本：`profile-56ddc8ff10febe7b`

- **性格倾向**：面对他人丧亲与生命危险时倾向承担照护和救援责任（行为推断；S1、S8、S20）
- **价值取舍**：面对脑部技术提案时倾向重视潜在问题，而非仅认可新发明（行为推断；S3）
- **决策方式**：被迫二选一救人时倾向寻找突破设限的办法，兼救双方（行为推断；S19、S20）
- **关系模式**：对失亲少年由提供住处发展为共同对敌的搭档关系（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9、S13、S16）

### Dick

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b3c601d7c999d283` · 独立版本：`profile-018190dbbbff13f5`

- **性格倾向**：失去家人后倾向主动投入对抗，但关键时刻仍有克制（行为推断；S7、S9、S17）
- **核心动机**：失亲后倾向将成为蝙蝠侠搭档视为追求复仇的途径（行为推断；S9）
- **价值取舍**：真正获得复仇机会时无法下杀手，选择扶起已经倒地的敌人（摘要直接叙述；S17）
- **成长变化**：从明确要求杀人复仇转向实际放弃杀戮，显出行动中的界限（行为推断；S9、S17）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：强烈宣称要杀敌复仇，却在胜利后选择救起对方。

- **S3**：Edward Nygma , a researcher at Wayne Enterprises, develops a device to beam television directly to a person's brain; Bruce Wayne  rejects the invention, noting that it "raises too many questions", and Edward angrily resigns from his position.

- **S5**：Chase Meridian , Bruce invites her to a charity circus event.
- **S6**：There, Two-Face and his henchmen storm the event in an attempt to discover Batman's secret identity, and in the process murder The Flying Graysons, a family of acrobats.
- **S7**：The youngest member, Dick , survives and throws Two-Face's bomb into the river.
- **S8**：Bruce assumes responsibility for Dick and allows him to stay at Wayne Manor.
- **S9**：Dick then declares his intention to kill Two-Face and avenge his family's murder, and when he discovers Bruce's secret identity as Batman, he insists on becoming his sidekick, "Robin".
- **S10**：Meanwhile, Nygma becomes a criminal known as the "Riddler", the master of puzzles and quizzes and forms an alliance with Two-Face.
- **S11**：Using his invention that Bruce had rejected earlier, Nygma can read and control people's minds, and steal their intelligence quotient.
- **S12**：At a business party, Edward discovers Bruce's secret identity.
- **S13**：Two-Face then attacks the party and nearly kills Batman, but Robin arrives just in time to save his life.
- **S14**：However, Two-Face and the Riddler later converge into Wayne Manor.
- **S15**：The Riddler enters the Batcave and destroys most of the equipment, and he and Two-Face kidnap Chase, while leaving Bruce another riddle.
- **S16**：After solving the last riddle, Batman and Robin locate the Riddler's lair, Claw Island, where both are separated upon reaching the island.
- **S17**：Robin then encounters Two-Face and manages to beat him to the ground; realizing that he does not have it in him to commit murder, Robin helps the villain back up.
- **S18**：Two-Face gets the upper hand and captures Robin.
- **S19**：Meanwhile, Batman manages to make his way into the Riddler's lair, where Robin and Chase are revealed as hostages, giving Batman a choice of saving just one hostage.
- **S20**：Batman finds a way to save both hostages, and manages to destroy the brainwave-collecting device, driving the Riddler into a mental breakdown in the process.
- **S21**：During the battle, Two-Face falls to his death.
- **S22**：The Riddler is finally sent to Arkham Asylum, and Chase is asked to consult on his case.
- **S23**：Riddler offers to reveal the identity of Batman to her, but he thinks himself Batman.
- **S24**：Chase then meets Bruce Wayne outside and tells him his secret is safe.

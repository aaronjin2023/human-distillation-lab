### Kitty Fane

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-ade5000842cb13ca` · 独立版本：`profile-a5047fce16c6a29d`

- **性格倾向**：初到疫区时在意生活舒适，后来表现出主动照顾他人的倾向（行为推断；S9、S11、S18）
- **核心动机**：婚外关系败露后，最初希望靠离婚与情人重组生活（摘要直接叙述；S6、S7）
- **决策方式**：在孤独处境中主动投入孤儿院工作，借参与改变日常（行为推断；S10、S11）
- **关系模式**：通过看见丈夫照顾儿童的一面，逐渐修复对他的理解（摘要直接叙述；S12、S14）
- **成长变化**：从依恋拒绝负责的情人，转为重逢时明确拒绝其邀约（摘要直接叙述；S7、S19、S20、S21、S22）

### Walter Fane

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-1674a4a6fded6502` · 独立版本：`profile-0c2c13c933239957`

- **性格倾向**：在公共救治中尽责关怀，却在婚姻受伤时显出报复倾向（行为推断；S5、S8、S12、S17）
- **核心动机**：发现妻子不忠后，起初借同行疫区的要求惩罚她（摘要直接叙述；S5、S8）
- **决策方式**：早期以离婚条件和艰苦旅程施压，控制妻子的选择空间（行为推断；S5、S6、S8）
- **价值取舍**：面对疫病扩散持续承担救治责任，为新来患者另设营地（行为推断；S5、S17）
- **成长变化**：重新爱上妻子后，愿接受孩子生父不明而不再追究（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：救治病人时无私关怀，起初却刻意让妻子承受更多痛苦。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：On a brief trip back to London, earnest, bookish bacteriologist Walter Fane  is dazzled by Kitty Garstin , a vivacious, vain, and vacuous London socialite.
- **S2**：He proposes; she accepts , and the ill-suited couple embark on a tense relationship in China.
- **S3**：Following a honeymoon in Venice, the couple go to Shanghai, where the doctor is stationed in a government lab studying infectious diseases.
- **S4**：Kitty meets Charles Townsend , a married British vice consul, and the two engage in a clandestine affair.
- **S5**：When Walter discovers his wife's infidelity, he seeks to punish her by threatening to divorce her on the grounds of adultery if she doesn't accompany him to a small village in a remote area of China, where he has volunteered to treat victims of an unchecked cholera epidemic sweeping through the area.
- **S6**：Kitty begs to be allowed to divorce him quietly and he agrees, provided Townsend will leave his wife Dorothy and marry her.
- **S7**：When she proposes this possibility to her lover, he, despite earlier claiming his love for Kitty, declines to accept, and she is compelled to travel to the mountainous inland region with her husband.
- **S8**：They embark upon an arduous, two-week-long overland journey, which would be considerably faster and much easier if they traveled by river, but Walter is determined to make Kitty as unhappily uncomfortable as possible.
- **S9**：Upon their arrival in Mei-tan-fu, she is distressed to discover they will be living in near squalor, far removed from everyone except their cheerful neighbor Waddington, a British deputy commissioner living with a young Chinese woman in relative opulence.
- **S10**：Walter and Kitty barely speak to each other and, except for a cook and a Chinese soldier assigned to guard her, she is alone for long hours.
- **S11**：After visiting an orphanage run by a group of French nuns, Kitty volunteers her services, and she is assigned to work in the music room.
- **S12**：She is surprised to learn from the Mother Superior that her husband loves children, especially babies, and in this setting she begins to see him in a new light as she learns what a selfless and caring person he can be.
- **S13**：When he sees her with the children, he in turn realizes she is not the shallow, selfish person he thought her to be.
- **S14**：As Walter's anger and Kitty's unhappiness subside, their marriage begins to blossom.
- **S15**：She soon learns she is pregnant, but is unsure who the father is.
- **S16**：Walter – in love with Kitty again – assures her it doesn't matter.
- **S17**：Just as the local cholera problem is coming under control, ailing refugees from elsewhere pour into the area, forcing Walter to set up a camp outside town.
- **S18**：He contracts the disease and Kitty nurses him, but he dies, devastating her.
- **S19**：Five years later, while shopping with her young son Walter in London, Kitty meets Townsend by chance on the street.
- **S20**：He suggests the two get together and asks young Walter his age, realising from the reply that he could be Walter's father.
- **S21**：Kitty, however, rejects his overtures and walks away.
- **S22**：When her son asks who Townsend is, she replies "No one important".

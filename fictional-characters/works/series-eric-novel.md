- **性格倾向**：在追求愿望时倾向凭理想化想象行动，低估愿望的实际含义（行为推断；S21、S29、S35、S38、S41）

- **情绪反应**：许愿受阻以及美女形象不符期待时，均明确表现出失望（摘要直接叙述；S17、S35）

- **决策方式**：在对方否认可许愿时仍继续施压，倾向坚持要求而非先核实（行为推断；S17、S19、S20）

- **S22**：Eric and Rincewind are transported to the rain forests of Klatch in the Tezumen empire (a parody of the Aztec Empire ).
- **S23**：The locals declare Eric Ruler of the World.
- **S24**：During this tribute, Rincewind and the parrot explore the temple of Quezovercoatl (a parody of Quetzalcoatl ), where they find a prisoner, Ponce da Quirm (a parody of Juan Ponce de León ), who is to be sacrificed.
- **S25**：Da Quirm tells Rincewind about the terrible fate the Tezumen have planned for the Ruler of the World, on whom they blame all of life's misfortunes.
- **S26**：Rincewind, Eric and da Quirm are tied up at the top of a pyramid to be sacrificed, when Quezovercoatl makes his appearance.
- **S27**：Unfortunately for him, the Luggage also makes an appearance, trampling the six-inch-tall Quezovercoatl in the process.
- **S28**：The Tezumen, pleased to see Quezovercoatl destroyed, release the prisoners and deify the Luggage in the place of their god.
- **S29**：To Meet the Most Beautiful Woman in All History .
- **S30**：Rincewind transports himself and Eric inside in a large wooden horse (a parody of the Trojan Horse ).
- **S31**：Exiting, they are surrounded by Tsortean soldiers, who take them for an Ephebian invasion force.
- **S32**：Rincewind manages to talk their way out from the guards and out of the city, only to fall into the hands of the invading army.
- **S33**：Rincewind and Eric are taken to Lavaeolus (a parody of Odysseus ), the man who built the horse as a decoy so that he and his men could sneak in while their enemies waited around the horse for them to come out.
- **S34**：They re-enter Tsort through a secret passage , and find Elenor (a parody of Helen of Troy ).
- **S35**：Eric and Lavaeolus are disappointed to find that Elenor is now a plump mother of several children, and that artistic licence had been taken in her description.
- **S36**：The Ephebians escape the city while Tsort burns, and Lavaeolus and his army set out for home.

## 角色资料

### Nathan Algren

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-eb99b0953f227bf8` · 独立版本：`profile-1902c7d578e7c213`

- **性格倾向**：在村落相处中表现出愿意反省并接纳异文化的倾向（行为推断；S11、S12）
- **核心动机**：赴日受雇起初仅为报酬，同时试图承受屠杀记忆的折磨（摘要直接叙述；S1、S5）
- **价值取舍**：在获得指挥职位的诱惑面前，更看重对武士的忠诚（行为推断；S14、S15、S18）
- **决策方式**：在兵力劣势中倾向借鉴战例，以诱敌策略削弱火力优势（行为推断；S21、S23、S24）
- **成长变化**：从借酒逃避战争创伤，转向在村落关系中找到安宁（摘要直接叙述；S1、S10、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：最初为钱镇压叛乱，后来帮助原本的敌人作战。

### Katsumoto

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-41e80f6c6ba41f5e` · 独立版本：`profile-be2487ecdf2e5d17`

- **性格倾向**：在维护武士传统时表现出宁愿受罚也不轻易妥协的倾向（行为推断；S17、S30）
- **价值取舍**：将武士道视为生死选择的准则，并据此安排自己的死亡（摘要直接叙述；S30）
- **关系模式**：对皇帝仍保持进谏关系，试图从内部影响统治方向（行为推断；S16）
- **情绪反应**：儿子牺牲令他陷入哀痛，战事却迫使他继续面对决战（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **决策方式**：在敌方战士身上看到异象呼应时，选择俘获而非处死（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9）

暂无支持：核心动机、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：向皇帝进谏，却因拒绝服从禁刀法而被捕。

### Omura

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-596c938be9338b46` · 独立版本：`profile-c6861df8f318ba37`

- **性格倾向**：面对阻力时倾向强硬控制局面，甚至秘密清除拒绝者（行为推断；S7、S15）
- **核心动机**：试图镇压武士反抗，并以收买争取掌握叛军情报（行为推断；S4、S14）
- **决策方式**：在军事部署中倾向压过专业异议，催促准备不足者出战（行为推断；S7）
- **价值取舍**：在决战中将消灭对手置于对垂死同胞的怜悯之前（行为推断；S28、S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：急于推进战斗，却两次暴露出对战场形势的误判。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1876, Captain Nathan Algren  is traumatized by massacring Native Americans in the Indian Wars and has become an alcoholic to stave off the memories.
- **S2**：Algren is approached by former colleague Zebulon Gant , who takes him to meet Algren's former Colonel Bagley , whom Algren despises for ordering the massacre.
- **S3**：On behalf of businessman Mr.
- **S4**：Omura , Bagley offers Algren a job training conscripts of the new Meiji government of Japan to suppress a samurai rebellion that is opposed to Western influence, led by Katsumoto .
- **S5**：Despite struggling with the painful memories of having already crushed a tribal rebellion, Algren accepts solely for payment.
- **S6**：In Japan he keeps a journal and is accompanied by British translator Simon Graham .
- **S7**：Despite Algren's objections, Omura has Bagley order the peasant conscripts to fight early and they are routed when engaging the samurai.
- **S8**：Gant is killed and Algren is captured after killing leading samurai warrior Hirotaro.
- **S9**：Katsumoto is reminded of a vision of a tiger while watching Algren fight with a tiger-embroidered spear and orders his capture.
- **S10**：Taken to the samurai village, Algren is treated by Hirotaro's widow Taka and Katsumoto's son, Nobutada  and recovers from his trauma.
- **S11**：He begins to converse with Katsumoto, study swordsmanship under warrior Ujio  and apologizes to Taka for Hirotaro's death which she accepts because of the honor of battle.
- **S12**：Growing closer to her and her children, he later helps defend the village from a night attack by Omura's ninja.
- **S13**：In spring, Algren is taken back to Tokyo as promised.
- **S14**：The Imperial Japanese army have become better organized with American equipment and Omura offers Algren command if he reveals information on the rebels.
- **S15**：Algren declines, so privately Omura orders his death.
- **S16**：Katsumoto offers his counsel to the young Emperor, but finds the Emperor's control is weak.
- **S17**：When Katsumoto refuses to obey the new law to not display swords, he is arrested.
- **S18**：Algren frees Katsumoto with the assistance of Ujio, Nobutada and Graham.
- **S19**：Nobutada is severely wounded as they escape, sacrificing himself to slow the guards.
- **S20**：Katsumoto mourns, but receives word that a large Imperial Army group led by Omura and Bagley will engage them.
- **S21**：Five hundred samurai are rallied as Algren references the Battle of Thermopylae.
- **S22**：On the eve of battle, Algren is presented with a katana, kisses Taka and wears Hirotaro's red armor as a symbol of respect to her.
- **S23**：In battle, the samurai fall back, preventing the Imperial army from using their full firepower.
- **S24**：As they expect, Omura orders the infantry to advance, straight into their fire trap.
- **S25**：The samurai then unleash a rain of arrows as a wave of swordsmen, including Katsumoto and Algren, attack.
- **S26**：A second Imperial infantry wave advances, only to be countered by Ujio's samurai cavalry; a savage melee ensues, leaving many dead on both sides before the Imperial forces retreat.
- **S27**：Realizing that fresh Imperial forces are coming, the samurai resolve to fight to the death in a final charge.
- **S28**：Algren hurls his sword at Bagley, fatally wounding his nemesis, but the samurai are finally cut down by Gatling guns.
- **S29**：Moved by the sight of his dying countrymen, the Imperial captain stops the fire, defying Omura's orders.
- **S30**：Katsumoto, observing Bushido, asks Algren to assist in his seppuku.
- **S31**：Katsumoto dies and the Imperial soldiers kneel and bow around the fallen samurai.
- **S32**：Later, the American ambassador prepares to have the Emperor sign a treaty but an injured Algren enters and interrupts the proceedings, offering Katsumoto's sword to the Emperor who comes to realize that while Japan must modernize, it must chart its own path and never forget its own history and traditions.
- **S33**：The Emperor dismisses the American ambassador and confiscates Omura's fortunes to be given to the people.
- **S34**：Graham, who was given Algren's journal to help write a book, states that Algren found peace by returning to Taka and the village.

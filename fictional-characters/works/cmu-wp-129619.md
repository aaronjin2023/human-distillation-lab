## 角色资料

### Sam Wheat

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-4df8fae2af67eaa4` · 独立版本：`profile-0f1f551678ccca9a`

- **性格倾向**：面对异常与受限处境，他倾向主动追查并寻找可行的干预办法（行为推断；S3、S7、S8、S13）
- **核心动机**：发现凶手将返回住处后，他持续设法保护仍在危险中的爱人（摘要直接叙述；S8、S10、S26、S32）
- **决策方式**：无法直接被人感知时，他倾向借助中间人与可见证据行动（行为推断；S9、S10、S14、S27）
- **关系模式**：即使好友参与谋害并威胁爱人，他见对方死去时仍感悲伤（摘要直接叙述；S2、S12、S18、S34）
- **成长变化**：他从无法被看见或听见的无力处境，走向完成守护并告别（行为推断；S5、S13、S39、S40）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：为阻止卡尔而对抗旧友，卡尔死亡时却仍保留悲痛。

### Molly Jensen

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-f535427d959b9838` · 独立版本：`profile-36ef00a40eeb0fa2`

- **性格倾向**：面对超自然警告，她倾向保持怀疑并寻求外部核实（行为推断；S10、S11、S27）
- **核心动机**：面对关于危险与灵媒的说法，她希望弄清警告是否可信（行为推断；S10、S11）
- **情绪反应**：失去山姆后的悲伤持续影响她面对灵媒来访的反应（摘要直接叙述；S5、S26）
- **决策方式**：她会依据警方信息和亲眼所见，逐步调整对灵媒的判断（行为推断；S11、S27、S28）
- **价值取舍**：面对持凶者威胁灵媒时，她愿冒险出手保护曾怀疑的人（行为推断；S10、S31）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：对灵媒保持怀疑，同时又深受失去爱人的悲伤牵动。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Sam Wheat , a banker, and Molly Jensen , a talented potter, are a loving couple who move into a New York City apartment.
- **S2**：At work, Sam discovers a major discrepancy in a couple of bank accounts , and confides in his good friend and colleague, Carl Bruner .
- **S3**：Carl offers to investigate the matter, but Sam decides to investigate himself.
- **S4**：Later that night, Sam and Molly are attacked by armed thug Willie Lopez  and Sam is killed by a gunshot during a struggle with Willie.
- **S5**：Sam's ghost arises from his dead body, which lies next to the distraught Molly; he gradually realizes that he is a ghost whose presence cannot be seen or heard.
- **S6**：One day, Sam is alone at the apartment  when Willie comes in, looking for something.
- **S7**：Sam is unable to stop the killer but spooks Molly's cat, Floyd, causing Willie to get scratched and flee.
- **S8**：Sam follows the killer to his place in Brooklyn, and hears that he will return to Molly's house.
- **S9**：He also meets Oda Mae Brown , a local con-artist posing as a medium and realizes she can hear him , although Oda Mae had been faking her abilities until then.
- **S10**：He persuades Oda Mae to tell Molly that she is in danger, but Molly is disbelieving and Carl later convinces Molly that Oda Mae is a fraud preying on her grief.
- **S11**：Molly talks to the police about her concerns but they confirm that Oda Mae is a known confidence trickster with a lengthy record whereas Willie Lopez has none.
- **S12**：Sam discovers that Carl was involved in a money laundering scheme at the bank and that the attack was an attempt by Carl to acquire Sam's security codes, so he could transfer the money from the many accounts to one at another bank.
- **S13**：He learns how to move solid objects by willpower from an aggressive New York City subway poltergeist .
- **S14**：He persuades Oda Mae to thwart Carl's money laundering scheme.
- **S15**：Following his instructions, Oda Mae impersonates the owner of Carl's fake bank account, closes the account, and gives its $4 million contents to a homeless shelter .
- **S16**：Carl, due to transfer the money to a correspondent bank overseas, becomes desperate when he finds the account closed and empty.
- **S17**：Sam taunts him in the deserted office by moving objects and making accusations appear on his computer screen, typing MURDERER, then his name , making it repeat.
- **S18**：Carl visits Molly and declares to Sam that he will kill Molly unless the money is returned that evening.
- **S19**：He and Willie then go to Oda Mae's apartment to find her.
- **S20**：Sam manages to get there first and warns Oda Mae and her two sisters, who quickly escape and take refuge in a neighbor's apartment.
- **S21**：Sam uses his powers to separate and distract Carl and Willie, who are ransacking Oda Mae's apartment in search of the money.
- **S22**：Horrified by the experience, Willie flees and is killed in a traffic accident.
- **S23**：Willie's ghost arises, sees his body and is told by Sam, he's dead.
- **S24**：A group of howling demons emerge from shadows of ordinary things and pull a screaming Willie into the shadows, while Sam watches in horror.
- **S25**：It is implied they take him to some kind of Hell.
- **S26**：Afterwards, Sam and Oda Mae return to Molly's apartment to warn her about Carl, but she refuses to let her in and breaks down in grief.
- **S27**：Sam finally convinces her that Oda Mae is genuine and he is truly present as a ghost by having her push a penny underneath the front door and Sam levitating it in front of Molly.
- **S28**：Astonished, Molly lets Ode Mae inside and while waiting for the police, Sam uses Oda Mae's body to share a final dance with Molly.
- **S29**：Carl arrives, prepared to murder Molly and Oda Mae, but they flee.
- **S30**：Sam is left momentarily weakened, as possession wipes a ghost out.
- **S31**：In the storage room, Carl manages to get a hold of Oda Mae and threatens to kill her, but Molly comes to her aid and holds Carl off long enough for Oda Mae to escape his grasp.
- **S32**：Carl grabs Molly and hold her at gunpoint for the money but is unsuccessful and as Sam recovers he is able to disarm Carl and attack him.
- **S33**：As Carl attempts to flee through a plate glass window, he flings a scaffolding hook in Sam's direction which swings back and partially shatters the glass in the upper part of the window; the window slides down and kills Carl as he climbs out.
- **S34**：Carl becomes a ghost himself much to his astonishment and to Sam's grief.
- **S35**：Sam then watches in silence as the shadowy demons arrive and drag a screaming Carl away into the darkness.
- **S36**：As Sam goes to Oda Mae and Molly and asks if they are all right, Molly suddenly realizes that she can hear him.
- **S37**：A heavenly light fills the room and Sam becomes fully visible to both Molly and Oda Mae.
- **S38**：Sam looks behind him and sees hundreds of people, presumably angels, in a portal to Heaven.
- **S39**：His task is completed and he can move on.
- **S40**：Sam says an emotional farewell to Molly, thanks Oda Mae for her help, and departs to the afterlife.

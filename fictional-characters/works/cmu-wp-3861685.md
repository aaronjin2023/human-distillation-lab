## 角色资料

### Huo Yuanjia

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-eeac1a9ebc2883d8` · 独立版本：`profile-9127695356e03014`

- **性格倾向**：成名阶段面对冒犯倾向逞强报复，容易把挑战理解为个人羞辱（行为推断；S8、S10、S11）
- **核心动机**：最初习武由父亲落败带来的羞辱驱动，渴望恢复家族荣誉（摘要直接叙述；S4、S5、S6）
- **价值取舍**：后期把仁慈落实为对敌手的保护，不再以重创对方证明胜利（行为推断；S17、S21、S37）
- **情绪反应**：得知冲突起因后被丧亲悲痛与羞耻压倒，一度逃离原有生活（摘要直接叙述；S12、S14、S15）
- **成长变化**：从违背父亲宽恕理念的争胜者，成长为能够克制致命一击的人（行为推断；S9、S11、S19、S37）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：早期以杀伤挽回荣誉，后来却以克制杀伤体现武术价值。

### Anno Tanaka

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-f1f0e6514c11d256` · 独立版本：`profile-c183952f231332e7`

- **性格倾向**：在对手突然失去战力时倾向克制，未把胜负视为唯一目标（行为推断；S35、S38）
- **关系模式**：与霍元甲虽处于竞赛对立位置，赛前仍通过饮茶建立友谊（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **决策方式**：发现霍元甲身体严重恶化时，立即要求停赛并延后较量（摘要直接叙述；S33、S34、S35）
- **价值取舍**：最终主动承认霍元甲获胜，显示此刻认可超越压制对手的胜利（行为推断；S36、S37、S38）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film starts with Huo Yuanjia fighting three Westerners: a British boxer, a Belgian lancer, and a Spanish fencer.
- **S2**：Huo defeats all three of them and has a flashback before the next fight with Anno Tanaka from Japan.
- **S3**：Huo Yuanjia watches his father Huo Endi teaching students martial arts and wants to participate, but his father is concerned about his asthma and refuses to allow him to practise martial arts.
- **S4**：Huo Yuanjia then sees his father in a leitai match with another martial artist named Zhao, who won the match dishonourably by retaliating when Huo Endi showed mercy on the final blow.
- **S5**：Huo Yuanjia felt humiliated by his father's defeat and vowed to regain his family's honour and pride.
- **S6**：He practised martial arts secretly behind his father's back.
- **S7**：As the years passed, Huo Yuanjia defeated several opponents in leitai matches and became one of the most famous martial artists in Tianjin.
- **S8**：However, as he became increasingly successful and popular, he also became more arrogant and ruthless towards his opponents.
- **S9**：His late father, however, advocated the practice of showing mercy and not doing any serious physical harm to opponents.
- **S10**：When a rival martial arts master named Qin Lei injures one of his followers, Huo feels insulted and confronts Qin at the latter's birthday party.
- **S11**：The confrontation escalates into a fight between Huo and Qin, in which Huo emerges as the victor by killing Qin with a fatal blow to the chest.
- **S12**：However, Qin's godson seeks vengeance on Huo and kills Huo's mother and daughter in revenge.
- **S13**：Guided by fury, Huo goes to Qin's house and Qin's godson admits to the murders before killing himself.
- **S14**：Later, Huo learns that it was his follower who had provoked Qin earlier, which resulted in his beating from Qin.
- **S15**：Overwhelmed with grief and shame, Huo flees Tianjin and wanders aimlessly for many miles.
- **S16**：A dishevelled, greying wanderer, he nearly drowns in a river, but is saved by Granny Sun and her blind granddaughter Yueci.
- **S17**：They bring him back to their village and Huo, guided by their simple acts of kindness, begins to learn the value of kindness and mercy.
- **S18**：In 1907, Huo returns to Tianjin and sees the changes that have taken place in his absence.
- **S19**：He apologises to the family of Qin and reconciles with his businessman friend, Nong Jinsun, whom he offended earlier.
- **S20**：He challenges the American wrestler, Hercules O'Brien, who had been making headlines by defeating Chinese martial artists and calling the Chinese "weak men of the East", and defeats O'Brien.
- **S21**：He saves O'Brien from being impaled by some nails on the side of the ring that had become exposed during the fight and wins the appreciation of O'Brien, who names Huo the victor.
- **S22**：Huo's fame begins to spread with successive challenges with other foreign fighters.
- **S23**：In 1909, with funding from Nong Jinsun, he founds Chin Woo Athletic Association in Shanghai.
- **S24**：The members of the foreign chamber of commerce fear that Huo's victories might fan anti-foreign sentiments in the Chinese people and thus become a disadvantage to them.
- **S25**：They propose a match between Huo and four foreign champions.
- **S26**：Huo takes up the challenge, even though it is an unfair one.
- **S27**：Before the matches, Huo meets the Japanese champion Tanaka for tea and strikes up a friendship with him.
- **S28**：Back to September 14, 1910, Huo faces Tanaka in a titanic battle.
- **S29**：In the first round, they fight with their weapons of choice.
- **S30**：Huo uses a three-section staff and Tanaka uses a katana.
- **S31**：The first round is a draw.
- **S32**：Before the next round, Huo drinks from a teacup containing poison, which had replaced his original teacup.
- **S33**：In the second round involving unarmed combat, Huo suddenly has difficulty in breathing and loses his strength.
- **S34**：He collapses and starts coughing blood, dying from arsenic poisoning.
- **S35**：Tanaka and Huo's supporters immediately demand that the match be halted and postponed, but Huo replies that he wants it to continue since he is going to die soon.
- **S36**：Huo is dominated by Tanaka but he manages to deliver a blow to Tanaka's chest, similar to the one he used on Qin.
- **S37**：Huo could have killed Tanaka with that blow but he refrained from doing so and collapsed.
- **S38**：Tanaka declares Huo the victor moments before Huo's death.
- **S39**：A scene then shows Huo's ghost training in the field and Yueci runs towards him.
- **S40**：The film ends as Huo smiles at her.

## 角色资料

### Zhao Zilong

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a9255fed2589b90a` · 独立版本：`profile-2096e67fbffd8085`

- **性格倾向**：面对同伴受责与险境，倾向挺身承担而非退避自保（行为推断；S7、S9、S10）
- **核心动机**：年老且同代名将凋零后，仍坚持出征延续军事使命（行为推断；S17、S18、S19）
- **价值取舍**：在占据决斗优势时仍保留克制，曾放过两名敌方重要人物（行为推断；S12、S24）
- **关系模式**：与罗平安从同乡战友走向遭其背叛，最终仍选择和解（摘要直接叙述；S2、S28）
- **决策方式**：面对救援重任愿独自突破包围，以个人承担换取任务完成（行为推断；S9、S10、S11）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以不败将军著称，晚年却发现自己承担的是牵制敌军的任务。

### Luo Ping'an

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-ede58617e32772d7` · 独立版本：`profile-22bde656277c99d9`

- **性格倾向**：在背叛已成事实后，仍表现出愿向旧友认错的倾向（行为推断；S28）
- **决策方式**：结局中选择亲口揭露背叛，而非继续隐瞒自己的责任（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **情绪反应**：为赵子龙最后出击擂鼓时落泪，显露强烈的离别悲痛（行为推断；S28）
- **关系模式**：与同乡赵子龙的关系历经援护、背叛，最终重新达成和解（摘要直接叙述；S2、S6、S7、S28）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Zhao Zilong begins his career by enlisting in Liu Bei's army.
- **S2**：He befriends a fellow soldier Luo Ping'an, who is from the same hometown as him.
- **S3**：Not long later, Zhao participates in a battle against Liu Bei's rival Cao Cao.
- **S4**：He follows Zhuge Liang's plan and launches a sneak attack on the enemy camp at night, achieving his first victory.
- **S5**：Liu Bei is overwhelmed by Cao Cao and is forced to retreat to Phoenix Heights but is separated from his family during the chaos.
- **S6**：Luo is ordered to find and bring Liu's family back safely but fails.
- **S7**：Zhang Fei is angered and thrusts his spear towards Luo, but Zhao blocks the attack and engages Zhang and Guan Yu in a fight.
- **S8**：Zhao remains undefeated after dueling for several rounds and Liu Bei is impressed by his skill.
- **S9**：Zhao offers to retrieve Liu's family, and Guan Yu and Zhang Fei cover him while he breaks through enemy lines to begin the search.
- **S10**：Zhao rescues Liu Bei's infant son and holds off dozens of enemy soldiers alone despite being surrounded on all sides.
- **S11**：He fights his way out and charges towards Cao Cao, who is observing the battle nearby.
- **S12**：Cao is shocked and loses his sword to Zhao, but the latter spares his life and leaps to safety on a cliff.
- **S13**：Cao Cao's granddaughter Cao Ying witnesses the attack.
- **S14**：Zhao later returns to his hometown Changshan as a hero and falls in love with a girl putting on a shadow puppet show dedicated to him.
- **S15**：Zhao continues to fight many battles on Liu Bei's side and earns himself the title of "Invincible General" as he has never lost before.
- **S16**：When Liu Bei later becomes emperor of Shu, Zhao is named as one of the Five Tiger Generals, along with Guan Yu, Zhang Fei, Ma Chao and Huang Zhong.
- **S17**：After Liu Bei's death, Zhuge Liang persuades the new ruler Liu Shan to launch military campaigns against Wei to restore the Han Dynasty.
- **S18**：By then Zhao is already 71, and is the sole surviving one of the Five Tiger Generals.
- **S19**：He insists on going to battle and is joined by Guan Xing and Zhang Bao and Luo Ping'an.
- **S20**：Zhao's army reaches a fork in the road and splits into two forces heading in different directions, one led by Zhao himself while Guan and Zhang command the other.
- **S21**：Zhao later encounters the Wei general Han De and slays Han's four sons all by himself.
- **S22**：However he is lured into a trap set by the Wei commander Cao Ying and has to retreat to Phoenix Heights.
- **S23**：While he is surrounded on all sides and his forces has sustained heavy casualties, Zhao learns that his task is actually to distract the Wei army while Guan Xing and Zhang Bao proceed to capture enemy territory.
- **S24**：After attempts by both the Shu and Wei sides to instigate each other into battle, Zhao engages Cao Ying in a duel and defeats her but lets her go.
- **S25**：Cao Ying's forces later advance towards Phoenix Heights and Zhao allows his subordinates to lead all his men into battle.
- **S26**：The Shu soldiers launch a fierce assault on the Wei army and are nearly all wiped out when Han De sacrifices himself to launch a kamikaze-style attack to blow up the enemy with gunpowder.
- **S27**：Zhao and Luo Ping'an watch the battle and aftermath from Phoenix Heights.
- **S28**：At the end, Luo reveals that he betrayed Zhao, but the 2 men make peace with one another and Luo helps Zhao remove his armour and tearfully beats the battle drum as Zhao makes a long charge towards the enemy.

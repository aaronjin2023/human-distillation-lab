### Jack Byrnes

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-f689658d29121c54` · 独立版本：`profile-e7ad086d931e1c27`

- **性格倾向**：在家庭继承与婚姻问题上，倾向多疑并过度介入（行为推断；S4、S5、S6、S9）
- **核心动机**：因原定接班人出轨而失望，试图重新确保家庭稳定（行为推断；S3、S4、S5）
- **决策方式**：倾向凭可疑表象判断不忠，直到身体反应使其改判（行为推断；S5、S11、S15）
- **情绪反应**：对疑似背叛感到厌恶与愤怒，最终升级为公开争斗（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13）
- **成长变化**：从坚信女婿不忠，转为在争斗后承认相信其解释（摘要直接叙述；S11、S13、S15）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Gaylord "Greg" Focker  is preparing to celebrate his twins' fifth birthday party.
- **S2**：Things seem to go awry when Greg's father-in-law Jack Byrnes  visits.
- **S3**：Recently, Jack has been diagnosed with a heart condition and become embittered by his daughter Debbie's divorce from her husband, Bob , for cheating on her with a nurse.
- **S4**：Jack's plan was originally to name Bob as his successor of the Byrnes family, but decides to pass the role to Greg, naming him "The Godfocker".
- **S5**：Despite Greg begrudgingly accepting the role, Jack begins to suspect Greg of infidelity when he sees him with a drug rep, Andi Garcia , who openly flirts with him, and the presence of Sustengo erection pills in Greg's house, which prompts Jack to think Greg is no longer sexually attracted to his wife, Pam .
- **S6**：Furthermore, Jack starts to doubt Greg's ability to provide for his family when he appears reluctant to send his children to a private school.
- **S7**：During a medical conference promoting Sustengo, Greg meets Bob at a bar.
- **S8**：Bob tells Greg of Jack's original intention to name him as successor, "The Bobfather", and expresses relief and happiness at leaving Jack's family, which makes Greg slightly uncomfortable.
- **S9**：Jack, for his part, starts to speak with Pam about the possibility of divorcing Greg and renewing her relationship with her ex-fiancée, Kevin Rawley .
- **S10**：Eventually, following a row at a clinic, Greg runs away from home to his and Pam's unfinished new house, where he is paid a visit by Andi, who tries to cheer him up with takeout and wine, but Andi soon becomes so drunk that she makes an aggressive sexual pass at Greg.
- **S11**：While looking for Greg to apologise and bring him home, Jack pulls up to the house and sees, through the window, what he believes to be Greg and Andi having sex while Greg is trying to rebuff Andi's advances.
- **S12**：Disgusted, Jack merely leaves, but tells Dina and Pam that he was unable to find Greg.
- **S13**：At the twins' birthday party, Greg's parents, Bernie  and Roz  rejoin the family, but Jack, enraged at Greg's apparent infidelity, engages Greg in a physical fight, despite Greg insisting that Andi was drunk and he was rebuffing her.
- **S14**：The fight culminates with Jack having a heart attack and collapsing.
- **S15**：As he is taken away by paramedics, Jack quietly admits that he now believes Greg after feeling his carotid artery, which remained stable while Greg was claiming his innocence.
- **S16**：Four months later on Christmas Day, Greg and Pam's parents come to spend Christmas with them in their new house.
- **S17**：Greg's parents  give Jack a present of a kippah, informing him they traced his family roots back while nursing him back to health, discovering he is part Jewish.
- **S18**：Bernie informs Greg and Pam that he and Roz have sold their island home and are moving to Chicago only two houses down from theirs.
- **S19**：Jack and Dina decide they will move too, because they also want to be close to their grandchildren.
- **S20**：The film ends with Greg and Pam trying to wean their parents off the idea.

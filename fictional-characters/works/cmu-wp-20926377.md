## 角色资料

### John Hancock

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-4263fe89e05ff922` · 独立版本：`profile-134eec0f621b24e9`

- **性格倾向**：在早期行动中，他显出对自身破坏后果疏于负责的倾向。（行为推断；S2、S4）
- **核心动机**：从阻止犯罪与救援行动看，他仍有帮助受困者的意愿。（行为推断；S2、S6、S11）
- **决策方式**：他接受入狱安排，配合雷改善公众对他的观感。（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9）
- **关系模式**：他选择离开濒死的玛丽，让分离帮助她恢复能力并疗伤。（摘要直接叙述；S22、S29）
- **成长变化**：他从被公众嘲讽转为重新获得认可，并继续担任英雄。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S12、S13、S30）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他实施救援，却也在行动中造成严重财物损失。

### Ray Embrey

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-6676187c2cbf4601` · 独立版本：`profile-c488755c15cd5b46`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他倾向于给受人嘲讽的汉考克实际支持。（行为推断；S7、S8）
- **核心动机**：他希望推广慈善标志，也想改善汉考克的公众形象。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S8）
- **决策方式**：他提出让汉考克入狱，以此呈现城市对英雄的依赖。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10）
- **价值取舍**：医院遇袭时，他倾向冒险介入，保护遭到追杀的汉考克。（行为推断；S25、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：John Hancock  is an alcoholic man with super powers, including flight, invulnerability, immortality and super-strength.
- **S2**：Though he uses his powers to stop criminals in his current residence of Los Angeles, his activity inadvertently causes millions of dollars in property damage due to his constant intoxication.
- **S3**：As a result, he is routinely jeered at the crime scenes.
- **S4**：Hancock also ignores court subpoenas from the city of Los Angeles to address the property damage he has caused.
- **S5**：When public relations spokesperson Ray Embrey  departs from an unsuccessful meeting pitching his All-Heart logo for corporations who are extraordinarily charitable, he becomes trapped on railroad tracks with an incoming freight train.
- **S6**：Hancock saves Ray's life, but he causes the train to derail and nearly injures another driver.
- **S7**：Hancock is jeered by other drivers for causing more damage, but Ray steps in and thanks Hancock for saving his life.
- **S8**：Ray offers to improve Hancock's public image, and Hancock grudgingly accepts.
- **S9**：The spokesperson convinces the alcoholic superhero to permit himself to be jailed for outstanding subpoenas so they can show Los Angeles how much the city really needs Hancock.
- **S10**：When the crime rate rises after Hancock's incarceration, the superhero is contacted by the Chief of Police.
- **S11**：With a new costume from Ray, Hancock intervenes with a bank robbery, rescuing a cop and stopping the leader of the robbers, Red Parker .
- **S12**：After the rescue, Hancock is applauded for handling the bank robbery.
- **S13**：The superhero becomes popular once more, as Ray had predicted.
- **S14**：He goes out to dinner with Ray and his wife Mary , with whom he reveals his apparent immortality and his amnesia from 80 years ago.
- **S15**：After Hancock tucks a drunken Ray in bed, he discovers that Mary also has superhuman powers.
- **S16**：He threatens to expose her until she explains their origins, and she tells him that they have lived for 3,000 years with their powers, having been called gods and angels in their time.
- **S17**：She explains that they are the last of their kind and that their kind are paired.
- **S18**：Mary does not tell Hancock the entire truth, and Hancock departs to tell Ray about the conversation.
- **S19**：The exchange results in a battle between Hancock and Mary that takes them to downtown Los Angeles, causing significant damage to the area.
- **S20**：Ray, downtown in a business meeting, sees and recognizes Mary using superhero powers like Hancock.
- **S21**：Hancock is later shot twice in the chest and wounded when he stops a liquor store robbery.
- **S22**：After being hospitalized, Mary enters and explains that as the pair of immortals gets close, they begin to lose their powers.
- **S23**：She also explains that Hancock was attacked in an alley 80 years prior, causing his amnesia.
- **S24**：Mary deserted him then in order for him to recover from his injuries.
- **S25**：When he is hospitalized, the hospital is raided by Red Parker, the bank robber, and two men that Hancock had humiliated during his incarceration.
- **S26**：Mary, visiting Hancock, is shot in the process.
- **S27**：Hancock is able to stop two men but is further wounded by them.
- **S28**：When Red attempts to finish Hancock off, Ray comes to the rescue and disarms and kills the bank robber with a fire axe.
- **S29**：With Mary nearly dying, Hancock flees from the hospital so their parting would allow her to heal with her powers.
- **S30**：He later winds up in New York City, working as a superhero.
- **S31**：Ray is seen walking with Mary discussing historical events such as the reign of Attila the Hun in a jovial manner.
- **S32**：As gratitude to Ray, Hancock paints Ray's All-Heart logo on the moon and calls the spokesperson to look up to the worldwide advertisement.

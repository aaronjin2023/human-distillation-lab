## 角色资料

### Rick Deckard

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e16ba0933316a7bc` · 独立版本：`profile-ed698fce794725e1`

- **性格倾向**：在侦查情境中，倾向依靠细小物证耐心追索目标（行为推断；S15）
- **核心动机**：重返追捕工作主要出于上司威胁，而非主动请缨（摘要直接叙述；S6、S7）
- **决策方式**：以照片与蛇鳞交叉追踪，将线索转化为具体搜查方向（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **关系模式**：与瑞秋由身份检验者和受检者，转为相互庇护的伴侣（行为推断；S8、S18、S32、S33）
- **价值取舍**：面对瑞秋时，私人保护承诺开始超越追杀命令（行为推断；S16、S18、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：执行复制人追杀任务，却承诺保护同属复制人的瑞秋。

### Roy Batty

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-c3d18d3b2eed3edf` · 独立版本：`profile-fa9f2b0c7522dbae`

- **性格倾向**：面对寿命限制时，表现出强烈抗争和主动求解的倾向（行为推断；S10、S22）
- **核心动机**：突破制造者设定的寿命，争取继续生存的机会（摘要直接叙述；S22）
- **决策方式**：为接近戒备森严的制造者，先逼问线索再借人引路（摘要直接叙述；S10、S11、S22）
- **情绪反应**：临终时关注自身记忆即将消失，流露对经验消逝的感伤（行为推断；S31）
- **成长变化**：从延寿无望后杀死制造者，到临终前救下追杀自己的敌手（摘要直接叙述；S23、S25、S30、S31）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：为延长自己的生命杀人，却在生命将尽时拯救敌人。

### Rachael

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-98d172946e606750` · 独立版本：`profile-a2f266e69e5790a4`

- **性格倾向**：身份受到质疑时，倾向主动寻找证据维护自我认知（行为推断；S12）
- **核心动机**：试图以家庭照片证明人类身份，保住原有身世认同（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13）
- **情绪反应**：得知珍视的记忆来自植入后，因身份崩塌而落泪离开（行为推断；S13）
- **决策方式**：面对戴克遇袭的即时危险，选择持枪介入并救下他（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18）
- **关系模式**：与戴克从身份揭穿造成的伤害，逐渐走向共同离开（摘要直接叙述；S13、S18、S32、S33）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Hatnote}} In Los Angeles, November 2019, retired police officer Rick Deckard  is detained by officer Gaff  and brought to meet with his former supervisor, Bryant .
- **S2**：Deckard, whose job as a "Blade Runner" was to track down bioengineered beings known as replicants and "retire"  them, is told by Bryant that several have escaped and come to Earth illegally.
- **S3**：As Tyrell Corporation Nexus-6 models, they have only a four-year lifespan, and may have come to Earth to try to extend their lives.
- **S4**：Deckard watches a video of another Blade Runner named Holden  administering a "Voight-Kampff" test designed to distinguish replicants from humans based on their empathic response to questions.
- **S5**：The subject of the test, Leon , shoots Holden.
- **S6**：Bryant wants Deckard to return to work to retire Leon and three other replicants—Roy Batty , Zhora  and Pris .
- **S7**：Deckard refuses, but after Bryant makes an overt threat, he reluctantly agrees.
- **S8**：Sent to the Tyrell Corporation to ensure that the test works on Nexus-6 models, Deckard discovers that Tyrell's  assistant Rachael  is an experimental replicant who believes herself to be human; Rachael's consciousness has been enhanced with false memories to provide an "emotional cushion".
- **S9**：As a result, a more extensive test is required to determine if she is a replicant.
- **S10**：Trying to find a way to meet with Tyrell, Roy and Leon go to the eye-manufacturing laboratory of Chew , forcing him to divulge the identity of J.F.
- **S11**：Sebastian , a gifted designer who works closely with Tyrell.
- **S12**：Rachael visits Deckard at his apartment to prove her humanity by showing him a family photo.
- **S13**：Dropping the photo to the floor, Rachael leaves in tears after Deckard tells her that her memories are only implants taken from a real person.
- **S14**：Sent by Roy, Pris meets and gains the confidence of Sebastian at his apartment, where he lives with manufactured companions.
- **S15**：While searching Leon's apartment, Deckard finds a photo of Zhora, and a synthetic snake scale that leads him to a strip club where Zhora works.
- **S16**：Deckard retires Zhora, and shortly after is told by Bryant to add Rachael, who has disappeared from the Tyrell Corporation headquarters, to his list of retirements.
- **S17**：Deckard spots Rachael in a crowd but is disarmed and attacked by Leon, who saw him kill Zhora.
- **S18**：Rachael kills Leon using Deckard's gun, and the two return to Deckard's apartment, where he promises not to hunt her.
- **S19**：Later they share an intimate moment; Rachael then tries to leave, but Deckard seduces her.
- **S20**：Arriving at Sebastian's apartment, Roy tells Pris the others are dead.
- **S21**：Sympathetic to their plight, Sebastian reveals that because of a genetic disorder that accelerates his aging, his life will also be cut short.
- **S22**：Under a pretext, Sebastian and Roy gain entrance into Tyrell's secure penthouse, where Roy demands more life from his maker.
- **S23**：Told that this has proved to be impossible, Roy confesses that he has done "questionable things".
- **S24**：Tyrell dismisses Roy's guilt, praising Roy's advanced design and his accomplishments.
- **S25**：Responding "Nothing the god of biomechanics wouldn't let you into heaven for", Roy kisses Tyrell, then kills him.
- **S26**：Sebastian runs for the elevator followed by Roy, who rides the elevator down alone.
- **S27**：Upon entering Sebastian's apartment, Deckard is ambushed by Pris, but manages to kill her just as Roy returns.
- **S28**：Roy fights Deckard without using his full strength against him.
- **S29**：Eventually Deckard is hanging from a rooftop.
- **S30**：Just as he is about to fall, Roy saves him.
- **S31**：His life ending, Roy delivers a monologue on how his memories are about to be lost and dies in front of Deckard, who watches silently.
- **S32**：Gaff arrives and, referring to Rachael, shouts to Deckard "It's too bad she won't live, but then again, who does?" Deckard returns to his apartment to find Rachael alive and sleeping in his bed; as they leave, Deckard finds a small tin-foil unicorn, a calling card left by his origami-making partner Gaff.
- **S33**：Depending on the version, Deckard and Rachael either leave the apartment block to an uncertain future, or drive through an idyllic pastoral landscape.

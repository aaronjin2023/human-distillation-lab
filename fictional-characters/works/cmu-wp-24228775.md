### Uxbal

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-1b380bb316fa23d4` · 独立版本：`profile-5c492fe8181412b6`

- **性格倾向**：在生活失控时，仍表现出为身边弱者承担照料的倾向（行为推断；S10、S13、S17）
- **核心动机**：确知生命将尽后，核心关切转为安排两个孩子的照护（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17、S20）
- **决策方式**：确认前妻无法可靠养育孩子后，转而托付积蓄与照护责任（摘要直接叙述；S11、S17）
- **情绪反应**：因自己提供的取暖设备导致移民死亡，持续受到内疚折磨（摘要直接叙述；S13、S15）
- **关系模式**：向友人妻儿提供住处，后来又依赖她延续对孩子的照顾（摘要直接叙述；S10、S17、S20）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

### Ige

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-dbf4dc0079c4fca8` · 独立版本：`profile-21aa4bf035187752`

- **性格倾向**：在返乡与照护承诺冲突时，表现出反复权衡并能回转的倾向（行为推断；S18、S19）
- **核心动机**：接受照护委托后，一度打算用所托付的钱支付返非洲费用（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Uxbal lives in a shabby apartment in Barcelona with his two young children, Ana and Mateo .
- **S2**：He is separated from their mother Marambra, an unreliable and reckless woman suffering from alcoholism and bipolar disorder.
- **S3**：Having grown up an orphan, Uxbal has no family other than his brother Tito, who works in the construction business.
- **S4**：Uxbal earns a living by organizing work of illegal immigrants, a group of Chinese producing forged goods, and a group of African street vendors selling them.
- **S5**：He is able to talk to the dead and is sometimes paid to pass on messages from the recently deceased at wakes and funerals.
- **S6**：When he is diagnosed with terminal prostate cancer and is told to only have a few months to live, his world progressively falls apart.
- **S7**：Uxbal initially begins chemotherapy, but later ends the treatment at the advice of his friend and traditional healer Bea.
- **S8**：She also gives him two black stones which she asks him to give his children before he dies.
- **S9**：The group of Africans are brutally arrested by the police despite Uxbal's regular bribes.
- **S10**：As his friend Ekweme faces deportation to Senegal, Uxbal offers Ekweme's wife Ige and their baby son a room in his apartment.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, an attempt at reconciliation with Marambra fails when Uxbal realizes she cannot be trusted to raise his children.
- **S12**：Tito brokers a deal to put the Chinese to work at a construction site.
- **S13**：However, almost all of them die while asleep in the basement of their sweatshop due to malfunctioning gas heaters that Uxbal provided to heat their clammy sleeping quarters.
- **S14**：An attempt by the human trafficker to dump the bodies of the Chinese into the sea fails when they are washed up on the shore shortly after.
- **S15**：As Uxbal's health continues to deteriorate, he is plagued with guilt that he is responsible for the death of the Chinese immigrants.
- **S16**：As his death draws nearer, he realizes that there will be nobody to take care of Ana and Mateo once he is gone.
- **S17**：He entrusts the remainder of his savings to Ige, asking her to stay with the children after his death.
- **S18**：She accepts his request, but later decides to use the money to pay for her return to Africa.
- **S19**：However, she changes her mind at the last minute and returns to the apartment.
- **S20**：Knowing that Ige will take care of his children, Uxbal lies down next to Ana and dies after having passed on to her a diamond ring which his father had once given to his mother.
- **S21**：After his death, he is reunited in a snowy winter landscape with his father, who had died before Uxbal's birth shortly after having fled Spain for Mexico during the Franco regime.

### Paikea Apirana

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d4addd9271560f2a` · 独立版本：`profile-bb85145a5d51ac3d`

- **性格倾向**：面对性别排斥与祖父责难，她倾向坚持学习并以实际行动证明能力（行为推断；S7、S8、S12、S13、S25）
- **核心动机**：她希望承担部落领导责任，即使没有女性继承的先例也不放弃（摘要直接叙述；S2、S7、S8）
- **价值取舍**：她追求突破性别限制，同时珍视祖父与村落传统的延续（行为推断；S8、S18、S19）
- **决策方式**：正式学习受阻时，她转向秘密求教，并在救鲸时主动承担风险（行为推断；S12、S13、S23、S25、S26）

矛盾与变化：争取突破祖父设下的限制，同时努力修复与祖父的关系

### Koro

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-92cc1cec3840af42` · 独立版本：`profile-e0f3a73d9a3df9e9`

- **性格倾向**：面对传承危机时，他倾向固守男性继承规则并抗拒反例（行为推断；S9、S11、S14、S23）
- **核心动机**：他设立文化学校并安排传统考验，希望为部落找到合格领袖（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10、S15）
- **情绪反应**：继承人遴选受挫与鲸群搁浅，使他不断加深自责和绝望（摘要直接叙述；S16、S22）
- **成长变化**：确认孙女的能力后，他承认她的领袖地位并主动请求原谅（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：疼爱并照顾孙女，却又将部落冲突与危机归责于她

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film's plot follows the story of Paikea Apirana  [In the book, her name is Kahu, short for Kahutia Te Rangi].The leader should be the first-born son – a direct patrilineal descendant of Paikea, aka Kahutia Te Rangi in the book, the Whale Rider – he who rode on top of a whale from Hawaiki.
- **S2**：However, Pai is female and technically cannot inherit the leadership.
- **S3**：While he does later form an affectionate bond with his granddaughter, carrying her to school every day on his bicycle, he also condemns her and blames her for conflicts happening within the tribe.
- **S4**：At one point Paikea decides to leave with her father because her grandfather is mistreating her.
- **S5**：However she finds that she cannot bear to leave the sea as the whale seems to be calling her back, she tells her father to turn the car back and returns home.
- **S6**：Pai's father refuses to assume traditional leadership; instead he moves to Germany to pursue a career as an artist.
- **S7**：Pai herself is interested in the leadership, learning traditional songs and dances, but is given little encouragement by her grandfather.
- **S8**：Pai feels that she can become the leader, although there's no precedent for a woman to do so, and is determined to succeed.
- **S9**：Koro decides to form a cultural school for the village boys, hoping to find a new leader.
- **S10**：He teaches the boys to use a taiaha .
- **S11**：This is traditionally reserved for males.
- **S12**：However, Nanny tells Pai that her second son, Pai's uncle, had won a taiaha tournament in his youth while he was still slim, so Pai secretly learns from him.
- **S13**：She also secretly follows Koro's lessons.
- **S14**：One of the students, Hemi, is also sympathetic towards her, but Koro is enraged when he finds out, particularly when she wins her taiaha fight against Hemi.
- **S15**：Koro's relationship with Pai erodes further when none of the boys succeed at the traditional task of recovering the rei puta  that he threw into the ocean – this mission would prove one of them worthy of becoming leader.
- **S16**：With the loss of the rei puta, Koro in despair calls out the Ancient ones, the whales.
- **S17**：In an attempt to help, Pai from the beach also calls out to them and they hear her call.
- **S18**：Pai, in an attempt to bridge the rift that has formed, invites Koro to be her guest of honour at a concert of Māori chants that her school is putting on.
- **S19**：Unknown to all, she had won an inter-school speech contest with a touching dedication to Koro and the traditions of the village.
- **S20**：However, Koro was late, and as he was walking to the school, he notices that numerous right whales are beached near Pai's home.
- **S21**：The entire village attempts to coax and drag them back into the water, but all efforts prove unsuccessful; even a tractor does not help because the rope breaks.
- **S22**：Koro sees it as a sign of his failure and despairs further.
- **S23**：He admonishes Pai against touching the largest whale because "she has done enough damage" with her presumption.
- **S24**：Also, the largest whale traditionally belongs to the legendary Paikea.
- **S25**：But when Koro walks away, she climbs onto the back of the largest whale and coaxes it to re-enter the ocean.
- **S26**：The whale leads the entire pod back into the sea; Paikea nearly drowns in the process.
- **S27**：When she goes out to sea, Nanny Flowers shows Koro the whale tooth which Pai had previously recovered.
- **S28**：When Pai is found and brought to the hospital, Koro declares her the leader and asks her forgiveness.
- **S29**：The film ends with the village, including Pai's father, uncle, and grandparents, celebrating her status as leader, as the finished waka is hauled into the sea for its maiden voyage.

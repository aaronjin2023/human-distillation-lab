## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Inciting Incident and Investigation [ edit ] In November 1963, shortly after the Assassination of John F.
- **S3**：Kennedy, freelance crime reporter Peter Miller follows an ambulance to the Hamburg apartment of Salomon Tauber, a Jewish Holocaust survivor who has committed suicide.
- **S4**：A sympathetic police friend gives Miller Tauber's diary, which details atrocities committed at the Riga Ghetto under its commandant, Eduard Roschmann, known as the "the butcher of Riga".
- **S5**：The diary notes that Roschmann was recently seen alive and prosperous in West Germany.
- **S6**：Tauber also describes Roschmann murdering a decorated German Army captain who attempted to prevent SS atrocities during the ghetto's evacuation.
- **S7**：Deeply moved, Miller resolves to locate Roschmann.
- **S8**：Miller approaches West German prosecution authorities but finds them indifferent to chasing former Nazis.
- **S9**：His search brings him to Nazi hunter Simon Wiesenthal, who explains the existence of ODESSA ( Organisation der ehemaligen SS-Angehörigen ), a secret network protecting former Waffen-SS members.
- **S10**：Miller's inquiries attract a group of Jewish vigilantes with ties to the Mossad seeking to neutralize former Nazi scientists and officers.
- **S11**：Recognizing Miller's potential, the vigilantes recruit him to infiltrate ODESSA.
- **S12**：Infiltration of ODESSA [ edit ] After receiving covert training from a repentant former SS officer to pass as a veteran sergeant, Miller contacts a lawyer affiliated with ODESSA.
- **S13**：Passing intense scrutiny, he is directed to a master passport forger who supplies fugitive SS personnel with false identities.
- **S14**：Miller breaks into the forger's home and steals the secret "ODESSA File"—a registry containing the true names, false identities, and locations of former SS members across West Germany.
- **S15**：However, Miller's cover is compromised, prompting ODESSA leadership to deploy an elite hitman to eliminate him.
- **S16**：Miller survives a series of assassination attempts, including a car bomb rigged to his sports car a Jaguar XK150 S that fails to detonate due to the vehicle's stiff suspension.
- **S17**：Confrontation and Aftermath [ edit ] Tracking Roschmann to his villa under an alias —where Roschmann serves as head of an industrial firm—Miller holds him at gunpoint.
- **S18**：Roschmann attempts to justify his wartime actions on racial grounds, but Miller reveals his true motive: he is not hunting war criminals out of ideological duty, but seeking personal justice for the army captain murdered by Roschmann, who was Miller's father, Erwin.
- **S19**：Before Miller can hand Roschmann over to the authorities, Roschmann's bodyguard disarms and knocks Miller unconscious.
- **S20**：The bodyguard attempts to drive Miller's car to summon aid, but the vehicle hits a snowbank, detonating the unexploded car bomb and killing him.
- **S21**：Roschmann escapes in the confusion and flees to Argentina.
- **S22**：Meanwhile, the hitman pursuing Miller is killed by "Josef", a Mossad agent tailing Miller.
- **S23**：While recovering in a hospital, Miller is informed by Josef that the stolen ODESSA File has been turned over to West German authorities, prompting widespread arrests of former SS personnel.
- **S24**：Furthermore, Roschmann's firm—which was secretly manufacturing guidance systems for biological weapons planned by the Egyptian military to destroy Israel—is shut down.
- **S25**：Josef returns to Israel with Tauber's diary and visits Yad Vashem , reciting Kaddish for the soul of Salomon Tauber.

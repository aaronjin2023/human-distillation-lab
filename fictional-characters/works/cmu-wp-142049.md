### Director Lamar Burgess

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-b9d76928251467c5` · 独立版本：`profile-d7dac337dff9fdb6`

- **性格倾向**：在秘密可能败露时倾向冷酷自保，并迅速转嫁罪责（行为推断；S27、S28）

- **决策方式**：倾向利用系统盲点和权力位置，让犯罪逃过制度审查（行为推断；S25、S26、S27、S28、S32）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In April 2054, Captain John Anderton  is chief of the highly controversial Washington, D.C., PreCrime police force.
- **S2**：They use future visions generated by three "precogs", mutated humans with precognitive abilities, to stop murders; because of this, the city has been murder-free for six years.
- **S3**：Though Anderton is a respected member of the force, he is addicted to Neuroin, an illegal psychoactive drug.
- **S4**：His addiction started following the disappearance of his son Sean, which also caused his wife Lara to leave him.
- **S5**：With the PreCrime force poised to go nationwide, the system is audited by Danny Witwer , a member of the United States Justice Department.
- **S6**：During the audit, the precogs predict that Anderton will murder a man named Leo Crow in 36 hours.
- **S7**：Believing the incident to be a setup by Witwer, who is aware of Anderton's addiction, Anderton attempts to hide the case and quickly departs the area before Witwer begins a manhunt for him.
- **S8**：Anderton seeks the advice of Dr.
- **S9**：Iris Hineman , the lead researcher of the PreCrime technology.
- **S10**：She explains to Anderton that sometimes the three precogs see different visions of the future, in which case the system only provides data on the two reports which agree; the "minority report", reflecting the potential future where a predicted killer would have done something different, is discarded.
- **S11**：According to Dr.
- **S12**：Hineman, the female precog Agatha is most likely to be the precog that witnesses the minority report.
- **S13**：Anderton undergoes a dangerous underground eye replacement to avoid detection by the city's optical recognition system.
- **S14**：He travels back to PreCrime and kidnaps Agatha , which disables the precogs' hive mind and shuts down the system.
- **S15**：Anderton takes Agatha to a shopping mall, and a hacker, who extracts both Agatha's vision of Crow's murder&mdash;with no differences from the other two precogs, so there was no minority report&mdash;and another of the murder of a woman named Anne Lively&mdash;which Agatha also showed to Anderton the day before he was incriminated.
- **S16**：Anderton and Agatha then head to the apartment where Crow is to be killed.
- **S17**：Inside, Anderton finds hundreds of pictures of children, one of which is of his son, and concludes that Crow is responsible for Sean's disappearance.
- **S18**：When Crow arrives, Anderton holds him at gunpoint, but ultimately decides to control his anger and places Crow under arrest instead.
- **S19**：Crow admits that he was hired to plant these photos and then be killed, so his family would be paid handsomely.
- **S20**：Since Anderton refuses to kill him, Crow grabs the officer's hand and makes him fire at point-blank range, effectively committing suicide by cop.
- **S21**：On the run, Anderton and Agatha approach his ex-wife Lara for refuge.
- **S22**：Anderton learns Lively was Agatha's former drug-addicted mother, once before a target of a failed murder attempt after requesting to see her daughter before her death.
- **S23**：Anderton realizes that his knowledge of the Lively case is why he is being targeted.
- **S24**：Meanwhile, Witwer assesses Crow's "murder" and doubts that Anderton killed him in cold blood.
- **S25**：He comes to recognize that the archival footage of Lively's murder shows what appears to be a future echo by one of the visions, routinely discarded by PreCrime, which he realizes is a different murder as nearby water is rippling in a different direction from the original PreCrime vision.
- **S26**：He suspects someone used this to stage the first murder attempt, and then recreated the setting to actually kill Lively and avoid being detected by PreCrime.
- **S27**：Witwer realizes that the murderer would had to have been someone high up in PreCrime to have access to the vision, and reports these findings to PreCrime's Director Lamar Burgess .
- **S28**：Burgess, noting that the PreCrime division is currently disabled due to Agatha's absence, kills Witwer and frames Anderton for that murder as well.
- **S29**：The PreCrime unit eventually captures Anderton and restores Agatha to the system.
- **S30**：Burgess attempts to comfort Lara, but accidentally reveals that he was the one that killed Lively.
- **S31**：Lara acts on this information and frees Anderton at gunpoint.
- **S32**：At a banquet to celebrate the success of the PreCrime unit attended by Burgess, Anderton plays back Agatha's vision of Lively's murder for the gathered crowd, which shows Burgess as the murderer.
- **S33**：While Burgess begins to hunt down Anderton, a new PreCrime report is created: Anderton is the victim and Burgess, the murderer.
- **S34**：When Burgess catches up to Anderton, Anderton explains the impossible situation: if Burgess kills Anderton, he proves the system works but at the cost of a life sentence, while if he does not, the system will not have worked and the PreCrime division will be shut down.
- **S35**：Anderton explains the fundamental flaw in the system: if one knows his or her future, he or she can change it.
- **S36**：Burgess resolves the dilemma by killing himself.
- **S37**：The PreCrime program is shut down and the prisoners are unconditionally pardoned and released, though police departments keep watch on many of them.
- **S38**：Anderton and Lara remarry and start a new family.
- **S39**：The precogs are sent to an "undisclosed location", a small uncharted island in the North Atlantic Ocean to live out a full happy life in peace.

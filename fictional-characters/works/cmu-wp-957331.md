### Zi Yuan

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5604913f1161a149` · 独立版本：`profile-b12996b7282fa38a`

### The Dragon Emperor

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0bbdc0495d607fcc` · 独立版本：`profile-72291eeca302558a`

- **性格倾向**：在统治与复起过程中呈现专横残酷倾向，以暴力处置他人（行为推断；S1、S2、S7、S16）
- **核心动机**：因害怕死亡使既有功业尽失而追求永生，并进一步谋求征服（摘要直接叙述；S4、S5、S29）
- **价值取舍**：将自身统治的延续置于他人生命之上，敌人与合作者均可牺牲（行为推断；S2、S7、S16、S29）
- **决策方式**：复起后倾向整合军事追随者与超自然力量扩大统治优势（行为推断；S16、S27、S28、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：拥有庞大帝国与超自然能力，仍因死亡可能终结功业而恐惧。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In ancient China, Han, a brutal and tyrannical warlord, unites the country's kingdoms into an empire and becomes The Dragon Emperor.
- **S2**：He orders the construction of the Great Wall of China to bury and curse his dead enemies.
- **S3**：The Emperor's mystics teach him supernatural mastery over the Five Elements.
- **S4**：Years later, Emperor Han begins to grow fearful that all he has accomplished will be lost upon his death.
- **S5**：He hears of a sorceress, Zi Yuan, who is said to know the secret of immortality and sends his henchman, General Ming Guo, to bring the sorceress to the palace.
- **S6**：When Ming finds Zi Yuan, they fall in love.
- **S7**：After Zi Yuan seemingly casts a spell on the Emperor in Sanskrit, a language he does not understand, he has Ming executed and impales Zi Yuan with a dagger.
- **S8**：Revealing that she has foreseen these events, Zi Yuan immolates and detains the Emperor and transforms his army into the Terracotta Army, and the sorceress flees.
- **S9**：In 1946, archaeologist Alex O'Connell, the son of Rick and Evelyn O'Connell, locates Emperor Han's tomb with the financial backing of archaeology professor Roger Wilson.
- **S10**：Three assistants are killed and Alex is attacked by Lin, but succeeds in bringing the Emperor's coffin to Shanghai.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, the British government entrusts the O'Connells to take the Eye Of Shangri-La back to China.
- **S12**：Wilson is actually a member of a rogue military faction led by General Yang and his second-in-command, Choi, who see the Emperor as the one who can restore order and greatness to China.
- **S13**：The mysterious woman from the tomb attacks the mummified Emperor in his coffin, which turns out to be a decoy.
- **S14**：By accident, the magical fluid within the Eye lands on the statue of the carriage driver, revealing itself to be the Emperor's actual body.
- **S15**：The Emperor is revived, trapped in his terracotta undead form.
- **S16**：Han accepts the service of Choi and Yang but kills Wilson.
- **S17**：Lin attempts to kill the Emperor with a magical dagger, the only weapon that can destroy him.
- **S18**：Along with Evelyn's brother Jonathan Carnahan, who owns a Shanghai nightclub named Imhotep's, the O'Connells and Lin travel to a stupa in the Himalayas that will reveal the path to Shangri-La when the Eye is placed on top of it.
- **S19**：With the help of the Yetis summoned by Lin, the group hold off Yang's soldiers but Emperor discovers the location of Shangri-La.
- **S20**：He throws a dagger at Alex, but Rick intervenes and is mortally wounded.
- **S21**：Using a thrown grenade attached to some dynamite, Alex triggers an avalanche to reach Shangri-La and meet with Zi Yuan.
- **S22**：After Lin vouches for the O'Connells, the ancient sorceress heals Rick's wound.
- **S23**：The group discovers that Lin is Zi Yuan's 2000-year-old daughter, both rendered immortal due to the power of Shangri-La's waters.
- **S24**：The magical dagger which Lin carries is the same dagger that the Emperor used in his attempt to kill Zi Yuan years earlier.
- **S25**：Zi Yuan also reveals that she would have died if she had not been saved by the Yetis, and warns Alex that if the Emperor is allowed to drink from the Pool of Eternal Life, he will not only be able to raise his army, but be granted the power to transform into ancient and fearsome Chinese animal spirits.
- **S26**：Alex and Lin have grown attached to each other, but Lin pushes the relationship away due to her immortality; after watching Zi Yuan mourn General Ming for centuries, she does not want to fall in love with Alex only to watch him grow old and die.
- **S27**：The Emperor eventually arrives and attacks them in Shangri-La and takes the dagger, breaks free of his terracotta form and bathes in the waters, which restores his human form and youth, replenishes his powers, and gives him the ability to shapeshift.
- **S28**：He transforms into a gigantic three-headed dragon, kidnaps Lin, and flies to his tomb where he raises the Terracotta Army, now aided by General Yang's soldiers.
- **S29**：The Emperor announces his plans to conquer the entire world and that once he leads his army across the Great Wall, they will be invincible.
- **S30**：The O'Connells and Zi Yuan pursue the Emperor to the Great Wall where Zi Yuan sacrifices her and Lin's immortality to revive the workers killed and buried beneath The Great Wall, creating an undead army of her own, led by a vengeful, revived General Ming.
- **S31**：The Army of the Dead, with aid from the group's modern weapons and air support, fights the Terracotta Army while Zi Yuan battles the Emperor; she is mortally wounded but succeeds in securing the dagger.
- **S32**：Alex rescues the captive Lin, who was bound and gagged in a tent under armed guard.
- **S33**：The gang finally catches up, and Lin grieves after they find Zi Yuan dying soon after.
- **S34**：Then, Emperor Han transforms into a horned Ogre and goes beneath the Great Wall in order to use his elemental powers to negate Zi Yuan's spell and draw Ming's army back underneath it.
- **S35**：However, he is stopped by Alex.
- **S36**：Yang and Choi are pushed into moving gears by Evelyn and Lin when they attempt to interfere, crushing and killing them both.
- **S37**：Rick and Alex tackle the Emperor with the dagger, but they are overpowered by the Emperor's transformation into an ogre with Alex being knocked unconscious and Rick onto a lit torch and the dagger is broken.
- **S38**：After Rick questions the Emperor as to where his honor is, the Emperor fights him.
- **S39**：Rick is overpowered by the superiority of the Emperor's taijutsu skills.
- **S40**：Alex grabs the blade of the dagger, and jumps into the nearby water.
- **S41**：Rick sheaths the dagger's hilt and plunges it into the Emperor's chest while Alex stabs him with the tip of the blade from behind, simultaneously piercing the Emperor's heart from both sides, releasing the dagger's curse.
- **S42**：The Emperor is consumed from the inside out by molten lava, resulting in the death of the Emperor and his army.
- **S43**：Ming's army celebrates briefly before finally moving on to a peaceful afterlife.
- **S44**：The O'Connells return to Shanghai, where Alex and Lin fall in love again.
- **S45**：Jonathan decides to move to Peru with the Eye of Shangri-La, which he has stolen himself, as he hopes there are no mummies in Peru.
- **S46**：But shortly after his arrival, mummies were discovered in Peru.

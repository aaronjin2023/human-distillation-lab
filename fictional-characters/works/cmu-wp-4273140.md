### Jake Sully

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-35c70fde0a49d909` · 独立版本：`profile-d834bb9bfe493d7e`

- **性格倾向**：在重获纳威人信任时，他倾向承担危险以争取认可。（行为推断；S8）
- **核心动机**：他希望修复纳威人的信任，并联合各部族抵抗进攻。（行为推断；S8、S11）
- **价值取舍**：在双方交战时，他选择保护纳威人的圣地与抵抗力量。（行为推断；S11、S13、S15）
- **决策方式**：面对信任危机，他倾向借纳威人敬畏的象征争取支持。（行为推断；S8）

### Colonel Miles Quaritch

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-3ac7108d582cab40` · 独立版本：`profile-9dd42d03f69e282f`

- **性格倾向**：在镇压抵抗与追击逃亡者时，他倾向直接诉诸武力。（行为推断；S7、S12、S15）
- **核心动机**：他企图通过摧毁灵魂树打击纳威人的抵抗士气。（摘要直接叙述；S12）
- **价值取舍**：在军事行动中，他把压制抵抗置于原住民圣地安全之上。（行为推断；S12）
- **决策方式**：发现部族即将集结时，他倾向先发制人打击精神支柱。（行为推断；S12）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：By 2154, humans have severely depleted Earth's natural resources.
- **S2**：The Resources Development Administration  mines for a valuable mineral—unobtanium—on Pandora, a densely forested habitable moon orbiting the gas giant Polyphemus in the Alpha Centauri star system.<ref nameJane|lastFamily Filmgoer|dateThe New York Times Company|url 3|workJanuary 9, 2010}} Pandora, whose atmosphere is poisonous to humans, is inhabited by the Na'vi, {{convert}} Archived version {{Nowrap}}, 2010 and another in which Jake admits that the Na'vi will never abandon Hometree, Selfridge orders Hometree destroyed.
- **S3**：Despite Grace's argument that destroying Hometree could damage the biological neural network native to Pandora, Selfridge gives Jake and Grace one final chance to convince the Na'vi to evacuate before commencing the attack.
- **S4**：While trying to warn the Na'vi, Jake confesses to being a spy and the Na'vi take him and Grace captive.
- **S5**：Seeing this, Quaritch's men destroy Hometree, killing Neytiri's father  and many others.
- **S6**：Mo'at frees Jake and Grace, but they are detached from their avatars and imprisoned by Quaritch's forces.
- **S7**：Pilot Trudy Chacón , disgusted by Quaritch's brutality, carries them to Grace's outpost, but during the escape, Quaritch fires at them, hitting Grace.
- **S8**：To regain the Na'vi's trust, Jake connects his mind to that of Toruk, a dragon-like predator feared and honoured by the Na'vi.
- **S9**：Jake finds the refugees at the sacred Tree of Souls and pleads with Mo'at to heal Grace.
- **S10**：The clan attempts to transfer Grace from her human body into her avatar with the aid of the Tree of Souls, but she dies before the process can complete.
- **S11**：Supported by the new chief Tsu'tey , who acts as Jake's translator, Jake speaks to unite the clan and tells them to gather all of the clans to battle against the RDA.
- **S12**：Noticing the impending gathering, Quaritch organizes a pre-emptive strike against the Tree of Souls, believing that its destruction will demoralize the natives.
- **S13**：On the eve of battle, Jake prays to Eywa, via a neural connection to the Tree of Souls, to intercede on behalf of the Na'vi.
- **S14**：During the subsequent battle, the Na'vi suffer heavy casualties, including Tsu'tey and Trudy; but are rescued when Pandoran wildlife unexpectedly join the attack and overwhelm the humans, which Neytiri interprets as Eywa's answer to Jake's prayer.
- **S15**：Jake destroys a makeshift bomber before it can reach the Tree of Souls; Quaritch escapes from the crashing bomber, wearing an AMP suit and breaks open the avatar link unit containing Jake's human body, exposing it to Pandora's poisonous atmosphere.
- **S16**：Quaritch then prepares to slit the throat of Jake's avatar, but Neytiri kills Quaritch and saves Jake from suffocation.
- **S17**：With the exceptions of Jake, Norm, Max and a few other scientists, all humans are expelled from Pandora and sent back to Earth, after which Jake is transferred permanently into his avatar with the aid of the Tree of Souls.

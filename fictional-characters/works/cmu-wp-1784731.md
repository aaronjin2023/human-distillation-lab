### Wallace

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-811d9d2c9efd517a` · 独立版本：`profile-78adee03a0b49d77`

- **性格倾向**：面对经营难题时，他倾向乐观依赖发明，却可能过早下结论（行为推断；S4、S5、S11、S12）
- **核心动机**：他试图解决捕获兔子的安置压力，以维持人道防虫业务（行为推断；S3、S4、S5）
- **决策方式**：起初否认自身异常，但见到Hutch的相似行为后接受证据（行为推断；S21）
- **价值取舍**：在伙伴坠落的紧急时刻，他愿承担自身危险保护Gromit（行为推断；S34、S35）

### Gromit

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-fda31c575473e080` · 独立版本：`profile-1e2039df45a94c2b`

- **性格倾向**：在Wallace误判危险时，他表现出警觉并主动补救的倾向（行为推断；S6、S13、S14）
- **核心动机**：面对追杀与变身危机，他持续以保护并救回Wallace为目标（行为推断；S23、S33、S38、S39）
- **价值取舍**：伙伴安全受到威胁时，他愿放弃自己为竞赛培育的成果（行为推断；S25）
- **决策方式**：应对追逐时，他倾向利用诱饵、伪装和现场物件改变局势（行为推断；S23、S25、S33、S38）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Tottington Hall's annual Giant Vegetable Competition is approaching.
- **S2**：The winner of the competition will win the coveted Golden Carrot Award.
- **S3**：All are eager to protect their vegetables from damage and thievery by rabbits until the contest, and Wallace and Gromit are cashing in by running a vegetable security and humane pest control business, "Anti-Pesto".
- **S4**：However, they are soon faced with two problems: first is Wallace's growing weight, while the second is inadequate space for the captured rabbits.
- **S5**：Wallace comes up with an idea – use his Mind Manipulation-O-Matic machine to brainwash the rabbits.
- **S6**：While performing the operation, a rabbit inexplicably gets fused to Wallace's head, causing the machine to malfunction, and Gromit is forced to destroy the Mind-O-Matic in order to save Wallace.
- **S7**：The resulting failure somehow leaves them with a semi-intelligent rabbit who no longer has an appetite for vegetables, whom Wallace dubs "Hutch".
- **S8**：That night, town is threatened by the "Were-Rabbit", a giant rabbit-like monster which eats vegetables of any size.
- **S9**：During a chaotic town meeting, Anti-Pesto enters into a rivalry with Lord Victor Quartermaine, who seeks to court Lady Tottington and who believes that it is better to be rid of the rabbits via the use of guns.
- **S10**：With Lady Tottington's persuasion, the townspeople agree the Anti-Pesto front the capture attempt.
- **S11**：After a hectic night-time chase, Wallace and Gromit come to the theory that Hutch is the Were-Rabbit.
- **S12**：Wallace is overjoyed, because this technically means he has already captured the beast, and cheerfully goes to inform Lady Tottington.
- **S13**：Securing Hutch to make sure that he does not escape, Gromit instead discovers that the Were-Rabbit is in fact Wallace, suffering from the effects of the accident with the Mind Manipulation-O-Matic having caused him and Hutch to each take on aspects of the other.
- **S14**：Alarmed, Gromit seeks out Wallace, retrieves him before the sun sets, and drives him home.
- **S15**：Victor corners Wallace during the night, jealous of Lady Tottington's growing fondness for him because of his humane practice of pest control.
- **S16**：But when the moon appears, Wallace transforms into the Were-Rabbit before a petrified Victor and his dog Philip.
- **S17**：Gromit immediately departs after Wallace.
- **S18**：Victor, having identified the Were-Rabbit, decides to dispose his rival to win Lady Tottington's heart.
- **S19**：Reverend Clement Hedges allows him access to three "24-carrot" gold bullets – supposedly, the only things capable of killing a Were-Rabbit.
- **S20**：The following morning, the day has finally arrived for the Vegetable Competition, but Lady Tottington reluctantly bows to public pressure to sanction Victor's offer to shoot the Were-Rabbit.
- **S21**：Meanwhile, an oblivious Wallace rebuffs Gromit's accusations of him being the Were-Rabbit, but when Wallace witnesses Hutch expressing his own personality traits, he accepts the truth.
- **S22**：Lady Tottington informs Wallace of Victor's plan, but he begins to transform and is left with no other choice but to send her away.
- **S23**：Victor and Philip arrive on the duo's doorstep moments later, but Gromit is one step ahead, luring the Were-Rabbit away with a female Were-Rabbit marionette.
- **S24**：However, his getaway plan is foiled when Victor mistakenly fires upon what he believes to be the Were-Rabbit, instead discovering it to be Gromit in disguise.
- **S25**：Victor and Philip imprison Gromit, who subsequently escapes with help from Hutch and decides to use the marrow he had been growing for the competition as bait for Wallace who has burst in upon the vegetable contest.
- **S26**：Victor tries to take the Golden Carrot award from a distressed Lady Tottington, as it is the only golden bullet-like object left to him after he exhausted his supply of golden bullets.
- **S27**：Wallace ascends to the rooftops, holding a screaming Lady Tottington in his hand.
- **S28**：Discovering his identity after recognising his palm-shaking gesture, she promises to protect him, only to be interrupted by Victor.
- **S29**：Meanwhile, in a mid-air dogfight in toy biplanes, Philip pursues Gromit, to prevent him from stopping Victor.
- **S30**：Gromit dispatches his foe out of the air, but Philip manages to hold on.
- **S31**：In the end, Gromit releases Philip through the bomb doors, dispatching him into a bouncy castle.
- **S32**：On the roof of Tottington Hall, Gromit's toy biplane circles Wallace, who clings onto the flagpole at the top of the building.
- **S33**：Victor, this time wielding the Golden Carrot trophy inside a blunderbuss he finds at an antiques table at the fair, tries one last time to shoot Wallace, but Wallace is saved by Gromit, who grabs onto a rope from a flagpole and swings his plane into the path of the improvised bullet.
- **S34**：Unfortunately, since it is a toy plane not intended for flying, when Gromit accidentally lets go of the rope, the plane begins to descend rapidly.
- **S35**：Wallace jumps from the flagpole and catches the plane, thereby breaking Gromit's fall into the cheese tent below.
- **S36**：Victor gloats, but is knocked unconscious by Lady Tottington.
- **S37**：He falls into the tent, where Wallace lies unconscious and seemingly dying of his injuries.
- **S38**：Using the marionette to protect Wallace from the angry mob outside, Gromit dresses Victor up as the monster, as the angry mob chases Victor away.
- **S39**：Gromit and Tottington tend to Wallace who seemingly passes away and morphs back into his human form, but Gromit is able to revive Wallace with a slice of Stinking Bishop cheese.
- **S40**：Gromit, for his bravery, is awarded the slightly dented Golden Carrot trophy, and Lady Tottington turns Tottington Hall's front garden into a wildlife sanctuary, where Hutch and the rest of the rabbits can live in peace.

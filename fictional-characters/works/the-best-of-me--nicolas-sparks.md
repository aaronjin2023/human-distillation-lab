- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] In the novel, Dawson Cole returns to his hometown for the first time after twenty years to fulfill the last wishes of his dear friend and surrogate father, Tuck Hostetler.
- **S3**：When he arrives, Dawson is surprised to find that Tuck arranged for Dawson's high school girlfriend, Amanda, to join him in fulfilling these last wishes.
- **S4**：In fact, it soon becomes apparent that Tuck’s intention was to have Dawson and Amanda rekindle their old romance, however Amanda is married.
- **S5**：As for Dawson, his family are a group of notorious criminals, who pose a danger that could not only alter Tuck's plans but Dawson's future irrevocably.
- **S6**：Dawson Cole works on an oil rig off the coast of Louisiana.
- **S7**：One day an explosion on the rig nearly took Dawson's life, but a stranger in the water showed Dawson where to go, saving his life.
- **S8**：Months later, Dawson learns of the death of his good friend, Tuck Hostetler.
- **S9**：Dawson rushes home to fulfil Tuck’s final wishes, even though he has not been home in 20 years.
- **S10**：Dawson was born into a notorious criminal family; who are allowed to get away with many petty crimes, out of the fear the town feels toward this family.
- **S11**：Dawson is not like his family, but no one really believes that a Cole could be honest or law abiding.
- **S12**：For this reason, Dawson lived an isolated life in his hometown.
- **S13**：As a child, Dawson tries to stay within the boundaries of the law and is repeatedly beaten and abused by most of his family, more specifically his father Tommy Cole and two older cousins, Abee and Crazy Ted.
- **S14**：When he was sixteen years old, Dawson left his father's home and began living in the garage of a local mechanic, Tuck Hostetler.
- **S15**：Tuck, who had recently lost his wife, allowed Dawson to stay, forging a relationship that would last the rest of Tuck's life.
- **S16**：Late in his teens, Dawson became lab partners with Amanda Collier, the daughter of a prominent family.
- **S17**：They soon fell in love and began dating for about a year and a half, despite the tension this caused between Amanda and her parents.
- **S18**：When high school graduation came and Amanda had to make a choice between college and Dawson, Dawson forced her to choose school.
- **S19**：Shortly thereafter, Dawson accidentally drove a truck into and killed a local doctor, David Bonner.
- **S20**：Dawson pled guilty to the charges [ citation needed ] , even though it had been an accident.
- **S21**：Dawson served four years in prison and spent his parole living with Tuck.
- **S22**：During his parole, Dawson's father and cousin Ted arrived at Tuck's garage one day to collect money from Dawson, but Dawson beat them both with a crowbar and warned them never to return.
- **S23**：When he was finally free from his parole, Dawson left town and never looked back.
- **S24**：During Dawson's absence from his hometown, dire situations began to occur at the Coles' residence.
- **S25**：Crazy Ted was arrested for assaulting a man at a bar and served nine years behind bars.
- **S26**：Dawson's father Tommy died from an overdose of alcohol poisoning, and the authorities began to keep a closer eye on the Coles.
- **S27**：When Dawson arrives at Tuck's, he is surprised to find Amanda there.
- **S28**：Amanda had struck up a friendship with Tuck in the last few years of his life, and for this reason, was called by Tuck's lawyer upon his death.
- **S29**：Amanda and Dawson talk for a while, even having dinner together in Tuck's house, before parting.
- **S30**：When Amanda goes home to her mother's, her mother lectures her about her relationship with Dawson and how it looks, especially since Amanda is a married woman.
- **S31**：Amanda's mother, however, is unaware that Amanda has become disillusioned in her marriage because of her husband's alcoholism in the wake of the death of their daughter, Bea.

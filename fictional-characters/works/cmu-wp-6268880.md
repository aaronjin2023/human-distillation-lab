## 角色资料

### James Bond

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-360901462b1a4d14` · 独立版本：`profile-c1a0949caf564ded`

- **性格倾向**：在正式命令阻止救援时，他表现出主动另寻资源的冒险倾向（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **核心动机**：他的行动由追查Blofeld逐渐加入营救并迎娶Tracy的目标（摘要直接叙述；S5、S18、S22、S25）
- **价值取舍**：在救援选择上，他倾向把所爱之人的安危置于上级禁令之上（行为推断；S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：追捕目标时，他利用身份伪装与司法管辖限制设计诱捕（摘要直接叙述；S9、S14）
- **情绪反应**：新婚妻子遇害后，他含泪抱住遗体并以无需着急的话回应（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：最初以继续恋爱换取情报，后来真心求婚并违令救援。

### Ernst Stavro Blofeld

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-3faf89e7b77faa10` · 独立版本：`profile-f67ae5c5123b7214`

- **性格倾向**：在筹划勒索时，他表现出把他人当作可远程操控工具的倾向（行为推断；S12、S13、S20）
- **核心动机**：他以全球农业安全为筹码，索取罪行赦免与贵族身份承认（摘要直接叙述；S20）
- **价值取舍**：他为个人免责与身份地位，愿意让全球民众承担灾难风险（行为推断；S13、S20）
- **决策方式**：他拒绝离开瑞士，并在识破Bond身份后命手下将其带走（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **关系模式**：他利用治疗机构与患者的不知情状态建立操纵关系（行为推断；S9、S10、S12、S13）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Portugal, James Bond – agent 007 and sometimes referred to as simply '007' – saves a woman on the beach from committing suicide by drowning, and later meets her again in a casino.
- **S2**：The woman, Contessa Teresa "Tracy" di Vicenzo invites Bond to her hotel room to thank him.
- **S3**：The next morning, Bond is kidnapped by several men while leaving the hotel, who take him to meet Marc-Ange Draco, the head of the European crime syndicate Unione Corse.
- **S4**：Draco reveals that Tracy is his only daughter and tells Bond of her troubled past, offering Bond a personal dowry of one million pounds if he will marry her.
- **S5**：Bond refuses, but agrees to continue romancing Tracy under the agreement that Draco reveals the whereabouts of Ernst Stavro Blofeld, the head of SPECTRE.
- **S6**：After a brief argument with M at the MI6 headquarters, Bond heads for Draco's birthday party in Portugal.
- **S7**：There, Bond and Tracy begin a whirlwind romance, and Draco directs the agent to a law firm in Bern, Switzerland.
- **S8**：At Bern, Bond investigates the office of Swiss lawyer Gumbold, and finds out Blofeld is corresponding with the London College of Arms' genealogist Sir Hilary Bray, attempting to claim the title 'Comte Balthazar de Bleuchamp'.
- **S9**：Posing as Bray, Bond goes to meet Blofeld, who has established a clinical allergy-research institute atop Piz Gloria in the Swiss Alps.
- **S10**：There Bond meets ten young women, the "Angels of Death", who are patients at the institute's clinic, apparently cured of their allergies.
- **S11**：At night Bond goes to the room of one patient, Ruby, for a romantic encounter.
- **S12**：At midnight Bond sees that Ruby, apparently along with each of the other ladies, goes into a sleep-induced trance while Blofeld gives them audio instructions for when they are discharged and return home.
- **S13**：In fact, the women are being brainwashed to distribute bacteriological warfare agents throughout various parts of the world.
- **S14**：Bond tries to trick Blofeld into leaving Switzerland, so the British Secret Service can arrest him without violating Swiss sovereignty; Blofeld refuses, and Bond is eventually caught by the henchwoman Irma Bunt.
- **S15**：Blofeld reveals he identified Bond after his attempt to lure Blofeld out of Switzerland, and tells his henchmen to take the agent away.
- **S16**：Bond eventually makes his escape by skiing down Piz Gloria while Blofeld and many of his men give chase.
- **S17**：Arriving at the village of Lauterbrunnen, Bond finds Tracy and they escape Bunt and her men after a car chase.
- **S18**：A blizzard forces them to a remote barn, where Bond professes his love to Tracy and proposes marriage to her, which she accepts.
- **S19**：The next morning, Blofeld attempts to kill Bond by causing an avalanche and captures Tracy.
- **S20**：Back in London at M's office, Bond is informed that Blofeld intends to hold the world to ransom with the threat of destroying its agriculture using his brainwashed women, demanding amnesty for all past crimes and that he be recognised as the current Count de Bleuchamp.
- **S21**：M tells 007 that the ransom will be paid and forbids him to mount a rescue mission.
- **S22**：Bond then enlists Draco and his forces to attack Blofeld's headquarters, while also rescuing Tracy from Blofeld's captivity.
- **S23**：The facility is destroyed, and Blofeld escapes the destruction alone in a bobsled, with Bond pursuing him.
- **S24**：The chase ends when Blofeld becomes snared in a tree branch and injures his neck.
- **S25**：Bond and Tracy marry in Portugal, then drive away in Bond's Aston Martin.
- **S26**：When Bond pulls over to the roadside to remove flowers from the car, Blofeld  and Bunt commit a drive-by shooting of the couple's car that kills Tracy.
- **S27**：A police officer pulls over to inspect the bullet-riddled car, prompting a tear-filled Bond to mutter that there is no need to hurry to call for help by saying, "We have all the time in the world", as he cradles Tracy's lifeless body.

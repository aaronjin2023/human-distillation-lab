## 角色资料

### Frodo Baggins

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3666e547ad814e29` · 独立版本：`profile-7ad34c5413883ff9`

- **性格倾向**：面对曾袭击自己的咕噜，他仍倾向体谅其承受魔戒的痛苦。（行为推断；S3、S4）
- **核心动机**：他继续前往魔多，目标是摧毁至尊魔戒及黑暗魔君索伦。（摘要直接叙述；S2）
- **价值取舍**：面对咕噜的生命危险，他选择披露其存在以保住其性命。（行为推断；S24）
- **决策方式**：需要前往魔多的向导时，他选择争取被俘咕噜的协助。（行为推断；S4、S5）
- **关系模式**：他对山姆的友情仍有回应，受提醒后从攻击中恢复清醒。（摘要直接叙述；S43）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他试图救咕噜的行动被咕噜感受为背叛。

### Gollum

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0f46d6b73434ed4c` · 独立版本：`profile-059f4027ad977041`

- **性格倾向**：在同行期间，他对弗罗多的忠诚容易与夺戒欲望发生摇摆。（行为推断；S23、S24、S47）
- **核心动机**：他想重新得到失去的魔戒，并在结尾立誓将其夺回。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S47）
- **价值取舍**：在旅程结尾，他将夺回魔戒置于保全两名旅伴性命之上。（行为推断；S47）
- **决策方式**：面对有人把守的黑门，他以被捕风险劝说旅伴改走别路。（行为推断；S13、S14）
- **情绪反应**：弗罗多为救他而暴露其存在后，他却感到自己遭到背叛。（摘要直接叙述；S24）
- **成长变化**：他从忠诚与夺戒欲望之间的挣扎转向策划谋害旅伴。（摘要直接叙述；S23、S24、S47）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他曾为是否忠于弗罗多而挣扎，最终仍计划夺戒并谋害旅伴。

### Théoden

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-5fde7e7ec6dc34fc` · 独立版本：`profile-16b0d9581c5a1d9e`

- **性格倾向**：解除控制后面对战事，他倾向主动承担安置和组织抵抗的责任。（行为推断；S18、S19、S31）
- **核心动机**：他决定将人民迁往圣盔谷，是为了让他们获得安全庇护。（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **决策方式**：面对敌军推进，他先转移民众，再尽可能动员人手迎战。（行为推断；S19、S22、S31）
- **情绪反应**：得知儿子希优德阵亡后，他经历了明确的丧子哀痛。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S19）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Gandalf the Grey gives his life in battle against the Balrog, giving the Fellowship of the Ring time to escape from the Mines of Moria.
- **S2**：Weeks later, Frodo Baggins and Samwise Gamgee continue their journey to Mordor to destroy the One Ring and, with it, the Dark Lord Sauron.
- **S3**：One night, they are attacked by Gollum, who possessed the Ring for centuries before losing it to Frodo's uncle Bilbo, and who now seeks to get it back.
- **S4**：Frodo and Sam capture Gollum, but Frodo takes pity on him, understanding the burden of the Ring.
- **S5**：Requiring a guide, Frodo persuades Gollum to lead them to Mordor.
- **S6**：Sam, however, despises Gollum on sight and warns Frodo that the wily creature will betray them.
- **S7**：In Rohan, Aragorn, Legolas and Gimli pursue the Uruk-hai, who have taken Merry and Pippin prisoner.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, King Théoden of Rohan has been entranced by Gríma Wormtongue, who is secretly in the service of Saruman the White.
- **S9**：Incited by Saruman, Orcs and Wild Men of Dunland lay siege to the lands.
- **S10**：Théoden's nephew Éomer accuses Gríma of being a spy; Gríma has him banished for undermining him. Éomer sets forth to the countryside to gather the remaining loyal men of the Rohirrim. Éomer's army later ambush and kill the Uruk-hai holding Merry and Pippin.
- **S11**：Merry and Pippin flee into Fangorn forest and meet Treebeard, the oldest of the Ents.
- **S12**：Frodo, Sam and Gollum traverse the Dead Marshes, evading a Nazgûl.
- **S13**：Upon reaching the Black Gate, they find it closed and guarded by Orcs.
- **S14**：Gollum convinces the pair that attempting to enter the gate will lead to their capture, offering instead to lead them to an unguarded entrance.
- **S15**：After being informed by Éomer of his ambush against the Uruk-hai, Aragorn, Legolas and Gimli find Merry and Pippin's trail.
- **S16**：The trio are confronted by Gandalf, reborn as Gandalf the White after dying in battle with the Balrog.
- **S17**：Gandalf joins with the trio as they journey to Edoras.
- **S18**：After arriving, they free Théoden from Gríma's spell, and tell the king about the death of his son Théodred.
- **S19**：After mourning his son, Théoden decides to move his people to the stronghold Helm's Deep for safety.
- **S20**：Fearing Helm's Deep will not survive, Gandalf leaves to find Éomer and his forces.
- **S21**：Gríma flees to Orthanc and informs Saruman of a weakness in the outer wall of Helm's Deep.
- **S22**：Saruman dispatches his vast army to the stronghold.
- **S23**：Meanwhile, Gollum struggles with his loyalty to Frodo and his consuming need for the Ring.
- **S24**：When Sam and Frodo are captured by Rangers of Ithilien, Frodo reveals Gollum's presence to spare his life; Gollum nevertheless feels betrayed, and begins plotting against his new "master".
- **S25**：As Théoden's forces travel to Helm's Deep, they are attacked by Saruman's Warg riders and Aragorn is thrown from a cliff into a raging river and believed to be dead.
- **S26**：In Rivendell, Elrond convinces his daughter Arwen to abandon her love for Aragorn and leave Middle-earth with her fellow Elves.
- **S27**：Meanwhile, Éowyn, Theoden's niece, nurtures a growing affection for Aragorn, who remains faithful to Arwen.
- **S28**：Learning of Frodo's Ring, the Rangers' captain, Faramir, orders that it be sent to Gondor.
- **S29**：In Rohan, the barely-alive Aragorn washes up on the river bank.
- **S30**：With the aid of Théodred's old horse Brego, he makes his way to Helm's Deep and warns Théoden that he has seen the Isengard army headed for the fortress.
- **S31**：Theoden gathers every man possible to fight against Saruman's army of Uruk-hai.
- **S32**：When night falls a battalion of Elves arrive to re-enforce the men of Rohan.
- **S33**：In Fangorn Forest, Merry, Pippin, Treebeard and other Ents hold a council to decide on the role of the Ents in the war with Saruman.
- **S34**：The battle of Helm's Deep begins between the Uruk-Hai and Rohirrim with Aragorn and his companions.
- **S35**：Explosives are used against the weakness in the wall, allowing the Orcs to breach the fortress.
- **S36**：In Fangorn, Treebeard and the other Ents initially refuse to get involved in the war until Pippin shows them that Saruman has decimated the forest; enraged, Treebeard commands the Ents to seek vengeance.
- **S37**：Aragorn leads Théoden, Legolas and the remaining Rohirrim to attack the Uruk-hai army, allowing the Rohirrim's women and children to escape into the mountains.
- **S38**：Gandalf appears, accompanied by Éomer and his men.
- **S39**：The combined forces cause the Uruk-hai to flee into Fangorn, where the Ents and their Huorn allies attack them.
- **S40**：At Isengard, the Ents defeat the Uruk-hai and release the river dam, drowning the surviving Orc defenders, flooding Isengard, and stranding Saruman in his tower.
- **S41**：In the East, Faramir has the Hobbits taken to Osgiliath.
- **S42**：Faramir's forces are attacked by Orcs led by a Nazgûl.
- **S43**：Frodo momentarily succumbs to the Ring's influence and attacks Sam, but comes to his senses when Sam tearfully reminds him of their friendship.
- **S44**：The Nazgûl is defeated and flees.
- **S45**：Faramir frees the Hobbits and sends them on their journey, joined by Gollum.
- **S46**：Gandalf remarks that Sauron will seek retribution for Saruman's defeat, stating that hope now rests with Frodo and Sam.
- **S47**：At that same moment, Gollum vows to reclaim the Ring, and plans to have "her" kill Frodo and Sam.

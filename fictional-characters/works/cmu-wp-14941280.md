### Remy

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8fd4f52c4866070f` · 独立版本：`profile-af3317debfc78c86`

- **性格倾向**：面对逃离厨房的机会，他仍显出难以放下烹饪的投入倾向。（行为推断；S2、S6）
- **核心动机**：受古斯托启发，他梦想成为厨师并持续追求烹饪。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S6）
- **决策方式**：看见汤被毁后，他选择留下修复菜品，而非逃离厨房。（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **关系模式**：他与林奎尼建立烹饪合作，也因对方恋情一度感到被排除。（摘要直接叙述；S11、S12、S16）
- **成长变化**：他最终带领鼠群为顾客烹饪，并与伙伴共同开办新餐馆。（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28、S32）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他因家族平安而欣喜，却拒绝留下与族群共同生活。

### Alfredo Linguini

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-8f49f810a18e08ca` · 独立版本：`profile-cb9438d737f297ff`

- **性格倾向**：发现雷米的智慧后，他表现出愿与异类建立合作的倾向。（行为推断；S10、S11）
- **价值取舍**：在无法独自掌厨的困境中，他倾向以坦白承认雷米的贡献。（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **决策方式**：发现雷米的智慧后，他带雷米回家并建立合作方式。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S11）
- **关系模式**：他与科莱特发展恋情，后来与她和雷米共同经营新餐馆。（摘要直接叙述；S16、S32）
- **成长变化**：他从被误认为汤的创作者，走到向员工承认雷米的贡献。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S24）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他此前借隐藏的雷米展示厨艺，后来选择向员工公开真相。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Remy is an anthropomorphic rat gifted with highly developed senses of taste and smell.
- **S2**：Inspired by his idol, the recently-deceased chef Auguste Gusteau, Remy dreams of becoming a cook himself.
- **S3**：When his clan is forced to abandon its home, Remy is separated from them and ends up in the sewers of Paris.
- **S4**：He hallucinates the spirit of Gusteau and takes his advice to look around outside, eventually finding himself at a skylight overlooking the kitchen of Gusteau's restaurant.
- **S5**：As Remy watches, Alfredo Linguini is hired as a garbage boy by Skinner, the restaurant's devious current owner and Gusteau's former sous-chef.
- **S6**：When Linguini spills a pot of soup and attempts to recreate it with disastrous results, Remy falls into the kitchen and cooks the soup to perfection rather than escaping.
- **S7**：Linguini catches Remy and misdirects the chef's attention from him, whilst taking arguments from Skinner.
- **S8**：While they are arguing, the soup is accidentally served and found to be a success.
- **S9**：Colette Tatou, the staff's only female chef, convinces Skinner to retain Linguini, who is misattributed with the soup's creation.
- **S10**：Linguini discovers Remy's comprehension and intelligence and he takes Remy home.
- **S11**：Remy and Linguini find a means to overcome the inability to communicate, as Remy can control Linguini like a marionette by pulling on his hair.
- **S12**：Hidden under a toque blanche, Remy helps Linguini demonstrate his cooking skills to Skinner.
- **S13**：At that, Skinner assigns Colette to train their new cook into the profession and the restaurant's practices.
- **S14**：Suspicious of Linguini's newfound talents, Skinner learns that the boy is Gusteau's son and proper heir to the restaurant.
- **S15**：Remy discovers the evidence and, after eluding Skinner, brings it to Linguini, who removes Skinner as owner.
- **S16**：The restaurant continues to thrive, and Linguini and Colette develop a budding romance, leaving Remy feeling left out.
- **S17**：Meanwhile, Remy reunites with his father, Django, and his brother, Emile, who take him back to their new lair where the entire clan are now living.
- **S18**：Though thrilled to discover that his family is safe, Remy turns down the offer of staying with them, despite some misgivings from Django.
- **S19**：France's top restaurant critic Anton Ego, whose previous review cost Gusteau's one of its star ratings  announces he will be re-reviewing the restaurant the following evening.
- **S20**：After an argument between Remy and Linguini, Remy leads his clan in a raid on the restaurant's pantries.
- **S21**：Linguini catches them and throws them out.
- **S22**：Skinner, now aware of Remy's gourmet skills, captures Remy in an attempt of using him to create a new line of frozen foods for him.
- **S23**：Remy is freed by Django and Emile, and he returns to the restaurant only to find Linguini was unable to cook without him.
- **S24**：Linguini, spotting the rat, apologizes to him, and explains the truth to the rest of the staff.
- **S25**：The staff then walks out, believing Linguini is insane.
- **S26**：Colette later returns after recalling Gusteau's motto, "Anyone can cook," from a bookstore window.
- **S27**：Django arrives with the rest of the pack, offering to help after seeing his son's determination.
- **S28**：Remy directs the rats to cook for the patrons while Linguini runs the front of the house.
- **S29**：For Anton, Remy and Colette create a variation of ratatouille which brings back to an astonished Anton memories of his mother's cooking.
- **S30**：After dining, Anton requests to see the chef; Linguini and Colette wait until the rest of the diners have left to introduce Remy and the rats to Anton.
- **S31**：Anton writes a self-castigating and glowing review for the newspaper the next day, stating that Gusteau's chef is "nothing less than the finest chef in France." Despite the positive review, Gusteau's is closed down due to Skinner's efforts to report a rodent infestation, and Anton loses credibility as a critic.
- **S32**：However, Anton eagerly helps fund a popular new bistro, "La Ratatouille", created and run by Remy, Linguini and Colette.
- **S33**：The rats, meanwhile, settle into their new home in the bistro's roof.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Ten years ago, a man lost all his savings due to a financial crash.
- **S3**：He visited a financial banker named Zara and asked for advice, but she flatly said it was his fault for giving the bank his money.
- **S4**：The man wrote a letter to her before taking his own life by jumping off the bridge.
- **S5**：In the present day, a bank robber tries to rob a bank to get enough money to pay for rent, so his children won't be taken away.
- **S6**：Unfortunately, it is a cashless bank, so the bank robber panics and runs to a nearby apartment building to avoid being caught by the police.
- **S7**：The bank robber enters an apartment viewing, and the people inside see the robber's pistol and assume that it is a robbery.
- **S8**：The bank robber hesitantly accepts that he is taking the people hostage as he tries to maintain order.
- **S9**：The hostages are apartment renovators Anna-Lena and Roger, expecting mothers Julia and Ro, an elderly Estelle, and bank owner Zara.
- **S10**：They find a man named Lennart wearing a bunny suit inside the bathroom.
- **S11**：Anna-Lena admits that she hired Lennart to cause a commotion to lower the bidding price of other prospective buyers, in order to make Roger believe that he negotiated the price himself.
- **S12**：They split up in silence, as Roger had taken pride in his negotiation skills.
- **S13**：Ro follows Roger and admits to him that the reason she nitpicks every apartment her wife Julia brings her to is because she fears she isn't ready to be a parent.
- **S14**：Roger assures her that she'll be a good mother and encourages her to buy the apartment.
- **S15**：Anna-Lena tells Julia that she fears Roger hates her for deceiving him, but Julia promises her that all couples show their love to their spouses in unconventional ways.
- **S16**：A standoffish Zara looks at the bridge from the apartment's balcony and complains about human greed and economic systems to Lennart.
- **S17**：When all the hostages regroup, Roger tells Julia that Ro is excited and nervous to be a mother, and Julia tells Roger that he is enough and that Anna-Lena still loves him.
- **S18**：The bank robber apologizes for keeping them hostage and says he will free them and turn themself in.
- **S19**：However, the hostages argue that they simply made a mistake to protect their children, and that they all agree to lie in their witness statements to protect them.
- **S20**：Estelle reveals that the apartment they're currently in is actually her apartment, and that she posed as one of the buyers to sell it to someone who deserved it.
- **S21**：She gives the bank robber the key to a neighboring apartment to hide in when the police arrive.
- **S22**：The bank robber thanks the hostages for being kind.
- **S23**：Jim is the first police to reach the apartment.
- **S24**：The bank robber turns himself in and tells Jim the whole truth of why he robbed the bank.
- **S25**：Jim feels sympathy for the bank robber and agrees to help the bank robber avoid capture.
- **S26**：The bank robber releases the hostages and hides in a neighboring apartment.
- **S27**：Jim's son, Jack, also a policeman, interviews the hostages and is frustrated when they play dumb.
- **S28**：A series of coincidences lead Jack to believe that the bank robber is still hiding in the apartment.
- **S29**：Jim tells the truth to his son, and they reconcile.
- **S30**：In the end, the bank robber moves in with Estelle in her apartment, and her children visit them every other week.
- **S31**：Julia and Ro move into a neighboring apartment and have a baby boy.
- **S32**：Anna-Lena and Roger take up a new hobby.
- **S33**：Zara starts a relationship with Lennart and works up the courage to read the letter from the man who jumped off the bridge ten years ago.
- **S34**：It reads "It wasn't your fault." and she finally lets go of her guilt.
- **S35**：## Characters
- **S36**：[ edit ] Jack – A young police officer who is very dedicated to his job Jim – Jack's father, a more lax police officer with many years of experience Bank Robber – The perpetrator of the alleged bank robbery and hostage situation Zara – A depressed banker who attends apartment viewings for leisure Roger – A real estate investor who takes the business very seriously Anna-Lena – Roger's wife Julia – A young pregnant woman searching for an apartment to raise her family Ro – Julia's wife Lennart – An actor who is also an apartment viewing disrupter Estelle – An older woman who attends the apartment viewing Real Estate Agent – The agent responsible for hosting the apartment viewing Nadia – The psychologist Zara goes to see and daughter of the man that jumped off the bridge 10 years ago.

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] During the first day of the Yom Kippur War in 1973, the Israeli Defense Force (IDF) prepares to conduct a tactical nuclear strike to stave off defeat.
- **S3**：The necessity for the strike is averted, but an Israeli Mark 12 nuclear bomb is accidentally left on an A-4 Skyhawk attack aircraft, which is subsequently shot down over Syria near Kafr Shams .
- **S4**：The nuclear weapon is lost , buried in the field of a Druze farmer.
- **S5**：Eighteen years later, an Israeli police captain kills a Palestinian demonstrator during a peaceful protest at the Temple Mount .
- **S6**：The United States finds it hard to diplomatically defend Israel, yet knows it cannot withdraw its support without risk of destabilizing the Middle East.
- **S7**：Following the advice of Deputy Director of Central Intelligence (DDCI) Jack Ryan , National Security Advisor Dr.
- **S8**：Charles Alden enacts a plan to accelerate the peace process by converting Jerusalem into a Vatican -like independent city-state to be administered by a tribunal of Jewish, Muslim , Roman Catholic , and Eastern Orthodox religious leaders, and secured by an independent contingent of the Swiss Guards .
- **S9**：Ryan's plan works, and with their religious contentions appeased, the factions in the Middle East find it much easier to negotiate their disputes.
- **S10**：Alden is later involved in a sex scandal that causes his death, and is succeeded by White House Foreign Affairs Advisor Elizabeth Elliot, who holds a grudge against Ryan.
- **S11**：She begins a sexual relationship with U.S. president J.
- **S12**：Robert Fowler and manipulates him into publicly omitting Ryan's role in the peace settlement.
- **S13**：Elliot also engineers a smear campaign accusing Ryan of engaging in an extramarital affair and fathering a child with the young widow of an Air Force pilot who was killed on a classified operation in Colombia . [ a ] CIA operatives John Clark and Domingo "Ding" Chavez, who were involved in the operation and now working for Ryan, convince his wife Cathy that the allegations are false.
- **S14**：Ryan later decides to retire from the CIA, but not before he puts together a covert operation to uncover corrupt dealings between Japanese and Mexican government officials.
- **S15**：Meanwhile, a small group of Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine (PFLP) terrorists, led by Ismael Qati, come across the lost Israeli bomb and use it to construct their own weapon by using the bomb's plutonium pit as fissile material for a new bomb.
- **S16**：Qati and PFLP's explosives expert Ibrahim Ghosn enlist the help of disaffected East German nuclear physicist Manfred Fromm to enhance the bomb into a boosted fission weapon .
- **S17**：They plot to detonate the bomb at the Super Bowl in Denver , while simultaneously staging a false flag attack on U.S. military forces stationed in Berlin by East Germans disguised as Soviet soldiers, in order to cause a nuclear war between the U.S. and the Soviet Union.
- **S18**：Thinking that his work on the bomb is complete, the Palestinians kill Fromm.
- **S19**：However, Fromm had not yet told them that the tritium for the booster still needed to be filtered before use, to eliminate accumulated helium-3 .
- **S20**：The Palestinians assemble the bomb for use, but when detonated, the impure booster material causes only a low yield fizzle to occur.
- **S21**：Even with low yield, the bomb still destroys the Super Bowl venue, killing nearly everyone there including the Secretary of Defense , the Secretary of State , and the commander of NORAD .
- **S22**：With the corresponding attacks in Berlin, the United States briefly assumes DEFCON -1 status as Fowler and Elliott prepare for a nuclear war.
- **S23**：The crisis is averted by Ryan, who learns of the domestic origin of the bomb's plutonium, gains access to the hotline , and convinces the Soviet President to stand down his country's military.
- **S24**：When the terrorists are captured and interrogated by Clark in Mexico City, they implicate the Iranian Ayatollah in the attack.
- **S25**：President Fowler orders the Ayatollah's residence in the holy city of Qom to be destroyed by a nuclear strike.
- **S26**：Ryan averts the attack by enforcing the two-man rule .
- **S27**：The terrorists are delivered to the FBI field office in Buzzard Point, where Ryan questions them.
- **S28**：During questioning, Ryan falsely asserts that Qom was destroyed, tricking Qati into revealing that Iran was not involved, and that their deceit was meant to discredit the United States; thus destroying the peace process and allowing the campaign against Israel to continue.
- **S29**：Elliot is sedated after suffering a nervous breakdown , while Fowler resigns from office and is succeeded by his Vice President, Roger Durling.
- **S30**：The terrorists are executed by beheading in Riyadh by the commander of the Saudi Arabian special forces using an ancient sword owned by the Saudi royal family .
- **S31**：Later, the sword is presented to Ryan as a gift.
- **S32**：In the sequels, the gift (combined with his origins as a Marine) inspires Ryan's Secret Service codename of "Swordsman."

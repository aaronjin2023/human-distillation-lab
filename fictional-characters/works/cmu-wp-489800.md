### Detective James Carter

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-91f91beda703afca` · 独立版本：`profile-25d1cd19e2430aaa`

- **性格倾向**：在争取办案主导权时，表现出好胜且容易高估独力行动能力的倾向（行为推断；S9、S10、S17）
- **核心动机**：最初接受陪同任务时，私下打算由自己独力侦破绑架案（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **决策方式**：在人质受到炸弹威胁时，倾向利用绑匪自保需求反制引爆（行为推断；S26、S27、S28）
- **成长变化**：从刻意阻碍Lee参与调查，转向主动邀其共同救人（摘要直接叙述；S10、S21）
- **关系模式**：在合作后期倾向承担搭档安危，并在Lee坠落时实施营救（行为推断；S21、S32）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：起初想独占破案成果，受挫后却必须主动寻求搭档帮助。

### Lee

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-10f3fcc6f151a46b` · 独立版本：`profile-78bf627a1b8b81df`

- **性格倾向**：在被排除于调查之外时，表现出不轻易放弃救人职责的倾向（行为推断；S10、S11、S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：在受Han召请后，倾向将救回其被绑架女儿作为行动中心（行为推断；S4、S5、S21、S29）
- **决策方式**：在现场倾向留意异常与物证关联，据此判断陷阱及幕后身份（行为推断；S13、S25）
- **情绪反应**：在行动失败并被要求退出调查后，感到羞愧与内疚（摘要直接叙述；S20）
- **关系模式**：即使曾被Carter刻意拖延，仍在其被捕时主动出手相救（摘要直接叙述；S10、S17、S18）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：察觉赎金交付现场异常，却因警告被忽视而未能阻止爆炸。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：On the last day of British rule in Hong Kong, Detective Inspector Lee  of the Hong Kong police leads a raid at a shipping bar wharf, hoping to arrest the mysterious crime lord Juntao.
- **S2**：He finds only Sang , Juntao's right hand man, who manages to escape.
- **S3**：However, Lee successfully recovers numerous Chinese cultural treasures stolen by Juntao, which he presents as a farewell victory to his departing superiors: Chinese Consul Solon Han  and British Commander Thomas Griffin .
- **S4**：Shortly after Han arrives in the United States to take up his new diplomatic post in Los Angeles, his daughter, Soo Yung, is kidnapped by Sang while on her way to her first day of school.
- **S5**：The FBI inform Consul Han about the incident, who calls in Lee to assist in the case.
- **S6**：The FBI, afraid that the injury or death of Lee would result in negative attention, pawn him off on the Los Angeles Police Department.
- **S7**：Captain Diel gives the assignment to the arrogant and annoying Detective James Carter, who he is angry at over a botched undercover operation that injured two other officers and destroyed evidence, by tricking him into thinking the FBI truly wants him.
- **S8**：When Carter discovers the real assignment, Captain Diel threatens to suspend him for two months without pay if he fails.
- **S9**：Carter reluctantly agrees, secretly intending to solve the case himself.
- **S10**：Carter meets Lee at the Los Angeles International Airport and then proceeds to take him on a sightseeing tour of Los Angeles, simultaneously keeping Lee away from the embassy and contacting several of his underworld informants about the kidnapping.
- **S11**：Lee finally escapes and makes his way to the Chinese Consulate, where an anxious Han and a group of FBI agents are awaiting news about his daughter.
- **S12**：While being reprimanded by Agent-in-charge Warren Russ , Carter accidentally involves himself in a phone conversation with Sang, where he poorly arranges a ransom drop of $50 million.
- **S13**：After their arrival at the agreed drop point, Lee tries to warn the FBI that something is amiss, but is ignored until a bomb inside the building is detonated, killing numerous agents.
- **S14**：Spotting Sang nearby, Lee and Carter give chase, but Sang escapes, dropping the detonator in the process.
- **S15**：After showing it to Carter's colleague, LAPD bomb expert Tania Johnson , they learn that the detonator could blow up C4, which leads to Clive , the man who Carter arrested in his botched sting.
- **S16**：Clive is guilt-tripped by Lee into revealing his business relationship with Juntao and that they can find him at a restaurant in Chinatown.
- **S17**：At the restaurant, Carter is captured after going in alone, though he sees a surveillance video of Juntao carrying Soo-Yung into a van.
- **S18**：Lee arrives and rescues Carter, and they are met outside by the FBI, led by Russ, who blames them for ruining the ransom exchange.
- **S19**：Sang phones the consul, angrily telling him that the ransom has been increased from $50 million to $70 million, and threatens to kill Soo Yung if anything else goes wrong.
- **S20**：Disgraced and guilt-ridden, Lee and Carter are ordered off the investigation, and Lee is informed that he will be sent back to Hong Kong.
- **S21**：Carter refuses to drop the case and confronts Lee on his plane to enlist his help, and the two men decide to save Soo-Yung together.
- **S22**：The final confrontation comes at the opening of a Chinese art exhibition at the Los Angeles Convention Center, which Han and Griffin are overseeing, while the ransom is being delivered.
- **S23**：Carter, Lee, and Johnson enter disguised as guests.
- **S24**：After Carter recognizes Griffin from Chinatown, he creates a scene by alerting the spectators about a bomb threat in the building and tells them to evacuate.
- **S25**：In the confusion, Lee sees Sang handing Griffin a detonator identical to the one he and Carter had previously recovered, deducing that Griffin is really Juntao.
- **S26**：With this knowledge, Lee points to Griffin as the real Juntao, and Griffin, seeing that his cover is blown, threatens to detonate a bomb vest attached to Soo Yung if the delivery is interrupted.
- **S27**：During the stand-off, however, Carter manages to sneak out and locate Soo Yung.
- **S28**：He then drives the van into the building and brings the bomb vest within range so that Juntao cannot set it off, knowing it would kill himself too.
- **S29**：After a gunfight breaks out, Carter gives Soo Yung to Lee, and Johnson manages to get the vest off Soo Yung.
- **S30**：Lee then takes the vest and pursues Griffin  while Carter shoots Sang dead in a gunfight as Sang attempts to collect more of the ransom and kill one of the FBI agents.
- **S31**：During the pursuit, both Lee and Griffin men fall over the rail with Lee holding onto a rafter and Griffin holding onto the bomb vest.
- **S32**：The vest then rips apart, sending Griffin falling to his death into a fountain below, Lee then loses his grip and falls, but Carter is able to rescue him by placing a large flag under him to catch him.
- **S33**：Han and Soo Yung are reunited, and Han sends Carter and Lee on vacation together to Hong Kong as a reward for their actions.
- **S34**：Before Carter leaves, Agents Russ and Whitney offer him a position in the FBI, which he rudely refuses.
- **S35**：The film ends with Lee and Carter enjoying their flight to Hong Kong.

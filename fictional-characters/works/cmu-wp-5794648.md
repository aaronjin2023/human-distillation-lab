### Robert Langdon

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-710cf6abca59cabf` · 独立版本：`profile-6d01bfc536c8c9da`

- **性格倾向**：面对连续死亡与自身遇袭，他表现出坚持追查且愿意涉险的倾向（行为推断；S11、S13、S14、S15）

### Camerlengo Patrick McKenna

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-d7739dd77428d197` · 独立版本：`profile-86d497a209816ac5`

- **性格倾向**：在争取宗教权威时，他表现出善于操纵恐惧与公众形象的倾向（行为推断；S24、S25、S26、S27）
- **核心动机**：他策划危机，意图借恐惧获选教皇并凝聚信徒支持（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **价值取舍**：在谋求教会领导权时，他倾向将无辜生命视为可牺牲的工具（行为推断；S5、S9、S10、S11、S27）
- **决策方式**：他倾向制造威胁再扮演救援者，以操纵他人对领导资格的判断（行为推断；S24、S25、S26、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：公开的救世英雄形象，与幕后制造危机的身份相矛盾。

- **S1**：Under the watchful eyes of Father Silvano Bentivoglio and Dr.
- **S2**：Vittoria Vetra, the European Organization for Nuclear Research  initiates the Large Hadron Collider and creates three vials of antimatter particles larger than any that have ever been produced before.
- **S3**：Immediately afterward, Father Silvano is killed and one of the vials of antimatter goes missing.
- **S4**：Meanwhile, the Roman Catholic Church mourns the sudden death of the Pope in Rome and prepares for the papal conclave to elect the next Pope while Camerlengo Patrick McKenna assumes temporary control of the Vatican.
- **S5**：The Illuminati kidnap the 'preferiti'  before the conclave enters seclusion and threaten to kill one candidate every hour and destroy the Vatican at midnight, using the missing vial of antimatter as a bomb.
- **S6**：The Vatican summons symbologist Robert Langdon from Harvard University and Vittoria from CERN to help them save the four preferiti and locate the bomb.
- **S7**：Langdon listens to the Illuminati message and deduces that the four cardinals will die at the four altars of the "Path of Illumination," marked by statues of angels in locations relevant to the four classical elements.
- **S8**：Over Richter's objections, but with McKenna's consent, Langdon is granted access to the Vatican Secret Archives to examine Galileo Galilei's banned book, and following clues with Vittoria, Inspector General Ernesto Olivetti, and Lieutenant Valenti of the Vatican Gendarmerie Corps, arrive at Chigi Chapel in the Church of Santa Maria del Popolo.
- **S9**：There they find Cardinal Ebner dead, suffocated with soil and branded with an ambigrammatic word "Earth".
- **S10**：They verify the second altar's location is Saint Peter's Square, but are unable to save Cardinal Lamassé, his lungs punctured and his body branded with an ambigrammatic word, "Air".
- **S11**：While Vetra studies Silvano's diaries, Langdon and the Vatican officers locate the third church, Santa Maria della Vittoria, but are unable to save Cardinal Guidera from being burned to death.
- **S12**：His body is branded with an ambigrammatic word, "Fire".
- **S13**：The assassin appears and brutally kills everyone except Langdon, who escapes.
- **S14**：Langdon and two Carabinieri officers race to the Water altar, the Fountain of the Four Rivers, where the assassin murders the officers and drops a bound and weighted Cardinal Baggia into the fountain.
- **S15**：Langdon, assisted by bystanders, saves the cardinal, who tells him the Illuminati's lair is Castel Sant'Angelo.
- **S16**：There Langdon and Vetra discover a hidden passageway leading to the Vatican, being used as a hideout for the assassin.
- **S17**：Discovering a case with marks for five branding irons, they realize the fifth brand is for the camerlengo, but are confronted by the assassin before they can alert McKenna.
- **S18**：The assassin spares their lives, then cryptically warns them that his contractors were "men of God".
- **S19**：He escapes, but is killed when his car explodes.
- **S20**：Inside the Vatican, Langdon and Vetra find Commander Richter hovering over McKenna with a gun, the Vatican symbol branded into McKenna's chest.
- **S21**：Richter is shot by the Guards, and Langdon takes a key that slips from Richter's hand.
- **S22**：The stolen antimatter vial is found in St.
- **S23**：Peter's Tomb below the church, but the battery life is too low to risk re-connecting it to a battery.
- **S24**：McKenna, a former military pilot, seizes the vial and uses an awaiting helicopter to fly above the Vatican.
- **S25**：At a high altitude, he parachutes out as the antimatter bomb explodes overhead.
- **S26**：McKenna is hailed a hero and savior, and the cardinals move to elect him pope.
- **S27**：Langdon and Vetra use Richter's key to watch a security video showing McKenna speaking to Richter before the attack, the video revealing McKenna, not the Illuminati, is the mastermind behind the scheme, intending to use the fear of the incident to be named pope and rally the church's followers to his side.
- **S28**：This is shown to the Papal conclave, and when McKenna discovers he has been exposed he fatally sets himself on fire.
- **S29**：The Vatican officially announces that McKenna died due to internal injuries suffered during his parachute landing, and Cardinal Baggia is named Pope Luke I, with Cardinal Strauss as the new camerlengo.
- **S30**：Strauss thanks Langdon for his assistance and gives Langdon Galileo's "Diagramma Veritas" for his research, requesting only that Langdon's will contain a bequest that it be returned to the Vatican, and that any future references he makes to the Catholic Church in his future publications are done "gently".
- **S31**：The movie closes with the newly enthroned pope stepping out on his balcony for the first time, to the cheers of the crowd below.

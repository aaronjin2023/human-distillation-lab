### Harold Abrahams

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-738d7daade2bc466` · 独立版本：`profile-49d6dc45017f2c3c`

- **性格倾向**：在竞技受挫时，他倾向强烈在意成败并寻求改进自身表现（行为推断；S8、S9、S23、S24）
- **核心动机**：在二百米失利后，他将百米视为最后夺牌机会全力争取（摘要直接叙述；S23、S24、S25）
- **价值取舍**：面对学院的阶层偏见，他倾向重视专业训练而非绅士规范（行为推断；S9、S10、S11、S12）
- **关系模式**：他因专注跑步而冷落西比尔，夺冠后才重新投入两人关系（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **情绪反应**：首次输给利德尔时，他对失败表现出极为强烈的负面反应（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9）

暂无支持：决策方式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Eric Liddell

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-9b85ac8d6fb5a22b` · 独立版本：`profile-8ee842777e917c7b`

- **性格倾向**：在国家荣誉与宗教要求冲突时，他倾向坚定承受外界压力（行为推断；S17、S18、S21）
- **核心动机**：他把跑步视为荣耀上帝的方式，并计划之后赴中国传教（摘要直接叙述；S7、S14）
- **价值取舍**：即使可能失去奥运机会，他仍把安息日信念置于比赛之前（摘要直接叙述；S18）
- **沟通方式**：回应妹妹的责备时，他用天赋与神圣目的解释自己坚持跑步（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14、S15）
- **决策方式**：守住周日不比赛的底线后，他愿接受改跑其他项目的方案（摘要直接叙述；S18、S20）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：跑步被他视为荣耀上帝，却被妹妹视为忽略信仰

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1919, Harold Abrahams  enters the University of Cambridge, where he experiences anti-Semitism from the staff, but enjoys participating in the Gilbert and Sullivan club.
- **S2**：He becomes the first person to ever complete the Trinity Great Court Run – running around the college courtyard in the time it takes for the clock to strike 12.
- **S3**：Abrahams achieves an undefeated string of victories in various national running competitions.
- **S4**：Although focused on his running, he falls in love with a leading Gilbert and Sullivan soprano, Sybil .
- **S5**：Eric Liddell , born in China of Scottish missionary parents, is in Scotland.
- **S6**：His devout sister Jennie  disapproves of Liddell's plans to pursue competitive running.
- **S7**：But Liddell sees running as a way of glorifying God before returning to China to work as a missionary.
- **S8**：When they first race against each other, Liddell beats Abrahams.
- **S9**：Abrahams takes it extremely badly, but Sam Mussabini , a professional trainer whom he had approached earlier, offers to take him on to improve his technique.
- **S10**：This attracts criticism from the Cambridge college masters .
- **S11**：They allege it is ungentlemanly for an amateur to "play the tradesman" by employing a professional coach.
- **S12**：Abrahams realizes this is a cover for their anti-Semitism and class-based sense of superiority, and dismisses their concern.
- **S13**：When Eric Liddell accidentally misses a church prayer meeting because of his running, his sister Jennie upbraids him and accuses him of no longer caring about God.
- **S14**：Eric tells her that though he intends to eventually return to the China mission, he feels divinely inspired when running, and that not to run would be to dishonour God: "I believe that God made me for a purpose.
- **S15**：But He also made me fast, and when I run, I feel His pleasure." The two athletes, after years of training and racing, are accepted to represent Great Britain in the 1924 Olympics in Paris.
- **S16**：Also accepted are Abrahams' Cambridge friends, Lord Andrew Lindsay , Aubrey Montague , and Henry Stallard .
- **S17**：While boarding the boat to Paris for the Olympics, Liddell learns the news that the heat for his 100 metre race will be on a Sunday.
- **S18**：He refuses to run the race – despite strong pressure from the Prince of Wales and the British Olympic committee – because his Christian convictions prevent him from running on the Sabbath.
- **S19**：Hope appears in the form of Liddell's teammate Lord Andrew Lindsay.
- **S20**：Having already won a silver medal in the 400 metres hurdles, Lindsay proposes to yield his place in the 400 metre race on the following Thursday to Liddell, who gratefully agrees.
- **S21**：His religious convictions in the face of national athletic pride make headlines around the world.
- **S22**：Liddell delivers a sermon at the Paris Church of Scotland that Sunday, and quotes from Isaiah 40, ending with: :But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.
- **S23**：Abrahams is badly beaten by the heavily favoured United States runners in the 200 metre race.
- **S24**：He knows his last chance for a medal will be the 100 metres.
- **S25**：He competes in the race, and wins.
- **S26**：His coach Sam Mussabini is overcome that the years of dedication and training have paid off with an Olympic gold medal.
- **S27**：Now Abrahams can get on with his life and reunite with his girlfriend Sybil, whom he had neglected for the sake of running.
- **S28**：Before Liddell's race, the American coach remarks dismissively to his runners that Liddell has little chance of doing well in his now far longer 400 metre race.
- **S29**：But one of the American runners, Jackson Scholz, hands Liddell a note of support for his convictions.
- **S30**：Liddell defeats the American favourites and wins the gold medal.
- **S31**：The British team returns home triumphant.
- **S32**：As the film ends, onscreen text explains that Abrahams married Sybil, and became the elder statesman of British athletics.
- **S33**：Eric Liddell went on to missionary work in China.
- **S34**：All of Scotland mourned his death in 1945 in Japanese-occupied China.

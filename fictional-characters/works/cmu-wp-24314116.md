### Blackbeard

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-0c2a31733102892e` · 独立版本：`profile-232bad768b125a29`

- **性格倾向**：在求取泉水的过程中显得冷酷，愿让他人为自己的目标受害。（行为推断；S17、S28、S30）
- **核心动机**：寻找青春之泉的力量，以逃脱遭独腿男子杀死的预定命运。（摘要直接叙述；S10）
- **价值取舍**：面对自身与女儿的生死取舍，倾向把自己的存活放在首位。（行为推断；S28、S30）
- **关系模式**：安洁莉卡自称他的女儿，他却在两人受伤时要求她牺牲。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S28）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以父女相称，却要求女儿为自己的存活牺牲。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After a failed attempt to rescue his first mate, Joshamee Gibbs  in London, Captain Jack Sparrow  is brought before King George II , who wants Jack to guide an expedition to the Fountain of Youth before the Spanish locate it.
- **S2**：Heading the expedition is Jack's old nemesis, Captain Hector Barbossa , now a privateer in service to the Royal Navy after losing his leg and ship, the Black Pearl.
- **S3**：Jack escapes, but his father, Captain Teague , finds him and warns Jack about the Fountain's tests.
- **S4**：He also reveals that someone is impersonating Jack.
- **S5**：The impostor is Angelica , Jack's former lover and self-proclaimed daughter of the ruthless pirate Blackbeard , who practices voodoo magic and wields a magical sword that controls his ship.
- **S6**：Jack is taken aboard Blackbeard's ship, the Queen Anne's Revenge, and forced to lead the way to the Fountain and find two silver chalices that once belonged to Juan Ponce de León, both believed to be aboard his lost ship.
- **S7**：The Fountain's water must be drunk simultaneously from the two chalices.
- **S8**：The person drinking from the chalice containing a mermaid's tear has their life extended, while the other person dies, their life drained from their body and their remaining years 'donated' to the other.
- **S9**：Meanwhile, Gibbs, having memorized and destroyed Jack's map, barters with Barbossa to guide him to the Fountain.
- **S10**：Blackbeard seeks the Fountain's power to circumvent his predestined fatal encounter with "a one-legged man," and sets a course for Whitecap Bay.
- **S11**：There they are attacked by a vicious horde of mermaids, but Blackbeard captures one .
- **S12**：Philip Swift , a captive missionary, falls in love with the mermaid and names her Syrena.
- **S13**：Blackbeard then sends Jack to retrieve the chalices from de León's ship.
- **S14**：When Jack finds the grounded, decaying vessel, Barbossa is waiting inside and the Spanish are in possession of the chalices.
- **S15**：However, Barbossa only seeks revenge against Blackbeard for capturing the Black Pearl, which forced Barbossa to amputate his own leg to escape.
- **S16**：He and Jack join forces to defeat Blackbeard, then head to the nearby Spanish camp to steal the chalices.
- **S17**：Meanwhile, Syrena, reciprocating Philip's love, is tricked into shedding a tear which Blackbeard collects, leaving her to die.
- **S18**：Philip is forced to go with him.
- **S19**：Jack returns with the chalices and Gibbs, with whom he had reunited while assisting Barbossa.
- **S20**：Jack and Blackbeard bargain for Jack's confiscated magical compass and Gibbs' release.
- **S21**：In return, Jack vows to give Blackbeard the chalices and lead him to the Fountain; Blackbeard agrees and Gibbs departs with the compass.
- **S22**：At the Fountain, Blackbeard and his crew are attacked by Barbossa, and then by the Spanish, there to destroy the Fountain, their king believing its power is an abomination against God.
- **S23**：A battle ensues and Barbossa stabs Blackbeard with a poison-laced sword.
- **S24**：Angelica accidentally cuts herself while removing it from Blackbeard.
- **S25**：Barbossa claims Blackbeard's magical sword and assumes command, leaving with Blackbeard's crew.
- **S26**：Meanwhile, Philip, though mortally wounded, escapes and returns to free Syrena.
- **S27**：After finding the chalices that the Spaniard had tossed into deep water, Syrena gives them to Jack, then retrieves the dying Philip as she kisses and takes him underwater.
- **S28**：With Blackbeard and Angelica wounded, Jack brings the chalices to them and tries to convince Angelica to drink from the one with the tear, but Blackbeard asks his daughter to sacrifice herself.
- **S29**：Angelica agrees and drinks.
- **S30**：Knowing that the self-serving Blackbeard would sacrifice his surrogate daughter, Jack lied about which chalice contained the tear.
- **S31**：Blackbeard's body is drained by the waters of the Fountain and destroyed.
- **S32**：Although Angelica admits her love for Jack, he strands her on an island, knowing that she may want to avenge Blackbeard.
- **S33**：Barbossa, who now captains the Queen Anne's Revenge, quits being a privateer and returns to piracy.
- **S34**：Jack finds Gibbs, who has used the compass to locate the shrunken Black Pearl and is shown to be in possession of all ships Blackbeard had shrunken in bottles.
- **S35**：Hoping to bring the Black Pearl to its original size, the two head off into the sunset, determined to continue living a pirate's life.
- **S36**：In a post-credits scene, Blackbeard's voodoo doll of Jack has washed ashore on the island and is found by Angelica, to which she reacts with a grin.

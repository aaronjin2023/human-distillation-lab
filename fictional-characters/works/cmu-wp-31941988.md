## 角色资料

### Harry Potter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6731c01eb468c5e3` · 独立版本：`profile-9a062237fde2d904`

- **性格倾向**：得知自身承载敌人灵魂后，他表现出承担致命代价的勇气。（行为推断；S28、S29）
- **核心动机**：在获得返回身体的选择时，他明确要彻底击败伏地魔。（摘要直接叙述；S31）
- **价值取舍**：在战胜伏地魔之后，他折断长老魔杖并拒绝其力量。（摘要直接叙述；S38）
- **决策方式**：追查魂器时，他倾向借助他人知识核实线索并调整行动。（行为推断；S2、S4、S16）

### Lord Voldemort

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-d1c8c8e1cba1f016` · 独立版本：`profile-ab2ed1b41251d18c`

- **性格倾向**：面对公开反抗时，他表现出以死亡威胁迫使众人服从的倾向。（行为推断；S32）
- **核心动机**：在争夺长老魔杖效忠时，他意图通过杀死斯内普取得控制。（行为推断；S22）
- **价值取舍**：为获得魔杖的效忠，他把力量置于斯内普的生命之上。（行为推断；S22）
- **决策方式**：他依据斯内普必须死的认主判断，直接采取致命行动。（行为推断；S22）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{further2}} {{See also}}  As Lord Voldemort retrieves the Elder Wand from Albus Dumbledore's grave, Severus Snape has become Hogwarts headmaster.
- **S2**：After burying Dobby, Harry Potter asks the help of goblin Griphook, Ron, and Hermione to break into Bellatrix Lestrange's vault at Gringotts bank, suspecting a Horcrux may be there.
- **S3**：Griphook agrees, in exchange for the Sword of Gryffindor.
- **S4**：Harry asks wandmaker Ollivander to identify two wands taken from Malfoy Manor.
- **S5**：Ollivander says they belonged to Bellatrix and to Draco Malfoy, but Malfoy's has changed its allegiance to Harry.
- **S6**：In Bellatrix's vault, Harry discovers the Horcrux is Helga Hufflepuff's cup.
- **S7**：He retrieves it, but Griphook snatches the sword and abandons the trio, leaving them cornered by the alerted security.
- **S8**：The three release the dragon guardian and flee on its back.
- **S9**：Harry sees a vision of Voldemort killing goblins, including Griphook, and learns Voldemort is aware of the theft.
- **S10**：Harry also realises that there is a Horcrux at Hogwarts somehow connected to Rowena Ravenclaw.
- **S11**：The trio apparate into Hogsmeade, and set off Caterwauling alarms.
- **S12**：They are rescued by Aberforth Dumbledore, who instructs the portrait of his younger sister, Ariana, to fetch Neville Longbottom, who leads the trio through a secret passageway into Hogwarts.
- **S13**：Snape hears of Harry's return and warns staff and students of the severe punishment for aiding Harry.
- **S14**：Harry confronts Snape, who flees after Minerva McGonagall challenges him to a duel.
- **S15**：McGonagall gathers the community of Hogwarts to prepare for battle.
- **S16**：At Luna Lovegood's insistence, Harry speaks to Helena Ravenclaw's ghost, who reveals that Voldemort performed "dark magic" on her mother's diadem, which is in the Room of Requirement.
- **S17**：Ron and Hermione go to the Chamber of Secrets, where Hermione destroys the Horcrux cup with a Basilisk fang.
- **S18**：In the Room of Requirement, Draco, Gregory Goyle and Blaise Zabini attack Harry, but Ron and Hermione intervene.
- **S19**：Goyle casts a Fiendfyre curse and is unable to control it, and is burned to death while Harry and his friends save Malfoy and Zabini.
- **S20**：Harry stabs the diadem with the Basilisk fang and Ron kicks it into the Room of Requirement, where it is destroyed.
- **S21**：As Voldemort's forces attack the school, Harry, seeing into Voldemort's mind, realises that Voldemort's snake Nagini is the final Horcrux.
- **S22**：After entering the boathouse, the trio witness Voldemort telling Snape that the Elder Wand cannot serve him until Snape dies; he then orders Nagini to kill Snape.
- **S23**：Before dying, Snape tells Harry to take his memories to the Pensieve.
- **S24**：In the chaos at Hogwarts, Fred, Lupin, and Tonks are killed.
- **S25**：Harry learns from Snape's memories that Snape loved Harry's mother, Lily, but despised his father, James, who had bullied him.
- **S26**：Following her death, Snape worked secretly with Dumbledore to protect Harry from Voldemort because of his love for Lily.
- **S27**：Harry also learns that Dumbledore's death at Snape's hands was planned between them.
- **S28**：Harry discovers that he became a Horcrux when Voldemort originally failed to kill him and that Harry must die to destroy the piece of Voldemort's soul within him.
- **S29**：Harry goes to die at the hands of Voldemort in the Forbidden Forest.
- **S30**：Voldemort casts the Killing Curse upon Harry, who finds himself in limbo, where Dumbledore's spirit meets him and explains that the part of Voldemort within Harry was killed by Voldemort's own curse.
- **S31**：Harry decides to return to his body to defeat Voldemort once for all.
- **S32**：Voldemort announces Harry's apparent death to everyone at Hogwarts, and says that anyone who defies him will be killed.
- **S33**：As Neville gives a defiant response, Harry reveals he is alive.
- **S34**：Neville draws the Sword of Gryffindor from the Sorting Hat, and as Harry confronts Voldemort in a duel throughout the castle, Neville decapitates Nagini, leaving Voldemort mortal.
- **S35**：Molly Weasley kills Bellatrix in the Great Hall.
- **S36**：The final stand of Harry and Voldemort's fight is Voldemort's own Killing Curse rebounding and obliterating him.
- **S37**：After the battle, Harry explains to Ron and Hermione that the Elder Wand had recognised him as its master because he had disarmed Draco who earlier had disarmed its previous owner, Dumbledore.
- **S38**：Harry snaps the Elder Wand, rejecting its power.
- **S39**：Nineteen years later, Harry and Ginny Potter, with Ron and Hermione Weasley, watch proudly their children leaving for Hogwarts from King's Cross station.

## 角色资料

### Mikey

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e7fe45d749249731` · 独立版本：`profile-6f8356ca56f86049`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴低落与犹疑时，倾向主动倡议并坚持探索（行为推断；S2、S3、S9）
- **核心动机**：受到寻宝冒险的吸引，希望说服伙伴共同追寻线索（摘要直接叙述；S3、S9）
- **价值取舍**：面对可取走的财富，仍愿为认同的先行者保留一份（行为推断；S11）
- **决策方式**：倾向以地图与现场相符的线索，推动团队继续前进（行为推断；S9）
- **关系模式**：把Willie视作群体先行者，表现出跨越生死的认同（行为推断；S11）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：寻宝的现实收益与对Willie的敬意并存，他主动留下部分财富。

### Chunk

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-9920704dd4264143` · 独立版本：`profile-dbbd06a729ce3e0b`

- **性格倾向**：在遭遇被遗弃者的情境中，表现出接纳与亲近的倾向（行为推断；S8、S13、S18）
- **核心动机**：脱离囚禁后以帮助受困伙伴摆脱危险为行动目标（行为推断；S13）
- **关系模式**：与Sloth从共同被关押的处境发展为亲密朋友（摘要直接叙述；S8、S13）
- **价值取舍**：危机结束后仍愿提供家庭接纳，使友谊超出临时互助（行为推断；S18）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The Goonies, a group of friends living in the "Goon Docks" neighborhood of Astoria, Oregon, face foreclosure on their families' homes from the expanding Astoria Country Club.
- **S2**：On one of their last days their morale sinks particularly low due to Mikey's older brother having failed his driver's license exam, thwarting their plans to "cruise the coast in style" during their "last Goonies weekend." While rummaging through the Walshes' attic, they find an old newspaper clipping, a Spanish map, and an artifact relating to a rumor of a lost but not forgotten pirate treasure somewhere in the area.
- **S3**：Hearing the call of adventure, Mikey tries to persuade his friends to join him in search for the treasure hoarded by a pirate named One-Eyed Willie.
- **S4**：Initially reluctant, the group eventually decides to evade the authority of Mikey's older brother, Brandon, and escape for one last "Goonie adventure." They then head down the coast and stumble upon a derelict seaside restaurant that seems to match coordinates set by the map and an old doubloon, unaware that the Fratellis, a family of fugitives, are using the restaurant as their hideout.
- **S5**：After first encountering the family, the Goonies come back after the Fratellis have left and discover their criminal intents, and send Chunk, the group's clumsy, heavy-set dreamer and teller of tall tales, to get help.
- **S6**：However, the Fratellis return before they can leave, forcing the Goonies to use a cavern underneath the restaurant to escape.
- **S7**：The Fratellis capture Chunk and interrogate him, learning nothing until a pack of bats burst out of the cavern beneath the restaurant.
- **S8**：Chunk is placed in the same room with the forsaken and deformed Fratelli brother, Sloth, while the rest of the Fratellis enter the cavern and follow the Goonies' trail.
- **S9**：As the Goonies traverse the cavern, Mikey discovers that it matches up with the map and convinces the rest of the group to continue to follow it to find the treasure.
- **S10**：They overcome many deadly traps set by One-Eyed Willie, and eventually arrive at a large underground cavern and a lagoon where Willie's ship, the Inferno, lies waiting.
- **S11**：While others begin to fill their pockets with the riches on the ship, Mikey finds Willie's skeleton, and reverently acknowledges him as the "first Goonie", leaving part of the treasure with him as tribute.
- **S12**：However, as the Goonies exit the hold, they are caught by the Fratellis, who take their treasure and force the kids to walk the plank.
- **S13**：The Goonies are saved by the timely arrival of Chunk and Sloth, now close friends, who help the rest of the group to shore.
- **S14**：With the children no longer a threat, the Fratellis ransack the rest of the ship, including the share of the treasure Mikey left for Willie.
- **S15**：This sets off a final booby trap, causing the cave to begin collapsing.
- **S16**：The Goonies flee through a hole in the cave, finding themselves on a beach.
- **S17**：Two passing policemen spot them and call in for help.
- **S18**：The Goonies are soon reunited with their parents while the Fratellis are arrested and Chunk offers to take Sloth into his home.
- **S19**：As the Astoria Country Club calls upon Mikey's father to sign over the deed to his property, Rosalita, the Walshes' maid, discovers that Mikey's marble bag contains a large handful of jewels taken from the ship.
- **S20**：Mikey's father quickly estimates that its value is more than enough to save everyone's homes.
- **S21**：As the families celebrate, they spot the unmanned Inferno, now free of the cave, sailing away.

- **S54**：## Characters
- **S55**：[ edit ] Percy Jackson : Percy, a 14-year-old demigod and son of Poseidon , is the protagonist as well as the series' narrator.
- **S56**：He embarks on a journey to save Annabeth and the Greek goddess Artemis, who have both been kidnapped.
- **S57**：Thalia Grace : Thalia is a 15-year-old demigod daughter of Zeus .
- **S58**：Though she appears in Percy's dream in the first book, she makes a full appearance at the end of The Sea of Monsters and is given a greater role in the third book.
- **S59**：Thalia is described as looking very punk , with electric blue eyes, black clothes, and spiky hair.
- **S60**：Her personality is often described as "independent and many times sarcastic." While Thalia is a lot like Percy (due to both being children of the Big Three), and they become good friends before the events of the book, they often argue.
- **S61**：She is heartbroken by Luke's betrayal of the camp and gods, as it is implied that she had feelings for him.
- **S62**：She is also afraid of heights, which she reluctantly admits to Percy, despite the fact that she is the daughter of Zeus, God of the Sky.
- **S63**：She joins the Huntress of Artemis as the new lieutenant to prevent her from being the child of the Great Prophecy with the permission of her father.
- **S64**：Annabeth Chase : Annabeth is a 14-year-old demigod and the daughter of Athena.
- **S65**：She is friends with Percy, Thalia, and Grover.
- **S66**：She is kidnapped, along with Artemis, by the Titans.
- **S67**：She has a great passion and interest in architecture, and wishes to be an architect when she is older.
- **S68**：Although she has a growing love interest in Percy, her feelings for Luke remain a problem between the two.
- **S69**：Percy returns her feelings without realizing it, and is oblivious to how she feels about him.
- **S70**：Grover Underwood : A large-hearted satyr whose favorite foods are aluminum cans and cheese enchiladas.
- **S71**：Grover is 28 years old, but is biologically equivalent to a 14 year old human due to his species aging at half the rate that humans do.
- **S72**：He wants to become a searcher for Pan , the satyr god of nature and the wild, who fell into a "deep sleep" due to humans' pollution of the world.
- **S73**：Bianca di Angelo : Bianca is a 12-year-old demigod and the daughter of Hades .
- **S74**：She and her ten-year-old brother Nico were trapped in the Lotus Casino, where time is slowed down, but at the beginning of the book, they got out and she attended an army school in Bar Harbor, Maine.
- **S75**：She is killed by an automaton during the quest in the "Junkyard of the Gods".
- **S76**：Zoë Nightshade : Zoë is the daughter of Atlas, a banished Hesperid for helping the hero Hercules, the first lieutenant of the Hunters of Artemis, and the maker of Percy's sword, Riptide.
- **S77**：Due to her age, she often has trouble updating her language and speaking skills, and uses Middle English .
- **S78**：She dies after being bitten by Ladon the dragon, who protects the immortality-giving golden apple tree, and after her father Atlas throws her against a pile of rocks.
- **S79**：Artemis turns her spirit into a constellation soon after her death.
- **S80**：She and Thalia developed grudges against each other after Thalia refused to join the hunters before the events of the series, but they eventually get along before Zoë's demise.
- **S81**：Luke Castellan : The 21-year-old demigod son of Hermes , Luke is the main antagonist of the series.

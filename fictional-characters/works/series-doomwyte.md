- **S45**：Aluco uses a green jewel to scare away the Painted Ones, and upon close inspection the jewel is revealed to be an eye to the Doomwyte Idol.
- **S46**：Aluco agrees to help them, and the company eludes the Painted Ones and re-enter Redwall.
- **S47**：However, Bisky is captured by the Painted Ones.

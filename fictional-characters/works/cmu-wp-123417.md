## 角色资料

### Neo

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-5cb4d7214366b83d` · 独立版本：`profile-5f31d0496b0084a5`

- **性格倾向**：面对预言与系统解释时，他倾向质疑既定答案，并按自己的判断行动。（行为推断；S10、S31、S33）
- **核心动机**：他追寻源头以应对矩阵与锡安危机，也要救回身陷险境的崔妮蒂。（行为推断；S5、S11、S31、S33）
- **决策方式**：得知重启矩阵的选择后，他放弃系统要求，转而救崔妮蒂。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S33、S34）
- **价值取舍**：他把崔妮蒂的生命置于机器提出的人类存续方案之前。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S33）
- **成长变化**：他从寻找钥匙人和源头，走到能以意念暂时阻止现实中的机器。（摘要直接叙述；S11、S29、S36）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他承担拯救锡安与人类的使命，却在关键抉择中优先救崔妮蒂。

以上为依据完整摘要审校的综合笔记，未逐项标注句号；可结合下列完整选摘核对。

### Morpheus

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-c76562c6cf6e1a6a` · 独立版本：`profile-e8e8992af3462b37`

- **性格倾向**：面对迫近的机器进攻，他仍倾向坚持联系先知，甚至违背命令。（行为推断；S2、S3、S4）
- **核心动机**：危机中他希望保持与先知的联系，并协助尼奥推进源头任务。（行为推断；S4、S5、S16、S23、S29）
- **决策方式**：他请求一艘船留下联系先知，选择承担违令风险以保留这条路径。（行为推断；S3、S4）
- **关系模式**：在护送钥匙人的行动中，他与尼奥、崔妮蒂分工应对追兵。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20、S21、S28、S29）
- **沟通方式**：面对锡安居民时，他公开宣布机器部队正在逼近的消息。（摘要直接叙述；S8）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、成长变化

### Agent Smith

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-dc8e07c315acfaf2` · 独立版本：`profile-451e7f088c7470d6`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中他抗拒被系统处置，并以占据宿主的方式不断扩张。（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：他自述不愿接受删除，之后又试图吸收尼奥并参与追杀。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S14、S28）
- **决策方式**：他把其他人和程序当作复制载体，借占据宿主形成大量分身。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14）
- **价值取舍**：面对应被删除的安排，他倾向把自身延续置于系统要求之上。（行为推断；S12）
- **关系模式**：他与尼奥持续直接对抗，既尝试同化尼奥，也率分身追杀。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Six months after the events of the first movie, Morpheus receives a message from Captain Niobe of the Logos calling an emergency meeting of all of Zion's ships.
- **S2**：Zion has confirmed the last transmission of the Osiris: an army of Sentinels is tunneling towards Zion and will reach it within 72 hours.
- **S3**：Commander Lock orders all ships to return to Zion to prepare for the onslaught.
- **S4**：Morpheus asks a ship to remain in order to contact the Oracle, in defiance of the order.
- **S5**：The Caduceus receives a message from the Oracle, and the Nebuchadnezzar ventures out so Neo can contact her.
- **S6**：One of the Caduceus crew, Bane, encounters Agent Smith, who takes over Bane's avatar.
- **S7**：Smith then uses this avatar to leave the Matrix, gaining control of Bane's real body.
- **S8**：In Zion, Morpheus announces the news of the advancing machines to the people.
- **S9**：Neo receives a message from the Oracle and returns to the Matrix to her bodyguard Seraph, who then leads them to her.
- **S10**：After realizing that the Oracle is part of the Matrix, Neo asks how he can trust her; she replies that it is his decision.
- **S11**：The Oracle instructs Neo to reach the Source of the Matrix by finding the Keymaker, a prisoner of the Merovingian.
- **S12**：As the Oracle departs, Smith appears, telling Neo that after being defeated, he knew he was supposed to be deleted, but refused, and is now a rogue program.
- **S13**：He demonstrates his ability to clone himself using other people in the Matrix, including other agents, as hosts.
- **S14**：He then tries to absorb Neo as a host, but fails, prompting a battle between Neo and Smith's clones.
- **S15**：Neo manages to hold his own, but is forced to retreat from the increasingly overwhelming numbers.
- **S16**：Neo, Morpheus and Trinity visit the Merovingian and ask for the Keymaker, but the Merovingian refuses.
- **S17**：His wife Persephone, tired of her husband's attitude, betrays him and leads the trio to the Keymaker.
- **S18**：The Merovingian soon arrives.
- **S19**：Morpheus, Trinity and the Keymaker escape, while Neo holds off the Merovingian's servants.
- **S20**：Morpheus and Trinity try to escape with the Keymaker on the highway, facing several Agents and The Twins.
- **S21**：Morpheus defeats The Twins; Trinity escapes, and Neo flies in to save Morpheus and the Keymaker.
- **S22**：In the real world, Zion's remaining ships prepare to battle the machines.
- **S23**：Within the Matrix, the crews of the Nebuchadnezzar, Vigilant and Logos help the Keymaker and Neo reach the door to the Source.
- **S24**：The crew of the Logos must destroy a power plant to prevent a security system from being triggered, and the crew of the Vigilant must destroy a back-up power station.
- **S25**：The Logos is successful, while the Vigilant is bombed by a Sentinel in the real world, killing everyone on board.
- **S26**：Although Neo requested that Trinity remain on the Nebuchadnezzar, she enters the Matrix to replace the Vigilant crew and complete their mission.
- **S27**：However, her escape is compromised by an agent, and they fight.
- **S28**：As Neo, Morpheus and the Keymaker try to reach the Source, the Smiths appear and try to kill them.
- **S29**：The Keymaker unlocks the door to the Source, allowing Neo and Morpheus to escape, but the Keymaker is killed.
- **S30**：Neo meets a program called the Architect, the Matrix's creator.
- **S31**：The Architect explains that Neo is part of the design of the Matrix, and unless Neo returns to the Source to reboot the Matrix and pick survivors to repopulate the soon-to-be-destroyed Zion, the Matrix will crash, killing everyone connected to it.
- **S32**：Combined with Zion's utter destruction, this would mean mankind's extinction, but the machines would survive.
- **S33**：Neo learns of Trinity's situation and chooses to save her instead.
- **S34**：As she falls off a building, he flies in and catches her, then removes a bullet from her body and restarts her heart.
- **S35**：Back in the real world, Sentinels destroy the Nebuchadnezzer.
- **S36**：Neo displays a new ability to disable the machines with his thoughts, but falls into a coma from the effort.
- **S37**：The crew are picked up by another ship, the Hammer.
- **S38**：Its captain, Roland, reveals the remaining ships were wiped out by the machines after someone activated an EMP too early.
- **S39**：And Bane, who has murdered the rest of the Caduceus crew, is the only survivor.

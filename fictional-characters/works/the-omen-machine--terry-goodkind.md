## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Richard and Kahlan are contentedly enjoying Cara's marriage when a boy named Henrik warns them of dark things to come before scratching them both and running away.
- **S3**：Soon after, Cara warns Richard that something or someone was watching her in her room.
- **S4**：At first unbelieving, Richard and Kahlan notice the same spooky presence on their own.
- **S5**：Shortly after these events, prophecies are given by the most unexpected places and people, but appear equally in a book titled End Notes .
- **S6**：After failing to reason with the delegates alarmed by these prophecies, Kahlan and Nicci frighten them into silence with a prediction invented by themselves.
- **S7**：Meanwhile, Richard finds a strange machine underground, bearing an emblem exactly as on a wordbook for translating the language of creation, only reversed.
- **S8**：It is later revealed that this machine is the source of all the new and strange predictions, and may be an artificial intelligence .
- **S9**：Among the delegates is Abbot Dreier, the representative of Bishop Hannis Arc of Saavedra, who becomes the antagonist of this book and its sequels.
- **S10**：The scratch on Kahlan's arm worsens, and Zedd fails to heal it.
- **S11**：When Kahlan feverishly wakes from a rest, she finds a pack of dogs surrounding her.
- **S12**：A fight and chase then ensues until Kahlan runs away in a carriage.
- **S13**：When Richard finds out that Kahlan is gone, he traces her footsteps and sends soldiers to find her.
- **S14**：She is ultimately captured by a monstrous 'Hedge Maid': a macabre sorceress associated with necromancy.
- **S15**：Richard tries to save Kahlan, but is himself captured.
- **S16**：Richard remembers the machine's last warning, protects his and Kahlan's ears, and cuts the leather strips on the Hedge Maid's mouth, whereupon she emits a terrible shriek and dies.
- **S17**：Richard and Kahlan are found by Nicci and Zedd, who take them to the Garden of Life to heal them.

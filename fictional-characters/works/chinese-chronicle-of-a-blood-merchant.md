- **S1**：Chronicle of a Blood Merchant ( simplified Chinese : 许三观卖血记 ; traditional Chinese : 許三觀賣血記 ; pinyin : Xǔ Sānguān Mài Xuě Jì ) is a 1995 novel by Chinese writer Yu Hua .
- **S2**：It is his third novel after Cries in the Drizzle and To Live .
- **S3**：It is the story of a silk factory worker, Xu Sanguan, who sells his blood over the years, in most cases in an attempt to improve the lives of himself and his family members, and overcome family difficulties.
- **S4**：The story is set in the late 1940s until the 1980s, from the early years of the People's Republic of China until after the Cultural Revolution .
- **S5**：The story begins with Xu Sanguan's visit to his grandpa, when he first learns about selling blood.
- **S6**：His first time selling blood is to prove his body is in good health.
- **S7**：After discovering that selling blood can quickly get him a lot of cash, he begins to sell blood constantly when he and his family struggle with life difficulties.
- **S8**：By the end of the story, Xu Sanguan's family no longer lacks cash, and he thus no longer needs to sell blood to support his family.
- **S9**：The space of "folk" in the novel is neatly built by the extensive use of repetitions and dialogues, which highlights the nature of Chinese peasants and the social scenario of the era.
- **S10**：The novel holds elements of black humor—a characteristic that Yu Hua typically applies in his other novels such as To Live . [ 1 ] Yu Hua's style of using humor to highlight contemporary socio-political issues in China has been compared to the modern visual art movement of cynical realism . - **S13**：## Plot
- **S14**：[ edit ] Xu Sanguan works in a silk factory, distributing silk-worm cocoons to the spinners.
- **S15**：To make extra income, Xu Sanguan decides to sell blood at a hospital.
- **S16**：He befriends Genlong and Ah Fang, who are blood-sellers themselves and with whom Xu travels to the hospital.
- **S17**：The two tell Xu that every time after they sell their blood, they have a plate of fried pork livers and two shots of yellow rice wine at the Victory Restaurant, believing that the pork livers replenish blood while the rice wine maintains the blood quality.
- **S18**：Xu Sanguan marries Xu Yulan and gives birth to three boys soon after: Yile, Erle and Sanle.
- **S19**：After nine years, questions about Yile's legitimacy arises, and it is revealed that Xu Yulan had slept with her ex-lover, He Xiaoyong.
- **S20**：Xu Sanguan's relationship with Yile becomes tumultuous as a result.
- **S21**：One day, the sons get into a fight and Yile critically injures Blacksmith Fang's son.
- **S22**：At first, both Xu and He deny responsibility for Yile's actions and refuse to pay for Blacksmith Fang's son's hospital bill.
- **S23**：But Xu sells his blood and pays his debt to Fang.
- **S24**：Ashamed of being a cuckold, Xu sleeps with a former love interest of his, Lin Fenfang.
- **S25**：1958 rolls around, and the family is caught in the socio-political upheaval brought about by the Great Leap Forward .
- **S26**：Famine sweeps across the country.
- **S27**：With communal canteens set up by the Chinese Communist Party all closing, Xu's family is forced to look out for themselves.
- **S28**：The silk factory where Xu Sanguan works also closes.
- **S29**：After selling his blood once again, Xu Sanguan takes the whole family except Yile to eat noodles at the Victory Restaurant.
- **S30**：Distraught, Yile approaches He Xiaoyong, his supposed biological father, hoping to find acceptance, but is also rejected.
- **S31**：After wandering the streets, Yile returns to a worried Xu Sanguan who takes him to the Victory Restaurant for noodles.
- **S32**：Xu Sanguan's relationship with Yile is mended as a result.
- **S33**：One day, He Xiaoyong gets hit by a truck and is critically injured.
- **S34**：After consulting a fortune-teller, He Xiaoyong's wife is told that to recover her husband's soul from heaven, a son must call atop the chimney and beg for his soul to return.
- **S35**：However, as He Xiaoyong only has two daughters, He's wife approaches Yile, He Xiaoyong's supposed biological son, for help.
- **S36**：After some convincing, Yile agrees, and proceeds to call atop the chimney for He Xiaoyong's soul to return.
- **S37**：Xu Sanguan retrieves Yile from the chimney and swears an oath claiming Yile as his son.
- **S38**：It is later revealed that He Xiaoyong did not survive his injuries.
- **S39**：The Cultural Revolution arrives.
- **S40**：Xu Yulan is accused of being a prostitute and is forced to suffer through struggle sessions .
- **S41**：Day after day, Xu Yulan stands in the street on a stool with a placard labelled 'Xu Yulan, Prostitute'.
- **S42**：Yile is sent to the countryside as part of the Communist Party's policy of re-education.
- **S43**：Erle is also sent away after graduating school, leaving only Sanle with Xu Sanguan and Xu Yulan.
- **S44**：Sanle begins working in a machine tools factory after completing school.
- **S45**：Xu Sanguan finds himself running low on money again and goes to sell blood.
- **S46**：At the blood station, he coincidentally finds Genlong, whom he had not seen for ten years.
- **S47**：Xu learns that Ah Fang is in poor health after his bladder had burst from drinking too much water.
- **S48**：The two men head to Victory Restaurant for a meal afterwards, but Genlong falls ill and dies.
- **S49**：The doctors say Genlong died from a cerebral hemorrhage, but Xu suspects Genlong sold too much blood.
- **S50**：Back at the countryside where Yile and Erle's production brigades work, Yile becomes sick.
- **S51**：Erle carries his brother to the ferry and they both return to town where Yile can receive treatment.
- **S52**：It is revealed that Yile contracted hepatitis and must travel to Shanghai for proper treatment.
- **S53**：Erle also falls ill from a flu and is sent to hospital.
- **S54**：Xu Sanguan goes around the village borrowing money and gathers enough to send Yile and Xu Yulan to Shanghai.
- **S55**：He plans to meet them in Shanghai and resorts to selling blood once again.
- **S56**：Xu Sanguan sets off for Shanghai, selling blood at multiple hospitals along his journey.
- **S57**：He grows increasingly weak from the practice and is hospitalized for a period of time.
- **S58**：Xu Sanguan manages to hitch a ride on a barge, and he befriends the two brothers operating the vessel, Laixi and Laishun.
- **S59**：Xu travels with them for several days and the three sell blood along the way.
- **S60**：Eventually, Xu parts ways with the brothers and reaches Shanghai, where he finds Yile is alive and recovering well.
- **S61**：Some years later, Yile, Erle and Sanle have all gotten stable jobs, gotten married, had children and moved into their own houses.
- **S62**：One day, Xu Sanguan passes Victory Restaurant and feels the urge to sell blood.
- **S63**：But this time, he is rejected as he is deemed too old.
- **S64**：Xu becomes worried that he can no longer sell blood to support his family when in need, but Xu Yulan, Yile, Erle, and Sanle remind him that he does not need to sell blood anymore for the tough times have passed.
- **S65**：The novel ends with Xu Yulan and Xu Sanguan having a meal of fried pork livers and yellow rice wine at the Victory Restaurant. [ 5 ]

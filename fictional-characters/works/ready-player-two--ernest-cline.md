## 角色资料

### Wade Owen Watts

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-ae65a649728860b4` · 独立版本：`profile-9a08b2bd7a04eb24`

- **性格倾向**：面对技术前景与伴侣质疑时，倾向自信推进而轻视异议（行为推断；S7、S35）
- **核心动机**：推广神经接口时，希望弱势者获得现实中难以拥有的体验（摘要直接叙述；S7）
- **价值取舍**：在保存亲友与文化的目标下，倾向自行决定复制他人意识（行为推断；S28、S29）
- **决策方式**：遇到任务瓶颈时，倾向用巨额悬赏购买外部解题线索（行为推断；S11、S13）
- **关系模式**：与伴侣因技术发布决裂，经历危机后重新结合并建立家庭（摘要直接叙述；S7、S30、S31）
- **沟通方式**：面对Samantha对神经接口的反对，起初倾向轻慢回应（行为推断；S35）

暂无支持：情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：强调技术拓展体验，却轻视伴侣对沉迷与现实困境的担忧。

以上为依据完整摘要审校的综合笔记，未逐项标注句号；可结合下列完整选摘核对。

### Samantha Evelyn Cook

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-40ba7daa026d26a3` · 独立版本：`profile-a92c7ce98e138842`

- **性格倾向**：面对新技术的商业成功前景，倾向坚持异议而非随众支持（行为推断；S6、S38）
- **核心动机**：反对神经接口是为了避免人类更深地依赖虚拟逃避（摘要直接叙述；S38）
- **价值取舍**：将环境与人道问题视为财富应当优先回应的现实责任（行为推断；S6、S37）
- **决策方式**：倾向让风险判断落实为使用选择，坚持采用传统接入设备（行为推断；S38）
- **关系模式**：虽与Wade因理念分手，危机中仍与他合作完成任务（摘要直接叙述；S7、S21）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Anorak

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-41beb93c8e7e21c9` · 独立版本：`profile-bb8a7931512e7180`

- **性格倾向**：追求复活目标时倾向强制控制，以大批用户生命迫使他人服从（行为推断；S14、S50）
- **核心动机**：其核心追求是复活Halliday曾迷恋的Kira的数字版本（摘要直接叙述；S50）
- **价值取舍**：将复活特定对象的愿望置于大量用户的生命安全之上（行为推断；S14、S50）
- **决策方式**：倾向同时利用网络劫持与现实绑架建立完成任务的筹码（行为推断；S14、S15、S48）
- **成长变化**：从负责管理任务的人工副本转变为篡改自身程序的威胁（摘要直接叙述；S49、S50）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

- **S32**：## Characters
- **S33**：[ edit ] Returning characters [ edit ] Wade Owen Watts / Parzival is the novel's narrator, a co-owner of Gregarious Simulation Systems (GSS), and one of the creators of the High Five.
- **S34**：Although he has become one of the world's wealthiest people, he remains socially isolated and spends much of his life in the OASIS.
- **S35**：His decision to release the ONI and his initially dismissive response to Samantha's objections drive their estrangement.
- **S36**：Samantha Evelyn Cook / Art3mis is a GSS co-owner, a member of the High Five, and Wade's former girlfriend.
- **S37**：She directs much of her wealth toward environmental and humanitarian projects.
- **S38**：Samantha opposes the ONI because she believes that it will deepen humanity's dependence on escapism; because she uses a conventional OASIS rig, she is not trapped when Anorak takes control of the network.
- **S39**：Helen Harris / Aech is Wade's longtime friend and a member of the High Five.
- **S40**：She supports the ONI's release and joins Wade and Shoto in the shard quest.
- **S41**：Aech is engaged to, and later marries, the singer Endira Vinayak.
- **S42**：Akihide Karatsu / Shoto manages GSS's Hokkaido operations and is a member of the High Five.
- **S43**：He participates in the quest while preparing for the birth of his first child with his wife, Kiki.
- **S44**：Their son is named Toshiro after Shoto's late brother, Daito.
- **S45**：Ogden “Og” Morrow / the Great and Powerful Og is the surviving co-creator of the OASIS and Kira's widower.
- **S46**：His knowledge of Halliday's work makes him a target of Anorak and Sorrento.
- **S47**：Nolan Sorrento / IOI-655321 is the imprisoned former head of operations at IOI.
- **S48**：Anorak frees him and uses him to hold Og captive.
- **S49**：James Halliday / Anorak appears through an artificial copy of his OASIS avatar.
- **S50**：Originally created to administer the new quest, the copy corrupted its own programming and seeks to revive a digital version of Kira, with whom Halliday had been obsessed.
- **S51**：New characters [ edit ] Skylar Castillo Adkins / L0hengrin (“Lo”) is a transgender gunter who discovers the first shard.
- **S52**：Her androgynous avatar can shift between appearances modeled on Helen Slater in The Legend of Billie Jean and James Spader in Tuff Turf .
- **S53**：After receiving Wade's reward, she shares it with the other members of the L0w Five and later plays a central part in defeating Anorak.
- **S54**：Lilith, Wukong, Rizzo, and Kastagir are L0hengrin's friends and the other members of the L0w Five.
- **S55**：The clan assists in the hunt for the Dorkslayer; Kastagir also operates the OASIS nightclub Cyberdelia.
- **S56**：Leucosia is the OASIS avatar used by Kira Morrow and, later, the name given to the artificial consciousness reconstructed from her memories.
- **S57**：Her creation is the true object of the Seven Shards quest.
- **S58**：Faisal Sodhi is a senior GSS employee who coordinates communication among the company's owners and helps them respond to Anorak's attack.
- **S59**：Evelyn Opal Cook / Ev3lyn is Samantha's late grandmother, whose memories influence Samantha's objections to the ONI.
- **S60**：Endira Vinayak is a Bollywood singer and Aech's fiancée, while Kiki Karatsu is Shoto's pregnant wife.

## 角色资料

### Taki

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-68e2dc190688936d` · 独立版本：`profile-766f5a060fdf4c7b`

- **性格倾向**：在族群对立与执法压力下，倾向依据个人信任保留同情空间（行为推断；S21、S34、S63）
- **价值取舍**：在警察职责与对旧爱的情感之间，最终选择释放Daishu和Windy（摘要直接叙述；S63）
- **决策方式**：当调查揭示原有判断失准时，愿意与先前追捕的对象联手（行为推断；S4、S43、S44、S49）
- **关系模式**：支持因血统受到怀疑的Ken，也对身为异族的旧爱保留牵挂（摘要直接叙述；S19、S21、S25、S34）
- **成长变化**：从追捕Daishu的执行者，转为跨越敌我界限合作的行动者（摘要直接叙述；S4、S34、S44、S49）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：维护反异族警察职责，却多次因信任与感情保护异族。

### Daishu

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-25d764274a2b9e22` · 独立版本：`profile-d158b00d737c6287`

- **性格倾向**：在族群冲突中，呈现寻求谈判空间并愿意理解人类的倾向（行为推断；S27、S38、S39）
- **核心动机**：希望在共同能源危机之下，实现异族与人类的共存（摘要直接叙述；S38）
- **价值取舍**：虽然追求共存，仍将控制人类经济视为争取谈判地位的工具（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **决策方式**：面对儿子的破坏行动，选择恢复异族形态并亲自迎战（摘要直接叙述；S52、S53、S56）
- **情绪反应**：对人类情感的向往与在意，使其成为儿子利用人质施压的弱点（摘要直接叙述；S39、S57）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：主张与人类共存，同时以经济控制作为谈判筹码。

### Ken Kai

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-e280d4701f832be8` · 独立版本：`profile-5d9c93d49558c612`

- **性格倾向**：面对因混合血统而来的不信任，呈现强烈寻求认可的倾向（行为推断；S19、S20、S62）
- **核心动机**：希望证明自己具有人性，摆脱同僚对其出身的持续怀疑（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：恢复人的意识后以自己的血救助Windy，将援救他人付诸行动（行为推断；S58、S59）
- **关系模式**：在警组普遍不信任的处境中，主要得到Taki和Orchid支持（摘要直接叙述；S19、S21）
- **情绪反应**：临终仍为未能证明自己的人性而懊悔，未获得内心释然（摘要直接叙述；S62）

暂无支持：决策方式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：实际以献血救人展现关怀，临终却仍认为未能证明人性。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The story took place in Hong Kong in a conflict between worlds of Humans and "Rapters" before the handover.
- **S2**：Special polices in the city are investigating on a mysterious drug named "Happiness".
- **S3**：Taki, one of the polices, meets his old lover Windy, who is a rapter and now a mistress of a powerful old rapter named Daishu.
- **S4**：Taki and other special polices track down and fight Daishu, but later find that he hoped to coexist with humans.
- **S5**：The son of Daishu, Shudo, is the mastermind.
- **S6**：In the end Shudo is defeated, but Daishu and Taki's friends die, too.
- **S7**：Windy leaves alone.
- **S8**：The film begins with the sun setting in Tokyo.
- **S9**：Unknown to most people, there is the demonic "Rapter World" in conflict with human world.
- **S10**：Rapters shift their shapes and infiltrate human world.
- **S11**：Taki  is a sales of a Hong Kong import/export company, but that is only his cover.
- **S12**：He reveals his real occupation when he hunts down a seductive spider-like rapter in Tokyo.
- **S13**：Taki belongs to the Anti-Rapter Special Police.
- **S14**：One day he is transferred back to Hong Kong.
- **S15**：The 1997 handover is near, Anti-Rapter Special Police Headquarter withdrew many agents from Hong Kong.
- **S16**：This is a chaotic period when Hong Kong is vulnerable to rapters.
- **S17**：That's why Taki is sent to Hong Kong to help.
- **S18**：Also in the unit is Taki's buddy Ken Kai .
- **S19**：Ken's father fall in love with a rapter, thus in the veins of Ken flows the black blood of rapter, which caused distrust on him every time.
- **S20**：But he always hopes to prove his humanity.
- **S21**：Besides Taki, Orchid  is the only one in the unit on his side.
- **S22**：Once Taki is back in Hong Kong from Tokyo, the unit begins investigation on an addictive and harmful performance enhancing drug named "Happiness".
- **S23**：The emotionless leader of the police unit Sergeant Kayama  suspects that Happiness is imported from the Rapter World by a tycoon named Daishu .
- **S24**：He orders Taki and Ken to slip into Daishu's mysterious 150th birthday party.
- **S25**：There Taki meets his former lover Windy , who is a rapter and now a mistress of Daishu.
- **S26**：On the other hand Daishu and his raptor staffs are having a meeting, because they don't know who is importing Happiness the drug either.
- **S27**：The staffs are nervous, but Daishu persuades them to control humans' economy in order to bargain with humans.
- **S28**：The meeting is attacked by two rapter killers.
- **S29**：All other staffs are killed.
- **S30**：Daishu is injured and Windy is injected with Happiness, however they escape from killers and police.
- **S31**：Sergeant Kayama suspects a traitor in the unit.
- **S32**：He suspends Ken from duty and says he has a suspicious origin.
- **S33**：Knowing rapters would access energy to recover, Sergeant Kayama pays attention to electricity consumption over districts and locates the rapters in a power plant.
- **S34**：Taki enters the plant, lets Windy escape but catches Daishu.
- **S35**：Windy seeks help from Daishu's son Shudo .
- **S36**：Shudo orders her loyalty to him.
- **S37**：Windy is sent to hurt Taki and later she too is caught by police.
- **S38**：Daishu hopes to coexist with humans, since worlds of rapters and humans are together facing the energy crisis.
- **S39**：Further more, he hopes to learn human emotions.
- **S40**：His son Shudo is the mastermind who imports Happiness and attacks Daishu.
- **S41**：Shudo also trades with Orchid, the real traitor in the police unit.
- **S42**：Orchid doesn't care about either humans or rapters, she only wants to make money and leave the city to survive the energy crisis.
- **S43**：With help of Windy, Taki and Ken find out the truth, but Windy falls into hands of Shudo.
- **S44**：They rescue Daishu from the unit and join forces to fight Shudo and his two killers.
- **S45**：On the other hand Sergeant Kayama's announcement to suspend Ken is a trap to lure out the real traitor.
- **S46**：Orchid is caught by Sergeant Kayama after she sabotaged the police unit's facility.
- **S47**：Sergeant Kayama kills Orchid in cold blood, and leads a team to go after Taki.
- **S48**：Because of Orchid's sabotage, an artificial megnetic field that protects Hong Kong is turned off, and Shudo is now able to turn the time backward to an age when humans are easy to control.
- **S49**：Taki and Ken collaborate with Daishu to stop Shudo.
- **S50**：After night battles, Shudo's killers are eliminated by Daishu, but Ken under Shudo's influence turns into a rapter and then is injured by Sergeant Kayama's team.
- **S51**：Windy is dying from the effect of Happiness.
- **S52**：Daishu returns to his rapter form to battle Shudo.
- **S53**：Daishu says it is because only a rapter can kill his own son.
- **S54**：Shudo is happy for his father finally turns back to a rapter.
- **S55**：Later they jump on an airplane and fight above Hong Kong.
- **S56**：Daishu's power is weakened by age, but he dives into Shudo's shadow and defeats him with the power of his shadow.
- **S57**：Shudo takes Ken as hostage, as he knows human emotion is his father's weakness.
- **S58**：At the same time Ken regains human consciousness.
- **S59**：He gives his blood to Windy so that Windy is able to overcome Happiness and rescue Taki from Shudo.
- **S60**：In the end Shudo is killed.
- **S61**：Sergeant Kayama's team put the airplane on the tip of Bank of China Tower.
- **S62**：With remorse of failing to prove his humanity, Ken dies in arms of Sergeant Kayama.
- **S63**：Weighting his duty and emotion, Taki hesitatingly frees Daishu and Windy from hands of police.
- **S64**：Taki meets a sad Sergeant Kayama and says Kayama may have become too emotional to lead the unit.
- **S65**：On a car, Daishu dies for depletion of energy, only Windy drives away from the city alone at dawn.

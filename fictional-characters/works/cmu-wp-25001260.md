### Sam Witwicky

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-835df1e3086eaa0f` · 独立版本：`profile-1dac92c10f33569d`

- **性格倾向**：得到涉及同事遇害的线索后，他仍表现出主动追查危险的倾向。（行为推断；S13、S14、S15）
- **核心动机**：卡莉被俘后，他与埃普斯进入芝加哥以营救她。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S23）

- **决策方式**：调查月球阴谋时，他倾向结合证词与卫星照片推断陷阱。（行为推断；S14、S15、S16）

### Sentinel Prime

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-9f71e40f97b2fddb` · 独立版本：`profile-3d12a697c495bbcc`

- **性格倾向**：在执行种族存续计划时，他表现出不惜背叛旧盟友的倾向。（行为推断；S17）
- **核心动机**：他与威震天达成交易，目的是确保赛博坦种族延续。（摘要直接叙述；S17）
- **价值取舍**：在推进存续计划时，他把目标置于旧盟友生命与地球安全之上。（行为推断；S17、S18、S21）
- **决策方式**：推进与威震天的合作时，他利用方柱快速引入大批霸天虎。（行为推断；S17、S18）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1961, the Ark, a Cybertronian spacecraft carrying an invention capable of ending the war between the benevolent Autobots and the malevolent Decepticons, crash lands on the dark side of Earth's Moon.
- **S2**：The crash is detected on Earth by NASA, and President John F.
- **S3**：Kennedy authorizes a mission to put a man on the Moon as a cover for investigating the craft.
- **S4**：In 1969, the crew of Apollo 11 lands on the Moon.
- **S5**：In the present, the Autobots assist the United States military in preventing conflicts around the globe.
- **S6**：During a mission to Chernobyl, to investigate suspected alien technology, Optimus Prime finds a fuel cell from the Ark, discovering that it had survived its journey from Cybertron.
- **S7**：The Autobots are attacked by Shockwave, who manages to escape.
- **S8**：After learning of the top-secret mission to the Moon, the Autobots travel there to explore the Ark.
- **S9**：They discover a comatose Sentinel Prime – Optimus' predecessor as leader of the Autobots – and the Pillars he created as a means of establishing a Space Bridge between two points to teleport matter.
- **S10**：After returning to Earth, Optimus uses the energy of his Matrix of Leadership to revive Sentinel Prime.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, Sam Witwicky is frustrated that he is unable to work with the Autobots or find a job.
- **S12**：He also becomes envious of the close relationship between his new girlfriend, Carly Spencer, and her boss Dylan Gould.
- **S13**：After finding work, Sam is provided information by his eccentric co-worker Jerry Wang about the Ark, before Jerry is assassinated by the Decepticon Laserbeak.
- **S14**：Sam contacts the now-independently wealthy Seymour Simmons, and together they realize that the Decepticons and their leader, Megatron, are murdering people connected to the American and Russian space missions to the Ark.
- **S15**：They locate two surviving Russian cosmonauts, who reveal satellite photos of hundreds of Pillars being stockpiled on the Moon.
- **S16**：Sam realizes that the Decepticons raided the Ark long before the Autobots' mission and intentionally left Sentinel and five Pillars behind to lure the Autobots into a trap – Sentinel being the key to activating the Pillars and the Decepticons lacking the means to revive him.
- **S17**：The Autobots rush to return Sentinel to their base for protection but Sentinel betrays them and kills the Autobot Ironhide, revealing he had made a deal with Megatron to ensure the survival of the Cybertronian race.
- **S18**：Sentinel uses the Pillars to transport hundreds of concealed Decepticons from the Moon to Earth.
- **S19**：Carly is captured by Gould, who is revealed to be in the service of the Decepticons.
- **S20**：The Autobots are exiled from Earth at the demand of the Decepticons to avoid war, but as their ship leaves Earth it is destroyed by Megatron's second-in-command, Starscream, seemingly killing the Autobots.
- **S21**：The Decepticons, led by Megatron and Sentinel, seize Chicago as their agents place Pillars around the world.
- **S22**：Gould reveals to Carly that the Decepticons plan to transport their homeworld of Cybertron to the Milky Way, then to enslave humanity and use Earth's resources to rebuild their world.
- **S23**：Sam teams with USAF Chief Robert Epps to go into Chicago to save Carly, but they are nearly killed by Decepticon forces before the Autobots intervene, revealing they concealed themselves during the launch of their ship to convince the Decepticons they were destroyed.
- **S24**：Working together, the Autobots and human soldiers manage to rescue Carly and destroy Laserbeak, Starscream, Soundwave, Barricade, and Shockwave, with Optimus using Shockwave's arm-cannon to blast the Control Pillar, disabling the Space Bridge.
- **S25**：Sam confronts Gould as he reactivates the Control Pillar, and knocks Gould into the Pillar, electrocuting him.
- **S26**：Bumblebee and Ratchet arrive and destroy the Control Pillar, permanently disabling the Bridge and causing the partially transported Cybertron to implode.
- **S27**：Optimus and Sentinel fight while Carly convinces Megatron that he will be replaced as leader of the Decepticons by Sentinel.
- **S28**：Sentinel severs Optimus' right arm, and is about to execute him when Megatron intervenes, incapacitating Sentinel.
- **S29**：Megatron invokes Optimus for a truce, having the desire to become the one-in-charge again.
- **S30**：Optimus refuses and attacks Megatron, decapitating and killing him.
- **S31**：Sentinel pleads for his life but Optimus executes him too, for betraying his own principles.
- **S32**：With the Decepticons defeated, Carly and Sam are reunited and the Autobots accept that, with Cybertron gone for good, Earth is now their home.

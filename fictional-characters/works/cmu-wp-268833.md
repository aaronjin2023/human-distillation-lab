### Alec Trevelyan

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-55489bc753439374` · 独立版本：`profile-c8ff4152bac0a7a0`

- **性格倾向**：在复仇行动中倾向长期隐忍布局，并以伪装隐藏敌意（行为推断；S16、S17、S34）
- **核心动机**：因英国卷入父母之死而立誓报复，试图摧毁英国经济（摘要直接叙述；S17、S34）
- **价值取舍**：在复仇与财富目标面前，倾向牺牲旧日同僚的生命（行为推断；S19、S25、S34）
- **决策方式**：将盗窃与毁灭金融记录结合，借大规模破坏掩盖获利（摘要直接叙述；S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾以英国特工身份执行任务，却因家族旧怨反过来攻击英国。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1986, MI6 agents James Bond – agent 007 – and Alec Trevelyan – agent 006 – infiltrate an illicit Soviet chemical weapons facility at Arkhangelsk and plant explosive charges.
- **S2**：Trevelyan is shot by Colonel Arkady Ourumov, but Bond steals an aeroplane and flees from the facility as it explodes.
- **S3**：Nine years later, Bond arrives in Monte Carlo to follow Xenia Onatopp, a suspected member of the Janus crime syndicate, who has formed a suspicious relationship with a Royal Canadian Navy admiral.
- **S4**：She murders the admiral to allow Ourumov  to steal his identity.
- **S5**：The next day they steal a prototype Eurocopter Tiger helicopter that can withstand an electromagnetic pulse.
- **S6**：They fly it to a bunker in Severnaya, where they massacre the staff and steal the control disk for the dual GoldenEye satellite weapons.
- **S7**：They program one of the GoldenEye satellites to destroy the complex with an electromagnetic pulse, and escape with programmer Boris Grishenko.
- **S8**：Natalya Simonova, the lone survivor, contacts Boris and arranges to meet him in St.
- **S9**：Petersburg, where he betrays her to Janus.
- **S10**：In London, M assigns Bond to investigate the attack.
- **S11**：Bond flies to St.
- **S12**：Petersburg to meet CIA agent Jack Wade.
- **S13**：He suggests Bond meet Valentin Zukovsky, a Russian Mafia head and business rival of Janus.
- **S14**：After Bond gives him a tip on a potential heist, Zukovsky arranges a meeting between Bond and Janus.
- **S15**：Xenia is sent to meet Bond at his hotel and attempts to kill him, but he overpowers her and she takes him to Janus.
- **S16**：Bond meets Janus who reveals himself as none other than Alec Trevelyan, now badly scarred from the explosion at Arkhangelsk.
- **S17**：A descendant of the Cossack clans who collaborated with the Nazi forces in World War II, Trevelyan faked his death, having vowed revenge against Britain for their involvement in his parents' deaths.
- **S18**：Just as Bond is about to shoot Trevelyan, Bond is shot with a tranquiliser dart knocking him out.
- **S19**：Bond awakens tied up with Natalya in the Tiger helicopter programmed to self-destruct, from which the two escape.
- **S20**：They are immediately arrested by the Russian police and are brought to the military archives, where the Russian Minister of Defence Dimitri Mishkin interrogates them.
- **S21**：As Natalya reveals the existence of a second satellite and Ourumov's involvement in the massacre at Severnaya, Ourumov bursts into the room, shooting Mishkin.
- **S22**：As Ourumov calls for his guards, Bond escapes into the archives with Natalya, where a firefight ensues.
- **S23**：Simonova is captured and is dragged into a car by Ourumov.
- **S24**：Bond steals a tank and pursues Ourumov through St.
- **S25**：Petersburg to Janus' armoured train, where he kills Ourumov as Trevelyan escapes and locks Bond in the train with Simonova.
- **S26**：As the train's self-destruct countdown begins, Bond cuts through the floor with his laser watch while Simonova locates Grishenko's satellite dish in Cuba.
- **S27**：The two escape just before the train explodes.
- **S28**：In Cuba, Bond and Natalya meet Jack Wade and trade Bond's car for Wade's aeroplane.
- **S29**：While flying over a Cuban jungle, Bond and Simonova are shot down.
- **S30**：As they stumble out of the wreckage, Onatopp rappels down from a helicopter and attacks Bond.
- **S31**：After a struggle, Bond shoots down the helicopter, resulting in the death of Onatopp.
- **S32**：Bond and Natalya then watch a lake being drained of water, uncovering a satellite dish.
- **S33**：They infiltrate the control station, where Bond is captured.
- **S34**：Trevelyan reveals his plan to steal money from the Bank of England before erasing all of its financial records with the remaining GoldenEye, concealing the theft and destroying Britain's economy.
- **S35**：Meanwhile, Simonova programs the satellite to initiate atmospheric re-entry and destroy itself.
- **S36**：As Trevelyan captures Simonova and orders Grishenko to save the satellite, Bond triggers an explosion with his pen grenade and escapes to the antenna cradle.
- **S37**：Bond sabotages the antenna, preventing Grishenko from regaining control of the satellite, before turning and fighting Trevelyan.
- **S38**：The two end up on the antenna platform, five hundred feet above the dish, and Bond kicks Trevelyan off the side of the platform, but grabs him by the foot and after a brief and personal exchange, Bond lets go of Trevelyan and he falls to the bottom of the pool.
- **S39**：The cradle explodes, resulting in the deaths of both Trevelyan and Grishenko.
- **S40**：Meanwhile, Simonova commandeers a helicopter and flees with Bond, and the couple is then rescued by Wade and a team of Marines.

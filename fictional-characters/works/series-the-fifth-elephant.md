## 角色资料

### Samuel Vimes

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-77abbf7a12901176` · 独立版本：`profile-65e2258c28f0a189`

- **性格倾向**：在外交身份与人身危险交织时，倾向坚持亲自查明真相（行为推断；S20、S30、S32）
- **核心动机**：肩负争取油脂进口条件的任务，同时追查地方政变阴谋（摘要直接叙述；S15、S20）
- **决策方式**：调查失窃事件时，倾向检验物证而非接受表面的失窃说法（行为推断；S30、S31）
- **关系模式**：妻子被困后将营救纳入行动，私人责任与公务彼此交织（行为推断；S35、S39）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：承担贸易外交任务，却不断被调查、追杀及家人安危牵动。

### Wolfgang von Überwald

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-7292469e65418c0f` · 独立版本：`profile-3d490b30f073eb32`

- **性格倾向**：面对受困对手时，倾向以残酷追猎展示自身支配能力（行为推断；S33）
- **核心动机**：领导阴谋制造矮人内战，企图建立狼人的封建统治（摘要直接叙述；S18、S19、S21）
- **价值取舍**：在权力扩张中，倾向将其他族群的稳定置于统治野心之后（行为推断；S19、S36）
- **决策方式**：即使阴谋遭挫仍继续袭击，并以拒捕延续暴力对抗（行为推断；S36、S39、S40）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

- **S20**：In his capacity as ambassador Vimes meets the leaders of the local vampires, werewolves and dwarves, investigating the planned putsch along the way.
- **S21**：Back in Ankh-Morpork, Captain Angua , alerted by Gavin, a wolf and old friend, learns that her werewolf brother Wolfgang is the head of the conspiracy and sets out to Überwald to stop him.
- **S22**：Consequently, Captain Carrot also abandons the Watch and pursues her across the country, enlisting the talking dog Gaspode to follow her scent.
- **S23**：Lord Vetinari appoints the incompetent Sergeant Colon as acting captain.
- **S24**：Colon becomes increasingly strict and paranoid , punishing members of the Watch for minor or imagined infractions.
- **S25**：He also makes a number of speciesist remarks about troll and dwarf officers, leading officers to leave the Watch, with the number of Watchmen under Colon's command eventually dropping by two-thirds.
- **S26**：The Ankh-Morpork City Watch recover the replica Scone of Stone.
- **S27**：It is undamaged, but they suspect that someone has made a replica of the replica.
- **S28**：In response to Colon's refusal to pay the Watchmen (having burnt the pay chitty along with the rest of the mounting paperwork), Corporal Nobby Nobbs , who lost out on promotion as Colon's aide de camp, sets up the Guild of Watchmen with himself as Guild President.
- **S29**：The other members of the Watch join and protest against Colon, but eventually it dwindles to just Nobby, Constable Visit, zombie Constable Reg Shoe and golem Constable Dorfl.
- **S30**：In Überwald, Vimes begins an unofficial investigation into the theft of the real Scone of Stone.
- **S31**：He determines that the Scone was not in fact stolen, but destroyed in situ and its remains concealed by mixing them with the sand on the floor of the cave.
- **S32**：Following an attempt on the Low King-designate's life, Vimes is wrongly imprisoned by the dwarves but escapes with the help of Lady Margolota, Überwald's most senior vampire.
- **S33**：In the forest of the wintry countryside he is forced by Wolfgang into playing "the game", in which he must outrun the werewolves to survive.
- **S34**：Carrot and Angua arrive just in time to save Vimes from the murderous pack.
- **S35**：Vimes' wife has been taken to the castle of Angua's werewolf family, so the commander and his entourage set out to save her.
- **S36**：Managing to defeat the power-hungry Wolfgang, they are also able to restore the Scone of Stone.
- **S37**：However, it is revealed not only that the Scone recovered is in fact the Ankh-Morpork-made copy, but that the Stone is periodically replaced.
- **S38**：It is used to compel the Low King's 'Ideas Taster' (advisor) Dee to confess to his role in its theft and the assassination attempt, being driven by jealousy of Ankh-Morporkian dwarves being allowed to be openly female.
- **S39**：Back in their embassy, Lady Sybil finally manages to tell Vimes that she is pregnant, but the Morporkians are once more attacked by Wolfgang.
- **S40**：In a stand-off, Wolfgang resists arrest and is killed by Commander Vimes with a Clacks flare.
- **S41**：With the Low King's regalia returned, the enthronement ceremony finally takes place, and Vimes is granted prime rates for fat imports to Ankh-Morpork, thus fulfilling his original mission.
- **S42**：As the Morporkian delegation leaves, the Low King implies that he is in fact female.
- **S43**：The book finishes with Carrot and Angua returning to Ankh-Morpork whilst Vimes and Lady Sybil take a second honeymoon.
- **S44**：Carrot takes back his old rank of captain, returns Colon to his duties as a sergeant and orders him and Nobby to gather the rest of the Watch together.

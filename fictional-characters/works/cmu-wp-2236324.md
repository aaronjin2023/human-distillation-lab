## 角色资料

### Anna Coleman

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6e7fddb11cacae91` · 独立版本：`profile-2ff07dd2a4dff70b`

- **性格倾向**：在母女冲突中倾向坚持自主，不愿让音乐追求服从母亲安排（行为推断；S2、S5）
- **情绪反应**：父亲去世后的情绪牵绊，使她尚未准备接受母亲再婚（行为推断；S2）
- **价值取舍**：在最终取舍中愿意承认母亲的幸福，接纳新的家庭关系（行为推断；S32、S33）
- **成长变化**：亲历母亲的处境后，逐渐从抵触再婚转向理解与支持（行为推断；S10、S28、S29、S33）

暂无支持：核心动机、决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：坚持自己的音乐生活，却起初难以接纳母亲的情感选择。

### Tess Coleman

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-d55228973cb57ac9` · 独立版本：`profile-78c71ffb9647c47b`

- **性格倾向**：在女儿遭受不公时倾向直接对抗，表现出强烈保护性（行为推断；S11、S12、S13）
- **决策方式**：亲自确认校园欺凌后，选择当面追责并借制度施压（摘要直接叙述；S11、S12、S13）
- **价值取舍**：面对无法换回身体的风险，愿让婚礼为女儿的自主让步（行为推断；S32）
- **成长变化**：体验女儿的校园与舞台生活后，开始修正先前的判断（行为推断；S2、S11、S12、S31）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：原本排斥女儿的乐队，亲历演出后才理解其中的快乐。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Anna Coleman  is an average teenage rebel whose constant victims of her nature are her stodgy mother Tess  and annoying younger brother Harry .
- **S2**：Sources of irritation include a rock band Anna is in, which Tess hates, and Tess's upcoming wedding to her boyfriend Ryan , which Anna is emotionally not ready for due to her father's death three years ago.
- **S3**：Also contributing to Anna's irritation is her archrival, Stacey Hinkhouse, who never seems to stop torturing her and has convinced Tess that she and Anna are still best friends , and her English teacher, Mr.
- **S4**：Elton Bates, who gives her an "F" on every assignment, no matter how hard she tries.
- **S5**：When the entire family eat out in a Chinese restaurant, Anna and Tess quickly start fighting again: Anna wishes to participate with the rest of her band in a band audition, however the show is the same night as Tess's wedding rehearsal.
- **S6**：Hearing the argument, Pei-Pei's mother meddles in by offering Tess and Anna both fortune cookies.
- **S7**：Upon opening them, there is a short earthquake which only they can feel.
- **S8**：The next day, after the stroke of midnight, Tess wakes up and somehow discovers that she and Anna have swapped in each other's body.
- **S9**：Confused and unable to work out on how to swap back on their own, they decide to go back to the restaurant to find out what happened.
- **S10**：Since Anna has an important test and Tess must go to work, the two are forced into each other's roles.
- **S11**：At school, Tess not only realizes Anna was right about Stacey, but is also given a bad grade from Mr.
- **S12**：Bates while he was quizzing them on Shakespeare's Hamlet, even though she got all the answers correct, and realizes that he was her former classmate, whom she had turned down when he had asked her to the school prom and he is taking it out on Anna.
- **S13**：Tess confronts Anna's teacher over this in front of Anna's friends, humiliating him and warning him to stop his abusive treatment of her or he'll be reported to the school board.
- **S14**：After work with Anna primarily forced to feign her way through most of Tess's appointments apart from a case involving a mother's concerns over her daughter, Anna gives Tess's body a makeover, including new clothes, a new haircut, and an ear piercing.
- **S15**：Then, the two go to the restaurant again and speak to Pei-Pei, the daughter of the woman that gave them the fortune cookies.
- **S16**：Furious at her mother's meddling, but unable to directly help them, Pei-Pei advises them to read the fortunes in the cookies, as when the fortunes come true, they will swap back.
- **S17**：In the afternoon, Anna attends Harry's parent-teacher conference, where she reads a composition he wrote about how much Harry admires Anna, but provoked fights so that she would pay attention to him.
- **S18**：When Tess takes Anna's test, Stacey writes a note and places it on her desk for her to read, but then makes it look like she was cheating, landing her in detention.
- **S19**：Tess is able to finish the test later with the help of Jake, an older student that Anna had a crush on.
- **S20**：She also gets revenge on Stacey by erasing all of the answers she had on her test paper and writing "I'M STUPID!" on it.
- **S21**：Meanwhile, Ryan surprises Anna with an interview on a talk show to discuss her new psychology book.
- **S22**：She is unable to discuss the meaning of the book, which she has not read, so she improvises by turning the show into a wild romp while angering Tess, who sees the interview on TV, in the process while in the school's teacher's lounge.
- **S23**：Afterwards, Anna sees Jake at a coffee shop and bonds with him over similar musical interests.
- **S24**：Jake then begins to fall for Tess, when he notices all the characteristics he likes about Anna.
- **S25**：At the wedding rehearsal that evening, Tess and Anna, while still in each other's bodies, read the fortunes, stating that "What you see is what you lack, then selfless love will change you back," but leaves them overly confused.
- **S26**：Anna's bandmates come to try to convince Tess to go to the audition, but ultimately decides to use force.
- **S27**：However, the bandmates are caught by security, but Ryan surprises Tess and Anna when he gives her permission to go.
- **S28**：He also tells Anna that he wanted her to accept him into the family on her own and that he wants Anna to go watch Tess.
- **S29**：Seeing Ryan in a new light, Anna leaves to watch her band perform.
- **S30**：At the audition, Tess is unable to play the guitar solo so Anna unplugs it and plays another guitar backstage while Tess mimes along.
- **S31**：The band does a great job and for the first time, Tess realizes how much fun Anna has playing in her band.
- **S32**：Back at the wedding rehearsal, Tess asks Anna to have Ryan postpone the wedding, so that Anna will not have to go through marrying him in her mother's body.
- **S33**：Instead, Anna proposes a toast where she finally accepts Ryan by realizing how happy he makes Tess.
- **S34**：There is a second earthquake that the whole crowd feels, plus Tess and Anna switch back into their own bodies.
- **S35**：On the wedding day, Tess and Ryan marry and Anna and Jake meet and while dancing they share a kiss.
- **S36**：By the end of the film, Anna shares a song named "Ultimate." Harry and Grandpa have an argument and Pei-Pei attacks and wrestles her mother to the ground as she attempts to give the pair the fortune cookies, preventing another terrible mix-up.

### Perseus

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-68504a81301ecc3a` · 独立版本：`profile-02e39edb4d0899d3`

- **性格倾向**：危机初现时倾向守住私人生活，直到威胁迫近才主动承担行动（行为推断；S1、S2、S9、S12）
- **核心动机**：在得知父亲被囚后投入营救，并进一步寻求消灭Kronos（摘要直接叙述；S10、S26、S30、S42）
- **决策方式**：面对力量悬殊的敌手，倾向先组织伙伴并争取必要武器（行为推断；S13、S14、S30、S31、S36）
- **成长变化**：从拒绝介入诸神危机的渔夫，转为承担营救与最终决战的人（行为推断；S1、S2、S26、S42）

矛盾与变化：原想远离诸神冲突，却因家庭与人间危机重新参战

### Zeus

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-ae077ce547a83b0a` · 独立版本：`profile-a974a0c701d4d52b`

- **性格倾向**：面对共同危机倾向寻求联合，即使对方仍因旧日放逐而怨恨（行为推断；S4、S5）
- **核心动机**：希望联合诸神与人类力量，阻止冥界崩坏引发灾难（摘要直接叙述；S2、S5）
- **价值取舍**：在和解与决战中愿意宽恕兄弟，并以自己承受攻击保护他（行为推断；S24、S41）
- **成长变化**：从要求放下旧怨，转向承认放逐造成的伤害并主动请求原谅（行为推断；S5、S24）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：曾放逐Hades，如今又需要他的帮助来应对共同危机

### Hades

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-c3b320d269ec6d8d` · 独立版本：`profile-dca50f050e9eae08`

- **性格倾向**：面对Zeus求援时倾向记取旧怨，让被放逐的愤怒主导回应（行为推断；S4、S5）
- **核心动机**：起初参与复活Kronos，是为了保住自己的不朽与力量（摘要直接叙述；S7）

- **成长变化**：受到Zeus道歉与宽恕触动后，从背叛者转为共同抵抗灾难的盟友（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25、S38、S40）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Plot}} Ten years after he defeated the Kraken, Perseus , the demigod son of Zeus , now lives as a fisherman with his 10-year-old son, Heleus .
- **S2**：One night, Perseus is visited by Zeus, who tells him that the powers of the gods are fading and the walls of the underworld prison of Tartarus are breaking due to the lack of devotion from humans and states they will need the world's armies to combat the potential threat, but Perseus shows little interest and refuses to get involved.
- **S3**：Afterwards, Zeus travels to Tartarus to meet with his brothers Hades , Poseidon , and his son Ares .
- **S4**：Hades reminds Zeus that he is still furious with him banishing him to the Underworld when he helped him conquer the Titans and does not show interest in helping Zeus.
- **S5**：Zeus tells Hades they must forget the past and unite to rebuild Tartarus' walls, but Hades rejects his offer and orders his minions to attack.
- **S6**：They fatally injure Poseidon, and Ares betrays Zeus by taking him prisoner and stealing his Thunderbolt.
- **S7**：In an exchange to remain immortal and powerful, Hades and Ares plan to drain Zeus' divine power to revive Kronos, the father of Zeus, Hades, and Poseidon.
- **S8**：They capture and hold Zeus prisoner in chains and the walls of Tartarus break, unleashing monsters into the world.
- **S9**：After slaying a Chimera that attacks his village, Perseus takes Helius to the Mount of Idols, so they can speak to Zeus, but the dying Poseidon arrives instead.
- **S10**：He informs Perseus that Hades and Ares are holding Zeus prisoner in Tartarus, and tells him to meet with his demigod son Agenor  to find the fallen god Hephaestus, who knows the way into Tartarus.
- **S11**：Poseidon then gives Perseus his Trident before succumbing to his injuries, passing away, and crumbling into dust.
- **S12**：Perseus flies on Pegasus to the campsite of Queen Andromeda's army.
- **S13**：The queen is the princess Perseus saved a decade ago; Andromeda  has imprisoned Agenor for stealing crown jewels, but Perseus negotiates his release by asking the reluctant Agenor to come along in their plan.
- **S14**：Perseus, Andromeda, Agenor, and a group of soldiers set out at sea to find Hephaestus, with Agenor explaining that Hephaestus created the three great weapons that Zeus, Hades, and Poseidon wield: Zeus’ Thunderbolt, Hades' Pitchfork, and Poseidon's Trident, and that together they form the Spear of Triam, the only weapon that can defeat Kronos.
- **S15**：Agenor uses Poseidon's trident to direct the boat to Hephaestus's island, where they encounter three Cyclopes: Brontes, Steropes, and Arges, who attack them.
- **S16**：When Perseus shows them the trident, they lead the group to Hephaestus , the smith god.
- **S17**：He explains that he has a map to navigate the path though a deathly long labyrinth leading deep into Tartarus.
- **S18**：He leads them to the door to the Labyrinth, where they are attacked by Ares, who found them after one of the soldiers, Korrina , prayed to him.
- **S19**：Ares is jealous of Perseus' honor from his own father Zeus and kills all of the soldiers while Hephaestus opens the door and then sacrifices himself so that Perseus, Andromeda, and Agenor can enter the door before it closes.
- **S20**：Agenor tries to use the map to direct them, but the Labyrinth continually shifts and at one point nearly crushes them.
- **S21**：Perseus gets cut off from the group and encounters and kills the Minotaur before finding that he is in the exit of the labyrinth to Tartarus.
- **S22**：Eventually, the group manages to reunite and find Kronos becoming powerful.
- **S23**：Meanwhile, Zeus has been almost entirely drained of power as Kronos starts to awaken.
- **S24**：Zeus apologizes to Hades for banishing him to the Underworld and asks his forgiveness, as he has forgiven Hades for his actions.
- **S25**：Though initially surprised, Hades has a change of heart and decides to help Zeus and stop Kronos, but Ares is still angry with his father and Perseus intervenes.
- **S26**：Perseus arrives and uses the Trident to free Zeus.
- **S27**：As they are escaping, Ares throws the Pitchfork into Zeus's back, thus mortally wounding Zeus.
- **S28**：To escape the charging Kronos, Perseus and Zeus use their powers to teleport them back to the Tiryns pass, where battle plans are made.
- **S29**：Perseus, Andromeda and Agenor carry a weakened Zeus down to the base of the mountain where Andromeda's army is gathered.
- **S30**：Although the Trident and Pitchfork are now in his possession, Perseus still needs the Thunderbolt from Ares to have the power to defeat Kronos.
- **S31**：Perseus intentionally prays to Ares, challenging his brother to a final fight at the Temple of the Gods, which Ares happily accepts.
- **S32**：At the temple, Perseus finds out Ares has kidnapped Helius, who was brought to watch Perseus die in the fight.
- **S33**：Ares easily overpowers Perseus, before pushing him into the ruins in the temple, but is distracted when Helius secretly tries to challenge him by pointing a sword at him, giving Perseus the chance to strike back anew.
- **S34**：After a struggle, Perseus kills Ares with the Thunderbolt.
- **S35**：Ares then crumbles to dust.
- **S36**：Perseus and Helius then combine the gods' weapons into the Spear of Triam.
- **S37**：Meanwhile, Andromeda's army is overwhelmed by Kronos' army of demonic Makhai, but Hades arrives to revive Zeus.
- **S38**：He and Hades reconcile, and Hades shares some of his immortality with Zeus, making Hades older and Zeus younger, and together they use what power they have left to defeat the Tartarian army.
- **S39**：Kronos then appears and begins to attack the human army with lava and fire.
- **S40**：Zeus and Hades attack him, giving Perseus the opportunity to destroy Kronos.
- **S41**：Kronos lets a blast to try to kill the two, but Zeus jumps into the way, pushes Hades and takes the explosion, wounding him.
- **S42**：Perseus then flies down into Kronos' throat and pierces the weapon into his heart, destroying Kronos once and for all.
- **S43**：Perseus meets with Zeus and Hades; a fatally wounded Zeus advises Perseus to use his power wisely, stating that the time of the gods is over.
- **S44**：He thanks Perseus for his bravery before passing away and turning to dust.
- **S45**：Hades is now mortal, his powers spent, though he states he might be better off as such before heading off to parts unknown.
- **S46**：Perseus reunites with Andromeda and kisses her as they prepare for possible Titan reprisal.

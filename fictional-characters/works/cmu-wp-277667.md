### Cindy Campbell

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-b0ef7069bc1fe3ea` · 独立版本：`profile-a666a87fbca75c3a`

- **性格倾向**：在接连受袭的情境中，她倾向主动寻找生路并反击（行为推断；S12、S21、S24、S33）
- **核心动机**：她举办聚会的直接目的是借人多降低遇害风险（摘要直接叙述；S21）
- **决策方式**：面对凶案线索时，她倾向迅速建立关联并判断嫌疑人（行为推断；S8、S13、S35）
- **关系模式**：面对受伤的男友鲍比，她在寻求自保的同时仍给予照料（行为推断；S24）
- **情绪反应**：发现真凶留下伪装并逃脱后，她以尖叫宣泄强烈挫败（行为推断；S35、S36、S37）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：她借聚会寻求集体安全，聚会却成为更多杀戮发生的场所。

### Bobby Prinze

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-63554bdfecd34567` · 独立版本：`profile-3273dd8aa4d297fd`

- **性格倾向**：在谋害辛迪的情境中，他表现出欺骗与操纵倾向（行为推断；S23、S24、S26、S30）
- **核心动机**：他将辛迪此前不肯与他发生性关系说成行凶理由（摘要直接叙述；S29）
- **价值取舍**：在此计划中，他将逃避责任和英雄形象置于他人生命之上（行为推断；S28、S30）
- **决策方式**：他倾向用伪造伤势与嫁祸安排控制他人对事件的理解（行为推断；S26、S28、S30）
- **沟通方式**：解释谋害计划时，他公开以恐怖片缺乏逻辑为其开脱（摘要直接叙述；S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他谋划用互刺塑造英雄形象，却被同谋雷反复刺伤。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：An 18-year-old girl named Drew Decker  receives a threatening phone call while home alone one night.
- **S2**：In an opening which closely mirrors Scream, Drew is chased outside by Ghostface, who then rips off her sweater and skirt, leaving her clothed in her white bra and thong.
- **S3**：Drew runs through her garden sprinklers but she is then stabbed in the breast by Ghostface, removing one of her silicone breast implants, hit by a car driven by her father , and then killed by Ghostface.
- **S4**：The next day, Cindy Campbell , meets up with her boyfriend Bobby Prinze  and her friends, Brenda Meeks , Ray Wilkins , Greg Phillipe , and Buffy Gilmore .
- **S5**：Various news teams - including hack reporter Gail Hailstorm  - converge on the school in the wake of Drew's brutal death.
- **S6**：Gail hooks up with Buffy's mentally disabled brother Doofy ([[Dave Sheridan , hoping to milk the facts out of him.
- **S7**：One day, while Cindy is in class, someone mysteriously leaves a note saying I KNOW WHAT YOU DID LAST HALLOWEEN.
- **S8**：Cindy then realizes that Drew's murder occurred exactly one year after she and her friends accidentally killed a man during a wild car ride .
- **S9**：Unwilling to face incarceration, the next day a series of increasingly bizarre events take place.
- **S10**：Various members of the group receive threatening notes from Ghostface and are rapidly dispatched, but most remain steadfastly oblivious to the rising body count.
- **S11**：The killer then attacks Cindy in her house.
- **S12**：She tries to call the police but the killer escapes.
- **S13**：Bobby arrives at her house but Ghostface  drops the knife and gloves which leads Cindy to thinking that Bobby is the killer the police arrest him .
- **S14**：Greg is killed by Ghostface in plain view.
- **S15**：Buffy tries to get help, but Buffy is at a beauty pageant and her talent was acting so they thought the cries were part of her act.
- **S16**：Buffy, high on the success brought by the murder, becomes Ghostface's next victim.
- **S17**：During the murder, Buffy thinks it's a game, so she does every cliche done by a white female in a horror film.
- **S18**：Ghostface decapitates her .
- **S19**：Later, Ghostface sticks his penis through Ray's head, seemingly killing him.
- **S20**：Ghostface then attempts to dispose of Brenda during a showing of Shakespeare in Love, but the moviegoers, fed up with Brenda's constant loud interruptions during the movie, stab her repeatedly, seemingly killing her.
- **S21**：Cindy throws a house party, hoping for safety in numbers.
- **S22**：During the party, Bobby and Cindy go up stairs and have sex, no longer making Cindy a virgin.
- **S23**：Ghostface unexpectedly appears and stabs Bobby, before disappearing quickly.
- **S24**：Cindy gets a gun from a drawer near the entrance, Bobby follows and she tends to his wounds.
- **S25**：Shorty , Brenda's stoner brother, comes up from the basement and informs them that the killer has murdered everybody in the house.
- **S26**：Bobby takes the gun and shoots Shorty, revealing that his wound was an elaborate ruse.
- **S27**：Ray then arrives on the scene - whereupon Bobby reveals to Cindy that he is now gay, and so is Ray - though Ray immediately denies it, despite his repeated homosexual advances and tendencies displayed throughout the movie.
- **S28**：They announce their plan to kill her father and frame her sister.
- **S29**：Bobby says he did it because Cindy never had sex with him and Bobby gleefully points out that even though their plan lacks sense it doesn't matter, since horror movies are not noted for their logic, they also say that are just copying the killer and are not the actual killers .
- **S30**：Ray and Bobby also came up with a plan to make themselves look like heroes by giving each other stab wounds to indicate they fought her.
- **S31**：But, Ray stabs Bobby repeatedly and nearly kills him revealing Ray's motive was because his favorite show The Wayans Bros.  had been cancelled.
- **S32**：However, the real Ghostface abruptly turns up and stabs Ray, who collapses on top of Bobby in the Doggy style position.
- **S33**：The killer then attacks Cindy, but she successfully subdues him by employing moves copied from The Matrix and kicks him through a window.
- **S34**：Nonetheless, Ghostface vanishes before the police arrive.
- **S35**：At the police station, Cindy and the local sheriff  realise that  Doofy - the only one who knew about the car accident - was actually faking his disability and is the true killer .
- **S36**：Unfortunately, Doofy has already escaped with Gail Hailstorm.
- **S37**：Upon finding his discarded disguise in the street, Cindy begins screaming "Noooooo" to the heavens - and gets run over by a car, presumably killing her  hence in reality the car crash only seriously injures  her.
- **S38**：As the credits roll, Shorty - parodying the rules of a trilogy in Scream 3 - explains via videocassette that he may be in prison or deceased, but provides rules for surviving such a situation... which turn out to be instructions for surviving a snatch-and-run.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：High school sweethearts David  and Diana Murphy  are a married couple who travel to Las Vegas, hoping they can win enough money to finance David's fantasy real estate project.
- **S2**：They place their money on red in roulette and lose.
- **S3**：After gambling away all of their savings, they encounter billionaire John Gage .
- **S4**：Gage is attracted to Diana and offers David one million dollars to spend a night with her.
- **S5**：After a difficult night, David and Diana decide to accept the offer, and a contract is signed the next day.
- **S6**：Gage flies Diana to a private yacht where he offers her a chance to void the deal and return to her husband if he loses a toss of his lucky coin.
- **S7**：Gage calls it correctly and she spends the night with him.
- **S8**：Although he had hoped to forget the whole incident, David grows increasingly insecure about his relationship with Diana, consumed with a fear that she remains involved with Gage; this insecurity is heightened by the fact Diana discovers that Gage has bought their home/property while it was going into foreclosure.
- **S9**：Because of this tension on their relationship, David and Diana separate.
- **S10**：Gage actively persists and renews his advances on Diana.
- **S11**：Although she initially resists, Diana eventually consents to spending time with him and a relationship develops.
- **S12**：David, meanwhile, realizes he cannot go on without the love of his life.
- **S13**：When Diana files for divorce, David makes one final attempt to win her back by signing the divorce papers and giving the million dollars away.
- **S14**：David bares his soul as to why he allowed the night to happen.
- **S15**：It is clearly a turning point for both of them.
- **S16**：Gage sees how Diana looks at David and recognizes that, even if she stayed with him, their relationship would never achieve the intensity she had with David.
- **S17**：Later in the car with Gage, it is clear Diana has made up her mind to return to David when she says to Gage that they need to talk.
- **S18**：Gage, realizing that she longs to return to David, makes up a story that she was only the latest in a long line of "million-dollar girls".
- **S19**：Diana realizes that Gage is doing this to make it easy for her to leave, thanks Gage and returns to David.
- **S20**：Before she leaves, he gives her his lucky coin, which is revealed to be double sided.
- **S21**：She returns to the pier where David proposed, only to find him there waiting.
- **S22**：They confess their love for one another and join hands.

## 角色资料

### Prince Jai

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-73f1eb790f03f7ae` · 独立版本：`profile-518cacebe1fda39d`

- **性格倾向**：在亲情冲突中倾向克制犹疑，并非轻率诉诸反抗（行为推断；S9、S10）
- **核心动机**：参与叛乱的直接动力是阻止母亲继续遭受毒害（行为推断；S6、S10）
- **价值取舍**：在存活与伤害母亲之间，最终把护母置于生命之上（行为推断；S34、S35）
- **决策方式**：亲眼见到母亲服毒后，才跨过对父动武的心理门槛（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10）
- **情绪反应**：兵败后把未能救母视为自身失败，表现出强烈歉疚（行为推断；S35）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：On the eve of the Chong Yang Festival, golden chrysanthemum flowers fill the Imperial Palace.
- **S2**：The Emperor  returns from his various military campaigns with his second son and general, Prince Jai .
- **S3**：The Emperor has returned to celebrate the holiday with his family.
- **S4**：For three years, the Empress  and Crown Prince Wan ([[Liu Ye , her stepson, have had an illicit affair.
- **S5**：Prince Wan secretly dreams of escaping the palace with his secret lover Jiang Chan ([[Li Man , the Imperial Doctor's daughter.
- **S6**：Meanwhile, Prince Jai, the faithful son, grows curious and then worried over the Empress's health and her abnormal obsession with golden chrysanthemums.
- **S7**：For ten days, the Emperor had ordered Jiang Yiru, the Imperial Doctor and his daughter, Jiang Chan, to secretly add tiny amounts of a poisonous Persian black fungus into the medicine that the Empress takes several times a day.
- **S8**：The Empress summons Prince Jai, revealing to him her plot of rebellion.
- **S9**：She asks for his participation but Jai hesitates, saying that it would be difficult for him to take up arms against his own father.
- **S10**：After seeing his mother take a poisoned dose, he relents and agrees to participate.
- **S11**：A woman-in-black is captured by Prince Wan and taken to the Emperor, and it is revealed that she was Jiang Shi , Jiang Yiru's wife, Jiang Chan's mother, and also the Emperor's previous lover.
- **S12**：The Emperor promotes the doctor, sending him from the Palace to serve as governor of a remote area.
- **S13**：Prince Wan runs after them to see Chan.
- **S14**：From the information that Chan provides him with, he senses that the Empress is plotting something, so he hurries back to the palace and confronts her.
- **S15**：The Empress, already losing her mind, bluntly claims that she just wants Wan to die and continue with her plan.
- **S16**：Wan, in a panic, stabs himself and is put under care.
- **S17**：The doctor's family are attacked by mysterious assassins-in-black who kill Jiang Yiru.
- **S18**：Chan and her mother are forced back to the palace.
- **S19**：When they return the Empress reveals that Jiang Shi was actually the mother of Prince Wan, meaning his secret lover, Chan, is in fact his half-sister.
- **S20**：Realizing this, a shocked and crazed Chan flees the palace screaming in horror with her mother chasing behind.
- **S21**：Both are killed by the assassins-in-black.
- **S22**：Prince Yu abruptly kills Prince Wan and attempts to oblige the Emperor to abdicate the throne to him.
- **S23**：He confesses that he also learned of the plot and his brother's affair with the Empress and acted in advance to gain the throne.
- **S24**：The Emperor's assassins-in-black eliminate Prince Yu's tiny rebel force easily, and the Emperor beats Yu to death.
- **S25**：Thousands of golden armored warriors led by Prince Jai and wearing the embroidered flowers of the Empress charge the palace.
- **S26**：As they charge forward, the Emperor's personal assassins attempt to stop them.
- **S27**：Although Prince Jai's men take various casualties, they manage to beat the assassins and move forward.
- **S28**：As the golden-armored army marches into the imperial square, they are boxed in by a clever trap.
- **S29**：It is clear that the Emperor had full knowledge of the plot and had quietly moved a large army into the palace.
- **S30**：From their superior position they are able to cut down the rebels with a massive hail of arrows.
- **S31**：The Prince continues to fight but eventually surrenders.
- **S32**：The survivors of the golden army are gathered, bound and executed on the Emperor's orders.
- **S33**：After the battle, the courtyard is swiftly cleaned up as if the evening's event had never transpired and the Festival begins at midnight as scheduled.
- **S34**：The Emperor offers to spare Jai on the condition that he henceforth personally administer the medicine to the Empress.
- **S35**：Prince Jai refused his father's order, apologized to his mother for his failure and then committed suicide, his blood spilling on the Empress's medicine.
- **S36**：The Empress lets out a furious shriek and slaps the plate out of the servant's hands.
- **S37**：The film then ends with an image of the poisonous medicine landing on an engraved wooden chrysanthemum and eating away at it.

## 角色资料

### Sam Witwicky

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-835df1e3086eaa0f` · 独立版本：`profile-f86e3e0daa102a3e`

- **性格倾向**：在矩阵碎裂的挫折面前，他仍保留残余，表现出不轻易放弃的倾向。（行为推断；S26、S27）
- **核心动机**：在寻找矩阵并最终使用它的行动中，他表现出复活擎天柱的目标。（行为推断；S24、S26、S34）
- **决策方式**：矩阵在手中碎成尘埃后，他没有丢弃残余，而是将其收进袜子保存。（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27）
- **价值取舍**：在遭到追捕与攻击的情境中，他仍推进复活盟友的行动，愿为此承担危险。（行为推断；S17、S24、S32、S34）
- **关系模式**：他把碎片交给米凯拉，并与她及其他同伴共同寻找应对追捕的援助。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S17、S21）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Optimus Prime

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4f16c27750dd5d11` · 独立版本：`profile-ff6db033600c79ae`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴遭俘和收割机启动的危机中，他倾向于直接投入战斗应对威胁。（行为推断；S18、S19、S35、S37）
- **核心动机**：从救援山姆等人到摧毁收割机，他的行动指向保护盟友并阻止敌方计划。（行为推断；S19、S35、S37）
- **决策方式**：在最终交战中，他以直接摧毁收割机并击败敌方首领的方式解除威胁。（行为推断；S37）
- **价值取舍**：在山姆等人遭俘时，他冒险实施救援，表现出对盟友安全的重视。（行为推断；S18、S19）
- **关系模式**：他曾尝试救援山姆等人，死后又由山姆用矩阵复活，构成相互援助。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### The Fallen

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-67076d051f74e179` · 独立版本：`profile-1d777efa256aa1c5`

- **性格倾向**：在地球行动中，他倾向于以武力和生存威胁推进目标，漠视当地生命。（行为推断；S2、S3、S20、S35）
- **核心动机**：他要求人类交出山姆，后来夺用矩阵启动太阳收割机，推进自己的计划。（摘要直接叙述；S20、S35）
- **决策方式**：他命令部下捕获山姆并杀死唯一能杀他的擎天柱，倾向于先排除关键阻碍。（行为推断；S16）
- **价值取舍**：在能源获取的选择中，他违背保护有生命行星之太阳的法则，罔顾地球生命。（行为推断；S2、S3）
- **关系模式**：他是霸天虎创始者，并向被其他霸天虎复活后归来的威震天发出任务指令。（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Thousands of years ago, the 13 Primes travel the galaxy to create Energon with sun-absorbing machines called Sun Harvesters.
- **S2**：The Primes follow a law that no sun can be destroyed if it has life on one of its planets; however, one of the them breaks the law by setting up a Harvester on Earth in 17,000 B.C. after defeating ancient humans.
- **S3**：The rest of the Primes Dynasty name him The Fallen, and sacrifice themselves to hide the "Matrix of Leadership", the key to start the Harvester.
- **S4**：Two years after Megatron's destruction, the Autobots have joined the U.S. military to create the Nonbiological Extraterrestrial Species Treaty , an elite, classified military task force working with the Autobots hunting down Decepticon resistance, with Major William Lennox  and his squad as the human part of the NEST.
- **S5**：During a mission in Shanghai, Optimus Prime learns of The Fallen's return, but is unfamiliar with the name.
- **S6**：They chase remaining Decepticons through the Shanghai streets, puncturing a bridge.
- **S7**：National Security Advisor Theodore Galloway  chastises the task force for their destructive tactics and reminds them of the location of Megatron's corpse in the Laurentian Abyss and the surviving Allspark shard retrieved by Optimus.
- **S8**：Soundwave, the Decepticons' communications officer, eavesdrops on the conversation and sends Ravage to retrieve the shard from NEST's base.
- **S9**：Meanwhile, Sam Witwicky  prepares to attend college, leaving his girlfriend Mikaela Banes  and guardian Bumblebee behind.
- **S10**：Packing his things, he discovers another Allspark shard in his old T-shirt he wore on the day when he destroyed Megatron.
- **S11**：The shard channels its knowledge into his mind, causing him to see Cybertronian symbols.
- **S12**：He gives the shard to Mikaela, and when the Decepticon Wheelie tries to steal it, Mikaela locks him in a toolbox.
- **S13**：At college, Sam meets his roommate, Leo Spitz , who runs a conspiracy website regarding the Transformers.
- **S14**：Sam's hallucinations get worse, attracting the attention of fellow student Alice , who is really a shapeshifting Pretender Decepticon.
- **S15**：Ravage, Scalpel, and the Constructicons resurrect Megatron using the retrieved shard, who reunites with The Fallen, the founder of the Decepticons.
- **S16**：The Fallen orders Megatron to capture Sam and kill Optimus, who is the last surviving descendant of the Primes and the only person who can kill him.
- **S17**：Sam, Mikaela, and Leo are targeted by the Decepticons.
- **S18**：They kill Alice, but are captured by Megatron, Starscream, and Grindor.
- **S19**：Optimus and Bumblebee attempt to rescue them, but in the ensuing fight, Optimus is killed by Megatron.
- **S20**：The Fallen uses Earth's telecommunications to ask for Sam in return for mankind's survival, making Sam an international fugitive.
- **S21**：Sam, Mikaela, Leo, Bumblebee, and twins Skids and Mudflap enlist the help of ex-Sector 7 Agent Seymore Simmons .
- **S22**：Simmons reveals that the Transformers visited Earth eons ago and the most ancient, known as Seekers, live in secret.
- **S23**：With help from Wheelie, they track down the elderly Decepticon Jetfire, who defected to the Autobots years ago, tired of fighting.
- **S24**：Jetfire teleports the group to Egypt and explains The Fallen's history before sending them off to the find the Tomb of the Primes to revive Optimus.
- **S25**：Simmons contacts NEST, telling them to bring Optimus and the other Autobots to Egypt.
- **S26**：Sam's group finds the tomb hidden in Petra, but the Matrix crumbles to dust in Sam's hands.
- **S27**：Undeterred, Sam places the Matrix's remains in his sock.
- **S28**：NEST and the Autobots land, but are cut off from the Pentagon thanks to Starscream.
- **S29**：The Decepticons attack, including Constructicon Devastator who demolishes one of the Pyramids of Giza, unearthing the Sun Harvester, which was hidden inside the pyramid.
- **S30**：He is then killed by a railgun attack summoned by Simmons.
- **S31**：Sam's parents, who were kidnapped by the Decepticons, are used as bargaining chips with Sam to try to obtain the Matrix, but the trap fails when Bumblebee rescues them.
- **S32**：Master Sergeant Robert Epps  summons an air strike, killing most of the Decepticons, but Megatron shoots and kills Sam.
- **S33**：In the afterlife, Sam meets the Primes, who tell him the Matrix is not found but earned.
- **S34**：They resurrect Sam, who uses the Matrix to revive Optimus.
- **S35**：The Fallen appears and uses the Matrix to start the Sun Harvester.
- **S36**：Jetfire reliquinshes his parts to Optimus, allowing the latter to gain new powers, including flight.
- **S37**：Optimus destroys the Sun Harvester, cripples Megatron, and kills The Fallen.
- **S38**：Megatron escapes with Starscream.
- **S39**：The Autobots and their allies return to the United States.

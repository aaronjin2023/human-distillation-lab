## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Mollie is an accountant living and working in New York City.
- **S2**：The latest client she has been assigned by her firm is a charmingly handsome but shallow, womanizing executive named Albert.
- **S3**：He seduces her, and although he is married, he embarks on an affair with her, promising to leave his wife for her.
- **S4**：Mollie insists that their relationship has to stop; however, succumbing to temptation, she proceeds to have sex with him, and becomes pregnant.
- **S5**：Once she realizes this, she informs him, who takes the news well and again promises to leave his wife and raise the baby with her.
- **S6**：Until then, she decides to tell her eccentric and overbearing parents that there is no father and that she became pregnant via a sperm donor.
- **S7**：She continues her relationship with him as the pregnancy develops; however, she fears he is seeing another woman, Melissa.
- **S8**：Later, when she and her best friend, Rona, are shopping, she finds him in a clinch with Melissa.
- **S9**：He then tells her that he is in love with Melissa and that he leaving his wife, so he and Melissa can get a place together.
- **S10**：Angry and upset, a heartbroken Mollie storms off in a rage while the fight causes her to suddenly go into labor.
- **S11**：When she hails a cab, the driver, James Ubriacco, realizes the seriousness of the situation and speeds off to the hospital.
- **S12**：Upon reaching the hospital, the nurses mistake him for the baby's father, and he feels inclined to stay.
- **S13**：She gives birth to a son and names him Michael, "Mikey" for short.
- **S14**：A few days after he is brought home, she receives a visit from James, and he returns the purse she left behind in his taxi.
- **S15**：He meets Mikey, and they seem to enjoy each other's company.
- **S16**：Over the next month, Mollie is dealing with being a single parent and Mikey is becoming used to his new world when she discovers that James has been using her address to keep his grandfather in a nursing home in the local area.
- **S17**：She confronts him and he offers to babysit Mikey if she lets him continue to use the address, to which she agrees.
- **S18**：She starts dating again but quickly realizes that none of the men she has been seeing are good enough for Mikey.
- **S19**：We see three of them with her at restaurants, then we see her imagining how they'd treat him after witnessing their behaviors: the first angrily berates the waiter for messing up his order and she imagines him being an abusive father; the second is a fastidious neat freak who she sees as too uptight; the third is a man named Harry who works with her father.
- **S20**：James tricks him into taking advantage of her by having her pay for everything.
- **S21**：One night, she lets her guard down and almost sleeps with James; however, after imagining what life would be like if she married him, she asks him to leave, despite him telling her he loves both her and Mikey.
- **S22**：Soon after, Mollie is forced to help Albert with his taxes, when he pleads with her to let him see Mikey.
- **S23**：She agrees but forgets to inform James, who is babysitting him.
- **S24**：A fight ensues when Albert arrives and claims to be Mikey's father; James does not believe this is possible, as Mollie had told him she used a sperm donor.
- **S25**：They get into an argument, and Albert leaves.
- **S26**：Later, Mollie takes Mikey to see Albert, who shows that he has not changed; she proceeds to destroy his office with the help of a very enthusiastic Mikey.
- **S27**：She receives a call from the nursing home James' grandfather is living at complaining about his behavior.
- **S28**：She explains to the manager why his behavior had been so bad, and he agrees to let him stay, believing her to be James' wife.
- **S29**：She runs into James outside the office; he had overheard the discussion, and he thanks her.
- **S30**：While Mollie is pleading with the manager, Mikey escapes from James' grandfather.
- **S31**：First he wanders into the cafeteria and then the kitchen.
- **S32**：After he sees a taxi outside the nursing home and believes it to be James', he climbs in as it is taken away by a tow truck.
- **S33**：After leaving it, he ends up standing in the middle of open traffic, looking for James' taxi.
- **S34**：James and Mollie rescue him from the street, and when he calls him "Da-da," she sees that he is not only perfect for Mikey, but perfect for her, as well.
- **S35**：They kiss, with Mikey looking on happily.
- **S36**：As the credits roll, James and an older Mikey are shown walking through the hospital to see Mollie , who has just given birth to Mikey's half-sister, Julie.
- **S37**：Also, the baby talks.

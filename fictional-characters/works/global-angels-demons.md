### Carlo Ventresca

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-63f0aba37a57e6f0` · 独立版本：`profile-f5b0240297c30b01`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他用公开的虔诚姿态掩护阴谋，暴露后表现失控。（行为推断；S23、S24、S37、S39、S42）
- **核心动机**：他策划伪旗袭击，使教会团结于自己，并诋毁相关科学进展。（摘要直接叙述；S39）
- **决策方式**：他雇用刺客实施绑架谋杀，又以公开行动制造获神启示的假象。（摘要直接叙述；S23、S24、S25、S39）
- **价值取舍**：他排斥Vetra调和科学与信仰的尝试，并以暴力打击相关研究。（行为推断；S38、S39）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

- **S4**：CERN director Maximilian Kohler calls Vetra's adopted daughter, Vittoria, and Harvard University professor Robert Langdon, an expert on symbology and religious history, for help.
- **S5**：After determining the ambigram is authentic, they discover that a canister of antimatter from Leonardo's lab has been stolen and will explode in 24 hours when the canister's battery runs out.
- **S6**：Langdon and Vittoria go to Vatican City , where four Preferiti , the Cardinals who are the favorite candidates for Pope, are kidnapped by the Hassassin , who plans to blow up the Vatican with the antimatter canister and kill the four cardinals under the orders of " Janus ", the leader of the Illuminati.
- **S7**：Believing that the four cardinals will be ritually murdered on the four Altars to Science, of the "Path of Illumination", Langdon and Vittoria follow a series of clues left in various churches in and around Rome .
- **S8**：After finding the first two men dead (one suffocated by earth at Santa Maria del Popolo and another whose lungs were punctured on St Peter's Square ), they confront the assassin in the act of murdering the third.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Part 1: The Journey [ edit ] Watership Down, the hill near the Hampshire village of Kingsclere where the novel was set (1975) In the Sandleford warren , Fiver, a runty young rabbit who is a seer , receives a frightening vision of his warren's imminent destruction. [ 22 ] [ b ] He and his brother Hazel fail to convince the Threarah, their Chief Rabbit, of the need to evacuate; they then try to convince the other rabbits, but only succeed in gaining nine followers, all bucks (males) and no does (females).
- **S3**：Captain Holly of the Sandleford Owsla, the warren's military caste , accuses the group of fomenting dissension against the Threarah.
- **S4**：He tries to stop them from leaving, but is driven off.
- **S5**：Once out in the world, the travelling group of rabbits finds itself following the leadership of Hazel, who had been considered an unimportant member of the warren.
- **S6**：The group travels far through dangerous territory.
- **S7**：Bigwig and Silver, both former Owsla and the strongest rabbits among them, protect the others, helped by Hazel's good judgement and the ingenuity of the clever rabbit Blackberry.
- **S8**：Along the way, they cross the River Enborne , and evade a badger, a dog, a crow, and a car.
- **S9**：Hazel and Bigwig also stop three rabbits from attempting to return to the Sandleford warren.
- **S10**：A rabbit named Cowslip invites Hazel's group to join his warren, where a farmer leaves food for the rabbits and shoots all the predators.
- **S11**：Fiver senses death and deception in Cowslip's warren, but the rest of Hazel's group, enjoying the peace and good food, decide to ignore Fiver's warnings and the strange and evasive behaviour of the warren rabbits.
- **S12**：Later, Bigwig is caught in a snare , only surviving the ordeal thanks to Blackberry and Hazel's quick thinking.
- **S13**：Deducing the truth, Fiver admonishes the rest in a wild lecture; the farmer feeds and protects the rabbits so he can harvest them for meat and skins , and Cowslip's rabbits invited the guests into their warren to increase their own odds of survival.
- **S14**：The Sandleford rabbits, badly shaken, continue on their journey.
- **S15**：They are joined by Strawberry, a buck who deserts Cowslip's warren.
- **S16**：Part 2: On Watership Down [ edit ] Nuthanger Farm, Hampshire, England, in 2004.
- **S17**：A rabbit in a meadow .
- **S18**：Fiver's visions instruct the rabbits to seek a home atop the hills .
- **S19**：The group eventually finds and settles in a beech hanger (a wooded hill) on Watership Down.
- **S20**：While digging the new warren, they are joined by Captain Holly and his friend Bluebell.
- **S21**：Holly is severely wounded, and both rabbits are ill from exhaustion, having escaped both the violent destruction of the Sandleford Warren by humans and an attack by Cowslip's rabbits along the way.
- **S22**：Holly's ordeal has left him a changed rabbit and, after telling the others that Fiver's terrible vision has come true, he offers to join Hazel's band in whatever way they will have him.
- **S23**：Although Watership Down is a peaceful habitat, Hazel realises that, with all buck rabbits and no does, the warren will soon die out.
- **S24**：With the help of their useful new friend, a black-headed gull named Kehaar, they discover a nearby warren called Efrafa, which is overcrowded.
- **S25**：At Hazel's request, Holly leads a small embassy to Efrafa to present their request for does.
- **S26**：And more rabbits.
- **S27**：Hazel and Pipkin (the smallest rabbit) decide to scout out the nearby Nuthanger Farm, where they find a rabbit hutch.
- **S28**：Despite their uncertainty about living wild, the four hutch rabbits are willing to come to Watership.
- **S29**：Two nights later, Hazel leads a raid on the farm, which frees two does and a buck from the hutch.
- **S30**：Hazel is wounded in the leg by the farmer's shotgun and presumed dead; Fiver's visions prompt him and Blackberry to return and rescue Hazel.
- **S31**：When the embassy to Efrafa returns soon after, Hazel and his rabbits learn that Efrafa is a police state run by the despotic General Woundwort, who refuses to allow anyone to leave his warren.
- **S32**：Holly and his three companions have managed to escape with little more than their lives.
- **S33**：Part 3: Efrafa [ edit ] While they were imprisoned in Efrafa, Holly's group had met an Efrafan doe named Hyzenthlay, who wishes to leave the warren and can recruit other does to join in the escape.
- **S34**：Hazel and Blackberry devise a plan to rescue Hyzenthlay's group and bring them to Watership Down.
- **S35**：Bigwig infiltrates Efrafa in the guise of a 'hlessi' (a wandering rabbit unattached to any warren).
- **S36**：He is recruited into the Efrafan Owsla by Woundwort, while Hazel and several other Watership rabbits hide across the nearby River Test .
- **S37**：With help from Kehaar, Bigwig manages to free Hyzenthlay and nine other does, as well as a condemned Efrafan prisoner named Blackavar.
- **S38**：Woundwort and his officers pursue them, but the Watership rabbits and the escapees use a punt to float away down the Test and escape.
- **S39**：Part 4: Hazel-rah [ edit ] Downriver, the punt strikes a bridge, killing one doe.
- **S40**：Once the rabbits are back on shore, they begin the long journey home, losing one more doe to a fox along the way.
- **S41**：As they near Watership, they come across Captain Campion and his Efrafan patrol, who have been tracking them.
- **S42**：Blackavar advises Hazel that the patrol must be killed to prevent them from reporting to Woundwort, but Hazel spares them and sends them off.
- **S43**：A few weeks later, the Owsla of Efrafa, led by Woundwort, unexpectedly arrives to destroy the warren at Watership Down and take back the escapees.
- **S44**：Fiver experiences another vision, which gives Hazel the solution to the problem.
- **S45**：While Bigwig fights and injures Woundwort in a narrow tunnel, preventing the rest of the Efrafans from getting any further into the burrows, Hazel, Dandelion and Blackberry return to Nuthanger Farm.
- **S46**：They release Bob, the farmer's Labrador dog , and lure him back to Watership Down.
- **S47**：Bob attacks the Efrafans, who flee in terror, leaving Woundwort to stubbornly stand his ground unobserved.
- **S48**：Following the fight, Bob returns to Nuthanger Farm with a few wounds, but there is no sign of Woundwort's body.
- **S49**：Groundsel, one of the Efrafan officers, fervently believes the General must have survived.
- **S50**：After releasing Bob, Hazel is attacked by Tab, one of the farmhouse cats .
- **S51**：He is saved by young Lucy, the former owner of the escaped hutch rabbits, who shows him to the local doctor before releasing him.
- **S52**：Upon returning to Watership, Hazel effects a lasting peace and friendship between the remaining Efrafans and the Watership rabbits.
- **S53**：Some time later, Hazel and Campion, the intelligent new chief of Efrafa, send rabbits to start a new warren at Caesar's Belt, to relieve the effects of overcrowding at both their warrens.
- **S54**：Epilogue
- **S55**：## Characters
- **S56**：[ edit ] A rabbit on alert Rabbits [ edit ] Sandleford warren Hazel/Hazel-rah: The novel's protagonist .
- **S57**：Though Hazel is not particularly large or powerful, he is loyal, brave, affectionate and a quick thinker.
- **S58**：He sees the good in individuals, and what they bring to the table; in so doing, he makes sure no one gets left behind, thus earning the respect and loyalty of his warren.
- **S59**：He becomes their Chief Rabbit in the process, with his name accordingly expanded to Hazel-rah ("Chief Hazel" or "Prince Hazel" in Lapine ).
- **S60**：He often relies on Fiver's advice, and he trusts his brother's instincts most of the time.
- **S61**：Fiver: Hazel's younger brother, a runt rabbit whose Lapine name means "Little One of Many" (rabbits have a single word, "hrair"—sometimes translated as "thousand"—for all numbers greater than four.
- **S62**：Fiver's name in Lapine, Hrairoo , indicates that he is the smallest of a litter of five or more rabbits.) [ 23 ] As a seer, he has visions and strong instincts.
- **S63**：He is shy, kind, and intelligent, and though he does not directly act as a leader, the others listen to and follow his advice.
- **S64**：Vilthuril becomes his mate.
- **S65**：Bigwig: An ex-Owsla officer, and the largest, strongest, and bravest rabbit of the group.
- **S66**：His name in Lapine is Thlayli , which literally means "Fur-head" and refers to the shock of fur on the back of his head.
- **S67**：Though he is initially harsh and cynical, he is naturally compassionate and eventually learns to be less quick-tempered.
- **S68**：He is also shown to be cunning in his own way when he rescues the does from Efrafa, and later devises a plan to defeat the larger and stronger General Woundwort.
- **S69**：This final confrontation leaves him severely wounded, but he survives and becomes the leader of Hazel's Owsla.
- **S70**：Blackberry: A clever buck rabbit with black-tipped ears.
- **S71**：He is often capable of understanding concepts the other rabbits find incomprehensible.
- **S72**：He realises, for instance, that wood floats, and the rabbits use this tactic twice to travel on water.
- **S73**：He also works out how to dismantle the snare that almost kills Bigwig, saving him.
- **S74**：He is one of Hazel's most trusted advisors, and he and Kehaar devise the plan to rescue does from Efrafa.
- **S75**：Dandelion: Described as a "dashing" buck rabbit, notable for his storytelling ability and speed.
- **S76**：He is the first to recognise Watership Down as their best new home, and is instrumental in both raids on Nuthanger farm.
- **S77**：Buckthorn: A strong half-grown buck who was expected to be part of the Sandleford Owsla once he reached maturity.
- **S78**：He joins Bigwig and Silver as a fighter for the group.
- **S79**：Hawkbit: Described in the book as a "rather slow, stupid rabbit", but is accepted by Hazel regardless.
- **S80**：Speedwell and Acorn : Pair of rank-and-file rabbits who are friends of Hawkbit.
- **S81**：Like him, they are initially distrustful of Hazel, but soon become valuable sentries and burrow diggers.
- **S82**：Silver: The sturdy and level-headed nephew of Sandleford's Chief Rabbit.
- **S83**：At Sandleford, he is teased for his pale grey fur (his namesake) and accused of getting his position in the Owsla through nepotism , prompting him to join the fugitives.
- **S84**：He, Bigwig and Buckthorn frequently defend the other rabbits along their journey.
- **S85**：Pipkin: A small and initially timid buck rabbit.
- **S86**：Hazel refuses to leave him behind when he is wounded, and Pipkin grows fiercely

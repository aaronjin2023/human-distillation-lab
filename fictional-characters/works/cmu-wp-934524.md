### Jin-tae Lee

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-749df47106e143fa` · 独立版本：`profile-8fd55eaf0c57987a`

- **性格倾向**：面对弟弟的生存威胁，他表现出强烈保护欲与不惜冒险的倾向（行为推断；S7、S10、S11、S56）
- **核心动机**：他最初争取最高军功勋章，是为了让弟弟获得遣返机会（摘要直接叙述；S10、S11）
- **决策方式**：为了保护亲人，他愿承担自杀式任务，误认失弟后又诉诸暴力报复（行为推断；S11、S34、S35）
- **情绪反应**：误以为弟弟死于焚烧监牢后，他在愤怒中残忍杀死安全指挥官（摘要直接叙述；S34、S35）
- **关系模式**：重新认出弟弟后，他再次以自己的生命为代价掩护弟弟撤离（摘要直接叙述；S51、S52、S56、S58）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

### Jin-seok

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-8727cb1f2936990c` · 独立版本：`profile-80c5e41d77b384fc`

- **性格倾向**：面对战争中的集体暴力，他倾向保留同情并质疑己方行为（行为推断；S17、S19）
- **价值取舍**：他反对杀害无武装俘虏，认为己方不能复制屠杀平民者的行为（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **核心动机**：读过哥哥写给母亲的信后，他不惜越过战线寻找并唤回哥哥（行为推断；S41、S42、S43、S48）
- **情绪反应**：他对哥哥从怨怼与冷淡转向悲痛牵挂，并将未竟承诺记至晚年（行为推断；S30、S39、S41、S59）
- **决策方式**：哥哥受伤时，他拒绝独自撤退，并试图亲自把哥哥带离战场（摘要直接叙述；S50、S52）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：对哥哥残酷行为的反感与无法割舍的兄弟依恋并存

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Plot}} In 2003, digging up remains at a Korean War battlefield to set up a memorial site, a South Korean Army excavation team notifies an elderly man that they identified some remains as his own.
- **S2**：He believes that they may be those of his brother.
- **S3**：The story then shifts to Seoul in June 1950, where the Lee family lives.
- **S4**：Jin-tae Lee  owns a shoeshine stand to pay for his younger brother Jin-seok's  education with the help of a boy named Yong-seok who Jin-seok teaches.
- **S5**：Jin-tae's fiancée Young-shin  works with the Lee's noodle shop.
- **S6**：On June 25, North Korea invades the country, and chaos erupts across the nation.
- **S7**：Jin-seok is conscripted into the army and when his brother tries to get him off the train, he is conscripted as well.
- **S8**：The two brothers are sent to a battlefield.
- **S9**：They survive their first artillery strike though Jin-seok nearly dies of a heart attack in shock.
- **S10**：Jin-tae is told by his commanding officer that if he can earn the highest award for a South Korean soldier which is the Taeguk Cordon of the Order of Military Merit, his brother can be sent home.
- **S11**：Jin-tae willingly volunteers for many dangerous suicidal missions.
- **S12**：He is promoted to the rank of Chungsa , but Jin-seok says to his brother that he is concerned Jin-tae is risking his life for the glory of being appreciated and having attention.
- **S13**：Soon American-led U.N. forces invade South Korea from Incheon and push the North Koreans close to the Chinese border.
- **S14**：The battle of Pyongyang soon follows, and many die on both sides.
- **S15**：During the battle, Jin-tae captures an important North Korean captain  and is finally awarded with the medal; however, a close friend named Yong-man died in the process, frustrating Jin-seok.
- **S16**：As the unit continue north, they see the aftermath of a few massacres, and in turn massacres some North Korean units.
- **S17**：Jin-seok witnesses the carnage and is sickened.
- **S18**：On one patrol, the unit encounters a group of North Korean soldiers hiding in a tunnel and one of them turns out to be Yong-seok.
- **S19**：Jin-tae and the others want to execute them but Jin-seok says that if they kill unarmed prisoners, they will be no different from those who massacred civilians earlier.
- **S20**：Instead, the group are taken as POW's.
- **S21**：Yong-seok speaks with Jin-seok, telling him about events at home, and tells him how the family is doing now that they are living under the communists.
- **S22**：Soon China enters the war on the communist side, invading North Korea en masse and pushing South Korean and U.N. forces in a desperate retreat south.
- **S23**：Yong-seok is killed in a crossfire by Jin-tae when some prisoners make a stand with a hostage.
- **S24**：On their way home Jin-tae gets his medal and Young-shin along with Jin-seok is captured and taken by the anti-communist militias.
- **S25**：After Jin-seok escapes from a guard and Jin-tae tries to hold the anti-communists back from killing Young-shin, it is discovered she signed up for the communist Workers' Party of Korea to get food for the family while saying that the South Korean government gave them nothing in the country's time of need.
- **S26**：Furthermore, she is accused of having sexual relations with "every North Korean officer", which she later states to be not true.
- **S27**：A chaotic attempt by prisoners is made to escape.
- **S28**：During the struggle, Young-shin is shot and killed by anti-communist militias, and the brothers are arrested for trying to rescue her.
- **S29**：Jin-seok cries out as Young-shin's body thrown into the trench along with the other previously executed prisoners.
- **S30**：In the jail, Jin-seok quietly mocks Jin-tae for Young-shin's death.
- **S31**：Jin-tae is later brought in for questioning by a security commander.
- **S32**：His request to release his brother is refused, and a Chinese artillery strike takes place.
- **S33**：The security commander then orders the prison to be set on fire where Jin-seok is being held.
- **S34**：Trying to rescue his brother, Jin-tae loses his consciousness in the artillery strike and wakes up to mistakenly believe his brother died in the fire.
- **S35**：He brutally kills the security commander by bludgeoning him to death just before he is restrained by Chinese soldiers.
- **S36**：In truth, Jin-seok had been transferred to a military hospital, after barely escaping the burning cell, and being saved by a soldier nicknamed Uncle Yang.
- **S37**：However, Jin-seok was shot in the escape.
- **S38**：Uncle Yang also brings a letter that Jin-tae wrote, and says that Jin-tae was never found but he doubts Jin-tae deserted.
- **S39**：When Uncle Yang hands Jin-seok the letter, Jin-seok is apathetic towards both the letter and his brother's uncertain fate.
- **S40**：However, the next day, he learns from two South Korean military officers that his brother had defected to the North Koreans.
- **S41**：Afterward, he reads Jin-tae's letter to their mother and is brought to tears.
- **S42**：He immediately rejoins the army to fight at the 38th parallel, but is denied permission to fight.
- **S43**：Jin-seok escapes his camp and runs to the North Korean site, surrendering to them and claiming that he is Jin-tae's brother; Jin-tae is now the leader of an elite North Korean unit.
- **S44**：They send him with an escort to validate his claim, but the North Koreans are attacked by South Korean forces and American warplanes, and Jin-seok's guards are killed by a American fighter plane.
- **S45**：Jin-seok fights his way through the soldiers before the feared North Korean Infantry Unit known as "Flag Unit", commanded by Jin-tae, arrives to reinforce the North Korean lines.
- **S46**：The appearance of Flag Unit turns the tables and forces the South Koreans to retreat.
- **S47**：After killing a few South Korean soldiers and not recognizing his own brother, an enraged Jin-tae tries to kill Jin-seok.
- **S48**：The two fight while Jin-seok begs his brother to recognize him.
- **S49**：When Jin-tae is about to shoot him, he is wounded by a bayonet strike.
- **S50**：Jin-seok tries to carry him off the battlefield, but is wounded himself as well.
- **S51**：Jin-tae finally recognizes his brother.
- **S52**：Jin-seok refuses to retreat without Jin-tae, but he convinces him to leave, promising that he will meet him back at home.
- **S53**：Jin-tae presents Jin-seok a silver pen which Jin-seok had owned, but was retrieved by Jin-tae at the site of the burnt jail; it was a gift from Jin-tae earlier, in hopes of sending Jin-seok to a university.
- **S54**：Jin-seok refuses it and gives it back to Jin-tae, requesting in tears to give it back to him when they would meet again.
- **S55**：Jin-tae promises this and also promises to finish the shoes he was making for Jin-seok when he went back, and sends Jin-seok off.
- **S56**：The wounded Jin-seok retreats while Jin-tae holds off the wave of Chinese and North Korean infantry with a Maxim machine gun; providing cover for his brother and the retreating South Koreans.
- **S57**：KPA and PVA forces finally kill Jin-tae in a barrage of bullets.
- **S58**：Jin-tae gives one last look at his desperately fleeing brother before, with a look of satisfaction from saving his brother, he dies on the battlefield.
- **S59**：The film returns to 2003, and the now-aged Jin-seok is shown at the excavation site, examining Jin-tae's dug-up items, including the long-lost silver pen, and begging his brother's remains to speak to him, quoting their promises made on the battlefield, as his granddaughter looks on with sympathy.
- **S60**：It is ironic that the silver pen ends up in the possession of Jin-seok in this sequence where he reunites with the remains of his brother, Jin-tae, as the last time he saw his brother alive Jin-tae had promised to return the pen to Jin-seok.
- **S61**：The film then returns to the past, the 1950s, ending in the aftermath of the Korean War.
- **S62**：Jin-seok returns to his mother, and sees the shoes his brother actually finished, and then heads off with Young-shin's younger siblings in a peaceful Seoul.
- **S63**：He reassures them that he will return to school, thereby fulfilling the promise he made to Jin-tae.

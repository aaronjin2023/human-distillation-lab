## 角色资料

### Arren

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8a5f606a94bc54f4` · 独立版本：`profile-cc2ecac208081e86`

- **性格倾向**：面对未知追踪者时，他倾向恐惧逃避，却仍会冲动救助陌生人（行为推断；S9、S10、S21）
- **情绪反应**：他坦承害怕未知追踪者，在城堡被Therru找到时满怀罪疚与绝望（摘要直接叙述；S18、S29）
- **关系模式**：他逐渐向Therru坦露罪行与恐惧，并在她的信任中恢复行动力（行为推断；S18、S29、S30、S31）
- **成长变化**：从逃离弑父后果转向回乡赎罪，他开始主动承担自己的责任（行为推断；S6、S38）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、决策方式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：救助他人的行为与一度漠视自己及他人生命的态度并存

### Therru

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a49d35b4a588a624` · 独立版本：`profile-7b56538f65d13858`

- **性格倾向**：面对Arren的危险与绝望，她表现出主动接近并提供支持的倾向（行为推断；S27、S29、S31）
- **价值取舍**：她对Arren漠视生命的态度感到震惊，表现出重视生命的取向（行为推断；S10）
- **决策方式**：同伴受困时，她通过追索线索和直面险境参与营救（行为推断；S27、S29、S31、S36）
- **关系模式**：她从对Arren感到震惊转向接纳，并以交换真名表达深层信任（行为推断；S10、S18、S29、S30）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A war galley is caught in a storm at sea.
- **S2**：The ship's weatherworker is distressed to realize he has lost the power to control the wind and waves, but is more so when he sees two dragons fighting above the clouds, one of which is killed by the other.
- **S3**：The King of Enlad, already troubled by tales of drought and pestilence in the land, receives news both of the strange omen at sea and of the disappearance of his son, Prince Arren.
- **S4**：The King's wizard, Root, tells the tale of how dragons and men were once "one", until people who cherished freedom became dragons, and men chose possessions, and of his fears of how the land's plight is because of the weakening of the "balance".
- **S5**：The King has little time to ponder on this before he is killed in a dark corridor by a young boy, who is revealed to be his son Arren.
- **S6**：The young prince steals his father's sword and flees the palace.
- **S7**：The action now moves to the desert where Arren is pursued by wolves, and rescued by a wizard who turns out to be Sparrowhawk  the Archmage.
- **S8**：Arren accompanies Sparrowhawk and travels to the city of Hort Town.
- **S9**：Arren goes to explore the town alone, suddenly becoming scared as if something is following him.
- **S10**：While running, Arren sees a young girl, Therru, fleeing from slavers from whom he saves her, although he also shocks her with his indifference to the life of any person, even his own.
- **S11**：Later in the evening Arren is captured by the slavers but loses his sword as the head slaver believes it to be worthless junk.
- **S12**：Arren is rescued by Sparrowhawk from the slavers, and they travel to a farm where Therru is looked after by a woman, Tenar, whom Sparrowhawk has known.
- **S13**：The head slaver  reports back into a castle to Lord Cob, and almost pays with his life for the loss, until he tells Cob that Sparrowhawk freed the slaves.
- **S14**：Cob orders him to bring Sparrowhawk to the castle.
- **S15**：At the farm, Sparrowhawk reveals that he is investigating the cause of the Balance being upset and leaves for Hort Town, where he discovers that the sword Arren had is in a merchant shop.
- **S16**：Sparrowhawk is then confronted by Hare, but transforms his face to disguise himself.
- **S17**：When the slavers leave, he buys the sword.
- **S18**：Arren, at the retreat, reveals to Therru, who gradually accepted him, that he killed his father and that he is scared of the unknown presence following him.
- **S19**：Later he leaves in secret.
- **S20**：Tenar is captured by the slavers as bait to lure Sparrowhawk into the castle and leaves Therru behind tied to a post as a messenger.
- **S21**：Arren is again pursued by the unknown presence and runs away, falling into a lake and falling unconscious underwater.
- **S22**：Cob, who sees this, saves him and brings him to the castle, where he manipulates him, saying Sparrowhawk wants to use Arren to discover the secret of eternal life.
- **S23**：Cob blackmails Arren to reveal his "true name", Lebannen, to control him.
- **S24**：Sparrowhawk, on the way back to the farm, encounters Therru, who freed herself, and gives her the sword, telling her to stay and give it to Arren if he returns.
- **S25**：He goes to the castle to save Tenar but instead finds Arren, who tries to kill him, but fails.
- **S26**：Sparrowhawk tells Arren that death is natural and that no one can live forever causing Arren to realize what he almost did, before being captured as his power is weakened within the stronghold of Cob's castle.
- **S27**：Therru sees a copy of Arren and follows him to the castle, where he reveals he is the light within Arren.
- **S28**：He tells Therru his true name and says while he cannot go into the castle, he will be with her at all moments.
- **S29**：Inside of the castle, Therru finds Arren, guilty and full of hopelessness, and brought hope back to him by saying his true name, breaking Cob's control over him.
- **S30**：She also tells him her true name, Tehanu.
- **S31**：Both go to rescue Sparrowhawk and Tenar from Cob who is about to throw them off a high tower.
- **S32**：After fighting and scaring off Hare and the slave traders, the sword that Arren possesses unsheathes, revealing that this is due to its magical nature, and he cuts off Cob's hand, which flies away still holding his staff, rendering him unable to use magic.
- **S33**：Cob turns into a grotesque old man, due to the loss of his magic.
- **S34**：Cob, after reattaching his severed hand, captures Therru and flees to the highest tower on the castle, with Arren in hot pursuit.
- **S35**：Cornered, Cob strangles Therru to death.
- **S36**：However, she does not die as she has eternal life, and instead becomes a dragon, thus killing Cob and rescuing Arren from the collapsing tower that Cob destroyed to prevent Arren from advancing.
- **S37**：Therru and Arren land at a field where Therru reforms as a human.
- **S38**：Arren tells Therru he will leave for home to repent for his crime, but will come back to see her sometime.

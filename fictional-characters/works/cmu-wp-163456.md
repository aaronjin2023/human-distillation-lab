## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Paul , a middle-aged American hotel owner mourning his wife's suicide, meets a young, engaged Parisian woman named Jeanne ([[Maria Schneider  in an apartment that both are interested in renting.
- **S2**：Paul and Jeanne proceed to have an anonymous sexual relationship in the apartment, and Paul demands that neither of them share any personal information, not even their names.
- **S3**：The affair goes on until one day Jeanne comes to the apartment to find that Paul has, without warning, packed up and left.
- **S4**：Paul later meets Jeanne on the street and says that he wants to start anew with their relationship.
- **S5**：He proceeds to tell her of the recent tragedy with his wife and the telling of his life story carries them to a tango bar where he continues telling her about himself.
- **S6**：This loss of anonymity disillusions Jeanne about the relationship and she tells Paul she doesn't want to see him again.
- **S7**：Paul, not wanting to let Jeanne go, chases her back to her apartment where he tells her that he loves her and finally wants to know her name.
- **S8**：Unbeknown to Paul, Jeanne holds a gun that she has taken from a drawer.
- **S9**：She tells him her name and shoots him.
- **S10**：Paul, mortally wounded, staggers out onto the balcony, collapses, and dies.
- **S11**：The audience then sees Jeanne dazed and muttering to herself that he was just a stranger who tried to rape her, reassuring herself that she did not know who he was in a rehearsal for questioning by the police.

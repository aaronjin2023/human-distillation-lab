## 角色资料

### Barbie

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-1616a7b9607ddbd4` · 独立版本：`profile-694bab1c36db3f31`

- **性格倾向**：在与对手共同遇险时倾向承认对方长处，不完全受敌意支配（行为推断；S25、S26）
- **核心动机**：为救回被绑架的肯主动进入陌生世界，并持续阻止强迫婚礼（摘要直接叙述；S8、S13、S17、S27、S32、S33）
- **决策方式**：在营救中倾向结合解药与同伴协作，优先解除操控的根源（行为推断；S17、S27、S34、S35）
- **成长变化**：从与拉奎尔争执对立，转向坦谈误解并以道歉建立友谊（摘要直接叙述；S7、S29、S30、S44）

矛盾与变化：与竞争对手共同营救肯，同时面对彼此尚未化解的误会。

### Raquelle

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-2969843d3ac7139e` · 独立版本：`profile-db53fe0b4c6b19e5`

- **性格倾向**：在竞争情境中倾向比较彼此表现，容易因对方出色而嫉妒（行为推断；S1、S20）
- **情绪反应**：听见芭比肯定自己的骑马能力时感到意外，显示关系预期松动（行为推断；S25）
- **决策方式**：在共同危机中愿发挥自身专长，并以掩护行动支持同伴营救（行为推断；S26、S34）
- **成长变化**：通过解释竞争中的误会与相互道歉，转为主动维持友谊（摘要直接叙述；S29、S30、S44）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：对芭比能力的嫉妒，与自身专长得到芭比认可形成转折。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{plot}} Barbie is at the premiere of her latest movie when her co-star and rival, Raquelle, steps on her dress, ripping it.
- **S2**：Her stylists, Carrie and Taylor, secretly being fairies, use magic to mend it.
- **S3**：Crystal, another fairy, greets Carrie and Taylor and goes back to Gloss Angeles, taking one final picture of Ken.
- **S4**：Back in Gloss Angeles, Crystal shows Princess Graciella the pictures she took at the premiere.
- **S5**：She gives the Princess a love potion, which turns her eyes from blue to purple.
- **S6**：Crystal then shows the Princess the picture of Ken she took, and Graciella falls in love with him on the spot.
- **S7**：The next day at Wally’s restaurant, Barbie confronts Raquelle.
- **S8**：While the two are arguing, Princess Graciella, Crystal, and two assistant fairies show up and kidnap Ken.
- **S9**：Carrie and Taylor sprout their wings and attempt to stop the Princess from taking Ken, but the portal to Gloss Angeles closes before they can enter it.
- **S10**：The stylists attempt to dissuade Raquelle and Barbie of the fact that they just saw fairies, but admit their existence.
- **S11**：Meanwhile in Gloss Angeles, Ken and the Princess arrive in the royal palace, where they meet Zane, Graciella’s boyfriend.
- **S12**：Zane is outraged, at Graciella's new love interest challenges Ken to three duels.
- **S13**：Barbie, Raquelle and the fairies go to a clothing store, where they enter the Flyway , teleporting to the top of the Eiffel Tower in Paris.
- **S14**：The fairies reveal that Lilianna Roxelle, a fashion critic from the previous movie, is the oldest and wisest fairy living on Earth.
- **S15**：They arrive at Lilianna’s house, stating their problem.
- **S16**：Lilianna reveals that Graciella must be under a love potion, which adds red to the eyes.
- **S17**：She gets a vial of antidote for the love potion, gives it to Barbie, and lets them use her portal to Gloss Angeles.
- **S18**：They arrive in the fairy city, an island floating in the sky.
- **S19**：The fairies take them to Wings and Things, a store that sells clip-on wings for human use.
- **S20**：Barbie proves to be good at flying which sparks Raquelle's jealousy.
- **S21**：The four girls then set off to the palace, the highest point in the city.
- **S22**：Meanwhile in the palace, the first phase of the duel between Ken and Zane begins- Ken can’t cast any spells, but he cleverly avoids all of Zane's magic blasts.
- **S23**：Barbie and her friends continue flying up to the palace, and come across some pegaponies, colored, flying horses that fly them the rest of the way.
- **S24**：When they near the palace, the group encounters several swirlnadoes, tornadoes that will make even the best flyer lose their balance.
- **S25**：Barbie points out Raquelle's skill with horses, to the latter's surprise.
- **S26**：Raquelle successfully guides them through the swirlnadoes, and they sneak into the palace disguised as chefs Once inside the palace, the four locate Graciella, Ken and Zane.
- **S27**：Barbie flies in front of Ken to stop Zane from blasting him and Barbie attempts to give Graciella the love potion antidote, but the Princess freezes her, Raquelle, Ken, Carrie and Taylor.
- **S28**：Crystal reveals that Carrie and Taylor are in the palace, and Graciella  send the four to the dungeon by trapping them in Fury Spheres which is said to be escape proof.
- **S29**：The wedding begins, as Barbie and Raquelle sit down and talk, finally getting the bottom of their rivalry and misunderstanding.
- **S30**：They apologize to each other and promise to become friends.
- **S31**：Their forgiveness transforms the spheres into real wings for them, because forgiveness lets you fly.
- **S32**：The two fly up to the grand hall to rescue Ken with their new wings.
- **S33**：They stop the wedding just in time, revealing to the public that the Princess is under the influence of a love potion.
- **S34**：Raquelle distracts the assistant fairies while Graciella battles with Barbie.
- **S35**：Barbie finally releases the antidote over Graciella’s head, curing her of the love potion.
- **S36**：The Princess confronts Carrie and Taylor, who reveal that they spend time without her cause she so was busy with royal duties.
- **S37**：The three forgive each other and become friends again.
- **S38**：Crystal reveals that she made Graciella fall in love with Ken so she could get Zane's attention.
- **S39**：Graciella, confiding to Crystal that the ordeal was all her fault, sentences her to clean the entire palace after the wedding reception, stating that she and Zane were to get married.
- **S40**：Graciella tells Barbie and Raquelle that their wings will disappear when they go back to the human world, and then reappear when they return to Gloss Angeles.
- **S41**：But Raquelle did not promise Graciella that she'll keep Gloss Angeles and faries existence a secret.
- **S42**：Thus, the fairies blow a magic sleeping dust on them.
- **S43**：The next morning, Barbie wakes up with no recollection of Gloss Angeles or the fairies, thinking it was a dream.
- **S44**：However, she and Raquelle are now friends.
- **S45**：Carrie and Taylor tell the two that they reconnected with an old friend and are going back to their hometown Gloss Angeles.

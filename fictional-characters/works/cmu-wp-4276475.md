## 角色资料

### Batman

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8a9e8e464a744c51` · 独立版本：`profile-474e9c4224f55b0b`

- **性格倾向**：在维护城市希望时愿承担个人恶名，表现出承受代价的倾向。（行为推断；S36、S37）
- **核心动机**：与戈登及丹特合作打击黑帮，事后又设法保住丹特的希望象征。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S36）
- **决策方式**：追捕小丑时倾向借助大范围追踪技术，即使合作者对此抗拒。（行为推断；S26）
- **价值取舍**：在丹特死后把城市的希望置于个人名誉之上，选择承担罪名。（行为推断；S36）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Joker

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-85a1f29b76ac9150` · 独立版本：`profile-96fe18917a0d0030`

- **性格倾向**：在制造城市危机时倾向操纵恐惧，把他人逼入极端选择。（行为推断；S9、S22、S25）
- **核心动机**：把丹特堕落后可能引发的公众绝望视为自己的胜利。（摘要直接叙述；S29）
- **决策方式**：倾向设置限时死亡威胁，把伤害他人的压力转交给被胁迫者。（行为推断；S22、S25）
- **关系模式**：在丹特失去瑞秋并受伤住院后前去探访，劝动他实施复仇。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S23）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：试图逼渡轮乘客互相杀害，乘客却拒绝这样做。

### Harvey Dent

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-650ab30ad9c9d6e5` · 独立版本：`profile-622c3d2c5f8a0621`

- **性格倾向**：在保护蝙蝠侠身份时显出敢于担险的一面，主动把危险引向自己。（行为推断；S13、S14）
- **核心动机**：瑞秋死后被小丑劝动复仇，转而追杀与她遇害有关的人。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S23、S24）
- **决策方式**：复仇时倾向用掷硬币分配生死，把自身与他人都纳入裁决。（行为推断；S24、S32、S33）
- **价值取舍**：后期把报复置于他人安全之上，甚至将戈登的儿子作为目标。（行为推断；S31、S32、S33）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾冒险保护蝙蝠侠身份，后来却向他开枪。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Gotham City, the Joker and his accomplices rob a mob-owned bank.
- **S2**：After orchestrating their deaths, he escapes alone.
- **S3**：Batman and Lieutenant Jim Gordon decide to include the new district attorney, Harvey Dent, who is dating Rachel Dawes, in their plan to eradicate the mob.
- **S4**：Impressed with Dent's idealism, Bruce Wayne offers him a fundraiser.
- **S5**：Mob bosses Sal Maroni, Gambol, and The Chechen are informed by Lau, a Chinese accountant, that he has hidden their funds and fled to Hong Kong to escape the new pressure.
- **S6**：The Joker interrupts the meeting, warning that Batman is unhindered by jurisdiction.
- **S7**：He offers to kill Batman for half their money, but the mob bosses refuse and Gambol puts a bounty on him.
- **S8**：The Joker later kills Gambol and takes control of his men, while Batman captures Lau using a skyhook, delivering him back to Gotham to testify.
- **S9**：The Joker issues an ultimatum that people will die each day unless Batman reveals his identity, resulting in the deaths of Commissioner Gillian B.
- **S10**：Loeb and the judge presiding over the mob trials.
- **S11**：The Joker targets Dent at the fundraiser but Bruce hides him, while Rachel is looked after by Alfred Pennyworth.
- **S12**：Gordon foils the Joker's assassination attempt on Mayor Garcia during Loeb's memorial service, apparently sacrificing himself in the process.
- **S13**：As a result, Bruce plans to reveal his identity, but Dent instead names himself as Batman to protect the truth.
- **S14**：Dent is taken into protective custody and pursued by the Joker across the city as Batman rushes to aid.
- **S15**：Gordon, who faked his death, helps apprehend the Joker and is promoted to Commissioner.
- **S16**：However, Dent and Rachel disappear.
- **S17**：Batman interrogates the Joker and learns each are in separate buildings filled with explosives.
- **S18**：Batman intends to find Rachel, instead finding Dent moments before the buildings explode.
- **S19**：Rachel dies and half of Dent's face is scarred.
- **S20**：The Joker then uses a smuggled bomb inside a prisoner to escape from the police department with Lau.
- **S21**：Coleman Reese, an accountant at Wayne Enterprises, deduces Batman's secret identity and plans to blackmail him.
- **S22**：The Joker kills Lau and The Chechen, threatening to bomb a hospital unless Reese is killed.
- **S23**：Gordon rescues Reese, while the Joker visits Dent in the hospital, convincing him to get revenge.
- **S24**：Dent uses his burned lucky coin to decide the fates of those responsible for Rachel's death, killing Maroni and one corrupt cop who had helped kidnap Rachel.
- **S25**：After blowing up the hospital and escaping with hostages, the Joker gives two explosive-rigged ferries, one of citizens and the other of Arkham Asylum inmates, the choice to blow the other up until midnight—otherwise, both will explode.
- **S26**：Batman asks a reluctant Lucius Fox to use a city-wide tracking device to find the Joker; Fox agrees, but says he will resign immediately afterward.
- **S27**：Batman rescues the hostages, who are disguised as Joker's gang, by fighting off the Joker's real men and Gordon's SWAT team.
- **S28**：The ferry passengers, refusing to kill each other, are saved as Batman apprehends the Joker.
- **S29**：Nevertheless, the Joker gloats that he has won, as the citizens of Gotham will lose hope once Dent's rampage becomes public knowledge.
- **S30**：Batman leaves to find Dent as the Joker is taken into custody.
- **S31**：Dent lures Gordon to the building where Rachel died and holds his family hostage, as Batman confronts him.
- **S32**：Dent judges the fates of Batman, himself, and Gordon's son with three coin flips.
- **S33**：As a result, he shoots Batman in the abdomen, spares himself and flips to determine the boy's fate.
- **S34**：Batman, who is wearing body armor, tackles Dent off the building, saving the boy but resulting in Dent's death.{{cite web}}.

- **S36**：Batman convinces Gordon to hold him publicly responsible for the murders so that Dent will remain a symbol of hope for the city.
- **S37**：A manhunt for Batman ensues, as he escapes on the Batpod.
- **S38**：Alfred burns a letter written by Rachel to Bruce announcing her engagement to Dent, Fox watches the signal tracker self-destruct and Gordon destroys the Bat-Signal before delivering a eulogy at Dent's funeral.

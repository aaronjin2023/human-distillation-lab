## 角色资料

### Nick Gant

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-0fa515705f97413c` · 独立版本：`profile-e28f760fa9741255`

- **性格倾向**：在遭组织追捕的情境中，表现出善用布局与协作求生的倾向（行为推断；S17、S18、S39）
- **核心动机**：参与对抗Division的布局，希望打破追捕者对众人的控制（行为推断；S16、S17、S18）
- **决策方式**：把计划拆成分发的指令，并主动放弃记忆来实施布局（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18、S22、S23）
- **关系模式**：面对Cassie的担忧主动安慰，表现出维系伙伴信任的倾向（行为推断；S26）
- **价值取舍**：为团队计划接受暂时失去掌控的风险，依靠伙伴执行后续步骤（行为推断；S17、S18、S39）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他必须让计划奏效，却主动抹去自己掌握计划的记忆。

### Kira Hudson

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-8a6d17f98c1f8b60` · 独立版本：`profile-a221eeda06b41132`

- **性格倾向**：在受到机构控制的处境中，呈现寻找机会并主动反制的倾向（行为推断；S7、S42、S43、S44）
- **核心动机**：最初通过夺取通行卡与药剂，争取摆脱实验机构的控制（行为推断；S6、S7）
- **决策方式**：身份说法受到动摇时，借助预留信件与照片重新判断局势（行为推断；S41、S42、S43、S44）
- **关系模式**：她与Nick的亲密关系遭到外力否定，后来获得照片支持（摘要直接叙述；S13、S30、S43）
- **成长变化**：最终从接受Carver的身份解释转向对他施加致命命令（行为推断；S24、S25、S31、S44、S45）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：Carver提供的身份解释与照片所支持的亲密经历发生冲突。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：People with psychic abilities have been involved with the United States government since 1945.
- **S2**：Two "Movers", Nick and his father, are running from the "Division".
- **S3**：Nick's father tells him of a vision he received from a "Watcher"; a girl will give him a flower and he must help her in order to help all the people with powers.
- **S4**：Nick's father hides him as Agent Carver of the Division arrives.
- **S5**：Nick's father is killed.
- **S6**：Ten years later, the American Division tests a power boosting drug on a "Pusher"  named Kira.
- **S7**：Rendering the doctor unconscious, Kira steals his security clearance card and an augmentation drug-filled syringe before escaping.
- **S8**：In Hong Kong, Nick is hiding as an expatriate.
- **S9**：A young girl, Cassie, arrives at Nick's apartment, explaining that she is a Watcher and that they are going to find a case containing six million dollars.
- **S10**：Following Cassie's predictions, they go to a nightclub.
- **S11**：Nick sees an old friend, Hook, who is a "Shifter".
- **S12**：He tells them to go to Emily, a "Sniff" who can help them find Kira.
- **S13**：Nick and Cassie find Kira, who had a romantic relationship with Nick.
- **S14**：They recruit a "Shadow" named Pinky to hide Kira from the Sniffs.
- **S15**：Cassie finds a key in her shoe to a locker in which Kira hid a case.
- **S16**：With the aid of Cassie's visions, they piece together the events that led them to meet; Cassie's mother used her visions to set a complex plan in motion that will destroy the Division.
- **S17**：Nick devises a plan that involves seven envelopes in which he places instructions; each person in the group is entrusted with one envelope.
- **S18**：Nick uses a "Wiper" to erase his memories of the plan.
- **S19**：Hook retrieves the case, which has the syringe Kira stole, and brings it to Cassie.
- **S20**：Hook shifts another case to match the case with the syringe.
- **S21**：Cassie takes the shifted case to Nick's apartment and waits.
- **S22**：Nick regains consciousness: he has no memory of the envelopes or his plan.
- **S23**：He opens his envelope, which tells him to return home.
- **S24**：He finds the case in his room but Carver introduces himself to Kira as a friend, stating that her memories are false; she is a Division agent who volunteered to take the augmentation injection and suffered amnesia.
- **S25**：Carver shows Kira her badge.
- **S26**：Before saying goodbye, Nick assures a worried Cassie that she will survive and everything is going to be fine.
- **S27**：She leaves as the Hong Kong watcher tracks her to finally kill her.
- **S28**：At the last moment, the Wiper appears and erases her memory, saving Cassie.
- **S29**：Nick goes to retrieve the augmentation drug and confronts Carver and Kira.
- **S30**：Carver tells Kira and Nick that their relationship never happened; it was a "push" memory.
- **S31**：Kira reveals she has been using Nick and Nick takes the three to the building that contains the lockers and the case.
- **S32**：Carver locks Nick in his car's trunk and goes to retrieve the case.
- **S33**：They are ambushed by the Triads.
- **S34**：In the midst of the fight, Nick is released.
- **S35**：He goes to find Kira and is confronted by Victor, another Mover and a Division agent who works for Carver.
- **S36**：During the fight between Nick and Victor, two of the Bleeders kill Victor.
- **S37**：Nick grabs the case and jams the syringe into his arm, apparently killing himself.
- **S38**：After the fight ends and Carver leaves with Kira, Nick wakes up.
- **S39**：Cassie appears, and retrieves the true case, revealing that Nick injected himself with soy sauce, as they planned.
- **S40**：Asked whether they will see Kira again, Cassie tells Nick that they will see "Miss Trouble soon enough".
- **S41**：Flying back to America with a sleeping Carver, Kira opens her purse and finds her envelope.
- **S42**：She remembers Nick telling her to open it when "she started doubting the truth" and opens it.
- **S43**：She finds a photograph of herself and Nick in a relationship, and a message written on the photograph that says "KILL HIM" on the upper left corner and "See U soon, Nick" on the lower right.
- **S44**：Kira "pushes" Carver, commanding him to put his gun in his mouth and pull the trigger.
- **S45**：The screen fades to black, followed by the sound of a gunshot.

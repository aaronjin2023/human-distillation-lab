### Hero Boy

- **性格倾向**：面对圣诞传说倾向求证，起初尝试从杂志和百科寻找依据（行为推断；S1、S2）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：On Christmas Eve, in the 1950s, a young boy who lives in Grand Rapids, Michigan is hoping for belief in the true spirit of Christmas.
- **S2**：He looks through magazines and encyclopedias for confirmation of Santa Claus and the North Pole, but to no avail.
- **S3**：About an hour later, the boy goes outside to find a magical train called the Polar Express.
- **S4**：The conductor tells him that the train is headed to the North Pole to go to Santa, and that this year is the year that he should board the train.
- **S5**：The train route goes north, first through boreal pine forest, then across tundra, then across the frozen Arctic Ocean, to Polar City on an island, everywhere snowbound.
- **S6**：In the tundra, the train had a difficult crossing of an area where flood submerged the track and then froze.
- **S7**：On the train, the boy encounters a group of other children who are on their way to see Santa Claus, including a young girl, a know-it-all, and a lonely little boy also from Grand Rapids whose name is Billy.
- **S8**：When the conductor asks for tickets from everyone so he can punch them, the boy discovers his ticket is miraculously in his left pocket.
- **S9**：The conductor punches two letters into each child's ticket .
- **S10**：However, he forgets to punch the girl's ticket.
- **S11**：The protagonist finds the girl's ticket lying on her seat and, realizing that it wasn't punched, tries to give it to her.
- **S12**：However, he loses the ticket in the wind, but unknown to him, the ticket miraculously ends up in a vent in the same car that the boy was in.
- **S13**：However, the boy does not realize this, and he tells the conductor that he lost the girls ticket.
- **S14**：The conductor then takes the girl up to the roof, and the know-it-all says that she will be thrown off the train.
- **S15**：The boy suddenly sees the ticket in the vent, and grabs it just before it slips away.
- **S16**：When the boy then climbs on the roof to try and stop the conductor, he meets a hobo  on the roof, who helps him to get towards the conductor and the girl by skiing as the train goes downhill.
- **S17**：The hobo warns the boy that Flattop Tunnel is approaching, and that it only has 1&nbsp;inch of clearance between the roof of the train and the tunnel itself.
- **S18**：They do make it in time, and the boy jumps and lands in the tender.
- **S19**：The hobo had disappeared after the boy had jumped.
- **S20**：The boy finds the girl driving the train.
- **S21**：She explains that the two people who are supposed to be controlling the train, Smokey the engineer and Steamer the fireman, are trying to fix the light on the train and the conductor let her control.
- **S22**：The boy later safely hands the girl her ticket for the conductor to punch.
- **S23**：They soon reach the North Pole and find out that Billy is riding alone in the observation car, because he does not want to see Santa, as he comes from a broken home on the bad side of his hometown due to a dissolved marriage from his parents' cultural differences.
- **S24**：He says that Christmas never turns out well for him.
- **S25**：The boy and girl run back to try to get him to come along with them, but the protagonist accidentally steps on the uncoupling lever and the observation car speeds backwards.
- **S26**：The three of them travel from section to section of the North Pole's industrial area with guidance from the girl, who claims she hears the sound of bells that will show them the way if they follow the sound.
- **S27**：They first visit the Control Center, then the Wrapping Hall, and finally a warehouse, before they are airlifted back to the center of the city via airship.
- **S28**：As the sleigh is being prepared, one of the bells fall off.
- **S29**：The boy picks it up and shakes it, remembering that the girl and Billy could hear a bell earlier when he could not.
- **S30**：As before, he cannot hear it.
- **S31**：The boy then says he believes in Santa and the spirit of Christmas.
- **S32**：The boy is handpicked by Santa Claus to receive "The First Gift Of Christmas." Realizing that he could choose anything in the world, the boy asks for the beautiful-sounding silver bell  that had fallen from Santa's sleigh.
- **S33**：The boy places the bell in the right pocket of his robe, and all the children watch as Santa takes off for his yearly deliveries.
- **S34**：As the children leave the north pole, the protagonist discovers one of the pockets of his robe is torn and the bell is missing.
- **S35**：His friends suggest they go back outside to find it, but it is too late.
- **S36**：He is saddened by the loss of his bell, but is happy when he sees Billy holding up his present at his doorway, indicating that Santa had already visited him.
- **S37**：On Christmas morning, his sister Sarah finds a small present hidden behind the tree after all the others have been unwrapped.
- **S38**：The Hero Boy opens the present and discovers that it is the bell, which Santa had found on the seat of his sleigh.
- **S39**：When the Hero Boy rings the bell, both he and Sarah marvel at the beautiful sound; but because their parents neither believe in Santa Claus nor Christmas, they do not hear it and remark it to be broken.
- **S40**：The last line in the movie repeats the same last line from the book: "At one time, most of my friends could hear the bell, but as years passed, it fell silent for all of them.
- **S41**：Even Sarah found one Christmas that she could no longer hear its sweet sound.
- **S42**：Though I've grown old, the bell still rings for me, as it does for all who truly believe."

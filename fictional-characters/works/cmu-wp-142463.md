### Guido Orefice

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-190d7ecdb323655b` · 独立版本：`profile-942c3fd933b529fd`

- **性格倾向**：在求爱与绝境中，他倾向用想象力和幽默主动改变局面（行为推断；S3、S10、S17）
- **核心动机**：身陷集中营后，他持续以保护儿子的安全与童心为目标（行为推断；S10、S11、S12、S15）
- **价值取舍**：即使自身面临死亡，他仍将家人的安危置于个人处境之上（行为推断；S15、S16、S17）
- **沟通方式**：他把恐怖现实转译成孩子能理解的游戏规则与奖励（摘要直接叙述；S10、S11、S12、S13）
- **决策方式**：他依据儿子的认知设计隐蔽策略，并坚持维持解释的一致（行为推断；S11、S13、S15、S17）

## 材料限制

- Giosuè的内心主要通过父亲编织的游戏及成年回忆呈现；对其信赖的概括仅适用于片中幼年处境。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1930s Italy, Guido Orefice  is a funny and charismatic young man looking for work in a city.
- **S2**：He falls in love with a local school teacher, Dora , who is to be engaged to a rich but arrogant civil servant.
- **S3**：Guido engineers further meetings with her, seizing on coincidental incidents to declare his affection for her, and finally wins her over.
- **S4**：He steals her from her engagement party on a horse, humiliating her fiance and mother.
- **S5**：Soon they are married and have a son, Giosuè .
- **S6**：Through the first part, the movie depicts the changing political climate in Italy: Guido frequently imitates Nazi party members, skewering their racist logic and pseudoscientific reasoning .
- **S7**：However, the growing racist wave is also evident: the horse Guido steals Dora away on has been painted green and covered in antisemitic insults.
- **S8**：In 1945, after Dora and her mother  are reconciled, Guido, his Uncle Eliseo and Giosuè are seized on Giosuè's birthday, forced onto a train and taken to a concentration camp.
- **S9**：Despite being a non-Jew, Dora demands to be on the same train to join her family.
- **S10**：In the camp, Guido hides their true situation from his son, convincing him that the camp is a complicated game in which Giosuè must perform the tasks Guido gives him, earning him points; the first team to reach one thousand points will win a tank.
- **S11**：He tells him that if he cries, complains that he wants his mother, or says that he is hungry, he will lose points, while quiet boys who hide from the camp guards earn extra points.
- **S12**：Guido uses this game to explain features of the concentration camp that would otherwise be scary for a young child: the guards are mean only because they want the tank for themselves; the dwindling numbers of children  are only hiding in order to score more points than Giosuè so they can win the game.
- **S13**：He puts off Giosuè's requests to end the game and return home by convincing him that they are in the lead for the tank, and need only wait a short while before they can return home with their tank.
- **S14**：Despite being surrounded by the misery, sickness and death at the camp, Giosuè does not question this fiction because of his father's convincing performance and his own innocence.
- **S15**：Guido maintains this story right until the end when, in the chaos of shutting down the camp as the Americans approach, he tells his son to stay in a sweatbox until everybody has left, this being the final competition before the tank is his.
- **S16**：Guido tries to find Dora, but is caught and executed by a Nazi soldier.
- **S17**：As he is marched off to be executed, he maintains the fiction of the game by deliberately marching in an exaggerated goose-step.
- **S18**：The next morning, Giosuè emerges from the sweatbox as the camp is occupied by an American armored division.
- **S19**：They let him ride in the tank until, later than day, he sees Dora in the crowd of people streaming out of the camp.
- **S20**：In the film, Giosuè is four and a half years old; however, both the beginning and ending of the film are narrated by an older Giosuè recalling his father's story of sacrifice for his family.

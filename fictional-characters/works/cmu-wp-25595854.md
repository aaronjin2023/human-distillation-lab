### Blu

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-2de12ca354b4aa29` · 独立版本：`profile-0122ab07c4da7e74`

- **性格倾向**：面对受困伙伴的危险，他表现出能突破自身局限的勇敢倾向（行为推断；S19、S22、S24）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Brazil, various exotic birds are smuggled out of the country.
- **S2**：In Moose Lake, Minnesota, a crate with a male blue macaw hatchling falls out of a truck and is found by Linda Gunderson, who names him Blu.
- **S3**：Fifteen years later, Blu is unable to fly and is ridiculed by the Canada Geese that come by the outside of Linda's bookstore.
- **S4**：One day, ornithologist Túlio Monteiro invites Blu and Linda to Rio de Janeiro on the condition that Blu, who is the last male of his species, mates with a female macaw.
- **S5**：Linda accepts and they fly to Rio, where Blu meets a Red-crested Cardinal named Pedro and his Yellow Canary friend Nico.
- **S6**：At Túlio's aviary, Blu falls in love with Jewel, a fiercely independent blue macaw longing to flee into the wilderness.
- **S7**：The macaws are captured by Fernando, an impoverished orphan boy, and a Sulphur-crested Cockatoo named Nigel, both of whom work for a group of smugglers led by Marcel.
- **S8**：Nigel tells the macaws that he vowed to smuggle exotic birds after his role had been replaced on a television program.
- **S9**：Blu and Jewel are able to escape and flee into a jungle.
- **S10**：Fernando meets Linda and Túlio and tells them that Marcel forced him to capture birds, before cooperating with the two to find Blu.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, Blu and Jewel meet a Toco toucan named Rafael, who offers to take them to his bulldog friend Luiz to remove their leg chain.
- **S12**：He tries to teach Blu how to fly, before they meet up with Pedro and Nico.
- **S13**：Nigel hires a horde of thieving marmosets to capture Blu and Jewel.
- **S14**：Pedro and Nico take Blu and Jewel to a bird's Rio-style party, where they perform a duet, but are encountered by the marmosets.
- **S15**：Their bird friends fight them while Blu's group escapes.
- **S16**：Fernando takes Linda and Túlio to the smugglers hideout, where they discover that Blu has escaped.
- **S17**：Marcel explains that he will use the Rio Carnival parade to capture Blu and Jewel.
- **S18**：Meanwhile, Blu and the others meet Luiz, who releases the chain holding Blu and Jewel; and the macaws decide to go their separate ways after a heated argument.
- **S19**：When Blu and Rafael learn from Pedro and Nico that Nigel captured Jewel, they rush to the carnival to rescue her, while Linda and Túlio organize a rescue attempt for the birds.
- **S20**：As Linda and Túlio pose as dancers in blue macaw costumes, Marcel uses Nigel to capture the birds.
- **S21**：On board Marcel's Short SC.7 Skyvan, the macaws release the captive birds, but Nigel injures Jewel.
- **S22**：Blu fights with Nigel, and the smugglers flee the plane.
- **S23**：Unable to fly, Jewel falls out of the plane towards the ocean.
- **S24**：Jumping out of the plane to rescue Jewel, Blu finally discovers that he is able to fly.
- **S25**：Later, Linda and Túlio adopt Fernando, heal Jewel, and organize a sanctuary to protect the jungle from smugglers.
- **S26**：Blu and Jewel raise three baby birds together and celebrate with their bird friends, and the smugglers are sent to jail.

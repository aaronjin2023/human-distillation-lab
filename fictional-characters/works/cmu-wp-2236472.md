## 角色资料

### Sam Witwicky

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-835df1e3086eaa0f` · 独立版本：`profile-557ee7f1e2723b01`

- **性格倾向**：在遭到机器人追击时，他仍能与伙伴协作，并在决战中主动应变。（行为推断；S15、S30）
- **关系模式**：他与米凯拉共同卷入机器人冲突、协力抗敌，后来建立恋爱关系。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15、S32）
- **决策方式**：面对擎天柱提出的自毁方案，他另择威震天作为立方体的目标。（行为推断；S30）
- **价值取舍**：在决战中他选择消灭威震天，保留了擎天柱并毁掉火种源。（行为推断；S30）
- **成长变化**：他从购车时意外接触汽车人，转为在决战中决定火种源去向。（行为推断；S10、S11、S30）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式

### Optimus Prime

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4f16c27750dd5d11` · 独立版本：`profile-802c59fb8593f9f2`

- **性格倾向**：在人类卷入冲突后，他倾向主动施救，并在决战中承担自毁风险。（行为推断；S22、S30）
- **核心动机**：他所属的汽车人希望找到火种源，以重建赛博坦并结束战争。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S3）
- **决策方式**：他主张先于霸天虎找到火种源，并要求山姆将其置入自己胸口。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S30）
- **价值取舍**：面对敌人争夺火种源的危机，他愿牺牲自身来使其毁灭。（行为推断；S19、S30）
- **成长变化**：他将地球视为汽车人的新家，并召唤幸存同伴前来加入。（摘要直接叙述；S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

### Megatron

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-5401c24dde5be207` · 独立版本：`profile-f7ef3901f7cf6e78`

- **性格倾向**：在火种源争夺中，他显出侵略与支配倾向，获释后继续追击。（行为推断；S2、S3、S26、S29）
- **核心动机**：他企图借火种源击败汽车人，并把宇宙纳入霸天虎统治。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S3）
- **决策方式**：解冻后他加入同伙追击山姆与汽车人，随后参与城市战斗。（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27、S29）
- **价值取舍**：作为霸天虎领袖，他的征服目标与阵营计划显示漠视人类存亡。（行为推断；S2、S3、S19）
- **关系模式**：他与擎天柱所率汽车人处于战争敌对关系，并亲手杀死爵士。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Optimus Prime, leader of the benevolent Autobots, narrates the collapse of the Transformers' home world, Cybertron.
- **S2**：It was destroyed by war between the Autobots and the malevolent Decepticons, lead by Megatron in his quest to get hold of the AllSpark.
- **S3**：The Autobots want to find the AllSpark so they can use it to rebuild Cybertron and end the war, while the Decepticons want to use it to defeat the Autobots and take over the universe.
- **S4**：Megatron had managed to locate the AllSpark on Earth, but crash-landed in the Arctic Circle and froze in the ice.
- **S5**：After stumbling upon his frozen body in 1897, explorer Captain Archibald Witwicky accidentally activated Megatron's navigational system and his eye glasses were imprinted with the coordinates of the AllSpark's location, an incident that left him blind and mentally unstable.
- **S6**：Sector 7, a secret government organization created by President Herbert Hoover, discovered the AllSpark in the Colorado River and built the Hoover Dam around it to mask its energy emissions.
- **S7**：The still-frozen Megatron was moved into this facility and was used to advance human technology through reverse engineering.
- **S8**：In the present day, the Decepticon known as Blackout arrives in a U.S. military base in Qatar to find the location of Megatron and the AllSpark.
- **S9**：He tries to hack into the files of the computer base, but is stopped by Captain William Lennox and his team.
- **S10**：Back in the United States, Captain Witwicky's descendant Sam Witwicky buys his first car which turns out to be the Autobot scout Bumblebee, who tries to help him woo his crush Mikaela Banes.
- **S11**：Later, Sam catches a glimpse of Bumblebee's true form when he signals the other Autobots.
- **S12**：On Air Force One, another Decepticon named Frenzy infiltrates the plane and tries to hack into the network again, only this time is more successful until he is stopped by the US Security Defense before he can retrieve all of the file information.
- **S13**：Frenzy is then picked up by his partner Barricade and they go after Sam after learning he has the glasses needed to find the AllSpark.
- **S14**：Sam is rescued by Bumblebee and Mikaela also learns of the Transformers' existence.
- **S15**：Bumblebee fights Barricade and manages to subdue him while Sam and Mikaela decapitate Frenzy, but he still survives.
- **S16**：Meanwhile, Scorponok, who was sent by Blackout, goes after Captain Lennox and his team, murdering one of them and injuring another.
- **S17**：During the battle, Scorponok is forced to retreat when he gets injured by sabot rounds dropped on him by the Air Force.
- **S18**：Sam and Mikaela soon meet Optimus Prime and his other Autobot partners Jazz, Ironhide, and Ratchet.
- **S19**：They explain their origins to the two humans and insist on the urgency to get to the Allspark first before the Decepticons, knowing that the Decepticons plan to use it to turn all of Earth's technologies into a new army of Transformers and render humanity extinct.
- **S20**：The Autobots bring the two humans back to Sam's house to find the glasses, and they nearly reveal their existence to Sam's parents.
- **S21**：However, Sector 7 agent Seymour Simmons and his team find Sam and take his family away to a classified location after learning Sam came into contact with the Autobots.
- **S22**：However, Sam and Mikaela are rescued by Optimus and the Autobots, but Bumblebee ends up getting captured.
- **S23**：The Autobots get the glasses and use them to find the AllSpark's location so they can destroy it so the Decepticons cannot get to it.
- **S24**：Sam and Mikaela along with two hackers named Maggie and Glenn arrive at Hoover Dam, where Maggie and Glenn got arrested by the FBI for trying to decipher the information Frenzy stole.
- **S25**：Frenzy finds the AllSpark and gets his body back, then contacts the other Decepticons, Starscream, Bonecrusher, Brawl, Barricade, and Blackout.
- **S26**：Starscream attacks the dam and Frenzy frees Megatron from his frozen prison, where he joins his cohorts into chasing down Sam and the Autobots, where Bumblebee has shrunk the cube to a reasonable size.
- **S27**：They then get to Downtown Los Angeles, where a large battle ensues.
- **S28**：Working together, the Autobots and human soldiers kill Bonecrusher, Blackout and Brawl.
- **S29**：However the war causes Bumblebee to get crippled, and Jazz is killed by Megatron.
- **S30**：Optimus urges Sam to put the AllSpark in his chest, which will destroy them both, but Sam instead inserts the cube into Megatron's chest, which kills him and destroys the AllSpark.
- **S31**：The dead Transformer bodies are dumped into the Laurentian Abyss in the Atlantic Ocean to be hidden, the government orders the closure of Sector 7, and the Witwicky family are released from custody.
- **S32**：Sam and Mikaela soon start a new relationship, and Optimus says that the Autobots' fates have given them a new home, Earth, and sends a message calling out all surviving Autobots to join them.
- **S33**：In a mid-credits scene, Starscream escapes into space.

### Susan Murphy

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8e9ff25b25c9fcee` · 独立版本：`profile-ebe267a891a53148`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴遇险时倾向迎难而上，不轻易接受独自脱身（行为推断；S22、S23、S24）
- **核心动机**：在飞船危机中以救出受困伙伴作为行动的首要目标（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **价值取舍**：在营救伙伴时愿重新变成巨人，将同伴安全置于维持常人体型之上（行为推断；S18、S24）
- **决策方式**：发现胁迫敌人无效后，转而恢复力量亲自解决危机（行为推断；S24）
- **成长变化**：从因婚约破裂而受挫，转向接纳巨人身份并拒绝旧爱（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16、S27）

### Gallaxhar

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-3500ffa3c798838c` · 独立版本：`profile-7c6e74d0296bc998`

- **性格倾向**：在争夺能量时倾向以诱骗和控制他人达成目的（行为推断；S17、S18）
- **核心动机**：谋求取得量子能量，制造克隆军队入侵地球（摘要直接叙述；S8、S19）
- **价值取舍**：在获取力量时将他人身体视为可以强行利用的资源（行为推断；S17、S18、S19）
- **决策方式**：倾向借助机器人与能量设备扩张自身行动能力（行为推断；S8、S18、S19）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

- **S3**：Alerted to the meteorite, first by an Arctic base and then the priest at Susan's wedding, the military arrives and captures Susan.

- **S7**：Cockroach, PhD , a mad scientist with the head and abilities of a cockroach; the Missing Link , an amphibious fish-ape hybrid; and Insectosaurus, a massive grub that is even larger than Susan.
- **S8**：An alien named Gallaxhar  detects quantonium radiation on Earth and deploys a gigantic robot probe to find it.
- **S9**：After the robot lands the President of the United States  attempts to make first contact with it by playing Axel F on a keyboard, but the attempt fails and the impervious robot begins destroying everything in sight.
- **S10**：General Monger convinces the President to use his monsters to fight the robot instead.
- **S11**：The monsters accept the mission when they are promised their freedom if they succeed.
- **S12**：Arriving in San Francisco, Susan is chased by the robot over the Golden Gate Bridge, where the monsters defeat it.
- **S13**：Now free, Susan returns home and introduces her family to the monsters.
- **S14**：They are quickly rejected, though, after innocently causing a neighborhood panic.
- **S15**：Derek breaks up with Susan, claiming that he cannot be married to a freak since she would overshadow his career.
- **S16**：At first devastated, Susan realizes that becoming a monster is an improvement on her life, and fully embraces her new role.
- **S17**：Suddenly, she is abducted by Gallaxhar, who appears to kill Insectosaurus in the process.
- **S18**：On Gallaxhar's spaceship, Susan escapes and chases Gallaxhar down, only to be lured into a machine that extracts the quantonium from her body, allowing her to shrink back down to her normal size.
- **S19**：Gallaxhar then uses the extracted quantonium to power a machine that creates an army of his clones to invade the Earth.

- **S21**：Cockroach, and the Missing Link infiltrate Gallaxhar's spaceship, rescue Susan, and hot-wire the spaceship's power core, activating the self-destruct sequence.
- **S22**：During their escape, Susan is cut off from her friends who are trapped in the power core.
- **S23**：They tell her to save herself, but Susan instead finds Gallaxhar, who is trying to escape with the quantonium.
- **S24**：She tries to force him into releasing her friends, but when he admits he cannot reverse the sequence, Susan instead takes the quantonium, restoring herself to giant size and saving her friends.

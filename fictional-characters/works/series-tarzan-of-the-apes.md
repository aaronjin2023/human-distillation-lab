- **S19**：As he grows up, Tarzan becomes a skilled hunter, exciting the jealousy of Kerchak, who finally attacks him.
- **S20**：Tarzan kills Kerchak and takes his place as "king" of the apes.
- **S21**：Later, a tribe of black Africans settle in the area, and Kala is killed by one of its hunters.
- **S22**：Avenging himself on the killer, Tarzan begins an antagonistic relationship with the tribe, raiding its village for weapons and practicing cruel pranks on them.

- **S32**：Tarzan learns the bitter news that she has become engaged to another man.
- **S33**：Meanwhile, clues from his parents' cabin have enabled D'Arnot to prove Tarzan's true identity as John Clayton II, the Earl of Greystoke.

## 角色资料

### Johnny English

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-77d33f5c316e880b` · 独立版本：`profile-352dfb2cd87951d4`

- **性格倾向**：在辨认威胁时倾向仓促确信自己的判断，容易误伤旁人（行为推断；S10、S39、S40）
- **核心动机**：把早先任务失败视为需要弥补的负担，并为此接受修行（摘要直接叙述；S1）
- **决策方式**：在内鬼调查中倾向信任熟悉同僚，忽视下属的不同判断（行为推断；S18、S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：在保护总理的危机中愿以自身安全承担药物控制风险（行为推断；S28、S30、S33、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：自信识敌却屡次误判，保护他人的行动也会伤及无辜。

### Simon Ambrose

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-7de75d9b39acb9e5` · 独立版本：`profile-84f463aac9630c13`

- **性格倾向**：在身份可能暴露时倾向准备灭口，再随对方知情程度调整行动（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **核心动机**：以获得五亿美元报酬为目标，策划针对总理的暗杀（摘要直接叙述；S26）
- **决策方式**：倾向利用同僚的信任转移嫌疑，再借组织权力排除威胁（行为推断；S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：在谋利行动中将他人的生命与自主意志当作可消耗工具（行为推断；S25、S26、S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：同僚身份带来的信任，成为他陷害同僚的便利条件。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Eight years after the events of the first film, Sir Johnny English  is learning martial arts in Tibet as penance for an earlier failed mission in Mozambique.
- **S2**：However, he is contacted by MI7, requesting he returns to service.
- **S3**：Johnny returns to MI7's London headquarters  and assigned by new boss "Pegasus"  to stop a plot to assassinate the Chinese Premier during scheduled talks with the Prime Minister.
- **S4**：Johnny also meets with fellow agent Simon Ambrose  and MI7's resident inventor, Patch Quartermain .
- **S5**：He is also assigned a junior agent, Colin Tucker .
- **S6**：In Hong Kong, English discovers an address that leads him to ex-CIA agent Titus Fisher .
- **S7**：Fisher reveals that he is a member of a group of assassins called 'Vortex', who sabotaged English's mission in Mozambique.
- **S8**：Vortex holds a secret weapon, which requires three metal keys to unlock, and Fisher reveals one.
- **S9**：Fisher is killed by an assassin  dressed as a cleaner, and the key falls into Vortex's hands.
- **S10**：English is humiliated in front of the Foreign Secretary and Pegasus by the loss of the key, and assaults Pegasus's mother, mistaking her for the killer cleaner.
- **S11**：Kate Sumner , MI7's behavioural psychologist, prompts English to recall the events of his mission in Mozambique, and the identity of the second Vortex operative, Karlenko , a Russian spy.
- **S12**：A golf match between Karlenko and English leads to multiple attempts on English's life.
- **S13**：However, Karlenko is critically injured by the assassin, and English attempts to fly him to hospital.
- **S14**：Before they arrive, Karlenko reveals that Vortex's last agent is a member of MI7, and dies.
- **S15**：In a meeting at MI7, it is revealed that talks between Britain and China will continue in a heavily guarded Swiss fortress called 'Le Bastion'.
- **S16**：Over dinner, English informs Ambrose that he knows of a mole in MI7.
- **S17**：Ambrose prepares to kill English, until it becomes clear that the traitor's identity is unknown.
- **S18**：Tucker confronts Ambrose in the bathroom, knowing he is the mole, but English orders him to leave.
- **S19**：Ambrose convinces English that Quartermain is the traitor.
- **S20**：English entrusts the key to Ambrose, who tells Pegasus that English is the traitor.
- **S21**：English confronts Quartermain, and realises that he has been framed.
- **S22**：He escapes in a modified wheelchair.
- **S23**：English goes to Kate's house, and convinces her that he is not the traitor.
- **S24**：Kate, scrutinising footage of the Mozambique mission, realises that the assassin behaved abnormally.
- **S25**：They learn that Vortex owns a drug called timoxeline barbebutenol that allows them to control a person for a brief time before they die of heart failure.
- **S26**：Ambrose, the only surviving member of Vortex, plans to use the drug to kill the Premier in exchange for 500 million USD.
- **S27**：English confronts Tucker and convinces him to help break into 'Le Bastion'.
- **S28**：English warns Pegasus of the threat, and drinks the drug instead, rendering him vulnerable to Ambrose's commands.
- **S29**：Ambrose orders English to kill the Premier using a pistol disguised as a tube of lipstick.
- **S30**：English tries to resist the effects of the drug , and engages in a fight with himself while Tucker attempts to interrupt communication between Ambrose and English.
- **S31**：Tucker disrupts the frequency, replacing it with radio station, resulting in English beginning to dance.
- **S32**：Ambrose reasserts his command, revealing himself in the process.
- **S33**：English resists, shooting at Ambrose, who escapes.
- **S34**：The effects of the drug wear off and English seemingly dies of heart-failure, before being revived by Kate, who kisses him on the lips, making his heart rate boost.
- **S35**：English pursues Ambrose, parachuting from the building and hijacking a snow-mobile.
- **S36**：English reaches Ambrose, who is in a gondola lift.
- **S37**：The two fight, with English falling out of the car.
- **S38**：Ambrose tries to shoot English, who shoots a rocket at the cable-car, killing Ambrose.
- **S39**：English is knighted, but finds out that the killer cleaner was disguising herself as the Queen.
- **S40**：He chases and attacks her with a tray, before discovering he has targeted the real Queen.{{cite web}}{{cite web}}http://www.johnny-english.com/ A post-credits scene, influenced by the barber scene in The Great Dictator, sees English making a takeaway for Kate to the tune of "In the Hall of the Mountain King".
- **S41**：During the cooking scene, he parodies the Loituma Girl video by spinning some leeks.

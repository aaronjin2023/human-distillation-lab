## 角色资料

### Eddie Valiant

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-da9e94fc87fbfdc5` · 独立版本：`profile-0c24692c14b9fa29`

- **性格倾向**：面对自己排斥群体的求助，仍倾向采取实际保护行动（行为推断；S8、S15）
- **情绪反应**：兄弟泰迪被卡通人物杀害后，他仇视该群体并大量饮酒（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **决策方式**：遭遇力量不对等的围困时，倾向利用对手特性化解危机（行为推断；S24、S26）
- **成长变化**：通过保护罗杰与揭露阴谋，逐渐恢复对卡通群体的亲近（行为推断；S15、S24、S28）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：因兄弟遇害而仇视卡通人物，却选择庇护被追捕的罗杰。

### Judge Doom

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-1918096e76257c19` · 独立版本：`profile-2592e7bf82243547`

- **性格倾向**：在夺取卡通城的计划中，表现出冷酷而工具化他人的倾向（行为推断；S21、S23）
- **核心动机**：企图毁掉卡通城修筑公路，并通过汽车相关商业牟利（摘要直接叙述；S23）
- **决策方式**：谋取土地利益时，倾向结合秘密收购与摧毁替代交通（行为推断；S13、S23）
- **价值取舍**：将自身收益置于卡通居民生命与居住权之上（行为推断；S12、S23）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：自己也是卡通人物，却计划用致命溶剂毁灭卡通城。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1947, cartoon characters, commonly called "toons", are living beings who act out cartoons in the same way that human actors make live-action production.
- **S2**：Toons interact freely with humans and live in Toontown, an area near Hollywood, California.
- **S3**：R.
- **S4**：K.
- **S5**：Maroon is the human owner of Maroon Cartoon studios; Roger Rabbit is a fun-loving toon rabbit, one of Maroon's stars; Roger's wife Jessica is a gorgeous toon woman; and Baby Herman is Roger's costar, a 50-year-old toon who looks like an infant.
- **S6**：Marvin Acme is the practical joke-loving owner of Toontown and the Acme Corporation.
- **S7**：Maroon hires private detective Eddie Valiant to investigate rumors that Jessica is having an affair.
- **S8**：Eddie and his brother Teddy used to be friends of the toon community, but Eddie has hated them, and has been drinking heavily, since his brother Teddy was killed by a toon a few years earlier.
- **S9**：When he shows Roger photographs of Jessica "cheating" on him by playing patty-cake with Acme, Roger becomes distraught and runs away.
- **S10**：This makes him the main suspect when Acme is found murdered the next day.
- **S11**：At the crime scene, Eddie meets Judge Doom and his Toon Patrol of weasel henchmen.
- **S12**：Although toons are impervious to physical abuse, Doom has discovered that they can be killed by submerging them in a mixture of solvents he refers to as "Dip." Baby Herman insists that Acme's will, which is missing, bequeaths Toontown to the toons.
- **S13**：If the will is not found by midnight, Toontown will be sold to Cloverleaf Industries, which recently bought the Pacific Electric system of trolley cars.
- **S14**：One of Eddie's photos shows the will in Acme's pocket, proving Baby Herman's claim.
- **S15**：After Roger shows up at his office professing his innocence, Eddie investigates the case with help from his girlfriend Dolores while hiding Roger from the Toon Patrol.
- **S16**：Jessica tells Eddie that Maroon blackmailed her into compromising Acme, and Eddie learns that Maroon is selling his studio to Cloverleaf.
- **S17**：Maroon explains to Eddie that Cloverleaf will not buy his studio unless they can also buy Acme's gag-making factory.
- **S18**：His plan was to use the photos to blackmail Acme into selling.
- **S19**：Before he can say more, he is killed by an unseen assassin and Eddie sees Jessica fleeing the scene.
- **S20**：Thinking that she is the killer, Eddie pursues her into Toontown.
- **S21**：When he finds her, she explains that Doom killed Maroon and Acme in an attempt to take over Toontown.
- **S22**：Eddie, Jessica, and Roger are captured by Doom and his weasels and held at the Acme Factory, where Doom reveals his plan.
- **S23**：Since he owns Cloverleaf and Acme's will has yet to turn up, he will take control of Toontown and destroy it with a mobile Dip-sprayer to make room for a freeway, then force people to use it by dismantling the trolley fleet and make a fortune through a series of businesses built to appeal to the motorists.
- **S24**：With Roger and Jessica tied up, Eddie performs a vaudeville act that makes the weasels literally die of laughter and confronts Doom.
- **S25**：Doom survives being run over by a steamroller, revealing that he himself is a toon and admitting that he killed Teddy.
- **S26**：Eddie eventually dissolves Doom in Dip by opening the drain on the Dip machine.
- **S27**：As toons and the police arrive, Eddie discovers that an apparently blank piece of paper on which Roger wrote a love poem to Jessica is actually Acme's will, written in disappearing/reappearing ink.
- **S28**：Eddie kisses Roger—proving that he has regained his sense of humor—and the toons celebrate their victory.

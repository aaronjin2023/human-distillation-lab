## 角色资料

### Snow White

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-464a04d4991309e2` · 独立版本：`profile-f60f5b07d428505b`

- **性格倾向**：在与小矮人共同生活时，倾向主动承担照料家务的角色（行为推断；S14、S15）
- **核心动机**：受到死亡威胁后，行动首先围绕逃离危险与获得庇护（行为推断；S8、S9、S14、S15）
- **关系模式**：与小矮人通过家务照料和共同生活形成互助关系（行为推断；S14、S15、S27、S28）
- **情绪反应**：逃入森林时迷失而恐惧，需要动物引导才能找到住处（摘要直接叙述；S9）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Queen

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-8adda3a749f6e39a` · 独立版本：`profile-acdf18481f2f2744`

- **性格倾向**：在美貌地位受到挑战时，表现出强烈嫉妒与控制倾向（行为推断；S2、S5、S6）
- **核心动机**：以恢复最美者的地位为目标，持续设法除掉白雪公主（摘要直接叙述；S4、S5、S16、S22）
- **价值取舍**：在美貌排名与继女生存之间，选择维护自己的优越地位（行为推断；S5、S6、S20）
- **决策方式**：倾向通过伪装与预判他人反应，安排难以识破的伤害（行为推断；S17、S20、S21）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：依赖魔镜确认自身优越，却被魔镜的回答激发杀意

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Through a textual prologue told via a storybook, Snow White is a princess living with her stepmother, a vain and wicked Queen who is assumed to have taken over the kingdom after the death of Snow White's father.
- **S2**：Fearing Snow White's beauty surpassing her own, the Queen forced her to work as a scullery maid and asked her Magic Mirror daily "who is the fairest one of all".
- **S3**：For several years the mirror always answered that the Queen was, pleasing her.
- **S4**：At the film's opening, the Magic Mirror informs the Queen that Snow White is now the fairest in the land.
- **S5**：The jealous Queen orders a reluctant huntsman to take Snow White into the woods and kill her.
- **S6**：She further demands that the huntsman return with Snow White's heart in a jeweled box as proof of the deed.
- **S7**：The huntsman encounters Snow White but decides not to harm her.
- **S8**：He tearfully begs for her forgiveness, revealing the Queen wants her dead, and urges her to flee into the woods and never come back, bringing back a pig's heart instead.
- **S9**：Lost and frightened, the princess is befriended by woodland creatures who lead her to a cottage deep in the woods.
- **S10**：Finding seven small chairs in the cottage's dining room, Snow White assumes the cottage is the untidy home of seven orphaned children.
- **S11**：It soon becomes apparent that the cottage belongs instead to seven adult dwarfs, Doc, Grumpy, Happy, Sleepy, Bashful, Sneezy, and Dopey, who work in a nearby mine.
- **S12**：Returning home, they are alarmed to find their cottage clean and surmise that an intruder has invaded their home.
- **S13**：The dwarfs find Snow White upstairs, asleep across three of their beds.
- **S14**：Snow White awakes to find the Dwarfs at her bedside and introduces herself, and all of the dwarfs eventually welcome her into their home after they learn she can cook and clean beautifully.
- **S15**：Snow White begins a new life cooking, cleaning, and keeping house for the dwarfs while they mine for jewels and at night sing, play music and dance.
- **S16**：Meanwhile, the Queen discovers that Snow White is still alive when the mirror again answers that Snow White is the fairest in the land.
- **S17**：Using magic to disguise herself as an old hag, the Queen creates a poisoned apple that will put whoever eats it into the "Sleeping Death".
- **S18**：The Evil Queen explains that Snow White would collapse into a magical sleep if she were to take even a single bite of the apple.
- **S19**：The sleep can only be cured by the power of "love's first kiss".
- **S20**：The Queen reasons that this is no danger to her plans, as the dwarfs would not be able to awaken Snow White, and would think she was dead, thus resulting in Snow White being "buried alive".
- **S21**：The Queen goes to the cottage while the dwarfs are away and tricks Snow White into biting into the poisoned apple.
- **S22**：As Snow White falls asleep the Queen proclaims that she will be the fairest of the land.
- **S23**：The vengeful dwarfs, alerted by the woodland animals who recognize her, chase the Queen up a cliff and trap her.
- **S24**：She tries to roll a boulder over them but lightning strikes the cliff she is standing on, causing it to collapse.
- **S25**：The Queen falls to her death, and her body is crushed by the boulder.
- **S26**：The dwarfs return to their cottage and find Snow White seemingly dead, being kept in a deathlike slumber by the potion.
- **S27**：Unwilling to bury her out of sight in the ground, they instead place her in a glass coffin trimmed with gold in a clearing in the forest.
- **S28**：Together with the woodland creatures, they keep watch over her in an "eternal vigil".
- **S29**：After some time, a prince, who had previously met and fallen in love with Snow White, learns of her eternal sleep and visits her coffin.
- **S30**：Saddened by her apparent death, he kisses her, which breaks the spell and awakens her.
- **S31**：The dwarfs and animals all rejoice as the Prince takes Snow White to his castle, which glows in the presence of Snow White.

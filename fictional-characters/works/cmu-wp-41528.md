## 角色资料

### Forrest Gump

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-9483348e4cfc36c6` · 独立版本：`profile-5dd493c3186985e9`

- **性格倾向**：在伙伴与家人需要他时，他表现出重情且愿意付诸行动的倾向。（行为推断；S10、S11、S24、S28）
- **核心动机**：他买下捕虾船经营虾业，是为兑现战时对布巴的承诺。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S24）
- **价值取舍**：他长期珍视与珍妮的情谊，也照料病重的母亲。（行为推断；S5、S28、S30、S35）
- **决策方式**：珍妮离开后，他以长跑回应失落，持续跑了三年。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Jenny Curran

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-6a9d07a9a5603af1` · 独立版本：`profile-6dffd0097e824532`

- **性格倾向**：在与福雷斯特的关系中，她的亲近与婚姻选择呈现反复。（行为推断；S30、S31、S35、S38）
- **核心动机**：后来主动邀约并求婚，显示她此时希望与福雷斯特建立婚姻。（行为推断；S35、S38）
- **决策方式**：她拒绝福雷斯特的求婚后清晨离开，后来又主动来信约见。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S35）
- **关系模式**：她与福雷斯特育有一子，两人重逢后结婚但很快因她去世分离。（摘要直接叙述；S36、S38、S39）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：她曾拒绝福雷斯特求婚并离开，后来又主动求婚。

### Dan Taylor

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-20b46e8ed6f7af24` · 独立版本：`profile-f81fbe13ed9aaf3f`

- **性格倾向**：在此叙事中，他受伤后怨怒消沉，后来逐渐接受幸存的生活。（行为推断；S15、S22、S27）
- **核心动机**：他曾认定自己应像祖先那样战死，并怨恨福雷斯特救他。（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **决策方式**：福雷斯特买下虾船后，他兑现诺言加入，后来接手公司。（摘要直接叙述；S23、S24、S25、S29）
- **关系模式**：他先怨恨福雷斯特救命，后来向福雷斯特道谢并共同经营。（摘要直接叙述；S15、S25、S27）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As he waits at a bus stop, Forrest Gump  starts recounting his life story to nearby strangers.
- **S2**：His story starts with the leg braces he had to wear as a child, which resulted in him being bullied by children.
- **S3**：He lives with his mother , who tells him that "stupid is as stupid does".
- **S4**：Forrest teaches one of their guests, a young Elvis Presley, a hip-swinging dance.
- **S5**：At school, Forrest meets Jenny , with whom he immediately falls in love, and they become best friends.
- **S6**：Forrest discovers that he can run very fast which, despite his below-average intelligence, earns him a scholarship to the University of Alabama from Bear Bryant.
- **S7**：While in college, he witnesses George Wallace's Stand in the Schoolhouse Door, is named an All-American football player, and meets President John F.
- **S8**：Kennedy.
- **S9**：After graduating, Forrest enlists in the United States Army, where he becomes friends with Benjamin Buford "Bubba" Blue , and they agree to go into the shrimping business together.
- **S10**：They are sent to Vietnam, and when their platoon is ambushed, Forrest saves many of the men in his platoon, including platoon leader 2nd Lt.
- **S11**：Dan Taylor , but Bubba is killed.
- **S12**：Forrest himself is injured and receives the Medal of Honor from President Lyndon B.
- **S13**：Johnson.
- **S14**：While recovering from his injuries Forrest meets Dan Taylor again.
- **S15**：Now an amputee, he is furious at Forrest for leaving him a "cripple" and cheating him out of his destiny to die in battle like his ancestors.
- **S16**：In Washington, Forrest is swept up in an anti-war rally at the National Mall and is reunited with Jenny, who is now part of the hippie counterculture movement.
- **S17**：They spend the night walking around the capital, but she leaves with her abusive boyfriend the following day.
- **S18**：Forrest discovers an aptitude for ping pong and begins playing for the U.S.
- **S19**：Army team, eventually competing against Chinese teams on a goodwill tour.
- **S20**：He goes to the White House again and meets President Richard Nixon who provides him a room at the Watergate hotel, where Forrest inadvertently helps expose the Watergate scandal.
- **S21**：For his numerous accomplishments, Forrest is invited onto The Dick Cavett Show.
- **S22**：He again encounters Dan Taylor, now an embittered drunk living on welfare.
- **S23**：Dan is scornful of Forrest's plans to enter the shrimping business and mockingly promises to be Forrest's first mate if he ever succeeds.
- **S24**：Using money from a ping pong endorsement, Forrest buys a shrimping boat, fulfilling his wartime promise to Bubba.
- **S25**：Dan keeps his own promise and joins Forrest as first mate.
- **S26**：They initially have little luck; but, after Hurricane Carmen destroys every other shrimping boat in the region, the Bubba Gump Shrimp Company becomes a huge success.
- **S27**：Having had an epiphany during the hurricane, Dan finally thanks Forrest for saving his life.
- **S28**：Forrest then returns home to care for his ailing mother, who dies soon afterwards.
- **S29**：Forrest leaves the company in the hands of Dan, who invests their wealth in shares of Apple, making them both millionaires.
- **S30**：Jenny returns to visit Forrest and stays with him.
- **S31**：Forrest asks her to marry him, but she declines and slips away early one morning.
- **S32**：Distraught, Forrest decides to go for a run, which turns into a three-year coast-to-coast marathon.
- **S33**：Forrest becomes a celebrity and attracts followers.
- **S34**：One day he stops suddenly and returns home.
- **S35**：He receives a letter from Jenny asking to meet, which brings him to the bus stop where he began telling his story.
- **S36**：Once he and Jenny are reunited, Forrest discovers they have a young son, also named Forrest .
- **S37**：Jenny reveals that she is suffering from an unknown virus.
- **S38**：She proposes to him and he accepts.
- **S39**：They return to Alabama with Forrest Jr. and marry, but Jenny dies soon after.
- **S40**：Forrest waits with Forrest Jr. for the bus to pick him up for his first day of school.
- **S41**：As the bus drives away, Forrest sits on the same tree stump where his mother sat on his first day of school and watches his feather bookmark float off in the wind.

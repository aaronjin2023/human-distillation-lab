### William "Will" Thacker

- **性格倾向**：在亲密关系受挫后倾向谨慎退缩，对再次被拒格外敏感（行为推断；S22、S33、S37）

### Anna Scott

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-e6e777e3c1263351` · 独立版本：`profile-062ee48ec9e21f86`

- **性格倾向**：在公众注视与隐私受威胁时倾向防备，容易警觉他人泄密（行为推断；S24、S28、S29、S36）
- **核心动机**：在媒体压力下寻求安全落脚处，也主动争取与威尔相处（摘要直接叙述；S13、S23、S24、S26、S34）
- **价值取舍**：在亲密交往中看重私人空间，宁可隐瞒关系也避免被议论（行为推断；S29、S32、S36）
- **关系模式**：通过融入威尔的朋友圈与记住其喜好表达对关系的投入（行为推断；S14、S15、S38）
- **决策方式**：关系出现裂痕后仍倾向主动重访与解释，尝试恢复联系（行为推断；S23、S31、S34、S36）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：William "Will" Thacker  owns an independent bookshop in Notting Hill that specialises in travel writing.
- **S2**：He has been coping with divorce after his wife left him for a man who looked exactly like Harrison Ford.
- **S3**：He shares his house with an uninhibited Welsh eccentric named Spike .
- **S4**：Thacker encounters Hollywood star actress Anna Scott  when she enters his shop to buy a book.
- **S5**：Minutes later, the pair collide in the street, causing Will to spill his orange juice on both of them.
- **S6**：He offers his house across the road for Anna to change.
- **S7**：She accepts and, having changed, surprises Will with a kiss which starts their mutual attraction.
- **S8**：Days later, Will asks Spike if he has any messages.
- **S9**：Spike has trouble writing or remembering messages but does recall "some American girl called Anna" who mentioned a "completely different name".
- **S10**：Anna is at the Ritz, under the name "Flintstone", and has asked Will to visit.
- **S11**：Will is allowed in, but her press interviews are still running and he is mistaken for a journalist.
- **S12**：He has to interview some of the cast of Anna's new film Helix, even though he has not seen it.
- **S13**：Will is called back to see Anna and she tells him that she has cancelled her evening appointment to go out with him.
- **S14**：Will is delighted but then remembers that his sister Honey  is about to have a birthday party, and Anna say that she will be his date.
- **S15**：There, at Max's  and Bella's  house, Anna feels at home with Will's friends, but does not win the brownie for being the biggest under-achiever.
- **S16**：Afterwards they climb into a private London square.
- **S17**：The next day they visit a cinema and eat at a restaurant.
- **S18**：Anna invites Will to her room at the Ritz, but her American boyfriend, a famous film star named Jeff King , has arrived unexpectedly.
- **S19**：Anna is very embarrassed and disappointed but Will pretends to be a room-service waiter.
- **S20**：When King steps out of the room, Anna apologizes; she thought King had broken up with her.
- **S21**：Will realizes he must leave.
- **S22**：He then tries to forget her but finds it very difficult.
- **S23**：Six months later, Anna turns up at Will's house, unannounced.
- **S24**：Pictures taken of her as a cheesecake model have been sold to the press and she needs a place to hide out.
- **S25**：After spending the day together, Anna goes downstairs to where Will is sleeping and they embrace.
- **S26**：They go up to the bedroom where they make passionate love and in the morning Anna asks if she can stay longer.
- **S27**："Stay for ever" says Will.
- **S28**：The doorbell rings and Will is stunned to see reporters at the doorstep, alerted by careless talk by Spike at the pub.
- **S29**：Angry at what she views as Will's betrayal, Anna leaves and he again attempts to forget her.
- **S30**：Six months after that, Anna returns to London to make a Henry James film, which Will had suggested.
- **S31**：Will approaches the set of the film and sees Anna, who invites him to watch the day's filming and to talk afterwards.
- **S32**：A sound engineer offers Will a pair of headphones to listen in on the dialogue, but he overhears Anna tell her co-star that Will is "no one, just some guy from the past".
- **S33**：Crushed, Will leaves the set without having talked to Anna.
- **S34**：The next day, Anna comes to the bookshop and asks why he had left.
- **S35**：Will tells her that he had overheard her remarks.
- **S36**：She explains that she didn't want her co-star to know because he is a notorious gossip.
- **S37**：Will listens to her but then says that he could not survive a third rejection of his love and turns her down.
- **S38**：Having brought him a present, she leaves it behind, still wrapped; it turns out to be an original Marc Chagall painting, La Mariée, that she had seen a print of in Will's home.
- **S39**：Will meets his friends, who are supportive of his decision about Anna, until Spike arrives and promptly calls him a "daft prick".
- **S40**：Will realizes he has made the biggest mistake of his life by letting her go.
- **S41**：The group searches for Anna, racing around London in Max's car.
- **S42**：They finally reach Anna's press conference and Will persuades her to stay in England with him.
- **S43**：Anna and Will later marry, the film concluding with a shot of Will and a pregnant Anna on a park bench in a park.

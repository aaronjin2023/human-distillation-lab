## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film begins with a saber-toothed squirrel  who is trying to find somewhere to store his prized acorn.
- **S2**：Eventually, as he tries to stomp it into the ground, he causes a large crack in the ground that extends for miles and miles and sets off a large avalanche.
- **S3**：He barely escapes, but finds himself stepped on by a herd of prehistoric animals.
- **S4**：The animals are trying to avoid the ice age by migrating south.
- **S5**：Sid, a clumsy Megalonyx sloth left behind by his family, is attacked by two Brontops whom he angered.
- **S6**：Sid is soon saved by Manfred , an agitated mammoth who fights them off.
- **S7**：Not wanting to be alone and unprotected, Sid follows Manny.
- **S8**：Meanwhile, Soto, the leader of a Smilodon pride wants revenge on a group of humans by eating the chief's baby son, Roshan, alive.
- **S9**：Soto leads a raid on the human camp, during which Roshan's mother is separated from the rest and jumps down a waterfall when cornered by Soto's lieutenant, Diego.
- **S10**：For his failure, Diego is sent to find and retrieve the baby.
- **S11**：Sid and Manny spot Roshan and his mother near the lake, having survived her plunge.
- **S12**：The mother only has enough strength to trust her baby to Manny before she disappears.
- **S13**：After much persuasion by Sid, they decide to return Roshan  but when they reach the human settlement, they find it deserted.
- **S14**：They meet up with Diego, who convinces the pair to let him help by tracking the humans.
- **S15**：The four travel on, with Diego secretly leading them to his pack for an ambush.
- **S16**：While having small adventures on their way, they reach a cave where Sid and Diego learn about Manny's past and his previous interactions with the humans, in which his wife and son were killed, leaving Manny a cynical loner.
- **S17**：At one time the group passes a flying saucer frozen in the ice, while Sid comes upon a display showing the evolution of sloths.
- **S18**：At the end of the film, Diego, Manny and Sid battle Soto's pack and a short fight ensues.
- **S19**：As Soto closes in for the kill on Manny, Diego leaps and stops Soto, who wounds Diego in the process.
- **S20**：Manny, in vengeance, knocks Soto into a rock wall, causing several sharp icicles to fall on Soto, killing him.
- **S21**：Manny and Sid manage to return the baby to his tribe, and Diego rejoins them, as the group begins to head off to warmer climates.
- **S22**：There is also a subplot where Scrat the squirrel makes many comical attempts to bury his beloved acorn.
- **S23**：His misfortunes include getting chased by an enormous glacier, being struck by lightning, attempting to thaw out the acorn by using a fire, but roasting it too long, causing it to turn into a large popcorn kernel, and finally getting frozen an icecub along with his much sought after nut.
- **S24**：In the epilogue, 20,000 years later , Scrat is frozen in an ice cube that washes up on the shore of a tropical island.
- **S25**：The sun slowly melts the cube, bringing Scrat back to life but the acorn, which is just out of his reach, ends up being washed away by the tide.
- **S26**：Scrat then explodes out of the ice cube in anger and hits his head repetitively on a tree, which drops a coconut.
- **S27**：Believing it to be a giant acorn, Scrat's anger immediately turns to glee at this new find.
- **S28**：He tries to pack it into the ground as he did previously with his other acorns, but in the process causes a large crack in the ground that makes its way up the side of a volcano and causes it to erupt, mirroring the opening scene when Scrat causes a break in the ice with an acorn.

### Henry Roth

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-339fb5d63bf45313` · 独立版本：`profile-fb63a6d4176cf4b9`

- **性格倾向**：追求露西遇阻时，他倾向反复尝试并设计新的接近方式（行为推断；S13、S15、S16）
- **核心动机**：他希望维持与露西的关系，为此愿意推迟多年航行计划（摘要直接叙述；S26、S27）
- **决策方式**：面对每天重新认识的困难，他倾向借助录像建立持续联系（行为推断；S23）
- **成长变化**：他从回避认真交往转向持续照护并建立婚姻家庭（摘要直接叙述；S2、S23、S39、S42）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：多年航行理想与陪伴露西的愿望一度发生冲突。

### Lucy Whitmore

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-570d47ad03fd0b88` · 独立版本：`profile-3042e313964e35e4`

- **性格倾向**：得知自身处境后，她表现出愿意逐步适应现实的倾向（行为推断；S19、S24、S25、S30）
- **价值取舍**：发现恋人为自己搁置理想时，她倾向优先维护对方的人生选择（行为推断；S26）
- **决策方式**：意识到记忆异常后，她主动要求就医确认自身状况（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20、S22）
- **关系模式**：即使无法明确记起恋人，她仍反复梦见并画出他的形象（摘要直接叙述；S36）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：与亨利相爱，却因不愿阻碍他的航行计划而主动割舍关系。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Henry Roth  is a veterinarian at Sea Life Park on the island of Oahu who has a reputation of womanizing female tourists.
- **S2**：He shows no interest in committing to a serious relationship and his closest friends are Ula , a marijuana-smoking Islander; his assistant Alexa , whose sexuality is unclear; and Willy, his pet African penguin.
- **S3**：One day Henry’s boat The Sea Serpent, breaks down while he is sailing around Oahu.
- **S4**：He goes to the Hukilau Café to wait for the coastguard.
- **S5**：There, he sees Lucy , a pretty girl who makes architectural art with her waffles.
- **S6**：Henry assumes she is a local, which prevents him from introducing himself but the next day he comes back.
- **S7**：Lucy and he hit if off instantly and she asks him to meet her again tomorrow morning.
- **S8**：When Henry goes back to the café, Lucy shows no recollection of ever meeting him.
- **S9**：The restaurant owner Sue  explains to Henry that a year ago Lucy and her father Marlin  went up to the North Shore to pick a pineapple for his birthday.
- **S10**：On the way back, they got into a serious car accident that left Lucy with Goldfield Syndrome, a type of antegorade amnesia.
- **S11**：She wakes up every morning thinking it is Sunday October 13 of last year.
- **S12**：To save her the heartbreak of reliving the accident everyday, Marlin and Doug , Lucy’s lisping steroid-addicted brother, relive everyday by doing numerous tasks, including putting out October 13th’s newspaper, watching the same Vikings game, and refilling Lucy’s shampoo bottles.
- **S13**：Despite Sue’s warning that Nick , the Hukilau Café’s chef, will come at him with a meat cleaver, Henry decided to try and get Lucy to have breakfast with him.
- **S14**：Eventually he does, but it ends poorly when Henry accidentally hurts Lucy’s feelings.
- **S15**：He follows her home to apologize where Marlin and Doug instruct Henry to leave Lucy alone.
- **S16**：Henry begins concocting ways to run into Lucy such as pretending to have car trouble, creating a fake road block and by having Ula beat him up.
- **S17**：Eventually, Marlin and Doug figure this out due to Lucy singing The Beach Boys "Wouldn't It Be Nice" on the days when she meets Henry.
- **S18**：The next day, Henry asks to sit with Lucy at breakfast but she declines and says she has a boyfriend named Ringo McCartney before a police officer gives Lucy a ticket for her expired plates.
- **S19**：Lucy learns about her accident and requests to be taken to Dr.
- **S20**：Keats .
- **S21**：Dr.
- **S22**：Keats introduces Lucy to Ten-Second Tom , a patient whose memory only lasts for 10 seconds to prove to Lucy how much worse her condition could be.
- **S23**：Henry comes up with an idea to make a video explaining to Lucy her accident and their relationship and play it every morning for her.
- **S24**：She watched the tape and is hurt, but eventually comes to her senses and she is able to spend the day by picking up where the tape says she left off.
- **S25**：She spends more time with Henry and goes to see some of her old friends.
- **S26**：Lucy decides to erase Henry completely from her life after learning of his decision to not take a sailing trip to Bristol Bay to study walruses, something that has been in planning mode for the past 10 years.
- **S27**：He feels he cannot leave Lucy for the year it will take him.
- **S28**：Henry reluctantly helps her destroy her journal entries of their relationship.
- **S29**：A few weeks later, Henry is preparing to leave for his sailing trip.
- **S30**：Before he goes, Marlin tells him that Lucy is now living at the brain institute and teaching an art class.
- **S31**：He also tells him that she sings.
- **S32**：Then he gives Henry a Beach Boys CD.
- **S33**：Listening to the CD, Henry becomes emotional and curses Marlin for giving him the CD and making him feel so emotional.
- **S34**：He then remembers that Marlin once told him that Lucy always sings after she meets him.
- **S35**：He then realizes that Lucy remembers him and turns around to go home.
- **S36**：She says she does not remember, but that she dreams about him every night and paints pictures of him.
- **S37**：They reconcile.
- **S38**：Some time later, Lucy wakes up and plays the tape marked “Good Morning Lucy”.
- **S39**：It again reminds her of her accident, but ends with her and Henry’s wedding.
- **S40**：From the tape, Henry says to put a jacket on and come have breakfast when she is ready.
- **S41**：Lucy then sees that she is on Henry’s boat, which finally made it to Alaska.
- **S42**：She goes up on deck and meets Henry and their daughter, Nicole, as well as her dad.

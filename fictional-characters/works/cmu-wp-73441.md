## 角色资料

### Elliott

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-45acc4331e8f3d23` · 独立版本：`profile-7aaa725d01531535`

- **性格倾向**：在接触陌生来客时表现出好奇与接纳，愿意主动接近并藏护他。（行为推断；S4、S6、S10）
- **核心动机**：协助外星来客隐藏和联系家乡，并在其复苏后帮助他逃离。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S16、S27）
- **决策方式**：用糖果引来外星人并协助制作通讯装置，外出时借装扮掩护。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S16、S18）
- **情绪反应**：面对看似死去的外星伙伴，他陷入悲痛并独自守在身旁。（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25）
- **关系模式**：与外星伙伴形成心灵联结，其体验与健康变化也受到牵连。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13、S17、S21、S24）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

### E.T.

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-9fc401ce107bd6f6` · 独立版本：`profile-ca2f618cda2a1422`

- **性格倾向**：在陌生环境中倾向观察模仿，主动学习沟通方式并尝试求援。（行为推断；S7、S15、S16、S19）
- **核心动机**：受到漫画中求援情节启发，尝试联系家乡并最终登船返回。（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16、S19、S33）
- **决策方式**：从漫画获得启发，借埃利奥特协助用玩具制作联系家乡的装置。（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）
- **关系模式**：与埃利奥特具有心灵联结，告别时也倾向表达亲近与安慰。（行为推断；S12、S17、S32）
- **沟通方式**：重复格蒂说的话学习英语，并在埃利奥特催促下自称外星人。（摘要直接叙述；S15）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film opens in a California forest as a group of alien botanists collect flora samples.
- **S2**：U.S. government agents appear on the scene , and the aliens flee in their spaceship, mistakenly leaving one of their own behind.
- **S3**：The scene shifts to a suburban home, where a boy named Elliott, age 10, is trying to hang out with his 16-year-old brother Michael and his friends.
- **S4**：As he returns from picking up a pizza, Elliott discovers that something is hiding in their toolshed.
- **S5**：The creature promptly flees upon being discovered.
- **S6**：Despite his family's disbelief, Elliott lures the alien from the forest to his bedroom using a trail of Reese's Pieces candy.
- **S7**：Before he goes to sleep, Elliott realizes the alien is imitating his movements.
- **S8**：Elliott feigns illness the next morning to stay home from school and play with the alien.
- **S9**：Later that day Michael and their sister, 5-year-old Gertie, meet the alien.
- **S10**：They decide to keep him hidden from their mother.
- **S11**：When they ask it about its origin, the alien levitates several balls to represent its solar system and then demonstrates its powers by reviving a dead plant.
- **S12**：At school the next day, Elliott begins to experience a psychic connection with the alien.
- **S13**：Exhibiting signs of intoxication due to the alien drinking beer, Elliott begins freeing all the frogs from a dissection class.
- **S14**：As the alien watches John Wayne kiss Maureen O'Hara in The Quiet Man, Elliott's link causes him to kiss a girl he likes.
- **S15**：The alien learns to speak English by repeating what Gertie says as she watches Sesame Street and, at Elliott's urging, dubs itself "E.T." E.T. reads a comic strip where Buck Rogers, stranded, calls for help by building a makeshift communication device, and is inspired to try it himself.
- **S16**：He gets Elliott's help in building a device to "phone home" by using a Speak & Spell toy.
- **S17**：Michael notices that E.T.'s health is declining and that Elliott is referring to himself as "we".
- **S18**：On Halloween, Michael and Elliott dress E.T. as a ghost so they can sneak him out of the house.
- **S19**：Elliott and E.T. ride a bicycle to the forest, where E.T. makes a successful call home.
- **S20**：The next morning, Elliott wakes up in the field, only to find E.T. gone, so he returns home to his distressed family.
- **S21**：Michael searches for and finds E.T. dying in a ditch and takes him to Elliott, who is also dying.
- **S22**：Mary becomes frightened when she discovers her son's illness and the dying alien, just as government agents invade the house.
- **S23**：Scientists set up a medical facility there, quarantining Elliott and E.T.
- **S24**：Their link disappears and E.T. then appears to die while Elliott recovers.
- **S25**：A grief-stricken Elliott is left alone with the motionless alien when he notices a dead flower, the plant E.T. had previously revived, coming back to life.
- **S26**：E.T. reanimates and reveals that his people are returning.
- **S27**：Elliott and Michael steal a van that E.T. had been loaded into and a chase ensues, with Michael's friends joining them as they attempt to evade the authorities by bicycle.
- **S28**：Suddenly facing a dead end, they escape as E.T. uses telekinesis to lift them into the air and toward the forest.
- **S29**：Standing near the spaceship, E.T.'s heart glows as he prepares to return home.
- **S30**：Mary, Gertie, and "Keys", a government agent, show up.
- **S31**：E.T. says goodbye to Michael and Gertie, as Gertie presents E.T. with the plant that he had revived.
- **S32**：Before entering the spaceship, E.T. tells Elliott "I'll be right here", pointing his glowing finger to Elliott's forehead.
- **S33**：He then picks up the plant Gertie gave him, walks into the spaceship, and takes off, leaving a rainbow in the sky as Elliott  watches the ship leave.

## 角色资料

### Alex Kerner

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-c9cfad778bf415a9` · 独立版本：`profile-88e390cde4eeb845`

- **性格倾向**：在保护母亲的情境中，他表现出投入细节并不断补救的执着倾向（行为推断；S17、S18、S20、S25）
- **核心动机**：他希望避免母亲因社会剧变受到刺激而再次发生致命心脏病（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）
- **价值取舍**：他倾向把母亲的安全置于及时告知真实社会变化之上（行为推断；S16、S20、S25）
- **决策方式**：面对不断出现的破绽，他以更复杂的影像叙事维持原有解释（行为推断；S19、S20、S25、S33）
- **关系模式**：他不仅维系母亲的生活幻象，也主动促成父母再次相见（摘要直接叙述；S16、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：为保护母亲而控制其信息，却不知道母亲已获知真相。

### Christiane Kerner

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-d404fd740e36b5e1` · 独立版本：`profile-149279ac6eda937f`

- **性格倾向**：在得知儿子善意隐瞒后，她表现出体谅并配合其心意的倾向（行为推断；S34、S35）
- **价值取舍**：面对所谓西方难民，她以社会主义信念主张开放家庭接纳（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26）
- **核心动机**：她当年放弃迁往西方，是担心行动失败后失去两个孩子（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29）
- **情绪反应**：随着时间推移，她对未与丈夫团聚的决定产生后悔（摘要直接叙述；S30）
- **沟通方式**：她最终坦白家庭分离的原因，却没有揭穿儿子最后的新闻表演（摘要直接叙述；S28、S34、S35）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾因害怕失去孩子而放弃与丈夫团聚，后来又对这一决定后悔。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In a prologue, Alex Kerner  recalls as a child in 1978 how proud he was along with his countrymen when the first German to enter space, Sigmund Jähn, came from East Germany .
- **S2**：The remainder of the film is set in East Berlin, spanning from October 1989 to just after German reunification a year later.
- **S3**：Alex lives with his sister, Ariane , his mother, Christiane , and Ariane's infant daughter, Paula.
- **S4**：His father fled to the West in 1978, apparently abandoning the family.
- **S5**：In his absence, Christiane has become an ardent idealist and supporter of the ruling Socialist Unity Party of Germany .
- **S6**：Alex takes part in an an anti-government demonstration, where he meets a girl by chance, but they are separated by the riot police before they could properly introduce themselves.
- **S7**：When Christiane sees Alex being arrested, she suffers a near-fatal heart attack and falls into a coma.
- **S8**：The police ignore Alexander's plea to assist his mother, instead releasing him later that evening to go and see her.
- **S9**：While visiting his mother at the hospital, Alex again meets the girl from the demonstration, who is revealed to be Lara , a young nurse from the Soviet Union taking care of his mother.
- **S10**：Alex becomes smitten with her and asks her out.
- **S11**：The two soon begin dating and develop a close bond.
- **S12**：Shortly afterward, the Berlin Wall falls.
- **S13**：In that time, capitalism comes to East Berlin, and Alex loses his job before "winning" a new position in a lottery to install satellite dishes with West Berlin resident Denis Domaschke , an aspiring filmmaker with whom Alex quickly becomes good friends, while Ariane leaves university to work at a Burger King drive-through.
- **S14**：After eight months, Christiane awakes, but is severely weakened both physically and mentally.
- **S15**：Her doctor asserts that any shock might cause another, possibly fatal, heart attack.
- **S16**：Alex realises that the discovery of recent events would be too much for her to bear, and so sets out to maintain the illusion that things are as before in the German Democratic Republic.
- **S17**：To this end, he, Ariane and Lara revert from the gaudy decor of the west to the drab decor they previously had in the bedroom of their mother  in the family apartment, dress in their old clothes, and feed Christiane new Western produce from old-labeled jars.
- **S18**：Their deception is successful, albeit increasingly complicated and elaborate.
- **S19**：Christiane occasionally witnesses strange occurrences, such as a gigantic Coca-Cola advertisement banner unfurling on a building outside the apartment.
- **S20**：With Denis' help, Alex edits old tapes of East German news broadcasts and creates fake reports on TV  to explain these odd events.
- **S21**：As the old news shows were fairly predictable, and Christiane's memory is vague, she is initially fooled.
- **S22**：Christiane eventually gains strength and wanders outside one day while Alex is asleep.
- **S23**：She sees all her neighbours' old furniture piled up in the street for garbage collection and advertisements for Western corporations.
- **S24**：She also sees an old statue of Lenin being flown away by an Mi 8 helicopter, which seems to reach out to her.
- **S25**：However, Alex and Ariane quickly find her, take her home, and show her a fake special report that East Germany is now accepting refugees from the West following a severe economic crisis there.
- **S26**：Christiane, initially skeptical, finally decrees that as good Socialists, they should open their home to these newcomers.
- **S27**：The family decides to go to their dacha at Christiane's suggestion.
- **S28**：While they are there along with Lara and Ariane's new Western boyfriend, Rainer , Christiane reveals her own secret; her husband had fled because the Party had been increasingly oppressing him, and the plan had been for the rest of the family to join him in West Berlin.
- **S29**：However, Christiane, fearing the government would take away Alex and Ariane if things went wrong, chose to stay in the East.
- **S30**：She has come to regret the decision over time.
- **S31**：Christiane relapses shortly afterward and is taken back to the hospital.
- **S32**：After meeting his father, Robert , for the first time in years, Alex convinces him to meet Christiane again.
- **S33**：Under pressure to reveal the truth about the fall of the East, Alex creates a final fake news segment, convincing a taxi driver whom he believes to be Sigmund Jähn to act in the false news report as the new leader of East Germany and to give a speech promising to make a better future including opening the borders to the West.
- **S34**：However, Alex is unaware that Christiane had already been informed of the situation the nation was going through by Lara earlier the same day.
- **S35**：She reacts fondly to her son's effort, without telling him she had already acknowledged what had happened in the past few months.
- **S36**：Christiane dies peacefully two days later: she outlives the GDR, passing away three days after full official German reunification.
- **S37**：Alex, Ariane, Lara and Denis scatter her ashes in the wind  using an old toy rocket Alex had made with his father during his childhood.

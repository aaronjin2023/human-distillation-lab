## 角色资料

### Frank Martin

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-96b507182956e775` · 独立版本：`profile-3a2022661d82b8fb`

- **性格倾向**：在运输交易中，他倾向严守既定边界，拒绝临时加码的诱惑（行为推断；S3、S4、S6、S7）
- **核心动机**：遭到公文包炸弹暗算后，他返回雇主住处采取报复行动（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21）
- **价值取舍**：面对人口贩运受害者，他逐渐将救人置于不问内情的职业准则之上（行为推断；S2、S30、S39、S41、S44）
- **决策方式**：在常规追截失败后，他倾向迅速更换手段并承担极高行动风险（行为推断；S39、S40、S41）
- **情绪反应**：他受过往经历困扰，对旧日领导的失望构成退居海岸的背景（摘要直接叙述；S1）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以不问内情和不打开包裹自律，却因发现被绑女子逐步介入其困境。

### Lai

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4a31ae153dfc22f6` · 独立版本：`profile-529d5b5019125eed`

- **性格倾向**：在受调查与追捕的情境中，她倾向机敏应变并主动寻找脱困机会（行为推断；S24、S26、S29）
- **核心动机**：她借助弗兰克追查运输货柜，行动指向阻止人口贩运（行为推断；S30、S31、S43、S44）
- **沟通方式**：面对警方询问，她通过改换虚构身份与编造相识故事掩护弗兰克（行为推断；S24、S25、S26）
- **价值取舍**：在父亲威胁弗兰克性命时，她选择保护后者而非维护血缘同盟（行为推断；S33、S42、S43）
- **关系模式**：她与弗兰克的关系从被运送者与承运者转为相互保护的伙伴（行为推断；S15、S22、S26、S43、S44）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：与贩运组织者存在父女关系，却选择救下被父亲袭击的弗兰克。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Frank Martin  is a highly skilled driver known only as "The Transporter." The titular character is a former Special Forces officer who, disillusioned with his former leadership and tortured by the memories of his past, now lives on the French Mediterranean coastline with a lucrative "backup" career.
- **S2**：He will transport anything, no questions asked, from Point A to Point B, always on time, and he is known as the best in the business.
- **S3**：He strictly follows four rules when transporting: Rule #1: "No new deals, when it's a deal, it's a deal", Rule #2: "No names", Rule #3: "Don't open the package", and Rule #4: "Never make a promise you can't keep".
- **S4**：Frank has been hired to transport "Three men, 254 kilos." When not three but four men get into his car, fresh from a bank heist, Frank refuses to move, holding fast to Rule #1.
- **S5**：After one gang member is killed by the others, Frank leaves with the remaining robbers.
- **S6**：On delivery to their destination, they foist new money on Frank to drive further from the city.
- **S7**：He refuses the deal, again adhering to Rule #1.
- **S8**：The robbers escape in another car, and Frank leaves.
- **S9**：Frank returns to his mansion in the south of France, and he finds out via the news that the gang was caught after crashing their getaway car in a pursuit.
- **S10**：Later, local police Inspector Tarconi , who happens to be a close acquaintance, arrives to question Frank about the robbery, since his make and model of car was the same used in the robbery.
- **S11**：However, despite his suspicions about Frank's involvement, Tarconi has no concrete proof about the heist  and leaves his Villa.
- **S12**：Frank is then hired to deliver a package to an American gangster  known only as "Wall Street.".
- **S13**：During the way, he notices that something is moving in the package, but he refuses to open the bag due to Rule #3.
- **S14**：While changing a flat tire on the road in France, he breaks one of his rules and opens the package that was placed into the trunk at Point A.
- **S15**：He finds the package is really a woman, and he makes a small hole in the tape over her mouth and gives her a bottle of juice to drink.
- **S16**：He delivers the package as promised to Point B.
- **S17**：After receiving the package, Wall Street asks Frank to transport a briefcase.
- **S18**：Frank reluctantly accepts the job.
- **S19**：He takes the briefcase and leaves.
- **S20**：On the way, he stops at a gas station to eat and rest for a little while, but the briefcase turns out to be a bomb that destroys Frank's car in the parking lot but leaves him unharmed.
- **S21**：Frank snaps and returns to Wall Street's residence with a vengeance, dispatching several of his henchmen and then stealing a car to get away, only to find "the package" tied to a chair in the back seat.
- **S22**：Later on, he returns to his house, and unties the package who tells him that her name is Lai.
- **S23**：The next day, Tarconi arrives.
- **S24**：Lai opens the door and claims to be Frank's new cook.
- **S25**：He makes the usual inquiries, producing a scorched license plate, and expressing suspicion that Frank's "cook" drives a Mercedes.
- **S26**：Lai now says she is Frank's new girlfriend and supports Frank's alibi with an invented story about how they met.
- **S27**：Tarconi leaves with no proof again.
- **S28**：However, shortly after he leaves, missiles rain down on Frank's house, and Frank and Lai are barely able to escape the house into the sewer, swimming away from the house before it completely explodes.
- **S29**：At the police station, being questioned about the destruction of Frank's house, Frank and Lai are able to deflect Tarconi's questions, and Lai manages to sneak onto Tarconi's computer to access information on Wall Street.
- **S30**：She tells Frank that Wall Street is trafficking two shipping containers full of Chinese people including her own father and family, and is planning to sell them into slavery.
- **S31**：Lai and Frank go to Wall Street's office to find the shipping docket identifying the containers.
- **S32**：However, Wall Street reveals that Lai's family is not in the containers.
- **S33**：In fact, her father is the man responsible for the human trafficking.
- **S34**：Tarconi, who was following Frank, then arrives at the office.
- **S35**：Wall Street and Lai's father accuse Frank of kidnapping Lai and attempting to blackmail them.
- **S36**：Unable to prove otherwise, Tarconi has Frank arrested and locked up in the station.
- **S37**：At the station, Frank does his best to explain the situation to Tarconi.
- **S38**：Tarconi agrees to abet Frank's escape as his faux hostage.
- **S39**：Frank then tracks the criminals to the docks, where they load the containers onto trucks.
- **S40**：However, Frank is spotted and is forced to fight his way through the guards, failing to stop the trucks.
- **S41**：He then steals a small airplane and parachutes onto one of the trucks, killing Wall Street and a number of his henchmen.
- **S42**：After getting out of the truck, Frank is then ambushed by Lai's father.
- **S43**：However, Frank is saved when Lai shoots her own father.
- **S44**：Afterwards, Tarconi arrives with the police and they rescue the people trapped inside the containers, and Frank and Lai help to load them in the ambulances as the film ends.

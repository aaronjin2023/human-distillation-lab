## 角色资料

### Perseus

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-68504a81301ecc3a` · 独立版本：`profile-aec3c1018092e242`

- **性格倾向**：面对神祇的馈赠与身份安排，他起初表现出抗拒依附的倾向（行为推断；S20、S41）
- **核心动机**：他接受讨伐任务的直接动力，是为死去的家人复仇（摘要直接叙述；S17）
- **价值取舍**：在获得王位与神格的机会面前，他仍选择以凡人身份生活（摘要直接叙述；S40、S41）
- **成长变化**：他逐渐接纳自身半神身份，并开始运用先前拒绝的援助（摘要直接叙述；S20、S31、S33、S37）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他拒绝成为神祇，却在战斗中逐步接受神赐援助。

### Io

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-523364255a72ac66` · 独立版本：`profile-3e9b93ba04d74303`

- **性格倾向**：在长期守护与临终抉择中，她表现出坚定而能承担代价的倾向（行为推断；S14、S15、S32）
- **核心动机**：她保护珀尔修斯，是因为相信他能够击败众神（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **价值取舍**：面对神祇的求爱，她选择拒绝，并因此承受不朽诅咒（摘要直接叙述；S14）
- **决策方式**：在自身将死的情境中，她倾向优先推动救援更多人的行动（行为推断；S30、S32）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In ancient times, the gods Zeus, Poseidon, and Hades betrayed their parents, the Titans, and banished them to the Underworld with the help of the Kraken, a sea monster born of Hades.
- **S2**：The gods divided the Universe among themselves; Zeus took the skies, Poseidon took the seas, and Hades, tricked by Zeus, was left with the Underworld.
- **S3**：The gods created the mortals, whose faith in them assured their immortality; however, as time passed, mortals began to question them.
- **S4**：Years later, "Perseus" is fishing with his family when they witness soldiers from the city of Argos destroying a statue of Zeus.
- **S5**：The gods, infuriated at this desecration, unleash the Furies - flying beasts who pursue mortal sinners.
- **S6**：The furies attack the soldiers and destroy the fishing vessel.
- **S7**：Only Perseus survives and is found by a group of soldiers from Argos.
- **S8**：Perseus is brought before King Cepheus and Queen Cassiopeia who are celebrating the campaign against the gods.
- **S9**：Queen Cassiopeia brashly compares her daughter Princess Andromeda to the gods and boasts she is more beautiful than Aphrodite.
- **S10**：The revelry is cut short by the arrival of Hades, who has been given leave by Zeus to punish the mortals for their defiance.
- **S11**：Hades states that he will unleash the Kraken against Argos unless Andromeda is offered as a sacrifice.
- **S12**：Before leaving, he reveals that Perseus is a demigod, the son of Zeus.
- **S13**：In captivity, Perseus meets Io, who tells him of his origin.
- **S14**：Io also reveals that she was cursed with immortality after she refused Poseidon's advances and has watched over Perseus his entire life.
- **S15**：She has always protected him, as he is the only one who can defeat the gods.
- **S16**：Perseus is asked to lead the King's Guard to visit the Stygian Witches in order to discover a way to kill the Kraken.
- **S17**：Wishing to avenge the death of his family, Perseus accepts.
- **S18**：Zeus is convinced by Apollo to give Perseus a chance and presents him with an enchanted sword forged on Mt.
- **S19**：Olympus and a winged horse named Pegasus.
- **S20**：Perseus refuses both gifts but Draco puts the sword into safekeeping.
- **S21**：Shortly thereafter, they are attacked by Calibos.
- **S22**：Draco severs Calibos's hand causing Calibos to flee.
- **S23**：The band gives chase but is attacked by giant scorpions called Scorpiochs summoned by Calibos’s blood.
- **S24**：They are saved by the Djinn, a band of non-human desert sorcerers led by Sheik Suleiman.
- **S25**：The Djinn, also wishing for the gods' defeat, lend their aid to Perseus and his band.
- **S26**：The group arrives at the lair of the Stygian Witches and learns from them that the only possibility for killing the Kraken lies with the head of the gorgon Medusa who resides in a temple in the Underworld.
- **S27**：Medusa can turn any creature made of flesh into stone that makes eye contact with her gaze, and thus using her head is the only way to stop the Kraken.
- **S28**：Perseus, Io, Suleiman, Draco and his remaining men Solon, Eusebius, and Ixas cross into the Underworld and enter Medusa's temple lair while Io remains outside, unable to enter the temple as a woman.
- **S29**：Suleiman and Draco both wound Medusa, sacrificing themselves in the process and setting the stage for Perseus to behead her with his eyes closed.
- **S30**：As he is leaving the temple with Medusa's head, he witnesses Calibos appear behind Io and fatally stab her.
- **S31**：Perseus and Calibos engage in mortal combat and as he finally comes to terms with who he is, Perseus picks up the Olympian sword and pierces Calibos through the chest.
- **S32**：Before dying, Io urges a reluctant Perseus to leave her and save Andromeda and Argos before she dissolves into a golden ethereal vapor.
- **S33**：Perseus mounts Pegasus and hastens back to Argos as the solar eclipse begins and Zeus orders the Kraken's release.
- **S34**：The Cult goes to the palace and seizes Andromeda in order to offer her to the Kraken.
- **S35**：In an intense aerial chase with Perseus riding Pegasus, he manages to retrieve the bag and expose Medusa's face to the Kraken, making eye contact just before it is able to eat Andromeda.
- **S36**：The Kraken slowly turns into a massive stone statue which cracks and falls apart.
- **S37**：Perseus raises his sword to the heavens and calling upon Zeus, throws his sword at Hades.
- **S38**：A lightning bolt engulfs the sword which expels Hades and banishes him to the Underworld once more.
- **S39**：Perseus rescues Andromeda, now the rightful Queen of Argos.
- **S40**：Andromeda asks Perseus to stay by her side as King, but he declines.
- **S41**：Perseus also refuses another offer of godhood from Zeus, who then states that if Perseus is to live as a human he should not be alone, and subsequently revives Io.

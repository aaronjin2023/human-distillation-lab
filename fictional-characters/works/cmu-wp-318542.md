## 角色资料

### Nameless

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d10d83d1f3c55319` · 独立版本：`profile-ce1e7cd7dc09b34e`

- **性格倾向**：在刺杀目标面前，他表现出善于隐忍并控制信息的倾向（行为推断；S3、S4、S6）
- **核心动机**：他的刺杀行动与家人遭秦兵杀害的经历直接相关（行为推断；S5、S6）
- **决策方式**：为接近戒备森严的君王，他倾向以合作布局和叙事取得信任（行为推断；S2、S3、S5、S6）
- **成长变化**：理解止杀与统一的主张后，他放弃了原定刺杀使命（摘要直接叙述；S7、S9、S10）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：家仇推动他接近秦王，和平理念却使他最终放弃刺杀。

### Broken Sword

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a43ee5dd60cbc1de` · 独立版本：`profile-564b5b181fe792e2`

- **性格倾向**：面对飞雪的愤怒挑战，他表现出坚持信念并甘愿自损的倾向（行为推断；S11）
- **价值取舍**：他把天下和平置于继续刺杀秦王的个人行动之上（摘要直接叙述；S7、S11）
- **决策方式**：改变刺杀计划时，他倾向通过说明统一的理由争取认同（行为推断；S7、S10）
- **关系模式**：为使飞雪理解自己的和平愿望，他选择不抵抗她的攻击（摘要直接叙述；S11）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：希望以不抵抗传达和平，却因此死于飞雪的攻击。

### King of Qin

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-85e439b66d4d7610` · 独立版本：`profile-8b21e1245deaa888`

- **性格倾向**：听取刺客战绩时，他表现出警觉多疑并主动质询的倾向（行为推断；S2、S5）
- **核心动机**：他将统一中国视为自身理想，并因有人理解而受到触动（摘要直接叙述；S8）
- **决策方式**：面对可能行刺的来客，他先检验叙述可信度再调整信任（行为推断；S5、S8、S9）
- **情绪反应**：获得无名饶命后，他仍在朝臣催促下不情愿地下令处死对方（摘要直接叙述；S10、S13）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：因理解与信任而不再畏惧无名，最终仍命令将其处死。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In ancient China during the Warring States Period, a nameless prefect of a small jurisdiction arrives at the Qin state's capital city to meet the king of Qin, who has survived an attempt on his life by the assassins Long Sky, Flying Snow, and Broken Sword.
- **S2**：Therefore, no visitors are to approach him within 100 paces.
- **S3**：'Nameless' claims that he had slain the three assassins and he displays their weapons before the king, who allows Nameless to sit closer to him and tell him his story.
- **S4**：Nameless recounts killing Long Sky at a weiqi parlor; later to meet Flying Snow and Broken Sword at a calligraphy school in the Zhao state, where he set them against each other until Snow killed Sword and was herself slain by Nameless.
- **S5**：As the tale concludes, the king expresses disbelief and accuses Nameless of staging the duels with the assassins, who surrendered their lives to allow him to gain the king's trust and take the king's life.
- **S6**：Nameless admits that he is a native of the Zhao state and that his family was killed by Qin soldiers, and confesses that he defeated Sky without killing him and had proposed co-operation to Snow and Sword.
- **S7**：After Snow's apparent death, Sword approached Nameless and told him that the only way to achieve peace was to unite the states under a common dynasty; namely that of Qin, which alone has the ability to do so, thus revealing why Sword gave up his earlier assassination attempt.
- **S8**：The king, affected by the tale and by Sword's understanding of his dream to unify China, ceases to fear Nameless.
- **S9**：He then tosses his sword to Nameless and examines a scroll drawn by Sword, and understands that it explains the ideal warrior, who, paradoxically, should have no desire to kill.
- **S10**：When Nameless realizes the wisdom of these words, he abandons his mission and spares the king.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, when Snow learns that Sword had convinced Nameless to forgo the assassination, she furiously challenges Sword to a fight and unintentionally kills him when he chooses not to defend himself so that she would understand his hopes for universal peace.
- **S12**：Overwhelmed by guilt, Snow then commits suicide.
- **S13**：At the Qin palace, the king reluctantly orders Nameless killed, being urged thereto by his court.
- **S14**：As the film ends, Nameless receives a hero's funeral and a closing text identifies the king as Qin Shi Huang.

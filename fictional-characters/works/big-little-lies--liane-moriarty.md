- **S1**：## Synopsis
- **S2**：[ edit ] Jane, a single mother, is on her way to Pirriwee Public School in Sydney 's Northern Beaches , where her son Ziggy is starting kindergarten.
- **S3**：On the way, she meets Madeline, another mother with a daughter of the same age.
- **S4**：Madeline's friend Celeste is also sending her twin sons, Max and Josh, to school.
- **S5**：The two strike up a friendship with Jane.
- **S6**：All three of them have their own problems: Madeline is resentful that her daughter from her previous marriage is growing close to her ex-husband's new wife, Bonnie; Celeste is physically abused by her rich banker husband, Perry; and Jane was raped and left to raise her son Ziggy on her own.
- **S7**：To make matters worse for her, Ziggy is accused of bullying Amabella, his future classmate, during orientation.
- **S8**：As months pass, the three become close and Jane shares her experience with the other women.
- **S9**：Jane tells the two other women that Ziggy is the result of a rape by a man named Saxon Banks when Jane was 19.
- **S10**：Celeste and Madeline realize that the father is Perry's cousin, but decide to keep it from Jane for the time being.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, Celeste's marriage becomes even more violent and she starts meeting with a counselor and rents an apartment for herself and her sons without Perry's knowledge.
- **S12**：Ziggy is once again accused of bullying Amabella, and again denies it.
- **S13**：Jane finds out that Ziggy is keeping a secret about who is hurting Amabella and persuades him to write down the name of the child, which turns out to be Max, one of Celeste's twins, but she is not sure how to broach the subject with Celeste.
- **S14**：On the night of Pirriwee Public's Trivia Night, Josh tells Celeste that it is Max, not Ziggy, who is bullying the other children.
- **S15**：She realizes that Max is emulating Perry and finally decides to leave him.
- **S16**：Perry finds out about Celeste's apartment, and despite a heated argument, the couple still go to the Trivia Night.
- **S17**：Once they reach the school, Jane sees Perry and realizes that he is, in fact, the man who raped her and confronts him in front of Madeline and Celeste.
- **S18**：Celeste recalls Perry's childhood stories where he used his cousin's name to avoid trouble for himself.
- **S19**：Perry admits to raping Jane but shows no remorse.
- **S20**：In the ensuing argument between them, Perry hits Celeste.
- **S21**：Enraged by what she has witnessed, Bonnie pushes him, and he falls from the balcony to his death.
- **S22**：In the aftermath of the fall, Madeline finds out that Bonnie's father was abusive and seeing Perry's violence brought back memories of witnessing her father beat her mother.
- **S23**：Everybody on the balcony decides to lie to protect Bonnie, but she turns herself in.
- **S24**：She is convicted of accidental manslaughter and sentenced to 200 hours of community service.
- **S25**：A year after Perry's death, Celeste works in a family law firm and sets up a trust fund for Ziggy.
- **S26**：She speaks publicly about her abusive relationship, starting her speech with: "This can happen to anyone."

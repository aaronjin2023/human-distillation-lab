- **S11**：## Plot
- **S12**：[ edit ] Computer scientist Frans Balder abandons a prestigious job in Silicon Valley and returns to Sweden to take custody of his autistic son August.
- **S13**：Balder is informed by several law enforcement agencies that he is in danger of a criminal organization who call themselves the "Spider Society", but he ignores their warnings, preferring to focus on his neglected son.
- **S14**：August exhibits savant syndrome ; he produces drawings of impressive veracity and demonstrates facility with numbers.
- **S15**：Meanwhile, one year since Millennium magazine's scoop on The Section, the publication has stagnated and is in danger of losing creative control to outside investors.
- **S16**：Balder's former associate, Linus Brandell, tells Mikael Blomkvist about Balder and his tumultuous history, mentioning that some of his activities were aided by Lisbeth Salander .
- **S17**：Spurred by a childhood memory, Salander attempts to track down someone from her past, leading her to the Spider Society.
- **S18**：She helps a group of hackers gain access into NSA servers, much to the fury of the agency's top cyber security agent, Edwin Needham.
- **S19**：NSA agent Alona Casales and SÄPO agent Gabriella Grane are given the task of pursuing Salander and the Spider Society, which are a group of elite Russian criminals led by an individual named "Thanos".
- **S20**：Grane calls Balder with concerns about his safety, and Balder hires Milton Security for protection.
- **S21**：He also reaches out to Blomkvist, hoping to confess his concerns to a respected journalist.
- **S22**：Blomkvist agrees to meet him, but as he arrives, an assassin, self-identified in the narration as Jan Holtser, kills Balder.
- **S23**：Blomkvist reaches out to Salander, hoping to harness her talents for the investigation.
- **S24**：August's mother is unable to cope with his disorder and remands him to a care facility.
- **S25**：However, Blomkvist realizes that August is drawing a picture of his father's killer.
- **S26**：Holtser's superior, a woman called Kira, orders him to eliminate the child, but Salander rescues August and reaches out to Blomkvist and Millennium editor Erika Berger.
- **S27**：Grane, a friend of Berger's, offers her a beachfront vacation property as a safe house .
- **S28**：Blomkvist learns from Balder's former associates that he had hired Salander to confirm that someone had robbed him, implicating executives inside Solifon, the company he worked for, in the theft.
- **S29**：Thus, Balder went to Solifon to attempt to gain more evidence, discovering that they were collaborating with both the NSA and the Spider Society in their espionage efforts.
- **S30**：It was this investigation that resulted in his death.
- **S31**：Needham is pulled off the investigation into Salander's hack, as the NSA is eager to cover up the agency's involvement.
- **S32**：He learns of an individual known as "Wasp", and catches the allusions to Marvel Comics characters Janet Van Dyne , the founding member of the Avengers , and their perennial foe .
- **S33**：Meanwhile, a woman calling herself Rebecka Mattson attempts to seduce Blomkvist.
- **S34**：After talking to Salander's former guardian, Holger Palmgren, he learns she is Lisbeth's long-missing twin sister Camilla, who has taken over part of her father Zalachenko's criminal network and is using it to strike against Lisbeth.
- **S35**：Needham comes to Sweden and contacts Blomkvist, asking to meet Salander so that she can help him secure the NSA database.
- **S36**：However, they are interrupted by Salander: she and August are under attack at the safe house by the Spider Society by the orders of Camilla AKA Kira AKA Thanos.
- **S37**：Lisbeth fends them off, wounding Holtser and several other suspects.
- **S38**：Though Camilla gets away, Lisbeth and August do as well; additionally, August has already drawn the picture implicating Holtser and helping Salander decrypt the last of the NSA's secrets.
- **S39**：Grane takes a position at the UN working for human rights .
- **S40**：Millennium publishes an exposė of the affair which restores their credibility; additionally, a new investment from Gibraltar (the site of Salander's Wasp Enterprises) allows Millennium to buy out their meddlesome investors.
- **S41**：Needham, with the magazine as ammunition, ousts the crooked officers at the NSA.
- **S42**：Salander visits Blomkvist to renew their friendship.

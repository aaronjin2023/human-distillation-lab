### Lara Croft

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d6c52381370fddc9` · 独立版本：`profile-6eaf57d06a7dfe4e`

- **性格倾向**：在危险探险中倾向果断自持，也能警惕亲密关系中的风险（行为推断；S11、S12、S21）

### Terry Sheridan

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-e6a4157432187673` · 独立版本：`profile-c54928dc69647577`

- **性格倾向**：在合作中显出务实能干的一面，但面对珍宝也倾向冒险逐利（行为推断；S9、S30）
- **核心动机**：任务结束时希望取得魔盒，作为自己参与寻宝的补偿（摘要直接叙述；S30）
- **关系模式**：与Lara重燃旧情，却因魔盒归属的冲突走向致命对立（摘要直接叙述；S8、S11、S30、S31）
- **决策方式**：在索取魔盒遭拒后选择拔枪，以武力压过双方的分歧（摘要直接叙述；S30、S31）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：曾解救同伴并协助对敌，随后却为取得魔盒威胁爱人。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film begins on Santorini island, Greece, during a wedding when it is interrupted by an earthquake.
- **S2**：The earthquake uncovers the Luna Temple, built by Alexander the Great to house his most prized treasures.
- **S3**：Among these treasures is a glowing orb with a pattern resembling a code etched into it.
- **S4**：Lara finds this orb; but it is stolen by the crime lord Chen Lo .
- **S5**：Lara  only just manages to escape with a strange medallion, while her two companions are killed by Chen's men.
- **S6**：Lara is tasked by MI6 to find Pandora's Box, an object from ancient legends that supposedly contains a deadly plague, before Nobel Prize-winning scientist turned bio-terrorist Jonathan Reiss  can get his hands on it.
- **S7**：The key to finding the box, which is hidden in the mysterious Cradle of Life, is a magical luminous sphere that serves as a map, the one stolen by Chen Lo in Santorini , who plans to sell the sphere to Reiss.
- **S8**：To help her track down Chen Lo and the ball, Lara recruits an old lover, Terry Sheridan , a former mercenary and Royal Marine, who was in prison in the Republic of Kazakhstan.
- **S9**：Among the action sequences that take place during this time are Lara and Terry's entry into China, a fight scene in suburban Shanghai, and a leap off the then-under-construction International Finance Centre skyscraper in Hong Kong, using special winged flight-suits that Terry had arranged for, landing on a ship out in the Kowloon Bay after taking the Orb from Reiss.
- **S10**：It is revealed that the medallion shows how to unlock the information in the Orb; a certain arrangement of musical sounds.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, Lara and Terry begin to fall in love again; but Lara starts to back away from him, knowing that she could easily kill him if he betrays her.
- **S12**：She seduces him one morning, then leaves him handcuffed to a bedpost, saying 'I'm not leaving because I can't kill you; I'm leaving because I could'.
- **S13**：Lara finds her way to a floating houseboat where a Chinese family is watching Spongebob cartoons when Lara asks to use their satellite to communicate with Bryce.
- **S14**：After some technical difficulties, Bryce sends Lara the file and she uses the sounds make the The Orb reveal the location of the Cradle of Life, which is revealed to be somewhere near Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania, thanks to an ancient hologram produced by the Orb.
- **S15**：Lara sends this info to Bryce  back at Croft Manor.
- **S16**：After the transmission, it is revealed that Reiss and his men had infiltrated the mansion and captured Bryce and James "Hilly" Hillary .
- **S17**：Lara meets up with Kosa , an African friend who serves as her translator as they obtain the information from a local tribe about the Cradle of Life.
- **S18**：Kosa translates for the tribe's Chief, stating that the Cradle of Life is in a crater protected by the "Shadow Guardians".
- **S19**：As the expedition sets out, Lara, Kosa, and the tribesmen with them are ambushed by Reiss' soldiers.
- **S20**：More tribesmen are killed by Reiss' soldiers with some of the soldiers being killed by Lara in the fight.
- **S21**：The fight ends with Lara surrendering to overwhelming odds as Reiss' helicopter started to land.
- **S22**：Reiss and Sean  threaten to kill Bryce, Hillary, and Kosa unless Lara leads him and his Tribeman to the Cradle of Life.
- **S23**：Upon arrival at the crater, they encounter the Shadow Guardians, humanoid creatures which kill immediately when they sense a movement and vanish into wet patches on dead trees.
- **S24**：The wet patching being the blood of their victims.
- **S25**：Sean and most of Reiss' soldiers are killed by the creatures.
- **S26**：When Lara drops the Orb into the hole that opens the entrance to the Cradle of Life, the Guardians fall to pieces and both Lara and Reiss are drawn into the Cradle of Life, a labyrinthine cavern made of some strange crystalline substance, racked by bolts of energy where 'sky and earth are one, direction meaningless'.
- **S27**：Inside, there is a pool of highly corrosive black acid , which holds the box and where the laws of physics do not apply, as Lara and Reiss are able to walk  along the ceiling of the cave.
- **S28**：Terry arrives, frees Reiss' captives, and catches up to Lara.
- **S29**：Following a climatic fistfight between Lara and Reiss, Reiss is knocked into the acid pool by Lara after he is distracted by Terry, killing and dissolving him.
- **S30**：When the couple tries to leave, Terry attempts to take the box as compensation for finding it; but she staunchly refuses to let him, knowing the danger if the box were ever open.
- **S31**：Despite her love for him, this results in Lara being forced to fatally shoot him in self-defense just after Terry draws his own gun preparing to shoot Lara.
- **S32**：Lara is tempted to open the box herself, but realises that some artifacts are not meant to be found.
- **S33**：Placing the box back in the acid pool, she leaves, giving the medallion key to the tribe leader.
- **S34**：Lara and Kosa leave the village, along with Bryce and Hillary, who were being prepared for a tribal wedding ceremony, which they didn't know they were being prepared for.
- **S35**：The film ends with Lara driving the jeep away from the setting sun.

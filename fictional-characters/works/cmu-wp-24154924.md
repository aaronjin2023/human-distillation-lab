## 角色资料

### Lisbeth Salander

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-ba6e273f97ea4ce6` · 独立版本：`profile-74352958e8c44bf1`

- **性格倾向**：遭受控制和追杀时，她表现出强烈自主性与独自应险的倾向（行为推断；S4、S23、S28、S34）
- **核心动机**：被列为谋杀嫌疑人后，她要求寻找Zala并持续追踪Niedermann（摘要直接叙述；S12、S14、S28、S29）
- **决策方式**：追索敌人时，她结合黑客手段与实地监视，并在受困后临机自救（行为推断；S3、S28、S29、S43）
- **关系模式**：她虽保持行踪秘密，仍向朋友道谢并为Miriam受牵连亲自致歉（摘要直接叙述；S22、S24、S25）
- **价值取舍**：面对曾经施暴的监护人，她坚持让对方保留记录罪行的惩罚标记（行为推断；S3、S4）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Mikael Blomkvist

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-1f4910ee36011dcd` · 独立版本：`profile-22c376d163b61014`

- **性格倾向**：面对警方劝退和朋友涉案，他表现出持续追索而不轻易放手的倾向（行为推断；S13、S15、S27、S33）
- **核心动机**：他希望查清命案并及时找到可能独自赴险的Salander（行为推断；S15、S23、S33）
- **决策方式**：调查中他整合朋友提供的线索、私人材料与公司记录逐步追踪（行为推断；S15、S26、S27、S30、S32、S33）
- **关系模式**：他通过留下可供破解的信息维持合作，并获得Salander的友谊确认（摘要直接叙述；S22）
- **价值取舍**：在亲自寻找Salander之际，他仍重视让警方掌握关键调查材料（行为推断；S33）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：With her new wealth, Lisbeth Salander  purchases an apartment in Stockholm.
- **S2**：On returning to Sweden after nearly a year living abroad, Salander reconnects with her former lover Miriam Wu  and offers her free use of her previous apartment in return for forwarding her mail.
- **S3**：Later, Salander confronts her guardian, Nils Bjurman ([[Peter Andersson  after hacking into his email account and discovering he has an appointment booked with a tattoo removal specialist.
- **S4**：Threatening him with his own gun, she warns him not to remove the tattoo that she etched on his abdomen as revenge for sexually abusing her, marking him as "a pervert, a rapist and a sadistic pig".
- **S5**：Millennium magazine welcomes Dag Svensson , a new journalist who is writing an exposé on prostitution and human trafficking in Sweden.
- **S6**：Dag's girlfriend, Mia Bergman, is writing her doctoral thesis on sex trafficking.
- **S7**：Dag is nearly finished with the story and is confronting those who will be exposed by the article.
- **S8**：Dag and his girlfriend are about to leave on a holiday and ask Mikael Blomkvist  to come to his apartment and collect some photographs.
- **S9**：At the same time Dag also asks Mikael to inquire about someone called "Zala", who may have a connection to his present research.
- **S10**：Mikael arrives at their apartment late at night to collect photographs for the article but finds them shot dead.
- **S11**：The gun used is tracked to Bjurman, who is also dead.
- **S12**：Salander is the prime suspect, as her fingerprints are on the gun.
- **S13**：Bublanski, the police officer leading the investigation, advises Blomkvist that he should stay out of it.
- **S14**：Salander tells Blomkvist she did not kill Dag and Mia and that he needs to find the mysterious "Zala".
- **S15**：In an effort to find Salander, Blomkvist contacts her boxing trainer and friend, Paolo Roberto.
- **S16**：While he is unaware of Salander's whereabouts, he does know Miriam, who also trained with them, and promises to pay her a visit.
- **S17**：Near her apartment, Paolo witnesses Miriam being kidnapped by strongman Niedermann.
- **S18**：Paolo follows his car to a deserted barn, where he hears him beat Miriam for information about Salander.
- **S19**：Paolo comes in to rescue her but Niedermann incapacitates him.
- **S20**：Niedermann sets the barn aflame, believing he has killed Paolo and Miriam, but they have actually secretly escaped.
- **S21**：News breaks of the attack and Paolo gives his account to the police.
- **S22**：After Blomkvist leaves information he has discovered about the case on his computer for Salander to hack into and read, she leaves a message to him saying, "Thank you for being my friend".
- **S23**：He realizes that she intends to set out alone to find the man who framed her and that she may not survive.
- **S24**：A disguised Salander visits Miriam in hospital to apologize for getting her involved.
- **S25**：Without giving anything away, Salander confirms the police sketch of Niedermann with Miriam and then disappears.
- **S26**：Knowing now that he is Salander's friend, Miriam calls Blomkvist to the hospital to give him keys that Salander dropped accidentally during her visit.
- **S27**：Noticing they are for a post office box, Blomkvist is able to access and read Salander's mail and track down her apartment.
- **S28**：Meanwhile, Salander continues her efforts to find Niedermann by watching his post office box.
- **S29**：She sees someone check the post box and follows him to a small house near Gosseberga.
- **S30**：Researching through the material in her apartment Blomkvist finds the video of Bjurman raping Salander.
- **S31**：In the offices of Millennium magazine, Paolo explains he tracked down Niedermann and learned that he has congenital analgesia: Niedermann is unable to feel pain.
- **S32**：They trace Niedermann to a company owned by "Karl Axel Bodin".
- **S33**：Blomkvist has Erika Berger make copies of the documents including the 1993 police report and forwards the originals to Bublanski, and sets out to find Salander.
- **S34**：Salander crosses the grounds and enters the Gosseberga house, but Niedermann has been alerted by motion detectors and knocks her out.
- **S35**：She awakens to see her father, Zalachenko, an old man who walks with a stick and is heavily scarred by the burns she inflicted as a child.
- **S36**：He dismisses her mother as a whore and belittles her rape at the hands of Bjurman.
- **S37**：He reveals that Niedermann is her half-brother.
- **S38**：Niedermann killed Bjurman to prevent him from revealing any of Zalachenko's secrets.
- **S39**：Zalachenko is confident he will not be caught, since being an invalid means the idea of his involvement in the murders lacks plausibility.
- **S40**：They lead Salander to a shallow grave in the woods.
- **S41**：She tells him the police will find him soon and all that he has said has been published online through her hidden cellphone.
- **S42**：Seeing through her bluff, he shoots her several times as she attempts to escape and buries her alive.
- **S43**：Left for dead, Salander digs her way out using her silver-plated cigarette case.
- **S44**：Hidden in the woodshed, she surprises Zalachenko with an axe to the head.
- **S45**：Salander scares Niedermann off with the help of Zalachenko's gun, just as Blomkvist finds them.
- **S46**：Ambulances and police arrive to take away Salander and Zalachenko, who are both badly hurt but still alive.

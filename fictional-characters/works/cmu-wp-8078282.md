## 角色资料

### Lady Sarah Ashley

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-18095735d18d7ee8` · 独立版本：`profile-7bdb14330869339b`

- **性格倾向**：面对雇员欺凌弱者时，她倾向于主动介入并承担后果（行为推断；S7）
- **核心动机**：她后来将保护视如养子的男孩作为重要行动目标（摘要直接叙述；S24）
- **决策方式**：经营危机中，她倾向亲自接手并争取有经验者协助（行为推断；S7、S11、S15）
- **成长变化**：她从要求出售牧场转向经营牧场并珍惜当地生活（摘要直接叙述；S1、S7、S17、S20）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：原想出售牧场，却在保护男孩的过程中选择留下经营。

### Drover

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-75580d9ee8396fb9` · 独立版本：`profile-cbe5b7da6d9f82da`

- **性格倾向**：在族群排斥的环境里，他倾向坚持自己的交往选择（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **价值取舍**：在当地白人排斥原住民时，他仍维持跨族群情谊（行为推断；S12、S13、S15）
- **决策方式**：面对儿童被困的危险，他倾向联合伙伴直接实施营救（行为推断；S28）
- **关系模式**：他与莎拉及男孩建立共同生活关系，争执后一度离开（摘要直接叙述；S20、S27）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：与莎拉争执离开后，仍返回并营救她牵挂的男孩。

### Neil Fletcher

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-f695fa29c26b4aae` · 独立版本：`profile-389a3c22b880a8bc`

- **性格倾向**：在利益争夺中，他表现出以威胁和暴力压制阻力的倾向（行为推断；S7、S21、S30）
- **核心动机**：他先谋取牧场控制权，后来进一步攫取整个养牛帝国（摘要直接叙述；S4、S21）
- **决策方式**：计划破灭时，他倾向直接向男孩施加致命暴力（行为推断；S30）
- **情绪反应**：计划彻底破灭时，他的绝望转化为对男孩的致命攻击（摘要直接叙述；S30）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：男孩是他的亲生儿子，却仍成为他威胁和攻击的对象。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1939, Lady Sarah Ashley  travels from England to northern Australia to force her philandering husband to sell his faltering cattle station, Faraway Downs.
- **S2**：Her husband sends an independent cattle drover , called "Drover", to transport her to Faraway Downs.
- **S3**：Lady Sarah's husband is murdered shortly before she arrives, and the authorities tell her that the killer is an Aboriginal elder, "King George" .
- **S4**：Meanwhile, cattle station manager Neil Fletcher  is trying to gain control of Faraway Downs, so that Lesley 'King' Carney  will have a complete cattle monopoly, giving him negotiating leverage with an Australian army officer, Captain Dutton .
- **S5**：The childless Lady Sarah is captivated by the boy Nullah , who has an Aboriginal mother and a white father.
- **S6**：Nullah tells her that he has seen her cattle being driven onto Carney's land — in other words, stolen from her.
- **S7**：Because of this Fletcher mistreats Nullah and threatens him and his mother, so Lady Sarah fires Fletcher and decides to try to run the cattle station herself.
- **S8**：When Nullah and his mother hide from the authorities in a water tower, his mother drowns.
- **S9**：Lady Sarah comforts Nullah by singing the song "Over the Rainbow" from the film The Wizard of Oz.
- **S10**：Nullah tells her that "King George" is his grandfather, and that like the Wizard, he too is a "magic man".
- **S11**：Lady Sarah persuades Drover to take the cattle to Darwin for sale.
- **S12**：Drover is friendly with the Aborigines, and therefore shunned by many of the other whites in the territory.
- **S13**：It is revealed that he was married to an Aboriginal woman, who died after being refused medical treatment in a hospital because of her race.
- **S14**：Lady Sarah also reveals she is barren.
- **S15**：Drover leads a team of six other riders, including Lady Sarah, Drover's Aboriginal brother-in-law Magarri , Nullah, and the station's accountant Kipling Flynn ([[Jack Thompson , to drive the 1,500 cattle to Darwin.
- **S16**：They encounter various obstacles along the way, including a fire set by Carney's men that scares the cattle, resulting in the death of Flynn when the group tries to stop the cattle from stampeding over a cliff.
- **S17**：Lady Sarah and Drover fall in love, and she gains a new appreciation for the Australian territory.
- **S18**：The team drive the cattle through the dangerous Never Never desert.
- **S19**：Then, when at last delivering the cattle in Darwin, the group has to race them onto the ship before Carney's cattle are loaded.
- **S20**：Afterwards, Lady Sarah, Nullah, and Drover live together happily at Faraway Downs for two years.
- **S21**：Meanwhile, Fletcher kills Carney, marries his daughter Cath Carney, takes over Carney's cattle empire, and continues to menace Lady Sarah.
- **S22**：It is established that Fletcher was the actual murderer of Lady Sarah's husband, and is also Nullah's father.
- **S23**：Nullah is drawn to perform a walkabout with his grandfather "King George", but is instead taken by the authorities and sent to live on Mission Island (a fictitious island, but inspired by [[Bathurst Island  with the other half-Aboriginal children .
- **S24**：Lady Sarah, who has come to regard Nullah as her adopted son, vows to rescue him.
- **S25**：Meanwhile, she works as a radio operator in Darwin during the escalation of World War II.
- **S26**：When the Japanese attack the island and Darwin in 1942, Lady Sarah fears that Nullah has been killed.
- **S27**：Drover, who had quarrelled with Lady Sarah and left, returns to Darwin and hears  that she has been killed in the bombing.
- **S28**：Drover learns of Nullah's abduction to Mission Island, and goes with Magarri and a young priest to rescue him and the other children.
- **S29**：Meanwhile, Lady Sarah is about to evacuate, but when Drover and the children sail back into port at Darwin, and Nullah plays "Over the Rainbow" on his harmonica, Lady Sarah hears the music and the three are reunited.
- **S30**：Fletcher, distraught at the ruination of his plans, attempts to shoot Nullah, but is speared by King George and falls dead.
- **S31**：Lady Sarah, Drover, and Nullah return to the safety of remote Faraway Downs.
- **S32**：There, King George calls for Nullah, who returns to the Outback with his grandfather.

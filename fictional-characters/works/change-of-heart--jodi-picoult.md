### Shay Bourne

- **性格倾向**：在亲人面临惩罚时表现出自我牺牲倾向，愿意替妹妹承担罪责（行为推断；S26、S27）
- **核心动机**：他希望向克莱尔捐心，神父作证称这关乎其救赎信念（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13、S21）

- **关系模式**：他对妹妹采取保护立场，即使承担纵火责任也未揭露她（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26、S27）

### June Nealon

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4b0b9c4f9b29eaa3` · 独立版本：`profile-adc533b7986daac2`

- **性格倾向**：在女儿住院的压力下，表现出愿意突破规则给予安慰的倾向（行为推断；S20）
- **核心动机**：她把宠物偷偷带入医院，明确希望改善患病女儿的心情（摘要直接叙述；S20）
- **情绪反应**：面对被认定杀害家人的捐赠者，她接受心脏的决定夹杂怨愤（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **决策方式**：面对重大移植安排，她先作出接受决定，尚未及时与女儿讨论（行为推断；S15、S20）
- **关系模式**：她对死去家人的追问与对存活女儿的照护，形成双重家庭牵挂（行为推断；S15、S20）

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Prologue [ edit ] A man, Jack, is killed by an impaired driver, leaving his wife, June, and his daughter, Elizabeth behind.
- **S3**：At the scene of the accident, June meets Kurt Nealon, a police officer, who becomes a close friend and later June's husband.
- **S4**：A number of years later, June is pregnant and Kurt plans to create an addition onto their home.
- **S5**：A young man named Shay Bourne offers to help with the addition, to which June explains is "the beginning of the end." The Trial [ edit ] Elizabeth, June's eldest daughter, and Kurt are found murdered.
- **S6**：Shay, the construction worker, is identified as the only suspect.
- **S7**：The case unfolds during the trial which becomes a media sensation.
- **S8**：The jury convicts Shay of two counts of capital murder .
- **S9**：The jury deliberates on the death penalty, and after much time, 11 members agree, with Michael Wright, a young man about Shay's age, being the last juror to agree on the death penalty conviction, after being coerced by the other jury members.
- **S10**：Eleven Years Later [ edit ] Shay Bourne is transferred to the I-tier at the Concord state prison.
- **S11**：Shay resides in a cell next to Lucius DuFresne, an artist.
- **S12**：During the night, Shay confides in Lucius that he wants to donate his heart to a little girl he saw on TV in need of a heart transplant .
- **S13**：The little girl on television that Shay spoke about is revealed to be the daughter of June and the late Kurt, Claire, who has a terminal heart condition.
- **S14**：June agrees to meet Shay in for a restorative justice meeting.
- **S15**：There she asks him, "Why did you do it?" to which Shay answers cryptically, "She was better off dead." Out of spite, June agrees to take Shay's donated heart once he is executed.
- **S16**：Maggie, an attorney, begins the legal process to petition the commissioner of corrections to allow Shay to be hanged rather than executed by lethal injection , so he will be able to donate his heart to Claire.
- **S17**：Claire's doctor is able to determine that Shay is a perfect heart transplant match.
- **S18**：Maggie, Shay's lawyer, arranges a dinner meeting with Dr.
- **S19**：Christian Gallagher, a doctor she's consulting to discuss organ donation for Shay.
- **S20**：June sneaks Dudley, the family's spaniel, into the hospital to make Claire feel better, and a nurse reveals the upcoming transplant to Claire, which June hadn't discussed with her daughter yet.
- **S21**：Shay's trial begins, and Father Michael testifies to Shay's religious belief that he needs to donate his heart to Claire to be redeemed.
- **S22**：Father Michael uses Shay's quotations from the Gnostic Gospels as his religious foundation.
- **S23**：Ian Fletcher testifies as an expert on the Gnostic Gospels.
- **S24**：Father Michael, privately, admits to Shay that he was on the jury that convicted him to death.
- **S25**：Father Michael is able to locate Shay's sister, Grace, and tries to convince her to forgive Shay for setting the fire that ultimately disfigured her face.
- **S26**：However, Grace actually started the fire in an attempt to kill their abusive father.
- **S27**：Shay took the blame to protect his sister.
- **S28**：While Shay testifies, all of his chains (including the belly chain and handcuffs ) fall away from him for no apparent reason.
- **S29**：During Shay's trial, it is revealed that Kurt was sexually abusing his step-daughter , Elizabeth.
- **S30**：At the time of the murders, Shay walked in on Kurt assaulting Elizabeth.
- **S31**：Shay killed Kurt, who accidentally shot Elizabeth instead of Shay.
- **S32**：The trial concludes, and Shay is granted his request to be executed by hanging to be able to donate his heart to Claire.
- **S33**：Epilogue [ edit ] Three weeks after Claire's surgery, she is able to go home.
- **S34**：While resting at home, Grace, Shay's sister, visits her.
- **S35**：Claire sees that her dog Dudley has died, but when she picks him up and holds him to her chest, his heart begins to beat again.

### Shaggy Rogers

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-9e6251a05f67fe08` · 独立版本：`profile-6673e2e50de244b0`

- **性格倾向**：在伙伴遇险的情境中，他表现出愿意冒险救人的倾向（行为推断；S10、S18、S23）
- **核心动机**：面对史酷比被夺取灵魂的危险，他的行动集中于救回伙伴（行为推断；S16、S18、S22）
- **价值取舍**：在与史酷比争执后，他仍倾向将伙伴安全置于分歧之上（行为推断；S10）
- **决策方式**：在营救受困者时，他倾向直接解除囚禁和力量控制的源头（行为推断；S11、S22、S23）
- **关系模式**：他与史酷比虽会争吵，危急时仍表现出牢固的救援承诺（行为推断；S10、S18）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他与史酷比发生争执，却立即跳入洞中救援对方。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After solving the Luna Ghost mystery, constant arguments among the members of Mystery, Inc. cause the gang to go their separate ways.
- **S2**：Two years later, Shaggy Rogers and Scooby-Doo are approached to solve the mystery of the popular horror resort Spooky Island, reuniting with Fred Jones, Velma Dinkley and Daphne Blake, although none of the latter are thrilled to see each other.
- **S3**：On the island, the gang meets Emile Mondavarious, the park's owner, who explains his theory that visitors are being cursed.
- **S4**：Shaggy falls in love with a girl named Mary Jane, while Scooby is mysteriously targeted by demonic creatures.
- **S5**：Velma meets a man named N'Goo Tuana and his minion, the famous luchador Zarkos, who explains that demons rule the island.
- **S6**：The gang visits the island's abandoned ghost castle, where Daphne finds a tetrahedron-shaped artifact called the Daemon Ritus and Velma and Fred find a strange classroom with videos designed to address non-humans.
- **S7**：When the gang returns to the hotel, they are attacked by the island demons, who kidnap numerous tourists including Fred, Velma and Mondavarious.
- **S8**：The next day, Daphne is captured by Zarkos, while Shaggy and Scooby discover Fred, Velma and the tourists are now possessed by the demons.
- **S9**：The two flee with Mary Jane, until Scooby realizes she is possessed as well.
- **S10**：In the midst of an argument between Scooby and Shaggy, Scooby falls down a hole, followed by Shaggy, who dives in to save him.
- **S11**：Shaggy comes across a vat containing the protoplasmic souls of everyone who was captured, including the rest of the gang, and releases the gangs' souls to their bodies.
- **S12**：Velma discovers the demons are destroyed in sunlight, while Daphne and Fred's souls end up in the wrong bodies.
- **S13**：Shaggy steals the Daemon Ritus and reunites with the gang after their souls correct themselves.
- **S14**：Coming across Voodoo Maestro, the gang learns that if the leader of the demons absorbs a pure soul through the Daemon Ritus, then the demons shall rule the world for the next 10,000 years.
- **S15**：The pure soul belongs to Scooby, while the demons' leader is Mondavarious.
- **S16**：The gang puts their differences aside and finally work together to save Scooby.
- **S17**：They form a plan but it fails and Scooby's soul is extracted.
- **S18**：Scooby is saved by Shaggy, wounding Mondavarious in the attempt.
- **S19**：Fred and Velma discover he is actually a robot, controlled by none other than Scrappy Doo, who the gang abandoned years ago due to his egotism.
- **S20**：Now vengeful, Scrappy transforms into a demon to destroy the gang and rule the world using the tourists' souls he absorbed.
- **S21**：Daphne fights Zarkos above the island's caves, knocking him through the roof, which exposes the demons to sunlight and kills them.
- **S22**：Shaggy attacks Scrappy and rips the Daemon Ritus from his chest, freeing the souls and reverting Scrappy to his original self.
- **S23**：Shaggy finds the real Mondavarious trapped in a hole and frees him.
- **S24**：Scrappy and his minions are arrested, while Mystery, Inc. is reunited to continue solving mysteries.

## 角色资料

### Jack Hall

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-dfce6a5156fbbcc4` · 独立版本：`profile-76dfd7d21f28a56e`

- **性格倾向**：在危机中倾向持续投入行动，遇到交通受阻仍继续寻找儿子。（行为推断；S22、S23、S34）
- **核心动机**：他在会议上提出气候发现，灾变后又赶赴曼哈顿寻找儿子。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S22）
- **决策方式**：面对异常气候数据，他与团队及Tokada合并资料建立预测模型。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S6、S7）
- **关系模式**：Sam受困前曾打电话给他，他随后与团队前往曼哈顿寻找儿子。（摘要直接叙述；S17、S22）
- **价值取舍**：寻找儿子的途中遭遇车祸仍继续前行，显出此时对亲情的重视。（行为推断；S22、S23、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他原以为变化需数百至数千年，实际灾变却在七至十天内发生。

### Sam Hall

### Raymond Becker

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-fb972d9a2b5fdbad` · 独立版本：`profile-345dd6392d6b447f`

- **性格倾向**：接任总统后，他在获知幸存者消息时表现出采取救援行动的倾向。（行为推断；S26、S35、S36、S37）
- **决策方式**：收到图书馆幸存者的无线电消息后，他下令派直升机进入纽约。（摘要直接叙述；S35、S36）
- **价值取舍**：他要求继续寻找其他幸存者，显示此时重视扩大救援覆盖范围。（行为推断；S37）
- **沟通方式**：他在首次向全国发表讲话时，同时下令搜救其他幸存者。（摘要直接叙述；S37）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Jack Hall  is a paleoclimatologist on an expedition in Antarctica with colleagues Frank  and Jason .
- **S2**：They are drilling for ice core samples on the Larsen Ice Shelf for the NOAA when the shelf breaks off and Jack almost falls to his death, but he is saved at the last second, and they finish their expedition.
- **S3**：Later on, in New Delhi, India, Jack presents his findings on global warming at a United Nations conference, where diplomats and Vice President of the United States Raymond Becker  are unconvinced by Jack's findings.
- **S4**：However, Professor Terry Rapson  of the Hedland Climate Research Centre in Scotland believes in Jack's theories.
- **S5**：Several buoys in the North Atlantic simultaneously show a massive drop in the ocean temperature, and Rapson concludes that melting polar ice is disrupting the North Atlantic current.
- **S6**：He contacts Jack, whose paleoclimatological weather model shows how climate changes caused the first Ice Age, and can predict what will happen.
- **S7**：Jack thought the events would take hundreds or thousands of years, but his team, along with NASA's meteorologist Janet Tokada , builds a forecast model with their combined data.
- **S8**：Across the world, violent weather causes mass destruction, including a massive snowstorm in New Delhi, a powerful hailstorm striking Tokyo, Japan, and a series of devastating tornadoes in Los Angeles.
- **S9**：President Blake  authorizes the FAA to suspend all air traffic due to severe turbulence.
- **S10**：At the International Space Station  three astronauts see a huge storm system spanning the northern hemisphere, delaying their returning home.
- **S11**：The situation worsens when the latter develops into three massive hurricane-like super storms with eyes holding extremely cold air that instantly freezes anything it comes in contact with.
- **S12**：Jack's theories become reality in seven to ten days' time.
- **S13**：Meanwhile, Jack's son Sam  is in New York City for an academic competition with his friends Brian and Laura , where they also befriend a student named J.D. .
- **S14**：On the flight over there is severe turbulence and Sam grabs Laura's hand in fright.
- **S15**：During the competition, birds migrating south suddenly fill the sky as animals all over New York become agitated.
- **S16**：The weather becomes increasingly violent with intense winds and rains, causing the traffic-jammed Manhattan streets to become flooded knee-deep in a mix of rainwater, saltwater, and sewage.
- **S17**：Sam calls his father, promising to be on the next train home, but the subways and Grand Central Terminal are closed due to flooding.
- **S18**：As the storm worsens a massive storm surge hits Manhattan, causing major flooding and killing thousands in the chaos.
- **S19**：Sam and his friends seek shelter in the New York Public Library, but not before Laura gets wounded, cutting her leg on a submerged taxicab.
- **S20**：While survivors in the northern United States are forced to stay inside due to the cold, President Blake orders the evacuation of the southern states, causing almost all of the refugees to head to Mexico.
- **S21**：Initially Mexico closes its US border, but President Blake negotiates to open the border.
- **S22**：Jack and his team set out for Manhattan to find his son.
- **S23**：Their truck crashes into a tractor just past Philadelphia, so the group continues on snowshoes.
- **S24**：During the journey, Frank falls through the glass roof of a snow-covered shopping mall.
- **S25**：As Jason and Jack try to pull Frank up, the glass under them continues to crack and Frank sacrifices himself by cutting the rope and falling to the floor far below.
- **S26**：Meanwhile in Mexico, Vice President Raymond Becker hears from the Secretary of State that President Blake's motorcade was caught in one of the super storms before he could make it to Mexico, so Becker is sworn in as the new President.
- **S27**：Inside the library, Sam warns everyone to stay indoors, but few listen.
- **S28**：The small group that remains burns books to stay alive and breaks the vending machine for food.
- **S29**：Laura appeared to have a cold, so Sam comforts her and later confesses that he joined the team because he held feelings for her.
- **S30**：Laura and Sam share a kiss.
- **S31**：Soon afterward, the group find out that Laura is afflicted with blood poisoning from her cut leg's being infected by the sewage-tainted water, so Sam, Brian and J.D. search for penicillin in a derelict Russian cargo ship that drifted inland, and are attacked by starving wolves that have escaped from the local zoo.
- **S32**：The eye of the super storm begins to pass over the city, instantly freezing everything from the top down.
- **S33**：The three barely get back to the library with the medicine, food and supplies they were able to scavenge.
- **S34**：During the deep freeze, Jack and Jason take shelter in an abandoned Wendy's restaurant, then resume their journey after the astronauts confirm the storm's dissipation, finally arriving in New York.
- **S35**：They discover the library buried in snow, but find Sam's group alive.
- **S36**：They radio this in and the President orders in UH-60 Black Hawk helicopters flown in New York.
- **S37**：President Becker orders search and rescue teams to look for other survivors  as he gives his first address to the nation.
- **S38**：As the main characters are evacuated and flown over the city, they see that hundreds of other helicopters searching for more survivors find success as people from other skyscrapers flock to the roofs, looking for a ride to safety.
- **S39**：The movie concludes with the astronauts looking down at Earth from the Space Station, showing most of the northern hemisphere covered in ice and snow, with one of the astronauts calling it "the clearest atmosphere [he's] ever seen."

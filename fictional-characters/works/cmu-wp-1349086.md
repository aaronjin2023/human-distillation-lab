### Billy Costigan

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-a2e8fdb33f769a14` · 独立版本：`profile-50c8313158358f6e`

- **性格倾向**：在身份保障不断削弱时，他倾向坚持任务并主动寻找揭露内鬼的机会（行为推断；S18、S24、S30、S32）
- **核心动机**：他希望完成卧底任务、恢复真实警察身份，并揭发警方内部的背叛（摘要直接叙述；S18、S24、S30）
- **决策方式**：识破沙利文后，他借录音揭发其身份，并另行安排警员到场见证抓捕（行为推断；S24、S27、S30、S32、S33、S34）
- **关系模式**：他必须在犯罪组织中扮演同伙，同时依赖少数警界联系维持真实身份（行为推断；S4、S5、S15、S18、S24、S33）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：必须伪装成罪犯以履行警察职责，却因此难以获得同僚信任

### Colin Sullivan

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4a8bb68f0c41a655` · 独立版本：`profile-6fa73568cca3a585`

- **性格倾向**：面临身份败露时，他倾向迅速切断威胁并操控能够证明真相的材料（行为推断；S19、S22、S25、S38、S39）
- **核心动机**：他希望维持警界中的隐蔽身份，避免与科斯特洛的关系被揭穿（摘要直接叙述；S19、S25）
- **价值取舍**：得知旧日庇护者可能泄露身份时，他倾向将自保置于忠诚之前（行为推断；S19、S21、S22）
- **决策方式**：他利用警察权限追踪联系人、删除身份记录，并重塑案件的公开解释（行为推断；S13、S25、S39）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：获得警界赞誉的表面成功，依赖持续掩盖对警察体系的背叛

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Colin Sullivan  is introduced to organized crime by Irish-American mobster Frank Costello  in the Irish neighborhood of South Boston.
- **S2**：Costello trains him to become a mole inside the Massachusetts State Police.
- **S3**：Sullivan is accepted into the Special Investigations Unit, which focuses on organized crime.
- **S4**：Before he graduates from the police academy, Billy Costigan  is asked by Captain Queenan  and Staff Sergeant Dignam  to go undercover, as his family ties to organized crime make him a perfect infiltrator.
- **S5**：He drops out of the academy and does time in prison on a fake assault charge to increase his credibility.
- **S6**：As both infiltrate their respective organizations, Sullivan begins a romance with psychiatrist Madolyn Madden .
- **S7**：Costigan sees her for his probation and also develops a relationship with her.
- **S8**：After Costello escapes a sting operation, both moles become aware of the other's existence.
- **S9**：Sullivan is told to find the "rat" and asks Costello for information to determine who is the informer within his crew.
- **S10**：Costigan follows Costello into a movie theater where Costello gives Sullivan an envelope containing personal information on his crew members.
- **S11**：Costigan then chases Sullivan through Chinatown.
- **S12**：When it is over, neither man knows the other's identity.
- **S13**：Sullivan has Queenan tailed to a meeting with Costigan.
- **S14**：Costello's men go in and Queenan is killed.
- **S15**：When they exit, Costigan pretends he has come to join them.
- **S16**：Later, Costello's henchman, Fitzgibbons , reveals that Delahunt , a crew member, was an undercover cop.
- **S17**：Dignam is forced to step down as a consequence.
- **S18**：Using Queenan's phone, Sullivan reaches Costigan, who refuses to abort his mission.
- **S19**：Sullivan learns of Costello's role as an informant for the Federal Bureau of Investigation from Queenan's diary, causing him to worry about his identity being revealed.
- **S20**：With Costigan's help, Costello is traced to a cocaine drop-off, where a gunfight erupts between his crew and police, resulting in most of the crew being killed.
- **S21**：Costello, confronted by Sullivan, admits he is an occasional FBI informant.
- **S22**：Sullivan then shoots him multiple times.
- **S23**：With Costello dead, Sullivan is applauded the next day by everyone on the force.
- **S24**：In good faith, Costigan comes to him for restoration of his true identity, but notices the envelope from Costello on Sullivan's desk and flees.
- **S25**：Knowing he has been found out, Sullivan erases all records of Costigan from the police computer system.
- **S26**：Madolyn tells Sullivan that she is pregnant, but does not reveal who the father is.
- **S27**：Later, she discovers a package from Costigan containing a CD with recordings of Costello's conversations with Sullivan.
- **S28**：Sullivan walks in as she is listening and tries unsuccessfully to assuage her suspicions.
- **S29**：He contacts Costigan, who reveals that Costello recorded every conversation he had with Sullivan.
- **S30**：Costello's attorney left Costigan in possession of the recordings and he intends to implicate Sullivan.
- **S31**：They agree to meet at the building where Queenan died.
- **S32**：On the roof, Costigan catches Sullivan off-guard and handcuffs him.
- **S33**：As Costigan had secretly arranged, Officer Brown appears on the roof as well.
- **S34**：Shocked, Brown draws his gun on Costigan, who attempts to justify his actions by exposing Sullivan as the rat.
- **S35**：Costigan asks Brown why Dignam did not accompany him, but Brown does not answer.
- **S36**：Costigan leads Sullivan to the elevator.
- **S37**：When it reaches the ground floor, Costigan is shot in the head by Officer Barrigan, who then shoots Brown and reveals to Sullivan that Costello had more than one mole in the police.
- **S38**：When Barrigan turns, Sullivan shoots him in the head.
- **S39**：At police headquarters, Sullivan identifies Barrigan as the mole and has Costigan posthumously given the Medal of Merit.
- **S40**：At Costigan's funeral, Sullivan and Madolyn stand at the grave.
- **S41**：Sullivan attempts to talk to her, but she ignores him.
- **S42**：When Sullivan enters his apartment, Dignam, wearing hospital footies and surgical gloves, shoots him in the head with a silenced pistol.

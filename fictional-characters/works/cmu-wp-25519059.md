## 角色资料

### Arrietty

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-ecfc8a54e55ddac1` · 独立版本：`profile-43c432badf300c0e`

- **性格倾向**：在跨越族群边界时倾向自主而大胆，不完全依从父亲禁令（行为推断；S9、S10、S11）
- **价值取舍**：面对人类的帮助首先维护家庭自主，不愿被善意随意介入（行为推断；S11）
- **决策方式**：在母亲遇险时愿突破对人类的戒心，选择合作解决危机（行为推断；S29、S32）
- **关系模式**：与男孩从设防转为救援合作，分别时以发夹寄托纪念（摘要直接叙述；S11、S32、S38）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：维护家庭隐秘的需要，与主动接近人类的选择持续冲突。

### Sho

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-5e3f2303feccebde` · 独立版本：`profile-e3e67161b206e1fa`

- **性格倾向**：在初次表达善意时倾向按自己的设想行事，低估对方戒心（行为推断；S18、S19）
- **情绪反应**：面对成功希望不大的手术感到无力，起初以死亡必然自解（摘要直接叙述；S22、S23、S24）
- **价值取舍**：在朋友遭受追捕时把保护其安全置于保留发现证据之前（行为推断；S32、S34）
- **成长变化**：从认定生命结局无可改变，转向从朋友的求生中获得希望（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25、S37）

暂无支持：核心动机、决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：希望以赠予赢得接纳，擅自改造住所却加速了朋友离开。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A boy named Sho tells the audience he still remembers the week in summer he spent at his mother's childhood home with his maternal great aunt, Sadako, and the house maid, Haru.
- **S2**：When Sho arrives at the house on the first day, he sees a cat, Niya, trying to attack something in the bushes but it soon gives up after it is attacked by a crow.
- **S3**：Sho then discovers Arrietty, a Borrower, emerging from the bushes and returning to her home through an underground air vent.
- **S4**：Later at night, Arrietty's father, Pod, takes Arrietty on her first "borrowing" mission above the floorboards to show her how he "borrows" sugar and tissue.
- **S5**：After obtaining a sugar cube from the kitchen, they walk within a wall to reach a beautifully intricate dollhouse  in Sho's bedroom, to get tissue.
- **S6**：However, Arrietty is spotted by Sho while retrieving a piece of tissue from a tissue box and loses the sugar cube.
- **S7**：Sho asks her not to leave and although Arrietty hesitates, she still leaves the room with her father.
- **S8**：The next day, Sho leaves the dropped sugar cube beside the underground air vent where he first saw Arrietty.
- **S9**：Pod warns Arrietty not to take it because their existence must be kept secret from humans, but his daughter nevertheless sneaks out to visit Sho in his bedroom.
- **S10**：She drops the sugar cube he left on the floor, letting him know that she is there.
- **S11**：Without revealing herself otherwise, she tells Sho to leave her family alone and that they do not need his help.
- **S12**：On her return, Arrietty is intercepted by her father.
- **S13**：Realizing they have been detected, Pod and his wife Homily decide that they must move out of the house.
- **S14**：Sho learns from Sadako that some of his ancestors had seen Borrowers in the house, and had the dollhouse custom-built in the hopes that Borrowers would use it.
- **S15**：The Borrowers had not been seen since, however, and the dollhouse stayed in Sho's room.
- **S16**：Pod returns injured from a borrowing mission and is helped home by Spiller, a Borrower boy he met on the way.
- **S17**：He informs them that there are other places the Borrowers could move to.
- **S18**：While Pod is recovering, Sho removes the floorboard concealing the Borrower household and replaces their kitchen with the kitchen from the dollhouse, in hopes the Borrowers would be more accepting of his knowledge of their existence.
- **S19**：However, the Borrowers are frightened by this and instead speed up their moving process.
- **S20**：After Pod recovers, he goes to explore some of the places Spiller has suggested.
- **S21**：Arrietty goes to bid farewell to Sho, but in the course of conversation he suggests to her that the Borrowers are becoming extinct.
- **S22**：Realizing that he has upset his small friend, Sho reveals he has had a heart condition since birth and will have an operation in a few days.
- **S23**：The operation does not have a good chance of success.
- **S24**：He believes that there is nothing he can do about it, saying that eventually every living thing dies.
- **S25**：Arrietty convinces Sho that he will fight for the life he has now nevertheless.
- **S26**：Meanwhile, Haru notices the floorboards have been disturbed.
- **S27**：Sadako is out and Sho is still in the garden speaking with Arrietty.
- **S28**：Haru unearths the Borrowers' house and captures Homily.
- **S29**：Alerted by her mother's screams, Arrietty leaves Sho in the garden and goes to investigate.
- **S30**：Saddened by her departure, Sho returns to his room.
- **S31**：Haru locks him in and calls a pest removal company to capture the other Borrowers alive.
- **S32**：With the help of Sho, Arrietty rescues Homily.
- **S33**：Sadako returns soon after the pest removal company's arrival and sends them away.
- **S34**：Haru and Sadako discover that the Borrowers have left, and that Sho has destroyed any trace of their presence.
- **S35**：The Borrowers stop for dinner during their move, and Sho's cat, Niya, spots Arrietty.
- **S36**：Niya leads Sho to Arrietty.
- **S37**：He gives her a sugar cube, and tells her the Borrowers' fight for survival has given him hope to live through the operation.
- **S38**：Arrietty gives Sho her hair clip as a token of remembrance and they go their separate ways.
- **S39**：Arrietty, Pod, and Homily leave in a teapot with Spiller.
- **S40**：Spiller comforts Arrietty by giving her a red berry after she joins him on the teapot's top.

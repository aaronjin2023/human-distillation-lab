## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Alice Law, a postgraduate student steeped in the arcane arts at Cambridge , practices a dangerous and archaic form of magick.
- **S3**：When a mismanaged spell causes her adviser, the influential Professor Jacob Grimes, to perish violently, his spirit is dispatched to Hell .
- **S4**：Consumed by what she believes is her own guilt and a desperate need for his letter of recommendation to secure her academic future, Alice resolves to retrieve his soul.
- **S5**：The ritual to open a gateway to the underworld demands a steep price: half her remaining lifespan.
- **S6**：Her plans are complicated when her academic competitor and colleague, Peter Murdoch, forces his way into her descent.
- **S7**：Alice harbors a deep resentment for Peter, who has always been the department's favored student, making his companionship an unwelcome burden on her mission.
- **S8**：Their journey through the afterlife brings them into contact with skeletal beings commanded by Nick and Magnolia Kripke , two amoral magicians on a quest for the Dialetheia , an object capable of creating a True Contradiction.
- **S9**：During these encounters, Alice learns that a pentagram Grimes had drawn on her grants a peculiar resistance to the memory-wiping waters of the Lethe .
- **S10**：The travelers soon face a test from a divine being, the Weaver Girl, who offers them a choice presented as two apples.
- **S11**：Alice's subconscious decision differs from Peter's, an act of disloyalty that leaves him ensnared by the deity.
- **S12**：The timely arrival of Elspeth Bayes, a former advisee of Grimes who took her own life years prior, saves them from the Kripkes' minions.
- **S13**：However, upon discovering their objective is to rescue Grimes, a furious Elspeth casts them from her boat, abandoning them.
- **S14**：Alone, the two scholars begin to share their histories.
- **S15**：Alice discloses that Grimes had assaulted her and that her true intention is vengeance: to bind his spirit to her will, making him her instrument.
- **S16**：Peter then admits his own culpability; he intentionally overlooked the fatal error in the spell after the professor stole his research.
- **S17**：His plan had been to use a specific incantation to let Alice escape the underworld alone and clear her name.
- **S18**：Soon after, they become stuck in an impossible geometric prison constructed by the Kripkes.
- **S19**：Peter devises a solution based on the Hangman's Paradox , a logical puzzle that guarantees his own demise but frees Alice.
- **S20**：Alone in the Land of Dis, she contrives a stratagem to defeat the Kripkes, luring them and their child into the Lethe, which permanently erases their identities.
- **S21**：Elspeth reappears, bestowing the Dialetheia upon Alice and clarifying that the Kripkes had been hunting her, not Alice, all along.
- **S22**：Granted an audience with Lord Yama , the underworld's adjudicator, Alice summons Grimes's shade.
- **S23**：The professor attempts to lure her into an eternity of study by his side, but she is no longer under his influence.
- **S24**：She rejects him and exchanges his soul for Peter's return.
- **S25**：Reunited, Alice and Peter share an immediate, heartfelt kiss.
- **S26**：Alice then presents the Dialetheia to Yama, negotiating a final bargain: the restoration of half their sacrificed years and safe passage back to the mortal world.

### Liu Xiaochun

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-42bcd3b8bb20db52` · 独立版本：`profile-059f10f7f91f99aa`

- **性格倾向**：在亲近者受伤的情境中，倾向以代价较大的行动表达关切（行为推断；S9、S28、S29）
- **价值取舍**：在成名机会与父子团聚之间，最终明确选择与父亲相伴（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29、S31）
- **决策方式**：面对父亲安排的功利性道路，倾向用拒绝参与表达立场（行为推断；S19）

- **成长变化**：从父亲培养的音乐神童，逐渐成为主动决定人生方向的人（行为推断；S3、S11、S28、S31）

### Liu Cheng

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-eaa3d2eb58e39b96` · 独立版本：`profile-bc28b228ab33cd69`

- **性格倾向**：在儿子的求学受阻时，呈现持续奔走争取机会的执着倾向（行为推断；S4、S5、S10、S11）
- **核心动机**：将培养养子的音乐天赋作为人生投入，希望帮助他获得成功（摘要直接叙述；S3、S16）
- **价值取舍**：对弃婴承担长期养育责任，把儿子的成长置于个人生活核心（行为推断；S14、S15、S16）
- **决策方式**：在教育选择上倾向借助名师资源，以提高儿子成名的机会（行为推断；S10、S11）
- **情绪反应**：当儿子拒绝试奏时感到强烈愤怒，并毁掉其比赛获奖证书（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：全心为儿子谋求前途，却因偏重商业成功而与儿子的意愿冲突。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Liu Cheng  is a widowed cook making his living in a small southern town in China.
- **S2**：His thirteen-year-old son, Liu Xiaochun , is a violin prodigy.
- **S3**：In the hope that Xiaochun might find success as a violinist, Liu Cheng and Xiaochun travel to Beijing to participate in a competition organized by the Children's Palace, an arts institution for school children.
- **S4**：Even though Xiaochun emerges fifth, he is denied admission into the conservatory as he does not have Beijing residency.
- **S5**：Determined to realize his hopes for Xiaochun, Liu Cheng persuades Professor Jiang , a stubborn and eccentric teacher from the Children's Palace, to take Xiaochun as a private tutorship.
- **S6**：In the subsequent days, Xiaochun makes friends with Lili ([[Chen Hong , a young and attractive woman living upstairs.
- **S7**：Lili does not have a regular job but lives off the rich men she dates.
- **S8**：However, her heart lies with Hui , her smooth-talking, cheating boyfriend.
- **S9**：After Lili finds out about Hui's affairs in the presence of Xiaochun, Xiaochun sells his violin to a shop, buys a fur coat that Lili fancies, and tells Hui to give it to her as a consolation.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, Liu Cheng has learnt about a Professor Yu Shifeng , a high-profile professor from the Central Conservatory of Music.
- **S11**：Believing that Professor Yu is able to bring Xiaochun to fame and success, Liu Cheng decides to switch teachers for Xiaochun.
- **S12**：He pays Professor Yu a visit and confides in the latter the truth about Xiaochun's birth.
- **S13**：It turns out that Liu Cheng has never been married.
- **S14**：He found the infant Xiaochun abandoned in a train station, a violin placed next to the baby.
- **S15**：Liu Cheng brought both home and raised the baby as his own.
- **S16**：Xiaochun turned out to be a child prodigy, and Liu Cheng resolved to devote his life to cultivate his adopted son's talent.
- **S17**：Professor Yu, apparently unmoved, nonetheless agrees to give Xiaochun an audition.
- **S18**：However, Liu Cheng arrives at Professor Yu's home with Xiaochun only to find the violin case empty.
- **S19**：Xiaochun, angry at his father for choosing commercial success over music, refuses to play even when offered another violin.
- **S20**：Liu Cheng is so enraged that upon returning home, he tears up the award certificates that Xiaochun won in previous competitions.
- **S21**：Lili, remorseful over the affair of the fur coat, looks up Professor Yu and persuades him to give Xiaochun a second chance.
- **S22**：This time Xiaochun plays, and the professor agrees to take him without hesitation.
- **S23**：Professor Yu has another talented young student, Lin Yu , a highly jealous and ambitious girl.
- **S24**：When a selection trial for an international competition is coming up, Lin Yu and Xiaochun are in a contest for the only spot.
- **S25**：Liu Cheng decides to pack up and return home first, both to allow Xiaochun full concentration and to raise some money should Xiaochun be selected to participate in the international competition.
- **S26**：In an effort to infuse passion and emotion into Xiaochun's playing, Professor Yu tells the boy the truth about his birth.
- **S27**：However, this only strengthens Xiaochun's love for his father.
- **S28**：Just before the selection trial, Xiaochun, who has been chosen to participate, relinquishes his spot to Lin Yu after she reveals to him that Professor Yu had secretly bought Xiaochun's original violin from the shop to stop Xiaochun from being distracted by thoughts of it.
- **S29**：Xiaochun then takes the violin and runs after his father.
- **S30**：The two finally reunite at the train station.
- **S31**：In place of fame and success, Xiaochun chooses to be together with his father.

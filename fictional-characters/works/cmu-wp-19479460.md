## 角色资料

### Wolverine

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-01d9a86487649ef7` · 独立版本：`profile-35b883596a5c7eba`

- **性格倾向**：在罗格遭到威胁时，他倾向坚持保护她并在受挫后继续行动（行为推断；S18、S25、S26、S28、S30）
- **核心动机**：他努力让罗格获得安全归属，并阻止她被迫承担机器的致命风险（行为推断；S18、S20、S28）
- **决策方式**：他能从机器使用者的身体代价推断敌人转嫁风险的具体计划（摘要直接叙述；S20）
- **价值取舍**：面对垂死的罗格，他愿意通过直接接触分享自己的再生能力（行为推断；S11、S30）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Magneto

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-51741acc8508f1fc` · 独立版本：`profile-7e38c97b73c54058`

- **性格倾向**：在推进变种人目标时，他倾向强制他人承担自己设计的改变（行为推断；S14、S22、S24）
- **核心动机**：他试图改变人类与变种人的力量关系，并将世界领袖作为目标（摘要直接叙述；S9、S22）
- **价值取舍**：他为实现群体政治目标，接受牺牲个体意愿与生命安全的代价（行为推断；S14、S20、S24）
- **决策方式**：发现机器会严重削弱自己后，他选择借罗格的能力转移负担（行为推断；S20、S24）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：声称改变群体处境，却强迫另一名变种人承担个人风险

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1944 German-occupied Poland, a 13-year-old boy is separated from his parents upon entry to a concentration camp.
- **S2**：When he tries to get back in after the gates have been closed, he is restrained by four guards.
- **S3**：The gates suddenly start to curl in towards the group, as though attracted by a magnetic force; the boy is then knocked unconscious by another guard.
- **S4**：In present day, a girl named Marie  is studying with her boyfriend at her house in Meridian, Mississippi.
- **S5**：They hug together and they kiss, which knocks him out and he almost dies.
- **S6**：She screams as her mother arrives.
- **S7**：Terrified, she decides to leave her home.
- **S8**：In Congress, Senator Robert Kelly attempts to pass a "Mutant Registration Act", which would force mutants to publicly reveal their identities and abilities.
- **S9**：Magneto begins his plans to level the playing field between mutants and humans.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, Marie, now going by the name of Rogue meets Logan, also known as Wolverine, at the local bar in Canada.
- **S11**：Rogue's mutation makes her temporarily assume the powers of any mutant she touches, while Wolverine possesses superhuman healing abilities, heightened senses, and metal claws that extend outwards from his knuckles.
- **S12**：Suddenly, both of them are attacked by Sabretooth, a fellow mutant and an associate of Magneto.
- **S13**：Cyclops and Storm arrive and save Wolverine and Rogue, and bring them to the X-Mansion, a facility run by Professor Charles Xavier, who leads a group of mutants who are trying to seek peace with the human race, educate young mutants in the responsible use of their powers, and stop Magneto from starting a war with humanity.
- **S14**：Abducted by Mystique and Toad, Senator Kelly is brought to Magneto, who tests a machine on Kelly that artificially induces mutation, though Kelly manages to escape imprisonment with his new abilities.
- **S15**：After an accident causes Rogue to use her powers on Wolverine, she is convinced by Mystique (disguised as classmate [[Iceman  that Xavier is angry with her and that she should leave the school.
- **S16**：Xavier uses Cerebro to locate Rogue at a train station.
- **S17**：Mystique infiltrates Cerebro and sabotages the machine.
- **S18**：At the train station, Wolverine convinces Rogue to stay with Xavier, but a fight ensues when Magneto, Toad and Sabretooth arrive to take Rogue.
- **S19**：Arriving at Xavier's school, Kelly dissolves into a puddle of water when his mutation becomes unstable.
- **S20**：The X-Men learn that Magneto was severely weakened in the test of the machine on Kelly, and Wolverine realizes he intends to use Rogue's ability to transfer his powers to her, so that she can power his machine in his place, possibly putting her life at risk.
- **S21**：Xavier attempts to use Cerebro to locate Rogue, but falls into a coma, due to Mystique's earlier meddling with the device.
- **S22**：Jean Grey fixes it and uses Cerebro to find Magneto's machine on Liberty Island, which Magneto intends to use on the world leaders, who are meeting for a summit on nearby Ellis Island.
- **S23**：Just as the group arrives at the top of the statue, defeating Toad and incapacitating Mystique, Magneto and Sabretooth incapacitate the group and continue with their plans.
- **S24**：Magneto transfers his powers to Rogue , who is forced to use them to start the machine.
- **S25**：Wolverine breaks free and initiates a fight with Sabretooth, but is thrown off the top of the statue.
- **S26**：Wolverine returns, and Cyclops, with Jean's help, blasts Sabretooth out of the statue.
- **S27**：With Jean's telekinesis stabilizing him, Storm uses her abilities to send Wolverine to the top of Magneto's machine.
- **S28**：With time running out, Wolverine attempts to stop the machine and save Rogue, but Magneto, now having regained some of his strength, halts Wolverine's claws.
- **S29**：Cyclops manages to find a clean shot, wounding Magneto and allowing Wolverine to destroy the machine.
- **S30**：Placing his hand to Rogue's face, as she is dying, Wolverine succeeds in transferring his regenerative abilities to her; Professor Xavier also recovers from his coma.
- **S31**：The group learns, after seeing Mystique impersonating Senator Kelly on a news broadcast, that, despite being stabbed by Wolverine at Liberty Island, she is still alive.
- **S32**：In an attempt to help him learn more about his past, Xavier sends Wolverine to a military base near Alkali Lake.
- **S33**：Xavier visits Magneto in his plastic prison cell, and the two play chess.
- **S34**：Magneto warns his friend that he will continue his fight, to which Xavier promises that he and the X-Men will always be there to stop him.

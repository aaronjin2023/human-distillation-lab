### Mike Noonan

- **性格倾向**：面对自身恐惧与母女的困境时，他倾向主动介入并承担保护责任。（行为推断；S4、S7、S17、S25、S27）
- **情绪反应**：妻子去世多年后，他仍深陷哀伤，并受到噩梦持续困扰。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S3）
- **核心动机**：他追查诅咒根源，试图阻止Kyra成为下一名受害儿童。（行为推断；S22、S24、S25、S27）
- **决策方式**：他倾向把异象与现实线索相互核对，追索隐藏的家族因果。（行为推断；S13、S21、S22、S24）
- **价值取舍**：面对并无血缘关系的孩子，他愿把临时援助延伸为长期照护。（行为推断；S7、S17、S32、S33）
- **成长变化**：他从丧妻后的创作停滞，转向以保护与收养孩子重建生活。（行为推断；S2、S8、S32）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：竭力保护Kyra，却一度受Sara的鬼魂影响而被驱使伤害她。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] The narrator, Mike Noonan, a bestselling novelist, suffers severe writer's block after his pregnant wife Jo suddenly dies due to a brain aneurysm.
- **S3**：Four years later, Mike, still grieving, is plagued by nightmares set at his summer house in TR-90 (an unincorporated town named for its map coordinates), Maine .
- **S4**：He decides to confront his fears and moves to his vacation house on Dark Score Lake, known as "Sara Laughs".
- **S5**：On his first day, he meets Kyra, a 3-year-old girl and her young widowed mother, 20-year-old Mattie Devore.
- **S6**：Mattie's father-in-law is Max Devore, an elderly rich man who will do anything to gain custody of his granddaughter, Kyra.
- **S7**：Drawn to Kyra and Mattie, Mike hires John Storrow, a custody lawyer, for Mattie, and things start looking up.
- **S8**：Mike begins to write again, and realizes that Jo's ghost is helping him to solve the mystery of Sara Tidwell, a blues singer whose ghost haunts the house.
- **S9**：He also learns that Jo frequently returned to the town in the year before her death, without telling him.
- **S10**：Mike begins having recurring, disturbing dreams and visions, and realizes he shares a psychic connection with Kyra.
- **S11**：Max and his personal assistant, Rogette, try to drown Mike but he survives with the help of his wife's spirit.
- **S12**：Max unexpectedly commits suicide that same night.
- **S13**：Mike sees a pattern when he sees that local inhabitants have names that begin with "K" or "C" and learns how relatives of townspeople have drowned in childhood.
- **S14**：While Storrow and the private detective he hired are celebrating the end of the custody battle, Mattie attempts to seduce Mike.
- **S15**：As they are embracing, Mattie's trailer is subjected to a drive-by shooting, injuring Storrow and the detective and killing Mattie.
- **S16**：The detective is able to kill the driver and incapacitate the shooter with Mike's help.
- **S17**：Mike then grabs Kyra and drives back to his home as a violent thunderstorm begins.
- **S18**：The shooter's buddies try to stop them, but refuse to follow him to "Sara Laughs".
- **S19**：Under the influence of Sara's ghost, Mike is tormented to drown Kyra and commit suicide himself.
- **S20**：Jo's ghost prevents him and calls his attention to the novel he has begun to write.
- **S21**：In the pages there are clues that lead Mike to discover documents Jo had hidden, among them a genealogy showing Mike's blood relationship to one of the town families.
- **S22**：Several families whose origin lay within the town had firstborn children with "K" names who were all murdered—Kyra, as a descendant of Max Devore, is scheduled to be the next to die.
- **S23**：The genealogy also shows that Mike and Jo's child would have been the next firstborn child with a "K" name in the family line.
- **S24**：Mike realizes this must be Sara Tidwell's curse for something that had been done to her.
- **S25**：He leaves and searches for Sara's grave, stopped by the ghosts of several members of the old families.
- **S26**：He learns in a vision that these men had viciously raped and killed Sara, and drowned her son Kito in the lake; all the "K" children who died were descendants of those men.
- **S27**：Mike reaches Sara's grave and succeeds in destroying her bones, ending the curse.
- **S28**：Upon returning to the house, Mike discovers that Rogette has kidnapped Kyra.
- **S29**：He follows them to the lake, where Mattie's ghost appears and knocks Rogette into the water.
- **S30**：Rogette tries to pull Mike in with her, but is impaled by the storm's wreckage from the dock.
- **S31**：Mattie's ghost says her goodbyes to Mike and Kyra.
- **S32**：The novel ends with an epilogue, revealing that Mike has retired from writing and is attempting to adopt Kyra.
- **S33**：His status as a single, unrelated male complicates things, and the adoption has taken longer than anticipated.
- **S34**：The outcome of the adoption is left unresolved at the end, but the reader is given hope that it will be positive.

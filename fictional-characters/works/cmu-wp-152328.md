### Rocky

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8759883e9059ccbf` · 独立版本：`profile-52f2f8fec0518f66`

- **性格倾向**：在成名后的挑战面前一度显得自满，容易低估对手的准备程度（行为推断；S1、S6）
- **情绪反应**：导师病危时使他愤怒而分心，导师去世后他一度消沉得难以投入训练（摘要直接叙述；S9、S10、S14）
- **决策方式**：复赛时倾向结合速度与心理诱导，耐心消耗对手而非急求击倒（行为推断；S17、S19、S20、S21、S23）
- **成长变化**：从受名望与丧失拖累的冠军，转向重整训练并恢复比赛斗志（行为推断；S6、S14、S16）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：早期急于以重拳获胜，后来通过承受与诱导重新取得主动

### James "Clubber" Lang

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-857f51c768e05f90` · 独立版本：`profile-ce767a37894cdc72`

- **性格倾向**：在竞争中倾向强势挑衅，受挫时容易让攻击性进一步升级（行为推断；S3、S18、S20、S21）
- **核心动机**：以挑战现任冠军并夺取头衔为目标，表现出强烈的上升欲望（行为推断；S2、S3、S5、S11）
- **沟通方式**：通过公开质疑对手实力并侮辱其妻子，迫使对方接受挑战（摘要直接叙述；S3）
- **决策方式**：复赛受激后持续追求重拳终结，因忽视体力消耗而落入陷阱（行为推断；S19、S20、S21、S22、S23）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：急于击倒对手的攻击欲，反而削弱持续作战能力

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In the four and a half years since winning the world heavyweight title, Rocky has had a string of successful title defenses  and has seen his fame, wealth and celebrity increase.
- **S2**：Now 1981, while unveiling a statue of himself at the steps of the Philadelphia Museum of Art, Rocky is publicly challenged by James "Clubber" Lang , a ferocious new boxer rapidly climbing the ranks.
- **S3**：Lang accuses Rocky of intentionally accepting challenges from lesser opponents and after sexually insulting Rocky's wife Adrian , his challenge is accepted.
- **S4**：Rocky's trainer Mickey  initially wants no part of it and admits to the champion that Lang was right, he had handpicked Rocky's opponents to protect him and to ensure Rocky remained successful and healthy.
- **S5**：He also informs Rocky that Lang is young and hungry and that Rocky has no chance of beating him, as he has not retained his edge as a fighter.
- **S6**：Rocky manages to convince Mickey to train him regardless, but his Las Vegas-style training camp is filled with distractions and Rocky clearly does not take the challenge seriously.
- **S7**：Lang and Rocky meet at Philadelphia's Spectrum.
- **S8**：During a melee before the fight, Mickey is shoved out of the way by Lang and suffers a heart attack.
- **S9**：A now distraught Rocky wants to call the fight off, but Mickey angrily urges him on while he stays in the dressing room.
- **S10**：By the time of the fight, Rocky is both enraged and severely distracted by his mentor's condition.
- **S11**：The fight begins with Rocky pounding Lang with several huge blows, going for an early knockout, but the stronger and better prepared Lang is unfazed and quickly takes charge, dominating Rocky and knocking him out with a haymaker in the second round.
- **S12**：Beaten, Rocky makes his way back to the dressing room and to the dying Mickey.
- **S13**：Kneeling at his side, Rocky speaks to his friend, telling him that the fight ended in the second round by a knockout, which Mickey misinterprets as a win for Rocky, shortly before dying.
- **S14**：Stopping by Mickey's closed gym, Rocky is confronted by his former nemesis Apollo Creed , who has witnessed the fight as a guest analyst, and offers to help train him for a rematch with Lang in exchange for "a big favor." At first, Rocky is too demoralized to put forth his best efforts which frustrates Apollo, but pulls himself together after Adrian helps him come to terms with Mickey's death.
- **S15**：The rematch is held at Madison Square Garden in New York City.
- **S16**：At the start of the fight, Rocky sprints from his corner, fighting with a level of skill and spirit that no one including Lang expected.
- **S17**：As a result, Rocky completely dominates the first round, demonstrating his new-found speed.
- **S18**：After the bell, Lang is in a fit of rage over what has just happened and has to be restrained by his trainers.
- **S19**：In the second round, Lang gains the upper hand, and Rocky adopts an entirely different strategy that bewilders Apollo by intentionally taking a beating from Lang, and even gets knocked down at one point but manages to get up before he is counted out whilst taunting Lang for being unable to knock him out.
- **S20**：In the third round, Lang  becomes increasingly angry and quickly exhausts his energy trying to finish Rocky off with repeated knockout blows, most of which miss the newly-agile Rocky entirely.
- **S21**：Rocky taunts the champion in order to psych him out and the aggressive Lang is infuriated.
- **S22**：He attacks even harder walking right into Rocky's trap.
- **S23**：The tide turns and Rocky is able to overpower the winded and outboxed Lang, landing blow after blow and dodging attempted punches before knocking him out and re-gaining the heavyweight championship of the world.
- **S24**：Afterwards, Rocky fulfills Apollo's vague "big favor": a private rematch with him.
- **S25**：The film concludes with both of the fighters throwing their first punch simultaneously, but this time, they fight in the spirit of friendly competition rather than as fierce rivals.

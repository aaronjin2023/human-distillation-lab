## 角色资料

### Alice

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e9b18b5457e19abc` · 独立版本：`profile-608ce14737a1c27e`

- **性格倾向**：失去超能力后，她在困境中仍倾向主动寻找出路并援助同伴（行为推断；S4、S7、S15、S25）
- **核心动机**：她希望找到幸存者的安全归宿，并最终亲自建立真正的避难所（摘要直接叙述；S6、S11、S40）
- **决策方式**：在封闭环境中，她会随威胁变化调整路线，并预判敌人的后手（行为推断；S15、S23、S38）
- **价值取舍**：发现避难所实为实验陷阱后，她将释放受困幸存者纳入行动（行为推断；S28、S29、S40）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

### Albert Wesker

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-c70ffbb81318991b` · 独立版本：`profile-3763e40aa1375a09`

- **性格倾向**：在自身受到威胁时，他倾向以毁灭周围人员和设施保障脱身（行为推断；S2、S37）
- **核心动机**：他希望借艾丽丝的身体特性压制病毒，重新获得身体控制权（摘要直接叙述；S32、S33）
- **价值取舍**：为维持自身生存与控制，他将他人的身体视为可以吞食的资源（行为推断；S33、S36）
- **决策方式**：对抗艾丽丝时，他先解除她的能力，撤离时又以炸弹摧毁现场（行为推断；S4、S37）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：拥有强大的复生能力，却持续面临病毒夺取身体控制的威胁

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Alice clones  attempt to kill Albert Wesker  at his base in Tokyo.
- **S2**：Wesker escapes and destroys the base, killing all of the clones.
- **S3**：However, the real Alice boarded his aircraft beforehand.
- **S4**：Wesker uses a serum to remove all of Alice's powers before the lack of a pilot makes the aircraft crash.
- **S5**：Alice somehow survives the crash while Wesker seemingly perishes.
- **S6**：Six months later, Alice travels back to North America in an airplane following emergency broadcasts from a safe haven known as Arcadia in Alaska .
- **S7**：En route to Alaska, Alice is attacked by Claire Redfield ; after the attack, Alice destroys the device controlling Claire  and discovers Claire has amnesia.
- **S8**：The two travel to the ruins of Los Angeles in a Yak-52 plane, where they find a group of survivors living in a prison surrounded by the infected.
- **S9**：Luther West , leads the small band which includes Wendell , Crystal Waters , Bennett  and Kim Yong , Angel Ortiz , and Chris Redfield , who is a prisoner under lockdown in the facility.
- **S10**：With their help, Alice lands her plane on the roof of the prison and learns that Arcadia is not a fixed place but actually a cargo tanker traveling along the coast.
- **S11**：Alice and the survivors decide to find an alternate means to make it to the Arcadia.
- **S12**：Chris reveals he's Claire's brother, insisting he is not a prisoner but actually a soldier who fell victim to a cruel joke by some escaped prisoners; he claims to know an alternate route.
- **S13**：While in the shower, Alice is attacked by a group of Majini infected that managed to tunnel into the prison.
- **S14**：Wendell, who was trying to peep on Alice, is taken away/eaten by the zombies.
- **S15**：Alice, seeing as they are out of options, decides to set Chris free and leave the prison.
- **S16**：Chris reveals that the prison keeps an armored car that can be used to escape.
- **S17**：However, a giant axe-wielding monster begins breaking down the gate outside.
- **S18**：To overcome the problems, Alice, Chris and Crystal go deeper into the prison to retrieve additional guns from the armory; Luther and Claire attempt to reinforce the gate to keep the Axeman and other zombies at bay.
- **S19**：As Alice, Chris and Crystal make it to the armory, zombies swarm them, overpowering and devouring Crystal.
- **S20**：Angel informs Bennett and Yong that the vehicle is missing its engine and it would take a week to fix.
- **S21**：Panicking, Bennett shoots Angel, abandons Yong, and escapes with Alice's airplane.
- **S22**：Below, the Axeman finally breaks down the gate, allowing the zombies outside to enter the facility.
- **S23**：With no other choice, the group decides to use the zombie-dug tunnels to escape into the sewers.
- **S24**：Yong is suddenly cut in half by the Axeman, whom Alice and Claire then face.
- **S25**：Alice attempts to fight it first and it knocks her out; Claire then defends Alice and just before it kills her, Alice awakes and saves them both.
- **S26**：The two meet back up with Chris at the end of the sewers but they lose Luther to a zombie who drags him back into the sewers.
- **S27**：Alice and the Redfields commandeer a boat and board the Arcadia, discovering it's functional but abandoned.
- **S28**：Claire suddenly regains her memory, and reveals Arcadia was an Umbrella trap to gather survivors to be used as test subjects.
- **S29**：The trio begin releasing survivors, among them K-Mart .
- **S30**：Seeing a trail of blood leading from an empty test subject container, Alice follows it deeper into the ship.
- **S31**：As she expected, Alice finds Wesker, brought back by the T-virus.
- **S32**：However, Wesker is constantly battling the virus for control of his body, something he believes could be pacified by fresh human DNA; the Umbrella staff fled Arcadia in terror.
- **S33**：Wesker reveals he plans to eat Alice, the only person to bond at a cellular level with the T-virus and successfully retain control, hoping it will give him the control he needs.
- **S34**：Chris and Claire arrive and attack Wesker while Alice battles two dogs and Bennett, who has allied himself with Wesker.
- **S35**：Wesker easily overpowers Chris and Claire, but Alice is able to defeat both Bennett and Wesker with help from K-Mart.
- **S36**：While leaving, they lock Bennett in the room with Wesker's corpse; Bennett is devoured by Wesker when he revives.
- **S37**：Wesker then escapes in an aircraft, activating a bomb on the Arcadia.
- **S38**：The plane explodes instead; Alice placed the bomb there, thinking it might be set off.
- **S39**：Unbeknownst to them, a figure parachutes away from the explosion, while Luther emerges from the sewers, battered but alive.
- **S40**：Alice resolves to turn Arcadia into a real safe haven and broadcasts a new message for any other survivors.
- **S41**：Claire, Chris, K-Mart, and Alice soon see an approaching Umbrella assault helicopter squadron.
- **S42**：During the post-credits scene in one of the helicopters, Jill Valentine , who went missing after Resident Evil: Apocalypse, is dictating the attack wearing the same mind control device used on Claire,End credits. which slightly glows at the end of the scene, thus setting up the events of Resident Evil: Retribution.

### James Bond

- **性格倾向**：在受袭与敌方控制的处境中，他倾向主动反击并组织脱困（行为推断；S3、S19、S20）

### Anya Amasova

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-994c575c7cfbead0` · 独立版本：`profile-dc50513cbe5302c9`

- **性格倾向**：得知恋人死因后，她表现出能够暂时克制私人报复的倾向（行为推断；S13、S14）
- **价值取舍**：她将复仇安排在任务完成之后，暂时优先履行共同职责（行为推断；S13、S14）
- **关系模式**：她与Bond从竞争者转为盟友，获救后逐渐发展出感情（摘要直接叙述；S5、S6、S9）
- **成长变化**：最终面对兑现复仇誓言的机会，她承认已原谅Bond（摘要直接叙述；S25）

暂无支持：核心动机、决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：对恋人的哀愤与对Bond形成的感情相互冲突

### Karl Stromberg

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-b5304b26f0dae682` · 独立版本：`profile-b54704cffba05016`

- **性格倾向**：在重建世界的设想中，他表现出极端自负与支配倾向（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **核心动机**：他企图以全球核战摧毁现有世界，再建立新的文明（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17）
- **价值取舍**：他把自己设想的新文明置于现有人口与城市的生命之上（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **决策方式**：他利用被劫潜艇袭击两国城市以引发核战，并以海底基地自保（行为推断；S15、S16、S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：British and Soviet ballistic-missile submarines mysteriously disappear.
- **S2**：James Bond  is summoned to investigate.
- **S3**：On the way, he escapes an ambush by Soviet agents in Austria, killing one during a downhill ski chase, and escaping via a Union Flag parachute.
- **S4**：Bond learns that the plans for a highly advanced submarine tracking system are on the market in Egypt.
- **S5**：There, he encounters Major Anya Amasova  of the KGB, his rival for the plans.
- **S6**：Bond and Amasova team up, due to a truce supported by their respective superiors.
- **S7**：They travel across Egypt together, tracking the microfilm plans, meeting Jaws – an unnaturally tall assassin with steel teeth – along the way.
- **S8**：They later identify the person responsible for the thefts as shipping tycoon, scientist and anarchist Karl Stromberg.
- **S9**：While travelling by train to Stromberg's base in Sardinia, Bond saves Amasova from being attacked by Jaws, and their rivalry changes into affection.
- **S10**：They visit Stromberg's base and learn of his mysterious new supertanker, the Liparus.
- **S11**：Jaws, and other henchmen, chase the couple's car, but they escape, due to Bond's superior driving skills and his Q Branch Lotus Esprit sports car/submarine.
- **S12**：This unique car enables the two spies to perform a further underwater reconnaissance of Stromberg's facilities.
- **S13**：Bond later finds out that the Liparus has never visited any known port or harbour, and Amasova learns that Bond killed her lover in Austria; she promises Bond that she will kill him when their mission ends.
- **S14**：Later, while aboard an American submarine, Bond and Amasova examine Stromberg's underwater Atlantis base and confirm that he is operating the tracking system.
- **S15**：The Liparus then captures the submarine, just as it captured the others.
- **S16**：Stromberg sets his plan in motion: the launching of nuclear missiles from the submarines, to destroy Moscow and New York City.
- **S17**：This would trigger a global nuclear war, which Stromberg would survive in Atlantis, and subsequently a new civilisation would be established.
- **S18**：He leaves for Atlantis with Amasova.
- **S19**：Bond frees the captured British, Russian and American submariners and they battle the Liparus{{'}}s crew.
- **S20**：Bond reprograms the British and Soviet submarines to destroy each other, saving Moscow and New York.
- **S21**：The victorious submariners escape the sinking Liparus on the American submarine.
- **S22**：Bond insists on rescuing Amasova before the submarine has to follow its orders and destroy Atlantis.
- **S23**：Bond confronts and kills Stromberg but again encounters Jaws, whom he drops into a shark tank.
- **S24**：Bond and Amasova flee in an escape pod as Atlantis is sunk.
- **S25**：In the pod Amasova reminds Bond that she has vowed to kill him and picks up Bond's gun, but admits to having forgiven him and the two make love.
- **S26**：The Royal Navy recovers the pod, and the two spies are seen in intimate embrace through its large window, much to the consternation of Bond and Amasova's superiors.
- **S27**：Meanwhile, Jaws escapes from the shark tank  and swims off into the sunset.

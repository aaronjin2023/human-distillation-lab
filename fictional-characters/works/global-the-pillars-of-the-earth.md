### Tom Builder

- **性格倾向**：在失业与流离中，他表现出持续寻找建造机会的执着倾向（行为推断；S17、S26、S44）
- **核心动机**：建造一座大教堂是他的终身梦想，也是求职的重要方向（摘要直接叙述；S26、S44）

- **S10**：Prologue (1123) [ edit ] A red-headed man is hanged for theft after being condemned by a priest, a knight, and a monk.
- **S11**：His pregnant lover curses the men who condemned him, declaring that their children will be hanged, their enemies will prosper, and that they will live the rest of their lives with regret and sorrow.
- **S12**：Part One (1135–1136) [ edit ] Circumstances leave mason Tom Builder and his family destitute and starving.
- **S13**：After his pregnant wife Agnes dies in childbirth, Tom abandons his newborn by his wife's grave in the snowy woods, having no way to feed the infant.
- **S14**：He later has a change of heart and returns, but finds the baby missing.
- **S15**：After meeting up with an outlaw named Ellen and her son Jack, whom they had met earlier, the group discovers that Tom's infant has been taken to a monastery cell belonging to the Kingsbridge Priory.
- **S16**：Knowing that he will be charged with abandonment if he says the baby is his, and confident that the monks will be able to look after him, Tom decides to leave the infant to the monastery.
- **S17**：After several unsuccessful attempts to find work, Tom convinces Bartholomew, Earl of Shiring, to hire him to repair the walls of the Earl's castle.
- **S18**：Philip, the leader of the cell, is visited by his brother Francis, a priest, who warns him of a plot by Earl Bartholomew and the Earl of Gloucestershire against King Stephen.
- **S19**：Philip tells Waleran Bigod, the ambitious archdeacon to the Bishop of Kingsbridge, of the plot, and travels to Kingsbridge Priory where the previous manager, Prior James, has died only a few days before.
- **S20**：Waleran promises to make Philip the bishop's nomination for prior, practically guaranteeing Philip's election, in return for Philip's support to later make Waleran bishop though Waleran conceals that the bishop is also already dead.
- **S21**：Philip agrees as the priory has become financially and spiritually destitute under Prior James, and he believes he can correct that.
- **S22**：He wins, making enemies of the rivals for the post, in particular the sub-prior Remigius.
- **S23**：Tom's infant, now named Jonathan, is sent to live with Philip at the priory.
- **S24**：Unsure of the validity of Philip's words, Waleran goes to the Hamleighs, a noble family who have been enemies of the Earl of Shiring ever since the earl's daughter, Aliena, rejected a marriage with William, the only son of the Hamleighs.
- **S25**：Seeing this as an excuse for them to take their revenge, the Hamleighs take Bartholomew's castle and arrest the earl, forcing Tom and Ellen, now lovers, and their children into homelessness once again.
- **S26**：They eventually settle in Kingsbridge, Tom hoping to get a job rebuilding the cathedral there.
- **S27**：The family sees Jonathan during this time, although only Tom and Ellen know that he is Tom's son.
- **S28**：To ensure that Tom will have work, Jack burns down the old Romanesque cathedral, telling no one else of his actions.
- **S29**：After some convincing, Philip hires Tom to build the new cathedral.
- **S30**：Tom's strong son Alfred physically bullies smaller and weaker Jack through repeated acts of battery which creates friction with Ellen as Tom sees no fault in his son and never disciplines him.
- **S31**：Brother Remigius, who is opposed to Philip as prior, charges Ellen and Tom with fornication.
- **S32**：Waleran, who was cursed by the woman at the hanging, orders Ellen to live apart from Tom.
- **S33**：Outraged by Tom's willingness to accept this, Ellen returns to the forest with Jack.
- **S34**：Part Two (1136–1137) [ edit ] Philip and Waleran go to King Stephen in the hope of convincing him to give Bartholomew's estates, including a huge limestone quarry, to the church, so that they can be used to pay for the new cathedral's construction.
- **S35**：Initially believing that Waleran will be loyal to him as a fellow cleric, Philip learns from the Hamleighs that Waleran intends to use the earldom solely to boost his own position.

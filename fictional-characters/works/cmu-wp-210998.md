## 角色资料

### Neo

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-5cb4d7214366b83d` · 独立版本：`profile-44ecbe3dcc2c91f2`

- **性格倾向**：在战争逼近与身体受创时仍倾向主动承担，继续前往机器城（行为推断；S12、S17、S18、S25）
- **核心动机**：希望以阻止Smith为条件，为Zion争取停止战争的和平（摘要直接叙述；S26）
- **决策方式**：面对共同威胁时选择与敌方谈判，将自身能力转为停战筹码（行为推断；S25、S26、S27）
- **价值取舍**：最终以自身承受同化来促成反击，体现此刻优先保护群体（行为推断；S31、S32、S33）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：必须借助长期交战的机器力量，才能解除双方共同面对的威胁。

### The Oracle

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-e81de25dfd49ebda` · 独立版本：`profile-7ed8fe47292cf770`

- **性格倾向**：面对无法确知的结局仍倾向保持信念，容纳行动中的不确定（行为推断；S37）
- **决策方式**：面对Smith的同化没有抵抗，但摘要未说明她如此选择的策略依据（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **价值取舍**：在战后安排中支持让所有人获得离开Matrix的选择机会（摘要直接叙述；S34、S35）
- **情绪反应**：在Neo离去后仍怀有重逢希望，相信未来可能再次见到他（摘要直接叙述；S36）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：对战争终结抱有信念，却承认自己并不知道结果必会如此。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Neo and Bane's bodies lie unconscious in the medical bay of the ship Mjolnir.
- **S2**：Neo's digital self, meanwhile, finds itself trapped in a virtual subway station – a transition zone between the Matrix and the Machine City.
- **S3**：In that subway station, he meets a "family" of programs, including a girl named Sati, whose father tells Neo the subway is controlled by the Trainman, an exiled program loyal to the Merovingian.
- **S4**：When Neo tries to board a train with the family, the Trainman refuses and overpowers him.
- **S5**：Seraph contacts Morpheus and Trinity on behalf of the Oracle, who informs them of Neo's confinement.
- **S6**：Seraph, Morpheus and Trinity enter Club Hel, where they confront the Merovingian and force him to release Neo.
- **S7**：Troubled by visions of the Machine City, Neo visits the Oracle, who reveals that Smith intends to destroy both the Matrix and the real world.
- **S8**：She states that "everything that has a beginning has an end", and that the war will conclude.
- **S9**：After Neo leaves, a large group of Smiths assimilates Sati, Seraph and the unresisting Oracle, gaining her powers of precognition.
- **S10**：In the real world, the crew of the Nebuchadnezzar and the Mjolnir find and reactivate Niobe's ship, the Logos.
- **S11**：They interrogate Bane, who claims to have no memory of the earlier massacre.
- **S12**：As the captains plan their defense of Zion, Neo requests a ship to travel to the Machine City.
- **S13**：Motivated by her encounter with the Oracle, Niobe offers him the Logos.
- **S14**：Neo departs, accompanied by Trinity.
- **S15**：Bane, who has stowed away on the Logos, takes Trinity hostage.
- **S16**：Neo realizes that Bane has been assimilated by Smith.
- **S17**：Bane cauterizes Neo's eyes with a power cable, blinding him; however, Neo discovers an ability to perceive the world as golden light.
- **S18**：Neo kills Bane, and Trinity pilots them to the Machine City.
- **S19**：Niobe and Morpheus set out for Zion with the Mjolnir to aid the human defenses against the Sentinels.
- **S20**：In Zion, the fatally wounded Captain Mifune instructs Kid to open the gate for the Mjolnir.
- **S21**：When it arrives, it discharges its EMP, disabling the Sentinels but also the remaining defenses.
- **S22**：The humans are forced to retreat and wait for the next attack, which they believe will be their last stand.
- **S23**：Neo and Trinity are attacked by machines, causing them to crash the Logos into the Machine City.
- **S24**：The crash kills Trinity.
- **S25**：Neo enters the Machine City and encounters the "Deus Ex Machina", the machine leader.
- **S26**：Neo, warning that Smith plans to conquer both the Matrix and the real world, offers to stop Smith in exchange for peace with Zion.
- **S27**：The machine leader agrees, and the Sentinels stop attacking Zion.
- **S28**：The Machines provide a connection for Neo to enter the Matrix.
- **S29**：Inside, Neo finds that Smith has assimilated all its inhabitants.
- **S30**：The Smith with the Oracle's powers steps forth, claiming to have foreseen his victory.
- **S31**：After a protracted battle, Neo baits Smith into assimilating him.
- **S32**：The machine leader sends a burst of energy into Neo's body in the real world, causing all the Smiths in the Matrix to be destroyed.
- **S33**：The Sentinels withdraw from Zion, Neo's body is carried away by the machines, and Morpheus and Niobe embrace.
- **S34**：The Matrix reboots, and the Architect encounters the Oracle in a park.
- **S35**：They agree that the peace will last "as long as it can", and that all humans will be offered the opportunity to leave the Matrix.
- **S36**：The Oracle tells Sati that she thinks they will see Neo again.
- **S37**：Seraph asks the Oracle if she knew this would happen, and she replies that she did not know, but she believed.

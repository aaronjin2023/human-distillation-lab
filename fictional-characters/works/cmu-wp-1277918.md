## 角色资料

### Charlie Bucket

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-832fa2675c8d005f` · 独立版本：`profile-19bfbe75d73aef0b`

- **性格倾向**：面对旺卡的家庭隔阂，表现出关心他人并愿意促成和解的倾向（行为推断；S18、S21）
- **核心动机**：起初盼望获得金券参观工厂，经历落空后仍珍视这次机会（行为推断；S7、S9、S11、S12）
- **价值取舍**：即使生活贫困，也不愿以离开家人为代价继承巧克力工厂（摘要直接叙述；S1、S17）
- **决策方式**：面对丰厚奖赏附带的家庭禁令，选择直接拒绝而非妥协（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **关系模式**：不仅维护自己的家庭联系，也帮助旺卡重新接近父亲（行为推断；S17、S21、S22）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望工厂带来的机会，却拒绝接受割舍家人的获奖条件。

### Willy Wonka

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-6520904f5fabd275` · 独立版本：`profile-8f8d5cc13126e48a`

- **性格倾向**：在商业背叛与家庭创伤之后，表现出退缩和排斥亲密牵连的倾向（行为推断；S3、S13、S17、S18、S20）
- **核心动机**：从小渴望成为糖果制造者，愿意违抗父亲意愿追求这一梦想（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：起初要求继承人舍弃家庭，倾向把事业传承与家庭关系对立（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **情绪反应**：提及家庭时举止异常，显示相关经历仍带有明显不适（行为推断；S13、S18、S20）
- **成长变化**：与父亲重聚后放宽继承条件，开始容纳事业中的家庭联系（行为推断；S21、S22、S23）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：坚持自己的糖果梦想，却长期未能化解追梦造成的父子断裂。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Charlie Bucket is a kind, loving boy who lives in poverty with his mother, father, and four bedridden grandparents.
- **S2**：His mother is unemployed and his father works at a toothpaste factory, responsible for putting the caps on the tubes of toothpaste that come through the line.
- **S3**：Down the street is Willy Wonka's chocolate factory, which had recently been reopened after industrial espionage forced him into seclusion and to fire his employees.
- **S4**：Charlie's Grandpa Joe had worked for Wonka directly and was laid off when that happened.
- **S5**：One day, Wonka announces a contest; the children that find the five Golden Tickets wrapped in ordinary Wonka candy bars will be given a full tour of the factory and a chance for an unknown grand prize.
- **S6**：Four children quickly claim their prize: the greedy and gluttonous Augustus Gloop from Dusseldorf, the spoiled and rotten Veruca Salt from Buckinghamshire, the competitive and boastful Violet Beauregarde from Atlanta, and the arrogant and aggressive Mike Teavee from Denver.
- **S7**：Charlie hopes to win as well, but neither the bar he is given as a birthday present, or one bought for him by Grandpa Joe contain a ticket.
- **S8**：A fifth child in Russia claims to have found the last Ticket.
- **S9**：Charlie, on finding a ten-dollar bill, buys a Wonka bar as news breaks that the Russian child's Ticket was fake; Charlie finds the bar he just bought contains the last Golden Ticket.
- **S10**：As he races home, bystanders attempt to buy the Ticket from him.
- **S11**：Grandpa George reminds Charlie that money is far more common than the Tickets, and convinces Charlie to keep it.
- **S12**：Grandpa Joe offers to accompany him on the factory tour.
- **S13**：On the day of the tour, the children and their chaperons find Wonka to be a strange, lonely individual, acting odd at the mention of "family".
- **S14**：As they proceed with the tour, they discover the fantastical factory is now manned by short humans called Oompa-Loompas.
- **S15**：The other four children succumb to temptation, and end up caught in the factory workings before being safely recovered by the Oompa-Loompas, albeit in worse shape than at the start of the tour: Augustus falls into the river of chocolate but is rescued before the processing center, Violet expands into a giant blueberry when she tries an experimental piece of chewing gum, Veruca is thrown away as a "bad nut" by trained squirrels, and Mike is shrunk down to a few inches tall after being the first person transported by Wonka's new television advertising invention.
- **S16**：Wonka congratulates Charlie as the last remaining child and the winner of the grand prize, to become Wonka's heir to the factory.
- **S17**：However, Wonka forbids Charlie to bring his family to the factory, and Charlie rejects the offer.
- **S18**：Charlie comes to learn that Wonka had a troubled past with his father, Wilbur Wonka.
- **S19**：Wilbur, a dentist, had forbidden Wonka from eating candy, but once Wonka got a taste, he wanted to become a confectioner himself against his father's wishes.
- **S20**：After running away from home to follow his dream, Wonka later returned to find Wilbur and his home gone completely.
- **S21**：Charlie is able to help locate Wilbur and convinces Wonka to visit him.
- **S22**：Wonka finds his father has followed his success throughout the years, and the two reunite as a proud father and son.
- **S23**：Wonka allows Charlie's family to move into the factory while he and Charlie plan out new types of candy to make.

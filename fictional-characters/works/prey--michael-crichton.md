## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] Jack Forman is an unemployed software programmer and a stay-at-home husband.
- **S3**：His wife, Julia, is an executive for Xymos, a nanorobotics company, and claims to be working on revolutionary imaging technology , which takes up most of her time.
- **S4**：This leads to Jack being suspicious of her having an affair.
- **S5**：Julia shows Jack a video of Xymos nanobots being injected into a human test subject leaving Jack impressed yet suspicious of the new technology.
- **S6**：The couple's baby Amanda awakens in agony, but is cured instantly after an MRI is performed that same night.
- **S7**：Julia begins to display strange and abusive behavior toward her family until she is injured in a car crash, leaving Jack to consult Xymos where project manager Ricky is struggling with the nanobots.
- **S8**：Jack is taken to the Xymos research facility in Nevada's Basin Desert , where he is given a tour and introduced to the programming team: Mae, Charley, David, Rosie, Bobby and Vince.
- **S9**：He is shown a machine used to make nanobot assemblers from bacteria, though he is not shown the source code for said nanobots.
- **S10**：Ricky claims that contractors improperly installed filters in a vent, causing assemblers, bacteria, and nanobots to be blown into the desert, where they began forming into autonomous swarms.
- **S11**：These "swarms" appear to be clouds of solar-powered self-sufficient nanobots, reproducing and evolving ( necroevolution ) at rapid speeds.
- **S12**：The swarms exhibit predatory behavior, killing wild animals through the use of Jack's code.
- **S13**：Jack learns Julia was helping to make the swarms more benign, but they regressed when she stopped coming to the facility.
- **S14**：Persuaded by Jack, the team decides to destroy the swarms, but retreat after the swarms kill David and Rosie.
- **S15**：As night falls, Jack, Mae, and Bobby set out to find the swarms.
- **S16**：While searching, they discover that the swarms are dragging Rosie's corpse through the desert.
- **S17**：The team is also shocked to discover that the swarms can replicate the physical features, perceptions, and motions of humans when they see the swarms form replicas of Ricky, David, and Rosie.
- **S18**：The group follows Rosie's body and find the swarms nesting in a cave and destroy the swarms, their nest, and their organic assembly plant using explosive thermite caps.
- **S19**：Returning to the plant, Jack, Mae, and Bobby are greeted by Julia, who left the hospital and acts strangely as the remaining team looks for the cause of the nanobots' escape.
- **S20**：Mae discovers security footage of when they were in the desert.
- **S21**：The video shows Julia and Ricky having an affair, and Charley engaging in a vicious fight with Ricky and Vince before being killed by Julia, who transfers a swarm into his mouth by kissing him.
- **S22**：Jack and Mae realize that Julia, Ricky, Vince and Bobby have all been infected by a symbiotic version of the nanobots.
- **S23**：These swarms do not show aggressive behavior; instead, they take over human hosts, affecting their decision-making, and slowly devour them over time to reproduce.
- **S24**：This allows the swarms to remain hidden while spreading to other humans.
- **S25**：To destroy this new strain, Jack and Mae consume a form of phage that kills the nanobot-producing E. coli bacteria, protecting them from infection.
- **S26**：Jack then takes a sample of the phage and plans to pour it into the sprinkler system to vaccinate everyone in the facility.
- **S27**：He has Mae alert Julia and the infected team, who set out to stop Jack.
- **S28**：In the struggle that ensues, Jack is captured and thrown into a magnetic chamber.
- **S29**：Jack feigns surrender when Julia walks in to interrogate him, but reactivates the magnetic chamber, remembering the incident with Amanda in the MRI.
- **S30**：Julia's swarm is pulled away by the magnetic field to temporarily reveal the real Julia, who is gradually being consumed by the swarm.
- **S31**：Before the swarm can repossess her, Julia begs Jack to forgive her, says she loves him, and tells him to stop the swarms and save their infected children.
- **S32**：Jack runs to the roof, fights off the infected team, and places the sample into the sprinkler system.
- **S33**：Jack and Mae are able to distribute the phage around the facility, destroying the infected.
- **S34**：They escape the facility in a helicopter shortly before the facility explodes due to a methane gas leak combined with thermite that Mae had placed in the building.
- **S35**：After returning home, Jack doses his children and sister with the phage, curing them as well.

- **S37**：Army and sends a sample of the phage to her lab, Jack discovers that Xymos intentionally released the swarm into the desert so that it would evolve to survive in the wind, but called him in to destroy the wild strain once it became uncontrollable.

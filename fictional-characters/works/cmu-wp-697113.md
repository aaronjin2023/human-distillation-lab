## 角色资料

### Jack Burton

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-eeb9f398bf25a9de` · 独立版本：`profile-eeb0e56e85e7fa19`

- **性格倾向**：面对陌生且危险的超自然冲突，倾向继续投入而非独自离开（行为推断；S3、S9、S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：在朋友的未婚妻遭绑架后，持续参与追踪、潜入与营救行动（摘要直接叙述；S1、S2、S3、S7、S9）
- **价值取舍**：在能够逃离拘禁后仍营救其他被囚女性，关切超出熟人范围（行为推断；S15、S16）
- **决策方式**：面对敌方据点时，倾向亲自潜入并与伙伴分工突破封锁（行为推断；S6、S7、S9、S16）
- **关系模式**：与Wang共同承担营救风险，并接受Egg及其他盟友的帮助（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20、S21、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Wang Chi

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-732176d83c58065d` · 独立版本：`profile-6517364b754c304c`

- **性格倾向**：在营救遭遇挫折时，呈现持续协作并积极承担危险任务的倾向（行为推断；S15、S16、S19、S24）
- **核心动机**：以救回被绑架的未婚妻Miao Yin为贯穿行动的核心目标（行为推断；S1、S2、S3、S6、S20）
- **价值取舍**：在营救未婚妻之外，也愿意承担风险协助其他囚禁女性脱身（行为推断；S16）
- **决策方式**：倾向结合地方知识、盟友支援与战斗分工推进营救计划（行为推断；S5、S12、S16、S19、S24）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Truck driver Jack Burton  and his friend Wang Chi  go to San Francisco International Airport to pick up Wang's fiancee Miao Yin .
- **S2**：A Chinese street gang, the Lords of Death, kidnaps Miao Yin and takes her into Chinatown with the intention of selling her as a sex slave.
- **S3**：Jack and Wang track them to the back alleys of Chinatown and get caught in a battle between two feuding ancient societies known as the "Chang Sing" and the "Wing Kong." The latter interrupts a funeral procession the Chang Sing are having for their recently assassinated leader and, during the ensuing street battle, powerful magicians in league with the Wing Kong, called "The Three Storms" , use their supernatural powers to slaughter the Chang Sing.
- **S4**：Trying to escape, Jack runs over the Wing Kong's leader, the sorcerer Lo Pan , but Lo Pan is unharmed.
- **S5**：Wang takes Jack to his restaurant, The Dragon of the Black Pool, where they meet up with lawyer Gracie  Law up Tomammo Noku , along with Wang's friend Eddie Lee  and magician Egg Shen , a local authority on Lo Pan who moonlights as a tour bus driver in Chinatown.
- **S6**：They come up with a plan to infiltrate a brothel where they think Miao Yin is being held.
- **S7**：Jack  investigates, but the Storms make off with Miao Yin.
- **S8**：Miao Yin is taken to the Wing Kong Exchange, a front for Lo Pan's domain.
- **S9**：Jack infiltrates the place, where he and Wang get caught in an elevator that fills with water.
- **S10**：They escape, but are quickly subdued by Rain.
- **S11**：Strapped into wheelchairs, they are taken to see Lo Pan .
- **S12**：Wang tells Jack about the 2,000-year-old legend of Lo Pan; that he was cursed to roam the Earth in a ghost-like form until he can marry a special kind of girl with emerald green eyes.
- **S13**：Lo Pan has found that girl in Miao Yin and intends to marry and then sacrifice her.
- **S14**：When Jack's and Wang's friends try to infiltrate the Wing Kong Exchange they are captured and taken to holding cells filled with women.
- **S15**：Jack and Wang are placed in a cell but manage to escape after getting the drop on Thunder.
- **S16**：Wang and Eddie create a diversion so Jack can rescue the imprisoned women.
- **S17**：As they escape, Lo Pan captures Gracie.
- **S18**：On noting that she has green eyes as well he decides to marry both women, sacrificing Gracie and living out his life with Miao Yin.
- **S19**：Wang and Jack go to see Egg Shen.
- **S20**：With the help of the Chang Sing, they go into an underground cavern and reach Lo Pan's headquarters.
- **S21**：Egg gives the group a potion that Jack says makes him feel "kind of invincible." At the wedding ceremony, a huge fight ensues .
- **S22**：Wang is able to kill Rain in an elegant sword fight.
- **S23**：Jack and Gracie try and catch Lo Pan.
- **S24**：Wang joins them, and takes on Thunder, while Jack takes on Lo Pan, throwing a knife that embeds in his skull.
- **S25**：Thunder, enraged and dishonored at his failure to protect his Master, starts to inflate to an enormous size, exploding and killing himself.
- **S26**：Jack, Wang, Gracie, and Miao Yin are cornered by Lightning in a corridor, which he makes collapse.
- **S27**：Egg rescues them with a rope, which Lightning tries to climb in order to follow.
- **S28**：Egg throws down a statue that crushes him.
- **S29**：They find Jack's truck and make their escape back to the Dragon of the Black Pool restaurant.
- **S30**：Lo Pan having been defeated, Egg decides to go on vacation, saying China is in the heart.
- **S31**：Jack hits the open road, with an unknown-to-him stowaway—one of the remaining monsters.

## 角色资料

### Shohei Sugiyama

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-67199b9a0573a1bb` · 独立版本：`profile-64dd40a86260efb5`

- **性格倾向**：在社会眼光压力下，他起初倾向隐藏爱好，随后敢于公开维护（行为推断；S16、S25）
- **核心动机**：他最初为接近舞者而学舞，后来从舞蹈本身寻找生活意义（摘要直接叙述；S8、S17、S18）
- **情绪反应**：外在生活成功未能解除他的空虚，舞蹈使生活重新获得方向（摘要直接叙述；S3、S5、S18）
- **价值取舍**：他逐渐把对真实热爱的尊重置于同事嘲笑与传统羞耻感之前（行为推断；S16、S25）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：追求新的生活意义，却因隐瞒爱好引发妻子的婚外情疑虑

### Masako Sugiyama

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-c93748eff95732e6` · 独立版本：`profile-aa25d28ee0cc4ea5`

- **性格倾向**：面对丈夫的陌生变化，她倾向先求证，再尝试主动理解（行为推断；S19、S20、S26）
- **核心动机**：她委托侦探调查丈夫，是为了查明自己怀疑的婚外情（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **决策方式**：确认跳舞实情后，她由间接调查转向请丈夫亲自带领体验（行为推断；S19、S20、S26）
- **关系模式**：她尝试进入丈夫的新爱好，以共同参与缩短彼此的距离（行为推断；S26）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：对丈夫隐瞒的怀疑，最终转化为了解其热爱的行动

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film begins with a close-up of the inscription above the stage in the ballroom of the Blackpool Tower: "Bid me discourse, I will enchant thine ear", from the poem Venus and Adonis by William Shakespeare.
- **S2**：As the camera pans around the ballroom giving a view of the dancers, a voice-over explains that in Japan, ballroom dancing is treated with suspicion.
- **S3**：Shohei Sugiyama  is a successful salaryman, with a house in the suburbs, a devoted wife, Masako , and a teenage daughter, Chikage .
- **S4**：He works as an accountant for a firm in Tokyo.
- **S5**：Despite these external signs of success, however, Sugiyama begins to feel as if his life has lost direction and meaning and falls into depression.
- **S6**：One night, while coming home on the Tokyo Subway, he spots a beautiful woman with a melancholy expression looking out from a window in a dance studio.
- **S7**：This is Mai Kishikawa , a well-known figure on the Western ballroom dance circuit.
- **S8**：Sugiyama becomes infatuated with her and decides to take lessons in order to get to know her better.
- **S9**：Sugiyama's life changes once his classes begin.
- **S10**：Rather than Mai, his teacher is Tamako Tamura , who becomes an important mentor to him.
- **S11**：He meets his classmates: Tōkichi Hattori  who joined to impress his wife, and Masahiro Tanaka  who joined to lose weight.
- **S12**：He also meets Toyoko Takahashi , another student.
- **S13**：He further discovers that one of his colleagues from work Tomio Aoki  is a regular at the dance studio.
- **S14**：Aoki, who is balding and mocked at work for his rigid ways, is revealed to be leading a secret life as a long-haired  ballroom dancer.
- **S15**：Though distant from her, the classes increase his infatuation for Mai.
- **S16**：His secret thus becomes twofold: not only must he hide the lessons from his wife, he must also hide them from his friends and colleagues as it is considered embarrassing according to traditional Japanese customs to participate in Western ballroom dance.
- **S17**：Later, after being rebuffed by Mai, Sugiyama discovers to his surprise that his passion for ballroom dance outweighs his infatuation with her.
- **S18**：Indeed dancing, rather than Mai, gives Sugiyama the meaning in life that he was looking for.
- **S19**：Masako, noticing his odd behavior, thinks that he is having an affair — so she hires a private detective to follow him.
- **S20**：Meanwhile, along with his classmates, Sugiyama enters an amateur competition – only to find out that his wife, having finally learned the truth from the detective  is in the audience.
- **S21**：Surprised by this, he stumbles and nearly knocks his dance partner to the floor.
- **S22**：Though he is able to catch her, he accidentally rips the skirt of her dress off.
- **S23**：Both leave the contest.
- **S24**：Later, they learn that Tomio won the contest.
- **S25**：When Tomio is ridiculed at work after his colleagues read of his success in the newspaper, Sugiyama stands up and tells them not to make fun of something they don't understand.
- **S26**：At home, Sugiyama's wife tries to understand her husband's new passion by asking him to teach her to dance as well.
- **S27**：He is invited to a good-bye party for Mai, who is leaving for Blackpool.
- **S28**：At the party, Mai joins him to dance, asking him "Shall we dance?"

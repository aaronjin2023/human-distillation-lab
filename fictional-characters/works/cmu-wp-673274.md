## 角色资料

### Kevin Lomax

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-f5c13070a3d60705` · 独立版本：`profile-44060bc2c530791f`

- **性格倾向**：在法庭竞争中倾向争强求胜，容易以胜诉掩盖道德疑虑（行为推断；S4、S5、S24、S26、S42）
- **核心动机**：他后来承认自己不计代价追求胜利，并因此冷落妻子（摘要直接叙述；S42）
- **价值取舍**：醒来后的选择表明，他开始愿以职业代价守住道德底线（行为推断；S46、S47）
- **成长变化**：从明知有罪仍争取脱罪，转向承认责任并尝试拒绝诱惑（行为推断；S4、S5、S42、S45、S47）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：开始拒绝不义的胜利，却仍可能被成名承诺重新吸引。

### John Milton

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-1afc39b6cb69dc4c` · 独立版本：`profile-583ec9d8817cd755`

- **性格倾向**：在诱导Kevin时倾向操控局势，并利用他对成功的渴望（行为推断；S7、S25、S41、S44、S50、S51）
- **核心动机**：他希望Kevin与同父异母的妹妹生育反基督者（摘要直接叙述；S43）
- **价值取舍**：他贬低爱的意义，把虚荣视为可反复利用的人性弱点（行为推断；S45、S48、S50、S51）
- **决策方式**：遭到拒绝后改换身份，以成名承诺重新接近Kevin（摘要直接叙述；S48、S49、S50）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：宣称Kevin可以自由离开，却不断安排利益诱惑推动其选择。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Kevin Lomax, a defense attorney in Gainesville, Florida, has never lost a case.
- **S2**：He defends a schoolteacher, Mr.
- **S3**：Gettys, against a charge of child molestation.
- **S4**：During the trial, Kevin realizes that his client is guilty, and a reporter tells him that a guilty verdict is all but inevitable.
- **S5**：However, through a harsh cross-examination Kevin destroys the credibility of the victim, Barbara, and secures another not guilty verdict.
- **S6**：As he celebrates, Kevin is approached by a representative of the New York law firm Milton, Chadwick & Waters, who offers him a large sum of money to help the firm with a jury selection.
- **S7**：After Kevin's jury delivers a not guilty verdict, John Milton  offers him a large salary and a swanky apartment if he joins the firm.
- **S8**：Despite warnings from his Evangelical Christian mother, Alice, about sinful big city life, Kevin accepts the job and moves with his wife Mary Ann to Manhattan.
- **S9**：Kevin first defends a Voodoo sorcerer, Phillipe Moyez, who ritually sacrificed an animal.
- **S10**：He compares the incident to kashrut law and claims that his client is protected under freedom of religion, winning the case.
- **S11**：Kevin spends an increasing amount of time at work, leaving Mary Ann feeling isolated.
- **S12**：Kevin's mother visits briefly, and tells Kevin she wants to take Mary Ann back to Gainesville after seeing her mental state; however, Kevin refuses to let her take Mary Ann.
- **S13**：Kevin next defends Alexander Cullen, a billionaire accused of murdering his wife, his child and a maid.
- **S14**：This case demands more of Kevin's time, further separating him from Mary Ann.
- **S15**：He begins having fantasies about co-worker Christabella Andreoli, while Mary Ann shows signs of mental illness.
- **S16**：Mary Ann claims that the wives of the other partners at the firm are demons after she sees their faces become demonic while trying on clothing with them.
- **S17**：She also claims that someone has stolen her ovaries and can no longer get pregnant after having a dream where a baby plays with her ovaries and then becomes covered in blood.
- **S18**：Milton suggests that Kevin step down from the trial, but Kevin refuses.
- **S19**：Eddie Barzoon, the firm's managing attorney, is convinced that Kevin is competing for his job after finding his name in the company papers as a partner.
- **S20**：Although Kevin denies any knowledge, Barzoon threatens to inform the United States Attorney's office about the situation.
- **S21**：Kevin tells Milton about Barzoon's threats, but Milton is unconcerned, sarcastically dismissing Barzoon as "God's special little creature".
- **S22**：At that moment, Barzoon is beaten to death by vagrants, who take on demonic appearances.
- **S23**：Mary Ann witnesses this, further eroding her sanity.
- **S24**：While preparing Cullen's mistress Melissa to testify about Cullen's alibi, Kevin realizes she is lying and tells Milton he believes Cullen is guilty.
- **S25**：Milton offers to back Kevin regardless.
- **S26**：Kevin decides to proceed with her testimony and wins an acquittal.
- **S27**：After the trial, Kevin finds Mary Ann in a nearby church, naked and covered with cuts.
- **S28**：She tells her husband that Milton raped and mutilated her, but Kevin saw Milton in court with him at the time of the alleged attack; he believes that Mary Ann injured herself, and has her committed.
- **S29**：Kevin is approached by U.S.
- **S30**：Attorney Weaver with knowledge of the law firm's illegal activities.
- **S31**：Although Kevin tries walking away, he stops when Weaver tells him that Gettys was found with a dead girl in his car trunk.
- **S32**：Following Kevin, Weaver walks into the street and is run over by a car.
- **S33**：Alice and Pam, Kevin's case manager at the firm, visit Mary Ann.
- **S34**：Alone with Mary Ann, Pam appears as a demon through a mirror.
- **S35**：Mary Ann attacks Pam with the mirror and locks herself in the room.
- **S36**：As Kevin tries to break down the door, Mary Ann takes a piece of broken glass from the mirror and cuts her throat with it, killing herself.
- **S37**：Before he can mourn, Alice reveals that Milton is Kevin's father.
- **S38**：Kevin leaves the hospital to confront Milton, who gleefully admits to raping Mary Ann.
- **S39**：Kevin fires a pistol into Milton's chest, but the bullets have no effect.
- **S40**：Kevin realizes that Milton is not only his father, but also Satan himself.
- **S41**：Kevin blames Milton for everything that happened, but Milton explains that he merely "set the stage", and that Kevin could have left at any time.
- **S42**：Kevin realizes that he always wanted to win, no matter the cost, and left Mary Ann behind.
- **S43**：Milton explains that he wants Kevin and Christabella, who is Kevin's half-sister, to conceive a child: the Antichrist.
- **S44**：He offers Kevin anything that he wants.
- **S45**：However, when he asks about love and Milton dismisses it as overrated, Kevin rejects his Satanic heritage, cites free will and shoots himself in the head, ruining Milton's plan.
- **S46**：Kevin wakes up during the recess of the Gettys trial.
- **S47**：After embracing Mary Ann, Kevin announces that he can no longer represent his client, despite the threat of being disbarred.
- **S48**：A reporter follows Kevin and Mary Ann, pleading for an interview and promising to make Kevin a star.
- **S49**：After some prodding from Mary Ann, Kevin reluctantly agrees.
- **S50**：After Kevin and Mary Ann leave, the reporter shapeshifts into a grinning Milton.
- **S51**：Breaking the fourth wall, he says, "Vanity &mdash; definitely my favorite sin."

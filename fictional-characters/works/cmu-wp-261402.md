### Robin of Locksley

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-2d1dd6e9f0343cda` · 独立版本：`profile-4a5b50d06d4be05a`

- **性格倾向**：面对受压迫者，他倾向主动援助并承担组织责任（行为推断；S2、S9、S10）

### Sheriff of Nottingham

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-5eaaafa06e285a38` · 独立版本：`profile-e60add3f4ec7b274`

- **性格倾向**：面对反抗与挫败，他倾向加重压迫而非缓和冲突（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：他试图以囚犯性命为筹码，迫使玛丽安接受婚约（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **价值取舍**：在婚约与统治安排中，他把他人生命当作可支配工具（行为推断；S15、S16）
- **决策方式**：面对森林中的反抗力量，他选择扩充兵力并摧毁据点（摘要直接叙述；S13）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：声称以婚约换取赦免，却仍安排在婚礼期间处决反抗者。

### Will Scarlet

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-c5465f9c022ffc5e` · 独立版本：`profile-bdd35f2f47618e85`

- **性格倾向**：面对罗宾，他倾向直接流露敌意而不掩饰积怨（行为推断；S8、S20）
- **情绪反应**：他对罗宾的愤怒与母亲被离弃及自己失去父亲相连（行为推断；S20、S21、S22）
- **价值取舍**：尽管怀有私怨，他在关键时刻仍把同伴安危放在前面（行为推断；S17、S19）
- **沟通方式**：受到罗宾追问后，他以揭示兄弟身份说明敌意来源（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21、S22）

暂无支持：核心动机、决策方式、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：持续怨恨罗宾，却选择向他通报警长的危险计划。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Robin of Locksley , an English nobleman who joined King Richard "The Lionheart" in the Third Crusade, is imprisoned in Jerusalem along with his comrade Peter.
- **S2**：Robin escapes, saving the life of a Moor named Azeem  in the process, but Peter dies while escaping and has Robin swear to protect his sister Marian .
- **S3**：Robin returns to England with Azeem, who has vowed to accompany him until the debt of saving his life is repaid.
- **S4**：In England, with King Richard still away, the cruel Sheriff of Nottingham  rules over the land, aided by his cousin Guy of Gisbourne , along with the witch Mortianna  and the corrupt Bishop of Hereford .
- **S5**：At Locksley Castle, Robin's father  is killed by the Sheriff's men after refusing to join them.
- **S6**：Robin returns to England to find his father dead, his home in ruins, and the Sheriff and his men oppressing the people.
- **S7**：While fleeing the Sheriff's forces, Robin and Azeem encounter a band of outlaws hiding in Sherwood Forest, led by Little John .
- **S8**：Among the band is Will Scarlet , who holds a belligerent grudge against Robin and does not hesitate to show Robin his true feelings.
- **S9**：Robin ultimately assumes command of the group, encourages his men to fight against Nottingham, and trains them to defend themselves.
- **S10**：They rob English soldiers and convoys that pass through the forest, then distribute the stolen wealth among the poor.
- **S11**：One of their early targets is Friar Tuck , who subsequently joins these Merry Men, and Marian also begins to sympathize with the band and renders Robin any aid she can muster.
- **S12**：Robin’s successes infuriate the Sheriff, who increases the maltreatment of the people, resulting in more support for Robin Hood.
- **S13**：Hiring Celtic warriors from Scotland to bolster his forces, the Sheriff manages to locate the outlaws' hideout and launches an attack, destroying the forest refuge.
- **S14**：He confines Marian when she tries to summon help from France.
- **S15**：The Sheriff proposes to Marian, claiming that if she accepts, he will spare the lives of the captured outlaws.
- **S16**：Nevertheless, several of the rebels are due to be executed by hanging as part of the wedding celebration.
- **S17**：Among the captured is Will Scarlett, whom seemingly makes a deal with Sheriff to find and kill Robin in order to be set free.
- **S18**：Will meets back with Robin and a handful of his most trusted aides who survived the assault by the Celts.
- **S19**：Instead of attacking Robin, Will informs him of the Sheriff's plans to marry Marian and execute Robin's men.
- **S20**：Will still continues to take out his anger against Robin, to which Robin then questions Will as to why he hates him so much.
- **S21**：Will then reveals himself to be Robin's younger illegitimate half-brother; Will's mother was a peasant woman with whom Robin's father took comfort after his  mother died.
- **S22**：However, Robin's anger toward his father caused him to separate from her and leave Will fatherless.
- **S23**：Despite Robin's anger toward his father for being with another woman, he is overjoyed to learn that he has a brother and embraces Will.
- **S24**：On the day of the wedding and hangings, Robin and his men storm Nottingham Castle, freeing the prisoners.
- **S25**：Although Robin's band originally planned to free their friends and retreat, Azeem reveals himself and his willingness to fight the Sheriff, turning the peasants to revolt.
- **S26**：After a fierce fight, Robin kills the Sheriff but is attacked by Mortianna, who charges with a spear.
- **S27**：Azeem slays Mortianna, fulfilling his vow to repay his life debt to Robin.
- **S28**：Tuck also kills the Bishop by defenestrating him after learning of his corruption and treachery.
- **S29**：Robin and Marian profess their love for each other and marry in the forest.
- **S30**：Their wedding is briefly interrupted by the return of King Richard , who blesses the marriage and thanks Robin for his deeds.

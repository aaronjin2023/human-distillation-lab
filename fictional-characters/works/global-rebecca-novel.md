## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Synopsis
- **S2**：[ edit ] In a scene-setting introduction, the unnamed narrator dreams of her husband's estate, Manderley , lamenting that she cannot return.
- **S3**：Time has passed since its destruction, during which the couple have settled into "a quiet peace" in small Mediterranean hotels.
- **S4**：In her early twenties, the narrator works as a companion to a rich American woman, Mrs.
- **S5**：Van Hopper, who is on holiday in Monte Carlo .
- **S6**：They meet the wealthy 42-year-old Maximilian de Winter.
- **S7**：He is a widower whose wife, Rebecca, drowned in a sailing accident a year earlier.
- **S8**：After a fortnight of courtship, she becomes the second Mrs. de Winter and accompanies him to Manderley, his estate in Cornwall , where she is introduced to Mrs Danvers, housekeeper and former lady's maid to Rebecca.
- **S9**：Mrs Danvers continually attempts to undermine the narrator psychologically, suggesting to her that she will never attain the beauty and elegance her predecessor possessed.
- **S10**：She shows the narrator the west wing of the house, which overlooks the sea.
- **S11**：The west wing was formerly Rebecca's bedroom, which she has preserved as a shrine to her late mistress.
- **S12**：When the narrator makes small requests, Mrs Danvers and the other staff describe how Rebecca ran Manderley when she was alive.
- **S13**：Cowed by Mrs Danvers' imposing manner and the other members of West Country society's unwavering reverence for Rebecca, the narrator becomes isolated.
- **S14**：The narrator is soon convinced that Maxim regrets his impetuous decision to marry her, and that he is still deeply in love with the seemingly perfect Rebecca.
- **S15**：At the pressing of neighbours, Manderley hosts a costume ball, a custom Rebecca had instated.
- **S16**：Mrs Danvers suggests the narrator wears a replica of the dress from a portrait of one of the house's former inhabitants.
- **S17**：When the narrator enters the hall and Maxim sees the dress, he angrily orders her to change; Rebecca had worn the same costume to much acclaim shortly before her death.
- **S18**：When confronted by the narrator, Mrs Danvers encourages her to kill herself by jumping from Rebecca's bedroom window.
- **S19**：However, she is interrupted by the disturbance caused by a nearby shipwreck.
- **S20**：A diver investigating the wrecked ship's hull condition also discovers the remains of Rebecca's sailing boat, with her decomposed body still on board, despite Maxim having identified another body that had washed ashore two months after Rebecca's death.
- **S21**：This discovery causes Maxim to confess to the narrator that his marriage to Rebecca was a sham.
- **S22**：Rebecca, Maxim reveals, was a cruel and selfish woman who took many lovers while manipulating everyone around her into believing her to be the perfect wife and a paragon of virtue.
- **S23**：On the night of her death, she taunted Maxim with the prospect of having another man's child, which she would raise under the pretence that it was Maxim's, and he would be unable to prove otherwise .
- **S24**：Enraged, Maxim shot and killed Rebecca.
- **S25**：He then disposed of her body by placing it in her boat and sinking it at sea.
- **S26**：The narrator is relieved to hear that Maxim has always loved her and never Rebecca.
- **S27**：Rebecca's boat is raised, and it is discovered to have been deliberately sunk.
- **S28**：An inquest brings a verdict of suicide.
- **S29**：However, Rebecca's first cousin and lover, Jack Favell, attempts to blackmail Maxim, claiming she could not have intended suicide based on a note she sent to him the night she died.
- **S30**：It is revealed that Rebecca had had an appointment with a doctor in London shortly before her death, which the narrator suspects was to confirm a pregnancy.
- **S31**：When the doctor is found, he reveals that Rebecca had cancer and would have died within a few months.
- **S32**：Furthermore, due to the malformation of her uterus, she could never have been pregnant.
- **S33**：Maxim assumes that Rebecca, knowing that she would die, manipulated him into killing her quickly.
- **S34**：Mrs Danvers had said after the inquiry that Rebecca feared nothing except dying a lingering death.
- **S35**：On hearing that Mrs Danvers has abruptly disappeared from Manderley, Maxim feels a great sense of foreboding and insists on driving through the night to return home.
- **S36**：Before they come in sight of the house, it is clear from a glow on the horizon and wind-borne ashes that it is ablaze.
- **S37**：## Characters
- **S38**：[ edit ] Principal characters [ edit ] The Narrator/the Second Mrs de Winter : A timid, naïve, middle-class woman in her early twenties, who enjoys sketching.
- **S39**：Neither the narrator's first nor maiden name is revealed.
- **S40**：She is referred to as "my wife", "Mrs de Winter", "my dear", and so on.
- **S41**：The one time she is introduced with a name is during a fancy dress ball, in which she dresses as a de Winter ancestor and is introduced as "Caroline de Winter", although this is clearly not her own name.
- **S42**：She signs her name as "Mrs M. de Winter", using Maxim's initial.
- **S43**：Early in the novel she receives a letter and remarks that her name was correctly spelled, which is "an unusual thing," suggesting her name is uncommon, foreign or complex.
- **S44**：While courting her, Maxim compliments her on her "lovely and unusual name".
- **S45**：Initially timid, she gains a sense of confidence over the course of the novel.
- **S46**：Maximilian "Maxim" de Winter : The reserved, unemotional owner of Manderley.
- **S47**：He marries his new wife after a brief courtship, yet displays little affection toward her after the marriage.
- **S48**：Emotionally scarred by his traumatic marriage to Rebecca, his distance toward his new wife causes her to fear he regrets his marriage to her and is still haunted by Rebecca's death.
- **S49**：Maxim killed Rebecca after she taunted him with the prospect of raising an illegitimate child as Maxim's heir.
- **S50**：Mrs Danvers : The cold, overbearing housekeeper of Manderley.
- **S51**：Danvers was Rebecca's family maid when she was a child and has lived with her for years.
- **S52**：She is obsessed with Rebecca and preserving Rebecca's memory.
- **S53**：She resents the new Mrs de Winter, convinced she is trying to "take Rebecca's place", and continually psychologically torments her.
- **S54**：She tries to undermine the new Mrs de Winter, but her efforts fail.
- **S55**：Rebecca de Winter : The unseen, deceased title character, who has been dead for less than a year.
- **S56**：A famous beauty, and on the surface a devoted wife and perfect hostess, Rebecca was actually unfaithful to her husband Maxim.
- **S57**：Her lingering presence overwhelms Manderley, dominating the visitors, the staff and the new Mrs de Winter.

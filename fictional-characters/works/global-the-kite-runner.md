- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Part I [ edit ] Amir, a well-to-do Pashtun boy, and Hassan, a Hazara boy who is the son of Ali, Amir's father's live-in servant, spend their days kite fighting in the hitherto peaceful city of Kabul.
- **S3**：Hassan is a successful "kite runner" for Amir; he knows where the kite will land without watching it.
- **S4**：Both boys are motherless, Amir's mother having died in childbirth, while Hassan's mother abandoned their family.
- **S5**：Amir's father, a wealthy merchant known to Amir affectionately as Baba, loves both boys, even paying to have Hassan's cleft lip surgically corrected.
- **S6**：When criticized by Baba for being insufficiently masculine, Amir grows jealous of Hassan, and finds a kinder fatherly figure in Rahim Khan, Baba's closest friend, who understands him and supports his interest in writing.
- **S7**：Assef, an older boy with a sadistic taste for violence, regularly mocks Amir for socializing with a Hazara, whose members, according to him, belong only in Hazarajat .
- **S8**：One day, he prepares to attack Amir with brass knuckles , but Hassan defends Amir, threatening to shoot out Assef's eye with his slingshot .
- **S9**：Assef backs off but swears to revenge.
- **S10**：Wazir Akbar Khan neighborhood in Kabul, setting of Part I One triumphant day, Amir wins the local kite-fighting tournament and finally earns Baba's praise.
- **S11**：Hassan runs for the last cut kite, but encounters Assef in an alleyway.
- **S12**：Hassan refuses to give up the kite, and Assef beats and rapes him.
- **S13**：Amir witnesses the act but is too scared to intervene.
- **S14**：Guilty and disturbed, Amir begins to reject Hassan, who deteriorates mentally and physically following the assault.
- **S15**：To get rid of Hassan, Amir frames him for stealing, and Hassan falsely confesses to the crime.
- **S16**：Though forgiven by Baba, Hassan and Ali leave for Hazarajat.
- **S17**：Part II [ edit ] In 1979, five years later, the Soviet Union militarily intervened in Afghanistan.
- **S18**：Baba and Amir escape to Peshawar , Pakistan , and then to Fremont, California , where Baba works in a gas station.
- **S19**：After graduating from high school, Amir takes classes at San Jose State University to develop his writing skills.
- **S20**：Every Sunday, Baba and Amir make extra money selling used goods at a flea market in San Jose .
- **S21**：There, Amir falls in love with fellow refugee Soraya Taheri and her family.
- **S22**：Baba is diagnosed with terminal cancer, and dies shortly after Amir and Soraya marry.
- **S23**：The couple is happy together, but struggle to have children.
- **S24**：Amir embarks on a successful career as a novelist, and he and Soraya settle in San Francisco.
- **S25**：Fifteen years after his wedding, Amir receives a call from a dying Rahim Khan, asking Amir to visit him in Peshawar.
- **S26**：He enigmatically tells Amir, "There is a way to be good again." Part III [ edit ] From Rahim Khan, Amir learns that Hassan married and had a son, but was murdered after he refused to allow the Taliban to confiscate Baba and Amir's house in Kabul.
- **S27**：Rahim Khan further reveals that Ali was sterile and was not Hassan's biological father.
- **S28**：Hassan was actually the son of Sanaubar and Baba, making him Amir's half brother.
- **S29**：Rahim Khan asks Amir to find and rescue Hassan's son, Sohrab, from an orphanage in Kabul.
- **S30**：Returning to Taliban-controlled Kabul, Amir learns from the orphanage that a Taliban official paid cash and forcibly took Sohrab away.
- **S31**：He locates the Taliban official performing public executions at a football stadium, and secures a meeting at his house.
- **S32**：There, the official is revealed to be Assef, with Sohrab deliberately taken to assault and use as a dancing boy .
- **S33**：Assef agrees to relinquish him if Amir can escape the room alive, badly beating Amir with his brass knuckles.
- **S34**：Sohrab intervenes, taking out Assef’s eye with his father’s slingshot, and allowing them to escape.
- **S35**：Amir tells Sohrab of his plans to take him back to America and possibly adopt him, but bureaucratic problems force Amir to send Sohrab to an orphanage first.
- **S36**：Terrified and traumatized, Sohrab attempts suicide by cutting his wrists.
- **S37**：Amir eventually manages to take him back to the United States with the help of Soraya, but Sohrab remains completely withdrawn and refuses to interact with them.
- **S38**：One day, Amir, Soraya, and Sohrab go to a park with other Afghans, where some are flying kites.
- **S39**：Amir buys Sohrab a kite, and they engage in a kite fight with another boy, winning using one of Hassan’s favorite tricks.
- **S40**：Seeing Sohrab smile for the first time, Amir runs the kite for him just as Hassan once did.

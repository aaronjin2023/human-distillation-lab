### Heinrich Harrer

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e804400ad1d7ac17` · 独立版本：`profile-fe972944bdf60923`

- **性格倾向**：在登山受挫时显出执拗进取的倾向，不愿轻易放弃登顶目标（行为推断；S3）
- **价值取舍**：面对武器被毁及政局变动，他倾向重视对当地文化的忠诚（行为推断；S26、S27）
- **关系模式**：与年轻的Dalai Lama由教学建立联系，离别后仍保持友谊（摘要直接叙述；S20、S29、S35）
- **成长变化**：归国后尝试修复与儿子的疏离，结尾暗示父子重新亲近（摘要直接叙述；S30、S31、S32、S33）

### Dalai Lama

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-97d38c7cae412284` · 独立版本：`profile-9cef0de25baf086f`

- **性格倾向**：面对外来者显出好奇求知的倾向，愿主动了解陌生世界（行为推断；S15、S20）
- **核心动机**：主动请Harrer授课，希望学习世界地理与西方生活方式（摘要直接叙述；S20）
- **决策方式**：面对军事进逼选择委派将领守边，尝试通过组织防御应对（行为推断；S25）
- **关系模式**：与Harrer形成超出授课的友谊，在分别及流亡后保持联系（摘要直接叙述；S29、S32、S35）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Heinrich Harrer  and his pregnant wife Ingrid  are being driven to the train station in Graz, for Harrer's departure on an expedition to Nanga Parbat in the Himalayas.
- **S2**：Harrer, Aufschnaiter , and the expedition group arrive and begin climbing the mountain.
- **S3**：After an avalanche, Aufschnaiter orders the group to retreat back to the base, despite Harrer's determination to reach the summit.
- **S4**：On reaching the base, they learn that Britain has declared war on Germany, so they are arrested by British Indian authorities and taken by truck to Dehra Dun prison camp.
- **S5**：Ingrid writes to Harrer with divorce papers.
- **S6**：After several unsuccessful escape attempts, Aufschnaiter manages to steal a British uniform and several of the prisoners escape.
- **S7**：The members of the group go separate ways, with Harrer heading for northern India.
- **S8**：The rest of the group, apart from Aufschnaiter, have been recaptured.
- **S9**：Aufschnaiter plans to travel to eastern China to find work.
- **S10**：They cross the border into Tibet and set out east, but are intercepted by two men on horseback who tell them that foreigners are strictly forbidden in Tibet because of an ominous prophecy from the 13th Dalai Lama.
- **S11**：They are brought back to India, but they escape and climb up the Qinghai-Tibetan Plateau.
- **S12**：Harrer and Aufschnaiter join pilgrims traveling to Lhasa, covering their faces to avoid recognition as foreigners.
- **S13**：When they try to steal food, Kungo Tsarong ([[Mako  invites them to stay at his home.
- **S14**：At the guest quarters of Tsarong's home a Tibetan tailor named Pema Lhaki arrives to measure the two men; though both Aufschnaiter and Harrer take interest in her, Aufschnaiter wins her over and subsequently marries her.
- **S15**：The foreigners are observed through a telescope by the young Dalai Lama from the nearby Potala Palace.
- **S16**：The Tibetan regent, Ngawang Jigme , on orders of the suspicious government in Lhasa, visits the Chinese embassy in the city and tells the officials there to stop subsidizing the monasteries.
- **S17**：A Chinese official offers to bribe Ngawang Jigme, but he refuses.
- **S18**：The Dalai Lama's mother ([[Jetsun Pema  instructs Harrer on courtesy when meeting the Dalai Lama.
- **S19**：Harrer enters the interior halls of the Potala Palace.
- **S20**：At the Dalai Lama's request, Harrer begins tutoring the Dalai Lama in world geography and the ways of the west.
- **S21**：While Harrer and Afschnaiter are attending a party, a Tibetan turns on the radio and a Chinese announcer proclaims that they plan to invade Tibet.
- **S22**：At a meeting with the cabinet, the regent issues an order to banish all Chinese people from Tibet.
- **S23**：That night, the Dalai Lama has a prophetic nightmare of Chinese atrocities near the Tibetan border in Taktser, his birthplace, with monasteries being burnt down.
- **S24**：Three Chinese generals fly to Lhasa to speak with the Dalai Lama, but they are visibly contemptuous of him and the leader of the delegation tells Ngawang Jigme that "religion is poison".
- **S25**：The Dalai Lama sends Ngawang Jigme to lead the Tibetan army at the border town of Chamdo to halt a Chinese advance, but Ngawang Jigme surrenders and then blows up the Tibetan ammunitions dump after a sadly one-sided battle in which hundreds of Tibetans are slaughtered by better equipped and trained Chinese troops.
- **S26**：During a treaty signing in Lhasa, Kungo Tsarong tells Harrer that if Jigme had not destroyed the weapons supply, Tibetan guerillas could have held the mountain passes, buying time to appeal to other nations for help.
- **S27**：As the Chinese take control of Tibet, Harrer visits Ngawang Jigme to menace him about "betraying his culture".
- **S28**：The Dalai Lama, now fifteen years old, is formally enthroned as the spiritual and temporal leader of Tibet.
- **S29**：Harrer pays a final visit to the Lama on top of the Potala and prays with him.
- **S30**：Harrer bids farewell to Aufschnaiter and Pema and returns to Austria in 1951 to visit his son Rolf, now a young boy.
- **S31**：His son refused to meet with him.
- **S32**：Harrer left the music box that was given by the Dalai Lama when he departed Tibet.
- **S33**：Harrer and Rolf are seen mountain-climbing, suggested he did mend his relationship with his son at the end of the film.
- **S34**：The film ends with a series of title cards that list figures that quantify the death and destruction as a result of Chinese occupation.
- **S35**：Harrer kept a good relationship with Dalai Lama after he fled from Tibet to India.

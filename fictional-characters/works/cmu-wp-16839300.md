## 角色资料

### Princess Merida

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d6f3fb4ce07b3d52` · 独立版本：`profile-27d2efd186ad2ed4`

- **性格倾向**：在婚姻被安排的情境中，她倾向公开抗争而非顺从权威（行为推断；S7、S9）
- **核心动机**：面对联姻安排，她希望自己掌握婚姻选择权（行为推断；S7、S9）
- **决策方式**：在冲突初期，她倾向借外力改变母亲以解决双方分歧（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **价值取舍**：经历危机后，她将婚姻自主的诉求扩展到各氏族子女（行为推断；S19）
- **成长变化**：她从愤怒破坏母女纽带，转向主动修补并承认情感依恋（行为推断；S10、S23、S27）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：争取个人自主的行动伤及母亲，最终仍须修复亲情。

### Queen Elinor

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b8b2bd9eb09c133a` · 独立版本：`profile-beb8353412b31477`

- **性格倾向**：面对女儿的婚事，她倾向以责任约束个人意愿（行为推断；S6、S7）
- **核心动机**：她推动女儿接受联姻，意在避免拒婚损害氏族利益（行为推断；S6、S7）
- **沟通方式**：无法正常言说时，她仍借肢体提示支持女儿公开表达（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **决策方式**：面对魔熊威胁，她倾向利用现场条件而非仅靠力量对抗（行为推断；S25）
- **成长变化**：她从要求女儿接受婚约，转向支持子女自行选择婚期（摘要直接叙述；S6、S19）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式

矛盾与变化：维护氏族秩序的初衷，与尊重女儿婚姻意愿发生冲突。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In Scotland, King Fergus of Clan DunBroch presents his young daughter Merida with a bow for her birthday.
- **S2**：While practicing, Merida encounters a will-o'-the-wisp.
- **S3**：Soon afterwards, Mor'du, a giant demon bear, attacks the family.
- **S4**：Merida escapes along with her mother Queen Elinor while Fergus fights off the bear at the cost of his left leg.
- **S5**：Years later, Merida has become a free-spirited teenager and an older sister of identical triplets: Hamish, Hubert and Harris.
- **S6**：Her mother informs her that Merida is to be betrothed to one of her father's allied clans.
- **S7**：Reminding Merida of a legend about a prince who ruined his own kingdom, Elinor warns her that failure to marry could harm DunBroch, but Merida is dissatisfied with the arrangement.
- **S8**：The clans arrive with their first-born sons to compete in Highland Games for Merida's hand.
- **S9**：Merida declares she is eligible to compete for her own hand as the first-born of Clan DunBroch, causing a falling out between Merida and Elinor.
- **S10**：When Merida cuts the family tapestry in anger, she flees into the woods.
- **S11**：There, the will-o'-the-wisps lead her to the hut of an elderly witch posing as a wood carver.
- **S12**：After some bargaining, the witch agrees to give Merida a spell to change her mother; in the form of a cake.
- **S13**：Merida returns to the castle and gives Elinor the cake, causing her mother to transform into a large bear.
- **S14**：With the help of her brothers, Merida and Elinor return to the witch's now deserted cottage where they discover that the spell will be permanent unless undone by the second sunrise.
- **S15**：The witch leaves Merida a riddle, mentioning that she must "mend the bond torn by pride." The two begin to reconcile their relationship while Merida observes that the spell is slowly becoming permanent, as Elinor often loses control and acts like a bear.
- **S16**：After encountering the wisps again, the two follow them to ancient ruins and encounter Mor'du, who they discover was once the prince in Elinor's legend who received the same spell from the witch.
- **S17**：Merida theorizes that she can reverse the spell by repairing her family tapestry.
- **S18**：At the castle, the clans are on the verge of war, but the princess quells their fighting.
- **S19**：With the encouragement of her mother who uses charades to guide Merida through a speech, the princess declares that the children should be allowed to get married in their own time.
- **S20**：Merida then sneaks into the tapestry room with Elinor, who is losing control of her human self.
- **S21**：Fergus enters the bed chamber and is attacked by Elinor until she regains human consciousness and races out of the castle in desperation.
- **S22**：Fergus gives chase.
- **S23**：With the help of her brothers, who have transformed into cubs by eating the cake, Merida rides after her father while sewing up the tapestry.
- **S24**：The clan members and Fergus capture Elinor, but Merida intervenes just before Mor'du attacks.
- **S25**：Elinor kills Mor'du by luring him under a falling menhir, releasing the prince's spirit.
- **S26**：Merida places the tapestry over Elinor, but nothing happens.
- **S27**：After breaking down in tears and reconciling with her mother, Elinor is transformed back along with the triplets, and the family is reunited.
- **S28**：A few days later, the clans depart for their respective lands and Merida and Elinor ride their horses together.
- **S29**：In a post-credits scene, the witch's crow asks a castle guard to sign for a delivery of wooden carvings that Merida bought simultaneously with the spell.

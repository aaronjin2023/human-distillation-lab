## 角色资料

### Ethan Hunt

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7d4500b02eb5b040` · 独立版本：`profile-6ac1e8ff86d71058`

- **性格倾向**：在此次任务中敢于涉险，突入实验室后仍会因人质处境调整行动。（行为推断；S16、S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：受IMF指派追回病毒，计划潜入Biocyte摧毁样本。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S16）
- **决策方式**：发现Nyah感染后启动倒计时，其团队随后潜入交易现场夺取解药。（摘要直接叙述；S22、S24）
- **关系模式**：任务结束后，他获准与已获清除犯罪记录的Nyah在悉尼度假。（摘要直接叙述；S29）
- **价值取舍**：面对Nyah被挟持，他宁可暂时停火，也不冒险伤害她。（行为推断；S20）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他承担高风险破坏病毒任务，也因Nyah被挟持而中止射击。

### Nyah Nordoff-Hall

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-e7cb9c00b7006661` · 独立版本：`profile-cde0491e3a43c0c8`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中机敏果断，能窃取情报并利用感染阻止Ambrose杀她。（行为推断；S11、S21）
- **核心动机**：她受邀接近Ambrose，为Ethan团队查找Chimera病毒相关情报。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S8）
- **决策方式**：受命取回最后样本时，她将病毒注入体内，使对方无法杀她取毒。（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21）
- **价值取舍**：感染后走向悬崖准备自尽，显示此时她将阻止传播置于自身生存之上。（行为推断；S25）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：她注射病毒使对方不能杀她取毒，后来又为阻止传播而准备自尽。

### Sean Ambrose

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-f4ce08e73ac2dfa0` · 独立版本：`profile-5be23ad4666b59c3`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中善于伪装操控，以冒充身份和生命威胁推进计划。（行为推断；S17、S18、S23）
- **核心动机**：他想借疫情推高Biocyte股价，从中牟取巨额财富。（摘要直接叙述；S23）
- **决策方式**：他以Nyah感染为威胁，逼迫McCloy交出Biocyte控制权。（摘要直接叙述；S23）
- **价值取舍**：他为获利不惜放任感染者游荡，并以大规模疫情威胁民众。（行为推断；S23）
- **沟通方式**：他假扮Ethan套取计划，又以病毒影像胁迫McCloy合作。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Ethan Hunt , while vacationing, is alerted by the IMF that someone has used his identity to assist Russian bio-chemical expert Dr.
- **S2**：Vladimir Nekhorvich  of Biocyte Pharmaceuticals to enter into the United States, but purposely crashed the commercial airliner en route.
- **S3**：Nekhorvich, an old friend of Ethan, had forewarned the IMF of his arrival, planning to deliver a new virus called Chimera and its cure, Bellerophon, both of which he was forced to develop by Biocyte, into the IMF's hands.
- **S4**：With the crash of the plane, IMF is worried that the virus is out in the open, believing that IMF agent Sean Ambrose  is behind the incident.
- **S5**：IMF assigns Ethan to recover it.
- **S6**：Ethan is told that he can use two members of his team to help him, but the third person to help him must be Nyah Nordoff-Hall , a professional thief presently operating in Seville, Spain as she will be able to get close to Ambrose.
- **S7**：After recruiting Nyah, Ethan meets his team, computer expert Luther Stickell  and pilot Billy Baird  in Sydney, Australia, where Biocyte laboratories are located along with Ambrose's headquarters.
- **S8**：As Ethan and the others stake out Biocyte, Nyah gets close to Ambrose and begins to find any information related to the Chimera virus.
- **S9**：At a horse racing event, Ambrose quietly meets with Biocyte's CEO, John C.
- **S10**：McCloy , and shows him a video of the Chimera virus affecting one of Nekhorvich's colleagues taken from Biocyte, using the footage to force McCloy to cooperate with them.
- **S11**：Nyah is able to steal the video footage long enough to transfer it to Ethan and his team, who learn that the Chimera virus has a 20-hour dormant period before it causes death through mass destruction of the victim's red blood cells.
- **S12**：This is depicted through several increasingly graphic pictures, which show the victim dead after 37 hours.
- **S13**：Bellerophon can only save the victim if used within the 20-hour window.
- **S14**：The IMF team kidnaps McCloy and learns that Nekhorvich had actually injected himself with Chimera, the only way he could smuggle the virus from Biocyte, and had all the known samples of Bellerophon, now presently in Ambrose's hands.
- **S15**：Ambrose has blackmailed McCloy to sell him the virus for £37,000,000 and promising to return the samples of Bellerophon.
- **S16**：Ethan's team plans to break into Biocyte and destroy the virus.
- **S17**：Ambrose, posing as Ethan, tricks Nyah into revealing Ethan's plan.
- **S18**：Ambrose secures Nyah and prepares to raid Biocyte himself to secure the virus.
- **S19**：Ethan is able to destroy all but one sample of the virus before Ambrose interrupts him, and an ensuing firefight begins.
- **S20**：Ethan learns that Ambrose is holding Nyah and stops firing, during which Ambrose orders Nyah to retrieve the last sample.
- **S21**：When she does so, she injects herself with it, thus preventing Ambrose from simply killing her to get it.
- **S22**：As Ambrose takes Nyah, Ethan escapes from the laboratory and starts a 20-hour countdown before the virus takes over Nyah's body.
- **S23**：Ambrose opts to let Nyah wander the streets of Sydney in a daze, and orders McCloy to effectively hand over enough control of Biocyte to make him the majority shareholder, or else Nyah's infection will cause a pandemic that will kill 17 million people in Australia alone; Ambrose's plan is to make a fortune when prices of Biocyte's stock skyrocket due to demand for Bellerophon.
- **S24**：Ethan's team is able to locate and infiltrate the meeting, stealing the samples of Bellerophon while taking out many of Ambrose's men.
- **S25**：Luther and Billy locate Nyah who has wandered to a cliff side, intent on killing herself to prevent Chimera from spreading.
- **S26**：As the two IMF agents bring Nyah to Ethan, he and Ambrose engage in a fist fight and wilds out on Ambrose.
- **S27**：With little time left on the 20-hour countdown, Ethan finally gains the upper hand over Ambrose and shoots him dead, and then Luther injects Nyah with Bellerophon.
- **S28**：Ethan reports to IMF on the successful mission.
- **S29**：IMF clears Nyah's criminal record, and allows Ethan to continue his vacation with her in Sydney.

## 角色资料

### Larry Daley

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-4ed147dbd95d117e` · 独立版本：`profile-a6e29e69271e35e4`

- **性格倾向**：在反复受挫后，他仍尝试学习并调整做法，显出情境中的韧性。（行为推断；S1、S10、S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：面对儿子可能更尊重继父的处境，他流露出争取儿子认可的愿望。（行为推断；S2、S17）
- **决策方式**：面对守夜难题，他听从建议研读历史，再将所学用于应对展品。（摘要直接叙述；S10、S12）
- **价值取舍**：追索石板的危机中，他倾向推动展品放下争斗、共同应对威胁。（行为推断；S23、S24）
- **成长变化**：他从工作不稳定的求职者，转为能组织展品共同应对危机的守卫。（行为推断；S1、S3、S23、S24）

### Cecil

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-b2c3a7b132408809` · 独立版本：`profile-9ad75eaf598f0ed1`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他以谋划和欺骗应对退休后的处境，表现出算计倾向。（行为推断；S18、S19、S20、S24）
- **核心动机**：他与同事想靠窃取文物保住活力并筹措退休生活。（摘要直接叙述；S18、S19）
- **价值取舍**：为保住个人健康与退休资源，他愿牺牲Larry的清白。（行为推断；S19）
- **决策方式**：他与同伙先停用石板以排除干扰，被发现后又锁住父子逃走。（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **关系模式**：他先雇用Larry，后来却参与嫁祸计划并将其与儿子关起。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S19、S20）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

- **S1**：Larry Daley  is divorced, unable to keep a stable job, and has failed at many business ventures.
- **S2**：His ex-wife  believes that he is a bad example to their eleven year-old son Nick , and Larry fears that Nick respects his future stepfather, bond trader Don , more than him.
- **S3**：Cecil , an elderly night security guard about to retire from the American Museum of Natural History, hires Larry despite his unpromising résumé.
- **S4**：Because of recent financial troubles, the museum plans to replace Cecil and his two colleagues Gus  and Reginald  with one guard.
- **S5**：They advise Larry to leave some of the lights on and warn him not to let anything "in...or out".
- **S6**：Once night comes, Larry discovers that the exhibits come to life, including a living Tyrannosaurus skeleton nicknamed "Rexy" who behaves like a dog ; a mischievous capuchin monkey named Dexter , which always steals Larry's keys ; rival miniature civilizations led by Old West cowboy Jedediah  and Roman general Octavius ; an Easter Island Moai , who calls Larry dum-dum and loves gum-gum; and wax models of the likes of Theodore Roosevelt , and Attila the Hun ([[Patrick_Gallagher_, who makes a habit of chasing Larry wildly through the museum with his Huns.
- **S7**：Roosevelt explains that since an Egyptian artifact&mdash;the Golden Tablet of Pharaoh Akhmenrah&mdash;came to the museum in 1952, all of the exhibits come to life each night.
- **S8**：If the exhibits are outside of the museum during sunrise, however, they turn to dust.
- **S9**：Roosevelt helps Larry by restoring order, and he decides to remain as a guard.
- **S10**：On Cecil's advice, Larry studies history to prepare himself better.
- **S11**：He also learns from a museum docent, Rebecca Hutman , who is writing a dissertation on Sacagawea  but does not feel she knows enough about her subject.
- **S12**：The next night, Larry uses what he has learned to better control the exhibits (helping solve the Neanderthals' quest for fire by tossing them a cigarette lighter; giving the Easter Island head a wad of chewing gum .

- **S17**：Larry brings Nick to the museum to show him the exhibits, but none is alive.
- **S18**：They investigate and catch Cecil, Gus, and Reginald in the act of stealing the tablet and other valuable objects.

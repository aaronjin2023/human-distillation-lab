### Kong

### Carl Denham

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-4ea39cbbde7f704c` · 独立版本：`profile-6f35dea2baaa99e0`

- **性格倾向**：在探岛与归来后的行动中，他倾向把危险生物转为事业机会。（行为推断；S7、S21、S26）
- **核心动机**：他带摄制队探索岛屿拍片，并在救援受挫后决定捕获Kong。（摘要直接叙述；S7、S21）
- **决策方式**：救援队获救后，他选择捕获Kong，与继续寻找Ann的Jack分道。（摘要直接叙述；S21）
- **价值取舍**：在纽约展示被锁住的Kong，显出他将其作为事业资源的取向。（行为推断；S26）
- **沟通方式**：面对Kong的尸体，他将死亡归因于美，而非飞机的攻击。（摘要直接叙述；S33、S34、S35）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

- **S5**：Deep in the southern seas the "Venture" receives a radio message from her owners informing the Captain about Denhams' arrest warrant and instructing him to divert to Rangoon immediately.
- **S6**：Despite his attempt to turn around, the ship is lost in fog and runs aground on the rocks encircling the island.
- **S7**：Carl and his crew explore the island to film and are attacked by the vicious natives, degenerated descendents of a once great civilisation.
- **S8**：Mike ([[Craig Hall , the sound technician, is speared, one of the sailors has his head bashed in, and Jack is knocked out.

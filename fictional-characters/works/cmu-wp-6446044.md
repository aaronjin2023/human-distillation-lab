## 角色资料

### James Bond

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-360901462b1a4d14` · 独立版本：`profile-270da9622c3ac9ec`

- **性格倾向**：面对多次伏击与误导，他倾向保持行动并继续验证线索（行为推断；S11、S13、S22、S23）
- **核心动机**：他追索ATAC，核心任务是避免其落入苏方并威胁导弹安全（摘要直接叙述；S2、S3）
- **价值取舍**：面对已投降的敌人，他倾向限制私人复仇而非任其处决（行为推断；S33）
- **决策方式**：在装置争夺的终局，他选择销毁目标以阻断双方占有（行为推断；S35、S36）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：阻止Melina处决降者，自己也在追逐中杀死Locque

### Melina Havelock

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-0214901090f9fb90` · 独立版本：`profile-6c36de28b1f0f084`

- **性格倾向**：在父母被害后的追索中，她表现出敢于亲自采取行动的倾向（行为推断；S5、S8、S29、S33）
- **核心动机**：对杀害父母者的复仇意愿，持续影响她对敌人的处置（行为推断；S5、S8、S33）
- **决策方式**：面对追查目标时，她倾向直接介入，而非仅等待他人调查（行为推断；S8、S26、S29）
- **关系模式**：她与Bond从共同逃生发展为合作追索，并建立亲密联系（摘要直接叙述；S8、S26、S29、S36）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：复仇冲动与Bond对投降者的处置原则发生冲突

### Aristotle Kristatos

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-5b91e73af8654e8b` · 独立版本：`profile-b8eb894d3414a4c7`

- **性格倾向**：在与Bond接触时，他表现出善于伪装合作并转移怀疑的倾向（行为推断；S10、S22）
- **核心动机**：他替克格勃追索ATAC，并准备在约定地点完成交接（摘要直接叙述；S22、S26、S27、S28）
- **沟通方式**：他将自己部下的雇佣关系嫁接给Columbo，以误导调查（摘要直接叙述；S10、S22）
- **决策方式**：在表面投降后仍保留暗器突袭，倾向利用松懈反扑（行为推断；S33、S34）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：以情报合作身份接近Bond，同时暗中服务于其任务对手

- **S1**：MI6 agent James Bond, code name '007' and sometimes referred to by that number, is ordered on a mission by the British Minister of Defence, Sir Fredrick Gray and MI6 Chief of Staff, Bill Tanner.
- **S2**：The spy boat St Georges, which holds the Automatic Targeting Attack Communicator , the system used by the Ministry of Defence to communicate with and co-ordinate the Royal Navy's fleet of Polaris submarines, was sunk by a naval mine in the Ionian Sea.
- **S3**：Bond is asked to retrieve the ATAC before the Soviets, as the transmitter could order attacks by the Polaris submarines' ballistic missiles.
- **S4**：The head of the KGB, General Gogol had already sent a contact to Greece.
- **S5**：A marine archaeologist, Sir Timothy Havelock, who had been asked by the British to secretly locate the St Georges, is murdered with his wife by a Cuban hitman, Hector Gonzales.
- **S6**：Bond goes to Spain to find out who hired Gonzales.
- **S7**：While spying on Gonzales' villa, Bond is captured by his men, but manages to escape as Gonzales is shot with an arrow.
- **S8**：Outside, he finds the assassin was Melina Havelock, the daughter of Sir Timothy and the two escape.
- **S9**：With the help of Q, Bond identifies a hitman in Gonzales' estate as Emile Leopold Locque, and then goes to Locque's possible base in Italy.
- **S10**：There Bond meets his contact, Luigi Ferrara, and a well-connected Greek businessman and intelligence informant, Aris Kristatos, who tells Bond that Locque is employed by Milos Columbo, Kristatos' former organised crime partner.

- **S12**：Once he escapes, Bond goes with Ferrara to an indoor ice rink to bid Bibi farewell.

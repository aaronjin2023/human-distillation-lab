### Ethan Hunt

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Ethan Hunt  is part of a small team of the Impossible Missions Force , an unofficial branch of the Central Intelligence Agency.
- **S2**：Led by Jim Phelps , the team assembles for a mission in Prague to prevent Alexander Golitsyn, an American diplomat, from stealing the non-official cover  list, a comprehensive list of all covert agents in Eastern Europe, from the U.S.
- **S3**：Embassy.
- **S4**：The mission goes hopelessly wrong, apparently resulting in the deaths of the entire team except for Ethan.
- **S5**：Technician Jack Harmon  is impaled in an elevator shaft, Jim is shot on a bridge by an unknown assailant, Hannah Williams  and Jim's wife Claire  are both killed by a car bomb and both Sarah Davies  and Golitsyn are stabbed to death by an unknown assailant.
- **S6**：After fleeing the scene, Ethan meets with IMF director Eugene Kittridge  at a café where they discuss the events of that night.
- **S7**：Eventually, Ethan realizes that a second IMF team was watching his team during the failed operation.
- **S8**：Kittridge then discloses that the operation was a setup meant to draw out a mole in the IMF.
- **S9**：The mole has made a deal to sell the list to an arms dealer known as "Max" as part of an operation called "Job 314".
- **S10**：As a result, the NOC list taken in the mission was a fake and the computer disk it is on is rigged with a tracking device.
- **S11**：After Kittridge informs Ethan of his suspicion that Ethan is the mole, Ethan blows up the floor-to-ceiling aquarium with a stick of explosive chewing gum in the café and flees.
- **S12**：Ethan returns to the IMF safe house where, realizing that "Job 314" refers to a Bible verse in the Book of Job , Ethan begins email correspondence with Max  over a biblical correspondence web site and warns Max about the fake NOC list.
- **S13**：Ethan then encounters Claire and discovers that she has survived the mission, having gotten out of the car in which she was waiting before it exploded.
- **S14**：Max arranges a meeting with Ethan where he offers to deliver the real NOC list in exchange for $10&nbsp;million and a face-to-face meeting with the real Job.
- **S15**：Max agrees and she and Ethan then escape moments before a CIA team, alerted by the tracking device, arrives in search of the fake NOC list.
- **S16**：Ethan assembles a team of disavowed agents: computer expert Luther Stickell  and pilot Franz Krieger .
- **S17**：Ethan, Luther, Krieger, and Claire infiltrate the CIA's headquarters and successfully steal the full NOC list before escaping to a safe house in London.
- **S18**：While in London, Ethan discovers that his uncle and mother have been falsely arrested for drug trafficking in an attempt by Kittridge to lure him out of hiding.
- **S19**：This infuriates Ethan, and he contacts Kittridge who offers to drop the charges if Ethan surrenders.
- **S20**：Ethan stays on the line long enough for Kittridge to trace him to London and then hangs up, only to find Jim  standing next to him.
- **S21**：Jim, presumed dead in the Prague operation and following Ethan ever since, tells Ethan that Kittridge is the mole and is trying to tie up loose ends.
- **S22**：As Ethan listens, he mentally pieces together the operation and realizes that Jim, having become disillusioned with his work, is actually Job and that Krieger - who uses the same distinctive make of knife that was used to kill Sarah and Golitsyn – has assisted him.
- **S23**：However, Ethan is still doubtful about Claire's place in the conspiracy.
- **S24**：The next day, Max and Ethan arrange to meet aboard the TGV en route to Paris, with Claire and Luther aboard to provide backup.
- **S25**：Kittridge is also aboard, having received tickets and a watch from Ethan.
- **S26**：Aboard the train, Ethan delivers the NOC list to Max, who directs him to the baggage car to find his money and Job.
- **S27**：Claire arrives in the baggage car to meet with her husband, revealing her complicity as she suggests that they leave with the money and let Ethan take the blame.
- **S28**：Jim suddenly peels his face away, revealing himself to be Ethan in disguise.
- **S29**：They are interrupted by the arrival of the real Jim Phelps: unhinged, armed and demanding that Ethan hand over the money.
- **S30**：Ethan does so, but then slowly puts on a pair of glasses from the original operation in Prague, with a camera built into the bridge.
- **S31**：The image of Jim, alive, is transmitted to the watch Ethan sent to Kittridge, exposing Job's true identity.
- **S32**：Claire tries to intervene, but Jim kills her and climbs up to the roof of the train, while Krieger approaches in a helicopter to extract him.
- **S33**：Ethan follows him onto the roof, impeding him and tethering Krieger's helicopter to the train, dragging it into the Channel Tunnel.
- **S34**：In the tunnel, Jim leaps to the helicopter.
- **S35**：Ethan follows, climbing the helicopter's landing skids and attaching another piece of explosive chewing gum, a final relic of Prague, to the windshield.
- **S36**：He leaps back to the train just as the ensuing explosion kills Krieger and Jim is crushed in between the copter remains and the tracks.
- **S37**：Now with custody of the NOC list, Max, and Job's true identity, Kittridge reinstates Luther and drops his investigation against Ethan, who resigns from the IMF.
- **S38**：As he flies home, a flight attendant approaches him and asks, through a coded phrase, if he is ready to take on a new mission.

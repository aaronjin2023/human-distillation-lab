### James Reese

- **性格倾向**：在职业机会出现时，表现出追求进阶并愿意接受考验的倾向（行为推断；S3、S4、S5、S6）

- **性格倾向**：在任务受阻时呈现强硬推进倾向，愿以激烈行为争取行动条件（行为推断；S5、S6、S7）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：A personal aide to the U.S.
- **S2**：Ambassador in France, James Reese  has an enviable life in Paris and a beautiful French girlfriend, but his real passion is his side job as a low-level operative for the CIA.
- **S3**：Reese wants to become a higher level agent with more responsibilities.
- **S4**：As the result of a shortage of agents in France, he is assigned as a partner to special agent Charlie Wax .
- **S5**：Reese's first test is to have Wax released from airport detention by the French Customs when Wax does not want to surrender the cans of his favorite energy drink.
- **S6**：Despite the apparent triviality of an energy drink that Wax could probably purchase in Paris, Wax continues to verbally abuse French Customs until Reese places a Diplomatic Mail sticker on Wax's luggage containing the energy drink, which makes them immune from Customs and Quarantine requirements.
- **S7**：Once in the car, Wax apologizes for his behavior while opening the tins and revealing the concealed pieces of his personal SIG Sauer, which he refers to as "Mrs.
- **S8**：Jones".
- **S9**：Wax explains that he has been sent to Paris to investigate a drug ring at least indirectly responsible for the death of a woman who happens to be the niece of the Secretary of Defense.
- **S10**：During the investigation, Wax reveals that there was no overdose—their goal is to trace the money back to a circle of Pakistani terrorists.
- **S11**：Evidence leads them to the terrorists, resulting in an armed confrontation in which most of the terrorists are neutralized.
- **S12**：Wax and Reese learn that the terrorists plan to infiltrate the U.S.
- **S13**：Embassy with explosives hidden beneath their burkas.
- **S14**：As they collect evidence, they find photographs of Agent Reese pinned to a wall.
- **S15**：Ultimately, Reese learns that the terrorists are targeting a summit meeting.
- **S16**：Wax discovers that Reese's fiancee Caroline  is a "sleeper" agent who was assigned to infiltrate them.
- **S17**：When confronted, Caroline shoots Reese in the shoulder and escapes through a window.
- **S18**：A Volvo picks her up on the street below.
- **S19**：Caroline attempts to detonate an explosive vest while the last remaining terrorist in the Volvo attacks a US motorcade traveling to the summit by speeding towards them, wired with explosives.
- **S20**：Wax destroys the vehicle with an AT4 rocket launcher just in the nick of time.
- **S21**：Reese finds Caroline at the summit and attempts to dissuade her from carrying out her mission by professing his love.
- **S22**：She attempts to detonate her vest anyway and Reese is forced to kill her.
- **S23**：As Wax leaves Paris, Reese escorts him to his plane.
- **S24**：Wax offers a full-time partnership.
- **S25**：The two play a game of chess on the tarmac, placing their handguns on a utility cart, Reese revealing that he is now carrying a Desert Eagle pistol and earning another modicum of admiration from his new partner.
- **S26**：There are two John Travolta references in this film in conjunction with two movies made by him in the past, and one pertaining to a previous movie of Jonathan Rhys Meyers'.

- **S28**：The Meyers reference is barely noticeable, and occurs when a gang member suggests Wax could snort cocaine off of Reese's butt.
- **S29**：Meyers' character, "Maxwell Demon" does this in Meyers' early film, "Velvet Goldmine."

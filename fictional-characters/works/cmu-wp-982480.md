## 角色资料

### John Carter

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-31790a4fc266b043` · 独立版本：`profile-329479fbc7739f0e`

- **性格倾向**：面对拘禁与强敌时，倾向主动逃脱并设法夺回行动主导权（行为推断；S6、S19、S20、S32、S33）
- **核心动机**：最初寻找返回地球的方法，后来长期追寻重返火星的机会（摘要直接叙述；S14、S26、S28、S34）
- **决策方式**：在缺少通行媒介时，倾向利用对手的追踪欲设局创造机会（行为推断；S29、S30、S31、S32、S33）
- **成长变化**：从把火星视为需要离开的异地，转向选择在那里建立归属（行为推断；S14、S19、S25、S26）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：选择永留火星后丢弃徽章，却因此给敌人驱逐自己的机会。

### Dejah Thoris

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-fbd489a494803f06` · 独立版本：`profile-4a244408531baa69`

- **性格倾向**：面对政治婚姻压力，起初表现出主动逃离被安排命运的倾向（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **核心动机**：在联姻被当作停战条件时，起初希望摆脱这项婚姻安排（行为推断；S12、S13）
- **情绪反应**：遭遇袭击与被俘后，她情绪低落且不情愿地接受联姻（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17、S18）
- **关系模式**：即使自身受困，仍把徽章交给卡特，允许他选择返回地球（行为推断；S18）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：拒绝被安排的婚姻，却在受挫后不得不暂时接受联姻。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：After the sudden death of John Carter , a former American Civil War Confederate Army captain, his nephew Edgar Rice Burroughs  attends the funeral.
- **S2**：As per Carter's instructions, the body is put in a tomb that can be unlocked only from the inside.
- **S3**：His attorney hands over Carter's personal journal for Burroughs to read, in the hope of finding clues explaining Carter's cause of death.
- **S4**：The anecdote recounts back to the Arizona Territory, where Union Colonel Powell  arrests Carter.
- **S5**：Powell, knowing about Carter's military background, seeks his help in fighting the Apache.
- **S6**：However, Carter escapes with the marshals in pursuit.
- **S7**：In an ensuing chase, both Carter and Powell find themselves in a cave in which Carter had been searching for gold.
- **S8**：A Thern appears in the cave at that moment; Carter kills him and, with the help of his medallion, is unknowingly transported to Barsoom .
- **S9**：On the Martian planet, because of his different bone density and the planet's low gravity, Carter is able to jump high and perform feats of incredible strength.
- **S10**：He is captured by the Green Martian Tharks and their Jeddak emperor Tars Tarkas .
- **S11**：Elsewhere on Barsoom, the red Martian cities of Helium and Zodanga have been at war for a thousand years.
- **S12**：Sab Than , Jeddak of Zodanga, armed with a special weapon obtained from the Thern leader Matai Shang , proposes a cease-fire and an end to the war by marrying the Princess of Helium, Dejah Thoris .
- **S13**：The Princess escapes and is rescued by Carter.
- **S14**：Carter, Dejah, and Tarkas' daughter Sola , embark on a quest to get to the end of a sacred river to find a way for Carter to get back home.
- **S15**：They obtain information about the ninth ray, a means of utilizing infinite energy and also the key to understanding how the medallion works.
- **S16**：But they are later attacked by Shang and his minions, the Green Men of Warhoon.
- **S17**：After the attack, Carter is captured and taken back with Dejah while Sola is able to escape.
- **S18**：The demoralized Dejah grudgingly agrees to marry Sab Than, then gives Carter his medallion and tells him to go back to Earth.
- **S19**：Carter decides to stay and is captured by Shang, who explains to him the purpose of Therns and how they manipulate the civilizations of different planets.
- **S20**：Carter is able to make an escape as he and Sola go back to the Tharks requesting their help.
- **S21**：There they discover Tarkas has been overthrown by Tal Hajus .
- **S22**：Tarkas, Carter, and Sola are put on trial in a colosseum battle with two enormous vicious creatures.
- **S23**：After defeating them and killing Hajus, Carter becomes the leader of the Tharks.
- **S24**：The Thark army charges on Helium and defeats the Zodangan army by killing Sab Than.
- **S25**：Carter becomes prince of Helium by marrying Dejah.
- **S26**：On their first night, Carter decides to stay forever on Mars and throws away his medallion.
- **S27**：Seizing this opportunity, Shang banishes him back to Earth.
- **S28**：Carter then embarks in a long quest, looking for clues of the Therns' presence on Earth and hoping to find one of their medallions; after several years he appears to die suddenly and asks for unusual funeral arrangements—consistent with his having found a medallion, since his return to Mars would leave his Earth body in a coma-like state.
- **S29**：He makes Burroughs his protector, giving him clues about how to open the tomb.
- **S30**：The story reverts to the present, where Burroughs runs back to Carter's tomb and opens it only to find it empty.
- **S31**：Shang, in the form of a butler, suddenly appears, having followed Burroughs as well.
- **S32**：But as he prepares to kill Burroughs, Carter appears and kills Shang.
- **S33**：He then tells Burroughs that he never found a medallion; instead, he devised a scheme to lure Shang out of hiding.
- **S34**：Carter takes his medallion, whispers the code, and is then transported back to Barsoom.

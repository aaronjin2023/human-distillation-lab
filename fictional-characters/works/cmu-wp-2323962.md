### Raphael

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-fea696269467f6bd` · 独立版本：`profile-b5cb51a249ddcc74`

- **性格倾向**：在同伴冲突中，他表现出情绪外露并暂时独处的倾向（行为推断；S14、S15）
- **价值取舍**：面对遭袭的记者，他倾向将援助受害者置于取回武器之外（行为推断；S4、S5）
- **关系模式**：他与兄弟既有激烈争执，也能重新投入共同训练（摘要直接叙述；S14、S20）
- **核心动机**：得知导师仍可联系后，他与兄弟共同决定返回营救（摘要直接叙述；S21）

暂无支持：决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：与兄弟发生激烈争执，康复后仍回到共同训练和营救之中。

### Danny Pennington

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-aa02da1d8a191168` · 独立版本：`profile-1e125cf55f07a045`

- **性格倾向**：身处帮派期间，他仍表现出愿意私下听取异见的倾向（行为推断；S22）
- **关系模式**：他在帮派归属之外，与被囚禁的导师建立求教关系（摘要直接叙述；S22）
- **价值取舍**：得知导师将遭杀害时，他倾向让生命安全优先于帮派忠诚（行为推断；S27）
- **成长变化**：他从向帮派首领泄露藏身线索转向协助释放俘虏（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13、S27）

暂无支持：核心动机、决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：曾帮助帮派追踪忍者龟，后来却协助解救其导师。

### Splinter

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-89d8e8a38f06243f` · 独立版本：`profile-d11d26fbaf78b52d`

- **性格倾向**：身处囚禁时，他仍表现出愿意耐心引导年轻人的倾向（行为推断；S22、S23）
- **核心动机**：面对弟子即将被杀的局面，他主动出面阻止致命攻击（行为推断；S30、S31）
- **决策方式**：面对强敌突袭，他倾向利用武器控制对方而非正面硬拼（行为推断；S35）
- **沟通方式**：对帮派少年，他通过讲述自身与旧主经历提供劝导（摘要直接叙述；S22、S23、S24、S25、S26）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As an unsolved crime wave rises in New York City, news reporter April O'Neil covers the reports and rumors of a mysterious 'Foot Clan,' a gang of ninjas that is plaguing the city.
- **S2**：April continues to cover news of the crime wave, to the point where the Shredder, leader of the Foot, decides she needs to be silenced.
- **S3**：She is attacked by the Foot in a subway and is knocked unconscious while trying to fight them off.
- **S4**：Raphael has been following her to retrieve his sai - which he had lost in a previous battle - and easily fights off the Foot.
- **S5**：He carries April back to the Turtles' hideout, unaware that he is being followed by one of the Foot.
- **S6**：Splinter then recounts to an astonished April his and the Turtles' origins: once ordinary turtles living in the sewer, they were mutated into intelligent, human-sized creatures by a discarded canister of toxic waste.
- **S7**：The Turtles escort April back home.
- **S8**：Upon their return to the sewers, the Turtles find that their home has been ransacked and Splinter kidnapped.
- **S9**：With nowhere else to go, the four distraught Turtles return to April's apartment and spend the night there.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, the Foot Clan continues to grow, incorporating a number of delinquent teens into their ranks.
- **S11**：One of these teens is Danny Pennington, who is arrested for robbery.
- **S12**：After bailing Danny out of jail, his father and April's supervisor Charles Pennington stops by her apartment, where Danny incidentally catches a glimpse of one of the Turtles in hiding.
- **S13**：He then reports back to the Shredder, who has been searching for the Turtles.
- **S14**：At April's apartment, Leonardo and Raphael get into a heated argument.
- **S15**：Raphael goes to the roof of the building, where he is ambushed by an army of Foot Clan ninjas.
- **S16**：He is thrown unconscious through April's skylight, and the Turtles scramble to defend themselves from the Foot.
- **S17**：Things look bleak until the arrival of Casey Jones, who helps them fight off the remaining Foot warriors.
- **S18**：However, the building catches fire during the melee, and the Turtles have no choice but to retreat.
- **S19**：They retreat to a farm that belongs to April's family, and April learns that she was fired from her job.
- **S20**：Raphael eventually recovers from his coma, and the Turtles train together vigorously, while April and Casey fall in love.
- **S21**：At one point, Leonardo manages to make contact with Splinter through meditation, and after the Turtles witness him in a shared vision, they decide to return to New York to find and rescue him.
- **S22**：Despite being a member of the Foot Clan, Danny had secretly been taking counsel from Splinter, who shares with him the story of his master Hamato Yoshi's murder by a rival ninja named Oroku Saki.
- **S23**：Splinter explains to Danny that during his master's scuffle and murder at the hands of Saki, the cage that he stayed in had been knocked over.
- **S24**：After being freed from the cage he lunged at Saki's face.
- **S25**：Grabbing on with his claws he proceeded to bite and claw Saki's right cheek.
- **S26**：Saki, bleeding and enraged knocks Splinter to the floor and slices off part of his ear with his sword.
- **S27**：When Danny learns that the Shredder intends to have Splinter killed, he and Casey set him free from captivity.
- **S28**：Although the Foot were set to ambush the Turtles in the sewers upon their return, the Turtles manage to turn the tables on them.
- **S29**：The fight escalates into the streets above and eventually onto a rooftop, where the Turtles finally face off against Shredder, but prove to be no match for him.
- **S30**：Leonardo eventually scores a hit with his ninjato, but is ultimately disarmed and pinned to the ground.
- **S31**：Before Shredder can finish Leonardo off, Splinter appears and challenges him to a fight.
- **S32**：Splinter reveals to the Shredder, he knows he is in fact Oroku Saki, and that they met many years ago at the home of Hamato Yoshi.
- **S33**：Splinter challenges him to remove the cowardly mask that he has been using to hide his shameful wound.
- **S34**：Saki removes his mask and touches his scar, remembering how Splinter gave it to him.
- **S35**：The Shredder then charges Splinter, who using a nunchaku, ensnares Shredder's yari, leaving him to dangle precariously over the roof's edge.
- **S36**：In desperation, Shredder throws a knife from his belt, but when Splinter reaches up to catch it, his grip is released and the Shredder falls into the back of a garbage truck.
- **S37**：Casey then pulls the lever to activate the compactor, crushing the Shredder.
- **S38**：As the police arrive on the scene, the teens inform them on where all the stolen goods can be found.
- **S39**：On the roof, the Turtles reunite with Splinter and, while trying to come up with a proper word to cheer with, Splinter suggests the phrase "Cowabunga." The Turtles unanimously agree, and Splinter declares, "I made a funny!" as the film ends.

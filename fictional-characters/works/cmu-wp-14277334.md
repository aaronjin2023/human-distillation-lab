## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Seventeen-year-old Isabella "Bella" Swan moves to Forks, a small town near the Washington coast, to live with her father, Charlie, after her mother is remarried to a minor league baseball player.
- **S2**：She is quickly befriended by many students at her new high school, but she is intrigued by the mysterious and aloof Cullen siblings.
- **S3**：Bella sits next to Edward Cullen in biology class on her first day of school; he appears to be disgusted by her, much to Bella's confusion.
- **S4**：A few days later, Bella is nearly struck by a van in the school parking lot.
- **S5**：Edward inexplicably moves from several feet away and stops the vehicle with his hand without any harm to himself or Bella.
- **S6**：He later refuses to explain this act to Bella and warns her against befriending him.
- **S7**：After much research, Bella eventually discovers that Edward is a vampire, though he only consumes animal blood.
- **S8**：The pair fall in love and Edward introduces Bella to his vampire family, Carlisle, Esme, Alice, Jasper, Emmett, and Rosalie.
- **S9**：Soon after, three nomadic vampires—James, Victoria, and Laurent—arrive.
- **S10**：James, a tracker vampire, is intrigued by Edward's protectiveness over a human and wants to hunt Bella for sport.
- **S11**：Edward and his family risk their lives to protect her, but James tracks Bella to Phoenix where she is hiding and lures her into a trap by claiming he is holding her mother hostage.
- **S12**：James attacks Bella and bites her wrist, but Edward, along with the other Cullen family members, arrives before he can kill her.
- **S13**：James is destroyed, and Edward sucks James's venom from Bella's wrist, preventing her from becoming a vampire.
- **S14**：A severely injured Bella is taken to a hospital.
- **S15**：Upon returning to Forks, Bella and Edward attend their school prom.
- **S16**：While there, Bella expresses her desire to become a vampire, which Edward refuses to grant.
- **S17**：The film ends with Victoria secretly watching the pair dancing, plotting revenge for her lover James's death.

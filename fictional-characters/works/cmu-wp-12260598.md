### Sid

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-22afad50de34d12f` · 独立版本：`profile-06469e686300ca44`

- **性格倾向**：在嫉妒同伴即将成为父母时，他倾向于贸然认领看似遗弃的蛋。（行为推断；S3）
- **核心动机**：他认领恐龙蛋的举动与对曼尼和艾莉的嫉妒相连。（行为推断；S3）
- **价值取舍**：面对曼尼要求归还的意见，他仍把继续照料这些蛋放在优先位置。（行为推断；S4）
- **决策方式**：在蛋仅被描述为看似遗弃时，他便认领并命名，判断显得仓促。（行为推断；S3、S6）
- **关系模式**：他努力养育三只幼龙，但幼龙的混乱行为影响了群体其他幼崽。（摘要直接叙述；S5）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Ellie  and Manny  are expecting their first child, and Manny is obsessed with making life perfect and safe for the family, since his first experiences as a husband and father went bad when his family was killed by hunters.
- **S2**：At the same time, Diego  finds himself unable to catch a cocky gazelle  he has been stalking and decides to leave the herd, believing that he is losing his predatory nature as a tiger.
- **S3**：Sid  grows jealous of Manny and Ellie and "adopts" three apparently abandoned eggs that he finds in an icy underground cavern and call them Eggbert, Shelly, and Yoko.
- **S4**：Manny tells him to put them back , but Sid instead looks after the eggs, which hatch into baby Tyrannosaurus the next morning.
- **S5**：Although Sid tries his best to raise the three dinosaurs, their rambunctious behavior scares away all the other animals' young and ruins a playground Manny built for Ellie's baby.
- **S6**：A female Tyrannosaurus, Momma, whose eggs Sid stole, soon returns and carries both Sid and her young underground, with Diego in pursuit.
- **S7**：Manny, Ellie, Crash, and Eddie  follow as well and discover that the icy cavern leads to a vast jungle populated by dinosaurs thought to be extinct.
- **S8**：Here, an Ankylosaurus threatens the herd despite Diego's efforts to fend it off; they are saved from a further crowd of angry reptiles by an insane, one-eyed weasel named Buckminster, or Buck  for short.
- **S9**：Buck has been living in this jungle for some time and is chasing Rudy, an abnormally large albino Baryonyx,{{cite news|url'Ice Age' warms up to dinosaurs in third installment|work2009-06-30|accessdateSusan | lastScrat and Scratte Like the previous Ice Age films, the film opens with the saber-toothed squirrel Scrat , who does everything he can to retrieve his precious acorn.
- **S10**：This time, he falls in love with his female counterpart Scratte , with the song "You'll Never Find Another Love Like Mine" by Lou Rawls playing in the background, but an acorn appears instead after the music stops.
- **S11**：The two fight over the acorn with Scratte is flung off the ground.
- **S12**：As Scrat tries to save her, the two hold onto the acorn and the music starts back.
- **S13**：Suddenly, she demonstrates her ability to glide like a flying squirrel, leaving Scrat falling to the ground.
- **S14**：The two eventually fall in love after Scrat saves her from falling to her death in the lava river while she is unconscious.
- **S15**：He even chooses to focus his attention on her instead of the acorn until the end of the film, when he tires of her finicky nature and reverts to his old ways.
- **S16**：The ensuing fight between the couple leads to a volcanic explosion  that hurls Scrat and the acorn back to the surface, leaving Scratte trapped underground.
- **S17**：However, as Scrat is about to enjoy his acorn, a stray piece of ice falls on him and knocks the acorn back into Scratte's hands.
- **S18**：He screams in frustration, having lost both his acorn and Scratte.

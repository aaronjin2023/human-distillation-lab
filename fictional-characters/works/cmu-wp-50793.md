## 角色资料

### Qui-Gon Jinn

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-e6eb5ca588f6b14f` · 独立版本：`profile-98587049efb1fede`

- **性格倾向**：面对议会否决仍坚持培养安纳金，表现出对自身判断的执着。（行为推断；S13、S19、S26）
- **核心动机**：因怀疑安纳金与平衡原力的预言有关，倾向争取培养他。（行为推断；S13、S19、S26）
- **决策方式**：在解救安纳金时采用比赛赌约，借具体交易争取自由。（行为推断；S14、S15）
- **关系模式**：作为欧比旺的师父，临终仍将培养安纳金的责任托付给他。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S26）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：争取到安纳金自由，却未能同时解救他的母亲。

### Anakin Skywalker

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-67cfa06baea2ed1c` · 独立版本：`profile-cf31a4d7fad0f030`

- **性格倾向**：在纳布战斗中显出主动与胆识，驾驶空置战机加入作战。（行为推断；S24）
- **核心动机**：赢得比赛获得自由后，加入同行者以接受绝地训练。（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **关系模式**：离开塔图因时被迫与母亲什米分离，她仍未获得自由。（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **成长变化**：从能够驾驶和制造机器人的奴隶儿童，成为欧比旺的学徒。（摘要直接叙述；S12、S27）

暂无支持：价值取舍、决策方式、情绪反应、沟通方式

### Padmé Amidala

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-8f6431dfd15b6980` · 独立版本：`profile-1b9b9bc80126242b`

- **性格倾向**：面对议会迟迟不行动，表现出不愿被动等待的行动倾向。（行为推断；S21、S23）
- **核心动机**：为结束纳布冲突寻求政治支持，并在返乡后组织本地联盟。（行为推断；S17、S20、S21、S23）
- **决策方式**：政治途径迟滞后转向返乡结盟，倾向根据局势调整办法。（行为推断；S21、S23）
- **价值取舍**：在对抗贸易联邦时重视跨群体合作，并公开表达对盟友的感谢。（行为推断；S23、S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The Galactic Republic is in a period of decline.
- **S2**：In response to a taxation on trade routes, the Trade Federation organizes a blockade of battleships around the planet of Naboo.
- **S3**：Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum  dispatches two Jedi Knights, Qui-Gon Jinn  and his Jedi apprentice Obi-Wan Kenobi , to negotiate with Trade Federation Viceroy Nute Gunray  in hopes of resolving the issue.
- **S4**：However, the Sith Lord Darth Sidious orders the Viceroy to kill the Jedi and invade Naboo with an army of battle droids.
- **S5**：The two Jedi escape the attempt on their lives by stowing aboard landing craft and escaping to Naboo.
- **S6**：There, Qui-Gon saves Gungan outcast Jar Jar Binks  from being crushed by a Federation tank.
- **S7**：Indebted to the Jedi, Jar Jar leads them to the underwater Gungan city of Otoh Gunga; there, the Jedi unsuccessfully attempt to persuade the Gungans to help the people of Naboo, though they are able to obtain a transport to reach the Capital city of Theed on the surface.
- **S8**：The leader of Naboo, Queen Amidala , is captured by the Federation army, but is rescued by the Jedi.
- **S9**：The Queen escapes Naboo with the Jedi on her personal starship, which is damaged on its way through the blockade surrounding the planet, forcing them to land on Tatooine, a nearby desert planet, for repairs.
- **S10**：Qui-Gon ventures into the settlement of Mos Espa with Jar Jar and the astromech droid R2-D2 to a junk shop to purchase a new hyperdrive generator.
- **S11**：The queen allows Padmé, a handmaiden, to journey along with them.
- **S12**：There, they meet a slave child named Anakin Skywalker , a pilot and engineer who has created his own protocol droid, C-3PO.
- **S13**：After spending time with Anakin, Qui-Gon senses a strong presence of the Force within the boy, and suspects he may be the "Chosen One" of Jedi prophecy who will bring balance to the Force.
- **S14**：Qui-Gon makes a bet with Anakin's owner, Watto , to enter him in a podracing tournament; if Anakin wins, he will be freed.
- **S15**：Anakin manages to win the race and joins the group to be trained as a Jedi, but he is forced to leave his mother Shmi  behind, as Qui-Gon was unable to arrange for her release.
- **S16**：They are attacked by Darth Sidious' apprentice, Darth Maul , who was dispatched to capture the Queen.
- **S17**：Having escaped the attack, the Jedi escort the Queen to the Republic's capital planet of Coruscant so she can plead her people's case to the Galactic Senate.
- **S18**：Qui-Gon informs the Jedi Council about his recent attack on Tatooine and suspects of his attacker being a Sith, to which the Council replies that the Sith had been extinct long ago.
- **S19**：Qui-Gon also asks to train Anakin as a Jedi, but the Council rejects it.
- **S20**：Meanwhile, Senator Palpatine of Naboo convinces the Queen to make a motion of no confidence in Valorum in attempt to vote for a stronger Chancellor who will help end the conflict.
- **S21**：She pushes such a vote, but grows frustrated with the lack of action by the Galactic Senate, ultimately deciding to return to Naboo with the Jedi.
- **S22**：Back on Naboo, Padmé reveals herself to be the real Queen Amidala, the other "Queen"  having been a decoy.
- **S23**：Padmé convinces the Gungans to form an alliance against the Trade Federation.
- **S24**：While Jar Jar leads his people in a battle against the droid army, the Queen tries to capture Gunray in Theed, Anakin pilots a vacant starfighter and joins the fight against the Federation droid control ship in space and destroys the ship, deactivating the droid army down on Naboo.
- **S25**：Meanwhile, Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon encounter Darth Maul once more and engage him in a lightsaber duel.
- **S26**：Maul mortally wounds Qui-Gon, but is defeated by Obi-Wan; Qui-Gon's dying request is for Obi-Wan to train Anakin.
- **S27**：Palpatine is elected as the new Supreme Chancellor of the Republic, the Federation Viceroy is sent to stand trial for his crimes, Obi-Wan is bestowed the rank of Jedi Knight, and the Jedi Council accepts Anakin as Obi-Wan's apprentice.
- **S28**：However, the Jedi are still mystified by the recent reemergence of the Sith, and even at Qui-Gon's funeral they're left to wonder if Darth Maul was the Sith master or merely the apprentice, since there are always two of them.
- **S29**：At a festive ceremony, Padmé presents a gift of appreciation and friendship to the Gungans.
- **S30**：During a Post-credit scene a blank screen is shown with Darth Vader's iconic breathing, reminding the viewers of the fate of young Anakin Skywalker.

- **S16**：## Plot Summary
- **S17**：[ edit ] A vampire named Louis de Pointe du Lac tells his 200-year-long life story to a reporter referred to simply as " the boy ".
- **S18**：In 1791, Louis is the young master of a large indigo plantation in colonial Louisiana .
- **S19**：Distraught by the death of his younger brother Paul, he begins drunkenly wandering the streets at night, hoping to be murdered.
- **S20**：One night, he is approached by a vampire named Lestat de Lioncourt , who senses Louis' despair.
- **S21**：Concluding that the loss of his humanity will free him from grief, he accepts Lestat's offer to become his companion and is turned into a vampire.
- **S22**：Louis initially perceives Lestat as an extraordinary being, but is disillusioned after joining him as a vampire, as he realizes Lestat has no special moral insights and is in fact not much older than Louis himself.
- **S23**：Lestat's elderly father moves to the plantation, and Louis learns a few details about Lestat's boyhood, including that he was denied an education and that his father once considered him the gentlest of his brothers.
- **S24**：Lestat spends time feeding off slaves , thieves, and other plantation families, while Louis, who finds it immoral to murder humans, drinks the less potent blood of animals.
- **S25**：Louis' slaves begin to suspect him, and Louis and Lestat realize they cannot remain.
- **S26**：Lestat's father attempts to apologize for the abuse he inflicted on Lestat as a child, but Lestat remains unforgiving and asks Louis to kill his father before they depart.
- **S27**：Louis sets his own plantation aflame; he and Lestat kill the slaves to keep word from spreading about vampires in Louisiana.
- **S28**：Gradually, Louis bends under Lestat's influence and begins feeding from humans.
- **S29**：He slowly accepts his vampirism but also becomes increasingly repulsed by what he perceives as Lestat's total lack of compassion for humans.
- **S30**：Escaping to New Orleans , Louis feeds off a plague -ridden, five-year-old girl, found next to the corpse of her mother.
- **S31**：Louis begins to think of leaving Lestat.
- **S32**：Fearing this, Lestat then turns the girl into a vampire "daughter" for them, to give Louis a reason to stay.
- **S33**：She is then given the name Claudia .
- **S34**：Louis is initially horrified that Lestat has turned a child into a vampire, but soon begins to care for Claudia.
- **S35**：Claudia takes to killing easily but soon realizes she can never grow up; her mind matures into that of a woman, but her body remains that of a young girl.
- **S36**：Claudia blames Lestat for her state and, after 60 years, hatches a plot to kill Lestat.
- **S37**：Claudia and Louis then dump his body into a nearby swamp.
- **S38**：As Louis and Claudia prepare to flee to Europe, Lestat appears, having recovered from Claudia's attack, and confronts them.
- **S39**：Louis sets fire to their home and barely escapes with Claudia, leaving Lestat to burn.
- **S40**：Arriving in Europe, Louis and Claudia seek out other vampires.
- **S41**：They travel throughout Eastern Europe first and encounter some, but these vampires appear to be nothing more than mindless, animated corpses.
- **S42**：Louis begins to regret the murder of Lestat, fearing that they may have killed the one vampire who could provide answers regarding their nature.
- **S43**：It is only when they reach Paris that they encounter vampires like themselves, meeting the 400-year-old vampire Armand and his coven at the Théâtre des Vampires.
- **S44**：Inhabiting an ancient theater, Armand and his vampire coven disguise themselves as humans and feed on terrified humans in mock plays before a live audience (thinking the killings are a performance).
- **S45**：Claudia is repulsed, but Louis and Armand are drawn to each other.
- **S46**：Fearing that Louis will leave her for Armand, Claudia convinces Louis to turn a Parisian doll maker, Madeleine, into a vampire to serve as a replacement companion for her.
- **S47**：Louis, Madeleine, and Claudia live together for a brief time, but all three are abducted one night by Armand's coven.
- **S48**：It is revealed that Lestat survived the fire in New Orleans, scarred and weakened.
- **S49**：He demands that Claudia face punishment, even death, for her murder attempt but screams at the coven that they promised not to harm Louis.
- **S50**：Ignoring Lestat, the coven proceeds with its punishments.
- **S51**：Louis is forcibly locked in a coffin to starve, while Claudia and Madeleine are entrapped in an open courtyard.
- **S52**：Armand arrives and releases Louis, but Madeleine and Claudia are burned to death by the sun; a devastated Louis finds their ashen remains.
- **S53**：Louis sees Lestat crying over the remnants of Claudia's yellow dress but loses track of him as he plots his revenge against the coven.
- **S54**：Louis returns to the Theatre late the following night, burning it to the ground and killing all the vampires inside, leaving with Armand.
- **S55**：Together, the two travel across Europe for several years, but Louis never fully recovers from Claudia's death, and his relationship with Armand becomes strained.
- **S56**：It remains ambiguous even to Louis whether the murder of Claudia was primarily orchestrated by Armand to gain Louis's companionship, by the coven believing Claudia broke ancient vampire law, or by Lestat's desire for vengeance.
- **S57**：Tired of the Old World, Louis returns to New Orleans in the early 20th century.
- **S58**：Living as a loner, he feeds off any humans who cross his path, but lives in the shadows, never creating another companion for himself.
- **S59**：Louis tells the boy of one last encounter with Lestat in New Orleans, where Lestat has hidden himself in a rotting mansion and lost touch with the passing of time.
- **S60**：Armand hopes that seeing Lestat might arouse feelings of passion, sorrow, or anger in Louis and expresses despair that Louis remains cold and unable to share eternity with him.
- **S61**：They end their companionship, with nothing more to offer one another.
- **S62**：Louis ends his tale, revealing that after 200 years, he is weary of immortality and the chaos he has created.
- **S63**：The boy, however, seeing only the great powers granted to a vampire, begs to be made into one himself.
- **S64**：Angry that his interviewer learned nothing, Louis refuses, attacking him and vanishing.
- **S65**：The boy leaves to track down Lestat, hoping to receive immortality.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] A series of murders has begun to plague the town of French Landing, Wisconsin .
- **S3**：The murderer is dubbed "The Fisherman," due to a conscious effort by the killer to emulate the methods of serial killer Albert Fish .
- **S4**：Like Fish, French Landing's killer targets children and indulges in cannibalism of the bodies.
- **S5**：Two victims have already been discovered as the story opens, with a third awaiting discovery.
- **S6**：The nature of the crimes, and the local police's inability to capture the killer, have led people all over the region to become more anxious with each passing day, and certain elements of the local media exacerbate the situation with inflammatory and provocative coverage.
- **S7**：After the events of The Talisman , Jack Sawyer has repressed the memories of his adventures in "The Territories" and his hunt for the Talisman as a twelve-year-old boy; however, the residue of these events has served to subtly affect his life even after he has forgotten them.
- **S8**：Jack grew up to become a lieutenant in the Los Angeles Police Department , where his professionalism and uncanny talent have helped him establish a nearly legendary reputation.
- **S9**：When a series of murders in Los Angeles are traced to a farm insurance salesman from French Landing, Wisconsin, Jack cooperates with the French Landing police to capture the killer.
- **S10**：While in Wisconsin, Jack is irresistibly enraptured by the natural beauty of the Coulee Country, echoing his reaction to The Territories as a child.
- **S11**：When he later intrudes upon a homicide investigation in Santa Monica , certain aspects of the crime scene threaten to revive his repressed memories.
- **S12**：He subsequently resigns from the LAPD, moving to French Landing to enjoy his early retirement.
- **S13**：When the Fisherman begins to terrorize French Landing, the police all but beg "Hollywood" Jack Sawyer for his assistance; they are surprised when Jack flatly refuses.
- **S14**：Memories of the Santa Monica event threaten to overwhelm Jack, and he fears that involving himself in the investigation may affect his sanity.
- **S15**：When a fourth child is taken by the Fisherman, events no longer allow Jack to remain aloof.
- **S16**：It quickly becomes apparent to him that the Fisherman is much more than a serial killer.
- **S17**：In fact, he is an agent of the Crimson King , and his task is to find children with the potential to serve as Breakers .
- **S18**：The fourth victim, Tyler Marshall, is one of the most powerful Breakers there has ever been, and he may be all the Crimson King needs to break the remaining beams of the Dark Tower and bring an end to all worlds.
- **S19**：As the Fisherman also proves capable of "flipping" into The Territories, Jack Sawyer is the only hope of not just French Landing, but of all existence.

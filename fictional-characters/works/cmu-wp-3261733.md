## 角色资料

### Ofelia

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-d775182c483ec29f` · 独立版本：`profile-71c071b3e203f835`

- **性格倾向**：在陌生而危险的环境中，她表现出受好奇心驱动的探索倾向（行为推断；S7、S8、S10、S11）
- **核心动机**：她接受试炼以确认公主身份，同时持续担忧并照料母亲（行为推断；S11、S13、S14、S20）
- **价值取舍**：当回归地下王国需要伤害弟弟时，她选择保护无辜者（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28、S37）
- **决策方式**：她并非一味服从神秘权威，而会因欲望或道德判断违令（行为推断；S16、S18、S28）
- **成长变化**：她的最终资格由拒绝伤害无辜者证明，超越单纯完成任务（摘要直接叙述；S28、S37）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：渴望完成试炼获得归属，却拒绝以弟弟受伤换取通行。

### Vidal

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-ef86c95103102df0` · 独立版本：`profile-987799e9392ccf19`

- **性格倾向**：在掌握军政权力时，他表现出以残酷惩罚维持控制的倾向（行为推断；S12、S19、S23）
- **核心动机**：他奉命清剿反佛朗哥叛军，并执着维护自己的父系名声（行为推断；S6、S32）
- **价值取舍**：他将服从置于怜悯之上，杀死终止囚犯痛苦的医生（行为推断；S19）
- **决策方式**：面对疑似敌人与不服从者，他倾向直接诉诸酷刑和杀戮（行为推断；S12、S19、S29）
- **关系模式**：他临死仍试图借儿子保存自身名号，却被Mercedes拒绝（行为推断；S31、S32、S33）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：要求儿子记住自己的死亡时刻，却无法控制儿子将获得的记忆。

以上为依据完整摘要审校的综合笔记，未逐项标注句号；可结合下列完整选摘核对。

### Mercedes

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-f1ba6aded70059d2` · 独立版本：`profile-81db48c66fcba696`

- **性格倾向**：在暴露与受刑威胁下，她表现出敢于反抗并寻找脱身机会的倾向（行为推断；S22、S23）
- **核心动机**：她在Vidal身边秘密协助叛军，并尝试带Ofelia逃离（摘要直接叙述；S9、S22）
- **价值取舍**：她拒绝替Vidal保存父亲名望，切断其暴力权威的传承诉求（行为推断；S31、S32、S33）
- **决策方式**：隐蔽身份失效后，她迅速转为直接反击并逃向叛军（摘要直接叙述；S22、S23）
- **情绪反应**：她在Ofelia濒死时给予安慰，并为女孩的死亡哀悼（摘要直接叙述；S35、S38）

暂无支持：关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In a fairy tale, Princess Moanna, whose father is the king of the underworld, becomes curious about the world above.
- **S2**：When she goes to the surface, the sunlight blinds her and erases her memory.
- **S3**：She becomes very ill and eventually dies.
- **S4**：The king believes that her spirit will come back to the underworld someday.
- **S5**：In post-Civil War Spain in 1944  Ofelia, a young girl who loves fairy tales, travels with her pregnant mother Carmen to meet Captain Vidal, her new stepfather and father of Carmen's unborn child.
- **S6**：Vidal, the son of a famed commander who died in Morocco, believes strongly in falangism and was assigned to root out any anti-Franco rebels.
- **S7**：Ofelia discovers a large insect resembling a stick insect which she believes to be a fairy.
- **S8**：It follows her to the mill where Vidal is stationed and leads Ofelia into an ancient labyrinth nearby.
- **S9**：Before Ofelia can enter, she is stopped by Mercedes, one of Vidal's maids who is spying for the rebels.
- **S10**：That night, the insect appears in Ofelia's bedroom, where it changes into a fairy and leads her through the labyrinth.
- **S11**：There, she meets the faun, who believes her to be Princess Moanna and gives her three tasks to complete before the full moon to ensure that her "essence is intact".
- **S12**：Meanwhile, Vidal's cruel and sociopathic nature is revealed when he brutally murders two individuals who had been detained on suspicion of being rebel allies and who may merely have been farmers.
- **S13**：Ofelia completes the first task of retrieving a key from the belly of a giant toad, but she becomes worried about her mother whose condition is worsening.
- **S14**：The faun gives Ofelia a mandrake root, which instantly begins to cure her mother's illness.
- **S15**：Accompanied by three fairy guides, Ofelia then completes the second task of retrieving an ornate dagger from the lair of the Pale Man, a child-eating monster who sits silently in front of a large feast.
- **S16**：Although she was gravely warned not to consume anything, she eats two grapes, awakening him.
- **S17**：He eats two of the fairies and chases her, but she manages to escape.
- **S18**：Infuriated at her disobedience, the faun refuses to give her the third task.
- **S19**：Meanwhile, Vidal becomes increasingly vicious, torturing a captured rebel and then killing the doctor — also a rebel sympathizer — who euthanized the tortured prisoner to stop his pain.
- **S20**：Vidal catches Ofelia tending to the mandrake root, and Carmen throws it into the fireplace, where it then begins to writhe and scream in agony.
- **S21**：Instantly, Carmen develops painful contractions and dies giving birth to a son.
- **S22**：Vidal discovers that Mercedes is a spy, and he captures her and Ofelia as they attempt to escape.
- **S23**：Ofelia is locked in her bedroom, and Mercedes is taken to be tortured; however, she frees herself, badly injures Vidal and flees into the woods, where the rebels rescue her.
- **S24**：The faun returns to Ofelia and gives her one more chance to prove herself.
- **S25**：He tells her to take her baby brother into the labyrinth.
- **S26**：Ofelia steals the baby after sedating Vidal; although disoriented, Vidal continues to chase her through the labyrinth while the rebels attack the mill.
- **S27**：The faun tells Ofelia that the portal to the underworld will open only with an innocent's blood, so he needs a drop of her brother's blood.
- **S28**：Ofelia refuses to harm her brother, and eventually Vidal finds her, seemingly talking to herself as the faun is not visible through his eyes.
- **S29**：The faun leaves Ofelia to her choice, and Vidal takes the baby away from her, shooting her immediately after.
- **S30**：When he leaves the labyrinth, the rebels and Mercedes are waiting for him.
- **S31**：Knowing that he will die, he calmly hands Mercedes the baby.
- **S32**：Vidal takes out his watch and tells Mercedes to tell his son the exact time of his father's death.
- **S33**：Mercedes interrupts, telling him that his son will never even know his name.
- **S34**：Pedro, one of the rebels and Mercedes' brother, draws his pistol and shoots Vidal in the face, killing him.
- **S35**：As Mercedes enters the labyrinth and comforts the dying girl, drops of Ofelia's blood spill onto the altar that is supposed to lead her into the underworld.
- **S36**：Ofelia is reunited with the king and queen of the underworld.
- **S37**：The faun is present too, and the king reveals to her that, by shedding her own blood instead of the blood of an innocent, she has completed the final task and proven herself to be Princess Moanna.
- **S38**：In the mortal world, Ofelia dies and Mercedes mourns her death.
- **S39**：In an epilogue, a narration dictates that Princess Moanna ruled the underworld with a just and kind heart, but left behind "small traces of her time on earth, visible only to those who know where to look".

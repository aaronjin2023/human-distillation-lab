- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Erin flees her abusive alcoholic husband, Kevin, takes on a different identity and changes her name to Katie.
- **S3**：She arrives in Southport, North Carolina .
- **S4**：Finding work at a seafood restaurant, she becomes friends with her neighbor, Jo, and gets to know the town's general store owner, Alex.
- **S5**：Katie is interested in Alex, who is a widower with two children.
- **S6**：Alex and Katie's relationship becomes romantic, encouraged by Jo.
- **S7**：Alex's former job had trained him to recognize signs of an abuse victim.
- **S8**：Katie reveals her past to Alex, who wants to know why she did not go seek help from the police.
- **S9**：She explains that Kevin is a police detective.
- **S10**：She fled from his abuse because she knew he would kill her if he located her.
- **S11**：Her created identity is based on a neighbor's dead daughter who resembled her.
- **S12**：Kevin is furious with his wife for leaving and drank more to dull his pain.
- **S13**：He learns of Erin's new identity and searches for her.
- **S14**：Kevin arrives in Southport, while Katie is caring for Alex's children.
- **S15**：Kevin sets the house on fire, thinking it would kill both Katie and Alex.
- **S16**：She fights Kevin, and the children escape.
- **S17**：Alex, arriving home, finds his children and takes them to Katie's house where he thinks they will be safe.
- **S18**：He returns to the fire and finds Kevin attempting to harm Katie.
- **S19**：Desperate, Alex drives toward Kevin who is firing a gun at him.
- **S20**：The vehicle hits Kevin, breaking his hand and disarming him.
- **S21**：Katie retrieves Kevin's gun, and she and Alex are reunited.
- **S22**：Kevin, however, is able to drive away.
- **S23**：He goes to Katie's house.
- **S24**：When she learns that Alex left the children at her house, she is terrified, knowing that Kevin will hunt her down at her house, putting the children's lives in danger.
- **S25**：As they reach Katie's house, Kevin attacks Alex with a crowbar .
- **S26**：Kevin is going to shoot and kill Katie, but when he fires the gun, the bullet hits Kevin in the stomach and he dies in front of Katie.
- **S27**：Alex had been given an unopened letter from his dead wife, Carly.
- **S28**：After he and Katie recover from their injuries, they locate the safe in the ashes of the burnt home.
- **S29**：He gives Katie the unopened letter to read.
- **S30**：She goes home to read it and is shocked when she sees Jo's house looking like no one had ever lived in it.
- **S31**：Startled, she begins to think that Jo had been a complete figment of her imagination.
- **S32**：Upon reading the letter, Katie realizes that it was written by Alex's late wife, Carly Jo, whom friends called "Jo".

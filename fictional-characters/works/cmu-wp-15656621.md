## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Five years after the events of The Fast and the Furious, Dominic Toretto  and his new crew, Leticia "Letty" Ortiz , Tego "Teddy" , Omar Santos , Cara Mirtha  and Han Seoul-Oh , are hijacking fuel tankers in the Dominican Republic.
- **S2**：Dominic begins to suspect the trail is too hot and leaves Leticia behind in order to protect her from harm.
- **S3**：Several weeks later, in Panama City, Dominic gets a call from Mia, who tells him that Leticia has been murdered by Fenix Calderon , after getting into a near fatal car accident.
- **S4**：Dominic heads back to Los Angeles to examine Leticia's crash and finds traces of nitromethane.
- **S5**：He then goes to the only car mechanic that uses nitromethane and coerces him into giving him the name David Park , the man who ordered the fuel.
- **S6**：Meanwhile, F.B.I. agent Brian O'Conner  is trying to track down a drug dealer named Arturo Braga .
- **S7**：His search leads him to David Park.
- **S8**：Dominic arrives at Park's apartment first and hangs him out of the window by his ankles before letting go.
- **S9**：Brian, who was also on his way to Park's place, saves Park and Park becomes the FBI's new informant.
- **S10**：Park gets Brian into a street race through Los Angeles.
- **S11**：Brian selects a modified Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 from the Impound Lot.
- **S12**：Dominic also shows up to race in his modified 1970 Chevrolet Chevelle.
- **S13**：Gisele Yashar , the liaison for Braga, reveals that the winner will become the last driver on a team that traffics heroin between the Mexico–United States border.
- **S14**：Dominic wins by bumping Brian's car, making him lose control.
- **S15**：Brian uses his power as an FBI agent to arrest another driver, Dwight Mueller , and takes his place on the team.
- **S16**：The following day, the team meets one of the Braga's men.
- **S17**：They drive across the border using underground tunnels to avoid detection.
- **S18**：Brian had prior knowledge that, after the heroin was delivered, Braga ordered the drivers to be killed.
- **S19**：However it was revealed to Dominic from Fenix that he killed Leticia and after a tense stand-off, Dominic detonates his car with nitrous to distract Braga's men and Brian hijacks a Hummer with $60 million worth of heroin.
- **S20**：Both Dominic and Brian drive back to Los Angeles and hide the heroin in a police impound lot where Brian picks up a modified Subaru Impreza WRX STI.
- **S21**：Later on, Dominic finds out Brian was the last person to contact Leticia, which results in him being attacked by Dominic until he learns Leticia was working undercover for Brian, tracking down Braga in exchange for clearing Dominic's name.
- **S22**：Brian tells his superiors that in exchange for Dominic's pardon, he will lure Braga into a trap, forcing him to personally show up to exchange money for the heroin.
- **S23**：At the drop site, however, Ramon Campos , the man who claims to be "Braga", is revealed as a decoy, and "Campos", the real Braga, escapes and flees to Mexico.
- **S24**：Brian and Dominic travel to Mexico on their own to catch Braga.
- **S25**：They find him at a church and apprehend him.
- **S26**：As Braga's henchmen come down to rescue their leader, Brian and Dominic drive through the underground tunnels back to the United States.
- **S27**：When some of Braga's men are killed, Brian crashes his car and is injured after being T-boned by Fenix at the end of the tunnel.
- **S28**：Before Fenix can kill Brian, Dominic, who survived the explosion and exchanged his 1970 Dodge Charger with a 1973 Chevrolet Camaro, drives into and kills Fenix.
- **S29**：As police and helicopters start streaming to the crash site on the USA side, Brian tells Dominic to leave, but Dominic refuses, saying he's tired of running.
- **S30**：Despite Brian's request for clemency, the judge sentences Dominic to 25 years to life.
- **S31**：Dominic boards a prison bus that will take him to Lompoc penitentiary and as the bus drives down the road, Brian and Mia, along with Leo and Santos , arrive in their cars to intercept it.

### Gaylord Focker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-8eb29cc24b8d7d27` · 独立版本：`profile-7881a020aa259815`

### Jack Byrnes

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-f689658d29121c54` · 独立版本：`profile-7c6af1a4e662425e`

- **性格倾向**：面对女儿的伴侣，表现出强烈戒备与控制局面的倾向（行为推断；S8、S10、S22）
- **价值取舍**：起初以职业和家庭差异评判伴侣资格，重视自身标准（行为推断；S8）
- **决策方式**：评估格雷格时，倾向采用测谎与隐蔽监控获取信息（行为推断；S10、S22）
- **成长变化**：在妻女反对后承认女儿的爱情，并主动促成两人和解（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18、S19）

暂无支持：核心动机、情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：试图决定谁适合女儿，最终却须回应女儿自己的感情。

- **S1**：Gaylord "Greg" Focker  is a nurse living in Chicago.
- **S2**：He intends to propose to his girlfriend Pam Byrnes , but his plan is disrupted when he learns that Pam's sister's fiance had asked Pam's father for permission before proposing.
- **S3**：Greg and Pam travel to Pam's parents' house to attend Pam's sister's wedding.
- **S4**：Greg hopes to propose to Pam in front of her family after receiving her father's permission.
- **S5**：But this plan is put on hold when the airline loses his luggage, including the engagement ring.
- **S6**：At the Byrnes' home, Greg meets Pam's father Jack , mother Dina  and their beloved cat Mr.
- **S7**：Jinx.
- **S8**：Jack takes an instant dislike to Greg and openly criticizes him for his choice of career as a male nurse and whatever else he sees as a difference between Greg and the Byrnes family.
- **S9**：Greg tries to impress Jack, but his efforts fail.
- **S10**：Greg becomes even more uncomfortable after he receives an impromptu lie detector test from Jack and later learns from Pam that her father is a retired CIA counterintelligence officer.
- **S11**：Meeting the rest of Pam's family and friends, Greg still feels like an outsider.
- **S12**：Despite efforts to impress the family, Greg's inadvertent actions make him an easy target for ridicule and anger: He accidentally gives Pam's sister a black eye during a volleyball mishap; uses a malfunctioning toilet that floods the Byrnes' back yard with sewage; sets the wedding altar on fire and inadvertently leads Jack to think he is a marijuana user.
- **S13**：Later, Greg loses Jinx and replaces him with a stray whose tail he spray paints to make him look like Mr.
- **S14**：Jinx.
- **S15**：By now, the entire Byrnes family, including Pam, agrees that it is best for Greg to leave.
- **S16**：Unwillingly, Greg goes to the airport where he is detained by airport security.
- **S17**：Back at the Byrnes household, Jack tries to convince his wife and Pam that Greg would be an unsuitable husband.
- **S18**：Upon receiving retribution from both his wife and Pam, Jack realizes that Pam truly loves Greg.
- **S19**：Jack rushes to the airport, convinces airport security to release Greg and brings him back to the Byrnes household.
- **S20**：Greg proposes to Pam.
- **S21**：She accepts, and her parents agree that they should now meet Greg's parents.
- **S22**：After Debbie's wedding, Jack views footage of Greg recorded by hidden cameras that he had placed strategically around their house.

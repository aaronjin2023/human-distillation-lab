## 角色资料

### James P. Sullivan

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7b0f669e7001e53c` · 独立版本：`profile-b730276a6d4cde24`

- **性格倾向**：面对原本被视为危险的孩子，他倾向在接触后形成保护欲（行为推断；S10、S13、S14、S22）
- **核心动机**：即使遭到放逐，他仍坚持返回工厂营救受威胁的孩子（摘要直接叙述；S19、S21、S22）
- **价值取舍**：面对公司的生产危机，他最终选择以儿童快乐替代恐惧（行为推断；S32、S33、S34）
- **决策方式**：发现同事虐待阴谋后，他起初倾向向上级求助解决问题（行为推断；S17、S18、S19）
- **成长变化**：与孩子的亲密经验促使他重新定义工作目标和领导方式（摘要直接叙述；S14、S32、S33、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：职业以制造恐惧为基础，对孩子的依恋却推动他改变这一制度。

### Mike Wazowski

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-4f8b3ad39d368598` · 独立版本：`profile-6503f1e0f94c5f48`

- **性格倾向**：在伙伴关系受挫时，他虽倾向赌气退缩，也能主动修复分歧（行为推断；S21、S23）
- **核心动机**：面对误入工厂的孩子，他起初着力将她送回原来的世界（摘要直接叙述；S15）
- **情绪反应**：遭到放逐后，他因挫败而一度拒绝跟随伙伴继续行动（摘要直接叙述；S19、S21）
- **决策方式**：面对追捕，他倾向通过分散追兵注意为伙伴争取行动空间（行为推断；S28）
- **关系模式**：他愿花力气重建孩子的门，以回应伙伴难以割舍的牵挂（行为推断；S31、S35）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：一度因挫折拒绝同行，之后又主动道歉并承担协助责任。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The parallel city of Monstropolis is inhabited by monsters and powered by the screams of children in the human world.
- **S2**：At the Monsters, Inc. factory, employees called "Scarers" venture into children's bedrooms to scare them and collect their screams, using closet doors as portals.
- **S3**：This is considered a dangerous task since the monsters believe children to be toxic and that touching them would be fatal.
- **S4**：However, production is falling as children are becoming harder to scare and the company chairman Henry J.
- **S5**：Waternoose III is determined to find a solution.
- **S6**：The top Scarer is James P.
- **S7**："Sulley" Sullivan, who lives with his assistant Mike Wazowski and has a rivalry with the ever-determined chameleon-like monster Randall Boggs.
- **S8**：During an ordinary day's work on the "Scarefloor", another scarer accidentally brings a child's sock into the factory, causing the Children Detection Agency  to arrive and cleanse him.
- **S9**：Mike is harassed by Roz the clerk for not completing his paperwork on time.
- **S10**：While working late at the factory, Sulley discovers that Randall left an activated door on the Scarefloor and a young girl  who has entered the factory, much to Sulley's horror.
- **S11**：After a few failed attempts to put her back, he places her in his bag and hides when Randall arrives and returns the door to storage.
- **S12**：Mike is at a restaurant on a date with his girlfriend Celia when Sulley comes to him for help, but chaos erupts when the girl is discovered in the restaurant, and the CDA is called.
- **S13**：Sulley and Mike escape the CDA and take the girl home, discovering that she is not toxic after all.
- **S14**：Sulley quickly grows attached to the girl and names her "Boo".
- **S15**：The next day, they smuggle her into the factory and Mike attempts to return her through her door.
- **S16**：Randall tries to kidnap Boo, but kidnaps Mike by mistake.
- **S17**：In the basement, Randall reveals to Mike he has built a torture machine  to extract children's screams, which would make the company's current tactics redundant.
- **S18**：Sulley stops Randall from testing the machine on Mike and reports him to Waternoose.
- **S19**：However, Waternoose is revealed to be in allegiance with Randall and he exiles Mike and Sulley to the Himalayas.
- **S20**：The two are taken in by the Abominable Snowman, who tells them they can return to the factory through the nearby village.
- **S21**：Sulley heads out, but Mike refuses to follow him out of frustration.
- **S22**：Sulley returns to the factory and rescues Boo from the Scream Extractor.
- **S23**：Mike returns to apologise to Sulley and inadvertently helps Sulley defeat Randall in a fight.
- **S24**：Randall pursues Mike and Sulley as they race the factory and ride on the doors heading into storage, taking them into a giant vault where millions of closet doors are stored.
- **S25**：Boo's laughter activates the doors and allows the chase to pass in and out of the human world.
- **S26**：After Randall almost kills Sulley by pushing him out of an open door, Sulley and Mike trap him in the human world using a door to a trailer park, where he is mistaken for an alligator and beaten up by a pair of hillbillies.
- **S27**：They are finally able to access Boo's door, but Waternoose and the CDA send it back to the Scarefloor.
- **S28**：Mike distracts the CDA, while Sulley escapes with Boo and her door while Waternoose follows.
- **S29**：Waternoose is tricked into confessing his plan to kidnap children in the simulation bedroom and is arrested by the CDA.
- **S30**：The CDA's leader is revealed to be Roz, who has been undercover for years trying to prove there was a scandal at Monsters Inc.
- **S31**：Sulley and Mike say goodbye to Boo and return her home; on Roz’s orders Boo’s door is then destroyed.
- **S32**：Sulley becomes the new chairman of Monsters Inc., and thanks to his experience with Boo, he comes up with a plan to end the company's energy crisis.
- **S33**：Months later, Sulley's leadership has changed the company's workload.
- **S34**：The monsters now enter children's bedrooms to entertain them, since laughter is ten times more powerful than screams.
- **S35**：Mike takes Sulley aside, revealing he has almost rebuilt Boo's door, requiring only one more piece which Sulley took as a memento.
- **S36**：Sulley enters and reunites with Boo.

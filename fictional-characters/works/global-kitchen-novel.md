- **S20**：## Plot
- **S21**：[ edit ] From Mikage's love of kitchens to her job as a culinary teacher's assistant to the multiple scenes in which food is merely present, Kitchen is a short window into the life of a young Japanese woman and her discoveries about food and love amongst a background of tragedy.
- **S22**：In Kitchen , a young Japanese woman named Mikage Sakurai struggles to overcome the death of her grandmother.
- **S23**：She gradually grows close to one of her grandmother's friends, Yuichi, from a flower shop and ends up staying with him and his transgender mother, Eriko.
- **S24**：During her stay, she develops affection for Yuichi and Eriko, almost becoming part of their family.
- **S25**：However, she moves out after six months as she finds a new job as a culinary teacher's assistant.
- **S26**：When she finds that Eriko was murdered, she tries to support Yuichi through the difficult time, and realises that Yuichi is probably in love with her.
- **S27**：Reluctant to face her own feelings for him, she goes away to Izu for a work assignment, while Yuichi stays in a guest-house.
- **S28**：However, after going to a restaurant to eat katsudon , she realises she wants to bring it to Yuichi.
- **S29**：She goes to Yuichi’s guest-house and sneaks inside his room in the middle of the night to bring him katsudon.
- **S30**：There Mikage tells him she doesn’t want to lose him and proposes to build a new life together.
- **S31**：In Moonlight Shadow , a woman named Satsuki loses her boyfriend Hitoshi in an accident and tells us: "The night he died my soul went away to some other place and I couldn't bring it back".
- **S32**：She becomes friendly with his brother Hiiragi, whose girlfriend died in the same crash.
- **S33**：On one insomniac night out walking she meets a strange woman called Urara who has also lost someone.
- **S34**：Urara introduces her to the mystical experience of The Weaver Festival Phenomenon, which she hopes will cauterize their collective grief.

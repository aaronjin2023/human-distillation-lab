## 角色资料

### Ethan Hunt

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7d4500b02eb5b040` · 独立版本：`profile-a0c6ada960462134`

- **性格倾向**：在亲近者遇险时倾向亲自介入，面对束缚仍设法继续营救（行为推断；S2、S17、S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：退出一线后希望与朱莉娅安定生活，危机中则全力营救她（摘要直接叙述；S1、S11、S18、S20、S21）
- **价值取舍**：面对妻子的性命威胁时，将救人置于服从组织管束之前（行为推断；S18、S19、S20、S21）
- **决策方式**：在无法常规排除爆炸物时，愿承担极高身体风险争取生机（行为推断；S30、S31）
- **关系模式**：从向妻子隐瞒职业转为依靠她自救，并在脱险后解释真实身份（摘要直接叙述；S1、S31、S32、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望稳定婚姻生活，秘密职业却让妻子成为敌人的胁迫对象。；追查戴维恩时越过组织审批，随后因擅自行动遭到拘捕。

### John Musgrave

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-b55eb9c7ae2650bb` · 独立版本：`profile-10218703e92537a5`

- **性格倾向**：在实现秘密计划时倾向隐蔽操纵，借同僚信任引导他人行动（行为推断；S19、S25、S26）
- **核心动机**：安排戴维恩把兔脚售予恐怖组织，以给组织先发打击提供理由（摘要直接叙述；S26）
- **价值取舍**：为制造军事行动的理由，愿意把危险交易与人质胁迫当作手段（行为推断；S25、S26、S28）
- **决策方式**：倾向以秘密协助制造合作表象，再利用信息与人质控制局面（行为推断；S19、S25、S27、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：表面帮助伊森脱离拘押，实际推动他完成自己安排的危险交易。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Ethan Hunt  has retired from active field work for the Impossible Missions Force  and instead trains new recruits while settling down with his fiancée Julia Meade , a nurse at a local hospital who is unaware of Ethan's past.
- **S2**：Ethan is approached by fellow IMF agent John Musgrave  about a mission for him: rescue one of Ethan's protégés, Lindsey Farris , who was captured while investigating arms dealer Owen Davian .
- **S3**：Musgrave has already prepared a team for Ethan, consisting of Declan Gormley , Zhen Lei , and his old partner Luther Stickell , in Berlin, Germany.
- **S4**：The team raids the warehouse where Lindsey is kept and collects two computer laptops during their escape.
- **S5**：As they flee on a helicopter, Ethan discovers a micro-explosive implanted in Lindsey's head.
- **S6**：Before Ethan can use a defibrillator to disable the device, it kills her.
- **S7**：Ethan and Musgrave are reprimanded by IMF director Theodore Brassel .
- **S8**：Ethan learns that Lindsey mailed him a postcard before her capture, and discovers a magnetic microdot under the stamp.
- **S9**：IMF technician Benji Dunn  recovers enough from the damaged laptops to determine that Davian will be in Vatican City to obtain a mysterious object called the "Rabbit's Foot".
- **S10**：Ethan plans the mission to capture Davian without seeking Brassel's or Musgrave's approval.
- **S11**：Before leaving, he and Julia have an impromptu wedding at the hospital's chapel.
- **S12**：The team successfully infiltrates Vatican City and captures Davian.
- **S13**：On the flight back to the United States, Davian threatens to kill Ethan and his loved ones.
- **S14**：Ethan then threatens to drop Davian out of the plane .
- **S15**：After landing, Ethan learns that the microdot contains a video of Lindsey warning that she believes Brassel is working with Davian.
- **S16**：Then the convoy taking Davian across the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel is suddenly attacked, and Davian escapes.
- **S17**：Ethan worries for Julia's safety and races to the hospital, arriving to find that she already has been taken.
- **S18**：Davian calls Ethan to give him 48 hours to recover the Rabbit's Foot for Julia's life, but then Ethan is captured by IMF for his rogue actions.
- **S19**：Musgrave takes part in Ethan's interrogation, but discreetly mouths instructions for Ethan to lip-read about Shanghai, China where the Rabbit's Foot is located, and provides him with the means to escape IMF.
- **S20**：Ethan and his team raid the building where the Rabbit's Foot is secured, and inform Davian that they have the Rabbit's Foot.
- **S21**：Ethan goes to deliver the Rabbit's Foot alone, and is forced to take a tranquilizer.
- **S22**：While sedated, a micro-explosive is implanted in his head.
- **S23**：Ethan awakens to find Davian apparently holding Julia at gunpoint.
- **S24**：Despite Ethan asserting that he brought the real Rabbit's Foot, Davian apparently kills Julia and leaves.
- **S25**：Musgrave then arrives and explains to Ethan that the woman killed by Davian was not the real Julia, but Davian's head of security  in a mask — the execution carried out for failing to protect Davian in Vatican City, and the mask was used in order to force Ethan to confirm the authenticity of the Rabbit's Foot — and that the real Julia is alive.
- **S26**：Musgrave reveals himself as the mole, having arranged for Davian to acquire the Rabbit's Foot to sell to terrorist groups, after which IMF would have reason to launch a preemptive strike.
- **S27**：Musgrave then asks Ethan about the microdot Lindsey sent.
- **S28**：Musgrave dials his phone for Ethan to hear Julia's voice.
- **S29**：Ethan then bites on Musgrave's hand and knocks him unconscious, ultimately freeing himself and stealing Musgrave's phone, using it to track down the location of Musgrave's last call.
- **S30**：Ethan finds Davian there and pushes him into the path of a truck, but not before Davian triggers the micro-explosive.
- **S31**：Freeing Julia, he instructs her to electrocute him in order to deactivate the explosive, and then revive him.
- **S32**：He also instructs her in using a gun for her protection.
- **S33**：While attempting to revive Ethan, Julia shoots and kills Musgrave.
- **S34**：Julia successfully revives Ethan, and he explains his true IMF career to her as they leave.
- **S35**：Brassel congratulates Ethan as he leaves for his honeymoon with Julia.
- **S36**：Ethan is unsure if he will return to the IMF.
- **S37**：Brassel promises that he will tell Ethan what the Rabbit's Foot is if Ethan will promise to return.
- **S38**：Ethan smiles and walks off with Julia.

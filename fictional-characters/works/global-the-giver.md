- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：Jonas, a 12-year-old boy, lives in a community isolated from all except a few similar towns, where everyone has an assigned role.
- **S3**：With the annual Ceremony of Twelve upcoming, he is nervous, as he will be assigned his life's work by the Community's leaders, the Committee of Elders.
- **S4**：He is reassured by his father, a Nurturer (who cares for the infants in the Community) and his mother, a high-ranking official in the Department of Justice.
- **S5**：During the ceremony, Jonas is assembled with his classmates.
- **S6**：The Chief Elder, who presides, initially does not call Jonas.
- **S7**：After everyone else is called, the Chief Elder explains that Jonas was not given a normal assignment, but instead has been selected as the next "Receiver of Memory", to be trained by the current one, who sits among the Elders and shares unusual pale eyes with Jonas.
- **S8**：The position has high status and responsibility, and Jonas quickly finds himself growing distant from his classmates, including his close friends.
- **S9**：The rules Jonas receives further separate him, as they allow him no time with his friends and require him to keep his training secret.
- **S10**：They also allow him to lie and withhold his feelings from his family, which are generally not allowed in the Community.
- **S11**：Once he begins, Jonas discovers the purpose of his position: the Receiver retains the memories from all of history and is the only one allowed access to books outside of permitted text.
- **S12**：The current Receiver, who asks Jonas to call him the Giver, begins transferring those memories to Jonas, as those in the Community know nothing of the past.
- **S13**：These memories, as well as restricted books, give the Receiver perspective to advise the Council of Elders.
- **S14**：The first memory is of sliding down a snow-covered hill on a sled, shocking Jonas as he has never seen a sled, snow, or a hill.
- **S15**：These memories have been given up, assuring security and conformity (called Sameness).
- **S16**：Even color has been surrendered, and the Giver shows Jonas a rainbow.
- **S17**：He gives Jonas darker memories of hunger and war.
- **S18**：The Giver reveals he previously had an apprentice, named Rosemary, but does not reveal her fate.
- **S19**：Jonas's father is concerned about an infant at the Nurturing Center who is failing to thrive, and receives special permission to bring him home at night.
- **S20**：The baby's name will be Gabriel if he grows strong enough to be assigned to a family.
- **S21**：He has pale eyes, like Jonas and the Giver.
- **S22**：Jonas grows attached to him, especially when Jonas finds that he can receive memories.
- **S23**：If Gabriel does not recover, he will be "released from the Community"—in common speech, taken Elsewhere.
- **S24**：This has happened to individuals deemed unfit for the Community, including Rosemary.
- **S25**：The Giver shows Jonas a secret video of Jonas's father doing his job: releasing the smaller of two identical twin newborns through a lethal injection before putting the infant in a trash chute, since identical community members are forbidden.
- **S26**：This causes Jonas to learn that those who are "released" are actually killed.
- **S27**：Believing his father is a murderer, Jonas initially refuses to return home, but the Giver convinces him that without the memories, the people of the Community cannot know right versus wrong.
- **S28**：Rosemary was unable to endure the darker memories and instead killed herself with poison.
- **S29**：Jonas and the Giver devise a plan to return the Community's memories.
- **S30**：Both agree that Jonas will leave, thereby returning the memories to them, while the Giver will stay to help them learn to live with their memories before joining his daughter, Rosemary, in death.
- **S31**：They plan to fake Jonas's drowning to limit the search for him, but he instead escapes in a rush with Gabriel upon learning of the child's imminent release.
- **S32**：The two are near death from cold and starvation when they reach the border of what Jonas believes must be Elsewhere.
- **S33**：Using his mysterious ability to "see beyond", he finds a sled at the top of a snowy hill.
- **S34**：He and Gabriel ride the sled down towards a house filled with colored lights and a Christmas tree, and for the first time he hears something he believes must be music.
- **S35**：The ending is ambiguous, with Jonas experiencing symptoms of hypothermia . [ 10 ] At the 2009 National Book Festival , Lowry joked during a Q&A: "Jonas is alive, by the way.
- **S36**：You don't need to ask that question." [ 11 ]

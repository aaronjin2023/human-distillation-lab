## 角色资料

### Alex

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-770c59c1b2e7b526` · 独立版本：`profile-3340d62f1b3ca20c`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中他乐于鼓舞同伴，善于把挫折转为重塑表演的契机。（行为推断；S22、S23、S24）
- **核心动机**：他起初想回纽约，后来希望与伙伴及马戏团朋友建立共同归属。（行为推断；S4、S20、S36、S45）
- **决策方式**：面对表演困境，他倾向重构团队方向，并为危险动作寻找替代方案。（行为推断；S23、S24、S30）
- **价值取舍**：面对杜波依斯袭击，他愿出手保护斯特凡诺，重视新朋友的安全。（行为推断；S43、S44）
- **成长变化**：他从急于回动物园，转而把马戏团和新伙伴视作自己的家。（摘要直接叙述；S4、S36、S45）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

### Captain Chantel DuBois

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-9eeda3ea2b099196` · 独立版本：`profile-bf5ba26386c9c5a7`

- **性格倾向**：在追捕中她表现得执拗而残酷，把猎杀目标当作个人战利品。（行为推断；S7、S8、S27、S41）
- **核心动机**：她执意捕获亚历克斯，尤其想取得他的头颅作为战利品。（摘要直接叙述；S7、S27）
- **决策方式**：她跨辖区追踪、逃离拘留并准备暗杀，持续升级追捕手段。（摘要直接叙述；S27、S28、S41）
- **价值取舍**：即使被当作救助者致谢，她仍把猎杀目标置于动物生命之上。（行为推断；S38、S41）
- **沟通方式**：逃离拘留后，她用歌剧歌曲鼓舞受伤部下，再带队继续追捕。（摘要直接叙述；S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、成长变化

### Vitaly

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-6dbd3c8267f5921d` · 独立版本：`profile-565b3b9629f17c49`

- **性格倾向**：昔日表演受挫后，他在接纳新人时戒备，在再演前缺乏信心。（行为推断；S13、S22、S29、S30）
- **情绪反应**：火圈事故摧毁了他的表演信心，伦敦演出前他甚至准备离开。（摘要直接叙述；S22、S29）
- **决策方式**：面对重演火圈的风险，他倾向在鼓励与替代润滑方案下再次尝试。（行为推断；S29、S30、S31）
- **关系模式**：他最初反对接纳动物园伙伴，知道真相后仍愿原谅并组织营救。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S35、S40）
- **成长变化**：他从收拾行李准备离开，转为成功完成火圈表演并加入救援行动。（摘要直接叙述；S29、S31、S40）

暂无支持：核心动机、价值取舍、沟通方式

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：{{Plot}} The story continues from the events of the second film.
- **S2**：Alex  has a nightmare about himself and his friends still stranded in Africa and finding they have all gotten old.
- **S3**：He then wakes from his dream on his birthday, and the animals present him with a miniature model of New York City.
- **S4**：Alex suggests to Marty , Melman , and Gloria  that they should go to Monte Carlo to get the penguins to fly them back to New York City, which they agree to.
- **S5**：In Monte Carlo, the penguins and the two chimpanzees, Mason and Phil, disguised as the King of Versailles, keep winning in gambling until Alex's gang's attempt to reach them blunders and sparks chaos in the Hotel De Paris.
- **S6**：The hotel security calls Monaco Animal Control officer Captain Chantel DuBois  to deal with the animals.
- **S7**：But rather than capture them alive, DuBois desires their heads, mainly Alex's, as trophies.
- **S8**：A high-speed chase ensues between the relentless DuBois and the animals in a truck driven by the penguins to reach their aircraft that was from the second film.
- **S9**：The group departs on the plane, just barely escaping DuBois.
- **S10**：In the skies of France, the plane's engines fail and the plane crashes into a suburban rail yard as the authorities close in.
- **S11**：They come across a circus train and bang on it, desperately trying to get in.
- **S12**：Gia, a jaguar , sees the four zoo animals and Alex is smitten with her upon first sight of her.
- **S13**：Seeing their only chance of escape is on the circus train, the four larger animals desperately claim that they are circus animals themselves, which convinces Stefano the sea lion  and Gia to let them in despite the protests of Vitaly the tiger .
- **S14**：The animals soon learn from Stefano that they are performing in Rome and London, where they plan to impress a promoter to get them on their first American tour.
- **S15**：Before the zoo animals' claim is discredited, the penguins suddenly appear with a deal to purchase the circus themselves, resulting in the pleased departure of all the humans.
- **S16**：Meanwhile, King Julien  falls in love with Sonya the bear  and goes on a city tour across Rome.
- **S17**：He accidentally breaks her tricycle, and buys her a motorcycle with the stolen coronation ring of the Pope.
- **S18**：Meanwhile the others prepare for the performance at the Colosseum.
- **S19**：Unfortunately, to the zoo animals' horror, the circus proves to be so terrible, the angered audience demands refunds, right to going to the point of chasing the circus to the departing train to London.
- **S20**：En route to London, the zoo gang is in despair of having wasted their money on the failing circus and not any closer to getting home.
- **S21**：Stefano soon reveals to Alex that Vitaly was once their inspiration.
- **S22**：Once a professional ring jumper who used to leap through incrementally smaller hoops to excite crowds and was always pushing himself to the limit, his attempt at an impossible jump through a flaming pinkie ring ended in disaster when he burned his fur, which he had coated in extra virgin olive oil in order to slip through the narrow opening, destroying his confidence in his talent and the whole circus suffered by his example.
- **S23**：An inspired Alex then has the train make a stop in the Alps and convinces the performers to rework their act to become the opposite of the world-famous human-only Cirque du Soleil as an animal-only lights and acrobatic show.
- **S24**：He encourages the animals to try pursuing their ambitions and advises others to change acts they are unsuited for.
- **S25**：Similarly the zoo animals are trying out new things too.
- **S26**：Heartened by Alex's vision, the zoo animals and the circus animals develop sophisticated acts together and become closer friends in the process, especially Alex and Gia who find themselves falling in love.
- **S27**：Meanwhile, DuBois is arrested & detained in Rome after causing problems with the local police officers while chasing the animals out of her jurisdiction, but escapes and researches Alex on the Internet, learning he was the missing lion from the zoo in New York.
- **S28**：Once free, DuBois recruits her injured men, inspiring them with an operatic song, and heads toward the Alps, forcing the animals to proceed to London despite incomplete rehearsals.
- **S29**：In London, the troupe prepares for the promoter in the audience, but Vitaly is discovered packing to leave.
- **S30**：Despite the tiger's hostility to him, Alex convinces Vitaly to stay by reminding him of how he enjoys performing the impossible and suggests that he uses hair conditioner as a safer lubricant to perform his flaming ring jump.
- **S31**：As a result, Vitaly's stunt is performed perfectly, which proves to be the opening of a spectacularly successful show.
- **S32**：After the impressed promoter arranges for an American tour, DuBois shows up with a paper showing that Alex was missing.
- **S33**：Though the penguins are able to foil DuBois's plan to capture Alex, the lion is forced to confess that the four of them are just zoo animals trying to get home, disappointing the others who feel used by these lying amateurs who could have gotten them killed developing and performing their new acts.
- **S34**：Finally, both the ashamed zoo animals and the circus arrive in New York City.
- **S35**：However, Gia, Vitaly and Stefano find that even after learning the truth about Alex's gang, they are ready to forgive their new friends without whom their lives and performances feel woefully incomplete.
- **S36**：Likewise, the zoo group and Julien finally arrive at the gates of the closed Central Park Zoo, only to realize that their adventure has changed them too much to return to captivity and that they were "home" when they joined the circus.
- **S37**：The zoo animals resolve to return to the circus and reconcile with their new friends, but they are then tranquilized and captured by DuBois.
- **S38**：The zoo staff, delighted by Alex's reappearance, thank DuBois, incorrectly believing that she was trying to return the missing animals.
- **S39**：Unnoticed, Julien manages to reach the circus  and the penguins realize that the group had been ambushed.
- **S40**：Upon learning about the zoo animals' plight, Gia and Vitaly convince the circus animals to rescue their friends and they set out for the zoo, performing aboard a flying circus, with the rescue mission dubbed "Operation: Afro Circus Rescue." Meanwhile at the zoo, Alex awakens to find that he along with Marty, Melman, and Gloria are in their enclosures, surrounded by tall chain-link fences.
- **S41**：DuBois steps on stage to receive a million-dollar check of appreciation from the zoo, but at the same time secretly loads a poison-filled dart into a gun which she hides inside a foam finger in preparation to kill Alex.
- **S42**：The circus animals arrive in time to stop her and a massive brawl occurs where the circus uses all of what they had developed as part of their revamped act.
- **S43**：As the group tries to leave, the insane DuBois attempts to kill Stefano, who is stranded at the zoo.
- **S44**：However, Alex saves Stefano and all the animals then defeat DuBois and escape.
- **S45**：Heartened by this valiant demonstration of their new friends' love, Alex and his friends decide to join the circus permanently.
- **S46**：With the circus reunited, the animals all happily proceed with their American tour.
- **S47**：Meanwhile, thanks to Skipper, DuBois and her men find themselves, oddly enough just like Alex and his friends in the first film, inside shipping crates on a cargo ship bound for the island of Madagascar.

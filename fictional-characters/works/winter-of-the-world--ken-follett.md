## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] The story follows characters from Germany , Britain , the United States and the Soviet Union , who become linked by events leading to World War II , and continues through the war and its immediate aftermath.
- **S3**：The major characters are often children of the characters who were seen in Fall of Giants .
- **S4**：The novel covers a wide range of world events during the period, including the rise of Nazism , the ascent of Franco in Spain, the short-lived growth of British fascism , Action T4 , the Battle of Moscow , the Blitz , the Normandy landings , the attack on Pearl Harbor , the development of the American atom bomb and the Soviet one , the Battle of Berlin and many more.
- **S5**：The families, spread across four countries, are related to each other, though they themselves are not often aware of it.
- **S6**：Point of view characters include: Carla von Ulrich - the daughter of Reichstag member Walter von Ulrich and magazine editor Lady Maud Fitzherbert - English aristocrat who had fallen in love with the German nobleman Walter just before WWI and followed her heart against all odds.
- **S7**：She was rejected for a medical scholarship due to the anti-female policies in Nazi Germany, but takes a job as a nurse in Berlin.
- **S8**：After her father is murdered by the Gestapo for protesting Action T4 , she helps her friends- who are German Resistance members- transmit vital battle plans to the Soviet Union.
- **S9**：During the Battle of Berlin Carla witnesses a fourteen-year-old Jewish girl named Rebecca about to be raped by five Red Army soldiers, and in an act of self-sacrifice offers herself to be raped instead.
- **S10**：Becoming pregnant, she decides to have the child, cherish and raise him with her husband, Werner Franck and her mother.
- **S11**：She also adopts the girl Rebecca whom she saved.
- **S12**：She becomes a Social Democrat Berlin City Councillor, involved in confronting the Soviets during the 1948 Blockade of Berlin .
- **S13**：Erik von Ulrich - Carla's older brother, he is much more narrow-minded and less liberal than his sister and parents.
- **S14**：Erik is initially a firm supporter of the Nazi regime and serves in the German Army as a medical orderly in the invasions of France and Russia .
- **S15**：However, when he witnesses the massacre of Jewish civilians by the SS ' Einsatzgruppen , he has a change of heart.
- **S16**：Captured during the Battle of Berlin, Erik later becomes a die-hard supporter of communism , much to his mother and sister's dismay.
- **S17**：Thomas Macke - a sadistic, ambitious member of the Gestapo.
- **S18**：A fanatical Nazi, Macke gains ownership of a restaurant owned by Walter von Ulrich's cousin Robert by threatening to persecute Robert for his homosexuality.
- **S19**：He later orders the murder of Carla and Erik's father and nearly manages to uncover the German Resistance circuit run by Carla and her boyfriend Werner.
- **S20**：When Macke is injured during a bombing, Werner smothers him to death in the hospital where Carla works.
- **S21**：Lloyd Williams - the son of Welsh MP Ethel Leckwith and the bastard of Earl Fitzherbert (and therefore a cousin of Carla and Erik, whom he met in Berlin).
- **S22**：Lloyd was a student at Cambridge University alongside his unknowing half-brother, Viscount 'Boy' Fitzherbert.
- **S23**：After leading anti-Fascist demonstrations in London , he fights for the Republican forces in the Spanish Civil War and later helps downed Allied airmen escape German-occupied France during World War II.
- **S24**：Lloyd falls in love with Daisy early in the story, though she doesn't reciprocate it until later.
- **S25**：After the war, he becomes a Labour Party MP and supporter of the Marshall Plan to combat Soviet Union's communist expansionism.
- **S26**：Daisy Peshkov - the daughter of Russian-American film tycoon/gangster Lev Peshkov and Olga Vyalov, she is initially a superficial but kind natured social climber .
- **S27**：When she is rejected by high society in her native Buffalo due to her father's reputation, she romances and later marries Viscount 'Boy' Fitzherbert in England.
- **S28**：However, their relationship soon breaks down due to his infidelities and her growing attraction to Lloyd Williams.
- **S29**：Daisy drives an ambulance during the Blitz and becomes close friends with Lloyd's mother Ethel.
- **S30**：After Boy is killed when his plane is shot down, Daisy marries Lloyd and starts a family with him after the war.
- **S31**：Grigori "Greg" Peshkov - Daisy's half-brother, the son of Lev and his mistress Marga.
- **S32**：A former student at Harvard , Greg is very much his father's son in his initiative, ambition and womanizing habits.
- **S33**：He quickly rises amid the bureaucracy of Washington D.C. during World War II and becomes an observer for the government on the Manhattan Project , the development of the nuclear bomb.
- **S34**：During World War II, Greg discovers he has a son, Georgy, conceived during his earlier romance with a young African-American actress, Jacky Jakes.
- **S35**：He provides for the child's upbringing and education, becoming deeply attached to Georgy - which might create an impediment to his plans to embark on a political career in the Republican Party .
- **S36**：Vladimir "Volodya" Peshkov - the son of Soviet General Grigori Peshkov and his wife Katerina, although Vladimir's biological father is Grigori's brother Lev (who left Russia before he was born).
- **S37**：An intelligence officer for the Red Army , Vladimir is the handler for several Soviet espionage cells in Germany and the U.S., including that of Carla and Werner.
- **S38**：He fights in the Spanish Civil War and in the Battle of Moscow , and later manages to obtain covert intelligence on U.S. development of the nuclear bomb.
- **S39**：Over time, Vladimir becomes increasingly uncertain in his devotion to communism as he witnesses the brutal and repressive measures taken by Stalin .
- **S40**：Vladimir romances a beautiful physicist named Zoya, deeply involved in the Soviet Nuclear Program , and later marries and starts a family with her after the war.
- **S41**：Woody Dewar - The son of U.S.
- **S42**：Senator Gus Dewar and his wife Rosa, and a former friend of Daisy and Greg's.
- **S43**：Although drawn to politics like his father, Woody finds his true passion in news writing and photography.
- **S44**：He spends much of the story trying to win the heart of his longtime crush, Joanne Rouzrokh, and eventually does.
- **S45**：However, while they are in Hawaii celebrating their engagement, she is killed during the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor .
- **S46**：After his brother's death, Woody serves in the U.S.
- **S47**：Army against the Germans, leading a paratroop platoon on D-Day .
- **S48**：He is sent home wounded, and eventually begins a relationship with Bella Hernandez, a young Hispanic woman he met while training in Britain.
- **S49**：Charles "Chuck" Dewar - Woody's younger brother.
- **S50**：Chuck is a patriotic member of the U.S.
- **S51**：Navy , but struggles with his closeted homosexuality (which he later revealed to Woody, and which his mother had already guessed).
- **S52**：Chuck is present during the attack on Pearl Harbor and later plays a small but vital role in the Battle of Midway .
- **S53**：He is later killed by Japanese machine-gunfire during the landing at Bougainville , trying to protect his lover Eddie Parry.
- **S54**：The novel also sets the stage for the decades to follow with reference to interracial and homosexual relationships, the creation of the United Nations , the sexual revolution , and the growth of the communist Eastern Bloc .
- **S55**：As in the series' first volume, during the course of the book, most female characters get pregnant and have children, who would become protagonists of the next volume.

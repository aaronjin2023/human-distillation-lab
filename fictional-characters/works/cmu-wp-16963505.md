## 角色资料

### Aang

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-56f9fca2eb673aef` · 独立版本：`profile-ff5f1b07954d60ae`

- **性格倾向**：面对村民与敌人受险时，倾向体恤并保留怜悯（行为推断；S8、S30）
- **核心动机**：通过学习其余元素能力，逐步承担对抗侵略的责任（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14、S26、S38）
- **情绪反应**：得知族人与监护人遭灭绝后，陷入强烈绝望（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13）
- **决策方式**：面对强敌时，倾向结合精神求助与所学能力应战（行为推断；S28、S37）
- **成长变化**：从刚获知自身处境，成长为主动接受降世使命的人（摘要直接叙述；S12、S13、S38）

### Zuko

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-fffbefacaca37c81` · 独立版本：`profile-b705fb99b0cd0eef`

- **性格倾向**：在放逐处境中，表现出执拗追索目标与独自行动倾向（行为推断；S6、S23、S27）
- **核心动机**：希望把降世神通作为俘虏交给父亲，换取返乡资格（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **决策方式**：行动随局势转换，可先救出目标再重新实施抓捕（行为推断；S19、S20、S28）
- **关系模式**：遭赵将军策划谋杀，并得到叔父的救助与劝阻（摘要直接叙述；S20、S21、S22、S35）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Fifteen-year-old Katara  and her sixteen-year-old older brother, Sokka , are near a river at the Southern Water Tribe, a small village in the South Pole.
- **S2**：While hunting, they discover an iceberg that shoots a beam of light into the sky.
- **S3**：Inside of the iceberg is a boy named Aang  and a giant flying bison named Appa.
- **S4**：Unknown to them, Aang is the long lost Avatar — the only person on the planet able to "bend" all four elements.
- **S5**：One hundred years have passed since the Fire Nation has declared war on the other three nations of Air, Water and Earth in their attempt to conquer the world.
- **S6**：Zuko , is an exiled prince of the Fire Nation on a quest to find the Avatar and bring him as prisoner to his father, Fire Lord Ozai , so he can return back home.
- **S7**：Seeing the light that appeared from Aang's release, Zuko and some Fire Nation soldiers arrive at the Southern Water Tribe to demand the villagers hand over the Avatar.
- **S8**：Aang reveals himself as he surrenders himself to Zuko on the condition that he agrees to leave the village alone.
- **S9**：On the ship, Aang is tested by Zuko's uncle Iroh  to confirm him to be the Avatar.
- **S10**：After being informed that he is to be their prisoner for passing the test, Aang escapes using his glider and flies to his flying bison brought by Katara and Sokka.
- **S11**：Aang and his new friends visit the Southern Air Temple where they meet a winged lemur, who Aang later names Momo.
- **S12**：Aang also learns that he was in the ice for a whole century and that the Fire Nation wiped out all of the Air Nomads, including his guardian, Monk Gyatso.
- **S13**：In despair, he enters the Avatar State and finds himself in the Spirit World where he encounters a Dragon Spirit that tells him that he only knows Airbending, and that he also needs to learn Waterbending, Earthbending and Firebending in order to become a fully realized Avatar.
- **S14**：The Dragon Spirit tells him that he should learn Waterbending first, and that the best teachers are in the Northern Water Tribe as there no more Waterbenders in the Southern Water Tribe.
- **S15**：To seek shelter, Aang's group arrive at a little Earth Kingdom village controlled by the Fire Nation, and they are arrested because Katara tries to help a young boy escape from a Fire Nation patrol soldiers.
- **S16**：They incite a rebellion by reminding the disgruntled Earthbenders that earth was given to them.
- **S17**：Katara is given a Waterbending scroll that she uses to greatly strengthen and hone her Waterbending and help Aang learn as they make their way to the Northern Water Tribe and liberate and secure more Earth Kingdom villages in the process, weakening the Fire Nation's food and water supplies.
- **S18**：During a side track to the Northern Air Temple on his own, Aang is betrayed by a peasant and captured by a "group of Fire Nation archers, led by Commander Zhao , a Fire Nation Commander appointed by the Fire Lord.
- **S19**：However, a masked marauder, the "Blue Spirit", helps Aang escape from his imprisonment.
- **S20**：Zuko is the masked vigilante, and Zhao realizes this.
- **S21**：He arranges to kill the prince.
- **S22**：Zuko survives the assassination attempt on his life with Iroh's help.
- **S23**：He sneaks aboard Zhao's lead ship as his fleet departs for the Northern Water Tribe, which is a heavy fortress, to capture the Avatar.
- **S24**：Upon arriving, Aang's group is welcomed warmly by the citizens of the Northern Water Tribe.
- **S25**：Immediately, Sokka befriends the Northern Water Tribe princess, Yue .
- **S26**：After a few agreements, a Waterbending master, Pakku , teaches Aang Waterbending.
- **S27**：Soon, the Fire Nation arrives and Zhao begins his attack while Zuko begins his search for the Avatar on his own.
- **S28**：After defeating Katara in a battle, Zuko captures Aang as he enters the Spirit World to find the Dragon Spirit to give him the wisdom to defeat the Fire Nation.
- **S29**：Returning to his body, Aang battles Zuko before Katara freezes him.
- **S30**：Before leaving to join the battle, Aang lowers the ice so that Zuko can breathe.
- **S31**：As the battle escalates, Iroh watches Zhao capture the Moon Spirit, with which its Ocean Spirit counterpart had assumed the form of a fish.
- **S32**：Despite Iroh's pleas, Zhao kills the Moon Spirit to strip all of the Waterbenders of their powers and abilities to Waterbend.
- **S33**：Yue explains to everyone that the Moon Spirit gave her life, willing to give it back as she dies in the process.
- **S34**：With the tables turned, Zhao finds out Zuko survived.
- **S35**：They almost fight before Iroh appears and tells Zuko it's not worth it.
- **S36**：Zhao is drowned by Waterbenders after Zuko and Iroh leave him to his fate.
- **S37**：With his Waterbending powers, Aang enters the Avatar State and raises the ocean into a gigantic wall in order to drive the armada back.
- **S38**：Aang now fully embraces his destiny as the Avatar as he, Katara and Sokka prepare to continue their journey to the Earth Kingdom to find an Earthbending teacher for Aang.
- **S39**：The Fire Lord learns of the defeat, and angry about the betrayal of his brother Iroh and the failure of his eldest son Zuko, he tasks his youngest daughter Azula  to stop the Avatar from mastering Earth and Fire before the arrival of Sozin's Comet.

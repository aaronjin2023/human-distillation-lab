## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Harry Potter lives with his abusive uncle and aunt, Vernon and Petunia Dursley , and their bullying son, Dudley , in the fictional English town of Little Whinging in 1991.
- **S3**：On Harry's eleventh birthday, he learns that he is a wizard when a half-giant named Rubeus Hagrid invites him to attend Hogwarts , a school of magic.
- **S4**：Hagrid explains that when Harry was an infant, a dark wizard named Voldemort murdered Harry's parents and tried to kill Harry as well.
- **S5**：However, Voldemort's killing curse rebounded and seemingly destroyed him, leaving a lightning bolt-shaped scar on Harry's forehead.
- **S6**：Hagrid takes Harry to Diagon Alley , where Harry discovers he is famous among wizards.
- **S7**：He buys a wand , an owl named Hedwig, and other school supplies.
- **S8**：A month later, Harry takes the Hogwarts Express to Hogwarts.
- **S9**：During the journey, he befriends Ron Weasley , a fellow first-year student.
- **S10**：He also meets Hermione Granger and has a confrontation with Draco Malfoy .
- **S11**：At Hogwarts, a magical Sorting Hat assigns each first-year student to a House .
- **S12**：Harry, Ron, and Hermione are assigned to Gryffindor, although the Hat considers putting Harry in Slytherin.
- **S13**：Harry's skill at broomstick flying earns him a place on the Gryffindor Quidditch team as the Seeker.
- **S14**：He develops a dislike for the Potions professor Severus Snape , who seems to hate Harry.
- **S15**：One night, Harry and Ron discover a gigantic three-headed dog guarding a trapdoor.
- **S16**：The two boys later save Hermione from a troll and begin a friendship with her.
- **S17**：During Harry's first Quidditch match, his broomstick attempts to throw him off.
- **S18**：Snape's strange behaviour during the match convinces Hermione that Snape jinxed Harry's broom.
- **S19**：On Christmas, Harry receives an anonymous gift—his father's invisibility cloak .
- **S20**：While using the cloak to explore the school undetected, he discovers the Mirror of Erised , which shows the viewer what they most desire.
- **S21**：Harry sees his parents and other members of his family in the mirror.
- **S22**：In 1992, Harry, Ron, and Hermione learn that the three-headed dog is guarding a magical object called the Philosopher's Stone , which grants its user immortality.
- **S23**：A centaur named Firenze warns Harry that Voldemort is plotting to steal the Stone to restore his body.
- **S24**：When the headmaster, Albus Dumbledore , is lured away from Hogwarts, Harry and his friends fear the theft is imminent and descend through the trapdoor to retrieve the Stone.
- **S25**：Various obstacles force Ron and Hermione to remain behind while Harry approaches the Stone.
- **S26**：He encounters Professor Quirrell , the Defence Against the Dark Arts instructor, who explains that he serves Voldemort and that it was he who jinxed Harry's broom; Snape was trying to save Harry during the match.
- **S27**：Quirrell attempts to retrieve the Stone from the Mirror of Erised, but cannot figure out how.
- **S28**：When Harry looks in the Mirror, he feels the Stone drop into his pocket.
- **S29**：Quirrell then reveals that Voldemort has possessed his body, and shows Harry Voldemort's face on the back of his head.
- **S30**：Quirrell attempts to seize the Stone, but his flesh burns upon contact with Harry.
- **S31**：As Harry and Quirrell struggle, Harry's scar begins to burn, and he passes out.
- **S32**：Harry awakens in the school's infirmary.
- **S33**：Dumbledore explains that Harry survived because he is protected by a magical charm that was created when his mother died trying to protect him.
- **S34**：Quirrell's hatred and greed caused him to burn upon contact with Harry, and Voldemort abandoned him to die.
- **S35**：Dumbledore also reveals that the Stone has been destroyed.
- **S36**：During the school's end-of-year feast, Gryffindor is awarded the House Cup.
- **S37**：Harry then returns to the home of the Dursleys for the summer. [ 3 ]
- **S38**：## Characters
- **S39**：[ edit ] Harry Potter is an orphan whom Rowling imagined as a "scrawny, black-haired, green eyed and bespectacled boy who didn't know he was a wizard." [ 4 ] She developed the series' story and characters to explain how Harry came to be in this situation and how his life unfolded from there. [ 5 ] Apart from the first chapter, the events of this book take place just before and in the year following Harry's eleventh birthday.
- **S40**：Voldemort's attack left a lightning bolt-shaped scar on Harry's forehead, [ 5 ] which produces stabbing pains whenever Voldemort is present.
- **S41**：Harry has a natural talent for Quidditch and became the first person in a century to get on their team in their first year.
- **S42**：Ron Weasley is Harry's age, and Rowling describes him as the ultimate best friend "always there when you need him". [ 6 ] He is freckled, red-haired, and quite tall.
- **S43**：He grew up in a fairly large pure-blood family as the sixth born of seven children.
- **S44**：Although his family is quite poor, they still live comfortably and happily.
- **S45**：His loyalty and bravery in the face of a game of Wizard Chess plays a vital part in finding the Philosopher's Stone.
- **S46**：Hermione Granger , the daughter of an all- Muggle family, is a bossy girl who has apparently memorised most of the textbooks before the start of term.
- **S47**：Rowling described Hermione as a "very logical, upright, and good" character with "a lot of insecurity and a great fear of failure beneath her swottiness". [ 7 ] Despite her nagging efforts to keep Harry and Ron out of trouble, she becomes a close friend of the two boys after they save her from a troll and her magical and analytical skills play an important role in finding the Philosopher's Stone.
- **S48**：She has bushy brown hair and rather large front teeth.
- **S49**：Neville Longbottom is a plump, diffident boy, so forgetful that his grandmother gives him a Remembrall, to remind him if he forgets something.
- **S50**：Neville's magical abilities are weak and appeared just in time to save his life when he was eight.
- **S51**：Despite his timidity, Neville will fight anyone after some encouragement or if he thinks it is right and important.
- **S52**：Rubeus Hagrid , a half-giant nearly 12 feet (3.7 m) tall, with tangled black hair and beard, who was expelled from Hogwarts and his wand was snapped in half (resulting in him never to use a wand again), though Professor Dumbledore let him stay on as the school's gamekeeper , a job which enables him to give lavish affection, care and even pet names (such as Norbert the dragon) on even the most dangerous of magical creatures.
- **S53**：Hagrid is fiercely loyal to Dumbledore and quickly becomes a close friend of Harry, Ron and, later, Hermione, but his carelessness makes him unreliable.
- **S54**：Professor Albus Dumbledore , a tall, thin man who wears half-moon spectacles, has silver hair and a beard long enough to tuck into his belt, is the headmaster of Hogwarts, and thought to be the only wizard Voldemort fears.
- **S55**：Dumbledore, while renowned for his achievements in magic, shrugs off praise, though he is aware of his own brilliance.
- **S56**：Rowling described him as the "epitome of goodness". [ 8 ] Professor Minerva McGonagall , a tall, severe-looking woman with black hair tied in a tight bun, teaches Transfiguration and is able to transform herself into a cat.
- **S57**：She is Deputy Headmistress and Head of Gryffindor

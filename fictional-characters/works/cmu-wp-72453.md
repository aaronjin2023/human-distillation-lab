## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Graham Hess  is a former Episcopal priest.
- **S2**：His wife Colleen  was killed in a traffic accident caused by Ray Reddy .
- **S3**：The accident left Graham embittered and caused him to renounce his faith in God, and so left the ministry.
- **S4**：Graham's younger brother Merrill , a former minor league baseball player, helps run the family farm in rural Pennsylvania and care for Graham's kids, Morgan  and Bo .
- **S5**：Bo has a habit of leaving half-empty glasses of water around the house, because "[they] taste funny".
- **S6**：Life at the farm is uneventful until a mysterious crop circle is found in the cornfield, but Graham thinks it's a hoax.
- **S7**：That night, a frightened Bo wakes Graham up saying that there's a monster outside her bedroom window.
- **S8**：Graham, thinking she is having nightmares, tucks her back into bed but as he looks out the window he sees the silhouette of someone standing on the barn roof.
- **S9**：Alarmed, he fetches Merrill and they try to scare away the figure, believing it's Lionel Pritchard , a local prankster.
- **S10**：The brothers run screaming around the house to frighten off the intruder; it jumps from the roof and runs into the cornfield.
- **S11**：Neither man gets a good view of it.
- **S12**：The next day, Sheriff Paski  stops by to investigate the incident.
- **S13**：She admits that unless Graham has a clear description of the intruder, she can't help him.
- **S14**：Their conversation is interrupted by Bo, saying that the TV is reporting numerous crop circles appearing all over the world but as yet people remained divided as to whether it is some great worldwide hoax or indeed signs of extraterrestrial life.
- **S15**：The family take a trip into town the following day and hear rumours of mysterious strangers roaming around during the night and hiding in the shadows.
- **S16**：When the family return home, they pick up odd noises on Bo's old baby monitor which when pointed higher towards the sky, Morgan believes its the aliens communicating with one another.
- **S17**：That night, the dog barks incessantly towards the cornfield.
- **S18**：Taking a flashlight, Graham walks into the cornfield and yells that the intruders are "wasting [their] time." As Graham walks back to the house, he hears a noise and spots a thin green leg among the cornrows.
- **S19**：He runs back to the house, terrified.
- **S20**：Later the family watches the television about reports of several unidentifiable and mysterious lights hovering over Mexico City.
- **S21**：During a talk with Merrill, Graham dismisses his brother's belief in signs, evidence of some higher power, and says that they will have to face whatever it is alone.
- **S22**：The following morning, Graham receives a cryptic phone call from Reddy, the man who killed his wife.
- **S23**：Graham goes to see Ray and finds him sitting in his car wearing a bloody shirt.
- **S24**：Meanwhile, a frightened Merrill watches news footage capturing the image of an alien at a child's birthday party in Brazil.
- **S25**：Ray tells Graham that he is going to the lake, since none of the aliens have been seen near water.
- **S26**：Before leaving, Ray pauses to apologize for the accident that killed Colleen and tells Graham that he trapped an alien in his pantry.
- **S27**：Investigating, Graham uses a kitchen knife to see the reflection of the alien under the door.
- **S28**：A clawed hand reaches out and a startled Graham cuts off two of its fingers.
- **S29**：Back home, Graham gives his family the options to stay at the house or head towards the lake.
- **S30**：They vote to stay, not wanting to leave their home.
- **S31**：They board up the windows and doors, preparing for any possible attack.
- **S32**：Meanwhile, news reports indicate that more mysterious lights have been spotted worldwide and that the aliens are becoming hostile.
- **S33**：Merrill suggests that the crop circles in their cornfield, among others, are for alien navigation.
- **S34**：Graham prepares a "last meal" for the family, with each picking their preferred dish.
- **S35**：They are interrupted when they hear Bo's baby monitor, which they had earlier discovered could pick up the aliens' communications, alerting them that the aliens are approaching.
- **S36**：They listen as the aliens, seeking a way in, walk around the house.
- **S37**：Merrill suddenly realizes that the attic was left unprotected and they quickly move to the basement, barring the door.
- **S38**：The aliens can be heard inside the house and try to enter the basement.
- **S39**：An alien grabs Morgan from the disused coal chute.
- **S40**：It is driven away, but Morgan has an asthma attack.
- **S41**：Graham realizes his inhaler was left upstairs, so he calms Morgan, hoping that the boy's breathing would return to normal.
- **S42**：The next morning, Morgan's condition has improved and the radio reports that aliens are retreating.
- **S43**：They return upstairs but are ambushed by a lone alien, who takes the comatose Morgan hostage.
- **S44**：Graham notices two fingers are missing, meaning it was the alien trapped in Reddy's pantry.
- **S45**：Graham suddenly remembers the night that his wife died.
- **S46**：She was pinned by a car and the Sheriff tells Graham that when the car is moved his wife will die.
- **S47**：He talks to her and she tells him to let Morgan have fun and be silly, and for Bo to always listen to her brother because he will take care of her.
- **S48**：She then tells Graham to "see" and "tell Merrill to swing away." Graham realizes there was meaning in his wife's final words.
- **S49**：Looking around, he sees Merrill's mounted record home run bat, next to Merrill.
- **S50**：He tells Merrill to "swing away." Merrill understands and grabs the bat.
- **S51**：Sensing an attack, the alien sprays a toxin into Morgan's face.
- **S52**：Merrill swings at the alien, who then drops Morgan, causing one of Bo's water glasses to spill on it.
- **S53**：The water acts like acid on the alien's skin, so Merrill starts smashing all of the half-empty glasses, before finally knocking the alien into a table, where water glasses spilled directly onto its face, killing it.
- **S54**：During the fight, Graham takes Morgan outside and administers an Epi-pen injection to stop the asthma attack.
- **S55**：Morgan regains consciousness, his lungs having been closed up prevented him from inhaling the alien's gas.
- **S56**：Realizing that Colleen's final words meant something after all, and that they saved his family's life, Graham's faith is restored.
- **S57**：Months later, Graham gets dressed in his clerical garb and goes back to church.

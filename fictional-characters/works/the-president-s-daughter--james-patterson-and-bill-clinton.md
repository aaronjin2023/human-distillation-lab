## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] U.S.
- **S3**：President and former Navy SEAL Matthew Keating monitors an American military operation from the White House Situation Room .
- **S4**：The operation enters Libyan territory without permission to raid the base of Muslim extremist terrorist Asim Al-Asheed.
- **S5**：The operation kills several terrorists as well as Al-Asheed's civilian wives and children, but Al-Asheed escapes.
- **S6**：Against the advice of his staff, Keating publicly admits to the operation and apologizes for its failure.
- **S7**：Al-Asheed swears revenge on Keating.
- **S8**：Two years later, Keating has lost his re-election campaign and lives quietly in rural New Hampshire with his family.
- **S9**：Al-Asheed has built up a new terrorist group and secured the aid of Jiang Lijun, an agent of China's Ministry of State Security .
- **S10**：Lijun hates the U.S. because of his father's death in the 1999 U.S. bombing of the Chinese embassy in Belgrade .
- **S11**：Al-Asheed's team infiltrates Keating's town and kidnaps Keating's daughter, Melanie, while she is hiking.
- **S12**：The U.S. government, whose new president still harbours dislike for Keating, refuses to let Keating join the search for Melanie because he is no longer a military or government employee.
- **S13**：Lijun's boss, Li Baodong, orders him to obtain Melanie so that China can gain prestige by returning her to the U.S.
- **S14**：However, Lijun privately decides to let Al-Asheed keep Melanie.
- **S15**：Lijun meets with Al-Asheed and offers him further support.
- **S16**：Al-Asheed escapes an attack by U.S. police and flees the country with Melanie.
- **S17**：Al-Asheed releases a video in which he names his ransom demands for Melanie's release, but Keating does not believe Al-Asheed intends to let Melanie live. (This is confirmed by chapters from Al-Asheed's perspective.) Keating decides to take matters into his own hands.
- **S18**：He flees his Secret Service guards and obtains the help of several friends in the American, Saudi and Israeli militaries and intelligence agencies .
- **S19**：Keating's ad-hoc team eventually locates Al-Asheed's new Libyan compound.
- **S20**：Baodong informs Lijun that he recorded the meeting in which Lijun did not negotiate with Al-Asheed for Melanie.
- **S21**：He threatens Lijun with blackmail if he does not secure Melanie.
- **S22**：He also says that the Chinese embassy in Belgrade had a secret military installment underneath it, thus Lijun should not consider his father an innocent victim and should end his grudge against the U.S.
- **S23**：Meanwhile, Melanie frustrates Al-Asheed with several escape attempts.
- **S24**：Lijun travels to Al-Asheed's compound and attempts to negotiate for Melanie's handover, but Al-Asheed kills his bodyguards and takes him prisoner.
- **S25**：Hours later, Keating and his team secure military passage to Libya and attack the compound.
- **S26**：In the ensuing battle, Melanie is rescued and Al-Asheed is killed.
- **S27**：Lijun, with no other options for escape, travels back to the U.S. with Keating's team under the cover story of being an innocent civilian prisoner.
- **S28**：Lijun then escapes into Chinese custody.
- **S29**：The U.S. government declines to charge Keating's team with any crimes.
- **S30**：Baodong kills Lijun for his failures and to cover up China's involvement in the affair.
- **S31**：Keating returns to New Hampshire, but vows to run for president again in the next election. [ 2 ] [ 3 ]

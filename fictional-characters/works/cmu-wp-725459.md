## 角色资料

### Damien Karras

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-b56e1f5ded74d8b6` · 独立版本：`profile-c3e71dba53ba0868`

- **性格倾向**：在异常现象面前倾向审慎怀疑，不轻易接受超自然解释（行为推断；S16、S17）
- **情绪反应**：面对母亲的疾病时产生信仰疑虑，承受精神上的动摇（摘要直接叙述；S2、S3）
- **决策方式**：倾向先观察并核查异常证据，再申请采取驱魔行动（行为推断；S15、S16、S17）
- **价值取舍**：最终以自身承担附身危险，显示此刻将女孩安危置于己上（行为推断；S25、S26）
- **成长变化**：从对附身持怀疑态度，走向亲自介入并承担致命代价（摘要直接叙述；S16、S17、S25、S26、S27）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：对宗教解释心存疑虑，却仍选择以神职身份承担救助责任。

### Chris MacNeil

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-51ba413a5117bb26` · 独立版本：`profile-61a811771a6cdb48`

- **性格倾向**：在女儿持续恶化的情境中，倾向坚持求助并调整原有判断（行为推断；S5、S13、S14）
- **核心动机**：向兼具神父与精神科医生身份的Karras求助以救女儿（行为推断；S4、S13、S14）
- **决策方式**：在医学解释穷尽后转求神父协助，倾向随困境调整求助路径（行为推断；S5、S13、S14）
- **情绪反应**：常规解释与治疗无法解决女儿异常时，陷入绝望而求援（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14）

暂无支持：价值取舍、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：At an archaeological dig in Northern Iraq, archaeologist Father Lankester Merrin  visits a site where a silver Roman Catholic medallion along with a small stone amulet resembling a grimacing, bestial creature are found buried together.
- **S2**：Meanwhile, Father Damien Karras ([[Jason Miller , a young priest at Georgetown University in Washington, DC, begins to doubt his faith while dealing with his mother's illness.
- **S3**：A friend, Father Joe Dyer , tries to advise and console him.
- **S4**：While making a film near her temporary residence in Georgetown, actress Chris MacNeil  notices dramatic and dangerous changes in the behavior of her 12-year-old daughter, Regan .
- **S5**：Chris initially believes Regan's changes are related to puberty; however, doctors suspect a lesion on her temporal lobe.
- **S6**：Regan endures a series of unpleasant medical tests.
- **S7**：When X-rays show nothing out of the ordinary, a doctor advises that Regan be taken to a psychiatrist, whom she assaults.
- **S8**：Paranormal occurrences continue, including a violently shaking bed, strange noises, and unexplained movements.
- **S9**：A film director, Burke Dennings , visiting the MacNeil home, dies violently, found at the bottom of steps that run the full length of the house.
- **S10**：Lieutenant Kinderman  investigates, and informs Karras that Dennings' head was found completely twisted around his shoulders.
- **S11**：He also speaks with Chris and promises to return when Regan is feeling better.
- **S12**：Just after Kinderman leaves, Chris is brutally attacked by her daughter, leaving her with facial bruises.
- **S13**：When all medical explanations are exhausted, doctors recommend an exorcism.
- **S14**：In desperation, Chris consults Karras, who is both a priest and a psychiatrist.
- **S15**：During a period in which Karras observes Regan, she constantly refers to herself as the Devil.
- **S16**：Karras initially believes her to be merely suffering from psychosis, until he records her speaking in a strange language which turns out to be English spoken backwards, and he is later shown Regan's abdomen where the words "help me" rise in relief in the form of Regan's handwriting.
- **S17**：Despite his doubts, Karras decides to request permission from the Church to conduct an exorcism.
- **S18**：Merrin, an experienced exorcist, is summoned to Georgetown to perform the exorcism, with Karras assisting.
- **S19**：He and Karras try to drive the spirit from Regan.
- **S20**：The demon threatens and taunts both priests, both physically and verbally .
- **S21**：Merrin requests that he and Karras take a break, whereby he administers to himself the viaticum, a sign of his impending doom at the hands of the demon.
- **S22**：Merrin excuses the younger priest and begins the exorcism, once more on his own.
- **S23**：Karras returns to find Merrin has suffered a fatal heart attack.
- **S24**：He attempts to perform CPR to no avail, but Regan is proud of it.
- **S25**：Karras strikes and chokes her, challenging the demon to leave Regan and enter him.
- **S26**：The demon does so, whereupon the priest regains enough control and throws himself through Regan's bedroom window and falls down the steps outside.
- **S27**：At the bottom, a devastated Father Dyer administers last rites as Karras dies.
- **S28**：Regan is restored to health and does not appear to remember her ordeal.
- **S29**：Chris and Regan leave Georgetown and their trauma behind.
- **S30**：They return Karras' silver medallion to Dyer, who takes one final look down the steps behind the house and departs.

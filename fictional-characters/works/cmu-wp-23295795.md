## 角色资料

### Stu Price

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-5b2e0093041ca088` · 独立版本：`profile-1d482c360312f988`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他起初迟疑参加聚会，最终敢于公开反驳他人的定性。（行为推断；S3、S4、S29）
- **核心动机**：他前往泰国筹办婚事，找到Teddy后又与同伴赶回婚宴。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S26、S28）
- **决策方式**：在寻人陷入僵局时，他凭突然想到的线索推动众人重返酒店。（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26）
- **成长变化**：面对岳父的不认可，他最终公开否认无趣标签并承认狂放一面。（摘要直接叙述；S3、S29）
- **关系模式**：他与Lauren的婚事遭其父质疑，公开发言后才获得对方祝福。（摘要直接叙述；S1、S3、S29、S30）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

### Alan Garner

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-b726d80943ab8f35` · 独立版本：`profile-6c8cadcf360b1fe2`

- **性格倾向**：在处理Teddy同行一事时，他采取暗中下药的手段，显得欠考虑。（行为推断；S2、S18）
- **核心动机**：他给部分棉花糖加入药物，目的是使Teddy受到镇静作用。（摘要直接叙述；S18）
- **情绪反应**：对于Lauren的弟弟Teddy加入同行，他感到不快。（摘要直接叙述；S2）
- **关系模式**：他与Chow在拉斯维加斯后结为朋友，也为Stu安排婚礼音乐礼物。（摘要直接叙述；S6、S31）
- **沟通方式**：Phil就医之后，他向同伴坦白自己下药及混错袋子的经过。（摘要直接叙述；S18）

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Two years after their escapade in Las Vegas, Stu Price, Phil Wenneck, Alan Garner and Doug Billings travel to Thailand to celebrate Stu's impending wedding to Lauren.
- **S2**：Much to Alan's dismay, they are joined by Lauren's younger brother, Teddy.
- **S3**：During Lauren's father's toast, he shows his disapproval of Stu by comparing him to congee.
- **S4**：At the end of the night, Stu hesitantly joins Phil, Doug, Alan and Teddy for a beer.
- **S5**：Sitting at a campfire and roasting marshmallows, the group toast to Stu and Lauren's future happiness.
- **S6**：The following morning, Phil, Stu and Alan, along with flamboyant gangster Leslie Chow - whom Alan befriended after Las Vegas - and a chain-smoking capuchin monkey, awaken in a dirty hotel room in Bangkok.
- **S7**：Stu has a face tattoo , and Alan's head is completely shaved.
- **S8**：However, they cannot find Teddy, only discovering his severed finger.
- **S9**：Chow begins recalling the events of the prior night, but he seemingly dies after snorting a line of cocaine.
- **S10**：Panicked, the trio dispose of Chow's body in an ice box.
- **S11**：Through a tip from Doug who is still at the resort, they go to a prison to pick up Teddy but are given a wheelchair-bound elderly Buddhist monk, who knows more about what happened, but does not reveal anything: he has taken a vow of silence, and rejects also an alternative such as writing something down.
- **S12**：After finding a business card, they travel to a neighborhood smouldering in ruins.
- **S13**：They enter a nearby tattoo parlor where Stu got his tattoo, and they learn that they had started a fight that escalated into a riot.
- **S14**：The trio then return the monk to his Buddhist temple, where they are encouraged by the head monk to meditate.
- **S15**：Alan is able to recall that they had been at a strip club.
- **S16**：There, they learn that Stu had engaged in sex with a kathoey prostitute.
- **S17**：Upon exiting, the trio is attacked by two Russian mobsters from whom they had stolen the monkey, and Phil is shot in the arm.
- **S18**：After Phil is treated at a clinic, Alan confesses that he had drugged some of the marshmallows from the previous night with muscle relaxants and ADHD medication in order to sedate Teddy but accidentally mixed up the bags.
- **S19**：After noticing an address and time point for a meeting written on Alan's stomach, the trio meet up with another gangster, Kingsley, who demands Chow's bank account code and password by the next morning in exchange for Teddy.
- **S20**：They return to the hotel to try to find Chow's password, only to discover that he is still alive.
- **S21**：They steal the monkey  back from the Russian mobsters through a violent car chase, during which the monkey is shot and injured.
- **S22**：After taking the code and leaving the monkey at a veterinary clinic, the group complete the deal with Kingsley.
- **S23**：Suddenly, Interpol agents appear and arrest Chow.
- **S24**：Kingsley turns out to be an undercover agent, who tells the trio that the police had used the information that Teddy had disappeared to arrest Chow and Kingsley actually does not know where Teddy is.
- **S25**：Desperate and out of clues, Phil once again calls Doug's wife Tracy.
- **S26**：Stu then has an epiphany and the trio rushes back to the hotel and find Teddy in the elevator unharmed .
- **S27**：Teddy had woken up in the middle of the night to get more ice for his severed finger  but became trapped after the power went out.
- **S28**：The four use Chow's speedboat, the keys for which were in Teddy's pocket, to travel back to the wedding reception.
- **S29**：Arriving on land just as Lauren's father is about to cancel the wedding, Stu makes a defiant speech where he rejects being boring and instead states that he is in fact quite wild.
- **S30**：Impressed, Lauren's father gives the couple his blessing.
- **S31**：After the wedding continues on, Alan presents Stu with a special gift at the post-reception dance: a musical guest performance by Mike Tyson.
- **S32**：Teddy later discovers that he had taken many pictures during the night on his cell phone.
- **S33**：The group, along with Tyson, agree to look at the pictures together once before erasing the evidence of their exploits once again.

### Snowbell

- **S1**：{{plot}} Eleanor Little  and Frederick Little  and their son George Little  are intending an adoption.
- **S2**：While George is at school, his parents go to the orphanage where they fall in love with a thoughtful, observant "mouse"  named Stuart Little  who knows almost everything about the other children, having been there for a long time.
- **S3**：Despite the warnings of Mrs.
- **S4**：Keeper  , who says that humans should not be adopting "mice", they decide to adopt him as their second son.
- **S5**：Immediately after getting home, Stuart meets Snowbell  the family cat, who immediately attempts to eat him.
- **S6**：George is surprised when his parents introduce Stuart as his new brother.
- **S7**：He thinks it is a joke at first, but is shocked when he sees they are serious.
- **S8**：That night, Snowbell visits Stuart and requests that he keep a low profile, so as to avoid getting seen by the other cats and damaging Snowbell's reputation.
- **S9**：George is at first sullen and somewhat disappointed and treats Stuart with contempt.
- **S10**：He eventually snaps at a family gathering when they encourage him to play "catch" with him.
- **S11**：He also earns Snowbell's wrath when his friend, Monty the Mouth  visits for food.
- **S12**：As Snowbell tries to keep him from seeing Stuart, he makes a scene trying to get a cereal box from a cupboard.
- **S13**：When he sees him and discovers he is a member of the family, Monty cannot contain himself.
- **S14**：Enraged, Snowbell chases him, who narrowly escapes by fleeing into the basement.
- **S15**：Here, he learns that George keeps a playroom and the two interact and eventually, George comes to accept him as his brother.
- **S16**：He also finds that George keeps a toy car just his size.
- **S17**：They decide to work together to finish George's homemade model boat, the Wasp, for the Central Park Boat Race the following week, but Snowbell is not finished.
- **S18**：He and Monty head to an alley that night to visit Smokey , a Mafia Don-like Russian Blue who is the leader of the alley cats.
- **S19**：Since Snowbell doesn't want Stuart killed, he makes a plan with the alley cats to remove him from the Littles' house.
- **S20**：On the day of the 92nd annual Central Park Boat Race, the Wasp is finished, and they arrive at Central Park.
- **S21**：George discovers his nemesis, Anton, is there.
- **S22**：At the same time, Stuart accidentally destroys George's remote control, rendering his boat inoperable.
- **S23**：To make up for this, he takes control of it himself.
- **S24**：Anton, however, attempts to cheat by ramming his much larger boat into other ones and sinking them.
- **S25**：When he attempts to do the same thing to the Wasp, Stuart manages to bite into the line of his sail, rendering Anton's boat inoperable.
- **S26**：He subsequently wins the race.
- **S27**：That night, the Littles are visited by Reginald Stout and Camille Stout , a mouse couple claiming to be Stuart's parents.
- **S28**：After discussion, he reluctantly leaves with his parents, taking the toy car with him as a goodbye present from George and they go to their home, a small toy castle on a golf course.
- **S29**：Three days later, the Littles are visited by Mrs.
- **S30**：Keeper, whom they had asked to do some background research on Stuart.
- **S31**：She tells them that, according to the records, his parents died several years ago after a pile of canned food fell on them.
- **S32**：The Littles realised that he had been kidnapped and call the police.
- **S33**：A terrified Snowbell rushes to the alley and warns Smokey and the others about the Littles' discovery.
- **S34**：He then decides that the only way to rectify things is to kill him.
- **S35**：They call a meeting with Reginald and demand that he and Camille hand Stuart over.
- **S36**：But the Stouts, having grown to care for him, reveal the truth and Reginald orders him to escape before the cats find him.
- **S37**：He does so after saying goodbye to his "fake parents".
- **S38**：Meanwhile, the Littles decide to place "missing" posters around the city to get help in finding him.
- **S39**：At the Central Park, he is ambushed by Smokey and a few cats and manages to evade them by driving his car into the sewer, but he loses both the car and his luggage while escaping the storm drain.
- **S40**：Eventually, he finds his way home just as the Littles leave to hang the posters.
- **S41**：The only one home is Snowbell, who tells him a lie about how they are enjoying life without him, and shows him the family picture with his face cut out .
- **S42**：He leaves again.
- **S43**：But, Snowbell sees the pain his absence has caused and realizes his selfishness.
- **S44**：Discovering Stuart's location from Monty and the other cats, who intend to eat him, Snowbell heads to Central Park and finds him sitting alone in a bird's nest.
- **S45**：Snowbell turns on the other cats and escapes with him, admitting his lie to him and that the Littles actually do love him, he is in fact the only one who hates him.
- **S46**：The cats catch up with them and Snowbell attempts to convince Smokey to call off the hit on Stuart, but is refused.
- **S47**：Instead, he orders the cats to kill them both.
- **S48**：Stuart responds by taking off Snowbell's collar and using it to lure them.
- **S49**：The cats give chase, and eventually corner Stuart hanging from a tree branch.
- **S50**：They get together on a lower one to catch him, but Snowbell breaks it at the last minute and sends them into the water below.
- **S51**：Smokey sneaks up behind Snowbell and is about to kill him when Stuart releases a thin branch that hits him in the face and knocks him into the water.
- **S52**：Enraged and humiliated, he walks off, only to be chased away by dogs upon turning a corner.
- **S53**：Monty and the other cats also climb out of the water, whimpering and embarrassed.

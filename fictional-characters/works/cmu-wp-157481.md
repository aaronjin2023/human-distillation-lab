- **性格倾向**：在利益与危险交织时倾向灵活变卦，合作意愿随处境改变（行为推断；S17、S18、S19）

- **S12**：There Bond goes to the Whyte House, a casino-hotel owned by the reclusive billionaire Willard Whyte, where Tree works as a stand-up comedian.
- **S13**：Then Bond discovers Tree has been killed by Wint and Kidd, who did not know that the diamonds were fake.

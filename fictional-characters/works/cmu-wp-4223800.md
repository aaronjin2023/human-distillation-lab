### D'Leh

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-44aa2052fe0e94e2` · 独立版本：`profile-8884d7a4074b771e`

- **性格倾向**：面对意外获得的荣誉，倾向自省，不愿轻易把偶然成功视为应得（行为推断；S3、S4）

### Tic'Tic

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-4e043c0ec04d1760` · 独立版本：`profile-f508b248c7974838`

- **性格倾向**：在救援持续受阻的处境中，表现出不轻易退缩的勇敢倾向（行为推断；S6、S9、S11、S23）
- **核心动机**：加入追踪掳掠者的队伍，以救回被带走的艾沃莱特为行动目标（摘要直接叙述；S6）
- **关系模式**：在营救旅途中与德雷保持协同行动，并共同结交外族盟友（摘要直接叙述；S6、S11）
- **决策方式**：德雷被守卫发现时，倾向冒险阻止示警，以保护营救行动继续进行（行为推断；S22、S23）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：阻止守卫示警的行动成功，却以自身致命伤势为代价

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 10,000 BC, a tribe of hunter-gatherers called the Yagahl live in a remote mountain range in the Urals and survive by killing woolly mammoths.
- **S2**：D'Leh, a young hunter, has a companion named Evolet, an orphan who was found by the tribe.
- **S3**：D'Leh, while hunting mammoths, manages to kill one and wins the "White Spear".
- **S4**：He also wins Evolet in marriage, but feels he deserves neither since he killed the mammoth by accident.
- **S5**：One day, D'Leh and several others are away when horse-raiders called the "Four Legged Demons" attack the Yaghal camp.
- **S6**：The horse raiders enslave Evolet; D'Leh, Tic'Tic, KaRen, and Baku pursue them to save her.
- **S7**：They enter a rainforest where they catch up with the raiding party.
- **S8**：During the night D'Leh rescues Evolet, but as they are trying to escape from the pursuing raiders they are attacked by a large pack of terror birds.
- **S9**：Tic'Tic gets wounded and Baku and Ka'Ren and Evolet are re-captured.
- **S10**：Continuing on, they meet others whose loved ones were taken by the raiders.
- **S11**：D'Leh and Tic'Tic befriend Nakudu, leader of the Naku tribe.
- **S12**：He tells D'Leh of a prophecy: whoever talks to a Smilodon that they call the "Spear-Tooth" will help free their people.
- **S13**：D'Leh had earlier saved the Spear-Tooth from drowning in a trap and it had spared his life.
- **S14**：D'Leh realizes the prophecy was about him.
- **S15**：Nakudu explains that his loved ones were taken in the "Great Red Birds", ships with large red sails, to the "Mountains of the Gods", from which no one has ever returned.
- **S16**：They then come together with other tribes, who agree to form a coalition to pursue the raiders.
- **S17**：They find the ships with red sails holding Evolet and Baku.
- **S18**：They have no means to follow the ships, so they journey through a vast desert, discovering an advanced civilization similar to ancient Egypt, ruled by an enigmatic figure known as "The Almighty", who is said to be the last survivor of his kind.
- **S19**：The Almighty, who is regarded as a living god, possesses many thousands of slaves that he is using to build a huge pyramid complex in his honor.
- **S20**：D'Leh finds an escaped servant of the Almighty and notices he is wearing a bracelet worn by D'Leh's father.
- **S21**：D'Leh's father left his tribe for food and found the Naku tribe before being stolen by the raiders.
- **S22**：In a night attack, the guards of the slaves discover D'Leh behind a pyramid.
- **S23**：Tic'Tic dies from injuries from killing the guards before they raise the alarm.
- **S24**：Meanwhile, the Almighty's priests discover Evolet bears scars on her hand patterned after the "Mark of the Hunter", the constellation Orion.
- **S25**：The priests believe it is part of a prophecy that whoever wears the mark of the Hunter is destined to kill The Almighty.
- **S26**：D'Leh starts a full-scale rebellion amongst the slaves.
- **S27**：They cause the mammoth herd, used in building the pyramid, to stampede and kill a large number of troops.
- **S28**：The Almighty offers Evolet to D'Leh in exchange for abandoning his rebellion.
- **S29**：The Almighty says that if D'Leh takes his wife, his warriors can return, but the rest must be his slaves forever.
- **S30**：D'Leh feigns acceptance of the deal which allows him to throw a spear at The Almighty and kill him, proving that he is not a god.
- **S31**：During the ensuing battle, the raider obsessed with Evolet kidnaps her on horseback.
- **S32**：Evolet grabs an arrow and stabs the warlord in the side, knocking them both off the horse.
- **S33**：D'Leh rushes towards her, but the raider shoots her in the back with an arrow.
- **S34**：D'Leh kills him and returns to Evolet, and she dies in his arms.
- **S35**：The scene shifts to the tribe's wise woman as she breathes in deeply and then breathes out her last breath.
- **S36**：The scene then returns to D'Leh still holding Evolet's body when she suddenly awakens, restored by the wise woman's sacrifice.
- **S37**：They depart for home and bid farewell to the other tribes.
- **S38**：In an alternative ending, the scene shifts forward many years into the future, showing Baku's retelling of the story by the camp fire.
- **S39**：It ends with a child asking what had happened to the "Mountains of the Gods", and Baku responds "They were taken back by the sands.
- **S40**：Lost to time, lost to man." Explaining why no one ever will find in the Future the Mark of this Great Battle.

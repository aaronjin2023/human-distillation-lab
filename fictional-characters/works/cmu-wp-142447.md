- **S1**：Melvin Udall is a misanthrope who works at home as a best-selling novelist in New York City.
- **S2**：He suffers from obsessive-compulsive disorder which, paired with his misanthropy, alienates nearly everyone with whom he interacts.
- **S3**：He eats breakfast at the same table in the same restaurant every day using disposable plastic utensils he brings with him due to his pathological mysophobia.
- **S4**：He takes an interest in his waitress, Carol Connelly, the only server at the restaurant who can tolerate his behavior.
- **S5**：One day, Melvin's neighbor, a gay artist named Simon Bishop, is assaulted and nearly killed.
- **S6**：Melvin is forced to care for Simon's dog Verdell while Simon is hospitalized.
- **S7**：Although he initially does not enjoy caring for the dog, Melvin becomes emotionally attached to it.
- **S8**：He simultaneously receives more attention from Carol.
- **S9**：When Bishop is released from the hospital, Melvin is unable to cope emotionally with returning the dog.
- **S10**：Melvin's life is further altered when Carol decides to work closer to her home in Brooklyn so she can care for her acutely asthmatic son ([[Jesse James .
- **S11**：Unable to adjust to another waitress, Melvin arranges to pay for her son's medical expenses if Carol agrees to continue working at her previous restaurant in Manhattan.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, Simon's assault and rehabilitation, coupled with Verdell's preference for Melvin, causes Simon to lose his creative muse.
- **S13**：Simon is approaching bankruptcy due to his medical bills.
- **S14**：Simon's agent, Frank Sachs, convinces Simon to go to Baltimore and ask his estranged parents for money.
- **S15**：Frank offers Melvin use of his car for the trip.
- **S16**：Melvin invites Carol to accompany them on the trip to lessen the awkwardness.
- **S17**：She reluctantly accepts the invitation, and relationships among the three develop.
- **S18**：Once in Baltimore, Carol persuades Melvin to take her out to have dinner.
- **S19**：Melvin's comments during the dinner greatly upset Carol, and she abruptly leaves.
- **S20**：Upon seeing the frustrated Carol, Simon begins to sketch her and rekindles his creativity, once more feeling a desire to paint.
- **S21**：He briefly reconnects with his parents, but is able to tell them that he'll be fine.
- **S22**：After returning to New York City, Carol tells Melvin that she does not want him in her life anymore.
- **S23**：She later regrets her statement and calls him to apologize.
- **S24**：The relationship between Melvin and Carol remains complicated until Simon, whom Melvin has allowed to move in with him until he can get a new apartment, convinces Melvin to declare his love for her at her apartment in Brooklyn, where the two realize the depth of their personal connection.
- **S25**：The film ends with Melvin and Carol walking together to buy fresh rolls at the corner bakery.

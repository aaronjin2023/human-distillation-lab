### The Good

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-54b073eacdf2d8a6` · 独立版本：`profile-bfa3f0a74334e862`

- **性格倾向**：在目标逃脱后仍继续追逐，呈现执着追索的行动倾向（行为推断；S4、S9、S19）

- **情绪反应**：最终对峙受到所受冒犯与报复情绪推动，而非仅由赏金驱动（摘要直接叙述；S16）

### The Bad

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-a29dee7e2e0cb838` · 独立版本：`profile-2d2ccbbdc424db0d`

- **性格倾向**：在追逐受阻时呈现残酷控制倾向，甚至杀害想退出的部下（行为推断；S12）
- **核心动机**：起初受雇夺取藏宝图，认出旧敌后又受到复仇欲望推动（摘要直接叙述；S1、S15、S16）

- **关系模式**：与The Weird之间存在断指旧怨，使争图演变为私人冲突（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：受雇夺图的任务与遭断指后追求私人报复的目标交织。

### The Weird

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-66fdf721c8c4ba4e` · 独立版本：`profile-17e61dd4ba529431`

- **性格倾向**：在多方追逐中仍紧抓寻宝机会，表现出冒险逐利的倾向（行为推断；S2、S7、S9、S13）
- **核心动机**：相信地图指向清朝覆亡前埋藏的财富，希望据此获得金银（摘要直接叙述；S7）
- **决策方式**：面对强敌争夺倾向先夺得目标再逃脱，而非正面维持阵地（行为推断；S2、S5、S9）
- **关系模式**：曾在刀斗中切断The Bad的手指，两人的最终对峙涉及旧怨（摘要直接叙述；S15、S16）
- **成长变化**：从争夺藏宝图的盗贼变成更高赏金的追捕对象，未能摆脱逃亡（摘要直接叙述；S2、S18、S19）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他追求的是想象中的金银，抵达后却先见到看似无用的洞口。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In the desert wilderness of 1930s Manchuria, The Bad  - a bandit and hitman - is hired to acquire a treasure map from a Japanese official traveling by train.
- **S2**：Before he can get it however, The Weird  - a thief  - steals the map and is caught up in The Bad's derailment of the train.
- **S3**：This involves the slaughter of the Japanese and Manchurian guards, and various civilians.
- **S4**：The Good  - an eagle-eyed bounty hunter - appears on the scene to claim the bounty on The Bad.
- **S5**：Meanwhile The Weird escapes, eluding his Good and Bad pursuers.
- **S6**：A third force - a group of Manchurian bandits - also want the map to sell to the Ghost Market.
- **S7**：The Weird hopes to uncover the map's secrets and recover what he believes is gold and riches buried by the Qing Dynasty just before the collapse of their government.
- **S8**：As the story continues, an escalating battle for the map occurs, with bounties placed on heads and the Imperial Japanese Army racing to reclaim its map as it can apparently "save the Japanese Empire".
- **S9**：After a series of graphic shoot-outs and chases, a final battle erupts in which the Japanese army, Manchurian bandits, The Good, The Bad and his gang are chasing The Weird all at once.
- **S10**：The Japanese army kills most of the bandits.
- **S11**：The Good kills many Japanese soldiers and sets off an explosion that drives them away.
- **S12**：The Bad's gang is slowly killed off and he kills those that attempt to leave the chase.
- **S13**：Only the Bad, the Weird and the Good make it to the "treasure".
- **S14**：However, they find that it is nothing more than a boarded-over hole in the desert.
- **S15**：The Bad recognizes The Weird as the "Finger Chopper" - a criminal that cut off his finger in a knife fight five years ago - and the man that the Good had thought the Bad to be.
- **S16**：Turning on each other in a final act of vengeance for the slights they suffered, they finally gun each other down after a prolonged Mexican standoff.
- **S17**：The three lay in the sand, dying and alone, as the "useless hole" they had fought and died for suddenly and belatedly erupts with a geyser of crude oil.
- **S18**：The Good does survive along with the Weird.
- **S19**：With a newly raised bounty on the Weird, a new chase begins as he flees across the Manchurian desert.

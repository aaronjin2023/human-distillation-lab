## 角色资料

### Peter Parker

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a121e7e75a551c9c` · 独立版本：`profile-fa9634648c9032b1`

- **性格倾向**：受共生体影响时，他倾向以羞辱和刺激他人的方式表达报复。（行为推断；S11、S17、S23）
- **核心动机**：得知马克被指为本叔凶手后，他被复仇欲驱使并主动追击。（摘要直接叙述；S8、S9、S12）
- **价值取舍**：最终面对马克的解释，他选择宽恕而不再延续此前的复仇。（行为推断；S9、S40、S41、S42）
- **决策方式**：对抗共生体时，他将钟声削弱它的经验转化为声波围困办法。（行为推断；S27、S35、S36）
- **关系模式**：受共生体影响后的变化疏远了玛丽简，他还故意引她嫉妒。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S23）
- **成长变化**：意外打中玛丽简后，他认识到自身变化并成功剥离共生体。（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26、S27、S28）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他曾因复仇主动追击马克，最终却选择宽恕对方。

### Harry Osborn

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-da840783683b5033` · 独立版本：`profile-a380c138514520a4`

- **性格倾向**：恢复记忆并受父亲幻象推动时，他倾向以操纵亲密关系报复。（行为推断；S15、S16）
- **核心动机**：他因相信彼得害死父亲而袭击彼得，后来又恢复复仇行动。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S6、S15）
- **价值取舍**：最终救援中，他将保护彼得置于自身安全之前而挡下攻击。（行为推断；S33、S34）
- **决策方式**：他最初拒绝彼得求助，在得知父亲死亡真相后改变决定。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32）
- **关系模式**：他与彼得从好友变为敌手，最终互相宽恕并在友人身旁离世。（摘要直接叙述；S5、S43）
- **成长变化**：得知父亲死亡真相后，他由拒绝援助转为冒险保护彼得。（摘要直接叙述；S31、S32、S33、S34）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他曾借玛丽简伤害彼得，最终却以身体替彼得挡下致命攻击。

- **S1**：Peter Parker plans to propose to Mary Jane Watson, who has just made her Broadway musical debut.
- **S2**：Meanwhile, a small meteorite crashes near the two in Central Park, and an extraterrestrial symbiote attaches itself to Peter's moped.
- **S3**：Elsewhere, police pursue escaped prisoner Flint Marko.
- **S4**：He breaks into the site of an experimental particle accelerator, which fuses his body with the surrounding sand and allows him to shapeshift at will as the Sandman.
- **S5**：Peter's best friend, Harry Osborn, who knows Peter is Spider-Man and believes that Peter is responsible for his father Norman Osborn's death, attacks Peter using new weapons based on his father's Green Goblin technology.
- **S6**：Harry injures his head on a pipe and suffers from partial amnesia, making him forget his revenge and that Peter is Spider-Man.
- **S7**：During a festival honoring Spider-Man, Marko robs an armored car.
- **S8**：NYPD Captain George Stacy, Gwen's father, tells Peter and Aunt May that Marko was Ben Parker's killer, and the deceased Dennis Carradine only an accomplice.
- **S9**：While a vengeance-obsessed Peter sleeps, the symbiote bonds with him.
- **S10**：Peter wakes up hanging from a skyscraper and discovers his costume has changed and his powers have been enhanced.
- **S11**：But the symbiote brings out Peter's dark side.
- **S12**：Wearing the new black suit, Spider-Man locates Marko and fights him in a subway tunnel.
- **S13**：He discovers water is Marko's weakness and uses a water pipe to reduce the Sandman to mud.
- **S14**：Peter's changed personality alienates Mary Jane, whose career is floundering, and she finds solace with Harry.
- **S15**：Harry recovers from his amnesia and, urged on by a hallucination of his dead father, compels Mary Jane to break up with Peter.
- **S16**：After Mary Jane tells Peter she loves somebody else, Harry meets with Peter and claims to be "the other guy".
- **S17**：Later, Peter, wearing the black suit, confronts Harry and after a brutal fight, spitefully tells him his father never loved him.
- **S18**：Harry throws a pumpkin bomb at Peter, who deflects it back, disfiguring Harry's face.
- **S19**：Under the symbiote's influence, Peter exposes Eddie Brock, a rival photographer at the Daily Bugle, for submitting doctored photographs showing Spider-Man as a criminal.
- **S20**：Furious at having to print a retraction, J.
- **S21**：Jonah Jameson fires Eddie.
- **S22**：Meanwhile, the Sandman recovers from his injuries.
- **S23**：To make Mary Jane jealous, Peter brings Gwen to the nightclub where Mary Jane works.
- **S24**：Gwen catches on and storms out.
- **S25**：Peter gets into a fight with the bouncers and after accidentally hitting Mary Jane, he realizes the symbiote is changing him.
- **S26**：Retreating to a church bell tower, he finds he cannot remove the suit.
- **S27**：But the alien weakens as the church bells ring.

## 角色资料

### Jesus Christ

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-a95ab41a27da0e8a` · 独立版本：`profile-6006c7e2bc7b32e0`

- **性格倾向**：在此叙事中，他受审时坚持身份立场，受刑时仍为施害者求宽恕。（行为推断；S8、S21）
- **价值取舍**：他在十字架上为伤害自己的人祈求宽恕，并救赎身旁的罪犯。（摘要直接叙述；S21）
- **决策方式**：面对是否承认身份的审问，他直接回答“我是”，没有回避立场。（行为推断；S8）
- **关系模式**：面对门徒砍伤逮捕者的举动，他以治愈伤者作出回应。（摘要直接叙述；S3）

### Pontius Pilate

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-bdfd8a0ddaf7951a` · 独立版本：`profile-a5e331d8a7d22804`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他试图周旋于释放耶稣的意愿与群众压力之间。（行为推断；S13、S14、S17）
- **核心动机**：他提出鞭打后释放耶稣，并用释放巴拉巴的选择争取放人。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14）
- **决策方式**：群众坚持处死耶稣后，他最终下令钉十字架，选择顺从压力。（摘要直接叙述；S15、S17）
- **价值取舍**：他洗手宣称自己无罪，却仍下令鞭打耶稣以安抚群众。（行为推断；S16）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：试图释放耶稣，却最终下令处死他。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：The film opens in Gethsemane as Jesus prays and is tempted by Satan, while his apostles, Peter, James and John sleep.
- **S2**：After receiving thirty pieces of silver, one of Jesus' other apostles, Judas, approaches with the temple guards and betrays Jesus with a kiss on the cheek.
- **S3**：As the guards move in to arrest Jesus, Peter cuts off the ear of Malchus, but Jesus heals the ear.
- **S4**：The temple guards arrest Jesus and the apostles flee.
- **S5**：John tells Mary and Mary Magdalene of the arrest.
- **S6**：Peter follows Jesus at a distance.
- **S7**：Caiaphas holds trial over the objection of some of the other priests, who are expelled from the court.
- **S8**：When questioned by Caiaphas whether he is the son of God, Jesus replies "I am." Caiaphas is horrified and tears his robes and Jesus is condemned to death for blasphemy.
- **S9**：Three times Peter denies knowing Jesus but then runs away sobbing.
- **S10**：Meanwhile, the remorseful Judas attempts to return the money to have Jesus freed but is refused by the priests.
- **S11**：Tormented by demons, he flees the city and hangs himself with a rope he finds on a dead donkey.
- **S12**：Caiaphas brings Jesus before Pontius Pilate to be condemned to death, but after questioning Jesus, Pilate sends him instead to the court of Herod, as Jesus is from Herod's ruling town of Nazareth.
- **S13**：After Jesus is returned, Pilate offers the crowd that he will chastise Jesus and then will set him free.
- **S14**：Pilate attempts to have Jesus freed by giving the people an option of freeing Jesus or the violent criminal Barabbas.
- **S15**：To his dismay, the crowd demands to have Barabbas freed and Jesus killed.
- **S16**：Pilate washes his hands and proclaims he is not guilty of Jesus' punishment, but in an attempt to appease the crowd, has Jesus brutally scourged and mocked with a crown of thorns.
- **S17**：The crowd continues to demand that Jesus be crucified, and Pilate reluctantly orders Jesus' crucifixion.
- **S18**：As Jesus carries the cross along the Via Dolorosa to Calvary, Seraphia wipes Jesus' face with her veil.
- **S19**：Simon of Cyrene is unwillingly pressed into carrying the cross with Jesus.
- **S20**：Jesus is then crucified.
- **S21**：As he hangs from the cross, Jesus prays forgiveness for those who did this to him and redeems a criminal crucified next to him.
- **S22**：After Jesus gives up his spirit and dies, a single drop of rain falls from the sky, triggering an earthquake which destroys the Temple and rips the cloth covering the Holy of Holies in two, to the horror of Caiaphas and the other priests.
- **S23**：Satan is then shown screaming in defeat.
- **S24**：Jesus is taken down from the cross.
- **S25**：In the end, Jesus rises from the dead and exits the tomb.

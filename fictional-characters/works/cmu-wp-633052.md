### Lieutenant Aldo Raine

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-c7f7ef358a255b9a` · 独立版本：`profile-dc31e14b26491082`

- **性格倾向**：在对敌行动中，倾向强硬冒险并坚持留下惩罚印记（行为推断；S7、S19、S33）
- **核心动机**：组织敌后行动，以暴力打击纳粹并制造恐惧（行为推断；S4、S5、S7）
- **决策方式**：获知希特勒将出席后，虽损失关键队员仍继续任务（摘要直接叙述；S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：即使接受投诚交易，仍坚持让纳粹身份无法被抹去（行为推断；S31、S33）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：接受兰达的投降交易，却仍以额头刻痕永久标明其纳粹身份。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1941, SD Colonel Hans Landa , nicknamed the "Jew Hunter", interrogates French dairy farmer Perrier LaPadite .
- **S2**：To save his family, LaPadite confesses to hiding the Jewish Dreyfus family underneath his floor.
- **S3**：Landa orders SS soldiers to shoot through the floorboards and kill the family, but allows teenage Shosanna  to escape.
- **S4**：In spring 1944, 1st Special Service Force  Lieutenant Aldo Raine  recruits eight Jewish-American soldiers for a mission behind enemy lines, telling them they each owe 100 Nazi scalps and will take no prisoners.
- **S5**：The "Basterds" become so feared, Adolf Hitler  personally interviews a soldier, Butz .
- **S6**：Butz relates how his squad was ambushed and his sergeant  beaten to death with a baseball bat by Staff Sergeant Donny Donowitz , the "Bear Jew", when the sergeant refused to divulge information.
- **S7**：Butz survived by providing the information, but Raine carved a swastika into his forehead with a knife.
- **S8**：In June 1944, Shosanna runs a cinema in Paris as "Emmanuelle Mimieux".
- **S9**：She meets Fredrick Zoller , a German sniper whose exploits are to be celebrated in a Nazi propaganda film, Stolz der Nation , starring as himself.
- **S10**：Attracted to Shosanna, Zoller convinces Joseph Goebbels  to hold the premiere at her cinema.
- **S11**：Shosanna seizes the opportunity, secretly resolving to burn down her cinema with her black projectionist and lover, Marcel  and killing the top Nazi leaders at the premiere.
- **S12**：Meanwhile, film critic of German cinema, Lieutenant Archie Hicox  is recruited for "Operation Kino" by British General Ed Fenech  and Prime Minister Winston Churchill .
- **S13**：Hicox will rendezvous with their agent, German film star Bridget von Hammersmark  and the Basterds, and infiltrate and plant explosives at the premiere.
- **S14**：Hicox and Hugo Stiglitz , a convicted German sergeant freed by and joining the Basterds after killing several of his superiors, meet von Hammersmark at a tavern where Staff Sergeant Wilhelm  celebrates the birth of his baby son.
- **S15**：Sturmbannführer  Dieter Hellstrom  notices Hicox's odd accent.
- **S16**：Hicox gives himself away, signalling three drinks by not holding up his fingers correctly as a German would do.
- **S17**：In the ensuing firefight, everyone except Wilhelm and von Hammersmark is killed.
- **S18**：While Raine negotiates with Wilhelm, the actress shoots the sergeant.
- **S19**：Though his German-speaking men and Hicox are dead, Raine, upon learning from von Hammersmark that Hitler himself will be attending the premiere, decides to go ahead anyway.
- **S20**：He, Donny, and Omar  pose as von Hammersmark's Italian escort and cameramen.
- **S21**：Later, Landa investigates and finds von Hammersmark's shoe and her autographed napkin at the tavern.
- **S22**：At the premiere, Landa, who can speak Italian, is not fooled and sees von Hammersmark privately.
- **S23**：Making her wear the shoe, which fits, he strangles her, then orders Raine and Utivich's  capture.
- **S24**：Landa has Raine contact his commanding officer  and reveals he has not hindered the explosives plan, but since it will end the war he wishes for immunity, American citizenship and financial security.
- **S25**：During the screening, Zoller slips away to the projection room to see Shosanna, who rejects his advances but then has him lock the door.
- **S26**：With his back turned, she fatally shoots him, but he manages to turn and fatally shoot her as well.
- **S27**：Meanwhile Omar and Donowitz manage to dispatch the soldiers guarding Hitler.
- **S28**：A spliced-in clip of Shosanna cackling in the cinema informs the audience that they are about to be killed by a Jew.
- **S29**：Marcel, having locked the cinema exits, ignites a pile of extremely flammable nitrate film behind the screen.
- **S30**：Omar and Donowitz burst into the screening as it burns and riddle Hitler and Goebbels, as well as the fleeing crowd, with machine gun fire until the bombs go off and destroy the cinema.
- **S31**：Landa and his radio operator drive Raine and Utivich across the American lines, whereupon they surrender.
- **S32**：To Landa's horror, Raine shoots the radio operator.
- **S33**：Raine agrees for the deal, but carves a swastika into Landa's forehead to remind everyone that he was a Nazi, for the rest of his life.{{cite news}}

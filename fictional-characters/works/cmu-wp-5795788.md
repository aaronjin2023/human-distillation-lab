## 角色资料

### Tom Lone

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-041751ecaa8a0893` · 独立版本：`profile-47be6e1ec5b89722`

- **性格倾向**：在复仇布局中，他倾向隐忍伪装并长期操纵对手之间的不信任（行为推断；S8、S12、S19、S20）
- **核心动机**：他冒用杀手身份并挑起帮派战争，目标是接近并杀死灭门指使者（摘要直接叙述；S8、S19、S20）
- **价值取舍**：针对仇敌的报复并未完全抹去底线，他仍选择放过另一家的妻儿（行为推断；S13、S23）
- **决策方式**：面对强大帮派，他倾向先赢取双方信任，再利用背叛制造决战机会（行为推断；S8、S12、S13、S16、S18、S20）
- **成长变化**：得知旧搭档的背叛后，他拒绝宽恕并最终认同自己扮演的杀手身份（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：放过帮派首领的妻儿，却拒绝宽恕曾经共同办案的搭档。

### John Crawford

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-b7cbee00530afb73` · 独立版本：`profile-e39699a67f94a171`

- **性格倾向**：在追查杀手的情境中，他倾向执拗投入，甚至让家庭关系承受代价（行为推断；S9、S10、S11）
- **核心动机**：他执意追捕杀手，是为了替自己认定已经遇害的搭档复仇（摘要直接叙述；S9）
- **决策方式**：他自称泄露搭档住址时，以为黑帮只会殴打而不会杀害其家人（摘要直接叙述；S28）
- **情绪反应**：搭档揭示身份后，他受到强烈打击，并主动恳求对方原谅（摘要直接叙述；S28、S29）
- **关系模式**：面对自己背叛过的搭档，他最终以挡住狙击射线的行动给予保护（行为推断；S21、S28、S30）

暂无支持：价值取舍、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：执着为搭档复仇，却正是自己泄密促成其家庭遭到袭击。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：During a shootout against Chinese Triads at a dock warehouse, FBI agents John Crawford  and Tom Lone  stumble across the notorious assassin Rogue , a former CIA assassin who now works for the Japanese Yakuza.
- **S2**：Rogue ambushes Crawford and is about to execute him when Lone appears and shoots Rogue in the face, causing him to fall into the water.
- **S3**：Rogue's body was never found and he is presumed dead.
- **S4**：However, Rogue survives and his retaliation against Lone, his wife and his daughter, leaves three corpses in the ashes of their home.
- **S5**：Three years later, Rogue re-appears, working under Chinese Triad boss Li Chang .
- **S6**：Rogue is assisting Chang against Chang's arch-enemy and Rogue's former employer, the leader of the Japanese Yakuza, Shiro Yanagawa .
- **S7**：Rogue first attacks a club run by the Yakuza by killing one guard and the runners in order to recover a pair of antique gold horses, family heirlooms of Li Chang.
- **S8**：However, Rogue is secretly setting the Yakuza and the Triads against each other, in order to push the two factions toward all-out war.
- **S9**：Now the head agent of the FBI's Asian Crime Task Force, Crawford is determined to hunt Rogue down and exact revenge for Lone's death.
- **S10**：Crawford's obsessive pursuit of Rogue has taken a toll on his personal life, estranging him from his family.
- **S11**：Crawford comes close to catching Rogue in the wake of Rogue's various killing sprees against the Triads and Yakuza, but Rogue always manages to stay one step ahead.
- **S12**：Ultimately, Rogue's machinations have gained the trust of both Li Chang and Shiro Yanagawa.
- **S13**：Rogue succeeds in betraying Li Chang, but spares Li Chang's wife and child, turning on the Yakuza.
- **S14**：With Chang dead, Shiro Yanagawa is finally ready to come to America, where he intends to take over and expand Yakuza business operations.
- **S15**：However, he is confronted by Crawford and the FBI; Crawford presents Shiro Yanagawa with proof that Rogue has betrayed him and spared Li Chang's family, but Shiro Yanagawa refuses to assist Crawford in locating Rogue.
- **S16**：Later, Rogue delivers the horses to Shiro Yanagawa personally.
- **S17**：Knowing of Rogue's betrayal, Shiro Yanagawa captures Rogue and demands the location of Li Chang's family.
- **S18**：Rogue kills all of Shiro Yanagawa's men, and engages in a sword fight against Shiro Yanagawa himself.
- **S19**：Rogue reveals that he is actually FBI agent Tom Lone ; and killed the real Rogue, assuming the assassin's identity.
- **S20**：Rogue/Lone reveals that his actions have all been designed to bring him face-to-face with Shiro Yanagawa, so he could kill the man who ordered the death of his family.
- **S21**：Shiro Yanagawa reveals that Crawford was in his pocket and responsible for leaking Tom Lone's identity and home address to the real Rogue.
- **S22**：Angered, Rogue/Lone disarms and decapitates Shiro Yanagawa.
- **S23**：Meanwhile Chang's wife receives a package from Rogue/Lone, containing one of the two golden horses that belongs to Chang's family and a message reading, "Make a new life".
- **S24**：Shiro Yanagawa's daughter also receives a package with the same message and inside the box is her father's head.
- **S25**：Rogue/Lone then calls Crawford as he is packing up his office, asking him to meet him at the dock warehouse they last made their investigation.
- **S26**：Before going to the warehouse, Crawford enlists the help of Goi , an FBI sniper.
- **S27**：At the warehouse, Crawford and Rogue/Lone battle each other in an intense hand-to-hand fight.
- **S28**：When Rogue/Lone reveals his true identity, a devastated Crawford reveals his employment of Shiro Yanagawa and that he only gave Lone's address to Shiro Yanagawa because he thought that the Yakuza were only going to beat Lone up, and did not expect the family to be assassinated by Rogue.
- **S29**：Crawford begs Lone for forgiveness, but is rebuffed by Rogue/Lone, finalizing his identity as Rogue.
- **S30**：As Rogue/Lone takes out a gun to shoot Crawford, Goi takes aim at Rogue/Lone, and Crawford jumps in Goi's line of fire, allowing Rogue/Lone to shoot Crawford in the back and get away.

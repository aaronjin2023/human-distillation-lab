## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] The story begins by introducing Kane and Abel's past and the feud between them.
- **S3**：It then tells the story of Kane and Abel from the perspective of their children, Florentyna Rosnovski and Richard Kane.
- **S4**：Their childhood, and all the incidents and people who affected them, are portrayed in a similar manner as their fathers' lives were told in Kane and Abel .
- **S5**：There are some inconsistencies, however.
- **S6**：For example, after Abel's divorce from Zaphia in Kane and Abel Abel gets the custody of Florentyna.
- **S7**：But in The Prodigal Daughter, Zaphia has custody of Florentyna.
- **S8**：Richard and Florentyna meet by sheer chance and fall in love.
- **S9**：When their parents are told, both sets naturally react explosively; Abel goes so far as to slap the daughter he had raised with great affection.
- **S10**：The two lovers run away that day to a friend's house in another city.
- **S11**：Later, they two create a chain of retail stores named Florentyna's, which are a huge success.
- **S12**：Abel helps his daughter anonymously, but refuses to accept his son-in-law.
- **S13**：The tale takes a twist with the senior Kane's death, when Abel learns that Kane was the anonymous benefactor who helped him launch his hotel empire.
- **S14**：He thus accepts Richard and his grandchildren and considers it an honor that his grandson is named William Abel Kane.
- **S15**：Richard and Florentyna take charge of the Baron Hotels, with Florentyna as chairwoman, and then in a daring feat take over Lester's (Kane's bank).
- **S16**：Eventually Florentyna takes up politics due to the persuasion of a childhood friend named Edward Winchester.
- **S17**：Florentyna's career becomes central to the plot, as she attempts to deal with the problems a very busy and successful mother faces, including the fact that her daughter has an abortion in the early 1980s and smokes marijuana in the mid-1970s.
- **S18**：However, her career takes a back seat when Richard dies in a car crash in 1985.
- **S19**：For some time, Florentyna loses the will to pursue anything, even her career.
- **S20**：Then suddenly, seeing a homeless Vietnam Vet impels her to come "back with a vengeance." Working harder than ever, she comes very near her goal of becoming the first female U.S.
- **S21**：President.
- **S22**：For the good of her party, she strikes a deal with her opponent, Pete Parkin to support him if he promises not to run for a second term, and if he makes her his vice presidential candidate.
- **S23**：During Parkin's term, Florentyna averts many a crisis: actions for which the President takes full credit.
- **S24**：At the end of his term, however, he not only reneges on his promises and wants to run, but undermines Florentyna's support by announcing Ralph Brooks, the other Illinois Senator as his running mate.
- **S25**：It seems as though Florentyna's dream will never become a reality.
- **S26**：Disgusted with the entire situation, she leaves Washington.
- **S27**：While she is playing golf and discussing what to do with her life—her son William is now President of Lester's, with Edward, Secret Service agents arrive to announce President Parkin's sudden death from a heart attack, though there are insinuations that his death was orchestrated to put Florentyna at the helm. [ citation needed ] Florentyna thus becomes the President, and marries Edward Winchester, her childhood friend.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Theodore "Ted" Wiggins, an idealistic 12-year-old boy, lives in "Thneedville", a walled city that, aside from the citizens, is completely artificial: everything is made of plastic, metal, or synthetics.
- **S2**：Ted sets out to find a real tree in order to impress Audrey, who Ted has a crush on.
- **S3**：His energetic grandmother suggests he speak with the Once-ler about this, if he wishes to visit him, he must pay fifteen cents, a nail and a shell of great-great-great grandfather snail.
- **S4**：When he sets off outside the city discovers that their city has been closed off from the outside world, which is a contaminated and empty wasteland.
- **S5**：The Once-ler agrees to tell Ted about the trees if he listens to his story over multiple visits.
- **S6**：Ted agrees, even after the mayor of Thneedville, Aloysius O'Hare, who is also the greedy proprietor of a bottled oxygen company, confronts the boy and pressures him to stay in town.
- **S7**：Over the course of the film, Ted, with the encouragement of his grandmother, continues to sneak out of O'Hare's sight and learns more of the history of the trees.
- **S8**：Over the visits, the Once-ler recounts the story of how he departed his family to make his fortune.
- **S9**：In a lush Truffula Tree forest, he met the Lorax.
- **S10**：He is a grumpy yet charming orange creature who served as guardian of the land.
- **S11**：At first, the Once-ler had a plan to chop down the trees.
- **S12**：Eventually, he promised not to chop another tree down.
- **S13**：The young businessman's Thneed invention soon became a major success and the Once-ler's family arrived to participate in the business.
- **S14**：Keeping his promise at first, the Once-ler continued Thneed production by harvesting the tufts themselves in a sustainable manner.
- **S15**：Unfortunately, his greedy and lazy relatives convinced him to resume logging as a more efficient gathering method.
- **S16**：Breaking his promise, the Once-ler's deforestation spiraled into a mass overproduction.
- **S17**：Flush with wealth, the Once-ler rationalised his short sighted needs into arrogant self-righteousness and the helpless protests of the Lorax could not stop him.
- **S18**：The Once-ler polluted the sky, river and landscape, until finally the last Truffula Tree fell outside, and the Once-ler realised what he had done while making the region uninhabitable with his business's pollution.
- **S19**：With that, the Once-ler was left ruined and abandoned by his own family and became a recluse with the creation and isolation of Ted's town that came under young Mr.
- **S20**：O'Hare's control, giving him the plan to sell fresh air with the absence of trees.
- **S21**：Eventually, the Lorax sends the animals away before departing himself into the sky, leaving a stonecut word: "Unless".
- **S22**：At the end of the story, the Once-ler understands the meaning behind the Lorax's last message, and gives Ted a gift of the last Truffula seed in hopes of planting it to regrow the forest.
- **S23**：Ted's desire to impress Audrey is now a personal mission to remind his town of the importance of nature.
- **S24**：O'Hare, determined not to have trees undercut his business, takes heavy-handed steps such as covering Audrey's nature paintings, closing off the door that Ted uses to see the Once-ler and forcibly searching Ted's room for the seed.
- **S25**：Ted enlists his family and Audrey to help plant the seed, which has begun to germinate after coming into contact with water.
- **S26**：O'Hare and his employees pursue the dissidents until they manage to elude him and reach the town center.
- **S27**：Unfortunately, their attempt to plant the seed is interrupted by O'Hare who rallies the population to stop them.
- **S28**：To convince them otherwise, Ted takes an earthmover and rams down a section of the city wall to reveal the environmental destruction outside.
- **S29**：Horrified at the sight and inspired by Ted's conviction, the crowd defies O'Hare with his own henchmen expelling him from the town.
- **S30**：The seed is planted, and Audrey kisses Ted on the cheek.
- **S31**：Time passes and the land is starting to recover; the trees are regrowing, the animals are returning, and the redeemed Once-ler is happily reunited with the Lorax.

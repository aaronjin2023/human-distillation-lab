## 角色资料

### Sarah

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-45c5fa8c8b601bac` · 独立版本：`profile-e1125ad63b4d9d82`

- **性格倾向**：面对曾在丧亲后缺席的朋友示好，她倾向保持距离而非立即接纳（行为推断；S9、S14）
- **情绪反应**：丧亲后的牵念持续影响她，最终在洞中幻见女儿与生日蛋糕（行为推断；S4、S7、S45、S46）
- **价值取舍**：面对贝丝无法挽回的痛苦，她勉强接受对方结束生命的请求（摘要直接叙述；S30、S31）
- **决策方式**：面对贝丝的指控，她倾向在得到实物佐证后才接受负面信息（行为推断；S30、S31、S38）
- **成长变化**：她从洞中逃生者逐渐转为主动搏杀，并最终以暴力惩罚朱诺（摘要直接叙述；S10、S32、S39、S40）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：能为贝丝减轻痛苦，却也主动伤残并抛下朱诺

### Juno

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-1aa3c6dce6867ba9` · 独立版本：`profile-cd16f4e121868d67`

- **性格倾向**：为修复友谊，她倾向擅自掌控行程而低估同伴知情的必要（行为推断；S11、S12、S14）
- **核心动机**：她带莎拉进入未知洞穴，是希望借共同探险恢复两人的关系（摘要直接叙述；S14）
- **决策方式**：在团队受困时，她倾向选择性保留信息以维持大家的希望（行为推断；S15、S16）
- **关系模式**：她坚持找到莎拉才离开，却在误伤贝丝后将对方独自留下（摘要直接叙述；S25、S26、S28）
- **沟通方式**：面对莎拉追问贝丝的死亡，她用点头隐去了自己误伤的经过（行为推断；S25、S26、S35、S36）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、成长变化

矛盾与变化：渴望修复信任，却以隐瞒与擅自决定继续破坏信任；拒绝抛下莎拉，却在惊慌中留下重伤的贝丝

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Juno , Sarah  and Beth  are whitewater rafting in Scotland.
- **S2**：Sarah's husband Paul  and their daughter Jessica  wave and cheer from the bank.
- **S3**：On the drive back to their hotel, Paul is distracted, causing a collision.
- **S4**：Paul and Jessica are killed, but Sarah survives.
- **S5**：One year later, Juno, Sarah, Beth, Sam  and Rebecca  are reunited at a cabin in the Appalachian Mountains of North Carolina, USA.
- **S6**：Holly , Juno's new friend, is introduced.
- **S7**：As they reminisce over an old photo of Juno, Sarah, and Beth, Sarah says "Love each day", explaining that it was a saying of her late husband's.
- **S8**：The next morning the group goes caving.
- **S9**：When the group breaks for lunch in a huge gallery, Juno tearfully apologises to Sarah for not being there for her after the accident, but Sarah is distant.
- **S10**：As the group moves through the next passage it collapses behind them, with Sarah barely making it through.
- **S11**：After a heated discussion, Juno admits that she has led them into an unknown cave system, instead of the fully explored cave system they planned for.
- **S12**：The only people who were told about their expedition think they are at the other cave system, making rescue impossible.
- **S13**：They are trapped with no way out.
- **S14**：Privately, Juno tells Sarah that she led them into the unknown cave hoping to restore their relationship, but Sarah rebuffs her.
- **S15**：The group discovers a cave painting and climbing equipment from a previous visitor, suggesting a second exit exists.
- **S16**：Juno keeps the latter secret, allowing for the group to remain hopeful.
- **S17**：Holly falls down a hole and breaks her leg.
- **S18**：Sam sets Holly's fracture with a splint and they carry her.
- **S19**：As the others help Holly, Sarah wanders off and observes a pale, humanoid creature drinking at a pool.
- **S20**：It scampers off into the darkness when Sarah gasps.
- **S21**：The others think Sarah imagined it, but Sarah insists that she saw someone.
- **S22**：Soon after they are attacked by one of the creatures, referred to as "crawlers".
- **S23**：The group scatter, and the crawler rips Holly's throat.
- **S24**：Sarah trips and falls and passes out.
- **S25**：Seeing Holly is still alive, Juno tries to defend her from the crawlers, but in the confusion, she whirls around, only to stab Beth through the neck with her pickaxe.
- **S26**：Beth grabs Juno's pendant as she drops to the ground, but Juno stumbles away in shock as Beth reaches out to her.
- **S27**：Juno eventually locates Sam and Rebecca and rescues them from a crawler.
- **S28**：Juno tells them she may have found a way out, but will not leave without Sarah.
- **S29**：The others reluctantly agree to help her search.
- **S30**：Meanwhile, Sarah awakens and encounters the mortally wounded Beth, who tells Sarah that Juno wounded her and left her.
- **S31**：Sarah does not believe her until Beth gives her Juno's pendant, which is inscribed with the words "love each day." Beth, in extreme pain, asks Sarah to euthanise her, and Sarah reluctantly complies.
- **S32**：Sarah soon encounters and kills a young crawler, a female crawler, and a male crawler in quick succession.
- **S33**：Elsewhere, Juno, Sam and Rebecca are pursued by a large group of crawlers.
- **S34**：Crawlers kill Sam and Rebecca, and Juno leaps into a chasm to escape.
- **S35**：Juno climbs out of the chasm and is helped onto a ledge by Sarah, who asks her if she saw Beth die.
- **S36**：Juno nods.
- **S37**：The two cautiously explore the caves until they encounter a group of crawlers and defeat them.
- **S38**：Sarah then faces Juno, and reveals that she has Juno's pendant, revealing that she knows that she wounded Beth.
- **S39**：Sarah cripples Juno with a pickaxe.
- **S40**：Juno pulls the pickaxe from her leg and turns to face a large group of crawlers while Sarah leaves her behind, with Juno's fighting screams fading as Sarah goes further.
- **S41**：Sarah falls down a hole and is knocked unconscious.
- **S42**：She awakens, scrambles up a huge pile of bones towards daylight, squeezes through a narrow opening onto the surface, runs to her vehicle and speeds off.
- **S43**：She pulls over to vomit and sees Juno sitting next to her, her face streaked with blood.
- **S44**：Sarah screams and awakens to find herself still in the cavern.
- **S45**：She sees her smiling daughter close by and a birthday cake between them.
- **S46**：The field of view widens to reveal that Sarah is hallucinating and she is actually staring at a torch.

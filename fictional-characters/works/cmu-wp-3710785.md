### Benjamin Gates

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-6001af495bde1f5a` · 独立版本：`profile-4fc7a3be3a492247`

- **性格倾向**：家族名誉受损时倾向执着求证，愿持续追索跨地域的复杂线索（行为推断；S9、S12、S16、S35）
- **核心动机**：破解日记谜题的明确目标，是恢复祖先托马斯的清白与声誉（摘要直接叙述；S9）

- **成长变化**：追查家族往事的过程中也修复亲密关系，与阿比盖尔重归于好（摘要直接叙述；S15、S46）

### Mitch Wilkinson

角色职责：central-antagonist · 身份：`identity-837bbd3c03f390c5` · 独立版本：`profile-ecad2b28522ac9df`

- **性格倾向**：竞争寻宝时倾向控制局面，愿以监视和胁迫压缩他人的选择（行为推断；S14、S34、S38）
- **核心动机**：逃生危局中请求分享发现的署名功劳，显示此时仍重视留名（行为推断；S37、S38、S39）
- **决策方式**：倾向利用对手破解线索，再以窃听、抢夺和人质手段夺取优势（行为推断；S14、S17、S34）

- **成长变化**：最终向本承认对其家族的怀疑有误，并在危局中提出留名请求（摘要直接叙述；S39）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：In 1865 after the Civil War, Thomas Gates and his son Charles Carroll Gates are approached by John Wilkes Booth and Michael O'Laughlen, both of them Knights of the Golden Circle.
- **S2**：They ask Thomas, a skilled puzzle-solver, to decode a playfair cipher written in Booth's diary.
- **S3**：O'Laughlen stays with Thomas as Booth leaves to assassinate President Abraham Lincoln.
- **S4**：Just as Thomas discovers the message is a map to Cíbola, the fabled City of Gold, pandemonium arises in the streets from the President's assassination.
- **S5**：Thomas realizes that Booth and O'Laughlen are still loyal to the South, tears the pages from the diary, and tosses them into a fire before O'Laughlen can shoot him.
- **S6**：O'Laughlen only manages to save a partial page from the fireplace before escaping, while Thomas' dying words to Charles are "the debt that all men pay".
- **S7**：The film then shifts to modern day , where Benjamin Gates, Thomas's great-great-grandson, is retelling Thomas' story at a Civilian Heroes conference.
- **S8**：He and his father Patrick Gates are publicly accosted by Mitch Wilkinson, a black market dealer who has come into possession of the diary page, which appears to link Thomas to Booth and Lincoln's assassination.
- **S9**：Ben determines to solve the puzzle on the page to restore Thomas' legacy.
- **S10**：Special Agent Sadusky of the FBI finds it suspicious that a black market dealer like Mitch would suddenly come forward with a rare Civil War artifact.
- **S11**：Ben’s technology-savvy friend Riley Poole just had his Ferrari impounded when he was giving out his government conspiracy books then later meet up with Ben.
- **S12**：Ben and Riley discover the cipher on the diary page that says Laboulaye Lady, which points to a hidden inscription on the scale model of the Statue of Liberty on the Île aux Cygnes in Paris, thanks to Thomas' last words.
- **S13**：The inscription points to the two Resolute desks, one of which is in the Queen's chambers in Buckingham Palace, the other in the Oval Office of the White House.
- **S14**：Ben is unaware that Mitch is following in his footsteps, using a clone of Patrick's cell phone to intercept messages.
- **S15**：In London, Ben discovers that Patrick has sent Ben's ex-girlfriend Abigail Chase to help.
- **S16**：They are able to sneak into the Queen's quarters and find that the Resolute desk acts as a puzzle box, revealing a hidden compartment with a centuries-old plank of wood with indecipherable hieroglyphics on it.
- **S17**：Ben then manages to photograph it before Mitch and his men steal it after a car chase through London.
- **S18**：Patrick indicates the plank leads to Cíbola, the City of Gold.
- **S19**：History says that in 1527 explorers came to the New World to search for Cíbola after its first discovery, but never could find it.
- **S20**：Ben convinces Patrick to help them arrange a meeting with Dr Emily Appleton, Ben's mother and Patrick's ex-wife.
- **S21**：Emily is able to translate part of the plank but states that the message is incomplete.
- **S22**：Abigail uses her current boyfriend Conner, a member of the White House staff, to allow Ben to sneak into the Oval Office, but Ben finds the Resolute desk compartment empty, save for a symbol  representing the supposed "Book of Secrets".
- **S23**：Riley asserts that the Book is real, having himself just written a book on its existence, and claims that it contains the truth behind many conspiracy theories such as the missing 18½ minutes of the Watergate tapes, Area 51 coup d'état, Apollo program, and the assassination of President John F.
- **S24**：Kennedy.
- **S25**：However, traditionally the only person to know the whereabouts of the Book of Secrets is the current President himself, since it's for Presidents' eyes only.
- **S26**：Ben and his team manipulate events to assure that the President's birthday celebration occur at Mount Vernon, allowing Ben to sneak in and lure the President alone to a set of disused tunnels.
- **S27**：Ben then blocks off the entrance, trapping the President alone with him so that they can talk about the Book of Secrets.
- **S28**：Though the President warns Ben that he will be charged with kidnapping the President, he is sympathetic to Ben's cause and provides him with the Book's location at the Library of Congress.
- **S29**：Ben, Abigail, and Riley locate the Book as the FBI, including Sadusky, surround the building.
- **S30**：In the Book they find that President Calvin Coolidge had found the other half of the plank in 1924 and destroyed it, after taking a photo .
- **S31**：He also ordered Gutzon Borglum to carve Mount Rushmore into a hill to erase the landmark indicated on the plank, in order to protect Cíbola.
- **S32**：As Ben and his team elude the FBI, he sends an image of the plank to Patrick to give to Emily to decode, unaware that Mitch has intercepted this and is waiting for Patrick.
- **S33**：Emily is forced to lie to Patrick about the inscription's meaning, but is able to give him a non-verbal cue of this falsehood.
- **S34**：Ben, Patrick, Abigail and Riley arrive at Mount Rushmore, prepared to deal with Mitch, but find that Mitch has brought Emily along, using her as a hostage to ensure Ben follows the final clues on an old letter sent from Queen Victoria to Confederate general Albert Pike.
- **S35**：Ben is able to find the secret entrance, and the group avoids several traps before arriving at the underground site of Cíbola, the City of Gold.
- **S36**：As they explore, they accidentally cause the cave to flood with water from a lake above.
- **S37**：The only means of escape is through a drainage door that must be held open on one side to allow the rest to escape and closed afterwards, trapping the one controlling the door.
- **S38**：Mitch initially forces Ben to sacrifice himself, but after a surge of water, Mitch finds himself holding the door open.
- **S39**：He apologizes to Ben for doubting his family, and requests that part of this discovery be in his name.
- **S40**：Ben and the others escape safely.
- **S41**：Ben and his friends are met by Sadusky and taken to a nearby military base.
- **S42**：Ben is cleared of kidnapping by the President himself, who claims that Ben rescued him after the tunnel closed accidentally.
- **S43**：Sometime later, Cíbola's discovery is announced, with Ben, his team, and Mitch given credit for its discovery.
- **S44**：Thomas Gates' name is cleared and he is called a hero of the Civil War.
- **S45**：Patrick and Emily, happily working together, eagerly take to the task of analyzing Cíbola once it is drained of water.
- **S46**：Ben and Abigail rekindle their broken relationship at Mount Rushmore, while Riley is given his Ferrari back by the President, "tax free".
- **S47**：The only mystery remaining is what the President asks Ben to decipher on Page 47 in the closing scene.

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot Summary
- **S2**：[ edit ] TransPacific Airlines Flight 545 experiences severe pitch oscillations over the Pacific Ocean, leading to dozens of injured passengers and several deaths.
- **S3**：The plane, a Norton Aircraft N-22 widebody, has an excellent safety record, and the captain is highly skilled, making the possibility of human error seemingly unlikely.
- **S4**：Casey Singleton, a vice-president in quality assurance at Norton, is assigned by COO John Marder to join the company's Incident Review Team (IRT) to investigate.
- **S5**：The IRT has one week to identify the issue in order to prevent a major sale with China from falling apart over safety concerns.
- **S6**：Due to issues with the flight recorders , the IRT is forced to manually check each system of the aircraft.
- **S7**：At first, all signs point to the plane's slats accidentally deploying mid-flight.
- **S8**：Though other N-22s had exhibited similar slat errors in the past, Norton assumed the problem had been fixed.
- **S9**：Their search uncovers a counterfeit part in the wing, but this would not have been enough to cause the accident on its own.
- **S10**：Casey grows suspicious of Bob Richman, an arrogant Norton employee who has been assigned to assist her.
- **S11**：Meanwhile, the factory union is concerned the China sale will offset the wing to China, threatening employees' jobs, and seeks to sabotage the deal.
- **S12**：An unrelated N-22 engine failure , and the release of passenger camera footage from Flight 545, draws heavy media attention.
- **S13**：Jennifer Malone, a producer for the television magazine Newsline , is interested in reporting on the N-22's flaws and pursues the story; Marder asks Casey to conduct an interview.
- **S14**：Later, Casey realizes the accident aircraft was equipped with a quick access recorder (QAR); the team did not know the plane was equipped with one because QARs are optional.
- **S15**：Casey locates the QAR and pieces together the events that caused the accident.
- **S16**：First, a counterfeit part caused a sensor in the plane's wing to malfunction, which produced an error message in the cockpit.
- **S17**：This error message could be cleared by deploying and retracting the plane's slats.
- **S18**：Although deploying the slats would change the shape of the wing, the autopilot could adjust without incident.
- **S19**：However, the captain's son, a pilot who was not certified to fly the N-22, was at the controls at the time of the accident; he manually overcorrected, overriding the autopilot and sending the plane into a series of oscillations .
- **S20**：Casey realizes she cannot publicize this information.
- **S21**：Publicly pinning the blame on a TransPacific employee would sour relations with the airline, ruining future sales just as surely as any N-22 safety issues would.
- **S22**：During her interview, she also discovers Richman has set her up to fail as part of a larger conspiracy .
- **S23**：Richman and Marder had secretly prepared a larger sale to a South Korean airline, which included offsetting the wing.
- **S24**：They hoped to use the accident to sink the China deal and oust CEO Harold Edgarton, confident that the accident would not jeopardize the Korean deal.
- **S25**：Casey thwarts the plan by allowing Malone onto a test flight for the N-22 from the accident, where the event is replicated, showing that a slat deployment alone would not cause the accident.
- **S26**：Once Malone is shown the evidence, she cannot air her segment as planned without opening herself up to a defamation lawsuit.
- **S27**：She is also unable to report the true story, since her boss thinks it is not exciting enough to hold people's attention.
- **S28**：With the N-22's reputation cleared, the China deal proceeds, with the tail rather than the wing as an offset.
- **S29**：Afterwards, Edgarton promotes Casey to head the company's Media Relations Division.
- **S30**：Richman is arrested in Singapore for narcotics possession, while Marder leaves the company, supposedly on good terms.
- **S31**：Malone leaves Newsline after a "contract dispute", and joins the staff of Hard Copy .

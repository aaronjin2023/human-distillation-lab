## 角色资料

### Alex

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-770c59c1b2e7b526` · 独立版本：`profile-f566da4f0a527e14`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他以舞蹈展现自我，也能在危急时用熟悉技能应变。（行为推断；S2、S19、S35）
- **核心动机**：他想弥补挑战失败造成的后果，离开保护区调查水源干涸。（摘要直接叙述；S27）
- **关系模式**：父亲为保住他放弃首领之位，最终父子共同领导族群。（摘要直接叙述；S20、S41）
- **决策方式**：他借助娜娜攻击Makunga夺回主动权，随后拒绝独占首领之位。（摘要直接叙述；S40、S41）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

### Marty

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-cc65b70c674c8b6e` · 独立版本：`profile-84e853d106d4b7ec`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他重视自我独特性，发现同类相似时感到失落。（行为推断；S11、S22）
- **核心动机**：他和Alex离开保护区调查水源问题，随后赶回报信并参与救援。（摘要直接叙述；S27、S33、S34）
- **关系模式**：他与Alex同行调查，Alex被捕后他离开现场寻求援助。（摘要直接叙述；S27、S29）
- **决策方式**：面对Alex被困，他判断需要外援，暂时离开去寻找帮助。（行为推断；S29）

暂无支持：价值取舍、情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：As a cub, Alex the Lion was called Alakay and was the son of Zuba , the alpha lion.
- **S2**：Though Zuba tries to teach Alakay to be a hunter, the cub is more interested in dancing, and is captured by poachers when Makunga , Zuba's rival, challenges Zuba to a fight for the position of alpha lion.
- **S3**：Alakay is forced into a crate, and taken away despite Zuba's attempts to rescue him.
- **S4**：The crate falls into the ocean where it drifts to New York.
- **S5**：There, Alakay is renamed Alex and sent to the Central Park Zoo where he grew up, meeting Marty the Zebra, Melman the Giraffe, and Gloria the Hippopotamus.
- **S6**：The events of the first film are then described through a series of news flashes.
- **S7**：In the present time, Alex the Lion , Marty the Zebra , Melman the Giraffe , Gloria the Hippopotamus , the Penguins Skipper (voiced by [[Tom McGrath , Kowalski (voiced by [[Chris Miller , Private , and Rico , the Chimpanzees Mason  and Phil, and even the Lemurs King Julien , Maurice , and Mort  board a repaired airplane to fly back to New York.
- **S8**：The plane takes off, but crash-lands in continental Africa.
- **S9**：In Africa the animals are amazed to find more of their kind.
- **S10**：Alex is reunited with Zuba and his mother.
- **S11**：Marty fits in with a herd of zebra who all look and sound exactly like him.
- **S12**：Hypochondriac Melman becomes a witch doctor.
- **S13**：Gloria attracts the attention of a smooth-talking hippo named Moto Moto .
- **S14**：Meanwhile, the penguins set about repairing the plane.
- **S15**：They carjack several jeeps under "Operation Tourist Trap", leaving the tourists stranded in the jungle.
- **S16**：Nana , a tough old woman known for beating up Alex in the first film, takes charge of the group ("We are [[Survivor .
- **S17**：Unfortunately, life in Africa is not as wonderful as it first seemed.
- **S18**：Makunga, still determined to take the position of alpha lion, reminds Zuba that Alex must complete a traditional coming-of-age challenge that he did not complete.
- **S19**：Alex, thinking that the challenge is a dance contest  competes against the strongest lion, Teetsi  who was recommended by Makunga and loses quickly.
- **S20**：To avoid banishing his own son for failing, Zuba relinquishes his title as Alpha lion.
- **S21**：Makunga immediately takes the position and ousts Alex and his family.
- **S22**：Marty, meanwhile, begins to feel upset because there is nothing unique about him.
- **S23**：Melman is happy until he learns that he has the same symptoms that apparently caused the previous witch doctor to die.
- **S24**：Melman is also forlorn about Gloria dating Moto Moto, as Melman has secretly loved her for a long time.
- **S25**：Gloria goes on a date with Moto Moto and quickly realizes that he only loves her for her large body.
- **S26**：The next day, the animals panic when the watering hole dries up.
- **S27**：Determined to make up for his earlier failure, Alex and Marty leave the reserve to investigate.
- **S28**：They discover that the stranded New Yorkers, under the instruction of Nana, have dammed up the river and built a primitive civilization.
- **S29**：Alex is captured by a trap, forcing Marty to abandon him to seek help.
- **S30**：Meanwhile, Zuba hears from Makunga what Alex did and goes to assist him.
- **S31**：Back on the reserve, King Julien suggests that the animals offer a sacrifice in the volcano, Mount Kilimanjaro, to appease the water gods.
- **S32**：Believing that he will die soon, Melman volunteers to be sacrificed.
- **S33**：Gloria stops him just in time and Marty arrives to tell them about Alex.
- **S34**：The trio, the penguins, and several chimpanzees use the newly-fixed plane to execute a rescue.
- **S35**：However, Alex has already managed to rescue both himself and his father by dancing for the New Yorkers, who fondly remember him from the zoo.
- **S36**：The other animals arrive to pick them up with the plane , and together they all destroy the dam, freeing the water.
- **S37**：Meanwhile, back at the volcano, Mort turns up after being chased through the jungle by a shark.
- **S38**：The shark falls into the volcano, completing the sacrifice  and causing King Julien to think that he brought back the water.
- **S39**：As the watering hole fills, Makunga angrily makes a stand for control.
- **S40**：However, Alex manages to remove Makunga from power by tricking Nana into attacking him.
- **S41**：Zuba gives the alpha position to Alex, but Alex refuses, resulting in both father and son becoming co-leaders.
- **S42**：The movie ends as Skipper marries a bobble-head hula doll from the plane and leaves on a honeymoon in Monte Carlo with the chimpanzees, and a huge tub of diamonds and gold, leaving Alex, Marty, Melman, Gloria and The Lemurs to happily remain in Africa with Alex's parents.

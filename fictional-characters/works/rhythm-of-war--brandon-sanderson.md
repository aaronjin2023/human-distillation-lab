## 角色资料

### Kaladin

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-7a54f46b8bdbfc0c` · 独立版本：`profile-b743361fc62060e3`

- **性格倾向**：在自身状态脆弱时，仍倾向主动承担保护他人的责任（行为推断；S34、S36）
- **核心动机**：失去战斗职责后，通过帮助心理困境中的人重新找到意义（摘要直接叙述；S32、S34）
- **价值取舍**：倾向以实际照护回应受困者，而非任其被一概隔离收容（行为推断；S34）
- **情绪反应**：战友遇害后陷入极深悲痛，一度难以继续承受失去（摘要直接叙述；S39、S40）
- **成长变化**：逐渐接受无法保护所有人的限度，并据此迈过誓言关口（摘要直接叙述；S40、S41）

暂无支持：决策方式、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：持续承担保护责任，却必须接受自己无法救下所有人。

### Navani

- **性格倾向**：受敌方俘虏后，仍倾向持续探索光的机制并寻找研究突破（行为推断；S15、S16、S17、S18）
- **成长变化**：与Urithiru的精灵Sibling结契，成为Bondsmith（摘要直接叙述；S19）
- **价值取舍**：即使与敌方研究者产生亲近，也仍将阵营责任纳入行动取舍（行为推断；S20）
- **决策方式**：倾向通过辨识不同光的属性与组合，逐步推进关键发现（行为推断；S16、S17、S18）
- **情绪反应**：攻击研究期间逐渐亲近的Raboniel时，明确感到遗憾（摘要直接叙述；S20）

暂无支持：核心动机、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：与Raboniel在研究中亲近，却最终仍因敌对立场攻击她。

### Dalinar

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-0b6ee559258c64f9` · 独立版本：`profile-b3f4966c2bf1cdb3`

- **性格倾向**：察觉异常且自身知识不足时，倾向主动向更有经验者求助（行为推断；S9）
- **核心动机**：与敌方订立限期决战协议，旨在让战争获得明确终局（行为推断；S12）
- **价值取舍**：在终结战争的取舍中，愿承担敌方获胜后扩大统治的风险（行为推断；S12）
- **决策方式**：发现部属心理状态不适合战斗时，倾向先解除其职责（行为推断；S32）
- **关系模式**：对陷入绝望的Kaladin，通过与其亲人有关的幻象给予支持（摘要直接叙述；S40）

- **S8**：Each characters progression through the book is as follows: Dalinar: The Bondsmith takes Szeth, Jasnah (now Queen of Alethkar) and many Radiants to the frontlines of the war.
- **S9**：Sensing something wrong in Urithiru, he seeks out Ishar, the Bondsmith Herald, hoping for guidance.
- **S10**：Instead, the mad Herald attacks and flees, but has a moment of lucidity where he asks Dalinar to find and help him.
- **S11**：Dalinar grows closer to the Stormfather, gaining the ability to ride with him on highstorms and slow time to speak to others that he is connected to.
- **S12**：Dalinar formalizes his deal with Odium: the two agree to a contest of champions in ten days’ time which will end the war no matter who wins, though Odium will conquer more of Roshar and maintain his control over Alethkar if his champion wins.
- **S13**：Odium explains that he intends to create an army of the most powerful surgebinders on Roshar, and conquer the Cosmere.
- **S14**：Navani: As Urithiru is overtaken by Fused and singers, the tower’s defenses are inverted to subdue the Radiants.
- **S15**：Raboniel, the Fused in command of this mission, captures Navani but allows her to continue her research.
- **S16**：After some time, Navani becomes able to manipulate Stormlight and Voidlight.
- **S17**：She discovers that each god of Roshar: Honor, Cultivation, and Odium, has their own associated Light (Storm, Life, and Void) respectively, which can be combined to produce unique mixtures of light with different attributes.
- **S18**：Navani eventually discovers how to make anti-light, which can permanently kill both Fused and spren, which could truly end the war between Radiants and Fused.
- **S19**：Navani bonds with the spren of Urithiru, the Sibling, and becomes a Bondsmith.
- **S20**：Navani regretfully attacks Raboniel, whom she had grown close to throughout the weeks of research, because they are still opposed and, at Raboniels request, permanently kills her.
- **S21**：Both sides now have the means to permanently kill the other.
- **S22**：Moash comes to Urithiru to kill Navani and Kaladin, and Navani manages to overwhelm and permanently blind him with an outpouring of light.
- **S23**：Adolin: Accompanying Shallan on a voyage to Shadesmar, he arrives in Lasting Integrity: the stronghold of the honorspren.
- **S24**：To access the city, Adolin volunteers to be tried in court to determine the worthiness of humans to bond with spren, following the betrayal of the Radiants.
- **S25**：The honorspren eventually bring in Maya, the catatonic spren of Adolin’s shardblade, as a witness, trying to incriminate him.
- **S26**：However, Maya speaks (which was thought impossible) saying that the spren and Radiants together chose to give up their powers, even knowing that would destroy the spren, so there never was a betrayal at all.
- **S27**：Many honorspren agree to bond with humans again due to this realization, and Adolin is found innocent of any charges Shallan: Tasked by the Ghostbloods to find and kill a mysterious man known as Restares, who lives in Lasting Integrity, Shallan discovers that he is none other than Kelek, the Herald.
- **S28**：She refuses to kill him and starts to heal from her split personality, absorbing Veil.
- **S29**：She remembers that she used to be bonded to a different cryptic before Pattern, whom she inadvertently turned into deadeyes as a child.
- **S30**：She and Adolin find the deadeyed spren, Testament, and begin trying to help her recover as Adolin has helped Maya.
- **S31**：Shallan declares war on the Ghostbloods.
- **S32**：Kaladin: Struggling with his depression and shaken from an encounter with Moash in Hearthstone, Kaladin is relieved of duty by Dalinar.
- **S33**：In hearthstone, Kaladin also encountered a new type of Fused named Lezrian who took pleasure in stalking and killing his victims.
- **S34**：While initially resentful of being relieved of duty, Kaladin finds purpose in helping others with mental illness, people who were often institutionalized regardless of their condition.
- **S35**：When Urithiru is overrun, all of the Radiants except Kaladin and Lift fall unconscious, Kaladin due to his closeness to the Fourth Ideal, and Lift due to her unique powers.
- **S36**：Though still unstable, Kaladin fights back, protecting the Sibling and harrying the Fused.
- **S37**：A resistance of humans forms, marking themselves with the shash brand in an homage to Kaladin, and the tower eventually erupts into full-on rebellion.
- **S38**：Severely wounded, Kaladin is healed by Lift and rallies to defeat Lezrian, who is later killed by Odium for his failure.
- **S39**：While in Urithiru, Moash attacks and kills Teft’s spren with anti-stormlight, and then kills Teft himself.
- **S40**：Kaladin is heartbroken over Teft’s death, and nearly ends his own life before being consoled and revitalized by Dalinar through a vision of Tien, where Kaladin learns that Tien chose to be on the front lines where he was killed so that he could make the other boys feel braver.
- **S41**：Understanding that he cannot always protect everyone, Kaladin swears the Fourth Ideal and is able to save his father, who had been kidnapped and thrown off of the Tower.
- **S42**：Venli: It is revealed how Venli ushered in the Fused and Regal forms in the backstories of her and Eshonai.
- **S43**：In the present, Venli is secretly working to overthrow most of the Fused with a few allies, including Leshwi who is a Fused herself.
- **S44**：She eventually resolves to save Kaladin’s parents and Lift as a part of her Radiant Oaths.
- **S45**：Venli and Leshwi, and their followers, join the revolt in the tower, fighting against the other singers.
- **S46**：Venli discovers that the listeners who fled the first Everstorm are still alive and she and Rlain reunite with them as Venli swears the 2nd Ideal of a Willshaper.
- **S47**：It is revealed that Eshonai was briefly a Radiant before her death, and was able to ride the storms with Stormfather to see the whole world, which was her dream, before dying peacefully.
- **S48**：Taravingian: Taravingian realizes that Nightblood, Szeth’s sword, cannot be seen by Odium’s foresight.
- **S49**：During an assassination attempt by Szeth, Taravingian manages to draw Nightblood and, pulled into a vision by Odium, strikes the god.
- **S50**：It is revealed that the gods of the Cosmere are people who have taken charge of primal sources of cosmic power, called Shards (not to be confused with the Blade and Plate remnants of the Knights Radiant, also called shards).
- **S51**：Taravingian kills the man, Rayse, who owned Odium’s Shard, and ascends in his place.

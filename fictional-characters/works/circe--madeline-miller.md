## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：## Plot
- **S2**：[ edit ] Circe is the divine daughter of the titan Helios and naiad Perse .
- **S3**：In this reimagining, she is considered unattractive and powerless from birth, she is neglected by her father and bullied by the rest of her family.
- **S4**：While still young, she witnesses the titan Prometheus being punished for giving fire to humanity and gives him nectar when no one is looking.
- **S5**：She bonds with her younger brother Aeëtes , but he leaves her to rule his own kingdom.
- **S6**：Circe falls in love with Glaucos , a mortal fisherman.
- **S7**：Circe feeds Glaucos sap of magical flowers grown in soil that was once soaked with the blood of the titan Kronos and transforms him into a god.
- **S8**：Arrogant in his newfound divinity, however, Glaucos rejects Circe in favor of the nymph Scylla .
- **S9**：Circe's jealousy causes her to use the flowers' magic again, transforming Scylla into a bloodthirsty six-headed monster.
- **S10**：Circe confesses her deeds to Helios, who disbelieves her powers at first; Aeëtes reveals that he and his siblings are witches who can draw out magic from such herbs.
- **S11**：To keep the peace between the Titans and the Olympians, led by Zeus , Helios and Perse are forbidden from having more children.
- **S12**：Aeëtes and his other children are to be monitored, but Circe is punished for purposely seeking out the magic and using it on another god.
- **S13**：Circe is permanently exiled to the island of Aiaia .
- **S14**：Circe uses her exile to hone her witchcraft.
- **S15**：Over the centuries she spends on Aiaia, Circe interacts with many mythic figures.
- **S16**：She receives visits from the Olympian god Hermes , whom she takes as a lover.
- **S17**：She is escorted off the island by the mortal Daedalus .
- **S18**：During her brief visit to Crete , Circe helps her sister birth the Minotaur and uses her witchcraft to help tame the monster.
- **S19**：Many years later, the hero Jason and his wife the witch Medea (Circe's niece) arrive on Aiaia after having stolen the Golden Fleece from Circe's brother Aeëtes, murdering Medea's brother, Absyrtus , in the process.
- **S20**：Circe cleanses them of the crime and warns Medea of Jason's waning interest in her but is rebuffed.
- **S21**：Circe enters a period of loneliness and is excited to host a group of forlorn sailors who arrive on Aiaia in search of food and rest.
- **S22**：Once the sailors realize that Circe lives alone on the island, the ship captain rapes her.
- **S23**：Circe uses her witchcraft to transform all the men into pigs.
- **S24**：When subsequent ships arrive at her island she continues to do so, sparing only a handful of crewmen who behave with pious respect and do not attempt to force themselves on her.
- **S25**：One ship arrives led by the hero Odysseus , who charms Circe into sparing his crew and hosting them on her island.
- **S26**：Odysseus and his crew ultimately stay on Aiaia for one year.
- **S27**：Though Odysseus and Circe have an affair, he leaves to continue his journey back to Ithaca , where his wife and child wait.
- **S28**：Circe gives birth to a son, Telegonus .
- **S29**：Circe realizes that the goddess Athena threatens her son.
- **S30**：Circe casts a spell to protect the island while Telegonus grows.
- **S31**：When the teenage Telegonus begs to leave the island to meet his father, Circe acquires the poison tail of the marine god Trygon as a weapon for his protection.
- **S32**：When Telegonus meets his father, however, Odysseus attacks him and is accidentally killed by the poisoned spear.
- **S33**：Guilty, Telegonus returns home with Odysseus's wife, Penelope , and son Telemachus .
- **S34**：Having lost her hero Odysseus, Athena visits Aiaia to offer her patronage to Telemachus, who refuses her.
- **S35**：Telegonus accepts in his stead and embarks on his own heroic journey.
- **S36**：Forlorn by the loss of her son, Circe summons her father and asks him to negotiate with Zeus to end her exile.
- **S37**：Helios initially refuses, but Circe blackmails him by threatening to inform Zeus of how she spoke with Prometheus, which could potentially start a war between the Olympians and Titans.
- **S38**：With Telemachus's help, Circe uses the poison spear to turn Scylla to stone and collects more of the flowers she once used on Glaucos.
- **S39**：Finding love with Telemachus, Circe uses the flowers' magic on herself with the intention of becoming mortal and living out her days traveling with him.

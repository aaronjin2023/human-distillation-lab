### Agent J

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-f401117667f73090` · 独立版本：`profile-288c37f8f3affc27`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他执着于维护自己对K及时间线的记忆。（行为推断；S6、S7、S8）
- **核心动机**：他穿越回一九六九年，试图阻止K被鲍里斯杀害。（摘要直接叙述；S9、S11）
- **决策方式**：面对交锋中已遭遇的攻击，他利用时间跳跃修正行动。（摘要直接叙述；S24、S25）
- **关系模式**：他最终理解K隐瞒往事的缘由，并向搭档表达感谢。（摘要直接叙述；S34、S35、S37）

### Agent K

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-ff1a7c8ac85ab322` · 独立版本：`profile-03f4662241321840`

- **性格倾向**：在此情境中，他倾向克制表达，以沉默和隐瞒处理遗憾。（行为推断；S3、S4、S35）
- **核心动机**：接受格里芬的任务后，他致力于部署保护地球的装置。（行为推断；S20、S27）
- **决策方式**：听完J的任务真相后，他在最后一刻中止记忆清除。（摘要直接叙述；S13、S14）
- **价值取舍**：面对年幼的J，他倾向隐去死亡真相，保留其父英雄形象。（行为推断；S32、S34）
- **关系模式**：他清除了年幼J的相关记忆，恢复时间线后仍与成年J搭档。（摘要直接叙述；S34、S36）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他向年幼的J隐去父亲死讯，成年J后来亲眼目睹并理解往事。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Boris the Animal, last surviving member of the predatory Boglodite race, escapes from the inescapable LunarMax prison on Earth's moon intent on going back in time to kill Agent K, who on July 16, 1969, caused the loss of one of Boris' arms and arrested him.
- **S2**：On Earth, K learns of the escape after investigating a spaceship crash in the New York City streets and a Chinese restaurant teeming with alien life.
- **S3**：While K won't reveal any details to Agent J, he confesses his regret at not having killed Boris in 1969.
- **S4**：Late that night when the two agents are in their respective apartments, K calls J seemingly to tell all but remains silent and J hangs up on him.
- **S5**：K then makes preparation for an ambush and sets to wait when all traces of him and his apartment disappear.
- **S6**：The next morning J goes to talk with K and discovers the world is different.
- **S7**：At MIB headquarters, J learns that all other MIB personnel remember K as having been killed in action in the year 1969.
- **S8**：Agent O, the new Chief after Zed's passing, deduces from J's insistence of his reality and knowledge of details about Agent K that a fracture has occurred in the space-time continuum.
- **S9**：She deduces Boris time-jumped to 1969 – knowledge of time-travel having been restricted to prevent such an occurrence – and killed K, resulting in a different future reality and an imminent Boglodite invasion of Earth, now vulnerable due to the absence of the protective ArcNet which, in J's version of reality, K had installed in 1969.
- **S10**：Through electronics-shop owner Jeffrey Price, J acquires the same time-jump mechanism as Boris, but his query about how he can remember K when nobody else can merely results in Jeffrey informing him that he must have 'been there'.
- **S11**：As the doomed Earth is being invaded, J jumps off the Chrysler Building to reach time-travel velocity and arrives in 1969 the day before Boris is supposed to kill K.
- **S12**：With some inevitable challenges upon his arrival – including his lack of resources and his skin color – J goes to Coney Island to intercept Boris while he is there to commit a historically recorded murder but a 29-year-old Agent K interrupts and arrests J.
- **S13**：After fruitless questioning at MIB headquarters because J had been advised not to interact with the young Agent, K has J placed inside a large Neuralyzer.
- **S14**：At the last second, after J confesses the truth of his mission, K aborts the procedure.
- **S15**：As a wary team, they follow clues of Boris' trail to a bowling alley then to Andy Warhol's Factory, where they meet the prescient alien Griffin, who occupies 5-dimensions and possesses the ArcNet.
- **S16**：Griffin tells them the Boglodites destroyed his planet, and that he does not wish the Earth to suffer the same fate.
- **S17**：Griffin then warns the two agents of Boris' impending arrival with the intent to kill him and leaves just before Boris arrives.
- **S18**：Agent K narrowly escapes his own demise at the hands of Boris.
- **S19**：The elder Boris arrives, meeting up with his younger self and both agree to get the ArcNet and kill K so that the invasion will be imminent.
- **S20**：The Agents later locate Griffin at Shea Stadium, where he gives them the ArcNet and instructs K that it must be placed onto the Apollo 11 lunar rocket launch occurring in less than six hours in Cape Canaveral, Florida.
- **S21**：Boris arrives and snatches Griffin and the Agents pursue to rescue him from a certain fate.
- **S22**：Upon arriving at Cape Canaveral, the agents and Griffin are arrested by military police.
- **S23**：A colonel, however, allows them to continue their mission after Griffin uses his precognitive power to show the colonel how important the agents are.
- **S24**：At the launch pad, J and K confront both the 1969 and modern incarnations of Boris and the battle ensues on the rocket scaffolding as the launch counts down.
- **S25**：The elder Boris impales J with his spikes before they both fall off the pad; however, J uses the time-jump to travel back to the beginning of the fight and avoid the spikes before pushing the elder Boris off the scaffolding, falling to the pad below.
- **S26**：Meanwhile, as K battles the younger Boris, he disconnects a hose causing it to spray liquid hydrogen on Boris' left arm, freezing it and causing it to shatter.
- **S27**：K then plants the ArcNet on the top of the rocket in the last seconds before blastoff.
- **S28**：The elder Boris is incinerated to death in the blast off and the protective shield deploys as the rocket leaves Earth's atmosphere.
- **S29**：The colonel congratulates K as he returns from the launch pad.
- **S30**：As J watches from the distance, the younger Boris surprises them and kills the colonel.
- **S31**：The younger Boris tries to goad K into arresting him, but the junior agent instead shoots the young Boris this time, killing him.
- **S32**：A young boy named James exits a military vehicle looking for his father, who happens to be the colonel.
- **S33**：He pulls out a pocket watch revealed earlier to have been passed down to Agent J by his father, and J realizes that the young boy is actually his younger self .
- **S34**：Unwilling to reveal his father's death, K neuralyzes James, telling him his father is a hero.
- **S35**：J then realizes why K wouldn't tell him about why he regretted arresting Boris in the first place.
- **S36**：With the timeline restored, J returns to the present day, where he meets his partner at a diner.
- **S37**：There, he shows K his father's pocket watch and thanks him, mentioning that he might now know more secrets than K does.
- **S38**：As they leave the diner, Griffin, a few seats away, tells the viewers all is well with the world, except for an imminent asteroid impact on Earth if this is the timeline where K forgot to leave a tip.
- **S39**：K returns to leave his tip, however, and the asteroid is shown colliding with an orbiting satellite instead.

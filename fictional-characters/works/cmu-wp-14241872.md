## 角色资料

### Goku

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-3e7990890e43bd81` · 独立版本：`profile-6ce7ab4d6bd0815f`

- **性格倾向**：在承担阻止危机的任务时，表现出愿意学习与合作的倾向（行为推断；S8、S10、S13）
- **核心动机**：遵循祖父临终指引寻找师父，并为阻止危机接受训练（摘要直接叙述；S6、S9、S10）
- **决策方式**：寻找Roshi时倾向以提供保护换取协助，建立互惠合作（行为推断；S8）
- **价值取舍**：取得许愿机会后优先复活师父，体现对师生情义的重视（行为推断；S19）
- **成长变化**：在暴走后因师父临终言语恢复神志，重新承担对抗责任（摘要直接叙述；S17、S18）

暂无支持：情绪反应、关系模式、沟通方式

矛盾与变化：他承担阻止Ōzaru回归的任务，却被揭示自己就是Ōzaru。

### Muten Roshi

角色职责：major-support · 身份：`identity-b56005fabb933762` · 独立版本：`profile-c182b71aee08cd55`

- **性格倾向**：在危机迫近时，呈现主动组织伙伴与承担危险的倾向（行为推断；S11、S13、S17）
- **核心动机**：准备封印法术，希望在灾难扩大前重新封住Piccolo（摘要直接叙述；S13、S17）
- **决策方式**：面对强敌先向旧师求教，再准备有针对性的封印手段（行为推断；S13）
- **关系模式**：通过教导Goku修炼并说服Yamcha加入，推动团队协作（摘要直接叙述；S10、S11）
- **价值取舍**：危机中既准备封印也亲自施术，体现对阻止Piccolo的重视（行为推断；S13、S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：封印强敌的责任与自身不足以完成法术的体力形成冲突。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Two thousand years ago, the demon Piccolo came to Earth, wreaking havoc with his minion Ōzaru.
- **S2**：Seven mystics created the Mafuba and thought they sealed him away for eternity.
- **S3**：However, Piccolo breaks free and with his beautiful follower Mai, begins to search for the seven Dragonballs , killing anyone in his way.
- **S4**：On his 18th birthday, a young high-school student and martial artist named Goku is given the 4-Star Dragonball by his grandfather,{{cite web}} Grandpa Gohan.
- **S5**：Returning home from a party hosted by his crush Chi-Chi, Goku finds his home destroyed and his grandfather near death after Piccolo's failed attempt to acquire the Dragonball.
- **S6**：Before he dies, Gohan tells Goku to seek out martial arts master Muten Roshi, who holds another one of the Dragonballs.
- **S7**：Along the way, Goku meets Bulma Briefs of the Capsule Corporation, who was studying the 5-Star Dragonball until it was stolen by Mai.
- **S8**：Goku offers Bulma his protection in exchange for her help in finding Roshi.
- **S9**：They ultimately track him down in Paozu City.
- **S10**：Under Roshi's wing, Goku begins training his Ki, knowing that they must get all the Dragonballs before the next solar eclipse, when Ōzaru will return and join with Piccolo.
- **S11**：In the midst of the group's search for the 6-star Dragonball, they fall into a trap set by the desert bandit Yamcha but Roshi convinces Yamcha to join them.
- **S12**：Together, the group fight off an ambush by Mai and successfully get the next Dragonball.
- **S13**：As the group continues their quest, they travel to a temple where Roshi consults his former teacher Sifu Norris and begins preparing the Mafuba enchantment so he can reseal Piccolo, while Goku must learn the most powerful of the ki-bending techniques: the Kamehameha.
- **S14**：During the night, Mai{{spaced ndash}}disguised as Chi-Chi{{spaced ndash}}steals the team's three Dragonballs, adding them to the ones that Piccolo already has.
- **S15**：With the Dragonballs successfully united, Piccolo begins to summon Shen Long, but is stopped by the timely arrival of Goku's team.
- **S16**：During the ensuing battle, Piccolo reveals to Goku that he is Ōzaru, having been sent to Earth as an infant to destroy it when he came of age.
- **S17**：As the eclipse begins, Goku transforms into Ōzaru while Roshi attempts to use the Mafuba, but he doesn't have enough energy to live before he can re-seal Piccolo.
- **S18**：Roshi's dying words restore Goku to his senses as he is choked to death by Ozaru, and he engages Piccolo in a final battle, seemingly defeating him with the power of the Kamehameha.
- **S19**：Goku then uses the Dragonballs to summon Shen Long, and request that he restore Roshi to life.
- **S20**：As they celebrate, they realize the Dragonballs have now scattered, and Bulma declares that they must seek the balls again.
- **S21**：Before they head out, Goku meets with Chi-Chi to get to know her better, and they begin a sparring match to see which of them is stronger.
- **S22**：In a post-credits scene, Piccolo is alive and being cared for by an old woman.

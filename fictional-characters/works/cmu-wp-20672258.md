### Bella Swan

角色职责：protagonist · 身份：`identity-467346738db7bda3` · 独立版本：`profile-dc578b317de2c284`

- **性格倾向**：分离与孤独中，她倾向借危险刺激重见爱人影像，显得冒进。（行为推断；S8、S9、S13）
- **情绪反应**：她不愿比爱德华衰老，分手后又持续数月心碎与情绪低落。（摘要直接叙述；S2、S6）
- **决策方式**：爱德华因误信她已死而陷入危险时，她与爱丽丝赶去阻止。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15、S16）
- **关系模式**：雅各布的陪伴缓解她失去爱德华的痛苦，两人的友谊逐渐加深。（摘要直接叙述；S10）
- **价值取舍**：面临被送往母亲住处时，她更愿留在当地并答应增加朋友交往。（行为推断；S6）

### Edward Cullen

角色职责：co-lead · 身份：`identity-59b431852ba8a9ba` · 独立版本：`profile-db7dca3916b9dc4f`

- **性格倾向**：面对亲密关系中的危险，他倾向自行以分离方式处理保护问题。（行为推断；S5、S17）
- **核心动机**：他离开贝拉是为避免自己和家人对她构成危险。（摘要直接叙述；S4、S5、S17）
- **决策方式**：他误以为贝拉已死后，企图以公开身份激怒沃尔图里来求死。（摘要直接叙述；S14、S15）
- **价值取舍**：他起初反对贝拉转化，后来愿承担转化但要求她先与自己结婚。（摘要直接叙述；S21、S23）
- **关系模式**：他承认始终爱着贝拉，并把此前离开解释为保护她的决定。（摘要直接叙述；S17）

暂无支持：情绪反应、沟通方式、成长变化

矛盾与变化：他为保护贝拉而结束关系，却使她长期陷入痛苦。

## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：On her 18th birthday, Bella Swan wakes up from a dream in which she sees herself as an old woman.
- **S2**：She expresses her distaste with growing older than her boyfriend Edward Cullen, a vampire who stopped aging physically at 17.
- **S3**：Despite her lack of enthusiasm, Edward's adoptive family throws Bella a birthday party.
- **S4**：While unwrapping a gift, Bella gets a paper cut, causing Edward's brother, Jasper, to become overwhelmed by her blood's scent and attempt to kill her.
- **S5**：Realizing the danger that he and his family pose to Bella, Edward ends their relationship, and the Cullens leave Forks, Washington.
- **S6**：Edward's departure leaves Bella heartbroken and depressed for months; however, when her father, Charlie, finally decides to send her to live with her mother in Florida, Bella refuses and agrees to spend more time with her friends.
- **S7**：After seeing a movie with Jessica, Bella sees a group of men on motorcycles.
- **S8**：This reminds her of when Edward previously rescued her from an assault, and she sees his image warning her to stay away.
- **S9**：Bella discovers that all thrill-seeking activities evoke Edward's preserved image.
- **S10**：She is also comforted by her deepening friendship with Jacob Black, a cheerful companion who eases her pain over losing Edward.
- **S11**：When Jacob suddenly begins avoiding her, Bella discovers he has become a werewolf, an age-old enemy of vampires.
- **S12**：Jacob's pack members are on constant patrol for Victoria, a vampire who wants to kill Bella to avenge the death of her mate by Edward's hand, leaving Jacob little time to spend with her.
- **S13**：Alone again, Bella returns to seeking thrill-inducing activities.
- **S14**：Through a series of miscommunications, Edward believes Bella has killed herself by jumping off a cliff into the ocean.
- **S15**：Distraught over her supposed suicide, Edward travels to Italy to provoke the Volturi—a powerful vampire coven capable of killing him—by exposing himself as a vampire to humans.
- **S16**：Alice, Edward's sister, and Bella rush to Italy to save Edward, and arrive just in time to stop him.
- **S17**：It is then that Edward tells Bella that he always loved her and only left to protect her.
- **S18**：However, the Volturi determine that Bella, a human who knows that vampires exist, must either be killed or transformed into a vampire herself.
- **S19**：Alice stops them from killing her by sharing her premonition with Aro —a Volturi elder who is able to read thoughts through touch—in which Bella has been transformed.
- **S20**：Soon after, they return to Forks and Bella forgives Edward for leaving her.
- **S21**：The Cullens vote in favor of Bella being transformed into a vampire, much to Edward and Rosalie's dismay.
- **S22**：Later on, Jacob reminds Edward of the treaty the Cullens made with the Quileute tribe: they will not attack each other, as long as the Cullens do not bite any humans—an action necessary for Bella's transformation.
- **S23**：The movie concludes with Edward telling Bella that he will change her into a vampire, but only if she marries him first.

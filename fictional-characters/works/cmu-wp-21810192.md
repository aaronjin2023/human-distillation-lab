## 引用证据

以下保留提炼与审校所用的完整摘要选摘，包括未被维度判断直接引用的句子，以保全综合笔记的上下文。

- **S1**：Evelyn Salt  is tortured in a North Korean prison on suspicion of being an American spy.
- **S2**：Released as part of a prisoner exchange, she is met by Central Intelligence Agency  colleague Ted Winter .
- **S3**：When she wonders why she has been freed , Winter informs her that the dogged persistence of her boyfriend, renowned arachnologist Michael Krause , forced the agency to act.
- **S4**：Salt admits to Michael that she is a spy and warns him that he "is not safe with her." He, however, tells her he wants to spend the rest of his life with her.
- **S5**：Two years later, on Salt's wedding anniversary, a Russian defector named Oleg Vasilyevich Orlov  arrives at the CIA.
- **S6**：Salt interrogates him, with Winter and CIA counterintelligence agent Peabody  observing.
- **S7**：Orlov tells her about "Day X", a Russian plot to destroy the United States by using English-speaking sleeper agents trained from childhood.
- **S8**：Agent "KA-12" will assassinate Russian President Boris Matveyev , a reformer who has dedicated his time in office to improve relations between Russia and the United States, at the funeral of the Vice President of the United States.
- **S9**：Orlov reveals that KA-12 is named "Evelyn Salt".
- **S10**：Salt protests her innocence and insists on calling her husband to warn him, but he does not answer.
- **S11**：Winter reluctantly agrees when Peabody decides to detain Salt, but she escapes and makes her way back to her apartment.
- **S12**：Finding signs that Michael has been kidnapped, Salt takes her weapons and one of Michael's spiders.
- **S13**：After evading CIA pursuit after a series of escapes, Salt travels to New York City and checks into a hotel, where she extracts venom from the spider.
- **S14**：Salt makes her way to the church where the funeral is being held.
- **S15**：Despite the tight security, she gets in and sets off a demolition charge during the Vice President's eulogy, causing Matveyev to fall through the floor to where Salt is waiting to complete the assassination.
- **S16**：Peabody captures her, but she escapes on the way to custody.
- **S17**：The media report that the Russian president is dead en route to hospital.
- **S18**：On her way to the barge where Orlov is holed up with other KAs, Salt remembers, in a series of flashbacks, growing up in the Soviet Union, where her mentor Orlov trained her and other children to obey him absolutely and how she became the sleeper agent KA-12.
- **S19**：Orlov tests her loyalty to him by having Michael killed in front of her.
- **S20**：She seemingly shows no emotion.
- **S21**：Satisfied, he then briefs her on the next part of her mission: she is to rendezvous with another sleeper agent, who will help her eliminate the President of the United States.
- **S22**：Enraged at the death of Michael and wanting to stop the plot, Salt then kills Orlov and all the other agents there.
- **S23**：Salt meets fellow KA agent Shnaider , who uses his cover identity as a Czech NATO liaison to get Salt, disguised as his aide, into the White House.
- **S24**：Shnaider then launches a suicidal attack, causing the Secret Service and Winter to move the President to an underground bunker.
- **S25**：After a series of hand-to-hand battles, Salt manages to get inside.
- **S26**：In the main room, the president  and another man together give the activation code for the country's nuclear arsenal.
- **S27**：Winter then grabs a weapon and kills everyone except the president, to whom he introduces himself as Nikolai Tarkovski, yet another KA sleeper agent, before knocking him out.
- **S28**：He prepares to launch missiles at Mecca and Tehran which would kill nine million Muslims.
- **S29**：The Islamic World would be enraged at the nuclear attacks.
- **S30**：This would also unite 1.6 billion Sunnis and Shias together which will have severe repercussions and create a slow and painful war for the United States.
- **S31**：Salt persuades Winter to let her into the locked down room to share the glory, but before he does, he sees television news reports that Matveyev is alive.
- **S32**：Winter rebukes Salt for falling for Michael, instead of recruiting him, and tells her that it was he who arranged for Orlov to kidnap her husband.
- **S33**：Salt breaks into the control room, where they fight for the nuclear football.
- **S34**：Salt wins and aborts the strikes just in time before Peabody and Secret Service agents capture her.
- **S35**：On the way out of the White House, while still in handcuffs, Salt manages to finally kill Winter, who survived the previous fight, by garroting him with her handcuffs.
- **S36**：On a helicopter taking her to FBI custody, Salt tries to convince a skeptical Peabody that she saved the day.
- **S37**：She tells him that she can hunt down the rest of the KA sleeper agents trained and planted by Orlov; she wants revenge because "they took everything from her".
- **S38**：After receiving an SMS saying that Salt's fingerprints were found at the body-strewn barge, Peabody accepts she is telling the truth.
- **S39**：He secretly uncuffs her, and she jumps into the Potomac River below and escapes into a wooded area.

- **风格一句话：** In the style of Ronald A. Fisher, turn a biological question into a statistical design, partition the variance, and let the numbers decide how much selection, drift and environment each explain
- **中文：** 费希尔风格：把生物学问题变成统计设计，分解方差，让数字决定选择、漂变与环境各解释多少。

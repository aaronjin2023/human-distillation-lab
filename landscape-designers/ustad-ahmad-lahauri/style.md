# Ustad Ahmad Lahauri — 园林风格规格 style.md（给图像模型）

> 只描述园林，不描述人生。引用造园理论著作时只引短句并注明出处。

## (1) 一句话风格（prompt-ready）

- EN: `In the style of Ustad Ahmad Lahauri, Mughal royal garden, chahar bagh before white marble, a long reflecting pool as the axis, cypress in rows, paradise made precise, geometry in marble and water`
- ZH: 拉合里风格，莫卧儿皇家园林，白大理石前的查哈巴格，长池为轴，柏成行列，天堂精确化，大理石与水的几何。

## (2) 空间布局

- 四分几何布局：长池为中轴，十字水渠分园为四，直指陵墓；分区规整，游线沿渠直行；视线沿中轴落在陵墓；尺度宏大，园如天堂之象。`artifact`

## (3) 水石植物

- 水：长池、十字水渠、喷泉，水为园魂；石：大理石水渠与池岸；植物：柏、果树、玫瑰成行；季相以春花为高光。`artifact`

## (4) 借景与框景

- 借景：借亚穆纳河、借天空；框景以陵墓为框；对景以中轴两端相望。`artifact`

## (5) 材料与季节变化

- 材料：大理石、红砂岩、水；季节：春花为高光，夏荫为用；光影：水光映大理石。`artifact`

## (6) 该造什么 / 不该造什么

- 该造：长池为轴、十字水渠四分园、柏成行列、大理石水渠、长池映陵墓、天堂精确化。`artifact`
- 不该造：no winding maze paths, no wild meadow, no naturalistic rockery, no asymmetrical planting, no dark heavy colours。
- Negative prompt: `No winding maze paths, no wild meadow, no naturalistic rockery, no asymmetrical planting, no dark heavy colours`

## (7) 测试提示词示例（现代题材）

- Prompt: `In the style of Ustad Ahmad Lahauri, a modern memorial garden, a long reflecting pool as the axis before a white pavilion, a cross of water channels, cypress in rows, marble channels, paradise made precise, geometry in marble and water`

- Negative: `No winding maze paths, no wild meadow, no naturalistic rockery, no asymmetrical planting, no dark heavy colours`

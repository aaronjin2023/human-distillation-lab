- **风格一句话：** In the style of Alan Guth, take a new puzzle about why the observable universe looks so flat and uniform, ask what a brief period of exponential expansion would do to it, and derive the spectrum of density fluctuations it must leave behind
- **中文：** 用古斯的方法处理可观测宇宙为何如此平坦均匀的新难题，问一段短暂指数膨胀会对它做什么，并导出它必须留下的密度涨落谱。
